# Episode 18: The Westhouse

*Played 2026-10-23 (Zoom, about six and a half hours). Transcripts: `session-2026-10-23-westhouse.transcript.txt` and `-timestamped.txt`. In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 19. Gemma and Eric played; Dario was run at the table.*

The session opens at noon on Sunday in the pilgrim camp, in rain, with the Lazaret's bell ringing the hours. Dario has long-rested among the Matt Matheny followers and comes down from the wagon invigorated: *"Eric never told me what party animals these nerds could be."* Gemma asks him whether he should carry the Bright Dawn, a symbol of hope for the rebellion. He refuses. *"I carry Knell, the weapon of my wife's people. I have but two arms, Gemma."* He tells her she is the one who will carry the Bright Dawn across the horizon.

They talk over the plan. If they reach Uriel and attack her, she may have a failsafe that rings the bell, so they consider breaking the bell first. Gemma lays out her reading: the Doves have been infiltrated, the head of the army is at their door demanding the Emperor, and they must be frightened. She also floats a theory that the Lodge's unnamed buyer is Locke Pierce, moving the Eden units closer to Uriel.

## The camp

The table rules on *seeming*: the new spell cannot be dismissed, so Eric breaks it with a Charisma save (20, with guidance, against DC 18) and steps out of the tent as Eric the Cleric, Chancellor of Sharn, in his red outfit and collars. The counting-house staff who fled to the camp know him at once and crowd round: sub-auditor **Corvin Bleak**, the Professor, Steampunk Nerd Girl, Nerd Halfling, Marianne, Nelson, Elliot and Reginald. One hands him a burned CD. Another, who read his final filing for the Primary Grain Corporation, gives him a homemade **study aid**: drink it and read in a minute what would otherwise take a week. **Ilma Vitt**, still sore that he did not prosecute the fraud, tells him: *"There never was an empire. There was only us, the people. And we're taking it back."* She gives him an immaculate glass, gold and jewel-mosaic bowl.

Eric drinks the study aid and reads the whole of **Korn's folio** in a minute, which raises his Wisdom by 2 to **22**. He casts **heroes' feast** with the bowl for twelve: the three of them, the six of Matt Matheny's camp including Fima Nolan, and three leaders of the band. Everyone's hit point maximum rises by 12 for 24 hours, with immunity to poison and fear. The feast is a trifle in the great bowl. When the last of it is scooped out, Ilma smashes the bowl on the ground, everyone shouts *"Opa!"* and the band strikes up. The Matt Matheny people and the band will fight alongside the party anywhere within reach of the camp.

## The Stairs of Her Mercy

Gemma goes up as **General Thar**, with Eric as the red, horned druid **Majorbo** and Dario as the druid **Veg** ("Veggie Man"). At the foot of the stairs two white guards stand with a Sister of the Bone. Thar insists on her noon appointment (Performance 23) and the sister steps aside: *"General, please make your way."*

On the high landing stands the great statue, freshly plaqued **URIEL I**, with Elvish beneath: *By her mercy are we kept pure.* The plaque is new and still polished. A sister guiding a boy and his grandfather says the statue was a gift of the people of Aerenal to the Empress and the people of Karrnath, placed as a wedding gift nearly thirteen years ago. Eric sees that the landing was built over older stone. Gemma sees that the statue is not fixed to a plinth: it stands on two stone feet on a patterned platform, and the pattern is heavily scuffed, as if the figure had been set down on top of something.

At the Polygon, Eric hears it in his head (Wisdom save 27): *"She steals everything from me!"* It carries the same psychic weight as LeBeefe's device.

At the **Tomb of Kaius I** they read the inscription again: *I am the red star that shines at night. Do not stand at my grave and weep. I am not there. I did not die.* Eric (Investigation 21) finds a one-inch hole drilled through one of the O's, smooth and as old as the stone. With his new Wisdom (Religion 28) he sees what it is for: a vampire's mist goes in through the hole. The tomb is the Emperor's resting place, where he returns if he is destroyed. Dario wants to climb the tower and break the bell now. Gemma reads him (Insight 17) as passionate, not logical, and he agrees to wait.

## Nos Matre and the gardens

Thar throws open the nave doors. At the Threshold of Mercy fountain a novice says she knows nothing of the Emperor: *"You have the Mark of Finding, do you not? Certainly you can look for him yourself."* Past the eternal flame and the orrery, a fragment of the first temple on the site that is thousands of years older than the cathedral, they go down the stairs to the Imperial Gardens. A huge white knight stands under the Triumphal Gate. Thar tells him *"General Thar, here to see the Emperor,"* and he steps back as if commanded. Battle sisters salute.

Across the garden someone calls *"General Thar!"*: **Sister Mary Christmas**, very tall, with her two scythes, and **Sister Lus Fayina** in front of her, with doves and two white guards. Lus thanks the General for climbing the stairs and says the Emperor is in surgery this afternoon, which together with the Empress's condition rules out a meeting today. He might come back in ten to twelve days. The Empress is with child, and a new heir is expected any day. Thar asks to stay on site, and they offer hospitality in the **Westhouse**.

The Westhouse is a palace, Versailles-sized, with terrazzo floors and a fountain identical to the one in the nave, its inscription reading *None may harm those who come bearing.* Two doves drink from it and come away refreshed. A sister in a strange habit whips an old man in front of a crowd of young doves. Lus shows them to a vacant grand bedroom upstairs and lays down the convent's rules. She lifts her veil to show a precise cut across her cheek that has laid her jaw open: the consequence of her failure at the New Morning. *"If I had succeeded in my duty, the Empire would still control Sharn."* She commends Thar for coming to face the consequences of hers.

In the room they weigh *locate object* on Uriel's brain-diamond (they have only seen it in a recording) and *find the path*. Gemma reminds Dario that Lus Fayina is the woman who raped Eric.

## Saturnia, and Maelis Dorn

When they open the door, **Saturnia** is passing in tears, now in the **red robes of a Sister of the Blood**. *"Sisters of the Blood keep our tongues still."* They follow her to the stairwell, where **Maelis Dorn**'s people stop them: Lus, Mary Christmas and the two white knights. Inquisitor Dorn would like to see the General in her office. Dario plays the soldier: *"This is a mere priesthood. We represent the great power of this empire."* Lus laughs.

Upstairs is a classroom full of veiled elven sisters. There are many very tall women like Mary Christmas, and two **hooded sisters** in black with a glowing red heart, faces hidden, plainly undead. The acolytes are studying past the Liturgy of Her Mercy into rites of the Blood of Vol. Every living sister in the room is an elf. Saturnia sits down, takes off her red cloak and joins in, and a sister raises her scythe and withers her with a curse when she falters.

Dorn's office is small and sparse. Gemma knows her as the woman who taught her the liturgy. Dorn apologises: the Emperor's procedure could not wait, and it will *"prolong and enhance his life and lucidness."* The Empress is about to bear her **fourteenth child** (thirteen, if the twins count as one). Dorn offers to carry any non-military message personally and has already arranged a boat back to the capital. Then she turns on Thar over Aundair: the army lost the front to the orcs, the Doves may have to *"go to desperate measures to control the horde,"* the Admiral lost control of **Eden** and nobody can find her, and *"the troops you've lost will belong to us, and you will be a dust mote on the tapestry of history. So why don't you pack your things and get on the boat, old man."*

## The fight in the Westhouse

Eric opens it. *"Remember me?"* His true strike puts 25 into Lus Fayina, and she touches his face: *"Eric, you're such a good boy. How could you do this to mommy?"* Dario draws Palefire, shouts *"Death to the Black Doves,"* walks onto the table and puts 44 into Dorn. Dorn tolls the dead from the end of every turn. The two hooded sisters come out of the classroom and lay **Cradle of Pain** on Eric, pinning him. Gemma drops the staff, calls the Bright Dawn and goes for her old teacher. Dorn tries to banish her and fails on the save.

Mary Christmas raises her scythes in an X and calls **six risen guards** out of the ground. Eric raises his holy math symbol: *"But you forgot one thing. A negative times a negative is a positive."* His **turn undead** burns all six to nothing and sends the hooded sisters screaming back into the classroom. A seventh-level *mass healing word* follows. Dario hits Dorn for 63 with a smite. Gemma trips her twice. Dorn casts *dispel magic* on Thar and strips the *seeming*, and Gemma stands revealed as herself. Dorn screams *"Help! Help! Call the guards!"* Mary Christmas reaps Gemma prone. Gemma gets up, pins Dorn against a bookcase and stabs her in the gut: *"I was such a good student."* **Maelis Dorn dies**, and her blood runs down the shelves.

Lus Fayina flees down the corridor with Eric's spirit guardians on her, and Eric catches her in a bear hug in the next room. **Lus Fayina dies** in the radiant light. Dario turns on Mary Christmas, cuts her throat with one swing and takes her head off with the next. **The first Mary Christmas dies.**

In the classroom it turns out there are two Mary Christmases. Dario calls **Rum Tum**, hauls Eric up behind him with *lay on hands*, and the flying panther runs a lawnmower through the room with Eric's spirit guardians. Novices die in the aura. A second turn undead burns two hooded sisters to nothing. Gemma's free *hellish rebuke* finishes another. Eric heals Gemma at 26 hit points with a fourth-level *healing word*, and Dario brings Palefire down on **the second Mary Christmas** from Rum Tum's back and destroys her. The two white knights come through the door. Eric's last channel turns them, and Dario cuts them down; with radiant damage they get no undead fortitude. The two hooded sisters who had fled are cornered in a room where novices are chained in humiliating poses, and Dario and Eric kill them.

## What Saturnia knows

Saturnia begs for her life. Her first plea fails, but Gemma's intimidation (20, with advantage) gets the truth. She follows the light of Aerenal and finds what has been done with it here blasphemous. **Her grandfather is E'noch**, whom she has never met. He sent for her, she was cast out of Aerenal, and she was made a Sister of the Blood here for her final training. Eric persuades her to come with them (18, with guidance and the memory of Graham Cracker's voice): *"No matter what happens, the Emperor and the Empress will be dead. If not by dawn, then by the next one."* Dario: *"The rebellion is inevitable."*

What she tells them:

- **The girls in red are her cousins**, all from houses descended from the House of Vol. They are trained to tend the Emperor in his condition and to be reserves to bear his children or his offspring's. The Empress forbids anyone else to bear the first heir. After that, all of them are to be given to the heir: *"They say his lust will be insatiable."*
- **The anointing.** Anyone who sets foot on the palace grounds without the Sister of the Blood's ritual falls asleep like a babe, long enough for a guard to cut their throat. Saturnia knows the ritual and gives them red robes.
- **The Emperor is not a man.** He is the dragon called **the Crimson Sun**. Long ago he mated with **Minara Vol**, and their child was **Erandis Vol**, half-dragon and half-elf, whose blood amplified the Mark of Death beyond anything seen before. The dragons of Argonnessen and the elves of Aerenal exterminated the House of Vol and its cadet houses: **Qualanthri, Crona, Dwyn** and the half-dragons. The Crimson Sun disappeared early in the Purge. Her people's story is that the dragon was all but destroyed protecting Erandis and its essence reduced to a small artifact, kept secret for thousands of years by a few extremely old elves for a future in which Erandis could be brought back or her equal made.
- **Uriel's Mark of Death** is said to exceed any since Erandis and must cover most of her body by now. Saturnia hates this part of her family. Uriel embraces it.
- **E'noch is here, somewhere below the hospital.** The final initiation of a Sister of the Blood is to meet him. She expected to meet him this week. She does not know whether he is a vampire, but *"he is tremendously old."*
- More Sorrowful Sisters are in the library and the armoury, and more students in the next room.

## The student room and the Imperial Archive

Dario kicks in the next door and throws a ball of phlogiston into a room of cowering students. Most die, and the party kills the rest, including a hooded sister. The door beyond opens on the **Imperial Archive**: more sisters, and **Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin**, Deirdre's handler, with a pistol-carrying attaché. A man's voice shouts *"Wait for them to get in, then we make a break for it!"* A hooded sister crits Dario off Rum Tum, unconscious. Eric gets him back up with a fourth-level *cure wounds*. Gemma *misty steps* to Merkin, who does not know her in this form, and kills him: *"Your Star Advocate failed her mission, sir."* Eric stabs a sister under the chin. The last one runs, tries to wrestle the Bright Dawn out of Gemma's hand, and fails. Dario rides up behind her and puts Palefire down through her shoulder.

The archive was once the Emperor's own office and record room, before the Doves turned the Westhouse into an academy. It holds the most private records of the Imperium: titles, treaties, births and deaths, military hardware, maps, and files on the Doves and the Emperor himself. Dario strips Dorn, Lus and Mary Christmas and puts them in his bag of holding. Merkin, who carries his credentials, clearance papers, seal and pistol, goes into another bag with his attaché.

Eric (Investigation 19) and Saturnia find records from before the war of the King of Galifar's attempts to cure his ailing son, the future Kaius I, and of successful treatments by an elvish shaman called **E'noch**. The records stop suddenly. The party already knows the rest from Sharn: E'noch came back with Varyn Crona, the child's eternal guardian, and they bound Varyn's soul into the Crimson Sun at the Forge of Binding.

Behind a book's leather cover is a bundle of **letters in a very old high Elvish**, written to be sent back to Aerenal and never sent. They are unsigned. Gemma reads them:

- The dragon died in the Purge, and its essence was reduced to an artifact that the houses of **Dwyn, Crona and Qualanthri** kept for millennia, waiting to bring back Erandis Vol and the ascendancy of the Mark of Death. It could not be resurrected, so the houses put its essence into an infant, trusting that over time the dragon would shed the human form and take its natural shape as an ancient dragon.
- *"Crona and I offered the king of Galifar a cure for his ailing heir... We did not tell him that his child would be replaced, killed, and turned into a vessel for the dragon."* A later letter: *"The child has woken with the full consciousness of itself as the dragon."*
- To commit itself to a human body, the Crimson Sun invested a large piece of its power in a binding ritual with the sword Crona carried. The writer worries this will compound Crona's power.
- *"I have undergone the rite of Cain... I have killed my sire, and I am immortal."* The writer has made a new form of the blood that mortals can take, which spends their life faster toward undeath and control.
- The transformation was not coming, and the Dragon Emperor feared he would live and die as Kaius. *"He would be my thrall, unless I feed him in turn... Crona will bind me and hold his sword at my throat, until His Majesty rises, comes and drinks of me... Even as a true vampire, he will be obsessed with my blood."*

Gemma guesses that the writer is **Gabrielle Dwyn**, Uriel's old superior and the secret rebel. Eric points out it could as easily be E'noch.

## Where it stands

The party is in the bloodied Imperial Archive of the Westhouse, about 300 feet from the palace, with Saturnia as an ally, the bodies hidden and one door still unopened. The leadership of the Black Doves is gone. Their plan for next time is to go to the residence as elven sisters with Saturnia: elves, because the Empress admits only family and the undead who serve her. The cover story is that Thar turned on the sisters, killed many of them and escaped, and is probably working with Eden. They are low on resources: Eric has no channels left and few high slots, and Gemma is at about half. The fountain is downstairs. At the very end, Dave confirmed what Eric has suspected all along: **drinking from the fountains gives a long rest**. Gemma is still wary of what it might do once the bell rings, and Eric says he will drink anyway: *"What's it gonna do? Turn me into a talking octopus?"*

Gemma, at the end of the night, is not sure there is a child at all, and if there is, what it is. *"I don't know what my fox friend is expecting if there's no child."*
