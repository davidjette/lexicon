The counting-house staff who fled to the camp know him at once and crowd round: sub-auditor **Corvin Bleak**, the Professor, Steampunk Nerd Girl, Nerd Halfling, Marianne, Nelson, Elliot and Reginald. One hands him a burned CD. Another, who read his final filing for the Primary Grain Corporation, gives him a homemade **study aid**: drink it and read in a minute what would otherwise take a week. **Ilma Vitt**, still sore that he did not prosecute the fraud, tells him: *"There never was an empire. There was only us, the people. And we're taking it back."* She gives him an immaculate glass, gold and jewel-mosaic bowl.

Eric drinks the study aid and reads the whole of **Korn's folio** in a minute, which raises his Wisdom by 2 to **22**. He casts **heroes' feast** with the bowl for twelve: the three of them, the six of Matt Matheny's camp including Fima Nolan, and three leaders of the band. When the last of it is scooped out, Ilma smashes the bowl on the ground, everyone shouts *"Opa!"* and the band strikes up. The Matt Matheny people and the band will fight alongside the party anywhere within reach of the camp.

## Nos Matre and the gardens

Across the garden someone calls *"General Thar!"*: **Sister Mary Christmas**, very tall, with her two scythes, and **Sister Lus Fayina** in front of her, with doves and two white guards. A sister in a strange habit whips an old man in front of a crowd of young doves. Lus shows them to a vacant grand bedroom upstairs and lays down the convent's rules. She lifts her veil to show a precise cut across her cheek that has laid her jaw open: the consequence of her failure at the New Morning. *"If I had succeeded in my duty, the Empire would still control Sharn."* She commends Thar for coming to face the consequences of hers.

In the room they weigh *locate object* on Uriel's brain-diamond (they have only seen it in a recording) and *find the path*. Gemma reminds Dario that Lus Fayina is the woman who raped Eric.

## What Saturnia knows

Saturnia begs for her life. Her first plea fails, but Gemma's intimidation (20, with advantage) gets the truth. She follows the light of Aerenal and finds what has been done with it here blasphemous. **Her grandfather is E'noch**, whom she has never met. He sent for her, she was cast out of Aerenal, and she was made a Sister of the Blood here for her final training. Eric persuades her to come with them (18, with guidance and the memory of Graham Cracker's voice): *"No matter what happens, the Emperor and the Empress will be dead. If not by dawn, then by the next one."* Dario: *"The rebellion is inevitable."*

What she tells them:

- **The girls in red are her cousins**, all from houses descended from the House of Vol. They are trained to tend the Emperor in his condition and to be reserves to bear his children or his offspring's. The Empress forbids anyone else to bear the first heir. After that, all of them are to be given to the heir: *"They say his lust will be insatiable."*
- **The anointing.** Anyone who sets foot on the palace grounds without the Sister of the Blood's ritual falls asleep like a babe, long enough for a guard to cut their throat. Saturnia knows the ritual and gives them red robes.
- **The Emperor is not a man.** He is the dragon called **the Crimson Sun**. Long ago he mated with **Minara Vol**, and their child was **Erandis Vol**, half-dragon and half-elf, whose blood amplified the Mark of Death beyond anything seen before. The dragons of Argonnessen and the elves of Aerenal exterminated the House of Vol and its cadet houses: **Qualanthri, Crona, Dwyn** and the half-dragons. The Crimson Sun disappeared early in the Purge. Her people's story is that the dragon was all but destroyed protecting Erandis and its essence reduced to a small artifact, kept secret for thousands of years by a few extremely old elves for a future in which Erandis could be brought back or her equal made.
- **Uriel's Mark of Death** is said to exceed any since Erandis and must cover most of her body by now. Saturnia hates this part of her family. Uriel embraces it.
- **E'noch is here, somewhere below the hospital.** The final initiation of a Sister of the Blood is to meet him. She expected to meet him this week. She does not know whether he is a vampire, but *"he is tremendously old."*
- More Sorrowful Sisters are in the library and the armoury, and more students in the next room.

The archive was once the Emperor's own office and record room, before the Doves turned the Westhouse into an academy. It holds the most private records of the Imperium: titles, treaties, births and deaths, military hardware, maps, and files on the Doves and the Emperor himself. Dario strips Dorn, Lus and Mary Christmas and puts them in his bag of holding. Merkin, who carries his credentials, clearance papers, seal and pistol, goes into another bag with his attaché.

Eric (Investigation 19) and Saturnia find records from before the war of the King of Galifar's attempts to cure his ailing son, the future Kaius I, and of successful treatments by an elvish shaman called **E'noch**. The records stop suddenly. The party already knows the rest from Sharn: E'noch came back with Varyn Crona, the child's eternal guardian, and they bound Varyn's soul into the Crimson Sun at the Forge of Binding.

Behind a book's leather cover is a bundle of **letters in a very old high Elvish**, using even Sylvan vocabulary, written to be sent back to Aerenal and never sent. They are unsigned, written in an informative, matter-of-fact, almost ceremonial hand, documenting these truths. Gemma reads them. From the letters she learns that the dragon did die in the Purge, and its essence was reduced to a tiny fraction in an artifact that the houses of **Dwyn, Crona and Qualanthri** protected for millennia. Their goal and their prophecy was to bring back Erandis Vol and the time when the Mark of Death was ascendant, when they could come out of the shadows. The nearly destroyed dragon could not be resurrected, so the elves of these houses devised a plan to place its essence in an infant, giving the dragon full domination over a mortal body, in the confidence that over time it would shed its human form and take on its full and natural form as an ancient dragon.

The letters, verbatim:

> *Crona and I offered the king of Galifar a cure for his ailing heir. We told the king some of what would follow, including that his son would become very powerful and effectively immortal. We did not tell him that his child would be replaced, killed, and turned into a vessel for the dragon.*

> *The child has woken with the full consciousness of itself as the dragon.*

> *To commit itself to a human body, the Crimson Sun will invest its power in a binding ritual with a terrific sword carried by Crona. This will invest a large piece of His Majesty's power. While it is weak, I am worried this will compound Crona's power and influence.*

> *I have stayed in the background. In my own time, I have undergone the rite of Cain to expend my life and experiment with true blood magic. I know you will not like this decision, but it is the right one. I have killed my sire, and I am immortal. I do not enslave others freely, but I have experimented with my blood. And I have developed a new form of it, which can be taken by mortals and expend their life more quickly, speeding them toward undeath and in control.*

> *The Dragon Emperor understands that his transformation is not going to happen, and that he might live and die as Kaius, a little longer lived than most mortals. He has asked Crona and I for a solution. I have put one forward. He knows the risks. He would be my thrall, unless I feed him in turn. He has made the terms very clear. Crona will bind me and hold his sword at my throat, until His Majesty rises, comes and drinks of me. I will be true to my word. But even as a true vampire, he will be obsessed with my blood.*

That is the end of the letters.

Gemma guesses that the writer is **Gabrielle Dwyn**, Uriel's old superior and the secret rebel. Eric points out it could as easily be E'noch.

## Where it stands

The party is in the bloodied Imperial Archive of the Westhouse, about 300 feet from the palace, with Saturnia as an ally, the bodies hidden and one door still unopened. The leadership of the Black Doves is gone. Their plan for next time is to go to the residence as elven sisters with Saturnia: elves, because the Empress admits only family and the undead who serve her. The cover story is that Thar turned on the sisters, killed many of them and escaped, and is probably working with Eden. They are low on resources: Eric has no channels left and few high slots, and Gemma is at about half. The fountain is downstairs. At the very end, Dave confirmed what Eric has suspected all along: **drinking from the fountains gives a long rest**. Gemma is still wary of what it might do once the bell rings, and Eric says he will drink anyway: *"What's it gonna do? Turn me into a talking octopus?"*

Gemma, at the end of the night, is not sure there is a child at all, and if there is, what it is. *"I don't know what my fox friend is expecting if there's no child."*
