# Dead Man's Hand: The Road to Skullport

*Played 2026-09-26 (in person, one player on Zoom, about four hours). Transcripts: `audio1987111329.transcript.txt` and `-timestamped.txt` in this folder. Dead Man's Hand has no episode numbering (CANON 5ab).*

**At the table:** BattleBeast, Barold Bonds, Maverick, Lenore with her steel defender Johnny, Heyu, and Tripp Sandhill. Shadowhigh and Rodriel do not appear in the recording.

## Summary

The last session ended with the party in possession of the Company's book, O-3255, and the STAR card, going down the stairs from the Arcane Chambers. The stair winds a long way, turning back and forth before it settles southward, and the air grows damp, mossy and fishy. The water is fresh, which tells them there is an underground river on this level. Their job is to get out of Undermountain. The Company's letter sends them to Skullport, reachable from the third level, to hand the book to their contact at the standard rendezvous. Maverick knows from Waterdeep what Skullport is: a rogue city under the city, built where a great underground river meets an underground sea, the Winking Depths, and held by the Xanathar Guild. They argue over whether to backtrack to the maze they questioned on the first level, and instead follow the river west. On the way they establish what they still carry. Everyone who was hanged at Fort Maximillien lost everything they had on them. Among the survivors' things are a ticket to Buttsniv City and the portal they used to reach Saltmarsh. Maverick's portal key, won gambling at the casino, is gone with the rest of his things.

Heyu leads with a light on the wand, tapping the walls. The first halls are a tomb, dusty and cobwebbed despite the damp, reeking of old decay and burned incense. The construction and the incense mark it as dwarven, and a religion check puts it in the faith of Moradin. The dwarves held a monotheism in which Brightmantle was a heresy, possibly because the obliviation of Brightmantle made the worship of lesser gods look like heresy or madness. The Infanta of that faith was **Mora**, whom an older party fought at the Battle of the River Lis, where she had allied with the orcs.

Past the tomb lies a rough-hewn cavern thirty feet high holding an ancient underground settlement of low stone buildings, most of them roofless and picked clean. An earthquake long ago collapsed whole sections and cut the town in two. Webs hang between every building, and one room holds recently hatched egg sacs. BattleBeast, leading in the dark with *foresight* from Heyu on him, hears three giant spiders coming. In the fight Barold's wild magic surrounds him with illusory butterflies and flower petals, and a spider webs BattleBeast, who slashes himself free and tears the stunned one apart. Maverick wants to split the party. It splits for a few minutes. BattleBeast and Tripp meet two more spiders alone, which flee, and then Heyu opens a door on three more that drop from the ceiling, poison her and pull back to the walls. Barold's cleave hits two at once, and Heyu's *shocking grasp*, a natural twenty, bursts one across the wall. BattleBeast runs the last fugitive down and beats it against a wall until it comes apart. Nine spiders are dead.

A smell of meat stew leads to a kitchen where a **bugbear** is chained to the wall and **three goblins** shackled together by the ankles are cooking, under three male drow guards. A fourth drow is in the water closet, and Maverick opens the door on him. When the party asks the way to Skullport the drow draw steel. BattleBeast punches one to death, Barold breaks another's skull with his bat, and a third drops magical darkness over the room. Lenore, feeling her way through it, collides with a goblin and the stew goes all over the floor. Johnny clotheslines the drow who runs, and Tripp finishes him with *true strike* and a dagger. The drow from the latrine answers the door with his sword in hand and escapes into a second darkness. The male drow own nothing but their light chain shirts, and Barold takes one.

The freed bugbear offers to take them to his boss, **Azrock**, who knows the way to Skullport. This whole side of the ruined town was Azrock's until the drow came: a house led by a red-headed priestess who calls her people **House Wendy's** (canon: Dave, 2026-09-28). The prisoners had been caught stealing supplies, and the drow drained one of them, **Blinky**, for days with spider venom. The party lets the prisoners arm themselves with the drow's hand crossbows and rapiers and declines to join the war. Maverick, coming out of the latrine, reminds everyone that he descends from drow. On the way south they pass the river bank, where rafts are moored and the current runs west, and the goblins lead them into another transplanted dwarven hall, canted slightly out of line with the rock around it. One goblin steps on a pressure plate and is crushed by a falling block. Lenore rolls a natural twenty to find the plates and walks everyone through them.

The guides point the way. A hole too small for anything but a goblin leads toward Azrock. The river goes to Skullport, but it swallows you and passes you out onto the ground or into the water. A long tunnel to the south also goes to Skullport, with worse things on it. The goblins expect to be beaten for getting captured, and say the beatings are what keep goblins motivated. Otherwise they'd sit around all day smoking, drinking, laughing and having babies. One of them wants that life, and a fig tree to sit under. Maverick promises that if they come and make it out, they can sit under their own trees, and a persuasion roll of 20 wins them over. The bugbear goes back to Azrock alone. Before he goes, the goblins let slip that Azrock was born blind and can somehow see, and that he has lost the magic dagger that makes him so strong. The party tells the bugbear about the dead bugbears they found earlier with sunken, empty eye sockets. The bugbear says those are Xanathar's, and that Xanathar can take a creature's brain out and put in another. He hurries off to warn Azrock that his own bugbears may be spies. The two goblins who stay are brothers, **Waddle** and **Snood**.

Just past the fork a dozen **hobgoblins** pour out of the western cave, alerted by the bugbear, and order the party to stop. BattleBeast tells their captain that the entrails of their enemies are strewn through everything north of here and that the blood on his claws should prove it, rolling 25 on intimidation. The captain wants to know whether they have come to take the territory and asks for help against the drow. BattleBeast refuses, and the captain demands the goblins back. Waddle answers that they are free and thinking men: *"If you prick us, do we not bleed?"* The captain lets them go and tells Waddle and Snood never to come back. The tunnel runs a couple of miles, in and out beside the river. The party makes a long rest along it, and on watch they see hobgoblin and bugbear patrols pass in both directions without spotting them.

They come out through Beggar's Rest Pass into an immense cavern. Skullport stands on three levels around Skull Island, where the sluggish river Sargauth runs out into the underground sea. The island is a fortress of the Xanathar Guild, garrisoned by hundreds of duergar and humans in dirty dark armour under the Xanathar's banners, with ballistae on the walls and thirteen flameskulls circling the city at different heights. A hungry **kuo-toa** squats at the water's edge. The party gives it a cantaloupe from the drow kitchen, and it dives in and comes back with a wet leathery thing like a manta ray's wing, with a hood and a tail: a **cloak of the manta ray**. Maverick puts it on. BattleBeast holds his breath and dives after him to scout a way across, and in the dark water two scavenger **merrows** attack them with harpoons. Tripp dives in, and the undersea fight turns on who can breathe. Maverick fills one merrow's head with a crackling cage, BattleBeast finishes it, and BattleBeast bounds back up onto the dock. The last merrow crits Maverick with its harpoon and vanishes into the deep with the harpoon still in his leg.

Duergar on the towers shout that there is no swimming and no pedestrian access this way. A rowboat brings two human thugs and a duergar lieutenant, who wants the Guild's cut of any business done in Skullport. The party's business is at the Flagon and the Dragon. They pay him a single gold coin. They are ferried into the island harbour, whose mouths can be closed with steel augers raised on chains to hole passing ships, past the barracks and the **Tower of the Seven Woes**. They get a light pat-down, the guards eyeing Barold and his bat and hardly bothering with the smaller members of the party, and are let through the main gate onto the Arkstone Bridge into the city, whose middle section is rigged to collapse. The session ends there, with the party making for the Flagon and the Dragon on the lower level.
