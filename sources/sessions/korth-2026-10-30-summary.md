# Episode 19: The Dinner Party

*Played 2026-10-30 (Zoom, about five and a half hours). In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 20. Gemma and Eric played; Dario was run at the table.*

Saturnia agrees to help the party against her grandfather E'noch. Under *seeming* as Maelis Dorn, Lus Fayina and Mary Christmas, the party bluffs Sister Mortis, drinks from the Westhouse fountain and goes down through the concourse, sending battle sisters toward cannon fire on the western beaches. The Empress is at three centimetres.

In the hospital they pass Esther Crona, alive on a table, with a glowing red ruby where her heart should be. A button in the morgue opens a gate to the vampires' lair. They crash a feast of vampires, find an ancient structure stuffed with bones down a tunnel, then lose Saturnia and find her with E'noch Qualanthri, Lady Ophelia Qualanthri and Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn beside a pool of blood holding the gold dragon Aurorix.

E'noch nearly kills Gemma twice. Dario wakes the dragon, the vampires are beaten and go to mist, and the party destroys them in their coffins. Gemma kills E'noch with the Bright Dawn. Aurorix says Saturnia bears the Mark of Death and should die, and Gemma refuses. On the way back Esther asks where Calcifer is. Gemma answers: "Alive." Eric sees a mummified heart in a jar.

They end at the Westhouse fountain with a warning bell ringing.
