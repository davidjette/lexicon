# Episode 21: The Reckoning of the Mother

*Played 2026-11-13 (Zoom, about eight and three-quarter hours). In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 22. Gemma and Eric played; Dario was run at the table.*

Flying at the ringing bell on Aurorix, Gemma calls up the circle of power in Zero-One's pendant. The bell's second toll drops the dragon out of the sky. Eric carries Gemma to the belfry by dimension door, and Dario breaks the Original with Knell; the tower falls. Eric, deafened, leaps from the tower onto Uriel ("You want to meet my son? I keep him warm") and is thrown to the ground. Gemma burns out her wand on Uriel's belly and feels her patron's anger.

In the forecourt the Polygon opens, and the dead and a purple-eyed Eden turn on the party at Uriel's word. Gemma trips Uriel out of the air and Dario takes her head ("after the long night comes a brighter dawn"). Her remains sink into the ground. Eric revives Aurorix, who names the Sept of Bones, then uses the summon dragon Hopper stored in a ring for him and calls Valtrex, and the two dragons go for each other. Gemma and Eric go down alone.

In the Sept of Bones a spectral Uriel is joined by a cord to the Heir of Vol, which breathes black fire and grows. Esther Crona arrives to kill Uriel and is stunned, bled and fed to the Heir. Above, the Polygon is struck ("Ask LeBeefe. I stole it from him"). Dario comes down near death with the greatberry and the news that the Polygon will blow. Gemma's summoned fox, Lalo Kitsune, cuts the cord ("The child is mine now"), Uriel's magic leaves her, and Eric destroys her with the mace of disruption. The freed Heir heals harder from every wound, and the fox changes his mind. They run.

Joseph Roberts lifts them off the cathedral roof in the Revenge with Zero-One, Ilma Vitt and Matheny's survivors. Valtrex has killed Aurorix and flies for the Korramont with Jelton Ahn and Madame Tso. A ship of the Hundreds drops a red gem into the storm, and the Lazaret, the valley and the Heir are gone in a mushroom cloud.
