# Episode 17: The Pilgrims Camp

*Copied 2026-10-17 from C:/dev/sharn-campaign/session-2026-10-16-summary.md (player-safe recap).*

*Played 2026-10-16 (Zoom, about two hours and forty-five minutes). Transcripts: `session-2026-10-16-pilgrims-camp.transcript.txt` and `-timestamped.txt`. In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 18. A short session with no fights, played straight on from Episode 16. Gemma and Eric for the first half; Dario's player was back for the second.*

The session opens at dawn on Sunday, on the river. Gemma and Eric are rowing Fora Dollar's canoe back across from the King's Forest after killing General Stockton Thar and meeting Eden on the peak. Dollar did not come back with them, and nobody went looking for him after the gunfire. He most likely died with his 1,000 gold in his pocket. They now know where his boat is kept: a good elvish canoe with a couple of oars, which may be useful on a peninsula.

## The sending

Before they push off, Gemma uses the sending stone Joseph Roberts gave her on the lightning rail, the day they killed Greenish Hughes. She speaks in her own voice and keeps it guarded, in case anyone is listening: she is the new visitor with the new stone, she thinks she is near, she had Pork Broccoli recently, she is near the cabin, and she looks a little different. How can she find him? About ten minutes later the answer comes back: *"If that's true, come to the library. Not safe to message."*

They have time to long rest before the noon audience. They decide to see Roberts first.

## The library

They come into the Lodge dirty, tracking leaves and mud, as the hunters are going to bed. Bimples tells Deirdre that the library is for members only, but that the Lodge has a lending programme if she names a title. She asks for *Attack on the Lightning Rail: The Death of Greenish Hughes*, says she has to return it to someone before she turns in, and tips him ten gold. He goes through the Red Bull room, turns left and comes back with the librarian's regrets and an alternative selection. It is in Gnomish, which both of them read, and it falls open at an illustration of the gnomish reproductive system: a book on how to conceive. Inside it is a parchment carrying Roberts's seal, a mark of some kind with nothing written on it.

Gemma changes her look without dropping the *seeming*: with her mask of many faces she shrinks Deirdre's ears and makes her hair long, black and curly. In hunting clothes they walk through a room where the members from the ballroom are singing an old song, something like a college song, and into a very old library. The books are mostly decorative, hunting manuals beside great volumes of members and classes that are the club's own records, which is what Deirdre was assigned to examine. Gemma's search (26) finds no sign of the seal, but there are tracks on the floor where a bookshelf slides. Eric knocks on it. One of the books is drawn into the shelf and an eye appears in the gap. *"I'm here to return your book."* Gemma holds up the seal. After a long second the shelf slides aside onto a door.

Behind it are three well-dressed swashbucklers she has never seen, who draw their weapons. *"We don't know who you killed or arrested to get that. But we think you should return it and leave this members-only area, Advocate Moro."* She says she is returning it to the man she met on the train, and they bring her in: a plain room with a square table covered in cards, cups and soup, and a pile of black silk strips. Eric asks whether they know who they are. They answer that they are loyal subjects and that she is an officer of the Empire, and they want to know how she came by property of the Grand Lodge. The book is stamped as Lodge property inside the front cover. When Gemma says the librarian lent it to someone else, they say that if she is holding that someone else they are willing to negotiate for her release. They warn her that her presence has drawn a lot of attention, and that it is presumptuous of her to come to a private club about member dues and trifles when such huge things threaten the Empire. They blindfold the two of them, Gemma tying her own, and lead them to stools in another room.

Sharp heels approach and a woman speaks. *"I noticed you went out with Master Hunter Fora. Where is he?"* He has been a member of the Lodge for eighty years. Gemma tells her the truth: he helped them hunt a giant bird that turned into General Thar, he helped them kill Thar and his druids, he left, and there was gunfire where he went. The room laughs. The woman is **the Fieldmaster**, and the club is hers. She never took Advocate Moro for an assassin of another Imperial officer, but Moro has come to the Lodge asking questions and leaving with beloved hunters, and they still cannot understand why she is in their little society. Gemma asks whether she expects her good business to last for ever. *"Not because they're searching for anything. Not because you're under their eye. It's because death is coming."*

Each of them circles the other without naming anyone. Gemma asks what they have done with Gemma Corso. The Fieldmaster will not admit to anything in front of an Imperial officer who has embedded herself in a business that runs on smuggling and war profiteering. Gemma spends a lucky on Persuasion and gets 13. In the end she drops the *seeming* on both of them. *"Do you morons not know who we are? We're fucking rebels."*

## Joseph Roberts

The room is taken aback. Then there is laughing and clapping, and a familiar voice: the Dread Pirate Captain **Joseph L. Roberts**. *"Deirdre Moro. That is a marvelous face."* The blindfolds come off. He is tired, relaxed and sitting back, while his men keep their hands on their weapons. *"Now why would you come here in that disguise and scare everyone in my entire organization? Really bad form. We almost sent someone in to kill you many times."*

Gemma gives him what she has: her Korth report on him, what she found at the Counting House, the bell report and the Ruby Nova report. *"Every empire ends in an estate sale,"* he tells her. *"I'm simply early."* He assumes they have come to do the deed: if not one of the Emperor's own generals, then someone is going to kill the Emperor. Gemma tells him Uriel is pregnant now. As he sees it, the succession will fall to the Iron Veil or the like, and what will really happen is that Locke Pierce blows everything up and the orcs take what is left.

He explains the Lodge. It was a good investment because of its grandfathered safe harbours. Enough of the Emperor's cousins were members that he and the kings before him kept it exempt from certain reporting and licensing requirements. Its boats travel up and down the river without stopping for every taxing authority, and his methods are valuable for getting things into and out of the Lazaret. The Doves have no chapels where he lives. The orcs, he says, have a tremendous amount of gold, perhaps from the demons, perhaps confiscated across Aundair, and they need materiel. He likes hard currency and can arrange it here. He appreciates the forewarning of something big.

*"But you disappoint me. You did say that you'd accepted my offer. Was that just a ruse to get behind my bookshelf?"* Gemma asks what it entails. *"Perhaps the Empress will do our ceremony."*

He greets Eric as Chancellor. His wife Blair still speaks very ill of Eric and of what was done at that council, although it was *"that repugnant dragon man"* who arrested her. *"Still feel very much in charge of your city?"* Eric tells him that as Sharn goes, so goes the continent, and that right now Locke Pierce is in charge. Roberts did not expect what Eric did in front of all the arcane eyes, and at Thronehold he was completely taken by surprise: *"You put a finger right in their eyes."* Eric says they are going to kill the Emperor and Uriel and may need his help with Locke Pierce. Roberts tells them he once tormented Uriel and Esther, back when they were friends with a vile bureaucrat from the middle of nowhere. Gemma reminds him that they killed that man in Newham, and that Roberts helped them move his people. He has bought every one of his wives an island.

He can see they are serious, and he expects them to do a lot of damage around here, which he thinks is great, but it means his time here is over. He tells the Fieldmaster to take up their buyer this morning and collect the payment. Someone is buying the Lodge, and he will not say who. *"We intend to leave the place a cinder, so caveat emptor."* They will be gone within a couple of hours. *"There's no one in the Lazaret to save. This isn't Leef. This whole place should go."*

Gemma reminds him that he played the violin for her on his island, and she writes out the fanfare of dawn for him: if a bell rings, this can be played. *"I'm not going to be anywhere near a bell when it rings."* She tells him what they have done (LeBeefe, the witch in the tower, Esther *dead-ish*) and asks what luxury will mean to him when the dead are all that is left. He has been around a very long time. He is proud of the people they saved together, many of whom owe him big favours now, and he hates the Empire's people, Uriel most of all: *"racist and stupid."* Gemma accepts him: *"I will gladly marry you, and you can give me an island, as long as I have freedom. If I make it through this."* *"Well, I suppose we'll see if you keep that promise."*

He will not go up the hill. A psychic he trusts has warned him that this place is bad for him. He will leave a good ship with good men behind, and she can call on them through her stone. He warns her that messages in the valley are a fairly new problem. The big ugly polyhedron over the cathedral is a new magical device. A few smaller ones went up outside powerful headquarters, and this is the biggest of them. Soon after they appeared, messages stopped arriving or arrived changed, and people were arrested and followed. He would not trust anything like it, and the Lazaret is sealed with *private sanctum* besides.

His men are already carrying out gold, bars, jewels, papers and luxury goods through a back door. Gemma tells him that at some point he will hear that the Emperor and his wife have been assassinated, and that it will have been them, and that he should expect a call about Locke Pierce, who is next. He puts on his coat, picks up his saber, grabs her round the waist and kisses her deeply, holding his hat on his head. He takes a book, and the Fieldmaster takes a whole volume of ledgers and glares at Deirdre as she goes. Gemma asks him once more who is buying the place. *"Maybe I'll tell you someday."* They leave the cat behind, and Gemma opens a window for it. She puts Deirdre and Graham back on before they leave the bookshelf.

## Sunday morning

They sleep, and wake at about eleven on a rainy, misty Sunday. The ledger Deirdre was sent to examine has gone out the back with the Fieldmaster. Bimples brings a hearty breakfast, which they take from outside the door.

In the bathroom mirror, braiding her hair, Gemma calls Kitsune, and his fox arms come round her from behind. She asks whether, if she gets him the child and survives and has a child of her own one day, whatever passed from her father to her will pass on again. He raises his hand. *"I solemnly swear that if you bring me the child unharmed, I will consider your obligation complete. You may keep everything I've given to you, and I will go on my way."* She asks what happens if she dies, and he does not answer that. She asks how to find Dario. *"He has found help. You should go to him. But be careful. Don't be followed."* When she turns around he is gone.

Eric, in the bath, casts *divination* to Matt Matheny. He thinks about the complex calculus of killing an emperor and balances the power equation, and the letters form. He asks how to kill the Emperor. He spells out a numerical sequence and works it through in Elvish and in Draconic, and the Elvish comes out as:

> Of course, the answer is so simple.
> I am the crimson star that shines in the night.
> Do not stand at my grave and weep.
> I am not there. I did not die.

He has seen the sequence before, carved into the Tomb of Kaius I. On Religion (21) he works it through. LeBeefe told him personally that the Emperor is a vampire, and he has told General Thar the same, which Thar shrugged off. They know how to kill a vampire because they have done it. Eric tells Gemma, who walks in on his meditation. They talk it over: Malrik Zier on the balcony, his mist going to his coffin, and Dario finishing him there; the Emperor as a vampire kept alive on dragon blood, which is what Valtrex was being bled for; *"they don't call him Kaius the Undying for nothing."* If they kill him, his mist will go to his resting place and they will have to kill him again there. The Tomb of Kaius I is the obvious place, although they have heard he is not in it.

While they dress, Gemma asks Graham whether, back in the desert, he ever pictured the two of them getting married. Perhaps going steady, he says. She always thought he had a crush on her, and he says she never seemed all that interested either.

## The pilgrim camp

They leave as Deirdre and Graham. Bimples hands them croissants and has not seen Master Rizzy. The Lodge is emptier than usual, and things are being taken down. Pork Broccoli does not know any Beef Taco, but he says a man has been asking for them over at the pilgrims' camp and could not leave it, and he drives them there in the rain, past the Counting House and the nearly empty plaza, and stops at the stables before the dirt road gets too bad. The camp holds sixty or eighty people: big dug-in tents and temporary ones, pilgrims who came to see the cathedral and cannot afford to stay anywhere nearby, worshippers of the Empress among people of many faiths. There are no Doves. Many of the musicians and dancers from the plaza are there eating lunch. As he leaves them, Broccoli asks Deirdre about a rumour that the Empress might have her baby and whether he should leave. She tells him yes, and he goes off to talk to the Lady of the Horses.

At the back of the camp they hear Dario's voice. A small knot of Matt Matheny followers in plain clothes has a camp of its own there, one of them from the Counting House, and on a cot lies **Zero-One**, wounded, bandaged and asleep. **Ilma Vitt** gets up in Deirdre's face: *"I gave you everything you needed. You didn't do anything about the corruption."* She has heard the Lodge is empty and that they took everything and got away. *"Are you here to help the Rebellion, or are you actually here to persecute us even more? Did Kesk send you?"*

Dario crawls out of a tent, happier than they have seen him in a while. After the *dimension door* from the slab room he and Zero-One came out on the peak, he hid for an hour, came down, left her in the bushes and found these people, who took them in. Eric tells the camp the truth: this is his friend Foxtale, who is not an advocate, and neither is he. Zero-One will need a few days' rest to get back to herself. When Gemma mentions that she has made contact with Joseph Roberts, Ilma asks whether they are in league with the Dread Pirate. *"He's committed crimes against math that are unheard of,"* Eric says. A nerdy steampunk woman and an old man come forward about novel analytic concepts, imaginary numbers back on the table, complex analysis and continuation. Eric tells them about the mathematics hidden in the warforged's heads that gave the faster-than-light drive, which the orcs tried to take and which Locke Pierce now has.

Dario stands on a stump and speaks to the whole camp. They are here to cut the head off the elf witch, he says, and they need every able-bodied man, everyone who can carry a sword or play a harp or a horn. When they try it something awful is bound to happen, but there is a song of triumph that can counteract it. *"It is a dark day. The sky is gray. And it will be a long night. But that means all the brighter dawn."* He points at Gemma's sword. He has been working the camp all night and drinking with them. The musicians crowd round Gemma to learn the song, and she plays it perfectly (Performance 29). **The Matt Matheny followers and the Plaza Band pledge their help**, as Joseph Roberts's corsairs already have. Gemma suggests to Dario that Zero-One could leave on Roberts's ship.

## Zero-One

After the speech Zero-One wakes, groggy, and understands that she has escaped. *"How is Izaak?"* He is alive, and he and Bantide were sent to Sharn. She weeps for Zilspar. Then she asks where Richard is. Whoever attacked the farm did it without tripping any of their alarms or sensors, which could only be done by someone who already knew how to switch them off: *"I looked up and every building was full of Eden units just shooting. It was carnage."* Three years ago Digma was to take the boy to the farm, and there was talk of moving him to Leef or the Talenta Plains. The only people who knew were Dario, Gemma, Jessica, Richard, Eric and Lorian.

She believes that is why she was made to raise Esther. Uriel thinks Esther knows where the boy is, and nobody could get closer to him than his once-dead mother. Esther could never have done that with her own heart, even undead, so they made Zero-One take it out and try to replace it. Gemma asks what was different about what she did to Uriel. *"Uriel's brain was missing when I first met her all those years ago, and I replaced it with a gem, which had raised her from the dead by the light of the Flame. Why this was the will of the Flame I will never understand. And I shudder to think that I'm the one that gave her the mind that led to everything we see."* With Esther she was dominated and could see everything she was doing. They tortured her mostly in her mind, because they needed her hands for surgery. She could not complete the rite. She did remove the heart, and it must still be somewhere. The whole thing felt like an academic exercise to her, as if they were practising on her and on Crona for someone else. Uriel can raise the dead and has done it everywhere, but there are levels of lifelike animation that still escape her, something in her dominion over death that she cannot mirror in life without a blemish.

Gemma tells her that Esther kept repeating *"Your pact is void,"* and that she has been told Esther has another patron, one that wants her back. She shows Zero-One the sword Esther wanted, which seems to have become a symbol of hope, although it is tied to someone who is not squeaky clean. Zero-One tells them she cannot reach anyone from here and fears that most of their friends are dead or taken. *"The rest of our movement has been shattered."* If they go to confront the White Death, she will summon all the Flame left in her to help. Gemma, shaken by the night, says she is a farm girl and a junkyard girl who once worked in a strip club with vampires, and that she does not know what she is doing. Zero-One smirks when they ask whether she knew the Emperor is a vampire. *"We had good evidence to that fact. The equation is correct."*

She takes off a homemade pewter necklace with a little flame in a circle and gives it to Gemma. *"If you call on it, it will protect you in your darkest hour."* It holds one casting of *circle of power*. The party talks about who should wear it: Eric will be concentrating on *spirit guardians*, so it should be Gemma or Dario.

## The plan

Eric suggests buying a bowl worth a thousand gold for a ritual in the market and doing it as fast as possible, because he has the feeling the Empress may be in labour already. Gemma is still carrying a bag of holding full of alchemist's fire, from Leef and from the three undercover years when she burned the bodies of people she killed.

They talk it through without settling it. If they go in as Thar and are found out, they fight their way out through every Black Dove there. If they are believed and brought to the Emperor, they could kill him then and there, follow the mist to the Tomb of Kaius I and kill him again. But if the child is about to be born, Uriel may not care whether the Emperor dies, because he has already passed on what she needed from him, and killing him might only do her a favour. Dario says that if he sees Eden he will destroy it and every one like it, and that perhaps the baby should die too: the child of the Queen of Death and a vampire dragon, the end of an ancient plan. Gemma wonders whether they should burn everything down or let Locke Pierce do it, but if Uriel rings the bell the disaster reaches far beyond the valley. Eric offers *clairvoyance* to leave an eye behind once they have been inside, and *arcane lock* for a tent. The camp eats a sombre lunch, the rain comes down harder, and they get ready to go up the hill as General Thar and his druid.
