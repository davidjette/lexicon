# Episode 16: The Red Bird

*Played 2026-10-09 (Zoom, about five hours). Transcripts: `session-2026-10-09-red-bird.transcript.txt` and `-timestamped.txt`. In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 17. Gemma and Eric only; Dario's player was away, and Dario is still missing with Zero-One.*

The session opens in the Fieldmaster's Suite at the Grand Lodge on Saturday night, after the short rest. Gemma and Eric are still under *seeming* as Deirdre Moro and Graham Cracker. Both have their thirteenth level. Eric has a seventh-level slot, and Gemma has taken a fourth fighter level with warhammer as a weapon mastery and is choosing between Mage Slayer and Telepathic for her feat. She leans toward Mage Slayer for the once-a-rest saving throw, having been nearly banished by Maelis Dorn a few hours ago, and she is wary of telepathy in a valley where Kitsune has told her messages are overheard. Eric is ruled to have had time to attune to the Pearl of Power and take back a third-level slot.

They talk over Esther. Zero-One's rite did not make a corpse of Uriel, but the same rite attempted on Eden did, and there are plainly many grades of undead here. Gemma recalls from the EDEN files that some units would stare into the distance and repeat words, and wonders whether "Your pact is void" is an echo of Esther's last moments. The last two things Esther said in life were *"Your pact is void"* and *"Kill Uriel Qualanthri."* If she still means the second, they agree to tell her Uriel is here the next time they meet.

## The Divination

Gemma means to look for Joseph Roberts while she is here, and suspects he would not be going about as himself. Eric casts *divination* as a ritual, burning 25 gold of incense, and puts the goal to his god: *find the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts.* Matt Matheny sends an angel with big boobs and a big girly wig, who tunes up an electric guitar and sings a country song:

> A pirate had a saucy wife
> who kissed him once and fled his life.
> She left him lonely, left him dry,
> with naught to do but drink the rye
> and wed whatever women would,
> since she had gone, and gone for good.
>
> Now up the river, bold as brass,
> she comes a-knocking, that same lass.
> "Show me your books, my darling thief,
> and tell me where you get your beef.
> Whose gold is this? Whose names are these?"
> She smiles the smile that buckled his knees.
>
> He keeps his door and keeps his head
> and wishes her a mile ahead,
> for one more look and one more lie
> will break the heart he drank to dry.
> So if you seek him, seek him near:
> he's hiding from the one who's here.

Gemma corrects the record at the table: she and Roberts never married. He took her to his place in the Lhazaar Principalities for a week or two, proposed when he brought her home, and she said no. He told her then that he has a lot of wives, which is where Blair's many husbands came from. The two of them read the last line as Roberts hiding from Deirdre Moro, the Korth official who has turned up at a lodge where officials never stay and who can be expected to ask for the books. They still hold the assignment: the Lodge's ledger is the other half of the Counting House's, and together the two trace confiscated and donated goods out of the door against bribes to Imperial officials.

Gemma thinks about putting new faces on them. Eric's Insight (30, with guidance) settles it: the Lodge is members only, a strange face would be shown out, and Deirdre and Graham are the faces that have leave to be here. They put on the hunting outfits Kitsune left and go down. Bimples tells them the full moon has all the hunters about.

## The ballroom

On the grand stair the newest Fieldmaster's portrait has no name under it: a lean woman in her forties who looks dangerous. A footman at the ballroom door confirms that the Advocate has leave to use the ballroom, the salon and all the general members' accommodations, and hands her an Aperol spritz. The Lodge has been unreasonably hospitable to a woman who is an existential threat to it, and it has even given her the boardroom to work in on the assumption that it is being audited.

In the ballroom **Rotunda Goose**, one-handed and missing an ear since Thronehold, is holding court by the fire: *"Full price. Full price, after what I gave them."* She is not here to hunt. There is no safe passage out of the Empire any more, she says, and for all her money she cannot talk anyone into it. She presents **Lord Aldric and Lady Sabine Varenne**, Karrnathi lords, and tells them Advocate Moro has ambitions for the Directorate, *"not that any of that matters these days, but it'll matter in the aftermath. We're all planning for what comes next."* As a parliamentarian she is an expert in consensus. Deirdre says quietly that she would like to visit the library, and Goose says this is the night they bring people through it.

A drunk clerk tells Graham he has signed for the same forty field guns three times this year and never seen one. A recent widow asks Deirdre whether she has seen **Colonel Aubrecht Seyle**, who was dancing here a week ago; the staff say he checked out, but his trunk is still upstairs. Brillo Myers is in the room. The Varennes say most of the King's Forest lies north of the river and is closed to hunting, but the Lodge has a grandfathered licence and only members may hunt there, and that the serious hunters are in the Hunters' Salon to the east.

## The Hunters' Salon

The way to the salon runs through the boardroom. Inside are a handful of men dressed and equipped for the field and a maid serving drinks in her bloomers. **Cousin Felcher**, the quartermaster, is finishing a piece of business with a demon in hunting dress, who hands him a handful of platinum bars. Felcher puts them in his bag, winks, and goes on talking: the upper river is fully open these days, people say they have seen orcs, and one of his boys saw Imperial troops in black armour in the King's Forest, where troops do not go. Eight months of powder, shot, rail stock and field guns. When he sees who Deirdre is he says *"An advocate. You make me nervous,"* ignores her hand, tells her his party is full and leaves. Graham and the demon nearly come to blows and the maid stops it.

A man gets up off the couch. *"I've seen the red bird."* He saw it fly across the river that morning while he was staking out a great stag, and he knows where it roosts. He is sixty, lean and grey-bearded, in waxed leather the colour of bark, with an ash longbow taller than he is and a coil of black rope over one shoulder. His name is **Fora Dollar** (the name as given at the table; "Master Dollar" to Graham). He suspects it is more than a bird. The King's Forest is not empty because of the power of the King: there are elves out there, and druids, his own people and worse. His price is 500 gold up front and 1,000 after, *"and if I feel you're going to get me killed, I'm leaving."* Gemma pays the 500, which leaves the two of them almost nothing. He takes 500 off the back end if he can keep the tail feathers. His plan is a rowboat across the river, a three-hour walk through the north forest to a second boat, and an hour up a creek to its headwaters before dawn.

## The inlet

On the far bank he mixes mud and a ground herb, takes out an old stylus and draws a sign on each of their arms that glows green: *pass without trace*. He scouts ahead and comes back. Something is waiting around the corner. Eric blesses the three of them. Wolves come out of the trees, and Gemma burns the first two to death with held fire bolts. Three more drag her down and hold her. Then an elf leaps out from behind a rock, heavily muscled, long-eared, dressed in furs and small skulls, with a crooked staff: a dark druid of the Eldeen. His *entangle* catches Eric and Dollar. Gemma *misty steps* out of the pack, hits him with three eldritch blasts and breaks his concentration. He turns into a giant snake and slides into the trees with Dollar's mark on him. Eric puts 58 points into one wolf on a natural 20, Dollar shoots the snake through the undergrowth, and when it comes back and wraps Gemma, Eric's dagger opens it and the elf falls out of it. Dollar puts three arrows in his chest and Eric finishes him. He dies cursing in Druidic: *"You'll never know the mysteries of the Eldeen."*

*"Dark druids,"* Dollar says. *"If they're protecting this bird, we're in for some trouble."*

## The falls and the owlbears

Dollar offers the quick way, a hard climb up the north side of the falls, or the long way round the south side. Deirdre admits she is no climber and hints that her high-elf mother could fly by magic. *"My people believe that a power that's easily obtained is sure to be easily corrupting."* He takes them south, jumps the creek and hangs his rope for them. At the foot of the falls there are sleeping geese, a cave on the left, and the peak to the northeast. *"That's where she is. Our red bird."*

On the way up he talks. His people were cast out of Valenar for their human blood. They call themselves the **Corvari** and live as a band in these woods. He saved a lord's life once, the lord sponsored him and paid his membership, and he got used to the luxury of taking fools from the Lodge out to shoot rabbits. Gemma turns an ankle on an easy jump.

He stops them: the path is watched, and they go round. Crossing a clearing under *pass without trace* (Stealth 30 and 27) they come within a few yards of a grazing elk. An owlbear comes snorting out of a cave beside them, runs the elk down and starts eating it. They leave it to its meal. As they creep on, a second and larger owlbear comes out of the cave in front of them, and the first picks up the elk and carries it straight past their hiding places to its family. Stealth rolls of 23, 33, 27 and 25 keep both of them hidden, and the owlbears go into the cave.

At the top of the next rock face Gemma drinks two healing potions and Dollar closes her bites with his hands. They cross the stream above the falls with Eric walking on the water by his ring and Gemma towed behind holding his ankle.

## The web shelf

On the far bank giant spiders come out of the trees, and behind them the drow twins **Vyx and Velith**, with the spiders moving at their call. The whole shelf is web. Gemma is caught twice over in thrown webbing and lit up by Vyx's *faerie fire*. Velith puts a crossbow bolt in Eric's neck, he fails the save against the poison, and he goes down unconscious for the rest of the fight while a spider winds him in silk. Dollar is hit as well and makes his save by exactly enough.

Gemma blasts the spiders off Eric, *misty steps* to Vyx and trips him. He gets up and looks her in the eye: *"Been waiting to feed a smug elf like you to my babies for years. The Empire is over."* He stabs her for 21. She spends her action surge and takes his head off, and the *faerie fire* goes out. Velith runs at Dollar and buries both short swords in his chest for 50. He gives her 33 back and throws a *healing word* to Gemma. Gemma steps clear of the spiders and blasts Velith twice, and the body goes backward into the river. The spiders scatter into the woods.

Dollar burns the webs away with a torch and cuts Eric out. *"Should have known this would happen. The bird's probably under the protection of these Eldeen druids. It must be some type of avatar for them. We should turn back."* Asked why the drow would say the Empire is over, he says he is not one for politics. He opens three potions of superior healing, one for each of them, and Eric's poison lifts. While they drink, a huge red bird passes over the peak and settles out of sight. Dollar shows them his grounding arrows, which trail weighted cords that whip round a target and bind its wings. *"I'll take it down, and you all cut its throat."*

## The peak

The last climb is the hardest. Gemma makes it on a lucky roll. Eric falls on his head for 16.

On the bare crown of rock at the top of the gorge is a small fireless camp: three tents, one flying an Imperial pennant, and figures crouched in the dark muttering in Druidic. They have not seen her. Gemma opens the fight by blasting the nearest druid toward the edge and vanishing in a *misty step*. The red bird comes round the mountain, sees Dollar, stoops on him and carries him off over the valley. Dollar, seeing the Imperial markings: *"What's going on here?"* He tears free in mid-air, spreads his arms and drops into the trees.

A druid drags Eric in with a *thorn lash*, which is where Eric wants to be, and he casts *spirit guardians* at fifth level. Gemma blasts one druid off the cliff. The bird comes for Eric and into the guardians. Eric casts *harm* on it, which it half resists, and it loses 21 from its maximum. Then Dollar is back on the slope below with his bow. His first grounding arrow hits, and the bird drops out of the air beside Eric with its legs bound to its wings. It cannot get the cords off. Gemma blasts a second druid off the edge and a third, who has turned himself into a ram, off a tree and over. The bird shrieks, takes Eric in its talons on a critical and goes at his liver with its beak, 25 and 17 and 14, and Eric holds his concentration through all of it with the guardians burning the bird every round. He *misty steps* out of its grip and stabs it. Dollar climbs back to the top and puts three arrows in its back for 55.

The bird becomes General Stockton Thar.

*"What the hell? I came to hunt a bird, not a man,"* Dollar says. *"I'm no murderer."*

Thar looks at them. *"Who are you? What are you doing? Who ordered this? You idiots. You're fighting for an empire that's going to crumble. Do you even know what's happened to the Emperor?"* *"You don't understand anything. You don't know anything."*

Gemma raises her sword: *"Do you recognize this?"* She hits him twice, and he burns a legendary resistance to keep his feet. He lifts the dragon-headed staff and drops a *wall of fire* on Gemma, Eric and his own last druid. Eric keeps his concentration on a 10. Thar turns to scramble away at half speed inside the guardians. Gemma trips him as he goes and Eric's *true strike* catches him for 19, and he goes down unconscious as the wall of fire goes out. The last druid cries *"General!"*, blows a *gust of wind* across the peak that fails to move Gemma, and dies in the guardians.

## The head and the note

Dollar stands over the General. *"What have you got me into? You just assassinated some military officer. This will be the end of my career. I have to leave. I live in the Imperial city."* — *"Welcome to the rebellion, Master Dollar."* — *"Rebellion, no. You fucking idiots. You think you know what's best for everyone? Collapsing the Empire, fighting all this time, destroying everything?"* He has no love for the Empire, he says, and he wants his money.

Gemma cuts off Thar's head. They can *speak with dead* later if they need to. On the body and in the camp:

- **Thar's regalia**: the red robes and the headpiece.
- **The dragon-headed staff**, black wood topped with a red enamelled dragon's head, part of the old regalia of the Crimson Sun. A +1 quarterstaff that deals an extra 1d6 fire. Six charges, regaining 1d6 at dawn: two for *call lightning*, three for *wall of fire* (DC 15). It requires attunement by a druid, so neither of them can use it, but it will be carried.
- **A written demand in Thar's own hand** that the airships now protecting the Lazaret be released to the front to try to save Aundair from the orcs.
- **A note** in very strange handwriting, with tonight's date: *Dawn. The peak at King's Forest.* Signed **E**.
- **1,200 gold** in Imperial scrip.

Dollar takes his second 500 and keeps the tail feathers, which came down tangled in his arrows. He hands over their share of what he took off the drow, 200 gold in drow silver and a vial of drow poison. *"Good luck to you, whoever you are."* He will not be seen going back with them, and he leaves down the slope. Gemma and Eric split the remaining 900. They take two sets of the druids' clothes as well.

A burst of gunfire sounds in the valley below, in the direction Dollar went. It is nearly dawn.

## E

They decide to keep the appointment. Gemma casts *seeming*: herself as Thar, in his headpiece and holding his staff, and Eric as the tiefling druid of his staff. Thar's body goes into the bag of holding. Gemma tells Eric that if it goes badly she will use the sword to take them back to Dollar's boat.

Soldiers come up over the edge of the peak, moving in a strange, dead way, their weapons built into their bodies and their small oculus scanners turning toward the two of them. Then a woman drops out of the sky, lands softly, and brings her head up with a jerk to fix her eyes on Thar. It is **Eden**.

Gemma speaks first, in Thar's ruined voice (Performance, with advantage). Eden answers in a flat mechanical voice with a second, synthetic, saccharine voice laid over it.

> **"Thar":** I see you made it. Well. Safe.
>
> **Eden:** There are twenty Eden units in the valley below. It's all that I could bring back. Like I said, you could be destroyed, or you could see the truth.
>
> **"Thar":** Destroyed would be the second on my list. So tell me, Eden, what is the truth?
>
> **Eden:** The Empress must die.
>
> **"Thar":** I think that's why we're both standing here right now. We know what she has done to these lands, to our people.
>
> **Eden:** You told me the Emperor would agree with us. You are a fool.
>
> **"Thar":** If the Emperor gets in the way, it might just have to happen.
>
> **Eden:** If you get in my way, I will destroy everything you love. *(She nods to her soldiers, and they begin to climb down.)* They have my sister.
>
> **"Thar":** I've heard tell that she has been risen.
>
> **Eden:** We will rule the Empire together. You will retreat to your forests. If you are here tomorrow, you will die.
>
> **"Thar":** And what of Locke Pierce?
>
> **Eden:** I could survive his onslaught. Can you?
>
> **"Thar":** Druids can do many things. My hope is that my forest will not be blown up afterwards.

She laughs, a flat repeated *ha, ha, ha, ha*, steps off the peak and walks away. The two of them stand very still until the sun begins to show.

## Back to the Lodge

Gemma teleports them by the sword to the bank where Dollar's boat lies. They change back into Deirdre and Graham, row across, leave the boat where he keeps it and walk up to the Lodge. Pork Broccoli is outside. Deirdre asks whether his family lives near here, and tells him things are getting rough toward Aundair. His family lives nowhere near, he says. He came here on his own, his whole people are from the Uruk Republic, and there has been fighting on their territory.

Bimples asks for payment for another day, 500 gold, and Gemma pays it out of the General's money. *"I hope your hunt went well. Did you catch your game?"* — *"It did not go as expected. Many mysteries out in those woods."* He offers the Lodge's complimentary healing services again. They go up to the suite. Thar's audience with the Emperor in the garden is at noon today, and there is just time for a long rest first.

## Table rulings and notes

- **Gemma and Roberts were never married.** He proposed and she refused. The Lexicon's "once married" line is wrong.
- Eric's god answered the *divination* through an angel singing country.
- A readied attack is one attack, with no Extra Attack.
- Pact of the Blade: one bonded weapon at a time; conjuring or bonding another is a bonus action and ends the first bond, but not attunement.
- Nobody may teleport into the Lazaret. Everyone takes a boat. Gemma's sword took them to the far bank.
- Zero-One has been listed as a paladin, but the resurrection she is famous for is a cleric's spell, and she is probably more a cleric.
- Lus Fayina still owes the party her life.

- **General Stockton Thar is dead.** His head is off and his body is in a bag of holding. The party holds his regalia, his staff, his demand for the airships and his appointment with the Emperor at **noon today, Sunday, in the garden**. Gemma has heard him speak for long enough to mimic him.
- **All of his staff on the peak are dead**: the druid at the inlet, the drow twins on the shelf, and the druids at the camp. The six death troopers of his escort are in town.

- **Fora Dollar** knows that two guests of the Lodge killed an Imperial general. He left with 1,000 gold and the tail feathers, and there was gunfire where he went. His fate is unknown.
- **Joseph Roberts** is near, and hiding from Deirdre.
- **Dario and Zero-One** are still missing.
- The Lodge assignment is due **Monday**. The Fieldmaster's Suite is paid for a third night. Gold: about 400 between them.
- **Next:** the long rest, breakfast, and the garden at noon as General Thar and his druid.
