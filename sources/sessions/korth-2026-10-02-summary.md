# Episode 15: Orientation Day

*Played 2026-10-02 (Zoom, about five hours). Transcripts: `session-2026-10-02-lazaret.transcript.txt` and `-timestamped.txt`. In the Lexicon's numbering this is Korth Ep 16.*

The party is where they stopped, in front of the Tomb of Kaius I, disguised by Gemma's *seeming* as three Black Doves, with Eric's *detect magic* still running. Up the processional walk comes an orderly group of novices behind a severe, elderly sister whose face is thick with white makeup. The sisters of the order are beginning to show their differences. The green-robed sisters are healers of every race, and some carry the holy symbols of the Sovereign Host or of older native gods, which suggests that the order has absorbed the best healers of the faiths it replaced. The novices wear plain black, the same habit the party has on. At the back of the group Eric recognises the blue-haired elf from the boat, **Saturnia**.

The guide stops them at the Polygon. *"This great blessing comes from our mother's mercy. The power collected here allows us to project our healing light all across the world. It is her great accomplishment. If only it could save and heal our Emperor."* While the novices pray, three of them wander over to the tomb and size up the strangers. Gemma gives her name as **Sister Apollonia**, "Apple" to her friends, the name she used as a novice in the Korth house. Eric gives his as **Sister Euphrasia**, and the girls ask whether she comes from some trailer-park convent. Dario introduces himself as **Sister Mr. Rogers**. The three laugh at them for being new and drift off to the edge of the terrace, and Dario wants to kill all three of them on the spot. They talk it through: *darkness*, *silence*, a fireball, and a hillside in plain view of the whole valley. The guide calls time, Saturnia comes back to the group, and the party falls in at the back with their heads down.

From the terrace they get the lay of the valley. The green building to the west is the **Westhouse**, once the palace wing where the royal family and the great courtiers lived, now the headquarters of the Black Doves, with a small harbour below it. West of that, old bridges cross a flooded swamp. To the north lies the pilgrim camp, where Ilma Vitt is waiting. The cathedral itself is **Nos Matre**: its west wing is the hospital and its east wing is the altar. Nobody they ask knows where in the valley the Emperor actually lies.

On the way in, a procession of red-robed sisters in heels comes out of the cathedral behind a large, mannish sister. A handsome young man walking with the novices points them out. They are the **Sisters of the Blood**, who care for the Emperor directly and are all trained in his unusual illness. Every one of them is the same type, slender and fair, and every one of them looks like Uriel.

## The Threshold of Mercy

Gemma knows the room the moment they enter it. It is the fountain from her Kitsune dream. The vestibule is everything the old account promised: stained glass full of doves and lilies, a dove mosaic underfoot, families on the benches kneeling far too still, the mural of Emperor Kaius at the far end, and two wardens, now in white cloaks, at the inner doors. Across the lintel, in Elvish: *None may enter who come bearing harm.* The crowd waits its turn at the basin. A man and his wife have carried her father up the hill, and he is next. A sister tells them the fountain was once a plain ornament of the temple, until the Mother of Mercy came and transformed it, and now it heals any wound and gives a full night's rest. They lower the old man's face into the water. He coughs, gets his feet under him, stands up straight, throws his arms in the air and shouts *Hallelujah!*, then falls to his knees praising the Empress. A farmer tries to fill his canteen and is stopped and shown out. On the way out the sister speaks quietly to the daughter, the daughter kisses her father goodbye, and the old man is walked off into the hospital wing alone. Eric's *detect magic* reads a tremendous weight of necromancy in the water. Nobody in the party drinks or washes.

## The Altar and the Gardens

The guide takes them east into the great worship hall, where a mass is under way. Eric remembers this building before Uriel, ten or twelve years ago, as an interfaith temple, crowded and open to everyone. It is now solemn, elvish and perfectly ordered, and the Doves live in great luxury here. They have taken over half the palace as the Westhouse, along with the church and most of the town, and they have replaced the Imperial court with themselves. The liturgy is in a priestly High Elven that most worshippers cannot follow, and it prays openly for the eternal night. Nobody seems to listen to what the prayers actually say. The table works out how the order got here. The Black Doves were once healers and hunters of the undead. The Emperor is Karrnathi, so necromancy was already normal, and Uriel turned people's wish for priests who could raise the dead into acceptance of the undead as a servant class.

From the rear terrace they see the gardens and the stair down to them, the triumphal arch with the great statue of Kaius I, Heatherhill to the southeast, the Westhouse to the southwest, an orrery of Eberron and its moons, and in the west tower a bell. Dario wants to go and break it. While they talk, Saturnia comes up behind them so as not to leave them behind *"like those other girls,"* and talks about why she is here. Her family dreamed of the day their kind would be in charge again, and she does her duty. She minds that she will never know a man. *"I understand that my virginity now belongs to the Emperor, and we'll see what he does with it."*

## The Trauma Ward and the Tower

The guide brings them back through the nave and in through the door the old man went through, and the smell of death meets them. The trauma ward is a church repurposed for triage. The marble is stained, blood runs along the grout, nurse-sisters are washing gore from their hands, and the dying cough and cry in rows of beds, with a cart of bodies in one corner. The old man already has a bed, and they have opened his forehead to bleed his humours. *"We do our best for those we can here,"* the guide says, *"but the war and disease and a number of other maladies caused by our enemies has meant that we have very little to give a very great many people. We've resorted to older magics and wisdoms."*

Up the stair, across a small bridge, in a surgery and library at the top of the tower, a woman in a head-to-toe covering with a white circle on its centre stands masked before the corpse of a very large knight. *"Welcome to the Ivory Lazaret. We trust that this will be the last time you will need to come to our Lazaret."* The novices laugh. She is **Director Lus Fayina**. Eric knows her as the sister who once subjected him to a thorough examination of the most personal kind, and whom Dario let go. She does not appear to know them. She tells the group they are a *sextet*: *"You've been placed together because of your specific attributes, and you will serve as a single unit of our sisterhood. You will rise and fall together. You will assume your colored robes together. You will live together. You will love together. And you will die together."* Of the knight: *"This man gave his life for the Empire, and he will give his death as well."* Everyone who goes down the stairs serves the Emperor one way or another.

Eric searches the room as the group files out, and finds books of anatomy and medicine, surgical instruments fine enough to make a surgeon's kit, two potions of greater healing, 200 gold of powdered diamond, a locked drawer, and **Umbra**, the drug from the Velvet Table cellars and Project EDEN. Here it comes in neat purple-blue ampules packed in Black Dove healing kits beside bone-white gauze and incense, ready to inject into someone still alive to prepare them for death. In this room they heal, they raise, and they dissect. He takes nothing.

## Down the Stair House

Below the tower, past silent guards, Lus Fayina opens a door in the hillside onto a stair that goes down eighty feet, three floors deep, with rails along it for stretchers. The bell, it turns out, hangs above the room they have just left. Under the hill is a whole hospital.

The first landing opens onto wood panelling, white-cloaked guards everywhere, and a reception laid out like a fine college dining hall. The novices are told to enjoy themselves and get to know their sextets. Young women in black shifts eat soup at round tables while senior sisters go among them measuring them, looking in their mouths and eyes, listening to their voices, and fitting them for new robes. A sister with a slate comes to each of them with the same three questions. Where were you born? Who was your mother's mother? Have you ever been pregnant? Eric answers *Korth*, *Esmeralda*, *no*. Dario gives his grandmother as *Captain Kangaroo*, and the sister writes it down. Saturnia gets a fourth question, in Elvish: *on what boat did your family travel from Aerenal?* *The Lightbringer.*

Saturnia tells Eric not to worry about serving the Emperor, since only elves of a certain type may join the **Sisterhood of the Blood**. Gemma's disguise is an elf, so the slate comes to her too. She gives Valenar as her birthplace and **Grylda** as her grandmother, and when the fourth question comes she answers *the Lightbringer*, in Elvish. Saturnia brightens: *"I did not realize we were kin."* When Gemma is evasive about her grandfather, saying he had many names, Saturnia names her own: **E'noch.**

Gemma leads grace from memory: *Come the night the bells will toll thirteen, the dead will rise to serve the living, and the dragon will be reborn.* Eric eats the soup and Gemma eats nothing. There are three other sextets in the hall, eighteen women in all. A novice in the identical outfit to Gemma's looks her up and down in disgust and walks off, and Eric confirms that Gemma wears it better.

Lus Fayina takes the six of them on through the kitchens. *In the future you'll come in through the harbour to the Westhouse like everybody else, but this time we're taking the long way around.* Next is **the last ward**, a row of private cells for patients whose cases are hopeless but whose bodies are strong enough for *the process of second life*. The patients have pox, war wounds and freak accidents, and apart from their illness they are mostly physically fine: strong, large or beautiful. Some of the novices may choose to work here, depending on their vows. Past it, in a workroom, sisters who are plainly undead themselves are fitting bodies on the tables with Imperial armour and augmenting them. *"Here's where we do our most difficult surgeries and body replacements. These are the **sorrowful sisters**. You will join their order only if you are among the most elite at the time of your death."*

## The Bottom Floor

At the bottom of the stair is a pleasant little courtyard and an assembly hall full of novices. With the doors to east, west and south, this is the party's last chance to slip away before the speeches, and they split three ways on good stealth rolls. Lus Fayina spots Dario and tells him the recovery ward can wait until after the presentation, so he sits down.

Eric finds the east door locked and puts his eye to its window (Perception 25). Inside is a surgical amphitheatre with sisters in the gallery watching a procedure. On the slab lies a woman with horns and wings, cut open. Two sorrowful sisters are tending her, and **Fema Nolan, Zero-One**, beaten, missing teeth, one eye swollen shut, is performing a ritual over the corpse. The corpse is **Esther Crona**.

Gemma goes out of the south door into an underground concourse that looks like a railway station, with carts and vendors run by sisters and civilians and a rabble of grunting from the west end. In the middle, sisters are performing a ritual in public over the enormous bloated corpse of a giant or ogre, and as she watches the body begins to stir with energy. A long vault runs south toward the Westhouse. Eric's history makes sense of the place. It began as the palace's secret underground vault, became a promenade where the royal household and its guests could meet in privacy in any weather, and has been extended into a Black Dove city and a hospital under the hill that nobody outside the order knows exists.

In the hall Lus Fayina addresses the novices. *"You are about to join the most prestigious religious order in the history of the world, called to the greatest calling that any human being can have, to serve the Emperor and to heal his people."* Some of them are destined for the green robes. Some will be **Sisters of the Bone**, called to the welcoming of the dead. Some will be **Sisters of the Light**. Some will tend His Majesty himself as **Sisters of the Blood**, and many others will find their own roles. A second sister sends them to their dormitories with a warning against gossip and dallying: evening prayers and washing before sundown. *"Yes, Mother."*

Dario takes the unlocked west door, and Gemma follows. It opens onto the transformation floor, a corridor of cells where the strongest bodies brought down from above are spending their final moments, with sisters going in and out. Gemma looks through the windows as she passes (natural 20, Investigation 25) and recognises the figure on one bed as **Donna**, the Silver Flame cleric from Zilspar. She has been tattooed, fitted with attachments and given a new jaw, and her wounds are stitched. Her hands are in perfect condition and there are holy implements all around her. They are trying to preserve her magic. Further on is the last stage: undead bodies so finely finished that they barely look dead, steeped in Umbra and being fitted with the red-and-white armour of the Imperial Guard, overseen by a tall sister with a pair of scythes named **Sister Mary Christmas**. She stops in the doorway and glares. Dario, playing a novice who has got lost, says the dorms are this way, he believes, and leaves on a performance of 25. The party puts it together: the Lazaret takes the finest dead and dying of the Empire, the strongest and most talented and most magically powerful, and turns them into soldiers loyal to the Empress. And for three years they have been trying to do something to Esther that needed them to go out and capture Fema Nolan.

## The Concourse

They regroup among the stalls. There are things to buy here, and Gemma buys hooded robes at two gold apiece and gets directions from the stallholder, who tells her the docks are down through the Westhouse. Behind one stall, a whole unit of Imperial guards stands in perfect stillness. **Sister Mortis** finds them dawdling: it is nearly curfew, the punishment for breaking it is severe, and she warns Sister Apollonia by name. She finds them again a little later and promises Apollonia a thrashing she will never forget.

They talk through the risks. Jessica warned of strong magical interference near them. The Polygon read as necromancy and abjuration both, and the hospital may be warded the way Donna once warded Sister Nora's cell. Gemma tests what works. *Guidance*, *thaumaturgy* and her invisible mage hand all work fine, which tells them nothing about teleportation. They decide to risk it anyway, before Esther can be raised. Gemma walks up to a butch, crop-haired armoured sister at the door and tells her something about a very naughty novice and a spanking (Deception 15; the sister's Insight roll is a 1). When the sister walks off, Gemma takes Dario by *dimension door* through the window into the slab room, and Eric follows by *misty step*.

## The Slab Room

They catch the room at prayer. A low vaulted chamber is lit like a surgery, with a sheeted body on a black slab and great dark wings folded against the stone on either side. Sisters in grey stand at the slab with their hands on her, singing. Zero-One kneels in irons with her hands on the body's chest and her lips moving. Esther has been taken apart and put back together. The wound in her chest is stitched shut, her arms, legs and wings have been grafted with the muscle and skin of larger creatures, bronze and black Khyber metal brace her skeleton, and a skull-faced mask is bolted directly to her horns.

Gemma goes first. Hooded, she draws her sword, runs up the steps and attacks a sorrowful sister. The sister answers with a curved surgical sickle, then lifts her veil, puts her toothless mouth to Gemma's and drinks from her, and Gemma burns her with *hellish rebuke*. Zero-One does not look up from the ritual. Eric climbs onto the observation gallery, shouts *"Down with the Empire!"*, and casts *spirit guardians* at sixth level. The choir of black sisters in the gallery takes the full force of it twice over. Eric tells them exactly who he is, Eric, the Chancellor of Sharn, *because I want them to know who it is.* The choir tolls him and Dario, fails, and bolts for the door, and Eric puts a *true strike* into one of them as they go. Dario lights Pale Fire, swings it two-handed under Sacred Weapon, and takes off two heads for fifty damage in two blows. In his female form, wings spread wide, he says: *"By the power of the Flame, heathens will perish."* The choir is down to its last sister. Gemma trips a sorrowful sister with a riposte, stabs her through the chest on a natural 20, and *misty steps* to Zero-One to give her a few temporary hit points. Zero-One is too deep in the trance to notice.

Then a voice Gemma has not heard since Korth: her mother superior, **Maelis Dorn**, whose seal is on the orders found under the Korramont. *"If you stop the ritual, she'll die. I didn't think I'd ever see you again, Apollonia. You were always a terrible sister, and now you're proving me right."* Gemma: *"I came back to get my report card."* Dorn has heard of the Chancellor of Sharn, and offers the room a rumour about the wetter side of his tastes. She tries to banish Gemma, who spends a lucky and holds on. Then she rings a black iron handbell. A sorrowful sister drains ten points off Gemma's maximum. Dorn steps out through the doorway, taking a *true strike* deep in the thigh from Eric as she goes, and seals the door behind her with her ring. Through the door she calls: *"Pathetic one. Enough of the demonstration. Complete your task."* Zero-One begins to levitate and convulse and pours a tremendous amount of energy into the body.

Eric dispels the seal and the door flies open, and his guardians catch Dorn and two armoured **battle sisters** in the corridor. Dorn's *hold monster* fails to hold Dario. Gemma wonders aloud whether to cut off Esther's head, and whether doing it with Esther's own sword would be a mistake. Eric touches Zero-One and casts *remove curse*, and nothing happens. Dario triggers the haste stone, which rises over his head as a whirling halo. The battle sisters hit him and Eric hard, and Dorn boils Eric's blood with *harm* for thirty necrotic. Then **Zero-One collapses**, the light going out of her eyes, and **Esther Crona sits up**. First her shoulders rise, then her head, then her wings, and she lifts off the slab dripping blood from a dozen wounds. Her first words are: *"Where is Richard Blaze? Which restaurant is he at?"*

Gemma has gone invisible, and Esther cannot find her. Eric drops a fifth-level fireball behind Dorn, who dodges it with the help of a legendary resistance, while both battle sisters burn. Dario, hasted, lands three blows and a blinding smite and leaves one of them blind and suffocating in her armour. When Gemma comes back into view on the stair, Esther points at her and then at her sword. She flies to Gemma, wrestles the sword out of her hand and throws her down the stairs for thirty-seven, and Gemma hits zero. A sorrowful sister kneels over her, drains another death save out of her and eight more from her maximum, and as she kisses her Gemma sees her true face, a beautiful elvish woman under the rot. *"You've done it. You've reached perfection."* Anyone who dies in this room rises as a sorrowful sister within a day. Eric's guardians kill the sorrowful sister and a battle sister, and his mass *healing word* brings Gemma back. A firebolt finishes the blinded sister against the wall. Dorn rings the bell again, and thunderous footsteps start coming down the hall.

Dario runs under Esther and lifts Zero-One from the floor, a pietà in a nun's habit and a bloodstained white robe. Lying on the stone, Gemma looks up to find **Kitsune** standing over her. *"You don't want her to get that. She has just as much of a claim to it as you do."* He adds that she may have wasted a good chance to do what he asked: the Emperor is in his bedroom somewhere, *just follow the girls in the red dress*. Gemma and Esther both call for the sword at the same moment, and it rises and hangs in the air between them. Esther rolls a 1 on the Charisma contest. The sword comes to Gemma glowing blue and she sends it away into nothing. Esther, in a mechanical voice: *"You are unworthy to hold the Crimson Sun."* Gemma: *"It's not even called that anymore."* She drinks a potion of greater healing.

Zero-One opens her eyes and looks up at Dario. *"Dario, I knew the Flame would warm my skin again."* Dario, in tears, says goodbye and vanishes with her on his spell-storing ring's *dimension door*, and nobody knows where. Esther descends on Gemma and shouts the words that broke the Iron Veil, ***"Your pact is void!"***, which ends Eric's guardians and strips Gemma of her pact magic for a turn. Eric gets behind Gemma, puts his hands on her shoulders, looks up at Esther and says: *"Did you know it was Richard who betrayed you to your death? It was Richard."* Then he *dimension doors* the two of them out, just as a huge shambling corpse, nine feet tall or more and with something of LeBeefe about it, comes through the door.

## Getting Out

The one place up there Eric can picture clearly is where the day began, so they land in front of the Tomb of Kaius I at twilight. A bell is ringing, and the white guards are moving. They scramble down the side of the hill, stealth rolls in single figures. A woman on horseback across the road lights a lantern and calls *"Who goes there?"* Gemma says they were sneaking into town for a bit of corn and thought they saw a possum. *"Not safe out here at night. All kinds of beasts out in the woods. Be safe, sisters."* She does not sound as though she means it. Out of her sight they stash the habits, and Gemma casts *seeming* again: Deirdre and Graham.

The Plaza of the Martyrs is having a summer night under a full moon, with a band in the middle and a crowd drinking, a little uneasy about the bells that started a few minutes ago. **Pork Broccoli** has spent his earnings on a designer suit and a fedora, and is horrified at the state of them: they should go up to the Lazaret and get those wounds seen to. Deirdre confides that she and Graham were watching the moon and things got a little wild in some prickly bushes. *"Naughty, naughty."* On the drive he tells them that plenty goes in and out of the Lodge, wine and tobacco and things in at the back door and out the other way. Graham tips him ten gold.

**Bimples** has the Fieldmaster's Suite ready, still at 500 gold a night, still the only room for non-members, and Deirdre pays 530. He pours them two glasses of very fine elvish brandy. Their colleagues are in the drawing room preparing for the night's hunts, which leave within the hour or two while there is still light to cross the river to the King's land on the far side. Hunting there needs a permit, and poaching is punishable by death, but a member's sponsorship covers it. He offers to send for sisters to see to their injuries. *"No!"*

In the suite there is a full dinner laid out and no sign of Dario or Zero-One. They take an hour's rest. Eric keeps his last high slots for the hunt. In the bath Gemma reaches for her patron, and Kitsune is suddenly lying in the tub with her, legs tangled with hers, holding a cup of tea. *"That was very dangerous. Don't pit me against her patron, if you can avoid it. There's a reason why it took three of you to kill her, and I couldn't give you that sword until after you did. If she's back, it's because it wants her to be back. It means it's unhappy with you. I can't bind its will forever."* Dario will be safe, he says, but messages between Gemma and Dario can be observed in transit here. She should dress for the woods. A wardrobe opens on two riding outfits, one with short pants, one with long boots, and a hat with a feather in it. *"You have to get close to the Emperor. Or more specifically, the future Emperor."* When Gemma asks what happens if she stops Uriel and the baby dies, he stands up out of the bath, wraps himself in a towel and turns on her. *"You're going to do what I asked you to do."* — *"Just like my dad did?"* — *"Your dad barely did anything. But he promised you to me. So you're going to do this. Don't harm that child."* He slams a door that was already closed.

The night hunt for the red bird moves to next session.

## Experience and levels

5,000 XP each, plus 1,500 each for the session's best moments: Gemma for taking the sword back from Esther, Eric for the guardians in the gallery, and Dario for cutting the sisters in half. **The party reaches 13th level.** Eric gains seventh-level spells. Dario's steed **Rumtum** now has wings, and he is choosing between *aura of life* and *aura of purity*, where *aura of life* would stop the Doves' life drain from lowering anyone's maximum. Gemma is deciding between a tenth warlock level and a fourth fighter level. Eric could cast *heroes' feast* if anyone can find a 1,000-gold bowl, and the party's gold is running low after two nights at the Lodge.
