Dolores Weathervein

Personality Traits
I have no qualms about stealing from the dead.
I fall in and out of “love” easily, and am always pursuing someone.
Flattery is my preferred trick for getting what I want.
I enjoy luxuries and the finer things in life.
Ideals
With every great discovery comes grave danger. The two walk hand in hand. 
Bonds
Ever since I was a child, I’ve heard stories about a lost family heirloom. I aim to find it and learn its secrets.
I have a lover whom I left on “bad terms”, I think about him every day. Perhaps we’ll meet again
Flaws
Your character is manipulative and overly dramatic
I can’t resist a pretty face.

BACKGROUND
Delores Weathervien was raised in a large Monastery / Temple to the God of Neptune in the city of Velkstaad off the Icy Sea. She believed she was a normal human orphan, but the temple priest would regale her with tales of a winged child who “Fell from the heavens during a blizzard.” and that this child was “Destined for Neptune’s sword Weathervien.” Insinuating that Delores was this child.... She thought they were full of shit.

Delores spent her time in the temple studying History, Cartography, Arcana and Religion, eventually becoming a Priestess of Neptune herself! She soon began traveling the coast, first to “spread the word of Neptune”, but soon she fell into “treasure hunting”, mostly consisting of grave robbing for treasures and coin. 

During this time she met a dashing man named (Undetermined). They fell in-love and traveled together for many years. Eventually they had a falling out when he started insisting on “settling down” and doing the “morally right” thing by NOT stealing from the dead..!?!?... Whatever! Dolores eventually left him without saying goodbye nearly a year ago.

Since then she’s been on the hunt to find this Weathervein sword that those Priests insist is HERS. This search has led her to the town of Saltmarsh.

Feature: Historical Knowledge
When you enter a ruin or dungeon, you can correctly ascertain its original purpose and determine its builders, whether those were dwarves, elves, humans, yuan-ti, or some other known race. In addition, you can determine the monetary value of art objects more than a century old.
