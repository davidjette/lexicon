Hopper

# **⭐ HOPPER’S REACTION WHEN THEY GIVE HIM THE DEED**

This is the moment where Hopper’s world changes — and he has NO idea who Edwin Graves is or why the Arcade was so weird.

Here’s the **corrected, copy‑ready version** of Hopper’s reaction:

⭐ Hopper’s Reaction (Canon Version)

> “Wait… wait wait wait…

> This is the DEED.

> To the ARCADE.

> You’re— you’re giving it to ME?”

> *He makes a tiny frog squeak.*

> “I don’t even know who owned this place\! Nobody did\! The records were sealed, the building was bought under some shell company, and the whole thing just… appeared one day. No staff. No owner. No deliveries. Just… opened.”

> He clutches the deed to his chest.

> “I thought it was cursed. Or haunted. Or both. But if YOU got the deed… then it’s yours to give. And if you’re giving it to me…”

> *He starts shaking with excitement.*

> “I LOVE YOU. I LOVE ALL OF YOU. I WILL NAME A SHELF AFTER YOU.”

Then he gets serious:

> “I don’t know who built that place, but it wasn’t anyone normal. I’ll take it off your hands. And I’ll make it something good.”

# **⭐ WHAT THE PLAYERS FIND INSIDE THE ARCADE** 

### **⭐ The Vibe**

* No employees  
* No owner  
* No signage  
* No maintenance  
* No deliveries  
* No human presence  
* Everything automated  
* Feels like a BioTec lab disguised as a fun center

### **⭐ The Games**

All BioTec‑influenced, but the players don’t know that yet.

* **Memory Dive** — reads surface thoughts  
* **Cog Runner** — Cogborn fleeing hunters  
* **Surgical Precision** — rhythm game with glowing scalpels  
* **The Collector** — claw machine with prosthetic limbs  
* **Pulse Sync** — a game that syncs to the player’s heartbeat

### **⭐ The Hypnosis Mechanic**

Some machines contain **BioTec chips** that:

* charm  
* mesmerize  
* loop dopamine  
* encourage compulsive play  
* drain money

Not mind control — more like magical casino addiction.

### **⭐ Hidden Clues**

If they investigate:

* BioTec serial numbers  
* shipping crates addressed to CircuitCity  
* maintenance logs signed “E.G.”  
* a hidden panel with a memory crystal slot  
* a locked back room with a small elevator shaft (now sealed)

This is the PERFECT breadcrumb trail.

# **⭐ WHAT HOPPER OFFERS THEM (NOW THAT HE DOESN’T KNOW THE OWNER)**

He’s overwhelmed with gratitude and sees this as life‑changing.

✔ 1–2 free uncommon magic items

✔ Permanent 25–30% discount

✔ Access to his “secret stash”

✔ A future favor

✔ A promise to expand his shop

✔ A promise to keep their secret

He also says:

> “Whoever owned that place… they weren’t normal.

> But it’s mine now. And I’ll make it safe.”

# **⭐ WHAT THE PLAYERS SEE WHEN THEY ENTER THE ARCADE**

The moment they step inside, the vibe is WRONG.

### **⭐ The Atmosphere**

* Neon lights flickering  
* A low hum like a heartbeat  
* The smell of stale sweat \+ metal  
* No employees  
* No music except the game sounds  
* Everything automated  
* The air feels… *charged*

### **⭐ The People Inside**

There are **3–6 people** inside, all Lower City locals.

They look:

* pale  
* exhausted  
* unwashed  
* dehydrated  
* eyes bloodshot  
* hands trembling  
* clothes dirty  
* pockets empty

They’ve clearly been here **for days**.

### **⭐ Their Behavior**

They don’t look up.  
They don’t speak.

They don’t acknowledge the trio.

They just keep:

* feeding coins  
* pressing buttons  
* staring at screens  
* repeating the same motions

If the trio tries to talk to them:

“Just… one more round…  
I’m so close…  
Just one more…

> I can win it this time…”

Their voices are dry and cracked.

### **⭐ The Mechanic**

Each of these people has failed a **Wisdom save** against the BioTec charm‑chip embedded in the machines.

They are not mind‑controlled.

They are **addicted** — magically, chemically, and psychologically.

If the trio tries to pull someone away:

* They resist  
* They panic  
* They beg to stay  
* They try to shove the trio away  
* They cry  
* They shake

It shows the cruelty of Edwin’s tech.

# **⭐ WHAT THE GAMES ARE DOING**

Each machine has a **BioTec chip** that:

* releases micro‑doses of dopamine  
* uses subliminal visual pulses  
* uses low‑frequency sound waves  
* uses micro‑hypnotic patterns  
* keeps players locked in a loop

This is how Edwin tested:

* memory extraction  
* emotional manipulation  
* neural conditioning  
* addiction cycles

The Arcade was a **field test site** for Project EDEN.

# **⭐ WHAT THE TRIO CAN FIND (IF THEY INVESTIGATE)**

* ✔ BioTec serial numbers  
* ✔ CircuitCity shipping crates  
* ✔ Maintenance logs signed “E.G.”  
* ✔ A hidden panel with a memory crystal slot  
* ✔ A locked back room  
* ✔ A sealed elevator shaft  
* ✔ A Vega Oracle 2.0 machine (optional)  
* ✔ A hidden camera system  
* ✔ A ledger of coin intake (absurdly high)  
* This is the PERFECT breadcrumb trail.

# **⭐ THE LAWYER SHOWING UP**

⭐ Who He Is  
**Candy Crush**  
Edwin Graves’ personal property lawyer.  
A fat, nervous man in a perfect candy color suit.  
Always sweating.  
Always looking over his shoulder.  
Knows WAY too much.  
Member of the Velvet Table Society   
Has ad’s all over Sharn

⭐ Why He Shows Up

He comes to:

* check on the Arcade  
* collect coin intake  
* reset the machines  
* ensure no one has tampered with anything

He has NO idea Edwin is dead.

⭐ His Entrance

He walks in holding a clipboard, muttering:

“Alright, let’s get this over with…

> Reset the intake counters, check the—”

⭐ What He Can Reveal

* Edwin owned the Arcade through a shell company.  
* Edwin used it as a “behavioral testing site.”  
* The machines are illegal BioTec prototypes.  
* The city never inspected the building.  
* The property records are sealed.  
* Edwin paid him in cash and memory crystals.  
* Edwin had “other sites” in Sharn.  
* Edwin was planning a “Phase 2.”

He does NOT know about:

* Project EDEN  
* The Underhive  
* The Nest  
* Esther  
* The Vault Underground

He only knows the legal side.

⭐ What He Does When They Show Him the Deed

He panics.

He will:

* offer information  
* offer documents  
* offer access codes  
* offer to sign over anything he can  
* beg them not to kill him
