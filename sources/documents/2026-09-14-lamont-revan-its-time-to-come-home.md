# "....It's time to come home....": LaMont's Revan short story

LaMont, who played Revan Darkcember and XIII in Temple Holdings, wrote this short story and sent it on 2022-09-14. Dave shared it on 2026-09-14 as a primary source: "original back story (short story) written by Lamont about Revan, add any details you find".

Source: `Downloads/Its-time-to-come-home-dnd-short.pdf` (5 pages, text extracted; two image-overlay fragments that did not extract are omitted; spelling as written).

The words spoken by Sable, The Immortal Tempest, of the Nimbus clan, was at one time all that
Revan needed to hear before he abandoned any and all to attend his sister. The last time Sable said
those words to Revan he made it home from self imposed isolation just in time to ﬁnd her, and
everyone else in their clan slaughtered.
So seeing that phrase written on the red dyed letter in his hand set him on edge. Like the goosebump
feeling right before being ambushed in an alley. If the words alone weren’t enough …
----------------------------------------------------
With regards,
Your Colleague in serving the dark Lord
Champain
---------------------------------------------------
“ Gritty Champain , You beautiful bitch,” The words left his mouth and into the Sigil night air. Slipping
out of the tavern, his eyes adjusting to darkness like a frog to water. The product of years of training,
and some inherited traits. Revan scanned the wrinkled letter one last time, crumpled it into a ball, and
with an underhand throw tossed it into the air. Focusing his mind and will, a ﬁrebolt shot from the
other hand and incinerated the letter. The ashes drifted lazily gently pulled by gravity, giving an acrid
smell to the air until a gentle breeze scattered it into nothingness.
“Rev...you gon go?”
Until EbonyBane the sentient shield thrummed on his back Revan hadn’t realized, but he’d been
holding his breath. It came rushing out. The vapor whisps climbing to the dark heavens. His thoughts
drifting like his breath.
“...50 gold Champain didn’t use red dye to turn the paper that color...”
“My dude. I can almost guarantee that wasn’t paper....And you didn’t answer my question. You
gon go?”
“ Home?”
The word exhaled, and his breath along with it. The condensed cloud drifting into the sky. Seemingly
pulled up into the clouds. The clouds that because of his family history he could see at night.
“Yeah, Onyx. You and I are gonna go home.”

“The third stair in the front is what gave you away. That and the fact that youre probably four hundred

Lazy almost tired.

So he knew I’d been here watching him. He’d been back here for some time. Alone this time. Talking

weapons when he went back inside. Armblades and wand sheaths from soulless bodies before they
were consumed by the ﬁre. Unbeknownst to me why, but this house called to me.
For years all I wanted to do was leave this house. Now that I’m free I’m somehow compelled to return.
“You play dragonchess?” Again his voice calls from the inside, just the other side of the wall. The
upward inﬂection gave it an almost hopeful sound, but perhaps it was me being eager and optimistic.
I’m in minimal danger. If he desired to destroy me he would have done so already. ...Or perhaps this is
the moment.
“Yes,” The ﬁrst time I’ve spoken out loud in quite some time. The words strange and distant.
 “Come in then.”
My legs carry me before I could register a decision.
Accustomed to following orders. Still.
The greeting room of the house still very much the same in its simplicity . Polished wood ﬂoors and
walls. Hundreds of years old. Shoji doors with tears in the paper. Squinting I see him sitting in the
middle of the room, on the ﬂoor, in the dark. Casually almost half asleep. Legs crossed in front of him.
His back and shield facing me.
I could shoot him right now. If I got the jump maybe-
“Do you need some light, dark never bothered me?”
A hilt in his left hand erupts in a slender tube of...pure sunlight. The room bathed in warm glow.
Somehow as well lit as if it was midday.
Only an imbecile would think he was defenseless, and for a moment you were one.

 That weapon... My ﬁrst impression of it was it pointed at my face after killing my overseer. “Now
would be a good time to get the hell out of my house!” Was all I needed to hear before I ran with fear
in my core.
What I would give to study that remarkable tool… that  weapon.
“Have a seat you’re making us nervous. I recognize you. You’re the one that gave us the code to
shutting down the big guy.” His swarthy face lit with the glow of contrived sunlight had a look of
bemused interest.
“Us?”
There was no one else here. The dragonchess set was a simple design, but elegantly made. It’s the
set from the master’s chambers. My overseer would occasionally challenge me to a game during our
occupation here. I had to let him win for fear of punishment.
Will you do the same for this killer?
“Would you mind if I utilized my own set? These pieces while excellent are often incongruent with my
hands.”
The words left my mouth and I immediately regretted how conspicuous they sounded.
A raised eyebrow followed by a barely perceptible lean forward were his only reactions.

Accidental as it was… it seems now I have your attention.
Reaching beneath my cloak I open the compartment in my abdomen that I made speciﬁcally to store
my set. If the man cared that I was reaching somewhere where I could pull out a weapon he didn’t
show it. The balanced demeanor didn’t changed until I ﬁnished setting up the pieces. It was replaced
with an eager determination. He put the saber of light to the side. Eﬀectively a lamp at this point,
Rubbing the tips of his ﬁngers together, and staring at the board. Irreverent of me as I took my seat
across from him.
I take my ﬁrst move; pawn to D13. Always my ﬁrst move in a match. Designation XIII was what I was
called for years. It coincided that pawn to D13 was a good starting move.
“What’s your name?”  The man asked. The question accompanied a move of paladin to H7. The move
typically ineﬀectual, but now a direct counter the checkmate I had planned in 5 moves.
May not have to worry about letting him win.
“My designation was Xeno-Imperial Interface-Indigo class.” I say. Moving another piece and
formulating another strategy.
Having memorized the board and possible developments. My attention now on him. Skin darker than
amberwood, his hair black and  braided in the tribal style of the infamous Nimbus clan. His
movements and strategy precise. Setting up a strong defense by controlling the middle of the board.
“Hmmm. What is it now?”
“I hadn’t put much thought to it.” I answered our next maneuvers in silence.
If he had thoughts on the matter he didn’t share them. Everyone else had names, and my kind had
designations. To step beyond that was something I wasn’t prepared to do at the moment.
What would it mean to have a name? Do I take my former masters name? Do I choose my own?
Children are given names-
“X-III?”
Sharply brought back from the mechanisms of deep thought the room is brought into focus. My turn.
Making my move, I return to my thoughts.
 He called me something...and I answered.
The game getting deeper, tactics and stratagems being revealed and changed on the ﬂy. The match is
the most competitive i’ve played in a long time, yet I ﬁnd myself unable to give it my full attention. My
eyes and curiosity continue to drift towards the sun like saber giving the room light.
“Pardon me Lord...”
“I’m not a LORD!”
“I mean Master of the hous-.”
“I’m wasn’t...not anymore-  Call me, Revan.”
The name elicited a memory. A snippet of a conversation I overheard when I was on watch outside of
the overseers oﬃce. A series of names mentioned as terrorists and conspirators against the company.
Deschain, Magnus, Kara, Drefan, and Revan was deﬁnitely amongst others.
A name can mean something.
Preparing for an immediate attack. I ask a question I know I should keep to myself.
“The Tempest?”
