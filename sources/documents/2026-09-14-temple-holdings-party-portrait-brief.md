## Image description (the brief)

Portrait of the following characters posing like a badass team. They are on the sandy shore of the Sword Coast. Behind them, coming out of the water and consuming the horizon is a GIANT alien golden egg. There is a small crack in the egg, with otherworldly UV light piercing through. In the sky, the moon has a chunk blown out of it. (From where the humongous egg hit it in its descent to Earth, a million years ago)



Kara - a charming brunette gypsy sorceress with copper dragon wings. She has a staff with a glowing moonstone on top, and inset into the wood is a small wand with an infinity symbol. She wears a brown tunic that is long enough to cover up because she has no pants. She wears several packs and pouches full of magical items. A white mouse sits on her shoulder. Perhaps she is locked in eye contact with St Cloud.

Eloise - A warlock who uses time and space to manipulate reality. She appears as a 13 year old human girl with delicate white moth wings.  She has dark skin, long wavy white hair and pale blue/white eyes. She wears white cloth / leather armor with a long white, almost iridescent silk-like scarf around her shoulders / neck which she likes to knit. She has a white moth familiar who is always in her hand or flying around her head. 

Zanzibar - A handsome human King Paladin in his early 30’s who wears gold plated armor, a gold crown and purple cape. He has blonde hair and a short blonde beard. His sword is made of glass and he has a shield. He is LARGE / tall and is the largest member of the group. 

Roland - Roland is a classic American cowboy. Think Clint Eastwood in the good the bad and the ugly. Cowboy hat and boots, 2 six shooters, perhaps bandoliers and a serape. Leathery skin, strong 5 o’clock shadow, etc.

Magnus - ​​a dwarven cleric from a remote monastery on a rocky island in the far reaches of the Moonshea Isles (the real world inspiration is Skellig Michael in Ireland). He wears green robes that are a little faded and rough around the edges. His belt helps him carry his holy book on his right hip and a pouch for magical components on his left. If the holy book is visible, it bears the symbol of a mountain with a heart inside it. His hands are wrapped with strips of cloth. His beard is red, perhaps with flecks of grey in it. As he poses in this picture, he holds in one hand the Helm of the Woods, a metal knight’s helmet with antlers, which he took when the party killed The Green Knight of Arthurian legend.

## Feedback, round 1

-such a great start! We are all pleased and grateful for your work. 

-the egg should be much bigger, taking up most of the horizon. It’s actually coming up out of the water rather than floating in the sky. So perhaps we see just the top ~half of it and it spans from Zanzibar to Yoshi, behind those mountain formations. With an eerie light shooting through the crack

-what are all the triangles floating in the sky? Would like to see the moon with the chunk blown out of it

-I don’t see St. Cloud here! I’d like to see the gang closer together (maybe we need to layer a bit, like a classic group photo) so that St. Cloud can be off To the side, clearly separate from the rest. Peering at us. He’s the bad guy!

-Kara should wear a tunic that comes down to her thighs, no pants, no gown, and boots. Her wings should be dragon skin and not feathery. Currently she is holding a flame in her hand but it would be better to just have her hand on her hip because she doesn’t really do fire magic. Staff should go all the way to the ground. Otherwise she is beautiful!!

-Magnus’s pants should be replaced with a robe (he’s a cleric from a monastery, so like a monk’s robe). I realize that might have been confusing, as he wears a belt over the robe, but the unpainted mini in the character art drive should help show what this looks like. 

-If Magnus can hold the Helm of the Woods in his right hand, that would be great. It’s fine if some of it goes behind his body to make it fit in the image nicely. The image for the Helm of the Woods is in the character drive now. Apologies for not including it before. I really thought it was in there.

-couch looks a little “too cool” could you please add a Baseball Bat—perhaps he is using it as a cane.

-Eloise: Could you have her right arm extended out slightly with the palm of her hand facing towards the “audience” and her white moth on her fingers. Similar to how she is posing in the photos of her 3D mini. Also, I’m not sure if she’s holding something in her left hand or if that’s part of her armor/scarf? She has no weapons.

-Zanzibar: So cool!! It kind of  looks like he has a ponytail? Can his hair look more similar to how his photos of his 3D mini looks ? Near shoulder length.

YOSHI: much taller and bigger—closer to Zanzibar’s size. Lightsaber in 1 hand, add a cocktail in the other. Can he be on a skateboard? Thanks! 

-Revan - one spear. Shaved head on the sides, ponytail of braids on top. Add pants and a spell book on his hip. New photo added to folder. 

Drefan: should have 4-6 inch mustaches, but not full beard. It resembles the feilong dragon he shapeshifts into. Love the sketch- so awesome.

Arana - great but less boobs please
