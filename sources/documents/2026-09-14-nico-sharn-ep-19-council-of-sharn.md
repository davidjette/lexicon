# **Episode 19: The Council of Sharn**

In the aftermath of Esther Crona’s death, Sharn entered an immediate need for stability before a full collapse. Digma Beeve arrived first, going straight to Richard and Calcifer. After a brief private exchange, he informed the trio that Richard and Calcifer would teleport to Zilspar Farm before anyone else arrived and proposed a cover story to protect them: Faith and Malrik Zeir had ambushed Richard’s private airship during the kidnapping, killing Richard, Calcifer, and two of Esther’s Honor Guards. With the Children of Ember already dead from the Vault Underground assault, the lie would hold. Digma left to coordinate the narrative.

Moments later, Jessica Grimpledink arrived, reporting that Zero‑One and Silver Flame paladins had seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up Black Dove clergy. Mass protests were erupting across Sharn, with citizens tearing down statues of Uriel. Nest and Fulcrum operatives had secured the Veil Building, and high‑ranking officials—including Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka—were being taken into custody. Locke Pierce’s Hundreds and R.U.S.T. were rounding up Iron Veil soldiers; many fled, many hid, and many surrendered.

Jessica warned the trio that representatives from every major faction were on their way to the penthouse. What followed became known as the first meeting of the Council of Sharn.

Representatives from Leef, NEST, the Silver Flame, R.U.S.T., the Hundreds, and the Underhive arrived with proposals and demands. Jessica opened the meeting, crediting the trio for preventing Sharn’s fall and framing the council’s purpose: stabilizing the city and preparing for continued resistance against the Empire.

Each faction presented its priorities: Leef sought alliance and stability; NEST called for trials and rebuilding funds; the Silver Flame pushed to outlaw the Black Doves and enforce citywide cremation; R.U.S.T. demanded Warforged citizenship, arrest of Iron Veil engineers, and a new infrastructure department; the Underhive requested autonomy, legal ownership of the Ziggurat and Vault Below, and a permanent council seat for Berenstain.

Tensions rose when Dario ordered Blair’s arrest during a dispute. She was placed on trial at a later time and eventually released.

Trials began immediately. Sister Nora, Hillary Heinrick, and Dr. Joanne Menka were found guilty and executed along with other high‑level Iron Veil officials. The Silver Flame’s cremation directive was enacted across the city. When the Silver Flame attempted to cremate Esther’s body, it was discovered missing. Witnesses reported two elven women, believed to be Black Doves, removing a corpse from the morgue during the chaos.

During the takeover of the Black Dove Hospital, The Nest and the Silver Flame uncovered a large golden bell inside a shipping crate and an unfinished bell tower. The artifact was identified as a replica of the ancient Fatanya (Hell’s) Nyel (Bell), a relic crafted by ancient elves from infernal metal and associated with raising the dead and inducing madness. A similar bell had been uncovered thirteen years earlier by the Unforeseen in the Greenbluff mines outside Newham.

By nightfall, a provisional Sharn government had formed. Eric was appointed Chancellor of Sharn and took residence in Esther’s former penthouse, now the seat of the new administration. Warforged forces offered troops and engineers to stabilize the city and restore the Cogs. Underhive drones joined the new Department of Infrastructure while maintaining their subterranean autonomy.

With the Iron Veil dissolved, the Black Doves dismantled, and Sharn’s leadership restructured, Sharn was free — but rebuilding had only begun.

**End of the Sharn Arc.**
