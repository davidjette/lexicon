# **Captive \- Lorian**

“My name is Lorian… I didn’t know the woman you found with me. She told me her name was Roma. She’d been there much longer than I had. Months, maybe more. She was already fading when they put me in that cell with her.”

“There are things I remember. Maybe it can help you? You saved us, I don’t know how else I can thank you.”

I was drugged most of the time. But some things stuck.”

“I’m from Korth. I worked as a dancer at a brothel there. One night a man came in — a half‑elf, handsome, charming, wealthy. He told me to call him Mr. H. Later I called him Hal. He said he could get me a real dancing job at the biggest club in Khorvaire. Said I’d make more gold than I’d ever dreamed of, performing for rich and powerful people. He paid me well, showed me a good time… and eventually I agreed. I followed him to Sharn.”

“He took me straight to a club in the upper city. I worked for nearly a week, made friends with staff, even stayed in a shared apartment with some staff (other dancers / servers) One day at work I was told that owner wanted to meet me. That’s when I first met Faith. She looked me over, said I’d ‘do nicely.’ After that… she took me to her private residence. It’s attached to the club, same building.”

“She asked me to perform for a man. A large man, sitting in shadow by a fireplace. I don’t remember much after that. I think that was the first time they drugged me.”

“They kept me in a chamber in their home. There were others there. Some tied up. Some bleeding out. Some already dead. They’d take me out sometimes… for him.”

“After a while, they drugged me again and moved me (from the upper city club / residence to the lower city club / residence). When I woke up, I was in that little cell where you found me. Roma was already there. 

“Malrik… when he fed off me, he’d complain. Said humans were scraps, said our blood was too weak. He talked about  ‘stronger stock,’ coming in from the monster lands. 

“Many times, Faith would beg him to change her, she would scream at him to not make her wait any longer. But he always refused. Said it wasn’t time.   
“One night though, after a lot of drugs and… a night with them… I passed out on their bed. We were all asleep. And then I woke up to a sound — a hatch opening in the floor.”

“A cloaked figure climbed up into the room. A woman. Malrik didn’t even open his eyes — he just moved. Like an animal. He leapt out of bed and pinned her to the wall by the throat.”

“Faith screamed at him to stop. And he did. Just like that. But then she came over to me and drugged me again. That’s the last thing I remember from that night.”

# ⭐ **THE VAULT UNDERGROUND**

Lorian takes a breath, steadying himself.  
LORIAN:  
“When you walk in from the street, you’re actually entering the top of the club. That’s where you pay your cover. There are bodyguards everywhere — big ones, and they don’t smile. They check bags, pockets, sometimes they pat you down. They’re looking for weapons… and for people they don’t want inside.” \- They don’t do this to VIP  
He rubs his arms, remembering.  
LORIAN:  
“Once you’re through the entrance, there’s a staircase that goes down. The first floor down — that’s the first real level of the nightclub.”

⭐ FIRST FLOOR DOWN — THE LAID‑BACK LEVEL  
LORIAN:  
“It’s the chill floor. Sofas, booths, a long bar, tables. You can look over the railing and see the other two floors below — everything is open in the middle like a big hollow cylinder.”  
He gestures a circle with his hands.  
LORIAN:  
“People hang out here to talk, drink, watch the crowd. It feels safe. That’s on purpose. Faith likes to watch from up here. She pretends she’s relaxing, but she’s scouting. Looking for people she thinks Malrik will like.”

⭐ SECOND FLOOR DOWN — THE PARTY FLOOR \- (where the vip tickets will bring them to a booth)  
LORIAN:  
“Go down one more level and you hit a hallway with the bathrooms. Past that is the second floor.”  
His voice lowers.  
LORIAN:  
“This is where the real partying happens. Bigger bar. Private dancers. Private booths with curtains. Loud music. People get drunker, looser. Easier to manipulate.”  
He looks away.  
LORIAN:  
“This is where Faith approached me. Told me I had ‘potential.’ Told me Malrik would want to see me.”

⭐ THIRD FLOOR DOWN — THE DANCE FLOOR \+ THE HALLWAY  
LORIAN:  
“The bottom floor is the dance floor. Packed. Loud. Dark. There’s a bar and some booths, but the important part is the hallway.”  
He points downward.  
LORIAN:  
“There’s a long hall with two doors.”  
He raises one finger.  
LORIAN:  
“One door leads to Faith’s private office.”  
He raises a second.  
LORIAN:  
“The other door leads to the lower floor of the Zeir penthouse.”  
He lets that sink in.  
LORIAN:  
“Yeah. Their home connects directly to the club. No one knows. No one except staff. And… people like me.”

⭐ FAITH’S OFFICE — WHERE IT STARTED  
LORIAN:  
“Faith’s office is small but fancy. Velvet chairs. A big desk. A mirror she stares into like she’s waiting for it to talk back.”  
He shivers.  
LORIAN:  
“She brought me there first. Told me I was ‘chosen.’ Told me Malrik would want to see me.”  
He swallows.  
LORIAN:  
“I didn’t understand what that meant.”

⭐ THE HIDDEN DOOR — INTO THE PENTHOUSE  
LORIAN:  
“In Faith’s office, there’s a door. It leads into the lower floor of the penthouse.”  
He draws a rough shape in the air.  
LORIAN:  
“You go through a hallway… and you pass a huge room. A ritual room. I wasn’t allowed inside, but I saw the floor markings. Circles. Chains. Candles. Something… wrong.”  
He shakes his head.  
LORIAN:  
“I don’t know what they do in there. But it’s not normal.”

⭐ THE PENTHOUSE — WHAT HE SAW  
LORIAN:  
“After the ritual room, there’s a staircase up into the kitchen. Big place. Too clean. Too quiet.”  
He looks down.  
LORIAN:  
“Then the living room. Low ceiling. Windows everywhere. You can see the clouds. The whole city.”  
His voice drops.  
LORIAN:  
“That’s where I met Malrik.”

⭐ MALRIK — THE FIRST MEETING  
Lorian’s hands tighten.  
LORIAN:  
“He sat in the dark. Watching me. He told me to perform. To dance. To… entertain him.”  
He shakes his head.  
LORIAN:  
“I didn’t want to.  
He looks away.  
LORIAN:  
“I performed.”

⭐ THE CHAINS — WEEKS OR MONTHS  
LORIAN:  
“After that, they drugged me. I woke up in a room off the kitchen. Chains on the wall. Other people chained beside me.”  
His voice cracks.  
LORIAN:  
“We were kept there. For weeks. Maybe months. It’s hard to know. They made us perform for Malrik. For guests. For… rituals.”  
He doesn’t elaborate.  
He doesn’t need to.

⭐ THE MOVE TO THE VAULT BELOW  
LORIAN:  
“One day they blindfolded me. Took me down. I thought they were going to kill me.”  
He exhales.  
LORIAN:  
“But they moved me to the Vault Below. The lower‑city club. That’s where you found me.”  
He looks at the trio with gratitude and shame.  
LORIAN:  
“You saved my life.”

⭐ WHY THIS MATTERS  
Lorian straightens, voice steady.  
LORIAN:  
“If you’re going after them… I can help. I know the layout. The entrances. The rules. The drinks. The staff. The bouncers. The way Faith talks. The way Malrik moves.”  
He meets their eyes.  
LORIAN:  
“I can get you inside. Or at least help you blend in.”  
He hesitates.  
LORIAN:  
“I’ll go with you if you want. I’m scared. But I’m not useless.”

Recruiter  

If the players enter the private residence from the bottom floor of the club (from a hall door or from a door in Faith's office) the first "room" they will get to is a large industrial looking ritual room with Children of Ember cultists. Unless they try to sneak past, they will most likely trigger a fight with them. Round the corner from this large room is a set of stairs that leads up to the main living floor and directly into a large kitchen. Part of the kitchen is hidden from the main great room, but there is a long bar / countertop that spans the length of one side of the great room, off this bar area is a door that leads to an outside shuttle platform with a parked private shuttle. Off the main part of the kitchen is a cell full of sick and drugged “cattle”. There are two doors into the cell, one from the kitchen and one from a private office (not the same office as the club office). In this office there is a back / secret door into the SECOND FLOOR DOWN — THE PARTY FLOOR. Now, back to the large great room, there are walls lined with windows and a low ceiling. A large fireplace and a round platform with chains on the floor. There are some wrap-around sofas and a door that leads to the master bedroom. Through the bedroom is a bathroom and a door to the outside terrace pool. 

When they enter the main floor, all the danger will be in the great room, Malrik and Faith sitting in chairs next to the large fireplace watching drugged “cattle” perform for them on the round platform in the middle of the room. Watching from one sofa is Hallorn, on the other sofa are "recruiters" with two young ladies who are scared and not drugged sitting between them men (not sure on how many yet. 

I am thinking that the recruiters, Faith, Hallorn and Malrik should all have monster stats, but the men should not be too hard, more like minions. Hallorn I imagine as a bard / swashbuckler type? Faith as a fire sorcerer of some kind, though I imagine that she won't openly fight at first, but does have some power. Malrik I want to have vampire stats and a two stage / change into beast mode.
