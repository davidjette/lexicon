Evil Campaign - Nichole DM 2

Six months have passed since Uriel and Esther met with the council of 12 in the Imperial Capital Korth, bringing them news of a new found alliance with the Children of Winter, the Dark druids of the Eldeen Reaches.

In that time there have been many changes. Imperial council member, Lord Albert Spear who is head of City planning and development granted New Ham an Imperial Charter to grow from a town into a city. Now known as Newham, the gateway to the East.

Things moved fast in Newham, caravans of merchants, train cars full of construction equipment and labourers and airships with arcane builders came in droves. New homes were built and high end stores and restaurants moved in, coming directly from the capital. The Lightning Rail Tavern was expanded into a glorious Hotel to accommodate all the new travelers to Newham’s new Casino, The Clamshell Casino. The river Dark which flows near Newham was redirected and turned into a man-made lake, named Lake LaBeef after the City's first Major. John C. LeBeefe now major of Newham, leaves his slave trade duties in the hands of his Overseerer Tibedeax but finds himself entangled in the world of gambling and development deals. He is also seen around town with a lovely young redhead on his arm. Her name is Ova Santoro, the 23 year old daughter of the Casio Pit Boss Christopher Santoro.  LeBeef is now proficient in gaming dice set ONLY if Ova is with him. He now dons a red velvet sash given to him during the swearing-in ceremony as Major of Newham. The Red Velvet sash is a magical item and gives the wearer 1 Lucky roll per 24 hours.

The Hospital that Uriel founded has grown and thrived into a medical research and educational facility. She heads the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases AKA,  U.N.D.E.A.D. but she notices that Gabriel Duine has kept her distance from Newham and in turn Uriel, only to make an appearance at the ground breaking ceremony for the expansion of the university. For these 6 months Uriel has buried herself in her studies. The sex magic she used on Flighter has proven useful as she now has a loyal ghoul at her side (although on ocation shes caught him vigorously masterbating to her anytime they’re alone.) Knowing she couldn’t openly keep a ghoul around the hospital she patched up the head wound he received in death and she discovered that applying the drug ”Embrace” on him once a week prevents him from decomposing. This only seems to work on him so far, as experiments on fresh corpses with Embrace have failed. People around the university whisper that this quiet, handsome soldier must be her lover. Flighter only follows Uriel’s orders, has the stats of a Ghoul (for now) but is also able to use the same weapons he used in life (Greatsword). He is still proficient in navigation and can speak a handful of words. After months of laboratory studies Uriel has and is now proficient with a Herbalism Kit.

Locke Pierce went back to the Rekkenmark Academy in Rekkenmark to continue his studies but was soon sent back to Newham after construction on the east side of town uncovered a series of ancient passageways seeming to lead away from the city. With this Locke Pierce now has a semi-permanent post in Newham as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex which consist of Director Lock Pierce, his secretary a young woman named Tessa Moongrove and a dig crew of 4. Pierce works and lives out a newly constructed brownstone near the docks of Lake LaBeef. Lock Pierce is now proficient in Catagraphy, has cartographers tools and dons a pair of brown leather gloves that when worn allow the wearer to cast the cantrip Mold Earth. 

After the meeting with the council, Esther Crona spent the first month back in the Eldeen Reaches, traveling with Imperial Council member, Minister Henry Heighrick, Chief diplomat and head of the ministry of diplomacy. There they finalized the Alliance treaty with the Children of Winter and Dark Queen Ivanka. Back in Karrnath Esther then headed up a team to infiltrate the supposed rebel base in the Shadowmount Forest which was found long abandoned. 

Once back in the Imperial Capital of Korth, Esther found herself awaiting her next assignment, which never came. Months went by, Esther, now living in the manor of her late master Varyn Crona, spent her time taking up Varyns old hobby of painting and reading up on art history in the manors library. She also began practicing new magics, including the cleaning magic director Pierce always used, but also unlocking something inside her that seemed to come naturally, expanding on the lightning magics she's used before to lure in her foes. Nights would bring heavy drinking and frequent sexual encounters with Chef Richard Blaze. Her sleep was restless, with vivid dreams replaying her Masters death and the masked assailant that murdered him. Varyns voice seems to speak to her through his old sword. “Esther, Together we will rule!” Months of practice has made Esther proficient with painters supplies. 

The warm spring weather brushes through Karrnath bringing  “The Rejuvenation '' a month-long celebration of Emperor Kaius III and the anniversary of the end of the Last War. One Spring day there is a knock at Esther's door, she was surprised to see two members of the Emperor’s Council standing at her doorstep, men she recognizes. Magnolia Steel, a Human Wizard and Chancellor of the Rekkenmark Academy, and Garth Brooks, Human Cleric and Bishop of the Sovereign Host.

She offers them wine in her study as they make their presence known. 

Construction in Newham has unearthed a broken tablet in an ancient language. They believe this may be part of one of 3 ancient tablets that foretell the coming of a great time of change in Eberron. Emperor Kaius has taken interest in these tablets and entrusts High Inquisitor, Esther Crona and the team who uncovered the Fatanya (Hells) Nyel (Bell) to retrieve these tablets. Whether the tablets foretell the coming of Kauis III and The Mourning, or something beyond that is not something she is to concern herself with. Retrieving the tablets and securing their return to the Emperor is the task at hand. Director Lock Pierce, Mayor John C. LaBeef and Doctor Uriel Qualanthri have all been notified and are to meet Esther at the Lightning Rail Hotel where Esther has a room,  on the last day of The Rejuvenation. 

A Bards Tale: (Hamilton Connor and his acting troupe)
Centuries ago there was a King named Galafar who united the 5 nations of Khorvaire into a mighty empire. King Jarot, a descendant of Galafar, had 5 children; upon his death he gifted them each their own kingdoms; Breeland in which The lightning rail originates. Aundair with its beautiful swaths of fertile fields that supply the nations with food and fine wines. Thrane with it’s lush forest and natural waterways. Cyre with its strong scientific and manufacturing power and Karnnath the first human established nations on the soil of Khorvaire which was ruled by the great Kaius the 1st. 

Jarot’s children were unable to live in peace and fought to claim the empire as their own. A 100 years of war ravaged the lands and took the life of Kaius the 1st, Khorvaire soon fell to ruin. The scientifically advanced people of Cyre came close to winning the war, as they had created the mechanical soldiers known as the warforged to aid them in battle. They were about to conquer when 21 years ago to this day,, Kaius the 3rd upon his Great Eagle and flanked by his loyal 13 stood upon a mountain top overlooking a great battle in the lands of Cyre. Kaius the 3rd raised his Greatsword to the sky, splitting the darkened clouds above revealing the morning sun, a great Crimson Sun. He pointed his greatsword back down to the battle and the Crimson Sun flashed bright and hot upon the land of Cyre. Only the Warforged survived. They worshiped him as their savior and he gifted the Mournlands to the Souled Machines. Ending the last war. Today we celebrate the morning sun, The Rejuvenation. Praise Emperor Kaius III
