DM group copy: Evil Campaign 3 - Nico (updated with notes after game)

After being dropped off at the coastal town of Aeire you all spent the next day reflecting on your “vacation” which brought both extreme highs and very low, lows. 
You successfully infiltrated and almost completely wiped out a rebel base located in the Dragon Watch Isles led by the Black Dove leader, Gabriel Dwin, and you were able to secure many items of great worth and importance.
On the other hand, the last leg of your vacation brought you all great humiliation when you were captured, stripped of your items then left stranded on an island by the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts. 
Roberts eventually returned your items (minus coin and the yellow diamonds) and brought you back to Aeire. You all agreed to keep what happened here a secret between you, and to go back to your everyday lives as if these events never happened. 

The next morning Locke Pierce was able to secure a horse drawn carriage to take you all back up through the Blade Desert to the mostly abandoned village of Arden Crest, which is the nearest settlement to the dig site where Locke and Esther are stationed. From there LaBeef and Uriel planned to continue by carriage up to South Bend where they could then catch the lightning rail back to Newham. 

Vargus the coachman keeps the carriage moving at a steady pace, but the ride through the Blade Desert is both long and rough and by midday the desert heat is a scorching 110 degrees. You all find yourselves drained from the past few days events and eventually LaBeef and Esther drift off into a restless sleep. Locke on the other hand switches his core processor into rest mode and Uriel, not requiring sleep, just closes her eyes to meditate on her thoughts.

LaBeef:
LaBeef, you’ve been feeling absolutely terrible since leaving the small island where Roberts stranded you and your companions. While you do feel like you gained some power when there you also feel like a dark shadow has attached itself to you. You continually feel weighed down and tired and move at a slower pace., You now have a movement speed of 20ft.

LaBeef, you easily drift off to sleep. In your dream you’re sitting at a dice table in the Clamshell Casino, next to you sits Ova clapping with excitement as the dealer pushes a giant pile of gold across the table right in front of you. But Instead of feeling the joy from winning this large amount of gold, you feel a deep despair overcome you. You look up across the casino and see a tall hooded figure draped in black robes. You quickly get up from the table and start walking towards the hooded figure, who turns and leaves out the front doors as you approach. You push yourself to run the last few steps out the doors. The figure is gone but the deep feeling of complete and total dread is even more intense as you stand outside, the sky bathed in a rose hue.. Something has happened...


Locke:
Locke, in rest mode your mind drifts to the events with the Pirate Joseph Roberts. How did he know so much about you when you know so little about your own past? You begin thinking back to the mechanical lizards you discovered in the catacombs near Newham. Besides Warforged you've never seen or heard of any other mechanical type beings in existence, especially ones that look to be near ancient. But, all of a sudden an intense sense of despair overcomes you. Something’s not right. An image flashes in your processor mind, a tall figure draped in hooded black robes. You feel the figure watching you as the world is cast in a red hue. 

Uriel:
Uriel, with your eyes closed you begin daydreaming about your work at the university and your experiments with sex magics. “I need to find more Embrace!” you think to yourself. You then start imagining yourself pregnant, walking nude through city streets with hordes of undead swarming behind you under your command, Pirate Joseph Roberts being one of them of course. 
But your daydreams are interrupted as a deep sense of dread and foreboding overcomes you. Somethings not right.  Your eyes snap open. You’re alone in the carriage. You look out the window up to the sky and the bright desert sun begins to darken as blood pools over it. Everything around you is bathed in a crimson red.. Something is very wrong. “Seven years!” a voice behind you says. You turn around and see a hooded figure next to you. As you try to scream you awake from your meditation with a jolt. Sitting in the carriage next to you are Locke, Esther and LaBeef, all looking as horrified and confused as you.

The carriage is no longer moving when you all come-to. When you look out the window Esther and Locke can see they’ve arrived in the village of Arden Crest. It is now dusk, and no one is to be seen, not even Vargus the carriage driver.



Throne Room
The room suddenly begins to spin. Your stomachs lurche, and you all stumble trying to maintain balance only to find yourself being pulled away. Not up or down or forward or back but in some new direction entirely incomprehensible. With a loud whoosh it ends, as soon as it had begun.

You are no longer in the ancient tomb outside of Arden Crest, but now you stand together in a vast ornate hall of gold and red. At the end of the hall a magnificent throne of gold sits atop a flight of stairs where you see two figures. One, the dark hooded figure from your dreams and upon the throne sits a man draped in fine silks and linens, his face mostly covered except for his glowing red eyes. A large familiar symbol of the Crimson Sun is laid before you, carved into the marble floor. Esther knows where she is, she knows who sits upon this throne. You all know, as there is no denying you all stand before the great Emperor, Kaius III.

The hooded figure slowly begins to descend the stairs, almost as if it were gliding. It speaks in a voice which carries around the great Throne room as if many voices were speaking at once. 

“We’ve been watching you, and you’ve proven yourselves time and again to be great assets to the Empire. You retrieved the ancient bell artifact, you’ve secured a powerful ally to the West in the Eldeen Reaches, you’ve turned a once slum of a town into a great new city, and it looks as if you’ve uncovered the second of 4 ancient stone tablets. But you’ve dug too deep this time. For 7 long years it took me to get where I was, to get in, to get close. I did it all for the Empire and now it is all ruined, because of you!”

The figure stops and pulls back the hood, dropping the black robes to the floor. Before you stands the most beautiful woman you’ve ever seen. Standing there nude with long flowing white hair and a large dragon mark on her pelvis and stomach stands Gabriel Duin.

“My cover stopped there, on that island where you burned my work. 7 year of infiltration and it’s destroyed because you kept digging. For 7 years not a soul knew I had joined the rebellion as a spy, that I gained their trust, that I fed them information which got imperial agents and soldiers killed, you too were expendable, just bodies used to hide my cover, I can never go back as the cell I belonged to is now gone. But I’m no longer angry, we (points to Kiaus) understand now. You 4 may be the best rebel hunters we have! 
 
Mayor John C. LaBeef, you have an interesting power… harness it to continue in aiding the empire. Perhaps you’ll find yourself with even more power and influence in the empire… but not with that thing following you… (cast fairy fire revealing the shadow creature attached to LaBeef) if tries to flee but Dwin flicks her hand and it screams in pain as it withers and dies of necromantic damage. 

Director Pierce, you’ve continued to help the empire acquire these relics of importance and it does not stop here. There is more to be found, much more. The ancient tablets themselves can not be decoded until we have all 4. The rebels too were after these tablets, and I believe they have been found by more powerful rebel forces than I was ever involved in. One was found by the dwarfs to the East in the Ironroot Mountains. Last I knew they planned to move it to Rebel territory in Zilargo, if it has happened, I do not know.. Another is being kept in Aundair at a rebel stronghold believed to be somewhere near Fairhaven…. Esther, you’re familiar with Fairhaven, are you not?

I trained you well Uriel, perhaps too well. I do find it….. cute, that you believe your mark means anything, or perhaps Vol blood runs through your veins as it does mine, but don’t let my forgiveness fool you Uriel, don’t play your silly games with me. Study the undead all you wish but get in my way and you will become another body to add to my collection. 

She waves her hand over the Crimson Sun symbol on the floor and it opens up revealing a rising stone platform underneath. In the middle stands a chained woman with a hood over her head, laying around her are 15 charred bodies, the bodies of the rebels you killed and burned. 
“A gift, for you dear Uriel.” Gabriel leads that chained woman over to Uriel, unhoods the woman. Before you, shivering with fright, is Lady Glow. 

Kaius will say something to Ether about Varyin Crona being a loyal Warrior and to avenge his death. Hinting that I could perhaps take Varyin’s place one day.

Magic Items:
Sentinel Shield - (uncommon) (Esther) While holding this shield, you have advantage on initiative rolls and Wisdom (Perception) checks. The shield is emblazoned with a symbol of an eye. A shield is made from wood or metal and is carried in one hand. Wielding a shield increases your Armor Class by 2. You can benefit from only one shield at a time.
Gauntlets of Ogre Power - (uncommon, a) (Esther) Your Strength score is 19 while you wear these gauntlets. They have no effect on you if your Strength is 19 or higher without them.
Heart of Commanding Dust Devil - (rare) (Uriel) A heart of an ancient mummy, you can use an action to speak the command word and summon a Dust Devil. The heart can't be used this way again until the next dawn.
Restorative Ointment - (common) (Uriel) This glass jar, 3 inches in diameter, contains 10 doses of a thick mixture that smells faintly of aloe. The jar and its contents weigh 1/2 pound. As an action, one dose of the ointment can be swallowed or applied to the skin. The creature that receives it regains 2d8 + 2 hit points, ceases to be poisoned, and is cured of any disease.
Pistol of Lightning - (rare, a) (Locke) This ornate platinum pistol has 7 charges. While holding it, you can use an action to expend 1 or more of its charges to cast the lightning bolt spell (save DC 15) from it. For 1 charge, you cast the 3rd-level version of the spell. You can increase the spell slot level by one for each additional charge you expend. The pistol regains 1d6 + 1 expended charges daily at dawn. If you expend the wand's last charge, roll a d20. On a 1, the pistol crumbles into ashes and is destroyed.
Gloves of Thievery - (uncommon) (Locke) These gloves are invisible while worn. While wearing them, you gain a +5 bonus to Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) checks and Dexterity checks made to pick locks.
Tentacle Rod - (rare, a) (LaBeef) This rod is a magic weapon that ends in three rubbery tentacles. While holding the rod, you can use an action to direct each tentacle to attack a creature you can see within 15 feet of you. Each tentacle makes a melee attack roll with a +9 bonus. On a hit, the tentacle deals 1d6 bludgeoning damage. If you hit a target with all three tentacles, it must make a DC 15 Constitution saving throw. On a failure, the creature's speed is halved, it has disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws, and it can't use reactions for 1 minute. Moreover, on each of its turns, it can take either an action or a bonus action, but not both. At the end of each of its turns, it can repeat the saving throw, ending the effect on itself on a success.
