# Who the Red Eye is (Dave, in chat 2026-09-28, verbatim)

Asked whether the Red Eye revered by the robot cult in The Starsong Awakens is Barrias (whose nom de guerre among the
robot resistance is "Red Eye") or Nine, as one line of JL's notes has it:

> The big reveal of the game was that the Red Eye that Kol and his partner were looking for wasn't Barrias from the beginnings of the Resistence but rather Barrias's 'son' who took his name Red Eye and let the robot resistence much farther into not just active hostility with the Sovereignty but an obsession with the now banned and vanished Lexicon, out in space somewhere, which is why he finds himself at Necis trying to commune with it again.

Notes on applying it:
- "Red Eye" began as Barrias's name at the beginnings of the robot resistance.
- The Red Eye whom Kol and Paytra hunted in The Starsong Awakens is **Nine**, Barrias's "son", who took the name.
- Nine led the robot resistance into open hostility with the Sovereignty and into an obsession with the banned,
  vanished Lexicon; that is why he is at Necis, trying to commune with it again.
- This was the big reveal of the game.
