## What this establishes

- The Lexicon has a Campaigns section: a tile on the front page and one page per campaign, each with a banner and a campaign index.
- The campaigns, in order: Ages of the Infanta, Ghosts of Twatmarsh, Dead Man's Hand, Unforeseen, The Unforeseen Strike Back, Starfall: TNG.
- The campaign is called Ages of the Infanta on its page, in place of Temple Holdings.
- Arcaneum has no page of its own. Its articles are listed under Ages of the Infanta.
- Unforeseen and The Unforeseen Strike Back are separate campaigns with separate pages, although they share a universe. An article that appears in both is listed under both.
- The player characters of Unforeseen are Esther, Uriel, Locke and LeBeefe. The player characters of The Unforeseen Strike Back are Gemma, Dario and Eric.
- Each index shows highlights first and the full list after. Highlights are chosen for what sets the campaign apart from the others.
- The text on each campaign card is Dave's own summary of the campaign, given above. "sieze" is corrected to "seize". The Unforeseen Strike Back card opens "In the rail outpost of Leef," ahead of his sentence, following his instruction to start it from the kickoff in Leef.
- The player characters shown first on the Starfall: TNG page are Jim, Caprica, Barrias, Buttsack, Carlos, Sandy and Se7en. Every campaign page groups its player characters at the top of People.
- The player characters of Ages of the Infanta, in Dave's order: Kara, Roland, Drefan, Magnus, Brownsleeves, Couch, Zanzibar, Stonecypher (shown sealed), Revan, Pim, XIII, Fulori and Cookie Dumptruck.
- Tripp Sandhill is a player character of Dead Man's Hand, with the eight already listed.
- Brownsleeves was JL's character for the first session of Ages of the Infanta. He died in the shipwreck, trying to free more caged slaves from the hold as the ship went down. The Brownsleeves on the moon, the Short King, is a different loop of the same character, possibly a Metra loop.
- The Twatmarsh and Starfall player-character lists stand as shown.
- Brownsleeves is not in the party's group portrait. His picture shows him at level 1, picking the lock of a crate that holds an elf family in the flooded hold, lit by a fleck of light the mother has cast, as the door opens.
- In his picture Brownsleeves wears brown sleeves, and the family is shut in with no gap to climb through.
