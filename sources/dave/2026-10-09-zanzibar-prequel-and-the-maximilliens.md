# Dave: the Zanzibar prequel and the Maximilliens (2026-10-09)

Dave's statement in chat, 2026-10-09, verbatim. It answers two questions put to him after his account
of Kara (sources/dave/2026-10-09-kara-narrative.md): whether Fortuno's daughter Andira survived, and what happened to Maximillien's family.

---

I gues Andira did die, oops.  When they first discovered her she was saved, but then she died. People loyal to the War Wizards and a rival house had the Maximillians tried for treason, after they were shown to have helped Maximillian (the one we know as Zanzibar's best friend who accompanied him through all down time and sometimes in the campaign) to hire a rogue (Stonecypher) to help Zanzibar leave the town un noticed, just after the tragic death of his father.  The opening of their prequel saga began at a wedding of one of Zanzibar's cousins, when the ancient temple held aloft by Zarusian priestly magic collapsed on the royal family and the guests, putting King Zanzibar in a coma and killing many of his kin including the bride and groom. Many suspected this meant Zarus has left his heaven like the other gods and had born as an infanta somewhere in the world. The War Wizards wanted this infanta like the others, but Zanzibar pledged to find it on his own, for his kingdom and the good of Punis and Suzail. Max accepted the help of Stonecypher, a fixer (who was secretly hired by Celion to follow Zanzibar and when finding the Zarus infanta - to kill it, and was given a cursed ring of Wish - this is the object hse used to make the Wish that sealed the lips of time and obliviated herself, Cthulhu, and the quest. Zanzibar Max and Stonecypher boarded a new lightning rail train from Suzail to Baldur's Gate which Max had discovered had cargo important to the war wizards. The party found it was the infanta Una, they stole the vault wand (which the conductor has used to steal the train's treasures including Una) and before the out of control engine crashed, turned the gates of the Baldur'sgate station into a portal to a void, swallowing it in nothingness (leading to its presence in the final moments of Cypher and Dark Zan) After th train crash, the three and Una made their way across the sea to Motherstone, which had just been liberated from the Company. It was once there Max was able to receive word that many of his brothers and father were hanged for treason and the deception and asasination of the younger Zanzibar. They were not cleared of this until Zanzibar's return during the coup at Suzail, where he laid the blame on the War Wizards, and then marched on the Arcaneum with the Purple Dragon Knights of Cormyr and the forces of Punis together.

## What this establishes

- Andira, Fortuno's daughter, was saved when the party first discovered her and died afterwards.
- The prequel of Zanzibar and Stonecypher opened at a cousin's wedding, when the ancient temple held aloft
  by Zarusian priestly magic collapsed, put King Zanzibar in a coma and killed many of his kin.
- Many suspected Zarus had been born as an Infanta. The War Wizards wanted it; Zanzibar pledged to find
  it himself.
- Maximillien, Zanzibar's best friend, hired the fixer Stonecypher to get him out of town unnoticed.
  Celion had secretly hired her to follow Zanzibar and kill the Zarus Infanta, and gave her a cursed ring
  of Wish, the object she used for the Wish that obliviated herself, Cthulhu and the quest.
- The three took the new lightning rail train from Suzail to Baldur's Gate, found Una aboard, stole the
  vault wand the conductor had used to steal the train's treasures, and turned the station gates into a
  portal to a void. They and Una then crossed the sea to Motherstone, just liberated from the Company.
- Max's father and many of his brothers were tried by people loyal to the War Wizards and a rival house
  and hanged for treason and for the deception and assassination of the younger Zanzibar. They were
  cleared when Zanzibar returned at the Suzail Coup and blamed the War Wizards, after which he marched
  on the Arcaneum with the Purple Dragon Knights of Cormyr and the forces of Punis.
