# Stonecypher: Dave's account, given in chat 2026-09-12 (verbatim)

> Stonecypher was a PC played by Nico, a rogue-warlock who met Marcus and swore to follow her and was a raven queen warlock of the chain (a raven named po) until Marcus disappeared with her sister the Lady of Pain, after which she met June, infanta of Fate (istus) at Refuge, and become Chronocypher a warlock of time, until later when she was plugged into the mind of Zoth at Rlyeh and later split in time, remaining in the deep past as Chronocypher in a hidden refuge with Pim and the Changelings of the Moon, and was incarnated as Eloise the Infanta of Time in Antiquity (in the 1st infanta crisis, this time the party witnessing it first hand in their 'second loop') it was Cypher's presence in all three times which allowed her to coordinate the three way attack on the egg, and in the end she cast the Wish which obliviated Cthulhu but also obvliated herself.  Her echo is felt in the three-fold god the Fates - Eloise, Istus, and Cypher, the gods of Time, Fate and Mystery, who also appear as a child, a mother, and crone.
