Notes on applying it:
- **General rule (JL):** the Starfall notes are played ("yeah that happened") except for plans he dumped. Known dumps:
  Nilpferd and Glup having a child (Schnuppy); Strahd and the Vodoni (never met); Karametra as the Astral archlich;
  the notes' version of the Prophet; the scripted parting words between Nine and Barrias.
- **Nilpferd is a hippo (Giff).** There was no mini.
- **Picon happened.** Se7en could not enter because he failed the trials.
- **Buttsack was on the Plane of Elemental Chaos** (where the Githzerai had been seen before).
- **The NO FU TU RE key puzzle happened.**
- **Sphinx doors:** the dragon door WAS entered (Nico, who made the map: Se7en and Barrias at the base of a large
  tree, a ship, many dragons, one of which spoke to them; then back out and through another door; this outranks
  JL's "I don't think"). The Cthulhu room was entered; it made them sick, they could understand nothing, and left.
  They "saw" inside the Egg during the fight, in a deliberately distorted map.
- **The Prophet was Tillamook**, who believed he was the prophet of Ao but was being played by the beholders.
- **Hermes' trial:** outcome not remembered; do not state one.
- **MacFlecknoe was found and the dragon's orb taken.**
