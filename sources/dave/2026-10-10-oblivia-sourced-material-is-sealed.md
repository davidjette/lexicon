Dave's instruction in chat, 2026-10-10, verbatim.

---

Anything sourced from "the Oblivia" should be redacted and in the secrets list.

---

## What this establishes

- Every passage marked *Recovered from the Oblivia* is sealed with a redaction, so the reader has to
  reveal it, and it is listed on the Sealed Records page.
- The marker stays visible above the sealed passage.

## How it was applied

- A marker at the top of a section seals that section, down to the next heading.
- A marker in the middle of a section seals the paragraph it marks.
- A marker under the lead of an article (Aurora & Astra Saharel, Carlysle St. Cloud, Kara(metra),
  the Waning One, Netheril, Zeal) seals the body of every section of that article. The lead stays
  readable and the headings stay visible.
- Paragraphs that say what the world still remembers, and the Related line, stay outside the seal.
