# Dave, 2026-11-14: end of the campaign, release of the canon locks

Verbatim, in chat, the day after Korth Ep 22 (played 2026-11-13), which ended the Korth arc of The Unforeseen Strikes Back:

> Do a full examination of the Lexicon. Review all canon locks in light of revelations and the end of the current campaign. release those locks. ... (feel free to include actual table-true material from the modules into the lexicon articles now)

The ellipsis stands for parts of the same message that concern other work.

## What this establishes

- Every lock, seal and embargo on the Eberron campaigns is released. The register of what was released is CANON 5av.
- The Korth and Lazaret module files may now be used as sources for articles, for material that is table-true: true in the world as the game was played. Where a module planned something that play contradicted, play stands and the plan is left out.
- Stat blocks and numbers are still not published as prose. Articles describe what things are and do.
