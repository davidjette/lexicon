# Dave's answers after Korth Ep 23 (2026-11-21)

Dave Jette, in conversation on 2026-11-21, answering questions raised by Korth Ep 23 (The Father of the Hundreds). Verbatim.

**How long is it between Eberron and Starfall, given Andromeda is alive in both?**

> we think about 100

**Is it the Sovereignty of "three realms" or "free realms"?**

> Free Realms

**Is her later turn against the Lexicon (the Starweaver Act) intended, and does the Act reach warforged like Pierce?**

> It's a long story, but the Lexicon betrays the Sovereignty during the arc between Light of Xaryxis and the Starsong Awakens.  Similar to the plot of Hyperion and Fall of Hyperion it engineers an attack by Beholders which are in fact cybrids and artificial, to use its chance to unleash the demoncore (also its doing) on the home worlds of the Soveriegnty, the Shrike comes to either stop ot help it (its unclear throughout) and after its all revealed by Small berries and his crew, the Sovereignty turns on the LExicon which flees into cyberspace/deep space and they ban artificial intelligence including all droids and warforged on all planets.

**How was Korth destroyed?**

> Korth was Ruby Nova'd

**Was the Polygon stolen from LeBeefe or given by him?**

> Stolen, either his plans or the literal object

**Should the final Andromeda portrait and model sheet go on her page?**

> yes

## Andromeda Starweaver in the Starfall campaign

Later the same day, on her part in Starfall. Verbatim.

> She was critical in the Starfall campaign, because she was the council member that the crew of the Zanzibar approached to convince of the betrayal of the Lexicon and the reality that the Beholder war was a trick, she made a speech against it in the Senate but then there was an assasination attempt on her.  she actually gave her luxury vessel to the crew to help them in their mission while she distracted the Grey Cloaks (Astral Command Intelligence) and helped their escape.

## After the tragedy

Later again the same day. Verbatim.

> After the tragedy, she becomes a symbol for anti-AI sentiment and reaction and spearheads the banning of the Lexicon and all artificial minds. The Grey Cloaks are disbanded and replaced by a force that hunts and destroys androids, warforged, and all AI. Thats the setup for the Starsong Awakens.
