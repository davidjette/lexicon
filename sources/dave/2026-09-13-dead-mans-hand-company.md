# Dead Man's Hand, O-3255 and "the Company": Dave's account, given in chat 2026-09-13 (verbatim)

Asked whether the O-3255 file was a player handout, whether its black bars are in-world, and whether
"the Company" is the Sword Coast Trading Company:

> yes they got as handout, yes, the Company -> after St. Cloud fled the "present" into the past, he left behind the St Cloud Trading Company, which was the true company in Sigil born from the Sword Coast Trading Company on Faerun.  The party ruined his tower and the Lady of Pain destroyed his home, and the company assets went up for auction.  Revan actually secretly bid for the company and beat the Party's group bid, so Temple Holdings LLC lost the auction to Nimbus Corp, which become "the Company" from then on.  Revan heads up Nimbus Corp until his disappearance about 200 years after the events of the campaign.  Nimbus corp ends up being critical to the astral travel of Faerun and the associated realms, consolidating power until it becomes the largest private company in the known universe, akin to CHOAM in Dune. It acquires (is acquired by?) the Lexicon and becomes a major arm of its control over the Sovereignty.  The story of the Dead Man's Hand is set during the beginning of that consolidation, with the Company, the Church of Cypher, and many others trying to create more Lexicons, copy the original, or avoid domination by it behind the scenes.
