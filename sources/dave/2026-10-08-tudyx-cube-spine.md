# Dave: the spine of the Tudyx Cube (2026-10-08)

Dave's instruction in chat, 2026-10-08, verbatim (typos kept). He asked for a rewrite of the Tudyx Cube article, for all related articles to be updated with it, and for the note to be added to canon and cited directly.

https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/items/the-tudyx-cube/ needs a rewrite.  Here's the spine of the info about the Tudyx Cube. - It's first appearance the party had no idea as to its purpose or design. In the first episode of the Ages of the Infanta (possibly the very heart of the entire campaign, it followed the end of the Motherstone campaign, where the party discovered (most of) the history of that city, Calix, they expelled the Company from the city, installed the preteen infanta of the Moon, and saw a hint of the "Leviathan" or Cthulhu in Calix's motivations) started with the arrival of Zanzibar and Stonecypher, as well as Revan, arriving at Motherstone to entreat to the new queen and only "independent" infanta to help them with thier personal quests - Revan to discover the roots of his family's story in the infanta lore, and Zanzibar to find the Zarus Infanta.  Elistrae blesses their joining the party, which in its own right was deputized to act for Motherstone with Magnus and Drefan as her surrogates. The first order of business for the newly enthroned queen was to expel the Company from the rest of the island of Snowdown.  (In their prequel episodes, Stonecypher and Zanzibar arrived at Snowdown on a boat and witnessed the frantic beginning of a fulls cale colonial evacuation - the Company was already in process of escaping with all the most valuable equiment and mined moonstones they could carry, and the people of the city were in fear of the Forsworn rebels comning to burn human towns and wholesale murder.) A report came in that St Cloud's personal ship was in the harbor, and that if they hurry, they could capture Wainwright, who had come to the island to escort some particularly valuable cargo, could be caught.  They rushed to Caer Westphal, the largest port on the island, and saw it was true.  Kara and Couch (? verification needed) impersonated some wealthy shareholders and secured a meeting with St Cloud before his departure, but when they were discovered, St Cloud rushed out and onto the rooftop of the city toward the harbor.  They chased him, surprised at how fast and nimble he was.  They chased him all the way to the harbor, where St Cloud was able to board his ship by flying striahgt across a large plaza using a mechanical jetpack, similar in design to Flex Gimble's machines (who we know worked for the Company) the party boarded the ship and immediately hit the defense of a cargo hold full of Warforged soldiers, a new type of being the party had never seen, who seemed to be mechanical fighters. As they fought and many of them fell on both sides, the ship begin to disembark, not out onto the ocean, but upward into the *air*.  A huge ring of elemental fire formed around a large wooden magical harness, and the sail unfurled, pulling the boat upward into the sky.  Drefan killed the pilot and took the helm, sending it into a slow spin around the Y axis (nose-tail) and the battle took on a barrel shape as the party walked around the slowly rotating hull to keep from falling while fighting off warforged.  Drefan managed to stablize the vehicle, when St Cloud called to "SCRAMBLE THE CORE" at which point four elementals (of each element) emerged from the core and the ship hung motionless in the air (it is made of Floatwood (?) so it hugn in the air without power, but could not move without sail unless its core was active and was at the mercy of currents, which could bring it into the ground very quickly.) Magnus pulled a huge quantity of sea water on top of the fire elemental, generating a massive steam explosion that damaged the ship (The Ariel) and killed the remainder of the elementals. The party mortally wounded St Cloud, but lo and behold, upon dying, he transformed into the grey and featureless form of a Changeling (the same thing Stonecypher was, though the party did not know). His papers indicated he was an officer of the Company and a part of a management team of changelings who ALL took the form of st cloud in their duties, giving the impression of an omnipresent omniscient CEO. The Ariel WAS in fact his personal ship, and it was here to move some precious cargo (the Warforged troops guarded a serious haul of high grade moonstone and mining tech the party took back to Motherstone to fix the Deepmaker (a fabrication device built by Brightmantle) and the Lexicon itself.  They also made items like Roland's Cube, and altered the Moonblades/Bane blades with these supplies.  In St Cloud's personal quarters, there were many of his personal items clearly saved for posterity - one was a fine silver-filigreed box, impossible to open, with something inside that could not be discovered.  Kara took this box and put it in her inventory where it sat for at least 3 years of real life game play.  She did not know then that this was a puzzle box later referred to as a Tudyx Cube, and it was the same box they would find in the deep past, still unlocked and empty, and by dropping in a perfect moonstone, were able to engineer the predestination of the first box they found, allowing them to open it to reveal a now perfect dark crystal, locked away for millions of years as the box would have been (st cloud having lost it possibly in the Starfall but found it a very long time later, now unable to open it).  The origin of this item itself, and its reason for being called a Tudyx Cube - during the first age of infanta, Peter Glans and his wife Alen Tudyx, the queen of Cormanthor and Myth Drannor and sister of Archmage Valen, adopted the Infanta of Puzzles (Dae) and raised them. Peter had purchased a puzzle box in his past and showed it to Dae, who then invented an even more difficult and surprising box, which the Queen had produced in quantity and became a popular item in Cormanthor, known as a Tudyx cube, after he houses' name. The Penteract, a five-dimensional demiplane created by Peter Glans and Dae to encase the Mammon Machine and Soul Syphon at the heart of Dis, to prevent infanta from accessing it and to end the wars over the power of Death, was a more grand incarnation of the same concept.  The Penteract still surrounds the Seat of Judgment, which is the Mammon Machine, and to which all souls wander in search of their origin in Zoth, but the puzzle has been solved and its mysteries form the basis of many occult traditions, divine mathematics, and deep planar lore known by the likes of the original House of Nimbus and the Banefae, who sought to capture this for themselves, and of course St Cloud.  It was this knowledge that allowed him to defeat the Raven Queen with Asmodeus and start the infanta crisis yet again.  update all articles with this, add this note to canon and cite it directly as needed.
