> To the Bota, Vanderoos' writings were scripture. In a way, they were right.

<small>— the Bota People Ally Card.</small>

**Human shamans · A mountain people · Fate not recorded**

The Bota People are a mountain people of human shamans who took the writings of **Vanderoos** as scripture, and one of the allies drafted for the final battle at the Egg. Nothing further about the Bota or about Vanderoos is recorded.

Their white action, **Remembrance**, dispels Feeblemind by touch — the only ability in the set of 41 that restores a mind rather than damaging one.

## At the Egg

**White — Remembrance: "Touch a creature to dispel effects of Feeblemind."**

**Black — Avalanche: "An avalanche sweeps enemies in 40 ft cube w/i 120 feet, dealing 50d10 cold and bludgeoning damage DEX 30 or half."**

## Status

Not recorded.
