**Duergar house - of the Deeptempura kindred**

**House Moo Goo Gai Pan** is a duergar house of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) kindred, known from two bodies. Its crest was recovered as a wax rubbing from [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/), an outrider for the mourning-party coming down into the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/), found weeks dead in a pocket cave off the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/). A [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) scout who bore the name Moo Goo Gai Pan died at the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) in the same period.

## The crest

In a pocket cave off the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), a grey-skinned duergar lay weeks dead, curled around a stomach wound. On him were thirty-five gold, a **mourning-braid** of white hair wrapped in ribboned Dwarvish prayers for the souls and the crafts of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) people, and a wax rubbing of a family crest. The crest was House Moo Goo Gai Pan.

He had died an outrider for the mourning-party coming down into the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/), and he had been carrying somebody else's grief in his coat. Returning the braid to the camp in the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/) is how anyone learned his name: [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/). <small>The crest rubbing is still in the party's possession.</small>

## The scout

The other body is a name. Two of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) scouting party went down the shaft to the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) and did not come back: Fried Tofu, and one called **Moo Goo Gai Pan**. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) recognised both of them by their screaming before anyone had seen them. They were found legless, their bodies melted away by acid, calling for their mothers, and they choked to death in the fumes when the dragon raised the water. Whether the scout's name and the house's are connected is not recorded.

The house's seat, its size, its trade, its relationship to the Deeptempura kingship, and whether any of it survives in the Mror Holds are all unrecorded.
