**Nation · the Khraalish peninsula, Darguun-Zilargo · Free and independent; member of the rebel alliance**

*Also known as:* the Republic of Khraal · the Hobgoblin State of Khraal · the Khraal

**The Free State of Khraal** is an independent hobgoblin state on the Khraalish peninsula of Darguun-Zilargo, in southern Khorvaire. It was declared by the hobgoblin pirate bands of the coast when they took the peninsula and drove the Imperial forces west, and it joined the rebel alliance as a full member. It came out of the secret mission that sent [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) to Darguun-Zilargo in the year after [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Eric's secret Khraal mission (dossier))</small>

## The Librarians' crisis

The rebels of Darguun-Zilargo were the **Librarians**, a powerful faction of gnomish craftsmen and scholars and legendary outlaw heroes of the rebellion. By the year after Newham they were so bedevilled by local pirates and brigands that they could no longer spend anything on the fight against Uriel, and had decided to let [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s soldiers into their territory to help them against the warlords. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Their envoy was **Potpie**, a battle-hardened gnome artificer, an older woman in shining heavy armour and perhaps a veteran of the Last War, who flew into [Leef](/places/leef/) in a small two-seater airship and would speak only with [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/), with whom she seemed to have a history. After their meeting Digma went straight to Eric in the town's meeting hall, and Eric left with her on a secret mission. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The dossier

The rebellion's dossier on the mission records it in six entries:

> CRISIS IN DARGUUN-ZILARGO: (ATTENTION) The Librarians claim territorial disputes with local bands of Hobgoblin pirates make any further military action against the empire impossible at this time. 1 LHAVARION
>
> RESOLVED: Eric Passagemarked &#91;clerk, rank 5&#93; will be dispatched to the shores of Khraal to find funds to allow the Librarians to fight their war on two fronts, against both the empire and the Hobgoblin pirates.
>
> UPDATE: Eric sends word that he has made contact with the Librarians and begun a full and complete accounting of their ledgers.
>
> UPDATE: Librarians inform us Eric has been abducted by pirates in a daring daylight raid of their rebel fortress. Whereabouts unknown. 3 BARRAKAS
>
> UPDATE: Eric released by captors. He negotiated an armistice between rival pirate gangs and the Librarians. The warlords have pledged to join the rebel fight against empire.
>
> UPDATE: The united front of pirates have broken armistice and declared war on both the Librarians and the empire.
>
> UPDATE: The Librarian stronghold has fallen to Hobgoblin forces. The pirates have taken control of the Khraalish peninsula and driven all imperial forces into the west. They have declared a free independent Hobgoblin State of Khraal. Khraal is now a full and participating member of the rebel alliance. 29 RHAAN

<small>(JL's DM notes: Eric's secret Khraal mission (dossier))</small>

News reached Leef weeks later that the Khraalish peninsula had declared a free and independent state of hobgoblins and joined the rebel alliance. Eric came home some time after, relaxed and more confident than before, with a hobgoblin companion named Fronque, a foot taller than he is, and a tattoo in Goblin on his neck. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## At war with the Empire

By the time of the Sharn arc the Khraal held the coast that the Empire's Embra shipments from Valenar had to use. Rebel hobgoblins took the shoreline, and Hallorn d'Lyrandar told his half-brother Malrik Zeir that his captains could not get within a mile of the docks: "Because the Republic of Khraal has decided to play warlord again." The Khraal had already sunk two of his transports. Resistance attacks in the Khraal-controlled lands slowed the shipments, and with them the production of new E.D.E.N. units. At the opening of [POSÉ](/places/pose/), [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) answered him: "The Khraal controlled lands will become one more scar of mourning if necessary." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>
