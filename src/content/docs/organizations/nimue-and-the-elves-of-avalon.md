**Elvish nobles · Avalon and the Summer Court · Allies from Arc VI**

Nimue and the Elves of Avalon are the elvish nobility of Avalon and the Summer Court, allies of the party from Arc VI. **Nimue** is the Lady of the Lake and the mother of **Titania**, Queen of Avalon, and of **Maab**, the Queen of Air and Darkness. Seeing the rise of the Netherese and with forewarning of the coming of the Infanta, she searched for a human king who could father a rival to Netheril and build "a culture of peace and justice." <small>(Nimue and the Elves of Avalon Ally Card.)</small>

That search produced [Arthur](/organizations/king-arthur-and-his-knights/), and the wizard set to watch over him was [Merlin](/people/steve-merlin/) — "Nimue asked her lover, the wizard Merlin, to watch over Uther and his line to ensure that Ironbane was wielded to destroy the Netherese."

Merlin's own origin is the Summer Court's bargain: the elves of Avalon, "desperate to break their curse of infertility and isolation from their divine aspect of the moon," made a deal with an infernal power to produce "the child of a fiend — Asmodeus — and a mortal woman — and create a sorcerer who could look backward into time." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VIII.)</small>

Kara travelled to Avalon to learn the Charm of Making and free Merlin from the oak tree Morgana sealed him in, and was welcomed by the witch elves of the heath "as a sister and a sorceress."

Their card type is **Legendary Land Creature**.
