> Admiral, prepare to disembark. General, prepare your troops for a grand march at dawn. I will lead our forces to the ships on my way to conquer Amn.

<small>— Emporer Jacob the day before his death, on the People of Amn Ally Card. Sic on "Emporer". The same words are spoken at the Council of Waterdeep in Arc III Part VI.</small>

**Human commoners · Amn · Allies at the Egg**

The **People of Amn** are the people **Emperor Jacob** set out to conquer, and allies at the Egg. Their card is labelled with his last recorded order, given the day before he died. Jacob is the **false** Infanta of Order, not [Obi](/people/obi/). The Empire mobilised "a force to invade Amn by sea and land" after the attack on Waterdeep. <small>(Arc III Part VII.)</small> Neither the campaign nor Jacob's death is narrated anywhere; the card's caption is the only statement of it, and the people it was aimed at stand with the party at the end.

## At the Egg

**White — Rally: "Cast Lesser Restoration on up to 3 creatures within 30 ft."**

**Black — Flip of the Coin: "All rolls made by target creature within 60 ft. until the end of its next turn are now 1d2, where 1 is a failure."**

<small>Flip of the Coin reduces a target creature's every roll to 1d2 with a 50% failure rate.</small>
