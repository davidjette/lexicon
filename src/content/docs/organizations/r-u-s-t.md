**Survival network · The Cogs and the undercity, Sharn · Active**

*Also known as:* Resistance Under Sharn's Tyranny · RUST

R.U.S.T. is a survival network of Cogborn, Warforged and human sympathisers in the Cogs and the undercity of Sharn, formed to move the Cogborn out of the city rather than to liberate it. It grew out of scattered acts of smuggling and shelter after the Lord Commander outlawed all Warforged in Sharn and the Iron Veil seized the factories, and it was held together by [Preacher](/people/preacher/), the one Warforged who stayed. After the Upper City riot and the executions that followed, the network turned to evacuation, walking the Cogborn out through the Underhive toward Gorgonhorn; at the Council of Sharn it carried all three of its demands.

Preacher gave his account of the network to the trio himself, in the control room of an abandoned Sub-Transit station reached through a hidden tunnel beneath [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/) and [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/)'s pawn shop. It sits beside the Imperial framing of the same weeks and beside the ledger R.U.S.T. keeps of its own dead.

## PREACHER'S ACCOUNT

RUST is one of the most fragile yet consequential resistance movements in Sharn, born not from ideology or rebellion but from necessity. Its roots lie deep in the Cogs, where generations of Cogborn workers and Warforged laborers built the city's industrial heart. When the Lord Commander outlawed all Warforged in Sharn, most fled toward Gorgonhorn or the Mournlands. One remained: Preacher, a Warforged from the Cogs and shaped by its furnaces, who refused to abandon the people he considered family.

Hidden beneath the city with his adopted daughter, Misty, Preacher watched as the Iron Veil slowly seized the factories, replaced Cogborn labor with trained soldiers, and tightened its grip on the undercity. The Cogborn, already weakened by decades of hazardous work, malnutrition, and the loss of Warforged laborers who once shouldered the most dangerous tasks have found themselves pushed out of their jobs, their neighborhoods, and eventually their homes. Many were forced to flee beneath the Cogs, where collapsing tunnels and abandoned sub-transit lines became their last refuge.

What began as scattered acts of survival, smuggling families through forgotten tunnels, hiding fugitives in abandoned rail lines, coalesced into a quiet network of Cogborn, Warforged, and human sympathizers determined to protect those the Empire had deemed expendable. This network became RUST.

Today, RUST stands at a turning point. The Cogborn are trapped beneath the city, their exits sealed or controlled by hostile forces, while the Iron Veil accelerates its takeover of the Cogs. RUST's mission is no longer to endure, it is to escape. Their hope lies beyond Sharn in Gorgonhorn; a Cogborn / Warforged stronghold on the border of the Mournlands. There, under the leadership of Locke Pierce, a blended society of Cogborn and Warforged has begun to take shape: a community strong enough to heal, grow, and one day resist the Empire on its own terms.

RUST's struggle is not for Sharn's liberation but for its people's survival. Their legacy, if they succeed, will not be measured in battles fought within the city, but in the new nation they hope to build far beyond its reach.

## IRON VEIL — PUBLIC ORDER BULLETIN

A peaceful protest in the Upper City erupted into a deadly riot when an explosion tore through the crowd. The Iron Veil's response was characterised in broadcast as swift and lawful. Raids, searches and forced removals intensified overnight; checkpoints divided the upper and lower city; the district was flooded with soldiers. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) executed Cogborn prisoners live from the deck of the Black Veil for the whole city to watch. Dissent, the broadcast said, would be crushed.

## R.U.S.T. CASUALTY LEDGER

The breaking point came only nights ago. A peaceful protest in the Upper City erupted into a deadly riot when an explosion tore through the crowd. The Iron Veil's retaliation was swift and merciless. RUST lost twenty-one members, and the Cogborn community lost forty-three civilians; workers, elders, parents, and children. Esther Crona's public executions that followed sent shockwaves through the Cogs, and the Iron Veil used the chaos as justification to flood the district with soldiers. Raids, searches, and forced removals intensified overnight, accelerating the collapse of Cogborn life in Sharn and leaving RUST with only one option: escape.

The Cogborn were sealed into a collapsing tunnel pocket called the Pit. The only viable route out ran through [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), and [Blair](/people/blair/) would open it only if [the Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) were cleared from the northern sewers. They were. The Cogborn walked out through the Spine, down the underhive river, past a dead stone golem and the corpses of four giant spiders, and were led in song by Preacher and the RUST Runners, praying to the love of something called Lexicon.

## COUNCIL OF SHARN — R.U.S.T. DEMANDS

Warforged citizenship. The arrest of the Iron Veil engineers. A new Department of Infrastructure. All three were carried. Warforged forces offered troops and engineers to stabilise the city and restore the Cogs; Underhive drones joined the new department while keeping their subterranean autonomy.
