**Faction · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Hospital and welfare · Faction ally of the party**

**Also known as:** Bleakers

**The Bleak Cabal** are a faction of [Sigil](/places/sigil/) "Who find no sense in the multiverse", headquartered in the Gatehouse under Factol Lhar, whose role in the city is hospital and welfare. When [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/) spread through [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), the Bleaker cleric [Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/) was searching for her missing assistant Craig in the Spillway. The party volunteered at the Gatehouse, went into the Spillway, saved Craig, killed the plague demon there and closed the portal that was the source of the plague. The Cabal opened the Gatehouse to the party and became their faction ally.

## The faction

<small>(DM notes)</small>

- **Factol:** Lhar
- **Headquarters:** the Gatehouse
- **Aligned plane:** Pandemonium
- **Members:** "Counselors, healers, nihilists, communists/mutualists"
- **Epithet:** Bleakers
- **Role:** hospital, welfare

The Cabal's healer Diana Rhea had leads on the cause of Cageblight, in the sewers under the Spillway.

## Diana Rhea

[Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/) is a cleric of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/), of the Knowledge Domain, and a member of the Bleak Cabal. She "Hates Cult of St. Magnus (Harmonium)". She wants to cure Cageblight. Her assistant Craig had been missing for three weeks, and she could not get close to the Spillway to look for him. She is suspicious of the party; if they convince her, she offers to help them if they locate her assistant. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Volunteers at the Gatehouse

A Bleaker cleric doing triage knew about Craig, and told the party to come at night and volunteer. They did, and were detained, disarmed and disrobed. They then met Diana Rhea, who gave them their belongings back and beseeched them to find Craig.

## The Spillway

Craig, a Bleaker intern carrying a scroll of *close portal*, had gone into the Spillway to finish his master's mission: to close a portal to a plague dimension that had opened in a drain there and was poisoning the water system. In doing so he angered a plague demon, and [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) ordered his men to stop him. The party ambushed and killed Rotten William's men and then the demon, Gorelach and Gunt, saved Craig, closed the portal and killed Rotten William. By closing the portal, they cured Cageblight. The full account is at [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/).

Diana Rhea gave the party 2000 gold and poultices.

## Faction ally

After the Spillway the Gatehouse was open to the party, and the Bleak Cabal became their **faction ally**, offering "Safehaven, assistance, and information." The Cabal's quests for the party are escort and infiltration. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Critics

[Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/), the thieves' boss of the Catwalk, dislikes the Bleak Cabal and dismisses Cageblight "as a fake plague engineered by the Company to control the city". <small>(DM notes)</small>
