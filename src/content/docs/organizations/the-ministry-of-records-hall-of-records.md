**Type:** Government, Archive · **Seat:** north across the Great Plaza, Korth · **Status:** Active, and hollowed out

*Also known as:* the Ministry of Records · the Hall of Records · the Hall

The Ministry of Records / Hall of Records is the Imperial archive of Korth, standing directly north across the Great Plaza from the parade throne. It has been a library, a university and a wizard's tower at different points in a very long history, and it exists to hold the state's most controlled material. Its legal section has been emptied and backfilled with filler, and every record naming a specific dragon has been removed by order. The Inevitables entered it on Ministry of Law credentials to read the law they were about to weaponise: they were stopped at a checkpoint by a Force Captain citing the Imperial Blackguards and Mercenary Act over an unlicensed sword, waved through when [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) produced Ministry authority, and left with a forged identity, a manuscript hidden under a false index, and the loremaster Master Armhair's account of the Emperor's nature.

## THE HALL AS CATALOGUED

Directly north across the Great Plaza from the parade throne; a giant building. It has been a library, a university, and a wizard's tower at different points in a very long history.

It is not a general library — the Rekkenmark and others are far larger. Korth's Hall exists to hold **the most controlled material**: sensitive government documents, secrets, licences, **records of the Emperor's ancestors**, and subjects of great magical knowledge. Access is restricted. A serious scholar comes here to prove why he *hasn't* seen a thing.

Scholars work and loiter in it at all hours, which is the single most useful fact about the place.

> "The secret of the Hall of Records is not so much the records themselves, but the people here."

## THE HALL AS IT ACTUALLY STANDS

It has been **emptied out and backfilled with fluff.** The legal section — in the archive of a state that governs by law — is down to a handful of tomes.

And: **any record naming a specific dragon has been removed by order.** Master Armhair, who advises the court on dragon lore, told the party that no black dragon had been sighted in over four hundred and thirty years by written record, that three black dragons descended from the First and were all presumed destroyed and driven underground when Khyber formed the Manifest Zones, and that **Valtrex**, as the youngest and smallest, was the only one able to move through the narrow ways. He knows this because he was there before the shelves were cleared.

## THE THIRD LEVEL

On the third level of the Hall of Records sits a clerk — a non-binary dwarf — who will forge identity papers and weapon registrations for a shakedown or a bribe. The party had [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) documented under the name **Sky Rizzy**, logging every weapon he carried *except* KNELL. The Leonin scholar **Jelton Ahn** is separately using the same clerk to arrange forged passports to move his wife out of the city to Kabara.

## FALSE INDEX — THE MATING HABITS OF CREATURES OF THE UNDERDARK

Master Armhair's original manuscript was confiscated, because his work led to questions the Empire did not want asked. He hid a copy in the Hall under a false index, catalogued as a treatise on the mating habits of creatures of the Underdark, and gave the Inevitables the number so that it would be preserved. He then left for the **Korramont** to find Valtrex and finish his book, expecting to die doing it.

Retrieved, the manuscript contains a family tree of Eberron's dragons traced to the progenitors, an identification of Valtrex's father, and an extended thread connecting the **Mark of Death**, the birth of **Erandis Vol**, and the dragon Armhair believes is currently occupying the Imperial throne. Its central claim — that [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) is the ancestral dragon **Viagra**, and the same being as Kaius I — is set out on the Emperor's own article as one of two competing accounts. He named it a blasphemy of the highest order and invited the party to have him executed for it.

## THE BUNKER

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) lived in a bunker deep beneath this building for nearly three years. <small>No further detail is recorded, and he has not volunteered any.</small>

## ASSESSMENT

The Ministry of Records holds what the Pax Imperium will neither destroy nor release. The purge of its shelves was carried out by clerks, and the clerks are still in the building at all hours, with a forger on the third floor. Everything the Inevitables learned about the Emperor's nature came out of this building, and none of it came off a shelf.

**Known personnel:** **Master Armhair**, elderly half-elf loremaster advising the court on dragon lore (departed for the Korramont) · **Jelton Ahn**, Leonin scholar and Armhair's self-appointed protector · an unnamed forger on the third level · a half-elf researcher overheard hunting information on the Korramont and its dwarves.

**Holdings:** Sensitive government documents, secrets, licences, the records of the Emperor's ancestors, and works of great magical knowledge — minus the legal section and minus every dragon. Armhair's manuscript, under a false index.
