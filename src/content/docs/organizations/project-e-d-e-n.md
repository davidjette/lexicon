**Classified Imperial military initiative · Hybridisation programme · Operated by BioTec under Dr. Joanne Menka · Deployed into the Mournlands · Active**

*Also known as:* Project EDEN · E.D.E.N. · "the project" · "the units"

**Project E.D.E.N.** is [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/)'s secret hybridisation programme, overseen by Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), which fuses humanoid bodies with warforged cybernetics to produce undead-warforged supersoldiers for deployment into the Mournlands. It is named after [Eden](/people/eden/), the youngest half-sister of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), who died beneath the Grand Aqueduct and whose failed resurrection became the prototype the rest of the work was reverse-engineered from; Imperial documents do not distinguish between the programme, the units and the woman. The Sharn end of the programme was dismantled piece by piece after the city rose and Dr. Menka was tried and executed, but units were still operating in the west three years later.

## How it came to light

The name came out of an encrypted BioTec folder stolen on the night of the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/) theft and cracked at the Nest by [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/). The shape of it came from fragments overheard at the opening of POSÉ. The substance came out of [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) in a farmhouse cellar under a Zone of Truth, over two sessions, with a BioTec control chip in her skull fighting her the entire time. The last piece came from a Warforged in a tunnel who recognised the monster from a personal memory.

## RECOVERED BIOTEC FILE — PROGRAMME ABSTRACT

Project E.D.E.N. is [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/)'s secret hybridization program, overseen by Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), dedicated to creating undead-warforged supersoldiers by fusing humanoid bodies with warforged cybernetics. The resulting hybrids retain corpse-like skin, mechanical limbs, and masked faces that hide their deteriorating features, giving them an unsettling blend of military precision and necromantic horror. The project represents one of the Empire's most ruthless scientific endeavors, designed to produce obedient, tireless troops capable of enforcing Imperial control without question. Its existence is tightly concealed, its ethics nonexistent, and its success a looming threat to anyone who stands against the Empire's expanding power.

<small>The company's own summary. The resistance's assessment of it: BioTec is a privately owned company whose shadow projects threaten the balance of our resistance; their work on Project E.D.E.N., the creation of hybrid undead-warforged super soldiers, is a newly discovered operation shrouded in secrecy.</small>

## INTERROGATION OF SISTER NORA — ZILSPAR FARM 02

Under magical pressure her conditioning fractured and she gave the whole assembly line. Project E.D.E.N. combines:

- **Undead bodies** — dead Iron Veil soldiers, and [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) supplied by Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/).
- **Warforged components**, manufactured and enhanced by BioTec.
- **[Umbra](/items/umbra/)**, grown and supplied by **Malrik Zeir** and transported across Khorvaire by [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/).
- **Umbra-infused necromantic rites** performed on the corpses by the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/).
- **Airship deployment** into the Mournlands by Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar.

The units are built to survive the cursed Mournlands. They were designed to do two jobs there: fight [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s Warforged resistance army entrenched in the Glass Plateau, and mine [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) out of the irradiated wasteland.

When the Inevitables asked her who supplied the corpses, Nora seized, clutched the side of her head near her ear, and bled from the nose. She had a BioTec-implanted mental block. Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) cut the chip out of her, and only then could she answer.

## THE PROTOTYPE

Nora also gave up the beginning of it. Eden — Esther's youngest half-sister — died tragically nearly four years ago. Esther, in desperation, brought the body to Sister Nora and asked for help. The resurrection failed. An Umbra infusion did not fix it. What stood up was [EDN-00](/people/eden/), and the Empire looked at the result and decided it could be manufactured.

> "She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her… but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes — but an incomplete one. A soul even Uriel may not reach."\
> — Sister Nora on EDN-00

The masks exist because the reproductions decay in ways the original did not.

## FIELD ACCOUNT — PREACHER, R.U.S.T.

The Hundreds have been fighting EDEN units in the Glass Plateau for years, alongside a hybrid they call **the Blue Devil** — blue-skinned, red-eyed, warforged-fused, dropped in by Iron Veil airship, and in their assessment unkillable. Preacher recognised her. Years ago, after the Lord Commander outlawed Warforged in Sharn, a young Tiefling woman found his hidden cell; he chased her through the drainage tunnels and she fell from the aqueduct's upper walkway onto the machinery below. He believed he had killed her.

He had. **Project E.D.E.N. is not primarily a military programme.** It is a system of body harvesting, resurrection and forced hybridisation, fed by Cogborn disappearances and protected by Iron Veil secrecy, which happens to produce soldiers.

## DOCTRINE — "THE ETERNAL NIGHTFALL"

The Black Doves have a third account of what the programme is. Nora used the phrase at POSÉ while trying to calm a furious Menka: *"the souls lost last night will be added to the eternal nightfall."* Under interrogation she explained it — [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s doctrine of endless resurrection and a world remade in undeath.

To BioTec, E.D.E.N. is a procurement contract. To the Iron Veil, it is a division that does not tire. To the Black Doves, it is a sacrament, and every body fed into it is a soul entered onto the Empress's ledger. Menka ignored Nora when she said so.

## THE UNITS

What the Inevitables met at level five, three of them released by a panicking scientist in a BioTec corridor, were horrors: corpse-skinned, mechanically limbed, masked. Barely survivable. Unit 0.3 was found later beside a dead senior scientist, having executed a full force pulse at shutdown.

What they met years later in the west was not the same machine. Eight units held the ruins of a burned farm, and by then the line had been refined: they **self-heal**, they stand on **undead fortitude**, they articulate on **impossible second elbows**, and they **burst into lightning when destroyed**. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) killed one on top of an antenna and the discharge electrified the whole tower field for the rest of the fight; Dario sheathed Pale Fire for KNELL purely to shove them clear before they detonated. All eight fell. The evidence at the site showed that many EDEN soldiers had died defending that farm before incendiaries were brought in to burn the buildings over people boarded up inside. Why the units that garrisoned the farm and the units that burned it were the same product has never been explained.

## Status

The Sharn end of the programme has been dismantled piece by piece: Edwin Graves killed and the Cogborn corpse supply severed; Malrik Zeir destroyed and the Umbra supply cut; the Black Dove head priests captured and the rites disrupted; the Black Dove hospital seized; Dr. Menka tried and executed with the other high Imperial officials after the city rose.

None of that recalls a unit already in the field. Eden units were still operating in the west three years later, and they took **Zero-One** alive.

**Members:** EDN-00 (Eden, prototype) · Unit 0.3 and at least two others held in Sharn containment · at least eight refined units operating in the west

**Command:** Dr. Joanne Menka (scientific lead) · Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar (deployment) · Esther Crona (authority) · Empress Uriel Qualanthri (doctrine)
