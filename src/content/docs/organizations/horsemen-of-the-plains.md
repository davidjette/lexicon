**Centaur cavalry · The Horsemen Clans · Allies from Arc VI**

The Horsemen of the Plains are the centaur cavalry of the **Horsemen Clans**, allied to the party from Arc VI and present as allies at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). They allied with the party after it killed **Cyric, the God of Murder**: the party found Memnon massacred and purged by Cyric's Death Knights, "and there is no sound of survivors," and rather than move on, "Party decided to seek revenge on Cyric, teleported to his lair and slayed him! Made allies with the Horsemen of the Dale." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part IX.)</small>

The card types them as **Centaur Cavalry**. Their pledge is recorded at Brightmantle's impromptu funeral, where the allies of that era stepped forward one by one with the armies of the world already gathering for the invasion of Netheril. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>
