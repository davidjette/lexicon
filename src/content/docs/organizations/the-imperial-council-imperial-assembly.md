*Also known as:* the Imperial Council · the Imperial Assembly · [the Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/)

The Imperial Council / Imperial Assembly is the body of thirteen Imperial government heads and leaders who govern the Empire under [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), meeting in a tower in [Korth](/places/korth/) called the Imperial Assembly. It governs on behalf of an Emperor who has been comatose for a decade. Its member [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), head of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/), was assassinated, and rebel field analysis names members of the Council itself as the likely architects. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), seemingly acting with the Emperor's approval, then systematically eliminated every council member and some of their immediate families; the last survivor, [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), was killed by her on her own balcony during the Sharn rising, on live broadcast. The Empire has never published a correction.

## THE ROSTER, AS THE EMPIRE LISTS IT

The Imperial Council / Imperial Assembly are the 13 Imperial government heads and leaders under the empire ruled by [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/).

The Council of 13 meet in a tower in Korth called the Imperial Assembly.

- [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) — **\*Dead\*** High Elf Paladin: Previous head of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/), was once the head Paladin for Kaius I.
- **Hayman Maw** — Human Sorcerer: Current head of the Crimson Sun
- **Dunkin Sawbrass** — Human Cleric: Pope of the Sovereign Host
- **Garth Brooks** — Human Cleric: Bishop of the Sovereign Host (Secret leader of [the Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) under the name Chris Gaines?)
- [Lord Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/) — Human Nobleman: Head of the Imperial trade federation
- **General Lucius Tiberius** — Human Fighter: General of the Imperial army.
- **Admiral Johan Frigg** — Human Rogue / Swashbuckler: In charge of Navy and aerial command.
- **Lord Albert Spear** — Human Nobleman: Head of city planning and development
- **Judge Artumus Reinhold** — Human Bard, former opera singer: Supreme Justice of Karnath.
- [Minister Henry Heighrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) — Human Nobleman: Chief diplomat and head of the ministry of diplomacy.
- **Minister Josef Conaway** — Human Bard, former actor and playwright: Minister of Media and Propaganda
- **Chancellor Magnolia Steel** — Human Wizard: Chancellor of the Rekkenmark Academy
- **Fanta Rhodes** — Cloaked figure, race unknown / Warlock?: Hand of Kaius III

<small>Twelve of the thirteen portfolios are human. The exceptions are the High Elf who held the Crimson Sun and the cloaked figure whose race is not recorded and who answers only to the Emperor. There is no Lord Commander on the roster; that office came later.</small>

## REBEL OBSERVATION FILE — WHAT BECAME OF THE ROSTER

Field analysis indicates that Varyn's death was not the result of rebel activity, despite official Imperial claims. Multiple intelligence fragments point to members of the Council of 13 as the likely architects of his assassination. Following this discovery, [Esther](/people/esther-crona/), seemingly acting with the Emperor's approval, systematically eliminated all council members, including some of their immediate family members. The sole survivor is [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), former Chief Diplomat and head of the Ministry of Diplomacy, now serving as Mayor of Sharn. His continued survival is considered politically significant and remains under review.

Henry Heinrick was killed by Esther Crona on her own balcony during the Sharn rising, in front of the city, on live broadcast. The roster above is now a list of thirteen dead people.

## WHAT VARYN CRONA WOULD AND WOULD NOT SAY

Esther's own account of her master's table talk records what Varyn said of the Council. Varyn, while secretive about the inner workings of the Council of 13 or Kaius III, would openly speak of his dislike of council member Haymen Maw. He never discussed the Emperor. He never discussed the Assembly. He named one enemy at that table, out loud, repeatedly, and the man he named inherited his chair.

> "Esther, together we will grow in power and rule Khorvaire and beyond."\
> \
> — Varyn Crona to his apprentice, in the years before his colleagues killed him

## THE ASSEMBLY TOWER

The tower stands in Korth and the Council of 13 meet in it. <small>The Inevitables have operated inside the Imperial capital using Ministry of Law credentials with standing to investigate nearly any Imperial facility, and have not yet crossed its threshold. Whether anybody still convenes there is not recorded. The Great Imperial Palace to the south is likewise still staffed by bureaucrats and nobles dealing with intermediaries of the court, ten years after the Emperor last sat the Throne of Bones.</small>

**Successor bodies:** [The Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/), raised as an undead honour guard. In Sharn, the **Council of Sharn**, convened in Esther's own penthouse the day after she died, with [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) appointed Chancellor and seats claimed by Leef, NEST, the Silver Flame, R.U.S.T., the Hundreds and the Underhive.

**Holdings:** The Imperial Assembly tower, Korth. Ministries of Diplomacy, Media and Propaganda, city planning, trade, the army, the navy, the courts, the Rekkenmark Academy, and the two churches of the Sovereign Host.
