**Rebel network · Breland farmsteads and Zilspar · Dismantled, remnant active**

Fulcrum is one of the oldest rebel groups in existence, its legacy stretching back beyond the current era of Imperial dominance. Based in the Breland farmsteads outside Sharn, with its stronghold centred in Zilspar, it grew from farmers, rangers and dissidents who resisted Imperial expansion into the countryside, and its name reflects its role as a pivotal axis of resistance, a balancing force between rural defiance and the Empire's urban dominance. It was founded by the entertainer [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), the healer Fema Nolan, the goods-trader Digma Beeve, and — concealed from every record — the restaurateur Richard Blaze. Three years after the fall of Sharn it was being dismantled across the continent, name by name, and Zilspar Farm was burned with people boarded up inside.

## WHAT FULCRUM IS

Fulcrum maintains close ties to Sharn's underground rebels, serving as a counterpart to [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) in the upper city. Together these factions form a network of defiance that bridges city and countryside.

## THE CONFESSION AT ZILSPAR FARM 02

The account below was given to [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) in a single sealed room at Zilspar Farm 02, the morning after they killed an Iron Veil patrol in the yard. [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) was waiting alone, her paladins outside the door; [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/), [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) came through the hidden teleportation circle.

It was in Newham that Richard met Digma Beeve, a halfling furniture dealer who became the first to treat him as a man rather than property. Digma whispered of rebellion, and Richard listened. Their friendship became the seed of Fulcrum.

Blaze's kitchens turned into lifelines: money, supplies and coded messages flowing through menus and supply chains, hidden in plain sight. Digma provided cover and contacts; Richard built the infrastructure. Fema Nolan and John Stamos joined them. Richard remained the ghost founder, his name absent from records, his role concealed behind the mask of a charming restaurateur. Only Digma, Zero-One and Stamos knew, until Jessica Grimpledink was brought in — and then, in that room at Zilspar, the Inevitables.

The confession set the party's course: find [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/), recover the Arcane Eye footage of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born, and uncover the origin of Varyn Crona's sword — the weapon [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) still wielded.

## THE EMPIRE'S PUBLIC STORY — THE LIGHTNING RAIL INCIDENT

Official account: Imperial forces intercepted a rebel operation aboard a train bound for Korth. Rebel terrorists killed the beloved Breland actor John Stamos in the fighting. A statue was raised in Korth to honour him as a fallen national icon. It still stands.

What actually happened: the Imperial forces were [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). Stamos was a Fulcrum founder. Richard Blaze was on that train, watched his friend die, and did not break cover. The same journey introduced him to Esther Crona.

<small>**Dating:** Richard's account places Stamos's death "over a decade ago"; Fulcrum's own bell file dates it twenty years back and ties it to the Greenbluff recovery. Greenbluff, and the rail journey that followed it, fall about ten years before the Inevitables.</small>

## FULCRUM BELL FILE

For over a decade Fulcrum ran a bell-counting operation across the continent — a quiet census of the great castings and where they hang, begun under a directive so old that the agent who compiled the packet did not know of it until he asked. Jessica presented the file to the Inevitables on their return from Mt. Silicon, alongside a partially burned foundry schedule recovered from a Lower City church.

## THE NIGHTINGALE'S LAST REPORT

Three years after Sharn, in a dead advocate's apartment in Korth, a woman recorded as Bantide Moro — head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital, the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years, codename the Nightingale, the one who fitted Izaak's prosthetic leg — told the Inevitables that Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent, as if the Empire had obtained a list of every name. Executions ran for days. All of it began after Izaak disappeared. She refuses to believe he informed, and insists he is alive. When she learned Zero-One had been taken by Eden units, she wept.

Zilspar Farm was burned with people boarded up inside. Eight Eden units were still standing in the tower field when the Inevitables landed. There was no sign of Zero-One, Izaak, [Donna](/people/donna/) or Grady Marsh.
