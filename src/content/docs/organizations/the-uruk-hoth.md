**Orc nation · Western Khorvaire · At war with the Empire · Active**

*Also known as:* the Uruk-Hoth Republic · the Uruk Republic · the Urukoth · the Empire of the Uruk

The Uruk-Hoth, also called the Uruk-Hoth Republic and the Uruk Republic, is the sovereign orc state of western Khorvaire and an enemy of the Empire. For years [Crona's Wall](/places/cronas-wall/), a force barrier held by [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) casters under [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), held its armies back on the western front. In the three years after the fall of Sharn the Wall collapsed and orc forces pushed into the Eldeen Reaches; orc delegates attended the Summit at [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/), and after the Ruby Nova the Uruk Republic stood on the verge of conquering Aundair. In [Korth Ep 5](/sessions/korth-ep-5-the-korranberg-descent/) and [Korth Ep 6](/sessions/korth-ep-6-the-long-night-at-the-mountain-door/) the trio destroyed an Uruk-Hoth recon regiment under War-Chief [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/) at the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/) above Korth.

## A sovereign state in the west

In the Leef arc, JL gave the party a map of Khorvaire as it stood, and among the things everyone knows: "the orcs in the west have created a sovereign state." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small>

## Crona's Wall

In the Sharn arc the Empire's western campaign was built around holding the Uruk-Hoth back. General Stockton Thar, a Dark Druid of the Eldeen Reaches, commanded the Iron Veil's ground forces there. High-level Iron Veil wizards stationed out of Fort Crona and along Crona's Wall maintained the mythal-like force wall that held and pushed back the Uruk-Hoth Republic, and Thar's command of that force made him indispensable in the Empire's war against it.

At the opening of [POSÉ](/places/pose/) in Sharn, Thar warned the Lord Commander that the Lhazaar pirates were supplying the orcs:

> "The Lhazaar pirates grow bold. Their ships run supplies to the Uruk-Hoth now."

## The fall of the Wall and Thronehold

In the three years after the fall of Sharn, Crona's Wall collapsed, and orc forces pushed into Dark Druid territory in the Eldeen Reaches. <small>([Korth Ep 1](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/))</small>

Delegates from the Orc tribes came to the Summit at Thronehold with the Hundreds, the Black Doves and the other factions. <small>([Korth Ep 1](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/))</small> The Nest went hoping for "a moment to overture to the Orcs, who have been uncooperative and frequently hostile to the Rebellion writ large." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small> Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) "did not want to offer a truce to the rebellion or the Uruk/hundreds, but was ordered to enter negotiations, but prepared for an ambush". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-19)</small>

After [the massacre](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/), Dave wrote: "The people of the free states are rightfully worried that the summit was the last chance to avoid a final confrontation between the hordes of the Uruk Republic, the awesome destructive capabilities of the Hundreds, and the imperial war machine and armies of undead". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

## The war reaches the Imperial core

After Korth Ep 3, Dave set out the state of the war: "The war has boiled over and is overrunning the imperial core, threatening the capital and triggering the endgame" and "The Uruk republic is on the verge of conquering Aundair, and the empire is on its back feet protecting Karnath". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

## The recon regiment at the Korramont

For about five months an Uruk-Hoth recon regiment under War-Chief Braga Kul held the Mountain Door at the top of the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) road as an observation post, watching troop movements in and out of Korth and intercepting communications. They pushed once down [the Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), came back retching and talking about whispers, and blocked the stair with rock and dirt.

In Korth Ep 5 the orcs at the mine entrance sounded a war horn and attacked. The trio fought through nearly twenty of them; Dave counted "about 20 orcs including two lieutenant-level and a captain level plus a caster". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-12)</small> Braga Kul struck [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) down before he was knocked unconscious and taken prisoner. Dave, afterwards: "we killed that whole Uruk Hoth recon unit, and technically they're not even our enemies, but they were ready to attack all imperials on sight". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-12)</small>

In Korth Ep 6 the regiment's scout party, eight orcs including a war-caster, came home. One of them came through an arrow slit as a mouse by lesser polymorph and lifted the bar of the front door. Eric killed Braga in his cell with magic missiles, and by the end of the battle every member of the recon regiment was dead. The bodies were burned and dropped into the chasm. Dave: "An orc recon squad of 40 in a well held stronghold means the war is moving very fast". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-15)</small> Of a Lesser Polymorph potion the party took from the Mountain Door, which turns the drinker into a mouse, he said it was "a common tool of warlocks attached to orc recon units it seems". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-16)</small>

**Enemies:** the Empire; [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/); Crona's Wall and its garrison.

**Known members:** [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/), War-Chief of the recon regiment at the Mountain Door. Dead.
