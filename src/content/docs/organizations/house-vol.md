**Extinct elvish house · Aerenal and Khorvaire · Eradicated thousands of years ago, and still the axis of Imperial policy**

House Vol was an aberrant elvish house of Aerenal and Khorvaire, eradicated by humans, giants and "good" elves thousands of years ago for crossing the elvish and draconic lines. Its leader was [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/), the Queen of Death, half dragon and half elf, who ruled over undeath and who has remained in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house. The destruction of the house was meant to extinguish its dragonmark with it, but the [Mark of Death](/lore/the-mark-of-death-and-the-dragonmarks/) survives on two living women, and the elvish houses that fled the purge remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline.

## WHAT THE INEVITABLES HOLD

The Empire keeps no file on House Vol; its position is that House Vol does not exist and did not exist recently enough to matter. The material the Inevitables hold reached them by four different roads: what [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s own dossier says about her mark and her ancestry; a decrypted Crimson Sun propaganda file; the sworn testimony of a black dragon who was bled for three years under the Korramont; and a loremaster's suppressed manuscript recovered from the Hall of Records at Korth under a false index.

## THE ERADICATION — WHAT EVERY ELF IS TAUGHT

House Vol was an aberrant elvish house, eradicated by humans, giants and "good" elves thousands of years ago. Its offence was the crossing of the elvish and draconic lines; its punishment was the destruction of the house and the extinction of its dragonmark. An alliance of elves and dragons wiped out the Mark of Death along with the bloodline that carried it <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>; the [Undying Court](/organizations/the-undying-court/) of Aerenal is the authority the surviving elvish lines answer to. Its leader was Erandis Vol, the Queen of Death, half dragon and half elf, who ruled over undeath and who remains in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house.

## TESTIMONY OF VALTREX, UNDER THE KORRAMONT

Freed of the collar the Empire had locked around his neck, and no longer insane, the [black dragon](/species/black-dragons/) Valtrex gave the Inevitables three statements about the house in a single sitting. First: **the elvish houses that fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline** — the purge did not end the project, it exported it. Second: **Erandis Vol bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun.** Third: Uriel has used his own stolen blood, drawn off him for three years on an Imperial rig and shipped out by rail, to sire her heirs — which, as he put it, makes him their father.

<small>"The Crimson Sun" in the dragon's mouth names a dragon. The same phrase names the Empire's secret service, whose head was [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/). Both usages are live and the connection between them is unresolved.</small>

## THE ARMHAIR MANUSCRIPT — HALL OF RECORDS, KORTH

Master Armhair's confiscated book, recovered from its false index, contains a dragon family tree traced back to the progenitors, an identification of Valtrex's father, and — the reason the book was confiscated — a thread connecting the Mark of Death, Erandis Vol's birth, and one particular dragon. The same manuscript argues that a vanished progenitor called [Viagra](/people/viagra/) took the place of the dying child Prince Kaius, and that Kaius III is that dragon. Armhair expected to be executed for writing it and said so aloud in a public hall.

## THE INEVITABLES' READING — INFERENCE, NOT RECORD

This section is the working theory of [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), assembled in a dead advocate's apartment in Korth from the Armhair manuscript, Valtrex's testimony and the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/). No document states it.

- Uriel bears the Mark of Death and descends from House Vol.
- Erandis Vol, her ancestor, bore a dragon's child.
- The Litany, recited under seal at every veiling, ends *"and the dragon / will be reborn."*
- Therefore Uriel is attempting to repeat the thing Erandis did: to bear a dragon-sired son.
- Which accounts for the fourteen daughters, and for three years of bleeding Valtrex on a rig built to harpoon, restrain and drain him.

The theory carries one hard consequence: the [bells](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) are waiting on a birth, and Locke Pierce's countermeasure is aimed at the same event from the other side. It also leaves one thing unresolved. Valtrex believes the heirs are his, and the Litany promises a dragon reborn, and those two statements are only the same statement if Valtrex's blood was ever the point. Nothing recovered says it was.

## SURVIVING CLAIMANTS

- **[Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)** — branded with the Dragonmark of Death, a twisting and growing draconic mark between her navel and her loins. She descends from the House and is an eager follower of Erandis Vol. She believes the Vol bloodline is superior and should rule all of Eberron as an undead wonderland.
- **Gabrielle (Gabriel) Duin** — claims direct descendance from the House of Vol and bears the Dragonmark of Death. Handler of the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/), commander of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) by murder, consort to the Emperor.
- **Fourteen daughters** — Uriel's children by Emperor Kaius, many sets of twins, all under twelve years old. Not one of them a son.

**Holdings:** none. The house holds no land, no seat and no title; what survives of it is the mark on two women's bodies and the consecration rite of the bells.

**Items:** the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) — the original bell, dug out of Greenbluff, *"some speculate that it was commissioned by Erandis Vol herself, though others claim the 'Celestial Queen' who commanded its creation was some other elvish source."* · the consecration rite of the copies, which binds them to *"the unholy blood and the great mother."*

> "It's leader, Erandis Vol, was the Queen of Death, a half dragon half elf that ruled over undeath. She's Uriel's ancestor." — from Uriel Qualanthri's own file
