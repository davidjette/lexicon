**Society · The Vault Underground, Upper City Sharn · Unresolved**

The Velvet Table Society is a society of the Upper City of Sharn that meets at the Upper City Vault. Its name appears exactly once in anything the rebellion holds: a note recovered from the chambers beneath The Vault Below, recording that Mayor [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) had joined it. No membership roll, charter, purpose or date accompanies the line, and every person who could have explained it is dead.

## THE SINGLE SOURCE

Beneath **The Vault Below**, in the chambers where [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) was being held and two starved prisoners were being kept as blood cattle, [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) gathered up scattered documents and relics before they fled through the trapdoor. Among the correspondence about Umbra and Project E.D.E.N. was a note recording that Mayor Henry Heinrick had joined **The Velvet Table Society**, which meets at the Upper City Vault.

> "…mention of Henry Heinrick joining The Velvet Table Society, which meets at the upper-city Vault location."

## THE VENUE AND THE KNOWN MEMBER

It meets at [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/)'s own address — the Upper City Vault, above the Zeir private residence, in the club [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/) owned and operated. It admits members, which means it has a threshold and someone who decides who crosses it. And its known member was the Empire's former Chief Diplomat, a survivor of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s purge of the Council of 13, and the architect of Sharn's propaganda machine.

It cannot be established from the evidence whether the Society is the recruiters' own name for themselves — the wealthy men from across Khorvaire who trafficked young people to [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) and treated the residence as a private den — or a separate body of Upper City patrons who merely shared the venue. The briefing on the Children of Ember is emphatic that the recruiters were *not* cultists. Nothing in it names them.

In Nico's notes, [Candy Crush](/people/candy-crush/), Edwin Graves' personal property lawyer, is a member of the Velvet Table Society. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Hopper)</small>

<small>**Unresolved.** The party never followed the thread. Malrik and Faith died on the rooftop of the Vault Underground, Henry Heinrick the following night on Esther Crona's balcony, and Hallorn d'Lyrandar is dead too — by Eric's hand, according to the Doves' accusation at Thronehold. Every person who could have explained the name is gone. Whether the Velvet Table Society survived its four known associates is not recorded anywhere the rebellion can reach.</small>

## Standing Query

At the first Council of Sharn [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) requested legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below. Whether it was granted, and whatever paperwork the Society left behind in those rooms, is not recorded.
