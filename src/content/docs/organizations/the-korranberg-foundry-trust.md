**LEAKED INTELLIGENCE DOSSIER - compiled from four seized documents and one interview**

The **Korranberg Foundry Trust** is the company that cast the great bells for the Houses, working the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) inside the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) with a dwarvish workforce it held as property. Its private schedule records thirteen great castings commissioned, eleven delivered and two in the yard, and three hundred forty-one minor parish castings delivered. About three years ago, the same year Sharn fell, roughly 97% of its dwarvish workforce was destroyed in what the office calls "the accident" - in fact a writ of warrant for the death of a long list of named dwarves, signed KAIUS III and carried out by the Trust's own overseer, [Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/), whose own name was last on the list. The foundry is now broken; the Korranberg office is still open, and its principal client is the House of Kaius.

The Trust's records reached the rebellion because a clerk wanted a good rating. Wearing the face of the Imperial Star Advocate Deirdre Moro, [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) presented credentials at the Trust office in [Korranberg](/places/korranberg/) and asked to audit the record crates; the clerk unlocked them and waited outside, smoking. [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) - an accountant before he was anything else - read the crates in one sitting and produced the largest single intelligence haul of the Korth campaign. Weeks later, on the night the mountain finished, the same clerk let them sleep in the office, and fifty pounds of the Trust's records went into a bag of holding and out to Sharn. Three further documents sit beside them: a leaf seized from a burning strong-box in Sharn, a teamster's statement in the Fulcrum bell file, and an overseer's own satchel.

## KORRANBERG FOUNDRY TRUST - PRIVATE SCHEDULE OF WORKS

<small>Second leaf; the first did not survive. Recovered by the Chancellery of Sharn from the sacristy strong-box of a Lower City church - the sisters were feeding the box to the stove when the writ was served. Evidence item 7 of 22.</small>

The arithmetic of the entire conspiracy, stated as a standing summary carried forward:

- **Great castings, consecration grade: THIRTEEN commissioned. ELEVEN delivered. TWO in the yard.**
- **Minor castings, parish grade: three hundred forty-one** delivered to the Houses to date - with the schedule of small consignments kept separately by the Sisters and *not* by this office.
- All castings consecrated **on delivery, by the Sisters alone**. Foundry personnel are excluded from consecration and are reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft.

The named consignments run: the ninth to the cathedral-house at **KORTH**; the tenth to the monastery precincts at **ATUR**, delivered standing with the tower to be raised over it afterwards at the consignee's expense; the eleventh to the Grand Infirmary at **FAIRHAVEN**, by twenty-ox dray with a Sister riding escort. The twelfth is consigned to a destination burned off the page - freight instructions withheld from the office, wagons provided sealed, drivers of the Sisters' own, and *"draft paid double for the blindness."* The thirteenth is **in the yard**, tuning not yet taken.

And then the entry that is not a bell at all:

> The Original. Not of this foundry, and the Trust will thank the House to record that it never was. Received under guard for the taking of tuning references only - our masters worked in wax and never with hammers, per the terms. Returned under escort to Her Radiance's keeping; destination withheld from schedule; escort Sister-Inquisitor M. D., dove seal. The Trust petitions that no further reference work be sent to this yard. The men will not stay in the shop with it after dark, and the Trust has stopped fining them for it.

The thirteenth cannot take its tuning until the reference comes back, and the House's factor writes that the reference *will not travel again before the appointed night*. The Trust notes for the record that it has met its dates, and that *"the choir wants only what the House itself withholds."*

Last line before the burn: *"Dockets burned per standing instruction, this leaf excepted in error."*

## THE RECORD CRATES, KORRANBERG OFFICE

What the audit found, and what it conspicuously did not:

- **No records at all for the last three years.**
- Roughly **seven years** of freight schedules before that: ore in, "large classified objects" out. The improved rail was built when the House of Kaius bought the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) about ten or eleven years ago.
- **Archaic property records of dwarvish slaves owned by the Trust.** A trust, holding title to people, filed alongside the freight.
- A handwritten, unofficial **death tally**: roughly **97% of the dwarvish workforce destroyed in "the accident,"** about three years ago - the same year Sharn fell. It is in nobody's official hand.

> Of course you understand there is no rail, and there never was a rail. - the Trust's clerk, handing over seven years of schedules for a rail line

## FULCRUM BELL FILE - TEAMSTER INTERVIEW, KORRANBERG

The rebellion's decade-long bell-count file carries one Korranberg entry, from a teamster who hauls night freight when the pay is right. It is the outside view of the same organisation:

> Paid double, no questions, to move "church fittings" out of a foundry yard at the third bell - their joke, not mine. Crates consigned "to the Houses." The foundry man burned the docket while I watched, on a brazier kept by the gate for exactly that. A yard that burns its own paperwork every night is a yard I don't work twice.

The brazier is still burning by the gate. The Trust's own schedule confirms the practice in its own words, in the same sentence in which it admits the leaf survived by mistake.

## THE PERSONNEL LEDGER, AND THE WRIT

The last two documents came out of a dead man's satchel three years late. [Overseer Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/) was found in a sealed Black Dove shrine on the foundry level, still on shift, still ordering his risen ogres to finish the count. He carried the **Korranberg Foundry personnel ledger and administration book**, his name on the cover, many quills spent.

He also carried a scroll on good parchment under the black ribbon and seal of the holy see of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), hastily retied: a **writ of warrant for the death** of a long handwritten list of names. It begins **Corn Deeptempura** and **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura**. It includes the Black Dove Sister Calvane. The last name on it is **Velen Marsk**, who signed to receive it. Most of the names are crossed out. Some dwarves are annotated as missing or potentially escaped. It is signed **KAIUS III**.

The room it was processed in has the administrative papers still on the table and large floor drains of the kind used to hose out a big space, caked with blackened three-year-old blood.

## The contradiction

The Trust's schedule is proud of hitting its dates. Its crates hold a slave register and a death tally in the same drawer. Its gate has a brazier for burning the evidence and its clerk has a script about a rail that never existed. The massacre that ended the works was an order, executed by the Trust's own overseer, on the House's own paper, over the Emperor's own signature, with a provision at the bottom instructing the overseer to finish with himself.

**Members:** an overseer at the works, dead and still counting; a clerk at the office, alive, helpful, and keen on his rating; a foundry man at the gate with a brazier; and an owned workforce that the paperwork lists as property.

**Holdings:** the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/); the freight yard and Trust office at Korranberg; a rail line that officially never existed.

## Present day

Fifty pounds of the Trust's records are in Sharn, in the hands of a Chancellor who intends to use them at trial. The foundry is broken, the moulds are in acid, and the office is still open - a clerk in an empty building keeping crates for a company whose works no longer exist and whose principal client is the House of Kaius.
