**The St. Stamos cultists** are a group of radical rebels in [Newham](/places/newham/), whose war cry is "St. Stamos". When the people of Newham think of the rebels, they are most likely to think of the St. Stamos cultists and their actions. They were the radicals behind the theater bombing of [Leef Ep 10](/sessions/leef-ep-10-the-theater-bombing/), the first Newham session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/), in which a singer with element stones in her torso was not saved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

## The theater bombing

In the first session after the trio moved on from Leef to Newham, a theater in the city was bombed. A singer with element stones in her torso was not saved; Dave wrote the next morning that he wondered "if we were supposed to intervene and save the singer". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-25; 2025-09-27)</small>

Dave wrote that [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) "is gunna feel very bad about letting people get harmed and will be v conflicted by the acts of terrorism", and that "theater bombing is rough stuff". Nico, who plays [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), answered that "Some may say The hero’s of Leaf also caused an act of terrorism when we attacked the train station and blew up their shipments and killed guards." Dave replied, "yeah but civilians is different", and "we've gotta root out the rebels who would do this to innocents". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

## The rebels of Newham

JL, who ran the arc, answered:

> When people in Newham think of “the rebels” they’re most likely to think of the St. Stamos cultists and their actions.

Asked whether these were the radicals, JL confirmed it: "Yep. That’s their war cry." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

Gemma tried to get one of the cultists to follow the party so that they could talk. Dave wrote that "if we had captured them or fought them, maybe they'd end up our enemies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

The trio later welded Newham's anti-LeBeefe rebel cells into a single resistance, and the city collapsed into the New Mourning when Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) was killed. See [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/).
