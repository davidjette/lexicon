> And in the distance, a group of hunters, covered in silvery blood, consuming their grim quarry. The Banefae are hunting unicorns.

<small>— the Lunar Unicorns Ally Card. Read-aloud text from Arc V, Starfall Part IV.</small>

**Fae unicorns · The Moon · Hunted by the Banefae**

The **Lunar Unicorns** are fae unicorns of the Moon, hunted by the **Banefae**, and one of the allies drafted for the final battle at the Egg. The party came over a rise on the Moon and found the Banefae "consuming a dead unicorn," covered in silvery blood. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part IV.)</small>

The Banefae are the people [the Saharel sisters](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/) cast into Despondos, "where all of them perished" — after taking their home while they "were feeding our people into oblivion." Whether the hunt on the Moon precedes that reckoning or shows that some survived it is not recorded.

Their black action, **The Last Unicorn**, resurrects anyone dead within a year without needing the body, and using it kills them.

## At the Egg

**White — Healing Aura: "Heals all creatures you choose within 60 ft by 10d4 hit points."**

**Black — The Last Unicorn: "Ressurrect any creature who died within a year, you do not need their body, they appear Prone in a space up to 30 ft away." <small>(Sic on "Ressurrect".)</small>**
