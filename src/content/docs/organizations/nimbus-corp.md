**Corporation · "The Company" · Successor to the St. Cloud Trading Company · Headed by Revan Darkcember until his disappearance · Active**

*Also known as:* the Company

**Nimbus Corp** is the corporation that has been "the Company" since [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) secretly bought the assets of the St. Cloud Trading Company at auction. When [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) fled the "present" into the past, he left behind the St. Cloud Trading Company, the true Company, in [Sigil](/places/sigil/), born from the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) on Faerun. The heroes of the Ages of the Infanta ruined his tower, [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/) destroyed his home, and the Company's assets went up for auction. Revan bid in secret and beat the heroes' group bid, and [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) lost the auction to Nimbus Corp. Temple Holdings and its **Bank of Harloch** persisted well into the future all the same; the company was rebranded Temporal Holdings after the Ages of the Infanta, and Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs" because of the bank. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Revan headed Nimbus Corp until his disappearance, about two hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta.

In time Nimbus Corp became critical to the astral travel of Faerun and the associated realms, and consolidated power until it was the largest private company in the known universe. Nimbus Corp and [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) came together, and Nimbus became a major arm of the Lexicon's control over [the Sovereignty](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta, is set at the beginning of that consolidation, while behind the scenes the Company, the Church of Cypher and many others try to create more Lexicons, copy the original, or avoid domination by it.

## From St. Cloud to Nimbus

Nimbus Corp descends from [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/), Revan's clan. Renaming the Company from St. Cloud to Nimbus preserved the "cloud" branding established over centuries while putting Revan's own stamp on it. [Maverick](/people/maverick/) is a distant descendant of Revan.

## The Company in the time of Dead Man's Hand

**Shareholders.** [Darius Klaxon](/people/darius-klaxon/), heir of a line that includes [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/), is a shareholder in the Company. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Departments.** The symbol of the Metallurgy, Ores, Oxides and Nano Materials Dept. is a moon. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Its work on the Lexicon.** The Company works on understanding and attempting to duplicate the Lexicon. It holds its own perspective on the Ordo Trium, the Order or Rule of Three, which summarizes the metaphysics of reality under the current pantheon, a pantheon legend describes as beholden to the action of the Three Fates at the end of the Second Time of Troubles. That orthodoxy "has mixed support within the Company":

- the **"Seekers"** (Mind's Eye), "who believe this paradigm supports existing powers to the detriment of rising, potential divinities";
- the **"defiers"** (Athar), "who believe the gods are frauds, believe that the Three sisters are a lie to cover a hidden conspiracy of gods";
- the **"Fated"** (Shareholders), who "are happy to keep the current order."

<small>(DM notes, the Sororia and the Ordo Trium)</small>

## In Sigil

The Company provides a jump portal in the Clerk's Ward, and it is researching cures for [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/), the plague of the Hive Ward. [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/) dismisses Cageblight as a fake plague engineered by the Company to control the city. At [the High Courts](/places/the-high-courts-of-sigil/), "Only factions in the Company or the most anarchic groups like the Wreckers deny the final authority of a high court judgment rendered by the Lexicon." <small>(DM notes)</small>

[Lenore](/people/lenore/), an artificer of the "Seekers", reports to the Company. From Sigil she sent to it and had the reply "Return to Buttsniv"; after the [Blackfish](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) she told the Company about [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Lexi

**Lexi** is a Company seeker who had a map to a secret entrance to [the Tomb of St. Magnus](/places/the-tomb-of-st-magnus/). Lexi hired [Bud Holhertz](/people/bud-holhertz/)'s rival adventuring party to raid it, offering 5,000 gold each plus miscellaneous treasure and access to the Company portals. On arrival the Salad triplets were killed by a monster and Lexi teleported away, leaving the party to its fate; Holhertz and Lou Sassle escaped. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Outside [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/) in Waterdeep, Holhertz took Lenore for Lexi; she was shot from behind, poisoned and left dying. <small>(Grub + Grog narrative; DM notes)</small> Holhertz's words for the woman he took for Lexi:

> And you can tell your supervisor or whateva that if this is how the Sword Coast Trading Co. treats its contractors, I'd prefer they take their business elsewhere. That is, if they'll chip in to bring you back to life. Not sure what kind of benefits they give to back stabbing grubby Cartographers.

<small>— Bud Holhertz (DM notes). Sic on "whateva".</small>

## In Waterdeep

In Waterdeep's plots and power plays the Company is paired with the Zhentarim: "Trade giant driven underground, joined with black market merchants in Skull port." Its allies are "whoever helps" and its enemy is [the Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/). Its plots are "playing factions against e/o". Dyksterhaus, the Unmasked Lord who desires independence from Cormyr, counts the Company and the Zhentarim among his allies, and among the threats to him that "Company/Zhents double cross him". [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) plots to "Flip the company, install new unmasked lord". <small>(DM notes, Waterdeep plots and power plays)</small>

## Containment

The Company keeps containment files, and possesses objects similar to O-3255. Its file on [O-3255](/items/o-3255/), volumes XIII to XVI of the *Ars Calixia* of [Calix](/people/calix/), directs Company staff not to open, read, or otherwise attempt to understand or reveal the tome, and to deposit it into a closed, secure, water-resistant vessel and return immediately to the department office. The file rates O-3255 Epsilon and makes it a priority acquisition for the Company's operating teams in Faerun, and it places the sister volumes of the *Ars Calixia* in the personal library of the Chairman of the Board. In Undermountain the party recovered the Company's mission file for O-3255.
