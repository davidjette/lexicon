**Priestly sisterhood · Devotees of Cypher · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Keepers of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/)**

**Also known as:** the Church of Cypher · the Faceless · the Sorority of Order

**The Sororia** is the priestly sisterhood of the devotees of **Cypher**, the god of Mystery, in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). Worship of gods or devils is taboo in Sigil, and temples there never grow too large for fear of [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/); the Sororia are the one exception. The order replaces the Fraternity of Order among the factions of the city, keeps [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) at the heart of [the High Courts](/places/the-high-courts-of-sigil/), and holds to the Ordo Trium, the Rule of Three. Its Factol is [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/), and its towering cathedral is [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/), where [Heyu](/people/heyu/) was brought before him.

## Cypher and her sisters

Cypher is one of the three **Fates**, laid out as Time, Fate and Mystery: **Eloise**, **Istus** and **Cypher**. Eloise is the god of Space and Time, Istus is the god of Fate and Time, and Cypher is the god of Mystery. The Factol speaks of "the Mother of Mysteries and her sisters". <small>(DM notes)</small>

The Faceless do not worship Cypher directly. They hold any appeal to her, any image of her, or any knowledge of her true nature to be "a blasphemous overreach into a holy and impenetrable unknown that she crafted around herself and the unspeakable truths which led to the Infanta Crisis itself." <small>(DM notes)</small> Their cathedral depicts no god and no recognisable creature, object or work.

Heyu, a kenku investigator wizard, is a follower of Cypher. [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/) worshipped Cypher, but was headed to the Abyss for his acts in life. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The exception to the taboo

> The Sororia is the priestly sisterhood of the devotees of Cypher. Worship of Gods or Devils is taboo in Sigil, and their temples never grow too large for fear of retribution from the Lady of Pain. But for some reason only known to her, the Sororia are the exception. Perhaps it is because the Faceless do not directly worship their god, believing any appeal, image, or knowledge of her true nature to be a blasphemous overreach into a holy and impenetrable unknown that she crafted around herself and the unspeakable truths which led to the Infanta Crisis itself. Perhaps what many say about Cypher and her sisters is true - that their actions ended the Crisis and established the current Interplanar Order, and in deference, the Lady of Pain looks the other way regarding their Towering Cloister Regardless of the reason, their presence and influence in Sigil establishes them as uniquely powerful and far-reaching faith, despite their disinclination to proselytise.
>
> <small>— DM notes (sic)</small>

## The Ordo Trium

The **Ordo Trium**, the Order or Rule of Three, rests on three aphorisms:

> **Center of the Multiverse:** everyone thinks they're at the center, they're right.\
> **Unity of Rings:** the universe is composed of infinite rings, follow all things long enough, you'll return to where you started.\
> **Rule of Threes:** when things happen, they happen in threes.
>
> These aphorisms underpin a greater wisdom: that binaries are false, that 3-ness, is more stable, and when faced with only 2 choices, seek a third option. This wisdom established itself in the aftermath of the INFANTA CRISIS, 500 years ago, when the 3 FATES ISTUS, ELOISE, and CYPHER pulled the world back from the brink.
>
> <small>— DM notes</small>

> The order of Three summarizes the mostly stable metaphysics of the dominant paradigm of reality under the current pantheon of divinities, which legend describes as beholden to the action of the Three Fates at the end of the Second Time of Troubles. The good aligned gods of Men, Dwarves, and Elves, along with the neutral gods of death, order, and fortune, devised the process of souls through the great Wheel.
>
> <small>— DM notes, "The Sororia + Ordo Trium"</small>

The crisis named is [the Infanta Crisis](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/).

### Within the Company

[The Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) studies the Ordo Trium alongside its work on understanding and attempting to duplicate the Lexicon. The orthodoxy divides it:

> This orthodoxy has mixed support within the Company - with some, namely the "Seekers" (Mind's Eye), who believe this paradigm supports existing powers to the detriment of rising, potential divinities; nor the "defiers" (Athar), who believe the gods are frauds, believe that the Three sisters are a lie to cover a hidden conspiracy of gods, - while the "Fated" (Shareholders) are happy to keep the current order.
>
> <small>— DM notes (sic)</small>

## Keepers of the Lexicon

The Sororia are the keepers of the Lexicon of the High Courts, "a tremendously powerful artifact, a magical oracle whose provenance and origin is of constant debate". <small>(DM notes)</small> It is a gargantuan construct, an eldritch machine made from an extraterrestrial crystal of hyperdense selenium, and its judgment is final for nearly every faction in Sigil. Its existence is not common knowledge. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Behind the scenes, the Church of Cypher, the Company and many others are trying to create more Lexicons, to copy the original, or to avoid domination by it.

## The Factol

The Factol of the Sororia is Brother Montegue, "Factol of the Faceless (Sorority of Order)", an "Attractive young Orcish priest (w/BD energy)". <small>(DM notes)</small> He looks like an orcish BD Wong. His magic word is "STONE".

At Our Lady of Mysteries, after Heyu had been given a vision at the altar, the Faceless surrounded her and brought her before him. His words to her:

> "You bear the mark of the Mother of Mysteries and her sisters. You were meant to bear it, and to hold the Cards. Find them, for her; return this piece of her holy Memory."
>
> <small>— the Factol to Heyu (DM notes)</small>

## Our Lady of Mysteries

The Sororia's towering cathedral admits followers and respectful tourists. Its masked nuns and acolytes bustle and convene among complex geometric art and architecture; an altar there, bearing a fractal mosaic and a silver candle, gives visions, and a visitor may return to pray once a day for a donation of 5 gold. Full treatment at [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/).
