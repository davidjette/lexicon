**Orcish archivists · Keepers of the Chronicle · Sworn to Istus, Lady Cypher**

*Also known as:* the Cypherene Orcs · the cypher orcs · the Orcs who served the Chronicle

The **Cypheric Orcs** are the orcish artificers and archivists who kept [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/) — which is to say they kept [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), with Lex inside it, without knowing what it was or that the party had left it there. Their name comes from their devotion to **Istus, Lady Cypher**, "the weaver of fate and the guardian of Time." Netheril destroyed them over the archives and obliviated their historians without ever getting into the Chronicle, and they return out of the land of the Dead at the final battle among the allies the Raven Queen brings.

## Their history

Their history is at [Zebulon the Meek](/people/zebulon/), the last of them: foreknowledge given to them by the party, an innumerable hoard held under a single mission to keep it from the wrong hands, then division when the queens of Netheril demanded the archives, then defeat. Netheril destroyed them and obliviated their historians, and never got into the Chronicle.

> There are ruins at Yog, Jhamdaath, Enkidu, but no one alive remembers how they were built or by whom. I have spent a lifetime trying to convince every Orc I see that they themselves and their ancestors built those ruins, and were betrayed. But to no avail.

<small>— [Zebulon the Meek](/people/zebulon/), on the Cypheric Orcs Ally Card. The same words are in Arc VI, Time of Troubles, Part IX.</small>

Lex was the centrepiece of their faith in Cypher, which was centred on "archiving the past, collecting magic items, keeping them away from bad guys, and Obliviating whatever led to knowledge of the Leviathan and the desire to find and trigger the egg". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> The orcs studied the nature of the Crystal Spheres. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

By Dave's account [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), as the Grey Sage, taught the Netherese about the Leviathan and led them into a prolonged war against the orcs. The Netherese won, took the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and other critical Eldritch artifacts, and fed the orcish urban population and intelligentsia, "men women and children", into the Obliviator. They erased the orcish civilization as far as they could, but could not access the Chronicle. Dave judged it likely that [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) and the [Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/), who invade Netheril, got the idea of obliviation from the Netherese, "who in a way got it from the Cypherene Orcs". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> Of St. Cloud, Dave said: "he’s the enemy the whole Orcish civilization worked against". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09)</small>

## How they became allies

They come back out of the land of the Dead at the final battle, named in the record as **"the Orcs who served the Chronicle"**, among the allies the Raven Queen brings. <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

## At the Egg

**White — Slow Time: "Cast Slow at a 3rd level with duration of 1 round."**

**Black — Time Rupture: "Target creature w/i 30 ft makes a WIS Save 30 or skips their next turn."**

<small>Both of their card's abilities are time effects, which no other people-card in the set has.</small>

Asked whether the cypher orcs board the Lost Train if they die, Dave answered: "The train is a metaphor, but no unless you’re erasing them from almost all memory". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-15)</small> See [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## The helm invocation

In the [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) era an invocation to Cypher was spoken in Orcish to a helm, with this translation:

> "Gorrukk nashok Cypher, urukk karok ghashra ug skarn, vrukk burzum-matok karnok brash. Shak'na urukk, oh urukk-dum, ug drukk ushnok ghash ghorok zhnok. Cypher's ghash gorrukk urukk, skarn dum urukk drokzha. Vorokk shruk astral zhrug, nashok gron Cypher!"

> "By the eternal march of Cypher, master of time and space, I command the currents of the cosmos. Hear my words, oh helm of old, and carry us to our ordained place. Cypher's path be our guide, his wheel our protection. Forward through the astral sea, in the name of Cypher!"

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-23)</small>
