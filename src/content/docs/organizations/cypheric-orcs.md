**Orcish archivists · Keepers of the Chronicle · Sworn to Istus, Lady Cypher**

The **Cypheric Orcs** are the orcish artificers and archivists who kept [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/) — which is to say they kept [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), with Lex inside it, without knowing what it was or that the party had left it there. Their name comes from their devotion to **Istus, Lady Cypher**, "the weaver of fate and the guardian of Time." Netheril destroyed them over the archives and obliviated their historians without ever getting into the Chronicle, and they return out of the land of the Dead at the final battle among the allies the Raven Queen brings.

## Their history

Their history is at [Zebulon the Meek](/people/zebulon/), the last of them: foreknowledge given to them by the party, an innumerable hoard held under a single mission to keep it from the wrong hands, then division when the queens of Netheril demanded the archives, then defeat. Netheril destroyed them and obliviated their historians, and never got into the Chronicle.

> There are ruins at Yog, Jhamdaath, Enkidu, but no one alive remembers how they were built or by whom. I have spent a lifetime trying to convince every Orc I see that they themselves and their ancestors built those ruins, and were betrayed. But to no avail.

<small>— [Zebulon the Meek](/people/zebulon/), on the Cypheric Orcs Ally Card. The same words are in Arc VI, Time of Troubles, Part IX.</small>

## How they became allies

They come back out of the land of the Dead at the final battle, named in the record as **"the Orcs who served the Chronicle"**, among the allies the Raven Queen brings. <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

## At the Egg

**White — Slow Time: "Cast Slow at a 3rd level with duration of 1 round."**

**Black — Time Rupture: "Target creature w/i 30 ft makes a WIS Save 30 or skips their next turn."**

<small>Both of their card's abilities are time effects, which no other people-card in the set has.</small>
