**Space fleet · [The Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) · Starfall era · Active**

*Also known as:* AC · AsCom

**Astral Command** is the exploration and defence space fleet of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), and its military, founded as the fleet of the Union of Free Space. It has a thousand-year history by the Starfall era, some five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta. Its old Sovereign Spelljammer [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) is the setting of [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). Its Academy sent its cadets to fight in [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), where a secret program first used [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/). In *Light of Xaryxis* the Zanzibar's captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) severed themselves from Astral Command to pursue [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), while elements of the fleet, the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), may have been involved in the Lexicon's cover-up. Astral Command later decommissioned its Zarus-class battle cruisers, introducing the Istus-class clippers after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) along with the Terrasque-class. It seized the wreck of the Zanzibar, a military tribunal sentenced Jim in absentia, and its admiral [Timothee Buckets](/people/timothee-buckets/) became Sovereign Supreme. Fifteen years later, in [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), Jim is a wanted man, and Astral Command has virtually replaced [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) with Hyperion.

## Mission

Astral Command's mission is to secure the galaxy for free trade and cooperation between the planets of the Union, to defend it from its enemies, and to explore and settle new worlds. It has no prime directive; its practical mission is to map the galaxy and establish basic colonial outposts, with a general mission to protect the freedom and safety of innocents. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

In 2023 Dave proposed three prime directives for it: "Make not yourself a God", "Leave time alone, and do not allow the changing of history", and "Reveal no magic to the mundane, but allow the natural progression of magic among all civilizations". His reasoning was that, if the Church of Cypher and the Leviathan crisis were the founding events of the civilisation, changing time and constructing human gods would be anathema. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-16)</small>

## Organization

Astral Command is led by the Admiralty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Its ranks, as posted on the Zanzibar's crew roster, run:

- Admiral · Vice Admiral · Rear Admiral
- Captain
- Commander / 1st Sergeant
- Lieutenant Commander / 2nd Sergeant
- Lieutenant / Chief
- 2nd Lieutenant / Marine
- Ensign / Private

<small>(crew roster)</small>

Officers belong to corps: Command, Enlisted/Logistics, Diplomatic, Arcane Science, Medical and Engineering. <small>(crew roster)</small> The Arcane Science corps covers all arcane and physical sciences, the Medical corps studies biological and mental phenomena, and the Engineering corps studies applied magic and engineering. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-20)</small> A corps represents an officer's training and does not line up exactly with a ship's divisions, so a scientist may serve in Engineering. Aboard the Zanzibar the squadron leader Chad "Kreen" Mantis, an arcana-trained officer and bladesinger, reports to the enlisted Sergeant-at-Arms Buttsack, because the security chief commands the support craft. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-13)</small>

The Church of Cypher has its own hand in Astral Command, through [the Cypherian Pontificate](/organizations/the-cypherian-pontificate/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Astral Command's intelligence corps is [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), the grey cloaks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

Spellcasters in Astral Command are treated as technologists, smart people and blessed people who use various means of affecting reality. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-02)</small>

**Uniforms.** Astral Command is a uniformed force with different colours for different departments. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-26)</small> Medical officers wear green; white is for communications and diplomatic officers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-28)</small>

**Equipment.** The standard-issue Astral Command device is [the Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), a limited communicator and tricorder. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small> It makes basic coordination possible but is extremely unreliable. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small> A later model works like a scroll-preparer: an attuned officer loads it with scrolls before a mission, with sector variants such as mediscrollers and arcanoscrollers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08; 2023-01-21)</small> A cyberdeck implant can replace the Scroller. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> Jim Smallberries's Force Rifle, which works as a rod of eldritch blast, became his standard issue when he made planetfall as mobile infantry. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> Pilots wear Astral Command Pilot Wings. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Academy

Officers train at the Academy. Caprica matriculated first in her class there, many hundreds of years older than her classmates. The prologue to her first session describes the choice that followed:

> "You stunned the Admiralty and the Academy but selecting the open helm on the SSJ Zanzibar, currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere." <small>(sic)</small>
>
> <small>— Prologue to Session 0/1 (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

Jim Smallberries, Katrina McQuaid and Horgus Simianis were Academy contemporaries. Smallberries is a one-star captain and Katrina a two-star captain, which technically makes her the captain of the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01)</small> When a beholder ship kamikazed into Neverwinter, the whole Academy was sent to war. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> In the era of *The Starsong Awakens* the Astral Command Academy is on Toril. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

## Order of Scribes

Wizards of the Order of Scribes in Astral Command are led by [Admiral Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), one of the most respected and influential officers in the fleet and the primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) network. Her work, and that of many officers of her order, is finding a way to overcome [Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/) to allow interstellar communication and teleportation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

## The Beholder War

During the Beholder War, Katrina McQuaid, a grey cloak of OSR3G, and Horgus Simianis served a secret program that was used only once, and chose recovering the Demoncore over saving the people with Jim Smallberries, who was left stranded on Negroni Prime with a dozen civilians. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09; 2024-03-16)</small> Dave described their divergence from Jim as mirroring Astral Command's divergence from its righteous path, and as the cause of Jim's disillusionment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> Astral Command possesses a weapon able to turn an entire plane undead. According to Dave, any knowledge Jim gained of the Demoncore was probably erased from his mind by OSR3G. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

The SSJ Zanzibar was a misfit ship in an unimportant and dangerous sector, stranded in one system for want of a pilot for its Major Helm until Astral Command sent Caprica's commission and ordered the ship to collect her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10)</small>

After the climax of the first season, Jim took hard news and was faced with retirement, which created tension between Astral Command and the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-10)</small> By November 2023 the crew had learned that the Lexicon was hiding the reality of the Shrike from mortals and that elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, might be involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

Jim, Sandy and Caprica then set out on a pilgrimage across [Necis](/places/necis/) that severed them from Astral Command. Dave said it would make them the greatest officers of Astral Command's thousand-year history, whether they lived or died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> By April 2024 Jim and his crew had gone off as an independent force. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, Jim Smallberries lives as Charles Largeberries on a mining asteroid, wanted on multiple felony counts of dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property, which could carry a penalty of fifty years in a military prison. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> The Company's dossier gives his alias as Charles Longberries, and records that a military tribunal tried Captain Smallberries in absentia and sentenced him to 20 years in a Sovereign penitentiary for seven counts of dereliction of duty and theft of Astral Command property. His location is unknown to Astral Command; Company agents tracked him to a mining colony in Scrospace. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

Radical government reforms in the Sovereignty after the crisis at Tannhauser Gate resulted in the appointment of the Astral Command admiral Timothee Buckets as supreme ruler, with broad powers over the military, the legislature and the judiciary. The dossier lists the Sovereign Supreme as alive and in Greyspace. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

The wreckage of the SSJ Zanzibar was seized by Astral Command. The Company's dossier on Caprica places it in a top-secret hangar in Realmspace, and its entry on the ship places it in a high-security facility in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) on the moon of Toril. Access to the vessel is restricted by Astral Command, and Company agents have been denied it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

### Ships

After decommissioning its heavy Zarus-class battle cruisers, the class of the SSJ Zanzibar, Astral Command took its fleet in a new direction, with light, fast and agile vessels. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships; Company dossier)</small>

- **The Istus-class clipper**, introduced by Astral Command after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, was built for stealth exploration, extreme speed and easy manoeuvrability. To compensate for its thin hull it mounts Arcane Artillery, which casts spells to protect the vessel. See [the Istus Cruiser](/items/the-istus-cruiser/).
- **The Terrasque-class**, inspired by legends of Torillian dreadnoughts, combines the security and firepower of a starbase with the dexterity of a Star Moth. Roughly the size of a small moon, it was built for planetary defence. See [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/).

<small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships; Company dossier)</small>

The dossier's entry on the Istus-class **SSJ Vanguard** says rumours of its top-secret destination range from Realmspace to deep within the Vodoni Empire, and names its commanding officer as Capt. Sandy Titzandassia and its executive officer as Lt. Commander Mordecai, both former crewmates of Caprica on the Zanzibar. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> At the start of the arc JL placed Mordecai as chief science officer of the SSJ Excelsior. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
