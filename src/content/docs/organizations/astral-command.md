**Space fleet · [The Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) · Starfall era · Active**

*Also known as:* AC · AsCom

**Astral Command** is the exploration and defence space fleet of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), and its military. Its old Sovereign Spelljammer [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) is the setting of [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). Its Academy sent its cadets to fight in [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), where a secret program first used [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/). In *Light of Xaryxis* the Zanzibar's captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) severed themselves from Astral Command to pursue [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), while elements of the fleet, the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), may have been involved in the Lexicon's cover-up. Fifteen years later, in [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), Jim is a wanted man, and Astral Command has virtually replaced [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) with Hyperion.

## Mission

Astral Command's mission is to secure the galaxy for free trade and cooperation between the planets of the Union, to defend it from its enemies, and to explore and settle new worlds. It has no prime directive; its practical mission is to map the galaxy and establish basic colonial outposts, with a general mission to protect the freedom and safety of innocents. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

In 2023 Dave proposed three prime directives for it: "Make not yourself a God", "Leave time alone, and do not allow the changing of history", and "Reveal no magic to the mundane, but allow the natural progression of magic among all civilizations". His reasoning was that, if the Church of Cypher and the Leviathan crisis were the founding events of the civilisation, changing time and constructing human gods would be anathema. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-16)</small>

## Organization

Astral Command is led by the Admiralty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Its ranks, as posted on the Zanzibar's crew roster, run:

- Admiral · Vice Admiral · Rear Admiral
- Captain
- Commander / 1st Sergeant
- Lieutenant Commander / 2nd Sergeant
- Lieutenant / Chief
- 2nd Lieutenant / Marine
- Ensign / Private

<small>(crew roster)</small>

Officers belong to corps: Command, Enlisted/Logistics, Diplomatic, Arcane Science, Medical and Engineering. <small>(crew roster)</small> The Arcane Science corps covers all arcane and physical sciences, the Medical corps studies biological and mental phenomena, and the Engineering corps studies applied magic and engineering. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-20)</small> A corps represents an officer's training and does not line up exactly with a ship's divisions, so a scientist may serve in Engineering. Aboard the Zanzibar the squadron leader Chad "Kreen" Mantis, an arcana-trained officer and bladesinger, reports to the enlisted Sergeant-at-Arms Buttsack, because the security chief commands the support craft. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-13)</small>

Spellcasters in Astral Command are treated as technologists, smart people and blessed people who use various means of affecting reality. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-02)</small>

**Uniforms.** Astral Command is a uniformed force with different colours for different departments. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-26)</small> Medical officers wear green; white is for communications and diplomatic officers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-28)</small>

**Equipment.** The standard-issue Astral Command device is [the Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), a limited communicator and tricorder. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small> Pilots wear Astral Command Pilot Wings. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Academy

Officers train at the Academy. Caprica matriculated first in her class there, many hundreds of years older than her classmates. The prologue to her first session records the choice that followed:

> "You stunned the Admiralty and the Academy but selecting the open helm on the SSJ Zanzibar, currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere." <small>(sic)</small>
>
> <small>— Prologue to Session 0/1 (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

When a beholder ship kamikazed into Neverwinter, the whole Academy was sent to war. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> In the era of *The Starsong Awakens* the Astral Command Academy is on Toril. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

## Order of Scribes

Wizards of the Order of Scribes in Astral Command are led by [Admiral Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), one of the most respected and influential officers in the fleet and the primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) network. Her work, and that of many officers of her order, is finding a way to overcome [Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/) to allow interstellar communication and teleportation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

## The Beholder War

During the Beholder War, Katrina McQuaid and Horgus Simianis served a secret program that was used only once, and chose recovering the Demoncore over saving the people with Jim Smallberries, who was left stranded on Negroni Prime with a dozen civilians. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09; 2024-03-16)</small> Dave described their divergence from Jim as mirroring Astral Command's divergence from its righteous path, and as the cause of Jim's disillusionment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> Astral Command possesses a weapon able to turn an entire plane undead. According to Dave, any knowledge Jim gained of the Demoncore was probably erased from his mind by OSR3G. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

The SSJ Zanzibar was a misfit ship in an unimportant and dangerous sector, stranded in one system for want of a pilot for its Major Helm until Astral Command sent Caprica's commission and ordered the ship to collect her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10)</small>

After the climax of the first season, Jim was faced with retirement; Dave used the prospect to create tension between Astral Command and the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-10)</small> By November 2023 the crew had learned that the Lexicon was hiding the reality of the Shrike from mortals and that elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, might be involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

Jim, Sandy and Caprica then set out on a pilgrimage across [Necis](/places/necis/) that severed them from Astral Command. Dave said it would make them the greatest officers of Astral Command's thousand-year history, whether they lived or died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> By April 2024 Jim and his crew had gone off as an independent force. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, Jim Smallberries lives as Charles Largeberries on a mining asteroid, wanted on multiple felony counts of dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property, which carry a penalty of up to fifty years in a military prison. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>
