**Deathless elvish council · Aerenal · Ancient, and holding to a judgement it made thousands of years ago**

The Undying Court is the council of the elvish dead of Aerenal, sustained past death rather than raised from it <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>, and the authority the surviving elvish lines answer to. Thousands of years ago it took part in the judgement that eradicated [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) and the [Mark of Death](/lore/the-mark-of-death-and-the-dragonmarks/), and its position since has been that the matter is finished business. No member of it has been met by the Inevitables; what they hold of the Court comes from the Aereni description of it, from [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s own dossier, and from the testimony of a black dragon about the elves who did not stay.

## THE COURT AS THE AERENI DESCRIBE IT

The Undying Court is the council of the elvish dead of Aerenal, sustained past death rather than raised from it. Uriel's own grandmother — the previous owner of the glimmering black-hilted longsword Uriel carries — lives among the Undying Court on Aerenal, and her parents are elders there. The [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), drawn from only a few clans within the Aerenai, are shown deference by other elves in all matters of healing and death; the Court's relation to the order is not recorded.

The Court's structure, its membership, its ruling on the current war, and whether it knows what its own daughters are doing in Khorvaire are likewise not recorded.

## THE COURT AND HOUSE VOL

The Court's single decision that still governs events in Khorvaire is thousands of years old. House Vol crossed the elvish and draconic lines; an alliance of elves and dragons eradicated the house and, with it, the Mark of Death. The Court's position is that this was finished business. The [dragon](/species/black-dragons/) Valtrex's testimony under the Korramont says otherwise: **the elvish houses that fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline.**

## FROM URIEL QUALANTHRI'S FILE

Uriel came to Eberron against the wishes of her parents, who are elders in Aerenal and did not think the high elves should get involved in the Great War. She holds the Court in open contempt. Her belief, stated flatly in her own dossier: *that any elves who stand against her, including the Undying Court, are unworthy of their immortality and must have it taken from them.*

The Empress of Khorvaire holds a doctrine of endless resurrection called the Eternal Nightfall, commands a continent-wide network of [Houses of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) tuned to raise the dead everywhere at once, and has named the deathless council of her own people as a legitimate target for having their immortality removed.

**Members of note:** Uriel Qualanthri's grandmother, previous owner of Uriel's longsword — the only individual member of the Court named in any recovered document.

**Holdings:** Aerenal. Nothing else is recorded.

**Standing toward the Empire:** Unknown. Whether Aerenal sent a delegation to the Summit at Thronehold is not recorded; the Black Doves sat there as delegates in their own right.
