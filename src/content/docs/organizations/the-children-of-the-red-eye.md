**Robot resistance · Devotees of the Red Eye · *The Starsong Awakens* · Active**

*Also known as:* the resistance · devotees of the Red Eye · Red Eye Revolutionaries

**The Children of the Red Eye** are the robot resistance of *The Starsong Awakens*, devoted to the Red Eye, the nom de guerre under which the resistance knows [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). They arose among the robots purged under [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/). They keep four holy sites on Selune, the moon of Toril, where the Bureau agents of [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) made a pilgrimage to them. After the finale of the Brownsleeve City storyline, the resistance crewed a stolen Istus Cruiser, and the Bureau agents Kol Hanaka and Paytra returned as robots who had found the Red Eye.

## Background

A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the Sovereignty passed the Starweaver Act, outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included, which led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market for robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> The robot resistance of this era uses "Red-Eye" as the nom de guerre of Barrias Ruby-Eye, the dwarf artificer who builds robots.

## Beliefs and symbols

The symbol of the resistance is a red eye. Its sayings and texts include:

- "in ships with painted sails did the red eye travel the galaxy"
- the Little Red Book, *The Way It Is*, by Red-Eye; the copy found in Brownsleeve City was hollowed out, with a pearl-handled pistol inside

<small>(character sheet)</small>

The movement's manifesto is the **Red Eye Manifesto**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-09-14)</small>

## The Red Eye Manifesto

*Main article: [The Red Eye Manifesto](/items/the-red-eye-manifesto/)*

The Red Eye Manifesto is titled *The Way It Is* and runs to two chapters. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small> Kol Hanaka's notes give its author as Red-Eye. <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Chapter One** opens with the writer's father teaching him that the Sovereign government must never be trusted, because that is the way it is, and asks the people of the Sovereignty whether there can be another way. It recalls that fifteen years earlier the elected Sovereign delegates voted to abolish intelligence they deemed artificial, calls it the greatest of all injustices to name a life artificial, and lists the things the artificial are told are false: their intelligence, their limbs, their minds, their hopes and dreams. It names them people, "People who live and love and learn and hunger and fight to be seen as people!", and calls on every reader with a mind, a conscience or a soul to declare that the way it is cannot be.

**Chapter Two** calls for a revolutionary party, built on revolutionary theory, to lead the humans, elves, dwarves, gnomes, orcs, halflings and constructs of the Sovereignty against its racism and bigotry. It looks to the intelligentsia, university youth, professors and wizards for a new vanguard of leadership that joins theory to practice, keeps close links with the masses and practises self-criticism. It sets the task of a better life for the several hundred billion people of the Sovereignty, and holds that among its humanoids only the reactionary ruling circles oppress the people branded artificial, and that those circles cannot represent the workers, farmers, artificers, wizards and sorcerers who make up the majority. It holds that "galactic struggle is a matter of class struggle", and closes by defining revolution as an insurrection, an act of violence by which one class overthrows another.

<small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

## Holy sites

There are four sites on Selune holy to the children of the Red Eye. When the mayor of Brownsleeve City was taken, the pilgrimage to them became the way to save him, which ended the first act of *The Starsong Awakens*:

> "There are 4 sites on Selune holy to the children of red eye. Make the pilgrimage, follow his righteous path, to save your mayor’s life."
>
> <small>— Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

The Bureau of Compliance agents in Brownsleeve City, [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) and [Paytra](/people/paytra/), followed clues bearing a symbol of a red eye. <small>(character sheet)</small> Dave, as Barrias's player, suggested that the Bureau suspects the Red Eye is a construct, because in battle his helmet flips up and his suit grows, moves fast and shoots off blasts. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-12)</small>

After the finale of the Brownsleeve City storyline on 2024-11-01, the epilogue showed an Istus Cruiser. Its crew are devotees of the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small> Paytra had died in that finale, and she and Kol were rebuilt as robots. JL, as DM:

> "Their mission is a success. They found the Red Eye."
>
> <small>— Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10</small>

At the table JL called the movement "Robo Bolsheviks". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09)</small>

## Members

- **The Red Eye**: Barrias Ruby-Eye.
- **[Umwelt](/people/umwelt/)**, called Weld: a warforged farmer and Wild Heart barbarian, a liberated farming and ranch-hand unit who joined the resistance. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small> His faith is "Red Eye Revolutionary". <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **[Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/)**: a high elf arcane trickster, once a famous Waterdeep musician and actor. In his backstory he is a devotee of the Red Eye working with the resistance aboard the stolen Istus Cruiser, fighting for robots since the Bureau dismantled his wife, the synth AI politician Robin Martel. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-07)</small>
