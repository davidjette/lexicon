**Human paladins · The Knights of the Round Table · Allies from Arc VI**

King Arthur and his Knights are an alliance of Arc VI: the human king **Arthur Els** and the **Knights of the Round Table**. Arthur Els is what [Nimue](/organizations/nimue-and-the-elves-of-avalon/)'s search was for, a human king raised to be a rival to Netheril and "the incarnation of mercy and justice." The Knights are listed among the allies who step forward at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s funeral, without a pledge of their own recorded. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

> Arthur Els grew and took his place as King of those forested lands, and many believed him to be the incarnation of mercy and justice that Nimue had hoped he would be, but the further treachery of men took root.

<small>— the King Arthur and his Knights Ally Card.</small>

## At the Egg

Their black action is **True Resurrection** cast in a single action: the party can bring one person all the way back, at the cost of Arthur and every knight with him.

**White — Sword of Power: "Deal 30 magical slashing and 10 radiant to target creature within 30 ft."**

**Black — Land and King: "Cast True Ressurection with a casting time of 1 action." <small>(Sic on "Ressurection".)</small>**

Not recorded.
