**Noble family · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Owners of [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) · Pactbound to [Asmodeus](/people/asmodeus/) · Voided**

*Also known as:* the von Lamps · Moth von Lamp <small>(JL's spelling)</small>

**The Von Lampes** were a noble family of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), the owners of [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/), bound by a pact with [Asmodeus](/people/asmodeus/). In the Age of the Infanta they worked with and against [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) over the Infanta he held in Waterdeep, promising Asmodeus 999 souls and the Infanta, and fled the city after the attack on the boy emperor's parade. They went after the [Amulet of Kai Ryssdall](/items/the-amulet-of-kai-ryssdall/), the largest emerald in the world and a gem big enough to cast *Greater Gate*, and at its auction [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) used the Vault Wand to void them. [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) inherited Tiffany House and its old debt to Asmodeus after them. The Von Lampes were on the Lost Train at the end of the Infanta.

## The family

After the heist JL, who played Zanzibar, asked whether any Von Lampes survived. Dave:

> The family is the two of them and their three kids, one of which who was older than the other two who are still toddlers\
> The oldest hasn’t been seen in years, the younger ones disappeared with moth and Tiffany\
> So except for their young kids who are missing yes you killed them all

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-20)</small>

The session text for Waterdeep names **Moth Von Lampe** and his sister **Tanning Von Lampe**. <small>(Arc III Part VI.)</small> The name Moth Von Lampe first appears in a list of names Adam, who played Drefan, offered Dave for the campaign. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-11)</small>

The session text for the family's hotel room in Zhentil Keep gives the children differently: "The Von Lampes sacrificed their oldest son (Triple Dick Darryl) and their middle child (Rachel) to become demons", and "Their youngest son was slated to die to bring the Army of Hell through the Gate from the Shadowfell." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small>

## Waterdeep

In the party's Arc III visit to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), where the boy Jacob had been proclaimed Imperator and the Infanta of Obus, the docks showed "snooping by the Von Lampes, including some magic runes left behind". At the Fainting Queen, "Moth Von Lampe is there with hood up, looking out of place", and when St. Cloud's man arrived Von Lampe pressed him:

> your boss made a promise to my boss, one that cannot be broken. You have lost the other lords. They will depose the boy if you do not send him to the battle. And we both know he won’t show himself to be much of a soldier. The time has come for you to turn the infanta over to me so that we may fulfill our mutual obligations.

The Von Lampes lived in the Sea Ward, in the northwest, in a home that was hard to penetrate; anyone caught inside was "taken alive and dominated to be interrogated and turned into a spy." At home Von Lampe and his wife spoke of their plans:

> Von Lampe says, “The changeling wouldn’t budge. St. Cloud doesn’t plan on honoring his word.”
>
> She says, “We have to kill him and the boy and take the infanta.”
>
> He says, “You can’t be discovered. None of this is worth it if we have to leave the city.”
>
> She says, “I will do my job. You do yours.”

She put on her Lord's Ensemble and went to the Hall of Justice as a Masked Lord. He performed a ritual in his villa with four other worshippers of Asmodeus: "Asmodeus speaks to them, and remind him his twins’ souls are on the line. Von Lampe pledges to give him the 999 souls plus the infanta by dawn. Asmodeus orders the other four cultists to murder him if he fails." <small>(Sic on "remind". Arc III Part VI.)</small>

Their warlocks guarded the sewer portal into the Observatory on Mount Waterdeep alongside St. Cloud's forces, with orders to betray St. Cloud's loyal men "(mainly Zhentarim rogues and Warforged Sentinels)" at a chosen moment. On the morning the boy emperor marched his army to the harbour, the route passed an area "prepared in advance by the Von Lampes", and "Tanning Von Lampe (Moth’s sister) springs from her seat, along with a few other masked lords, and rushes to kill St. Cloud (if he is present) and grab the boy king." <small>(Arc III Part VI.)</small> See [the Zhentarim](/organizations/the-zhentarim/) and [Obi](/people/obi/).

In the briefing that followed, the emperor had survived the assassination attempt, saved by St. Cloud in the final moments, and "The Von Lampes have fled Waterdeep and their property seized by the crown. Their whereabouts are unknown." <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small> Weeks later Dave listed the open threads of the campaign: "There’s always Asmodeus in the shadowfell, now orcs are laying waste to Thay, and someone was talking about investigating the Von Lampes a bit more". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

## The auction heist

The party went to the auction of the Amulet of Kai Ryssdall because St. Cloud wanted it: "the largest emerald in the world", a gem big enough to cast *Greater Gate*, key to his plan to summon Asmodeus into the heart of Waterdeep. Dave:

> the Von Lampes are there.  These are analogs to the Cassalanters in dragon Heist, in this telling, they are the owners of Tiffany House and a pact with Asmodeus, they are there to get the Amulet as well.

While [Zebediah](/people/zebediah/) went through the vault below, the Von Lampes met their end upstairs:

> In the auction above, Stonecypher used the Vault Wand from the train episode to create a temporary space in the bathroom, waited for the Von Lampes to enter, and then closed the space, voiding them.  They later end up on the train in the finale scene.

<small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

At the table the Vault Wand was also called Jenkin's Portal Wand. <small>(Nico. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> Nico, in 2025: "I used the Jenkins wand on the von lamps during the auction house heist". JL, the same day: "I thought we killed Moth von Lamp. Didn’t we?" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small>

At the table it was a laxative ambush. Dave: "Looking back I’m marveling that You guys used Hershey squirt and the portal key to kill three different people in plain site, incredible work" / "Two major villains and a random guy". <small>(Sic on "site". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-20)</small> And: "the laxative -> ambush in the bathroom is a pretty classic move, but you guys took it to a new level". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-22)</small> Of the amulet the Von Lampes had wanted: "It's reportedly the largest emerald in the world", and "Lex suggests that the gate spell can be cast with gems besides diamonds, and that large quality gems may be needed to summon very powerful creatures". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-20)</small>

## The hotel room

The party's next session opened with downtime "including investigations into the Von Lampes, Una and Carlysle St Clouds heart". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-02)</small> The Von Lampes had stayed in the penthouse of the Black Diamond, "an upscale lodging in the center of the city of Zenthil Keep". Their room had been stripped of valuables with care, and their bags held the hotel's own belongings, "they were stealing them, presumable because they are broke". <small>(Sic. Arc III Part XII.)</small>

Illusion magic hid a door to the next room, which they had also rented. Inside was "a ritual room with black velvet, satanic symbols and statues, a large pentacle in the center of the room, a Supreme Scroll of Gate (Shadowfell), and an amulet-sized pedestal. The scroll calls for a 15,000g worth emerald, sapphire or ruby and a child sacrifice." In a wooden cage lay an unconscious young child who, if healed, said his sister had been turned into a devil. A minor devil appeared and attacked. <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small>

## Where they went

Dave, on the Vault Wand:

> like where are the Von Lampes\
> disappearing them in stead of killing them put Asmodeus of your tail for a long time\
> …\
> bc if you kill them go to hell!\
> or not actually, they go to the shadowfell, but he's in charge there! in that time\
> …\
> they are not in hell\
> they clipped through the environment\
> my guess is they're at the end of time, where you saw June and she made you god of time, for this go round

<small>— Sic on "in stead" and "of your tail". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small>

The Vault Wand's non-room also swallowed a train. At Baldur's Gate Stonecypher used the wand on the railway gate, and the out-of-control steam engine careened into the void. That train is the Lost Train of the finale. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> In the heart of the Egg a player finds themself in a luxurious car of it: "In a seat nearby, you see Moth and Tanning Von Lampe, they ignore you. They are sitting with two other people reading from a book as a group with some papers spread around the small table." <small>(Arc VIII, End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small> Dave, in 2025: "They were on the train at the end". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small> See [Arc VIII: End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## Tiffany House after them

Tiffany House stayed bound to Asmodeus. In Arc V the party weighed an option to "Go to Tiffany House and defeat the Asmodeans". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small> In Waterdeep Revan was confronted by Violet, an erinyes who served as a housecarl and as a spy for Asmodeus. <small>(Arc IV, Winking Depths Part IV.)</small> She is [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/), the same devil through every game, the bound servant of the pactbound owner of Tiffany House. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Nico recalled Griddy as the manor's "house Carl" and "a liaison for asmodeus", and Dave answered: "And yeah Griddy is it’s hauscarl". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small>

Dave on how the house passed to Revan:

> First you guys disappeared the Von Lampes in the heist of the Amulet of Kai Rhyssdal\
> Then Revan signed papers assuming the lordship of Marsember\
> Which includes Tiffany House and an old debt to Asmodeus which Revan assumed\
> So he inherited it after the Von Lampes had died (edited)

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small>

Revan's line is saddled with that debt forever. In the age of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) it is carried by [Darius Klaxon](/people/darius-klaxon/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Darius wanted to take the RUIN card to disinherit himself and so free himself of it; [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), called Mach, talked him out of it, and had Darius taken it the house would have fallen to [Maverick](/people/maverick/). <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-authority-factol-tiffany.md)</small> When Zanzibar was crowned King of Punis he gave Revan his hold, and Marsember became Darkcember. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-c30-coronation.md)</small>
