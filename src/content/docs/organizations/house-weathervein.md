**Dynasty · Feudal house and trading cartel · The Azure Sea coast, Oerth · Fallen**

*Also known as:* the Weathervein dynasty · the Weatherveins · Weathervien

**House Weathervein** is an ancient feudal house and trading cartel of the Azure Sea coast on Oerth, which ruled the beaches, harbors and shipping lanes around the land that is now [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) centuries before the Sea Princes. It fell within a single generation. [Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/) buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter [Vivica](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) in the Weathervein Tomb beneath Saltmarsh's [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/), and members of the line were bound into the artifacts of the family's treasure hoard, Crestus among them inside [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/); [Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/) made many horcruxes. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), a campaign on the *Ghosts of Saltmarsh* module, the house's heir [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) entered the tomb and took Vivica's [Ring of Shooting Stars](/items/ring-of-shooting-stars/), recovered the family sword [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), and with her party won the hoard's three weapons in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/). The sword later became the holy relic of the Temple of Neptune.

## The name

"Weathervein" names the house, its sword and its bound artifacts:

- **House Weathervein**, the dynasty.
- **Weathervein, the Tempest Sword**, the family blade. The first party of Ghosts of Twatmarsh died over it, and an assassin took it, "Weatherbrand" in Dave's question "Who took Weatherbrand" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14, 2021-12-17)</small>. Vivica called it "the Tempest Sword" and "the key to my uncle’s foolish game". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>
- **The treasure hoard**: Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/), recovered from White Plume Mountain, into which members of the line were bound.

The name also stands for the heir. Vivica told Dolores: "You are Weathervein, an angel of the Storm." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> After White Plume Mountain, Dave said of [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/): "They begrudgingly acknowledge Weathervein as owning Wave but don’t fully trust her not to use it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

## Skerrin's telling

On the night of *Salvage Operation – Part I*, after the Saltmarsh town council granted the party its naval commission, [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) acted surprised that Dolores did not already know her family's history, and told it:

> The Weathervein dynasty was an ancient trading cartel and feudal house in along the coast of the Azure Sea, centuries before the emergence of the Sea Princes. Long ago they ruled the beaches, harbors and shipping lanes with ruthless mercenaries of every sea-based race. That the lords themselves were terrifying fish-like men and women who were rumored at times to bear angels wings, swim like sharks, and sprout lightning like a storm cloud. accused of stealing and immense treasure from the people of the land which is now Saltmarsh. This unstoppable regime demanded a hefty tax in goods and gold for any ship that was unfortunate enough to cross its territory. Its ill gotten treasure was hidden by that family long ago, somewhere in the sea-carved caves around the coastline.  Their downfall took just a single generation, when one of the Weatherveins attempted to bottle the secrets that made their family strong and accidentally destroyed everything.

He added: "If you are the heir to that dynasty, then you will inherit much more than treasure and blood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12, 2021-11-13, *Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small>

## Angels of the storm

The story JL told at the table, as Nico recalled it, was that the Weathervein "were a tribe that came from heaven but lost their powers?" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13)</small> A fresco in Saltmarsh's Temple of Neptune retold the same story. <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The stonework of Count Crestus's tomb carries a "fresco of heavenly storm angels mating w/ women, ruling the seas". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, *Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small>

Dave on Dolores's Aasimar blood: "I figured as a aaismar it’s many generations ago for Dolores" and "Angels kicked from heaven to make a life on earth". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-25)</small>

## Members

- **[Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/)**, "prince of the sea and master of the coves". His soul is inside Blackrazor.
- **Amphibiana Mermadon Weathervein**, his wife. She was murdered while she hunted, and he buried her "along with her father’s sword". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>
- **[Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/)**, their daughter, whose ghost waited in the Weathervein Tomb.
- **Vivica's two uncles**, "Cresto’s brothers in law she blamed for their house’s downfall". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>
- **[Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/)**, called PW and Pengua Liquidius Weathervein, the skeletal alchemist whose bones lay in the tomb.
- **Whelm**, "The one Weathervein that didn’t consent to being bound into ancient artifacts from the treasure hoard". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>
- **Dolores Weathervein**, the heir, Nico's player character, first played as Talia Weathervien. She was raised in a temple of Neptune in Velkstaad on the Icy Sea, whose priests told of a winged child who "Fell from the heavens during a blizzard." and was "Destined for Neptune’s sword Weathervien." <small>(Dolores Weathervein character concept)</small>

Dave posted four of the names together during the session of 2021-11-19: "Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein", "Pengua Liquidius Weathervein", "Amphibiana Mermadon Weathervein" and "Crestus Octopaius Weathervein". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-19)</small>

## The fall

Vivica's account of the fall, given in the tomb:

> “My family, the guardians of the Atlas, those who walk upon the water, wielders of the Shooting Stars and the Tempest Blade, our very fatherhood, all wasted for the vanity of a single generation.”
>
> “My uncles were fools, they squandered our riches and our fame. When my father knew the mob would come for him, he left me here, to be safe. They murdered my mother while she hunted, he buried her anon, along with her father’s sword. Her brothers were to blame. They weakened our stock, our seed. They siphoned off our wealth in gold and blood. And now look at our sorry house. I have nothing left to build.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

In Skerrin's telling the downfall came when one of the Weatherveins "attempted to bottle the secrets that made their family strong and accidentally destroyed everything." That Weathervein was [Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/). <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-open-questions.md)</small>

On 2022-04-23 Dave, as a player, offered what he called "my current fan theory": that [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) "might have been built to depose the Weathervein dynasty a long time ago", that "Salmor is here to stop the Weathervein from rising again", and that "If there’s a big trove down there, it could be that the mission of the SB is to cover it UP". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

## The Weathervein Tomb

The Weathervein Tomb lies beneath Saltmarsh's Temple of Neptune. Count Crestus "buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter Vivica Amphibiana in that tomb, with his own tomb above as the entrance". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Cujo, the gravedigger, told Skerrin "that he believed there was more to the Weathervein tomb, that there have been reports of hauntings below the temple." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, *Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small> The tomb beneath the temple is a different place from Dolores's own tomb under the sea.

[Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) had taken the tomb's contents by 2021-09-18, when Dave had them added to the party's inventory as "the Weathervein Masoleum", and Nico entered them under the temple. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The party ledger's inventory:

> Weathervein Tomb Inventory (thieves locked): Wellgar Brinehanded (Temple Cleric)
>
> - Magic Mouth on fountain
> - Bones of PW
> - Sapphire (500g)
> - 30 silver (paid to betray a friend)
> - 2 Pouches of Powder (Steel from a murderer’s blade)
> - Alchemist's Weight
> - 5 Discs 25 g
> - Gold Skull 20g, Rose 5g, Apple 5g, Ribs 30g
> - 3 Sturge Blood
> - Black Ichor
> - Receipts signed by PW
> - Alchemical Equip
> - Spellbook Cantrips (at will): minor illusion
> - 1st level (4 slots): charm person, color spray, silent image

<small>(Neptune's Rejects ledger)</small>

Dave's list of the same haul, posted that night, also names a "Curious Stone of Fortune". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

On 2021-11-12 Skerrin led the party to the tomb at night and ambushed them there with Brotherhood assassins. Dave: "Skerrin knew there was no sword in there, he knew he could watch you and ambush you in secret. He may not have known about the other room opened with your blood". Dolores's blood opened that room, and Vivica's ghost appeared over the casket. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12, 2021-11-13)</small> During that session Dave posted the lines "Rest thee, heralds of the storm / Close thy sun0soaked eyes and rejoice from evermore", "thy purpose is fulfilled" and "for heaven wets its mighty gears with the blood of angels". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

## Heirlooms and treasure

Vivica named her family "the guardians of the Atlas, those who walk upon the water, wielders of the Shooting Stars and the Tempest Blade".

- **The Atlas of Endless Horizons**, "a map to heaven, an heirloom of the fam", of which an image and description appear in Count Crestus's tomb. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, *Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small> [Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/) later carried "the Atlas". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>
- **A Ring of Water Walking**, listed in Crestus's tomb at DC 19. <small>(*Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small>
- **[The Ring of Shooting Stars](/items/ring-of-shooting-stars/)**, Vivica's ring, "the signet of the Princess of Clouds".
- **[Weathervein](/items/weathervein/)**, the Tempest Sword.
- **The Weathervein coves.** Crestus was "master of the coves", and after *Salvage Operation* Nico noted "now we have those weathervien coves to search too!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>
- **The Weathervein summer home.** In the marriage deal [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) struck with [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/), "he can use the Weathervein summer home again". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small>

**The treasure hoard at White Plume Mountain.** Dave: "I also love the idea of White Plume Mountain being the dungeon with the Weathervein treasure, and that the dwarves found it and extracted The artifacts, and that we have to bring them back in". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> By the end of the session of 2022-08-13 the party held its three weapons, Wave, Whelm and Blackrazor. Dave: "All three weapons have true natures we can try to bring our, and their corrupted natures from Keraptis". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14, 2022-09-16)</small> Whelm was the Weathervein who did not consent to being bound, Crestus is inside Blackrazor, and Penguinus "made lots of horcruxes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15, 2022-09-16)</small> Nico, of her character, wrote that Dolores might start "researching how to rid the weathervien family from the weapons and get rid of them for good". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

## The heir and the rebirth of the house

On the night of the tomb ambush the party blew up a building and executed six members of a secret society. Dave to Nico the next day: "people witnessed your full and terrible power and the rebirth of your house. Vivica’s Ghost remains quite present to you through the ring, and is ecstatic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Of [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/)'s box of deeds and notes Dave wrote, "we could get back the whole house of Weathervein in one fell swoop" and "I know what Vivica wants you to do". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

On 2022-03-12 the party killed Skerrin and Dolores recovered Weathervein. JL, who ran the session: "XP: 2750 for SKERRIN, 100 for Gabby for finding Weathervein. 500 Dolores for achieving her destiny." Dave reposted Vivica's charge that night, and added: "Gabriella ready to be the enforcer for the stormgodqueen". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12, 2022-03-13)</small>

After White Plume Mountain Dave wrote that "the Weathervein arc is mostly complete". Nico's plan for Dolores was to retire "to being at the temple and on the city council and making the name “weathervien “ powerful again in Saltmarsh." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## After Twatmarsh

Weathervein became the holy relic of the Temple of Neptune, "tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore", and was stolen when the temple was desecrated. Dolores Weathervein became the temple's High Priestess and died, and her tomb lies under the sea. <small>(DM notes; see [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/))</small>

When the party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) was offered the sword, Dave remarked that it would have been strange had [Heyu](/people/heyu/) taken it and become "obsessed with going back to Saltmarsh to assert the supremacy of the Weathervein dynasty". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-21)</small> Dave pictured the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/) as "an angelic ally or even descendent of Dolores, who sort of reconnected with the ancient angelic line and remingled with the ocean Aasimar". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>
