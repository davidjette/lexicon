**Crusade · The Thrane front · In the field**

**The Dawn Crusade** is a crusade fighting the Empire in the kingdom of Thrane. In the three years between the fall of Sharn and the Thronehold Summit, [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) joined it and led a cavalry of knights into the most brutal and hopeless fighting of the war, against the undead [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units raised under the Empress's command. He was still deployed there when the Summit was called, and the party found him in the Mournland among a small group of [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) paladins holding a Cyrean portal against the dead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06; Korth Ep 1; Korth Ep 3)</small>

## Dario joins

Dave's account of the three years after [the fall of Sharn](/history/the-fall-of-sharn/):

> Dario joins the Dawn Crusade, leading a cavalry of knights into the most brutal and hopeless fighting against the Empire in the kingdom of Thrane. The Empire might be losing ground on the front, and pushed out of the City of Towers, but it is as ruthless and dominant as ever in the interior, where it still fields thousands upon thousands of troops, who when they fall, rise up again, now under the Empress's direct command.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small>

Asked whether Dario had gone into full Silver Flame gear now that he was with his own people, Dave answered: "Yeah but whatever version of it would be soaked in blood and smoke from fighting in the front lines of a war against Eden". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-09)</small>

## The Thrane front

When the three years ended, Dario was on the Thrane front with the Silver Flame, leading cavalry against undead E.D.E.N. units raised under the command of Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). He was still deployed there while Eric and Gemma went to the Summit at Thronehold. <small>([Korth Ep 1](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/); [the Thronehold massacre](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/))</small>

## The Cyrean portal

Flying south over [the Mournland](/places/the-mournland/) after the massacre, Eric spotted a flaming sword through the haze: a small group of Silver Flame paladins holding a massive Cyrean portal against waves of undead and E.D.E.N. hybrids, with Dario among them. The party fought beside them to evacuate the survivors, and every paladin who fell rose moments later to swell the enemy. Dario came out alive, gravely wounded, with the signs of Mistborn illness. <small>([Korth Ep 3](/sessions/korth-ep-3-the-last-mourning/))</small>

Dave on the portal: "He was defending it, they’re remnants from the kingdom of Cyre, they’re are permanent portals through out the kingdom that can be used to move troops and material around the mournlands" <small>(sic)</small>, and "Capturing them means being able to deploy around". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) then dropped the [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) on the army and the portal the party had fled. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>
