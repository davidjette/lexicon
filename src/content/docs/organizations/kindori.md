**Space whales · Wildspace · Allies at the Egg**

The **Kindori** are space whales, and allies at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). The deep-past attack on the Egg happens **in orbit of Toril**, after the Egg struck the moon, and the Kindori are what lives there. They are not named in the session record; their card is one of two in the set with no flavour text.

## At the Egg

**White — Sonic Pulse: "Deal 10d8 thunder damage to and deafen all creatures in a 60 ft cone, half and not deafened on a save."**

**Black — Lunar Flare: "Explodes and deal 30d10 radiant damage to and blinds all creatures in a ft sphere up to 500 feet away." <small>(Sic — the radius is missing from the card.)</small>**

## Status

Not recorded.
