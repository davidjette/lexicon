**Rebel cell · Upper City, Sharn · Active**

The Nest is the clandestine stronghold of Sharn's rebel faction, built inside the abandoned lightning rail Station 5 beneath [Skyroot Park](/places/skyroot-park/) in the Upper City. It was made by [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) and a small group of survivors of the Station 5 detonations nine years ago, who retreated into the sealed tunnels when the city blamed the Sub-Transit Union. From it the rebellion's leaders coordinate intelligence, plan strikes and maintain covert communication lines with allied factions beyond the city. [Izaak](/people/izaak/) sent [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) into it from the Astra Bar in their first month in Sharn; three years on it survived the rising and became a state organ in everything but name.

## THE NEST, AS THE REBELLION DESCRIBES IT

The Nest is the clandestine stronghold of Sharn's rebel faction, hidden beneath Skyroot Park in the Upper City. To the public the park is a city block of greenery and leisure; beneath its tranquil surface lies abandoned lightning rail Station 5, once part of Sharn's famed Lightning Rail Sub-Transit network. The rebels seized upon this forgotten infrastructure and turned the station and its surrounding tunnels into a secure base of operations.

From the Nest, resistance leaders coordinate intelligence, plan strikes and maintain covert communication lines with allied factions beyond the city. Entry is by a hidden puzzle set in the park above; solve it and the ground opens. To those within the rebellion it is the beating heart of Sharn's underground struggle, a sanctuary carved from the city's forgotten past, and a reminder that resistance thrives even beneath the Empire's watchful gaze.

## THE OFFICIAL STORY OF THE SUB-TRANSIT

Nine years ago Sharn dismantled its once-famous Lightning Rail Sub-Transit, a network of miniature rail lines that snaked through the city's levels and connected dozens of towers. The system was a marvel of Orien-industrial engineering, but a series of catastrophic events led to decommissioning. Responsibility was assigned to the Sub-Transit Union. In its place Sharn adopted the current dual system of Airbuses and Zephyr Pods, which proved more flexible and safer within the vertical maze of the city.

<small>Remnants of the old sub-rail tunnels still exist throughout the city. The official account does not say how many, or where. The Nest sits in one of them; [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) sits in another.</small>

## NEST MISSION BOARD — ZILSPAR FARM 02

Jessica Grimpledink was on duty at Station 5 when the detonations occurred. She coordinated rescue efforts, saved multiple survivors, and witnessed enough to know the official story was a fabrication. When the city blamed the union, she and a small group of survivors retreated into the sealed tunnels beneath Skyroot Park and rebuilt Station 5 into a base. The board she keeps there is the rebellion's working memory: Project E.D.E.N., the Cog Collector, [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and the Forge of Binding, [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) and the ArcEye Crystallizer, the fate of Sister Nora, the Sharn Broadcast Override. Missions are marked open, critical or completed in her hand.

The Nest supplies its operatives with Ghost Chips for false identity, Sending charms, and cover employment with the Vilspar Farm-Table Co-operative. It maintains an Upper City safe house, a collapsible tunnel into the command centre, and the EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node — a salvaged device capable of hijacking Sharn's ArcEye live feeds and broadcasting anything the holder chooses across the entire city.

## COUNCIL OF SHARN — FIRST SESSION, NEST PROPOSAL

With the Iron Veil's pact dismantled, Nest and [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) operatives secured the Veil Building and took Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka into custody. At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn the Nest called for trials and for rebuilding funds. It got both. Sister Nora, Hillary Heinrick and Joanne Menka were found guilty and executed alongside other high-level Iron Veil officials.

## Three Years On

The Nest survived the rising and became a state organ in everything but name. It runs deep-cover agents across Khorvaire, it feeds the provisional Chancellor's office, and its engineers bled the Eye of Corruption's rising output off as fire for the city's furnaces when Sharn ran out of fuel. Jessica now weighs whether Eric is how the Empire found Zilspar Farm — a question she has put to Gemma privately and to no one else.

> Jessica opened the meeting, crediting the trio for preventing Sharn's fall.

**Holdings:** Station 5 beneath Skyroot Park; an Upper City safe house (tunnel collapsed, Sharn Ep 17); the family lockbox in the Sharn Library; the EBT-7 repeater node.
