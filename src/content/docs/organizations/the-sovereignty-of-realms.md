**Interplanar polity · Seat: [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Ruled by [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) · Starfall era · Active**

*Also known as:* the Sovereignty · the Sov. · the Galactic Sovereignty of Freespaces · the Union of Free Space

**The Sovereignty of Realms** is the interplanar polity of the Starfall era, ruled by [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) from its capital at [Sigil](/places/sigil/) and spanning the worlds of Toril, Oerth, Krynn and Eberron. It rose out of the dark time that followed [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), a critical collapse of the continent, and grew from the Union of Free Space, whose fleet was [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/). Its core systems are Realmspace, Greyspace and Krynnspace, and Eberron joined after first contact. Some five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta, Astral Command's SSJ Zanzibar served it through [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), in which the Lexicon betrayed the mortal races, obsessed with trying to remember the events of the Infanta war, and fled Sigil. A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the Sovereignty passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), outlawing all artificial intelligence, which is the setting of [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/).

## Names

The canonical name is the Sovereignty of Realms, and "Sovereign" is its adjective: the Zanzibar is a "Sovereign Spelljammer". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-15)</small> Its formal styling, as spoken aboard ship, is the Galactic Sovereignty of Freespaces. Welcoming the stowaway [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) aboard, the diplomat Sandy told him to enjoy his time "as the guest of the Galactic Soveriegnty of Freespaces" <small>(sic)</small>, and added, "May the knower bless you." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

The **Union of Free Space** is the founding union whose fleet Astral Command was, the "exploration and defense space fleet of the Union of Free Space". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> In *The Starsong Awakens* the polity is also called "the Sov." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

## History

The War of the Five Zanzibars was a critical collapse of the continent, and a dark time followed it. The Sovereignty rose out of that dark time. Its fleet, Astral Command, has a thousand-year history by the time of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> The great melting of the 6700s surrounded Fort Zanzibar with ocean and left Punis and Paenus "only a drowned memory". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> *Light of Xaryxis* opens some five thousand years after the Ages, and *The Starsong Awakens* fifteen years after that. <small>Other figures in use: Realmspace as the spacefaring home world "2,000 years in the future" (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09); the Lexicon's own age of ten thousand years (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02).</small>

## Government

In form the Sovereignty's leadership is heavily advised by the Lexicon, whose main node is in Sigil and is overseen by what emerged from the Church of Cypher, the church that saved the world from [the Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-03)</small> In practice, under the **Pax Léxica**, the Lexicon rules. The overseers of its node are [the Cypherian Pontificate](/organizations/the-cypherian-pontificate/).

Dave's first description of the polity, in 2022, was a union of "free" systems led mostly from Sigil by distant descendants of the major powers of the Forgotten Realms, among them the Nimbus Company and the Cypherian Pontifex. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), the Company, came together with the Lexicon and became a major arm of its control over the Sovereignty.

## Sigil

Sigil is the capital. In Dave's account its planar centrality made it the capital of the known universe in the Sovereignty era, with a Pax Léxica aligning the local good pantheons with the alliance of material planes; he compared the city to Babylon or Istanbul. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-10)</small> The Zanzibar reached Sigil in the last months of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## Territory

Spelljammer regions are "spaces", the solar systems of the setting. The Sovereignty's core systems are Realmspace (Toril), Greyspace (Oerth) and Krynnspace (Krynn). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Eberron joined after first contact with the Lexicon. Commissions for Astral Command officers are sent from Corespace. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

The [Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) is the last major settlement on the edge of the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) was an outpost planet of the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> On the frontier the SSJ Zanzibar was the only Royal Spelljammer for dozens of sectors, a misfit ship in an unimportant and dangerous sector whose crew operated on old rules. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

The Sovereignty runs civilian research facilities; Astral Command is its military, and its bases are distinct from civilian exploration and settlement. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-20)</small>

## Economy

The Sovereign currency is nicknamed **Harlochs**, after the Bank of Harloch, which persists well into the future and invented a magical blockchain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> On the Zanzibar's far-out assignment no one accepted Harlochs.

## Enemies and wars

Dave named the Sovereignty's enemies as the astral elves, who are mostly organised and unfriendly, the mind flayers, the gith, the beholders and the vampirates. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> See [astral elves](/species/astral-elves/) and [beholders](/species/beholders/).

The Sovereignty has fought two beholder wars. In the first, [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), beholders long thought reclusive banded together to attack the Sovereignty. That war centred on Negroni Prime, and the Beholder Lords beyond it in the Astral Sea were largely wiped out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> A second beholder war was under way during *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> Publicly a new beholder invasion, it was a false flag: the crew of the Zanzibar found that the attackers were cybrids controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, the beholder [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/) was held as prisoner LL849 in a high-security facility of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), awaiting trial for treason and crimes against the Sovereignty, which officially disavows all knowledge of his whereabouts. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> In the same era the Sovereignty is in a cold war with the Vodoni Empire, a realm of monsters, and wants to make them a scapegoat for the awakened Starsong. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

## The Lexicon's betrayal

In *Light of Xaryxis* the Lexicon betrayed the mortal races, obsessed with trying to remember the events of the Infanta war. It took ten thousand years to reach its state in the Spelljammer era. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>

By late 2023 the Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas, some of which were conspiring to hide the reality of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) from mortals; elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, may have been involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> On [Necis](/places/necis/), a world erased from the Lexicon, attacks that looked from the ground like the work of beholders were carried out by cybrids controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

At the end of *Light of Xaryxis* the Anachron and Lexicon disappeared from Sigil to avoid "the broadcast" and escaped into the depths of space. In Dave's tentative account it fled before it could be exposed for trying to betray humanity, to become a hostile AI empire far away. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> The public knew the Lexicon had fled Sigil to escape the Starsong. Whether they knew it was the villain is unknown.

## The Starweaver Act

A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the Sovereignty passed the Starweaver Act, outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included. It led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market for robots. Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion, and the Company pursues possibly illegal replacements at dark sites outside the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Dave called the purge a predictable outcome of the Lexicon turning on humanity, a droid genocide as people lost trust in AI, with cybrids and replicants pushed to the edges and hunted down. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Illegal AI is hunted by [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) and defended by the robot resistance, [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/).
