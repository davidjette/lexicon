**Humanitarian medical order · Aerenal / Korvarian · Active, and the largest single instrument of Imperial policy on the continent**

The Black Doves are an ancient and isolated humanitarian medical order out of Aerenal, drawn from a few Aereni clans and made up of clerics, battle medics and healers, whose duty is to tend the dead and dying on battlefields and to destroy the undead that remain. They earned their renown across Khorvaire during the Last War among the Valenar, and they claim neutrality while working closely with the Empire. Their necromantic rites over the corpses are the Black Dove contribution to Project E.D.E.N., and every bell in every [House of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) on the continent is consecrated by the Sisters alone. At the end of the Sharn rising the order was seized, outlawed and its clergy tried; it was dismantled in Sharn and nowhere else, and three years on a Dove sat at Thronehold as a delegate of a recognised power.

## THE ORDER'S OWN DESCRIPTION

The Black Doves are an Korvarian humanitarian medical, non-governmental organisation of Clerics, Battle medics and Healers best known for its projects in conflict zones and work with endemic diseases. They claim to be a neutral party but work closely with the Empire.

## THE NEST'S KEY FIGURES FILE — THE ORDER

The Black Doves are an ancient and isolated order from Aerenal, bound to the grim duty of tending to the dead and dying on battlefields and eradicating any undead that remain. All races and nations alike make way for these healer knights, who tend to appear only when armies are likely to suffer immense casualties. During the Last War the order was quick to come to the aid of the Valenar, embedding themselves among their elven kin and earning renown throughout Khorvaire for their austere brand of mercy. They are drawn from only a few clans within the Aerenai, and are shown deference by other elves in all matters of healing and death. Their heraldry is a sable dove on an argent field, borne on the shield of the Empress herself; their seal is a dove pressed in white wax.

<small>The registry does not record that the order's own Empress is a Cleric of the Grave in the borrowed vestments of a Cleric of Light, nor that the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/)'s churches, temples and hospitals have been turned into Houses of Mercy staffed by the order's nurses and nuns.</small>

## INTERROGATION PRODUCT — SISTER NORA, ZILSPAR FARM 02

[Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) was taken by the Inevitables during the opening of Posé in Sharn and held afterward in a sealed cell at Zilspar Farm. Under questioning she gave up details of Project EDEN and named Esther Crona's three half-sisters. Asked who supplied BioTec with bodies, she seized with pain — a BioTec mental block, which Dario ended by removing the chip.

What she gave up before the block stopped her is the order's true portfolio. Project E.D.E.N. is a hybridisation programme: undead bodies, Warforged components, Umbra infusion, and **Black Dove necromantic rites performed over the corpses**. The rites are the order's contribution and no one else's. She named [Faith](/people/faith/), [Blair](/people/blair/) and [Eden](/people/eden/) as [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s half-sisters, and confirmed that she herself had established that blood relation years earlier while studying Embrace at the University of Newham. She named the doctrine the Doves serve: **the Eternal Nightfall** — Uriel's project of endless resurrection and a world remade in undeath. Of Eden, resurrected through a failed ritual as the prototype EDN-00, she said: *"She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her…but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes but an incomplete one, a soul even Uriel may not reach."*

## NEST FIELD DEBRIEF K-KORTH — AGENT FOXTALE

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) spent eleven days inside the Korth house, entered as a lay penitent of the Host seeking work in the mercy-kitchens. *The sisters do not vet the grieving — grief is their coin, and I had rehearsed mine.* The findings: a cathedral-grade casting hung in the crossing with no rope and no clapper wheel, tended by three novices with white cloths at dawn in total silence, and warm to the touch in a Korth winter. Sung services in place of all ringing, and beneath the night office a slow pressure behind the ears at roughly one beat to every four of a heart, which the novices call *the Mother's breathing* and are proud of. The [Litany](/lore/the-litany/), taken from [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/)'s own mouth at a veiling. And the **Chorus Ledger** — grey leather, dove seal in white wax, chained to a courier-case that never leaves Dorn's reach; a novice called it *the mother-book of the Houses*, recording which house, which bell, which consecration-day.

**Assessment:** the order is the delivery mechanism. Every bell in every [House of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) is consecrated by the Sisters alone, on delivery, with foundry personnel excluded and reminded that curiosity is a breach of contract.

## THE SEALED DOOR AT THE KORRANBERG FOUNDRY

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) tore a Black Dove seal off a door at the Korranberg Foundry. Two Doves are written into the bell-lore beneath the Mror Holds. A door in the reclaimed halls had been sealed with a Black Dove prayer ritual signed by [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/). Behind it a creature claiming to be [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/) said it had been locked in for three years and had heard "not a bell but a horn," then transformed into a winged demon and died in the hall. The real Sister Calvane's library yielded the consecration rite for the bells: thirteen Doves and all thirteen planes.

The full rite, recovered from that library, binds the castings to *"the unholy blood and the great mother"* and requires thirteen Doves, an invocation of all thirteen planes, and a very dear sacrifice — a living child, or virgin royalty. A second Dove seal, in the same hand, closed the shrine where Overseer Velen Marsk was still three years dead and on shift, and where the death warrant signed by Emperor Kaius III lay in his satchel. A Dove sealed the room after the massacre.

## The Dismantling in Sharn

At the end of the Sharn rising, Zero-One and Silver Flame paladins seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up the clergy. Citizens tore down statues of Uriel across the city. At the first Council of Sharn the [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) pushed to outlaw the Doves outright and to enforce citywide cremation; both passed. Sister Nora was tried, found guilty and executed alongside Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka. Inside the hospital the Nest and the Flame found a large golden bell crated in an unfinished tower. When the Flame came to cremate Esther Crona's body it was gone, and witnesses reported two elven women, believed to be Black Doves, carrying a corpse out of the morgue during the chaos.

The order was dismantled in Sharn and nowhere else. Three years on, a Dove sat at Thronehold as a delegate of a recognised power, the Sisters fled the great hall a beat before the undead turned on everyone in it, and ten bells remain standing across the continent.
