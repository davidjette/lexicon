**Subterranean settlement and sovereign territory · beneath the Lower City, Sharn · Autonomous**

The **Underhive** is a sovereign subterranean settlement beneath the Lower City of Sharn, ruled by [Blair](/people/blair/) and peopled by her Drones. It keeps no records for outsiders. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) won a fight at Breaker's Pit and was invited down; [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) came with him, and Blair traded them passage for the Cogborn and custody of [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/). When the rising came the Underhive rose with it, and afterwards its Drones took a place in Sharn's new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy.

## TRAVELLER'S ACCOUNT — THE ROUTE DOWN

From the Grand Aqueduct the way runs down through the lower service levels, into a massive drain pipe, and out into a flooded sewer chamber patrolled by giant crocodiles and swarms of rats. Beyond that is a reinforced door. Behind the door a Drone meets you — [Booti'slave](/people/booti-slave/), if you are lucky — and walks you through a sprawling subterranean settlement to its tavern.

The tavern serves honoured guests food, drink and a bunk room. It houses curiosities, among them a magical Vega Oracle machine built by one Carlos Vega, which takes a token and returns a fortune; the party's token returned a ring granting a minor wish. Crystallizer screens on the wall carry the surface broadcasts. Behind the tavern lie Blair's private chambers, her husbands, and whoever she is currently holding.

**Places of Interest:** **The Tavern** — the settlement's heart and its diplomatic floor. **Blair's Chambers** — throne room, interrogation room, and family home at once. **The Spine** — an ancient hidden tunnel running toward the caved-in passage where the Cogborn were trapped. **The Chamber Room** — a long-sealed webbed hall guarded by four giant spiders and an ancient Stone Golem, where past suitors of the Queen vanished. **The Underhive River** — the water route the Cogborn walked out along. **The Vault** — Blair's own strongroom, reachable from beneath the Forge of Binding.

**Password at the fast exit:** honeycomb.

## TERMS OF THE QUEEN

Blair does not give and she does not refuse; she trades. Clear [the Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) from the undercity tunnels and secure the route toward the mountain pass, and the Cogborn of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) may pass through her territory. Do it well and she may release [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) into your custody. Both bargains were kept on both sides, which in Sharn made her more reliable than the government.

Her people are the Drones. Her inner circle is her husbands, each contributing to the Underhive's infrastructure and security; [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/) the bear-man thanked the party for the Cogborn and gave them gloves enchanted with move earth. Her first husband, the [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), is a political connection reaching far past the city.

> "To the city above, she is a shadow; to those below, she is the closest thing to order they have."

## THE IRON VEIL INCURSION

During the Lord Commander's sweep of the Lower City, Iron Veil soldiers broke in and killed three Drones. The Drones killed seven of them and took the eighth alive: **Brice Peters**'s testimony, given in Blair's chambers, was that he knew nothing above his own orders and that most of Sharn's Iron Veil had been City Watch before the pact took them.

Blair, enraged that [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) had dared encroach on her territory, ordered Brice's family brought to her. When the rising came, the Underhive rose with it — her Drones and Roberts's pirate fleet appeared on every screen in Sharn.

## COUNCIL OF SHARN — UNDERHIVE DEMANDS

Autonomy. Legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below. A permanent council seat for Berenstain. The demands were tabled, tempers rose, and Dario ordered Blair arrested during the dispute; she was tried later and released. Underhive Drones nevertheless joined the new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy — the only faction to take a seat in the new city and keep its own country underneath it.
