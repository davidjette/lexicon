**Black market network · Founded by Manshoon · Allies of Temple Holdings LLC · Active in the Dead Man's Hand era**

*Also known as:* the Zents · the Xents · the Xentharim · the Zentharim · Zhentarum

**The Zhentarim** are a black market network of mercenaries and gangs, founded by the wizard Manshoon. In the Ages of the Infanta they held the Citadel of the Raven near the Ride and supplied rogues to [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/)'s forces in Waterdeep, and in Suzail their agent Emery Grim allied them with Pim and Temple Holdings LLC. During the Winking Depths the party installed a Zhentarim agent, Jonathan Silverhand, as Unmasked Lord of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/). They held Zebediah before the heist of the Amulet of Kai Ryssdall, and before the Battle of the River Lis Dave counted the party among their closest allies. [Stoth Youngglade](/people/stoth-youngglade/) became a legendary leader of the Zhentarim. In the [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) era the Zhentarim are allied with the Company, [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/).

## Manshoon

> The wizard Monshoon famously made 40 clones of himself and when he died they activated and claimed to be the real Monshoon
>
> Spent eternity hunting each other down to be the last
>
> He founded the Xentharim

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

## The Citadel of the Raven

When Marcus, the Infanta of Death, fled the tomb at [Whitehorn](/places/whitehorn/), she made for the Citadel of the Raven, where worshippers of the Raven Queen were close. St. Cloud's men tracking her knew that "the Citadel is occupied by the Zhentarim, a black market network of mercenaries and gangs", and that they had to reach the Infanta before she reached the keep. <small>(Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part IV.)</small> See [Marcus](/people/marcus/).

## St. Cloud's forces in Waterdeep

The Observatory atop Mount Waterdeep had a simpler entrance through a portal hidden in an underground sewer complex, guarded by warlocks in the employ of St. Cloud and the Von Lampes. At a chosen moment the Von Lampes would betray St. Cloud's loyal forces, "mainly Zhentarim rogues and Warforged Sentinels". <small>(Arc III Part VI.)</small>

Dave later described the organisation's position across the campaign: "When planning this entire campaign I always considered the Zents totally up for grabs - for you or St Cloud." <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

## Jonathan Silverhand, the Unmasked Lord

In the Winking Depths arc in Waterdeep, while ousting the false Infanta Jacob, the party put a Zhentarim agent, Jonathan Silverhand, in as Unmasked Lord. Dave: "You guys put a zhentarim agent in as unmasked lord during the winking depths" / "Jonathan Silverhand". JL, who played Zanzibar: "Yeah we were thinking of our immediate needs and not the geopolitical and historical ramifications." Dave: "Yeah ofc you were ousting the false infanta". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> See [the Lords of Waterdeep](/organizations/lords-of-waterdeep/).

## Emery Grim and the alliance

In [Suzail](/places/suzail/) the Zhentarim secretly own the Bareback Saloon, a tavern near the South Market "where they manage the ins and outs of thieving and mercanaries in the city." Revan and [Pim](/people/pim/)'s contacts brought the party there to "A Zent bigwig named Emery Grim", who holds court at the Bareback and "knows pretty much everything, and will tell you for a price." She offered to tell the party how Una came to be on the train if they stole a shipment of healing potions, four Potions of Greater Restoration, from the Temple of Obus. <small>(Sic on "mercanaries". Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part VII, Suzail.)</small>

After Suzail the Zhentarim owed the party one:

> Emory Grim and the Xentharim of the central continent are now allied with Pim and therefore the party. They likewise hate the St. Clouds and don't want to see the world destroyed by god-children, and will now actively help Temple Holdings.

<small>(Arc III Part VIII, War Wizards, "The Xents Owe You One".)</small>

Grim greets Pim as a great friend and the heroes as allies, and gives them an **antimagic flare**, a bright one-time flare which casts Antimagic Field for an hour, targeting the flare. The Zhentarim will come to the party's aid in combat if possible and attempt to save them should they fall near one of their agents, "(for a price.)" Grim may also help smuggle [Tiko](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/) out of the city, through Paenis, and on to Cormanthyr. <small>(Arc III Part VIII.)</small>

The Company's own threat list recorded the contact, under [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/):

> Revan …"The Tempest": An extraplanar entity of unknwon origin who has made contact with numerous Xentharim agents in the last month

<small>(Sic on "unknwon". Arc III, VIII Handouts.)</small>

## The heist

Dave started the heist of the Amulet of Kai Ryssdall from [Punis](/places/punis/), "since it’s closer to Xenthil Keep and the hook flows through Punis". The party first had to break out Zebediah, Zanzibar's twin, who held the map and intel of the target: "He got nabbed by the Xents but they don’t know what he’s planning". <small>(Sic on "Xenthil". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> Zebediah had got himself arrested on purpose, because his cell connected through the cellars to below the vault. Of the amulet, Dave later said: "Zeb went and stole it back from the Zentharim during the Heist episode". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-08-11)</small>

## The Battle of the River Lis

Before [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/), Dave told the players of Revan, Zanzibar and Stonecypher that "Arc after arc you guys have made major moves to help them all over the world. You three plus Pim are their closest allies in earth". Zhentil Keep lay "just across the sea from the battle". Revan's player said they would need Zebediah "to help convince the Zentarim", and Dave pointed them to their contact in Waterdeep: "You have a strong contact in Jonathan Silverhand" / "He’s a top 3 Zent and he owes you the city of WD to a Certain extent" / "Esp the Panther". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> The Panther is Revan's name among the Masked Lords.

Dave's list of the allied army closed with its reserves: "Zentarim Corsairs? / Obi? / Elistrae?" <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small>

## Stoth Youngglade

Dave: "Stoth is a legendary former leader of Zhentarum and the Youngblade clan still rules it". <small>(Sic on "Youngblade". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small> See [Stoth Youngglade](/people/stoth-youngglade/).

## Dead Man's Hand

Five centuries after the Infanta Crisis, Waterdeep's plots and power plays pair the Company with the Zhentarim: "Trade giant driven underground, joined with black market merchants in Skull port." The Unmasked Lord ap Dyksterhaus counts the Company and the Zhentarim among his allies, and fears that "Company/Zhents double cross him". See [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/). Dave: "And the company and the Zentharhim team up too so that's consistent". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small>
