**Freed children · Crew of the Arielle · Temple Holdings LLC, Arcs III and IV · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Dref's Kids

**Drefan's Kids** are a band of enslaved children freed by [Drefan](/people/drefan/) at [Whitehorn](/places/whitehorn/) in [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) of the Ages of the Infanta, who had lived underground for ten years with [Marcus](/people/marcus/), the Infanta of Death. The party's airship already had a crew and the children did not want to live on a warship, so whether Drefan could keep any of them was rolled. Those who stayed spent the downtime training and making him macaroni art, and by [Arc IV](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/) they ran the ship, [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/). [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) freed a second band of children at Whitehorn.

## Whitehorn

The Gravedigger of Whitehorn's temple kept orphans, and employed them to maintain the gravesites, since they were small enough to use the tiny access tunnels into the deeper parts of the tomb. Before the party arrived he told them to hide in the tomb. <small>(Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part IV.)</small>

The party freed the children in the first Whitehorn session. Dave: "Oh Drefan and Roland both saved bands of enslaved children". Adam, who played Drefan, named them at once: "DREFS KIDS". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>

## Keeping them

Drefan's player declared that "They now maintain our airship, in return for room/board. Rascals, all." Dave answered: "You already had a crew, the kids do not want to live on a warship", and "All respect to dref they might want to follow the goddess of death they’ve lived with underground for 10 years". Drefan used Quendi's Boon on them, which Dave limited to "Only 1 per day", and made his case in character:

> why do you guys wanna live in an orphanage, and follow the god of death? wouldn't you rather have adventures with mythical heroes on an airship?
>
> I've got sweets FYI

<small>— Drefan (Adam). (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>

Jenny, who played Kara, proposed an orphanage in Harloch for "Dref’s Kids (and Roland’s kids)". Dave settled it: "We’ll roll it to see if you can retain any of them" / "In person", adding: "You either die the hero or live long enough to justify the use of child labor". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>

## Downtime

In one downtime, "Drefs kids use the downtime make me macaroni art" (Adam). Asked whether it needed a roll, Dave ruled: "They crit" / "It’s so good". When [Pim](/people/pim/)'s player proposed selling the art as an Elven relic, Drefan refused: "Dref's kids, Dref's rules. Mac art is not for resale." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-02)</small>

Before the assault on the Arcaneum, Drefan's player said the children "have been training up". Revan's player proposed sending "Defs Kids and the Sigil Superior" into the school to learn its portal codes, and Dave summed it up: "Revan wants to use the kids as cannon fodder". Drefan refused: "im not putting drefs kids in harms way" / "they can defend the airship". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-15)</small>

## Roland's children

Roland's group of children at Motherstone went by two names at the table. LaMont, who played Revan, styled himself "a defense against the dark arts professor of the Sigil Superior (Rolands group of children at motherstone)". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-22)</small> Joseph, who played Roland, called them "The Sigil Squad, (the counterparts to Dref’s kids)", and had them picketing outside the prison where Roland was held for trial. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small>

## Crew of the Arielle

By the end of Arc IV Drefan's Kids ran the party's airship. When JL supposed they had spent months aboard making healing potions, Dave corrected him: "Drefs kids run the boat, they’re not a potion factory". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>
