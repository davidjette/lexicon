**Duergar (deep dwarves) - forge-clan of the Korramont - all but extinct**

*Also known as:* Deep Tempura - the Tempura Clan - the lost forge-clan

The Deeptempura Clan was a duergar forge-clan of the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/), now all but extinct, and the only smiths known to work **Shavat**. Under [King Corn](/people/king-corn/) they found the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/), bargained with a devil for the art of forging its infernal ore, and made peace with the black dragon who lay deeper still. The elves of Aerenal came to marvel at the work and stayed to enslave the workers, setting the mine to casting bells; about a decade ago the House of Kaius bought the Korramont outright and ran the works through the [Korranberg Foundry Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/). Three years ago a writ signed KAIUS III ended Corn's planned uprising and killed roughly 97 per cent of the dwarvish workforce. The clan's descendants, the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/), have since come home from the Mror Holds to consecrate the dead and reclaim the hold.

## Names

Deeptempura names are dish-names. [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), [Queen Pu-Pu Platter](/people/queen-pu-pu-platter-deeptempura/), [Sedda Tiddes](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/), [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/), [Scallion Pancake](/people/scallion-pancake/), [Crab Rangoon](/people/crab-rangoon/), [Egg Roll](/people/egg-roll/), [Wonton](/people/wonton/), Fried Tofu, Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat, and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long are all recorded exactly as given. So are [House Moo Goo Gai Pan](/organizations/house-moo-goo-gai-pan/) and the clan's descendants, the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/).

## History

**The ore.** Under King Corn the clan found the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/), a cavern of infernal ore, and bargained with a devil for the art of forging it. The stair reliefs record the pact as a triumph; [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) later put the price at seven pledges, recorded in the **Crown of Seven Stars**.

**The treaty.** A black dragon lay deeper still. Corn made peace with him, and the reliefs show Corn bringing him tribute.

**The craft.** Hammered thin as paper and layered, the ore holds *millions of imperceptible shapes and circuits of golden wire*, and a body built that way will cast vast and devastating spells across distance, like sound. That knowledge is a clan secret; the runesmith [Bofric](/people/bofric/) still reads the shorthand it was written in.

**The enslavement.** The elves of Aerenal came to marvel at the work and stayed to enslave the workers. The mine was set to making a bell, then several, then hundreds. About a decade ago the House of Kaius bought the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) outright and ran the works through the [Korranberg Foundry Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/), whose cabinets still hold archaic property records of the dwarvish slaves it owned. A king and his heir were on the roster.

**The massacre.** Corn and Sedda planned an uprising and secretly forged a horn to blunt the bells. They were discovered. A **writ of warrant** signed **KAIUS III** ordered the death of Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, a Black Dove sister, every dwarf in the foundry, and finally the overseer who carried it. Sedda rang the Thirteenth Casting before they could take her. The Trust's own unofficial tally puts the loss at roughly 97% of the dwarvish workforce, three years ago - the same year Sharn fell. Tens of thousands of kin, across the long occupation, were left in mass graves inside their own mountain.

## Custom

- **The mourning-braid** - white hair wrapped in ribboned Dwarvish prayers *for the souls and the crafts of the Deeptempura people*. Returning one to the clan is how you learn the dead person's name.
- **The hospitality of Rak'tafur** - a formal law of guest-right that can be begged for in Dwarvish, and granted for a fixed and stated term.
- **Speak with dead as a burial rite** - the clan's clearing parties use it on their own fallen before consecration, as much to ask what happened as to say goodbye.
- **Grave-goods** - ceremonial belts, held in living wreaths in the sepulcher gallery, and jewels left on the body. Taking them makes you a grave robber and it is said to your face.
- **Kinship by marriage** - traced and verified aloud. A knight of the Silver Flame talked his way into Rak'tafur by naming Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long in the right order.

## Holdings

The whole interior of the Korramont: the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/), the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), the sepulcher gallery, the Glitterhame, the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/), the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) and the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/).

## Right now

The clan is functionally extinct in the Korramont. Its cousins the Teryaki came home from the Mror Holds to consecrate the dead and reclaim the hold, and are camped in the throne room under a dragon who has announced he intends to enslave them. Its king lies unburied on that dragon's hoard. Its heir has been consecrated. Its secrets are in a lead-bound folio in Sharn, and in the head of the one runesmith who agreed to leave.

> I will not let go of the secrets of the dwarves so that you can replicate her horrors. You robbed from my dead. You do not have the right. - Madame Tso Teryaki
