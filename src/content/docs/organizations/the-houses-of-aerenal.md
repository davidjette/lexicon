**Elvish noble houses · Aerenal and Khorvaire · Remnants of House Vol · Active**

*Also known as:* the elvish houses · the houses of Aerenal

The Houses of Aerenal are the elvish houses of Aerenal that made their way to Khorvaire: **Duin**, **Qualanthri** and **Crona**, all of them striving remnants of [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small> House Vol was eradicated thousands of years ago, and by the testimony of the dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) the elvish houses that fled after its purge remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline. Their members include [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), Star Prelate of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/); Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and the priest [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/); and [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), head of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) and Eternal Guardian to three Emperors. Dave sees these houses "behind it all", the making of the Crimson Sun and the Kaius dynasty.

## House Vol and the flight

House Vol was an aberrant elvish house, eradicated by humans, giants and "good" elves thousands of years ago for crossing the elvish and draconic lines. Its leader was [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/), the Queen of Death.

Freed beneath the Korramont in [Korth Ep 11](/sessions/korth-ep-11-the-domain-of-valtrex/), Valtrex testified that the elvish houses that fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline.

Dave:

> "I think the elves have had these designs since they first came to Eberron, and they're tried it multiple times and we're defeated last time with the house of vol, Caius is the new house of vol and he and the Elves have made an empire, but the elves were ready to betray him, because they've been at it for a long time and have plans measured in centuries. tale as old as time"

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-13)</small>

> "And more and more I see these elvish houses behind it all, the houses of Aerenal that for one reason or another made their way to Khorvaire to dominate here"

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

He named them "Duin, Qualanthri, Crona", and called all of them striving remnants of House Vol. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## House Duin

[Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) is a High Elf, the Star Prelate and leader of the Black Doves, and consort to the Emperor. She claims direct descent from House Vol and bears the Dragonmark of Death across her pelvis and stomach. In the throne room at Korth she told Uriel: <small>([Episode 9](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/))</small>

> "I trained you well Uriel, perhaps too well. I do find it….. cute, that you believe your mark means anything, or perhaps Vol blood runs through your veins as it does mine, but don't let my forgiveness fool you Uriel, don't play your silly games with me."

Uriel later took the Black Doves from her. Dave: "the Black Doves which were a truly holy order when she took them from Duin, are now a vehicle for the Blood of Vol and Emerald Claw". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## House Qualanthri

[Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) is a high elf war cleric of Aerenal. Her parents are elders in Aerenal, and her longsword belonged to her grandmother, who lives among [the Undying Court](/organizations/the-undying-court/). She came to Eberron against her parents' wishes. She bears the Mark of Death, which marks her as a descendant of House Vol, and she is now Holy Empress.

[E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) is the priest who performed the Eternal Guardian ritual with Varyn Crona at the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/) beneath Sharn, drawing on forbidden Vol rites. At the Forge, Varyn's echo named him in its last words, in Common after Aereni Elvish: "E’noch… Qualanthri… my brother…" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## House Crona

[Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) was a High Elf paladin of Aerenal, head of the Crimson Sun, a member of the Imperial Council, and the Eternal Guardian, the sworn protector of Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III. His apprentice [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) had been his house slave. His echo's last words at the Forge were:

> "…we’ll bleed the sun, eternal night. / Our bloodline shall reign…As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine"

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## The Kaius dynasty

Dave, on the making of the Empire:

> "What's clear is Kaius was clearly a key leader of this effort, and he had a whole host of evlish houses working to make him emperor - I think the big question was did Kaius KNOW and DIRECT the Council to murder Crona and turn on the plan? Or did the Council do that on their own?"

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

The same day he said that "the architects of the Crimson Sun and the Kaius dynasty obviously used every dark art they could find, including goblin smithing, dwarvish and infernal smithing, draconic blood magic, necromancy, artificing, elemental magic, red khyber, black khyber, everything", and raised the possibility that "they could have been responsible for the Last War and the collapse of Galifar itself". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

The next day he added: "I feel like the Council turned on Varyn Crona and the long term plan, but the unforeseen came and set it back in motion, plus much more", and "Uriel and Esther are the bastard children of the original architects of it all". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-23)</small>

## In Khorvaire

The elves of Aerenal came to the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) to marvel at the dwarves' Shavat work and stayed to enslave the workers, setting the mine to casting bells.

Elves from Aerenal are divided over Uriel's takeover of Eberron; by Dave's account it depends on whether they are elven supremacists. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

[Korth](/places/korth/) under the Empress is "racially segragated, with more and more of the city and kingdom reserved for purely elven use, citing cultural differences and needs for the Empress's peoples". <small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-06)</small>
