**House Gallidann** is the elven noble house bound to the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) and to the wilds of the Moonshae. It was founded by **Eldamir**, who came north out of the Feywild around the Time of Troubles; it received the crystal city from the goddess and founded [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), raised the first Infanta **Elistrada**, and swore to protect the Infanta forever. Its members bear the sentient **moonblades**. Eldamir and Calix fought beside [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/) and Elistrada in the war that obliviated the Netherese, which followed the founding of the city. After the Forgetting the house blamed the dwarves, made war on the remaining dwarven armies and carried out a genocide against them; Eldamir then discovered the plot of her brother **Calix**, and the two were cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other. The house is led today by **Ilana**.

## THE HOUSE'S OWN ACCOUNT

The house's own account begins with **Eldamir**, who "came with a wave of elves from the Feywild into the north around the time of Troubles." It raised the first Infanta **Elistrada** through the guardian **Star Quendi**, and through her son **Renos**, who defended the Gildergreen against Calix and founded the city of Mirellyn, held [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/) — anciently Wain Gallidan. Quendi's grandson, the *baelnorn* **Daenan**, an elf who volunteered to be made undead by an act of Elistraee herself in order to stay and fight, watches over the Gildergreen in spirit. Its members bear the sentient **moonblades**. Its oath is simple and permanent: **to protect the Infanta forever.**

When the goddess granted the elves the crystal city, it was this house that received it and founded [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) with her and with [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/). The founding came before the war with the Netherese: Eldamir and Calix rode in that war beside Stone and Elistrada, who obliviated the Netherese and returned to the city with the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) before inviting the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) of the world to live there in peace. Its great enemy, in this telling, was Eldamir's own brother **Calix**, who turned *baelnorn*, allied with **Queen Maab** against his kin, and betrayed everything.

## SILVERBANE'S RECOVERED MEMORY

The moonblade remembers across the Oblivia. What it remembers of the house's founding is that Eldamir and Calix stood "moments away from drowning their entire landing camp in the Sea of Swords" before the goddess stayed their hand and gave them the Motherstone.

Of what the house did after the Forgetting, when the city was gone and nobody could remember why, the blade says:

> …blamed the dwarves and pledged to ride them from the Moonshae… led a war against the remaining dwarven armies… (and then) a genocide, capturing entire populations and leading them still alive into their tombs.

Only afterwards did Eldamir discover Calix's plot, and slay him, and die by his hand in the same stroke. The blade served throughout, and knows it.

The cause of the madness was the obliviation of Motherstone. When [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, threw himself into the Obliviator, everything about Motherstone became cursed and obliviated knowledge, and it drove Calix and the Gallidan elves insane: they slew the dwarves, and Calix and Eldamir were cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other on the beach. Calix, immortal, survived as a lich, mad, no longer knowing how to find his phylactery.

## THE PHYSICAL EVIDENCE

In the dungeon floor beneath Motherstone sits an iron **blood seal** marked with Calix's symbol, made with supreme malice; the symbol glows when [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) is brought near it, and the blade must take the blood of a dwarf to open it. It is the lock on the way into the deepest chamber of the city.

## THE DRAGON'S HATCHLINGS

A further charge against the house comes from outside the family entirely. The Copper Dragon **Tylerjynex** conceived seven eggs to be the Infanta's honour guard. Calix beguiled and banished the dragon; the hatchlings were slain in their sleep — and, per the elder priest who survived them, by the agents of House Gallidann, who "could not stand to see anyone but elves at the side of the Infanta." Two eggs that could not be breached were discarded into a seam in the rock and hatched anyway.

## PRESENT DISPOSITION

The house is led by **Ilana**, radicalised by the Forsworn and Calix yet still the Infanta's closest ally after Untari, who has led House Gallidan since Drefan disappeared; it is strained between its sworn oath to protect the Infanta forever and an alliance with the Forsworn — and with Calix — that cannot quickly be set aside.
