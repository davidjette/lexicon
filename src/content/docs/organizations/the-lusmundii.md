The **Lusmundii** — the name means **Light of the World** — are the druidic cult formed around the birth of the reborn [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/). They are elves and humans together, which on [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/) is itself a political act. They are led by the elven chief **Untari**, they were charged in the barrow at Bask Falls with protecting the Infanta and bringing her to the **Gildergreen**, and the community they became bought an old temple in the walled town of Harloch under the name **Temple Holdings, LLC**.

## The programme

They "believe the Infanta will turn the tide of history against all forms of civilization and allow nature to reclaim Faerûn," and seek "to end the three-way conflict between the Sylvan Elves, the human settlers, and the renegade Forsworn by uniting them all behind a fanatical devotion to nature."

## The charge

Their charge was given by the echo of the guardian **Star Quendi** in the barrow at Bask Falls: protect the Infanta and keep her from falling into the wrong hands, and bring her to the Gildergreen, the sacred tree at the heart of Snowdown, where the spirit of Quendi's grandson Daenan can watch over her and her cult "until Elistrae comes of age."

The [Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/), the kingdoms, the churches and [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/) all sought to use a god-child; the Lusmundii's charge was to let her come of age. The journal of an explorer who read further than she should have records the nature of the Infantas: they were never eternal children. "She was a human girl who lived and died at a ripe old age."

## Membership

Led by the elven chief Untari. Its members appear all through the chronicle: the rogue **Raylin**, who tried to free the infant aboard the slaver *Evening Star*; the priestesses who chant the sacred charm of making with Untari beneath the protective mythal of the Gildergreen; and the community that becomes the refuge bought and named Temple Holdings, LLC — a cult that acquires an old temple in the walled town of Harloch and, in doing so, gives its name to the whole chronicle.

Untari also serves as the assessor of claimed Infantas. When a prince of Cormyr arrives at the Deepgate with a girl three days dead and newly resurrected, it is Untari who judges her not an Infanta and keeps her close to be educated anyway.

## Seat

**The Gildergreen:** the sacred tree; the cult's heart, and the child's nursery.\
**Temple Holdings, Harloch:** the bought Old Temple and the community around it.

## The Tomb of St. Magnus

Five hundred years later, in the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), [the Tomb of St. Magnus](/places/the-tomb-of-st-magnus/), reached by a hidden entrance on a Moonshae isle, is protected by the Order of St. Magnus, the Harmonium Seraphim, by traps and magic, and by the Lusmundii, "Priests of Motherstone". Treasures believed to be hidden in the tomb include a helm and armor, and perhaps a [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). <small>(DM notes)</small>
