**Militant order · Khorvaire · Allied with the Blood of Vol · Active**

*Also known as:* the Order of the Emerald Claw

The Order of the Emerald Claw is a militant order of Karrnath that serves the Blood of Vol and [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/). <small>(published Eberron canon)</small> Under Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) have become a vehicle for the Emerald Claw and the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/). Nico and Dave both liken [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) to the order.

## The Black Doves

Dave, on the Black Doves after Uriel took them from [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/):

> "So the Black Doves which were a truly holy order when she took them from Duin, are now a vehicle for the Blood of Vol and Emerald Claw, she has completely corrupted them"

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

He added: "So she can’t purge them all". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## The Crimson Sun

Nico: "The Crimson Sun is basically The Order of the Emerald Claw". Dave: "Exactly". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-21)</small>

The next day, after calling the elvish houses Duin, Qualanthri and Crona all striving remnants of [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/), Dave wrote: "Emerald Claw > Crimson Sun". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small> See [the Houses of Aerenal](/organizations/the-houses-of-aerenal/).

## The dragon

In this campaign the Emerald Claw is the Crimson Sun: the dragon [Viagra](/people/viagra/), who with Minara Vol fathered Erandis Vol, and whose essence was put into the infant who became [Emperor Kaius](/people/emperor-caius-iii/). The goal of the conspiracy of the elvish houses was never only to raise the dragon. It was to mate it with the House of Vol again. In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) Aurorix told the party that Vol was an abomination created by Viagra.
