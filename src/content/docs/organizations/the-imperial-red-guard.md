**Type:** Military, Honour Guard, Undead · **Seat:** Korth and the Imperial court · **Status:** Active

*Also known as:* the Red Guard · the Imperial Guard in red robes · the masked Empire guards

The **Imperial Red Guard** are the undead honour troops of the Empire: silent, eyeless soldiers in red robes who garrison the Imperial court at [Korth](/places/korth/) alongside the army proper, stand at attention all night, and watch the jail of [the Ministry of Law](/organizations/the-ministry-of-law/). They are made at the **Hall of Submission**, where the very old go willingly to begin their "second service to the Emperor" and where the dead are brought to be transformed. At the Summit at [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/) the masked guards present were determined to be undead, and on [Empress Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s instruction they turned on [Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) in his own hall.

## THE OCCUPATION OF KORTH, AS THE INEVITABLES SAW IT

The army proper garrisoning the Imperial capital is paladins, knights and stormtroopers "of various castes." Alongside them stand the **Imperial Guard in red robes**: silent, eyeless, undead honour troops, the same ones the party fought at Thronehold. **They stand at attention all night.** Columns of soldiers, living and undead, move between the barracks in the west and the rest of the city through the hours of darkness, and the Empire's last airships and its death troopers ring the walls from outside.

The Korth record's own line on them is the only description anybody has managed: *whatever is inside the red does not sleep.*

## THRONEHOLD, AS BROADCAST ACROSS KHORVAIRE

Korth Ep 1. Nearly every major faction sent representatives to the Summit called by [Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/). During the afternoon's intelligence-gathering, [Lorian](/people/lorian/) determined that **the masked Empire guards present at the Summit were all undead**.

Korth Ep 2. When Vex broadcast *"Kill Chancellor Eric for the Empire"* through the ArcEye cameras carrying the Summit across the continent, the masked guards surged forward — and their movements were unnatural. **They were undead soldiers loyal not to Vex, but to Empress [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/).** The Black Dove sisters fled immediately as the undead turned on Vex and anyone in their path, confirming Uriel's trap and leaving the Supreme Admiral of the Imperial fleet furious and screaming of her betrayal in front of every delegation in Khorvaire.

The Empire's honour guard does not answer to the Empire's highest-ranking officer. It answers to the Empress, and it will kill an admiral in his own hall on her instruction.

## THE JAIL AT THE MINISTRY OF LAW

Korth Ep 13. In the northeast corner of [the Ministry of Law](/organizations/the-ministry-of-law/), two levels of cells hold prisoners in varying states of distress and undress, **watched by Imperial Red Guard**. **Bantide Moro** was chained to a wall on the upper level under their supervision: starved, dehydrated, and still lucid enough to argue.

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) tested them there. Standing at the cell bars in front of two of them, he said aloud that he was the Chancellor of Sharn and that the Emperor was not a man but a dragon — high treason and blasphemy in the same breath, at conversational volume. **He got no reaction at all.** Then, an hour later, Bantide cast *feign death* under those same eyes, was carried out as a corpse and dropped down the building's corpse chute into the stacks. <small>The Red Guard neither reported the treason nor noticed the escape.</small>

## THE HALL OF SUBMISSION — WHERE THEY COME FROM

South of the Great Plaza, open to the public, and busy. This is where the very old go **willingly** to cross over and begin their **"second service to the Emperor,"** and where the dead are brought to be transformed. Officers wait outside with troop transports to collect the converts. It is a good place to talk, because almost no one in earshot is alive.

The rest of the supply chain is the justice system. The High Court's balcony holds an **Iron Maiden** — a device that kills the condemned while preserving the body intact for its second service. Behind the jail, a stair descends to the dumpster yard, where a corpse chute empties the dead into stacks for reuse. Karrnathi law scales its penalties by race and defines execution methods for monster races specifically so that they can be executed at all. **This is a society that effectively mandates that you give your corpse to the state.**

## WHY KARRNATH HAS THEM AT ALL

Korth was and outwardly remains a human city with big human institutions, but High Elven influence has thickened steadily since the Emperor's birth and is now heavy. The elves do not rule openly; they shape. And the reason Karrnath has the military and undead system it does is that **it was learned from the elves of the Undying Court, who brought official necromancy with them**.

<small>Which places the origin of the Red Guard in the same room as [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) of Aerenal, the Sacred Rite performed over the newborn Kaius by the priest E'noch Qualanthri, and the Empress who is herself an Aerenai cleric of the Grave. The Empire's dead soldiers, its Eternal Guardian and its Empress all come from the same island.</small>

## RELATED FORMATIONS

**The Lord Commander's Honor Guard.** [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s four masked, fully armoured bodyguards — identities, ranks and origins completely unknown for a decade — were revealed on her balcony as undead members of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/). Same principle, applied to a murdered government rather than to volunteers.

**The E.D.E.N. units.** Undead and hybrid constructs raised under the Empress's command, fought by the Silver Flame and the Hundreds across Khorvaire, built on a body-supply pipeline fed by Cogborn disappearances. In the Mournlands every fallen paladin rose moments later to join them.

**Death troopers.** Ringing the walls of Korth from outside, alongside the Empire's last airships.

## ASSESSMENT

The Empire mandates the surrender of your corpse, executes you with a machine designed to keep the body usable, and staffs its own throne room with the silent product of both, while telling the very old that it is an honour and getting volunteers. The Red Guard are not a secret. They stand in the open, all night, in red, and the population walks past them to collect rations. What, if anything, is still inside the red is not recorded.

**Known deployments:** the Imperial court and the Great Plaza, Korth · the jail of the Ministry of Law · the Summit at Thronehold, where they were Uriel's, not Vex's.

**Command:** nominally Imperial. Demonstrably [Empress Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/).
