**Type:** Military, Secret Police · **Seat:** the Veil Building, Upper City, Sharn · **Status:** Dissolved; remnant active

*Also known as:* the Veil · the Iron Veil, Sharn Division · *formerly* the Sharn City Watch

The Iron Veil was the Empire's militarised police force in Sharn, seated in the Veil Building in the Upper City. When [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) assumed command of the Empire's military the Sharn City Watch was dissolved and absorbed into it, and its soldiers were bound to her by a pact that gave each of them a longsword pact weapon and warlock spellcasting. It held operational control of the Arcane Eye surveillance network, garrisoned the Cogs after the Upper City riot, and fielded the Empire's western ground forces out of Fort Crona. Dying on her own balcony at the end of the Sharn rising, Esther Crona voided the pact by broadcast, and every Iron Veil soldier in the city lost their powers in the same instant; most surrendered or fled, and a small number vanished to form a hidden remnant.

## CIVIC ORIENTATION — SHARN, PUBLIC ISSUE

When Esther Crona assumed command of the Empire's military, the Sharn City Watch was dissolved and absorbed into her elite force: **The Iron Veil**. What was once a patchwork of local law enforcement became a unified, militarized presence; disciplined, brutal, and loyal only to Esther. Their black armor and crimson insignia now dominate the city's skyline and streets. Patrols move in tight formations, propaganda screens echo their creed, and citizens know better than to run or resist. **The Iron Veil made a pact; they don't just enforce law, they enforce loyalty.**

Their headquarters is a towering monolith in the Upper City, a brutalist arcane-tech structure housing government offices, Arcane Eye Security, and a high-security prison for political dissidents and rebel sympathizers. At its summit lies Esther Crona's penthouse, a fortress in the clouds where she and her family reside when in Sharn. From this perch, she watches the city like a hawk, whispering orders and deciding who lives, who dies, and who disappears.

### The soldier, as advertised

Iron Veil soldiers stationed in Sharn represent the elite enforcement arm of Esther Crona's command; disciplined, heavily trained, and unmistakable in their presence. Clad in dark, angular armor marked with the crimson Iron Veil insignia, they operate as a hybrid of military police and covert security, maintaining order in key districts and safeguarding Veil-controlled infrastructure such as the Skyports and transit hubs. Their ranks in Sharn are known for precision, restraint, and an almost ritualistic loyalty, making them both respected and quietly feared throughout the city's towering streets. They are often seen patrolling the city with **Iron Veil Patrol Beast**.

## THE ARCANE EYE SECURITY NETWORK

**Implementation:** roughly ten years ago. **Operational control:** the Iron Veil, Sharn Division. **Head of Security:** [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/).

The Arcane Eye Security Network, commonly referred to as **ArcEyes**, was implemented nearly a decade ago as part of the Empire's expanded internal surveillance initiative. ArcEyes are cast, maintained, and remotely operated by government employees stationed within the Veil Building. These operators are low-level wizards trained specifically in Arcane Eye protocols; their spellcasting ability is limited, but their specialization allows for extended-duration scrying, coordinated patrol routing, and multi-eye monitoring. Each ArcEye is capable of archiving visual and auditory data. Patrol footage is recorded using an [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) and transferred to the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), where it is cataloged and stored in restricted archives accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorized Imperial officials.

In addition to surveillance duties, ArcEyes are integrated into the city's public information network. They can transmit images, alerts, and state-approved messages to display screens throughout Sharn, allowing the Empire to broadcast news, directives, and emergency notices in real time. Full file: [The Arcane Eye Network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/).

## INTERROGATION — BRICE PETERS, PRIVATE, TAKEN ALIVE

Recovered when Iron Veil soldiers broke into [Blair's](/people/blair/) Underhive, killed three drones and lost seven of their own. Peters was the survivor: a terrified low-ranking soldier who knew little beyond his orders. He confirmed only that the Iron Veil had been sweeping the Lower City aggressively while the Lord Commander was present, following directives far above his station.

He also spoke of **the pact** — and of how many Sharn Iron Veil were old-time Sharn City Watch guards before they were turned to Iron Veil.

## DEFECTOR'S ACCOUNT — IZAAK, FORMERLY OF THE SHARN WATCH

Once a ranger patrolling the farmlands between Sharn and Zilspar in Breeland, Izaak witnessed firsthand the shifting tides of Imperial control *before* the Watch was absorbed into the Iron Veil. He turned away from the Empire and joined the rebels in the Breeland farmsteads nearly a decade ago, embedding himself within **Fulcrum**. For over nine years he has served as the liaison between his half-sister [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and the Fulcrum rebels in Zilspar.

## R.U.S.T. CASUALTY LEDGER — THE COGS

A peaceful protest in the Upper City erupted into a deadly riot when an explosion tore through the crowd. The Iron Veil's retaliation was swift and merciless. R.U.S.T. lost twenty-one members; the Cogborn community lost forty-three civilians — workers, elders, parents and children. Esther Crona's public executions that followed sent shockwaves through the Cogs, and the Iron Veil used the chaos as justification to flood the district with soldiers. Raids, searches and forced removals intensified overnight.

The Veil seized the factories, replaced Cogborn labour with trained soldiers and tightened its grip on the undercity until the Cogborn were trapped beneath their own city with their exits sealed. <small>Evacuating them through the Underhive is what put the Inevitables in the same room as Blair, and eventually in the same room as Esther.</small>

## THE WESTERN COMMAND

The Iron Veil is not only Sharn. [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) commands its ground forces on the western front, bolstered by Iron Veil soldiers and high-level casters stationed out of **Fort Crona** and along **Crona's Wall**, where they maintain the mythal-like force wall that holds and pushes back the **Uruk-Hoth Republic**. <small>Crona's Wall has since collapsed, allowing Orc forces to push into Dark Druid territory in the Eldeen Reaches.</small> The airships that carry them were designed and built by [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) for the Veil directly. Crownsgate Lightning Rail Terminal outside Sharn's gates is patrolled by Imperial Iron Veil soldiers.

## THE NIGHT THE PACT BROKE

Sharn Ep 18. Bleeding out on her own balcony with the allied fleets filling the sky above the city, Esther Crona spent her last strength on a broadcast rather than a spell.

> "Your pact is void."

Every Iron Veil soldier in the city felt their pact collapse. Longsword pact weapons vanished. Warlock spells evaporated. Their connection to Esther was severed in a single sentence, citywide, simultaneously. Then Dario broadcast her corpse on the same screens and declared that the Empire would fall.

Iron Veil soldiers, suddenly powerless and terrified, surrendered in droves. Others fled. **A small number vanished into the shadows, forming the first seeds of a hidden Iron Veil remnant.** In the days that followed, Locke Pierce's Hundreds and R.U.S.T. rounded up what was left; the high-ranking officials were tried, and the guilty executed. Before that night, Iron Veil presence in the Lower City had already returned to pre-Esther levels: the factories and The Cogs still Veil-run and patrolled, but patrols present, predictable and spread thin across the district.

## WHAT REMAINS

The remnant has no patron and hides in a city that hunts it. The Empire's forces elsewhere never made the bargain, and remain in the field.

**Known leadership:** [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), Lord Commander and operational head (dead) · [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), ground forces, western front · [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/), ArcEye Security, Sharn division (executed).

**Holdings:** The Veil Building, Upper City Sharn — government offices, Arcane Eye Security, a high-security political prison, and the Lord Commander's penthouse at its summit. The GoldSky Precinct Station. Fort Crona and Crona's Wall. The Skyports and transit hubs. The Crownsgate Lightning Rail Terminal.

**Assets:** The Arcane Eye Security Network and its Crystallizer archive in the Sharn Library. Veil Hounds. The airship **Black Veil**, thrown by its own commander into a statue of the Empress. Airframes built by House Lyrandar.

**The Pact:** every soldier received a longsword pact weapon and warlock spellcasting, bound to Esther Crona personally, and it made "an almost ritualistic loyalty" a literal description.
