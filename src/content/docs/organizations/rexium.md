**Sales guild · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Leader [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/) · Active**

**Also known as:** the Rexium cult

Rexium is a sales organization and self-betterment group, a guild founded and led by the warlock [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/), which sells the pyramid-shaped Charm of the All Seeing and, with it, the business of selling the charm. Its members wear the charms they sell, and through them Rex can charm and command a room. In [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) the seller Megan Moron recruited [Maverick](/people/maverick/) at [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/), and he and the party attended a Rexium sales meeting in the inn's converted barn. There they attacked Rex, who turned the charm-wearers on [Heyu](/people/heyu/); Megan and some of the followers were killed that night, in [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/). Maverick afterwards persuaded [Sir Merriam Wordsworker](/people/sir-merriam-wordsworker/) to investigate Rex and his pyramid scheme.

## The Charm of the All Seeing

The Charm of the All Seeing is a pyramid-shaped trinket on a chain with a sinister eye in it. <small>(DM notes)</small> Megan Moron wore hers as a pendant, a pyramid with an eye at its centre; the audience at the Grub + Grog wore golden metal charms, and the charm Maverick took from Megan's neck was silver. It is also called the Amulet of the All-Seeing. <small>(Session notes)</small>

Megan said she lived "in the steady flow of psychic energy that the charms provide." At the Grub + Grog Rex quickly cast a charm over the whole audience through the charms they wore, and when the party attacked he dominated Maverick and all the charm-wearers at once, commoners mostly, and sent them against Heyu. Afterwards the crowd seemed to have no memory of being charmed or of the violence. <small>(Session notes)</small>

Rex is a warlock in debt to a Mummy Lord. While Rex lives and holds the **THRONE** card the Mummy Lord is in torpor; if not, it wakes "and gains direct control of all wearing the Charms". <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Buy 7, Wear 1, Sell 6

Rexium does not really sell the charm. It sells the business of selling it. <small>(DM notes)</small> The scheme is laid out as **BUY 7 · WEAR 1 · SELL 6**, each of the six sold becoming a seller of seven more, branching down as a tree of sevens. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Megan put it to Maverick another way: his own charm was actually free, and he would agree to sell seven more, keep all the profit, and let Megan take a small cut. That way he would become rich through the power of the downline. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

Paying 100 gold and wearing a charm earns an invitation to a sales meeting. The meetings are for sellers only, and a recruit can meet Rex once sold. <small>(DM notes)</small> At the Grub + Grog, Maverick's selling in the bar gained Megan three more followers and qualified both of them to attend.

## Megan Moron's pitch

Megan Moron, a commoner and a Rexium seller, pitched the charm with her own story:

> "I was just like you. I was a nearly divorced mother of six looking for **something** that could pay the bills, but more than that, give me **some** sense that my life mattered to **someone** out there, you know?"
>
> "That all changed, not **because** of my Charm of the All Seeing, but to me, it represents a change that I gave to myself. And its paid off seven-fold...
>
> "Since I started selling for Rexium, I have been able to pay off my horse, bring my kids to the Chultan blood pits for spring break, and I live in the steady flow of psychic energy that the charms provide. It's completely changed my life.
>
> Join us. You'll see how easy life can be."

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Rexium Protocols

Rex Bedsheets is the inventor of the Rexium Protocols. His introduction at a meeting runs:

> "It's now my distinct pleasure to introduce the man who needs no introduction. He's the inventor of the Rexium Protocols, the founder of the feast, and he literally saved my life...."

<small>(DM notes)</small> The Seller's Rally ends on the line **TRUST THE PROTOCOLS**.

## The Seller's Rally

Rex holds packed sales hype meetings "to juice followers and collect energy + gold." <small>(DM notes)</small> A Seller's Rally is an awkward inn setup with bad food and nervous peasants milling, and a District Captain works the room:

> "Alright - alright - no, **keep clapping**, because that sound? That's **momentum**. … And once that arcane mo-mo gets started **no one** can stop it."
>
> "I look around this room, I see more than faces. I see **commitment**."
>
> "You're not here because you need gold. You're here because you understood what most people don't. They **can't**."
>
> "Wealth doesn't come from holding on, hoarding like a dragon. It comes from circulation, commerce, sovereignty; like a **King**"
>
> Every charm you sell is a position, an asset you own forever, **PASSIVE INCOME**
>
> I know some of you are ready to move up tonight. **Where are my Foundation Neophytes?**
>
> TRUST THE PROTOCOLS

<small>(DM notes)</small>

Rexium's pamphlet shows Rex Bedsheets seated on a throne, and in his pocket is the THRONE card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). <small>(Session narrative; DM notes.)</small>

## The sales meeting at the Grub + Grog

Megan Moron approached Maverick in the bar of the Grub + Grog and sold him on Rexium. Maverick agreed, knowing it for a pyramid scheme, because he suspected there was money to be made or appropriated <small>(session narrative)</small>, and he talked many in the bar into buying from her. She gave him the pamphlet with the THRONE card in it.

The meeting was held in the inn's converted barn event room. It was sparsely attended, about ten people in all. On a small stage a man the party recognised as the footman who had unloaded boxes from Rex's carriage gave a sales pitch that was "obviously hokum, but the people eat it up" <small>(session narrative)</small>, and introduced Rex Bedsheets. Rex came out, cast his charm over the audience through their charms, and took a small chest of gold.

The party attacked. The dominated charm-wearers fell on Heyu, and Maverick, dominated with them, stabbed her before he broke free. [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) ran in and began shooting the dominated peasants, and nearby guards ran to arrest her. Maverick took the silver charm from the neck of the dead Megan Moron, thinking it might be a way into Rex's lair. Rex fled in his carriage, and the fight ended in the street with the party's arrest by [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/).

## After the incident

At [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), Maverick convinced Sir Merriam Wordsworker of his innocence and persuaded him to investigate Rex Bedsheets and his pyramid scheme. <small>(Session notes; DM notes.)</small>

**Members:** Rex Bedsheets, founder and leader; Megan Moron, seller, killed at the Grub + Grog; Rex's footman; the charm-wearers of Waterdeep, commoners mostly.
