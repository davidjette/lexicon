> The war was declared with much pomp more than four months ago, and the people of Sembia and the Dales girded their loins, only to be left waiting.

<small>— the Purple Dragon Knights Ally Card.</small>

**Human paladins · Cormyr · Allies at the Egg**

The **Purple Dragon Knights** are the paladins of Cormyr and allies of the party at the Egg. Their card records a war that was declared with much pomp and then did not happen for four months. Cormyr is where the party was — Zanzibar's arc, and the attempt to separate the Arcaneum's wizards from Archmage Valen — while Carlysle's heart was digging its way toward the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/).

Cormyr's alignment is uneasy: "Cormyr is a close ally of the Empire, though independent," and Zanzibar "must decide his loyalties." That Cormyr and the Purple Dragons, "their former enemies," secured the elves' Infanta "is a shock and they are still processing." <small>(Arc III, Interlude, and Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>
