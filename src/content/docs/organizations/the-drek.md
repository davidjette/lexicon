The Drek are the people of the depths of Motherstone, the carvers of the tunnels and pits beneath the ancient crystal mine, who live in the **Drembles**, a vast slum lining the mine wall. Two accounts of them survive, and they are exact mirror images of each other: the [Mundi](/organizations/the-mundi/) hold that the ancestors of the Drek betrayed the city by inviting the spider god **Ayror** into it, and the Drek hold that the Mundi turned on them and slaughtered them by the thousands, and that Ayror cursed them all afterwards. They attacked the party on its arrival at Motherstone, and later spoke with it.

## THE MUNDI'S ACCOUNT

King **Chad** of the [Mundi](/organizations/the-mundi/), from his oaken throne in the Trunks, is unambiguous:

> Fowl creatures lurk below. The loathsome Drek, who carved the tunnels and pits into this rock and tried to steal these crystals from the Earth herself still poison the deep holes and stinking troughs of the old city. They toil for the love of their wretched spider god Ayror who controls these infernal machines from deep below these ruins.

In this telling, a child god granted the city to the Mundi and "she was betrayed by the ancestors of the Drek who invited Ayror into the city." Ayror banished her and sealed Motherstone from the world forever. The Mundi stayed to guard the stones. Chad's own title is *watcher of the stones and the destroyer of Drek*.

## THE DREK'S ACCOUNT

The Drek reverse every term. In their legend, **it was the Mundi who turned on the Drek and slaughtered them by the thousands**; and **Ayror**, "who was once a great god of light and knowledge," cursed them all afterwards. Same betrayal, same curse, opposite direction.

## THE DREMBLES

The Drek carved the tunnels and pits — both sides agree on that. They live in the **Drembles**, a vast slum lining the wall of the ancient crystal mine, descending hundreds of feet to a pool of rising, fetid water. They are starving and dying, "trapped between the rising waters and the threats outside": the flood swallowing the city from below, the awakened machines hunting them from the deep, and the Mundi above who hate them. Their scouts raid the upper city for the protective diadem. A Drek priest kept the temple of the copper dragon Tylerjynex in the depths.

The Drek attacked the party when it first reached Motherstone, because the lower city had flooded as a result of steps the party took in the huge drain pipes they used to enter the hidden city. The party met Chad and repelled the attack. The Drek captured a living Mundi, and the party explored the city to try to rescue her. They also went down and spoke with the Drek, who told them of shuffling corpses attacking them.

## AYROR

<small>The god both peoples name is not a god. The [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) — the mind of the city — announces its own broken state on a terminal as **AY-ROR (error)**. What the Drek worship, what the Mundi blame them for inviting, and what the machines obey are all the same thing: a system fault, wearing the flesh of the slain and reaching for something it can no longer name. Its avatar in the depths is a massive horned devil made from the stitched-together remains of thousands of dead humanoids, with a smooth patch where its face should be; the machines of the dungeon wear the skins of living creatures to try to commune with it, and the being that calls itself Marv, High Daegon of Ayror, is one of them.</small>

<small>When [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, threw himself into the Obliviator, the Lexicon hit a fatal error — Ayror — and attacked everything while trying to learn the identity of its master, and of itself. The robotic spider that attacked the party wearing a dead dwarf's skin was under Ayror's command.</small>

Neither people can name the betrayal they accuse each other of, because it was committed with the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), which removes history from every mind that ever held it.
