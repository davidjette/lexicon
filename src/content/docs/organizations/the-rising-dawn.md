**Order · Oerth · Foes of the sahuagin and the Scarlet Brotherhood**

*Also known as:* the Dawn · RD · the Dawn-boys

The **Rising Dawn** is an order in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), to which [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/), JL's kobold character in the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/), belonged. Its two main foes were the sahuagin and [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/), both crippled by the party around [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/). Had the party taken [Malenti](/people/malenti/)'s offer in July 2022, they would have been at open war with the Dawn. JL's kobold one-shot of July 2022 was played by Rising Dawn recruits. After [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/) the Dawn expected to take the three weapons Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/) into storage.

## Scrunt and the Dawn

In February 2022 Dave suggested that "Skrunt should get in with Kezzie and join the Dawn-boys on his own". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-05)</small>

## Malenti's offer

The party refused Malenti's offer in the Saltmarsh finale of July 2022. Dave, afterwards: "a real RPG moment, because yes we would have been in open war w the Rising Dawn". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

## The kobold one-shot

In July 2022 JL ran an improvised kobold dungeon with level-7 kobold characters: Dave's Bullseye, Nico's Charlee and JL's Scrunt. Dave asked, "Actually are we all part of the Rising Dawn?", and Nico answered, "I think we are recruits to the RD". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-12, 2022-07-13)</small> JL planned the dungeon as the Dawn's way of weeding out weak recruits. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-13)</small>

## The weapons of White Plume Mountain

With Wave, Whelm and Blackrazor in the party's hands, Dave wrote: "The king will know we have them, he’s allied with the Rising Dawn somehow right"; Nico: "Yeah I think so". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

> I guess for your sake Scrunt the Dawn excepts to take all three and put them in storage now that we crippled their two main foes for them the soggies and the brotherhood

<small>— Dave, 2022-08-14. Sic on "excepts". "The soggies" are the sahuagin.</small>

Dave continued: "They begrudgingly acknowledge Weathervein as owning Wave but don’t fully trust her not to use it", and "Probably they expected us all to die and Scrunt to run out with just the weapons". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> Five days later: "And the Dawn/Scrunt want v different outcomes than the swords, it’s gunna be a whole thing I guarantee it." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-19)</small>

## The neck tattoo

Scrunt bears a Rising Dawn tattoo on his neck. Nico, in play: "btw Scrunt Rising Dawn Neck Tattoo = +2 to Dmg to any Scarlet Brotherhood Assassin." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-07)</small>
