**Duergar (deep dwarves) - the mourning-party of the Deeptempura - fourteen bedrolls in the mountain**

The Teryaki clan is a duergar family of the Mror Holds, descended from the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/). When word reached the Holds of what had happened under the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) they came south to consecrate the dead and take the hold back, and have camped inside the mountain since, opening the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) a room at a time. Fourteen remain. Warned that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) is free and has claimed the mountain, they refused to leave.

## The errand

They announced it before they went. Descendants of the Tempura clan visited **Chief Yellowmeat** and stated plainly that they were returning to their ancestral mines at Korranberg, which is how the word reached the rebellion at all - through Orange Chicken's letter, relayed by Digma Beeve, arriving at exactly the moment the party was trying to find the foundry from the other direction.

Inside the mountain the errand is slow and stubborn work: opening the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/)'s sealed doors one at a time, clearing the undead behind them, and searching for the fallen remains of the Deeptempura so that they can be given proper burial rites. [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) casts speak with dead on the fallen as part of the process. They were managing less than a room a day.

## The camp

Fourteen bedrolls in the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/), under a shielded truce-lamp, around a field-shrine assembled from recovered tokens, with an old dwarf sitting off to one side writing names onto a roll. They can turn invisible at will. They fish for mushrooms and dry them on cords. They are poor, tired and badly armed.

## Standing with the Inevitables

First contact came at weapons' point: the party arrived in Imperial disguise, wearing the matriarch's dead husband's belt, having just robbed the clan's own sepulchers. They were named grave robbers to their faces and granted one cold night of hospitality on the strength of a returned mourning-braid and a marriage-kinship recited correctly in Dwarvish.

It was repaired in stages: grave-jewels left untouched on an altar body and reported rather than pocketed; the foundry level cleared in an afternoon; the truth of a dead boy told out loud in front of the whole camp rather than managed; and finally the princess and heir carried three hundred feet up a chain ladder and through the middle of the camp to be consecrated. Madame Tso returned the belt herself and blessed the party's claim to the folio and the horn.

## Right now

Warned to their faces that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) is free, has claimed the mountain, and intends to enslave or consume them, the Teryaki refused to evacuate - twice, from both siblings, and once against a direct argument that the mountain should be blessed as a tomb and abandoned. Only Bofric left. The rest remain in a throne room under a dragon, planning to renew a pact with him.

> So he'll renew our pact. - Madame Tso Teryaki, told that the dragon had been enslaved and bled exactly as her own people were

**Notable members:** [Madame Tso Teryaki](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/), matriarch; [General Tso](/people/general-tso/), her brother; [Bofric](/people/bofric/), the runesmith; [Crab Rangoon](/people/crab-rangoon/); [Wonton](/people/wonton/), who rides a giant spider; [Hetta](/people/hetta/), the scout; [Egg Roll](/people/egg-roll/), a boy who drew the best map anyone in the mountain has; [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/), dead on the way in; and the scouts Fried Tofu and Moo Goo Gai Pan, who died in the acid at the bottom.

## Beliefs

They believe the hold is the purpose of the clan, and that a purpose is not something you are permitted to survive.

They believe the secrets of Shavat die with them before they are handed to anyone who might repeat what was done with them.

They believe a pact once made can be made again - with the dragon their ancestors swore to, or failing that with the devil their ancestors bought the ore from.
