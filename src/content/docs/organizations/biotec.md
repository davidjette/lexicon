**Privately owned Imperial research contractor · Arcane-cybernetic augmentation and experimental implants · Upper Central District, Sharn · Publicly a medical research institute**

BioTec is a privately owned Imperial research contractor in the Upper Central District of [Sharn](/places/sharn/), specialising in arcane-cybernetic augmentation and experimental implant technology. Publicly a medical research institute, it operates as a covert development arm for the Empire, supplying the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) and military intelligence with prototype devices and classified enhancements under its head Hybrid Specialist, Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/). Its ownership was held through shell companies and inherited estates and resolved to no natural person on the public record; the owner was Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), who ran the body-supply pipeline for [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) out of a hidden laboratory behind his shop [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) and silenced staff with implanted control chips. Graves died in that laboratory when the Inevitables found it; Menka was tried and executed after the fall of Sharn, and every link in the company's supply chain has since been cut in the city.

## HOW THE NEST'S FILE WAS GATHERED

The spine of it is a folder of encrypted high-level data lifted out of BioTec's own labs on the night the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/) was stolen — [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) went in on a stolen keycard and came out through a corridor full of things nobody had ever seen upright before. [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) broke the encryption at the Nest and pulled one name out of it: **Project EDEN**. The rest was won piecemeal, over months — a technician drunk in an upper-city bar, a Black Dove cleric bleeding from the nose in a farmhouse cellar, a shopfront on Gear Street with a slaughterhouse behind it, and an overheard conversation at a restaurant opening.

## IMPERIAL COMMERCIAL REGISTRY — PUBLIC ENTRY

BioTec is a privately held research institute in the Upper Central District specialising in medical arcane-cybernetics: prosthesis, implant enhancement, restorative grafting. It contracts to the Empire — a private company renowned for its unwavering support of the Empire and the Iron Veil. Its filings are current and its ownership is held through a chain of holding companies and inherited estates, none of which resolve to a natural person on the public record. To the public, its head Hybrid Specialist, Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), represents the face of BioTec's innovation, a scientist whose intellect and rigor have secured her place among the Empire's most respected minds. Her legacy is defined not by warmth or charisma, but by the authority of her work and the lasting impact of her discoveries in service to the Empire.

## NEST THREAT ASSESSMENT — FACILITY PROFILE

BioTec is a privately owned Imperial research contractor specializing in arcane-cybernetic augmentation and experimental implant technology. Publicly, the company presents itself as a medical research institute. In reality, BioTec operates as a covert development arm for the Empire, supplying the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) and military intelligence with prototype devices and classified enhancements. Under Menka's leadership, BioTec has advanced the Empire's capabilities in [chip enhancements](/lore/implants-and-chip-enhancement/), interrogation technologies, and other cutting-edge bio-engineering applications — among them the [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) and the hybridisation work that became [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/)

The Sharn facility is located in the Upper Central District. The building's access is restricted; however, unlike most Imperial installations, BioTec does not employ Arcane Eye security inside the building. This absence is considered deliberate and unusual, suggesting the presence of sensitive research that cannot risk ArcEye recording or oversight. Exterior patrols still occur.

Every significant Imperial installation in Sharn sits inside the [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/). BioTec runs **no ArcEyes inside its own walls**.

## TESTIMONY OF MARTIN KROSS — THE ASTRA, UPPER CITY

[Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) is a junior enhancement-technician in BioTec's enchantments department, a quiet mid-tier employee with predictable habits and limited situational awareness. Zero made contact over several weeks at [The Astra](/places/the-astra-bar/), where Kross routinely drinks after his shift. Under subtle pressure and strategic intoxication, he disclosed details absent from the recovered BioTec files, including confirmation that the Ghost Chip is an implant designed to disrupt ArcEye scry-signatures.

- The Ghost Chip is an implant enhancement module designed to make the user undetectable to Arcane Eye surveillance systems (ArcEyes).
- The project is overseen by Lead Scientist Joanne Menka, a senior researcher with full Imperial clearance.
- **The decision to exclude ArcEyes from the facility was made by Menka herself**, citing "data contamination risks."
- Kross also mentioned a restricted laboratory door off the East lab he's never had access to / does not know what work is being done in there.
- Kross holds mid-level building access — his key-card is now in the possession of [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/).

Kross also mentioned, later and from Blair's quarters in the Underhive, that BioTec was moving [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) shipments and that a private owner was pulling the strings. He could not say the owner's name. He physically could not: there was a control chip in his skull that stopped his mouth every time he tried. Dario cut it out, and Kross said **Edwin**.

## INTERNAL — WHAT THE OWNER ACTUALLY DID

Behind his façade as the polite owner of [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) Chip Enhancement Parlor, Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) lived a second life. He was the secret owner of BioTec, controlling the corporation through shell companies and inherited assets, including the estate of [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/). From this position, he directed the body-supply pipeline for Project EDEN, using Circuit City as the entry point to a hidden laboratory behind the shop. For over four years, he abducted [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) laborers, experimented on them, harvested their organs, and shipped their altered and [Umbra](/items/umbra/)-prepped bodies to the Black Dove hospital in the upper city. The Cogborn whispered of a killer they called the **Cog Collector**; none knew the culprit was the quiet man who walked past them each day in Gear Street Market.

Graves' influence extended far beyond his storefront. He implanted obedience-blocking chips in BioTec employees and [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/), preventing them from speaking his name or revealing his involvement. He owned multiple properties in the Lower City, including the Trio's apartment building, using these holdings to maintain control and monitor the district. His automated Arcade, his financial reach, and his surgical precision allowed him to operate without witnesses, accomplices, or loose ends. <small>The Arcade sits in the Sub-line District, next door to Hopper's; the bodies went up by a private cargo skylift on Circuit City's covered rooftop, still programmed to run to the Hospital.</small>

His downfall came when the Trio uncovered the truth. Hidden behind Circuit City, they found a laboratory of Horrors, with a large cage of abducted Cogborn, medical tables, monstrous experiments, and a corpse-delivery system feeding BioTec's research. The confrontation that followed ended with Graves' death, exposing the full extent of his crimes and severing the central link between BioTec, the Cogborn disappearances, and Project EDEN.

## OVERHEARD AT POSÉ — MENKA TO THE LORD COMMANDER

Three of the Inevitables were working as servers at a restaurant opening when Dr. Menka reported a security incident to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) in the middle of the party:

> **Menka:** "Lord Commander… we had an incident at BioTec last night. Martin Kross, a junior enhancement-technician, is missing. Two of our hired guards were killed. And one of my senior scientists. All found this morning."\
> **Esther:** "And the units?"\
> **Menka (whispering):** "Unit 0.3 was discovered beside the scientist's body. It executed a full force pulse at shutdown. The other two units were still in their containment rooms. Someone knew exactly where to strike. The Ghost Chip is missing, And Kross…"\
> **Esther:** "Clean the mess and find Kross."\
> **Menka:** "Yes, my Lady."

<small>The party had a stolen keycard and no idea what was in the room. The panicked scientist opened the containment himself.</small>

Elsewhere in the same room, Menka was overheard with Sister Nora — tone urgent, anxious. Menka is furious about "last night's breach." She mentions employee Martin Kross and that he is missing. Menka mentions "Project EDEN" but only in fragments. Nora tries to calm her, saying "the souls lost last night will be added to the eternal nightfall," which Menka ignores.

## THE SUPPLY CHAIN

BioTec is the middle of a pipeline. Reconstructed from Sister Nora's interrogation and Preacher's account:

- **Bodies** — dead Iron Veil soldiers, and Cogborn abducted by Edwin Graves.
- **Warforged components** — manufactured and enhanced in-house by BioTec.
- **Umbra** — grown and supplied by **Malrik Zeir**, transported across Khorvaire by [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/).
- **Rites** — Umbra-infused necromantic rites performed on the corpses by the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/).
- **Deployment** — airship delivery into the Mournlands under Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar.

Every one of those links has now been cut in Sharn. Graves is dead; the Zeirs are dead; the Black Dove head priests were taken; the hospital was seized; Menka was tried and executed. The units are still in the field.

**Products:** The Ghost Chip · the ArcEye Crystallizer · obedience-blocking control chips · commercial and military implant enhancement · a bespoke teleportation chip commissioned by Hallorn d'Lyrandar as a gift for Faith Zeir · the hybrid units of Project E.D.E.N.

**Known personnel:** Dr. Edwin Graves (owner, deceased) · Dr. Joanne Menka (head Hybrid Specialist, executed) · Martin Kross (junior enhancement-technician, defected) · BioTec Security field agents · hired guards

**Holdings, through Graves:** Circuit City, Gear Street · the Arcade, Sub-line District · residential property across the Lower City · the estate of Cob Cornwell
