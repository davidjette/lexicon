**Sea elves · Bearers of Glassbane · Allies from Arc VII**

**Atlas and the Sea Elves** are the sea elves under **Atlas IV**, one of the five houses descended from the **Banefae**, and the line whose shard of Perfect moonstone is **Glassbane**. They joined the party after it saved them from the mindflayers and killed **the Waning One**, and they name the party "the true bearers of Glassbane." Their pledge was made at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s impromptu funeral, where the allies of that era stepped forward one by one with the armies of the world already gathering for the invasion of Netheril, and they stood with the party again at the Egg. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>

[Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) holds Glassbane by birthright and plunder, and earns it again fighting beside the water elves and Prince Atlas against the Drow in the Winking Depths, and in the founding of Atlantis. <small>(Full account at [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).)</small>

Glassbane is one of the **Bane** swords — the set the record gathers as "Silverbane, Ebonbane, Ironbane, and Glassbane," with Copperbane and Onyxbane named elsewhere. Each is the heirloom of an elven line, and in each case the pledge to the party is phrased as a transfer of custody: the party are the *rightful* bearers. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII; Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

An Atlas appears repeatedly across the arcs — "Atlas II" is among those to be dealt with in Arc VI, and an Atlas is among those hunting unicorns in Arc V. The numeral changes; the succession is not recorded.
