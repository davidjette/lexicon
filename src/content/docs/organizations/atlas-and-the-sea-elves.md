> You saved us from the mindflayers and defeated the Waning One. You are the true bearers of Glassbane. We are with you to the end.

<small>— Atlas IV, on the Ally Card. The same pledge is in the session record of Brightmantle's funeral, word for word.</small>

**Sea elves · Bearers of Glassbane · Allies from Arc VII**

**Atlas and the Sea Elves** are the sea elves under **Atlas IV**, one of the five houses descended from the **Banefae**, and the line whose shard of Perfect moonstone is **Glassbane**. They joined the party after it saved them from the mindflayers and defeated [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), whom the party charmed and then imprisoned in [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/), and they name the party "the true bearers of Glassbane." Their pledge was made at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s impromptu funeral, where the allies of that era stepped forward one by one with the armies of the world already gathering for the invasion of Netheril, and they stood with the party again at the Egg. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>

[Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) holds Glassbane by birthright and plunder, and earns it again fighting beside the water elves and Prince Atlas against the Drow in the Winking Depths, and in the founding of [Atlantis](/places/atlantis/). <small>(Full account at [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).)</small>

Glassbane is one of the **Bane** swords — the set the record gathers as "Silverbane, Ebonbane, Ironbane, and Glassbane," with Copperbane and Onyxbane named elsewhere. Each is the heirloom of an elven line, and in each case the pledge to the party is phrased as a transfer of custody: the party are the *rightful* bearers. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII; Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

An Atlas appears repeatedly across the arcs — "Atlas II" is among those to be dealt with in Arc VI, and an Atlas is among those hunting unicorns in Arc V. The kings of Atlantis are all named Atlas. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Atlas II is the Atlas of Antiquity, and Atlas III and Atlas IV belong to the present, which Dave's quest board called the Renaissance: "In this time … Atlantis, find Atlas II, enter the Egg" and "In the Renaissance: … Atlantis, find Atlas III and IV, defeat the spider bros, enter the egg". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> The sea elves are also called tritons. Dave named Atlas IV "the Triton prince you met in the winking depth". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small>

## Across the eras

Atlas IV was a prince when the party met him in the Winking Depths. His blessing gives Glassbane's bearer a level of storm sorcery, and he became "the third wheel to Max + Zan in the offscreen future war VS the spiderbros". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09, 2021-10-23)</small>

In Antiquity the Atlas of that time refused to go along with Archmage [Valen](/people/valen/) and the Waning One, as [Calix](/people/calix/) did. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> The Bane blades told the party that the Egg rises from the sea as the sea elves attempt to enter it, and that the defeat of the Netherese causes it to crash back into the ocean and destroy Atlantis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small>

After the battle in Despondos, Calix and Atlas returned to their Infanta "to lead attacks on Netherese cities and the Egg itself." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> The same night Dave set out Glassbane's four runes, all from kings of Atlantis: the blessing of the first Atlas, the blessing of the last Atlas, the blessing of the last Atlas's father, and "The last rune is from the Atlas of antiquity here in this time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Among the threads still open before the end, Dave listed "Atlantis in two eras". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-05)</small> Nichole, who played [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/), recalled that the party "ended up teleporting to Atlantis with glass". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-15)</small>
