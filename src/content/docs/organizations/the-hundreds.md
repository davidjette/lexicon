**Warforged nation · The Mournlands and the Glass Plateau · Active, influence growing**

The Hundreds are the Warforged nation of the Mournlands, led by [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) from the fortified stronghold of Mt. Silicon and holding the Glass Plateau. They have fought Project E.D.E.N.'s hybrid units in the waste for years, fed Sharn in exchange for access to its airspace, and hold the Ruby Nova, a controlled recreation of the Mourning, which Pierce has already detonated once. Their intent is recorded nowhere.

## NEST FACTION BOARD — ENTRY IN FULL

**THE HUNDREDS — The Warforged Nation of the Mournlands.** Leader: Locke Pierce. Status: Alive. Influence: Growing. Intent: Unknown.

<small>That is the entire entry. The Nest has never known what the Hundreds want.</small>

## WHAT SISTER NORA GAVE UP — PROJECT E.D.E.N.

Project E.D.E.N. exists in large part because of them. Hybrid undead-Warforged soldiers were built from dead Iron Veil troops and abducted Cogborn, fitted with BioTec components, infused with Umbra grown by [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) and moved by [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/), consecrated by [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) necromantic rite, and deployed into the Mournlands by Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar. Their purpose: to survive the cursed waste, to mine Red Khyber from the Glass Plateau, and to destroy the Warforged resistance entrenched there.

[Preacher](/people/preacher/), speaking to the trio in the Sub-Transit control room, added that the Hundreds have been fighting those units for years, along with a blue-skinned, red-eyed hybrid killer they call the Blue Devil and believe to be unkillable. Preacher recognised her. She is Eden, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s youngest half-sister, resurrected as EDN-00.

By Nico's account, the Project Eden units east of [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/) in the Mournlands are now the Cogborn's biggest fight, and the Hundreds are the ones who mainly hold them off. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-31)</small>

## WHAT LOCKE PIERCE SHOWED THEM AT MT. SILICON

Mt. Silicon is a fortified Warforged stronghold carved into a mountainside, and behind its doors is a control chamber walled in screens and filled with the Hundreds — whom Locke calls his children. Three years after the Sharn rising he brought [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) inside it. There he told them that the Fantanya Nyel are real, that Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) intends to ring them, and that if she succeeds the dead will rise everywhere.

Then he showed them his answer: the Ruby Nova, a weaponised catastrophe, a controlled recreation of the Mourning itself. He detonated it on the battlefield from which the party had just evacuated the last surviving Silver Flame paladins. A red dome bloomed across the horizon, silent at that distance, visible from Thronehold, and swallowed the portal site and the remnants of Vex's fleet along with it. Across Khorvaire the free states named it the Last Mourning.

> "Stop Uriel before she rings the bells. If you fail, I will end this world before she can, and only my kind will survive it."

Dave's account of the weapon: "Locke and his team called it Ruby Nova which is a callsign for the bomb", and "the purpose was to demonstrate the weapon, so it was close enough to see it up close but not enough to kill the witnesses, not immediately at least". The people of the free states now fear "a final confrontation between the hordes of the Uruk Republic, the awesome destructive capabilities of the Hundreds, and the imperial war machine and armies of undead". Dave's own idea is that Locke Pierce used Red Khyber to weaponise the Mourning, though "no way he would tell you guys that"; of Red Khyber, "all sides are using it". Mt. Silicon also keeps the secrets of crystallic fusion. Dave offered the party an espionage job there: "Steal the secrets of crystallic fusion, and how the Hundreds discovered it before the Empire of the Uruk". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

## THE PRICE OF BREAD

The Hundreds gave freely and then presented an invoice. Locke Pierce himself teleported in at the mountain tunnel beneath Sharn, once the Cogborn were clear, to thank the party for saving them, and handed out R.U.S.T. communication necklaces. He offered transport anywhere on the continent, five upgraded Air Bubble breathers inscribed *Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers*, a sealed intelligence dossier on the Hell's Bells, and Mournland pomegranates. Captain Edday opened a direct emergency extraction channel to the party's sending stones.

Then Zilspar Farm burned. Days later three Warforged arrived under Himmie Joffa offering to feed a city calculated at forty-seven days from famine, in exchange for military and logistical access to Sharn's airspace. Eric granted it, told Gemma he had handed Locke Pierce the keys, and said he knew it was a trick. By morning a capital ship had settled out of the fog over Sharn to hand out fruit in orderly lines.

**The open question:** did Pierce learn of Zilspar and give it to the Empire to buy the sky over Sharn? Or did someone else betray [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/)? The Hundreds' own dossier confirms there were no survivors at the farm. Intent: still unknown.

**Known members:** Locke Pierce, leader · Captain Edday · Himmie Joffa.

**Holdings:** Mt. Silicon · Gorgonhorn, shared with [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) on the Mournland border · the Glass Plateau · a capital fleet · the skies of Sharn.
