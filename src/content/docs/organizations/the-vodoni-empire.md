**Empire · Vodonispace · Rival of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) · Active**

*Also known as:* the Vodoni · the Vodani Empire

The **Vodoni Empire** is an empire of Vodonispace whose people are lycanthropes, fiends and humans. Generations before *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the threat of a Vodoni incursion drove a confederacy of disparate systems to form the Sovereign galactic government, [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/). The two empires later allied against the menace of the [beholders](/species/beholders/), and they now wage a cold war of espionage and fractured diplomacy. The Sovereignty suspects the Vodoni of violating their treaty and orchestrating the sonic disturbance called the T.U.N.E. [The Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) keeps a policy of neutrality. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## History

In the Company dossier, the threat of Vodoni incursion urged a confederacy of disparate systems, generations ago, to join and form a Sovereign galactic government. Later the two empires allied against the Beholder menace. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## The cold war

By the time of *The Starsong Awakens* the Sovereignty and the Vodoni wage a cold war "of espionage and fractured diplomacy". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> JL, as DM, set the conflict up as a cold war in which the Sovereignty wants to make the monsters an easy scapegoat for the awakened Starsong. The Company, knowing that this would disrupt trade, wants to resurrect [Caprica](/people/caprica/) to find the true source, which JL called "the hook for the new campaign". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small> See [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/).

### The T.U.N.E.

The Temporarily Unknown Noise Energy (T.U.N.E.) lies in Wildspace, somewhere between Realmspace and the Vodoni Empire. Company artificers first detected it after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), and over fifteen years it grew louder and more unstable until it became audible to the unaided ear within 500 lightyears of the Vodoni border. The sound can destroy phlogiston, making interstellar travel and communication extremely difficult, if not impossible. Company statisticians rate the event between 8.7 and 9.0 on the Universal Catastrophe Scale. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

Sovereign leadership has implicated the Vodoni Empire, and the Company's Board of Directors has flagged the source and nature of the T.U.N.E. as its utmost high priority. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> See [Timothee Buckets](/people/timothee-buckets/).

### The threat of war

On [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/), a Sovereign protectorate, the threat of Sovereign war with the Vodoni Empire has agitated the populace. Long lines of evacuees mark the docks, leaving the planetoid has become a challenge, and crime on the surface has reached a fever pitch. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, The Rock of Bral)</small>

Rumours of the top-secret destination of the SSJ Vanguard, an Istus-class vessel of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), range from Realmspace to deep within the Vodoni Empire. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## The Company

The Company dossier, which [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/) gives the crew, states the Company's position: "Though the Sovereignty suspects the Vodoni have violated their treaty and orchestrated the current sonic disturbance (T.U.N.E.), the Company's policy is neutrality." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11; The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## Population

The dossier gives the empire's population as half lycanthrope, two-fifths fiend and one-tenth human. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> JL described the Vodoni as "literally a bunch of monsters". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

## Origin

JL drew the Vodoni Empire from the villains of *Under the Dark Fist*, a second-edition campaign in which there is a war between the monsters and the good guys. For *The Starsong Awakens* he made the conflict a cold war. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small> The dossier spells the name "Vodani" once, in its heading for the empire.
