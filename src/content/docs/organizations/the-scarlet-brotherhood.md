**Secret society of assassins · Saltmarsh, Oerth · Enemies of Neptune's Rejects · Every named member killed**

*Also known as:* the Brotherhood · the scarlet b · the Scarlet scourge · SB

The **Scarlet Brotherhood** is a secret society of assassins in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/). In November 2021 [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) led the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) to the Weathervein Tomb beneath [the Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) and ambushed them there with Brotherhood assassins; the party survived, and devastated a secret society in a single night. The Brotherhood fought the party again at the Saltmarsh finale of July 2022, where it was beaten, and by September 2022 the party had killed every named member.

## The ambush at the Weathervein Tomb

Dave's notes for the session "Salvage Operation – Part I": "Either way, Skerrin will order their assassination by leading them to the Weather vein tomb and ambushing them with Brotherhood assassins." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> The party's ally [Oshi](/people/oshi/) was killed in the fight. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave, afterwards: "the scarlet b gave us a chance to bounce". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small>

Dave on the aftermath: "we sailed into town with gunboats, had an attempt on no our lives, and immediately blew up a building and publicly executed four members of the community" / "Six!" / "Members of a secret society which we devastated in a single night, and people witnessed your full and terrible power and the rebirth of your house." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

When the party weighed what to do with the merchant [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/), JL said he wanted revenge on Skerrin, and of Drallion: "He has to get on the council and vote with Dolores to invest resources in removing the Scarlet scourge". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

## Enemies in Saltmarsh

[Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) is a sworn enemy of the Brotherhood. JL, running the session: "He’s scared of the church and their reign of terror even though he’s a sworn enemy of the brotherhood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-07)</small> Nico, of her character [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/): "Delores does really hate the brotherhood though". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small>

The Brotherhood was one of the two main foes of [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/). Dave: "we crippled their two main foes for them the soggies and the brotherhood". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

## Dave's theory: the Weathervein trove

In April 2022 Dave put forward what he called "my current fan theory". He recalled that "they hinted that the SB might have been built to depose the Weathervein dynasty a long time ago", and that "the SB opposed the Sea Princes". His reading: "If there’s a big trove down there, it could be that the mission of the SB is to cover it UP" / "That’s why they enable the Sauhagin to invade - to disrupt the town and stop them from digging more" / "They want Saltmarsh poor and sleepy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

## The finale and after

The Brotherhood fought the party at the Saltmarsh finale of 2022-07-09, after which the party reached level 10; Nico wrote that level 10 felt earned for dealing with the Brotherhood. Dave listed the open leads: "the mine / WPM / Eldabrin and then Scarlett Brotherhood (the Devil lives?)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

Of [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/), which the party took in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/), Dave wrote: "obvs the Brotherhood can’t get it back". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

In September 2022, when Nico asked whether only the Brotherhood was left, Dave answered that the party had beaten them too: "Maybe we rescue the boy" / "The baby" / "We killed every named character in the brotherhood". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

In October 2022 Nico ruled in play: "btw Scrunt Rising Dawn Neck Tattoo = +2 to Dmg to any Scarlet Brotherhood Assassin." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-07)</small>
