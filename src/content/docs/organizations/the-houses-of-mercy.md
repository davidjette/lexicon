**Continent-wide medical network · Former Sovereign Host foundations · Active in every major city in Khorvaire**

The Houses of Mercy are a continent-wide network of hospitals, mercy-kitchens and paupers' wards operating in every major city in Khorvaire. They were made out of the churches, temples and hospitals of the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/), whose dioceses and abbeys were impounded by Imperial decree and their considerable assets seized, and were re-staffed with [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) nurses and nuns, the Empress bankrolling the work from the personal wealth of the Emperor and the House of Kaius. Specially manufactured [Hell's Bells](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) were installed in as many of them as possible, making the network a set of laboratories and deployment centres for a widespread necromantic apocalypse which the sisterhood's own liturgy names **the Eternal Nightfall**.

## THE PUBLIC FACE

What the cities see is a mercy-kitchen that takes in the grieving without vetting them, a Sister-Inquisitor who preaches mercy in the square to crowds that love her, and, at the oldest house of all, the memory of "the elf who came when the plague did."

## DOSSIER HB-CHORUS-13 — ASSESSMENT OF THE THREAT

The Progenitor's own threat assessment, cracked out of the Mt. Silicon archive by [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). Uriel and the Black Dove sisterhood have used the Sovereign Host churches, temples, and hospitals, turning them into Houses of Mercy staffed by Black Dove nurses and nuns, and to the farthest extent possible, installed specially manufactured Hell's Bells in every one of them. She has developed a continent-scale network of medical facilities which double as laboratories and deployment centers for a widespread necromantic apocalypse, rung in with a chorus of bells.

The Progenitor's conclusion on scale: *"Everything hanging in a House of Mercy tonight is a copy… The copies are weaker, and they do not need to be strong. They only need to resonate the mother bell's call. This means there can be many many more than thirteen. I suspect there are hundreds."*

## KORRANBERG FOUNDRY TRUST — PRIVATE SCHEDULE OF WORKS, SECOND LEAF

One surviving leaf, pulled half-burned out of a sacristy strong-box in Sharn. Undertaken for the House of Kaius, by standing draft. The standing summary carried forward on the leaf gives the network's dimensions in a clerk's hand:

- **Great castings, consecration grade:** thirteen commissioned, eleven delivered, two in the yard.
- **Minor castings, parish grade:** three hundred forty-one delivered to the Houses to date — and the schedule of small consignments is kept separately by the Sisters, and not by the foundry.
- All castings consecrated on delivery, by the Sisters alone. Foundry personnel are excluded from consecration and are reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft.

**Named consignments on the surviving leaf:** the ninth to the cathedral-house at **Korth**; the tenth to the monastery precincts at **Atur**, delivered standing, the tower to be raised over it at the consignee's expense; the eleventh to the Grand Infirmary at **Fairhaven**, by twenty-ox dray with a Sister riding escort; the twelfth to a destination burned out of the page, freight instructions withheld from the office, wagons sealed, drivers of the Sisters' own, *draft paid double for the blindness*. Every receipt under the dove seal.

## THE FULCRUM BELL FILE — FOURTEEN SCRAPS

A courier packet of fourteen scraps that [Fulcrum](/people/blair/) agents spent over a decade collecting, one witness at a time. The witnesses are cooks, lamplighters, porters, midwives, carters, gravediggers, drovers, pursers, teamsters and muleteers, and no two of them know each other. What they describe is the same building over and over:

- **Korth** — the old Host cathedral. Biggest casting the witness ever saw; they knocked the west door wider to bring it in. It has never rung once.
- **Atur** — the Crimson Monastery precincts. They built the tower around the bell. Nuns wash it at dawn, with cloths, like grooming a horse. It has no rope.
- **Fairhaven** — the Grand Infirmary. It hangs over the long ward where the dying go. *"The patients say you can feel it anyway, like a tooth about to ache."*
- **Wroat** — a midwife asked them to ring for a christening and was told the bell is consecrated and does not ring for such things. Novices are whipped if they touch it. *"What does a bell ring for, if not for us?"*
- **Vathirond** — a gravedigger of thirty years: the plots nearest the mercy-house do not settle right. The ground stays loose.
- **Talenta Plains** — the old frontier hospital, and the first of them: the Empress's own hospital from before she was Empress, when she was just the elf who came when the plague did. Its bell went up years before any other. Halfling drovers will not bed their herds within a mile of it. The beasts will not graze. The grass grows fine.
- **Sharn, Lower City** — the Chancellor's men impounded the bell with wagons and writs, all proper, and the nuns wept and screamed and cursed the soldiers by name. *"Whatever that bell is, they don't grieve like that for brass."*
- **Karrlakton** — third-hand and unverified: the cellar hums at night and the sisters keep something down there they call *the little sister*.
- **Starilaskur** — a new bell at the Sovereign shrine, raised by subscription, **and it rings every dawn**. It is the one entry in the file that fails the test.

<small>Scrap 5 describes the same Wroat bell as Scrap 4 under a different spelling and a different witness.</small>

## NEST DEBRIEF K-KORTH — WHAT A HOUSE IS LIKE FROM INSIDE

Eleven days of eyes-on from [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) inside the Korth house. A cathedral-grade casting in the crossing, dark metal, no rope, no clapper wheel — not rigged to be rung by anyone standing below it. A washing-liturgy at dawn: three novices, white cloths, total silence. The metal is warm in a Korth winter. Sung services replace all ringing, and under the night office there is a slow pressure behind the ears, about one beat to every four of a heart, which the novices call *the Mother's breathing* and are proud of. The house defers absolutely to [Sister-Inquisitor Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), who inspects the bell the way an armourer inspects a blade before a duel, and who carries the **Chorus Ledger** — which house, which bell, which consecration-day.

## Ten Bells Remain

The Thirteenth Casting was broken with KNELL under the Korramont, and every finished bell in the Bladeworks with it. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) then destroyed two more in a single morning in Sharn: the crated one in the unfinished tower above the hospital, packed in straw by people too frightened to touch it, cleared by his own hands with *"may our children forgive us"*; and the one in a small temple of Boldrei in the lower city, whose destruction brought the tower down through the floor and turned the crowd against him. Research in Sharn established that breaking a bell does not notify the Empress. Ten bells remain, at least one of them a Master in Korth, and the Houses that hold them are still full of patients.

**Known Houses:** Korth (cathedral-house, Master, ninth casting) · Atur · Fairhaven · Wroat · Vathirond · the Talenta Plains frontier hospital (the first) · Sharn Lower City — the small church whose bell the Chancellor's men impounded, and the small temple of Boldrei whose bell Dario broke, bringing the tower down (whether these are one building or two is not stated) · the Sanctum of the Last Mercy, Upper City Sharn (seized; a golden bell found crated in an unfinished tower) · Karrlakton (unverified) · a twelfth consignment whose destination was burned off the page · the hospital in the mountains northeast of Qarth, at the top of the road the dove seal travels, which the Inevitables identify with the Ivory Lazaret.

**Holdings:** the seized estates, plate and endowments of the Sovereign Host dioceses and abbeys · a foundry town's whole output under standing draft · a mercy-kitchen and a paupers' ward in every city that will have one.

> "They never ring. Not for mass, not for fire or the dead. Whatever these are, they're waiting for something and the nuns tend to them like they know what." — Fulcrum cover letter, signed "— I"
