> Together they formed a Cult of the Infanta, they call themselves Lusmundii (Light of the World).

<small>— the Bladesingers of Motherstone Ally Card. The same line is in Arc I, Hope in Exile, Part I.</small>

**Elf wizards · The Lusmundii, Cult of the Infanta · Allies from Arc I**

The **Bladesingers of Motherstone** are the warrior order of the **Lusmundii** — "Light of the World" — the Cult of the Infanta, and they are among the oldest allies in the set. They were guarding the Infanta when "Imperial soldiers slaughtered and enslaved the Lusmundii and wood elves" around her, and the survivors have been with the party from the beginning. <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile, Part I.)</small> By Arc IV they are a congregation as well as a guard: when Obus goes missing, [Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) "rushes out to find the Bladesingers and his burgeoning congregation of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) worshipers in consternation." <small>(Arc IV Part II.)</small>

## At the Egg

**White — Enchant Weapon: "+3 to attack/damage of a weapon until end of your next turn."**

**Black — Wrath of the Gildergreen: "A giant tree appears w/i 120 feet, restrains a creature, STR 30/turn."**

<small>The black action calls up the Gildergreen, the mythal-tree of the elves that shelters the Infanta in Arc I.</small>
