**Cult · The Vault Underground, Upper City Sharn · Leadership destroyed**

The Children of Ember were a secretive cult of masked cultists based in the private Zeir residence attached to [the Vault Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/) in Upper City Sharn, led by [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/) and [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/). They abducted civilians, drugged them, and used them as "blood cattle" for Malrik's monstrous feeding rituals, and their doctrine centred on "Ember" — [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/), the son of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), whom they believed bore a divine mark. They were unknown to every major faction until the trio's infiltration exposed their sanctum, freed captives and took key documents. Every named leader of the cult died within an hour of each other in the assault that followed, and the surviving masked faithful have not been heard from since.

<small>Rebel mission board, posted between the Vault Below raid and the Vault Underground assault. Risk was rated Extreme: if recognized, the trio would be targeted for abduction or elimination. Standing order until Jessica made contact: avoid all contact.</small>

## MISSION BRIEFING — VAULT UNDERGROUND

**Mission Objective:** Locate and gain access to the Zeir private residence from the lower floor of the Vault Underground club, gather intel, rescue any captives, and eliminate the threat.

**Location:** [The Vault Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/) (24-hour Upper City Club & Attached Zeir Residence)

**Primary Subjects:** Faith Zeir, Malrik Zeir, Children of Ember masked cultists

The Vault Underground operates publicly as a normal Upper-City club with regular employees; bartenders, dancers, servers, bouncers, none of whom appear to be involved in or aware of any trafficking, rituals, or captive "cattle" being held in the private residence.

[Lorian](/people/lorian/) was held in the Zeir residence and remembered its architecture, its staff and its schedule, and the briefing rests almost entirely on his testimony. According to it, the private Zeir residence is accessed from the lowest level of the club through Faith's office. Inside this residence is where Faith and Malrik Zeir live and operate. During his captivity, Lorian witnessed:

- Children of Ember cultists gathering in a large ritual room beneath the main floor of the residence
- Wealthy recruiters (4 men, and Hallorn) bringing in new victims
- Captives kept in a cell called the Pantry off the kitchen — drugged, isolated, and used by Malrik and the recruiters

The Children of Ember are a small, silent group of masked cultists who follow Faith's orders and participate in ritual activity. They believe in and worship "Ember" — Calcifer, whom they consider a prophesied figure: the son of Esther Crona and Richard Blaze, whom they believe bears a divine mark.

> "They believe in and worship Ember — Calcifer, whom they consider a prophesied figure."

The recruiters, however, are not cultists. They are wealthy men from across Khorvaire who traffic young people to Malrik, trade captives, and treat the Zeir residence as a private den when visiting.

## MISSION NOTES

Malrik Zeir is a Valenar high elf who has recently been revealed to be a vampire. Eliminating him would severely disrupt the Umbra supply for Project E.D.E.N., shut down the Children of Ember cult, and end the trafficking of his victims.

[Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/), Malrik's half-brother, is one of the primary recruiters and the man who brought Lorian to Sharn. Removing him would further destabilize their network. Taking out Malrik and Hallorn would deal a major blow to [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/)

- Malrik grows and supplies Umbra
- Hallorn transports it across Khorvaire

Parts of Project E.D.E.N. already crippled:

- Edwin Graves (the Cog Collector) — eliminated, cutting off the Cogborn corpse supply
- Black Dove head priests — captured, disrupting necromantic rites

The Zeirs' operation is one of the last major pipelines feeding Project E.D.E.N. in Sharn.

## PAPERS RECOVERED BENEATH THE VAULT BELOW

Correspondence between Malrik and Hallorn concerning Umbra and its connection to Project E.D.E.N. Notes on a ritual attempted on [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) to strip a memory out of his head, which failed. Two emaciated prisoners kept as blood cattle for Malrik's feeding. And one line naming Mayor Henry Heinrick as having joined [The Velvet Table Society](/organizations/the-velvet-table-society/), which meets at the Upper City Vault.

**Position in the machine:** Malrik grows and supplies the Umbra; Hallorn transports it across Khorvaire; the Black Doves consecrate the bodies; BioTec assembles them. With Edwin Graves dead and the Black Dove head priests captured, the Zeir operation was one of the last major pipelines still feeding Project E.D.E.N. in Sharn.

## TESTIMONY OF A CAPTURED CULTIST

A frightened young woman, taken alive outside the Upper City safe house after the Children of Ember cut the teleportation chip out of [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/)'s skull, said that every masked cultist had once been "Father" Malrik Zeir's cattle. Each was personally groomed and reshaped by the Mother Prophet, Faith. The cult was not recruited; it was made out of its own victims.

<small>The trio could not bring themselves to kill her; they left her tied to a chair and went on to the club.</small>

## The End of the Cult

The strike came that night. Cultists were performing a ceremony over a bound and terrified [Whole Foods](/people/whole-foods/), Calcifer's nanny, in the ritual room beneath the residence; the trio cut them down and freed her. Upstairs the recruiters attacked, Hallorn confronted them, Gemma was chained and dragged away, and Malrik fled to the rooftop pool deck with both Gemma and Calcifer. There Dario killed him outright — the vampire's mist escaped into his coffin, but the body was destroyed.

Esther Crona descended from her airship as Malrik fell, saw her son in danger and the Mother Prophet running, and put her sword through her own half-sister's chest. Eric delivered the final blow. Every named leader of the Children of Ember died within an hour of each other, and the surviving masked faithful have not been heard from since.
