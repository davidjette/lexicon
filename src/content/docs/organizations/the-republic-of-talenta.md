**Nation · eastern Khorvaire, formerly the outpost of Leef · Sovereign, allied**

The **Republic of Talenta** is a sovereign nation in eastern Khorvaire, founded at the former Imperial outpost of [Leef](/places/leef/) by the townsfolk of Leef, the refugees of [Newham](/places/newham/) and the swamp-dwarf tribes of [the Mror Holds](/places/the-mror-holds/) after Newham was destroyed. It stands between the corrupted wasteland of the New Mourning to the west and the allied Mror Holds to the east, and is one of the few places in Khorvaire largely beyond the reach of the Empire. [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) serves as its acting Comptroller. Three accounts of its founding follow: the Imperial gazetteer entry for Leef, the Republic's own founding proclamation, and Digma Beeve's private account.

## IMPERIAL GAZETTEER — LEEF, TALENTA PLAINS

A dusty desert outpost, known only as the last eastern stop on the lightning rail. Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) expanded his holdings eastward, constructed an airship yard, and flooded the town with illithid city guards and Iron Veil soldiers. Classification: secured, productive, unremarkable.

## FOUNDING PROCLAMATION OF THE REPUBLIC

Once a dusty desert outpost known only as the last eastern stop on the lightning rail, Leef changed forever when Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) expanded his empire eastward, constructing an airship yard and flooding the town with illithid city guards and Iron Veil soldiers. It was here that [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) met. Together the trio liberated Leef with the help of local defenders: Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke, a hardened veteran of the Last War. Zero of Leef, an elderly Tabaxi follower of the Silver Flame; and Princess Orange Chicken, a young dwarven leader from the swamp tribes of the Mror Holds. Their victory earned them the title "**The Heroes of Leef**."

At Rourke's urging, the trio traveled west to confront the architect of Newham's oppression, Grand Duke John C. LeBeefe, a former member of the Unforeseen and a sorcerer of unknown powers. In [Newham City](/places/newham/) they met Digma Beeve, Rourke's old ally, who introduced them to a network of anti-LeBeefe rebel cells hidden throughout the metropolis. With Digma's guidance, the trio united these disparate factions into a coordinated resistance strong enough to challenge LeBeefe's rule. Their assault succeeded, and LeBeefe was killed, but the victory came at a catastrophic cost. In a large otherworldly explosion the city collapsed into ruin, and a vast region around Newham became corrupted, toxic, and uninhabitable: the New Mourning.

The survivors fled east, evacuating en masse to Leef. There, the townsfolk, Newham refugees, and the swamp-dwarf tribes forged a new nation: The Republic of Talenta. Now positioned between the corrupted wasteland of the New Mourning to the west and the allied Mror Holds to the east, the Republic stands as a fortified rebel stronghold—one of the few places in Khorvaire largely beyond the reach of [Esther](/people/esther-crona/)'s imperial forces. Born from loss, defiance, and unlikely alliances, the Republic of Talenta endures as the beating heart of eastern resistance.

## COMPTROLLER'S PRIVATE ACCOUNT

"The victory came at a catastrophic cost." In a large otherworldly explosion the city of Newham collapsed into ruin, and a vast region around it became corrupted, toxic and uninhabitable — the **New Mourning**. The proclamation calls the Republic a nation born of defiance and unlikely alliances. Digma, who keeps the ledgers, records that it was born of an evacuation from a city his allies destroyed.

<small>Digma now serves as acting Comptroller of the Republic of Talenta while Eric is stationed in Sharn. Leef's old name survives in daily speech; the Republic's own paperwork still reads "formerly known as Leef".</small>

## The name

At the close of the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/), JL, its DM, wrote of the trio: "We are now firmly established as heroes of the realm, and we have serious roots in Talenta." He gave Dario the rank of "Field Marshall of Talenta" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> Asked what Leef was now called, Dave answered: "Yeah "Talenta" or the "talenta republic" the town can stay Leef but long term we'll probably rename it after whichever one of us dies". Nico offered "The Eric the Cleric Republic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

The trio's road to Sharn began in the Republic. Digma handed them a sealed dossier passed from a trusted rebel contact in Sharn, who was travelling to meet them and would "arrive in the Republic of Telenta within a day’s time" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

## The Republic at War

After the fall of the Iron Veil, Leef sent representatives to the first meeting of the Council of Sharn seeking alliance and stability, and Leef soldiers appeared on every screen in the city during the rising. Three years on, the Republic sends delegates to the Thronehold Summit alongside Sharn, and the Republic's alliance with the Mror Holds remains the eastern rebellion's best line to the dwarf clans.

Before the Summit the Emperor's spokespeople were "calling the Rebels in Sharn and Talenta traitors and deathbound but leaving room for terms with the larger forces approaching his capital". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small>

**Places of Interest:** **Leef** — the old outpost, the capital in everything but name. **The airship yard** — built by LeBeefe when he flooded the town. **The New Mourning** — the toxic border to the west, which no army crosses. **The Mror Holds** — allied, to the east, and the source of the swamp-dwarf tribes who helped found the nation.
