**Royal house of Punis, then imperial dynasty of Faerûn · The Glass Throne, Suzail · Contested in the War of the Five Zanzibars**

*Also known as:* the Zanzibars · the line of Zanzibars · the Zanzibar line

**House Zanzibar** is the dynasty descended from [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), Zanzibar the Elder, King of Punis and of Cormyr and First Emperor of Faerûn. It was the royal house of the Principality of [Punis](/places/punis/) and Paenis, where Zanzibar was crowned king after the Arcaneum arc and his twin, [Zebediah](/people/zebediah/), was disinherited. After [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) he married Princess [Roseanne](/people/roseanne-danzig/) of Cormyr and was crowned king of Cormyr, and their child Zara was an Infanta. Immediately after the Ages of the Infanta he declared himself Emperor of Faerûn and founded [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/), with his capital at [Suzail](/places/suzail/), and every emperor after him has been called "the Zanzibar". The god **Zarus** blessed the bloodline to rule forever. For five hundred years the line held out against a government led by [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). In the reign of [Zanzibar William the Melancholy](/people/zanzibar-william-the-melancholy/), son of [Zanzibar the Penitent](/people/zanzibar-the-penitent/), four claimants rose against the throne in [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), and rival empires later claimed to be the true Zanzibar.

## The royal house of Punis

In January 2019 the party set out for the Principality of Punis and Paenis to recapture Zanzibar's birthright. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-01)</small> The steward's sheet styles him "Zanzibar the Younger, Heir of Punis and Paenis". His father, the king of Punis, died of his injuries in the presence of his wife, his sons and a priest of Zarus. <small>(Arc III, see [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/).)</small> The Punisean crown had recovered the sea elves' blade **Glassbane** in a war, and Zanzibar carries it as King of Punis. <small>(See [the Bane blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).)</small>

After his exile at the hands of the Arcaneum, when Valen was defeated, Zanzibar returned to Punis and was crowned King of Punis. He named [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) and [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) Thanes of Punis and gave them holds, named [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) his general and promoted [Maximillien](/people/maximillien/) to Steward. "Seppy is a foot note. Zebediah his twin was disinherited." <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-c30-coronation.md)</small>

## Cormyr, and Zara

King Glenn Danzig of Cormyr, turned into a snail and turned back, appreciated Zanzibar "rooting out the evil of Valen and his tainting by the Leviathan" and offered him Roseanne's hand. After the Battle of the River Lis Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne, and when the king fell ill Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr after the wedding. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-punis-and-roseanne.md)</small>

**Zara** is the child of Zanzibar and Roseanne. In September 2019, listing the Infanta, Dave wrote "Zaria is an embryo". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-19)</small> Of the Infanta on the field at the River Lis: "Tico and Mora at a minimum, likely Zara in utero". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> Before the battle JL, who played Zanzibar, planned to bring Roseanne, writing that he would "convince my preggo girlfriend to come to an almost unwinnable battle". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small> In December 2022 Dave wrote: "The main issue with Zan coming back is why would he leave his own infanta and CHILD Zara to save June and infanta he’s never met". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-24)</small> The spelling is Zara; Zaria is an alias.

## Zanzibar the Elder

"Zanzibar the Elder" is a relative title: it means the elder Zanzibar at any given time. In the Age of the Infanta it named the king of Punis, the father of the player character Zanzibar, and [Maximillien](/people/maximillien/)'s character sheet records that he "was knighted by Zanzibar the Elder". The title later stuck to the player character, and the Zanzibars after him took new epithets, such as the Penitent and the Melancholy. Dave: "zanzibar the elder refers to the elder zanzibar at any given time, until it sticks on the PC and future zanzibars call themselves by new epithets". <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small>

## The first Emperor

The founder of the dynasty is the Zanzibar who stayed in his own time. Dave: "The True zanny because he didn’t go on the time hopping adventure with you all, he left after the River Lis and you guys went off with Zeb!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> The Zanzibar who went into the past is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/).

Immediately after the Ages quest, Zanzibar, crowned king of Cormyr and with his forces in the old imperial capital of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), declared himself Emperor of Faerûn and consolidated control of the continent, mostly peacefully. "From then on, the Emperor was called the 'Zanzibar'." <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-dead-mans-hand-secrets-and-zanzibar.md)</small> He set his imperial capital in Suzail. The world remembers that troops on the surface led by Zanzibar and [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) defeated Asmodeus and ended [the Infanta Crisis](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/).

Dave in 2024: "Zanzibar is the Caesar of Faerun", and "why wouldn’t Zanzibar conquer the known world and force the elves and dwarves to stick to their remote homelands". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>

The state patron deity **Zarus**, god of sentinels, conquest and humanity triumphant, blessed the Zanzibar bloodline to rule forever, and every emperor since has claimed descent from holy blood. The emperors hold [the Glass Throne](/items/the-glass-throne/). <small>(Fort Maximillien, world background.)</small>

## Five hundred years

The Empire stood for five hundred years before the War of the Five Zanzibars. <small>(Fort Maximillien, world background.)</small> In November 2025 JL asked whether Toril would effectively become a government of the Lexicon. Dave:

> Whereas the previous line of Zanzibars resisted that for 500 years because Zanny wasn’t about to let Revan take over his part of the universe

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small>

Modle Negen, one of the four risen against the last of the line, "claims descent from the Zanzibars of old". <small>(Fort Maximillien, world background.)</small>

## The Penitent and the Melancholy

[Zanzibar the Penitent](/people/zanzibar-the-penitent/), "a holy and pious man to all but those who knew him", gave over nearly all political and military control of the Empire "to his armies, his rivals, and his laconic son", and named [Peterbilt Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) Marshall of the Western Vanguard and governor of Waterdeep. He died elderly, and his son, born Prince William Wenly Zarusson, took the Glass Throne as [Zanzibar William the Melancholy](/people/zanzibar-william-the-melancholy/). The Emperor's favoured cousin, Princess **Zythara**, a prodigy of the Arcaneum, had been stripped of her titles for defying ecclesiastical oversight of arcane study. <small>(Fort Maximillien, world background.)</small>

## The War of the Five Zanzibars

One month into William's reign four factions had risen against him, and the War of the Five Zanzibars began: a critical collapse of the continent, before a dark time preceding the ascendance of the Sovereignty. [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) takes place during its beginnings. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-and-the-war.md)</small> Dave asked JL for "the names and nicknames of the five zanzibars" for his lore <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-16)</small>, and JL sent them:

> The 5 Zanzibars were Lady Zythara the Witch Queen, General Varkas who commanded the Purple Dragon Knights, Little Lord Zellmo the child pope of the Zarus cult,  William the Melancholy (the bard prince who goes on to become Sad King Billy), and the mysterious Negen Zanzibar (an outlaw folk hero whose identity was shrouded in mystery until he was revealed to be an autognome).

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-17)</small>

The five claimants are:

- **Zanzibar William the Melancholy**, Emperor of Faerûn, on the Glass Throne; "Sad King Billy".
- **Zellmo** of the Zarus Cult, raised by the Horsecock Prophecy to believe himself the new Infanta of Zarus; "Little Lord Zellmo the child pope".
- **Zythara**, the Witch Queen of [Myth Drannor](/places/myth-drannor/).
- **Peterbilt Varkus** of the March of the Free West; "General Varkas".
- **Modle Negen** of the Negende Zonen, who claims descent from the Zanzibars of old; "Negen Zanzibar", revealed to be an autognome.

The Heroes' Hill rhyme gives the war a line:

> In old Faerun, 5 Zanzibars fought til each had lost their head.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-31)</small>

## Rival Zanzibars

In January 2024 JL proposed that "Zanzibar is now a title for the autocratic ruler of Faerun. Whenever one Zanzibar is assassinated, the usurper is declared Zanzibar." Dave: "Yes and different empires claiming to be the true Zanzibar and emperor", and "So then every power for a thousand years claimed his throne and their gods will to be Zanzibar, King of Men". JL: "The War of the 5 Zanzibars shook the world for a decade, with each army claiming to have the true Zanzibar at its head." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>

## The name after the Empire

Camp Vengeance, in Antiquity, is "the future site of Fort Zanzibar". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-05-27)</small> In the Starfall era Fort Zanzibar is "surrounded by oceans after the great melting of the 6700s", with "Punis and Paenus only a drowned memory". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

The Spelljammer of Starfall is [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), and Dave "put a statue of Big Z in there, namesake". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small> Dave: "Zanzibar is I think the most famous person in our dnd universe". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> In [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) a cyborg recited a historical poem, "4 21 5 Zanzibars, 6 Statues". <small>(Kol Hanaka's character sheet)</small>
