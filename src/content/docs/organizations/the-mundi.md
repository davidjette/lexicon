The **Mundi** — the moon people — are the white moon-elves of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), who dwell inside the city's great oaks and call themselves the protectors of its crystal gardens. They have lived there for many centuries under a king, **Chad**, watcher of the stones and destroyer of [Drek](/organizations/the-drek/), holding a protective diadem against a prophecy. They cannot name who built the city, who carved its stones, or who made its guardians.

## THE KING'S ACCOUNT

> I am Chad, watcher of the stones and the destroyer of Drek. Outsiders are very rare in this place… You are in our home. We are the protectors of the crystal gardens, this slice of the moon. We live in harmony with the water, stone and air, and we harvest the breath of the mountain to sustain and strengthen our people. We have lived here for many, many, many centuries. But we are not alone here.

They dwell inside the city's great oaks — carved rooms and balconies rising through the trunks, a helix staircase of knotted branches, and a beam of soft light from the roots that carries them between the parapets. Their king sits on an oaken throne surrounded by servants.

## WHAT THEY DO NOT KNOW

Asked where the city came from, Chad answers:

> We know not who built these walls or carved out the stones. Our ancestors grew these great oaks around the crystals to protect them, and to give our people a home… We know not who built the guardians, or the great steel tunnels, or the ring and orb.

They have guarded an object for centuries without knowing what it is, in a city whose builders they cannot name, beneath a machine they have no word for. The cause is [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

## THE DIADEM AND THE PROPHECY

The Mundi exist to hold one object against one prophecy. A child god granted them the place, Chad says, and was betrayed by the ancestors of the Drek who invited **Ayror** in. So long as the Mundi keep their protective **diadem**, "their evil magics cannot take hold." The prophecy they guard against runs:

> To find the seven princes betrayed, and don the diadem on the empty throne.

<small>The Terminal Room beneath the city holds eight thrones around a runed stone table. Seven hold short skeletons in diadems identical to Chad's. The eighth is empty. Worn on a throne, the room reveals itself as an academy's instruction room from before the Betrayal, and the "seven princes betrayed" are sitting there already.</small>

## CONDUCT

The Mundi are hospitable for as long as they are impressed. They bow to [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) and ask its bearer whether he has come to claim the rightful throne of Motherstone; they marvel at the dragonborn Yoshi and are deathly terrified of Magnus. Their king is distant, withholds warnings from the party as they explore, and is more forthcoming with Silverbane's bearer than with anyone else, sowing distrust. When the Company's mining operation blasts open the Deepgate, Chad's pledge is a retreat to the Drembles and a last stand there if need be.
