<small>— the Saharel card, sic on "Banefaes". See [Aurora & Astra Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/).</small>

**Fae progenitors · The five elven houses · Bearers of the Bane blades**

*Also known as:* the five Banefae

**The Banefae** are the five original progenitors of the high elven races, fae of the Moon who came to Toril after the Starfall. Each carried a [Bane blade](/items/the-bane-blades/), a shard of Perfect moonstone, and their houses became Gallidan, Tudyx, Carls, Atlas and the elves of Avalon. They hunted on the Moon before the Starfall, crossed into Ydryssyl, were exiled and became the Elves, and built Starfall Tower atop the Spire that became [Sigil](/places/sigil/). In Antiquity [Drefanmetra](/people/the-waning-one/), the Waning One, united the remaining heads of the houses to steal the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/). They attacked [Despondos](/places/despondos/), enslaved [Tylerjynex](/people/tylerjinex/) to reach the Penteract, and planned to unite their blades into the Fatherstone. The Queens of [Netheril](/places/netheril/) and the Grey Sage defeated them, took their home, and cast them into Despondos.

**Nimue**, "aka Vivien, one of the five Banefae and founder of Avalon", foresaw the rise of Netheril and sought a human king to rival it. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.)</small>

## On the Moon

The Banefae evolved as fae on Ydryssyl and the Moon. They hunted Changelings and crossed into Ydryssyl, and in time left the Moon. <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).)</small>

On the Moon before the Starfall, "Galidan, Tudiks, Viven, Carls, and Atlas are hunting unicorns":

> They are pre-language hunters who stand out from the others of their kind by adopting names and specific forms, and by making real objects with artful skill and fine furs and wools and precious metals.

<small>— Arc V, Starfall Part IV. See [Lunar Unicorns](/organizations/lunar-unicorns/).</small>

The same passage has them attack strangers on sight, "assuming they are dragons who have taken humanoid forms to fool them."

In the deep past "Drefanmetra is saving the five Banefae and the dragons fight each other and then lose a battle against the aboleth." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-11)</small> On the party's own return to the Moon, Dave put the choice to them: "Will you guys want to save the Banefae and the other fae on the Moon before it’s impact with the Egg or are you skipping that side quest to go straight to the Obliviator". <small>(Sic on "it’s". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-06)</small>

## Exile and Starfall Tower

When the Moon was cut off from the Earth and the Feywild and Shadowfell split away, the Banefae were exiled and became the Elves. They built **Starfall Tower** atop the Spire, forming Sigil, though St. Cloud reached the top of the spire and set up house before them. <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).)</small>

Onyxbane's memory, given to the party in the Time of Troubles, names the families and the place. A **Masked Prophet** "led us to the Gates of Izar Erori and showed Gallidan, Nimue, Atlas, Carls, and Tudix the place to build Starfall Tower. The other families left, but the Carls stayed at Izar, and we kept the histories." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> In the Time of Troubles the era held "Sigil ruled by the Banefae". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small>

## The Bane blades

The blades are "moonblades cut from A pristine shard of the primordial moon, FRAGMENTS OF THE FATHERSTONE, often called the BANES". <small>(The Waxing Together rune. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-09)</small> A Baneblade also opens the way into Despondos: the Sword Coast Trading Company asked of [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/), "How can they access Despondos without a baneblade, or do they have one?" <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

## Drefanmetra's Banefae

[The Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/) is Drefanmetra, a version of [Drefan](/people/drefan/) from another arc, who went forward in time to preempt St. Cloud "by rganizing the Banefae (the remaining heads of each elvish house) to steal the mammon machine for themselves and send him back to try again." <small>(Sic on "rganizing". Dave, 2026-09-14.)</small>

In Antiquity the united Banefae went for the Seat of Death:

> The Banefae United by Drefanmetra attack the maze and enslaved TJ to get into the Penteract at the heart of Despondos
>
> Their plan is to unite the bane blades into the Fatherstone and use it to trap the power of the Netherese and destroy all mortal souls in a rite to undo time and restore the Moon

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

[Tylerjynex](/people/tylerjinex/) had built the Penteract to keep the Banefae and the Netherese from the Seat. Archmage **Valen**, brother of the Tudyx queen Alen, rode with the Waning One. Not every house followed him: "There’s another atlas here in Antiquity who refused to go along with Valen/Waning One - Atlas III", "As did Calix". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> Onyxbane remembered that the prophet who guided Carls Jr to the City of Dis promised "we could end the rule of Gods and mankind in one fell swoop, send their souls into Oblivion while capturing that power for elvenkind again. And that with anout power, and with no mortal rivals, we could restore the MOON and leave the realms of dwarves and men destroyed and forgotten for eternity." <small>(Sic on "anout". Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>

The Banefae also made war on Netheril. Dave: "It’s likely that Brightmantle and the Banefae who invade Netheril in your era’s past got this idea from the Netherese themselves, who in a way got it from the Cypherene Orcs". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small>

## Defeat and banishment

"They are confronted by the Queens and Grey Sage and defeated." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> The Queens, [Astra and Aurora Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/), took the Banefae's home while the Banefae "were feeding our people into oblivion", held the cities by the time they returned, and cast them into Despondos, "where all of them perished." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small>

The campaign still brought Netheril down: "But Drefanmetras efforts still lead to the fall of Netheril and Brightmantles victory, and St Cloud has to wait another 1000 years to try again…" <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

In that loop the defeat split the Carls from the other elves. Dave: "this defeat left their colonies in the underdark isolated and their hatred of men and the moon gods of the elves was cemented" / "They gave in to their worse slaver tendencies and became the Drow, and Starfall tower was more or less empty for hundreds of years" / "As Sigil was built around it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

## Against the party

Before the battle Dave told the table: "You are about to fight the banefae and possibly prevent that invasion, so you all might open the door to the Netherese having no foe to interrupt them in whatever they are doing, and the world where their civilization is not also obliviated is very different indeed." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> The Waxing power was "available to everyone with bane blades in this next battle, including the three main enemies!" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-09)</small>

In [Despondos](/places/despondos/) the party confronted Valen, Carl's Jr. and the Waning One at the peak of Sigil. They charmed the Waning One, convinced Carl's Jr. and the Drow to join them, and left Valen in a puddle of acid, and "the Bane blades merge and evolve." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> Dave: "You guys just saved the Drow and prevented their split from the elves". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

The five houses end the chronicle with the party. [Drefan](/people/drefan/) is a Gallidan, [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) is Carls IV, [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) carries Glassbane, [Kara](/people/kara/) is Tylerjynex's heir to Copperbane, and [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/)'s mother was a long descendant of the Els. See [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).
