**Drow nobles · Bearers of Onyxbane · Allies from Arc VII**

**Carls Jr and the Drow** are what is left of the Carls, a drow noble house reduced to its hardiest survivors in the Underdark, and an allied people of the final battle at the Egg. The line was once numerous enough to fill a tower and was worn away over generations in far-flung planes, foolish quests and botched robberies, until the survivors hid themselves away underground. **Carls Jr** leads them. At [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s funeral the house pledged itself to the rightful heir of **Onyxbane**.

## The decline of the house

The family's decline is told twice over. The card gives the long attrition — a tower full of them, then isolation in far-flung planes, foolish quests and botched robberies. [Marcus](/people/marcus/) supplies a closer view of one of them: "Carls III probably died 300 years ago. Her ghost is still as racist as ever." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V, on a lich's phylactery in Stonecypher's trunk.)</small>

> The whole tower was full of them, but one by one the family found itself isolated in far flung planes, dying in foolish quests, botched robberies, you name it. The heartiest hid away in the Underdark.

<small>— the Carls Jr Ally Card.</small>

A "Carls" is among those hunting unicorns in Arc V. <small>(Starfall Part IV.)</small>

## Onyxbane

The Carls are one of the five houses descended from the **Banefae**, and their shard of Perfect moonstone is [Onyxbane](/items/the-bane-blades/). It passes to [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/), who is **Carls IV**.

## Their servants

The humans who served House Carls became [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) — the only people able to solve [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) and reach the Seat of Death at [Dis](/places/dis/). **House Carls asked them to do it, and they refused.**

## The pledge

The house's pledge is recorded at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s impromptu funeral, where the allies of that era stepped forward one by one with the armies of the world already gathering for the invasion of Netheril: "You brought light to our deep darkness and stopped us from stumbling into a calamity. You are the rightful heir of **Onyxbane**. We are with you to the end." <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>
