> They are here to do the same as the Abolethi courtesan, to convince her gloriousness to destroy the Moon.

<small>— Naga to Nefarion, on the Chromatic Elder Dragons Ally Card.</small>

**Elder dragons · Tiamat's brood · Allies at the Egg**

The **Chromatic Elder Dragons** are Tiamat's brood, and allies of the party at the Egg. They came on the same errand as [Karametra](/people/karametra/): to persuade "her gloriousness" — Tiamat — to destroy the Moon. Karametra's own working was the mirror of it, done on **Bahamut** instead, and it is what put the Moon in the path of Cthulhu's egg. In the final arc the party killed Tiamat defending the platinum temple while Karametra cast Move Planet, and her brood then sided with the party.

The two speakers both appear elsewhere in the chronicle. **Nefarion** is a black dragon in St. Cloud's service at Starfall, who "returns immediately with the flayed body and skin of [Drefan](/people/drefan/)." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

## The deep past

In the deep past the dragons "went into the ocean to try to kill the aboleth and destroy Zoth, this is because they believed Zoth was calling the Leviathan". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> They fought each other, and then lost a battle against the aboleth. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-11)</small> [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) had brought them against the aboleth to reach Zoth's ship, steering them through that belief. See [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/).

Dave set out Tiamat's fate: "Tiamat and her forces are destroyed before the egg arrives and she is lost in the abyss until she reconstitutes in Hell". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> His quest board for the deep past read: "Dragon Mountain, help Karametra and bring Tiamat into the team, stop the dragon war and lead them into space". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> Tiamat did not join the party.

In the final arc the party defended the platinum temple at [Dragon Mountain](/places/dragon-mountain/) while Karametra cast Move Planet, against [the Tarrasque](/items/the-terrasque/), some dragons, and then Tiamat herself. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small> After the Tarrasque, Dave promised "an all out dragon ambush with Tiamat". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small> The party killed Tiamat there and held the temple. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small> That killing is the destruction "before the egg arrives": she re-formed in Hell, and her brood then sided with the party.
