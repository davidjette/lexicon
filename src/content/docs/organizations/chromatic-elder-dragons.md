> They are here to do the same as the Abolethi courtesan, to convince her gloriousness to destroy the Moon.

<small>— Naga to Nefarion, on the Chromatic Elder Dragons Ally Card.</small>

**Elder dragons · Tiamat's brood · Allies at the Egg**

The **Chromatic Elder Dragons** are Tiamat's brood, and allies of the party at the Egg. They came on the same errand as [Karametra](/people/karametra/): to persuade "her gloriousness" — Tiamat — to destroy the Moon. Karametra's own working was the mirror of it, done on **Bahamut** instead, and it is what put the Moon in the path of Cthulhu's egg. How or when the chromatics changed sides is not recorded.

The two speakers both appear elsewhere in the chronicle. **Nefarion** is a black dragon in St. Cloud's service at Starfall, who "returns immediately with the flayed body and skin of [Drefan](/people/drefan/)." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

## At the Egg

**White — Tiamats Voice: "Cast Divine Word."**

**Black — Avarice of Ages: "Creatures in a 90-foot cone make a DC 24 DEX Save, taking 20d6 each of fire, cold, acid and lightning damage on a fail, or half on a success."**
