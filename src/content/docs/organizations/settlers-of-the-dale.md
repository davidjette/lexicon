**Human commoners · Suzara's City and Camp Vengeance · Allies from Arc VI**

The **Settlers of the Dale** are the people of **Suzara's City** and **Camp Vengeance**, and an allied people of the final battle at the Egg. Two things were done to them and undone: a working of Archmage **Valen**'s that their pledge remembers as "a rain of snakes," and the rule of **Gunch**, Suzara's brother.

## Suzara and Gunch

The session record has Gunch "cowed by the encounter but seems very…" and, as an optional beat, attempting to ambush the party as they leave. Suzara himself "sent his boy SUE to the elf witch to seek healing." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>

## The pledge

> You saved us from Valen and a rain of snakes, and freed us from the cruel whip of Gunch. We are with you to the end.

<small>— the Settlers of the Dale Ally Card.</small>

The same people are named "The People of Suzara's City and Camp Vengeance" at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s impromptu funeral, where the allies of that era stepped forward one by one with the armies of the world already gathering for the invasion of Netheril, and there the pledge runs "You saved us from Valen and a rain of snakes, and freed us from Gunch's cruel whip." <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>

## At the Egg

**White — God of Protection: "Grant +2 AC to all allies within 90 ft until the end of your next turn."**

**Black — Suzara's Legacy: "You gain an additional Action this turn."**

<small>Theirs is the only card in the set of 41 whose sacrifice buys the party a whole turn's worth of tempo rather than damage.</small>
