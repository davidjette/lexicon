**Faction · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Keepers of [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/)**

**Also known as:** Heralds of Dust · Dusters

**The Dustmen**, the Heralds of Dust, are a faction of [Sigil](/places/sigil/) "Who believe Everyone is Dead". Headquartered in [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/) under Factol Skall, they manage the city's dead, and they pay [Pharod](/people/pharod/), master of the corpse-pickers who bring bodies in. The party woke in the Dustmen's Mortuary, marked with [fatemarks](/lore/fatemarks/), and escaped it.

## The faction

<small>(DM notes)</small>

- **Factol:** Skall
- **Headquarters:** the Mortuary
- **Aligned plane:** Hades
- **Members:** corpse-collectors, undead
- **Epithets:** Dusters, Dustmen
- **Role:** manage the dead

## Belief

> "We Dustmen believe that life is an Illusion, a false existence. True Death is the final release from the Cycle of pain and suffering."
>
> <small>— a Dustman (DM notes)</small>

## Dhall

**Dhall** is the Dustman bookkeeper in the Mortuary, and can be persuaded to explain how the party came to be in the Mortuary. <small>(DM notes)</small> His words:

> "An ancient hand bade the Caretakers return you to this Torment."
>
> "You are marked by fate, it is not for me to strike thy name from the Book of the Living."
>
> "You arrived as we all do, a tumble down a long chute and then a heavy thud."
>
> <small>— Dhall (DM notes)</small>

On Pharod:

> "Pharod is a collector, a scavenger of the dead. He resides in Ragpicker Square. If anyone knows how you came to be here, it is him."
>
> <small>— Dhall (DM notes)</small>

## The corpse trade

Pharod's corpse collectors take bodies and deposit them in the Mortuary for junk, and Pharod, master of the corpse-pickers, is paid by the Dustmen. The Dustmen offer to buy corpses. <small>(DM notes)</small> Pharod's corpse-pickers are hard to find, and they did pick up and dump the bodies of the party. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Outside the Mortuary, after the party had escaped death and been jumped by thugs, a corpse-picker came to them and asked "these yours?" It did not speak otherwise, except to say that it was Pharod's, and "FOR PHAROD".

**Graham Cracker** is a kind Dustman with information on the corpse-pickers. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Places of Interest

**The Mortuary:** the Dustmen's headquarters, where the dead of Sigil arrive down a long chute.\
**Ragpickers Square:** Pharod's home, where vendors sell, among other things, Dustman shrouds.
