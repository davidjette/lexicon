The **Sword Coast Trading Company** is an imperial charter house that dates back to the remnants of the Old Empire which used to rule both the North and South from [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/). Under **Wainwright St. Cloud** it grew from a Baldur's Gate shipping concern into an interplanar power, built its fortune on the [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and on the secrets of the **Infernal Machines**, mass-produced the **Warforged** and the **Arcane Core**, and had St. Cloud named Chancellor of Waterdeep. It is run by **Dox**, a changeling collective whose members each wear the face of the chairman. After two Battles of Motherstone and the destruction of its hub in [Sigil](/places/sigil/) it is crippled and scattered.

## DOCKSIDE GOSSIP

The origin story the waterfront tells is a merchant's fairy tale. **Wainwright St. Cloud** "arose to initial prominence by traversing the route from Calmport through Straights at Suldolphor in less than four days, a remarkable feat." He has always sailed with a silent partner, "a dark elf known as the Navigator who some say can control the weather." A client who owed him a fortune paid in the rights to the *Evening Star*, whose keel had just been laid, and St. Cloud custom-built his perfect vessel, the Company's flagship privateer, with a cannon on the bow.

The same file records what the Company actually is: "a charter house that dates back to the remnants of the Old Empire which used to rule both the North and South… with its capital at [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/)." And what it wants: to monopolise trade on the Inland Sea, which is why it backs the King of Cormyr's ambition to crown himself a new Emperor.

## THE WAR WIZARDS' REPORT

In Arc III the [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) of the Arcaneum reported to their masters on the theft of [Una](/people/una/) from their campus tower in [Suzail](/places/suzail/). They tracked the thieves "to the Charter House of the Sword Coast Trading Co. near the docks," and wrote that they "have long regarded the Company as a threat to Cormyran domination over trade and military matters, and that the chairman of the company is suspected of being an agent of Asmodeus or some other extraplanar power." They could not enter: "we currently believe the inside has been transformed into a pocket dimension or similar demi-verse which is preventing us from entrance." From the Charter House the thieves took the Infanta to the platform of the train to Baldur's Gate. <small>(Report signed by [Celion](/people/celion/). Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small>

## THE SCTC ARCHIVES

On its own account the Company is "the largest imperial charter company in the Western sea and beyond," which grew from a Baldur's Gate shipping concern into an interplanar power that "altered the face of civilization." Its fortune was built on the [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), mined at a rate unprecedented in the world and credited with "altering the face of civilization," and on the secrets of the **Infernal Machines**.

The Company found Motherstone during the ten years the party spent in the slow time of Calix's vault. It had only begun colonising Snowdown when the party went into the mountain; by the time they emerged it had turned the city into a massive moonstone mining operation. St. Cloud had forgotten Motherstone along with everyone else, and once it was remembered it became a centrepiece of his empire. A workers' rebellion started by the party, and the army of the ten-year-old Infanta of the Moon, then pushed the Company out — St. Cloud's first real blow since the party sank his brother's ship.

**What it built.** From the Deepmaker at Motherstone, mass-produced **Warforged** war-constructs, and the **Arcane Core** that drives its airships — derived from the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/). From the Netherese machines, consumer devices wired to the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/)'s leylines, able to "broadcast any message… into the minds of nearby sentient beings." The archives record of its merchant marine that once its forces outnumbered the standing army of Waterdeep, the Company began to dictate that city's policy and eventually had St. Cloud named Chancellor of the realm. Dave's sheet for the [Arielle](/items/the-arielle/) calls its core "an arcane contraption called an Elemental Core. It can store (and trap) up to five elementals and use their power to operate and enhance the ship." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-06)</small>

**Special Projects.** Hunts the buried [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/), works the "Calix puzzle" of the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) for more than two years without success, then uses the Palantir to observe the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) remotely and override its control to an administrative key held by St. Cloud; and compiles full dossiers on the reborn [Infantas](/lore/the-infanta/) of Order, Time, Destiny, Reason and Revelry, and partial ones on Moon, War, Nature, Forge, Death, Sea and Murder. Its file on [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) records: "The Company believes Magnus' patron god [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) could have been the dwarven king who built Motherstone. Review of cursed historical record confirms this." <small>(SCTC Archives, the file on the heroes.)</small>

**The Anachron.** Special Projects "declassified the object known as the Anachron for a brief time before reclassifying it and sequestering all files." R&D "opened a propulsion lab devoted to the Anachron and devised the Arcane Core which powers the dozen or so Company airships that were ever built." The archives describe the device itself as able to make "a temporary portal" for "rapid travel across planes"; the device itself the Company put back under seal, and what it kept was the Arcane Core. On the legend that the Anachron warps time as it warps space, the researchers concluded that it "may require a mind more vast than the God of Knowledge, or a perfect moonstone so far beyond its half life that it was twice as old as the oldest moonstone on Faerun (an hypothetical Phase V the researchers called Perfect Dark)." "After R&D determined that such a task was impossible, Special Projects resequestered the Anachron." <small>(SCTC Archives, the Anachron file. Sic on "an hypothetical." When St. Cloud later delivered the device to the Arcaneum it "was inert" (Arc III, Part VIII Handouts). Full account at [The Anachron](/items/the-anachron/).)</small>

## INSIDE ST. CLOUD TOWER

The organisational chart in the archives omits what is visible the moment anyone walks in. The Company is run by **Dox** — a changeling collective whose members each wear the exact face of the chairman. Inside the Tower in Sigil, "almost all the staff are dressed and look exactly like Wainwright St. Cloud." Every department — shipping, manufacturing, security, R&D, Special Projects — is headed by a Dox distinguished by a Greek letter: Dox Xi in Manufacturing, Dox Omicron in Security. The chief executive is **[Dox Prime](/people/dox-prime/)**, the only one who meets the Boss in person.

Behind a portrait in the executive suite lies the **Old Man's House**, St. Cloud's millennia-old palace, emblazoned throughout with the sigil of **Calix**.

## WHAT IT WAS FOR

St. Cloud's design is the **Apotheosis of Man** — marrying the Mammon Machine to [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) — which he sold to the Netherese as the making of a New Star and which is in fact the waking of [Cthulhu](/lore/the-leviathan/). He is the **Traveler** — "It's their destiny to destroy the Traveler," as one of his own creatures put it.

## PRESENT DISPOSITION

After two Battles of Motherstone and the breaking of its Sigil hub — the Lady of Pain destroyed the Old Man's House in a blaze of radioactive starlight — the Company is "crippled and scattered, leaving assets and employees up for grabs." Its products, however, are still in people's homes, and they are still wired to the Obliviator's leylines.

The party kept a captured Dox in the brig of their ship. With the Company defeated, he would trade information for his life, and Dave ruled that he would "make a deal with Gritty for his life to serve Asmodeus and Darkcember": "He’s convinced Asmodeus has the best chance to reach the egg first so serving him will have the same outcome as serving Cthulhu himself, since they will become one". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> Gritty is the devil [Griddy](/people/griddy-champagne/). In January 2020 Dave told the party: "Every Company product is now broadcasting knowledge of the Leviathan to every brain nearby". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-13)</small>

When St. Cloud fled the "present" into the past, he left behind the St. Cloud Trading Company, the true Company in Sigil, born from the Sword Coast Trading Company on Faerun. Its assets went up for auction, where [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) secretly outbid the party's [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), and the winner, [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), was "the Company" from then on.
