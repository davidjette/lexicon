**Gang · the northern sewers and the Sub-line District undercity, Sharn · Destroyed**

The Drain Snakes were a gang that fortified the northern sewer network beneath the Ziggurat and the Sub-line District of Sharn and blocked every safe route out of the Lower City. [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) paid them for captives, which made them the street end of the pipeline that ran through the Zeir residence and [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) and out into Project E.D.E.N. The party destroyed a patrol of theirs in the Grand Aqueduct and then their northern lair in a single brutal fight, and the gang no longer exists.

## WHAT THE FACTIONS SAID THEY WERE

Accounts of the gang come from [Blair](/people/blair/) and [Preacher](/people/preacher/), from what was found on the bodies in the Grand Aqueduct, and from what was found in the lair. The three do not describe the same organisation.

To [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) the Drain Snakes were an obstacle: a gang that had fortified the northern sewer network beneath the Ziggurat and the Sub-line District and blocked every safe route out of the Lower City. Cogborn families sealed in the collapsing tunnel pocket called the Pit could not move while they held it, and [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) had been escorting vulnerable groups through gang-controlled stretches.

To the Queen of [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) they were a trespass on her territory, and their removal was the price she set for opening her routes to the Cogborn — and for releasing [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/).

## EVIDENCE FROM THE BODIES — GRAND AQUEDUCT PATROL

A patrol of Drain Snake thugs ambushed the party in the aqueduct's lower levels and was destroyed. One of the dead was wearing a **BioTec Security jacket**. A dead BioTec Security agent lay nearby with his badge still intact, and two more BioTec agents were found floating in the sewer chamber further down. What BioTec was doing in those tunnels is unrecorded.

## EVIDENCE FROM THE LAIR

The northern lair fell in a single brutal fight. In it the party found a kidnapped tourist, still alive, and piles of stolen goods that were carried out and distributed to the Cogborn. And they found a note indicating that Malrik Zeir had been paying the Drain Snakes for captives — the note that places them at the street end of the same pipeline that ran through the Zeir residence, the Children of Ember, and out into Project E.D.E.N.

**Territory:** the northern and north-eastern sewer network beneath the Ziggurat and the Sub-line District; the lower levels of the Grand Aqueduct.

**Status:** destroyed. Blair honoured her side of the bargain the same afternoon.
