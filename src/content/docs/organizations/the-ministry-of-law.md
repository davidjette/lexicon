**Type:** Government, Judiciary · **Seat:** northeast of the Great Plaza, Korth · **Status:** Active

*Also known as:* the Ministry · the High Court

The Ministry of Law is the judiciary of the Empire, seated northeast of the Great Plaza in Korth: where laws are made and negotiated, where trials are held, and where the advocates work. Its doctrine descends from the old Galifar courts, and in a capital case the accused is the condemned rather than the defendant. The Inevitables worked inside it: [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) spent months tracking a Star Advocate named **Deirdre Moro**, killed her in her own apartment, took her face and her clearance, and prosecuted a capital case in her name, with [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) as her paralegal and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) as her registered bodyguard. The Ministry lost that case.

## THE BUILDING

Northeast across the Great Plaza, next door to [the Ministry of Records](/organizations/the-ministry-of-records-hall-of-records/). There is a separate side entrance for officers and upper floors full of advocates.

- **The High Court** — capital cases, on an upper level. Eight judges. Overlooked by a balcony holding an **Iron Maiden**, a device that kills the condemned while leaving the corpse intact for use.
- **The jail** — the northeast corner of the same complex. Two levels of cells, prisoners held in varying states of distress and undress, watched by [Imperial Red Guard](/organizations/the-imperial-red-guard/).
- **The dumpster yard** — behind the building, down a stair. A **corpse chute** empties the dead into stacks for reuse.

<small>Court, execution device, cells and corpse chute are all one structure. A person can be charged, condemned, killed, and enter Imperial service as a body without ever leaving the building.</small>

## KARRNATHI LAW, AS FOUND IN THE LEGAL SECTION

The system descends from the old Galifar courts and was genuinely reformist when it split off: it created a judicial bureaucracy notionally independent of the throne, and its panels are mythologised as paragons who have exonerated people later celebrated as heroes of the people.

In practice it does not seek truth. **It seeks atonement.** The doctrine is a kind of original sin — the condemned may be innocent of the charge but is guilty of something, and absolution requires submission to the will of the court, which stands in for the Emperor.

- **You are the condemned, not the defendant.** Guilt is the premise.
- There is **no defence counsel**, **no discovery**, and no complex evidentiary rules — only a panel of judges whose stated function is to interrogate the prosecution.
- **Acquittal is common.** The panel is not a rubber stamp; it is an auditor of the prosecutor's work.
- In place of a defence there is a **kin and patron system**: a person of standing qualified by the state — a spouse, a child, a sibling, an employer or a patron — may testify to the condemned's value to society.
- **Sabotage, espionage and embezzlement all carry death.** Falsifying records carries a term equivalent to twenty-five human years, scaled by race and expected lifespan.
- **Monster races receive harsher penalties, are denied advocates entirely, and have execution methods defined specifically so that they can be executed at all.**

## THE REDACTED BRIEF

The Ministry's briefs are issued redacted: name and identifying details struck out throughout, so the advocate learns who they are killing when the condemned turns around. This is the brief pushed under Deirdre Moro's door, verbatim in substance.

**CHARGE.** An Imperial medical records keeper, to be prosecuted at first light for falsifying Imperial documents, espionage, sabotage and embezzlement.

**SUPPORTING EVIDENCE.** Inflated casualty, supply and staffing reports from the Anduin theatre. A written assessment that the Battle of Anduin would be lost for want of supplies and that surrender was acceptable. Personal letters mocking Imperial duty and hierarchy. A documented love of orcish music and dance.

**NOTE.** Every letter is addressed to a beloved sister.

**AS READ IN COURT.** Falsifying Imperial records; false claims of defeat of the Imperial army at Anduin; suspected sabotage and false orders to surrender; embezzlement of medical supplies and Imperial wartime resources; conspiracy to defraud official records; perjury; adultery; idolatry; high treason; and sodomy. <small>Several judges registered admiration at the clarity of the delivery.</small>

## MINISTRY-WIDE DIRECTIVE

Recovered from a paralegal's preliminary notes. **Mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties**, with executions expected over the following weeks. <small>Bantide Moro reports separately that Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent as though the Empire had obtained a list of every name, with people pulled from every posting on invented charges and executions running for days.</small>

## THE HANDLER — VICE ADMIRAL FREYD MERKIN

Star Advocates report to [Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/), who handed a prosecutor a redacted brief against her own sister without telling her. When it failed he intervened personally to prosecute from the floor, and was forced to concede on the record that bearing witness for kin *"is her right."*

> "I thought this would be a chance for you to show your absolute allegiance and commitment to the Empire. Was I wrong?"\
> \
> "You're too credulous, Moro. You believe too much. You think everything is for the good of the Emperor. You have to understand that there are contravening forces you don't understand."

## THE CASE THE MINISTRY LOST

Korth Ep 13. The condemned turned to face the advocate and was unmistakably Deirdre Moro's sister: **Bantide Moro**. Watching her sister recite her death, Bantide threw herself on the mercy of the court and confessed to having failed the Emperor.

Then the advocate broke script. She set down the advocate's role, took up the kin's — a right the Ministry's own doctrine grants — and testified that she could not reconcile the charges with the woman she grew up with, that her first assumption had been that her sister was framed, and that she did not understand where the evidence came from. Merkin objected that Advocate Moro had confused her role, and conceded that it was her right. **The panel deliberated and found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims**, granted the state one additional month to produce evidence, and remanded the condemned to the local garrison.

The Ministry then lost her entirely. Bantide cast *feign death* in her cell under Red Guard supervision, was carried out as a corpse and dropped down the building's own corpse chute, and was revived in the yard. She is officially recorded as a suicide.

<small>Her real post: head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital and the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years, supplying medical support from surgery to revivification. Codename **the Nightingale**.</small>

## Star Advocate clearance

A Star Advocate has standing to investigate nearly any Imperial facility. One is currently boarding a boat up the river toward the Empress with the Grand Lodge assignment in her hand.

**Known personnel:** [Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/) · **Deirdre Moro**, Star Advocate (dead; impersonated) · **Graham Cracker**, paralegal (dead; impersonated) · eight judges of the High Court, unnamed.

**Holdings:** The High Court and its Iron Maiden. The jail and the corpse chute. The advocates' floors. A ministry-wide docket against everyone who ever filed a casualty return.

**The Redacted Brief:** a capital case with the target's identity struck out, issued to an advocate who will not learn who it is until the condemned turns around in the dock.
