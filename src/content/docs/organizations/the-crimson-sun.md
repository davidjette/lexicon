**Type:** Government, Secret Service · **Status:** Active · **Seat:** Korth

*Also known as:* *separately and unresolvedly, the name of a dragon.*

The Crimson Sun is the Empire's secret service, seated at Korth — a limited, elite order of Masters and apprentices, mostly Knights, Paladins or Sorcerers, working as spies and rebel hunters, whose Master sits on the Imperial Council by right. It was led by [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), sworn protector to three Emperors under the title Eternal Guardian, until his assassination; Hayman Maw holds the Master's chair after him, and the order's most famous High Inquisitor, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), is three years dead in Sharn. The same three words separately name a dragon, who fathered a child on Erandis Vol, and the connection between the two usages is unresolved.

## WHERE THE FILE CAME FROM

The Inevitables' material on the Crimson Sun came out of five separate acquisitions, none of which were looking for the Crimson Sun at the time: an Imperial service summary of the kind issued to new Inquisitors; the rebel observation dossier on the Crona–Blaze household; what Locke Pierce said out loud at Mt. Silicon while he was arming the Ruby Nova; the testimony of a black dragon on the floor of his own lair beneath the Korramont; and Master Armhair's confiscated manuscript, recovered from the Hall of Records under a false index. Two of them are not describing the same kind of thing.

## THE IMPERIAL RECORD — THE CRIMSON SUN

The Crimson Sun is the Imperial Empire's Secret Service. It's member consist of "Masters" and their "apprentice". They are mostly Knights / Paladins or Sorcerers and are a limited, elite group. They work as spy's and rebel hunters and are a feared government agency.

In order:

- Apprentice
- Inquisitor
- High Inquisitor
- Master

[Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) was the leader of the Crimson Sun, his apprentice is High Inquisitor [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/). After Varyn Crona's death, Hayman Maw became the head of the Crimson Sun.

A giant Crimson Sun symbol is carved into marble in the throne room of [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/).

<small>Note the seat on the Council. The Master of the Crimson Sun sits on [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) by right — first Varyn Crona, then Hayman Maw, listed on the roster as "Human Sorcerer: Current head of the Crimson Sun." The Empire's secret police is not a bureau under a ministry. It is one of the thirteen heads of state.</small>

## REBEL OBSERVATION FILE — CRONA–BLAZE

Rebel intelligence confirms that Esther was the personal apprentice of Varyn Crona, a High-Elf Paladin, head of Emperor Kaius III's [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/), and former head of the Crimson Sun. Recently recovered Imperial records indicate that Varyn served as head Paladin to Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III, and was appointed the Emperor's sworn protector from birth under the title **"Eternal Guardian."** His influence shaped Imperial military doctrine for over centuries.

Field analysis indicates that Varyn's death was not the result of rebel activity, despite official Imperial claims. Multiple intelligence fragments point to members of the Council of 13 as the likely architects of his assassination. Following this discovery, Esther, seemingly acting with the Emperor's approval, systematically eliminated all council members, including some of their immediate family members.

The Crimson Sun's own Master was murdered by the government he served, and the Crimson Sun's High Inquisitor then killed the government. Why the Empire tolerated that, or why the Emperor blessed it, is not explained anywhere in the Imperial record. Every attempt to look into it — including one archivist merely asking after Varyn Crona at the Sharn Library — has drawn [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) within the hour.

## MT. SILICON — LOCKE PIERCE, IN HIS OWN CONTROL ROOM

Standing in a chamber of screens inside a Warforged stronghold, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) explained his countermeasure to the ringing of the bells: a weaponised catastrophe, a controlled recreation of the Mourning itself. He spoke of **the Crimson Sun and the power it once unleashed** — and then he activated the device, and a red dome bloomed silently across the Mournland horizon and was visible from Thronehold.

He named the weapon **the Ruby Nova**. He did not explain the connection between the name of the Empire's secret service and the power he had just demonstrated, and nobody in the room asked him to. <small>Locke Pierce is a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) and rose through the Empire's ranks alongside Esther Crona.</small>

## TESTIMONY OF THE DRAGON VALTREX

Given freely, in his own lair beneath the Korramont, immediately after the Inevitables broke the bell-collar off his neck. The dragon had been bled by the Empire for three years and was not, by his own account, entirely sane for most of it.

Valtrex stated that the elvish houses which fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline; that **Erandis Vol bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun**; and that [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) has used his stolen blood to sire her own heirs, making him their father. He then destroyed the original bell moulds and ordered the party never to return.

In the Imperial summary the Crimson Sun is a police organisation with four ranks and a symbol in the throne room. In the dragon's mouth it is a person — a dragon who mated with the Queen of Death and fathered a line. Both usages are current, and the connection between them is unresolved.

## MASTER ARMHAIR'S CONFISCATED MANUSCRIPT

Hidden in the Hall of Records under a false index as a treatise on the mating habits of creatures of the Underdark, recovered by the Inevitables in Korth. It contains a family tree of Eberron's dragons traced back to the progenitors, an identification of Valtrex's father, and an extended thread connecting the **Mark of Death**, the birth of **Erandis Vol**, and the same dragon that Armhair believes is currently occupying the Imperial throne.

Set against the Litany's promise that *"the dragon will be reborn,"* the party's conclusion was that Uriel is attempting to bear a son by a dragon. <small>Renn Tal reached the same junction from the other direction, working on Varyn Crona, the Eternal Guardian and the Forge of Binding.</small>

## APPENDIX: THE OTHER SUN

Varyn's Echo, raised at the Forge of Binding beneath Sharn, repeated a fragment before it shattered: *"…we'll bleed the sun, eternal night. Our bloodline shall reign…"* Whether the sun in that sentence is the order or the dragon is not established.

## CURRENT DISPOSITION

Hayman Maw holds the Master's chair and the Council seat, if the Council still meets — the Empire has never announced that its thirteen heads of state were murdered by its own Lord Commander, and the Assembly tower in Korth has not been reported empty. The order's most famous Inquisitor is three years dead in Sharn and her sword is missing. The symbol is still carved in the marble of a throne room nobody has sat in for a decade.

**Known members:** [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) (Master, dead) · **Hayman Maw** (Master, current) · [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) (High Inquisitor, dead).

**Holdings:** Crona Manor in Korth. A seat on the Imperial Council. The marble of the throne room floor.

**Rivals and entanglements:** [The Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) — devised by the Crimson Sun as a covert project, run by a handler codenamed Godseed, and allowed to grow past control. [The Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) — the force Esther commanded as its Director, built by dissolving the Sharn City Watch into it, and for a while the larger instrument.
