**Religious order · Outlawed by the Empire · Active and armed**

The Silver Flame is a religious order of paladins and clerics, held together by discipline, compassion and the protection of the innocent, and outlawed by the Empire. The proscription has not reduced its numbers; it has moved them onto farms, into cellars, and onto the Thrane front, where Silver Flame paladins lead cavalry against undead E.D.E.N. units raised under the Empress's command. The Flame named the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) evil before anyone had proof. When Sharn fell to the coalition it seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up the clergy, and at the first Council of Sharn it brought two demands and won both. Three years on it opposed the Thronehold Summit outright, and the Summit ended in massacre.

## THE ORDER AS IT DESCRIBES ITSELF

A faith of paladins and clerics, held together by discipline, compassion and the protection of the innocent. Service is a family trade — *all the strong ones of clan Dawnmantle do it*. Its people run to fires: Donna, a fifty-nine-year-old cleric, is the steady heart of Zilspar Farm 02, tending the wounded and comforting the frightened while the paladins are away; Zero of Leef, an elderly Tabaxi follower of the Flame, was among the local defenders who liberated Leef from the Grand Duke's illithid city guards. [Lorian](/people/lorian/) came to the Flame out of the cell beneath the Vault, and was told by **Zero-One** to let purpose and not hatred shape his path.

**The Flame's judgement on the Doves:** the Silver Flame has decided the Black Doves are obviously evil. It reached this position before the bell files existed, before the Foxtale debrief, and before anyone had stood under a Master casting.

> "The Silver Flame has decided the Black Doves are obviously evil."

## THE RITE THAT TAKES A NAME

Certain women of the faith undertake [an ancient and nearly forgotten rite that strips away the name and the past](/lore/the-silver-flame-name-stripping-rite/), emerging as an embodiment of the Flame's discipline and sacrifice and carrying a numbered codename in place of an identity. Fema Nolan took it after the death of [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) and emerged as **Zero-One**, the first woman in centuries to do so. Her daughter carries the name [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/). There is a Zero of Leef.

## The Council of Sharn

When Sharn fell to the coalition, Zero-One and Silver Flame paladins seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up the clergy. At the first session of the Council of Sharn the Flame brought two demands and won both: **outlaw the Black Doves**, and **enforce citywide cremation**. Sister Nora, Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka were tried, found guilty and executed with other high-level Iron Veil officials. The cremation directive was enacted across the city. When the Flame came for [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s body it had already been carried out of the morgue by two elven women.

Three years on the Flame and Zero-One opposed the Thronehold Summit outright. The Summit ended in massacre. At the hospital afterward, Bofric the runesmith played the fanfare of the Dawn for a representative of every faith on the continent, and the clergy took up teaching what they named the **Horn of Mourning**, congregation by congregation. Dario told that room he had broken three bells and would break every one — and that the woman who rings them must be cut down along with everyone who helped her.

## THE REBEL FACTIONS FILE — WHO THE FLAME IS

- **Zero-One (Fema Nolan)** — Paladin of the Silver Flame, born and raised in Thrane, founding member of Fulcrum and its current leader. Operates out of Zilspar, vanishes for long stretches with a select group of personal knights. Mother of [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and of Izaak. **Taken alive by E.D.E.N. units at the destruction of Zilspar Farm.**
- **Zero of Sharn** — young human cleric, covert agent of the Nest, onboarded the Falcon Crew. Convened the meeting of every faith on the continent at the hospital.
- **[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/)** — Squire Templar, born in a jungle near Q'barra, blacksmith, of clan Dawnmantle. Broke the Thirteenth Casting with KNELL and has sworn to break every remaining bell.
- **Donna** — cleric, 59, Zilspar Farm 02. *"The Flame that stays lit when the rest of the world is dark."* Unaccounted for after the farm burned.
- **Bantide Moro, "the Nightingale"** — head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital and the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years, from surgery to revivification; fitted Izaak's prosthetic leg. Tried in the Korth High Court as an Imperial records keeper, condemned in all but sentence, and asked for one thing in her cell: a holy symbol of the Silver Flame. Dario gave up his prayer book for it. She escaped by feigning death down the corpse chute.
- **Zero of Leef** — elderly Tabaxi, one of the defenders who liberated Leef alongside Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke and Princess Orange Chicken.

**Holdings:** Zilspar Farm 02, the Fulcrum stronghold in the Breeland farmsteads *(destroyed)* · a Silver Flame civilian hospital, its location unrecorded · shrines and cells across Thrane and Breland · the Thrane front itself.
