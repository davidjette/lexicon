**Adventuring party · Ghosts of Twatmarsh · Temple of Neptune, Saltmarsh · Saltmarsh's navy**

*Also known as:* the Daughters of Neptune

**Neptune's Rejects** is the second adventuring party of [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/): [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/), [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/). Dave coined the name on 2021-09-10 and the party took it on 2021-09-17. [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/)'s council gave them the deed to its [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/), and they won the ship [the Sea Ghost](/items/the-sea-ghost/). In November 2021 they captured the *[Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/)* and won a commission for the naval protection of Saltmarsh Harbor, and the town admired its new navy and "its protection by the Daughters of Neptune". The party's ledger records the weekly payout of Neptune's Navy as 1500 gold for a crew of 20, and the Temple of Neptune's treasury as 809 gold.

## The name

Dave named the party in chat on 2021-09-10, with "Neptune’s Rejects" following his line on the band. The Messenger group was renamed on 2021-09-17: "You named the group Neptune's Rejects." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-10, 2021-09-17)</small>

In the town's eyes they were the Daughters of Neptune. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, attachment 1654377024765671, 2021-11-13)</small> Nico's name for what they would become if they abandoned Saltmarsh was "Neptune’s Pirate’s": "we would just raid the warehouse of magic items and set sail with our fleet and become Neptune’s Pirate’s". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small>

## Members and allies

- [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) (Dave), captain of the Sea Ghost.
- [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) (JL), its first mate.
- [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) (Nico), priestess of Neptune and ship's chaplain.
- **[Oshi](/people/oshi/)** (Oceanus), a sea elf ranger, boatswain; died 2021-11-12.
- **MacGruber**, a deaf sailor, navigator.
- **Bimz**, Scrunt's pseudodragon familiar.
- **[Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/)**, a celestial warlock of a mysterious holy book, from 2021-11-27.
- **Hardbread**, played by JL in June 2022.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18, 2021-11-13, 2021-11-28, 2022-06-21; `sources/documents/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-neptunes-rejects-ledger.md`)</small>

All three carried High Quality swords under the campaign's house rule: "Don’t forget we all got swords that are High Quality (can’t break, 1 size dice larger when sharpened)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

## The Temple of Neptune

After the haunted house of *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh*, the council gave the party the deed to the Temple of Neptune. Dave: "The whole council did that" and "And the temple was a burden". The town gave "900g in donations after fines", and "800 of that was from One Anonymous Donor". Dolores became "a local celebrity", and Dave ruled her sermons could bring in 1d8 gold a day. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

The party kept on old Wellgar ("I think we should keep good Ol’ Wellgar around", Nico) and left Brinehandle, a level-0 priest who knows *guidance*, to run the church while they were away. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13, 2021-09-18)</small>

## The ship and the faction

On 2021-09-18 the party won the Sea Ghost. Dave: "We have a legit home base, ship, we can hire more crew and ARM them if we want", "We hire a small village of sailors and their families become part of our faction", and "We sail under Neptune’s flag for none on the sea have any choice but do his will". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

## Saltmarsh's navy

Dave, on 2021-11-03: "yeah if we don't look out we will become sea princes ourselves and/or responsible for the naval and spiritual defense of Saltmarsh". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-03)</small> In the session of 2021-11-05 the party captured the *Stinking Albatross*, stormed a town council meeting to announce it, and demanded a commission for the naval protection of Saltmarsh Harbor. The council approved the expenditure. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, attachment 1654377024765671, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave, on 2021-11-15: "While your warship looms over the city and you just demanded 20% of the public revenue". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small>

In the session of 2021-11-12, Brotherhood assassins ambushed the party at the Weathervein tomb. The party blew up a building and publicly executed six members of the society. Dave: "Members of a secret society which we devastated in a single night". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

By March 2022 Gabriella commanded a private fleet. Dave: "But a private fleet allied with a living god child of the sea is a potent combo". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> At the Saltmarsh finale of 2022-07-09 the party beat the [Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) and reached level 10. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>
