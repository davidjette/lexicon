The Unforeseen is a secret organization founded by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), established during Episode 9 in Arden Crest when the group vowed to work together to grow in power. Serving the Empire, the four recovered the Fantanya Nyel from the Greenbluff mines, secured the alliance with the Children of Winter in the Eldeen Reaches, turned a slum into the city of Newham, uncovered the second of four ancient stone tablets, and were present at the Rekkenmark when Professor Marble's undead overran it. They were promoted and threatened in a single audience before the Throne at Korth. The alliance did not hold: within a generation the four had become rivals for the same Empire, and by the time of the Sharn rising they stood on three different sides of it.

## THE RECORD OF SERVICE, AS THE EMPIRE CREDITED IT

Four operatives of exceptional record. Recovered the ancient bell artefact, the Fantanya Nyel, from the Greenbluff mines and delivered it to the Empire by rail through a rebel assault on the Talenta Plains Express. Secured a powerful alliance to the west with the Children of Winter of the Eldeen Reaches, confirming Dark Queen Ivanka in the Reaches and finalising the treaty. Turned a slum of a town into the great new city of Newham. Uncovered the second of four ancient stone tablets and surrendered both to the Emperor. Present at the Rekkenmark when Professor Marble's undead overran it; all knowledge of that incident is to be kept top secret.

The Unforeseen are also held responsible for the death of [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) on the lightning rail, an act the Empire blamed on rebel terrorists.

<small>The Empire's public reporting from the same period also records that Magnolia Steele stepped down from the Imperial Assembly and as Chancellor of the Rekkenmark with no replacement named; that Gabrielle Duin was missing and [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) was blamed for her assassination; and that at least a dozen other Black Dove commanders and associates were killed or reported missing in the following weeks, some believed to have fled to the rebellion. None of these items were connected to the four in print.</small>

## HAYMAN MAW'S TERMS TO LOCKE PIERCE

Hayman Maw's terms to Locke Pierce after the Rekkenmark, as the campaign record has them, were that he could continue his work at Rekkenmark East under Esther Crona's supervision on two conditions: notify Maw the instant the records cracked, and mention absolutely nothing to Esther if anything about [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) turned up.

The Empire built the alliance and then compartmented it. Esther Crona's master had been killed by the Council she served, and the man in the room who could prove it had been ordered to keep his mouth shut.

Maw also had a standing alternative prepared. If Locke had admitted downloading the Rekkenmark's contents, he was to be declared Imperial property and ordered into full seclusion for processing, with four Crimson Sun agents, Maw himself and up to eight city guards on hand to enforce it.

## THRONE ROOM TRANSCRIPT — KORTH

Delivered by [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), out of the black robes, standing nude before Kaius III with the Dragonmark across her pelvis and stomach, to all four founders at once:

> We've been watching you, and you've proven yourselves time and again to be great assets to the Empire. You retrieved the ancient bell artifact, you've secured a powerful ally to the West in the Eldeen Reaches, you've turned a once slum of a town into a great new city, and it looks as if you've uncovered the second of 4 ancient stone tablets. But you've dug too deep this time. For 7 long years it took me to get where I was, to get in, to get close. I did it all for the Empire and now it is all ruined, because of you!

> For 7 years not a soul knew I had joined the rebellion as a spy, that I gained their trust, that I fed them information which got imperial agents and soldiers killed, you too were expendable, just bodies used to hide my cover, I can never go back as the cell I belonged to is now gone. But I'm no longer angry, we understand now. You 4 may be the best rebel hunters we have!

She then gave Uriel a chained and hooded prisoner as a gift — Lady Glow — warned her not to play games, told LeBeefe to harness his power, and briefed Pierce on where the remaining tablets were; Kaius himself told Esther that Varyn Crona had been a loyal warrior, to avenge his death, and hinted that Varyn's seat on the Council could be hers in time. Four people were promoted and threatened in a single audience.

## REBEL OBSERVATION FILE — CRONA–BLAZE

Compiled a decade later, under the heading of a High-Priority Observation Target, and stated with far less certainty than the Empire's own file:

> Intelligence suggests that Esther, Uriel, John C. LeBeefe, and the Warforged resistance fighter Locke Pierce once rose through the Empire's ranks together as a covert unit informally referred to as "The Unforeseen."

By the time the rebellion assembled that file, one founder was the Lord Commander of Sharn, one was the Empress, one was the Grand Duke who had made Leef a garrison town, and one was described in the rebellion's own files as a Warforged *resistance fighter* — and the analysts still hedged on whether the four had ever been a unit.

## What became of them

The alliance did not hold. Within a generation the four founders had become rivals for the same Empire, and by the time of the Sharn rising they stood on three different sides of it.

Uriel Qualanthri rose highest. Married to Emperor Kaius III and ruling in his stead while he lies ill, she is now the Holy Empress — "the White Death."

John C. LeBeefe was made Grand Duke and carried the Empire east, until the rising at [Newham](/places/newham/) destroyed the city outright. The blast corrupted the region into New Mourning, and the survivors founded the Republic of Talenta in his despite.

Esther Crona became Lord Commander and Grand Inquisitor of Sharn. She died there at the end of the rising. Her last broadcast voided the Iron Veil's pact across the whole city, and her final command was to kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri.

Locke Pierce turned. He now leads The Hundreds, and brought them to Sharn to fight alongside the rebellion in the assault on the Veil Building.

## The party that became the villains

The Unforeseen were the player characters of the first campaign. They recovered the bell, held the train, built the city, met the Emperor, and swore in a ruined village to grow in power together. Ten in-world years later the same four are the antagonists of the second: [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Lorian](/people/lorian/) — the Falcon Crew at onboarding, and now the Inevitables — have spent their entire careers taking them apart one at a time.

<small>**Dating:** the [Greenbluff recovery](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/) was the first and second sessions of this campaign, about ten years before the Inevitables; the written records put it at "twenty years" (Fulcrum's bell file), "thirteen years" (Sharn Ep 19) and "over a decade" (the rebel-factions page). In the master chronology the Eberron campaigns — The Unforeseen, Sharn and Korth — sit after the Infantaverse and Dead Man's Hand era and before Starfall: The Next Generation.</small>

LeBeefe went first, at Newham, and the explosion poisoned a province. Esther went second, on her own balcony in Sharn, and took the Iron Veil's pact down with her. Locke did not have to be beaten because Locke turned, and turning made him more dangerous rather than less: he holds the Ruby Nova, and he has told the people who beat two of his co-founders that if they fail against the third he will end the world before she can. Uriel is what is left. Ten bells remain, and the Inevitables are on the river to the Ivory Lazaret.
