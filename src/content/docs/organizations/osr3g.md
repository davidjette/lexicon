**Secret intelligence corps · [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) · [The Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) · Disbanded**

*Also known as:* the grey cloaks · the Office of Special Regiments for Intergalactic Intelligence and Information Gathering · OSRIIIG

**OSR3G** is the secret intelligence corps of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), formally the Office of Special Regiments for Intergalactic Intelligence and Information Gathering and known as the grey cloaks. In *Light of Xaryxis* the grey cloaks may have been involved in the Lexicon's concealment of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), and the corps probably erased Jim Smallberries's knowledge of the Demoncore program of the Beholder War. In the last weeks of that arc Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/) drew the grey cloaks off while the crew of the SSJ Zanzibar escaped Sigil. A replacement crew of grey cloaks manned the Zanzibar after the ship was decommissioned in its captain's absence and outfitted with the Demoncore, and the Demoncore leaked into that crew. After the betrayal of the Lexicon was revealed the corps was disbanded and replaced by [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/).

## Name

The corps is known as the grey cloaks. Its formal name, the Office of Special Regiments for Intergalactic Intelligence and Information Gathering, abbreviates to OSRIIIG, which Dave shortened to **OSR3G**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> The spelling OSRIIG also occurs. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

In November 2023 the crew of the SSJ Zanzibar learned that parts of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), evolved into a hidden society of conflicting magical AIs, were conspiring to hide the reality of the Shrike from mortals, and that elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, might also be involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

[Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/), a tabaxi horizon walker, is a grey cloak of OSR3G. In the flashback to [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), she and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) served a secret program, used only once, and she chose recovering [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/) over saving the civilians stranded with Jim Smallberries on Negroni Prime. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09; 2024-03-16)</small> Any knowledge [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) gained of the Demoncore was probably erased from his mind by OSR3G, according to Dave. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> Katrina's player called the level-10 character "Grey Cloak Catrina". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-03)</small>

The grey cloaks are Astral Command Intelligence. In April 2024 the crew of the Zanzibar, at Sigil, brought the truth of the Lexicon's betrayal to Admiral Starweaver, a member of the Sovereign Council. She made a speech against it in the Sovereign Senate and survived an attempt on her life, made by the Lexicon and its cybrid agents. She then gave the crew her luxury vessel, distracted the grey cloaks and helped the crew escape. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27; 2024-05-02; Dave, 2026-11-21)</small>

### The replacement crew of the Zanzibar

The SSJ Zanzibar was decommissioned in the absence of Captain Smallberries and given a replacement crew of grey cloaks. It was outfitted with the Demoncore to deliver it toward Toril. Before the fleet launched at [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), Smallberries's crew broadcast the signal that disabled the Lexicon in the fleet. The Zanzibar was disabled, and the Demoncore leaked into the grey cloaks aboard, who became undead. Katrina McQuaid and Horgus Simianis were on the bridge when Jim Smallberries appeared there as [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/). He killed Katrina, and Horgus teleported away with her body. <small>(Dave, 2026-11-21)</small>

## Disbanding

After the Lexicon's betrayal was exposed at the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the grey cloaks were disbanded and replaced by [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/), a force that hunts and destroys androids, warforged and all artificial intelligence, created to enforce [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) as a paramilitary arm of the Sovereign government. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The Act was passed a year after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> The date of the disbanding is not stated.

## The Starsong Awakens

### Necis

The grey cloaks were still at work in the period after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, when the Sovereignty moved in on [Necis](/places/necis/) and tried to rebuild the Lexicon. In [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/), fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/) and [Umwelt](/people/umwelt/) found the traces of Astral Command's Project Paradox Dawn, a level-four classified operation on Necis, secured by OSRIIG and shut down permanently. In a log recovered there an Astral Command captain asks: "And why are there so many blasted grey cloaks watching our every move?" At the end of the Labyrinth the pair found a room of grey-cloak scientists moving in reverse for eternity around a prisoner whose psychic energy they were siphoning in an attempt to summon the Lexicon. The scientists were caught in a time loop of that period. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

Umwelt's notes include the words "necis" and "secured by OSRIIG". <small>(character sheet)</small>

### Large Luigi

JL's first outline of the old crew's fates, written the day after the finale of *Light of Xaryxis*, has the beholder [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/), known only as prisoner LL849, held for fifteen years in a high-security OSRIIIG facility awaiting trial for treason and crimes against the Sovereignty, which officially disavows all knowledge of his whereabouts. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> The Company dossier given to the crew in play names his prison as [the Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center](/places/the-katrina-mcquaid-memorial-prison/) in Shadowspace, and does not say which body runs it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The prison bears the name of Katrina McQuaid, who was a grey cloak.
