> Fourteen dead. The girl who did it, they said she was a sorcerer and the War Wizards came and scooped her up. Thats what they do, no punishment, just give them a wand and say fight for the King! It's wrong if you ask me.

<small>— the War Wizards Ally Card. Sic on "Thats". The speaker is the bartender of the Shadowhigh Inn, in Arc III Parts IX–XI.</small>

**Wizard knights · Cormyr · Allies at the Egg**

The War Wizards are Cormyr's order of wizard knights, and allies of the party at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). Their card records a bartender of the Shadowhigh Inn describing how the order recruits.

The girl he speaks of is **Una**, the miller's daughter. The full account: "They caught the little girl stealing grain and brought her to the constable. When they put her in shackles to teach her a lesson, poof, she vaporized the lot of them. Fourteen dead." There is "a small altar to a handful of people killed by Una many years ago" in the inn, with "small reminders of her parents… visible." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI, the Shadowhigh Inn.)</small>

Una is one girl, and she is the Infanta of War, **Tempus**. The child of the Shadowhigh Inn is the same Una who, in Arc III Part XII, is enticed away and captured by Carlysle in robot form. The War Wizards' own report to the archmagi calls her "Una, the infanta of War." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small>

The War Wizards also held [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), the Infanta of Nature, at their magical stock farm in Suzail, until the party delivered him to Cormanthor and Myth Drannor. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-27, 2019-01-28)</small>

The order's own conduct is a live question in the chronicle: [Celion](/people/celion/) says "[Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) sought to subvert the War Wizard's efforts to contain the Infanta, so naturally he had to die."

## Suzail and the Arcaneum

The War Wizards had a campus in [Suzail](/places/suzail/), and their headquarters was the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/) in Punis, about two days' ride away. They knew of the Infanta early enough to find and sequester Una many years before. Dave called them "the DOD of the biggest inland human kingdom", and in the Age of the Infanta they were on high alert after [Kara](/people/kara/) ambushed their leadership. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>

Their research summary to the archmagi, signed by Celion, reports five Infanta apprehended and experimented on: Una, their "first godchild", and the Infantas of Lothander, Tyr, Tymora and Sylvanus. Tyr, Tymora and Lothander were killed, and the report gives Tyr's death as the true cause of the sinking of the warship *Crescenta* at the battle of West Sembia. It states that psychological torment sets an Infanta's default nature to self-destruct, "we consider this a safety feature", and that the War Wizards built the Tempus shrine to test their theory about the proximity of Infanta. It proposes releasing the Sylvanus Infanta to allies of the Cormanthyrian elves, overrunning Cormanthyr when he self-destructed, and bringing Una to the field "to lay waste to Obus". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small>

During [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/), Krapsmere was sent to attack the War Wizards' campus and kill every War Wizard on sight, and many of Cormyr's paladins and knights came to believe that the War Wizards had attempted a coup. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16, 2019-01-21)</small> In the crisis that followed, the War Wizards meant to torture an Infanta until it released an explosion big enough to destroy the party and possibly the whole city, and the party forced them to set it off early. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-04)</small>

The War Wizards kept [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/), one of the Crown Jewels of Cormyr, "to use to spy on everyone". After Valen was beaten at the Arcaneum, Celion inherited the school and was to return the Palantir to the king. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

## The Battle of the River Lis

At [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) the War Wizards stood with the allies. Dave: "This their jam, they’re 100% on board". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> They fought as an airborne unit, and helped build Roland's war machine, the 24-Hour News, from specs stolen from St. Cloud. With the Fabricate spell, "it only takes a few wizards a few days to make something like this if they have a skilled craftsman with them". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small>
