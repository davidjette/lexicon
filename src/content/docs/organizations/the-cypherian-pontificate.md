**Religious institution · Faith of Cypher · Seat: [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Starfall era · Active**

*Also known as:* the Cypherian Pontifex (its head) · the Cypherian Hegemony

**The Cypherian Pontificate** is the Church of Cypher of the Starfall era, the successor to [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), led by the Cypherian Pontifex from [Sigil](/places/sigil/). The Church of Cypher saved the world from [the Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/), and the Church and the Leviathan crisis are held to be founding events of the civilisation of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/). The Pontificate oversees the main node of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) in Sigil, and has its own hand in [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/).

## History

In the Dead Man's Hand era the Church of Cypher in Sigil is the Sororia, which keeps the Lexicon at the heart of the High Courts; the Sororia's Factol is the Lexicon. In the Starfall era the Church's successor, the Pontificate, oversees the Lexicon's main node in Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-03)</small>

Dave's 2022 description of the polity of the era had it led mostly from Sigil by distant descendants of the major powers of the Forgotten Realms, among them the Nimbus Company and the Cypherian Pontifex. Cypher's legacy continues into that distant future, and her church has its own hand in Astral Command. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Styling itself the Cypherian Hegemony, the Pontificate claims to rule Realmspace. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

The leadership of the Sovereignty is heavily advised by the Lexicon, whose main node is in Sigil and is overseen by what emerged from the Church of Cypher. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-03)</small> Because the Church of Cypher and the Leviathan crisis are the founding events of the civilisation, changing time and constructing human gods would be anathema to it, and Astral Command's proposed prime directives rest on that premise. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-16)</small>

## Faith

In the Starfall era the truth about Cypher is cloaked in myth, cursed information which only madmen and poets ever understand. Her godly incarnations and their domains exist. Her three aspects are worshipped as a set of three goddesses, sometimes shown as sisters and at other times as one being who lives through multiple ages of the universe: Elohim, goddess of time and space; Istus, goddess of fate; and Cypher, goddess of mysteries and secrets. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> Elohim is the Starfall-era name of Eloise.

An avatar of Cypher existed in the distant future, and spoke to [Caprica](/people/caprica/) in the Starsong. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

Aboard the SSJ Zanzibar, an Orcish invocation to a spelljammer helm calls on Cypher as master of time and space and speaks of "his wheel", where the Sororia calls Cypher "her". Its translation, as spoken in play, includes:

> "By the eternal march of Cypher, master of time and space, I command the currents of the cosmos. Hear my words, oh helm of old, and carry us to our ordained place. Cypher's path be our guide, his wheel our protection."
>
> <small>— Orcish helm invocation (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-23)</small>
