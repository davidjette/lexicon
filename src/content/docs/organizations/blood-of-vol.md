**Religious cult · Human, merchant-class and city-guard base · Active, and not what its own members think it is**

The Blood of Vol is a religious cult of humans who believe they share an ancestral kinship with the Emperor and regard that kinship as next to divine. It was devised by the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) as a covert project to breed radical support among disaffected workers in the merchant class and city guard forces, and was run by an agent codenamed Godseed until it grew too large to control. That agent, Gabrielle Duin, still leads it. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) decoded the Imperial encryption codes and cracked the Blood of Vol files, which were held at a lower encryption level than the Imperial secrets.

## IMPERIAL REPORT - BLOOD OF VOL

A cult known as the Blood of Vol is filled with humans who believe they share an ancestral kinship with the Emperor which they regard as next to divine. They believe that a dark and sinister cabal of politicians, media figures, financiers, and college professors are collaborators in a global plot to commit mass pedophilia and degenerate the core values of society. They believe the Emperor is secretly fighting this cabal from behind the scenes and any criticism of him or failure of his reign can be laid at the feet of this deep state or even explained away as intentional and ‘setting the stage’ for a tremendous purge and storm that will wipe the corrupt from the Earth, leaving them in control.

## INTERNAL MEMOS

The cult is not entirely wrong, in that the Emperor knows of their cult and secretly agrees with their assertion of his divine blood and his fatherhood over all of Eberron. He has on multiple occasions leaked information through communique and rumor to support their conspiracies and even encourage terrorist acts against his enemies.

## PROJECT GODSEED

The Blood of Vol was originally devised by the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) as a covert project to breed radical support among disaffected workers in the merchant class and city guard forces who were susceptible to propaganda that they were better than [Warforged](/lore/warforged/), goblins, gnomes, halflings, orcs and dwarves. It was provided its original resources and nuggets of information by an unnamed covert agent codename Godseed embedded inside a civilian org, who served as their primary handler until they grew too large to control.

## PERSONAL LETTERS OF GABRIELLE DUIN

Duin is that covert agent who leads the Blood of Vol and its cult of Kaius-worshipers. She claims direct descendance from the [House of Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) and bears the [Dragonmark of Death](/lore/the-mark-of-death-and-the-dragonmarks/). After killing the former Star Prelate of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) on the fields at Cyre (and blaming it on the undead) she took command of the Doves and immediately subjugated them to Imperial will, all while feigning rebel sympathy and building a strong conduit of control to them. She is now in hiding in the Imperial Palace where remains consort to the Emperor.

<small>The letters are signed **Gabrielle**. Imperial Council rosters and the Black Doves' own registry both record the same person as **Gabriel Duin**. Which spelling is the forgery is not established.</small>

## THE IMPERIAL COUNCIL ROSTER

Two seats on the [Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) belong to the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/): Dunkin Sawbrass, Pope, and Garth Brooks, Bishop. The roster carries a parenthetical query against the second name — *Secret leader of the Blood of Vol under the name Chris Gaines?* — and no answer to it.

## THE MT. SILICON DOSSIER — HB-CHORUS-13

Locke Pierce's dossier states that the Empress has used the personal wealth of the Emperor and the House of Kaius to bankroll the fabrication of the Hell's Bells, while seizing the considerable assets of the Sovereign Host dioceses and abbeys impounded by Imperial decree; the Host's churches, temples and hospitals were turned into [Houses of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) staffed by Black Dove nurses and nuns. How the cult of Kaius-worshippers regarded the seizure of the Host is not recorded.

## TESTIMONY OF THE DRAGON VALTREX, UNDER THE KORRAMONT

Freed of his collar, the [black dragon](/species/black-dragons/) Valtrex gave evidence that cuts directly across the cult's founding claim. The elvish houses that fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline. [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/) bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun. And [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) has used his own stolen blood, drawn from him for three years on an Imperial rig, to sire her heirs — which makes him their father.

<small>"The Crimson Sun" here names a dragon. It also names the Empire's secret service. Nothing reconciles the two usages.</small>

## THE ARMHAIR MANUSCRIPT — HALL OF RECORDS, KORTH

Master Armhair, an elderly half-elf loremaster, hid a copy of his life's heresy in the Hall under a false index and gave the number to strangers, inviting them to have him executed for it. His claim: a great dragon called [Viagra](/people/viagra/), the most powerful known, vanished from the record long before the elves came to Khorvaire; it came to King Jarret Ilwenarn with a bargain; the sick child Prince Kaius died and was replaced; and Kaius III is that dragon, the same being as Kaius I. His evidence is contemporaneous accounts and courtiers' journals scrubbed from the archives. The manuscript also carries a dragon family tree traced to the progenitors and a thread connecting the Mark of Death, Erandis Vol's birth, and the same dragon.

A competing account exists and is not reconciled with this one. [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)'s lingering voice told [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) that the Emperor is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun, kept alive as a vessel and fed dragon blood to strengthen his bloodline. Both are claims. Neither is established.
