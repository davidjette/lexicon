**Interstellar temple · Faith of Hyperion · Starfall era · Active**

*Also known as:* the Consular (its missionaries)

**The Children of Hyperion** are an interstellar temple which professes to represent mediation and peaceful coexistence throughout the systems, and whose members are possessed by Hyperion, a lawful good extraplanar entity. Hyperion took over a race of psionically enabled humans on Eberron a very long time ago and lives in them and their children as a collective memory and personality. Its missionaries, the Consular, serve on vessels and bases as interpreters, negotiators and spies; one of them, [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), served as diplomat of the SSJ Zanzibar in [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). By the era of [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/) [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) has virtually replaced [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) with Hyperion.

## Hyperion

Hyperion is an extraplanar entity with a church, and it is benevolent. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> A very long time ago on Eberron it took over a race of psionically enabled humans. It inhabits them and all their children as a collective set of memories and personality, and when they are close they can meld minds, which updates the nodes of the network. It is lawful good. In the Eberron cosmology it comes from the dream realm of horrors, and was escaping that abyss. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

A priestess of Hyperion is possessed with a dual soul, shared with her god Hyperion, a powerful alien consciousness from the realm of dreams; her people are missionaries of peace who volunteer as translators and spies aboard ships' crews. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24)</small> The Children themselves believe that they are possessed by ancient creatures called quori. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Beliefs and practice

The temple's own account, as carried by a Lesser Consular:

> An interstellar temple which professes to represent mediation and peaceful coexistence throughout the systems. Violence is forbidden in their domains and many )but not all) respect that sanctuary.
>
> The Children of Hyperion believe themselves to be (and likely are) possessed by ancient creatures called Quori, which guide them and dictate a moral code and practice that leads them to be diplomats, arbitors, judges and lawful soldiers.
>
> The Consular are missionaries of peace who volunteer for service on vessels and bases, mainly as interpreters, negotiators and spies, all while spreading their knowledge and code.
>
> <small>— Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya's character sheet (sic)</small>

Its ranks include the **Consular** and the **Lesser Consular**. <small>(character sheet)</small>

Hyperions who spend a long time away from others of their kind may form close relationships that are assimilated into the whole when they meld again, turning a specific love into a general love for all. Dave offered this as a possibility, with the consequence that Sandy would partly disappear each time she melded with another. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-10)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya, called Sandy, a kalashtar born possessed with an aberrant quori, joined the SSJ Zanzibar as communications officer and diplomatic attaché, and later served as its Vox Sovereign. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18; crew roster; character sheet)</small> Issuing civilian credentials to the stowaway Carlos Vega, who bore a mind flayer's mark, she warned him:

> "If I find that you are harboring a mindflayer spawn aboard this ship I will burn you with wrath of all of Hyperion."
>
> <small>— Sandy to Carlos Vega (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

After the wreck of the Zanzibar, Sandy carried the memory of what happened on Necis, with the Consul and at the Time Tombs, so the Hyperion entity knows everything that happened to her there. She did not know where the rest of the crew had gone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Dave expected the Hyperion entity to gain new relevance in a Sovereignty after the Lexicon. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the Starweaver Act outlawed all artificial intelligence, and Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, Sandy is a captain and a Lesser Consular of the Children of Hyperion, and stands with Astral Command against the legacy crew of the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-13)</small> Her former crewmates' players hoped to win her back, and one warned that bringing her near other Sovereign ships would connect her to other Hyperions. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09)</small>
