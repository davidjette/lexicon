**News broadcaster · Sharn · Imperial media · Anchor: Tim Raisins**

*Also known as:* Tim Raisins (anchor)

The Sharn News Network is the news service that broadcasts to the crystal screens of [Sharn](/places/sharn/), a city recorded by the [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/). The celebrity anchor [Candice Kurt](/people/candice-kurt/), host of *Serving Sharn Nightly*, was shot dead at the opening of [Posé](/places/pose/) in Sharn Ep 2, and that night the network's anchor **Tim Raisins** carried the Imperial account that Cogborn agitators had turned a peaceful protest into a riot, and reported Sister Nora missing, Iron Veil raids in the Cogborn neighbourhoods and checkpoints between the upper and lower levels. In Sharn Ep 8 a breaking Sharn News Network broadcast named a silver dragonborn, a red-haired tiefling and a human man in short-shorts as suspects in a "coordinated terrorist attack".

## Candice Kurt and *Serving Sharn Nightly*

Candice Kurt was a celebrity news anchor and political commentator, best known as the host of *Serving Sharn Nightly*, and a frequent master of ceremonies at Sharn's high-profile galas and public events. She was known for flattering coverage of Mayor [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/)'s policies and kept personal ties to the Heinricks. <small>(Sharn site, Key Figures)</small> The same register names Henry Heinrick the architect of Sharn's propaganda machine, who "shapes the city's narrative with a smile that never reaches his eyes." <small>(Sharn site, Key Figures)</small> On the streets, the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/)'s patrols move under "propaganda screens" that "echo their creed". <small>(Sharn site, Home)</small>

In Nico's script for the opening of Posé the programme is *Serving Sharn Nightline*. Candice was already on the terrace before the guests arrived, set up with her personal media ArcEye on an overhang balcony with a clear view of the grand staircase, where Iron Veil guards held back a small group of protesters. She opened her balcony interview with the Mayor: "Serving Sharn Nightline brings you the voices shaping our city. Tonight — Mayor Henry Heinrick." When she put it to him that the Cogborn protest was a sign of growing instability, he answered, "Candice, if Sharn isn't dramatic, it isn't Sharn. We thrive on spectacle." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

## The Posé attack

At the height of the night the [Cogborn protest](/history/the-cogborn-protest/) pushed through the barriers and up the stairs, and a shot from the crowd struck the balcony and killed Candice Kurt. The official story was that she had been assassinated by protesters from [the Cogs](/places/the-cogs/). <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2; Sharn site, Key Figures; Episode Summaries)</small> The shooter was a member of [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/), in a false flag planned by Faith and Malrik Zeir. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-nico-docs-questions.md; Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

In Nico's notes Candice's media ArcEye was the only ArcEye at Posé, and it disappeared when she died. Anyone who recovered the live recording would see the trio in some of the footage in their server uniforms, with their cover intact; nothing was recorded after her death, when they turned themselves and Sister Nora invisible. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

Later that night every screen in Sharn turned red and showed [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), in full armour atop her Imperial warship, executing Cogborn prisoners live for the whole city. <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small> In Nico's notes the red screens carried the words "What was first just a dream has become a frightening reality for those who may oppose us." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

## Tim Raisins

Tim Raisins is a young half-elf journalist and the anchor who read the Sharn News Network's breaking report on the Posé attack. Planning the session, Nico considered having a new news person, taking Candice Kurt's place after her death, report the attack, Sister Nora's disappearance and the missing BioTec employee. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

The broadcast reached the crystal screen in the trio's upper-city safehouse at the start of [Sharn Ep 3](/sessions/sharn-ep-3-zilspar-farms/), cutting from the red message to a studio bathed in red light. Tim sat at the desk with his hands clasped tightly and his eyes red-rimmed but steady, under a banner reading BREAKING NEWS — POSÉ ATTACK / COGBORN RIOT. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

> "Good evening, Sharn. I… I wish we were coming to you under better circumstances."

He confirmed that "our colleague and dear friend, Candace Kurt" <small>(sic)</small> had been killed during the violent disturbance at the Posé grand opening, and a photo of her appeared. He gave the Imperial officials' statement that "Cogborn agitators escalated a peaceful protest into a riot". He reported that "Sister Nora of the Sanctum of the Last Mercy remains unaccounted for", believed caught in the chaos as the Cogborn mob surged through the Veil level plaza, under a new headline: SISTER NORA MISSING — IRON VEIL SEARCH UNDERWAY. Over a map of Sharn's lower wards he announced that Iron Veil forces had increased surveillance across the lower city and that raids were ongoing in Cogborn neighbourhoods, with EXPECT DELAYS — SECURITY CHECKPOINTS BETWEEN LOWER & UPPER LEVELS scrolling beneath. In other news, BioTec had reported [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), "a junior technician", missing since the night before the Posé event, over a grainy photo, and anyone with information was urged to contact the Imperial tip line. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

> "We will continue to bring you updates as they come in. This is Tim Raisins… reporting for the Sharn News Network."

The screen faded back to the red banner and the broadcast looped. When [Izaak](/people/izaak/) reached the safehouse and found [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) there, he told the trio, "They think the Cogborn grabbed her", and gestured at the screen: "And now your friend Kross is missing too." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small> The trio watched a flood of crystal-screen broadcasts that night covering the attack, the riot, Nora's disappearance and the tightening of Iron Veil security; the reports confirmed Kross missing, checkpoints dividing the upper and lower city, and raids sweeping the Cogborn neighbourhoods. <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small>

In Nico's notes the news media and the government blamed the Cogborn for the attack and for Sister Nora's disappearance, and tied Martin Kross's disappearance to them too. Iron Veil raids in the surface Cog neighbourhoods drove many Cogborn down into the deeper tunnels and sewers. The same notes have the city and the mainstream media ignoring the Cogborn deaths that the Cogs blamed on a killer they called the Cog Collector, later identified as [Dr. Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/). <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

## The terrorist bulletin

In [Sharn Ep 8](/sessions/sharn-ep-8-r-u-s-t/), after the violent clash outside [the Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), a breaking broadcast from the Sharn News Network identified a silver dragonborn, a red-haired tiefling and a human man in short-shorts as suspects in a "coordinated terrorist attack". With the Iron Veil hunting them, [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) disguised themselves before going out, and that night Dario won his fight at [Breakers Pit](/places/breakers-pit/). <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small>

## Other broadcasts on Sharn's screens

- **Sharn Ep 10.** A crystallizer screen showed a live broadcast of [the Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), with Esther Crona's airship the Black Veil hovering above [the Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/) and [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) casting a spell over the panicking crowd before the screens cut to black. <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small>
- **Sharn Ep 18.** Henry Heinrick broadcast a city-wide emergency alert announcing a full lockdown. Later that night Dario used the [EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) to broadcast a pre-recorded message and the reclaimed memory of [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/)'s birth, and then Esther Crona's body live, in [the Sharn Broadcast Override](/lore/the-sharn-broadcast-override/). <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small>

**Technology:** The EBT-7 predates all modern scry-tech, including ArcEye surveillance systems and the Sharn News Network. <small>(Sharn site, EBT-7)</small>

**Known staff:** Tim Raisins, anchor. Candice Kurt, whom Tim Raisins called "our colleague and dear friend"; deceased.
