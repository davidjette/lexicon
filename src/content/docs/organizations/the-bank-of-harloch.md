**Bank and trust · Half-owned by [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) · Charter houses in many major cities · Persists into the far future**

*Also known as:* the First Harloch Trust · the 1st Harloch Trust · the Harloch Trust

**The Bank of Harloch**, first known as the **First Harloch Trust**, is a bank started by the girl the party saved from the brothel. The party invested half their holdings in it, which made [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) its owner by half and a major shareholder in "the second of two banks in the whole world". In Arc III it offered insurance for the crew of the party's airship, [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/), and it kept charter houses in many major cities. With its holding company Temple Holdings, later rebranded Temporal Holdings, the bank persisted well into the future, and Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs" because of it.

## The First Harloch Trust

When the party took the airship in Arc III, Dave offered them insurance for its crew. Joseph, who played [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/), refused: "no one in harloch is in any position to pay out, nor is there any regulatory body overseeing this "insurance", and even if there was i'm certain that the activities we'll be using the ship for will void any kind of policy. this sounds like a cash grab." Dave answered:

> In the absence of established services and registrations, this policy puts your boat under the coverage of the First Harloch Trust started by the girl you guys saved from the brothel.  You invest half your money in her bank and she's got plenty of leverage now to pay on a policy

> please remember that you are effectively 50% owners of the First Harloch Trust which you bankrolled with 1/2 of your holdings, so you are major shareholders in the second of two banks in the whole world

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> The Old Temple the company bought stands in Harloch, on [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/).

## Insuring the Arielle

The terms Dave set out:

> Ok, the First Harloch Trust will extend liability, causality and pension insurance for crew at a cost of 50,000 Gold, and you'll need a crew of ten at 1,000 Gold to operate it full time.
>
> Your insurance with the Trust will cover birthing costs and taxes at major ports. You'll pay that $1,000 every time you have to replace crew. You'll probably want to build a home for the boat, either in Harlock or in Motherstone, that'll cost you another $50,000 (for an airdock capable of repairing the boat).

<small>— Sic on "causality", "birthing" and "Harlock". (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small>

"So are you effectively self insured? Yes. But you'd outsource the management of that insurance to the Trust to put to work in the economy". Under the policy the party would have "a crew and a repair deductible". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> Patrick, who played [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), accepted the plan, and said he was "now more excited about leveraging our bank to have a hostile takeover of the East Empire Trading Company". Dave: "YES". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small>

## Trade

The Trust's reach went beyond Snowdown. Dave: "The unmasked Lord of Waterdeep allows for free trade and the 1st Harloch Trust has charter houses in many major cities. You guys could absolutely drop some coin to corner a market or two, starve them out of diamonds or grain.  You already have a monopoly on Moonstone". Trade into and out of Snowdown still ran through the Stones of Safe Passage in the storm mythal, and "again, the Harloch Trust will manage all of this for you for access to your capital, or we can customize it and you can go hogwild with tariffs, whatever you want". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> See [Moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) and [the Lords of Waterdeep](/organizations/lords-of-waterdeep/).

## In the War Wizards' file

The War Wizards' top secret report on Snowdown, which Dave posted to the players in January 2019, described the heroes as "extremely bawdy and reckless adventurers who lack a central identity but keep their wealth hidden in various shell companies", and proposed to "prosecute based on a few un reported gains and money laundering schemes which include a brothel and a questionably insured bank." <small>(Sic on "un reported". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> See [the War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/).

## After the Ages

The Bank of Harloch and its holding company, Temple Holdings, persist well into the future. The company was rebranded Temporal Holdings after the end of the game, and it lost the bid for the Company to [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/)'s [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/). Dave: "the Bank of Harloch and its holding company Temple Holdings (rebranded Temporal Holdings after the end of the game) presists well into the future." <small>(Sic on "presists". Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

When a player asked after the finale what interest rates were like once the world was remade, Dave described the economy the bank inherited: "Huge demand hole left from the end of wartime sovereign  deficits unbalanced by bc of consumer demand from returning heroes, plus extreme deflationary pressure from eldritch arcane technology introduced by Nimbus Corp causes widespread unemployment, kingdoms cut interest rates in response creating a major buying opportunity for banks like yours to acquire competitors and assets on the cheap". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-10-16)</small> See [Arc VIII: End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## Harlochs

Five hundred years after the Ages, in [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), [Bud Holhertz](/people/bud-holhertz/) tells the woman he takes for Lexi that she owes him "three lives, 20000 HARLOCHS, and the map to get back and find my dead friends and property." <small>(DM notes)</small>

In the Starfall era JL asked, "Is the Harlock bank still a major economic concern?" Dave: "The galactic currency could be Harlochs" / "They invented a magical blockchain" / "Again, all background to our far out assignment where no one even accepts harlochs". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs" because of the bank. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c1-c10.md)</small> See [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) and [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/).
