**Youth gang · [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/)**

*Also known as:* the Starfish Boys

**The Starfish** are a gang of young delinquents in Saltmarsh, led by the bratty Gareth Brownwater, whom the smuggler [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) recruited while the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) were away on Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon, in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh. They made a mess of the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/), and took up residence in the old buildings of Crabber's Cove and refused to leave. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The temple

The party came home to a temple much changed: the stained glass dirty, the grass overgrown, empty wine bottles everywhere, a fat naked man passed out on a pew, and a goat in the rectory chewing ancient scrolls. The Starfish were the ones who had wrecked it. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>
