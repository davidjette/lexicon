**Half dragon, half elf · Queen of Death · House Vol · In hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house**

**Also known as:** the Queen of Death · *the great mother* of the consecration rite · possibly the Celestial Queen who commanded the making of the original bell, and possibly not.

Erandis Vol is a half dragon and half elf who ruled over undeath, the Queen of Death and the leader of [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/). Her house was an aberrant elvish house eradicated thousands of years ago by humans, giants and "good" elves, and its [Mark of Death](/lore/the-mark-of-death-and-the-dragonmarks/) was eradicated with it by an alliance of elves and dragons; Erandis survived and has not been seen since. She bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun. Her bloodline survives in [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Empress of Khorvaire, and in Gabrielle (Gabriel) Duin, and the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) invoke her as *the great mother* at every consecration.

## Physical description

No living witness has seen her. She is recorded as a half dragon and half elf who ruled over undeath, and nothing further about her person is recorded. Every image of her that circulates in Khorvaire is devotional, produced by people who have never seen her either.

## The house she led

House Vol was an aberrant elvish house eradicated by humans, giants and "good" elves thousands of years ago, and its Mark of Death eradicated with it by an alliance of elves and dragons. Erandis was its leader. Her bloodline survives, the Mark surfaces on living bodies in the present day, and the elvish houses that fled after the purge remain obsessed with it. Her own daughter-in-doctrine, Uriel Qualanthri, is Empress of Khorvaire.

> "It's leader, Erandis Vol, was the Queen of Death, a half dragon half elf that ruled over undeath. She's Uriel's ancestor."

## Public and private

In the Empire's public record she does not exist; named dragon records have been removed from the archives at Korth wholesale, and the Vol matter is a closed antiquarian question. In the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/)'s street theology she is barely mentioned — the cult worships the Emperor's blood, not hers, though it bears her house's name. In the Black Doves' liturgy she is not named at all: the consecration rite of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) binds the bells to *"the unholy blood and the great mother,"* and leaves the identification to the officiant.

## Competence and circle

She ruled over undeath. An alliance of elves and dragons was raised to end her line. Her known descendants and claimants: Uriel Qualanthri, who bears the Mark as a twisting draconic brand between navel and loins and calls Erandis her ancestor; and Gabrielle (Gabriel) Duin, who claims direct descendance from the House of Vol, bears the Dragonmark of Death, commands the Black Doves by murder, and is consort to the Emperor.

## The child by a dragon

Under the Korramont, freed of his collar and no longer insane, the [black dragon](/species/black-dragons/) Valtrex testified that **Erandis Vol bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun**. Master Armhair's suppressed manuscript, recovered from the Hall of Records at Korth under a false index, carries a dragon family tree traced to the progenitors and a thread connecting the Mark of Death, Erandis Vol's birth and the same dragon.

<small>"The Crimson Sun" names a dragon here. It also names [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s order, the Empire's secret service, whose sigil is carved in marble in the throne room. Both usages are live and the connection is unresolved in-world.</small>

## Origin

She was born of the elvish and draconic lines both. Armhair's manuscript ties her birth to the same dragon that fathered her child, which read literally makes the Vol line a closed loop rather than a crossing. She led House Vol. The house was destroyed by humans, giants and elves, and the Mark of Death with it. She survived and vanished. The Mark on Uriel's body, the Mark on Duin's, the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/)'s promise, the fourteen daughters and the rig built to bleed a dragon all follow that vanishing.

## The Bell at Greenbluff

The original Fantanya Nyel was buried by a Celestial Queen centuries ago, fused by ancient elvish craftsmen from an infernal metal. The Directorate of Relic Intelligence assesses that Erandis Vol commissioned it. Others claim the Celestial Queen was some other elvish source entirely. The Progenitor, who carried the thing out of the Greenbluff mine with his own hands, wrote: *"The Queen did not destroy it. I have come to believe she could not."*

The two readings run opposite ways. If Erandis commissioned it, the chorus is her project and Uriel is executing it. If the Celestial Queen was one of Vol's enemies, the bell was made *against* the Mark of Death and Uriel is running her ancestors' murder weapon backwards. Nothing recovered settles it.

## Conduct in role

Erandis Vol has not been seen, heard, quoted or located in thousands of years. She remains in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house; where she is, whether she is still capable of acting, and whether the people acting in her name have ever received an instruction from her are not recorded.

## Right now

She has not surfaced. The Empress bears her mark, the Doves invoke her as the great mother at every consecration, a dragon has testified about her child, and ten bells are still hanging. Whether she is a participant, a patron, or a corpse being spoken for is not established.
