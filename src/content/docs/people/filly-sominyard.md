**Aundairian noble · Member of the Royal Economic Commission · Fascist · Level Unknown · Alive at last record**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

**Filly Sominyard** is an Aundairian noble and a member of the Aundairian "Royal Economic Commission", described in the only surviving characterisation as a fascist. Sominyard was in the audience at the Grand Theatre in Sharn on the night the conductor Krespo turned on the house, and was one of four people carried out alive by the four Imperial agents who became [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). Face, age, species, family, holdings and history are not recorded.

## The Commission

The Royal Economic Commission is an Aundairian body; the quotation marks around its name are the source's own. Its remit, its membership, and its relationship to the Imperial Trade Federation under [Lord Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/), who holds supreme power over Imperial industrial policy, are not recorded. The symphony at which Sominyard was met was attended by members of the Summit and the Imperial Assembly; whether Sominyard sat on either is not recorded. Nothing of what Sominyard believes is recorded either.

## The Grand Theatre

Sominyard was in the audience at the Grand Theatre in Sharn on the night the conductor Krespo turned on the house. The musicians became dozens of demonic apparitions and slaughtered much of the audience; Krespo destroyed himself in a final act of mass murder. Four Imperial agents got a handful of people out, and Sominyard was one of them: **the party escaped with Heighrick, Goldenhew, Tremblepike and Sominyard.** See [City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/).

The other three carried out of that theatre were an Imperial Minister, the master of Imperial industrial policy, and the Transportation Secretary of the metro Karnathi state. It is the only recorded sighting of Sominyard.
