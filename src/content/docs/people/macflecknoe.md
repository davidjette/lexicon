**Species unknown · Legal scholar and poet · [The Cube](/places/the-cube/) · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* MacFleckno

**MacFlecknoe** is a reclusive expert in intergalactic law who has made his home aboard [the Cube](/places/the-cube/) for over two hundred years. Little is known about him except that he has published a few well-received books of poetry, and a comprehensive ten-volume set of law tomes considered the final word on the convoluted legal system of the Xaryxian Empire. He is one of the only beings outside [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/) with any knowledge of its laws. He is also a mediocre poet. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes; Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

In *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* Astral Command ordered the captain of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) to stop at the station and seek him out. The Astralex could not say how to find him or even what he looked like, and because he did not want to be found, the station's factions would only point the way in return for a service. [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Caprica](/people/caprica/) found him. See [the Chingwa's wish](/history/the-chingwas-wish/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>
