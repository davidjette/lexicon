**Old man · Prisoner of the bandit camp · [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), Level 1 · Walked away**

**Soggy Blankets** is an old man the party met on the first level of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), where he was the bandit camp's prisoner. He lies in wet blankets with a chain around his neck, and when he pops up he pulls the chain off and immediately starts resetting the traps. He speaks in riddles. He walked away from the party.

<small>DM notes: "(this asshole is a portal)"</small>

## Physical description

An old man lying in wet blankets with a chain around his neck. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3101)</small> He is drawn in a hood, with long hair, a long shaded beard and a slight smile. <small>(DM sketch, IMG_3101)</small>

## Undermountain

He was the bandit camp's prisoner on Level 1 when the party met him. By then they had met [Tripp Sandhill](/people/tripp-sandhill/) and recovered the mission file for [O-3255](/items/o-3255/). <small>(DM notes, IMG_3105)</small>

He pops up, pulls the chain off, and immediately starts resetting traps:

- the secret door trap
- the fallen chandelier
- the spear trap

He will walk away, but not attempt to run or hide, and as soon as the party's attention is elsewhere he slips away. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3106)</small> He walked away. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3107)</small>
