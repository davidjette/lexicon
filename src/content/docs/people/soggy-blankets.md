**Old man · Halaster Blackcloak in disguise · Prisoner of the bandit camp · [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), Level 1 · Walked away**

**Soggy Blankets** is [Halaster Blackcloak](/people/halaster-blackcloak/), the Mad Mage, in the guise of an old man the party met on the first level of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), where he was the bandit camp's prisoner. He lies in wet blankets with a chain around his neck, and when he pops up he pulls the chain off and immediately starts resetting the traps. He speaks in riddles. He walked away from the party.

<small>DM notes: "(this asshole is a portal)"</small>

## Physical description

An old man lying in wet blankets with a chain around his neck. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3101)</small> He is drawn in a hood, with long hair, a long shaded beard and a slight smile. <small>(DM sketch, IMG_3101)</small>

## Undermountain

He was the bandit camp's prisoner on Level 1 when the party met him. By then they had met [Tripp Sandhill](/people/tripp-sandhill/) and recovered the mission file for [O-3255](/items/o-3255/). <small>(DM notes, IMG_3105)</small>

He pops up, pulls the chain off, and immediately starts resetting traps:

- the secret door trap
- the fallen chandelier
- the spear trap

He will walk away, but not attempt to run or hide, and as soon as the party's attention is elsewhere he slips away. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3106)</small> He walked away. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3107)</small> He walked away during the fight with the shadows. <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

## The Mad Mage

Soggy Blankets is Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Mage, and he knows where the book the party are hunting, [O-3255](/items/o-3255/), is. Dave asked the table, "We're your theories about soggy blankets true?" <small>(sic)</small>, and Nico answered, "That he was the mad mage and knew where that book is? Yeah". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

## At the table

Dave added Soggy Blankets to the Undermountain adventure: "Soggy blankets isn’t in the book but I’m so glad I added him , there’s like no natural primer for the dungeon". In the published adventure that role falls to a Halaster simulacrum, which needs its mask found and figured out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>
