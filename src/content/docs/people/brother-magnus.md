**Hill Dwarf · Cleric of Brightmantle · Temple Holdings LLC · Level 5 · Alive**

*Also known as:* Magnus

Brother Magnus is a hill dwarf cleric of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/), the God of Discovery and Invention, and a member of Temple Holdings LLC. With [Kara](/people/kara/) he broke into the buried moonstone machine beneath Snowy Mountain, the trespass that opened [Arc II — Motherstone](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/). After the insurgency at Motherstone he reactivated the Obliviator and the Lexicon. Lex was then built into the party's airship, the Arielle, whose drive is [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), and at the Second Battle of Motherstone Lex transferred himself to the ship and the Motherstone Lexicon died. Magnus and [Drefan](/people/drefan/) lost their divine spells in the Time of Troubles, and Magnus raised **Percival**, [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/)'s squire. When Brightmantle sickened in Arc VII his power faded again, to third-level spells; at the god's funeral he received a final blessing that restored him in full and let him empower the faithful of Brightmantle within a mile.

## Bearing and arms

Magnus is not physically described in the session record. His sheet records a hill dwarf of Neutral Good alignment, hermit-raised, standing at AC 11 with 36 hit points and a 25-foot stride. He sees sixty feet into the dark, shrugs off poison by dwarven resilience, and reads the grain of worked stone by Stonecunning. He has WIS 16 against a strength of 9 and a dexterity of 7, and speaks Common, Dwarvish and Undercommon.

## The Church of Brightmantle

Brightmantle is the God of Discovery and Invention, the god who made the music box called *the Infanta's Dream*, whose recovered memory is the one thing that can wake a goddess from a poisoned crown. He is a dwarven god with an avatar who once walked as a man, and the love between that avatar and the first Infanta — **a genuine blasphemy**, the moonblade Silverbane calls it — is the union that founded Motherstone itself. Magnus's channelled divinity turns undead.

Brightmantle is the dwarven king who built [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), and he built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) that ran the heart of the city, infusing it with his own personality and intelligence. The Company's archive reached the same identification: "The Company believes Magnus' patron god Brightmantle could have been the dwarven king who built Motherstone. Review of cursed historical record confirms this." <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

## Snowy Mountain

Magnus and Kara followed the half-elf apothecary Fhastine's moon-marked clue to the mine beneath Snowy Mountain and broke into the buried moonstone machine — the two of them alone, while the rest of the company sheltered the Infanta at Leftfork. That break-in opened Arc II — Motherstone and the hidden history of the setting with it.

## Public and private

At the court of the [Mundi](/organizations/the-mundi/) king in the crystal city, Magnus's booming both cowed the moon-elves and antagonised them, and King **Chad** responded by quietly working to turn [Drefan](/people/drefan/) against him — the first crack the Mundi tried to open in the party. He had a **vision** about the fading of the gods, and word of it travelled far enough that the high priest of Punis sent [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), a prince of Cormyr, across the world to ask him what he saw.

## The Lexicon and the Anachron

The Company's archive credits Magnus with restarting the machines after the revolution: "Following the insurgency at Motherstone, it is believed the dwarf heretic Magnus used some special knowledge to reactive the Obliviator and the Lexicon, based on reports from Carlysle and other operatives on Snowdown." <small>(Sic on "reactive." Arc IV, SCTC Archives, which also lists him as "sentenced to death by Church of Moradin.")</small>

Lex had been built into the party's airship, the Arielle, which the Anachron drives. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-05)</small> After the party destroyed the body taken by the mechanical heart of [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/), Roland touched the Palantir, and Wainwright St. Cloud mind-controlled him through it. Roland destroyed the Lexicon and let the Company into Motherstone. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small> About three rounds into the Second Battle of Motherstone, Lex transferred himself to the ship and lost all connection to the Motherstone Lexicon, which is dead. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small>

Aboard the Arielle he kept working on the machine: "Pim and Magnus are working with Lex on the Anachron when Nub Clitman runs in." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small>

## The Time of Troubles

In the era of [the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), Magnus and Drefan lost their divine spells. After one victory the party was told to "Maybe go find some of the Infanta that would give Magnus and Dref their spells back". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-11)</small>

Magnus raised **Percival** (Parsifal), the young would-be knight sworn as Roland's squire.

## The death of a god

In [Arc VII — Leviathan](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/), word reached the party at sea that "Brightmantle is suddenly unwell," and Magnus's power faded again with him: "Magnus's power fades and he is limited to Level 3 spells (retains slots and other abilities)." By the time the ship returned to Motherstone, the god was dead and "Elistrae and the entire city weeps." The funeral that followed gathered their allies from all over the world. During the ceremony "Lex and Magnus touch the body before it is entombed," and "Magnus sees a vision of Brightmantle and receives his goodbye and final blessing." No attempt to raise the god succeeded, but "he leaves sufficient trace of himself in his last act inside of Magnus that he regains his full power and can empower the faithful of Brightmantle within a mile radius," which is how the muster against the [Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/) is blessed.

<small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears." The ship on that voyage carries "no anachron, a standard arcane core which requires a new Power Stone to teleport.")</small>

## Right now

Magnus stands at Motherstone with a full-strength blessing, empowering the faithful of every people the company has saved as they muster for the march on Netheril.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation:** To understand why the gods are fading, and to keep his own from going quietly.

**Likes & Dislikes:** Likes: Brightmantle · discovery · invention · saying it out loud · breaking into machines nobody has opened in a century · Kara's company on a bad climb. Dislikes: the Mundi court · being managed · undead · a god who slips away without explaining himself.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** His volume at the Mundi court both cowed the moon-elves and antagonised them.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **[Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)** — his patron god, the builder of Motherstone and the Lexicon. Dead in Arc VII, having spent his last act through Magnus.
- **Lex** — the Lexicon, built into the Arielle, whose drive is the Anachron. Worked on the machine with Magnus and Pim aboard the Arielle; touched Brightmantle's body with him at the funeral.
- **Roland Deschain** — fellow founder. Alive.
- **Percival** — Roland's squire, raised by Magnus.
- **Kara** — his partner on the Snowy Mountain break-in that opened Motherstone. Alive.
- **King Chad of the Mundi** — cowed by him at court, and thereafter working against him. Whereabouts after the Second Battle of Motherstone unrecorded.
- **Zanzibar** — the Cormyrean prince sent to interview him about his vision, who stayed on as a hero. Alive.
- **Drefan** — fellow founder; the man Chad tried to turn against him. Alive.

**Organizations / Groups:** Temple Holdings LLC · the Church of Brightmantle

**Holdings:** the Old Temple at Harloch, bought by the company and given the name Temple Holdings, LLC

**Items:** Channel Divinity (Turn Undead), the standing gift of his order. A hermit's "powerful and unique discovery" — his sheet records that he has one, but not what it is.
