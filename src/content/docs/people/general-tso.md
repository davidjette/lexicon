**Duergar (deep dwarf) · General of the Teryaki · The Teryaki clan · Alive**

**General Tso** is a duergar commander of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan and the brother of its matriarch, [Madame Tso](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/). He commands the clan's clearing parties in the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/), opening sealed doors one at a time to recover the fallen of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) for burial rites, and casts *speak with dead* on the fallen as part of the rite. He finished the consecration of [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) as princess and heir alongside [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), and when warned that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) was free above the peak he refused to evacuate the clan.

## Physical description

A deep dwarf met at the head of a search party: a small group of duergar, one of them mounted on a giant spider. No further description is recorded.

## The work

General Tso commands the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/)'s clearing parties in the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/). The job is a corridor of sealed doors with undead behind them, opened one at a time, so that the fallen of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) can be found and given proper burial rites. The clan was managing less than a room a day.

He casts *speak with dead* on the fallen as part of the rite - which is how the party got [Scallion Pancake](/people/scallion-pancake/)'s testimony, and with it the first outline of the uprising, the horn, and the name of the woman who rang the bell.

## Public and private

In front of outsiders he is his sister's instrument and keeps discipline accordingly. When a kinsman suggested asking the pink skins to help look for a missing boy, the General shut it down on the spot: *"Don't talk to them. Just go look for him."* He sent his own scout, [Hetta](/people/hetta/), instead - and Hetta came back infected and rang the bell.

Away from the politics he is the clan member most willing to work alongside the strangers. When [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) laid [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) on the shrine altar and began a Silver Flame prayer in Dwarvish, it was General Tso who crossed the floor and finished the rite with him. They consecrated her together as princess and heir.

## Right now

Warned that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) is free above the peak and means to enslave or eat his people, the General refused to move them, and it was his word that cancelled out the argument to evacuate:

> We cannot leave. This is the purpose of our clan. We have given so much.

He is in the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/) with the rest of the kin, a burned child, and a plan to renew a pact with a dragon who has already stated his terms.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki clan; the mourning-party out of the Mror Holds.

**Contacts & Relations:** [Madame Tso Teryaki](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/) - his sister and the matriarch; he defers to her in public without exception. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) - the clan's runesmith, who left with the party and would have carried the Crown of Seven Stars straight to the General had it been given to him. [Hetta](/people/hetta/) - his scout, sent out and lost. [Wonton](/people/wonton/) - a young kinsman in his escort, boxed round the ears by his own people for talking out of turn.
