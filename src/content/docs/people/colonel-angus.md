**Human · Colonel, commanding the urban detachment · [Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) · Lawful neutral · Alive**

**Also known as:** the Colonel · <small>"the perfect soldier"</small>

**Colonel Angus** is the human Purple Dragons officer commanding the urban detachment at [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) in occupied [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), where he holds Command and Dispatch, the fort's nerve centre. On the night the fort's prisoners broke out he fought [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and was brought down grievously wounded by the prisoner and an escaped owlbear. He was revived in time for dawn and attended the hanging of [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/), [Maverick](/people/maverick/) and Barold Bonds.

## Physical description

Medium height, lean, clean-shaven, and still. His breastplate is polished but not decorative. A saber hangs at his left hip; a flintlock pistol rides in a tooled leather holster at his right; the second key ring of Fort Maximillien is clipped to his sword belt where he can feel it. He stands at the map table studying the Dock Ward "with the focus of a surgeon reading an x-ray." He does not look up when people enter the room. He already knows they are there.

<small>The session record describes him differently: "a large bull of a man with a close cropped haircut and oversized barrel chest and arms, a greatsword on his back."</small>

## The command post

Angus holds Command and Dispatch, the nerve centre of the fort: a heavy oak table under pinned maps of Waterdeep marked in coloured tacks, a pegboard of keys, and — mounted by the door on iron brackets like a weapon in its scabbard — the brass dispatch horn, the only thing in the fort that can raise a general lockdown. Two elite soldiers hold the room with him: **Sergeant Hale**, devoted "the way a weapon is devoted to the hand that wields it," and **Corporal Thresh**, whose job is to notice things. The room smells of ink, leather, and cold discipline.

## Public and private

Publicly, Angus does not shout — the barracks' own explanation is *"Angus doesn't shout. That's why men fear him."* He walks a ninety-minute circuit between the command post, the courtyard and the barracks, and the fort keeps time by it.

Privately, he keeps a personal journal in cipher. What it contains is his doubts about whether [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) can hold Waterdeep at all if the farmlands fully revolt.

## Beliefs

He believes peace is imposed and not negotiated. He believes a city that has forgotten discipline can be reminded of it. He believes a clean execution with no speeches produces no martyrs.

## Conduct in role

Escalation under his standing orders is local — doubled checks, a runner sent walking rather than running, doors watched, patrols tightened — and only the horn goes global, which is why the horn is guarded and he carries the key to the alarm cabinet himself. He does not chase; he seals rooms. Faced with intruders he offers terms exactly once.

> Weapons down. You have made a series of poor calculations, and I will give you one opportunity to unmake them.

Wounded: *"Good. You are competent. I had hoped so. It makes what comes next less wasteful."* Losing, he does not run: *"Hale. Thresh. Orderly retreat. Seal the courtyard. Do not run. We do not run."*

## On the Empire

> Zanzibar united an empire. He did not ask permission. He did not hold a vote. He imposed order and called it peace. That is what peace is. That is all it has ever been.

On his general's dinner parties: *"The Governor courts old money because old money understands permanence. Merchants understand profit. Dyksterhaus mistakes taxation for tyranny because tyranny is a word that sells."* On the hanging: *"Shadowhigh hangs at dawn. The scaffold will be clean. The crowd will be orderly. There will be no speeches. There will be no martyrs."*

## The night of the escape

A disguised Nicollo shoved forged orders under his nose in the command centre demanding to see Varkus; Angus pushed him aside, told him to sit down, and sent troops to investigate the animal screams outside. **Sir Loin**, the white-clad knight beside him, went to the kitchen and fell to a blood-soaked BattleBeast. Angus and a crowd of guards then fought BattleBeast themselves until the owlbear broke through the wall behind them and tore the crowd apart; together the beast and the prisoner brought the Colonel down, grievously wounded. He was revived in time for dawn, and attended the hanging of Shadowhigh, Maverick and Barold Bonds with an angry face.

The prisoners had been taken after [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/), and Maverick was upstairs, where he had been questioned by [Sir Merriam Wordsworker](/people/sir-merriam-wordsworker/). Lenore, BattleBeast and Heyu escaped down the fort's trash pit into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/). The full account is at [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/).

## Items

**Ring B** — keys to the stairs up, the alarm cabinet and the upstairs protocols; clipped to his sword belt.\
**The ciphered journal** — his doubts about Varkus and the farmlands, written where nobody in his own garrison can read them.\
**A sealed letter in violet wax** — addressed to General Varkus, on his desk, not yet delivered.\
**A letter draft in his own hand** — *"When Roe abdicates — and he will — Waterdeep must already be aligned. The old houses understand this. The merchants will be made to understand."*

## Contacts and relations

**General Peterbilt Varkus** — his commander; the subject of his cipher. Active.\
**Sergeant Hale** — devoted to him personally; would die for him. Active.\
**Corporal Thresh** — watchful, stands near the horn. Active.\
**Sergeant Drask** — a cynic in the pens who thinks executions are bad for morale. Active.\
**Surgeon Alaire** — a civilian in his fort who does not want bloodshed and says so. Active.\
**Sir Loin** — a handsome white-clad knight with long black hair, a pencil moustache and a thin ornate longsword; fell in the kitchen doorway, revived for the dawn. Active.\
**Shadowhigh** — a prisoner he intended to hang without ceremony. [Hanged at dawn](/people/shadowhigh/), and raised in Undermountain.
