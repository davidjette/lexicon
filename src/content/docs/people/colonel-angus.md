**Species unknown · Colonel · [Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) · Fort Maximillien · Alive**

**Colonel Angus** is a Purple Dragons officer at [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) in occupied [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), second to [General Peterbilt Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/). On the night of [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/) he was in the fort's command center with Sir Loin when [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), in disguise, demanded to see Varkus; Angus pushed him aside and sent troops out. He then fought [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) with a crowd of guards and was brought down by BattleBeast and an escaped owlbear. He was revived, and attended the hanging of [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/), [Maverick](/people/maverick/) and [Barold Bonds](/people/barold-bonds/) at dawn.

## Physical description

"A large bull of a man with a close cropped haircut and oversized barrel chest and arms, a greatsword on his back." <small>(Fort Maximillien narrative)</small>

## The night of the breakout

The prisoners had been taken after [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/). Angus and **Sir Loin**, a handsome white-clad knight with long black hair, a pencil mustachio and a thin, ornately adorned longsword, were in the command center, distracted by the melee and animal screams coming from outside, when Nicollo shoved forged orders into Angus's face and demanded to see Varkus. Angus pushed him aside, told him to sit down, and ordered troops to investigate the ruckus. Sir Loin went to the kitchen and fell to BattleBeast in the doorway.

Lenore found BattleBeast in combat with Angus and a crowd of guards. The owlbear broke into the room from behind and tore the crowd of soldiers apart, and together BattleBeast and the owlbear brought Angus down, both of them grievously wounded in the process. Angus and Sir Loin were both killed that night, and revived after. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The hanging

At dawn Barold, Maverick and Shadowhigh were led to the gallows on the parade ground before the Purple Dragon garrison, the War Wizard detachment and General Varkus. Sir Loin and Colonel Angus, both grievously wounded, had been revived, and attended with angry faces. The three were killed by blasts of magic from wizards on the battlements when Maverick and Barold tried to break free, and their bodies were thrown down the fort's trash pit into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/).
