**Medium humanoid · Warlock · Founder of [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/) · Holder of the THRONE card · Alive**

Rex Bedsheets is a warlock bound by debt to a Mummy Lord, the founder and leader of the sales guild [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/), and the holder of the **THRONE** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). Through Rexium he sells the Charm of the All Seeing, and the business of selling it, to the people of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/). On the night of [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/) the party attacked him at a Rexium sales meeting in the inn's converted barn. He dominated [Maverick](/people/maverick/) and every charm-wearer in the room, fled in a carriage that [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) set alight, and in its wreckage used the THRONE card to turn Nicollo on [Lenore](/people/lenore/). As the party were arrested he shouted that they were murderers and thieves. Afterwards Maverick persuaded [Sir Merriam Wordsworker](/people/sir-merriam-wordsworker/) of the Purple Dragons to investigate him and his pyramid scheme.

## The debt

Rex is a "debt-bound warlock of Mummy LORD". <small>(DM notes)</small> His patron's sleep is tied to the card he carries:

> While alive and in possession of THRONE Mummy lord is in torpor, if not, Lord wakes and gains direct control of all wearing the Charms

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## Rexium

Rex started Rexium "to give people a route to guaranteed success." He holds packed sales hype meetings "to juice followers and collect energy + gold", and he "sells the DREAM." <small>(DM notes)</small> He is the inventor of the Rexium Protocols. Rexium's members sell the pyramid-shaped Charm of the All Seeing and wear one themselves. Its meetings are for sellers only, and a recruit can meet Rex once sold. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Rexium's pamphlet shows Rex on a throne, and in his pocket, the THRONE card. <small>(Session narrative; DM notes.)</small>

His introduction at a sellers' meeting runs:

> It's now my distinct pleasure to introduce the man who needs no introduction. He's the inventor of the Rexium Protocols, the founder of the feast, and he literally saved my life....

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Grub + Grog

Rex came to [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/) in a carriage smaller but much nicer than the party's, while his footman unloaded cardboard boxes. His follower Megan Moron recruited Maverick in the bar and gave him the pamphlet with the THRONE card in it.

At the sales meeting in the converted barn, before about ten people, the footman gave a sales pitch and introduced Rex. Rex came out, quickly cast a charm over the audience through the golden metal charms they all wore, and took a small chest of gold. When the party attacked, he cast *dominate person* on Maverick, forcing him and all the charm-wearers, commoners mostly, to attack [Heyu](/people/heyu/), and ran for his carriage. [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) leapt on him; Rex was terrified, but a magical shield of ice interposed, froze, and exploded in fire, knocking Battlebeast to the ground grievously wounded. Nicollo set the carriage alight with oil and fought the footman on its roof. Maverick's hypnotic pattern stunned Rex's horses, the footman and Lenore, but not Rex, who shot rays of magic into his horses to wake them and drove off down the road in the burning carriage with Nicollo holding on.

Nicollo killed Rex's horse, and the carriage toppled, nearly killing them both. In the flaming wreckage Rex pulled the THRONE card from his pocket and dominated Nicollo's mind, sending him back to attack Lenore, whom Nicollo stabbed before he came to. When Battlebeast came after him in the party's cart, Rex shot a blast into the horses and toppled that cart too. As the party were led away, Rex yelled "they're murderers! thieves!"

The Purple Dragon Knights who broke up the fight found the party in conflict with "the hypnotized followers of Rex Bedsheets and the Amulet of the All-Seeing." <small>(Session notes)</small> The charmed crowd that attacked Heyu seemed to have no memory afterwards of being charmed or of the violence.

## Under investigation

Questioned at [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) by Sir Merriam Wordsworker about the incident at the Grub + Grog, Maverick convinced Merriam of his innocence, and then convinced him to investigate Rex Bedsheets and his pyramid scheme. <small>(Session notes; DM notes.)</small>

**Magic:** Rex's recorded spells include *darkness*, *hex*, *eldritch blast*, *blight*, *counterspell*, *fear*, *dominate person*, *hellish rebuke*, *raise dead* and *animate dead*. He heals as he fights, and faster for every follower near him. <small>(DM notes)</small> In the fight at the Grub + Grog he shielded himself with ice that burst into fire, shot rays of magic, and dominated minds through the THRONE card.

**Organizations:** [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/), founder and leader.

**Items:** The **THRONE** card of the Deck of Many Things, carried in his pocket. A fine carriage, burned and wrecked at the Grub + Grog.

**Contacts & Relations:**
- **The Mummy Lord** - his patron, to whom he is bound by debt; in torpor while Rex lives and holds the THRONE card.
- **Megan Moron** - a Rexium seller who recruited Maverick; killed at the Grub + Grog.
- **His footman** - unloaded his boxes, gave the sales pitch and introduced him, and fought Nicollo on the burning carriage.
- **Maverick** - recruited into Rexium by Megan, dominated by Rex, and then the man who set Sir Merriam Wordsworker on him.
