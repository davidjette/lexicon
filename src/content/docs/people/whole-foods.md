**Nanny to the Lord Commander's son · The Veil Building penthouse · Deceased**

<small>Also known as: Whole Foods</small>

**Whole Foods** was the personal nanny to [Calcifer Blaze](/people/calcifer/), the Lord Commander's son, in the penthouse at the summit of the Veil Building in Sharn. When [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) took Calcifer and his father, they took her with them; [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) found her bound and terrified in the ritual room beneath the Zeir residence with masked cultists performing a ceremony over her, cut the cultists down and freed her. She is dead. No description of her survives.

## The household

Imperial observation files list her by function only: Calcifer Blaze, age eight, is rarely observed outside Imperial compounds and is monitored by dedicated personnel at all times; he has a personal nanny, Whole Foods, and one of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s personal Honor Guards.

The penthouse sits above the government offices, the Arcane Eye control rooms and a high-security prison, and is staffed by people vetted by the Iron Veil and watched by the same surveillance network that watched the rest of Sharn. She raised the most closely monitored child in the Empire, day to day.

**Right now:** Whole Foods is dead. She was alive and freed on the ritual-room floor; how and when she died is not recorded.
