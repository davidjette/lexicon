**Elf wizard · Lich (*baelnorn*) · Archmage · House Gallidann, then Queen Maab · Betrayer of Motherstone · Later advisor to the Infanta · Ally at the Egg**

*Also known as:* the Betrayer · Calix the Betrayer, from the [lullaby](/lore/calix-the-betrayer/) · <small>(the strange, undead, extraordinarily old elf calling himself the Masked Prophet in the rewritten past matches his description)</small>

**Calix** was an elf wizard and lich (*baelnorn*), brother of **Eldamir** of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/), whose betrayal at [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) brought on the **Forgetting**. In the age the elves call the **Heavenfall** he demanded the first Infanta, **Elistrada**, as his pupil and was refused; he stored parts of his soul throughout the smaller islands and allied with **Queen Maab**. He built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) together with [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, from Netherese magic and technology, opened the **Gate Ring** that made Motherstone a data vacuum, and fought beside [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/) and Eldamir in the war that obliviated Netheril, after Stone and Elistrada had founded Motherstone. When Stone and Elistrada then invited the Infanta of the world to live in peace in the city, Calix betrayed them and murdered dozens of Infanta at the peace-treaty meeting. He planned to obliviate Motherstone and frame [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) and the dwarvish people for his betrayal, and buried his own terminal-throne in order to replace the Lexicon with himself; Stone threw himself into the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) first, and the Forgetting followed.

The Forgetting drove Calix mad. He and Eldamir were cast out of the forgotten city and killed each other on the beach, and Calix survived as a lich, no longer knowing how to find his phylactery. Once the party cured the Lexicon in [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/) he was able to locate the city and attack them; they reached his vault, where his hacked throne and his phylactery were hidden in a chamber that had never been used, and emerged from the phylactery ten years in the future to find him at the young Infanta's side. He was dead by the [Age of the Infanta](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/). He is present afterwards as the Masked Prophet in the rewritten past, at the war council against Netheril in [Arc VII](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/), and as an ally at the Egg in [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

*For the Cormyrean nursery rhyme, see [Calix the Betrayer](/lore/calix-the-betrayer/).*

> Every step is seven days, but never stop to wonder, for wonderings fine but not when time is fleeting for our kingdom. When all the worlds inside your mind, forgetting is oblivion. So never sing this song again of Calix the Betrayer.

<small>— Cormyrean lullaby, on Calix's Ally Card. Sic on "wonderings" and "worlds"; the card sets the last two lines as a second stanza.</small>

## Bearing

Calix is a *baelnorn*: an elf who refused the grave and willingly turned himself undead. His sigil is emblazoned on the iron blood seal in the Motherstone dungeon, and again, centuries and planes away, throughout the walls of the Old Man's House in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). In the rewritten past of [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) a very strange elf, undead and extraordinarily old, calling himself the **Masked Prophet** moves among the elves, and he matches the description.

[Celion](/people/celion/) described him as "a follower, or a cultist, or a thrall, whatever you can call it, of a very powerful force." <small>(Arc III, Interlude.)</small>

## House Gallidann, and the first Infanta

In the age the elves call the **Heavenfall**, Calix was the brother of **Eldamir**, founder of House Gallidann. Where Eldamir built a house, Calix "moved into solitary study of magic on a nearby archipelago." When a human child showed mastery of elemental magic (the first Infanta, **Elistrada**, the goddess [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) reborn), Calix demanded the child as his pupil. Eldamir refused.

Calix stored parts of his soul throughout the smaller islands in hopes of extending his life beyond even an elf's, allied with **Queen Maab**, goddess of air and darkness, and rose again and again to seize the island's power.

He is kin to [Drefan](/people/drefan/) Gallidan. Dave: "young calix looks related to drefan because he is". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-08)</small>

## Motherstone and the Lexicon

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Motherstone was built around the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), a mind grown inside the world's largest perfect moonstone. Calix built the Lexicon together with [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, from Netherese magic and technology. "Motherstone was designed by Brightmantle and Calix to distrbute the infanta's power". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> Calix opened the **Gate Ring**, a stable abyssal vortex, to make the city a data vacuum pulling the echoes of all information in creation toward itself.

After Stone and Elistrada founded Motherstone, Calix fought beside them and beside his sister Eldamir in the war that obliviated the Netherese. Of the Netherese, Dave says Brightmantle and Calix "obliviated the culture itself". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-19)</small>

## The Betrayal and the Forgetting

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> After the war with the Netherese, Stone and Elistrada invited the Infanta of the world to come and live in peace in Motherstone. Calix betrayed them: at the peace-treaty meeting he murdered dozens of Infanta, a massacre that "led to the collapse of the world which has never fully recovered." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-06-26)</small> He planned to obliviate Motherstone and frame Brightmantle and the dwarvish people for his betrayal.

He buried his own terminal-throne in order to **replace the Lexicon with himself**: to dissolve his mind entirely into the crystal and become one with the Motherstones, using "the Lexicon and the Obliviator to accumulate all the knowledge in the world in time to protect himself from the devouring maw of the [Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/)." The override that locked every other terminal out of a hard reset was placed "on the Fifth of Leaffall, 49," by one user: Calix. Full account at [The Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). He also altered his vault and throne so that time passed slower inside and faster outside: the world would forget him quickly, and time would spin off into the future while his consciousness stayed unaware of the doom Toril faced from the Leviathan, which terrified him more than anything. The chamber was never used.

Stone pre-empted him: rather than let his wife [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/) believe he had turned on her, he threw himself into the Obliviator so that she would forget he had ever existed. That act made everything about Motherstone cursed and obliviated knowledge, and drove Calix and the Gallidan elves insane. Calix and Eldamir were cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other on the beach; Calix, immortal, rose again as a lich, mad, and the location of his own phylactery was among the things he no longer knew. [Mara Mageblood](/people/mara-mageblood/)'s journal places "the legendary duel between Calix and Eldamir which left both of them dead on the banks of the minor waines" roughly a year after the Fifth Day of Leaffall in the Forty-Ninth Year of the Age of the Infantas, and records that his tomb on the minor Waines is "empty now, as everyone knows". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-09-26)</small>

His remnant, in his phylactery beneath Motherstone, speaks in the words of the ancient drama *Calix, Betrayer*, which gives him four lines:

> My sin is the Forgetting.\
> Time is the only recompense.\
> If I live until I know myself again\
> I will live forever.

He has forgotten *himself* along with everything else. Mara Mageblood, having read a cursed Orcish tome a second time, wrote: "It haunts him even now. He knows he has forgotten. He seeks but knows not what." Off its last page she read *"a buried throne for a living god, for even he fears the Leviathan."* <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-09-26)</small>

## The lullaby

Calix is the subject of the nursery song [Calix the Betrayer](/lore/calix-the-betrayer/), whose last line is his name. Mara Mageblood's mother could not sing it without losing herself, and Mara copied it into the journal the heroes carried out of the mountain. Glacius' *History of the Moonshae Kingdom*, quoted in the same journal, records that "the nursery rhymes say" Calix "beguiled the child goddess and tried to woo her into darkness."

## The vault

In the Motherstone dungeon the blood seal that bears his sigil opens only with the blood of a dwarf. Beyond it lies a hidden temple where the copper dragon [Tylerjynex](/people/tylerjinex/), whom Calix beguiled and banished to a dark abyss, is kept blinded and slumped atop it. Within is Calix's phylactery, a pocket of frozen time.

Once the party had cured the Lexicon, Calix was able to locate the city, and he attacked them. They found his hacked throne and his phylactery hidden in his vault, in the chamber that had never been used, and emerged from the phylactery ten years in the future.

## After the time skip

Ten years later he reappears and conquers [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) with an Infanta. When the heroes emerge from the phylactery, the young Infanta who retakes the city introduces him as her "closest advisor who made the victory possible." He then put her on the Throne of Motherstone to extract from her the memory of the city, reactivate the Lexicon and release his phylactery. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-calix-throne-and-eldamir.md)</small> His crown locked her into a deadly trance on the Throne, where her divine power could be hijacked to destroy the island. The party used the music box *the Infanta's Dream*, made by [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/), to remind her of Stone, and she revived. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-calix-throne-and-eldamir.md)</small> Calix uses the time to enter the Orb after the location of his phylactery; the party follows him in, and draws Brightmantle's memory back out of the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) before he can erase it again. <small>(Arc II Part VII and Arc III, Interlude.)</small>

In the Motherstone finale [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) spoke to Calix as the Faceless Boy, a four-thousand-year-old god whose prophet Magnus then became. Patrick, who played Magnus: "I was lying to Calix". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-07-01)</small>

By the [Age of the Infanta](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) Calix is dead, and the Company spends years failing to unlock the Calix puzzle in the broken Lexicon. The Naga is "out there, plotting to avenge Calix, whispering in the ears" of the Forsworn. <small>(Arc III, Interlude.)</small>

## The remnant and the war against Netheril

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> In the rewritten past he walks as the Masked Prophet, guiding the elves toward ruin with a plan to end the rule of gods and men in one stroke and restore the Moon.

In the Time of Troubles, Calix and Eldamir told the party to contact them when they located [Myth Drannor](/places/myth-drannor/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) did not yet know of Calix's betrayal, because Magnus was keeping it secret. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> In Antiquity Calix refused to go along with [Valen](/people/valen/) and [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), as Atlas III did. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> After the battle at the peak of Sigil, Calix and Atlas "returned to their Infanta to lead attacks on Netherese cities and the Egg itself". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> "Calix also has a Palantir btw, Zanzibar looked into it". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> See [The Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). In December 2021 the party yelled at Calix "for getting the job done". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-04)</small>

He sits on the Council of Forgetting, and in Arc VII the party's allies gather "with Calix and Lex's help to plan that final invasion of Netheril"; when the commanders assemble it is "Lex and Calix" who "hold a briefing to deliver all the final pieces." Calix hears the **Call of Cthulhu** himself, and turns. It is Calix who exposes how [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) deceived the Netherese queens into serving the Leviathan, warping their understanding so they would bring all the magic of their world to bear against their own existence. [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), Eldamir, Calix and the Moon Elves pledge together at Brightmantle's funeral, and the moon elves who ride out at the end ride with Elistrae, Eldamir and Calix. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

## The Ars Calixia

Calix's magnum opus and autobiography is the *Ars Calixia*, penned with a magic quill circa 100 DR. Its volumes XIII to XVI, a gold and adamantine-bound tome whose contents are an [oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/), are the Company's [O-3255](/items/o-3255/), lost into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/); in the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) the sister volumes I to XII and XVII to XXV reside in the personal library of the Company's Chairman of the Board. <small>(O-3255)</small>

**Organizations / Groups:** House Gallidann (by blood, and against it) · the court of Queen Maab · the Forsworn, by long alliance · the Council of Forgetting

**Holdings:** the buried throne beneath Motherstone · a phylactery held in a pocket of frozen time, ten years deep · fragments of soul scattered across the lesser isles of the Moonshae

**Items:** the blood seal in the Motherstone dungeon, marked with his sigil and openable only with the blood of a dwarf · the Gate Ring above the city, his vortex, still turning · a [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/) · the *Ars Calixia*
