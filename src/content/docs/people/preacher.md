**Warforged · Founder of RUST · The last Warforged in Sharn, in hiding · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Preacher</small>

**Preacher** is a Warforged, the founder and leader of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), and the last Warforged in Sharn. Raised in the Cogs, he spent decades working alongside Cogborn labourers, taking on the most dangerous industrial tasks that would have killed a flesh-and-blood worker. When the Lord Commander outlawed his kind, most Warforged fled toward Gorgonhorn or the Mournlands; Preacher refused to leave and vanished into the undercity with his adopted daughter [Misty](/people/misty-kross/), spending years in the abandoned sub-transit tunnels sheltering Cogborn fugitives and smuggling families away from Iron Veil patrols until that work became R.U.S.T. He led the Cogborn out of Sharn through [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) and the Spine to the mountain tunnel toward Gorgonhorn, and R.U.S.T. now holds a seat on the Council of Sharn.

## Physical description

He stands like a clergyman and is built like a furnace fitting. The head is bare grey metal, smooth over the crown, with dark hollows where a face's eyes would be and a heavy vented respirator plate closed over the lower jaw; a high segmented collar rises behind it. Over the frame he wears a long black vestment, cross-sashed at the chest and belted, with a round silver medallion pinned high on the left shoulder, and a full black cloak over that. Black boots. In the Sub-Transit control room the red-bordered display screens throw the only colour on him.

## R.U.S.T.

R.U.S.T. — Resistance Under Sharn's Tyranny — is a network of Cogborn, Warforged and human sympathisers built out of scattered acts of survival: smuggling families through forgotten tunnels, hiding fugitives in abandoned rail lines, keeping ahead of Iron Veil patrols in a district that was being emptied. It runs out of an abandoned Sub-Transit station beneath the Cogs, through Misty's Pawn Shop in the Lower City, and along a chain of safe routes and hidden shelters Preacher built himself. Its objective is to get its people out of Sharn alive, and Preacher's long-term aim is to lead them to Gorgonhorn and unite them with [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s Hundreds in the Mournlands, where a blended Cogborn-Warforged society is beginning to take root.

To the Empire he is a capital offence. When the Lord Commander outlawed all Warforged in Sharn, most fled toward Gorgonhorn or the Mournlands and the decree was recorded as complied with. One remained. He vanished into the undercity with his adopted daughter Misty and spent years in the abandoned sub-transit tunnels, using his knowledge of the Cogs' forgotten infrastructure to shelter Cogborn fugitives and move families away from patrols. He is calm, steady and deeply principled, and he does not seek to liberate the city — only to save the people trapped beneath it.

He was raised in the Cogs and spent decades working alongside Cogborn labourers, taking on the most dangerous industrial tasks that would have killed a flesh-and-blood worker. His inner circle is Misty and her husband [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/), who run the pawn shop over the rail-line entrance; [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/), who carries word through the Lower City; and, outside the organisation but trusted absolutely, [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) and [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/).

**The thing he did not know:** years ago, after the outlawing, a young tiefling woman found his hidden cell in the Lower City. He chased her through the drainage tunnels and in the struggle she fell from the aqueduct's upper walkway and was impaled on the machinery below. He believed he had killed her. He had not. She is Eden, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s youngest half-sister, and the Empire raised her as EDN-00 — the blue-skinned, red-eyed hybrid the Hundreds call the Blue Devil and believe cannot be killed. He recognised her on a briefing screen and understood what he had begun.

He believes the Cogborn are his people and that the argument ends there. He believes liberation is a luxury and evacuation is the duty. He believes something called Lexicon is worth praying to, and leads his people in song and prayer sheets when they walk out of the city.

He briefed three armed strangers by telling them the truth of the crisis in plain order — the Cogborn are trapped, the Iron Veil is tightening its grip, the window is closing — and then told them the price [Blair](/people/blair/) had set, without recommending they pay it. He has never once been recorded raising his voice, and he has never been recorded leaving anyone behind.

Generations of Cogborn workers and Warforged labourers built the city's industrial heart, and he was among them; the furnaces shaped him and decades of the worst jobs on the line kept him there. Then the decree came, the Iron Veil seized the factories, and Cogborn labour was replaced with trained soldiers. The community — already weakened by hazardous work, malnutrition, and the loss of the Warforged who had shouldered the deadliest tasks — was pushed out of its jobs, its neighbourhoods and finally its homes, into collapsing tunnels beneath the Cogs. Preacher stayed hidden through all of it, raising Misty, opening routes, and gradually turning a survival habit into a network. Then a peaceful protest in the Upper City erupted into a riot when an explosion tore through the crowd, twenty-one of his people and forty-three Cogborn civilians died, and there was no longer any option but to leave.

In Nico's notes for the first meeting of the Council of Sharn, Preacher sat in the unified Hundreds and R.U.S.T. bloc with [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/), [Doppler](/people/doppler-klink/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). Warforged citizenship and legal personhood was Preacher's demand, backed by Locke, and the bloc also asked for a memorial for the Cogborn victims, especially those lost in the POSÉ riot. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 19)</small>

**Right now:** the Cogborn walked out of Sharn through [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) and the Spine, and Preacher led them in song at the mountain tunnel while Locke Pierce teleported in to meet him. R.U.S.T. holds a seat in the new Sharn: Warforged citizenship, the arrest of the Iron Veil engineers, and a Department of Infrastructure, all carried at the first Council. Warforged troops and engineers are stabilising the city and restoring the Cogs he was raised in.

**Organizations / Groups:** founder and leader of R.U.S.T.; partner of the Hundreds at Gorgonhorn; seated faction of the Council of Sharn.

**Holdings:** the abandoned Sub-Transit control room beneath the Cogs · the tunnel network, safe routes and hidden shelters he built over years · the rail-line entrance beneath Misty's Pawn Shop · the mountain tunnel toward Gorgonhorn.

**Items:** R.U.S.T. communication necklaces, distributed to allies by Locke Pierce's Warforged · prayer sheets, handed out at the mountain tunnel.

**The Routes:** He carries the forgotten infrastructure of the Cogs in his head — collapsed lines, maintenance shafts, sealed platforms, drainage runs — and has spent years turning that map into an evacuation. It is why a sealed population in the Pit could be moved at all, why the Iron Veil never found the shelters, and why the RUST Runners can still cross a district the Empire believes it controls.

## Lexicon

At the mountain tunnel, with the Cogborn assembled and the way out finally open, Preacher and the RUST Runners led the people in song and handed out prayer sheets, praying to the love of something called Lexicon. No source explains what Lexicon is.

## The Blue Devil

It was Preacher who identified her. The Hundreds had been fighting a hybrid soldier deployed from Iron Veil airships that they believed unkillable; Preacher recognised the face from a chase through the drainage tunnels years before, and the recognition closed the last gap in the rebellion's picture of Project E.D.E.N. — that it is not a military programme but a system of body harvesting, resurrection and forced hybridisation, and that its prototype was a woman he thought he had killed.
