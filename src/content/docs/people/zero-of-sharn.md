**Human · Cleric of the Silver Flame · The Nest · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Zero** — which is not a nickname but the whole of her name. Whatever she was called before the rite is not recorded anywhere, and that is the point of the rite.

Zero of Sharn is a young human cleric of the Silver Flame and a covert agent of the rebel cell known as The Nest, known by her codename "Zero", a title that marks a rite of passage among certain women of the faith. Those who take the name strip away their former identity, devoting themselves fully to the Flame and living as embodiments of its discipline and sacrifice. She is the daughter of **Fema Nolan** of Thrane — later **Zero-One**, founder and leader of Fulcrum — and the half-sister of **Izaak**, the Fulcrum liaison, with whom she has held a covert line between Sharn and the Breeland farms through a shared falcon for years. She identified the BioTec technician [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) and worked him for weeks in an upper-city bar until he gave up the specification of the Ghost Chip, then onboarded the three strangers sent to steal it — [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) — into the Nest. Three years after the Empire lost Sharn she convened a representative of every faith on the continent in the Black Dove hospital and gave them the tune they named the **Horn of Mourning**.

Zero's half-brother, Izaak, is a member of the Fulcrum rebel crew in Zilspar, though he does not follow the Silver Flame himself. Their mother, known as Zero-One, is an original member of the rebel group known as Fulcrum and is a Paladin of the Silver Flame who often resides in Zilspar but travels with her personal knights throughout Khorvaire. This lineage places Zero of Sharn within a living tradition of Silver Flame's ideals, while also binding her to a network of resistance that stretches beyond Sharn.

A young human woman in the habit of a faith the Empire has outlawed, working openly in a city that until recently executed people for it. She keeps a falcon — a familiar shared with her half-brother Izaak, and the line by which the Nest and Fulcrum have talked to each other for years. No source gives her height, her colouring, her eyes or her face, and the Silver Flame rite she took is designed to leave exactly that gap. **Height, hair, eyes, skin: Unknown.** She is described as young, as a cleric, and as covert.

**The Silver Flame** is proscribed under the Pax Imperium and organised for it. Among certain women of the faith there is an ancient rite that strips away the name and the past entirely; those who take it live as embodiments of the Flame's discipline and sacrifice and answer to a number. Zero's mother took the same rite after the death of her companion John Stamos and emerged as **Zero-One**, the first woman in centuries to do it, and now leads Fulcrum out of the Breeland farmstead at Zilspar with a cell of loyal guards and a habit of vanishing for months. The tradition is a living one and Zero of Sharn stands inside it. It also binds her into a resistance network that reaches well past her own city: Fulcrum in the countryside, the Nest in the towers, and a family that is all three.

**In public** Zero is a cleric — polite, present, one more religious in a city with too many. **In private** she is a covert agent who runs surveillance operations for weeks at a time. The Ghost Chip file that started the whole war came out of a Fulcrum lightning rail infiltration; Zero was the one who took it forward. She identified Martin Kross, a junior enhancement technician at BioTec, tracked him to an upper-city bar called The Astra where he drinks after every shift, and spent several weeks on surveillance and strategic intoxication until he told her everything the recovered document did not say: that the Ghost Chip creates a persistent blind spot in the Arcane Eye network; that Dr. Joanne Menka is the lead scientist and personally excluded ArcEyes from the facility citing data-contamination risks; and that there is a restricted laboratory door off the East Lab he has never been allowed through. His keycard is in the Nest's hands. Kross never worked out what he had done.

Zero's circle is the whole rebellion. She works with Jessica Grimpledink at the Nest, with her half-brother Izaak as the standing liaison to Fulcrum, and with the clergy of every faith on the continent, whom she can apparently get into one room on short notice. Her inheritance is not an object but a rite: her mother's name-stripping, taken a generation earlier and for a different grief, which Zero took in her turn. Zero-One is still alive, so far as anyone knows, and is in the hands of the Empire.

**The secret:** Zero's own mother, when she was still Fema Nolan, resurrected [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) — pulled her out of death after a mind flayer killed her in Sharn's sewers, and embedded the diamond that has been glowing at the base of the Empress's skull ever since. Zero-One admitted it to the rebellion herself. The woman the Inevitables are sailing upriver to kill exists because Zero's mother saved her.

She believes the Flame is worth a name and gave up her own to prove it. She believes that the slow, patient, unglamorous work — weeks in a bar buying drinks for a technician — wins wars that heroics only decorate. She believes the faiths of Khorvaire can be made to act together, and she has now got them into a room and taught them a tune.

**On the job** Zero is an onboarder and a convener. She was the one waiting when the trio solved the puzzle in Skyroot Park and came down into the Nest; she helped them redact the stolen BioTec data and pull the name Project EDEN out of it. She keeps a covert line to Zilspar open through a bird. Three years later, with the Empire broken in Sharn and the bells still hanging all over the continent, she put a representative of every faith on the continent in the Black Dove hospital and had a dwarf play them a song.

**Origin.** The Silver Flame rite that gave Zero her name also took her history. What survives is the family: she is the daughter of Fema Nolan of Thrane — a Paladin of the Silver Flame known for compassion, discipline and a fierce devotion to protecting the innocent, later Zero-One and leader of Fulcrum — and the half-sister of Izaak, a former Sharn Watch ranger who turned away from the Empire when the Watch was absorbed into the Iron Veil and joined the Breeland rebels nearly a decade ago. Izaak does not follow the Flame. For years the two of them have held the line of communication between Sharn's upper city and the Breeland farms, sister to brother, through a shared falcon. Zero took the name, took Sharn as her posting, and has worked it as both cleric and covert agent ever since — which is how she came to be standing in the Nest on the night three strangers from Leef arrived with a stolen prototype.

## The Ghost Chip

A BioTec letterhead document recovered by an operative during a Fulcrum lightning rail infiltration named a prototype called the Ghost Chip and said it was entering final testing. Zero took it from there. Weeks of surveillance on Martin Kross at The Astra produced the specification the Empire had not written down: an implant that disrupts the scry-signature the Arcane Eyes rely on and creates a persistent blind spot around the person carrying it. Menka's name. The excluded ArcEyes. The locked door off the East Lab.

The Nest sent three strangers to steal the prototype itself. They came out with the chip and a folder of encrypted high-level data, solved the puzzle in Skyroot Park, and met Zero underground while the city slept. She helped them redact the files and pull **Project EDEN** out of them, and Izaak implanted a Ghost Chip in each of them before they went back up. Everything after that — Posé, Sister Nora, the Underhive, Edwin Graves, the Forge of Binding, the balcony — proceeds from a woman buying drinks for a technician who liked to talk.

## The Horn of Mourning

Three years after the Empire fell out of Sharn, with [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) hanging in towers all over Khorvaire and the Inevitables holding the only hammer that breaks them, Zero convened a meeting at the Black Dove hospital with a representative from Sharn of every faith on the continent. Bofric the Teryaki runesmith put King Corn's brass horn to his lips and played the fanfare of dawn — a simple dwarvish tune that hummed in the adamantine in everyone's pockets — and the assembled clergy took up the work of teaching it congregation by congregation. They named it the **Horn of Mourning**.

It was in that room that Dario stood up and told every faith on the continent that he had broken three bells, that he would break every one, and that the woman who rings them and everyone helping her must be cut down. Then three warforged walked in under Himmie Joffa and offered to feed a city forty-seven days from famine, and the price was the sky.

## The family

Zero's mother is **Zero-One**, leader of Fulcrum, the woman who resurrected Uriel Qualanthri and put the diamond in her skull. Zero's half-brother is **Izaak**, the Fulcrum liaison and artificer who implanted the party's Ghost Chips and fitted a prosthetic leg he did not build. Fulcrum has since been dismantled across the continent — pulled apart posting by posting, as though the Empire had obtained a list of every name, with executions running for days — and all of it began after Izaak disappeared. Zilspar Farm was burned with its people boarded inside. Zero-One was taken alive by E.D.E.N. units and has not been recovered. **The Nightingale refuses to believe Izaak informed on anyone and insists he is still alive.**

Jessica Grimpledink has said that if they cannot save Zero-One, they must not let her be taken. She was already taken.

## Right now

Zero is in Sharn, teaching a song. The clergy she convened have named it the Horn of Mourning and are spreading it congregation by congregation, because a horn played well drowns a bell out. Her mother is a prisoner of the Empire. Her brother is missing. Ten bells remain, and the three operatives she onboarded are on a boat in Karrnath with a Monday deadline.

**Items:**

- **Martin Kross's keycard** — mid-level BioTec building access, taken off a drunk technician after weeks of work. It is the key that opened the entire campaign.
- **The falcon** — a familiar shared with Izaak; the covert channel between the Nest in the upper city and Fulcrum at Zilspar, held open for years. Izaak is missing and the record does not say whether the bird still answers.
- **Her name** — surrendered to the rite.

**The Rite of the Name:** Zero's signature is not a spell. It is an ancient and nearly forgotten Silver Flame rite that strips away a woman's name and her past and leaves her as an embodiment of the faith's discipline and sacrifice. Her mother was the first woman in centuries to undergo it, and did so in grief after the death of the Fulcrum founder John Stamos. Zero took it in her turn. The practical effect in a police state is considerable: there is no former identity for the Iron Veil to pull on, no family name in any register, and no earlier self for an interrogator to threaten. The cost is that a woman who dies under that name leaves nothing behind for anyone to bury.
