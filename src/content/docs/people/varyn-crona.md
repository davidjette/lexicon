**High Elf · Paladin · The Crimson Sun / The Imperial Council · Dead**

*Also known as:* Vayrn Crona · General Varyn Crona · the Eternal Guardian · Master (to Esther)

**Varyn Crona** was a High Elf paladin of Aerenal, leader of [The Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/), one of the thirteen of [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/), and the **Eternal Guardian** — the Emperor's sworn protector, appointed from birth and serving Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III, shaping Imperial military doctrine across those reigns. From his manor in Korth he bought a five-year-old slave, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), taught her to read, write and harness her natural powers, took her as his apprentice at twelve and later as his lover, and hunted rebel cells with her. A masked assailant killed him in a cave system outside Fairhaven in Aundair, at around fifty-six human years old; rebel field analysis attributes the assassination to members of [the Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) rather than to rebels. His manor passed to Esther, and his soul is bound in his red-and-black longsword, which she wielded as her Hexblade until her death.

## Physical description

A High Elf of Aerenal in Imperial paladin's harness, roughly fifty-six human years old at the time of his death and carrying none of the softness that number implies in a human. His weapon is the only part of him the rebellion ever managed to describe with confidence: a red-and-black longsword, worn plainly, and now the property of another woman entirely. <small>No portrait, no colouring and no heraldry survive in any file the party has recovered; the Empire scrubbed his likeness along with everything else about him.</small>

## The Crimson Sun

[The Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) is the Imperial Empire's Secret Service. Its members consist of "Masters" and their "apprentices" — mostly Knights, Paladins or Sorcerers, a limited and elite group who work as spies and rebel hunters and are a feared government agency. Rank runs Apprentice, Inquisitor, High Inquisitor, Master. Varyn Crona was the leader of the Crimson Sun; his apprentice is High Inquisitor [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/). After Varyn Crona's death, Hayman Maw became the head of the Crimson Sun. He also held a seat on [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/), one of thirteen.

## Public and private

Varyn Crona is a High Elf from Aerenal. He was the head Paladin for Kaius 1st and eventually became a Paladin / Imperial council member for Kaius III. He was the leader of the secret service organization, The Crimson Sun. He took on an apprentice named Esther who was his house slave. He trained her as a Crimson Sun Inquisitor and they worked together as rebel hunters. They eventually became romantically involved.

That is the public shape of him. Recently recovered Imperial records give the private one: Varyn served as head Paladin to [Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), and was appointed the Emperor's sworn protector from birth under the title **"Eternal Guardian."** His influence shaped Imperial military doctrine for over centuries.

## Competence and circle

He kept a manor in Korth and a slave girl he chose to educate. He was secretive about the inner workings of the Council of 13 or Kaius III, but would openly speak of his dislike of council member Haymen Maw — the man who took his job. Rebel field analysis indicates that Varyn's death was *not* the result of rebel activity, despite official Imperial claims; multiple intelligence fragments point to members of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) as the likely architects of his assassination.

## The bloodline claim

The Echo raised at the Forge of Binding named a brother: **E'noch Qualanthri**, the priest who performed the Sacred Rite over the newborn Kaius. Renn Tal's research and, later, Master Armhair's confiscated manuscript both run the same thread — Varyn Crona, the Eternal Guardian, the [Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) bloodline and the Emperor's true nature are one problem.

## Beliefs

He believes that he and Esther will grow in power together and rule Khorvaire and beyond. He believes the bloodline *is* the power — "as the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine." He believes Haymen Maw is unworthy of the Council table, and says so out loud.

## Origin

Esther Crona was born into slavery two years after the end of the Last War. At the age of 5 she was purchased to work as a servant at the manor of High Elf, Crimson Sun leader and member of the imperial Council, Varyn Crona in the Capital city Korth. Varyn took a liking to Esther and taught her to read, write and harness her natural powers. He saw something "special" in her, saying she had a force power that is a rare natural talent. When she turned 12 she officially became his apprentice and trained with Varyn as a Crimson Sun agent, working with him to investigate and eradicate cells of rebel forces.

Esther and Varyn spent their last two years together investigating a rebel stronghold in the city of Fairhaven in Aundair. They became romantically involved with each other during this time. They believed they had tracked a rebel leader to a cave system outside the city.

Varyn Crona was killed by a masked assailant in a cave system outside of Fairhaven in Aundair while he and Esther were tracking a rebel leader. His manor in Korth was left to Esther Crona and his soul is trapped in his sword, now being wielded by Esther as her Hexblade. Varyn was around 56 human years old when he was killed.

## The Eternal Guardian

Esther Crona believes Varyn's soul resides in his sword and that it guides her still. [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) warned the Inevitables that facing her while that sword remained bound would be suicide.

Esther now wields Varyn's personal sword, a red-and-black blade believed to be both a deadly weapon and a symbolic inheritance. But field reports suggest the sword may be more than symbolic. Richard Blaze is quoted as saying:

> "I've seen her whisper to it. I've seen it change her. That sword is a chain around her neck. A ghost she can't escape."\
> \
> — Richard Blaze, as quoted on the rebel mission board

## The Forge of Binding

Esther set the Sharn Library archivist Renn Tal to cataloguing the texts on Varyn Crona, the Eternal Guardian and the Forge of Binding. What she found instead were missing pages, altered chronologies and impossible subterranean structures — and she came to believe the binding ritual could be reversed, given a priest to serve as anchor and a skilled forge-worker. Merely asking after Varyn Crona at the library was enough for a librarian to report the enquiry to [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/).

Deep in the sewers of Sharn, in the ruins of a Dhakaani-era foundry, the party reached the Forge itself. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) performed the Rite of Recall and Varyn's Echo manifested, repeating fragments of itself before shattering into red-black light. Esther arrived moments later, dropping onto a drill that had broken through the ceiling.

> "…we'll bleed the sun, eternal night. Our bloodline shall reign… As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine. E'noch… Qualanthri… my brother…"\
> \
> — Varyn's Echo at the Forge of Binding, its last words before it shattered

## Right now

The Echo is spent and the Forge is dark. Esther Crona lies dead at the base of the Veil Building, and the sword she carried appears in no report the Inevitables hold — it was not recovered, and the body itself went missing from the Sharn morgue, carried out by two elven women during the chaos of the rising. Whatever remains of Varyn Crona left the city in somebody else's hands.

**Organizations / Groups:** Head of [The Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/); seat on [The Imperial Council / Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/); Eternal Guardian to the House of Kaius.

**Holdings:** Crona Manor, Korth — willed to Esther Crona, who lived in it alone. **Fort Crona** and **Crona's Wall** on the western front carry his name.

**Items:** A red-and-black longsword, believed by Esther to hold his soul and to guide and train her. Bound at the Forge of Binding beneath Sharn; last seen in Esther's hand on the night she died; present whereabouts unknown.

**The Eternal Guardian:** a binding, not a courtesy title — the Emperor's sworn protector appointed at the cradle and carried across three reigns, drawing on forbidden Vol rites at a Dhakaani forge. Renn Tal believes it can be reversed. Eric proved it can at least be *called*.

- **E'noch Qualanthri** — named "my brother" by the Echo; the priest of the Sacred Rite over the newborn Kaius.
