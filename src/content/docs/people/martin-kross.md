**Junior enhancement-technician, BioTec · Fugitive · Alive, departed Sharn**

<small>Also known as: Martin · STATUS: FOUND — Location: Gorgonhorn</small>

**Martin Kross** is a junior enhancement-technician in BioTec's enchantments department in Sharn, a quiet mid-tier employee with predictable habits and limited situational awareness. [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) cultivated him over several weeks at [The Astra](/places/the-astra-bar/), the Upper City bar where he drank after every shift, and on the night of the Ghost Chip theft three strangers befriended him, escorted him home and lifted his mid-level key-card. He went on the run, was found wandering the Grand Aqueduct by [Blair](/people/blair/)'s Drones, and once [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) had cut a control chip out of his skull he named BioTec's private owner: **Edwin**. He left Sharn during the Cogborn exodus and is now in Gorgonhorn.

No likeness of him survives. What the record has instead is the state he was found in twice: once drunk enough at an Upper City bar to let three strangers walk him home, and once exhausted, frightened and wandering the Grand Aqueduct alone, convinced his employer believed he had robbed it.

BioTec is a private company renowned for its unwavering support of the Empire and the Iron Veil, advancing chip enhancements, interrogation technologies and bio-engineering in Imperial service — and, in secret, directing Project E.D.E.N. Kross holds mid-level building access and no clearance for the restricted laboratory off the East Lab, which he has never been allowed to enter and cannot describe.

To BioTec he is the inside man on the Ghost Chip theft: his card opened the door, and he never reported for work the next morning. Dr. Joanne Menka was furious about the breach and named him missing within hours. What is true is that he was cultivated. [Zero](/people/zero-of-sharn/) made contact over several weeks at [The Astra](/places/the-astra-bar/), where Kross routinely drinks after his shift. Under subtle pressure and strategic intoxication, he disclosed details absent from the recovered BioTec files, including confirmation that the Ghost Chip is an implant designed to disrupt ArcEye scry-signatures. Kross identified Joanne Menka as the project's lead scientist. He also mentioned a restricted laboratory off the East Lab that he has never been allowed to enter. Kross holds mid-level building access — his key-card is now in the possession of The Nest. Then, on the night, the trio befriended him, escorted him home and lifted the card.

He was, without knowing it, the most wanted witness in the Lower City. The Nest wanted him for what he had seen inside BioTec. BioTec wanted him for what he could say about who had taken his card. The Iron Veil simply wanted him. He went to his son [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/) and [Misty](/people/misty-kross/) at the pawn shop, said he meant to hide in the Grand Aqueduct, and asked Kriss to meet him there; when Kriss arrived he was gone. He turned up one morning at his ex-wife [Karin](/people/karin-kross/)'s corner store claiming he was running from BioTec, and BioTec agents were on the same street shortly after. [Blair](/people/blair/)'s Drones found him wandering the Grand Aqueduct and brought him in for questioning.

**The chip:** Martin could not say the name of BioTec's private owner. When he tried he seized — the same reaction Sister Nora suffered under interrogation at Zilspar Farm 02. [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) cut the control chip out of his skull, and Martin said it at once: **Edwin**. That single word led the party to Circuit City, to the laboratory behind it, to the cages, and to the end of the Cog Collector.

He believes BioTec thinks he did it, and he is right. He believes he did nothing wrong, and he is also right. He believes his family is safer with him gone.

Under questioning he cooperated fully and at once. Held in Blair's private quarters in [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), he gave up everything he had: BioTec's internal operations, the Ghost Chip prototypes, Red Khyber shipments, and the fact that a private owner was pulling the strings — and then, chipless, the name itself.

He lives alone in Astra Level Apartment 01, divorced with three children, and presents as a low-risk, easily influenced source with no awareness of the broader implications of the information he provided. He drinks at the same Upper City bar after every shift, and that routine is the whole reason a rebellion could reach BioTec at all. He lost his job, his home and his city in a single night over a theft he had no part in, spent time underground as a criminal queen's guest, was traded out as a clause in a treaty about tunnels, and then walked out of Sharn entirely.

**Right now:** Martin Kross has been found. Status: alive; location Gorgonhorn, the RUST / Hundreds settlement and stronghold on the border with the Mournlands. Martin Kross left the city of Sharn to Gorgonhorn during the Cogborn exodus. <small>The mission board records him as departing "with his son Martin," which is plainly a slip — his son is Kriss, and Kriss remained in Sharn. The record has not been corrected.</small>

**Organizations / Groups:** BioTec, enchantments department (absent without leave); the Kross family of the Lower City; latterly attached to the Cogborn exodus.

**Holdings:** Astra Level Apartment 01, Upper City, abandoned. Nothing else recorded.

**Items:** a mid-level BioTec key-card, now in the possession of [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) · a control chip, formerly in his skull, removed by Dario.

**The Keycard:** three strangers newly arrived in the city needed a way into the BioTec labs, and the way in was a man walking home from his usual bar. The Ghost Chip, the encrypted folder, Project E.D.E.N., the Cog Collector and the fall of the Iron Veil all followed from the card taken off him that night.

> "He is exhausted, frightened, and convinced BioTec believes he is responsible for the break-in that used his stolen keycard."

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to not be killed for something he did not do, and to keep it away from his family.

**Likes:** a drink after work · The Astra · his son · being believed · the aqueduct, briefly, as somewhere nobody looks.

**Dislikes:** BioTec · being watched · the restricted door off the East Lab · the word Edwin, which he could not say.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** predictable habits and limited situational awareness, in a city that surveils everything.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown. He drinks at The Astra.

**Social — Contacts & Relations**

- **Zero of Sharn** — cultivated him over weeks at The Astra; he never knew. Alive.
- **Karin Kross** — ex-wife; runs the Kross Roads Corner Store. He went to her on the run. Alive.
- **Kriss Kross** — son; agreed to meet him in the aqueduct and arrived too late. Alive.
- **Misty Kross** — daughter-in-law; questioned by BioTec because of him. Alive.
- **Blair** — held him under watch and traded his release for a cleared tunnel. Alive.
- **Dr. Joanne Menka** — his lead scientist, who named him missing within hours. Executed after the trials.
