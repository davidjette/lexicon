**Infanta · Incarnated at Refuge to guard the Mammon Machine · Freed by the party**

The **Infanta of Time** is an Infanta who incarnated near the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) at [Refuge](/places/refuge/) for one purpose: to protect it from [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). Her method was to **encase the machine in a one-hour bubble that repeated over and over**, and it held until the party arrived, freed her, and tried to stop him — at which point he went through the portal anyway and they followed him to [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/). Her name, and what became of her after she was freed, are not recorded.

Whether she is any of the named Infanta elsewhere is not recorded either. [Tami, Infanta of Luck](/people/tami-infanta-of-luck/) and the **Infanta of Fortune** are both separately attested and are **not** assumed to be her.

The office is close to two others in the setting: **Istus, Lady Cypher**, "the weaver of fate and the guardian of Time", whom the [Cypheric Orcs](/organizations/cypheric-orcs/) serve; and **Chronocypher**, who takes June's place as the Watcher at the end of time. No connection between them is recorded.
