**Species unknown · mapmaker of the descent into the Korramont · Presumed dead**

Motha was the maker of **Motha's bone map**, the map of the descent into the Korramont that the **Inevitables** carried down the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/). It was drawn on bone, in blood, by somebody who was dying while they drew it, and its marks stop partway through the descent. Motha's clan, house, trade, sex, age and fate are not recorded.

## The map

The marks legible on the map cover the stair, the pocket cave off it, and the high cavern beyond - and then they stop. Whoever Motha was, they got that far, wrote down what they had seen, and did not finish. The map's content places them somewhere between the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/) and the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/) when they drew it.

The bodies on that stretch of the descent are two: a grey-skinned duergar in a pocket cave, weeks dead of a stomach wound, whom the clan named [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/); and a scattering of small eaten skeletons and ruined provisions on the cavern floor beneath the grells, of the same vintage. Neither has been connected to Motha.

The mountain has produced a second map, drawn by a boy looking for gold.

## Right now

The map is in the party's hands. Motha's marks got three people down a black-ore staircase they could not see, past a stirge colony and a nest of grells, to the door of a hold that had been sealed for three years.
