**Female Tiefling · Queen of the Underhive · Sovereign of the undercity, Sharn · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Queen B · the Queen of the Underhive</small>

**Blair**, known as **Queen B**, is a tiefling and the sovereign of Sharn's Underhive, the nation beneath the Grand Aqueduct. Adopted as an infant and raised in the Lhazaar Principalities among pirates, smugglers and sea-born mercenaries, she married the [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) and afterwards built and ruled the Underhive through her Drones and a circle of husbands. She is a daughter of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and half-sister to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/) and Eden, a lineage she discovered late. She sold the Inevitables the passage that carried the Cogborn out of Sharn, was arrested during the first Council of Sharn and later tried and released, and her Drones now staff the city's new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy.

A tiefling with short curled horns set back in wavy blonde-brown hair shot through with a streak of green, and green eyes that do the greeting before her mouth does. She wears a long brown leather coat worked in gold scroll at the lapels and cuffs, a tan henley beneath it, leather trousers and thigh boots, a wide double belt, and a necklace strung with claws and teeth from something she has not named. Up on the surface none of that is visible: at Breaker's Pit she is a hooded, masked woman at the rail, and nobody there knows they are looking at a head of state. She keeps her husbands around her the way another ruler keeps a council — an elf, a human, a dragonborn, a white-furred tabaxi, and the bear-man [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/) — and they are as much part of her silhouette as the mask is. <small>Her species is sourced: the rebel file on the Crona-Blaze family records that [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) "is the biological father of three Tiefling daughters", and names an unverified "Tiefling pirate figure exerting control over portions of the Sharn sewer system".</small> <small>The physical particulars above are taken from her portrait on the site's Key Figures page; no written description of her survives.</small>

[The Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) is not a gang and not a slum; it is a country. It sits beneath the Grand Aqueduct at the end of a flooded sewer chamber and a reinforced door, and it has a tavern, a bunk room, a river, sealed ancient halls, a working economy and a fortune-telling machine. Its people are the Drones, and they are loyal in a way the Empire above never managed to buy. Its law is Blair's word, and its foreign policy is the single principle that a bargain struck is a bargain kept. She governs her territory through consistency, discipline, and a reputation for honoring her word, built over years of survival, negotiation, and decisive action in the tunnels beneath Sharn's lower city.

To the city above, Blair is a rumour, a criminal, a shadow at the edge of the sewer maps. That is what the Imperial file says, and the Imperial file is why Iron Veil soldiers walked into her territory during the Lord Commander's sweep expecting a squat and found a standing army. To those below she is the closest thing to order they have: the one authority in Sharn that does not disappear people, does not sell them, and does not change the terms after the work is done. She is also, privately, the daughter of a Grand Duke and the half-sister of the woman who ruled the city.

She reads people fast and she reads fighters faster. She picked [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) out of a Tier One bout at Breaker's Pit and invited three strangers into her home on the strength of it, then spent the evening establishing that they had known her father and met her husband before she committed to anything. Her circle is her marriages: each husband contributes to the Underhive's infrastructure and security, and it was a permanent Council seat for Berenstain that she asked of the first Council of Sharn. Her first husband, the [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), remains a powerful ally and a political connection that extends far beyond Sharn; his fleet answered when she called, and it was in the sky over the Veil Building on the night the Empire fell.

**Bloodline:** Blair is a daughter of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), which makes her half-sister to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), to [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/), and to Eden — the prototype the Empire raised as EDN-00 and the Hundreds call the Blue Devil. Sister Nora confirmed the blood relation years ago while studying Embrace at the University of Newham. Blair discovered the lineage late and it did little to change her course.

On her own ground she is unhurried and enormously hospitable. Guests are fed, housed, given tokens for the oracle machine and left to wander the tavern while she decides about them. Terms come afterwards, stated once, without embellishment, and she does not haggle. Cross into her territory uninvited and the register changes instantly: when the Iron Veil killed three of her Drones she had the survivors bring her the family of the soldier they took alive.

## Origin

Adopted as an infant and raised in the Lhazaar Principalities, Blair spent her early life among pirates, smugglers, and sea-born mercenaries. Her first husband, the infamous [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), remains a powerful ally and a political connection that extends far beyond Sharn. Her maritime upbringing shaped her leadership style: pragmatic, loyal to those who earn it, and unflinching when challenged.

Blair's later discovery of her true lineage, daughter of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), did little to change her course. She spent time with him when he first reached out, recognizing both his intelligence and the danger beneath his charm, though she saw through his illusions, she maintained a cautious, measured relationship with him until his death, keeping her distance from the ambitions that consumed him. <small>LeBeefe died at Newham.</small> Her sisters' ambitions took them elsewhere: a nightclub and a cult for Faith, a laboratory table for Eden, the Iron Veil for Esther.

## The Cogborn Passage

The Cogborn were trapped behind a cave-in, [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) and the mountain tunnel lay on the far side of her country, and every route out of the Lower City ran through her. She set her price to the Inevitables, they paid it, and she then went further than the terms required — revealing the Spine, warning them about the Chamber Room, having Berenstain and her people help clear the collapsed tunnel themselves. The Cogborn walked out through her country. She has never once described this as charity.

## The Forge and the Drill

When Esther Crona put a drill through the ceiling of the Forge of Binding, it was Blair who burst out of a hidden tunnel below and shouted for the trio to follow. She took them out through her own escape passage and into the vault — and only then told them what her sister's soldiers had done in her house while they were underground.

## Arrested by Her Own Allies

At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn she asked for autonomy, for the Ziggurat and the Vault Below, and for a permanent seat for Berenstain. Tempers rose; Dario ordered her arrest during the dispute. She was tried later and released. By nightfall her Drones had joined the new Department of Infrastructure while maintaining their subterranean autonomy.

## Right now

Today, Blair rules the Underhive. Her husbands form her inner circle, each contributing to the Underhive's infrastructure and security. Her Drones are her loyal followers. She avoids Imperial entanglements and the volatile agendas of her siblings, focusing instead on the safety and autonomy of her domain. She rules an autonomous nation beneath a city that once tried to arrest her, and her Drones staff its new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy. Sharn is out of fuel and weeks from severe food shortages, and the Underhive controls the tunnels every one of those problems has to move through. She has not said what she intends to charge.

> "To the city above, she is a shadow; to those below, she is the closest thing to order they have."

**Organizations / Groups:** Sovereign of the Underhive; represented at the first Council of Sharn, where she requested a permanent seat for Berenstain; married into the pirate fleet of Joseph Roberts.

**Holdings:** the Underhive in its entirety — tavern, chambers, bunk room, the Underhive river, the Spine, and the long-sealed Chamber Room where past suitors vanished trying to impress her; legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below, requested of the Council of Sharn.

**Items:** the Vega Oracle machine, an enchanted curiosity built by Carlos Vega, kept in her tavern and operated by token — one of hers produced a ring granting a minor wish. A hidden fast route in and out of her domain, opened by the password *honeycomb*. A direct means of contacting her, given to the Inevitables and to nobody else who has been named.

**The Bargain:** Blair's one recorded method. She never gives, refuses or threatens; she prices. Clear [the Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) out of the northern tunnels and the Cogborn may cross her territory. Secure the route to the mountain pass and she will consider releasing [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/). Both were paid, both were delivered, and an entire people walked out of Sharn alive on the strength of a criminal's word being better than a government's.

**Beliefs:** She believes a bargain struck is a bargain kept, and that this is not sentiment but the only currency that survives underground. She believes her siblings' ambitions are diseases she has already been inoculated against. She believes the Underhive owes the surface nothing, and will owe it nothing after the Empire is gone.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** the safety and autonomy of her domain, and nothing above it.

**Likes:** a deal honoured to the letter · good fighters · her husbands · the Drones · guests who eat what they are given · Breaker's Pit · the Vega Oracle machine · being underestimated in writing · her territory left alone.

**Dislikes:** Imperial entanglements · her siblings' agendas · trespassers · people who renegotiate · being asked for favours instead of offered terms · statues · the surface deciding things about the undercity.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** her word is absolute, which means she will hold to a bad bargain as fiercely as a good one.

**Social — Contacts & Relations**

- **Joseph Roberts** — first husband, Dread Pirate, fleet commander. Ally. Alive; his ship turned and fled Thronehold when the bombardment began.
- **Berenstain** — husband, bear-man, gift-giver, and the man she asked the Council of Sharn to seat permanently. Alive.
- **Booti'slave** — Drone, door-warden and escort of honoured guests. Alive.
- **John C. LeBeefe** — biological father. Met him as an adult, saw through him, kept her distance. Deceased.
- **Esther Crona** — half-sister and Lord Commander. Never allies; Esther's soldiers killed three of her Drones. Deceased.
- **Faith Zeir** — half-sister, the Mother Prophet. No recorded contact. Deceased.
- **Eden** — half-sister, resurrected as EDN-00. Status contested.
- **Preacher** — leader of R.U.S.T., whose trapped Cogborn crossed her country to reach the mountain tunnel. Alive.
- **The Inevitables** — the only outsiders she has given a direct line to. Alive.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown. She attends the fights.
