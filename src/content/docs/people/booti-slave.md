**Drone · Door-warden and escort of the Underhive · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Booti'slave · Bootislave</small>

**Booti'slave** is a Drone of [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) who serves as door-warden and escort to visitors of the subterranean settlement beneath Sharn. The Drones are [Blair](/people/blair/)'s loyal followers and the whole of her state apparatus; Booti'slave is the one visitors meet. Whoever crosses the flooded sewer chamber and its crocodiles arrives at a reinforced door, and Booti'slave is waiting on the inside of it. Nothing of Booti'slave's appearance is recorded.

## The protocol

Booti'slave escorts arrivals through the sprawling settlement to its tavern, sees them fed and housed as honoured guests, and withdraws until the Queen is ready; then returns and summons them to her private chambers. It is a diplomatic protocol executed by one person, and it does not vary.

When the party's business in the Underhive was done, Booti'slave showed them a faster way in and out of the Queen's territory and gave them the password: *honeycomb*. That single word is the rebellion's standing access to a sovereign nation under Sharn.

> At the door they were met by Booti'slave, an Underhive Drone who escorted them through the sprawling subterranean settlement and into its bustling tavern.

## Right now

Booti'slave is a Drone of an autonomous state whose people staff the free city's Department of Infrastructure, and is still on the door.
