**Infanta of Discovery · the Brightmantle Infanta · Built the Lexicon with Calix · Ally at the Egg**

**Stone** is the Infanta of Discovery, also called the Brightmantle Infanta, and he is [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) — an incarnation of the god, and at the same time a specific man; Brightmantle is named at his funeral as **the God of Discovery and Invention**. He and [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/) met as teenagers on the battlefield, fell in love and founded [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/); they then obliviated the Netherese, returned with the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and invited the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) of the world to live in peace in the city. He built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) together with [Calix](/people/calix/), from Netherese magic and technology. Before Calix could obliviate Motherstone and frame Brightmantle and the dwarvish people for his betrayal, Stone threw himself into the Obliviator so that Elistrada, his wife and love, would forget he had ever existed. The party cured the Lexicon in Arc II, uncovered the secret and freed his memory, and he returned as an ally at the Egg.

He has the face of BD Wong, and that is why the Lexicon appears as BD Wong in almost every circumstance.

> Ive read the book again. Some great genius (who??) solved the Troubles, but to end one slavery for another? The gods were born and destroyed the world. What hope for peace could survive such horror?

<small>— Stone's Ally Card. Sic on "Ive"; the "(who??)" is the card's own.</small>

The card's speaker is reading a history of the Time of Troubles and cannot name who solved them; the [Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) removes the doer and leaves the deed.

## Motherstone

Stone and [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/) met as teenagers on the battlefield and fell in love, and the two of them founded [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). The founding came before the war with the Netherese. In the first loop Stone, with Elistrada, Calix and Eldamir and with the help of the masses, slaughtered the Netherese and obliviated their entire race and culture, to end the [Infanta crisis](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/) and purge worship of the [Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/) — **the Nether**, as the Netherese called it — forever. They returned to Motherstone with the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and invited the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) of the world to come and live in peace in their paradise city.

Stone built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) together with [Calix](/people/calix/), from Netherese magic and technology — "but thats another story." <small>(Sic on "thats.")</small>

Calix planned to obliviate [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and frame Brightmantle and the dwarvish people for his betrayal. Before that could happen, Stone could not bear that [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/), his wife and love, would think he had turned on her, and threw himself into the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) so that she would forget he had ever existed.

That act made everything about Motherstone cursed and obliviated knowledge. It drove Calix and the Gallidan elves insane, turned Elistrada into a mysteriously depressed and always-weeping queen, and put the Lexicon into the fatal error called Ayror. Calix and Eldamir were cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other; Calix survived as a lich, mad, no longer knowing how to find his phylactery. In Arc II the party cured the Lexicon and uncovered the secret, and so freed the memory of Stone.
