**Tiefling wizard · Son of Asmodeus and a mortal woman · Allies from Arc VI**

**Steve Merlin** is a tiefling wizard, the son of the fiend **Asmodeus** and a mortal woman, made by the elves of the Summer Court and charged by **Nimue** with watching over Uther and his line so that **Ironbane** would be wielded to destroy the Netherese. The elves of the Summer Court, "desperate to break their curse of infertility and isolation from their divine aspect of the moon," bargained with an infernal power: "they would use their fertile magics to produce the child of a fiend — Asmodeus — and a mortal woman — and create a sorcerer who could look backward into time. This wizard — THE MERLIN — would be an extraordinary man, and would stand astride the world, with one foot in the land of dreams." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VIII.)</small> He was sealed in an oak tree by a stream in a grove by **Morgana**'s spell until [Kara](/people/kara/) travelled to Avalon to learn the Charm of Making and unwind it, and he stands among the party's allies at the final battle.

## How the party encountered him

Sealed in an oak tree by a stream in a grove, by Morgana's spell. Kara travelled to Avalon to learn the Charm of Making and unwind it. His pledge at Brightmantle's funeral is the shortest of any ally in the record: **"You got me outta that dang tree!"** <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>
