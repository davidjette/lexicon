> Wainwright St. Cloud is a man, crude and comically short sighted. He was wise enough to bring the Anachron to me, so that I may assume his quest and rightly witness the final moments at her side. I will be the last, and I alone will see the truth.

<small>— Valen, in his sanctum at the Arcaneum. (Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

**Elf archmage · Founder and Archmage of the Arcaneum · Brother of Alen Tudyx · Died in Despondos**

*Also known as:* Archmage Valen · the elf witch

**Valen** is an elven archmage, the founder of the **Arcaneum** and the brother of **[Alen Tudyx](/people/alen-tudyx/)**, queen of the Cormanthyrean elves at Myth Drannor. In Antiquity he betrayed the Cormanthyrians to the men of the Dale and was granted the land on which he built the Arcaneum, the start of its lasting rivalry with the kingdom of Punis. He murdered his sister to take her Baneblade, served the Waning One in the Banefae's attack on [Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/), and received the Netherese [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) from [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). In the age of the party he was Archmage of the Arcaneum; the party assassinated him in Cormyr and defeated him in his sanctum while his clone regrew. When the party travelled back to Antiquity they met him again, fought him with the Waning One and Carl's Jr. at the peak of Sigil in Despondos, and killed him there.

## The founding of the Arcaneum

Dave, on the elves of Antiquity: "Blackstaff is still around", "and Valen", "who betrayed the Cormanthryians against the men of the Dale and in exchange was granted the land to build the Arcaneum". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2020-07-26)</small> The school stands just outside Punis, and has been Punis's rival ever since:

> Valen is the original villain of the Pubis world, he created the Arcaneum and the permanent rivalry with the kingdom of Punis

> His cult is what you destroyed in the very first session with Zan the Younger

<small>— Dave, sic on "Pubis". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> See [the Arcaneum campaign](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/) and [the War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/). Zan the Younger was a player character in the original Punis game, Dave's one-shot set in another loop; see [Celion](/people/celion/).

## Alen Tudyx and Copperbane

Valen's sister Alen Tudyx, "Lode Star of Myth Drannor" <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>, married Peter Glans, who was Tylerjinex in human form. She died mysteriously. Valen murdered her, because he wanted her Baneblade, **Copperbane**, for a plan to kill the god of death and use [the Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) for the glory of the elves before the Netherese could take it. The elves of Myth Drannor told the party "that Valen is that queens brother, and that he also absconded with many of their clans magic objects". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-05-27)</small>

Glans left Copperbane "in a block of stone just outside the Penteract and walked away". "Glans hates VALEN and left the sword there to torment him and protect it forever". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> See [the Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/) and [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/).

## Tylerjinex and the Banefae

In the first loop the party knew, Valen defeated Tylerjinex, who was enslaved to Calix. In the second loop, the party and Tylerjinex foiled Valen. The defeat was the Banefae's: "The Banefae United by Drefanmetra attack the maze and enslaved TJ to get into the Penteract at the heart of Despondos." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> Valen was the arm of that coalition, and the Waning One, a loop of [Drefan](/people/drefan/) radicalised against humans and dwarves, led him.

Some of Antiquity refused to join them. Dave: "There’s another atlas here in Antiquity who refused to go along with Valen/Waning One - Atlas III", "As did Calix, which no one seems to have noticed!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> See [Atlas and the Sea Elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/) and [Calix](/people/calix/). In the previous loop the pair had held Ironbane through Mordred; in the party's loop "the Waning One and Valen have one less Bane (mordred + iron bane) than they did last time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-22)</small>

## The Anachron

The Netherese had an Anachron: "it’s the first one that was hidden in the Penteract until st cloud stole it and gave it to Valen. It’s the same one as yours but BEFORE you take it to the past." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>

In the age of the party, the Arcaneum held the Anachron and could not start it. Valen, in his sanctum: "This magnificent device baffles me. We’ve tried everything to try and turn it on. We’re certain this device will bring us to where and when we have to be to summon her, to summon the Leviathan…" <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small> See [the Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/).

## Archmage of the Arcaneum

[Celion](/people/celion/), Valen's lieutenant, told [Kara](/people/kara/) what he had seen after St. Cloud's visit to the college: "Over the last year or so, I’ve noticed more and more bizarre behavior from my colleagues, including and especially Archmage Valen. Key resources have gone missing, or delayed in their availability, in ways that made me suspect what work was being done in his personal sanctum." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

During the crisis in Cormyr the party stormed Castle Danzig and killed the archmagi. <small>(Arc III Part VIII.)</small> Dave's recap: the information they bought "on how to break into Castle Danzig" was what "you used to assassinate Valen and Celion and almost King Danzig himself, using a forced marriage for Zanzibar to assume the Cormyrean throne". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-08-11)</small> Valen: "After you assassinated us at Cormyr, I had these clones moved here for safe keeping." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

## The sanctum

The party reached Valen's sanctum sanctorum, the tenth and final sphere of the Sephiroth and the deepest dimension of the Arcaneum, where four grandfather clocks tick in unison around the Anachron. Valen lay in a glass vessel, recovering from death while his clone regrew, beside the tubes holding Celion and a clone of Kara. <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small> From inside the vessel he greeted them: "YOU INSOLENT VERMIN. HOW DARE YOU TRESPASS IN MY SANCTUM SANCTORUM. MORE THAN ANY OTHER, YOU WILL SUFFER AT THE ETERNAL TORMENT OF THE LEVIATHAN." To Kara he said, "Yes, we take a tissue sample of all students upon admission." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

Dave, after the battle: "a strictly timed exercise with a prismatic wall, lich-type wizard, black dragon, and the three other mages you didn’t kill. Most of my planning was around what would happen if you failed and they turned on a time machine". The tanks "had 200 HP", "But the tanks absorbed all damage so trying to kill him with damage from outside wouldn’t work. The exhaustion was very clever". Valen "definitely had means to escape with his magic and his dragon", "But when you killed the dragon and used the AM field I my only option remaining was bringing in the other three archmages". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> [Holden Tudyx](/people/holden-tudyx/) died in the fight.

The tanks stood on the 10th level of the college. Valen was in a tank until the final battle and had the Anachron and Kara's clone there, and Celion, tank-bound in that loop after Drefan cast Dream on him while he was reforming, helped the party from the tanks. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small> See [Celion](/people/celion/).

Zanzibar was named King of Punis immediately after the Arcaneum arc, "when Valen is defeated". King Danzig of Cormyr, turned back from a snail, "sees why Zanzibar held the coup, appreciates himrooting out the evil of Valen and his tainting by the Leviathan" (sic), and offered Roseanne's hand to Zanzibar. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## Camp Vengeance

In the Time of Troubles the party came to Camp Vengeance, where the young druids of Myth Drannor told them of "their Queen ALEN TUDIX, now dead, and her brother VALEN." The humans knew Valen "to have established a temple of sorts in the hills of Panae not far from the Camp", and when snakes appeared during the fight between settlers and elves, the High Druid "seemed to reference Valen". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> Dave: "the Archdruid blamed Valen for the snake attack". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> The settlers' pledge at Brightmantle's funeral begins "You saved us from Valen and a rain of snakes". <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small> See [the Settlers of the Dale](/organizations/settlers-of-the-dale/).

**Sue.** "SUZARA sent his boy SUE to the elf witch to seek healing". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> Dave: "you met Sue, the son of Suzara, who his father sent to Valen to get healed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> Sue betrayed the party and died. Dave: "The poor boy had Valen in his head and had little choice", "Plus the promise of a better body and the pressure from daddy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-04)</small> Two days later: "think of what kind of story would have emerged if you all had not come to Fort Vengeance and attracted the eye of Valen". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small>

Dave had earlier offered the party a trip to "Corma and Panae" to deal with "Valen and/or the Cormanthyr elves and copperbane". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-11-22)</small>

The Bane Blades spoke of him as the war approached. Silverbane said that "soon CALIX will receive a missive from VALEN", claiming that Valen and his allies had found the City of Dis in the Shadowfell and that the Netherese sages had murdered Thanatos and usurped the Seat of Judgment. Ebonbane: "I am with Carls Jr, who is with Valen now as well as with the Masked Prophet who first guided them to the City of Dis." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> See [Dis](/places/dis/).

## Despondos and his death

Dave's recap: "you all entered the labrynthian dimension of Despondos through the gate foretold, Valen and co had already gone in ahead of you", and "finally the party confronted Valen, Carl’s Jr. and The Waning One at the peak of Sigil". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-10)</small> See [Carl's Jr. and the Drow](/organizations/carls-jr-and-the-drow/) and [Sigil](/places/sigil/). Wainwright St. Cloud watched the battle "against Valen and Waning One" through [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09)</small>

In the battle Valen "was invulnerable to all damage!!", and "All his spells are point you can see". Dave: "Valen had you on the mind but Kara kept him blind the whole fighter that was HUGE" (sic). When Stonecypher tried to dispel him, "Valen was holding a reaction and counterspell specifically for that". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Dave was "still quited chafed that you blinding valen the whole fight kept me from doing my epic Dark Matter combo move", which "would have killed Percy for sure". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-27)</small>

Dave's summary at the end of the session: "Valen lies in a puddle of acid and the Bane blades merge and evolve." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> The party took his Palantir. Asked whether a bag of holding was safe for it, Dave answered: "That’s where Valen kept it", "Or in his Oculary". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> Afterwards: "You guys ravaged valens corpse". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-11)</small>

JL, who played Zanzibar, after the fight: "I never get tired of killing that piece of dog shit." / "Let’s find him in every timeline and fuck him up again." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>
