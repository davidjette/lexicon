**Celebrity news anchor and political commentator · Host of Serving Sharn Nightly · Imperial media · Deceased**

*Also known as:* the face of Serving Sharn Nightly · the voice of the Sharn News Network

**Candice Kurt** was a celebrity news anchor and political commentator of Sharn, best known as the host of *Serving Sharn Nightly*. Elegant, charismatic and always poised before the [ArcEye](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/), she was a fixture of the city's cultural life and a frequent master of ceremonies at Sharn's high-profile galas and public events, and her polished delivery and keen sense of spectacle made her both a trusted broadcaster and a sought-after personality in elite circles. She was known for flattering coverage of [Mayor Henry Heinrick's](/people/henry-heinrick/) policies and maintained personal ties to Sharn's influential families, the Heinricks most of all. In Sharn Ep 2 she was shot dead on the balcony at the opening of [Richard Blaze's](/people/richard-blaze/) restaurant [POSÉ](/places/pose/); the Empire declared the shot the work of Cogborn protesters, and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) executed Cogborn prisoners for it on live broadcast within the day. No physical particulars, species or age are recorded for her.

## The Heinrick connection

Kurt's career was closely tied to the city's political currents. She shaped public perception of Mayor Heinrick's policies through her commentary and interviews, and beyond the newsroom she was often seen with the Heinricks at private gatherings and social functions. The Arcane Eye network recorded the city and the Sharn News Network reported it; Kurt fronted the nightly programme.

## The opening at POSÉ

Sharn Ep 2. Candice was killed during the opening of Richard Blaze's restaurant POSÉ. Outside, a Cogborn protest erupted into chaos; as the crowd surged up the grand staircase, shots rang out from within the protesters, striking the balcony and killing her. Panic consumed the event.

**The official story** is that she was assassinated by a group of protesters from the Cogs. Esther Crona executed Cogborn prisoners for it on live broadcast within the day.

[The Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) were in the room, serving. They did not fire the shot and have never established who did. In the confusion they turned invisible, abducted [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the Black Doves, and escaped through a hidden elevator route revealed by [Izaak](/people/izaak/).

## Right now

The official account of her death has never been revised, and the shooter has never been identified.
