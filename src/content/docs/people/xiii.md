**Warforged · Survivor of House Nimbus · Played by LaMont · In the deep past**

*Also known as:* X-III · X-3 · Xeno-Imperial Interface-Indigo class

**XIII** is a Warforged, the only one to survive the party's assault on [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) in [Sigil](/places/sigil/) during [Arc IV](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/). He gave the party the code that let them defeat [Dox Prime](/people/dox-prime/) in about two rounds. In [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/) LaMont, who played XIII and [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/), proposed him as a new captain for [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/). After [Kara](/people/kara/)'s Wish rewrote the past, XIII's parts were found in the transformed citadel, and during the Time of Troubles Dave counted XIII among the party's characters still living in the deep past.

## House Nimbus

In Arc IV, Revan, [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/), [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/), [Drefan](/people/drefan/) and Stoth "approach the House of Nimbus, ready for an assault on those inside. (Full battle)." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small> The session record adds: "XIII is the only surviving Warforged."

At Nimbus XIII gave the party a code they used against Dox Prime, chief executive of the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/). Dave: "he was the warforged that gave you the code" / "at nimbus" / "that let you beat Dox PRime in like two rounds" (sic). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small>

## Before the party: LaMont's story

In LaMont's short story "…It's time to come home…", XIII's designation was "Xeno-Imperial Interface-Indigo class", and for years he was called by the designation X-III. He served the Company's overseer at the Nimbus Stronghold, the house the Company occupied, and let the overseer win at dragonchess for fear of punishment. On watch outside the overseer's office he overheard the names of "terrorists and conspirators against the company": "Deschain, Magnus, Kara, Drefan, and Revan". When [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) killed the overseer and turned his lightsaber on XIII ("Now would be a good time to get the hell out of my house!"), XIII fled. <small>(LaMont, "…It's time to come home…")</small>

The house kept calling him back. For days he watched Revan burn the other Warforged on a pyre, and slipped in to take supplies, armblades and wand sheaths from the bodies. Revan, who had heard him on the third stair, invited him in to play dragonchess. XIII keeps his own set in a compartment he built in his abdomen, and always opens with pawn to D13. He bet thirteen months of "obedient employment and binding servitude" against Revan's lightsaber, the Sunsaber, and lost. Revan released him from the bet, since "I don't need a slave", offered him the house to look after, and then took him on as his attaché. XIII's one question was whether he could study the Sunsaber. Revan: "Only when you win, and just wait until you see the Ariel." <small>(LaMont, "…It's time to come home…")</small>

XIII is of immense height, towering over Revan, and by Revan's guess "probably four hundred pounds". His face is an expressionless mechanical mask. <small>(LaMont, "…It's time to come home…")</small>

## A captain for the Arielle

On the evening Dave recalled [Obi](/people/obi/) "sacrificing himself to save the survivors on the battlefield from that Meteor Swarm", JL, who played [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), wrote "We need a new captain for the Ariel." LaMont answered "I know just the guy!" and "X-III has proficiency in vehicles and a history of understanding SC tech." Dave: "thats a good idea". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small>

When Dave asked every player for a headshot of their character, LaMont posted XIII's, and reacted in character to a robot portrait Dave had found for Roland: "XIII: .......this is awkward." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-03-20)</small>

## The Time of Troubles

[Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) opens with Kara's Wish that the party had moved the Moon in the past, which rewrote reality and transformed the citadel around them. [Pim](/people/pim/)'s writings covered the walls, and "XIII’s part are found" (sic). <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.)</small>

Dave, listing the party's characters spread across the ages: "People have characters in other times - in the deep past you have Chronocypher, XIII, Pim, Karametra, Drefanmetra and Revan, and Roland all still around". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> See [Karametra](/people/karametra/) and [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/).

**At the table:** XIII was played by LaMont. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>
