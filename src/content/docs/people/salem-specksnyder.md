**Warlock · The city of Greyhawk · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Status unknown**

**Salem Specksnyder** is a warlock whose patron is Tharizdun, a Great Old One, met by the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) in the city of Greyhawk on Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon, in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh, run by JL. He became obsessed with Gabriella's wife, [Garbanzo](/people/garbanzo/), and tried to abduct her. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The warlock

Specksnyder spent years trying to summon his master, at the cost of his post at the university, his family and his sanity; when his wife tried to leave him he strangled her. He had had little success in touching the Far Realm, but he had summoned slaadi from the realms of elemental chaos and used them as minions. He collected bodies for necromantic experiments, and when the graveyard ran dry he paid two river rats, Spuzz and Egg, to abduct people. He saw Garbanzo in the city with two goblin escorts, found that she looked like his dead wife, and decided her corpse would suit his collection. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The hunt

The goblins told Gabriella about a tall man in black who had been following her wife. At the party's hotel Specksnyder tried to take her, with a red slaad and gibbering mouthers, and his creatures teleported away rather than die, leaving a thick, black, shimmering, obviously magical ichor. The party followed the trail to the river rats, who gave up his name and tried to rob them. A woman named Coral was murdered, with the same black ichor at the scene, and the city constables Tannhauser and Blumkopf began questioning the party. Specksnyder's house stood abandoned, with a trap door into a dungeon beneath it, and an old woman selling flowers for the dead outside warned them not to go in. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> How the hunt ended is not remembered. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
