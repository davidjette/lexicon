**Elf-noble, rank of Star · House Gallidann · Guardian of the first Infanta · Ascended — dwells in Arboria**

*Also known as:* Quendi · the goddess mother

**Star Quendi** was an elf-noble of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/), daughter of its founder **Eldamir** and its heir, and guardian to the first Infanta. When the child became a sentient god in a mortal body — **Elistrada**, the goddess [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) reborn — Quendi raised her, served as her seneschal, governed the elven empire of the Moonshae in her name, and rose to the highest order of elven nobility: **Star**. She built the barrow at **Bask Falls** for her mother; her son **Renos** and his son **Daenan** kept the island of Wain Gallidan after her. She ascended, and dwells immortal in the throne-forests of Arboria in the deepest heart of the Feywild. In Arc I her spirit was nearly bound into a mortal child by the Forsworn at her own barrow; the rite was broken and her echo blessed the heroes instead, charging them that the reborn goddess must be raised as a child and not as a god.

## Appearance

No description of her body survives. What the heroes meet at Bask Falls is a spectral echo, nearly bound by the Forsworn into a new child and speaking instead. Her rank is the only description recorded: **Star**, the highest order of elven nobility, held by a woman who ruled an empire on a child's behalf and then declined to be its god.

## The office she invented

There was no precedent for what Quendi did. In the Heavenfall the gods were cut off from the world, and one of them — the cast-out moon-goddess — entered a dying human child through its mother's prayers. Quendi was the noble who took charge of the result. She raised the goddess-queen — "though young for an elf, was mother to the Elistrada" — served as her seneschal, governed the elven empire of the Moonshae in her name, and set the pattern every later guardian of an Infanta is measured against. The empire she ran is remembered in later, mournful ages as **the throne of tears**. Her son **Renos**, and his son **Daenan**, kept the island after her — and Daenan's sarcophagus, with a dormant moonblade in it, is in the deep hold of the *Evening Star* when the chronicle begins.

## Public and private

Publicly Quendi's legacy is a dynasty, a barrow and a rank. Given the chance to have a goddess raised as a goddess, in a house she founded, her one recorded instruction was the opposite: the goddess must be raised as a child.

## The charge at Bask Falls

Quendi built the barrow at **Bask Falls** for her mother, and it is there, in [Arc I](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/), that the Forsworn and their dark-fey patrons work a rite to bind her spirit into a mortal child, so that Queen Maab's faction can rule the Moonshae as Elistrada once did. The rite is broken. Her echo blesses the heroes instead, and names their purpose.

> Who are you, champions? Who are you, champions of Elistrae and the Moonshae Isles? Tell me your names so I may sing songs of you among the gildergreens in throne forests of Arboria… But she mustn't be raised as a god. She must be a child, living beneath the Gildergreen.

<small>— Star Quendi, at Bask Falls Barrow</small>

## Beliefs

She believes a god born into a child should be allowed to be the child, whatever it costs the people who need the god. She believes a house is worth a life and says so plainly — she will grant a moonblade only to someone who pledges to live and die for hers. She believes names are worth singing, and asks for the heroes' before she gives them anything.

**Organizations / Groups:** House Gallidann, as daughter of its founder and its heir

**Holdings:** the barrow at **Bask Falls**, built for her mother and now her house's ancestral tomb · the throne-forests of **Arboria**, in the deepest heart of the Feywild, where she sits immortal · the island of Wain Gallidan, ruled in the Infanta's name

**Items:** the moonblade [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) — hers to give, and given at Bask Falls to [Drefan](/people/drefan/) on the condition that he pledge to live and die for House Gallidann. Its earlier bearers are in the barrow around her.

## Signature capability — the boon of children

At Bask Falls she grants the party the immediate trust of any child. The following arc is a race between empires, cults and traffickers to lay hands on reborn gods who are, all of them, small children.

## Right now

Quendi sits immortal in the throne-forests of Arboria and does not come back. Her echo is spent. What she left behind is a sword in a druid's hands, a barrow that nearly became a birthing chamber for Maab's faction, and one instruction about how to raise a goddess.

**Hobbies & Pets:** She sings the names of champions among the gildergreens. That is the only pastime recorded.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Eldamir** — her mother, "the patroness of the house before the birth of the infanta" and its founder; she and her brother Calix slew each other.
- **Elistrada / Elistrae** — the first Infanta. Quendi raised her and ruled in her name.
- **Renos** — her son, who kept the island after her.
- **Daenan Gallidann** — her grandson; a *baelnorn* who turned himself undead to fight the tide of man, sealed in his tomb by an earthquake and dredged up as cargo by slavers.
- **Calix** — her uncle, who demanded her charge as a pupil and killed her mother over the refusal.
- **Drefan** — the last person she gave anything to.
