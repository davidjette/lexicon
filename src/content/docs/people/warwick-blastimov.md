**Hippo-Gith · Captain, [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) · Veteran of Negroni Prime · Alive**

*Also known as:* Blastimov

**Warwick Blastimov** is a great bellowing Hippo-Gith, a captain of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) who fought at the Battle of [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/), the final showdown of Astral Command's decades-long war with the Beholder Warlords. In *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* he came aboard [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) knowing the secret of [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-22)</small>

## Negroni Prime

In [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/)'s memory of the battle, Blastimov wore a captain's stripes and barked orders into a sending stone for more men as the enemy approached. When the beholders turned the whole 76th division against their own lines, he shouted for Barrias to get them out, and led the retreat. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small>

> "Fall back! Fall back, ye fucks! Fall back!"
>
> <small>— Warwick Blastimov, at Negroni Prime.</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

In April 2023 JL put him aboard the Zanzibar: "Blastimov is on the Zanzibar. He knows Cat and Horgus' secret." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-22)</small>
