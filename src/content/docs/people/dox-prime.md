**Changeling · Chief of the Dox · Chief executive of the Sword Coast Trading Company · Defeated at the Old Man's House**

*Also known as:* Prime

**Dox Prime** is the chief of **Dox**, the changeling collective that [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) calls "the Team" and that wears his face in public, and the chief executive of his [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/). St. Cloud directed all executive business through him from 1468 DR and resigned in his favour in 1491 DR. Dox Prime ran the Company from St. Cloud Tower in [Sigil](/places/sigil/), the only member of the Team who could travel to St. Cloud and speak with him face to face. In the finale of [Arc IV](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/) the party, using a code given them by [XIII](/people/xiii/) at [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/), beat him in about two rounds, before he could deliver his monologue. Port Somewhere was destroyed.

## The Team

The War Wizards' report "To the archmagi" describes St. Cloud's household: "we could watch his underlings, and we found something very interesting: he is his own underlings." In his public role as chairman of the Company and Chancellor of Waterdeep, St. Cloud is "a cabal of changelings who report to St. Cloud and portray him public. Nearly all instances of Wainwright St. Cloud are in fact this group of changelings he calls ‘the Team’ but who go by the collective identity of ‘Dox.’" The report says his Team "has notably been in the company of the cult of Asmodeus", and gives the theory that St. Cloud "has left the Material Plane and his Team are on their own for now." <small>(Sic on "portray him public". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> See [Asmodeus](/people/asmodeus/) and [the War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/).

In [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) the "St. Cloud" the party chased across the rooftops proved to be a Dox, and Dox crewed the Company's ships at the Second Battle of Motherstone. JL, who played [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), called them St. Cloud's "Oompa Loompas, the impossible tissue that holds together his sinful empire." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-23)</small> In Sigil the party broke [Cuthbert Couch](/people/cuthbert-couch/) out of the Company brig, "but not before encountering part of The Team." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-08-22)</small>

## Chief executive

St. Cloud's memos in the Company archives record the handover. On 15th Hearthfire, 1468 DR: "All executive business is to be conducted through Dox Prime. I will be focusing on Special Projects going forward." On 10th Evening Star, 1491 DR, after the Company "almost certainly lost a major operative to the Obliviator", he resigned as Chief Executive Officer of St. Cloud Trading Partners, LLP: "Until further notice, Dox Prime is chief executive and has the full faith of the board." <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small> See [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/).

## St. Cloud Tower

St. Cloud Tower at Port Somewhere is staffed by Dox. Its third floor is "the business heart of the enterprise":

> Dox Prime, as they are called, is seen here by only a few people who need to. Prime is the member of the team who can travel to and from the Boss’s location and speaks with him face to face, the rest must rely on devices and other forms of remote transmission to get orders from St. Cloud himself.

<small>— Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.</small>

The fourth floor, the executive suite, holds portals to the main locations on Faerûn and to the Fire and Earth Planes. Dox Prime guards that portal nexus. The portrait of St. Cloud on its wall is a portal to [the Old Man's House](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/), opened "with a special silver key that Dox carries on himself, which he will destroy if possible before giving up." <small>(Arc IV Part V.)</small>

## The Old Man's House

The party raided the Old Man's House in the Arc IV finale, played on 2019-11-09. Among Dave's "huge moments" of that game: "no alarm being set off at nimbus", "going through the warehouse instead of the tower", and "most of all, not having any living Dox make it to the house with the knowledge of your presence". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-10)</small>

Dave: "You also fucked up Dox Prime enough that he didn’t have a chance to give his big villain monologue, which probably would have made more sense of the end room etc". On his armour: "his magic energy armor didn’t protect from regular bludgeoning, slashing or piercing so slamming him against the walls instead lightning blasting him was very good because that would have reflected on him". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> Two months later Dave credited XIII with "the code" at Nimbus "that let you beat Dox PRime in like two rounds" (sic). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small>

Dave posted the undelivered scene afterwards, "the part where the villain didn't get to monologue bc of time/gravity":

> Dox Prime is here with 1d6 Doxes. He claims to be the man himself. When defeated he admits he’s not.
>
> “You’re too late. He’s already gone. One of the gods will reach the egg, and then it will all be over.”

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-15; Arc IV Part V.)</small>

## Port Somewhere

JL asked "Is St Cloud’s base vaporized???" Dave: "Yeah I’ll flashback the destruction of Port Somewhere and Dox Prime’s parting words", and then gave the words: "And yet, you lost before you even started." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> The demon riot tore apart the western half of Sigil, and [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/) vaporised it. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>

## The captured Dox

The party held one Dox in the brig. Dave: "the Dox you have in the brig will trade information for his life, now that the Company is defeated". LaMont, who played [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/), added that "Gritty champagne was just torturing him", and Dave answered: "I guess he’ll make a deal with Gritty for his life to serve Asmodeus and Darkcember", "He’s convinced Asmodeus has the best chance to reach the egg first so serving him will have the same outcome as serving Cthulhu himself, since they will become one". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> Gritty is [Griddy](/people/griddy-champagne/).

Dave then posted the rest of Dox Prime's scene, "because you successfully tortured the dox you captured":

> If intimidated/persuaded (DC 25) he reveals “When he activates the Mammon Machine, every one of those will broadcast the good news to the world.” He points to the St. Cloud manufactured rings of Send /Spell storing. “ Soon every one across the planes will know the cosmic oblivion that beats at the heart of the universe. Ph'nglui mglw'nafh Cthulhu R'lyeh wgah'nagl fhtagn. Leviathan is coming. May it know that I helped it come to be.”

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-15; Arc IV Part V.)</small> See [the Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) and [the Leviathan](/lore/the-leviathan/).

## Capabilities

**Energy armour:** a permanent shield "from Proton Pack", which offers no protection from ordinary bludgeoning, slashing or piercing and reflects lightning. **Attacks:** lightning strikes and a 15-foot cone of lightning. **In his lair:** he can call up to eight efreeti and use Hypnotic Pattern and Wall of Force. **Spells:** Counterspell, Misty Step, Fly, and Jump, always active. <small>(Arc IV Parts II and V; Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>
