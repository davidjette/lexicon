**Seraph · Seraph of Poseidon · Protector of Saltmarsh · Dead, desecrated**

**Also known as:** the Drowned Seraph

**Aqueus**, called **the Drowned Seraph**, was a seraph of Poseidon and the protector of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/). He was a former comrade of [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/), who betrayed him: Aqueus was slain and pinned to the tomb of Dolores Weathervein on the sea floor by the blade [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), his heart pierced outside his body, and his desecrated corpse was used to open a portal on the sea floor. The party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) saw the desecration of the altar with his corpse in the hidden temple of Neptune beneath a whirlpool, and know of the desecration.

## Betrayal

Aqueus was Jeremiah's former comrade. Jeremiah betrayed him in a ritual of desecration to solidify Jeremiah's fall from grace, and pinned him to the tomb of Dolores by Weathervein. Corpses rose from the nearby sea. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The desecrated corpse

The party traveled to the whirlpool over the hidden temple of Neptune, killed beasts and devils, and saw the desecration of the altar with Aqueus's corpse. <small>(DM notes)</small> The undead called the Drowned come from the desecrated temple under the water. <small>(DM notes)</small>

On the sea floor, the fading radiance of the desecrated tomb of Dolores Weathervein lights the impaled corpse of the drowned seraph. His heart is pierced outside his body by Weathervein. Beside it, the demon lord **Dagon** tries to emerge from a growing portal, opened by Jeremiah with Aqueus's desecrated corpse and Weathervein, while the Dunwater Horror clashes with him. Weathervein must be removed from the angel's disembodied heart. <small>(DM notes)</small> These are the depths of [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## Papa Diaz's call

The priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/), pointing at the ocean, calls on his brothers to dive for the seraph's corpse:

> "All of you! Brothers! We must hurl ourselves into the waves, to recover the corpse of Aqueus, a drowned seraph, He lies beneath the sea, upon the grave of the Lady of Tears, violated and profaned.
>
> From this crime something vile has been unleashed disrupt it, I beg you, lest the world of men be consumed by the rotting dead..!
>
> DIVE!

<small>(Papa Diaz, DM notes)</small>

**Items:** Weathervein, the holy relic of Neptune's sept, which pierces his heart.
