**Red faerie dragon · Animal companion of Yoshi · Champion of the Evening Star · Status unknown**

**Denver** is a red faerie dragon, the tiny animal companion of [Yoshi](/people/yoshi/) in [Arc I — Hope in Exile](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/). He had 4 hit points, and his stat block was kept with Yoshi's character sheet. When the infant [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) was taken into the care of a nursing mother, Denver followed her, invisible, wherever the she-elves took her, and Yoshi trained him to describe terrain and count the enemy. At the Conclave at Leftfork he was named with the party among the **Lusmundi**, the light of the world, and the **Champions of the Evening Star**. From August to November 2017 the players' chat was named "Denver's God Parents", before it became Temple Holdings, LLC.

## Description

Denver is a Tiny dragon, a red faerie dragon, chaotic good. <small>(His stat block, with Yoshi's sheet. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-09-04)</small> A faerie dragon's colour changes as it ages, from red in its youngest years through the spectrum to violet in its oldest. <small>(Published D&D canon.)</small> He has 4 hit points. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-23)</small>

## Arc I

In the camp, the baby "has gotten hungrier and more unrestful. A woman in the camp who lost her child in the Imperial raid volunteers to feed her from her breasts. The child does seem calmed by her presence. It’s obvious the cultists are very uneasy when the baby is not in their care. Denver follows her, invisible, wherever the she-elves take her." <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile Part III.)</small>

The nurse carried the child off. Yoshi's thread in the next part: "You gave the baby to a stranger because you can’t tell the difference between dark elves. But Denver is learning fast, and you’re training him to describe terrain and count the enemy." <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile Part IV.)</small> Drew, who played Yoshi, had already announced: "Denver will be used for recon and data gathering from here on in." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-23)</small>

## The Conclave at Leftfork

At the Conclave at Leftfork the heroes were named:

> Kara, Roland, Yoshi, Denver, Fulori, Drefan, Viceroy Couch and Brother Magnus. You have proven your bravery and goodness many times in such a short time. You have saved our goddess from certain death. You have freed us from bondage. And you have brought justice to our captors. I name you Lusmundi, the light of the world, and the Champions of the Evening Star.

<small>— Arc I, Hope in Exile Part V, "The Conclave at Leftfork".</small> See [Kara](/people/kara/), [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/), [Fulori Splitheel](/people/fulori-splitheel/), [Drefan](/people/drefan/), [Cuthbert Couch](/people/cuthbert-couch/), [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) and [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/).

## Denver's godparents

Drew added Denver to Yoshi's miniature: "how do you add a tiny animal? trying to add Denver". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-23)</small> When Dave promised that Yoshi would not die, Drew asked for the same promise for his companion:

> Drew: AND DENVER\
> Dave: can't, sorry\
> Patrick: It's remarkable denver isn't dead yet\
> Drew: You're all Denvers godparents. If he dies, I hold you all responsible.\
> Jenny: Is that what godparents are?? The person you blame if your kid dies young?

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-23)</small>

Dave added: "I want to see him get to Purple, I do, but the guy only has 4 hit points". Patrick, who played Magnus: "Okay, I will spend the game healing Denver and not you". That night Jordan, who played Fulori, renamed the chat "Denver's God Parents". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-23)</small> Two days later Drew proposed "a ceremony to officially make you all Denvers godparents. Rolls will determine order of succession." Dave: "yes that is fine". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-08-25)</small> The chat kept the name until Jenny, as Kara, renamed it "Temple Holdings, LLC." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-11-11)</small>

When Dave sent the players their scanned character sheets, Drew asked "WHAT ABOUT DENVER???" Dave: "Denver doesn't go on here" / "you have his sheet" / "it's in your scanned sheet here". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-09-04)</small>

## Capabilities

The red faerie dragon stat block on Yoshi's sheet gives Denver Superior Invisibility, Limited Telepathy and Magic Resistance; innate spellcasting of dancing lights, mage hand and minor illusion, each once a day; and a Bite and a Euphoria Breath. It rates him Challenge 1 (200 XP). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2017-09-04)</small>
