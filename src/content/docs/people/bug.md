**Infant · Son of the Dragon of the Dark Sea · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Hieronymus Greenblatt

**Bug**, properly Hieronymus Greenblatt, is the baby of the Greenblatt orphans, whom the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) brought home to [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) from Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh. He showed unusual arcane powers, making objects float and turning them into gold, and in June 2022 a ruby dragon, the Dragon of the Dark Sea, came to Saltmarsh to claim him as its son. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## The Greenblatts

The Greenblatts are a family of orphans. Bess, a teenage girl, had become mother to the rest of the brood: Reed, Byron and Chip, three young scamps with dirty faces, and the infant Hieronymus, whom Bess calls Bug. In Saltmarsh they stayed with Brother Steve, a priest of Chauntea, at the farmhouse outside town that the party set him up in. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The Dragon of the Dark Sea

A ruby dragon swooped down on Saltmarsh, its breath turning everything it struck into gold, and delivered a message: "I am the Dragon of the Dark Sea, and I have come for my son." <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small> JL, as DM, the day of the next session: "We left off with a Ruby dragon threatening us." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-17)</small> Dave, the next morning, "thinking about bug", explained why the party did not fight: "the idea of Saltmarsh and my house and all my stuff getting gilded into nothing was not worth the risk of using my swords and powers etc to kill the dragon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-18)</small>

In September 2022, with the Brotherhood beaten, Dave wondered whether what was left was to rescue him: "Maybe we rescue the boy" / "The baby". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>
