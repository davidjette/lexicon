**Autognome · Artificer 1 · Autopilot, SSJ Zanzibar · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Fully Lingual Automatic Pilot · Fully Linguistic Autonomous Pilot · FLAP · Flap

**F.L.A.P.** (Fully Lingual Automatic Pilot) is an autognome artificer and the built-in robot autopilot of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), a non-player character built by Dave for *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. He came with the ship.

## Name

The ship's crew roster gives his name as Fully Lingual Automatic Pilot, under the post of Autopilot (droid). <small>(crew roster, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> An earlier form is Fully Linguistic Autonomous Pilot, abbreviated F.L.A.P. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-29)</small>

## Background

F.L.A.P. is an android, a level-one autognome with the sailor background, proficient in land, sea and space vehicles. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-29, 2022-09-26, 2022-10-19)</small> He handles the piloting of the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-29)</small>

The Zanzibar has a rare Major Helm, and before [Caprica](/people/caprica/) joined, no one aboard could operate it, leaving the ship limited to Tralfamadorespace. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11, 2022-10-19)</small> Dave's setup for the ship had it limping around on a backup minor helm, with F.L.A.P. as autopilot. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small>
