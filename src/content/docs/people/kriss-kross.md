**Species unrecorded · Logistics and security · RUST inner circle · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Kriss</small>

**Kriss Kross** is the logistics and security man of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/)'s inner circle in Sharn's Lower City, and joint keeper with his wife [Misty](/people/misty-kross/) of Misty's Pawn Shop — the faction's primary safehouse, meeting point and covert access route into the undercity. The son of [Karin](/people/karin-kross/) and [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), he married into R.U.S.T.'s inner circle and helped Misty transform the pawn shop into a disguised operations hub. Together they maintain the hidden rail-line entrance beneath the shop, coordinate the movement of Cogborn families into the tunnels, and manage the flow of information between the Lower City and R.U.S.T.'s deeper hideouts; Kriss handles logistics and security while Misty repairs mechanisms and keeps the shop's façade convincingly mundane.

Both are fiercely loyal to [Preacher](/people/preacher/) and deeply embedded in the Cogborn community, and their shop is the first refuge for those fleeing Iron Veil raids. Their stability, discretion and willingness to risk everything for their people make them indispensable to R.U.S.T.'s survival.

## Physical description

Tall and lean, with dark hair worn shoulder-length in loose twists and a few days of stubble. He dresses in unremarkable black — a plain jacket open over a black shirt printed with a skull and crossed wrenches, black trousers, black trainers — and stands with his hands in his pockets in a way that makes him look like a man waiting for someone rather than a man watching a door. Beside his wife, who is red-haired, tattooed and visibly armed with an arm, he is the one people's eyes slide off. That is the job. <small>Taken from his likeness on the Rebel Factions page; no text source describes him.</small>

## Misty's Pawn Shop

The shop sits among the street-level shops across from the Ziggurat, and beneath it runs the hidden rail-line entrance into the undercity — the covert access route that makes R.U.S.T. possible. Misty, a Cogborn engineer raised in the Cogs, was taken in by Preacher as a child after her family was displaced by early Imperial crackdowns; she grew up navigating the tunnels, learned to repair machinery older than Sharn itself, and became one of R.U.S.T.'s most trusted technical minds. She is now pregnant, a fact that has intensified Preacher's determination to evacuate the Cogborn.

To the neighbourhood Kriss is one of the Kross family: his mother Karin runs the Kross Roads Corner Store, his father Martin worked at BioTec, and he married a pawnbroker up the street. The ordinary family shopfront is what makes the operation invisible. His people are Misty, Preacher, [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/), and a mother and father on the same two streets. His beliefs are not recorded.

## The night in the aqueduct

When Martin Kross ran from BioTec he came to Kriss, said he intended to hide in the Grand Aqueduct, and asked his son to meet him there. Kriss went. His father was already gone. BioTec agents questioned him and Misty about his father; what he told them is not recorded. When three strangers came asking, he and Misty shared that much reluctantly and stayed guarded and suspicious.

## Origin

Kriss is the son of Karin and Martin Kross, raised in the Sub-line District among the corner stores and repurposed rail concourses, and he married into a network rather than joining one. He and Misty converted the pawn shop into something that could hide an evacuation route in plain sight. The week that broke the pattern was the week his father vanished, BioTec came asking, and three foreigners with new faces started asking questions on his street.

## Right now

The Cogborn are out and R.U.S.T. sits on the Council of Sharn. Martin Kross left the city for Gorgonhorn during the exodus. The shop is still open, the tunnel beneath it still runs, and Kriss is about to become a father.
