**Elf · The Black Doves · The Grey Doves · Alive when last recorded**

*Also known as:* Luz Fayina · Fayina

**Luz Fayana** is a member of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) whom [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) spared and let go in the temple of Dol Arrah at [Leef](/places/leef/). In Newham she led the Grey Doves, Black Doves open to betraying the Empress. JL, who ran [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/), placed her among the pro-empire, anti-Uriel faithful who want imperial reform. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23; 2025-10-19)</small>

## Spared at Leef

Dario spared a Black Dove, an elf, during [Leef Ep 4](/sessions/leef-ep-4-the-train-station/), in [the temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/) at Leef, the Black Doves' listening station where the trio found [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) caged. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small> That night JL asked, "Dario, what is the name of the Black Dove you spared???" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22; 2025-08-23)</small> JL, as DM, gave her name: "Luz Fayana is the Black Dove Dario let go". Dave answered, "Yeah I know". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## Reform

JL described Luz Fayana as possibly "a Martin Luther type who has grief with Uriel’s corruption and her interpretation of scripture". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> He placed her among the Empire's internal dissidents:

> I think there’s pro-empire, anti-Uriel factions (like Fayina) who could be friends or foes. Like, they want imperial reform not the end of empire.

Dave agreed: "Absolutely". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## The Grey Doves

In [Newham](/places/newham/) Luz Fayana led the **Grey Doves**, Black Doves of the city who were open to betraying [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and who kept to a temple of Kol Korran. She came back to the trio with demands of her own. After the trio's fight with the vampire Gary at [Club Mawniq](/places/club-mawniq/), she brought them dossiers on three Inquisition soldiers disciplined for anti-imperial sentiment, Sergeant Tam Chodely, Corporal Russ Nuggets and Private Harry Foxtrot, to be found at the Slippery Eel tavern. She told them the Doves could be found at a temple, and gave them the password *Columba nigra*, Sylvan for "black dove". <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The order she serves

Dave described the Black Doves as "a truly holy order" when [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) took them from [Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), and as "now a vehicle for the Blood of Vol and Emerald Claw", which Uriel has "completely corrupted". He added: "So she can’t purge them all", and that plenty of the elves of Aerenal would be divided on whether her taking over Eberron is good or bad, depending on whether they are elven supremacists. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

The doctrine Uriel has brought to the faith of the Sovereign Host installs herself and her unborn son as the eighth Sovereign. Dave described her as "A pregnant god of life and death, the reincarnation of the lunch queen Erandis Vol" <small>(sic)</small>, "birthing a race marked with the dragonmark of Death and ascending to rule the Host as its sole primateur". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>
