**Saltmarsh, Oerth · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Enemy of [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) · Dead**

Skerrin Wavechaser was a man of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh. He did the party Neptune's Rejects a favor after their arrest, and then, after they captured the [*Stinking Albatross*](/items/the-stinking-albatross/) and won a naval commission from the town council, he questioned them over the disappearance of two men, led them to the Weathervein tomb at night and ambushed them there with [Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) assassins. The party survived. Skerrin fled Saltmarsh with the sword [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), and on the adventure called Gabriella's honeymoon the party tracked him through a gnome inn and a caravan and killed him, and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) recovered the sword.

## The favor

Early in the campaign the party was arrested in Saltmarsh. Unarmed assault there carries 3 to 30 days and a fine, and brandishing a weapon 3 to 10 years at the county capital. Dave: "So not an insignificant favor Scaren did for us but also not punishable by death thankfully". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-10)</small>

When the council gave the party the deed to the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/), Nico counted Skerrin and Anders Solmor among its backers, and Dave answered: "The whole council did that". Of the haunted house that Salmor had cleared by a priest, Dave wrote that "scaren seemed ok with it probably because it fucked the sea princes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

## The ambush at the Weathervein tomb

The session "Salvage Operation – Part I" opened at the tail end of a council meeting that the party had stormed to announce the capture of the *Stinking Albatross* and demand a commission for the naval protection of Saltmarsh Harbor. Three people approached them before the night was out: [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/), Skerrin and [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

Skerrin pulled the party aside and interrogated them on the disappearance, or death, of **Murtog** and **Riggs**. Privately he gave Dolores a clue to the whereabouts of Weathervein:

> Cujo (gravedigger) admitted to me that he believed there was more to the Weathervein tomb, that there have been reports of hauntings below the temple.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

He acted surprised that Dolores did not already know the history of her house. He told her the [Weathervein dynasty](/organizations/house-weathervein/) "was an ancient trading cartel and feudal house in along the coast of the Azure Sea, centuries before the emergence of the Sea Princes", whose lords were "terrifying fish-like men and women who were rumored at times to bear angels wings, swim like sharks, and sprout lightning like a storm cloud", whose ill-gotten treasure was hidden "somewhere in the sea-carved caves around the coastline", and whose downfall "took just a single generation, when one of the Weatherveins attempted to bottle the secrets that made their family strong and accidentally destroyed everything." He finished: "If you are the heir to that dynasty, then you will inherit much more than treasure and blood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

He led the party to the tomb beneath the temple at night, and Brotherhood assassins ambushed them there. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave, after the session:

> But check it - Skerrin knew there was no sword in there, he knew he could watch you and ambush you in secret. He may not have known about the other room opened with your blood

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

The party's sea elf ally [Oshi](/people/oshi/) died that night, and Dolores went down and rolled a natural 20 on her death save. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, 2021-11-15)</small> In the room opened with Dolores's blood, the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) gave her a ring. The party then blew up a building and publicly executed six members of the secret society. Dave: "And now Skerrin, we have enemies and we have power". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> "Skerrin thought he could use you but he fucked up". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-18)</small> Dave said he had put "lots of clues/hooks in Vivica’s speech, and Skerrin’s too". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

Nico, who played Dolores, asked why Skerrin cared about the sword so much, and offered a theory: "Perhaps Mr. SKERRIN WAVECHASER is a descendent of the Weathervien bros ??" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> [Scrunt](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/)'s player JL said of the box from Aubreck Drallion's ship: "I don’t want money. I want revenge". Asked "On Skerrin?", he answered "Yeah". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

At the end of December 2021 Dave, as DM, told the party that Xendros could get them "a lead on the sword + Skerrin, which is probably the big boss of the campaign at this point". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>

## The hunt and his death

The hunt for Skerrin ran through Gabriella's honeymoon, a frontier adventure run by JL. The party helped a gnome inn "so we could get info on skerrin" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-05)</small>, and went looking for him in a caravan. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-11)</small> On 2022-03-12 they killed him, and Dolores took Weathervein. JL's award for the session:

> XP: 2750 for SKERRIN, 100 for Gabby for finding Weathervein. 500 Dolores for achieving her destiny.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12)</small>

## Afterward

Dave, as a player, recapped Skerrin's part a month later. The devil artifact dealer had been trafficking in items the Brotherhood was trying to hide, "so much that Skerrin fled Aaltmarsh with Weathervein and tried to disappear with it to get away from you", "After attempting to assassinate us multiple times". Nico asked whether Skerrin had warned Salmor when the party reached the caravan, and whether that was why Alexander, the Duke, had returned. Dave: "He had a letter from Alexander so they were communicating", and "But Skerrin was a shapeshifter, who fucking knows, maybe we didn’t really kill him and Alexander is Skerrin". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

**Organizations / Groups:** he ordered the Brotherhood assassins' ambush at the Weathervein tomb.\
**Items:** [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), which he carried out of Saltmarsh and which Dolores recovered at his death.
