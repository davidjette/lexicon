> I sometimes dream of heaven, like I’ve been there before. But in the dream the angels are fighting each other. And the devil is laughing.

<small>— Una, on the boat to Motherstone. (Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small>

**Human · Infanta of War (Tempus) · The War Wizards' first godchild · Killed by the party at Waterdeep**

*Also known as:* the Infanta of War · the War Infanta · the miller's daughter

**Una** is a human girl and the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) of War, the incarnation of **Tempus**. She is the daughter of the miller of Coxswain in Paenis. After she killed fourteen people at the Shadowhigh Inn, the [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) of Cormyr took her, and for years they held and tortured her in their tower at Suzail as the first of their captured god-children. Thieves working for the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) stole her and put her aboard the doomed train to Baldur's Gate. [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) and [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) brought her, resurrected, to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), where she harmed other children and was lured away by [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/). She went down the pit beneath the city, and Zanzibar and [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) jumped after her. She came to the Underdark with the Spider Bros, invaded [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), and summoned [Asmodeus](/people/asmodeus/) to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), and the party killed her. In the history the party did not change, she and the driders win the war for [the Egg](/places/the-egg/).

## The miller's daughter

Una's own account of her home: "My parents own the mill in Coxswain, in the west of Paenis. I miss them so much." <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small>

The bartender of the Shadowhigh Inn in Paenis told [Kara](/people/kara/) what she did there:

> Damn tragedy, that. They caught the little girl stealing grain and brought her to the constable. When they put her in shackles to teach her a lesson, poof, she vaporized the lot of them. Fourteen dead. The girl who did it, she was the miller’s daughter Una, they said she was a sorcerer and the War Wizards came and scooped her up.

<small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI, the Shadowhigh Inn.)</small> The inn keeps "a small altar to a handful of people killed by Una many years ago." [Celion](/people/celion/), appearing to Kara in the inn's mirror, said, "I’m sorry for your parents, they were always very nice people." Kara's player, Jenny, called Una "the girl who killed my parents." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>

## The War Wizards

Dave: "The war wizards were aware of the Infanta early enough to find and sequester Una many years ago. They are the DOD of the biggest inland human kingdom so they def have intel". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small> Their own report to the archmagi, written by Celion, dates it "three years since we found Una right in our own backyard", and calls her "our first godchild". Only she had been "able to withstand her torture, and even she is beginning to wear." The War Wizards built a shrine of Tempus to test "our theory about the proximity of infanta", and planned, "Once positioned against the Empire", to "bring Una to field to lay waste to Obus." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small>

At their campus in Suzail, "The top turret of the tower if the living quarters of Una, the girl who they have tormented to near suicide over the last few years." <small>(Sic on "if". Arc III Part VIII.)</small>

## The theft and the train

Celion's next report to the archmagi:

> A group of professional thieves and burglars infiltrated the campus tower, waylaid Una, and slipped out without detection and evidently without magical means whatsoever.

The thieves took her to the Company's Charter House near the docks, a building the War Wizards could not enter. From there "the thieves took the Infanta from the Charter House to the platform for the doomed train ride to Baldur’s Gate that led to the disappearance of Crown Prince Zanzibar, among others." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> In the Charter House there is "proof that Una was brought here, obliviated and then packed into a crate to ship to Waterdeep." <small>(Arc III Parts VII and VIII.)</small> Dave later noted that "she's not immune to enchantment magic, as evidenced by her mind wipe". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>

Stonecypher used the Vault Wand on the railway gate at Baldur's Gate, and the out-of-control steam engine careened into the void instead of the station. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small> The War Wizards' report: "It is unknown whether Una was recovered from the wreckage by St. Cloud or others, but the shrine of Tempus remains active, indicated that she is alive an possibly near Suzail." <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small>

In the War Wizards' intelligence file on Wainwright St. Cloud: "We now believe St. Cloud is directly culpable for more than the theft of Una, the infanta of War, and other high profile terrorist attacks throughout Faerun." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> See [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/).

## Resurrection and Motherstone

Zanzibar and Stonecypher "drift on a small sailing boat piloted by a halfling named Josa, their eyes on the young girl wearing bloody clothes who is vomiting off the side of the boat. The light of resurrection has mostly worn off, and the child is suffering from the common effects of being brought back to life after three days. She whimpers and snuggles up closer to Zanzibar." Of Stonecypher she asked, "Why do we have to bring her?" Of her capture she remembered: "It was morning and some purple knights showed up with two wizards. They took me with them. I don’t remember what happened after." And she asked Zanzibar, "What did you save me from?" <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small>

JL, who played Zanzibar, later wrote: "Zanzibar has known Una longer than he’s known any of the rest of the party (besides Stoney). He carried her dead body into Motherstone and fought an army of robot sharks to bring her back to life." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-07)</small>

At the Deepgate the girl was given to Untari to be tested and educated. <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small> Untari called Zanzibar to the learning garden: "Una is in distress. She harmed another child. Something must be done." Una, alone and crying in another chamber: "I don’t belong here. I miss my family. I want to go home." <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small> While the party was away at the Arcaneum, Stonecypher prayed to [Marcus](/people/marcus/), "who let her know that Una May have harmed some children in Motherstone and is displaying signs of extreme mental distress". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small> Stonecypher's player, Nico, the same day: "Una is pretty picky about who she speaks with. We think she will only interact with humans." "She likes Zanzibar though." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>

## Missing

"Una went missing from her tutor’s home the night before the assault on the Arcaneum. She had been accused of the sudden disappearance of a teacher and four children the week before, but people were too terrified to confront her for fear of what she could do." The other Infanta, Ella, Obus and Marcus, were kept away from her. "The morning she went missing, the city leaders kept it quiet. Most people believe she has been transported back to her home in Paenis. Secretly, they panic that there is an atomic bomb missing in their city." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small> Dave, at the end of the Arcaneum finale: "Did @Patrick Baker leave before I revealed Una is missing?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-31)</small>

Her tracks ended at the transport level where the mechanical body of Carlysle St. Cloud had gone with [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). Carlysle's heart had used the Palantir "to entice Una to runaway, then captured her", and took her "deeper into the collapsed dungeon" toward the Lexicon. <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small>

In the Second Battle of Motherstone Una went down the pit below the city, and Zanzibar and Revan went after her. "the only witnesses to the infanta jumping down were Pim, Roland, Zan and Rev", and before they went in, the party "told folks you were following the trail left by the missing Infanta". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small> See [Pim](/people/pim/) and [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/). Zanzibar "did it without a ring of featherfall". Dave: "You did a very selfless thing for a very small chance to save the kid that nobody likes and that shows real growth". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-07)</small> Patrick, who played Magnus, put it as "You guys jumped into a bottomless after a god of war who does not care for us and kills other children". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-07)</small>

## The Underdark and the Egg

Dave's survey of the Infanta in Arc IV: "The Infanta are scattered and making moves to shore up theirs power." JL added, "And Una in the Underdark with the Spider Bros.", and Dave answered, "Oh, right". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-19)</small> In Dave's 2022 checklist of the campaign, item ten read "deal with Una/Spiderbros/Asmodeus?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-03-19)</small>

In the history the party did not change, Dave told the players, "in the “present” era Asmodeus comes to Waterdeep and there is a massive final battle of the Infanta, followed by a prolonged underground and undersea campaign to capture the Egg, which Una and the driders win". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> Dave, on a possible game set in that future: "A cyberpunk gang about the underwater Atlantans fighting mind flayers and spider bros who conquer the planet with Una leviathan". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small> He suggested a quest line through Atlantis and [the sea elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/): "you can basically get a jump on the Spider Bros by getting the Sea Elves moving toward the Egg to head off Una". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small>

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Una, who had invaded Undermountain with the Spiderbros, summoned Asmodeus to Waterdeep. The party killed her, but not before the summoning, and a portal was used for the archdevil to step down toward the Egg. The party defended the portal from the tavern to the crack in the Egg to keep Una and any other Infanta out, because all the Infanta are drawn to the Egg to merge with Cthulhu and bring about the end, as St. Cloud designed. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-five-zanzibars-and-asmodeus.md)</small> The Egg and the final battle were obliviated. See [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/) and [the Infanta Crisis](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/).

In the Lost Train at the end, June "recognizes the train to Baldur’s Gate where they met Una." <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

## The report to the archmagi

The War Wizards' research summary, with the report on her theft, as Dave posted it to the players. Sic throughout.

> To the archmagi, a summary of our research for your discussion –
>
> It has been three years since we found Una right in our own backyard. The girl has withstood incredible stressors and has required an enormous amount of energy and time to keep her contained. But I do believe we have finally broken her. My team and I have apprehended and experimented on five Infanta and we have made incredible progress while maintaining an impossible level of safety. This has required the full might of our college, and I’m happy to say that effort has paid off.
>
> To summarize for your masters, Una was our first godchild, but we have since found four others – the infantas of Lothander, God of Dawn and Spring, Tyr God of Justice, Tymora God of Good Fortune, and Sylvanus God of Nature. Three were killed – Tyr, Tymora and Lothander, and as we have seen, killing an infanta has devastating local effects and ends their beneficially impact on clerics of their ‘Word’. Tyr’s death, for example, was the true cause of the sinking of the warship Crescenta during the battle of West Sembia, which we previously reported as caused by sabotage.
>
> Some other notes:
>
> - Our experiments on the Infanta have involved intense mind control and containment magics in order to keep them a secret and avoid the death of the wizards involved. We appreciate the steady stream of supplies and manpower.
> - Infanta can be controlled by subjecting them to psychological torment. This sets their default nature to self destruct, we consider this a safety feature.
> - Their power can’t seem to be stolen by any known means
> - Some shrines are active without nearby infanta. The theory is that these infanta have found a way to project their influence across the Weave on ley lines or similar arcane phenomenon.
> - The infanta have incomplete memory of their godhood and very limited access to their former power. Their senses are limited to their human senses.
> - It is unknown why only human have been infanta.
>
> Only recently did we find Sylvanus, the nature god, and through our work we have quadrupled the output of our key magical stock farm here in Suzail. But we are worried this boy cannot take the intense discomfort required to harness his powers effectively.
>
> Only Una has been able to withstand her torture, and even she is beginning to wear. We built the Tempus shrine to test our theory about the proximity of infanta and were proven correct. The elves of the city know that their shrines are active and their magic has returned. This gives us a powerful and proximate enemy to rally the King and his Purple Dragons to a cause other than abolishing the Arcaneum.
>
> We propose to release the Sylvanus infanta to allies of the Cormanthyrian elves to provide a common threat and keep Cormyr independent. When the Sylvanus infanta self-destructs, we can overrun Cormanthyr and take the center.
>
> Once positioned against the Empire, we can bring Una to field to lay waste to Obus. We will continue our quests to subdue and strip the infanta of their power until they can be used for the glory of Cormyr and the Arcaneum.
>
> Best,
>
> Celion

> To your masters, a summary of our investigation to date –
>
> A group of professional thieves and burglars infiltrated the campus tower, waylaid Una, and slipped out without detection and evidently without magical means whatsoever. This is why they evaded our detection at first. We have tracked the movement of these thieves to the Charter House of the Sword Coast Trading Co. near the docks. Your master know that we have long regarded the Company as a threat to Cormyran domination over trade and military matters, and that the chairman of the company is suspected of being an agent of Asmodeus or some other extraplanar power. His previous dealings with he Arcaneum show he is a dishonest dealer, and it now seems he h as declared open war on our college and perhaps the world at large.
>
> We are unable to enter the Trading House, we currently believe the inside has been transformed into a pocket dimension or similar demi-verse which is preventing us from entrance. We do know that the thieves took the Infanta from the Charter House to the platform for the doomed train ride to Baldur’s Gate that led to the disappearance of Crown Prince Zanzibar, among others. It is unknown whether Una was recovered from the wreckage by St. Cloud or others, but the shrine of Tempus remains active, indicated that she is alive an possibly near Suzail.
>
> Best,
>
> Celion

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> The same packet's report on the Obus Infanta recommended "the release of the Sylvanus infanta even though the War Infanta has not been recovered." See [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), the Sylvanus Infanta.
