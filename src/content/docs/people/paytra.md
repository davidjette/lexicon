**Tiefling · Fighter 5 (Battle Master) / Warlock 3 (Archfey) · Agent, Bureau of Compliance · Rebuilt as a robot**

*Also known as:* Patyra

**Paytra** is a winged tiefling fighter and warlock of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) in [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by Nico. With her partner [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) she worked as a rookie Blade Runner in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) on Selune, the moon of Toril, searching for the source of illegal artificial intelligence. She died in the finale of [Brownsleeve City Nights](/history/brownsleeve-city-nights/) in November 2024 and was rebuilt as a robot, and she and Kol later brought the body of [Caprica](/people/caprica/) to [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/).

## Background

Paytra's background is that of a wayfarer. She speaks Common and Infernal. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

### Brownsleeve City

Paytra and Kol were two rookie Blade Runners of the Bureau of Compliance, assigned to the sunny beachside resort community of Brownsleeve City on Selune, the moon of Toril, to search for the source of illegal AI. Bureau agents of their rank were issued a Club of Compliance, a Bureau badge and a standard-issue flying car. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small>

Their investigation followed the trail of the Red Eye across the moon, to four sites on Selune holy to the children of the Red Eye, the followers of Barrias Ruby-Eye under his nom de guerre. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16)</small>

### Death

In the finale of Brownsleeve City Nights, played on 1 November 2024, Paytra fell unconscious in a fight with robots aboard a truck, one of them "the inevitable". The truck was crashed to destroy the robots, and Paytra, meant to fly clear, died in the crash. She was killed in the line of duty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small> Of her end the arc's DM, JL, said: "At least she went peacefully, rocketing against the surface of the moon." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

An epilogue that night showed an Istus Cruiser, on which new devotees of the Red Eye would serve as crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

### Rebuilt

Paytra and Kol were rebuilt as robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10; 2024-11-17)</small> Their mission was a success: they had found the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10)</small> Red Eye is the nom de guerre the robot resistance uses for [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small>

By May 2025, according to Nico, she and Kol had brought Caprica's body back to the Terrasque and met up with [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/) and Weld ([Umwelt](/people/umwelt/)). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Paytra is a Battle Master fighter and a warlock of an archfey patron. She has wings that give her a flying speed of 30 feet while she is not in heavy armour, darkvision, and resistance to fire. Her feats are Two-Weapon Fighting, Speedy and Lucky, and she knows three manoeuvres: Disarming Attack, Riposte and Trip Attack. Her warlock magic includes *eldritch blast* with the Agonizing and Repelling invocations, Devil's Sight, *lightning lure*, *hex*, *hellish rebuke*, *darkness*, the archfey spells, and the Steps of the Fey, which let her cast *misty step* four times a day. <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:**
- **Club of Compliance.**
- **+1 scimitar**, **+1 shortsword** and **+1 longsword**, a longsword and six javelins.
- **+1 shield**, **half plate** and **plate**.
- **Potions of healing:** two greater and one superior.
- **Thieves' tools**, holy water and travelling gear.

## Relationships

- **Kol Hanaka:** her partner in the Bureau of Compliance. Nico called the pair their "Jedi and sith builds". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-11)</small>
- **Marcel Martel** and **Umwelt:** devotees of the Red Eye whom she and Kol met up with.
