**Cleric of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) · Knowledge domain · Healer of the [Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/) · The Gatehouse, [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Alive**

Diana Rhea is a healer of the [Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/) in [Sigil](/places/sigil/) and a cleric of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) of the Knowledge domain, working at the Bleakers' headquarters, the Gatehouse. She wants to cure [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/), and her assistant Craig had been missing for three weeks in the Spillway. The party came to the Gatehouse at night, met her, and were asked to find Craig. They found him in the Spillway Depths, saved him, and closed the plague portal that was the source of Cageblight; she rewarded them, and the Gatehouse was opened to them.

## The Bleaker healer

The Bleak Cabal, "who find no sense in the multiverse", keeps its headquarters at the Gatehouse and serves Sigil as hospital and welfare; its members are counselors, healers, nihilists, and communists or mutualists. <small>(DM notes)</small> Diana Rhea is one of its healers. She hates the Cult of St. Magnus, the Harmonium. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Her assistant, Craig, a Bleaker intern, had been missing for three weeks, and she could not get close to the Spillway. Her leads on the cause of Cageblight point to the sewers under the Spillway. She is suspicious of strangers, but if convinced will help those who locate her assistant. <small>(DM notes)</small> Her quests for the party were to find the missing cleric and the cause of Cageblight. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## At the Gatehouse

After the Grindhouse, one of the party's options was to go to the Gatehouse and see Diana Rhea. <small>(DM notes)</small> A Bleaker cleric doing triage who knew about Craig told them to come at night and volunteer. They did; they were detained, disarmed and disrobed, and then met with Diana Rhea, who had their belongings returned and "Beseeched them to find Craig..." <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## Craig and the plague portal

Craig had a scroll of *close portal* in process. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party pursued [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) into the Spillway Depths and cornered him in the Sanitizing Room, where a portal to a plague dimension had opened in the drain and was poisoning the water system. Craig had attempted to finish his master's mission to close it, and had angered a plague demon. The party killed the demon, Gorelach and Gunt, saved Craig, closed the portal and killed Rotten William. By closing the portal they cured Cageblight. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## Faction ally

The party received 2,000 gold and poultices from Diana Rhea. The Gatehouse was opened to them, and the Bleak Cabal became a faction ally, offering "Safehaven, assistance, and information", with further work in escort and infiltration. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

**Contacts & Relations:**
- Craig: her assistant, a Bleaker intern; saved by the party.
- The [Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/): her faction.
- The Harmonium, the Cult of St. Magnus: hated.
