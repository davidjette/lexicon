**Dwarf · Shaman · The swamp dwarves · Unknown**

*Also known as:* **Yellow Meat**

**Yellameet** is an old dwarven shaman of the swamps in the dwarflands east of [Leef](/places/leef/), and the grandfather of Princess [Orange Chicken](/people/orange-chicken/). The rebels sent [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) to him to win the dwarves to their side, and he set the terms, among them a marriage between his granddaughter and Dario. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The dwarves

The dwarves to the east have been fighting the Empire since before there was an Empire. They built a nation on a narrow strip of land beyond the Ironroot Mountains, and for hundreds of years they have harried Imperial convoys and battlements, keeping their own gods and customs. An Imperial garrison watched them closely. The way to Yellameet ran east to the statue of Lady Ghallanda, north to the old well, north-east past the guard post into dwarven territory, and due east from the big tree in the swamps, where the dwarves would find the traveller. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The tree of wisdom

Dwarven archers challenged the trio in Dwarvish, and as they came near, visions of Imperial soldiers slaughtering dwarves filled the sky around them. A voice demanded how they dared approach the holy place after all they had done to its people. Yellameet would not treat with outlanders who came without a gift, and asked for a fruit from the top of the tree of wisdom. The tree spawned a green fungus that poisoned anyone who touched it, and its fruit was itself poisonous; Yellameet told them that even what is dangerous may be useful, that the fruit could fight infection, and that though they were enemies they might serve each other. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The terms

His conditions for an alliance were four. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

1. The rebels would preserve dwarven autonomy and the sanctity of the dwarven homelands.
2. The dwarves would serve under no outlander, but would fight as equals.
3. The party would forge a meaningful alliance with the dwarves by marriage to his granddaughter, Orangemeet.
4. They would take his gift, an **Eversmoking Bottle**.

Dario was engaged to Orange Chicken, and Yellameet followed the party back toward Leef with a small army of thirty-five dwarves. The dwarves also warned the party not to trust the fox. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> Years later, Dario talked his way into a night's hospitality at Rak'tafur by naming Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long in the right order. <small>([Korth Ep 7](/sessions/korth-ep-7-the-glitterhame-and-the-grave-robbers/))</small>
