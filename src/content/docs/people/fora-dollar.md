**Half-elf · Master hunter · The Corvari · The Grand Lodge · Whereabouts unknown**

**Fora Dollar** is a master hunter of the Corvari and a member of [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), who guided [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) through the King's Forest to the peak where they killed [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/). He was hired to hunt a huge red bird and did not know that the bird was a man. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

> I came to hunt a bird, not a man. I'm no murderer.

## Description

He is sixty, lean and grey-bearded, and dresses in waxed leather the colour of bark. He carries an ash longbow taller than himself and a coil of black rope over one shoulder. His grounding arrows trail weighted cords that bind a bird's wings. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

## The Corvari

His people were cast out of Valenar for their human blood. They call themselves the **Corvari** and live as a band in the King's Forest, across the river from the Summer Palace. Dollar saved a lord's life, and the lord sponsored him into the Lodge. He said that the forest holds elves and druids, his own people and worse. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

## The red bird

[Korth Ep 17](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/). In the Hunters' Salon of the Grand Lodge he got up from a couch and said, *"I've seen the red bird."* He had watched it fly across the river that morning and knew where it roosted. His price was 500 gold before and 1,000 after, less 500 if he could keep the tail feathers. Gemma, as Deirdre Moro, paid him.

He rowed the two of them across the river, drew a sign in mud on each of their arms that glowed green and let them pass without trace, and led them north. At an inlet a dark druid bound him and Eric in vines. When the druid was dead he said, *"Dark druids. If they're protecting this bird, we're in for some trouble."* Above the falls the drow Velith drove both her swords into his chest. He cut Eric out of the spiders' silk afterwards and gave each of them a potion that closed their wounds and lifted the poison, and he said they should turn back.

At the camp on the peak the red bird seized him and carried him out over the valley. He tore loose and dropped into the trees. His grounding arrow then brought the bird out of the air with its legs bound to its wings, and when his next arrows struck it the bird became General Thar.

When Thar was dead he said, *"What have you got me into? You just assassinated some military officer. This will be the end of my career."* Eric welcomed him to the rebellion, and he answered, *"Rebellion, no. You think you know what's best for everyone? Collapsing the Empire, fighting all this time, destroying everything?"* He took the rest of his fee and the tail feathers, gave them a share of the drow's silver and a vial of drow poison, and left down the slope. *"Good luck to you, whoever you are."* <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

## Right now

Dollar knows that two guests of the Lodge killed an Imperial general. A burst of gunfire sounded in the valley in the direction he had gone, and what became of him is not recorded. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) the Grand Lodge's Fieldmaster asked Deirdre Moro where Master Hunter Fora was, and said he had been a member for eighty years. He did not return to the Lodge. Gemma and Eric kept his elvish canoe. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>
