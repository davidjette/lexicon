**Beholder · Bartender · SSJ Zanzibar · Imprisoned**

*Also known as:* Luigi · prisoner LL849

**Large Luigi** is a beholder bartender who kept his bar aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) during *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. In the fifteen years that followed he was held by [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/) as prisoner LL849, awaiting trial for treason and crimes against the [Sovereignty](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/).

## Light of Xaryxis

The Zanzibar's bartender is a [beholder](/species/beholders/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> According to Dave, the Zanzibar may have been grounded for so long that Luigi opened his bar aboard the ship instead of in town. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

[Se7en](/people/se7en/), the brewing robot built by [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), became Luigi's best friend, at least as the supplier of his booze. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> JL joked that Luigi was planning to make gabagool out of Se7en's pig, Mr Hamburger. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>

The Zanzibar's crew roster lists Large Luigi among its civilians and guests. <small>(crew roster, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

By the time of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, fifteen years later, Luigi was known only as prisoner LL849 and had spent those years in a high-security OSR3G facility, awaiting trial for treason and crimes against the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

> The Sovereignty officially disavows all knowledge of Large Luigi’s whereabouts. <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
