**Female Elf · Neophyte Sister of the Blood · [The Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) · Alive when last seen ([Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/))**

---

**Saturnia** was a blue-haired elf of the Qualanthri blood, a granddaughter of [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) and a cousin of Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). She came to the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) to take the vows of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and was made a Sister of the Blood, one of the elven women kept to tend [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) and to bear children for his line. She bore the Mark of Death.

[The Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) met her on the boat to the Lazaret and again among the novices. In the Westhouse she changed sides. She told the party what the Emperor was, took them under the hospital to her grandfather, anointed them against the Emperor's sleep and led them into the [Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/). She left the valley with Uriel's two surviving daughters, Drakiel and Ysabel, bound for Aerenal.

## Family

Her family came from Aerenal to Khorvaire on a ship called the *Lightbringer*. The houses that came on it descend from the House of Vol, and only elves of that descent could join the Sisters of the Blood. The other girls in red were her cousins. <small>(Korth Ep 16, Korth Ep 19)</small>

Her grandfather was E'noch, whom she had never met. He was the author of the plan to breed the Qualanthri line with the dragon the Volite houses call the Crimson Sun. Saturnia said her family had dreamed of the day their kind would be in charge again, and her mother called the Sisterhood the future of elvendom in the world. Like Uriel and E'noch she carried Qualanthri blood, which gave a hold on the Emperor and spared her from the sleep that lay over his palace. <small>(Korth Ep 14, Korth Ep 16)</small>

On the boat she told [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) that she had never seen Aerenal and had lived her whole life in Khorvaire. In the Westhouse she said that when her grandfather sent for her she was cast out of Aerenal. <small>(Korth Ep 14, Korth Ep 19)</small>

## The boat

In [Korth Ep 14](/sessions/korth-ep-14-the-listening-post/) she stood alone at a window of the boat that carried the party up the river, an elf woman perfectly turned out in a gown of no common cut. Eric spoke to her about his great-grandmother's tales of the sunset over Aerenal. She said she was going to take the vows of poverty, virtue and chastity, and that the boat ride might be her last chance. Eric pitied her and left her alone.

## Novice

In [Korth Ep 16](/sessions/korth-ep-16-orientation-day/) Eric recognised her among the novices on the processional walk at Nos Matre. On the rear terrace she came up behind the party so as not to leave them behind, and talked about why she was there.

> I understand that my virginity now belongs to the Emperor, and we'll see what he does with it.

![Saturnia at prayer](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-saturnia.webp)
*Saturnia at prayer.*

At the reception under the tower a sister with a slate asked the elves a fourth question, in Elvish: on what boat did your family travel from Aerenal? Saturnia answered *the Lightbringer*. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), disguised as the elf novice Sister Apollonia, gave the same ship, and Saturnia took her for kin: *"I did not realize we were kin."* She named her grandfather as E'noch.

## Sister of the Blood

In [Korth Ep 19](/sessions/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse/) the party, lodged in the Westhouse as General Thar and his druids, opened a door and found her passing in tears, in the new red robes of a Sister of the Blood. *"Sisters of the Blood keep our tongues still."* In the classroom upstairs she sat down among veiled elven sisters studying the rites of the Blood of Vol, and a sister withered her with a curse when she faltered.

After the party killed [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), Lus Fayina and Sister Mary Christmas in Dorn's office, Saturnia begged for her life.

> I follow the light of Aerenal. What they've done with it here is blasphemous. It's disgusting. They only want me because of who my family is.

![Saturnia begs Gemma for her life](/images/album/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse-saturnia-begs.webp)
*Saturnia begs Gemma for her life.*

Gemma frightened the truth out of her, and Eric persuaded her to come with them: *"No matter what happens, the Emperor and the Empress will be dead. If not by dawn, then by the next one."* [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) told her, *"The rebellion is inevitable."*

She told them the following.

- The Sisters of the Blood were trained to tend the Emperor in his condition and kept as reserves to bear his children or his offspring's. The Empress would let no one else bear the first heir. After that, all of them were to be given to the heir. *"They say his lust will be insatiable."*
- Anyone who set foot on the palace grounds without a Sister of the Blood's anointing fell asleep *"like an innocent babe, long enough for one of the guards to come over and cut your throat."* She knew the anointing, and she gave them red robes.
- *"The Emperor is not a man. He is the dragon, whom we call the Crimson Sun."* Long ago he mated with Minara Vol, and their child was Erandis Vol. The dragons of Argonnessen and the elves of Aerenal exterminated the House of Vol, and her people held that the dragon was all but destroyed protecting Erandis.
- Uriel's Mark of Death was said to exceed any since Erandis. Saturnia hated that part of her family.
- E'noch was somewhere below the hospital. The final initiation of a Sister of the Blood was to meet him, and she expected to meet him that week. *"He is tremendously old."*

In the Imperial Archive she and Eric found the records of the King of Galifar's attempts to cure his son, the future Kaius I, and of treatments by an elvish shaman called E'noch.

## Under the hospital

In [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/), with the fire climbing the wall of the next room, she told them what her house had dreamed of: *"to all go back to this distant past where our kind mated with a dragon. And even though I thought it was a fantasy, it seems my grandfather has made it come true."* She called vampires a perversion of the magic of the Undying Court: *"It's why they were banned from Aerenal and fled to Khorvaire, for their work with the undead and magic of the blood, and for fornicating with dragons."*

> I've been brought here to be a broodmare for this line. My cousin, she sits in the throne now, but I think if my grandfather had his way, she would step aside or die. She has failed in her mission.

> I'm willing to help you do what you must. Even if it leads to the death of my grandfather. He is a monster.

She had been in the residence twice: once to learn to wash the Emperor, and once to meet his children, *"the most horrible children I have ever met."* Gemma offered her a future: *"Maybe if you help us, you can come out the other end and be the new face for Black Doves. What it truly means to be a Black Dove."*

![In the faces of Dorn, Mary Christmas and Lus Fayina, the party drinks at the Westhouse fountain with Saturnia](/images/album/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party-the-fountain.webp)
*The party, in the faces of Dorn, Mary Christmas and Lus Fayina, drinks at the Westhouse fountain with Saturnia.*

She went down through the concourse with the party to the morgue of the hospital, where a button lay under a dragon skull. Gemma thought Saturnia should be the one to step on it.

> So I'm to meet my grandfather after all these decades. He wants to drink all of my blood and turn me into his slave, or preserve me as a virgin for my cousin's child to mate with, so that future emperor half-dragons can be Qualanthris.

Three vampire spawn burst out of the coffins and one bit her to the floor.

![A vampire spawn seizes Saturnia as Dario fights another on the stairs](/images/album/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party-the-spawn-and-saturnia.webp)
*A vampire spawn seizes Saturnia as Dario fights another on the stairs.*

In the vampires' lair, while the party was at the barred wall of the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/), she screamed behind them and was gone. They found her laid on a table beside the blood pool, with E'noch, Lady Ophelia Qualanthri and Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn. During the fight her throat was cut over a ritual ewer, and Ophelia bent down to drink.

![Lady Ophelia turns her magic on Saturnia at the table](/images/album/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party-ophelia-and-saturnia.webp)
*Lady Ophelia turns her magic on Saturnia at the table.*

When the gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/) rose out of the pool, Saturnia got up, holding her throat, and went for her cousin with a dagger. E'noch, Ophelia and Trillo Dwyn were destroyed.

### The mark

The fight tore her robe and showed a black mark running up from her midriff, the Mark of Death. Standing with her neck held closed, she looked very like a young Uriel. Aurorix said that the Volite houses kept the mark secret and that all their scions bore it.

> The Empress is just one among many. That one there, you should destroy as well.

Gemma refused.

## The Summer Palace

In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) Saturnia took a red-black salve from a pouch and drew a simple bird on each of their foreheads, and on her own.

> It's made from the blood of the dragon himself. Without it, you would fall asleep, stepping on the grounds.

She led them across the Emperor's gardens in the rain. *"Don't stop in the garden. They'll wonder what you're doing."* In a walled garden, under a statue of Lathander made in the Emperor's likeness, she found the body of Archbishop Pom Pilot of Arawai and screamed.

> There shouldn't be anyone in this garden at all. The fact that he was here meant he must have been here to visit the Emperor. The Emperor is not alive in any proper sense. He's in a coma. He's held there by Uriel. I can't say any more.

Gemma wanted to go straight to the Empress, and Saturnia warned that she was extremely powerful. Saturnia wanted the children's wing, the only part of the palace she had been in. The palace doors had no handle and no lock, and Sister Marshall held the only key. When Saturnia had minded the children, Muriel, the eldest, had shown her that the girls wedged their door open. She led the party along the back of the palace to the girls' sunken garden.

![Mary Christmas, Dorn and Lus Fayina fight a Cursed Knight, with Saturnia behind them](/images/album/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace-the-cursed-knights.webp)
*The party, in their stolen faces, fights a Cursed Knight, with Saturnia behind them.*

In the daughters' hall the girls saw through the disguises and fought. Chanel, who was eight, came at Saturnia with a little watering can and breathed fire. Saturnia called down sacred flame on Muriel: *"You awful creature! You awful, awful girl! This is what you deserve!"* When Dario beheaded Muriel, Saturnia threw herself on the body and beat at its chest.

### Drakiel and Ysabel

When the fight ended, every daughter was dead except Drakiel, who was eleven and had wept in a corner throughout, and the baby Ysabel. Dario drew his sword over Drakiel, and Saturnia stood between them.

> This one's never done anything cruel.

She held onto his clothes and begged. Eric, as Chancellor, would not sanction the killing, and Dario sheathed his sword. Gemma put the baby in Drakiel's arms. Saturnia took both girls out to the river to swim across.

> I promise I'll bring them to Aerenal. We'll leave Khorvaire. You'll never hear from us again. By her mercy. I mean, by your mercy.

## Aftermath

The Emperor was destroyed that afternoon, and Uriel was destroyed in the Sept of Bones in [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/). A [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) then burned the valley. Saturnia and the two girls had left for the river before the bell rang. They were not seen again.

**Status:** Alive when last seen, crossing the river with Drakiel and Ysabel, bound for Aerenal.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **[E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/)** — her grandfather, who sent for her and whom she had never met. She called him a monster and helped the party destroy him.
- **[Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)** — her cousin, the Empress. Saturnia hated what Uriel embraced in their family.
- **Lady Ophelia Qualanthri** — her cousin, a vampire of E'noch's household, who drank from her cut throat. Destroyed in Korth Ep 20.
- **Drakiel** and **Ysabel** — Uriel's surviving daughters, her young cousins, whom she took across the river.
- **Muriel** — Uriel's eldest daughter, whom Saturnia had minded and called an awful girl. Killed by Dario.
- **[Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)** — pitied her on the boat, and persuaded her to change sides in the Westhouse.
- **[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/)** — whom she took for kin as Sister Apollonia, and who refused to kill her when Aurorix asked it.
- **[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/)** — whom she stopped from killing Drakiel.
- **[Aurorix](/species/aurorix/)** — the gold dragon, who wanted her destroyed for her mark.
