**Archivist · Old Histories Wing, Sharn Library · Trusted by R.U.S.T. · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Renn · the archivist</small>

**Renn Tal** is an archivist of the Old Histories Wing of the Sharn Library, trained in pre-Galifar ritual history and Dhakaani archaeology. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) personally tasked her with cataloguing ancient texts on [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), the Eternal Guardian and the Forge of Binding, and the work turned up missing pages in Imperial-approved tomes, altered chronologies and references to subterranean structures beneath Sharn that should not exist. Her questions took her into the Lower City, where [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/) brought her into the rebel network and [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) put her Dhakaani literacy to work. She deciphered and opened the puzzle door to the Forge of Binding, lost her post, her apartment and her city to the Iron Veil, and now directs the Eye of Corruption works on behalf of Sharn.

Renn Tal is an archivist from the Old Histories Wing of the Sharn Library and one of the few scholars in the city who has uncovered evidence surrounding the ritual of the Eternal Guardian. Trained in pre-Galifar ritual history and Dhakaani archaeology, she was personally tasked by Esther Crona with cataloging ancient texts related to Varyn Crona, the Eternal Guardian, and the Forge of Binding. Her research revealed missing pages in Imperial-approved tomes, altered chronologies, and references to subterranean structures beneath Sharn that should not exist.

Her pursuit of answers led her out of the library and into the Lower City, where she interviewed Cogborn elders, studied sewer-line schematics, and traced forgotten Dhakaani tunnel systems. These questions drew the attention of DJ Rust, who recognized she was not a spy but a scholar wandering into danger. He brought her into the Lower City network, where Misty Kross quickly realized Renn's value in deciphering Dhakaani symbols and mapping ancient tunnel routes. Through Misty, Renn was eventually introduced to Preacher.

Renn is not a formal member of RUST, but she is trusted. She knows the Cogborn are hiding in a section of the undercity. She knows the blocked tunnels near them lead to an ancient Dhakaani tunnel systems. She knows the Forge of Binding is real, because her research has led her closer to it than any Imperial historian has dared to go. And she believes that if she can reach the Forge, she may be able to reverse or unbind the Eternal Guardian ritual that ties Esther Crona to Varyn's sword. Her notes indicate that such a counter-rite would require a priest to anchor the invocation and a skilled forge-worker capable of operating the ancient mechanisms, making the ritual impossible to perform alone.

**Status:** Alive.

## Physical description

A young woman with short black hair set in deep finger waves, dark eyes, and the level unhurried look of someone who has decided to be difficult about a detail. She dresses like the reading room: a cream shirt with the sleeves rolled to the elbow under a dark red pinstripe waistcoat and matching high-waisted trousers. She is almost never recorded without a stack of books held against her chest — seven of them in her own likeness, old, gilt-stamped and none of them hers. <small>(Appearance taken from her portrait on the Rebel Factions page.)</small>

## The commission

The Old Histories Wing of the Sharn Library is a department of an Imperial institution, and its protocols reflect that: a librarian named Peter confirmed to Renn's face that he was required to contact the Iron Veil if anyone enquired after [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), and did. Renn is trained in pre-Galifar ritual history and Dhakaani archaeology, and was personally tasked by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) with cataloguing ancient texts relating to Varyn Crona, the Eternal Guardian and the Forge of Binding. The Lord Commander gave her the assignment.

To the library she is a compliant specialist doing a prestigious commission. What she found instead was missing pages in Imperial-approved tomes, altered chronologies, and references to subterranean structures beneath Sharn that should not exist. Her pursuit of those answers led her out of the reading room and into the Lower City, where she interviewed Cogborn elders, studied sewer-line schematics and traced forgotten Dhakaani tunnel systems — the questions of a scholar wandering into lethal danger, which is exactly how [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/) read her. He recognised she was not a spy and brought her into the Lower City network, where [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) quickly grasped her value in deciphering Dhakaani symbols and mapping ancient routes. Through Misty she reached [Preacher](/people/preacher/).

She is not a formal member of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), but she is trusted. She knows the Cogborn are hiding in a section of the undercity. She knows the blocked tunnels near them lead into ancient Dhakaani systems. And she knows the Forge of Binding is real, because her research has led her closer to it than any Imperial historian has dared to go.

**What she is actually trying to do:** reverse the Eternal Guardian ritual. Renn believes the rite that bound Varyn Crona — and through his sword, Esther — can be unbound at the Forge itself. Her notes are specific about the cost: such a counter-rite would require a priest to anchor the invocation and a skilled forge-worker capable of operating the ancient mechanisms, which makes it impossible to perform alone. She has been assembling those two people ever since.

## Origin

Renn Tal was an Imperial archivist with a specialism nobody in the Empire had bothered to be curious about: pre-Galifar ritual structures and Dhakaani ceremonial architecture. That specialism made her the natural choice when the Lord Commander wanted her mentor's history catalogued, and she noticed that the chronology did not close. She lost her apartment, her post and her city — Esther Crona came to the library after the party's escape, questioned the archivists and left, and Renn has been in hiding ever since. She lived in the trio's Lower City safe house, then at Zilspar Farm, and never once stopped working.

## The Library

She met the party at [Jessica](/people/jessica-grimpledink/)'s family lockbox and warned them quietly that the building was not safe, then told them to find DJ Rust at Breaker's Pit if they needed to speak further. Minutes later the Iron Veil arrived at the door. The party killed them and teleported out; Renn lost everything and went down into the Lower City.

## In the field

She warned three armed strangers to leave her own library because it was not safe, and gave them a contact rather than an argument. She deciphered the inscription on the puzzle door beneath Sharn and opened the Forge. When a Mind Flayer had [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) in a grapple and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) dominated, Renn broke its concentration with the Wand of Magic Missile [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/) had given her. She then explained, standing in the chamber, how Varyn Crona and E'noch Qualanthri had used the Forge as part of the Eternal Guardian ritual, drawing on forbidden Vol rites.

## The Forge of Binding

Behind the puzzle door she opened, the long-lost Forge stood intact. Eric performed the Rite of Recall there with Dario binding elemental cold into his weapon, and Varyn's Echo manifested — repeating fragments of his final moments rather than fighting — and spoke: *"…we'll bleed the sun, eternal night. Our bloodline shall reign… As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine. E'noch… Qualanthri… my brother…"* Then a drill came through the ceiling and Esther Crona dropped down onto it.

> "She knows the Forge of Binding is real, because her research has led her closer to it than any Imperial historian has dared to go."

## Right now

With the farm gone and the city out of fuel and a month from severe food shortages, Renn Tal stood beside the Eye of Corruption while Eric bled its rising output off as fire for Sharn's furnaces — and then declared the Eye the property of Sharn and informed the Nest engineers that they now worked for her. [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) was cut out of her own operation in the process. Renn also briefed the Chancellor on the famine and handed him a spell scroll.

## Beliefs

She believes an archive that has been edited is evidence of what was removed. She believes the Forge of Binding is a real place under a real city and that everyone insisting otherwise has been told to. She believes that if she can reach it, Esther Crona can be unbound from a dead man's sword.

**Organizations / Groups:** Old Histories Wing, Sharn Library (in absentia); trusted outsider to R.U.S.T.; de facto director of the Eye of Corruption works.

**Holdings:** an Upper City apartment she can never return to · a place in the trio's Lower City safe house · the Eye of Corruption and the engineers assigned to it.

**Items:** a Wand of Magic Missile, made for her by Doppler Klink and used to break a Mind Flayer's concentration · her research notes on the Eternal Guardian counter-rite · sewer-line schematics and Dhakaani tunnel maps · a spell scroll given to Eric.

**Dhakaani Literacy:** Renn reads a language the Empire's approved histories do not admit is relevant. It let her map tunnel systems beneath the Cogs that appear on no municipal plan, identify the probable region of the Forge of Binding, decipher the inscription on its puzzle door, and recognise the markings on an Ioun Stone recovered from a sealed Underhive chamber. It is also why the Iron Veil wants her.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to reach the Forge and unbind the ritual, and, latterly, to keep a starving city warm.

**Likes:** primary sources · Dhakaani inscriptions · Cogborn elders who remember · a chronology that closes · being given a wand · running the works herself.

**Dislikes:** edited tomes · librarians who follow protocol · Imperial historians · being told a place does not exist · waiting for permission.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** she will take command of something that is not hers if she is certain she is right.
