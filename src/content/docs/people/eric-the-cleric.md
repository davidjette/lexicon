**Cleric · Chancellor of Sharn · The Inevitables · Level 12 · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Graham Cracker** (Korth cover, taken off a corpse) · **The Nerd** · **Harry Potter** (on the books of the Zilspar Farm-Table Co-op) · **Eric Passagemarked** (his official style, after his mark) · one of the **Heroes of Leef** · <small>styled "Chancellor of Char" in one internal note — the live record says Sharn</small>

Eric the Cleric is a cleric of a Karrnathi order of mathematics and order, a former auditor of the Pax Imperium, and Chancellor of the free city of Sharn. He was an orphan forced to learn mathematics and to worship it, served the Empire as an auditor, and lived three years in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records in Korth. In the desert rail-town of Leef he fell in with [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/); the three liberated the town and were named the Heroes of Leef, then went west to Newham and killed Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) as the city collapsed into the New Mourning. Sent into Sharn to steal the Ghost Chip, they fought the war that broke the Empire's hold on the city, and its Council seated Eric as Chancellor in [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s former penthouse. Since the Glitterhame beneath the Korramont he has been hearing the voice of LeBeefe, which nobody else can hear.

He wears metal armour that rings when he brushes a wall, which has woken at least one colony of stirges. On the back of his hand he carries a **dragonmark of Passage** that flares with lightning when he uses it and gave away his identity to a dying admiral. Since attuning a Belt of Dwarvenkind he has been growing a dwarven beard he did not previously have, along with darkvision and a resistance to poison. He is physically feeble — Strength 7, an unarmed strike that does nothing — and carries a tarot deck for divination. Height, colouring and eyes: **Unknown**. By JL's account Eric tried on shorts for the first time out on the coast, and they "might be part of his new look". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>

**His clerical order** is one of mathematics and order, which is less strange in Karrnath than it sounds. In a country whose state religion is bookkeeping applied to the dead — where the very old walk willingly into the Hall of Submission to begin their second service, where a death sentence specifies both the duration of pain and the length of service owed afterward in undeath, and where sentences are scaled by race against expected lifespan — a priesthood of arithmetic is a civil institution as much as a faith. It made Eric an Imperial auditor. It also made him the only person in the rebellion who could read an Imperial ledger and know which line was the lie, and the only one who looked at a dead dwarf king's bell-forging folio and recognised harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms on sight. Its god is Matt Matheny. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-26)</small>

**In public** Eric is Chancellor of Sharn, seated by the Council in Esther Crona's former penthouse in the Veil Building, and he speaks well: at Thronehold he stood in front of the Empire's own cameras and called for the Empire to be outlawed, with Sanctuary up. In Korth he is Graham Cracker, a paralegal, a younger elf, an orphan, a Korthian old-town boy — a man whose whole function is to carry the preliminary material. **In private** he is a self-described mediocre accountant who spent three years in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records, whose own file in the Imperial Navy's hands says exactly that, and who is currently hearing a voice that no one else can hear. He concealed the whispers from his own party for four sessions of hard travel. He argued in the open for ringing the Thirteenth Bell and then denied having raised it at all. Twice he had to be physically restrained from ringing a bell himself. He is aware of all of this and has offered to be tied up.

Eric is the party's opener of things that will not open — a membrane-like door in the Dhakaani tunnels beneath Sharn, the Rite of Recall at the Forge of Binding that called up Varyn Crona's echo, an Iron Door that answered "shave and a haircut" by saying their names back. He works with Renn Tal, Jessica Grimpledink, Digma Beeve and the Council of Sharn. His heirloom is not his: the **Belt of Dwarvenkind** he wears was robbed from a sealed sepulcher, identified at weapons' point as the property of Madame Tso Teryaki's late husband, taken back — and then given to him freely, by her, after Dario carried her king's daughter home.

**The secret:** the voice is John C. LeBeefe, who Eric helped kill in Newham, and it did not stop when the fungal bloom was purged out of him. The compulsion to ring went quiet when the Thirteenth Casting broke. LeBeefe did not. On the walk out of the mountain he delivered a lecture Eric has told exactly one person: that Emperor Kaius is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun, kept alive as a vessel and fed dragon blood to strengthen the bloodline.

He believes that a city is a ledger and that the arithmetic will tell you what is true before anyone confesses it. He believes that whoever rings the bell controls the magic — an argument he made aloud, twice, and then denied making. He believes he has already handed [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) the keys to Sharn, that it was a trick, and that the only hope left is surviving Korth in time to take them back.

**In the chair** Eric governs by negotiation and by exposure. He inherited a city out of fuel, a month from severe shortages, and by the Hundreds' own arithmetic forty-seven days from famine, and he paid for bread with airspace. He found that the Eye of Corruption in Esther's penthouse resonates with his psychic power and sharpens him, and used that borrowed clarity to help the Nest's engineers bleed its output off as ordinary fire under the city's furnaces. He hides by being underestimated, and he tests his cover by telling the truth in front of armed men to see whether anybody twitches.

**Origin.** Eric was, by his own account, an orphan forced to learn mathematics and to worship it. By JL's account he "probably grew up in the Depths of Sharn, went to the university to study maths just to get out." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> He served the Pax Imperium as an auditor and spent three years living in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records. The Empire then sent him east as, in JL's words, "a tax attorney sent by the empire to investigate irregularities in LeBeefe’s accounting". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> His memory carries flashes from his time around John C. LeBeefe, including a tune out of the Black Palace that he heard again years later behind a membrane door under Sharn. In the desert rail-town of Leef he fell in with Gemma Corso and Dario Argentino; the three of them liberated the town with Thadric Rourke, Zero of Leef and Princess Orange Chicken, and were named the Heroes of Leef. He joined Gemma and Dario after the train-station attack, on the rebels' side, and was with them in what JL called "the belly of the beast", where LeBeefe was attempting to build a mind flayer army. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-22; 2025-08-30)</small> He is found caged in the fourth session and first played in the fifth session of the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> They went west to Newham, united Digma Beeve's scattered cells, and killed LeBeefe — and the city collapsed into the New Mourning behind them. Sent on to Sharn to steal the Ghost Chip, they met [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and never left the war. Digma Beeve holds Eric's comptrollership of the Republic of Talenta in his absence.

## Leef and Newham

Eric came east for the Imperial Revue Service; his superior there was a gnome, Master Clipperfast. Imperial soldiers took him on arrival and handed him to the Black Doves to verify his story, and the Doves imprisoned and tortured him in the basement of the abandoned [temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/) at Leef, their listening station, and cast *feeblemind* on him. The trio found him there in a cage. A Black Dove who survived the fight offered them an alliance against LeBeefe, and told them that the boy in the cage bore the imperial mark of audit and was the key to LeBeefe's fortress. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> In Newham his mark let the trio pass freely in and out of the Black Fortress, where LeBeefe welcomed them to inspect his books and the ink bled off every page. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Comptroller of Leef

In the year after Newham fell, Eric ran for office in Leef and was elected comptroller unanimously, as the only choice on the ballot. The old lightning rail depot became the township's meeting hall. He put three policies to a public vote: the land the town had used for rail transport would become public housing for refugees from Newham and Mrorian immigrants; farmers would give fifteen percent of their annual crop to the public good; and citizens over eighteen could volunteer for the public defence force, which would absolve them of annual taxation. He added a humble fane to Matt Matheny to the Falcons' garage, a place for prayer and reflection that turns out holy healing charms. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The gnome **Potpie**, an envoy of the Librarians, came to Leef in a two-seater airship to see [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/), and after that meeting Digma went straight to Eric. When the two came out, hours later, Eric said: "It seems I have a secret mission to go on." <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> The rebel dossier on the mission records the rest. The Librarians of Darguun-Zilargo could fight no further against the Empire while Hobgoblin pirates contested their territory, and Eric Passagemarked, clerk of rank 5, was dispatched to the shores of Khraal to find the funds for a war on two fronts. He began a full accounting of the Librarians' ledgers; he was abducted by pirates in a daylight raid on their rebel fortress; he was released after negotiating an armistice between the rival pirate gangs and the Librarians, and the warlords pledged themselves to the rebellion. The united pirates then broke the armistice and declared war on the Librarians and the Empire both. The Librarian stronghold fell, the pirates took the Khraalish peninsula and drove the Imperial forces west, and they declared the free and independent [Hobgoblin State of Khraal](/organizations/the-free-state-of-khraal/), a full member of the rebel alliance. <small>(JL's DM notes: Eric's secret Khraal mission (dossier))</small>

Eric came home looking very relaxed, with a confidence nobody had seen in him before, a tattoo in Goblin on his neck, and a hobgoblin girlfriend, **Fronque**, a foot taller than him. The two of them had an appointment for a couples massage with the vampires. On that mission he learned Deep Speech, gained proficiency with the bagpipes, and came away with a Repulsion Shield. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

> "The mission was a smashing success. But you can read all about it in the dossier."

<small>— Eric the Cleric, back in Leef.</small>

## Thronehold

Before the Summit, the Hundreds sent a separate invitation for Eric to meet with [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) there before he gave his speech. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small> By Dave's account, "Eric played his role perfectly, showing the rebellion to be recalcitrant and unwilling to deal with the Empire on equal terms, now all are on Death Ground". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-19)</small>

## The whispers

In the **Glitterhame** beneath the Korramont, the fungal bloom spoke to Eric with a clear voice — *"Have you come to deliver the message… as my lord sent you?"* — and he answered it aloud, failed the save, and felt something look out through his eyes. The whispers had already begun at the head of the Grand Stair and never stopped. They were aimed at him alone and they said: *Deliver the message.*

He concealed them for the whole descent. He advocated ringing the bell in the sepulcher gallery. He argued for it again in the open in front of the party — *"whoever rings the bell, they control the magic"* — and then deflected with *"I haven't mentioned it all day."* Twice he moved to ring a bell himself and had to be stopped: once tackled and restrained in the great hall, once grappled prone on the scaffold by Dario at Strength 20 against Eric's 7, while Gemma blanketed the platform in darkness and LeBeefe's voice said *"Yes, yes, stop her. You fool. Stop her."* He remembers all of it perfectly. Asked what was wrong with him, he said: *"I don't know. Couldn't help myself."*

Gemma has told him she will kill him in his sleep. The compulsion went quiet when the Thirteenth Casting broke.

## Chancellor

After the Empire's grip on Sharn broke, the Council seated Eric as **Chancellor** in Esther Crona's former penthouse. A new city charter was, in Dave's words, "designed and fathered by Eric the Cleric, who governs as deftly as anyone could." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small> He inherited a city out of fuel, roughly a month from a food shortage, and — by the Hundreds' arithmetic, delivered by a warforged named Himmie Joffa — forty-seven days from famine. Days earlier, Zilspar Farm had burned with its people boarded inside.

His solution was a bargain he understood perfectly while making it: Locke Pierce would feed Sharn, and in exchange the Hundreds would have military and logistical access to the city's airspace. By morning a capital ship had settled out of the fog over the upper city, hanging without sails, and warforged were handing out fruit in orderly lines. Some of them were armed. Nobody had to be made to obey. Eric told Gemma afterwards that he had handed Pierce the keys, that he knew it was a trick, and that he only hoped they lived long enough to take it back.

Somewhere over the Breeland farms on the flight back from Zilspar, the arithmetic finished itself: the warforged offered food, then the food burned, then they offered more for the price of the sky, and it had been paid that same afternoon — and any one of those hulls could be carrying a Ruby Nova.

## What the balcony cost

On the balcony where Esther Crona died, Eric saw the **LeBeefe Eye** sitting on the mantel and did not take it. Three years later, standing near the same object in the same room as Chancellor, he found that it resonates with his psychic power and sharpens him, and used it to help bleed its rising output off as fire for the city's furnaces. Renn Tal then declared the Eye the property of Sharn and told the engineers they work for her.

## The jail at Korth

Faced with a starving woman chained to a wall who was entirely unconvinced by a rescue, Eric broke the deadlock by telling her the truth without ornament: that he agreed her sister had betrayed her; that the woman in front of her was not Deirdre, because he had helped kill Deirdre himself; that he was Eric, Chancellor of Sharn; and that the Emperor is not a man but a dragon. He said the last of it aloud in front of the Imperial Red Guard, deliberately, to see what would happen. Nothing did. Bantide Moro asked for one thing — a holy symbol of the Silver Flame — and Eric passed Dario's prayer book through the bars.

## The Counting House

In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/), at [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), the sacristy of Eric's bastion, staffed by followers of Matt Matheny who hold that numbers and information must be free and true, crafted him a scroll of *hold person* for two hundred gold. [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/), his deputy in Sharn, was to be asked to put the foundries of Sharn to work making horns.

At the Counting House, still wearing Graham Cracker's face, he was handed to Sub-Auditor Corvan Bleek, who had been in Eric's Exchequer intake twenty years earlier and half-recognised him. In the commissary he recognised [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/), who trained him, signed him into the Hall of Records and wrote the assessment that got him the job: *"a mediocre accountant with an unusual eye."* In her office she handed over her private reconciliation of the Counting House's probates in her own cipher, with an eight-letter key that is what you do when you really love someone. Eric tried MARRIAGE, and Vitt, reading over his shoulder, said *"You cross your sevens. That's very unusual. Where did you learn accounting?"* before telling him it was MULTIPLY. When Director Aurel Kesk came to the door with troopers holding Vitt and demanded a warrant, Eric produced the *hold person* scroll as a warrant signed by the Magister, and Gemma, as Deirdre, sold it. On the way out he gave Bleek a salute of their shared faith and said, *"Be seeing you."* <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

Climbing the Stairs of Her Mercy disguised as an old Black Dove nun, Eric was asked how his faith was holding up and said he cares about maths, not the gods. In the forecourt of Nos Matre the floating object called the Polygon sent a rush of hate through his veins. He cast *detect magic*, found enchantment with transmutation holding it in the air, and judged it a store of immense power that can cast spells: the focus of [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), her version of the thing that floated outside LeBeefe's stronghold. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

> "The strength of Eberron is in its people — not in your weak Empire. All you've managed to do here, Admiral, is bring more unnecessary bloodshed to forestall your own doom."

<small>— Eric the Cleric to Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, on the deck of a rising airship over Thronehold, broadcast live to the whole Empire.</small>

> "We're not looking for a nest of monsters. We're looking for a foundry."

<small>— Eric, overruling the wet crawl on the Grand Stair into the Glitterhame.</small>

## Right now

Eric is on a river boat to the Summer Palace wearing a dead paralegal's face, with the Emperor's own death warrant in his bag and a voice in his head that will not stop lecturing him. The Grand Lodge records are due Monday. Ten bells remain, and the ninth great casting hangs in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace he just walked away from.

- **A charm of *healing word*** — from the fane to Matt Matheny in Leef.
- **Graham Cracker's face and papers** — an elf paralegal of the Ministry of Law. Bantide Moro remembers the original well enough to have remarked, mid-jailbreak, "I thought you were gay, Graham."

**The Passage Mark:** Eric's dragonmark is his escape hatch and his tell. It is the Mark of Passage, a mark tied to no noble house and recognised internationally as a mysterious symbol of authority associated with passage; it was first recorded as a mark of House Orien. Eric is an orphan raised by Imperial monks and has no family name, so officially he would be called Eric Passagemarked. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> It lets him cast phantom steed, which JL described as "Mostly an overland travel spell… but… it does have a speed of 100 ft per round." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-31; 2025-12-14)</small> It broke him out of a grell's tentacles thirty feet above the floor of the Glitterhame after the thing had paralyzed him and begun trying to open him. And at Thronehold it flared with lightning in front of Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, who looked at it and said *"Now I see. Now I see what she's done."* and died without explaining what he meant.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

To keep Sharn fed and free long enough to prove, in a court that does not yet exist, what the Empire did — and to find out what is speaking to him before it uses him.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Ledgers, manifests and personnel rolls
- Harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms in a dead dwarf's shorthand
- Being the only person in the room who has actually read the document
- Tarot spreads before a descent
- The Belt of Dwarvenkind, and the beard it is growing him
- Doors that are not supposed to open
- A Cyrean Red Pomegranate, believed extinct since the Mourning
- Trials, and the idea that there will be some
- Speeches to live cameras

**Dislikes:**

- Nests of monsters, when there is a foundry to find
- Chain ladders — he fell roughly a hundred and fifty feet off one for 43 damage and a sprained ankle
- Strength checks
- Being tackled by his own friends, which keeps happening
- His own Imperial file, which reads "a mediocre accountant"
- The LeBeefe Eye on the mantel
- Being asked how long he has been hearing the voice

**Vices & Personality flaws**

He conceals, and then denies concealing, and remembers every word of it perfectly afterwards.
