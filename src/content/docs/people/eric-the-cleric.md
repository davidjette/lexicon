**Cleric · Chancellor of Sharn · The Inevitables · Level 12 · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Graham Cracker** (Korth cover, taken off a corpse) · **The Nerd** · **Harry Potter** (on the books of the Zilspar Farm-Table Co-op) · one of the **Heroes of Leef** · <small>styled "Chancellor of Char" in one internal note — the live record says Sharn</small>

Eric the Cleric is a cleric of a Karrnathi order of mathematics and order, a former auditor of the Pax Imperium, and Chancellor of the free city of Sharn. He was an orphan forced to learn mathematics and to worship it, served the Empire as an auditor, and lived three years in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records in Korth. In the desert rail-town of Leef he fell in with [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/); the three liberated the town and were named the Heroes of Leef, then went west to Newham and killed Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) as the city collapsed into the New Mourning. Sent into Sharn to steal the Ghost Chip, they fought the war that broke the Empire's hold on the city, and its Council seated Eric as Chancellor in [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s former penthouse. Since the Glitterhame beneath the Korramont he has been hearing the voice of LeBeefe, which nobody else can hear.

He wears metal armour that rings when he brushes a wall, which has woken at least one colony of stirges. On the back of his hand he carries a **dragonmark of Passage** that flares with lightning when he uses it and gave away his identity to a dying admiral. Since attuning a Belt of Dwarvenkind he has been growing a dwarven beard he did not previously have, along with darkvision and a resistance to poison. He is physically feeble — Strength 7, an unarmed strike that does nothing — and carries a tarot deck for divination. Height, colouring and eyes: **Unknown**.

**His clerical order** is one of mathematics and order, which is less strange in Karrnath than it sounds. In a country whose state religion is bookkeeping applied to the dead — where the very old walk willingly into the Hall of Submission to begin their second service, where a death sentence specifies both the duration of pain and the length of service owed afterward in undeath, and where sentences are scaled by race against expected lifespan — a priesthood of arithmetic is a civil institution as much as a faith. It made Eric an Imperial auditor. It also made him the only person in the rebellion who could read an Imperial ledger and know which line was the lie, and the only one who looked at a dead dwarf king's bell-forging folio and recognised harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms on sight.

**In public** Eric is Chancellor of Sharn, seated by the Council in Esther Crona's former penthouse in the Veil Building, and he speaks well: at Thronehold he stood in front of the Empire's own cameras and called for the Empire to be outlawed, with Sanctuary up. In Korth he is Graham Cracker, a paralegal, a younger elf, an orphan, a Korthian old-town boy — a man whose whole function is to carry the preliminary material. **In private** he is a self-described mediocre accountant who spent three years in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records, whose own file in the Imperial Navy's hands says exactly that, and who is currently hearing a voice that no one else can hear. He concealed the whispers from his own party for four sessions of hard travel. He argued in the open for ringing the Thirteenth Bell and then denied having raised it at all. Twice he had to be physically restrained from ringing a bell himself. He is aware of all of this and has offered to be tied up.

Eric is the party's opener of things that will not open — a membrane-like door in the Dhakaani tunnels beneath Sharn, the Rite of Recall at the Forge of Binding that called up Varyn Crona's echo, an Iron Door that answered "shave and a haircut" by saying their names back. He works with Renn Tal, Jessica Grimpledink, Digma Beeve and the Council of Sharn. His heirloom is not his: the **Belt of Dwarvenkind** he wears was robbed from a sealed sepulcher, identified at weapons' point as the property of Madame Tso Teryaki's late husband, taken back — and then given to him freely, by her, after Dario carried her king's daughter home.

**The secret:** the voice is John C. LeBeefe, who Eric helped kill in Newham, and it did not stop when the fungal bloom was purged out of him. The compulsion to ring went quiet when the Thirteenth Casting broke. LeBeefe did not. On the walk out of the mountain he delivered a lecture Eric has told exactly one person: that Emperor Kaius is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun, kept alive as a vessel and fed dragon blood to strengthen the bloodline.

He believes that a city is a ledger and that the arithmetic will tell you what is true before anyone confesses it. He believes that whoever rings the bell controls the magic — an argument he made aloud, twice, and then denied making. He believes he has already handed [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) the keys to Sharn, that it was a trick, and that the only hope left is surviving Korth in time to take them back.

**In the chair** Eric governs by negotiation and by exposure. He inherited a city out of fuel, a month from severe shortages, and by the Hundreds' own arithmetic forty-seven days from famine, and he paid for bread with airspace. He found that the Eye of Corruption in Esther's penthouse resonates with his psychic power and sharpens him, and used that borrowed clarity to help the Nest's engineers bleed its output off as ordinary fire under the city's furnaces. He hides by being underestimated, and he tests his cover by telling the truth in front of armed men to see whether anybody twitches.

**Origin.** Eric was, by his own account, an orphan forced to learn mathematics and to worship it. He served the Pax Imperium as an auditor and spent three years living in a bunker beneath the Hall of Records. His memory carries flashes from his time around John C. LeBeefe, including a tune out of the Black Palace that he heard again years later behind a membrane door under Sharn. In the desert rail-town of Leef he fell in with Gemma Corso and Dario Argentino; the three of them liberated the town with Thadric Rourke, Zero of Leef and Princess Orange Chicken, and were named the Heroes of Leef. They went west to Newham, united Digma Beeve's scattered cells, and killed LeBeefe — and the city collapsed into the New Mourning behind them. Sent on to Sharn to steal the Ghost Chip, they met [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and never left the war. Digma Beeve holds Eric's comptrollership of the Republic of Talenta in his absence.

## The whispers

In the **Glitterhame** beneath the Korramont, the fungal bloom spoke to Eric with a clear voice — *"Have you come to deliver the message… as my lord sent you?"* — and he answered it aloud, failed the save, and felt something look out through his eyes. The whispers had already begun at the head of the Grand Stair and never stopped. They were aimed at him alone and they said: *Deliver the message.*

He concealed them for the whole descent. He advocated ringing the bell in the sepulcher gallery. He argued for it again in the open in front of the party — *"whoever rings the bell, they control the magic"* — and then deflected with *"I haven't mentioned it all day."* Twice he moved to ring a bell himself and had to be stopped: once tackled and restrained in the great hall, once grappled prone on the scaffold by Dario at Strength 20 against Eric's 7, while Gemma blanketed the platform in darkness and LeBeefe's voice said *"Yes, yes, stop her. You fool. Stop her."* He remembers all of it perfectly. Asked what was wrong with him, he said: *"I don't know. Couldn't help myself."*

Gemma has told him she will kill him in his sleep. The compulsion went quiet when the Thirteenth Casting broke.

## Chancellor

After the Empire's grip on Sharn broke, the Council seated Eric as **Chancellor** in Esther Crona's former penthouse. He inherited a city out of fuel, roughly a month from a food shortage, and — by the Hundreds' arithmetic, delivered by a warforged named Himmie Joffa — forty-seven days from famine. Days earlier, Zilspar Farm had burned with its people boarded inside.

His solution was a bargain he understood perfectly while making it: Locke Pierce would feed Sharn, and in exchange the Hundreds would have military and logistical access to the city's airspace. By morning a capital ship had settled out of the fog over the upper city, hanging without sails, and warforged were handing out fruit in orderly lines. Some of them were armed. Nobody had to be made to obey. Eric told Gemma afterwards that he had handed Pierce the keys, that he knew it was a trick, and that he only hoped they lived long enough to take it back.

Somewhere over the Breeland farms on the flight back from Zilspar, the arithmetic finished itself: the warforged offered food, then the food burned, then they offered more for the price of the sky, and it had been paid that same afternoon — and any one of those hulls could be carrying a Ruby Nova.

## What the balcony cost

On the balcony where Esther Crona died, Eric saw the **LeBeefe Eye** sitting on the mantel and did not take it. Three years later, standing near the same object in the same room as Chancellor, he found that it resonates with his psychic power and sharpens him, and used it to help bleed its rising output off as fire for the city's furnaces. Renn Tal then declared the Eye the property of Sharn and told the engineers they work for her.

## The jail at Korth

Faced with a starving woman chained to a wall who was entirely unconvinced by a rescue, Eric broke the deadlock by telling her the truth without ornament: that he agreed her sister had betrayed her; that the woman in front of her was not Deirdre, because he had helped kill Deirdre himself; that he was Eric, Chancellor of Sharn; and that the Emperor is not a man but a dragon. He said the last of it aloud in front of the Imperial Red Guard, deliberately, to see what would happen. Nothing did. Bantide Moro asked for one thing — a holy symbol of the Silver Flame — and Eric passed Dario's prayer book through the bars.

> "The strength of Eberron is in its people — not in your weak Empire. All you've managed to do here, Admiral, is bring more unnecessary bloodshed to forestall your own doom."

<small>— Eric the Cleric to Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, on the deck of a rising airship over Thronehold, broadcast live to the whole Empire.</small>

> "We're not looking for a nest of monsters. We're looking for a foundry."

<small>— Eric, overruling the wet crawl on the Grand Stair into the Glitterhame.</small>

## Right now

Eric is on a river boat to the Summer Palace wearing a dead paralegal's face, with the Emperor's own death warrant in his bag and a voice in his head that will not stop lecturing him. The Grand Lodge records are due Monday. Ten bells remain, and the ninth great casting hangs in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace he just walked away from.

**Holdings:** the penthouse at the top of the Veil Building in Sharn, formerly Esther Crona's, now the Chancellor's residence and the seat of the Council · the deeds to Hopper's Magic Emporium and the Arcade in the Lower City, taken off Edwin Graves' estate and traded into a standing supply arrangement for the rebellion · a bunker beneath the Hall of Records in Korth, lived in for three years.

**Items:**

- **The Emperor's death warrant** — a writ of warrant signed KAIUS III ordering the execution of King Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Deeptempura, Sister Calvane and every dwarf in the Korranberg foundry, ending with Overseer Velen Marsk, who countersigned to receive it. Most names crossed out. Recovered from Marsk's satchel; Eric is keeping it, in his words, for the trials that follow.
- **The Belt of Dwarvenkind** — robbed from a sepulcher, confiscated, then returned to him as a gift by Madame Tso Teryaki. Attuned.
- **A Ring of Spell Storing** loaded with *Summon Dragon* by Hopper, on the understanding that a true name calls that dragon for one hour and then frees it. It is meant for Valtrex.
- **A scroll of Greater Silence** from Renn Tal, given on the theory that amplified by enough casters it could disrupt the bells' resonance. He accepted it knowing he would have to grow into it.
- **A tarot deck** — used for augury. The five-card spread before the descent read Tower, Moon, Devil, Hanged Man, Judgement, and the spell answered weal and woe.
- **An adamantine mace** — the weapon that broke the collar off a black dragon's neck · a wand of magic missile built at cost by Doppler Klink · an amulet of proof against detection and location, unattuned, taken off Edwin Graves.
- **Graham Cracker's face and papers** — an elf paralegal of the Ministry of Law. Bantide Moro remembers the original well enough to have remarked, mid-jailbreak, "I thought you were gay, Graham."

**The Passage Mark:** Eric's dragonmark is his escape hatch and his tell. It broke him out of a grell's tentacles thirty feet above the floor of the Glitterhame after the thing had paralyzed him and begun trying to open him. And at Thronehold it flared with lightning in front of Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, who looked at it and said *"Now I see. Now I see what she's done."* and died without explaining what he meant.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

To keep Sharn fed and free long enough to prove, in a court that does not yet exist, what the Empire did — and to find out what is speaking to him before it uses him.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Ledgers, manifests and personnel rolls
- Harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms in a dead dwarf's shorthand
- Being the only person in the room who has actually read the document
- Tarot spreads before a descent
- The Belt of Dwarvenkind, and the beard it is growing him
- Doors that are not supposed to open
- A Cyrean Red Pomegranate, believed extinct since the Mourning
- Trials, and the idea that there will be some
- Speeches to live cameras

**Dislikes:**

- Nests of monsters, when there is a foundry to find
- Chain ladders — he fell roughly a hundred and fifty feet off one for 43 damage and a sprained ankle
- Strength checks
- Being tackled by his own friends, which keeps happening
- His own Imperial file, which reads "a mediocre accountant"
- The LeBeefe Eye on the mantel
- Being asked how long he has been hearing the voice

**Vices & Personality flaws**

He conceals, and then denies concealing, and remembers every word of it perfectly afterwards.
