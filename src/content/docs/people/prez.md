**Tomcat · Rogue · [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s gang · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Alive**

**Also known as:** President James A Garfield

Prez is a thief in [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s gang in the [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), "Tom Cat", a failed conman and mediocre burglar who owes Sharegrave 600 gold. Sent to spy on [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/), he was discovered and captured, and the party rescued him from Rotten William's hideout in the Spillway. Back on [the Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/), Sharegrave suspected him of turning coat. He directed the party's effort at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/) and went with them to [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/).

## Spying on Rotten William

Sharegrave sent Prez to spy on Rotten William and possibly to send him a message. He was discovered and captured. While a prisoner he overheard [Pharod](/people/pharod/) accepting bodies for disposal, "no questions asked", in a deal arranged by Rotten William: the likely route for the disposal of murder victims throughout the city. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party invaded Rotten William's hideout in the Spillway and rescued "PREZ a thief in Sharegrave's gang". <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## Back on the Catwalk

Sharegrave had thought Prez was dead, and now suspected him of turning coat. <small>(DM notes)</small> On the Catwalk's intake level, Prez waves off the Tabaxi thugs who stare at newcomers, and a Tabaxi pickpocket greets him:

> "Hey Prez who are your pig friends? Make sure they wipe their feet. Can't have berks tracking shit all over..."

<small>(DM notes)</small>

Prez is about 85% loyal to Sharegrave, but would help the party, and "Knows more than he lets on". <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave sent him to show the party how to escape the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Grindhouse

At Battlebeast's prize fight in the Grindhouse, Prez directed the party's search of the crowd for the caster operating the rigged golem. Rotten William's thugs were to confront him in the first round. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Fortune's Wheel

Sharegrave sent Prez with the party to Fortune's Wheel to deal with Eaton the acrobat, who had the "Cabinet of Mysteries". <small>(DM notes)</small> At the party's next downtime, Sharegrave and Prez were among the contacts they could return to. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Skills:** A common rogue, with a talent for "2nd Story work". <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Contacts & Relations:**
- [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/): his boss; Prez owes him 600 gold.
- [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/): captured him.
- [Pharod](/people/pharod/): overheard taking bodies for disposal.
