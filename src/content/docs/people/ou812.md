**Species unknown · Servant of the Red Eye · [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/) · Status unknown**

**OU812** is a servant of the Red Eye, [Nine](/people/nine/), in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), run by JL. Aboard the SSJ Vanguard he brought the Red Eye's orders to the new recruits [Umwelt](/people/umwelt/) and [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/), was with them for the seizure of [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/), and led the expedition to [Necis](/places/necis/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The Vanguard

The recruits had been told only to wait in a room, and did not know each other. OU812 came in with news:

> Brothers, I have good news and bad news. Which one would you like to hear first? I lied. There is no good news.

<small>— OU812, aboard the Vanguard.</small>

The Inevitable had died on the moon of Toril in a showdown with [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/); OU812 allowed that he had been unpleasant but called him a valuable asset to the cause. The Red Eye, "our lord and savior", had asked him to read the recruits a message and to give them a leather pouch holding two rings, of *zone of truth* and of *command*. Their orders were to find out what the ship's commanding officers knew about the Lexicon. See [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Necis

When the Terrasque jumped to the coordinates of Necis there was nothing there but rubble, and OU812 explained that the planet was supposed to be there. He said he would have to bring the Red Eye back, by a ritual with a marble, and hoped the Red Eye would not be too cross. At Necis he assigned the tombs: [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) to the Pyramid, [Nine](/people/nine/) and himself to the Jade Temple, Marcel and Weld to the Labyrinth. He gave them sending stones and reminded them that this was their destiny. See [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

He gave Weld a golden orb, which Nine called a seed. No one knew what it did. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Relationships

- **The Red Eye:** his lord, whose messages he carries: [Nine](/people/nine/), who took the name Red Eye from Barrias. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-the-red-eye.md)</small>
- **Umwelt** and **Marcel Martel:** the recruits he briefed on the Vanguard.
- **Nine:** with whom he explored the Jade Temple at Necis.
