**Warforged · Barbarian 5 (Path of the Wild Heart) · Crew, Istus Cruiser · Children of the Red Eye · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Weld

**Umwelt**, called **Weld**, is a warforged barbarian of the Path of the Wild Heart and a devotee of the Red Eye in [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by Dave. A liberated farming and ranch-hand unit, he joined the robot resistance of [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/) and serves as crew aboard the Istus Cruiser with [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/). He later met up with [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) and [Paytra](/people/paytra/).

## Background

Weld is a warforged farming and ranch-hand unit that was liberated and joined the resistance. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small> His background is that of a farmer, his alignment Chaotic Good, and his faith is given on his sheet as "Red Eye Revolutionary". <small>(character sheet)</small>

His cloak is his only clothing. His chest carries engravings and mechanisms. He is shown with his head cocked slightly upward, watching his flock or crops and dreaming of the future. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-08)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

The new crew of *The Starsong Awakens* after [Brownsleeve City Nights](/history/brownsleeve-city-nights/) were devotees of the Red Eye, the nom de guerre the robot resistance uses for [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-10-26; sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> They serve as crew on the Istus Cruiser seen in the epilogue of the Brownsleeve City Nights finale, played on 1 November 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

Weld and Marcel Martel first appeared on 8 November 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-08)</small>

By May 2025, according to Nico, Kol and Paytra had brought the body of [Caprica](/people/caprica/) back to [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/) and met up with Marcel and Weld. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

His sheet's notes record the words "Paradox Dawn", "shut down", "necis" and "secured by OSRIIG". <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Weld is a fifth-level barbarian of the Path of the Wild Heart. He can cast *beast sense* and *speak with animals* as rituals, and with smith's and carpenter's tools he is the perfect rancher. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02; character sheet)</small> As a warforged he does not eat, drink, breathe or sleep, is immune to disease, resists poison, and rests in an inactive, watchful state. He has the Durable and Tough feats, and speaks Common and Gnomish. <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:**
- **Living Gloves (smith's tools):** gloves that make him a welding machine. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-06)</small>
- **Sickle armblade:** an armblade in the form of a sickle. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-06)</small>
- **Herdenfrieden:** a versatile bludgeoning weapon with six charges that casts *calm emotions*.
- **Maul**, quarterstaff and two javelins.
- **Farm and field gear:** carpenter's tools, a shovel, rope, string, a crowbar, a grappling hook, a hooded lantern and ten flasks of oil.
- **A book** and a dragonchess set.

<small>(character sheet)</small>

## Relationships

- **Marcel Martel:** fellow devotee of the Red Eye and crewmate aboard the Istus Cruiser.
- **Kol Hanaka** and **Paytra:** the rebuilt Bureau agents whom he and Marcel met up with.
