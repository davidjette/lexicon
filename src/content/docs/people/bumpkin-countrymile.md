**Human · Gambler · Guest at [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/)**

Bumpkin Countrymile is a human staying at [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/), the casino in [Sigil](/places/sigil/), where he is losing a [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) fortune at the gambling tables. His family discovered moonstone on their land and used it to travel and buy treasures, among them a magic playing card, the RUIN card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/), which he lost to [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/). He will sell his plane ticket home to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), and he tells [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) where RUIN is.

## Description

Bumpkin is a "Human Simp" and a "Sad Sack". <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The moonstone fortune

His family discovered moonstone on their land, and used it to travel and buy treasures. These included many magic shoes and garments, and a magic playing card with a picture of a crumbling sword and crown: RUIN. He bought the card from Spam Risk. <small>(DM notes)</small>

He lost the card to a thief, "a beautiful seductress who robbed him": Griddy Champagne. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## At Fortune's Wheel

Bumpkin was invited to stay at Fortune's Wheel, and is losing his fortune at its tables. The casino does not know he is almost broke. <small>(DM notes)</small> One portal at Fortune's Wheel is a book of tiny monsters with Bumpkin Countrymile. <small>(DM notes)</small>

He will sell his plane ticket home to Waterdeep for 25 to 100 gold. <small>(DM notes)</small> Bumpkin not only tells Nicollo where RUIN is, but sells him a plane ticket to Waterdeep. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Card:** Fool <small>(DM notes)</small>
