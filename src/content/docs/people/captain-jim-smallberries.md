**Human · Bard 11 (College of Eloquence) · Captain · [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) · Alive**

*Also known as:* James X. Smallberries · Jim · Starberries · Charles Largeberries · Charles Longberries · Jimbo

**Captain Jim Smallberries** is a human bard, played by JL, who commanded the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). His full name is James X. Smallberries. As a young officer he fought in the [Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/) and was left stranded on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/). Captain of a ship confined to a single system, he collected his new pilot [Caprica](/people/caprica/) at Northspire, and after hard news raised the prospect of his retirement he, Caprica and [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) severed themselves from Astral Command to seek [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) on [Necis](/places/necis/). He lost his ship and many of his friends in the finale. Fifteen years later, in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, he lives under the name Charles Largeberries on a mining asteroid, wanted for dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property. The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier records him under the alias Charles Longberries, sentenced in absentia by a military tribunal, and of vital interest to its Board because of Caprica.

## Background

JL took the name from one of the villains of *Buckaroo Banzai*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-25)</small> The middle initial X was uncertain when the crew roster was first drawn up. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-13)</small> Dave's early picture of him was of an officer who might have served on missions in the Core systems before being assigned to the boondocks, where he was riding it out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

### The Academy

In the Academy flashbacks of April 2023, the young Smallberries appeared with his contemporaries Catrina ([Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/)) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/). JL, running those episodes, gave Smallberries as a one-star captain and Catrina as a two-star captain who was "technically now the captain of the Zanzibar". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01)</small> Katrina outranked Jim on paper while Jim commanded the ship.

### The Beholder War

Jim's origin was told in a three-episode flashback in 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24, 2024-03-16)</small> The Beholder War began when a beholder ship crashed deliberately into Neverwinter and the whole Academy was sent to war. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> The junior officers of the flight crew of the SSJ Balduran, on their first assignment, answered a beholder invasion while Jim was in hypersleep, training for the mobile infantry. His Force Rifle, standard issue once he made planetfall, works as a rod of eldritch blast. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

After the Black Hole Sun, the first use of [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/), Jim was stranded on Negroni Prime with [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), the boy [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/) and a dozen civilians. Jim knew Katrina had been aboard the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> Jim, Barrias and Tillamook were the only ones with guns. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-15)</small> Katrina and Horgus left the group without transport or communication to recover the Demoncore, taking the artillery robot [1.0](/people/1-0/), though Katrina gave Jim a sending stone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> Jim believed Tillamook had died at Negroni Prime; the boy survived. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

After he last saw Katrina and Horgus, Jim's memories were altered with a potion of modify memory, and any knowledge he gained of the Demoncore was probably erased by [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/). Dave tied the divergence of Katrina and Horgus from Jim to Astral Command's own divergence from its righteous path, and to Jim's disillusionment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### Captain of the Zanzibar

The crew roster lists him as **SOVEREIGN OFFICER, Captain James X. Smallberries** in the Command Corps. <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> He started the campaign at level 5. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-29)</small> When he took command he had his quarters expanded by removing a research station, and in place of a photon cannon or deflector array he chose to turn the sub-bridge room into a private hangar for a personal craft, which fits two comfortably and three tightly. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-17, 2022-10-18)</small> His secret item can serve as a *dominate monster* or *mass suggestion* spell, depending on how it is dispersed, with 1d6 charges. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

The Zanzibar had no one able to run its Major Helm and was confined to the space of Tralfamadore III. Jim was supposed to collect Caprica, the new pilot, a month before she reached the seaside village of Northspire on a one-line message from a "potentially drunk Captain Smallberries". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> He did not know why she had chosen his ship, and might suspect she was after his command. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small> Dave saw their first episodes, in October 2022, as giving the two a basis for their relationship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-22)</small>

In the [mind flayer crisis](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) of November 2022, [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) used his monk abilities to overpower the captain, and Caprica took command and saved him. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-19, 2022-11-27)</small> On a small paradise world in December 2022, Jim ordered the Zanzibar to leave, but passed on his duress code with a successful Charisma save, and the crew turned hostile to rescue him. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-18)</small> A thread between Smallberries and [Princess Xedalli](/people/princess-xedalli/), Rightful Empress of Xaryxia, set off the events of the Drumhead episode in January 2023. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-21)</small> JL said of his captain in April 2023 that Jim cared deeply about the Xaryxis mission and refused to let it fail. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-14)</small>

### Going rogue

The season premiere of 2023-06-09 was almost all Jim and Caprica. Jim took hard news badly, and his possible retirement created tension between Astral Command and the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-09, 2023-06-10)</small> In November 2023 the Voodoo Boys hacked Jim's brain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

In December 2023 Jim, Caprica and Sandy were on the remote planet of Necis 1, and in January 2024 the three set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command. Dave foretold that it would make them the greatest officers in Astral Command's thousand-year history, whether they lived or died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22, 2024-01-06)</small> On the [Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) across the sea of grass Jim fought as control and utility beside Caprica and Sandy. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-10)</small> By April 2024 Jim and his crew had left their roles as Astral Command officers behind and gone off as an independent force. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

Jim reached level 10 in May 2024 and gained Magical Secrets. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-03)</small> Before the finale his Barrier Tattoo was upgraded, without his knowledge, when Horgus upgraded his own and Katrina's, raising Jim's AC from 13 to 17. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

### The finale

In the finale of 2024-05-31 the Zanzibar took critical damage and Caprica died. Wishes shaped the ending: the third wish of [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) had scattered the crew without warning, and [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/) cast a final Wish. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-02, 2024-06-09)</small> Dave summed up Jim's story afterwards as a tragic one: a pure soul in a world that took advantage of his good nature, betrayed by his closest friends and his government, lifted from rock bottom by Caprica and his crew, nearly retired for stopping a great evil, gone rogue to discover a greater one, passed through the veil, and, having lost many of his friends and his ship, ended back where he started. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

In *The Starsong Awakens*, which JL ran, fifteen years have passed and Jim is an old man. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> He lives under the assumed name Charles Largeberries on a small mining asteroid, works a daily grind in the mines, is usually drunk, and has not piloted a vessel in more than a decade. He is wanted on multiple felony counts of dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property, which could carry fifty years in a military prison. JL's account ends: "Sad and lonely, Jim has returned to the life he longed to escape as a boy, trapped on a desolate rock." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

### The Company dossier

The Company's dossier lists James X. Smallberries as alive, located in Scrospace, with the alias Charles Longberries. It states that Captain Smallberries was tried in absentia by a military tribunal and sentenced to 20 years in a Sovereign penitentiary for 7 counts of dereliction of duty and theft of Astral Command property. His location remains unknown to Astral Command, but Company agents tracked him to a Scrospace mining colony. The dossier describes his relationship with [Caprica](/people/caprica/), whom it lists as deceased, as what makes him a vital interest to the Board of Directors. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> JL's account of the arc gives the charges as multiple felony counts of dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property, which could carry fifty years in a military prison, and his alias as Charles Largeberries. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Jim's sheet makes him a human Bard 11 of the College of Eloquence with the Wildspacer background and 86,246 XP. He has 69 hit points and AC 17, speaks Common and Elvish, and casts with Charisma at spell save DC 17 and attack +9. His features include Bardic Inspiration, Silver Tongue, Unsettling Words, Unfailing Inspiration, Universal Speech, Countercharm and Magical Secrets. <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:** Ioun Stone of Reserve, Barrier Tattoo (rare) and [Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), all attuned; leather armor, rapier and dagger; fine clothes, perfume and a map case. <small>(character sheet)</small> His Force Rifle works as a rod of eldritch blast, and a feat gives him the eldritch blast cantrip. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> His private craft is kept in the captain's hangar of the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>
