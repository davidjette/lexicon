**Doctor, shopkeeper, corporate owner · BioTec (secret proprietor) / Circuit City · Dead**

*Also known as:* The Cog Collector · E.G. (on Lower City leases) · "Edwin" — the only word Martin Kross could produce once the chip came out

Dr. Edwin Graves was a doctor and shopkeeper of Sharn's Lower City, the public proprietor of [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) on Gear Street, the secret owner of [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/), and the killer the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) called the **Cog Collector**. He controlled BioTec through shell companies and inherited assets, including the estate of [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/), and for over four years he abducted Cogborn labourers, experimented on them in a laboratory hidden behind his shop, and shipped their altered and [Umbra](/items/umbra/)-prepped bodies up a private cargo skylift to the Black Dove hospital as the body supply for [Project EDEN](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/). He implanted chips in his employees that made his name physically unsayable. He died in his own shop when the Trio pushed through the locked front door, and the six Cogborn found alive in his cages were handed to the RUST Runners.

## Physical Description

Doctor Edwin Graves was a quiet, impeccably mannered businessman whose presence in the Lower City was so understated it bordered on invisible. His storefront was clean, his paperwork immaculate, and his demeanor consistently calm. He did not raise his voice and was never observed to lose composure, including at the moment he locked the front door of his own shop behind the armed strangers who had just walked in, and walked calmly into the back.

## The Trade He Fronted

Circuit City is the largest building on Gear Street, in the busiest commercial artery of the Lower City, and it is known citywide for chip implants and refurbished BioTec equipment. Its customers are the workers of the Cogs and the Lowline Ward — the people for whom an [implant](/lore/implants-and-chip-enhancement/) is the difference between employment and destitution. Known publicly as the owner of Circuit City Chip Enhancement Parlor, Graves cultivated a reputation for professionalism, rare biotech inventory, and a polite distance from neighborhood politics, and he operated for years without drawing suspicion from the communities he preyed upon.

## Public Face and Private Truth

Publicly: the owner of a chip parlour. A doctor, a ratepayer, a landlord, a man who nodded to people in Gear Street Market.

Privately: behind this façade, Graves lived a second life. He was the secret owner of BioTec, controlling the corporation through shell companies and inherited assets, including the estate of Cob Cornwell. From this position he directed the body-supply pipeline for Project EDEN, using Circuit City as the entry point to a hidden laboratory behind the shop. The Cogborn whispered of a killer they called the Cog Collector; none knew the culprit was the quiet man who walked past them each day in Gear Street Market.

## The Secret

Hidden behind Circuit City was a laboratory of Horrors, with a large cage of abducted Cogborn, medical tables, monstrous experiments, and a corpse-delivery system feeding BioTec's research. For over four years he abducted Cogborn laborers, experimented on them, harvested their organs, and shipped their altered and Umbra-prepped bodies to the Black Dove hospital in the upper city. They went up by the private cargo skylift on Circuit City's covered rooftop; the skylift is still programmed to run that route. The building also has a hidden back exit into the Grand Aqueduct.

## Competence and Circle

Graves' influence extended far beyond his storefront. He owned multiple properties in the Lower City, including the Trio's apartment building, using these holdings to maintain control and monitor the district. His automated Arcade, his financial reach, and his surgical precision allowed him to operate without witnesses, accomplices, or loose ends. <small>The Arcade is in the Sub-line District, next door to Hopper's.</small> He never appeared on a document as himself. The only person of rank who could plausibly have named him was Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), who ran his laboratory's flagship programme and is nowhere recorded discussing him.

## Conduct in Role

He implanted **obedience-blocking chips** in BioTec employees and in [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), preventing them from speaking his name or revealing his involvement. When Nora was asked directly who supplied the corpses she seized, clutched the side of her head near the ear, and bled from the nose. When [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) tried to name the private owner in the Underhive, he could not. Kross had to have the chip cut out before he could speak; Nora's was never removed.

## Origin

Graves' life before Circuit City is not recorded. He appears already assembled: the title of doctor, the corporate control, the inherited estate of Cob Cornwell, the property portfolio. The Cogborn disappearances ran for over four years across the Lower City, long enough to become folklore, a bogeyman with a name and no face, and long enough that the community had stopped expecting anyone above the Aqueduct to care.

In that time Graves was also the landlord of the building the Inevitables would later occupy — a unit whose previous tenants, Tony and Tidy Danza, had prepaid a six-year lease with an owner recorded only as **E.G.** and then died there in a manner nobody ever explained. Their ghosts were still in it. Among the flyers left behind in the apartment was an advertisement for Circuit City.

## The Fall of Circuit City

His downfall came when the Trio uncovered the truth. It began with a body that fell from a service lift in Gear Street Market. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) performed a Divination ritual and it pointed at Circuit City. Inside, the owner took a suspicious interest in their implants, locked the front door and disappeared into the back; they pushed through and found the backroom complex, the experiments and the cages.

The fight that followed was against Graves and his creations. It ended with Graves' death, exposing the full extent of his crimes and severing the central link between BioTec, the Cogborn disappearances, and Project EDEN.

## Right Now

He is dead, and the six Cogborn found alive in his cages were handed to the RUST Runners. What survives him is a paper trail: bank records, property deeds, 800 gold, and an [ArcEye Crystallizer Lens](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) that turned out to be the key to the whole rising.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation** — Supply. He had a contract to fill and a district full of people nobody counted.

**Likes & Dislikes** — Likes: clean paperwork · rare biotech inventory · property · automated systems that need no staff · being nodded to in the market · surgical precision · the polite distance of a good shopkeeper. Dislikes: witnesses · accomplices · loose ends · neighbourhood politics · strangers with implants he did not fit.

**Vices & Personality flaws** — He could not resist inspecting other people's hardware, and it is what tipped him into locking the door.

## Social

- **Dr. Joanne Menka** — head Hybrid Specialist at the company he secretly owned. Executed after the rising.
- **Sister Nora** — Black Dove cleric, chipped by him to keep his name out of her mouth. Executed after the rising.
- **Martin Kross** — his employee, chipped, who named him the moment the chip came out. Alive, in Gorgonhorn.
- **Cob Cornwell** — whose estate gave him his hold on BioTec.
- **The Cogborn of the Cogs** — his customers, his tenants, and his raw material.
- **Tony and Tidy Danza** — tenants of his who prepaid six years and died in the flat. Still there, after a fashion.

**Organizations / Groups:** BioTec (secret private owner) · Circuit City (proprietor) · Project E.D.E.N. (body supply)

**Holdings:** Circuit City, Gear Street · the Arcade, Sub-line District · residential buildings across the Lower City · the estate of Cob Cornwell · a cargo skylift running to the Black Dove Hospital

**Items:** An ArcEye Crystallizer Lens, held in stock as refurbished BioTec equipment, recovered from his shop after his death and used to extract Grady Marsh's memory · an amulet of proof against detection and location, taken from him when he died and carried since, unattuned, in Eric's pack.

**Signature Capability — The Block:** Surgical suppression of testimony. Anyone who worked for him carried a chip that made his name physically unsayable. Dario had to cut the chip out of Martin Kross before the rebellion learned a first name.
