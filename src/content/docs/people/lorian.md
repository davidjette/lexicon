**Paladin of the Silver Flame, Oath of Vengeance · The Nest · The Inevitables · Level 5 · Alive**

*Also known as:* no aliases recorded.

**Lorian** is a young paladin of the Silver Flame under the Oath of Vengeance, an intelligence asset of [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) and the newest of the Inevitables — the only one who came to the rebellion as a rescue rather than a recruit. He was living in Korth when [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) approached him posing as a wealthy patron with a prestigious dancing job on offer; instead he was trafficked to Sharn and delivered to [Faith](/people/faith/) and Malrik Zeir, and spent months drugged, exploited and moved between the Zeir penthouse and The Vault Below. Gemma, [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) found him in a holding cell beneath the lower-city club, one of the few captives still alive.

He was taken to the Zilspar farms, where he spoke openly with Zero-One about what had been done to him and about his desire for vengeance; she guided him toward the Silver Flame, urging him to let purpose rather than hatred shape his path, and he began training as a paladin. Once stabilized he was brought to The Nest, where Jessica Grimpledink and Izaak worked closely with him, gathering intelligence on the Zeirs' operations, staff and hidden architecture — his firsthand knowledge of both Vault clubs and the Zeir residence became one of The Nest's most valuable assets, and the strike that ended the Children of Ember was planned on it. He fought alongside the trio during the assault on Malrik's residence, fought at Thronehold and in the running fight over the water, and stayed behind in Sharn on rebel intelligence when the rest of the party went to Korth.

**Status:** Alive.

## Physical description

No source describes him. The record says only that he was young. He now wears the Flame and rides a light-brown palomino called **Tira**, summoned rather than stabled. Species, height, hair, eyes and skin are all **Unknown**. The one temperamental note on file is from a Silver Flame cleric who nursed him: *hot-headed*.

## The Nest

The Nest took him in when he had nothing else. It is the clandestine stronghold of Sharn's rebellion, hidden beneath Skyroot Park in the Upper City in the shell of abandoned lightning rail Station 5, dismantled nine years ago after catastrophic failures and rebuilt into a base by the survivors of that collapse. Jessica Grimpledink, once an engineer of the Sub-Transit and now the cell's de facto leader, designed most of its defences. The Nest coordinates intelligence, plans strikes, and keeps covert lines open to Fulcrum in the Breeland farmsteads.

## The Vault Below

He was held beneath **The Vault Underground** by Faith and Malrik Zeir, in the cell the household called the Pantry, off the kitchen — drugged, isolated, and used by Malrik and by the recruiters. What he saw there became the rebellion's best intelligence on the Zeir operation: the ritual room beneath the residence's main floor, the four wealthy recruiters and Hallorn bringing in new victims, and the route down to Faith's office on the club's lowest level. Every masked cultist of the Children of Ember had once been Malrik's cattle, groomed and reshaped by Faith; Lorian is one of the very few who came out the other way. The strike that ended them was planned on his word.

At the Zilspar farms he told Zero-One all of it and told her plainly that he wanted vengeance. She did not talk him out of it. She told him to let purpose rather than hatred shape the path, and pointed him at the Flame. He took the Oath of Vengeance.

## Thronehold

At the Peace Summit he attended as a representative of Sharn and Leef alongside Gemma, in disguise, and Eric. When Sister Maelis Dorn's accusation broke the hall and Vex d'Lyrandar rose into the air wrapped in lightning, the masked Imperial guards — [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s undead — turned on the Admiral, and the trap was hers rather than Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar's coup.

Lorian touched Eric's shoulder, told him he had his back, and cast *Protection from Good and Evil* on him rather than putting a sword into anyone. Then he summoned Tira, pulled Eric onto the saddle, and ran through the collapsing hall. Aboard Locke Pierce's rising ship he fought Vex on the open deck over the water while the storm tore past, and was nearly killed twice. He was carried down to medical when the ship crossed into the Mournlands, healed, and fitted with a mask to survive the air.

> "I got you. I got your back, man."

<small>— Lorian, touching Eric's shoulder in the great hall at Thronehold as the Admiral called for his death, having just been offered a seat on the peace council by anyone willing to take it.</small>

> "No — whatever you want to do, I got your back."

<small>— Lorian, when Eric said he had expected him to take the offer.</small>

## Left behind

He stayed at Mt. Silicon to recover while Locke Pierce took Eric and Gemma up to the bridge. He came back to Sharn with them, and when the trio decided to go under cover into Korth he remained in Sharn — reluctantly, the record says — to assist Jessica and coordinate rebel intelligence. He has not been with the party since. He was level 5 when they left and they have reached 12 without him. What he has been doing since, and whether anyone has told him what happened at Zilspar, is not recorded.

## Right now

Lorian is in Sharn working rebel intelligence with Jessica Grimpledink. A capital ship of the Hundreds hangs over the upper city handing out fruit in orderly lines. Ten bells remain. The party's deadline at the Grand Lodge is Monday.

**Holdings:** none. He was trafficked into this city and has lived at the Nest and on the Zilspar farms since; the only room in Sharn that was ever his was a cell off a kitchen called the Pantry.

**Items:**

- **An Air Bubble breathing device** — one of five upgraded units issued by Locke Pierce for travel through the toxic Mournland mist, inscribed in ancient gnomish: *Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers*.
- **His testimony** — the layout of the Zeir residence and both Vault clubs, room by room, given to the Nest and never recanted. It is what he brought out of the Pantry, and the strike on the Children of Ember was planned on it.
- No named weapon, no relic and no heirloom is recorded. <small>Everything he carries he was given after he was taken out of the cell, including the faith.</small>

**Tira:** Lorian's steed, a light-brown palomino, summoned rather than kept. She has been called once in the record: in the great hall at Thronehold, as the masked guards surged and the roof came off, Lorian put Eric on her saddle and got him out.

- **Zero-One (Fema Nolan)** — leader of Fulcrum, the paladin who nursed him at Zilspar, heard his whole account, and pointed him at the Flame. Taken alive by E.D.E.N. units at Zilspar Farm; not recovered.

- **Faith** and **Malrik Zeir** — his captors. Both dead; Malrik killed outright by [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) on a rooftop pool deck, though the vampire's mist escaped to its coffin.
- **Eric the Cleric** — pulled off the floor at Thronehold and carried out on Tira. Alive, and currently on a river boat in Karrnath without him.
- **Gemma Corso** — attended Thronehold beside him in someone else's face. Alive; aboard the same boat.
- **Locke Pierce** — extracted him from Thronehold and issued him the mask that let him breathe in the Mournlands. Alive, and now holding the sky over Sharn.
