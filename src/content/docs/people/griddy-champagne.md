**Fallen angel · Devil in human form · Housecarl at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/)**

**Also known as:** Garidriel · Violet · Gritty Champagne

Griddy Champagne is Garidriel, a fallen angel who lives as a devil in human form and serves as housecarl at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) in [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), the bound servant of the pactbound owner of the house. She is the same devil in every age: in the Ages of the Infanta, under the alias **Violet**, she served [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) as his devil attaché. She obtained the RUIN card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/) for both [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) and his son [Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/). At Tiffany House the party found her seducing Beef Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN; [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) surprised them there, Griddy and Jeremiah fought, and the party resisted him and stole the card. At [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), when [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) betrayed the party, she took RUIN back and slipped it into her bra.

## Description

**Appearance:** "A beautiful and dangerous white woman, secretly a devil in human form, often in black versions of normal fantasy clothing." <small>(Character roster.)</small>

At Fort Maximillien she wore "a reserved but nonetheless smoldering black leather armor and duster", her head covered with a demure scarf. <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small> She is an erinyes. <small>(DM notes)</small> Her sister is Dusty Grapes. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## In the Ages of the Infanta

In Waterdeep, during his contract negotiations over Tiffany House and his debt to Asmodeus, Revan Darkcember was confronted by **Violet**, an erinyes who served as a housecarl and as a spy for Asmodeus: "If killed, she returns to the House." <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV)</small> Revan then had "luxury estates in two major cities and a devil as a personal attaché", and his player asked, "Can her name be Grittey Champagne?" At the table she was Gritty Champagne from then on. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-08-27, 2019-11-11)</small>

In LaMont's short story "…It's time to come home…", she wrote to Revan in Sigil, in red on something that may not have been paper, with Sable's words "…It's time to come home…", signing "With regards, Your Colleague in serving the dark Lord, Champain". Revan greeted it with "Gritty Champain, You beautiful bitch" and burned it. <small>(LaMont, "…It's time to come home…")</small>

## RUIN and the Klaxons

Griddy took the RUIN card from Bumpkin Countrymile, a gambler losing his family's moonstone fortune: he lost it to a thief, "a beautiful seductress who robbed him". <small>(DM notes)</small> See [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/).

Phillip Klaxon has dealt with Griddy for RUIN, which he intends to use on a Masked Lord. His son Darius finds the pact with Asmodeus made through Griddy unbearable, and is seeking the card to divest himself of it. Griddy has obtained RUIN for both father and son, and intends to play them off each other. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Tiffany House

At Tiffany House the party witnessed Griddy seducing Beef Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN, and then Jeremiah surprised them. Jeremiah knows Griddy, and calls her Garidriel; both are fallen angels. <small>(DM notes)</small> Their exchange:

> **Jeremiah:** Garidriel, you are lost. Moriel will face justice once and for all.\
> **Griddy:** Jeremiah, I heard you'll be joining us soon in the Hells! What could they possibly have tempted you with to cause your fall from Grace?\
> **Jeremiah:** I am not tempted. I am resolved to cleanse your master from all realities.\
> **Griddy:** You know my master will not stand for this intrusion on his personal estate, nor the murder of his more useful servants. Now that I think of it, I don't believe your heavenly masters will appreciate the breach of custom much themselves. There is a delicate balance that keeps our old enmities from scorching this plane and others into ash.\
> **Jeremiah:** Let justice be done, though the heavens fall.

Griddy and Jeremiah fought, and the party resisted him and stole RUIN. Nicollo convinced Phillip Klaxon and Griddy that he was on their side and that Jeremiah had taken the card. Beef Wellington was ruined. Griddy, suspicious, demanded that the party steal the card back from Jeremiah. See [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).

## Fort Maximillien

After the incident at [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/), [Maverick](/people/maverick/) was questioned in the upper bastion of Fort Maximillien. [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) opened the door, followed by Phillip Klaxon and Griddy Champagne, who came to recover RUIN. <small>(Post-session narrative; DM notes)</small> She was livid, but restrained, "staring holes into MAVERICK". <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small> The question she and Klaxon brought for him: "if you're not with Jeremiah and Shemeska, then who are you?" <small>(DM notes)</small>

Nicollo was brought in and betrayed the party, claiming he was in the service of Klaxon and that he had tried to take the card the moment he found out [Lenore](/people/lenore/) had it. Griddy picked up the RUIN card from the desk and slipped it into her bra. Varkus sentenced Maverick to hang at dawn. <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small>
