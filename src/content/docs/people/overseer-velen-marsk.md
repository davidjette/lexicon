**Species unknown · Overseer of the Korranberg Foundry · Imperial administration / Korranberg Foundry Trust · Dead, and destroyed**

*Also known as:* Vellin Marsk

**Overseer Velen Marsk** was the [Korranberg Foundry Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/)'s overseer at the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/), which was commissioned for thirteen great castings, delivered three hundred and forty-one parish bells for the House of Kaius, and was staffed by enslaved [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) the Trust held title to. He signed to receive a writ of warrant, signed **KAIUS III**, for the deaths of a long handwritten list of names beginning with **Corn Deeptempura** and ending with his own, and he worked through most of the list before the bell rang. Three years later the **Inevitables** tore down the sheet of protective scripture sealing a Black Dove shrine on the foundry level and found him still in post with two risen ogres; [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s turn undead broke the ogres and Marsk fell with them.

> Back to work.

## Physical description

Three years dead and still in post. He was found in the sealed shrine with two risen ogres, ordering them to *finish the count* and exterminate the dwarves - a dead administrator still running the last instruction he was given. His two words to the intruders were *"Back to work."*

## The office

Marsk was the [Korranberg Foundry Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/)'s man at the works: the overseer of a foundry commissioned for thirteen great castings, and which delivered three hundred and forty-one parish bells, for the House of Kaius using a workforce the Trust held title to. He kept the **personnel ledger and administration book** himself, his name on the cover, many quills spent on it.

## Public and private

On paper he is a middle manager with a headcount. In practice his job was to run an industrial concern staffed by enslaved [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) under a regime that excluded him from knowing what he was making - the Trust's own schedule reminds foundry personnel that consecration is for the Sisters alone and that *curiosity is a breach of the draft*.

What he actually was is written in his satchel. Under the black ribbon and the seal of the holy see of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), hastily retied, he carried a **writ of warrant for the death** of a long handwritten list of names, beginning **Corn Deeptempura** and **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura**, including the Black Dove Sister Calvane, and ending with **Velen Marsk** - who signed to receive it. Kill everyone, then kill yourself. It is signed **KAIUS III**.

Most of the names are crossed out. Some dwarves are annotated as missing or potentially escaped. **Marsk's own name is not crossed out.** He did the work and did not get to the last line, because the bell rang first.

## The room

The shrine where he was found is where the warrant was processed. The administrative papers are still on the table with names crossed off as they were dealt with, and the floor has the large drains of the kind used to hose out a big space. They are caked with blackened three-year-old blood.

## The end

The shrine had been sealed from the outside by a muslin sheet of protective scripture signed by **Sister Maelis Dorn** - the party's reading being that a Black Dove came to this room after the bell rang and sealed it rather than cleansed it. The **Inevitables** tore the sheet down and went in. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s turn undead broke the ogres and Marsk fell with them.

His satchel also held a bone tube with silver caps containing scrolls of *web* and *spider climb*, which is now [Gemma Corso's](/people/gemma-corso/) scroll case.

## Right now

Marsk is destroyed. The warrant he carried bears the Emperor's signature on the extermination of a named people, on Imperial paper, in the hand of the man who executed it, and [Chancellor Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) is keeping it for the trials that follow.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Korranberg Foundry Trust; the Imperial administration; a working relationship with the Black Doves that ended with his name on their warrant.

**Items:** The foundry personnel ledger and administration book; the writ of warrant signed KAIUS III; a bone scroll tube.
