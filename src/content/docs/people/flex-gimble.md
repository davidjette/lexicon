**Rock gnome · Artificer · Betrayed the party, then returned as an ally at the Egg**

*Also known as:* Third Grade Master Artificer, Sword Coast Guild of Alchemists

**Flex Gimble** is a rock gnome artificer, a Third Grade Master Artificer of the Sword Coast Guild of Alchemists, who came to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) chasing tales of arcane science and was exploring the Grand Concourse alone when the party found him. He led them to the Seat of the Lexicon, and the moment they had cured the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) he turned on them, shrinking them with a shrink ray and flooding them down the crystal into the Winking Depths and the world of the Deep Fae. His tampering knocked the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) out of stasis, which is why it is unstable for the rest of the chronicle. He prospered on what he learned — a giant statue of him welcomed visitors to the spire in Arc IV — and he came back out of the land of the Dead at the final battle among the allies the Raven Queen brought, his face long since replaced by a bronze mask and his limbs by six mechanical prostheses.

> My apologies brave heroes, but I have a job to do. The Company can have their Moonstones… The secrets we have learned here will change the world, and I, FLEX GIMBLE, will be their inventor. Goodbye, fools!

<small>— Flex Gimble, on his Ally Card. The session text of the moment reads slightly longer: "The Sword Coast Trading Company can have their Moonstones, but I will harness this knowledge and construct a new age of machines beyond all their understanding." (Arc II, Motherstone Part III, "The Betrayal.")</small>

## How the party encountered him

In the Grand Concourse of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), exploring it on his own — "a jolly and eccentric rock gnome," thrilled to find anyone: "Thank Glittergold I've found other adventurers! I never thought I'd see anyone else from this century again!" He saves the party from the spiders if they need it.

He introduces himself at length: "Flex Gimble, engineer, inventor and entrepreneur, at your service. I am a Third Grade Master Artificer, member of the Sword Coast Guild of Alchemists, registered to practice Thamauturgical Engineering in both Waterdeep and Suzail. I also hold a junior degree in Lute Performance Studies." <small>(Sic on "Thamauturgical." Arc II, Motherstone Part I.)</small>

On why he came: "I came here chasing tales of spectacular technology and arcane science the likes of which I'd never imagined. When I got here it was impressive, but nothing worked. Then — boom! — today it turns on and I see things you wouldn't believe!"

## The Betrayal

It was Flex who led the party to the Seat of the Lexicon, where they connected to its terminal.

The moment the party has finished interrogating the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), they are "pulled from the interface and find themselves at Flex's mercy, who has shrunk them to a tiny size." He makes the speech above, touches a panel, and heads up the shaft — flooding the room and flushing the party hundreds of feet down the crystal, into the world of the Deep Fae.

He turned on them just as they cured the Lexicon: he shrank them with a shrink ray, then used a flood spell to push them to the bottom of the crystal, which opened over the Winking Depths. Tiny, they caught the roots of the Eldergreen and entered the world of the worms and beetles.

His tampering is also what knocked the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) out of stasis, which is the reason it is unstable for the rest of the chronicle.

He prospers on it. A "giant, conspicuously new statue of Flex Gimble welcomes visitors" to the spire in Arc IV, where the engineer of the spire is described as "the former partner of Flex Gimble." <small>(Arc IV Parts III–IV.)</small>

## How he became an ally

He comes back out of the land of the Dead at the final battle, among the allies the Raven Queen brings — "a sheepish Flex Gimble who is barely recognizable as such, his face long since replaced by a bronze mask and his limbs replaced with six mechanical multiarticulated prosthesis. He bows and his gizmo eyebrows indicate a smile." <small>(Sic on "prosthesis." End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

The card art matches: a gnome riding a mechanical spider body.

## At the Egg

**White — Clockwork Orange:** "Deal 5d8 acid to all creatures in a 15 ft. cube within 60 ft."

**Black — Honey, I Shrunk Cthulhu:** "Target creature makes a DC 30 Con save or size is reduced by 3 sizes till end of its turn."

<small>The black action is the same trick he used on the party at Motherstone, turned on a god.</small>

## Status

Not recorded. He is one of the few allies whose card is **not** Legendary — the type line reads simply "Creature — Gnome Artificer."
