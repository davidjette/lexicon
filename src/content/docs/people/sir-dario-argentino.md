**Dragonborn · Paladin, Squire Templar of the Silver Flame · The Inevitables · Level 15 · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Flintscale** (his name as a journeyman smith) · **Sir Beef Taco** (first Korth cover) · **Sky Rizzy** (his registered Korth papers, forged in the Hall of Records with a wand-drawn portrait and an official seal) · **Sister Mr. Rogers** (as a Black Dove novice at the Ivory Lazaret) · **Greyscale** · **Ronaldo Weasley** (on the books of the Zilspar Farm-Table Co-op) · **Mortimer Bunk** (Newham cover) · one of the **Heroes of Leef** · **of clan Dawnmantle**

Sir Dario Argentino is a dragonborn paladin, Squire Templar of the Silver Flame, a blacksmith, a member of **the Inevitables**, and the only person recorded to have destroyed a **Fantanya Nyel**. He was born and raised in a jungle near Q'barra and took the oath of the Silver Flame — a religion outlawed by the Empire — because it is his family's trade, as all the strong ones of clan Dawnmantle do. He met [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) in the rail-town of Leef, where they were named the Heroes of Leef; went west to Newham and killed Grand Duke John C. LeBeefe; fought through the rising at Sharn, and afterwards spent three years on the Thrane front leading cavalry against undead E.D.E.N. units.

Beneath the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) the orc war-chief **Braga Kul** killed him; Eric brought him back with a diamond, which sits in the eye socket he lost. On the hoard of the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) he lifted King Corn's warhammer out of the crowned corpse's arms, and with the hammer the kin named **Knell** he has destroyed the Thirteenth Casting, every small casting in the Bladeworks, the crated bell in the unfinished tower above the Black Dove hospital and one in a temple of Boldrei in Sharn's lower city. At Nos Matre, above [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), he broke the Original and took off the head of the Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). He left the valley aboard [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/)'s airship *Revenge*.

## Physical description

A dragonborn of enormous physical strength — Strength 20 unaided, recorded jumping a collapsing bridge and landing on a colleague. He is **blind in one eye**: the socket holds the diamond Eric spent to revivify him, and it gleams. He wears a finely wrought suit of plate commissioned for [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and never delivered, which fits him with uncanny precision, over a Silver Flame tabard that is currently torn. He has blindsight, a breath weapon he has used to put out a straw fire, and wings he can raise at will. His scales are **silver**, and his breath weapon is cold: "Silver dragon so ice". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-13)</small> Dave first built him as a "Paladin Acolyte Gold Dragonborn". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Eye colour and height: **Unknown**. His own working alias is Greyscale.

## The Silver Flame

**The Silver Flame** is an outlawed religion under the Pax Imperium and a fighting order in exile. It holds that the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) — the Aereni order of battlefield morticians who tend the dead and destroy the undead, and to which the Empress herself belongs — are obviously and irredeemably evil. Its paladins hold the line in the Mournlands against E.D.E.N. constructs and the walking dead; twelve of them sat on a stairwell in Sharn reading and praying and smoking while one of their squires broke a bell above their heads. Its leadership rests with Zero-One, once Fema Nolan of Thrane, who took the ancient name-stripping rite and became the first woman in centuries to do so. In Dario's family the Flame is simply the trade: all the strong ones of clan Dawnmantle take the oath.

## Stated positions

At the Korth assembly, before a representative of every faith from Sharn, he announced that he had broken three bells, that he would break every one, and that the woman who rings them and everyone helping her must be cut down without quarter. <small>(Korth Ep 12)</small>

Asked what he knew about black dragons, at the top of the shaft down to the black lake, he said: *"They are my progenitors as much as they are yours — but I descend from the couatls."* Nobody in the Empire is recorded as knowing this. <small>(Korth Ep 10-11)</small>

Leaving the Korramont after the Teryaki refused to evacuate the mountain: *"Then we made allies of an evil creature and left the people who helped us to their doom."* <small>(Korth Ep 13)</small>

At Zilspar he stopped Eric mid-question and refused to let the orphaned boy **Noot Gunray** be interrogated, saying the boy had been through the worst thing you can imagine and they were taking him home. <small>(Korth Ep 13)</small>

## Recorded conduct

He prayed alone in a chapel in a torn tabard after the hospital bell, holding **Pale Fire** up to the altar, and named his dead aloud; at the end of the list he added Izaak, whom nobody has confirmed is dead. <small>(Korth Ep 12)</small>

He left roughly 280 gold in dwarven grave-jewels lying on an altar body and told the kin they were there — the party had robbed graves in the same complex the day before. He carried **Sedda Deeptempura's** remains through the Teryaki camp himself and consecrated her beside the unknown soldier with General Tso, as princess and heir. He carried a burned and eyeless duergar child back to its mother and stood there while Eric explained how the child had died and been brought back. He gave his personal prayer book through the bars of an Imperial jail to a starving prisoner who asked for a holy symbol of the Silver Flame.

He also ordered [Blair](/people/blair/)'s arrest after Sharn fell; she was tried and released.

In combat he is recorded grappling rather than striking when the target is an ally, and refusing the second attack. He has swapped sword for warhammer to shove an exploding object clear of somebody else, and when that failed he picked Eric up and threw him into the brush. He carries the wounded and the dead through camp himself rather than sending them ahead. His aura nullified a black dragon's legendary frightful presence outright. Breaking bells has left him deaf in both ears for hours at a time.

## Trade and kin

Dario is a working blacksmith with smith's tools, which is how the party learned the name of the ore they were standing in. He speaks Draconic and Dwarvish, and both have got them out of rooms that were about to become fights. He is married to **Princess Orange Chicken**, a young dwarven leader of the swamp tribes of the Mror Holds, and they have a child, **Red Dragon Shrimp**. Kinship by marriage through Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long is what secured the Inevitables a night of hospitality at Rak'tafur, where the Teryaki kin had otherwise treated them as grave robbers.

## Origin

Dario was born and raised in the jungle near Q'barra, a country boy who joined the Silver Flame because it is his family's trade. He trained as a Squire Templar and as a blacksmith, and married into the swamp-dwarf tribes of the Mror Holds. In the eastern rail-town of Leef he met Gemma Corso and Eric the Cleric, and with Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke, Zero of Leef and Princess Orange Chicken they broke Grand Duke John C. LeBeefe's hold on the town and were named the Heroes of Leef. They went west to Newham, united Digma Beeve's rebel cells, killed LeBeefe, and watched the city collapse into the New Mourning. Sent on to Sharn, Dario fought at Breaker's Pit — he beat a man named Biff for a Feather Token — cleared the undercity, and cut the BioTec control chip out of Martin Kross so he could speak. In the three years afterwards he was on the Thrane front with the Silver Flame, leading cavalry against undead E.D.E.N. units raised under the Empress's command, and was extracted from a losing portal fight in the Mournlands with wounds carrying the telltale signs of Mistborn illness.

## Flintscale

Dario began the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) under a cover name. Dave: "Goes by Flintscale as a journeyman smith, but he secretly dreams to be oathbound to the silver flame". His background combined acolyte with smith's tools. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-26)</small>

He made his own gear. Dave: "I think all his starting kit he made himself from scrounged scraps and tossed out pieces" / "Except the sword, which is first real quality work, made in secret, named it quicksilver". The sword was **Quicksilver**, and Dave described the image of Dario drawing it as "the aspirant hero drawing a blade he made himself knowing he could be arrested for it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-09)</small>

He also made a **masked helm**: "dario has made a masked helmet as part of his paladin gear, so that he can stay anonymous when fighting for justice". Dave: "It would be rigged so he could use his breath weapon and having a humanoid face would be somehow even scarier". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-13)</small>

To identify fellow Flames he used the [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/)'s pass phrase, *"Long night?"* answered by *"Means a brighter day."* <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18)</small>, also given as *"Long night, means a brighter dawn"*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-08)</small>

In the first session of the Leef / Newham arc Dario decapitated a guard in front of a scrapyard. Dave named the guard **Morlo Gint**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-19)</small>

At the table the trio's group took the name **Falcon's Junkyard**, which Nico credited to [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/): "Gemma named the group Falcon's Junkyard." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-21)</small> In Sharn the Nest onboarded them as the Falcon Crew.

## Leef and Newham

The Colonel's first job for the trio was the Imperial stockyard at Leef, where a supply train marked MILITARY SUPPLIES NEWHAM had unloaded 135 crates for the soldiers heading east to fight the dwarves. Dario and Gemma went in disguised. Dario was incapacitated in the blast, and Gemma dragged him clear of it. He came out with a **Longsword of Vengeance**, a +1 blade possessed by an aspect of Tira Miron. When the Inquisition began executing citizens at random in the aftermath, Dario became a folk hero in Leef, and Zero, the Silver Flame's agent in the town, sought him out and told him there was a treasure in [the temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/). The treasure was [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), caged in its basement. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

In the swamps of the dwarflands the shaman [Yellameet](/people/yellameet/) set the price of an alliance, and one of his conditions was a marriage with his granddaughter. Dario became engaged to [Orange Chicken](/people/orange-chicken/), and came back toward Leef with Yellameet and a small army of thirty-five dwarves trailing behind. His squire in Leef was **Calaban**, aged ten, who came with an orphan foster sister, Kuna, aged two, and another brother. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> His *find steed* was a large cat named **Rum Tum Tugger**. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small>

In Newham his cover name was **Mortimer Bunk**. In the halls of LeBeefe's Black Fortress a small dragonborn girl walked beside him and asked why he had left, and whether he was sad that she had died after he did: his sister. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

In the morning after the trio came home from Newham, Orange Chicken told Dario she was pregnant. In the year that followed he built an annex onto the Falcon's garage as a barracks for up to twelve warriors, and chose an old building on the north side of town for a redoubt, a fortress for defence and military training. On his downtime mission to the Mror Holds, with a hill giant named Bungus beside him, he learned Giant, gained proficiency with carpenter's tools and came away with Horseshoes of Speed. Zero, the high priestess of the Flame, asked him what should be done with the temple on the hill that the Black Doves had desecrated. On the day Orange Chicken went into labour, word came that [Colonel Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/) had died in his sleep, and Dario had to choose between the funeral and the birth. The child is **Red Dragon Shrimp**. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Sharn

Dario fought at Breaker's Pit, crossed the Grand Aqueduct into the Underhive, cleared the Drain Snakes and the Chamber Room, and killed **Malrik Zeir** outright on the rooftop pool deck of the Vault Underground, though the vampire's mist escaped to its coffin. On the last night he told Esther Crona to her face that he knew what [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) had done to [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/), and she froze. Then he broadcast his own pre-recorded message and the reclaimed Arcane Eye memory of Calcifer's birth across the entire city — Uriel's hand raised over the newborn in a sickly green glow, the vein-like lines forming on his cheek, the freeze when Esther screamed. Esther's composure broke. When she was dead he broadcast her corpse live and declared that the Empire would fall.

## The eye

At the Mountain Door beneath the Korramont, the orc war-chief **Braga Kul** opened Dario's skull and killed him. Eric brought him back with a diamond, and the cost was permanent: he is blind in one eye and the revivification diamond sits gleaming in the empty socket. The table's note at the time was that he now has a diamond head like the Empress — twinning. He came out of that death with a vision of dragons and white sands that has not been decoded.

## Knell

Deep beneath the mountain, on the hoard of the black dragon **Valtrex**, lay the crowned corpse of **King Corn** of the Deeptempura with a warhammer folded into its arms. The dragon had just been freed from a three-year Imperial bleeding-rig, and his position on ownership was unambiguous: *"That hammer doesn't belong to King Corn. It belongs to me. So does his crown."* Dario stepped forward, spoke in Draconic, rolled 21, laid a Ring of Lesser Wish on the hoard, and lifted the hammer out of the corpse's arms — heavy even for him — along with the Crown of Seven Stars.

The kin named it **Knell**. Bofric the runesmith had already told them that the horn only masks a bell while it plays, and that breaking one requires the hammer that made it.

## Breaking the bells

Dario is the only person recorded to have destroyed a Fantanya Nyel, and he has done it repeatedly. A bell struck with Knell does not ring: the crack runs across it, the metal loses its symmetry and folds in on itself in a lopsided cyclone, dropping so hot it can light straw. The recoil throws him backwards; breaking a Master casting deafened him and the dwarf standing next to him for hours.

He has destroyed the **Thirteenth Casting**, every small casting in the Bladeworks, the crated bell in the unfinished tower above the Black Dove hospital, and one in a small temple of Boldrei in Sharn's lower city. That last strike brought the whole tower down through the floor into the building below, buried him, and turned against the party the crowd Gemma had just pacified. The last bell he broke was the Original, in the belfry of Nos Matre, in [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/).

> "may our children forgive us"

<small>— Sir Dario Argentino, clearing the straw muffling from the crated bell in the unfinished tower above the Black Dove hospital, before the first swing. Korth Ep 12. **The line is a deliberate reference to Independence Day and is quoted exactly; it is not to be corrected into more natural English.**</small>

## The Stairs of Her Mercy

At the Counting House, wearing Gemma's *seeming* as the bodyguard Sky Rizzy, he followed a load of sealed hazardous-materials containers outside after a man in a swashbuckler's coat paid for them; they went by carriage straight to the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/) and around the back. Later that day, disguised as a winged aasimar Black Dove sister, he climbed the Stairs of Her Mercy with Gemma and Eric. At the fountain at the feet of Uriel's statue he spat into the water in front of the pilgrims. The pilgrims gasped, Gemma turned on him, and he said he was glad he had not pissed in it. <small>([Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/))</small>

## Orientation Day

In [Korth Ep 16](/sessions/korth-ep-16-orientation-day/) Dario joined a novices' tour of the Ivory Lazaret in the black habit of a Black Dove, under the name Sister Mr. Rogers. When three novices came over to size up the strangers he wanted to kill them on the spot, and was talked out of it. On the bottom floor of the hospital under Nos Matre he and Gemma found [Donna](/people/donna/), the Silver Flame cleric from Zilspar, on the transformation floor, fitted with attachments and a new jaw.

In the slab room [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) knelt in irons, forced to raise [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/). Dario lit Pale Fire and took two heads in two blows: *"By the power of the Flame, heathens will perish."* He blinded a battle sister with a smite. When Esther had risen he lifted Zero-One from the floor. She opened her eyes and said, *"Dario, I knew the Flame would warm my skin again,"* and he took her out by *dimension door*. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

## The Pilgrims Camp

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) Dario was found at the pilgrim camp, happier than his friends had seen him in a while. After his *dimension door* out of the slab room he and Zero-One had come out on a peak, and he hid her in the bushes and found followers of Matt Matheny who took them in. He spent the night drinking with the camp, then stood on a stump and asked for everyone who could carry a sword or play a harp or a horn: *"It is a dark day. The sky is gray. And it will be a long night. But that means all the brighter dawn."* He said that if he sees Eden he will destroy her and every one like her. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

## The Westhouse

In [Korth Ep 19](/sessions/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse/), at the camp, Gemma asked Dario whether he should carry the Bright Dawn, because he had named it and it could be a symbol of hope. He refused. *"Rebellions are built on hope,"* he said, and when she doubted that anyone would remember her he put his hand on her chest: *"Gemma, you must have faith in yourself. We are the heroes who will deliver this land. You are the one who will carry the Bright Dawn across the horizon."* Of his own weapons he said: *"I have made my oath. Pale Fire is a symbol of my oath. I carry Knell, the weapon of my wife's people, to bring down upon them. I have but two arms, Gemma."*

He climbed the Stairs of Her Mercy as Veg, one of the druids of [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), beside Gemma in Thar's shape. At the Tomb of Kaius I he wanted to climb the tower and break the bell at once, and agreed to wait. In the Westhouse, in the office of [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), he drew Pale Fire, shouted *"Death to the Black Doves,"* walked onto the table and struck her. He cut the throat of the first Sister Mary Christmas and took off her head with the next stroke. He called Rum Tum, hauled Eric up behind him, and rode the flying panther through the classroom with Eric's *spirit guardians* around them. He destroyed the second Mary Christmas from Rum Tum's back and cut down two white knights after Eric had turned them.

When Saturnia, a Sister of the Blood, begged for her life and agreed to help them, he told her, *"The rebellion is inevitable."* He threw a ball of phlogiston into a room of cowering students. In the Imperial Archive a hooded sister struck him unconscious off Rum Tum, and Eric brought him back. Afterwards he stripped the bodies of Dorn, Lus Fayina and one Mary Christmas and put them in his bag of holding. <small>(Korth Ep 19)</small>

## The Dinner Party

In [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/) Saturnia described the Empress's children, and Dario said: *"Every one of them is a potential Uriel. They all die."* He walked out of the Westhouse under Gemma's *seeming* as Mary Christmas. He had never heard her speak, so he pointed down the stairs and glared, and the guards went. In the slab room he saw that the body on the table was living: *"Esther is not dead. That's not a corpse. She's alive over there."*

Beyond the morgue he kicked in the door on four vampires at dinner. His smites turned vampires to dust, and he tore one off Gemma's back. In the chamber beyond, [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) ordered Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn to destroy the dragonborn. Dario wounded Trillo and blinded him with a smite. Then he flew to the pool of blood where a gold dragon lay half-sunk and laid hands on it: *"Noble dragon, if you can hear me, join the fight. By the light of the Silver Flame, awaken!"* The dragon was [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/), and it woke. Dario put Pale Fire through E'noch, and when E'noch rose again as a bat-winged master vampire he answered it: *"I will purge the earth of you and your entire bloodline. You're a blasphemy on this world. And I'm only sorry that my ancient ancestors couldn't finish the job."*

In the room of coffins he showed the others how the vampires were to be finished, dropping his sword through the heart of the first. He destroyed Lady Ophelia Qualanthri and two more in their coffins. <small>(Korth Ep 20)</small>

## The Summer Palace

In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) Dario argued against going back to free Esther Crona, because she could join [Eden](/people/eden/) against them. In the Emperor's Gardens he cut down Archbishop Pom Pilot, who had been murdered and had risen as a wight. He killed two of the bone knights at the palace stair.

In the hall of the Empress's daughters he shouted *"Death to the Witch Queen!"* and cut Purell in half with his first stroke. He beheaded Muriel, the eldest. When the fight ended, Saturnia stood between him and the eleven-year-old Drakiel, who had wept in a corner throughout. He drew Pale Fire and said: *"By the name of the Silver Flame, I charge you with treason against all that is holy and name you an abomination."* And: *"There cannot be any legitimate heirs to the Emperor. We've come too far for half measures."* He looked to Eric, as Chancellor, for a verdict, and Eric could not sanction it. Dario sheathed his sword and walked away: *"This is a mistake."*

In the Emperor's chamber he rode up to the Empress in labour and pointed Pale Fire at her: *"Empress Uriel, I charge you with mundicide, genocide, perversion of all things holy, and I sentence you to death."* She called him a lizard and an imitation. He killed Sister Marshall, the head of the Sisterhood of the Blood, and stood on the delivery table over the burning Emperor: *"In the name of the people of Eberron, I will end this empire and free the land."* When Uriel vanished and the bell began to ring, he fell from Rum Tum clutching his head, maddened by it, and Aurorix dragged him out. Gemma played the fanfare of dawn on King Corn's horn and he came out of the madness. He put Pale Fire away, took out Knell, and rode Aurorix toward the bell with the others. <small>(Korth Ep 21)</small>

## Nos Matre and the Sept of Bones

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) the bell brought Aurorix down out of the sky. Dario laid hands on the dragon (*"Wake up, my friend! There's work to do!"*), and Rum Tum leapt under him. He flew past the Empress to the belfry of Nos Matre and broke the Original with Knell. The tower fell under him. He wheeled Rum Tum around on Uriel: *"In the name of every hero, of every soul, I strike thee!"* The stroke opened her from the neck to the navel.

In the forecourt, beside [the Polygon](/items/the-polygon/), Gemma brought the Empress to the ground and Dario floated down over her with his wings spread: *"For a while, the world was dark. You sucked the hope from the world. And now, after the long night, comes a brighter dawn."* He said, *"It is over,"* and took off her head. Her head and body melted and sank into the earth, and every dead thing in the yard turned on him. He told Gemma: *"It seems this is where we end, little fox. I am proud that it is with you and that we have accomplished our great quest."* She answered that they were not done yet, and carried him back to Eric by *dimension door*.

He put Pale Fire away, took out Knell, and went for the Polygon. He did not reach it. When Gemma and Eric went underground he stayed on the surface, saw Matt Matheny's people and Fema Nolan on the hillside with the dead closing on them, and flew to defend them instead. Valtrex came to their aid and dissolved the field of zombies in acid. From there Dario saw Eden go down into the hospital to free Esther Crona, and saw Esther fly for the crypt. He and Matheny's people rushed after her and opened the crypt from above. He was mobbed by the dead and badly hurt. He came down into [the Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/) close to death, threw Eric the greatberry that the people above had saved for him, and gave the news: *"That thing up there? It's going to blow."* Breaking it had weakened the hordes, he said. He told the others to run and leave Uriel to him. When Eric destroyed her with the mace of disruption, Dario said, *"Hey man, nice shot."* He killed two of the bone guards as [the Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/) grew, and offered to stay and cover the others, which they refused. Gemma carried him out by *dimension door* to the cathedral roof, and Joseph Roberts pulled them aboard the *Revenge*. On deck, after the valley had burned, he slid down against the wall and wept. <small>(Korth Ep 22)</small>

## The Father of the Hundreds

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/), on the deck of the *Revenge*, the three weighed [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s summons and what they knew of the destruction of Zilspar Farm. Dario said: *"And if they did Zilspar, I will kill Locke Pierce and cut his wires or whatever he's got."*

Under [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/) Pierce set out his offer to Eric and Gemma, and turned to him last: *"Dario, uh, listen. I don't have much for you, except you look really cool."* Left alone with the others for an hour, Dario pointed out that the bells are more than a bomb, since they raise the dead. He said he wanted to go home to his children, and named them: Red Dragon Shrimp, Orange Chicken, Yellow Curry, Green Coconut Soup and Blue Sorbet. His wife had laid a dozen eggs. He took out his sword and threw it on the floor.

> "I hold my oath complete. The rebellion's over. We've won."

He told Eric that without him Pierce would get everything he wanted: *"You could lead Sharn for 50 years. You could find a way to take down Locke Pierce. It's impossible to stay on top forever. You have things he doesn't know that you have."* He said he would rather die than see one of Uriel's children on the throne, and that if Eric chose to go down fighting he would go with him, though he would sooner see Eric take down "that rusted mechanical devil" in time.

When [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), emissary of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), came into the room alone, he dropped to one knee by instinct, thought better of it and stood. She spoke to him of his faith:

> "I have learned of the Faith of the Silver Flame. It is a beautiful one, one of the purest I have found in all my travels across the stars. Rarely is there a tradition which exalts the spirit of all without the vain personification of such a power as being the same as one race or another. I commend you, Paladin. I believe you are an example of what could make the Sovereignty great."

When Eric decided to resist, Dario said that if the rebellion was not over then his oath still held. Aboard the *Revenge* that night he sat with [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and told her he could not see the Flame, only darkness, fog and smoke. She answered: *"Where there's smoke, there's fire."* On Roberts's island Eric said he would go back to Sharn and start a new rebellion, and Dario said: *"Absolutely, dude. Yes, brother."* He lit Pale Fire for the jump to [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/). <small>(Korth Ep 23)</small>

## Fate

After the destruction of the valley Dario was aboard Joseph Roberts's airship *Revenge*, above the storm at sunset, climbing out toward the Lhazaar seas with Knell and Pale Fire. Gemma and Eric were aboard with him, with Zero-One, Ilma Vitt and the survivors of Matt Matheny's camp. The Empress he charged with mundicide was destroyed and the Original was broken. The Polygon, the Ivory Lazaret, Nos Matre and the Summer Palace were gone, burned out of the valley by a weapon dropped from a vessel of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/). Rum Tum was not seen after the fight, and his fate is not known. At last count ten bells remained, at least one of them a Master, and the ninth great casting hung in a tower attached to the Imperial Palace in Korth. What became of those bells, and of Sharn and Leef, had not been heard.

A day later he was at the Nest in Sharn. The bells had been brought down in every city. He had not gone home. His oath to the rebellion had become an oath of protection over Eric, who had been declared dead and led an underground movement against Locke Pierce, and Dario stood beside him as his bodyguard. Lorian and Zero-One were expected to side with him. He had told Zero-One that he could no longer see the Flame.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Inevitables (formerly the Falcon Crew) · Squire Templar of the Silver Flame, Covenant of the Flame · clan Dawnmantle of Q'barra · the Heroes of Leef · kin by marriage to the swamp-dwarf tribes of the Mror Holds and, through them, to the Deeptempura line.

**Holdings:** No fixed residence — he has been on campaign or underground for three years, and after Korth Ep 23 he lived at the Nest in Sharn beside Eric. Through Princess Orange Chicken he holds standing in the Mror Holds and at the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long.
