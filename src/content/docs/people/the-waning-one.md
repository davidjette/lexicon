> The Waning-One loved many girls, but only 1 he wed.

<small>— the Heroes' Hill rhyme, first line. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-31)</small>

**Another loop's Drefan · Villain of the Antiquity quest line · Leader of the Banefae · Archfey patron · Imprisoned in Silverbane**

*Also known as:* Drefanmetra · Drefmetra · Dref Metra · Anti-Drefan

**The Waning One** is Drefanmetra, a true alter ego of [Drefan](/people/drefan/) from another turn of the loop and a major villain of the Antiquity quest line. He loved the primal purity of the ancient moon and vowed to stop it being moved into the path of the Egg, which would have saved the home of the elves and doomed Toril, and he failed. He went forward in time to Antiquity, drew a cult following as a primal force of nature, and united [the Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/), and with them all the oldest elvish houses, to take the throne of Death, disrupt the Netherese and [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), and find a way back in time to stop [Karametra](/people/karametra/) moving the moon. The Banefae he united attacked [Despondos](/places/despondos/) and enslaved [Tylerjynex](/people/tylerjinex/). In the Time of Troubles he fought the party beside Valen and Carl's Jr. at Deadfall Peak and turned into a gargantuan fae dragon; the party charmed him and imprisoned him in his own sword. He is held in **Silverbane**, which Drefan carries. Many elves still worship him or pay him homage, and the Short King of the moon became his warlock.

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small>

## Drefanmetra and the moon

Drefanmetra is a version of Drefan from another loop. He was a member of the party in the loop in which Karametra was St. Cloud's wife, and so was not in the party herself. It is not clear whether that was the loop just before the one the campaign played, or some earlier one. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

Drefanmetra, like Drefan, was in love with the primal purity of the ancient moon, which he saw as an elvish utopia. He was outraged to find that Kara had destroyed it on purpose to crack open [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). At the time he believed, as many of the party did until they learned the truth, that her efforts were part of helping St. Cloud. He vowed to foil those plans. That set him against the defeat of Cthulhu, because breaking open the Egg with the moon was a critical part of it. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

He failed to stop the moon being moved, and went forward in time to Antiquity, and possibly to other eras, to try again. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-oral-histories-cast.md; sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

Dave counted him among the party's "characters in other times - in the deep past you have Chronocypher, XIII, Pim, Karametra, Drefanmetra and Revan, and Roland all still around". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> In the deep past "Drefanmetra is saving the five Banefae and the dragons fight each other and then lose a battle against the aboleth." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-11)</small> When the party returned to help [Karametra](/people/karametra/) move the moon, he would also have tried to stop them: "Drefanmetra also would have tried, I almost had him ambush you after you killed Tiamat but I thought that was too many battles in one place". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

## The cult of the Waning One

In Antiquity he gathered a cult following among extreme druids and elves as a primal force of nature, a proto-archfey who sought to return the elves to their former natural state. He agitated that the elves must seize the moment and defeat or enslave the human Infanta. His message resonated with Carls Jr and with Valen Tudyx. He would have turned [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), Atlas and Nimue as well if circumstances had been different. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

To this day many elves worship the Waning One or pay him homage. He represents the dark side of the Archfey Drefan Gallidan, and the changeable and dangerous aspect of the moon goddess, whom he serves. It is said that in Arborea Drefan takes the form of the Waning One, a nude man with a stag's head over his own, holding a gleaming bow, for around half the time, like the phases of the moon. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

## Leader of the Banefae

In Antiquity he united the Banefae, and through them all the oldest elvish houses, against the Seat of Death. They meant to use the throne of Death to disrupt the Netherese and St. Cloud, and to find a way to go back in time and stop Karametra moving the moon. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small> Dave's notes at the time:

> The Banefae United by Drefanmetra attack the maze and enslaved TJ to get into the Penteract at the heart of Despondos
>
> Their plan is to unite the bane blades into the Fatherstone and use it to trap the power of the Netherese and destroy all mortal souls in a rite to undo time and restore the Moon

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

The Queens of [Netheril](/places/netheril/) and the Grey Sage defeated the Banefae, "But Drefanmetras efforts still lead to the fall of Netheril and Brightmantles victory, and St Cloud has to wait another 1000 years to try again…" <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

He led Archmage **Valen**, who murdered his sister Alen Tudyx for her Bane blade as part of a plan to kill the god of death and use the Mammon Machine for the glory of the elves. Against the party's loop they came one blade short: "We’ll the Waning One and Valen have one less Bane (mordred + iron bane) than they did last time". <small>(Sic on "We’ll". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-22)</small> Not every elf lord followed them: "There’s another atlas here in Antiquity who refused to go along with Valen/Waning One - Atlas III", "As did Calix". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small>

## The meeting seen in the Palantir

A key moment in his story was a meeting between the Waning One, Carls Jr, Valen, Atlas and [Calix](/people/calix/). [Drefan](/people/drefan/) and [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) used [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/) to spy on it. The meeting revealed that the Waning One intended to enter Despondos and cut off the Netherese in [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/), and that Atlas and Calix were not open to such an action. Calix refused to join the Waning One. He was loyal to his Infanta, and he did not want to reverse the past event that had created Motherstone in the first place. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>

## The battle in Despondos

Scheduling the session, Dave wrote: "Would be a bummer for dref not to be there to fight his own double". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-01)</small> The [Waxing](/items/the-bane-blades/) power was "available to everyone with bane blades in this next battle, including the three main enemies!" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-09)</small>

The party crossed the maze of Despondos, and "finally the party confronted Valen, Carl’s Jr. and The Waning One at the peak of Sigil, and Anti-Drefan transformed into a gargantuan fae dragon, and the Bane Blades finally awakened". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-10)</small> The fight is also called the Deadfall Peak battle: Deadfall Peak is the peak of Sigil, the place where [Sigil](/places/sigil/) is built in the future. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-batch2-questions.md; sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small> Before the second session Jenny, who played [Kara](/people/kara/), looked for a way to "exploit Kara & Dref’s BFF spell so that every time Kara gets hit, the Waning One takes damage. But Dref would also take the damage". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-20)</small>

"You charmed the Waning One with an hour left, you bested Carl’s Jr. and convinced her and the Drow to join your effort … Valen lies in a puddle of acid and the Bane blades merge and evolve." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> Wainwright St. Cloud watched "your battle against Valen and Waning One succeed" through the [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09)</small>

## Charmed, then imprisoned

After the battle "Drefanmetra is under a spell". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-05)</small> "Drefanmetra has a copy of Drefans equipments except for some stuff"; the party were dividing it up, "but not a good idea since he’s only dominated for 1 hour", "And is still a dragon." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-11)</small> The next session's business was "figuring out what to do with the charmed Drefanmetra". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-20)</small>

"Congrats on imprisoning Drefmetra in his own sword". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-04)</small> The Waning One is held in [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/), which Drefan carries, and Drefan lets him out occasionally "for carnal purposes". <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c1-c10.md)</small>

## Patron of the Short King

When the party went back to the deep past a second time, to help Karametra move the moon, they went to the moon again. The Short King, **Brownsleeves** from another loop, had been seduced by Drefanmetra, was a warlock of the Waning One (Archfey), and joined the party. He died in the final round of the fight against Cthulhu. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small> JL proposed the build at the table, "What if BS was a rogue/warlock who made a pact with Drefenmetra?", and Dave answered "That is awesome, follows the waning one". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small> See [Lunar Fae](/organizations/lunar-fae/).

## The titles

The sea elves' pledge at [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s funeral: "You saved us from the mindflayers and defeated the Waning One. You are the true bearers of Glassbane. We are with you to the end." [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), Eldamir, Calix and the Moon Elves named the party "the great hunter, the Deadfall Raining, the Waning and the Waxing One." <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small> See [Atlas and the Sea Elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/).

"Deadfall Raining" was a hint to the party about who the Waning One was. Its letters rearrange into "I Drefan Gallidan". <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-drefanmetra-the-waning-one.md)</small>
