**Actor and entertainer · Founding member of Fulcrum · Level Unknown · Deceased**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

**John Stamos** was a beloved Breeland actor and entertainer whose charm and talent made him famous across Khorvaire, and one of the founding members of the rebel group known as **Fulcrum**. Born in the small town of Zilspar outside Sharn, he rose from local stages to national acclaim and became a symbol of Breland's spirit and humour; his Fulcrum connection was unknown to the public, and he used his fame and mobility to quietly support resistance efforts. He was killed on an Imperial lightning rail when a Fulcrum crew boarded it to steal a Hell's Bell, dying at the hands of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), who took his eyes and has them still.

The Empire publicly blamed the attack on "rebel terrorists" and named the singer [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) as responsible for his death, then erected a statue of Stamos in the Imperial capital Korth to honour him as a fallen national icon, never revealing the truth of the hero he really was. It still stands there today. No source describes his face.

**Status:** Deceased.

## Fulcrum

Fulcrum is one of the oldest rebel groups in existence, older than the current era of Imperial dominance. It grew out of the Breland farmsteads outside Sharn, from farmers, rangers and dissidents who resisted Imperial expansion into the countryside, with its stronghold centred on Zilspar. The name is the claim: a pivotal axis, the balancing point between rural defiance and the Empire's urban dominance. It was formed over two decades ago; its founders were the paladin **Fema Nolan**, the goods-trader **Digma Beeve**, the entertainer John Stamos, and, secretly, the restaurateur [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), whose name appears in no record of it. Today Fulcrum is the countryside counterpart of the Nest in the city, and Zero-One leads it.

## Public and private

**In public** Stamos was one of the safest men in Khorvaire: a household face, a career of tours and openings and appearances, a man whose politics nobody thought to ask about because he was too famous to have any. **In private** he was a founder of the rebellion, and his fame was the instrument. His Fulcrum connection was unknown to the public, and he used his celebrity and his mobility — the touring schedule, the invitations, the freedom to be anywhere with a reason — to quietly support resistance efforts.

The founding four are the only associates recorded for him. He was one of the very few who knew that Richard Blaze — the celebrated chef whose restaurants across the continent moved money, supplies and coded messages through menus and supply chains — was Fulcrum's ghost founder. He carried that to his death, on the same train as Blaze, without giving him up. Fulcrum's own file says he died for the bell.

## Origin

Zilspar, a small Breland town outside Sharn, and local stages. From there national acclaim, and from acclaim a fortune's worth of unquestioned access to every city on the continent. Somewhere in that ascent he met Fema Nolan and Digma Beeve and Richard Blaze, and the four of them turned whispered rebellion into a living resistance. Nothing else of his early life is recorded.

## The train

**John Stamos was discovered to be a rebel when a rebel crew infiltrated an imperial Lightningrail train in hopes to steal the Hell's Bell "Fantanya Nyel".** <small>(Also written Fantanya Nyel.)</small> The Empire had dug the bell out of a played-out mine at Greenbluff, moved it under Assembly seal, and shipped it east by lightning rail bound for Korth. Fulcrum boarded that train to take it. Somewhere between Newham and the capital they failed.

**Stamos was killed during the battle by John C. Lebeefe. LeBeefe is in possession of Mr. Stamos's eyeballs.** Richard Blaze was aboard, watched his friend die at the hands of The Unforeseen, and did not break cover. The same journey introduced Blaze to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), whom he would marry.

## The statue

**The Imperial media reported that famous singer Lady Glow was a rebel and terrorist and was responsible for the attack on the Lightningrail and blamed for the death of beloved actor John Stamos.** **A statue commemorating his life and talent was erected in the Imperial capital Korth.** It still stands there today.

The Empire knew Stamos was a rebel. It killed him, blamed a second rebel for his murder, and then built him a monument as a martyr to rebel terrorism. One operation buried two founders of the resistance and produced a piece of public sculpture arguing against them.

## Zero-One

Fema Nolan was a respected Paladin of the Silver Flame from Thrane, known for compassion and discipline. After the death of her companion and fellow founder John Stamos, she undertook an ancient and nearly forgotten Silver Flame rite that strips away one's name and past — the first woman in centuries to do it — and emerged as **Zero-One**. She now leads Fulcrum out of Zilspar.

## How long ago

Two sources date his death differently. The rebel-faction record says *over a decade ago*. Fulcrum's own file on the bells says *twenty years ago* and ties the journey precisely to the recovery at Greenbluff. Greenbluff itself falls about ten years before the Inevitables, which places the rail journey there; both older figures stand as their sources wrote them.

### Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Fema Nolan / Zero-One** — fellow founder and his companion. His death drove her to strip away her name and past. She now leads Fulcrum. Alive.
- **Digma Beeve** — fellow founder, halfling furniture trader of Newham, Last War veteran. Now acting Comptroller of the Republic of Talenta. Alive.
- **Richard Blaze** — fellow founder, the ghost whose name is in no record. Was on the train, held cover, married Esther Crona. Alive.
- **John C. LeBeefe** — killed him and kept his eyes. Later Grand Duke of Newham; later dead at the hands of the Heroes of Leef.
- **Lady Glow** — fellow rebel on the same train, blamed by the Empire for his murder, later killed and raised by Uriel Qualanthri. Dead.
