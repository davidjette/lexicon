**Tiefling · Undead-Warforged Hybrid, Prototype Unit EDN-00 · Project E.D.E.N./Iron Veil · Deployed · Dead, and walking**

*Also known as:* EDN-00 · The Blue Devil

**Eden** is the prototype unit of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), designated **EDN-00** and known in the Mournlands as **the Blue Devil**: a Tiefling woman of Sharn's Lower City, raised from the dead as an undead-Warforged hybrid. She was the youngest of the four daughters of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and half-sister to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Faith](/people/faith/) and [Blair](/people/blair/). Nearly four years before the Ghost Chip theft she was chased through the drainage tunnels by the Warforged **Preacher**, fell from the Grand Aqueduct's upper walkway and was impaled on the machinery below; Esther Crona took her body to [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the Black Doves, and the failed resurrection rite and [Umbra](/items/umbra/) infusion that followed produced the prototype the whole Imperial programme was reverse-engineered from. She is deployed into the Mournlands by [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/)'s Iron Veil airships and remains in the field.

**Disambiguation.** The Imperial programme that brought her back — and then took her name — has its own article: [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) The programme is named for the woman.

## Physical Description

The Hundreds describe her as a blue-skinned, red-eyed, warforged-fused killer deployed by Iron Veil airships. That is the only physical description of her in any record the party holds. The hybrids built after her retain corpse-like skin, mechanical limbs, and masked faces that hide their deteriorating features; whether the prototype shares any of that is not recorded.

## The Family She Was Born Into

Eden was the youngest of the four daughters of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), and the only one who never got the chance to decide what to do about it. [Blair](/people/blair/) took the Underhive and refused the Empire outright. [Faith](/people/faith/) took a nightclub, a vampire husband and a cult. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) took Sharn. The blood relation between the four of them was confirmed years ago by [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/), who established it while studying Embrace at the University of [Newham](/places/newham/) — which means that when a body arrived on her table, she knew exactly whose it was.

## Public Face and Private Truth

There is no public Eden. Nothing in the rebel record gives her a life before her death beyond a name and a family; to the programme she is a designation, **EDN-00**.

What is true is that a Warforged resistance army in the Mournlands has spent years fighting something it calls **the Blue Devil**: blue-skinned, red-eyed, warforged-fused, delivered to the field by Iron Veil airship, and in the flat professional judgement of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s soldiers, unkillable. The Hundreds had no idea they were fighting the Lord Commander's little sister. Until **Preacher** said her name out loud in a lower-city tunnel, neither did anyone else outside the programme.

## Competence and Circle

She is the most effective single asset Project E.D.E.N. has ever fielded, and the only one of its products with a person attached. The programme around her is run by Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), BioTec's head Hybrid Specialist; Supreme Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/), who deploys the EDEN units into the Mournlands by airship; and her own sister, who refused to bring her home. **Sister Nora** of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) made her, and never stopped flinching about it.

## The Secret

Eden did not die in an accident nobody understands. She died because a Warforged chased her. Preacher — the last of his kind hiding in Sharn after the Lord Commander outlawed Warforged in the city — caught a young Tiefling woman who had found his concealed cell, pursued her through the drainage tunnels, and watched her go over the aqueduct's upper walkway onto the machinery below. He believed for years that he had killed her. He founded R.U.S.T.

## What Preacher Recognized — Rebel Mission Board

Nora also revealed that the prototype unit, EDN-00, was created from the resurrected body of Eden, Esther's youngest half-sister. Eden, who died tragically nearly 4 years ago, and Esther in desperation came to Sister Nora for help. This resulted in the Project EDEN prototype, EDN-00. Nora believed Eden's soul was lost.

During the trio's meeting with RUST, Preacher revealed that The Hundreds have been fighting the EDEN units along with a terrifying hybrid soldier they call the Blue Devil; a blue-skinned, red-eyed, warforged-fused killer deployed by Iron Veil airships. The Hundreds believe she is unkillable. Preacher, however, recognized her. Years ago, after the outlawing of the Warforged in Sharn, a young Tiefling woman discovered his hidden cell in the Lower City. Preacher chased her through drainage tunnels, and in the struggle she fell from the aqueduct's upper walkway, becoming impaled on the machinery below. He believed he had killed her.

Only now does he understand the truth: The Blue Devil is Eden, The Lord Commander's sister, resurrected, reforged, and weaponized as EDN-00. This revelation confirms that Project E.D.E.N. is not merely a military program it is a system of body harvesting, resurrection, and forced hybridization, fed by Cogborn disappearances and protected by Iron Veil secrecy.

## Beliefs

She believes nothing that anyone has managed to record; no one has interviewed EDN-00 and reported back.\
She believes, or at minimum obeys without exception, the chain of command that ends at her sister.\
She believes — Faith Zeir insisted on this, to Esther's face, in a room full of Imperial officers — that she is suffering.

## Conduct in the Field

Iron Veil airships put her into the Glass Plateau and she works: killing Hundreds, holding ground no living soldier can hold, covering the extraction of [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) out of irradiated wasteland. Three years after Sharn fell the Eden units were still in the field: **Zero-One** was taken alive by Eden units when Zilspar Farm was destroyed, and the rebel surgeon who heard it wept.

## Origin

She was a young Tiefling woman in the Lower City, of no rank and no protection, in the years after the Lord Commander outlawed the Warforged of Sharn. She found something she was not meant to find — a hidden cell in the Lower City, and the Warforged living in it. She ran. He followed. She went off the Grand Aqueduct's upper walkway and was impaled on the machinery beneath. That was the end of Eden's first life, nearly four years before anyone stole a Ghost Chip out of BioTec.

Her body went to her sister. Esther Crona, who has never once in her life accepted a loss she was not prepared to accept, took it to Sister Nora of the Black Doves and asked for a miracle. What she got was a failed ritual and an [Umbra](/items/umbra/) infusion, and what came off that table was the prototype of an Imperial weapons programme. Everything [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) later became — the corpse pipeline out of the Cogs, the [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) grafts, the Black Dove rites, the mask bolted over every later unit's failing face — is engineering reverse-derived from one desperate night and one incomplete result.

## Right Now

She is over the Mournlands, or in them. Eden units came across the farms at Zilspar and laid them flat, and carried off Zero-One. Faith Zeir is dead and can no longer ask for her. Esther Crona is dead and can no longer refuse. Nobody living holds the authority to bring her home, and nobody has told her that.

**Organizations / Groups:** Project E.D.E.N. (prototype unit, EDN-00) · the Iron Veil, by deployment · the Crona–LeBeefe line, by blood

**Holdings:** None. She owns nothing, and is herself carried on the books as materiel.

**Items:** Warforged components enhanced by BioTec, fused to her body. No inventory survives.

**Signature Capability — Unkillable:** The Hundreds have fought her for years and report as settled fact that she cannot be put down. Later production units self-heal, stand on undead fortitude, articulate on impossible second elbows, and burst into lightning when destroyed; the prototype predates every one of those refinements and is still the one they name.

## What Sister Nora Said Under a Zone of Truth

In a farmhouse cellar at Zilspar Farm 02, her mental block still in her skull and her nose bleeding, Sister Nora told the Inevitables what she had done. She named Esther's sisters — Faith, Blair and Eden. She named [the Eternal Nightfall](/lore/the-eternal-nightfall/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s doctrine of endless resurrection and a world remade in undeath. And she gave the only honest clinical assessment of EDN-00 that exists:

> "She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her… but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes — but an incomplete one. A soul even Uriel may not reach."\
> — Sister Nora, under interrogation, Zilspar Farm 02

## "Bring Her Home"

At the opening of POSÉ, Faith Zeir crossed the room to her sister and asked for exactly one thing, and it was not power:

> **Faith:** "Esther, bring her home, it's eating at her. She should not suffer any longer. Bring her home. The project is ready."\
> **Esther:** "She stays until YOUR husband and his slut brother increase supply."

<small>Faith Zeir and Malrik Zeir are both dead. The supply they were being leaned on to increase was Umbra. In that exchange Eden was a hostage, held against her own sisters' production quota.</small>

- **Faith Zeir** — half-sister. Argued for her release, publicly, at POSÉ. Dead.

- **Sister Nora** — Black Dove cleric who performed the resurrection rite and judged it incomplete. Executed after the Sharn rising.
- **Dr. Joanne Menka** — head Hybrid Specialist, BioTec; maintained her and the units built from her. Executed after the Sharn rising.

- **Locke Pierce** and the Hundreds — her opponents in the Glass Plateau, who named her the Blue Devil and believe her unkillable.
