# Eden

**Tiefling · Undead-Warforged Hybrid · Prototype Unit EDN-00 · Project E.D.E.N. / Iron Veil · Deployed · Dead, and walking**

*Also known as:* **EDN-00, Subject Zero, the Blue Devil**

Eden was once a young, idealistic tiefling who believed in the Empire she served. At eighteen, she was recruited as an Imperial researcher, tasked with investigating the Mournlands and the growing threat posed by the Hundreds in Sharn. She was bright, bubbly, and genuinely loyal to the Empire, with little reason to imagine that her own government would eventually turn her into one of its most terrifying weapons.

After a brutal attack left her dying, her sister Esther Crona refused to accept her death. A forbidden resurrection attempt brought Eden back, but not whole. She became the prototype for [Project E.D.E.N.](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), an Imperial programme that transformed the dead into undead-Warforged hybrids capable of operating in environments ordinary soldiers could not survive.

Now known as the Blue Devil of the Mournlands, Eden is one of the Empire's most effective military assets. Whether the person who returned is truly the same Eden who died remains uncertain.

![Eden - The Blue Devil 3.png](/images/uploads/eden-the-blue-devil-3-mu0x2af4.webp)
*Eden — The Blue Devil*

## Physical Description

Before her death, Eden was a short, blue-skinned tiefling with white hair and an expressive, youthful appearance. Her bright personality made her seem almost out of place among the political intrigues and military ambitions surrounding her family.

Her transformation left her body partially necrotic and fundamentally altered. She is neither wholly organic nor a conventional Warforged, but a hybrid of the dead and the mechanical. The original restoration was imperfect, and the programme that grew out of it developed increasingly sophisticated methods of binding undead bodies to mechanical systems.

In the field, Eden's movements can be unnervingly abrupt. She may land with impossible softness before snapping her head toward a sound or target. Her voice is layered: a mechanical tone overlain by a sweeter, synthetic voice that recalls the person she once was. The contrast is particularly unsettling when she speaks with the calmness of someone who no longer understands the limits of her own humanity.

## Family and Early Life

Eden was the youngest of four half-sisters, all daughters of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), each born to a different mother. Her older sisters were [Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/), Faith, and [Blair](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/blair/).

Eden spent her early life with her mother in the Demon Waste. After her mother died, she was found by [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/), who arranged for her to have a home in Newham through LeBeefe. She was not sold into slavery, and her early life was distinct from the experiences of some of her sisters.

![LeBeefe's Daughters - Esther, Faith, Eden and Blair. First time meeting.png](/images/uploads/lebeefe-s-daughters-esther-faith-eden-an-mv65jtry.webp)
*LeBeefe's Daughters - Esther, Faith, Eden and Blair*

Eventually, Eden met her sisters, and the women travelled together to Sharn. Their lives soon diverged. Esther became an influential figure within the Empire, Faith's life became entangled with Malrik Zeir and the Children of Ember, and Blair made her way through the thieves' guilds and piracy before establishing herself in the Lower City and becoming Queen B of the Underhive.

Eden took a different path. She remained idealistic and embraced the Empire's promise of order and progress. Her intelligence and loyalty eventually led her into Imperial research, where her family connections helped open doors that might otherwise have remained closed.

## The Imperial Researcher

Eden was recruited as an Imperial researcher in part because she was Esther's sister. Her superiors considered her a promising scientist whose family ties made her likely to be loyal, discreet, and useful to the state.

Her work brought her into contact with the consequences of the Last War and the continuing instability of the Mournlands. She was assigned to study the region, monitor Warforged migration, and investigate the resurgence of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and the movement known as [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/). Her work included examining the dangers of the Mournlands and the military implications of the forces gathering there.

![Eden in Sharn - Young.png](/images/uploads/eden-in-sharn-young-mv65iobt.webp)
*Eden in Sharn*

Eden's investigation eventually led her to a Hundreds cell operating in Sharn. Graffiti bearing the words **“THE HUNDREDS REMEMBER,”** stolen shipments, and rumours of a Warforged preacher spreading Locke Pierce's message through the Cogs pointed toward a hidden workshop beneath the Lower City. The location served as a place where Warforged could receive repairs and where opposition to the Empire could organize beyond the reach of ordinary authorities.

Eden's research brought her too close to the truth. The cell discovered her identity, and her Imperial badge and research notes made her affiliation clear. 

## The Attack and the Resurrection

The Hundreds cell attacked Eden and her research team. A Warforged fist shattered her sternum, and a blade left her incapacitated. Her colleagues were slaughtered, and Eden was abandoned barely alive. Before leaving, the attackers carved a message into the scene:

**“THE HUNDREDS REMEMBER.”**

The attack was not simply an attempt to silence an Imperial researcher. It was a message intended for Esther.

Eden managed to send a broken Sending before she lost consciousness. Esther teleported into the Lower City and found her sister dying. She carried Eden to the [Sanctum of the Last Mercy](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/), the Black Dove hospital, and begged its clerics to save her.

The head cleric, [Sister Nora](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/sister-nora/), had been transferred from Newham to Sharn. Esther demanded that the Black Doves attempt a modified version of the Last Mercy rite she had witnessed [Zero-One, Fema Nolan](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/), perform on [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). She was unwilling to accept that Eden could not be saved and threatened the clerics when they hesitated.

Three senior Black Dove clerics channelled the rite. [Dr. Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/) monitored Eden's vital decay while Nora recorded the procedure. A Khyber-cut diamond was embedded in Eden's sternum to replace her destroyed heart. This differed from Uriel's restoration, in which the diamond was placed at the base of the skull.

The ritual did not restore Eden as she had been. It was a failed or incomplete resurrection, and her returned body was infused with [Umbra](/items/umbra/). She woke confused and cold, speaking in fragments and whispering Locke's name. She seemed to hear voices associated with the Warforged and repeated a phrase that echoed the message left by her attackers:

**“The Hundreds remember… the land remembers…”**

Her body remained partially necrotic. Her mind, soul, and sense of identity were uncertain. It was impossible to know whether Eden's original self had survived intact, whether the rite had left her connected to something else, or whether the voices she seemed to hear were only fragments of memory.

## Subject Zero: The Birth of Project E.D.E.N.

Eden's survival became the foundation of [Project E.D.E.N.](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), an Imperial programme designed to create undead-Warforged hybrids capable of operating in cursed and arcane-scarred environments. Eden was designated **EDN-00**, or **Subject Zero**: the prototype from which the programme developed.

The programme's name is associated with *Empire's Design for Enemy Neutralization*. A broader Imperial doctrine also used the title *Empire Directive for Eternal Nightfall*. These names reflect the programme's place within a wider military effort to turn the dead and the mechanically enhanced into instruments of Imperial power.

Eden's initial restoration was an imperfect foundation. Later development drew on the work of the [BioTec] (https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/biotec/) Hybridization Division, the Black Dove Church, House Lyrandar, and the Imperial military and [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/). The programme combined necromantic rites, mechanical assemblies, arcane binding agents, and stabilizing compounds derived from Embra.

The resulting units were designed for environments where living soldiers were at a severe disadvantage. They could operate in cursed regions such as the Mournlands and the Glass Plateau, withstand many effects that made those places hostile to ordinary personnel, and retrieve or transport dangerous materials without the same vulnerabilities as conventional troops.

Later units incorporated Warforged limb assemblies, Formax-9, Redline Circuitry, and Neural Regulator Masks intended to suppress identity bleed-through and reinforce obedience. Their bodies were often visibly corpse-like, with mechanical limbs and masks concealing decaying faces. Stabilizers reduced further decay, while the masks and regulators attempted to keep the remnants of the original person from interfering with military function.

These refinements belonged to the wider programme and should not all be attributed to Eden's original prototype. Her restoration came first; the later units were built from what Imperial researchers learned from her.

The programme also relied on a supply of bodies, including corpses sourced from the Cogs and rites associated with the Black Doves. Despite increasingly sophisticated controls, signs of memory bleed-through persisted. Records describe EDN-31 attempting to speak Valenar Elvish and EDN-07 staring at the Glass Plateau for eighty-nine minutes while whispering, “The land remembers.”

Eden's own condition remains the unresolved question at the centre of the programme: whether its prototype was successfully brought back, or whether the Empire built an army around a miracle that never fully worked.

## The Blue Devil of the Mournlands

Eden was deployed into the Mournlands aboard the Iron Veil airships of [Supreme Admiral Vex d’Lyrandar](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/). There, she became one of the Empire's most effective assets in the war against the Hundreds.

Her hybrid body allowed her to operate across the Glass Plateau and other dangerous areas where ordinary Imperial troops could not hold ground. She fought the Hundreds, supported military operations, and helped secure the extraction of [Red Khyber](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/items/red-khyber/). Her endurance and apparent inability to remain dead made her a formidable opponent.

Among the Hundreds, she became known as the **Blue Devil**. The name reflected not only her blue skin but the fear surrounding a soldier who could continue fighting despite injuries that would kill an ordinary person. Her existence also carried a bitter irony: she had once been sent to investigate the Hundreds, and now she was one of the Empire's weapons against them.

The Warforged preacher known as [Preacher](/people/preacher/), associated with [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), later recognized Eden as the Blue Devil. Her identity linked the Imperial researcher who had investigated the Hundreds to the undead hybrid now fighting them.

## “Bring Her Home”

Eden's deployment was not simply a military decision. Her continued use was entangled with the interests of her family and the supply of Umbra.

During the events at [POSÉ](/places/pose/), Faith pleaded with Esther to bring Eden home. Faith understood that Eden was suffering and wanted her sister removed from the programme.

> **Faith:** Esther, bring her home, it's eating at her. She should not suffer any longer. Bring her home. The project is ready.
>
> **Esther:** She stays until YOUR husband and his slut brother increase supply.

The supply in question was Umbra. Esther's answer made clear that Eden's fate had become tied to production demands and the interests surrounding the substance. The sister who had refused to let Eden die was now keeping her in service because the programme and its supply chain demanded it.

For Eden, the transformation was not only physical. She had once believed in the Empire and volunteered her intelligence in its service. Now she was bound to an institution that treated her body as a military resource, while even her family struggled to separate their love for her from their investment in the project.

## The Peak at King's Forest

At Korth, during the events recorded in [“The Red Bird”](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/), Eden appeared in the valley below the Summer Palace with twenty E.D.E.N. units. They were described as **“all that I could bring back.”**

An appointment had been arranged for dawn on a peak in the King's Forest with General [Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/). The note arranging the meeting was written in a strange hand and signed with the initial **E**. By the time of the meeting, Thar had already been killed by Gemma Corso and Eric the Cleric, who approached the encounter disguised as Thar and a tiefling druid.

Eden's soldiers climbed toward the peak. Their weapons were built into their bodies, and small oculus scanners watched their surroundings. Eden herself descended from the sky and landed softly. Her head jerked sharply as she looked toward the people waiting for her, and her voice emerged in two layers: a mechanical voice overlaid with a sweeter synthetic one.

She spoke as though she had come to offer a choice, but her words quickly revealed the scale of her ambitions.

> **Eden:** There are twenty Eden units in the valley below. It's all that I could bring back. Like I said, you could be destroyed, or you could see the truth.
>
> **Eden:** The Empress must die.
>
> **Eden:** You told me the Emperor would agree with us. You are a fool.
>
> **Eden:** If you get in my way, I will destroy everything you love.
>
> **Eden:** They have my sister.
>
> **Eden:** We will rule the Empire together. You will retreat to your forests. If you are here tomorrow, you will die.
>
> **Eden:** I could survive his onslaught. Can you?

Eden's words suggested that she had come to a conclusion about the Empire and its leadership. She wanted the Empress dead and was furious that the Emperor had not supported the plan she had expected him to accept. She offered her audience the chance to recognize what she called the truth, but threatened to destroy everything they loved if they stood in her way.

The most revealing moment came when she said, **“They have my sister.”** When told that her sister had been risen, Eden responded, **“We will rule the Empire together.”** The exchange suggests that she believed her sister had been brought back and was being held somewhere. Esther is a possible referent, but the identity of the sister Eden meant was not confirmed.

Her statement also raised questions about how much of the original Eden remained. She spoke of ruling the Empire with her sister, but it was unclear whether this ambition came from Eden's own surviving will, the altered state created by her resurrection, or some combination of memory, programming, and other influence.

After the exchange, Eden laughed in a flat, repetitive manner and descended the mountain to rejoin her soldiers. The units withdrew from the peak, leaving the threat hanging over the forest.

## Right Now

Eden's last confirmed appearance was in the valley and on the peak in the King's Forest, where she arrived with twenty E.D.E.N. units and declared that the Empress must die. Her current whereabouts are unknown.

Three years after the fall of Sharn, E.D.E.N. units remained active in the field. During the destruction of Zilspar Farm 02, [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) was captured alive by E.D.E.N. units. The wider programme continued to operate even after the deaths of Faith, Malrik Zeir, and Esther Crona. Sister Nora and Dr. Joanne Menka were later executed after the Sharn rising, taking with them much of the direct testimony surrounding Eden's resurrection.

Eden's stated intention to rule the Empire with her sister remains unresolved. It is unknown which sister she meant, what she believed had happened to that person, or whether her plan was her own. The Blue Devil is still a weapon created by the Empire, but her words at King's Forest suggest that she may no longer be acting in accordance with the purposes for which she was made.

## What Sister Nora Knew

Sister Nora's account is one of the few direct testimonies about Eden's resurrection and its limits. During questioning under a Zone of Truth at Zilspar Farm 02, Nora described Eden as more alive than the hybrids created after her, but fundamentally incomplete.

> “She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her… but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes — but an incomplete one. A soul even Uriel may not reach.”

Nora believed Eden's soul might be lost or beyond Uriel's reach. Her words were a judgement based on what she had witnessed, not proof that Eden's soul was absent. The uncertainty remains central to Eden's story: the rite brought her body back, but no one can say with certainty what returned with it.

## Personality and Characteristics

**Before her death:** Eden was bubbly, bright, idealistic, and genuinely loyal to the Empire. She believed her work could contribute to a better future and did not approach Imperial service with the cynicism that shaped some of her family members.

**After her resurrection:** Her speech became fragmented and her behaviour occasionally unsettling. She appeared to hear voices associated with the Warforged and repeated phrases about the Hundreds and the land. Her later statements at King's Forest showed purpose, anger, and ambition, but it remains unclear how these traits relate to her former personality.

**As the Blue Devil:** Eden is a formidable combatant capable of operating in environments hostile to living soldiers. Her mechanical enhancements, necrotic body, and apparent resilience make her difficult to stop. She can move with unnatural precision and deploy alongside E.D.E.N. units as both a military asset and a symbol of the programme's power.

## Signature Capability

Eden's defining capability is her ability to operate as an undead-Warforged hybrid in cursed and arcane-scarred environments, particularly the Mournlands. She can withstand conditions that limit conventional troops and participate in operations involving dangerous materials such as Red Khyber.

Her capabilities are not proof that the resurrection succeeded completely. The programme's later stabilizers and control systems improved the performance of subsequent units, but signs of memory bleed-through continued to appear in their records.
