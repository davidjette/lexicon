**Human · Nobleman / Chief Diplomat · Council of 13 · Mayor of Sharn · Dead**

*Also known as:* Minister Henry Heighrick · Mayor Heinrick

Henry Heinrick was a human nobleman, the Empire's Chief Diplomat and head of the Ministry of Diplomacy, a member of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/), and Mayor of Sharn. A Brelish boy who rose through charm, wit and deceit, he attached himself to the Imperial State after the war as the Brelish representative in the new bureaucracy and took a seat among the thirteen heads of government. He was the only member of the Council of 13 to survive the purge [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) carried out after [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s murder, and was installed as Mayor of Sharn and architect of the city's propaganda machine. At Sharn Ep 18 he broadcast the city-wide emergency alert announcing the lockdown, then went up to the penthouse of the Veil Building, where Esther Crona killed him on the balcony.

## Physical description

The Heinrick family stands as one of Sharn's most carefully curated public faces — polished, powerful, and quietly feared — and Henry was the face they curated first: a man who *shapes the city's narrative with a smile that never reaches his eyes.* <small>(Key Figures, written while he lived.)</small> No further physical particulars are recorded.

## The Ministry of Diplomacy

Henry Heinrick ran the Empire's diplomatic service. On the roster of [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) he is *Minister Henry Heighrick — Human Nobleman: Chief diplomat and head of the ministry of diplomacy*, one of thirteen government heads who met in the tower in Korth called the Imperial Assembly. He held that seat under [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) alongside Varyn Crona, and he was still holding it on the day Varyn died. His peers on the Council were the Pope of the Sovereign Host, the General of the Imperial army, the Supreme Justice of Karrnath, the Minister of Media and Propaganda, and Varyn Crona himself.

## The purge

When Varyn was murdered in a cave outside Fairhaven and the official story blamed rebels, rebel field analysis reached the opposite conclusion: the Council had done it. Esther Crona, apparently with the Emperor's blessing, hunted down and executed every member of that Council and, in several cases, their immediate families. Henry Heinrick alone walked out of it, and was installed as Mayor of Sharn. Rebel intelligence recorded that survival as politically significant: *"His continued survival is considered politically significant and remains under review."*

## Public and private

Publicly, Henry Heinrick was the Mayor of Sharn: a civic figure at official events, the man whose policies received the flattering coverage of **Candice Kurt** on *Serving Sharn Nightly* — she maintained personal ties to the Heinricks and was often seen at their private gatherings. Privately he was the architect of the city's propaganda machine.

## Competence and circle

His wife, [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/), is equally formidable: a poised, calculating operator who commands the Arcane Eye surveillance network, Sharn division, from within the Veil Building. Their daughter, eight-year-old [Chelsea](/people/chelsea-heinrick/), is the lone soft edge in the household, often seen playing with [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) during official events. The household held both the mayoralty and the ArcEyes.

## The Velvet Table Society

Correspondence recovered from the cult chambers beneath The Vault Below records Henry Heinrick joining **"The Velvet Table Society,"** which meets at the upper-city Vault location. The same cache held letters between [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) and his half-brother Malrik Zeir about Umbra and its connection to Project EDEN. The Velvet Table's purpose is not recorded; it met in the same building where the Children of Ember kept blood cattle in the cellar.

**Rumour.** It is rumored that he has carried on an affair with the wives and girlfriends of several other Assembly members. <small>Filed among the Assembly gossip the rebellion collected and never managed to corroborate.</small>

## Conduct in role

On the night Esther Crona seized [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) and Calcifer from the roof of the Vault Underground, it was Henry Heinrick who broadcast the city-wide emergency alert announcing a full lockdown across Sharn — the last civic act of the Empire in that city. Then he went up to the penthouse to stand beside her.

## The balcony

Sharn Ep 18. The lockdown was already running when Richard Blaze led [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) up through Iron Veil guards into the penthouse at the Veil Building. On the balcony stood Esther, her son, her two masked Honor Guards, Gemma unchained and terrified — and Henry Heinrick. Dario triggered the EBT-7 and broadcast the recovered memory of Calcifer's birth to the entire city. The memory shattered Esther's composure. In her rage she revealed the true faces of her Honor Guards — undead members of the Council of 13 — and killed Henry Heinrick where he stood.

The last surviving member of the Thirteen was killed by the woman who killed the other twelve, in front of the other twelve, on live broadcast.

> "…the sole survivor is Henry Heinrick, former Chief Diplomat and head of the Ministry of Diplomacy, now serving as Mayor of Sharn. His continued survival is considered politically significant and remains under review."\
> \
> — Rebel intelligence assessment, Crona–Blaze family file

## Right now

Henry Heinrick is dead and the office he held is gone with him. Within a day of his death the first Council of Sharn convened in the same penthouse, Eric was appointed Chancellor, and Sharn stopped having a mayor at all. His wife was taken into custody at the Veil Building and executed after trial. His daughter is eight years old and appears in no subsequent record.
