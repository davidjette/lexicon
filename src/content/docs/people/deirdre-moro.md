**Species unrecorded · Star Advocate, Ministry of Law · The Empire · Dead — killed by the Inevitables**

*Also known as:* Advocate Moro · Deedra Mora · Deirdre Mora · (worn as a cover identity by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/))

**Deirdre Moro** was a Star Advocate of the Ministry of Law in [Korth](/places/korth/), a high advocate carrying LAZARET clearance whose speciality was crimes against records. Born to the Moro family, she stayed when her younger sister [Bantide](/people/bantide-moro/) ran away to an anti-war religion, and she assembled the evidence that brought Bantide before the High Court on a capital brief. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) tracked her for months, killed her and her paralegal in Deirdre's own apartment, and burned both bodies at the bottom of the bridge with the orc dead; her face, her post and her clearance are now being used to reach the Ivory Lazaret.

## Physical description

Enough of a family resemblance to her younger sister [Bantide](/people/bantide-moro/) that people who had studied one face recognised the other across a courtroom. In the field she wore Ministry dress and Ministry manners, and she had a particular salute that [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) copied exactly. <small>Her colouring and build are not on any record the party kept; the only surviving likeness of her is the one Gemma wears.</small>

## The Ministry of Law

The Ministry of Law stands in the northeast of [Korth](/places/korth/), next door to [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/), with a separate side entrance for officers and upper floors full of advocates. Laws are made and negotiated there, capital trials are heard in [the High Court](/places/the-high-court/) on an upper level, and the jail sits in the northeast corner of the same complex. Its advocates do not defend anyone — under [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) there is no such office. They prosecute, and the panel argues back.

## Origin

Born to the Moro family, with a younger sister who rebelled against it and ran away to an anti-war religion. Deirdre stayed. She rose through the Ministry of Law to Star Advocate, took the Empire's most sensitive category of prosecution as her speciality, and acquired the clearances of somebody being groomed for more. No record the Inevitables recovered says whether she ever wrote back.

## Star Advocate

Deirdre Moro's papers named her a high advocate of the Ministry — functionally a federal agent. The standing let her prosecute capital cases before the High Court panel, investigate very nearly any Imperial facility, **seize records on her own authority** with the instrument already in hand, and travel on Ministry business to restricted sites. Her real credentials carried **LAZARET clearance**.

Her speciality was **crimes against records**: falsification, forgery, embezzlement of Imperial goods, conspiracy to defraud official documents. She prosecuted the people Imperial auditors caught. She was feared, exacting, and above all a winner — her superior respected her precisely for a killer instinct he said he was trying to cultivate — and she was, in the small ways, insufferable. She yelled at baristas.

## Competence and circle

Her superior was Vice Admiral **Freyd Merkin**, who supplied her briefs, set her deadlines, and answered to unnamed betters. Her paralegal was [Graham Cracker](/people/graham-cracker/), who carried her preliminary material to her apartment. She lived in an Empire-owned tower on the lower plaza — one shared bathroom to a floor, like everyone else at the centre of Korth. She had a sister she had not spoken to in a very long time, and twenty years of that sister's letters.

## What she did to her sister

Bantide was told in custody that **Deirdre assembled the evidence against her**, and believes it. The party thinks she is right. What tipped the Empire's suspicion onto Bantide in the first place was proximity — *"being that close to the Emperor is why your suspicion was laid on you"* — and what turned suspicion into a capital brief was twenty years of private correspondence, mocking Imperial haircuts and ritual and hierarchy, that only one person in the world had ever received.

## Conduct in role

Exacting, feared, procedurally immaculate and personally unpleasant — a woman who ran her office by pressure and who did not need to raise her voice at a Force Captain because the papers did it for her. She took *little jaunts*, claimed to be working from home, and was not always working; Merkin knew it, did not fully trust her, and had begun to say so. Gemma's impersonation held up in front of the man who signed her orders.

> "You've managed to wriggle out of it, and you don't seem to hold any grudge. It's almost as if you considered it fair play, and that's the instinct I'm trying to cultivate. It's a killer instinct." — Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin, to the woman he thought was Deirdre Moro

## The Docket

**The State v. Bantide Moro** — delivered as a sealed folder under her own door, on Imperial letterhead from Merkin's desk, bearing only the note *"Where are you?"* The condemned's name was redacted throughout and every identifying passage struck. It was a loyalty test, and Merkin admitted as much afterward: *"I thought this would be a chance for you to show your absolute allegiance and commitment to the Empire. Was I wrong?"* Adjourned one month.

**The Grand Lodge** — handed over in person the moment the trial ended. Travel to the Summer Palace, the Ivory Lazaret and the surrounding grounds; seize all records of the Grand Lodge — guests, names, dates; arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates. The file carries a likeness of Captain Joseph L. Roberts. Due Monday, with a bounty attached to failure.

**The casualty-reporting purge** — a ministry-wide directive found in Graham's preliminary material, opening mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties, with executions expected over the following weeks. Bantide's case is one of them.

## Right now

Dead. Gemma Corso spent months tracking her specifically — where she lived, what she did on a Wednesday evening, how she spoke to baristas, how she saluted — then killed her and her paralegal in Deirdre's own apartment, put both bodies in a Bag of Holding, and burned them at the bottom of the bridge with the orc dead. Her face, her post and her clearance are now being used to reach the Ivory Lazaret.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Ministry of Law, Korth (Star Advocate) · the Empire

**Holdings:** An Empire-owned apartment on the lower plaza of Korth, now a safe house for the sister she condemned.

**Items:** Ministry papers carrying authority to seize records and LAZARET clearance — the instrument that walked the Korranberg Foundry Trust's manifest out of a locked office, and the party's authorised route to the Empress.

## Beliefs

She believed that everything she did was for the good of the Emperor, and her handler thought her too credulous for exactly that reason.

She believed she had to win — more than anything, above the merits, above her family — and everyone who knew her knew it.

She believed her own conduct was fair play, including the parts of it that were done to her, which her superior called a killer instinct and admired.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

Winning, and being seen to have won by the Empire.

**Likes & Dislikes**

- Clearances other advocates do not have

- Her assistant misplacing things

**Vices & Personality flaws**

The compulsion to win was total, and it is the thing that gave the impersonation away to the only person who loved her.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Bantide Moro** — younger sister, the condemned, and almost certainly framed by her. Alive and in hiding; she does not know Deirdre is dead by the party's hand until they tell her.
- **Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin** — superior at the Ministry of Law. Supplies her briefs, suspects her jaunts, respects her instincts. Alive and hostile.
- **Graham Cracker** — her paralegal. Dead, killed alongside her.
- **Gemma Corso** — tracked her for months, fought her to the death, and wears her face. Alive.
- **Captain Joseph L. Roberts** — a likeness in her last case file; the Dread Pirate the Empire wants arrested at the Grand Lodge. Alive.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown.
