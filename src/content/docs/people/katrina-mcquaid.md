**Tabaxi · Ranger 5 / Fighter 3 / Rogue 3 · Captain, Astral Command · Dead**

*Also known as:* Catrina · Captain Catrina McQuade · Kat · Cat · Grey Cloak Catrina

Captain **Katrina McQuaid** is a tabaxi ranger, horizon walker and swashbuckler of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) and a grey cloak of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/) in [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by Nico. She trained at the Astral Command Academy alongside [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/), and as a two-star captain was technically the captain of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) while Jim commanded it. In [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/) she chose to recover [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/) with Horgus over saving the civilians stranded with Jim on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/). She fought in the finale of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, and by the time of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* she was dead and memorialised in the name of [a prison in Shadowspace](/places/the-katrina-mcquaid-memorial-prison/).

## Background

Her name appears as Katrina McQuaid and as Catrina; her character sheet names her Captain Catrina McQuade. <small>(character sheet; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> The sheet gives her background as Criminal / Spy. <small>(character sheet)</small> Her birthplace and age are unknown.

## Light of Xaryxis

In the Academy flashback of 1 April 2023, run by JL, the young Smallberries, Catrina and Horgus were at the Academy together. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01; 2023-04-04)</small> Smallberries is a one-star captain and Catrina a two-star captain, which makes her technically the captain of the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01)</small> In fact Jim commands the ship, and the crew roster names him its Sovereign Officer. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

In the session of 14 April 2023, which brought tension over the Xaryxis mission, Horgus and Katrina were a nasty pair. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-14)</small> Blastimov, aboard the Zanzibar, knew Cat and Horgus's secret. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-22)</small>

## The Beholder War

The third season, from February 2024, brought Katrina and Horgus back, tracing Jim's story to its origin. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> The season opened with a three-episode flashback to the Beholder War. In its second episode Jim was stranded on Negroni Prime with [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), Tillamook and a dozen civilians in the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, and the Demoncore weapons system was used for the first time. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09; 2024-03-16)</small>

Katrina chose to recover the Demoncore over saving those people. She was a horizon walker, and Horgus a necromancer, to support a secret program that was only ever used once. Their story diverging from Jim's mirrors the divergence of Astral Command from its righteous path, and leads to Jim's disillusionment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> Her player recounted that Kat and Horgus left the group with no transport and no way to communicate and took their artillery droid, [1.0](/people/1-0/), though Kat gave Jim the sending stone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

Jim's memories were changed with a potion of modify memory after he last saw Katrina and Horgus, and any knowledge he gained of the Demoncore would probably be erased by OSR3G. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

## The finale

By May 2024 her player had her at tenth level as a grey cloak, with five levels of horizon walker, three of swashbuckler and two of fighter. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-03)</small> Her character sheet gives eleven levels: ranger 5 (Horizon Walker), fighter 3 (Battle Master) and rogue 3 (Swashbuckler). <small>(character sheet)</small>

Before the finale of 31 May 2024 she received a rare Barrier Tattoo. Horgus upgraded his tattoo and Katrina's, and Jim's matching tattoo was upgraded with them, without his knowledge. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

After the finale her player joked that she was probably now a super zombie robot. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

By the time of *The Starsong Awakens*, fifteen years later, Katrina is dead. The Company's dossier for the mission names the Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center in her memory. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small>

The dossier describes [the Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center](/places/the-katrina-mcquaid-memorial-prison/) as a massive prison complex in Shadowspace, active and at maximum capacity. It houses prisoner LL9807, [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/), whom the dossier calls "the traitor of the [Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/)". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Her shortbow is a two-handed plasma rifle. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-05)</small> Her character sheet arms her with a +1 rapier, a pistol, a light crossbow and two shortswords, and gives her scale mail and the Barrier Tattoo. The sheet's notes read "Fades on command / orange shaddy workmanship" (sic). She can detect planar portals and strike with force as a horizon walker, and fights with the Battle Master's parry, riposte and trip attack. She is proficient with calligrapher's supplies, navigator's tools, playing cards and thieves' tools, speaks Common, Deep Speech, Elvish, Hadozee and Thieves' Cant, and as a tabaxi has claws and a climbing speed. <small>(character sheet)</small>
