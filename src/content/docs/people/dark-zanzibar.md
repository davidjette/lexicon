**Species unknown · Zanzibar of another loop · Temple Holdings LLC · Player character (JL) · Erased**

*Also known as:* Dark Zan · Future Zan · future Zanzibar

**Dark Zanzibar** is the [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) who was left behind when the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) left [Refuge](/places/refuge/) for the past, and who survived that loop. He went forward with Max and the sea elves to fight the Drow and the mind flayers for [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and there [Blackstaff](/people/khelben-arunsun/) sent him back in time, with Max, to stop [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). He reached [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) just before the party's first visit to the deep past; he and Maximillien entered, at the cost of Max's life, and he freed [Chronocypher](/people/stonecypher/) from the Zoth matrix. He travelled with the party through the rest of the past quest, and at the end he was erased. Dave: "Dark Zan is kind of erased because his timeline never “happens”".

## The loop he came from

The party teleported out of the Winking Depths arc Dave had built and went to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), where they played a political intrigue game instead. The war below came later, after the party left Refuge for the past. Zanzibar was left behind, and in that loop he went forward to fight beside the sea elves against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach the Egg. At the Egg, Blackstaff sent him back in time with Max to stop St. Cloud. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small>

The sea-elf prince Atlas IV fought beside them: "the Triton prince you met in the winking depth and who become the third wheel to Max + Zan in the offscreen future war VS the spiderbros". <small>(Sic on "become". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> In that war Zanzibar fought beside the water elves and Prince Atlas against the Drow in the Winking Depths, which is how he earned [Glassbane](/items/the-bane-blades/) by deed. The sea elves' pledge on their Ally Card: "You are the true bearers of Glassbane." See [Atlas and the Sea Elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/).

Dave's loose dating has "future Zan lives all the way to 2100 before coming back". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> Among the Anachron's stored time points is "the far future" from which Dark Zan and a Dark Max came to find the party in the ancient past. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-02)</small>

## Blackstaff's crystal

He came back on another instance of [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/): "Zan used Anachron “2”to come back using a dark crystal provided by Blackstaff". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small> Dave on the crystal:

> Fun fact: The black crystal at the end of Blackstaff’s iconic staff was the crystal he procured for you which you never came to retrieve, because St Cloud killed you in the deep past. He kept it his whole multi thousand year life until he gave it to Zanzibar to go back to the deep past to save you. He didn’t save the old you, but met with the next you and went forward in time and ended up meeting him and getting the crystal as planned.

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-07-11)</small>

## R'lyeh

He arrived at R'lyeh just before the party's first time in the deep past. He and Maximillien entered, sacrificing Max's life, and he freed Chronocypher from being attached to the Zoth matrix, where she had been turbocharged with the God power. From then on she was the three-fold goddess, the Sisters. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small> See [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/).

> Just sitting here thinking about how Zanzibar started as a racist rich guy but  found out too late that his enemy was right, thought all his friends were dead, led a war to stop the end of the world, failed, carried on for years in total terror, earned the brotherhood of his enemies, traveled back in time, then watched his closest friend and ally salute and sacrifice his life to open the door of Rlyeh, moments before his own twin brother did the exact same thing, just to rescue Stonecypher and then go with her to truly save the world after all

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-19)</small>

## With the party in the past

The Zanzibar who travelled the rest of the past quest with the party is Dark Zanzibar. The Zanzibar of the party's own time "didn’t go on the time hopping adventure with you all, he left after the River Lis and you guys went off with Zeb!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> Dave called that other one "Zanzibar Prime", in the present. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

JL, who played him, gave his own account in the Time of Troubles: "After you guys left I commanded an army of wood elves and celestial (to miserable defeat)", and, asked whether he had thought the party left him, "I assumed you died like everyone else". JL also wanted a name for Dark Zan to call Eloise, and Dave offered "“Star” is a high title in Elvish". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2020-07-26)</small> JL on sneaking into Netheril: "I’m rather loud when I walk. Or I could leave my suit behind but someone needs to carry me. I’m paralyzed." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-09-26)</small>

In the Time of Troubles Zanzibar looked into [Calix](/people/calix-the-betrayer/)'s [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> Dave set out the runes of Glassbane for him that day. For Zanzibar's own rune he proposed the elvish word "belaw", Friend, "in the tradition of Tolkien “speak friend, and enter”", letting him cast Knock at will in honor of Max's sacrifice to open the gates of R'lyeh: "At the very least it should honor Zanzibar’s journey to becoming a friend of elves". The other runes came from the kings of Atlantis, all named Atlas, and Dave described him in his suit as "an avenging angel of Time, size Large". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

He was the only member of the party never cloned. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-03-28)</small>

## The final arc

By September 2022 he carried St. Cloud's ring. Dave: "Wow Zanzibar is probably invincible now that he has St Clouds ring", which gave "3 legendary resistances per day". <small>(Sic on "St Clouds". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small> He fought the Tarrasque at [Dragon Mountain](/places/dragon-mountain/). JL: "Zan doesn’t care if he lives or dies. In fact he’d prefer to die. But it has to be fighting his hardest." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small>

Dave: "both Revan and Zanzibar turned down offers from the devil to have everything they ever wanted which was a nice surprise". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-04-23)</small>

For the finale Dave proposed that "if you guys rescue the Short King from the Moon you could leave Dark Zan in the egg and play Brownsleeves". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-25)</small> JL played [the Short King](/people/brownsleeves/) in the finale. The Ally Card "Zanzibar, King of Punis" is the other Zanzibar: "Dark Zan is a sep guy, This is Zan the King". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-07)</small> See [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## Erased

After the finale Nico made pictures of Zan and Stonecypher on the train, and JL wrote "Basically they’re stuck in purgatory together forever". Dave:

> The train is a metaphor, but no unless you’re erasing them from almost all memory
>
> Dark Zan is kind of erased because his timeline never “happens”
>
> And it’s poetic

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-15)</small>

Of Blackstaff, Nico wrote: "Even though he worked closely with future Zanzibar he would have forgotten him". Dave: "Zan did yeah, but Blackstaff obviously has advantages in being able to remember or somewhat understand what maybe happened". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small>

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Maximillien (Max)** — Zanzibar's steward, who came back in time with him and gave his life to open R'lyeh.
- **Blackstaff** — sent him back from the Egg with the dark crystal from his staff.
- **Atlas IV** — the sea-elf prince, third of the future war beside Max and Zan.
- **Chronocypher** — freed by him from the Zoth matrix at R'lyeh.
- **Zanzibar** — his other self of the party's present, "Zanzibar Prime", later King of Punis and First Emperor of Faerûn.

**Items:** **Glassbane**, a shard of Perfect moonstone, earned by deed in the war beside the sea elves. **St. Cloud's ring**, three legendary resistances a day. A suit, in which he is size Large.
