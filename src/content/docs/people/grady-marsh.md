**Human · Retired ArcEye surveillance wizard · Late 70s · Missing**

<small>Also known as: Grady · Graddy Marsh</small>

**Grady Marsh** is a retired Arcane Eye surveillance wizard of Sharn, a human male in his late 70s. Eight years ago he wandered off his patrol route, looked into the Lord Commander's penthouse, witnessed the birth of [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/), and did not legally file the surveillance report, so that no record of that night exists anywhere in the Empire's archive. He retired to the Lower City and drank at the Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar, where a Nest informant overheard him bragging about it; [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) took the rumour straight to [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), and two organisations went hunting for him at once.

He was taken from his apartment, held beneath The Vault Below, drugged, interrogated by [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/)'s cultists and used in a memory-extraction rite that failed. The Inevitables followed a matchbook left in his apartment and got him out; he was teleported to Zilspar Farm 02 and nursed back by [Donna](/people/donna/). The rebellion then put a Crystallizer Lens taken from Circuit City to his head, drew the memory out whole, and broadcast it over every screen in Sharn from the balcony he had once looked into. No portrait of him is recorded.

## The Arcane Eye Security Network

The Arcane Eye Security Network was implemented nearly a decade ago as part of the Empire's expanded internal surveillance initiative. ArcEyes drift along streets, alleys and transit routes on continuous patrol cycles, cast, maintained and remotely operated by government employees stationed inside the Veil Building under the Iron Veil's Sharn Division. Grady Marsh was one of those employees: a surveillance wizard on the patrol rota. He retired to the Lower City, where his circle was a bar and its regulars — the Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar, where he was a fixture until he had not been seen in days. He had no faction and no protection.

## The Nest's file

Recently, a Nest informant in the Lower City overheard a drunken tale at the Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar. An old man named Grady Marsh bragged about his time working at ArcEye HQ, recalling the night eight years ago when he "didn't legally file a surveillance report." He claimed he had wandered off his patrol route and glimpsed into the Lord Commander's penthouse, witnessing the strange birth of her son. Jessica Grimpledink brought this rumor straight to Richard Blaze. If true, it means one man, Grady Marsh, a retired ArcEye officer holds knowledge of a secret no one else has ever recorded.

The Nest knows only this:

- Human male in his late 70's, former ArcEye surveillance wizard.
- Grady moved to the Lower City after retiring.
- He frequents Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar
- His exact address remains unknown.

## What he saw

[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) collapsing into sudden labour on the penthouse balcony while [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) stood motionless and watched. Uriel delivering the baby and examining him closely. Then, with a cold deliberate motion, a hand raised over the newborn's face; a sickly green glow; breathing that faltered and stopped; dark vein-like lines forming across the cheek. At Esther's scream the glow faded, the baby gasped, and Uriel turned away — and the lens caught a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull, and then Uriel lifted her gaze and looked directly at the unseen observer.

Marsh confirmed the core of his tale — what he saw the night Esther and Richard's son Calcifer was born — and confirmed it was never officially recorded. He has never changed the account. He insisted that an ArcEye Crystallizer is the only device that can safely extract and stabilize the full memory, and he was right.

## The Vault Below

His apartment was found with signs of a struggle, blood on the floor, and a matchbook advertising The Vault Below left where it fell. Under interrogation he gave the Children of Ember nothing they could use, though not by choice: the memory-extraction ritual Faith Zeir's cultists attempted on him simply failed. He was made to watch [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) feed, and to sit through Faith's questions, and when the party got him out he was still fogged from the drugs — and he still managed to name several places where a functioning ArcEye Crystallizer might be found.

## The Extraction

The lens came out of Circuit City with the rest of Edwin Graves's stock. Zero-One led everyone into the private Fulcrum room at Zilspar, and the memory was pulled successfully. Donna and Grady stepped out before it was played. Six people watched it: the trio, Jessica, Zero-One and Richard Blaze. Three copies were made; the party kept two and Jessica took one.

> He recalled the night eight years ago when he "didn't legally file a surveillance report."

## Right now

Grady Marsh was last recorded safe at Zilspar Farm 02. Zilspar Farm has since been destroyed by Eden units; Zero-One was taken alive and Izaak is presumed dead. No report the Inevitables hold names Grady Marsh among the dead or the taken.

**Organizations / Groups:** formerly the Arcane Eye Security Network, Sharn Division, under the Iron Veil. Latterly a protected witness of Fulcrum.

**Holdings:** an apartment in the Lower City, abandoned with blood on the floor · a stool at the Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar.

**Items:** a matchbook for The Vault Below, left in his apartment, which is how the party found him · the unfiled report that does not exist · his own memory, now in three copies.

**The Unfiled Report:** every ArcEye feed in Sharn is archived. This one is not, because he was off his patrol route and did not legally file it. Eight years later that gap in the archive was the only place the record of that night still existed, and it existed in a drunk man's head at a sake bar in the Lower City.
