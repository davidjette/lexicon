**Human · Aberrant Mind Sorcerer · [The Unforeseen](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/the-unforeseen/) and the Imperial Ministry · Magister of New Ham · Mayor of [Newham](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/places/newham/) · Grand Duke of the Eastern Provinces · Dead — killed at Newham, and still audible**

**Also known as:** LaBeefe · LeBeef · LaBeef · Magister LeBeefe · Mayor LeBeefe · Grand Duke John C. LeBeefe · [Inspector LeGranouille](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/inspector-legranouille/) (a disguise)

John C. LeBeefe was a con artist, actor, lothario, slaver, sorcerer, and Imperial statesman whose rise transformed a frontier village into a major city—and whose death destroyed it. Born on a farm in Breland, he made his fortune through deception before becoming the magistrate of New Ham, where he operated a slave-trading business moving people both into and out of the Demon Wastes. When Imperial agents [Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel Qualanthri](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/uriel-qualanthri/) arrived to investigate a missing Imperial shipment, he bought their cooperation instead of facing justice. Together with [Locke Pierce](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/locke-pierce/), the three became founding members of the Unforeseen.

An encounter with a mind flayer in the Demon Wastes corrupted LeBeefe and left him slowly transforming into something otherworldly. His influence continued to grow alongside his power. New Ham became Newham, he rose from mayor to Grand Duke of the Eastern Provinces, and the Empire expanded eastward under his authority. Eventually, a rebellion united against him. LeBeefe was killed during the assault on Newham, but his death unleashed an otherworldly catastrophe that poisoned the surrounding land and created the New Mourning.

His body died. His voice did not disappear with it.

## Description

No reliable likeness of LeBeefe in his final years is known to have survived the fall of the city he built, and the Empire has not published one. What the record holds instead is the account of a man from Breland who was attacked by a mind flayer in the Demon Wastes and slowly transformed into something otherworldly.

He could manipulate how others perceived him, reduce himself to miniature, and project his voice into places far beyond his physical location. His influence could linger after his death. He left eyes behind him, including one that sat on the mantelpiece of [Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/)'s penthouse in Sharn. When Eric the Cleric found it, he felt it pulling at him but refused to pick it up.

LeBeefe's own article, written in his own hand, described him in three words: sexual, sensual, multi-orgasmic.

## Origin

LeBeefe was born on a farm in Breland and had little interest in hard work. He made his way through life as a con artist, an actor, a lothario, and eventually a slaver. He became wealthy by exploiting opportunities other people overlooked—or were too honest to pursue.

At some point, while in the Demon Wastes, he encountered a mind flayer. The encounter corrupted his mind and began a transformation whose ultimate nature remains unknown. He survived, but he emerged with disturbing psychic abilities and visions of the end of the world.

![John C. LeBeefe - Young Slaver.png](/images/uploads/john-c-lebeefe-young-slaver-mv8x35nd.webp)
*John C. LeBeefe - Young Slaver*

He first appears in the record as the magistrate of New Ham, a small village on the eastern line with slave pens and an Imperial administrative post. The position gave him a respectable public office from which to continue his private business.

LeBeefe sold Empire-owned slaves to private investors in the Demon Wastes for roughly three times what the Empire had paid. When an Imperial shipment disappeared, he claimed an unknown disease had killed the slaves. He burned the bodies as a precaution and blamed the supposed outbreak on a rebel cell, ensuring that the authorities would pursue a fabricated enemy rather than discover his own involvement.

The Empire sent [Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/), a Crimson Sun Inquisitor, and [Uriel Qualanthri](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/uriel-qualanthri/), a Black Dove, to investigate the missing shipment, assess LeBeefe's loyalty, and identify the alleged rebels.

In the old thieves' den at Oldham, they encountered members of [the Black Doves](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/the-black-doves/) under Adulfphus Mudge, who had been sent by Gabrielle Duin to investigate the same supposed disease. The encounter ended in violence. LeBeefe, Esther, and Uriel agreed on a cover story and took the black box associated with the investigation.

Rather than expose LeBeefe, the two Imperial agents accepted a share of his operation. The arrangement gave all three something they wanted, and it established the pattern that would define their relationship: mutual advantage, carefully managed secrets, and very little trust.

## New Ham and the Rise of Newham

Under LeBeefe, New Ham grew into Newham, a bustling city with a lightning-rail hotel, [the Clamshell Casino](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/places/the-clamshell-casino/), a university hospital, a Rekkenmark annex, and a month-long Imperial festival.

LeBeefe became Mayor and was eventually elevated to Grand Duke of the Eastern Provinces. He pushed the Empire eastward from the outpost of [Leef](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/places/leef/), developing the territory as an extension of his own power. An airship yard expanded the Empire's reach, while illithid city guards and the [Iron Veil](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/the-iron-veil/) helped hold the territory.

The currency of the Grand Duchy became known as **LeBeefes**.

LeBeefe's administration was not known for its diligence. Every Imperial mayor in Khorvaire was expected to attend the annual Council of Mayors at Thronehold. LeBeefe attended for the brothels and was volunteered onto a rail-gauge committee for his trouble.

He was also involved in the Empire's transportation politics. At a transportation summit, Kwanti d'Orien pushed for his House's standards and centrality in the Empire's infrastructure. LeBeefe persuaded the delegates to centralize that power instead of relying on the houses, earning Kwanti's lasting hostility.

As Mayor, LeBeefe left much of his former slave-trade operation in the hands of his Overseer, Tibedeax. He became increasingly entangled in gambling, development deals, and the pleasures his position afforded him. He was seen around Newham with [Ova Santoro](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/ova-santoro/), the 23-year-old daughter of casino pit boss Christopher Santoro. LeBeefe was proficient with gaming dice only while Ova was with him.

At his swearing-in as Mayor, he received a **red velvet sash**, a magical item that grants its wearer one lucky roll every 24 hours. LeBeefe took to wearing it.

Publicly, he was the Empire's success story on the frontier: the administrator who had transformed a struggling settlement into a metropolis and pushed Imperial influence further east. Privately, he continued to value money he had not worked for, gambling, sex, and the ability to make other people take the risks on his behalf.

## The Unforeseen

LeBeefe eventually joined forces with Esther, Uriel, and [Locke Pierce](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/locke-pierce/). In the undead-overrun village of Arden Crest, the four Imperial operatives agreed to work together and named their group **the Unforeseen**.

LeBeefe's talents suited the group in its early operations. He was a persuasive liar, a practiced actor, and a man who could make other people believe that his interests were their own. He preferred to negotiate, deceive, or profit from a situation rather than face its dangers directly.

He also brought increasingly disturbing abilities to the group. During their early adventures, he showed Esther and Uriel a vision of the end times. He later dreamed of a connection between himself and the four ancient tablets associated with the coming catastrophe.

The group's relationships did not remain stable. Uriel eventually murdered Ova Santoro, LeBeefe's girlfriend in Newham, between episodes. LeBeefe came to regard Uriel as the person he most wanted dead.

The Unforeseen also encountered the Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), who stripped LeBeefe of his equipment and marooned the party on an island. There, LeBeefe acquired another problem: an invisible shadow-like creature that attached itself to him and quietly drained memory and intelligence from people nearby.

At Korth, [Gabrielle Duin](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/gabriel-duin/) revealed the creature with *faerie fire*. It tried to flee, but Duin killed it with necromantic damage. LeBeefe had been left feeling tired and slow after the island, and the encounter exposed another strange influence surrounding him.

Duin recognized his unusual abilities and encouraged him to harness them in the Empire's service.

LeBeefe believed the end of the world was coming because he had seen it and made other people witness it. He also believed he was connected to the tablets that foretold it. His ambitions were simpler: to get rich, gain power, and—above all—kill Uriel.

## Rekkenmark

The Rekkenmark episode marked the end of the original sequence of the Unforeseen's campaign. During the events there, LeBeefe fled when Locke Pierce set the Rekkenmark to self-destruct.

His departure marked another break in the history of the Unforeseen. The group would later be drawn into the affairs of Sharn, where LeBeefe's connections to Esther, Uriel, and the Empire would continue to shape events.

## Sharn: City of Knives and the Temple of Closed Circle

Immediately after the Rekkenmark events, LeBeefe travelled to Sharn with the Unforeseen. The Sharn operations included the events known as [City of Knives](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/history/city-of-knives/) and [City of Knives, Part II](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/), followed by the Temple of Closed Circle.

At the Sharn Interoperability Summit, the Crimson Sun sought to identify a mole leaking deliberately false intelligence. The suspected individuals included Henry Heinrick, Lord Gregory Goldenhew, and Lord Albert Spear. The investigation became entangled with a catastrophe at the Grand Theatre, where Krespo turned an orchestra into demonic apparitions and caused a massacre.

During the operation, LeBeefe accidentally witnessed Heinrick with a woman. Heinrick erased LeBeefe's memory of the encounter. The party eventually escaped with Heinrick, Goldenhew, Billy Tremblepike, and Filly Sominyard.

The Temple of Closed Circle brought a far more devastating encounter. An ulitharid known as **the Brain** killed Uriel and consumed her brain. Fema Nolan, who would later become Zero-One, resurrected Uriel. A diamond was embedded at the base of Uriel's skull, and an ArcEye detected it on the night [Calcifer](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/calcifer/) was born.

The encounter did not mean that the mind flayers had been eliminated. Later, one was found in the Dhakaani depths near the Forge of Binding, accompanied by infected Cogborn thralls.

These Sharn events were the last known period of LeBeefe's life in which he operated openly alongside the Unforeseen. Approximately ten years later, his rule in Newham would end in catastrophe.

## Last Public Appearance

Roughly a year or two after the Sharn events, [Richard Blaze](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/richard-blaze/) and Esther Crona married in a small, private ceremony at their lake house outside [Korth](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/places/korth/). Uriel and LeBeefe attended.

The wedding was LeBeefe's **last known public appearance**. By the later chronology, it took place perhaps eight or nine years before his death, although the exact dates have not been established.

Soon afterward, before Calcifer was born, Esther received correspondence from [Greenish Hughes](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/greenish-hughes/) stating that LeBeefe wished to meet her at his manor in Newham. Esther travelled there with Richard but entered the palace alone.

Inside, she met three tiefling women: Faith, Blair, and Eden. LeBeefe explained that he had used harvested sperm to father four daughters by four different mothers. Esther, Faith, Blair, and Eden shared the same biological father and were half-sisters.

[Sister Nora](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/sister-nora/), a cleric from the Black Dove hospital, performed a ritual that confirmed their blood relationship.

![LeBeefe's Daughters — Esther, Faith, Eden and Blair. First time meeting](/images/uploads/lebeefe-s-daughters-esther-faith-eden-an-muxqhpm6.webp)

This gathering was the last known time Esther, Faith, Blair, Eden, and Sister Nora saw LeBeefe. Sister Nora would later be transferred to Sharn, where she became head cleric of the [Sanctum of the Last Mercy](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/).

Greenish Hughes continued serving LeBeefe after the meeting.

## His Daughters

LeBeefe's four known daughters were:

* **[Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/)** — his daughter and a founding member of the Unforeseen. Rebel intelligence identified her as the eldest of the four, though she did not acknowledge the relationship in any recovered record.
* **[Faith](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/faith/)** — a leader of the Children of Ember. She was killed on a rooftop in Sharn.
* **[Blair](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/blair/)** — also known as Queen B, sovereign of the Sharn Underhive. She reached out to LeBeefe herself and spent time with him before his death. She recognized both his intelligence and the danger beneath his charm, saw through his illusions, and kept a careful distance from him.
* **[Eden](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/eden/)** — one of the three daughters who met Esther at LeBeefe's manor.

The daughters' origins and nature have been described differently in different accounts. Some records identify the three younger daughters as tieflings and suggest that they were partly mind flayer, serving as Esther's deadly agents in Sharn. Rebel intelligence instead emphasizes that Esther was the eldest and identifies all four women as LeBeefe's daughters.

LeBeefe was also the grandfather of Esther's son, Calcifer. One account described Calcifer as a *kwisatz haderach*, attributing the significance to his grandfather's identity.

## The Black Palace

After the gathering at his manor, LeBeefe withdrew from public life and remained hidden inside the **Black Palace** in Newham. His seat of power was Castle LeBeefe, a black tower in the middle of a lake that the rebels called the Black Fortress.

In Leef, where he held the title of Grand Duke Dominus Imperium, tavern conversations reportedly ended with the same curse: “Gods-damned LeBeefe, curse his soul.”

The rebels of Newham considered him one of the most powerful sorcerers on the continent and believed his fortress was alive with dark magic. His soldiers and city guards were under parasite control, making his authority extend beyond ordinary military command.

During the years he remained hidden, Greenish Hughes was the only known person to maintain an ongoing relationship with him. The Empire's public accounts continued to present LeBeefe as a heroic protector, but resistance intelligence described a ruler attempting to build a mind flayer army and seize the Dwarflands with the help of Esther's troops.

The precise extent of LeBeefe's transformation during these years is unknown. His body was changing into something otherworldly, and he could manipulate how others perceived him. In the Black Fortress, he appeared to the resistance as a young human, intangible and seemingly a trick of the mind. He welcomed the intruders to inspect his books, but the ink bled off every page.

## The Fall of Newham

Approximately ten years after the Temple of Closed Circle events, resistance against LeBeefe's rule reached its conclusion.

The trio who would become the Inevitables—[Gemma Corso](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/gemma-corso/), [Sir Dario Argentino](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/sir-dario-argentino/), and [Eric the Cleric](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/eric-the-cleric/)—had liberated Leef. Thadric “The Colonel” Rourke pointed them toward the Grand Duke as the architect of Newham's oppression. With [Digma Beeve](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/digma-beeve/), they brought together the city's many resistance cells.

The resistance included St. Stamos cultists, Deuce Loosely's cutpurses, Luz Fayana's Grey Doves, the Diggers, former Blood of Vol vampires from Club Mawniq, Inquisition turncoats, deep-cover Silver Flame faithful, Locke's warforged, and orcs from the Uruk Republic brought by Paulina Gunter-Gooch. Digma Beeve helped unite these groups into a force capable of challenging LeBeefe directly.

Eric's dragonmark allowed the trio to pass in and out of the Black Fortress. LeBeefe appeared to welcome them, but the encounter was a trap. During the fighting, he travelled with the trio disguised as [Inspector LeGranouille](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/inspector-legranouille/), an inspector in his own service who appeared to turn against his master and join the resistance. The betrayal was a ruse.

![John C. LeBeefe — Drops his illusion of Inspector LeGranouille](/images/uploads/john-c-lebeefe-drops-his-illusion-of-ins-mu65321r.webp)

When his disguise fell away, LeBeefe confronted the resistance in his own form.

> “You thought it would be that easy? You simpering nitwits. My name is LeBeefe. I cannot be killed.”

— John C. LeBeefe, in the Black Fortress.

He promised the trio a little present, inserted directly behind the eye, that would make them his slaves. But the resistance killed him in the assault.

His death unleashed the power contained in his arcane eye. The eye exploded, poisoning the land with illithid magic and causing an otherworldly catastrophe that collapsed Newham.

## The New Mourning

![New Mourning — City of Newham, eastern Khorvaire, west of Leef: corrupted wasteland](/images/uploads/new-mourning-city-of-newham-eastern-khor-muvsh3yq.webp)

The destruction of Newham created the **New Mourning**, a contaminated region covering approximately 12,000 square miles, from Karrnath to Q'barra. The disaster severed Leef and the Dwarflands from the rest of the Empire.

Rekkenmark scientists sent to examine the flaming wreckage concluded that the ground would remain uninhabitable for a hundred years. Native plants and animals began to mutate, and the region became toxic and corrupted by illithid magic.

The survivors fled east. Among the displaced were swamp dwarves from the Mror Holds, and the eastern refugees eventually founded the [Republic of Talenta](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/).

The Empire's response to LeBeefe's death was deeply contradictory. An eight-foot bust of solid gold, honoring him as a heroic protector of the Empire, was erected outside the Hall of Justice in Karrnath. Some blamed the rebel attack for the catastrophe; others believed the gods had exacted retribution for LeBeefe's malice and avarice.

The currency, titles, and institutions he had created survived in records and stories, but his city was gone.

## What Still Listens

LeBeefe's death did not end the appearances of his voice. His songs and speech have continued to surface in distant places, including locations he never visited.

A **LeBeefe Eye** sat on the mantelpiece of Esther Crona's penthouse in Sharn. When Eric the Cleric found it, he felt it pulling at him but refused to pick it up. The eye remained intact and seemed to be watching.

Eric later heard LeBeefe's voice and songs at the Forge of Binding beneath Sharn, including a tune he recognized from the Black Palace. Someone who had spent time near LeBeefe also appeared to retain a residue that reduced psychic damage by half.

In the deep workings beneath the Mror Holds, the party found a dead Imperial courier carrying a note in LeBeefe's hand:

> “Observe the deep workings and report what listens back.”

The courier's satchel had been partly consumed by a fungal bloom that grew from a shattered terrarium in his kit. The bloom delivered a warning:

*Ring it here. When HE is born, it will be too late.*

LeBeefe's voice has since urged Eric to ring bells. It also pushed Sedda Deeptempura to ring one, and she did. In a dream in the Glitterhame, Eric encountered the same voice bent over a small duergar boy. The boy has not been seen since.

The voice has also appeared to the Chancellor of Sharn, urging him to ring a bell that would raise the dead of Khorvaire. In a dream three years after LeBeefe's death, Eric heard him say:

> “Good idea to ring that bell.”

Whether LeBeefe survived in some form, left behind an echo of his consciousness, or is being imitated by something in the deep workings remains unresolved. His voice is real enough to influence events, but its source is unknown.

## Right Now

John C. LeBeefe is dead. Newham is a corrupted wasteland, his rule is over, and the Empire still commemorates him as a hero.

Yet his voice continues to travel. It appears in dreams, in distant underground places, and in warnings associated with bells and the coming birth of someone important. His Eye remains intact, and someone—or something—is still carrying out his instructions in the deep workings.

No surviving account explains how a dead man can continue to speak in places he never visited. The question is no longer simply what LeBeefe became during his life, but what remains of him now.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivations**

* Get rich.
* Gain power.
* Kill Uriel Qualanthri.

**Likes**

* Money he did not work for
* Gambling at the Clamshell Casino
* The brothels of Thronehold
* Ova Santoro
* Embrace as a commodity
* Singing
* Cutting an investigator in on a deal rather than fighting one

**Dislikes**

* Hard work
* Uriel Qualanthri
* Committee meetings
* Being stripped of his equipment by pirates

**Vices and flaws**

LeBeefe described himself as “sexual, sensual, multi-orgasmic.” He was a con artist, actor, lothario, and slaver, in that order. He preferred manipulation to direct confrontation and rarely undertook work he could persuade someone else to do for him.

**Hobbies and pets**

Gambling at the Clamshell Casino and pursuing the pleasures his position made available to him. No pets are recorded.

## Organizations and Holdings

**Organizations and roles**

* Former magistrate of New Ham
* Mayor of Newham
* Founding member of [the Unforeseen](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/organizations/the-unforeseen/)
* Grand Duke of the Eastern Provinces
* Delegate of the Eastern Provinces to the Interoperability Summit at Sharn
* Ruler of Newham and its eastern territories

**Holdings**

* The magistracy and slave pens of New Ham
* Newham City, now the New Mourning
* The Black Palace / Black Fortress
* The airship yard at Leef, now part of the Republic of Talenta

**Items**

* **The LeBeefe Eye:** At least one eye sat for years on the mantel of Esther Crona's penthouse in Sharn. Eric felt it pulling at him but refused to pick it up. The eye remained intact and appeared to be watching.
* **Red velvet sash:** The sash from his swearing-in as Mayor grants one lucky roll every 24 hours.
* **Tentacle Rod:** A rare magic weapon with three rubbery tentacles. Each can strike at a creature within 15 feet; a creature hit by all three is slowed and hampered for a minute.

**Aberrant Mind**

Gabrielle Duin called LeBeefe's unusual ability “an interesting power” and urged him to harness it in the Empire's service. His power manifested less as conventional spellwork than as psychic pressure: visions shared with others, dreams linked to the ancient tablets, a voice that travels where he is not, and a lingering psychic effect in those who spent time near him.

## Social: Contacts and Relations

* **[Esther Crona](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/esther-crona/)** — fellow founder of the Unforeseen and his daughter. Rebel intelligence identified her as the eldest of his four daughters. She did not publicly acknowledge the relationship. Deceased.
* **[Uriel Qualanthri](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/uriel-qualanthri/)** — fellow founder, accomplice in the New Ham slave arrangement, murderer of his girlfriend Ova Santoro, and the person he most wanted dead. Alive and Empress.
* **[Locke Pierce](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/locke-pierce/)** — fellow founder of the Unforeseen. LeBeefe fled when Locke set the Rekkenmark to self-destruct. Alive.
* **[Ova Santoro](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/ova-santoro/)** — his girlfriend in Newham. Killed by Uriel between episodes.
* **[Blair, “Queen B”](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/blair/)** — his daughter and sovereign of the Sharn Underhive. She met him, saw through his illusions, and kept her distance. Alive.
* **[Faith](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/faith/)** — his daughter and a leader of the Children of Ember. Killed on a rooftop in Sharn.
* **[Eden](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/eden/)** — his daughter and half-sister to Esther, Faith, and Blair.
* **[Greenish Hughes](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/greenish-hughes/)** — his servant and intermediary. She arranged the meeting at which Esther learned of her half-sisters and continued serving LeBeefe afterward.
* **[Sister Nora](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/sister-nora/)** — Black Dove cleric who confirmed the blood relationship between LeBeefe's daughters. Later became head cleric of the Sanctum of the Last Mercy in Sharn.
* **[Joseph Roberts](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/)** — the Dread Pirate who robbed and marooned LeBeefe, and later became his son-in-law.
* **[Digma Beeve](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/digma-beeve/)** — furniture trader whose routes doubled as rebel supply lines. He helped unify the resistance that killed LeBeefe and later became acting Comptroller of the Republic of Talenta.
* **Taby PC** — leader of the Booty Salad gang in New Cyre. Cut a deal with LeBeefe to move Embrace through New Ham.
* **[Eric the Cleric](https://davidjette.github.io/lexicon/people/eric-the-cleric/)** — part of the resistance that killed LeBeefe. He carries a lingering psychic residue, recognizes a tune from the Black Palace, and continues to hear LeBeefe's voice urging him to ring a bell.
