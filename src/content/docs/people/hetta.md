**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Scout of the Teryaki · The Teryaki clan · Dead**

Hetta was a duergar scout of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan, serving under [General Tso](/people/general-tso/). When the boy [Egg Roll](/people/egg-roll/) failed to return from fetching water, Tso refused outside help and sent Hetta alone; he found what the boy had found, and [the bloom](/species/the-bloom/) reached him. He returned carrying it, and later struck the Thirteenth Casting with *shatter* in the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/) while the party were below at the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/). He never recovered from the toll, and fell from the ledge to his death while the room was being saved around him.

## Physical description

Hetta is the sort of scout who is already in the room before anyone notices him; he stepped out of the shadows beside [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) during the camp's first crisis, whispered with his general, and was gone again. The last description of him is mould visible across his face, and mushrooms forming at the corners of his lips.

## The errand

When the boy Egg Roll failed to come back from fetching water, General Tso refused the offer of outside help and sent his own man. Hetta was the only person in the mountain who went looking. The Teryaki did not raise it with the strangers again.

He found what the boy found. The bloom reached him, and the errand became a delivery.

## The last ringing

An alarm laid twenty feet around the Thirteenth Casting fired while the party were below at the black lake. They teleported up into their own smoke and found him there. Hetta called out an old Dwarvish word for cave-in - a warning, or the memory of one - and cast *shatter*, grazing the bell.

The toll took everybody. All three of the party failed, and so did he. Every kin in the next hall, and the risen dead climbing the shaft out of the lake, were pulled into one mind and one thirst - for blood and flesh, and the urge to tear one's own skin off. Two members of the party attacked each other. A knight forgot what he was holding.

Hetta never recovered from it. Still confused, in the smoke, he slipped off the ledge and fell to his death while the room was being saved around him.

## Right now

Dead, fallen from the ledge in the Bladeworks, the room where he did the last damage. The bell he struck was broken minutes later. The clan sent one man to find one child, and lost both to the same fungus.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki clan; General Tso's scouts.
