**Orc · Archivist and prophet of Istus · The last of the keepers of the Chronicle · Alive as of Arc VI**

*Also known as:* Zebulon the Meek

Zebulon the Meek is an orc, the last surviving archivist of the orcish artificers who kept [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/), and a prophet pledged to Istus, Lady Cypher. His people were archivists working from foreknowledge the party itself had given them, and they were destroyed by the Netherese after their own leaders divided over the queens of Netheril's demand for access to the archives. He was given a vision of the party at Motherstone and of five blades piercing an opened eye, and carried it to Motherstone years before they arrived. After the party rescued him he guided them to the Chronicle, at a volcanic vent northeast of Enkidu. He appears in Arc VI, [Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), Part IX, "The Chronicle."

## How he meets the party

Safe back in Motherstone and still catching his breath, he stares into the face of Eloise "looking like a grandfather meeting his both his child and his own mother for the first time all at once," sees the devotion of Zanzibar and Percival and the rest of the party, and bows deeply. <small>(Sic on "his both his child.")</small>

He is exhausted and cannot say much beyond his testimony without a long rest.

## His allegiance

> You saved my life, for that alone I owe you everything. And I have already pledged my body, mind and eternal soul to Istus, Lady Cypher, the weaver of fate and the guardian of Time. I am one of the few who still remember you.

## What his people were

By his account the orcish artificers were archivists working from foreknowledge the party itself gave them: "You gave us everything, visions of the future, tremendous foreknowledge that allowed our people to accumulate so much, with our sole mission to protect it and prevent it from falling into the wrong hands. We knew a time of troubles was on the way, and it was our mission to guard against it."

His account of how it ended:

> But as the story always goes, too much power and convenience made us distracted and pompous, and our elitist archivists and prophets fell into vanity, and proposed that now the end of history had arrived, that we were triumphant.\
> \
> The elves gave magic to men. Men used it to reorder their own societies — those with magic would rule on high, those without would serve. Those that stood in the way of that progress were to be destroyed. Those that withheld the engines of their progress from their control were to be obliviated.

Then: "after the queens of Netheril demanded that we open our archives to their archmages, our people were divided on how to answer. We had grown beyond the need for mistrust and that those seeking beneficial power in the name of the science of magic should be permitted. Our leaders took sides against themselves, so that when the Netherese attacked we were already defeated." He relates this wincing, and says "It causes me great pain to relate this to you, but it is a pain that I am trained and devoted to endure."

## Curtis Washington

Zebulon's master, **Curtis Washington**, was one of the handful of archivists and prophets who knew of the most dangerous devices in the archive. It is from Washington's salvaged notes and readings that Zebulon knows of the great orb that produced an "Obliviation" field — the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), described by a researcher who never learned its name.

Washington himself has been erased:

> These revelations are stuff of madness to every priest and shaman I have spoken to in my long, long years, mainly because besides these paltry notes and a few specks of memory that haunt me in my sleep, I have no record or evidence that Curtis Washington ever existed. I searched every scrap of rubble at Yog, where the devices were held, for his home or his family, I can remember none of it, and neither can I find any evidence of it. There are ruins at Yog, Jhamdaath, Enkidu, but no one alive remembers how they were built of by whom. I have spent a lifetime trying to convince every Orc I see that they themselves and their ancestors built those ruins, and were betrayed. But to no avail.

<small>(Sic on "how they were built of by whom.")</small>

## The vision

The reason he seeks the party out, and the reason he can lead them to the Chronicle:

> Then you gave me a vision. I saw you, and a band of immortal heroes, step through the Deepgate at Motherstone and bow to the Queen of the Moon. At the same time, I watched the moon explode and form its rings, I saw the sinking of a ship, and I five blades piercing an opened eye. The eye had five pupils and an swirling iris made of eldritch runes, like the ones in my master's notes. It bled a blood so dark that the white was obscured and filled with an inky void that was inpenetrable somehow crawling, as if the arms of a hundred trillion squid pulsed just beyond my sight, and I was falling in.

<small>(Sic on "and I five blades," "an swirling iris," "inpenetrable.")</small>

He carried it to Motherstone years before the party arrived: "Many years ago I traveled here to Motherstone to tell them of my vision. They were kind to me and told me that if my vision was to come to be, they would welcome Istus into the pantheon of benevolent gods. And now I see that my vision was true, because of course it was. Which gives me hope, that my master was real, that my nation existed, that my realm is not forgotten?"

## What he sends them to do

> The Chronicle will know how to interpret my vision and give you the knowledge you need to enter the Eye. The Chronicle was the mythical living archive of my people. It is located near a volcanic vent from which it draws its power. Northeast of Enkidu, hidden there. Only the blessed of Cypher can access it, but it has been corrupted by those who have tried thus far.

He guides the party to the volcanic outcropping himself, and supplies the history of the site — that it was once "a towering and glittering ivory fortess" the Netherese destroyed trying to reach the Chronicle.

<small>Zebulon does not know that the Chronicle is Lex, or that the party is the reason it is there. See [The Anachron](/items/the-anachron/).</small>

## Other testimony

On the politics of the region: to the best of Zebulon's knowledge, Memnon was under the control of the Calimshites, who are led by the Infanta of Fortune.

## Status

Alive as of Arc VI. **The Orcs who served the Chronicle** appear among the allies the Raven Queen brings out of the land of the Dead at the final battle. <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter. The record names the group, not Zebulon individually.)</small>
