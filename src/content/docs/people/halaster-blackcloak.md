**The Mad Mage · [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/) · Disguised as [Soggy Blankets](/people/soggy-blankets/) · Alive**

**Also known as:** the Mad Mage; Soggy Blankets; Halastar Blackcloak (the Company's spelling)

**Halaster Blackcloak** is the Mad Mage of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), the dungeon far below [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), where he gathers tomes and relics stolen over the centuries. His personal sanctum was in close proximity to [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and in the Ages of the Infanta the party, following the Blackstaff's trail, passed through it in a rush at the end of their quest, when Halaster was not there. Five hundred years later [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) believes a robbery of the library of the House of Wonder, around 1651 DR, was carried out at his direction, taking the tome [O-3255](/items/o-3255/) of the *Ars Calixia* into Undermountain. On the dungeon's second level he assembled Motherstone Hall, an exhibit of the ancient city of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). Undermountain blocks teleportation except through his gates, and when [Lenore](/people/lenore/) opened a portal in the trash hole of [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), she opened one of them. On the first level the party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) met him disguised as the old prisoner Soggy Blankets, who knew where the book was, and he walked away during their fight with the shadows.

## The Ages of the Infanta

In the Ages, [Khelben Arunsun](/people/khelben-arunsun/), the Blackstaff, went into Undermountain, and his trail led the party to the level with access to the Egg. Dave, recalling it:

> Yeah well remember Blackstaff went down the hole right before Obus revealed himself in the Winking Depths arc and following his trail got you guys straight to the 23rd level where there was access to the Egg, and yeah you went through his personal sanctum at the end but you were in this huge rush you didn’t really look at anything! But that was the slice of Undermountain that quest had and Halaster was not there\
> But Blackstaff knew the way down\
> So Halasters personal sanctum was in close proximity to the Egg

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

See [the Winking Depths](/places/the-winking-depths/) and [Obi](/people/obi/).

## The robbery of the House of Wonder

The Company's file on O-3255, volumes XIII to XVI of the *Ars Calixia* of [Calix](/people/calix/), records that the tome passed through the library of the House of Wonder in Waterdeep and disappeared in a robbery around 1651 DR:

> Other recent findings lead he Company to believe this theft was at the direction of Halastar Blackcloak, and that the missing tomes and items found their way into Undermountain. Blackcloak is almost certainly aware of the nature of O-3255 and has likely isolated it from other books, symbols, and vulnerable media, and from himself and his person, though his reputation as a "Mad" mage may indicate exposure to Oblivia, both known and unknown to the Company.

<small>— O-3255. Sic on "lead he Company" and "Halastar".</small>

The contents of O-3255 are an [oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/), and the Company forbids its staff to read it.

## Motherstone Hall

Motherstone Hall is rooms 23 to 26 on Level 2 of Undermountain, the Arcane Chambers:

> Segments of Motherstone architecture, bas relief, tombs, statuary, and relics stolen by Halaster over the centuries, and assembled as a kind of exhibit and "model" for understanding and admiring the ancient city. Built before any obliviation was lifted on Brightmantle, so constructed with profound mystery around the origin of the city. Halaster presumably extracted and reinstalled sections of the ancient city as a district in his vast dungeon, securing inside of it some of the artifacts he held from its glory days.

<small>(DM notes; Dave added the last sentence. See [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/).)</small>

In the hall lie the Sleeping Fomorian, a hall of wedding frescoes, a faceless crone statue and the elven tomb of Daenan Gallidan of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/), empty except for the **STAR** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Halaster's gates

Undermountain blocks teleportation into and out of the dungeon except through Halaster's gates. On the night of [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/), Lenore opened a portal far down in the fort's trash hole, and she, [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Heyu](/people/heyu/) jumped through it into Undermountain. Dave:

> Well Undermountain suppresses any form of teleportion magic into or out of the dungeon except for Halastars gates, but I think Lenore will have a great edge on finding and opening those\
> It also means the thing you opened was one of halastars gates

<small>— Sic on "teleportion" and "Halastars". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-08)</small>

## Soggy Blankets

On Level 1 the party met [Soggy Blankets](/people/soggy-blankets/), an old man lying in wet blankets with a chain around his neck, the bandit camp's prisoner. He pulled the chain off, began resetting the traps and spoke in riddles. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party had by then recovered the Company's mission file for O-3255 and directions to "Ars Calix (Bookcase in office Level 2)". <small>(DM notes)</small>

Soggy Blankets was Halaster in disguise. After the session Dave asked, "We’re your theories about soggy blankets true?", and Nico answered, "That he was the mad mage and knew where that book is? Yeah". Of the old man, Dave added: "Yeah he walked away during the shadow fight". <small>(Sic on "We’re". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

Dave added Soggy Blankets to the dungeon himself: "Soggy blankets isn’t in the book but I’m so glad I added him , there’s like no natural primer for the dungeon". Of the published adventure's guide to the dungeon: "I guess you’re supposed to find the halastar simulacrum and ask questions but there’s a high chance you don’t find the mask AND figure that thing out". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

## Nico's theory

On the same night Nico offered a reading of the Mad Mage's madness: "No wonder he went mad. So he probably knew of the egg then he woke up the next day and just forgot it all… but maybe he went looking for that book to help him remember but the book is makes him mad too". Dave: "Love it". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>
