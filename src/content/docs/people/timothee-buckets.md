*Also known as:* the Sovereign Supreme · Admiral Timothee Buckets

**Timothee Buckets** is the Sovereign Supreme of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) in the era of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. An admiral of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), Buckets was appointed supreme ruler of the Sovereignty after radical government reforms that followed the crisis at [Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), with powers over the military, the legislature and the judiciary. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## The Sovereign Supreme

The Company dossier, which [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/) gives the crew in *The Starsong Awakens*, carries an entry headed "Sovereign Supreme". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> It lists the Sovereign Supreme's status as alive and location as Greyspace, and states:

> Radical government reforms in the Sovereignty following the crisis at Tannhauser Gate resulted in Astral Command Admiral Timothee Buckets' appointment as supreme ruler with an incredibly broad range of powers over the military, legislature, and judiciary.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier</small>

## The Sovereignty under the Sovereign Supreme

In the fifteen years after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the Sovereign Senate passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), criminalising the possession or dissemination of artificial intelligence in Sovereign territory, and [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) was created as a paramilitary arm of the Sovereign government to enforce it. Sovereign leadership has implicated [the Vodoni Empire](/organizations/the-vodoni-empire/) in the sonic disturbance called the T.U.N.E., and the Sovereignty suspects the Vodoni of violating their treaty. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> Buckets's species, age and appearance are unknown.
