**Changeling · Rogue / Warlock · Temple Holdings LLC · Player character (Nico) · Obliviated**

*Also known as:* Chronocypher · Cypher · Eloise, the Infanta of Time · Elohim · June

**Stonecypher** is a changeling rogue and warlock of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), played by Nico. She was hired by [Celion](/people/celion/) to shadow [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), became his best friend, served [Marcus](/people/marcus/) as a warlock of the Raven Queen, and became the time-warlock **Chronocypher**. Split across three eras, she cast the Wish at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) that obliviated Cthulhu and herself.

## Zanzibar

Celion, of the Arcaneum, hired Stonecypher to follow Zanzibar on his quest to find the Zarus Infanta, and to kill the Infanta if she had the chance. She became Zanzibar's best friend. Celion gave her a ring. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-30)</small> During [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/) she had a rendezvous arranged at the shrine of Elistrae with a contact, the lutist of Punis, to learn more about the other Infanta. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small> When he returned to [Punis](/places/punis/) after his exile at the hands of the Arcaneum and was crowned King of Punis, he named Stonecypher his general. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-31)</small>

With Zanzibar and Revan she saw [Obi](/people/obi/) fly over Waterdeep in a blinding light. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small> Obi had shown her charts describing how an object would approach "but NOT strike" the planet at some time in the past; St. Cloud was looking for the date when it would come closest. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-13)</small>

She briefly served as a general in his army at [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/). Dave told her, Zanzibar and Revan that they were "enlisted and compelled to fight", and as landed gentry in Cormyr and Punis they were commissioned officers in [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) and given command of units in the battle. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21, 2019-12-19)</small> She gave him a cursed ring of Wish that would destroy her arm if she used it.

She rode with Zanzibar on the small sailing boat that carried [Una](/people/una/) from her resurrection.

## The Baldur's Gate train

At Baldur's Gate, Stonecypher used the Vault Wand on the railway gate, and the out-of-control steam engine careened into the void instead of into the station. The train that appears at the end, the Lost Train of [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/), is that same train, gone into the Vault Wand's non-room.

## Warlock of the Raven Queen

Stonecypher met Marcus and swore to follow her. She was a warlock of the Raven Queen, Pact of the Chain, and her familiar was a raven named **Po**. She served in that pact until Marcus disappeared with her sister, [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/).

During the Arcaneum campaign she prayed to Marcus, who told her that Una might have harmed some children in [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and was showing signs of extreme mental distress. Dave: "he did you a solid and gave you the heads up". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>

In Arc IV she was one of the assault team that went against the House of Nimbus in Sigil: "Revan, Roland, Stonecypher, Drefan and Stoth approach the House of Nimbus, ready for an assault on those inside." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small> A lich's phylactery in her trunk carries a line about Carls III of [Carls Jr. and the Drow](/organizations/carls-jr-and-the-drow/): "Carls III probably died 300 years ago. Her ghost is still as racist as ever." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small>

## Chronocypher

At [Refuge](/places/refuge/), Stonecypher met [June](/people/the-infanta-of-time/), the Infanta at Refuge and the incarnation of Istus, and became **Chronocypher**, a warlock of time. At [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) she was plugged into the mind of Zoth. [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/) and Maximillien entered R'lyeh at the cost of Max's life, and Dark Zanzibar freed her from the Zoth matrix, where she had been charged with the God power; from then on she was the three-fold goddess. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> In the deep past she failed a Wisdom save against the fish apes, was charmed, and teleported away with them. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-29)</small> At the end of time the dying goddess June named Chronocypher her successor, the Watcher, and told her that Time itself is the Obliviator ([Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/)). Dave: "Cypher literally served the infanta of death, then time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small>

## Split in time

Chronocypher was later split in time. One part remained in the deep past as Chronocypher, in a hidden refuge with [Pim](/people/pim/) and the Changelings of the Moon. Another was incarnated in Antiquity as **Eloise, the Infanta of Time**, during the first Infanta Crisis, which the party witnessed firsthand in their second loop.

As Eloise she offered herself as patron to [Drefan](/people/drefan/) and [Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), who both turned her down. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-08-09)</small> Nico, who played her, wrote: "Eloise isn’t looking for worshipers, she’s looking for allies to defeat the leviathan". At [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/) she froze time for herself, accessed the terminal in [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), and "zipped into the Matrix". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-12-19)</small> In a week-long downtime she and Zanzibar made a priceless reliquary. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-01-16)</small>

On reaching Level 21 she took an epic boon, a mystic arcanum that lets her cast up to a 10th-level warlock spell, with an option to cast it as a reaction. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> After the battle in Despondos she held one of the Palantirs, and close inspection showed her, as god of time, that [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/) is one of a kind. Dave: "here at the end, it only makes sense that you would hold the Rod and the Orb, omnipresence and omniscience". Banishment would send her to the End of Time, her inherited divine realm, which is "a depot to other times". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> By December 2021 she was immortal and did not age. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> In the session of 2022-04-09 Drefan brought her back in the first turn. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-04-10)</small>

When the party returned to the "present", Chronocypher replaced June, a separate Infanta, at Refuge, and lived through that arc again as the child June. At the last moment, instead of dying, she was blown free of Refuge and rescued by the party, who appeared to have just left for the past and came back at level 20 or higher from the back half of the campaign.

Her presence in all three times let her coordinate the three-way attack on the Egg. In the end she cast Wish through the cursed ring she had given Zanzibar, and the Wish obliviated Cthulhu and her as well.

Dave made a wish bargain with Stonecypher in her "first" session, and she did not use it until the last moment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> At the end she was missing her arm. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-17)</small> Dave: "Cypher killed Cthulhu but also severed the ancient reality that Zod left in the mechanism of death". The sudden end of the Mammon Machine warped the space around it, sending [Dis](/places/dis/) into the Hells and twisting the area around [Sigil](/places/sigil/) into a great torus. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>

## The Fates

Her echo is felt in the three-fold god **the Fates**, laid out as Time, Fate and Mystery: Eloise, Istus and Cypher. Eloise is the god of Space and Time, Istus is the god of Fate and Time, and Cypher is the god of Mystery. The Fates also appear as a child, a mother and a crone.

**Elohim** is the liturgical and poetic name of the Eloise aspect. In worship the three aspects are named "Elohim the goddess of time and space, Istus goddess of fate, and Cypher goddess of mysteries and secrets". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

Stonecypher and her deeds, with the true nature and existence of Cthulhu and the origins of the eldritch machines, were erased from memory and are now "cursed information which only madmen and poets ever understand". Her three aspects are worshipped as three goddesses, "sometimes shown as sisters, other times as one being who lives through multiple ages through the epoch of the universe". Elistrae, Brightmantle, Obus, the Lady of Pain and the Raven Queen are gods "who all regard her as a balance keeper that transcends alignment, not unlike Ao". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

## Martin's final cantos

[*Martin's Final Cantos*](/items/martins-final-cantos/) is a poem in three books by the poet Martin that retells her erased sacrifice, the "cursed information which only madmen and poets ever understand". Books I and II of the cantos open with lines from Keats's *Hyperion* and Pope's *Eloisa to Abelard*. Martin's own stanzas follow.

> **Book I**
>
> When the ocean beats its solemnity too loud,\
> And one forgotten goddess who once trod\
> The bounds of earth, yet dared the fates malign,\
> To challenge royal blood and beastly kin,\
> Her memory lost to every mortal mind,\
> To keep the world in sweet oblivion blind.
>
> A budding woman put aside\
> Her harsh divinity, the doom to hide;\
> And there she stood as mortal as her prey,\
> A prince beloved whom she could not betray.\
> But in the forest lurked a darker fate:\
> A leviathan, cloaked in hate’s dire state.\
> Her weapon aimed, with heart misled by chance,\
> Struck not the man, but monster's cold expanse.
>
> Upon that death, the woods did silent fall,\
> The seas retreated, stars wept at the gall.\
> And she, all three and more\
> in every time,\
> Did disappear from every thought and rhyme.
>
> **Book II**
>
> And in the hush of peace that followed her,\
> Where once her laughter echoed through the pine,\
> A silence lay, the price of keeping pure\
> The heart of lands that never would recall\
> The sacrifice that halted ruin’s brawl.
>
> A raven vanished from the minds of all,\
> A moth beyond her power to enthral.\
> Her name unspoken, an answer unobtained,\
> a stone, a cypher, now naught but a stain\
> Upon the fabric of the universe;\
> A tale untold, a transcendental curse.
>
> **Book III**
>
> Thus in despair, the titans bore their grief,\
> Zanzibar, once lord, now laying like a leaf\
> His lady at his side perpetually,\
> Elohim the old, with thoughts that wander free,\
> And Juno, softest-natured of the three.
>
> But where Moradin’s old palace stood,\
> Where songs of power once had stirred the blood,\
> There now was silence, so profound, so deep,\
> That even gods might lay them down to sleep,\
> Forgetting battles and the power they keep.
>
> And she who cast herself out from the stars,\
> To save a prince and slay a beast of wars,\
> Now wanders through the astral without trace,\
> Her noble act beyond the world's embrace,\
> A mystery by choice, her tale, her face.
>
> In twilight realms where gods no longer dream,\
> She treads the shadows, silent as a stream;\
> Where no monument or sacred verse is seen,\
> Her story hidden as if it had never been.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-20)</small>
