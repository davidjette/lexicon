**Species unknown · Employee of the Company · Sister of Barrias Ruby-Eye · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Holly Barrias

**Halle Barrias** is the sister of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and an employee of [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), whose call to action and dire warning open [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis* she gave the crew a Company dossier of information for their quest, shared a drink with old friends and family in the arc's first episode, told her brother of a "star scream" related to [Caprica](/people/caprica/), and tipped him off to a stash that was a prize from the Company.

## Name

JL, who ran *The Starsong Awakens*, gave her name as Holly Barrias, the name the table had jokingly given her in what he called "the Die Hard Xmas episode". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> Barrias's sheet also has "holly barrias". <small>(character sheet)</small> Dave spells it Halle. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-11)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

During *Light of Xaryxis* Barrias's sister already worked for the Company. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

*The Starsong Awakens* is set fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, when Caprica is long dead. It opens with a call to action from Barrias's sister, who brings a dire warning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> She gave the crew a dossier from the Company with information pertinent to the quest. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> The Company knew that the Sovereignty's plan to blame the Vodoni Empire for the awakened Starsong would disrupt trade, and wanted to resurrect Caprica to help find the true source of [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

The first episode, played on 11 July 2024, had two scenes over drinks: one among new partners, and one among old friends and family. In the second, Halle accepted the drink. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-11)</small>

Barrias's sheet notes record that his sister told him about a "star scream" related to Caprica, and tipped him off to a stash, a "prize from company", held in the name of Emily Rugburn at Ragnor XII (also spelled Ragnar XII), which Barrias collected. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Relationships

- **Barrias Ruby-Eye:** her brother.
- **The Company:** her employer.
