**Bugbear · Barbarian 8 · Sergeant-at-Arms, SSJ Zanzibar · Alive**

*Also known as:* Butsac · Buttsac · Lt Buttsac · 2nd Sgt. Butsac · Buttsack (Sgt., Ret.) · Bootysack · Monksack

**Buttsack** is a bugbear barbarian and the chief of security of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by JL. He served as the ship's Sergeant-at-Arms under [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) through *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, and when [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) became a djinn bound in a bottle, the bottle came to Buttsack. In the fifteen years before *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* he played professional sportsball, was paralysed, and became a monk at a Githzerai temple, still carrying the bottle.

## Background

Buttsack was first introduced as Lieutenant Buttsac, Chief of Security, a bugbear barbarian of the Path of the Ancestral Guardian. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24)</small> Lieutenant stands as his earlier rank. His character sheet gives his background as Criminal / Spy. <small>(character sheet)</small> His birthplace and age are unknown.

## Light of Xaryxis

On the crew roster of the Zanzibar he is the Sergeant-at-Arms, 2nd Sgt. Butsac, and that is his title and rank from then on. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> The ship's corps follow training more than division: the squadron leader [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/), an arcana-trained officer, reports to Buttsack, who is enlisted, because Mantis is a bladesinger. As security chief, Buttsack commands the ship's support craft. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-13)</small>

During [the mind flayer crisis](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) of November 2022, Buttsack and possibly [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) were the only people aboard who knew much about mind flayers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small>

In January 2023, when an engineer was killed in a blast and Carlos Vega was suspected, Buttsack tossed crew members' lockers. The search drew the propulsion engineer [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/) into the case, and she became a witness to Carlos's innocence. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small>

After [the Zanzibar's own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/), leaving the ship adrift in the Astral Sea with its captain and command crew far away, Buttsack, Carlos Vega and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) were aboard the helpless ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16; 2024-01-26)</small>

In April 2024, with the ship exploding, Carlos made his third wish and became a djinn in a bottle, and the Zanzibar reached Sigil. The bottle is with Buttsack. He made his Investigation roll, so he knows Carlos is inside it somehow. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> His player said Buttsack would want to take the bottle to a healer or a caster to find out whether Carlos is really in there. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Over the fifteen years after the wreck of the Zanzibar, Buttsack played a few seasons of professional sportsball with the Khorvaire Kestrals until he was paralysed from the waist down, ending his career. He gave up his rage and retreated to an isolated Githzerai temple in the Outer Planes to focus on inner peace, meditation, and "the true meaning of the mysterious bottle he carries with him". He is content to be paralysed, and is now a monk. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

Carlos spent those fifteen years in the bottle Buttsack carries, and was back in play in *The Starsong Awakens* by November 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-11-22)</small> How Carlos came out of the bottle is unknown.

For the new arc his player considered making him an Astral Self monk whose astral form looks like Carlos, then a Way of Mercy monk who thinks he can see Carlos. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-07)</small>

### The Company dossier

The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier lists Buttsack (Sgt., Ret.) as alive and located on the Elemental Chaos Plane. It states that after the [Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) he was awarded, and declined, the Medal of Valorous Service. As a Tight/Round-End in the Professional Sportsball League he twice won MVP (Most Vertical Performance) before "a tragic accident on field" cut his sporting career short. He retired to a Githzerai community and devoted his time to prayer and meditation. The dossier reports that he "entertains fantasies of communing with the spirit of his crewmate from the SSJ Zanzibar, Carlos Vega", whom it lists as deceased. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> JL's account of the arc places the temple in the Outer Planes and names his team the Khorvaire Kestrals. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Carlos was back in play by November 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-22)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

His character sheet makes him an eighth-level barbarian of the Path of the Ancestral Guardian. He fights with a glaive, two handaxes and four javelins, is proficient with playing cards and thieves' tools, and speaks Common and Goblin. As a bugbear he has darkvision and long limbs that extend his reach. <small>(character sheet)</small>
