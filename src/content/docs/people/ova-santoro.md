**Human · Newham · Level Unknown · Dead, and raised**

*Also known as:* None recorded.

**Ova Santoro** was a twenty-three year old human woman of [Newham](/places/newham/), daughter of the Clamshell Casino pit boss [Christopher Santoro](/people/christopher-santoro/), and the girlfriend of Newham's Mayor [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). She was captured and held hostage by succubi in her own apartment and rescued by LeBeefe, Esther, Uriel and Locke, and her father rewarded LeBeefe with 300 gold for it. She was afterwards murdered and turned undead by [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), one of the four who had saved her. No description of her survives beyond her age and her city: not her face, her hair, her height or her colouring.

## Newham

Newham was a slum that four Imperial agents turned into a city, and the Emperor's consort listed it in the throne room as one of their services to the Empire: *"you've turned a once slum of a town into a great new city."* Ova Santoro was one of the people who lived in it. She had an apartment there. Her father ran the floor of [the Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/); her boyfriend was the Mayor.

**Origin.** Newham; twenty-three years of it. Nothing else of her life before the succubi is recorded — no mother, no trade, no history.

## The apartment

**Ova was once captured and help hostage by succubi and was saved by LeBeefe, Esther, Uriel and Locke in her apartment. LeBeefe was rewarded 300 gold for saving her by her father, Christopher Santoro.** <small>(Sic on "help hostage.")</small>

All four of her rescuers were founding members of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). One of them killed her.

## What Uriel did

**She was the once girlfriend of Newham Mayor John C. LeBeefe before she was murdered and turned undead by Uriel Qualanthri.** Uriel was a Cleric of the Grave passing as a Cleric of Light, branded with the Dragonmark of Death, working through the campaign toward a necromantic magic she would later perform on [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) in front of the Emperor. Ova was killed inside that project. Why her, what state she was left in, and where she is now are not recorded.

Nothing indicates the Empire ever investigated it, and nothing indicates her father was ever told.

In public Ova was the Mayor's girlfriend in a boom town. What she wanted, what she thought of [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), what she believed, and whether she had any idea what the people around her were are all **Unknown**.
