**Pirate · Former fleet commander · The Collector · Alive, retired**

<small>Also known as: the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts · Captain Joseph L. Roberts · "The Collector" · Trevor Alan Blake (his real name, by his own account)</small>

Pirate Joseph Roberts, called the Collector, is a Lhazaar fleet commander known to sail the seas near the Dragon Watch Isles, who collects secrets and information rather than cargo in order to get what he wants. He took the Unforeseen — LeBeefe, [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — stripped them of their treasures and gear including a gnome tablet from a nearby rebel base, and left them stranded on a small island; on his return he said he had sold the tablet, gave back everything but the coin, and set them to gather information on the Imperial Assembly council members. He married [Blair](/people/blair/) out of the Lhazaar Principalities and remains her first husband and ally; years later he met her successors in Newham, and his fleet was in the sky over the Veil Building on the night the Iron Veil fell. When Uriel's bombardment fell on the Thronehold Summit he turned his ship and left the island. He was a named target of the Imperial Ministry of Law, with his likeness in the Grand Lodge file. He took the Inevitables out of the valley of the Ivory Lazaret aboard his airship *Revenge*, and a day later told Gemma Corso that his real name was Trevor Alan Blake and that he had taken the name of the Dread Pirate from an earlier Roberts. He gave her the ship, the guild and the name, and left with his wife Vanessa Redwing.

## Physical description

There is a likeness of him in the Imperial Ministry of Law's Grand Lodge file, which is the only official portrait anyone has made. He commands from the deck of an airship now as readily as from a hull, and both the Lhazaar seas and the sky over Thronehold have seen the same silhouette arrive and, on one notable occasion, leave.

## The fleet and the trade

**NicoPico's record:** *Pirate Joseph Roberts aka "The Collector" is a pirate known to sail the seas near the Dragon Watch Isles. He is known to have a large fleet of ships and collects "secrets and information" in order to get what he wants.*

A pirate fleet in the Lhazaar tradition is a standing negotiation between captains, and Roberts sits at the head of one large enough to matter to governments. His stock is leverage — what people did, who they did it with, and who has not yet been told. He trades in it the way a merchant house trades in ore.

To the ports he is a raider with an unusual pricing structure. To the rebellion he is a fleet that answers when [Blair](/people/blair/) calls, and the reason a pirate armada appeared over the Veil Building on the night the Iron Veil fell. To the Empire, as of three years later, he is a name on a Grand Lodge warrant. What is true underneath all three is that Roberts collects, and that no one who has dealt with him has ever established the full extent of what he already knew going in.

## The Unforeseen

**NicoPico's record:**

*When LeBeefe, Uriel, Esther and Locke run into Roberts he forces them to surrender all their treasures and gear including a gnome tablet they found at a nearby rebel base. The crew fought back but in the end they were stripped of their treasures and gear and left stranded on a small island to "await his return".*

*When Joseph Roberts returns he states that he sold the gnome tablet. He returned the group's items and gear besides the coin, and wished for them to gather info on the Imperial Assembly council members.*

*Roberts seems to have background information on [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and seems to know him personally. Locke claims to not remember.*

**Episodes:** [Episode 8 (a&b) "Pirate Joseph Roberts"](/sessions/episode-8-aandb-pirate-joseph-roberts-dm-lamont/)

**The unresolved thread:** Roberts knew [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) before the Unforeseen ever boarded his ship, and Locke says he does not remember him. Locke Pierce now leads [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) and holds a weapon capable of ending the world. Nobody has followed up on what a pirate who collects secrets knows about him.

## Beliefs

He believes information keeps better than gold and sells later at a higher price. He believes a fleet exists to be preserved. He believes a bargain made with him is complete when he says it is.

## Conduct

In practice he takes everything, gives back most of it, keeps the coin, and names a task instead of a ransom. It is a pattern he ran on four Imperial agents on a beach in the Dragon Watch Isles. It also makes him unreliable in a fight he did not choose. When Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s bombardment fell on the Thronehold Summit and the rooftops erupted, Roberts wasted no time. He turned his ship and fled the island entirely, leaving the delegations to Locke Pierce's vessels.

## Origin

His history before the Isles is not recorded. He married [Blair](/people/blair/) out of the Lhazaar Principalities, where she had been raised among pirates, smugglers and sea-born mercenaries after being adopted as an infant; he remains her first husband and a powerful ally, and the political connection he gives her extends far beyond Sharn. He met the Unforeseen at the height of their service to the Empire and robbed all four of them. Years later he met their successors: [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) encountered him in Newham before ever reaching Sharn, and it was learning that they had met her husband — and known her father — that decided Blair to receive them at all. Word reached Sharn during the occupation that he was coming; his fleet was in the sky for the rising.

## Newham and Leef

On the night train to Newham, while [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) were fighting [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/) and her half-illithids, the train rocked as an airship landed on top of it, and three pirates and the Dread Pirate himself, by then quite old but tough, came crashing through the windows. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

> "Congratulations. You're being robbed by the dread pirate Joseph Roberts, an honor offered to few."

<small>— Joseph Roberts, on the night train to Newham.</small>

In the year after Newham fell he landed a small corvair near Gemma's crops at Leef one cold autumn night, pointed his rapier at her throat, told her she owed him a favour and had come to collect, and went down on one knee to ask her to marry him. He said he had decided to leave his playboy ways behind: he was 83, though he might not look it, and he never smoked and seldom drank. He wanted to be married and to father a child, and asked her for nothing more, fidelity included. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Gemma's Complication

The Grand Lodge file names him plainly: the Dread Pirate, Blair's husband, Gemma Corso's former fling. Gemma was handed the warrant for his arrest while wearing a dead Imperial lawyer's face, with a deadline of Monday and a bounty on her own head if she failed to return.

> *He is known to have a large fleet of ships and collects "secrets and information" in order to get what he wants.*

## Fate

Roberts was a named target of the Imperial Ministry of Law. The Grand Lodge assignment handed to Gemma in her cover identity as the Star Advocate Deirdre Moro was to travel to the Summer Palace and the Ivory Lazaret, seize all guest records, and arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates — with his likeness in the file. The Empire was hunting him at the exact place the Inevitables were already going.

In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/), over breakfast in the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), Gemma told the others that she had messaged him from the Lodge door the night they arrived, short and sweet, and that he had answered. She made the case for him from memory: he had spared them on the train, given them a sending stone, and moved people from Newham to Leaf without asking anything but a mural. The party decided to mingle and find out where he was, and had not yet found him. On the Lodge's grand stair two men passed a purse between them and a coin fell; it was minted in [the Uruk-Hoth Republic](/organizations/the-uruk-hoth/), and Gemma remembered General Thar telling Esther at Posay that the Lhazaar Principalities were helping the Uruk-Hoth. Later that day a load of sealed hazardous-materials containers, paid for in platinum at [the Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/), went by carriage straight to the Lodge and around the back. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

In [Korth Ep 17](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/) Eric the Cleric cast *divination* and asked his god how to find the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts. An angel answered in a country song about a pirate whose saucy wife left him to drink and to marry other women, and who has now come up the river asking to see his books and where his money comes from. It ended: *"So if you seek him, seek him near: he's hiding from the one who's here."* Gemma and Eric took the one who is here to be Deirdre Moro. Gemma never married Roberts. He proposed to her after a week or two at his home in the Lhazaar Principalities, and she refused. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) Gemma reached him by sending stone and was told *"If that's true, come to the library. Not safe to message."* He had been working from behind the Grand Lodge's old library, with the Lodge's Fieldmaster as his second and swashbucklers of his own in the guard room. When Gemma dropped her disguise he laughed: *"Deirdre Moro. That is a marvelous face."* He told her that every empire ends in an estate sale and that he was simply early, that the orcs pay in gold and need materiel, and that the Lodge was exempt from Imperial reporting, licensing and river tolls because so many of the Emperor's cousins had been members. He called Uriel racist and stupid, and said his wife [Blair](/people/blair/) still spoke very ill of Eric. He renewed his proposal, and Gemma accepted on condition of her freedom, if she survived. He sold the Lodge that morning to a buyer he would not name, meaning to leave it *"a cinder, so caveat emptor,"* and left the valley on the advice of a psychic. He left a ship and crew the party could call through Gemma's stone, and he carried the fanfare of dawn in her hand. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) Gemma called him by sending stone from the Sept of Bones. He brought the airship *Revenge* in low over the cathedral of Nos Matre and took her and Dario off the roof: *"You know, if you're going to be one of my wives, I'm going to make you work."* At her asking he went back for Eric, Zero-One, Ilma Vitt and Matt Matheny's people. He ran the ship up through the storm ahead of the Hundreds' vessels and told everyone aboard not to look at the flash. It was the third time he had come for the party. Asked whether he had sold the Grand Lodge to Locke Pierce, he said that he did not mix pleasure and business, that he knew how to time these things, and that the buyer had wanted it for a teardown.

After the destruction of the valley he was aboard the *Revenge* with Gemma, Dario and Eric, climbing out toward the Lhazaar seas.

## The last of Joseph Roberts

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) two huge spelljammers crewed by [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) broke the cloud layer behind the *Revenge* and fired a warning shot across her bow. Each was ten times her size. Roberts said: *"Those things are huge, we can't fight them. What should we do?"* He offered the party sanctuary in the Lhazaar Principalities, where he said Locke Pierce would not find them. When Gemma chose to answer Pierce's summons he asked her to come back to him: *"Please come back to me. I might have something important."* When the party returned to the deck from [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/) the ship was below the clouds and sailing low over rough ground, and he said the pursuers had got what they wanted and left.

That night Gemma asked him how well he knew Locke Pierce.

> "Never met him. He still signs all his letters to me as the Lord of Blades."

She told him of the people from beyond the sky and their council of worlds. He knew the old shanties and sailors' tales about people who could sail into the sky and beyond the stars, and he did not like the sound of a large government giving him orders. He said he had known things would go badly when the Empire began posting its big undead guards outside Korth, and that he had made a good business of stripping the Empire for parts.

At dawn the *Revenge* came down on a platform on the eastern peak of a tiny island in an empty sea, with a fishing village, a small port and a minority of well-dressed swashbucklers in the better huts. He took Gemma upstairs to meet the Fieldmaster of the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), now dressed as a swashbuckler: *"Gemma, this is Vanessa Redwing, Master Huntress, and my wife."* She is a half-elf. He then asked Gemma whether she had considered piracy, and gave this account of himself:

> "Vanessa and I are in love. We want to leave. My real name is Trevor Alan Blake. I was a real bastard before, and I met another real bastard. He told me his name was the Dread Pirate Joseph L. Roberts, and I thought he was gonna kill me, but he let me live. And every day, he would wake me up, bring me to see some other part of his business. At the end of the day, he would tell me, Good night, Trevor. I'll probably kill you in the morning. One day he took a bunch of money from four Imperial operatives, a lot of money, and something else from them, which he told me was really valuable. He wanted to retire before they would kill him, and told me that he was not the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts. Ever since then, I've commanded the *Revenge*, and I'm done. So I ask you again, what do you think? Do you want to be the captain?"

Gemma accepted. He told her how the name passes: he had taken over by trading disguises with his predecessor for a time and letting the crew turn over, and there had been Roberts of different genders before. He gave her the *Revenge*, the ledger of the Grand Lodge with all his dealings in it, command of the guild with its commodore's fleet, hideouts, contacts and lines of credit, the island, and his network of portals across the continent. One of those portals, in the library of the Lodge, had been the way goods and fleeing Imperials left the Lazaret, where teleporting was impossible. Her first mate and bosun was to be Stanley Green Jeans. Asked what the Roberts who met the Unforeseen had looked like, Roberts blew his nose in a magical handkerchief, became a man Gemma had never seen, and did the voice of an arrogant man who loved to drive the knife in when he had people cornered. He advised her to keep the *Revenge* far from anything the Hundreds cared about for a while. Blair did not know the island existed.

He kept a large sum, including most of the money from the sale of the Grand Lodge and stacks of gold bars unlike any Gemma had seen, and would not say who had bought the Lodge. Trevor Alan Blake and Vanessa Redwing left for parts unknown. In Sharn [Blair](/people/blair/) was furious to learn who the new Roberts was, and then said she saw why he had given it to Gemma.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to know more than the person across the table, and to still have the fleet afterwards.

**Likes:** secrets · coin, specifically · a debt he can call in later · the Dragon Watch Isles · his wife's territory · people who owe him errands · leaving before the shooting starts.

**Dislikes:** ransoms, which end the relationship · standing bombardments · being on a warrant · anyone who thinks he has told them everything.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** he will abandon an allied position instantly to preserve a hull.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown.

**Social — Contacts & Relations**

- **Blair** — wife, Queen of the Underhive. Standing alliance; his fleet answers hers. Alive.
- **Locke Pierce** — knows him personally, by his own account. Locke denies remembering. Alive.
- **Gemma Corso** — former fling, later betrothed to him, and his successor as the Dread Pirate Roberts; she once held a warrant for his arrest under a false name. Alive.
- **Vanessa Redwing** — Fieldmaster of the Grand Lodge, Master Huntress, and his wife. Left with him.
- **The Unforeseen** — robbed all four, marooned them, then hired them. Esther, LeBeefe and Locke's fates all recorded elsewhere.
- **The Inevitables** — met in Newham; his introduction opened Blair's door to them. Alive.

**Organizations / Groups:** commander of his own fleet; married into [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/); allied to the rising in Sharn; wanted by the Imperial Ministry of Law.

**Holdings:** until Korth Ep 23, a large fleet of ships, and at least one airship · the waters and anchorages around the Dragon Watch Isles · an unnamed small island used for holding people until he gets back. The *Revenge*, the fleet, the guild, the hideaway island and the portal network passed to Gemma Corso.

**Items:** the gnome tablet taken from the Unforeseen at a rebel base near the Dragon Watch Isles, and sold on to a buyer he has never named · a great deal of Imperial coin, none of it returned.

**The Collection:** Roberts's actual trade. He takes secrets and information in order to get what he wants, which means every transaction with him is also a deposit. He knew about Locke Pierce. He knows what the Unforeseen were doing at that rebel base. He knows what the gnome tablet was and who paid for it. None of that has ever been recovered.
