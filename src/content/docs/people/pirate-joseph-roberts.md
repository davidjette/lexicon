**Pirate · Fleet commander · The Collector · Alive, and wanted**

<small>Also known as: the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts · Captain Joseph L. Roberts · "The Collector"</small>

Pirate Joseph Roberts, called the Collector, is a Lhazaar fleet commander known to sail the seas near the Dragon Watch Isles, who collects secrets and information rather than cargo in order to get what he wants. He took the Unforeseen — LeBeefe, [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Esther and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — stripped them of their treasures and gear including a gnome tablet from a nearby rebel base, and left them stranded on a small island; on his return he said he had sold the tablet, gave back everything but the coin, and set them to gather information on the Imperial Assembly council members. He married [Blair](/people/blair/) out of the Lhazaar Principalities and remains her first husband and ally; years later he met her successors in Newham, and his fleet was in the sky over the Veil Building on the night the Iron Veil fell. When Uriel's bombardment fell on the Thronehold Summit he turned his ship and left the island. He is now a named target of the Imperial Ministry of Law, with his likeness in the Grand Lodge file.

## Physical description

There is a likeness of him in the Imperial Ministry of Law's Grand Lodge file, which is the only official portrait anyone has made. He commands from the deck of an airship now as readily as from a hull, and both the Lhazaar seas and the sky over Thronehold have seen the same silhouette arrive and, on one notable occasion, leave.

## The fleet and the trade

**NicoPico's record:** *Pirate Joseph Roberts aka "The Collector" is a pirate known to sail the seas near the Dragon Watch Isles. He is known to have a large fleet of ships and collects "secrets and information" in order to get what he wants.*

A pirate fleet in the Lhazaar tradition is not a navy and not a gang; it is a standing negotiation between captains, and Roberts sits at the head of one large enough to matter to governments. He does not primarily deal in cargo. His stock is leverage — what people did, who they did it with, and who has not yet been told. He trades in it the way a merchant house trades in ore.

To the ports he is a raider with an unusual pricing structure. To the rebellion he is a fleet that answers when [Blair](/people/blair/) calls, and the reason a pirate armada appeared over the Veil Building on the night the Iron Veil fell. To the Empire, as of three years later, he is a name on a Grand Lodge warrant. What is true underneath all three is that Roberts collects, and that no one who has dealt with him has ever established the full extent of what he already knew going in.

**The unresolved thread:** Roberts knew [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) before the Unforeseen ever boarded his ship, and Locke says he does not remember him. Locke Pierce now leads [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) and holds a weapon capable of ending the world. Nobody has followed up on what a pirate who collects secrets knows about him.

## Beliefs

He believes information keeps better than gold and sells later at a higher price. He believes a fleet exists to be preserved, not spent. He believes a bargain made with him is complete when he says it is.

## Conduct

In practice he takes everything, gives back most of it, keeps the coin, and names a task instead of a ransom. It is a pattern he ran on four Imperial agents on a beach in the Dragon Watch Isles. It also makes him unreliable in a fight he did not choose. When Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s bombardment fell on the Thronehold Summit and the rooftops erupted, Roberts wasted no time. He turned his ship and fled the island entirely, leaving the delegations to Locke Pierce's vessels.

## Origin

His history before the Isles is not recorded. He married [Blair](/people/blair/) out of the Lhazaar Principalities, where she had been raised among pirates, smugglers and sea-born mercenaries after being adopted as an infant; he remains her first husband and a powerful ally, and the political connection he gives her extends far beyond Sharn. He met the Unforeseen at the height of their service to the Empire and robbed all four of them. Years later he met their successors: [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) encountered him in Newham before ever reaching Sharn, and it was learning that they had met her husband — and known her father — that decided Blair to receive them at all. Word reached Sharn during the occupation that he was coming; his fleet was in the sky for the rising.

## Gemma's Complication

The Grand Lodge file names him plainly: the Dread Pirate, Blair's husband, Gemma Corso's former fling. Gemma was handed the warrant for his arrest while wearing a dead Imperial lawyer's face, with a deadline of Monday and a bounty on her own head if she failed to return.

## Right now

Roberts is a named target of the Imperial Ministry of Law. The Grand Lodge assignment handed to Gemma in her cover identity as the Star Advocate Deirdre Moro was to travel to the Summer Palace and the Ivory Lazaret, seize all guest records, and arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates — with his likeness in the file. The Empire is hunting him at the exact place the Inevitables are already going.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to know more than the person across the table, and to still have the fleet afterwards.

**Likes:** secrets · coin, specifically · a debt he can call in later · the Dragon Watch Isles · his wife's territory · people who owe him errands · leaving before the shooting starts.

**Dislikes:** ransoms, which end the relationship · standing bombardments · being on a warrant · anyone who thinks he has told them everything.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** he will abandon an allied position instantly to preserve a hull.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown.

**Social — Contacts & Relations**

- **Blair** — wife, Queen of the Underhive. Standing alliance; his fleet answers hers. Alive.
- **Locke Pierce** — knows him personally, by his own account. Locke denies remembering. Alive.
- **Gemma Corso** — former fling; now holds a warrant for his arrest under a false name. Alive.
- **The Unforeseen** — robbed all four, marooned them, then hired them. Esther, LeBeefe and Locke's fates all recorded elsewhere.
- **The Inevitables** — met in Newham; his introduction opened Blair's door to them. Alive.

**Organizations / Groups:** commander of his own fleet; married into [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/); allied to the rising in Sharn; wanted by the Imperial Ministry of Law.

**Holdings:** a large fleet of ships, and at least one airship · the waters and anchorages around the Dragon Watch Isles · an unnamed small island used for holding people until he gets back.

**Items:** the gnome tablet taken from the Unforeseen at a rebel base near the Dragon Watch Isles, and sold on to a buyer he has never named · a great deal of Imperial coin, none of it returned.

**The Collection:** Roberts's actual trade. He takes secrets and information in order to get what he wants, which means every transaction with him is also a deposit. He knew about Locke Pierce. He knows what the Unforeseen were doing at that rebel base. He knows what the gnome tablet was and who paid for it. None of that has ever been recovered.
