**Infanta · At the head of the Moradinate, an army of orcs and dwarves · Opponent at the Battle of the River Lis · Fate unknown**

*Also known as:* none recorded.

Mora is an Infanta of the Age of the Infanta who marched an army of orcs west across Faerûn, with the dwarves lined up behind. The army, the **Moradinate**, devastated each kingdom to the east in turn, sacked Mulmaster, and came to the River Lis on the eastern border of Cormanthor in armored dreadnoughts the size of city blocks. The allied host of Cormyr, [Punis](/places/punis/) and the elves, with the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) commanding units as Purple Dragon officers, met it at [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/), and "stopped the cities". Mora's fate after the battle is unknown.

## The orcs and their Infanta

When the party reached Suzail, "Rumors are brewing that the Orcs have also found an infanta and are holding it at the ruined city of Mordulkin on the Bay of Chessenta." <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small> After [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/), Stonecypher's player Nico listed among the party's aims to "Try and approach the Orcs who now have an infanta." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-30)</small> [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/)'s player JL gave his proposed Infanta council its "First order of business: making contact with the Orcs." Dave, listing the open threads after the war with the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/): "There’s always Asmodeus in the shadowfell, now orcs are laying waste to Thay". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

After [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/)'s trial, JL's news update for Revan reported: "Moradin-worshipping dwarves across the world have announced they wish to side with the marauding Orcs because they think the Orc’s infanta is Moradin." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-22)</small>

## The march west

In September 2019 Dave summed up the state of the Infanta:

> The Infanta are scattered and making moves to shore up theirs power. Zaria is an embryo, Obi is hiding in Waterdeep, Elistrae is losing it and Marcus wants you all to defy the Lady of Pain so she can go to Sigil, Mora is marching an orc army to your gates

<small>— Dave. Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-19</small>

Two weeks later Zanzibar was "close to a way to bring a host of angels to Cormyr to stop the Orc invasion of Cormanthor." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-01)</small> Dave on the kingdoms in the army's path: "Unfortunately each kingdom to the east has had to face the Moradinate alone and have been devastated, it’s scorched earth behind the dreadnoughts", and "Might have been different if you guys moved on the east earlier but they’ve had free reign for two years of play time!" <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

## The Moradinate

The Moradinate is Mora's army. "You have most of humanity and elven kind behind you," Dave told the players, "the dwarves and orcs have lined up behind Mora". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> The Church of Moradin is a separate body.

On the front at the Lis:

> Mora's army has sacked Mulmaster and are using rubble from the dynamited mountains and city walls to send endless missile fire across River Lis into Cormanthor

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21</small>

On its dreadnoughts:

> They will be approaching in massive armored tanks the size of city blocks, but enough to Ford the river and filled with troops and spellcasters, and presumably at least one Infanta

<small>— Dave. Sic. Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-17</small>

## The River Lis

"Forces will engage the Moradinate as it crosses the river as it’s the last chokepoint before their dreadnoughts enter the West." There would be "Infanta deployed on the battlefield on both sides regardless", Dave said: "Tico and Mora at a minimum, likely Zara in utero". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> See [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/).

On the Infanta's power in the field Dave said "it's not just Zan's powers, it's half the Purple Dragons as well. Likewise, the Mora and Tico being near the battle is essential to their followers", and "Infanta also draw power from their nearby followers, so a well attended Infanta will be more powerful than one who is alone". The party were to arrive "just as the majority of the angelic refugees are being suited up for the imminent attack from Mora’s Army crossing the river." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small>

Dave set the objective: "The goal is to stop the Moradinate from crossing the river". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-17)</small> The battle was played on 22 December 2019, and Dave's word on the result was "you stopped the cities". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-12-22)</small>

> A million died at River Lis, so says the Elven lore.
