**Orc · War-Chief of the Uruk-Hoth recon regiment · Uruk-Hoth · Dead**

*Also known as:* Bragga Kul

Braga Kul was the orc War-Chief of an **Uruk-Hoth** recon regiment of roughly twenty, emplaced at the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/) for about five months to watch troop movements in and out of Korth and intercept communications. His men pushed down the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), came back retching and talking about whispers, and blocked the stair with rock and dirt on his order. He cut the throat of a paladin, surrendered on his knees when the fight turned, and spent his captivity warning his captors against the stairs and asking them for a dagger. Three magic missiles through the bars of his cell killed him as his returning scout party opened the great door. The whole regiment was destroyed inside two days, and his body was burned with the rest and dropped into the chasm.

## Physical description

Recorded chiefly by what he carried and what he did with it: a named orcish katana whose inscription has never been read, a campaign book kept in his own hand, and - at the end, in a cell - a rock, palmed and held as a weapon.

## The regiment

Braga Kul commanded a roughly twenty-strong **Uruk-Hoth** recon regiment emplaced at the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/) for about five months, watching troop movements in and out of Korth and intercepting communications. His campaign book is the source for what that job actually meant, and for the fact that the war is coming to Korth and the first troops are already moving.

It also records the one thing everybody wanted to know and nobody expected. His men pushed down the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), fought the things that came up from deeper inside, and came back - in his phrase - retching and talking about whispers. So they blocked the stair with rock and dirt and stayed upstairs. There is **no mention of the bell anywhere in his records**. The orcs were not here for the same reason as anyone else. They were doing reconnaissance and they were unlucky.

## Public and private

Standing over the body of a paladin whose throat he had just cut:

> All of his unloved and neglected children will have no father.

And, later, to a cleric wearing an Imperial disguise: *"We've been here for five months. This is the first time any of you have even found us."* Then, to the same party:

> You pretend like you're so holy... you stink of aberration. I know what you are.

He was the first person in the mountain to say it out loud.

He surrendered on his knees when the fight turned, and then spent his captivity arranging not to survive it. He warned his captors against the stairs - *"You'd be a fool to unblock it. I'm safe in here, unless you intend to unlock this door"* - and asked them for a dagger:

> Do you think I'm going to allow myself to be taken alive by Imperial troops and Black Doves?

## Beliefs

He believed capture by the Empire or the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) was worse than any death, and he acted on it consistently from the moment he surrendered.

He believed whatever lived below the blocked stair was not worth a second visit, and he blocked it rather than send men back.

He believed the party were something other than what they claimed, and he told them so without any leverage to gain from it.

## The last night

A truth serum, delivered into his mouth by invisible mage hand while he slept, failed on his save; he woke, swiped at a floating potion, and made his warning instead: *"You should get out of here right now. That's my advice to you. My reason is that it's your only chance to avoid what's about to happen."*

He had already answered it. Around midnight he had been rapping a rock against his cell bars, exchanging tap-code with signals coming through the stone from above - his scout party, eight strong, coming home. One had eaten a charm of lesser polymorph, come through an arrow slit as a mouse, and lifted the bar on the great door from the inside.

He never got out of the cell. Three magic missiles through the bars killed him as the fight began, and the rock fell out of his hand. He had palmed it after the last signal, and held onto it as his last weapon.

## Aftermath

The entire Uruk-Hoth recon regiment was destroyed inside two days - Braga, the scout party, and a wounded straggler who was taken prisoner, forgotten until morning, and executed at the bridge gap before the descent. Every body in the complex was burned, faces destroyed first, and dropped into the chasm, so that Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) could never raise any of them. Braga was burned with the rest.

**Organizations / Groups:** Uruk-Hoth; the recon regiment watching Korth.
