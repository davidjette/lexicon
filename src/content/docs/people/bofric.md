**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Runesmith of the Teryaki · The Teryaki clan / the Inevitables · Alive**

**Bofric** is a duergar runesmith of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan and the last living reader of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) clan shorthand, which makes him the key to [King Corn](/people/king-corn/)'s lead-bound folio and to the science of the bells. He translated the section of the folio that recorded King Corn's last song, played it on a whistle and then on the king's own brass horn, and named the discipline behind it **arcane harmonics**. He played the same tune at [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) on a broken bridge over the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) and again in the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/), where lifting the bell's confusion off the room allowed the Thirteenth Casting to be broken. When the Teryaki refused to evacuate the mountain he left with the party, and he is now in Sharn, deaf, working through the rest of the folio.

## Physical description

Bent, milk-eyed on the left, with fingers permanently stained black with lampblack from a lifetime of rubbings and inks. He is miserable in daylight and had to be disguised as a halfling to walk through [Korranberg](/places/korranberg/) in the evening. He is deaf at present, and has been since a hammer broke a bell next to him.

## The craft

Bofric reads the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) clan shorthand, which makes him the last living key to [King Corn](/people/king-corn/)'s lead-bound folio and therefore to the whole science of the bells. He estimates a week for the full translation. He has already produced the part that mattered.

He translated one section first, and only because it interested him: King Corn spent his final days enraptured with a muse, perfecting a song. Twenty-four bars with a short eight-bar bridge, in a very dwarven style. Bofric played it on a whistle, and [Eric's](/people/eric-the-cleric/) compulsion softened. Then he was handed the king's own brass horn, recognised the smith-mark on it, leaned into it and played the same tune - and the sound of the bell left Eric's mind entirely.

He named what it was, too: **arcane harmonics**, procured by the elves and only ever mastered by the dwarves, a secret weapon in the elves' war against the dragons. And he named its limit, immediately and without being asked:

> It can only maybe drown out the sound as long as you play it. To break the bell, you would need to break it with the hammer that made it.

## Public and private

He is not a warrior and says so before anyone can find out the hard way. Poor armour, a war axe, a shield, and the duergar gift of turning invisible at will. *"I am terrified of facing such an awful thing. But you are mighty heroes. You will protect me, right?"* He volunteered anyway.

At the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) he stood on a broken dwarven bridge in front of [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) and played, and kept playing while the dragon landed in front of him to kill him and lost a contest of will to a runesmith with a horn. Later, in the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/), with zombies climbing the ladder behind him and a clear route out in front of him, he turned round and played the fanfare instead of running - and lifted the bell's confusion off the entire room, which is the only reason the Thirteenth Casting got broken at all.

## The one who left

When the party told the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) the mountain was lost and urged them to evacuate, [Madame Tso](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/) and [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) both refused. Bofric came. His stated reason was not sentiment:

> The dragon didn't seem like he was going to honour the deal.

He also refused to touch the Crown of Seven Stars, and said plainly that if it were given to him he would carry it straight to the General, and that his people would probably use it - which is precisely why it stayed in [Gemma Corso's](/people/gemma-corso/) bag.

## Right now

Bofric is in Sharn, deaf, working through the rest of the folio. He has already helped establish the method for building more of Corn's horns, and confirmed that the wedding gift of the late Lord Commander of Sharn is made of both black and red Khyber. At a meeting convened at the hospital by [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/), with a representative of every faith on the continent present, Bofric played the fanfare of the Dawn and the clergy took it up - teaching what they have named the **Horn of Mourning** congregation by congregation, across Khorvaire.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki clan; travelling with the Inevitables.

**Items:** A war axe and shield he has never usefully used; the folio, in translation; a whistle.

**Signature capability - arcane harmonics:** Between the horn, the notes and Bofric, more horns can be made and more players trained.
