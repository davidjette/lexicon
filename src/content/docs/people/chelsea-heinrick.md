**Species unrecorded · Child · The Heinrick household, Sharn · Fate unrecorded**

*Also known as:* Chelsea

**Chelsea Heinrick** is the eight-year-old daughter of [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), Mayor of Sharn, and [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/), head of the Arcane Eye surveillance network in the city. Rebel intelligence recorded her in one sentence, as "the lone soft edge in the household," often seen playing with [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) during official events. Her father was killed on the Lord Commander's balcony in Sharn Ep 18 and her mother was tried and executed in Sharn Ep 19. What became of her is not recorded.

## Physical description

Eight years old. <small>No description of her face, hair or colouring survives. She is named in two recovered documents, described once, and appears in the record of the Sharn arc not at all.</small>

## The household

Her father [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) was the Empire's Chief Diplomat, a member of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) and the only man to survive its purge; he became Mayor of Sharn and the architect of its propaganda machine. Her mother [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/) commanded the Arcane Eye surveillance network, Sharn division, from within the Veil Building. Chelsea is the third name in the rebel dossier on the family and the only one filed without a portfolio.

She appears at official events with her parents. Rebel intelligence, which had nothing else to say about an eight-year-old, wrote her down as **"the lone soft edge in the household."** The Heinricks lived inside the network her mother ran, and no source the Inevitables recovered records anything further about her.

## Competence and circle

Her circle is one name long. She is often seen playing with [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) during official events — Calcifer Blaze, age eight, son of the Lord Commander and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), a bright, sheltered child rarely observed outside Imperial compounds, monitored by dedicated personnel at all times, with a nanny and one of Esther's masked Honor Guards always nearby. Neither child knew that the Honor Guards were dead ministers of the Empire.

There is no record of her birth or her birthday, no record of schooling, no record of a nanny, no line of dialogue, and no episode in which she appears. Nothing she has said or thought is recorded.

## Origin

Born into the Heinrick household in Sharn, some eight years before the rising — which places her birth in roughly the same season as Calcifer Blaze's, and within the same decade in which her mother's surveillance network was built and her father was spared a purge that killed twelve of his colleagues and, in some cases, their immediate families. <small>Esther Crona killed council members' families. Henry Heinrick was the exception.</small>

## The two nights

Sharn Ep 18. Her father broadcast a city-wide emergency alert announcing a full lockdown across Sharn, then went up to the penthouse at the Veil Building and stood on the Lord Commander's balcony. Esther Crona revealed her Honor Guards as undead members of the Council of 13 and killed him there, in front of the city, on live broadcast.

Sharn Ep 19. Nest and Fulcrum operatives secured the Veil Building, and high-ranking officials including her mother were taken into custody. Trials began immediately. Hillary Heinrick was found guilty and executed.

Chelsea Heinrick was eight years old, and between Sharn Ep 18 and Sharn Ep 19 both her parents were dead — one killed by the Empire, one by the people who replaced it.

> "Their daughter, eight-year-old Chelsea, is the lone soft edge in the household, often seen playing with Calcifer during official events."\
> \
> — the whole of the rebel file on her, written before either of her parents died

## Right now

Her fate is not recorded. Chelsea Heinrick does not appear in the account of the Council of Sharn, in the trials, in the Silver Flame's citywide cremation directive, or anywhere in the three years of the Korth arc. Her father's death and her mother's execution are both documented; the disposition of an eight-year-old in the Veil Building on the night the rebellion took it is not. <small>Her only recorded friend was smuggled out of Sharn under a fabricated story about an airship, and is being raised somewhere else under a name the Council agreed on.</small>
