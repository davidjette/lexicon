**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Child of the Teryaki camp · The Teryaki clan · Alive, disfigured**

**Egg Roll** is a duergar boy of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) camp inside the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/). He went missing three hours after going for water; his mother, going through his bedroll, produced a hand-drawn map of the entire foundry level, and everything the party found afterwards came off it. He had gone into the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/), where [the bloom](/species/the-bloom/) found him. Discovered invisible beside the Thirteenth Casting, he told [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) that ringing the bell would stop the queen of death and that he could not say no, then ran through a wall of fire and died. Eric revivified him. He is alive, blind and disfigured, in the Teryaki camp.

## Physical description

Before: short, in a green tunic, and hard to keep track of. After: hairless and eyeless, with most of the skin burned away around his mouth so that the teeth stand in a permanent skeletal grin, weeping. He can turn invisible at will, like all his people, and did.

## The map

Egg Roll went missing three hours after going for water, and the camp's response was a closed door - [Madame Tso](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/) told [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) it was not her concern. His mother, going through his bedroll, produced the thing that changed the campaign: a hand-drawn map of the entire [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) level, beautifully done, unmarked but complete.

> He didn't mark it at all, but he obviously found a lot more than the rest of us did.

A boy poking around a dead factory looking for gold had mapped, alone, more of the level than a clan of adults had cleared in weeks. Everything the party found afterwards - the sealed Black Dove shrine, [Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/), the death warrant signed KAIUS III, the library with the consecration rite, the pit and the chain ladder down to the lake - came off Egg Roll's map.

## What happened to him

He had gone into the Glitterhame, and the bloom found him there. Eric dreamed him first: a little duergar boy in the Glitterhame with [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) standing over him, murmuring *"Yes, my little one. Good idea to ring that bell, a jolly good idea."*

He was found later, invisible, peering out of a mine tunnel a few feet from the Thirteenth Casting and just outside the alarm radius. Eric penned him inside a ring of fire deliberately clear of him and tried to talk him down. The conversation is one of the plainest accounts of the bloom anyone has given:

> There's a wriggling in my brain.

Asked whether the wriggling looked like a giant man with an octopus's body, he said that it did. Told that they shared an affliction and that he had to fight it, he answered that ringing the bell would stop the queen of death, and that **he could not say no**. Then he ran for the bell, straight through the wall of fire, and died.

## Coming back

Eric spent a diamond and a spell slot on *revivify*. The boy came back burned to the bone around the mouth, blind and hairless, and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) laid on hands and carried him across the bridge to camp. His mother's first words were *"What have you done, my son?"*

Eric gave the account out loud, deliberately, so that the whole camp heard it: that the boy's own map led them to him, that something in the Glitterhame made him want to ring the bell, that [Sedda](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) had described the same thing, that he ran through a wall of fire before they could stop him and died, and that a cleric brought him back. Madame Tso put a hand on his mother's shoulder, turned to Eric, said thank you, and led them away.

## Right now

Egg Roll is alive, blind and disfigured, in a camp of dwarves inside a mountain claimed by a black dragon who has said out loud that he intends to enslave them. His map left the mountain with the party.
