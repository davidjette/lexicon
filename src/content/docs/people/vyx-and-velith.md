**Drow · Twins · Hired guards of General Thar's camp · Dead**

**Vyx and Velith** were drow twins, a brother and a sister, hired to guard the approach to [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/)'s camp in the King's Forest. They kept giant spiders on the bank above the falls, and both died there. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

> Been waiting to feed a smug elf like you to my babies for years. The Empire is over.

## Above the falls

[Korth Ep 17](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/). When [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Fora Dollar](/people/fora-dollar/) crossed the stream above the falls, giant spiders came out of the trees at the twins' call. Velith's poisoned bolt put Eric to sleep for the whole fight, and a spider began to wind him in silk. Vyx taunted Gemma, who was wearing the face of Deirdre Moro, and she cut off his head. Velith drove both her swords into Dollar's chest, and Gemma blasted her into the river. Dollar took silver and a vial of poison from them and shared it with the other two afterwards. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>
