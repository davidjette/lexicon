**Weathervein lord · Count · Prince of the sea and master of the coves · Bound inside Blackrazor**

*Also known as:* Count Crestus · Count Cresto Octopailius Weathervein · Cresto Octopailus · Crestus Octopaius Weathervein

**Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein** is a lord of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/), "prince of the sea and master of the coves", whose soul is inside the sword [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/). His wife Amphibiana was murdered while she hunted, and he buried her with her father's sword. When he knew the mob would come for him he left his daughter [Vivica](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) in the family tomb to be safe, and he buried wife and daughter there, beneath the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), with his own tomb above as the entrance. Vivica's ghost waited there for his return. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) the party won Blackrazor, with Crestus inside it, in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/).

## The count and his house

Vivica named him to [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) in the tomb:

> I am Vivica Amphibiana, my father is Count Cresto Octopailus Weathervein, prince of the sea and master of the coves. I am here waiting for him to return, so that we may leave this place and return to the land of the Endless Horizon. There, we will be one again, with the Storm, and leave this coil to the brine and flotsam.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

Of her mother and the fall of the house she said: "When my father knew the mob would come for him, he left me here, to be safe. They murdered my mother while she hunted, he buried her anon, along with her father’s sword. Her brothers were to blame." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> Dave, the next day: "Count Cresto Octopailias buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter Vivica Amphibiana in that tomb, with his own tomb above as the entrance", and "Vivica’s two uncles / Cresto’s brothers in law she blamed for their house’s downfall". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

He gave Vivica her ring. Handing it to Dolores, she said: "Take my ring, it is the signet of the Princess of Clouds and a gift from my father Count Crestus." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> See [the Ring of Shooting Stars](/items/ring-of-shooting-stars/).

## His tomb

The *Salvage Operation – Part I* document lists in Crestus's tomb a Ring of Water Walking (DC 19), a "fresco of heavenly storm angels mating w/ women, ruling the seas" in the stonework, and an image and description of the Atlas of Endless Horizons, "a map to heaven, an heirloom of the fam". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## Inside Blackrazor

Blackrazor was one of the three weapons of the Weathervein treasure hoard in White Plume Mountain, and by the end of the session of 2022-08-13 the party held it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> [Keraptis](/people/penguinus-weathervein/)'s riddle for the dungeon placed it "Underneath inverted ziggurat." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small> [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) carried it, and resisted it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17, 2022-09-24)</small>

Dave, on 2022-08-15:

> It wants to end the world one soul at a time so I don’t think so? It’s like a super devil

> Blackrazor is a competitor to that, at least with Crestus inside it. Could be it could be brought to serve hoff goofa in which case who knows, but otherwise, it is a very dangerous item to all of us

"That" is Gabriella's pact with [Hafgufa](/lore/hafgufa/), and Dave judged the sword "worse than Weathervein probably". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

Members of the line were bound into the artifacts of the treasure hoard. Dave said that all three weapons "have true natures we can try to bring our, and their corrupted natures from Keraptis", and that Blackrazor was still evil on its own. <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

Centuries later, in the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), Dave wrote: "Now I guess there’s a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> The demikraken is the Dunwater Horror, Gabriella Hellwood. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-oral-histories-cast.md)</small>
