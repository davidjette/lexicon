**Goddess incarnate (the Infanta) · Chaos, beauty, moonlight, the hunt · House Gallidann / the Lusmundii · Living, enthroned at Motherstone**

*Also known as:* the Infanta of the Moon · the Dark Maiden · Elistraee · Elistrada (in her first incarnation)

**Elistrae** is the cast-out moon-goddess at the centre of the chronicle: "an Elven god of chaos, beauty, hunting, moonlight and song… the god of all dark elves who have left the Underdark to come to live on the surface." Sister of **Lolth**, daughter of **Sylvanus**, she is the deity who hides inside mortal children to be born into the world as the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/). In the age the elves call the Heavenfall she entered a dying human child on Snowdown and grew into the living goddess-queen **Elistrada**, who met [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/) — the incarnation of the dwarven god [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) — as a teenager on the battlefield, fell in love with him, founded the crystal city of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) with him, took his part in the war that obliviated the Netherese, and was left a weeping queen when Stone threw himself into the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) to be forgotten.

Reborn, she was carried in secret through hostile waters as an infant in the Moonshae; ten years later she rode into Motherstone on an elephant at the head of a small army raised around Snowdown and, with the party, pushed the Sword Coast Trading Company out of the city. She convened the Council of Forgetting to decide the city's place in the new world, lost her connection to moon worshippers across the Universe when the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) broke at the Second Battle of Motherstone, and set her servants to finding the spellcasters and engineers who could repair it. After Brightmantle's death in Arc VII she rallied her people for the assault on Netheril, and at the final fold she is one of three Infantas who part the way into the Egg.

> Our true queen, she saw the boy, atop his shining stallion, and with a sigh of true love's power she gave him all her kingdom.

<small>— a Forsworn lullaby, on Ella's Ally Card. The set holds two variants of this card; the other reads "Few mainlanders on the island have heard of the changeling child worshipped by druids in the north, but most assume it is Forsworn propaganda." The boy is [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/).</small>

## Appearance

She has no fixed body. In the Moonshae she is an infant carried in secret through hostile waters; ten years later she is a young Empress who retakes her capital riding an elephant at the head of an invading army — "her divine form is awesome, but she is still a small human girl." The heraldry that stands in for her across the record is older than any of her bodies: a naked woman holding a sword, silhouetted by the Moon, drawn on papers in a slaver captain's cabin above a single line — *"To keep this, you must first give it to me."*

At ten years old she rode into Motherstone on an elephant, leading a small army from around Snowdown, and with the party she pushed the Company out of the city.

## How an Infanta happens

In the age the elves call the **Heavenfall**, a great cosmic mist descended and cut the gods off from the world — conjured, it is now known, by Sylvanus himself, to conceal his plan to invade the Underdark and bring his daughter Lolth to heel. His plan failed. Severed from their creations, the gods warred, and some fell to earth. On Snowdown the first Infanta was born: Elistrae entered a dying human child through its mother's prayers and grew into the living goddess-queen **Elistrada**. It is not possession and it is not avatar-work. The elvish god fused to a mortal soul in order to be born and spread her grace, and, as the moonblade Silverbane puts it, *"they had no choice in the matter."*

A scholar's note in [Mara Mageblood's](/people/mara-mageblood/) journal corrects the other great misconception: the Infanta was no deathless child. "She was a human girl who lived and died at a ripe old age."

## Public and private

Publicly she is a war-banner. The Lusmundii believe the child will turn the tide of history against all kingdoms, laws and cultivation and return the world to nature; House Gallidann fights for her; the Company hunts her; the Forsworn try to counterfeit her. Privately she is a person with a temper the world cannot survive. Harm her and you get the **Cries of Elistrae**, a scream that shakes the earth, wracks the entire island and brings the walls of Harloch to the ground. And when the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) breaks and she is cut off from moon worshippers across the Universe, she does not rage — she grieves, and invades her own champions' dreams night after night, raiding their memories for how the city was broken. [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/) suffers worst.

## Beliefs

She believes her worshippers are a connection, which is why losing them breaks her. She believes a kingdom is worth giving away to a person she loves — she gave Motherstone to Stone, a dwarven god's incarnation, over the objection of two elf-nobles, Eldamir and her brother Calix, who were moments from drowning his entire landing camp. She believes, at the Council of Forgetting, that a goddess may put her own realm's existence to a vote.

## The charge that governs her upbringing

The whole first arc turns on one instruction, spoken by the spectral echo of [Star Quendi](/people/star-quendi/) at Bask Falls Barrow:

> Who are you, champions? Who are you, champions of Elistrae and the Moonshae Isles? Tell me your names so I may sing songs of you among the gildergreens in throne forests of Arboria… But she mustn't be raised as a god. She must be a child, living beneath the Gildergreen.

## The Council of Forgetting

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> With her capital reclaimed she convenes a Council — Kara, Magnus, Drefan, Roland, Yoshi, and the survivors of Motherstone — to decide the city's place in the new world. There are two paths: tune the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) to hide Motherstone and Snowdown from the rest of the world and operate from the shadows, or open the city and fulfil the destiny of the Lexicon by gathering the next generation of Infanta together to preserve divine power in the Material Plane. House Gallidann, radicalised by its long alliance with the Forsworn and Calix, strains toward forgetting. The Lexicon argues to stay open — but will do as she asks.

## Brightmantle and the broken Lexicon

In her first incarnation, **Elistrada**, she was the wife of [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta — [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) himself, an incarnation of the dwarven god and at the same time a specific man. They met as teenagers on the battlefield and fell in love, and they founded Motherstone together before the war with the Netherese. When Calix planned to obliviate Motherstone and frame Brightmantle and the dwarves, Stone could not bear that she would think he had turned on her, and threw himself into the Obliviator so that she would forget he had ever existed. It turned Elistrada into a mysteriously depressed and always-weeping queen. The party freed Stone's memory in Arc II.

Brightmantle built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) to run the heart of the city — together with Calix, from Netherese magic and technology — and infused it with his own personality and intelligence. Lex's account of what that meant to her, given as Arc IV opens:

> When he was in his fullest form, Ella would talk to him every night, asking about his construction and details of the city, hanging on to any detail about her lost love and his designs.

<small>— Lex, Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part II, "Order of Awakening."</small>

The same passage records her state after the Second Battle of Motherstone: "Now that the Lexicon is broken, she is cutoff from moon worshipers all across the Universe, and she is desperate to repair it and complete Brightmantle's vision of a City of Infanta, a holy arc through the Time of Troubles." She "has directed her servants to seek out the oldest and wisest of spellcasters and engineers from across the land" — the archmagi of the Arcaneum, the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, the Grand Wizard of Thay, the arcanagnomes of Greygarden and the forgemaster dwarves of the Sword Mountains — while Ilana and Untari urged her to abandon Motherstone and retreat to the Gildergreen. <small>(Sic on "cutoff." Arc IV Part II. By then the Lexicon had been moved into the Anachron by Roland and Magnus, and Lex speaks from there; see [The Anachron](/items/the-anachron/).)</small>

In Arc VII, when the party returns to Motherstone, "Brightmantle is dead. Elistrae and the entire city weeps." She "is heartbroken but rallies her people to their final calling," the assault on Netheril. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Throne of Tears.")</small>

## What she is beneath the goddess

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> At the end of time, the goddess **June** gives the floor under everything: the gods of this realm are the split soul of the Star Child, whose name was **Zoth-Ommog**, who left their father somewhere deep in the dark and fled him across all of time. Elistrae is also a fragment of divided memory, hiding from a blind devourer in the one place he cannot look — a self that happens *in Time*.

## Right now

She rides at the muster with Eldamir and Calix at the head of the moon elves, and at the final fold she is one of three Infantas — with Eloise and Tymora — who part the way into the Egg. The Age that bears her name is about to end, and she is about to be forgotten out of it.

**Organizations / Groups:** the Lusmundii, her druidic cult · House Gallidann · the moon elves of every age

**Holdings:** [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), the crystal city — founded by her first incarnation with Stone, lost to the Forgetting, and retaken by her second at the head of an army · the Gildergreen

**Items:** the sibling moonblades of the elven houses, [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) chief among them, borne in her name. The **Flask of Starfire** — "a flask of white light" that, with a prayer to Elistraee, can bring a dying body fully back to life; found in Daenan Gallidann's sarcophagus in the deep hold of the *Evening Star*.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Lolth** — her sister, whom Sylvanus blinded the world to bring to heel. Estranged is putting it gently.
- **Sylvanus** — her father, whose failed plan caused the Heavenfall.
- **Star Quendi** — guardian and mother-figure to her first incarnation; ascended to Arboria.
- **[Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)** — the dwarven god whose incarnation she loved, "a genuine blasphemy," and for whom she gave away Motherstone; her husband Stone in her first incarnation; builder of the Lexicon, which carries his personality. Dead in Arc VII.
- **Calix** — demanded her as a pupil, betrayed her, nearly crowned her to death, and finally fought beside her.
- **Untari** and the Bladesingers · the [Lusmundii](/organizations/the-lusmundii/) — the people who carried her when she was too small to carry herself.
