**Heir to [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Son of [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/)**

**Also known as:** Darius Klaxon - Darkcember - Montevideo; Darius Montevideo

Darius Klaxon is the son of the Waterdeep minor noble [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) and the heir of a line that includes [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/). His name joins Klaxon, Darkcember and Montevideo. His father sent him the protocol droid [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) as a tutor, but the shipment was waylaid by a handsome man with a glowing sword. Darius seeks the RUIN card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/) to free himself of a pact made through [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/); his father planned to place him on the Lords' Council by ruining Beef Wellington, and after the heist at Tiffany House, Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep.

## Name

His full name is Darius Klaxon - Darkcember - Montevideo. Montevideo is part of his last name.

## Family and the pact

Darius is a shareholder in [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) and the heir of a line that includes Tiffany House. <small>(DM notes)</small> His father married a Darkcember, and wants Darius to become the Panther and take the Panther Mask. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The pact with Asmodeus made through Griddy Champagne is unbearable for him, and he is seeking the card of RUIN to divest himself of it. <small>(DM notes)</small> Griddy has obtained RUIN for both Darius and his father, and intends to play them off each other. <small>(DM notes)</small>

After the Klaxons, [Maverick](/people/maverick/), a distant descendant of [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/), is heir to Tiffany House.

## Nicollo

Nicollo was built by a clandestine engineer who modifies protocol droids into diplomacy and spycraft tutors for young princes. Phillip Klaxon sent him to Darius as a tutor for leadership and cunning, but the shipment was waylaid on its way, attacked by a handsome man with a glowing sword. <small>(DM notes)</small> That man was [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/). Nicollo's own quest is to contact Darius. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Tiffany House

At Tiffany House Phillip Klaxon told Nicollo about his plan to place Darius on the Lords' Council by ruining Beef Wellington. Darius did not want this. <small>(DM notes)</small> After the heist, Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep; Wellington was ruined, and the Lords' meeting was four days off. See [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).
