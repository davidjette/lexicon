**Master rogue · Extortionist and enforcer · [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Dead**

**Also known as:** RW

Rotten William was a rogue who led a group of extortionists in the [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), enforced [Pharod](/people/pharod/)'s corpse-collecting racket for a cut, and warred with the thieves' guild of [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/). His thugs robbed [Heyu](/people/heyu/) of her spellbook on her arrival at [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/). The party raided his hideout in the Spillway, took and then abandoned his contract on Sharegrave's life, and later stormed his lair again. He fled into the sewers, and in the Sanitizing Room of the Spillway Depths, where a plague portal had opened in the drain, he was killed. Closing that portal cured [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/).

## The extortionists

Rotten William worked in and around the Marketplace, at the head of a group of extortionists. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "Pay up or I'll make an example of you right here..."

<small>(Rotten William, DM notes)</small>

His thug Enya worked near the Mortuary and shook down newcomers: "What do you want Berk? asking for trouble? Hand over your junk." <small>(DM notes)</small> In Ragpicker Square his thugs harassed the storyteller Reekwind, jeering "go on and die so we can sell your corpse!" <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Heyu's spellbook

When Heyu came to Sigil and found her way alone to the Mortuary, she was ambushed by Hive thugs working for Rotten William, who took her spellbook and threw her into a corpse chute. <small>(Heyu's story, DM notes)</small> After the party escaped the Mortuary and fought Ratbone in the street, they learned that Ratbone worked for Sharegrave, not Rotten William, and that Enya, who did have the book and was Sharegrave's enemy, worked for Rotten William and was something in the Night Market. The party recovered the spellbook. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## The corpse racket

Pharod has a lock on the whole business of the corpse collectors, and Rotten William enforces it for a cut. <small>(DM notes)</small> [Prez](/people/prez/), sent by Sharegrave to spy on him, was discovered and captured, and overheard Pharod accepting bodies for disposal with no questions asked, in a deal arranged by Rotten William. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave counts Rotten William as a big problem: he has been killing Sharegrave's men, and Sharegrave fears the murder racket will bring a crackdown on the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Pharod is plotting the death of the party, and "RW + Ph.'s guys" are climbing the Hive to ambush them. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The first raid and the contract

At the party's first rest they heard that Rotten William was in the Spillway, and saw a cranium rat running northwest toward it. They invaded his hideout there, found evidence of a fight with Tabaxi, saw cranium rats, and rescued Prez. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

There they made a deal with Rotten William to kill Sharegrave. He gave them a wand, which he said would paralyse Sharegrave and track them. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small> In fact it held a single charge of *fireball* and a *command* to cast it in Sharegrave's presence. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave's counter-offer was to double-cross Rotten William and kill him, for 200 gold each, and the party's outstanding quests became "Doublecross RW, kill him". <small>(DM notes)</small>

At the Grindhouse, Rotten William had agents in the crowd. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sir Guy Standing, having lost a Ring of Jumping and a Blade of Woe to [Maverick](/people/maverick/), meant to hire Rotten William to murder him and get them back. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Spillway Depths

The party assaulted Rotten William's lair in the Spillway, "killing everyone except 'Whitey,' who they tortured (lightly) but then escaped." Rotten William had been planning a kidnapping and a murder for hire, and fled into the sewer. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

They pursued him deeper, past slimes and hazards, and met a giant spider and a colony of cranium rats called Many-as-One. They talked their way through, and found Rotten William and his cronies afraid to enter a chamber filled with black tendrils. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## Death in the Sanitizing Room

In the Sanitizing Room a portal to a plague dimension had opened in the drain and was poisoning the water system. Craig, the missing Bleaker intern sought by [Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/), was there, and so was the plague demon Gorelach and Gunt. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

> Craig had attempted to finish his Master's mission to close it, but in so doing, angered a plague demon. RW ordered his men to stop him, but the party ambushed and killed them and then the Demon.. RW did try to appeal to it to save him, but it held no mercy for him.

<small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

The party killed Gorelach and Gunt, saved Craig, closed the portal for an hour and killed Rotten William, and by closing the portal cured Cageblight. [Barold](/people/barold-bonds/) was killed in the fight and reincarnated as a tiefling. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small> Among the spoils were a Wand of Secrets, an Epee of Temperance, various potions and a ring of invisibility. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

**Arms:** A wand of ice spikes, a dagger, and a ring of invisibility with one use. <small>(DM notes)</small>
