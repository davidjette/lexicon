**Dwarf (Mark of Warding) · Artificer 16 · First Artificer · [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Red-Eye · the Red Eye · 1st Sgt. Barrias Red-Eye · Barris · Berrias · Barrías · Barry

**Barrias Ruby-Eye** is an Eberron-marked dwarf artificer and shipwright, played by Dave, who served as First Artificer and chief engineer of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). Born in orbit over a Giff world and raised on ships, he built robots, among them [Se7en](/people/se7en/), and was stranded on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) with [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) in the [Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/). Fifteen years after the wreck of the Zanzibar, with artificial intelligence outlawed across the Sovereignty, he returned with Se7en in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, where "Red-Eye" is the nom de guerre used by the robot resistance, and travelled through the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/) with Se7en and [Nine](/people/nine/).

## Background

Barrias comes from a Giff-dominated world. He was born in orbit and raised on ships. The one that got away was a Giff woman whose father was too rich to let her leave with a grease monkey like "Barry". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-21)</small> His character sheet gives him the Mark of Warding; when Dave first built the dwarf in October 2022 he chose the Eberron mark of transport, because the character was born on a starship. <small>(character sheet; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-21)</small>

Dave's first concept for the character, in May 2022, was **Karris Ruby-Eye**, an Eberron dwarf armorer and engineer with a magic spacesuit. By September he was "Barris Red Eye", the engineer who wears a spacesuit at all times because he is always out on EVAs, and in October he was built with the Shipwright background as "an artificer and shipwright" who carries a gun. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-05-01, 2022-09-26, 2022-10-09, 2022-10-10)</small>

### The Beholder War

In the Beholder War, fought before *Light of Xaryxis* and played as a three-episode flashback in 2024, Barrias was stranded on Negroni Prime with Jim Smallberries, the boy [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/) and a dozen civilians in the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, the first use of [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/). Jim, Barrias and Tillamook were the only ones carrying guns; everyone else was a civilian. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09, 2024-03-15)</small> [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) left the group to recover the Demoncore and took the artillery robot [1.0](/people/1-0/) with them. Jim and Barrias were left stranded on Negroni Prime. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

The crew roster of the Zanzibar lists him as **FIRST ARTIFICER, 1st Sgt. Barrias Red-Eye**, in the Engineering department. <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

Barrias makes robots, and carries an Alchemy Jug that he calls a Matter Replicator. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Se7en is the seventh robot he built. Dave's explanation for the failed units before them was that Barrias "maybe" keeps trying to build an automated brewing unit, and the units keep gaining sentience. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small>

He was aboard for the short trip to the asteroid where the party killed their first void scavver. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-11)</small> On the small paradise world of December 2022, when Captain Smallberries gave the Zanzibar his duress code and ordered it to leave, Dave judged that Barrias would probably have overruled Ensign Frissa and broken protocol to ignore the order. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-18)</small>

Barrias's old flame returned in an episode of December 2022, in which the Iridium Complicator appeared. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-31)</small> In August 2023 Barrias was left unsure whether a lich had actually cast the wish that saved Tillamook. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-11)</small>

Barrias's sister works for the Company, [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>

After the finale, Dave considered where Barrias would be. Without Jim and the Zanzibar he would not go back to Astral Command. He might build a workshop robot utopia, hire on to a smuggler crew as a grease bucket, or live alone on the periphery except for Seven, Eight and Nine, on a ramshackle pirate ship, collecting salvage to repair it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-04)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

*The Starsong Awakens*, which JL ran, is set fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*. A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the [Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) outlawed all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty, warforged and autognomes included, which led to a violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market in robots. The call to action came from Barrias's sister, [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/), who brought a dire warning and a Company dossier. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-02, 2024-06-11)</small>

Barrias and Se7en returned at level 12, each with a feat learned in the fifteen years between. The arc ran as two storylines that later joined: one followed Barrias and Se7en, and the other two rookie agents of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) on the moon of Toril. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small> Before play began, Dave thought Barrias was missing an arm and becoming part machine, even as the world turned against machines in a frenzy of hatred. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-18)</small>

The first episode opened with two scenes over drinks. In the second, with old friends and family, Halle accepted the drink. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-11)</small> On 2024-08-09 Barrias and Se7en slaughtered the diners and worked through riddles, and on 2024-08-23 each earned 4,600 XP in a session at a prison. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-09, 2024-08-24)</small>

Halle told Barrias of a "star scream" related to [Caprica](/people/caprica/), and tipped him off to a stash held under the name Emily Rugburn at Ragnar XII, a "prize from company". He escaped the BOC to the Ebethor system, noted as "dead god", and collected the stash at Ragnar XII: a topaz dragon egg, a laser rifle and a laser pistol, 29,000 gold (1,000 of it to Tiberius at the Cube), healing potions, an eldritch musket, a heavy platinum crossbow +1, a light plasma crossbow +1 and three clubs of compliance. He went to Bral to get a nautiloid, ended up in a temple under the city with a golden eagle and a fae god, and stole the nautiloid along with robots and Grover Stevens. <small>(character sheet)</small> In late September 2024 the story returned to [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-09-25)</small>

In December 2024 Barrias acquired a new weapon. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-07)</small> In January 2025 he and [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) went back to Picon to continue the search for Caprica. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-18)</small>

By May 2025 Barrias and Se7en were in the Sphinx Tomb with Nine, going through its time doors, with the ship *Pussyfoot* parked outside. Through the doors they saw earlier events: Dave recalled the last door as a visit to the Five Zanzibars, and Se7en's player recalled it as the final fight on the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small> Dave, as a player, described the arc as centred on Nine, who set out to fix everything bad that happened after Tannhauser Gate, and on his father and brother eventually bringing him home. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small>

The climax of Starfall came at [Necis](/places/necis/), at the end of both *Light of Xaryxis* and *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

### The Red Eye

"Red-Eye", also "Red Eye", is the nom de guerre used by the robot resistance in *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Four sites on Selune are holy to [the children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/). The party were told: "Make the pilgrimage, follow his righteous path, to save your mayor’s life." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16)</small> When the dead Bureau agents [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) and [Paytra](/people/paytra/) were rebuilt as robots, JL called their mission a success, since they had found the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10)</small>

In battle Barrias's helmet flips up and his suit grows, moving fast and firing blasts, until he looks like a flying robot tank. Dave, as a player, took this to be why the Bureau suspects that Red Eye is a construct. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-12)</small>

## Appearance

Barrias is a red-headed dwarf, four feet tall, with ruddy skin and red eyes. <small>(character sheet)</small> His sheet describes him as young, "with more than a few billions miles on his face" <small>(sic)</small>. One eye holds a heads-up display and a glowing red ruby, "which sparks with energy when ever he is angry" <small>(sic)</small>. His shield-console is always glowing, beeping and demanding his attention. He wears a spacesuit twice as heavy as himself that hums as it works, its gyros and pistons letting his small frame do the work of a man five times his size. He is often followed by a large, faceless loading and repair droid whose forehead also holds a glowing ruby, which he calls SPANNER. <small>(character sheet)</small> The Zanzibar's roster lists a cargo loader droid named "Spanner". <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Barrias's sheet makes him an Artificer 16 of the Armorer specialty with the Shipwright background and 197,195 XP. He has 147 hit points, AC 22, a walking and a flying speed of 25 feet, Strength 21, Constitution 19 and Intelligence 20, darkvision, resistance to poison, and the Durable feat. The Mark of Warding lets him cast *alarm*, *mage armor* and *arcane lock*. He speaks Common and Dwarvish, and is proficient with carpenter's, glassblower's, jeweler's, smith's, thieves' and tinker's tools and water vehicles. <small>(character sheet)</small>

His weapons are a Monkey Wrench, the thunder gauntlets of his guardian armor, *fire bolt* and *shocking grasp*. <small>(character sheet)</small> At level 12 Dave described his build as 20 AC without a shield, with infusions that let him fly, Strength 19 from a gauntlet, an improved arcane focus and a reaction to save on Constitution checks, and a spell-storing item that casts *enlarge/reduce* ten times, so that his armor can turn him into a Large creature. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-12)</small>

**Items:**
- **Red-Eye Helm** and **Ruby Eye Piece**, attuned. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Exosuit**, his spacesuit. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Matter Replicator**, an Alchemy Jug. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>
- **Iridium Complicator**, a +1 musket dealing 1d12 piercing damage; on a hit the target must make a DC 10 Intelligence save or take 1d6 psychic damage, and its bullets are rare and farmed from meteors. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-31)</small>
- **Rocket Boots**, **Belt of Hill Giant Strength**, **Magic Missile Launcher**, **Rod of Security**, **Mind Sharpener**, **Arcane Gizmo** and a shield. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Personality

Barrias is "clever and dry". <small>(character sheet)</small> His sheet gives:
- **Traits:** "I’m not afraid of hard work — in fact, I prefer it. I don’t mind getting my hands dirty. I thrive under pressure."
- **Ideals:** "Crew. If everyone on deck pitches in, we’ll never sink." and "Invention. Make what you need out of whatever is at hand."
- **Bonds:** "I repair broken things to redeem what’s broken in myself." and "I will craft a boat capable of sailing through the most dangerous of storms."
- **Flaws:** "I’m judgmental of those who are not skilled with tools of some kind." and "I get frustrated to the point of distraction by shoddy craftsmanship."

His faith is given as "Realist". <small>(character sheet)</small>
