**Plasmoid · Monk 10 · First Apothecar, SSJ Zanzibar · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Glup · Doctor Glup · Dr Glup

**Dr. Glup** is a plasmoid monk of the Way of Mercy and the First Apothecar, or medical officer, of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), a non-player character played by Dave. During [the mind flayer crisis](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) of November 2022, in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, Glup's monk abilities were used to overpower the ship's captain, and Glup reported the mark the mind flayer left on [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/).

## Background

Dr. Glup was one of the non-player crew built for the Zanzibar before the campaign began, with the autopilot [F.L.A.P.](/people/f-l-a-p/) and the cook [Kili Tiddleywink](/people/kili-tiddleywink/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> The character sheet gives Glup's background as Astral Drifter and faith as the Ooze of Zivilyn. <small>(character sheet)</small> Glup's birthplace and age are unknown.

## Light of Xaryxis

On the crew roster of the Zanzibar, Dr. Glup is the First Apothecar, with Ensign [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) as medical assistant. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> Mordecai, a tiefling warlock of the medical corps, works for Dr. Glup. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small>

During the mind flayer crisis, [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) and possibly Dr. Glup were the only people aboard who knew much about mind flayers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small> In the second of its three sessions, on 18 November 2022, Glup's monk abilities were used to overpower [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-19)</small>

At the close of the crisis, [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) warned Carlos Vega as she issued his civilian credentials:

> Doctor Glup told me that the mindflayer left a mark on you. I witnessed this curse and heard it’s demands.

<small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

The character sheet makes Dr. Glup a tenth-level monk of the Way of Mercy, able to heal or harm with a touch and to end diseases and conditions. Glup has the Magic Initiate feat from the cleric list, with *spare the dying*, *mending* and *cure wounds*, and is proficient with the herbalism kit and potter's tools. As a plasmoid, Glup can reshape the body and extrude a pseudopod, and resists acid and poison. Glup speaks Celestial, Common, Gith and Quori. <small>(character sheet)</small>

Glup carries a superior potion of healing and three potions of healing; potions of heroism and water breathing; potions of fire, cold, poison, psychic and radiant resistance; a wand of magic detection; two beads of nourishment; two healer's kits; two herbalism kits; and three vials of antitoxin. <small>(character sheet)</small>
