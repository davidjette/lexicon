**Cleric of the Black Doves · The Korranberg Foundry · The Empire · DEAD — twice, three years apart, in the same suite**

**Also known as:** None recorded.

**Sister Calvane** was a cleric of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and the Sister resident at the Korranberg Foundry, where the enslaved Deep Tempura Clan cast bells for the House of Kaius and the Sisters consecrated them on delivery. Her own name stands on a death warrant signed by [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) ordering the execution of Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, Sister Calvane, and every dwarf in the foundry. When Sedda rang the Thirteenth Casting and killed everyone in the mountain, Calvane's door was sealed from outside by a Black Dove prayer ritual under a writ from [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/). Three years later the Inevitables opened it and destroyed two bodies in the same afternoon: a succubus wearing her face, and Calvane herself, undead and hostile.

## Physical description

No description of the living woman survives. What the Inevitables met were two bodies wearing her: a nun in a locked room who became a red-skinned demon woman with wings, horns and a tail; and, deeper in the same suite, the real Sister Calvane, undead and near-vampiric, who attacked without speaking. Both were destroyed in the same afternoon. The only artefacts of her actual life are her rooms, her library, and her name on a death warrant.

## The order she served

The Black Doves were the consecrating authority at the Korranberg Foundry: castings are consecrated on delivery, *by the Sisters alone*, with foundry personnel excluded and reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft. Calvane was the Sister resident at the foundry — the one who lived on site while the Deep Tempura Clan were worked to death in their own halls making bells for the House of Kaius. Her suite had a bedroom, a study and a library, and the library held the full consecration rite of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/).

## Public and private

In the Empire's version, the resident Sister of a working foundry is the chaplain: she consecrates the castings, tends the sick, and buries the dead. In the version written on Overseer Velen Marsk's warrant, **Sister Calvane's own name appears on the kill list** — signed by Emperor Kaius III, ordering the execution of Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, Sister Calvane, and every dwarf in the foundry, ending with Marsk himself. The Empire's own consecrating priest was scheduled to die with the slaves.

## Competence and circle

Her library was worth fifty pounds. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) pulled the consecration rite out of it entire — thirteen Doves, an invocation of all thirteen planes, the bells bound to *"the unholy blood and the great mother,"* and a very dear sacrifice, a living child or virgin royalty. She knew the dwarves were planning an uprising. Her door was sealed by a superior officer of her own order.

## Secret

Something else was in her rooms. The demon that wore her face had come through an Abyssal breach opened by an Alkilith in the drainage tunnels beneath the Glitterhame — the tunnel was open to the Abyss, and a succubus walked up it into a sealed suite. The tunnel's crevice is one of "the cracks between this realm and Khyber" <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-15)</small>; the Alkilith living in it had opened it into the Abyssal doorway, since "any crack they live in could became such a portal". <small>(sic; Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-20)</small> Whether Calvane invited it, was replaced by it, or simply died beside it is not recorded.

## Beliefs

She believes the rite is hers to perform and the count is not hers to question. She believes the uprising below her floor is a matter for the Overseer and not for a Sister. She believes she is protected by the order she serves.

## Conduct in role

Resident, quiet and thorough. She consecrated what came off the floor, kept a working liturgical library rather than a devotional one, and knew what the dwarves were planning without acting on it. When the bell rang she did not leave — she could not; the door was sealed behind her by a Black Dove prayer ritual, a muslin sheet covered in protective scripture and sewn into the frame, and the writ was signed by [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/).

## Origin

Unknown. Nothing of Calvane before her posting to the foundry is recorded, and the foundry burned its own dockets nightly on a brazier kept by the gate for exactly that. What is known begins with the Korranberg works: hundreds of parish castings and the great castings for the House of Kaius, made by the enslaved Deep Tempura Clan under Overseer Velen Marsk, consecrated on delivery by the Sisters. Three years ago King Corn and his daughter Sedda were discovered planning a rising. Sedda was taken below to the dragon's cavern — but not before she rang the Thirteenth Casting with a rock, and killed everyone in the mountain.

## The Door and the Demon

At the end of the living quarters the Inevitables found a door consecrated shut: a white ceremonial sheet, modest geometric embroidery, meticulously sewn into the wood frame as a sealing ritual, carrying a writ of binding from Sister Maelis Dorn. The duergar had left it alone for three years. [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) tore the sheet down and Gemma picked the lock.

Inside was a nun who said she was Sister Calvane, that she had been locked in the room since the bell rang three years ago, that she knew the dwarves were planning an uprising — and that what she had heard through the rock was **not a bell but a horn**. When she tried to leave, Gemma blocked the door. She attacked, transformed mid-fight into a red-skinned demon woman with wings, horns and a tail, fled into the hall, and died there.

> "She said she had been locked in the room since the bell rang three years ago… and that she had heard not a bell, but a horn."

Deeper in the suite, in the bedroom, was the real Sister Calvane: undead, almost vampiric in nature, and hostile. Gemma and Dario killed her. During that fight the voice in [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s head overwhelmed him and showed him Sedda Deeptempura, spore-infected, hearing the same voice and ringing the bell.

<small>King Corn spent his last days perfecting a melody for a handheld horn built to answer the casting; the demon in the sealed room heard it through three years of rock.</small>

## Right now

Both Calvanes are dead and her library is in the Inevitables' hands. Her name stands on a death warrant signed by the Emperor, in a room whose drains are caked with three-year-old blood, one line above the Overseer who was ordered to kill her and then kill himself.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation** — Unknown. Nothing she said in life was recorded.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:** a complete liturgical library · consecration performed correctly · a locked suite · knowing what the labour is planning · the rite of the thirteen planes.

**Dislikes:** being sealed in · the sound that is not a bell · people at her door · the Overseer's paperwork.

**Vices & Personality flaws** — She knew about the uprising and said nothing, and it killed her.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Sister Maelis Dorn** — sealed Calvane's door with a Black Dove prayer ritual after the bell rang. Alive, whereabouts unknown.
- **Overseer Velen Marsk** — held the warrant with her name on it. Three years dead and still on shift when the Inevitables destroyed him.
- **Emperor Kaius III** — signed the warrant.
- **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura** — rang the bell that killed them both. Consecrated by Dario and General Tso.
- **The thing that wore her face** — a succubus, in by way of an Abyssal breach in the drainage tunnels. Dead in the hall.

**Hobbies & Pets** — None recorded.
