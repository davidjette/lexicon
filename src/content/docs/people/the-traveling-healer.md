**Dragonborn · Cleric of the Life Domain, Level 6 · Temple Holdings LLC · Chaotic Good · Alive**

*Also known as:* the traveling healer · "a weathered dragonborn woman" · <small>she is unnamed in every surviving source</small>

The **traveling healer** is an unnamed dragonborn cleric of the Life Domain who guided [Kara](/people/kara/) and [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) to the buried city beneath the snowy mountain. Raised an orphan in a human village, she was taught holy magic by the local convent, which tried to keep her as a nurse; she took the act on the road instead, "bringing healing to people wherever it was most dangerous." A month before she met the two of them she was ambushed and kidnapped by a clan of goblins while roving the forest north of Westphal, woke in an old mine, and climbed out through a strange cave holding a hundred-year-old camp and four pages in Elvish — [Mara Mageblood's](/people/mara-mageblood/). She recognised the symbol the two strangers in the tavern were carrying, and offered to take them back to the mine through a forest crawling with the Company, on condition of knowing what they intended to do with the world.

## Bearing and arms

She is described in play only as weathered. Her sheet leaves height, weight, eyes, hair, colouring, age and even her draconic ancestry blank, and her deity is not named. What she carries is specific: **Mithral Ring Mail** and a **Ring of the Ram**, with a shield, a mace, a shortbow, holy symbol and vestments, a censer and two blocks of incense, an alms box, brewer's supplies, a shovel and an iron pot. AC 16, 33 hit points, WIS 16, Medicine and Insight and Animal Handling and Survival all at +6. She casts Revivify and Beacon of Hope, and her Channel Divinity preserves life rather than destroying undead — though she can do that too.

## Origin

She was raised an orphan in a human village full of kind people who considered her one of them. She does not know her parentage and does not care to. As a young dragonborn she was stronger and better than all the other children: at eleven she saved a whole clan from a burning barn during a hoedown, and at sixteen she defeated a band of marauders with a hammer and a holy book. The local convent taught her holy magic and tried to keep her.

## How she found the mountain

A month before the heroes met her, roving the forest north of Westphal, she was ambushed and kidnapped by a clan of goblins and woke up in an old mine. Her account of the escape is the first description anyone gives of the buried machine:

> I climbed for what felt like a mile and found myself inside a strange cave. Inside the cave, I found a makeshift camp maybe a hundred years old. Those symbols were on the cave and on some papers I found at the camp.

The camp was [Mara Mageblood's](/people/mara-mageblood/). The papers were four pages in Elvish, "mostly a letter to a young girl named **Fhastine**, apologizing for leaving her alone to seek the meaning of some strange family mystery." A kidnapping victim climbing out of a goblin mine is the reason the party ever finds Motherstone.

## Public and private

Publicly she is a wandering field medic with expensive armour and no fixed congregation. Privately she is a fugitive from something: her sheet's Allies box is empty except for one line under Enemies — **The Company** — and her stated flaw is that "the tyrant who rules my land will stop at nothing to see me killed." She says as much when the heroes ask her to guide them:

> That depends on your intentions. Are you people seeking to do harm? Or do you try to spread goodness through the world?… Besides, Westphal's a march through the City, and the forest itself is crawling with the Company. I can make it through, but I'm not so sure about you all… I'm not welcome there.

## Beliefs

She believes tyrants must not be allowed to oppress the people, which is the ideal written on her sheet and the reason the Company is on it. She believes she protects those who cannot protect themselves. She believes healing belongs where it is most dangerous rather than where it is most comfortable, and left a convent posting to prove it.

## Signature capability — the ferry through the Company's forest

She can cross ground the party cannot. The route to the mine runs through the city and a forest crawling with Company men, and she has already made the trip once, in the wrong direction, on foot, alone, after being kidnapped. She offers to take them — conditionally, after asking what they intend to do with the world.

## Right now

She is guiding two strangers toward a hundred-year-old grave and a steel cave that should not exist under a mountain, with an enemy she cannot name in a kingdom she cannot enter.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation:** To put healing where it is most needed, and tyrants where they belong.

**Likes & Dislikes:** Likes: helping anyone in trouble · hard drink and the vices of the city · brewing · animals · long words. Dislikes: tyrants · the Company · being made into a nurse · going anywhere near Westphal.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** A weakness for the vices of the city, especially hard drink. And she misuses long words in an attempt to sound smarter.

**Hobbies & Pets:** brewing. She carries the supplies everywhere.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Kara** and **Brother Magnus** — the two she approached in the tavern; the people she leads to the mountain.
- **Fhastine Mageblood** — never met her; carried her mother's letter out of a mine for a month without knowing who it was for.
- **The Company** — the only entry in the Enemies box on her sheet.
- **The tyrant who rules her land** — unnamed in the record; the reason she is not welcome in Westphal.
- **A childhood sweetheart** — unnamed. Her stated regret is that they did not come with her.
- **The convent** — trained her, wanted to keep her indoors.

**Organizations / Groups:** the convent that trained her, left behind · Temple Holdings LLC, by recruitment

**Holdings:** none. She travels.

**Items:** **Mithral Ring Mail** (worn) · a **Ring of the Ram** · an alms box · brewer's supplies · a censer and incense. Provenance for the two magic items is not recorded.
