**Khoravar · Fleetmaster Admiral · House Lyrandar / Imperial Navy and Aerial Command · Dead**

*Also known as:* Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar · Vex Lyrandar

Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar was a Khoravar of House Lyrandar and the Empire's Fleetmaster Admiral, commanding its war-sea and airship armada and holding the Imperial Council's portfolio for navy and aerial command. Born in Aundair and bearing the Dragonmark of Storm, he called the Summit at Thronehold to negotiate an end to the civil war. At the Summit, Sister Maelis Dorn accused Chancellor [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) of responsibility for the death of his son [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/); Vex broadcast a kill order through the ArcEye cameras, the masked Imperial guards turned out to be undead loyal to [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and attacked him, and he called his own warships down on the Summit grounds. He pursued the party onto Locke Pierce's airship and was killed at Korth Ep 3 when [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) drove the Bright Dawn up his spine.

## Physical description

Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar stands as the Empire's Fleetmaster Admiral, commanding its formidable war sea and airship armada. Born in Aundair, this Khoravar of half-elf, half-human lineage cuts an imposing figure. His stern, quiet demeanor lend him an aura of authority that requires no words. Vex is rarely seen without his ceremonial blade, forged from rare **Khyber-Skyglass**, a symbol of his station and the Empire's reach across the skies. At Thronehold he rose into the air glowing with crackling lightning, the **Dragonmark of Storm** running.

## House Lyrandar and the Imperial fleet

Bearing the Dragonmark of Storm, often called the Dragonmark of Flight, Vex embodies the legacy of House Lyrandar, his mark granting him unmatched skill in command of the skies and seas and mastery over the Empire's fleet. Though his reputation precedes him, he is not a man of flamboyant gestures or public speeches. He carries himself with restraint, speaking only when necessary, and his presence alone often silences a room. To most, he is the embodiment of discipline and command, a living reminder of the Empire's military might and the traditions of the Lyrandar line.

His young Khoravar son, Hallorn d'Lyrandar, has recently risen to prominence as the new Head of Transportation, ensuring the family's influence extends beyond the battlefield into the lifeblood of the Empire's commerce and travel. Together, they represent a dynasty that bridges military power and civic infrastructure, positioning House Lyrandar at the center of Imperial strength. <small>(Key Figures, written before both deaths: Hallorn died at Sharn Ep 17 and Vex at Korth Ep 3.)</small> The fleet portfolio is the one that sits on [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) as navy and aerial command.

## Public and private

He called the Summit at Thronehold and declared it was time to negotiate an end to the civil war. His push for peace was controversial: the Silver Flame and Zero-One opposed it, NEST saw propaganda value in it, and *the Emperor's own spokespeople denounced the gathering as illegitimate*. He held it anyway, and nearly every major faction sent representatives. He did this while the Emperor lay comatose and while being lied to about it by everyone around him.

The Summit was presented as "a neutral attempt to end the civil war by priests and scholars of the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/)", and Vex was its convener for the Empire under orders. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small> Dave's account of him: "Vex takes his orders from the Emperor, so he somehow doesn't know/doesn't believe that the emperor is fully comatose", and "He did not want to offer a truce to the rebellion or the Uruk/hundreds, but was ordered to enter negotiations, but prepared for an ambush". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-19)</small>

## Conduct in role

He silences rooms by entering them and gives orders in the fewest possible words. When the accusation about his son was made in front of the whole assembly and the masked guards began moving, he did not withdraw, negotiate or investigate — he broadcast a kill order through the ArcEye cameras carrying the Summit across Khorvaire, and then called down his own warships onto the ground he was standing on.

## Origin

Nothing of his early life is recorded beyond his birth in Aundair. He came up through House Lyrandar into the Imperial fleet and rose to Supreme Admiral, holding both the war-sea and airship armada under one command — the single largest concentration of Dragonmark-driven force in the Empire's service. He fathered at least one son, Hallorn, in Aundair: father commanding the fleets, son building them and then running the Empire's roads, rails and skyports. Then the Chancellor of a freed Sharn killed his son in a nightclub in the Upper City, and three years later a Black Dove said so out loud in a room full of delegates.

## The Summit at Thronehold

Korth Ep 1. During the final assembly in the great hall, Eric delivered a speech calling for the Empire to be outlawed. As the delegates reacted, **Sister Maelis Dorn**, attending as the Black Doves' representative, confronted Admiral Vex and publicly accused Eric of being responsible for Hallorn d'Lyrandar's death. Moments later Vex began to rise into the air, glowing with crackling lightning, and the hall erupted into chaos. Every masked Empire guard at the Summit was undead.

## Storm above Thronehold

Korth Ep 2. Vex's voice boomed through the ArcEye cameras broadcasting the Summit across Khorvaire: *"Kill Chancellor Eric for the Empire."*

The masked guards surged forward — and their movements were unnatural. They were undead soldiers loyal not to Vex but to Empress Uriel. The Black Dove sisters fled immediately as the undead turned on Vex and anyone in their path, confirming Uriel's trap and leaving Vex furious and screaming of her betrayal. He called down his warships and ordered them to fire on the Summit grounds. Explosions ripped through the surrounding buildings, killing delegates and civilians. By Dave's account, "Vex blamed the Empress for deceiving everyone, revealing he has a difference of opinion on strategy from her - she wants a giant confrontation regardless of the losses on the Imperial side, he wanted a solution that preserves the Empire and likely himself and his forces". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-19)</small> Then, wrapped in lightning and rage, he launched himself into the sky and clung to Locke Pierce's rising ship, refusing to fall away.

## The Last Mourning

Korth Ep 3. On the open deck of a fleeing airship racing south toward the Mournlands, with his conjured storm cloud tearing across the sky behind it, Vex d'Lyrandar fought Eric, Gemma, [Lorian](/people/lorian/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). His fury was fuelled by betrayal on three fronts at once: the Emperor lay comatose, Uriel had deceived him, and the man responsible for his son's death was standing on the deck in front of him. Lorian was nearly killed twice. The group held the line long enough for Gemma to drive **The Bright Dawn** up Vex's spine.

The admiral fell dead and his storm vanished, just as the ship crossed into the northern tip of the Mournlands. Hours later, Locke Pierce detonated the Ruby Nova; the blast pushed past the Mournland border and obliterated the remnants of Vex's airships still hovering near it.

## Right now

He is dead on an airship deck somewhere over the water north of Cyre, killed by a rebel in a stolen face, and his fleets are scattered or slagged. The ArcEye footage that carried his kill order across Khorvaire is the same footage that shows the Empire's own honour guard turning on him.
