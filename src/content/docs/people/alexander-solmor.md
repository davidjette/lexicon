**Duke of Dreadwood · Councillor of Saltmarsh · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Dead**

*Also known as:* Salmor · Salmoor · the Duke

**Alexander Solmor**, the Duke of Dreadwood, was a councillor of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), the father of [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/), and a high father of [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/), in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh. Presumed dead for ten years after his ship sank, he came home in April 2022 with a new wife, [Malenti](/people/malenti/), and took back his seat on the council. The party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) killed him on 2022-06-30. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small>

## The return

When the party came home from Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon, the town cared less for its heroes than for the return of the Duke. He announced it publicly. By his account his ship had been sunk by a summer squall while he and his wife were on a pleasure cruise; his wife died, and he swam to an island and lived for ten years on shrimp and berries until a warrior from the sea, an elven beauty, rescued him. He married her. He asked for a day of rest with his family, then meant to resume his work on the council, promising "a bold new future for Saltmarsh. May Neptune bless us and keep us." <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

His seat had stood open: in January 2022 Dave wrote that "Salmor is gone and they want to forfeit his stuff". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small> On his return [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) wanted assurances that the illicit trade could continue, Sergeant Eliander Fireborn was unconvinced that he was the real Solmor, and Manistrad Copperlocks warned that he had ties to the Brotherhood and was all but untouchable. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> Dave, as a player: "I am thinking this Duke is here specifically to fuck with us". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

## The sinking

Copperlocks told the party that no summer squall had sunk his ship. An agent of [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/), she had come to Saltmarsh on the trail of the Brotherhood's high father, found him in the Duke, learned that he was after a source of great arcane power beneath the ground, and planted a bomb on his ship. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## The Brotherhood's design

What he was after was [the Eldabrin](/lore/the-eldabrin/). His marriage to Malenti, a sahuagin princess in the guise of a sea elf, sealed the union of the sahuagin and the Brotherhood, and the sahuagin invasion of Saltmarsh was a ruse to cover the Brotherhood's way into the mines. Some of the dwarves passed him information. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

He had found [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) as a young changeling on the streets of Saltmarsh, and the two corresponded. After Skerrin's death his new butler was Peter Stormcrow, an assassin with magical skills. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

## Death

With a sahuagin invasion coming, the party slipped back into Saltmarsh to kill him. Dave: "I’m a commodore I decided I can summarily execute him for reason so we’re good", correcting "reason" to "Treason". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small> JL ran the session that night, and the party killed him. Nico: "We kicked Salmors’s ass, good fight!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>
