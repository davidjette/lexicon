**Human · Fighter, Fathomless warlock · Captain of the Sea Ghost, later of the Blackfish · The Dunwater Horror, dying**

**Also known as:** Gabby; Ladybeard; Captain G. Hellwood; the Commodore; the Warlock Pirate of the Kraken; the Dunwater Horror; the Horror of Dunwater; Sir Martina Navitilovachan

**Gabriella Hellwood**, called **Gabby** and later **Ladybeard**, is a human fighter and warlock, a player character of [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) played by Dave, who became, centuries later, the elderly captain of the pirate ship **Blackfish**, known as the Warlock Pirate of the Kraken, and the Dunwater Horror. A Marine imprisoned for desertion, she washed ashore at [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) chained to [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/), became captain of the [*Sea Ghost*](/items/the-sea-ghost/) for [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) and commodore of the town's navy, married the daughter of the smuggler Primewater, and went on to [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/). She swore a warlock's pact with [Hafgufa](/lore/hafgufa/), which made her an eldritch horror and kept her alive for a few hundred years.

Long before the events of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) she held the **FATES** card and gave it to a crewman as plunder. When [the Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) was desecrated and its relic [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/) stolen, the town's authorities suspected her and offered a bounty for her death; she came to kill whoever had attacked the temple of [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) and found it was a trap for her. At [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) her place is below the waves, battling what comes through the portal [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) opened on the sea floor. At the end of her story her last words were written down by [Maverick](/people/maverick/)'s quill and the party collected the bounty on her; she remains near Saltmarsh as a dying demikraken.

## Physical description

An elderly human warlock with grey hair and a beard in braids, and inky black eyes. <small>(DM notes)</small>

In her Twatmarsh days she was Latina, with a black, wispy beard. Asked its colour, Dave answered: "Black I think, whispy like Johnny depp probably" / "She’s Latina". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-02)</small> JL captioned a picture of her "Gabrielle with her mustache" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12)</small>; when Nico asked in August 2022 whether it was time for her to grow out her beard, Dave posted a bearded portrait <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>, and his art prompt for her with [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/) gave her "a long beard". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small>

## Ghosts of Twatmarsh

Hellwood was Dave's player character in Ghosts of Twatmarsh, the table's campaign on *Ghosts of Saltmarsh*, and she stayed with the party as a character Dave ran in the sessions he DMed. He introduced her on 2021-08-14: "ok all around fighter build Gabriella Hellwood comin at ya". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14)</small> Dave also posted her as "Gabrielle Hellwood a.k.a. Sir Martina Navitilovachan". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-03)</small>

### The Hell's Belles

Dave's account of her past: "Gabriella Hellwood was imprisoned for desertion" / "She commanded a Marine unit fighting the Sea Princes and was sent into a suicide mission and certain death by her superior (Major Francisco Dickhead)". Her all-women Marine unit, "her best friends and compatrtiots (who called themselves Hell's Belles)", was obliterated; she was the sole survivor, vowed to murder the Major, and was picked up for desertion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> Dave on how that shaped her: "She lost what she cared about so now everyone is expendable" / "Except you guys who she will neurotically protect to avoid losing you too". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-28)</small>

### Saltmarsh

JL, who ran the prologue, set it up: "Gabs and Scrunt wash up on shore chained together." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> Nico's summary has Dolores meeting them as they fled a sinking prison ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16)</small> A few nights later Hellwood got drunk and climbed the rooftops, and the guards knocked her out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16; 2022-08-14)</small> Her drinking was a negative trait Dave played, which he admitted "was disruptive" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small>; Nico later credited a belt of dwarvenkind: "That  belt of dwarvenkind alone helped with the drinking problem !" <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

She was a Battle Master fighter with 19 Strength who took the Archery fighting style <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15; 2021-08-27)</small>, wore Mariner's armor <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-12)</small>, and later wore scale mail, with several suits of armour carried on the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small> She took Sanbalet's coat <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13)</small>, and named her weapons: "Got myself a 1d8 saber (snake eyes) and a 1d12 long sword (Yvette)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> Dave: "I think Gabbys gunna get obsessed with this alchemy puzzle and book and make herself an expert" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-12)</small>; she made explosives, hand mortars at 10 gold each and "Chemical detonators, ranged weapons which explode on a hit only". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

When a boat was about to blow, she jumped off it to save Dolores; Dave: "I rolled the actual fuse length after gabby jumped off the boat to save you". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The party won the *Sea Ghost* that day, and Dave set its officers: "Mr Scrunt is first mate, captain Hellwood and ships chaplain sister Dolores". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The party ledger lists "Captain Hellwoood" at the head of a crew of ten sailors and named officers. <small>(sic; Neptune's Rejects ledger)</small>

She was the only one of the party who spoke Dwarvish, and so drove the talks between the dwarf clans. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small> After the party captured the *[Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/)*, the town council approved a commission for its naval protection <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>, and Hellwood held the rank of commodore: "I’m a commodore I decided I can summarily execute him for reason so we’re good". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small> By 2022 she had a private fleet and a house in Saltmarsh. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13; 2022-06-18)</small>

### The Commodore and the Smugglers Daughter

In a session JL ran in January 2022, Hellwood got engaged to the daughter of the smuggler Primewater. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small> Dave titled it "The Commodore and the Smugglers Daughter", and gave the terms: "the Navy will look the other way on smuggling and he can use the Weathervein summer home again". He also said he would marry her to "make some bank, shore up an actually useful ally, and be the wall between Dolores's rage and the rest of the power brokers". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small> Nico ran the wedding, with gaming tables, and gave Gabby "an extra 150 for your wedding". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-22)</small> In 2025 the table settled her wife's name as **[Garbanzo](/people/garbanzo/)**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small>

### White Plume Mountain

In White Plume Mountain Dave ran the dungeon, and Hellwood went along as a character the other players directed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small> A vampire there charmed her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> She carried the sword Blackrazor, and Dave weighed a ninth fighter level so she could use Indomitable "whenever blackrazor tries to force me into killing someone". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small> Dave on the last sessions: "Gabriella was absolutely ready to hear the Eldabrin’s message and spent the last two sessions of White Plume resisting it with saves and stuff as basically an NPC", and "I decided during that game that the death of Scrunt was very impactful for Gabriella and she resolved to overcoming the negative influence of blackrazor and getting home". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24)</small>

Dave expected her to retire: "If we get out of the Underdark intact, I think Gabriella will retire", with Dread Pirate Anders to follow: "He can take over for Gabriella if she retires". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## The pact with Hafgufa

Dave weighed the dip in March 2022: "The Fathomless as a patron, basically davy jones, you get a bonus action tentacle and water breathing and swim speed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> By the Saltmarsh finale she was a Fighter 8 / Fathomless warlock 2, and Dave "would put her and the Sea Ghost up as a boss fight in any campaign". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

Her patron is **Hafgufa** (Dave's spellings "Hoff Goofa" and "offgoofa"):

> Hoff Goofa to me is the unyielding maw of the sea itself, a primeval incarnation of the sailors nightmare and the end of all drowned men, Gabriella made a deal so that she would send it deserving souls

<small>(Dave; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

Dave named Blackrazor a rival: "Blackrazor is a competitor to that, at least with Crestus inside it." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small> For her to bond it through Pact of the Blade, Blackrazor would have to "also serve Hoff goofa". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> JL captioned a picture "Neptune and Hafgufa fighting for Gabby’s soul". <small>(JL; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-18)</small> Hafgufa is the kraken of her later title, the Warlock Pirate of the Kraken, and the same power whose depths Keisha Milosh calls "the deepest ocean in the Abyss".

The pact kept her alive. Dave: "Hellwood only lived this long because she became an eldritch horror from her pact with offgoofa". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small> When Dead Man's Hand reached Saltmarsh, Dave on whether the party could beat her: "Yes unless you got her it to a one on one I don’t think you could take her, she is a highly optimized character even before becoming. A big boss" / "But it’s been a few hundred years….". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small>

## Captain of the Blackfish

Hellwood is very private, and works through her first mate, a young genasi named **Quart**. <small>(DM notes)</small> To her crew and her foes she appears to be a recluse; in fact she lives beneath the waves, "seeking a quiet end to a violent life." <small>(DM notes)</small> At the time of the Blackfish Encounter she was locked in her quarters, and no one was to see her. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The Blackfish's crew:

- **Quart**, first mate
- **[Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/)**, deckhand
- **Parko**, gunner

Wanted posters of Keisha and the crew hang around Saltmarsh's temple and town square. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The card and the trap

Hellwood once held the **FATES** card. She gave it long before, as plunder, to a crewman; Jeremiah knew she had had the card, but did not know that she had given it to [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/) and sent him home. He discovered that Feng had it, and robbed him. <small>(DM notes)</small> Dave: "at some point she plundered the Fates card (the most important one) and gave it to a crewman (milosh) because it was more trouble than it was worth". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>

When news came that Dolores's temple had been attacked, Hellwood came to kill whoever had done it, and found it was a trap for her. <small>(DM notes)</small> Saltmarsh's authorities suspected her of the desecration and want her dead. Investigation in the temple found no gunpowder and no evidence that it was pirates. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Aboard the Blackfish, the one who directs the party to Keisha says of Feng: "He was crew here." "Feng won it from Gooch in a game of pink Monty." <small>(DM notes)</small>

Dave's account of the plot, after the fight:

> Goodface wanted the card, killed Milosz looking for it, figured Hellwood still had it, desecrated the tomb to draw her out, Hellwood used the ship as bait (she didn’t have it) so she could try and save the temple and close the abyss

<small>(Dave; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small>

Jeremiah, failing to find the card on Feng, judged that Hellwood still had it, and desecrated Dolores's tomb and the temple so that Hellwood would take the blame and come out. Hellwood, who no longer had the card, left the Blackfish as bait with herself locked away from view, and went below to fight at the portal, to save the temple, close the abyss, clear the desecration from Dolores's tomb and trap Jeremiah. Dave: "Hellwood’s fate as the Dunwater Horror, tricked by Jeremiah into taking the blame for the Temple of Neptune, and then trapping him so she could removed the desecration of Dolores’s underwater tomb". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>

## Below the waves

> Ladybeard is below the waves battling something coming through the portal opened by Jeremiah with Aqueus's desecrated corpse.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

On the sea floor beneath the Blackfish, the portal opens by the tomb of Dolores Weathervein, where the corpse of the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/) is pinned by Weathervein, and through it the demon lord **Dagon** tries to emerge while the Dunwater Horror clashes with him. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Ladybeard below the waves and the Dunwater Horror are one: Hellwood in the form her pact gave her, the huge squid-like creature described at [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small> Dave also calls her "the Horror of Dunwater" <small>(Dave)</small>, a name taken from [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/), the lizardfolk stronghold of Twatmarsh, whose Queen Okathent the party had set out to win over in 2021. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

## The end of Hellwood

At the resolution of her story in Dead Man's Hand, Maverick's quill, which writes people's last words, let her tell her story. Dave: "I’d totally forgotten that Maverick has that quill and writes people’s last words, so when that started and I realized she’d get to tell her story i was really happy". The party collected the reward on her: "Also didn’t think about how you could collect the reward for her so you guys got a lot of gold!" Dave added: "She’s lucky she didn’t get consumed by blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-21)</small>

She persists. Dave: "Now I guess there’s a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor". When Nico asked "Is she guarding her bff’s underwater temples?", Dave answered: "Yeah that’s what the whole thing was about!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small>
