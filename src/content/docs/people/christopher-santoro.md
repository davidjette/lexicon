*Also known as:* none recorded.

**Christopher Santoro** is the Pit Boss and floor manager of the [Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/) in [Newham](/places/newham/). His 23-year-old daughter [Ova Santoro](/people/ova-santoro/) was captured and held hostage by succubi in her own apartment and rescued by [Esther](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Locke and [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), and Santoro rewarded LeBeefe, then Mayor of Newham, with 300 gold. Ova, who was LeBeefe's girlfriend, was later murdered by Uriel Qualanthri — one of the four who had rescued her — and raised as undead. Nothing of Santoro's face, age, build, dress, birth, or family beyond Ova is recorded.

## The house

The Clamshell Casino sits in Newham, the town the Unforeseen turned from a slum into a city — an achievement the Emperor's consort listed to their faces in the throne room at Korth. John C. LeBeefe was its Mayor and would become Grand Duke. Santoro is the man on the floor who settles disputes, watches the tables and keeps the room orderly; he answers to the house, and the house answers to whoever the city belongs to that year.

## The reward

Ova Santoro was captured and held hostage by succubi in her own apartment. Esther, Uriel, Locke and LeBeefe went in and got her out. **He once rewarded Mayor John C. LeBeefe 300 gold for saving his daughter from Succubi.**

## What happened afterwards

**He has a 23 year old daughter named Ova Santoro who was murdered by Uriel Qualanthri.** Ova was LeBeefe's girlfriend. Uriel — one of the four who had rescued her, and who was standing beside her father when he counted out the reward — killed her and raised her as undead.

Whether Christopher Santoro was ever told the truth, whether he knows who did it, and what became of him afterwards are not recorded.
