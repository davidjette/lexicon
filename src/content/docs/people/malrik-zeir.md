**Valenar high elf · Vampire · "Father" of the Children of Ember · Body destroyed; mist escaped**

<small>Also known as: Malrik Zeir · Father</small>

**Malrik Zeir** is a Valenar high elf and a vampire, the husband of [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/) and the "Father" of [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/). He lived in the private residence beneath the Vault Underground, the Upper City club his wife owned and operated, kept captives drugged in a cell off his kitchen called the Pantry as blood cattle for himself and the wealthy men he entertained, and grew and supplied the **Umbra** that fed **Project E.D.E.N.**, which his half-brother [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) moved across Khorvaire. [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) killed him on the rooftop pool deck of the residence; his body was destroyed and his vampire mist escaped into his coffin, which has never been produced.

## Physical description

A Valenar high elf, soft-spoken and unmistakably imposing. No further description of him is recorded.

## The household

The Zeir household is a business, a residence and a cult headquarters stacked vertically. **The Vault Underground** is a twenty-four-hour Upper City club and gambling house owned and operated by his wife Faith Zeir; the private residence is reached from the club's lowest level through her office. Inside it are the living quarters, a ritual room beneath the main floor, a kitchen, and off the kitchen a cell the household calls the Pantry. He lives at the top of that stack and feeds at the bottom of it.

Publicly he was Faith's loyal husband, a Valenar gentleman with family connections. Privately he is a vampire; captives were kept drugged and isolated in the Pantry as blood cattle and used by him and by the wealthy men he entertained; he grew and supplied the Umbra which fed Project E.D.E.N.; and the masked congregation that called his wife Mother called him Father. He was one of the last major pipelines still feeding the project in Sharn.

His half-brother Hallorn d'Lyrandar — Head of Transportation, son of Supreme Admiral Vex, reputed richest man in Khorvaire — was one of the primary recruiters and moved the Umbra across the continent for him. Four further wealthy recruiters from across Khorvaire brought victims in and traded captives.

**The family line:** Malrik's family ties extend into imperial infrastructure through his younger half-brother, Hallorn d'Lyrandar of Aundair, the newly appointed Head of Transportation and son of Supreme Admiral Vex Lyrandar, giving the Zeirs a quiet but potent reach into both Sharn's underworld and its political machinery. His wife was [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s half-sister. He was, by marriage, the Lord Commander's brother-in-law.

Beliefs: Unknown. No words of his are recorded.

The work divided four ways: recruiters brought the victims; Faith performed the reshaping that turned survivors into masked cultists; Hallorn moved the product; and Malrik's own part was the feeding and the growing of Umbra. When the fighting reached his roof he took a chained woman and a child and ran.

Malrik Zeir's history before Sharn is not recorded beyond his origin — Valenar, high elf, half-brother to an Aundairian scion of House Lyrandar — and the fact that his vampirism was not publicly known until the night he died. He married into [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)'s scattered family, acquired a nightclub, a residence beneath it and a wife who could manufacture worshippers, and lived as a fixture of Upper City society. Eliminating him, the Nest's briefing noted, would sever the Umbra supply, close the cult and end the trafficking at once.

**Right now:** Dario Argentino killed him outright on the rooftop pool deck in a devastating series of blows. His body was destroyed. His vampire mist escaped into his coffin. Nobody has produced the coffin.

**Organizations / Groups:** "Father" of [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/); the Zeir household; Umbra supplier to Project E.D.E.N.

**Holdings:** the Zeir private residence beneath the Vault Underground, with its ritual room, kitchen, Pantry and rooftop pool deck · the Vault Below in the Lower City, where he fed, operated by his wife · a coffin, location unknown.

**Items:** correspondence with Hallorn concerning Umbra and Project E.D.E.N. · his coffin.

**Umbra:** Malrik's crop. He grew and supplied it; Hallorn transported it across Khorvaire; the Black Doves used it in necromantic rites on corpses; BioTec built the hybrid units. It is also what was used on his captives — [Lorian](/people/lorian/) was lured to Sharn, drugged with Umbra and kept as blood cattle for months. The Zeirs' operation was, in the Nest's words, one of the last major pipelines feeding Project E.D.E.N. in Sharn.

## The Pantry

The cell off the kitchen. Captives kept there were drugged, isolated and used by Malrik and the recruiters. Two emaciated prisoners were found alive beneath the Vault Below alongside [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) and freed. Every masked cultist in the Children of Ember had been through it first.

## The Roof

With the residence collapsing into a running fight, Malrik fled upward with [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) in chains and [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) under his arm, his wife and a recruiter chasing after him. Dario caught him on the pool deck and killed him. The mist went into the coffin; the body did not survive. Moments later Esther Crona came down out of the sky and killed Faith.

> "Malrik grows and supplies Umbra. Hallorn transports it across Khorvaire."
