**Elf · Priestess of Avalon · Temple Holdings LLC · Player character (Kathy)**

*Also known as:* Mrs mageblood <small>(Dave, welcoming her to the party)</small>

**Arana** is an elder elf priestess of [Avalon](/organizations/nimue-and-the-elves-of-avalon/) and a member of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), played by Kathy. She joined the party in October 2020, during [Arc VI, the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), in the dungeon inside the castle at Mirar.

## Appearance

Arana is petite and not very tall, very fair-skinned, with green eyes and long, straight white-silver hair. She is ethereal, with a shimmer or glow about her. Around her neck she wears a moonstone amulet on a leather tie, a crescent of moonstone at her throat. She carries a longbow, with a quiver on her back, and a short sword at her hip. In the party portrait she wears a long dark hooded coat over a white blouse and pale leggings, with a laced leather bodice, leather belts, leather bracers and tall leather boots. She also wears a simple shift of forest green with a leather belt, leather bracers and leather boots, and a green hooded cloak trimmed in gold thread. <small>(Temple Holdings party portrait and its commission brief)</small>

## Temple Holdings

After Camelot the party chose a zombie-killing mission to Mirar. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-04)</small> Arana joined them in the session of 24 October 2020, in the dungeon inside the castle, where every room had a numbered twin and leaving a room brought the party out of its twin in the same direction. In that dungeon [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) pressed her to drink a potion. JL, who played [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), recalled "Revan trying to convince Arana drink the potion he thought might be poison" <small>(sic)</small>. LaMont, who played Revan, answered: "I was trying to heal her which is what it was!!!!!" The potion was "clear refreshing water from an alter" <small>(sic)</small>, in LaMont's words, and JL agreed: "In the end it wasn’t poison." Dave welcomed her to the party as "Mrs mageblood". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-24)</small>

LaMont counted her among the party's healers: "She’s the other HEALER!!" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-12-02)</small>

Her relation to [Mara Mageblood](/people/mara-mageblood/) and Mara's daughter Fhastine is unknown.
