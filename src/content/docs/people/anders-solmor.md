**Saltmarsh, Oerth · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Ally of [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) · Alive**

*Also known as:* pirate Anders · Dread Pirate Anders

Anders Solmor is a man of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) and a member of the Solmor family, in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh. He courted [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) and proposed that she be given a vote on the town council as a special religious delegate. He idolized his late mother, Petra, blamed the Sea Princes for her death, and hoped Neptune would help Saltmarsh become an independent nation. He survived the battle of the Saltmarsh finale, gaining a level in it, and wears a ring Nico called "his daddy’s ring". Afterward he wanted to serve Dolores as her cabin boy, and Dave named him as the man who would take over from [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) when she retired.

## Courting Dolores

Nico counted Anders among those who backed the party's hold on the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/): "we know Skerren and Anders is down with us having power in the church, otherwise they wouldn’t have given us the deed". Dave: "The whole council did that". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

After the council meeting at which the party announced the capture of the [*Stinking Albatross*](/items/the-stinking-albatross/), three people approached them: Anders, [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) and [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/). Anders wooed Dolores again, and suggested that she be appointed a special religious delegate to the council, giving her a vote in town affairs. He compared her glowingly to his own mother, **Petra**, "the center of his whole world and his primary example". He told her that he believed his mother's death was no accident and that the Sea Princes were responsible, and that he hoped Neptune would support the people of Saltmarsh and "enact an independent nation dedicated to freedom and trade." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

Nico said of Dolores that "she’s into the idea of the council and Anders". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-18)</small>

## After the finale

The party beat the [Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) in the Saltmarsh finale of 2022-07-09 and reached level 10. Dave, the next morning, set out what would have happened had the party accepted [Malenti](/people/malenti/)'s offer to parley with Sekolah: "you launch an invasion of Saltmarsh and Anders dies in the battle". Nico: "Only way Delores will let Anders die is by her own hands lol". Dave: "Anders wants to be your little cabin boy". Nico: "Every Sturgeon General needs a loyal cabin boy. Maybe his destiny will be to become a swashbuckler and help us defeat the brotherhood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

Dave rolled him up as a character: "I rolled him up", and "he level in the battle". Nico: "And he has his daddy’s ring. He’ll make a good lookout". Nico asked "aasimar…?", and Dave answered "Infernal blood…?" Nico: "Jeff said he’s played by the guy who plays Anders on Battlestar Galactica". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small> Nico posted an image of a "Double date with Garbanzo and pirate Anders". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-14)</small> See [Garbanzo](/people/garbanzo/).

## Dread Pirate Anders

Dave recalled that the party had never raised the corpse of [Penguinus Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/): "You considered mustering Anders to raise him", then "Murdering". Nico: "Nah, Delores likes pirate Anders". Dave: "Dread Pirate Anders", and:

> He can take over for Gabriella if she retires / If we get out of the Underdark intact, I think Gabriella will retire / From adventuring

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

Nico's plan for Dolores's retirement, the same night, was the temple, the city council and the Weathervein name, "Use that gold, update her home, get some pirate Anders and get to know Neptune more". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## The Solmor family

The Solmors are a family of Saltmarsh. The name is spelled **Solmor**, **Salmor** and **Salmoor**. Its two named men are Anders, who survived the Saltmarsh finale, and **Salmor**, whom the party killed on 2022-06-30. Anders's mother **Petra** is dead, and Anders holds the Sea Princes responsible. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

**Salmor's holdings.** Salmor had the haunted house cleared by a priest because of its hauntings. Dave: "Scrunt was right that Salmor had the house cleared BT a priest because of the spooks". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small> That house, on the coast, is "PW's house, owned by Salmoor, formerly Sanbalet's smuggling hideout". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-03)</small> Dave measured the power the party would gain by keeping Aubreck Drallion's box as putting them "at Salmor level + the church + the navy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small> By April 2022, in Dave's words, "now the Salmoors own the land around here". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

**Vivica and Salmor.** When Dolores passed a Charisma save against the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/), Dave wrote: "But gambled a lot on that one CHA save because Vivica would have ended Salmor and used his body to bring back PW". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small>

**Salmor gone.** In January 2022, in JL's session, Salmor was gone and the town moved to forfeit his property. Dave: "Salmor is gone and they want to forfeit his stuff - that’s not good for us necessarily", and "We either need to restore Salmor so there’s no open seat, or get around the rest of that". Nico proposed to visit Salmor and "give him a pep talk". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small> Nico later listed "possible threats from A. Salmor?" among the dangers waiting in Saltmarsh. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

**Alexander, the Duke.** A Duke appeared in the ship combat of 2022-04-22. Dave, as a player, took him to be there to harass the party, recalled a hint that the Scarlet Brotherhood "might have been built to depose the Weathervein dynasty a long time ago", and set out what he called "my current fan theory": "Salmor is here to stop the Weathervein from rising again", and "after Alexander left or was marooned", his son "let the dwarves dig into the old ruins and released the Weathervein artifacts again". Nico asked whether Skerrin had warned Salmor when the party reached the caravan, "and that’s why Alexander returned?" Dave: "He had a letter from Alexander so they were communicating". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

**The death of Salmor.** In June 2022, with a sahuagin invasion coming, the party planned to kill Salmor. Dave thought he might have intelligence from the sahuagin. They slipped back into town, "amphibious", to Keledek's for help with surveillance. Dave's plan: "sneak in, watch him for a day, then plan the attack, super quiet, kill him and then Call in firebrand", adding "We don’t even know what he is". Nico's version was "Kill Salmor and wife". Dave: "I’m a commodore I decided I can summarily execute him for reason so we’re good", correcting "reason" to "Treason". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small> JL ran the session that night, and the party killed him. Nico: "We kicked Salmors’s ass, good fight!!" Afterward Dave wondered whether Salmor had been paying Firebrand to look the other way. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

**Father and son.** After the finale Nico noted that Anders "has his daddy’s ring". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small> Salmor was Anders's father, and Alexander, the Duke, is a Solmor. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-open-questions-2.md)</small>

**Organizations / Groups:** the Solmor family.\
