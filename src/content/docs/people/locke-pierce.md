**Warforged · Artificer / Armorer · [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), then The Hundreds · Ancient Relic Specialist, Director of the Rekkenmark Newham Annex, leader of the Warforged nation of the Mournlands · **Alive****

**Also known as:** Locke Peirce · Lock Pierce · Director Pierce · the Director

**Locke Pierce** is a Warforged artificer, a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and the leader of The Hundreds, the Warforged nation of the Mournlands. He entered the record as the Rekkenmark’s Ancient Relic Specialist, sent to Greenbluff to retrieve the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) and then to carry it to the Empire aboard the Talenta Plains Express, and took a semi-permanent post as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex. After an assassination attempt sent the four founders back into the Rekkenmark he found that Doctor Amber was DR4MB — three-hundred-year-old Cyrean tech built to eliminate and decommission warforged — downloaded it, and set the academy to self-destruct. Of the four founders he is the one who turned: he now rules from a stronghold at Mt. Silicon with an airship fleet, shelters a blended Cogborn–Warforged society at Gorgonhorn on the Mournland border, and holds the Ruby Nova, a controlled recreation of the Mourning that he detonated once on a live battlefield and has promised to use on the world if [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) rings the Hell’s Bells.

## Description

Warforged. Blue <small>(from his portrait; no text source gives a colour)</small>. Beyond that the record is thin: he can shut himself down until he passes for dead, and did so on the way into the Rekkenmark; he can enter a login state in which he is fully incapacitated while another person’s voice and encryption phrase are presented through him; and his own memory has holes in it that other people can see from outside. Somewhere in his chassis is a download taken off a three-hundred-year-old Cyrean machine that was built to decommission his kind, and he has never told anybody what was in it.

## Origin

He enters the record at Greenbluff, sent by the Rekkenmark as an Ancient Relic Specialist to retrieve an artefact from a mine where slaves had died and survivors had gone mad. The artefact was the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). He got it out, and was then tasked with returning it to the Empire by train — the Talenta Plains Express, which the rebels boarded in force. He took a semi-permanent post as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex, and put his dig crew onto a series of ancient passageways leading away from the city.

Then a warforged R-0 unit called Radd Orgasm tried to assassinate him in a restaurant in Korth, and named his employer: DR4MB, in the Rekkenmark. The four of them went in — Locke shut down to appear dead — and found the academy overrun by undead that Professor Marble had raised by accident while researching whether ancient dwarven necromancy was how the Cyreans put souls into machines. Deeper down they found that Doctor Amber was DR4MB: three-hundred-year-old Cyrean tech obtained by Karrnath two years before the end of the war, built to eliminate and decommission warforged. Locke downloaded it, set the Rekkenmark to self-destruct, and stood off against Esther and Uriel while LeBeefe ran.

## The Rekkenmark, and after it

The Rekkenmark is the Empire’s academy and its archive, and its mainframe is where the Imperial Assembly and the higher command keep what is worth keeping. Only the highest-ranking officers and the top secret projects are recorded there, but most of the intelligence the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) collects goes there for analysis. Every permissioned officer has a personal encryption key, delivered precisely and in their own voice. Locke Pierce was its Ancient Relic Specialist and later Director of the Newham Annex, with a secretary named Tessa Moongrove and a dig crew, and his brief was to bring the Empire its relics.

He now leads The Hundreds: the Warforged nation of the Mournlands, run out of a fortified stronghold cut into the mountainside at Mt. Silicon, with a capital-scale airship fleet and a control chamber full of screens. He calls its members his children. Its outpost at Gorgonhorn, on the Mournland border, is where a blended Cogborn–Warforged society is taking root, and it is the destination every Cogborn family driven out of the Cogs of Sharn is trying to reach.

## The keys

In the basement of the Rekkenmark, in a standoff with [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Locke realised he knew who had killed Esther’s master — and could not decrypt it without a key held by an Assembly member or higher. Then he realised the larger thing: with every personal encryption key he collected, he would unlock more and more of the Empire’s secrets, secrets no one else has access to. He took the file on the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) apart in downtime because it sat at a lower tier than the Imperial secrets, and what came out of it was the cult, Project Godseed, the Emperor’s complicity, and Gabrielle Duin’s personal letters. That was the low-security material.

Hayman Maw allowed him to keep working at Rekkenmark East under Esther Crona’s supervision on one condition: notify Maw the instant the records cracked, and say absolutely nothing to Esther if anything about [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) turned up.

## Public and private

Publicly, Locke Pierce acts as the rebellion’s ally. He teleports into an undercity chamber after strangers have shepherded a Cogborn community to safety, thanks them, hands out communication necklaces and leaves. He brings his Hundreds to the assault on the Veil Building. He arrives at a famine with airships full of bread.

Privately, Locke Pierce is the only person alive holding a weapon that can end the world, and he has told the rebellion to their faces that he will use it. Of the four founders of the Unforeseen he is the one who turned; he demonstrated the Ruby Nova by detonating it on a battlefield the party had just been rescued from, and within days of offering Sharn food, Zilspar Farm was destroyed and he came back offering more food for the price of military and logistical access to Sharn’s airspace. The Chancellor paid that afternoon, said out loud that he knew it was a trick, and hoped to survive Korth in time to take it back.

## Circle

Captain Edday flies for him and rebuilt the Inevitables’ sending communicators to give them a private emergency channel. Himmie Joffa fronts the food. Preacher of R.U.S.T. has organised the survival of a whole population around the promise of reaching Gorgonhorn. Tessa Moongrove kept his office in Newham. And the [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), who collects secrets, has background on Locke Pierce and seems to know him personally from a past Locke does not remember — the pirate is still at sea, married into the Underhive, and has never said what he knows.

## On the job

Locke Pierce gives before he asks. Communication necklaces to R.U.S.T. Air Bubble breathing devices to people who have to cross the Mournlands. A sealed intelligence dossier on the Fantanya Nyel, everything he and the Hundreds have. Fruit that has been extinct since the Mourning. Transport anywhere in Khorvaire. Then, once, he asks — and what he asks for is the sky over a city.

## From founder to antagonist

Four Imperial operatives agreed in Arden Crest to work together and named their group, and Locke Pierce is the one who turned. He fights alongside the rebellion, he shelters the people the Empire discards, and he is simultaneously the reason the free states now speak of "The Last Mourning" as the true end of the war. The party of Campaign 1 became the antagonists of Campaign 2.

## The Hundreds

**THE HUNDREDS — The Warforged Nation of the Mournlands**

**Status:** Alive. **Influence:** Growing. **Intent:** Unknown.

Their hope lies beyond Sharn in Gorgonhorn; a Cogborn / Warforged stronghold on the border of the Mournlands. There, under the leadership of Locke Pierce, a blended society of Cogborn and Warforged has begun to take shape: a community strong enough to heal, grow, and one day resist the Empire on its own terms.

Of the four who founded the Unforeseen, Pierce is the one who turned. He now leads The Hundreds , and works with the resistance rather than the Empire.

He first appeared to the [Inevitables](/people/gemma-corso/) in the undercity beneath Sharn, teleporting in after they had brought the trapped Cogborn through the Spine to R.U.S.T. He thanked them and left communication necklaces for the network. When the rising came he answered the call: the Hundreds joined the assault on the Veil Building alongside the Nest, R.U.S.T., the soldiers of Leef, [Blair](/people/blair/)'s Underhive and the Dread Pirate's ship.

## The Ruby Nova

Three years later he sent the Chancellor of Sharn a private message and a Cyrean Red Pomegranate, and asked for a meeting at the Thronehold Summit. When the Summit collapsed into slaughter he ran for his vessels and took the survivors with him. Aboard, over open water, he and the party fought Supreme Admiral Vex d’Lyrandar off the hull, and then he flew them south into the dead kingdom of Cyre, dropped the ship over a Silver Flame line holding a Cyrean portal, and pulled the last of them off it.

At Mt. Silicon he showed them the screens, and told them the Fantanya Nyel were all but real and Uriel intended to ring them. Then he showed them his countermeasure. He spoke about the Crimson Sun and the power it once unleashed, and he activated the device on the battlefield they had just left. A red dome bloomed silently across the horizon, engulfed the portal site, pushed past the Mournland border, and was visible from Thronehold. It obliterated what was left of Vex’s airships. To the free states, watching on the arceye, it looked like the last chance for peace dying and a new weapon being born, and they named it the Last Mourning.

> Stop Uriel before she rings the bells. If you fail, I will end this world before she can, and only my kind will survive it.\
> — Locke Pierce, Mt. Silicon

## Right Now

Locke Pierce has taken the skies of Sharn. A capital ship settles out of the fog each morning and hands out fruit in orderly lines to a city his dossier says is forty-seven days from famine, and the access that lets it do so was granted by a Chancellor who knew what he was granting. Somebody gave Zilspar Farm away to the Empire. The Inevitables have not decided whether it was him.

## Beliefs

He believes the Hell’s Bells are real, that [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) intends to ring them, and that if she does the dead will rise everywhere. He believes a controlled recreation of the Mourning is a legitimate answer to that, and has proved it in front of witnesses. He believes only his kind will survive it.

**Organization / Groups:** Ancient Relic Specialist and Director of Rekkenmark, Newham Anex. Founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). Now leader of The Hundreds, the Warforged nation of the Mournlands.

**Holdings:** Mt. Silicon, the fortified Warforged stronghold cut into the mountainside. Gorgonhorn, the Cogborn–Warforged settlement on the Mournland border. The Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex — buried with Newham. And, as of this week, the airspace over Sharn.

**Items:** The Ruby Nova, a weaponised catastrophe — a controlled recreation of the Mourning — held at Mt. Silicon and demonstrated once, on a live battlefield, from a distance. The DR4MB download, contents undisclosed. A sealed intelligence dossier on the Hell’s Bells. Air Bubble breathing devices inscribed in ancient gnomish, *Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers*. Cyrean Red Pomegranates, a fruit believed extinct since the Mourning, grown in the Mournlands and handed out freely.

**Login State:** Locke can present encrypted Imperial records to a permissioned user, but only by entering a login state in which he is fully incapacitated while the key is delivered precisely and in the owner’s own voice. Every key he collects opens more of the mainframe.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

That his kind survive whatever comes — and, failing everything else, that they be the only ones who do.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Encryption keys, one at a time
- Ancient relics, and the crews who dig them up
- Cyrean Red Pomegranates
- Giving people equipment before he asks for anything

**Dislikes:**

- Being declared Imperial property
- Processing, seclusion, and anything that ends in decommissioning
- Uriel Qualanthri
- Machines built to kill his kind

**Vices & Personality flaws**

He has decided that the correct answer to an apocalypse is a smaller apocalypse under his own hand.
