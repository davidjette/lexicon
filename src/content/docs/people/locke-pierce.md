**Warforged · Artificer / Armorer · [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), then The Hundreds · Ancient Relic Specialist, Director of the Rekkenmark Newham Annex, leader of the Warforged nation of the Mournlands, and of Eberron under the Sovereignty of Realms at last report · **Alive****

**Also known as:** Locke Peirce · Lock Pierce · Director Pierce · the Director

**Locke Pierce** is a Warforged artificer, a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and the leader of The Hundreds, the Warforged nation of the Mournlands. He entered the record as the Rekkenmark’s Ancient Relic Specialist, sent to Greenbluff to retrieve the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) and then to carry it to the Empire aboard the Talenta Plains Express, and took a semi-permanent post as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex. After an assassination attempt sent the four founders back into the Rekkenmark he found that Doctor Amber was DR4MB — three-hundred-year-old Cyrean tech built to eliminate and decommission warforged — downloaded it, and set the academy to self-destruct. Of the four founders he is the one who turned: he now rules from a stronghold at Mt. Silicon with an airship fleet, shelters a blended Cogborn–Warforged society at Gorgonhorn on the Mournland border, and holds the Ruby Nova, a controlled recreation of the Mourning that he detonated once on a live battlefield and has promised to use on the world if [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) rings the Hell’s Bells. He used a second Ruby Nova on the valley of the Ivory Lazaret, and in [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) he stood under [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/) beside [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), emissary of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), having agreed with her that Eberron would become a sovereign realm with himself as its leader.

## Description

Warforged. Blue <small>(from his portrait; no text source gives a colour)</small>. Beyond that the record is thin: he can shut himself down until he passes for dead, and did so on the way into the Rekkenmark; he can enter a login state in which he is fully incapacitated while another person’s voice and encryption phrase are presented through him; and his own memory has holes in it that other people can see from outside. Somewhere in his chassis is a download taken off a three-hundred-year-old Cyrean machine that was built to decommission his kind, and he has never told anybody what was in it.

## Origin

He enters the record at Greenbluff, sent by the Rekkenmark as an Ancient Relic Specialist to retrieve an artefact from a mine where slaves had died and survivors had gone mad. By Dave's account the Unforeseen were first told, "There's an academic from the Rekkenmark who is here to oversea the excavation of this ancient bell, meet him at the dig site" <small>(sic)</small>, "and the local mayor is a creep". He was an expert in the bells, and before he separated from the Empire and the other Unforeseen he would have been "the empire's foremost academic at least in reputation and power". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small> The artefact was the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). He got it out, and was then tasked with returning it to the Empire by train — the Talenta Plains Express, which the rebels boarded in force. He took a semi-permanent post as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex, and put his dig crew onto a series of ancient passageways leading away from the city.

He had gone back to the Rekkenmark Academy to continue his studies, and was sent back to Newham when construction on the east side of town uncovered the passageways. The Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex, consisted of Director Pierce, his secretary Tessa Moongrove, a young woman, and a dig crew of four. He lived and worked out of a newly constructed brownstone near the docks of Lake LaBeefe, became proficient in cartography, and wore a pair of brown leather gloves that let him cast the cantrip *mold earth*. <small>(Episode 6 "6 Months Later / The Tablet")</small>

Then a warforged R-0 unit called Radd Orgasm tried to assassinate him in a restaurant in Korth, and named his employer: DR4MB, in the Rekkenmark. The four of them went in — Locke shut down to appear dead — and found the academy overrun by undead that Professor Marble had raised by accident while researching whether ancient dwarven necromancy was how the Cyreans put souls into machines. Deeper down they found that Doctor Amber was DR4MB: three-hundred-year-old Cyrean tech obtained by Karrnath two years before the end of the war, built to eliminate and decommission warforged. Locke downloaded it, set the Rekkenmark to self-destruct, and stood off against Esther and Uriel while LeBeefe ran.

## The Rekkenmark, and after it

The Rekkenmark is the Empire’s academy and its archive, and its mainframe is where the Imperial Assembly and the higher command keep what is worth keeping. Only the highest-ranking officers and the top secret projects are recorded there, but most of the intelligence the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) collects goes there for analysis. Every permissioned officer has a personal encryption key, delivered precisely and in their own voice. Locke Pierce was its Ancient Relic Specialist and later Director of the Newham Annex, with a secretary named Tessa Moongrove and a dig crew, and his brief was to bring the Empire its relics.

He now leads The Hundreds: the Warforged nation of the Mournlands, run out of a fortified stronghold cut into the mountainside at Mt. Silicon, with a capital-scale airship fleet and a control chamber full of screens. He calls its members his children. Its outpost at Gorgonhorn, on the Mournland border, is where a blended Cogborn–Warforged society is taking root, and it is the destination every Cogborn family driven out of the Cogs of Sharn is trying to reach. By Dave's account his kingdom "doesn’t have breathable air". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-04)</small>

## The keys

In the basement of the Rekkenmark, in a standoff with [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Locke realised he knew who had killed Esther’s master — and could not decrypt it without a key held by an Assembly member or higher. Then he realised the larger thing: with every personal encryption key he collected, he would unlock more and more of the Empire’s secrets, secrets no one else has access to. He took the file on the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) apart in downtime because it sat at a lower tier than the Imperial secrets, and what came out of it was the cult, Project Godseed, the Emperor’s complicity, and Gabrielle Duin’s personal letters. That was the low-security material.

[Hayman Maw](/people/hayman-maw/) allowed him to keep working at Rekkenmark East under Esther Crona’s supervision on one condition: notify Maw the instant the records cracked, and say absolutely nothing to Esther if anything about [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) turned up.

<small>Locke uncovered info about his past, not shared with the group.</small>

## Unit LK-PRC

The binary order found on his would-be assassin gave Locke's designation as **LK-PRC** and declared him due for decommissioning. The Knife Sprinters, the Cyrean warforged who hunted obsolete units, had been run by the same machine that sent Radd Orgasm, [DR4MB](/items/dr4mb/). <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

LK-PRC was first built as a prototype in Cyre about 175 years before the Rekkenmark, before the Last War. After testing, Cyre's scientists decided not to put it into mass production, because of instabilities in its programming: willful, obstinate, contrarian, prone to rash decision-making, of questionable loyalty. His memory has been wiped five times. He fought for Cyre against the Karrnathi invaders in the Last War and was decorated for meritorious service, and was later captured with a train of Cyrean warforged and sold to a pirate lord in the Lhazaar Principalities. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

He has a sister unit that still functions in the Mournlands, **Lick Price**, reprogrammed as a pleasure unit. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

Radd Orgasm, the R-0 unit who tried to kill him, had studied for the same degree at the academy, washed out and disappeared. Professor Marble, turned vampire, told Locke that he existed only because someone else had given their soul, and asked who they had been; that is her claim. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## Public and private

Publicly, Locke Pierce acts as the rebellion’s ally. He teleports into an undercity chamber after strangers have shepherded a Cogborn community to safety, thanks them, hands out communication necklaces and leaves. He brings his Hundreds to the assault on the Veil Building. He arrives at a famine with airships full of bread.

Privately, Locke Pierce is the only person alive holding a weapon that can end the world, and he has told the rebellion to their faces that he will use it. Of the four founders of the Unforeseen he is the one who turned; he demonstrated the Ruby Nova by detonating it on a battlefield the party had just been rescued from, and within days of offering Sharn food, Zilspar Farm was destroyed and he came back offering more food for the price of military and logistical access to Sharn’s airspace. The Chancellor paid that afternoon, said out loud that he knew it was a trick, and hoped to survive Korth in time to take it back.

## Circle

Captain Edday flies for him and rebuilt the Inevitables’ sending communicators to give them a private emergency channel. Himmie Joffa fronts the food. Preacher of R.U.S.T. has organised the survival of a whole population around the promise of reaching Gorgonhorn. Tessa Moongrove kept his office in Newham. And the [Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), who collects secrets, has background on Locke Pierce and seems to know him personally from a past Locke does not remember — the pirate is still at sea, married into the Underhive, and has never said what he knows.

## On the job

Locke Pierce gives before he asks. Communication necklaces to R.U.S.T. Air Bubble breathing devices to people who have to cross the Mournlands. A sealed intelligence dossier on the Fantanya Nyel, everything he and the Hundreds have. Fruit that has been extinct since the Mourning. Transport anywhere in Khorvaire. Then, once, he asks — and what he asks for is the sky over a city.

## From founder to antagonist

Four Imperial operatives agreed in Arden Crest to work together and named their group, and Locke Pierce is the one who turned. He fights alongside the rebellion, he shelters the people the Empire discards, and he is simultaneously the reason the free states now speak of "The Last Mourning" as the true end of the war. The party of Campaign 1 became the antagonists of Campaign 2.

## The Hundreds

**THE HUNDREDS — The Warforged Nation of the Mournlands**

**Status:** Alive. **Influence:** Growing. **Intent:** Unknown.

Their hope lies beyond Sharn in Gorgonhorn; a Cogborn / Warforged stronghold on the border of the Mournlands. There, under the leadership of Locke Pierce, a blended society of Cogborn and Warforged has begun to take shape: a community strong enough to heal, grow, and one day resist the Empire on its own terms.

Of the four who founded the Unforeseen, Pierce is the one who turned. He now leads The Hundreds , and works with the resistance rather than the Empire.

He first appeared to the [Inevitables](/people/gemma-corso/) in the undercity beneath Sharn, teleporting in after they had brought the trapped Cogborn through the Spine to R.U.S.T. He thanked them and left communication necklaces for the network. When the rising came he answered the call: the Hundreds joined the assault on the Veil Building alongside the Nest, R.U.S.T., the soldiers of Leef, [Blair](/people/blair/)'s Underhive and the Dread Pirate's ship.

## The Council of Sharn

In Nico's notes, on the morning after the rising Locke Pierce's Hundreds and R.U.S.T. were taking Iron Veil soldiers into custody. At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn the Hundreds and R.U.S.T. sat as a unified bloc under Locke, with [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/), [Doppler](/people/doppler-klink/) and [Preacher](/people/preacher/). The bloc backed Preacher's demand for Warforged citizenship and legal personhood. Locke wanted tribunals for every Iron Veil soldier and no amnesty for any of them, the immediate arrest of the Iron Veil engineers holding the Cogs hostage, a Department of Infrastructure run by R.U.S.T. engineers and Warforged specialists, the Hundreds as Sharn's standing army, the whole ArcEye surveillance system dismantled, a permanent council seat for the Warforged diaspora, and a memorial for the Cogborn victims, especially those lost in the POSÉ riot. Blair would support his militia everywhere except the tunnels: "Locke controls the city; Blair controls the tunnels." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 19)</small>

## The Ruby Nova

Three years later he sent the Chancellor of Sharn a private message and a Cyrean Red Pomegranate, and asked for a meeting at the Thronehold Summit. By Dave's notes after the first Summit session, Locke Pierce met the party there and said he wanted to bring them to the Mournlands to see some fruit. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 19)</small> When the Summit collapsed into slaughter he ran for his vessels and took the survivors with him. Aboard, over open water, he and the party fought Supreme Admiral Vex d’Lyrandar off the hull, and then he flew them south into the dead kingdom of Cyre, dropped the ship over a Silver Flame line holding a Cyrean portal, and pulled the last of them off it.

In the fight on the hull, by Dave's account, the party "took down a Storm Giant with Locke Pierce": Vex fought with a storm giant's statistics. By Nico's account Vex "took down Lorian twice and almost took out Locke". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

At Mt. Silicon he showed them the screens, and told them the Fantanya Nyel were all but real and Uriel intended to ring them. Then he showed them his countermeasure. He spoke about the Crimson Sun and the power it once unleashed, and he activated the device on the battlefield they had just left. A red dome bloomed silently across the horizon, engulfed the portal site, pushed past the Mournland border, and was visible from Thronehold. It obliterated what was left of Vex’s airships. To the free states, watching on the arceye, it looked like the last chance for peace dying and a new weapon being born, and they named it the Last Mourning.

Locke and his team called it **Ruby Nova**, a callsign for the bomb. By Dave's account the purpose was to demonstrate the weapon, "so it was close enough to see it up close but not enough to kill the witnesses, not immediately at least". Dave's account is also that Locke used [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) to weaponize the Mourning, which Locke would not tell the Inevitables. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

> Stop Uriel before she rings the bells. If you fail, I will end this world before she can, and only my kind will survive it.\
> — Locke Pierce, Mt. Silicon

Pierce has not moved on Karrnath, by Dave's account, because "as soon as Uriel knows the game is over, she may ring the bells". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-11)</small>

## The father of the Hundreds

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/), minutes after the Ruby Nova fell on the Lazaret, two huge old spelljammers crewed by the Hundreds broke the clouds behind the *Revenge* and fired a warning shot across her bow. Gemma sent Pierce a message through his necklace in the binary code Eric had once broken, and the ships dropped back. His answer came on the stone:

> Foxtale, congratulations. You stopped something awful. You probably don't know. They're playing the song in every city. And they're bringing down the bells. I want to talk to you. Give word. I'll send for you.

The stone brought Gemma, Eric and Dario into the Hundreds' base under [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/), where Pierce stood waiting without guards. On the board behind him were red circles over the Lazaret valley and over Korth.

> I gotta congratulate you. Very good work. You slew the bitch, and as a result, likely staved off that thing's development long enough for us to get a good shot. And because the bells started going off, everyone understands why we had to do it. That's good for us. I was worried about that. I'm also glad that you were smart enough to destroy the bells in Sharn.

He said that Sharn full of zombies under Uriel's control would have been a necessary target, and that he hoped not to use the Ruby Nova again because the weapons are very expensive to make and very harmful to the environment. Eric said the moment had to be seized before someone installed themselves as emperor: "The empire is dead. We must keep it that way." Pierce said: "I think that in this room, we're going to decide the future of Eberron."

**The Sovereignty.** With him was [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), emissary of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), whose people worked inside the base alongside the Hundreds. Her diviners had seen the Mourning from across the stars, and when their ships reached Eberron they met the Hundreds. She called Pierce "the father of the Hundreds" and said the two had already agreed that Eberron would become a sovereign realm, abide by the laws of the Sovereignty and live under its protection. She told Eric: "Owning atomics, Locke Pierce has rightful claim over this planet." She said he had pledged to submit to the Sovereignty's laws and to grant Sharn and its region autonomy. Pierce said the Empire had very likely been aware of extraterrestrials and had been negotiating separately with the Sovereignty's enemies, and that the destruction of Korth had taken care of that, though its libraries were valuable and the Hundreds had worked hard to reach them. He said the fruit the Hundreds fed to Sharn was a species Andromeda had brought, from a world pelted by meteors and whole planetoids of red Khyber, where the plants survive such explosions.

**The Lexicon.** Pierce's own condition was that Eric and his friends from the Counting House work with him for a time on [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), the oracle that advises the Sovereignty:

> There is much to learn about this Lexicon and I need the greatest math minds in Eberron if we are not to be ruled by this Lexicon forever.

Eric answered: "It must be understood. It's a thing of divine creation." Pierce's offer was that Eric submit Sharn to governance by the Sovereignty in interplanetary affairs, turn over everything he had on the bell to Pierce and Andromeda, and join him in a secret project to implement the Lexicon on the Hundreds' own equipment. Gemma could go free wherever she liked so long as she caused no problems. To Dario he said: "Dario, uh, listen. I don't have much for you, except you look really cool."

**Zilspar.** Gemma asked him outright whether he knew anything about the attack on Zilspar Farm. He answered: "The fog of war is real, but it's also a convenience. It's a convenience for the survivors who must live in the world that remains. What happened at Zilspar must be seen through the fog of war." Gemma judged that he was lying about knowing nothing. Pressed by Eric, he answered at length:

> There was little reason to think that you were able to do any of the things that you did. You came out of the desert and LeBeefe was stupid enough to play with you, to toy with you, and he paid for it. But he was a genius and he knew how to get the things that were really of value. He was friends with some truly bad people and things. And they gave him a lot of secrets. So as much as you take credit for those things, just remember who you killed and stole it from. And remember that a very long time ago, four people divided this world. And we have jurisdiction. I had no idea that Uriel would do what she did.

He called Fulcrum a great asset for as long as it lived, and went on:

> But when I saw you on that broadcast from Thronehold, I realized that you did not respect that decision made so long ago as to who would choose the destiny of this planet. And forgive me if I assumed that you would fail in your quest, but nonetheless I could not allow for there to be such a destabilizing force in the world that continued. So I'm sorry for what happened to your friends. It's not what I would have chosen. Tipping off the Empire to the location of a handful of Fulcrum agents to close out a long chapter of mutual spying and general lawlessness is a natural end of such things. And if you know what's good for you, you'll take your secrets and get far away from here. It's the last I'll ever speak about it, Chancellor.

> We all did things we're ashamed of in war, and we all get blamed for things that aren't our fault. But hopefully you understand your position, and you can go down like fools, or you can join the new century.

**The Situation Room.** After an hour he showed Eric a screen labelled "Astralex", a map of the space between Eberron and one of its moons. The Sovereignty had given the Hundreds a small fleet to protect Eberron's realm space, everything from the clouds to the moon. His people had learned to fly the ships, somewhat. The thing that really operated them was the Lexicon, and the fleet answered to the Sovereignty. See [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/).

> We will choose representatives to sit on this Sovereign Council. You will be my nominee to represent Eberron.

Eric refused: "I don't think I wish to be a part of your new murderous empire. I think I wish to leave this place. And the next time I see you, Locke Pierce, I will kill you for Zilspar." Pierce answered, "Understood," and let them go:

> You're free to leave. Chancellor, what you've done for Eberron will live in legend forever.

Eric replied: "And I won't be forgetting what you've done either." Afterwards [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/), as the new Chancellor of Sharn, announced Eric's death on the broadcast and welcomed Pierce and Andromeda Starweaver. Pierce nodded and stepped back for Andromeda to speak.

## Fate

By the end of the campaign Locke Pierce had taken the skies of Sharn. A capital ship settled out of the fog each morning and handed out fruit in orderly lines to a city his dossier said was forty-seven days from famine, and the access that let it do so was granted by a Chancellor who knew what he was granting. Somebody gave Zilspar Farm away to the Empire, and in [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) he said that he had tipped off the Empire to the location of a handful of Fulcrum agents. In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) a ship of his Hundreds dropped a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) on the valley of the Ivory Lazaret, and Korth was destroyed by a Ruby Nova as well. At last report he led Eberron as its claimant under [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), with a Sovereignty fleet in orbit operated by the Lexicon and his Hundreds holding the airspace and nearly all military power in Sharn. Eric the Cleric, publicly declared dead, had told him to his face that he would kill him. Eberron later joined the Sovereignty. About a hundred years later, after the betrayal of the Lexicon, the Sovereignty banned artificial intelligence on all its planets, all droids and warforged included. Whether Pierce was alive by then is not stated. See [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/).

## Beliefs

He believes the Hell’s Bells are real, that [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) intends to ring them, and that if she does the dead will rise everywhere. He believes a controlled recreation of the Mourning is a legitimate answer to that, and has proved it in front of witnesses. He believes only his kind will survive it.

**Organization / Groups:** Ancient Relic Specialist and Director of Rekkenmark, Newham Anex. Founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). Now leader of The Hundreds, the Warforged nation of the Mournlands.

**Holdings:** Mt. Silicon, the fortified Warforged stronghold cut into the mountainside. Gorgonhorn, the Cogborn–Warforged settlement on the Mournland border. The Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Annex — buried with Newham. And the airspace over Sharn. A small fleet for Eberron's realm space, given by the Sovereignty of Realms.

**Items:** The Ruby Nova, a weaponised catastrophe — a controlled recreation of the Mourning — held at Mt. Silicon, demonstrated on a live battlefield from a distance, and used since on the valley of the Ivory Lazaret and on Korth. The DR4MB download, contents undisclosed. A sealed intelligence dossier on the Hell’s Bells. Air Bubble breathing devices inscribed in ancient gnomish, *Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers*. Cyrean Red Pomegranates, a fruit believed extinct since the Mourning, grown in the Mournlands and handed out freely. Cartographer's tools, and brown leather gloves that let the wearer cast *mold earth*. <small>(Episode 6 "6 Months Later / The Tablet")</small> The **Pistol of Lightning**, an ornate platinum pistol with seven charges that casts *lightning bolt*, and the **Gloves of Thievery**, invisible while worn, which sharpen sleight of hand and lock-picking. <small>(Episode 9 "The Dig Site / Throne Room")</small>

**Login State:** Locke can present encrypted Imperial records to a permissioned user, but only by entering a login state in which he is fully incapacitated while the key is delivered precisely and in the owner’s own voice. Every key he collects opens more of the mainframe.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

That his kind survive whatever comes — and, failing everything else, that they be the only ones who do.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Encryption keys, one at a time
- Ancient relics, and the crews who dig them up
- Cyrean Red Pomegranates
- Giving people equipment before he asks for anything

**Dislikes:**

- Being declared Imperial property
- Processing, seclusion, and anything that ends in decommissioning
- Uriel Qualanthri
- Machines built to kill his kind

**Vices & Personality flaws**

He has decided that the correct answer to an apocalypse is a smaller apocalypse under his own hand.
