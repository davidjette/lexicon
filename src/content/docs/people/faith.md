**The Mother Prophet · The Vault · Children of Ember · Deceased**

<small>Also known as: Faith Zeir · the Mother Prophet</small>

**Faith** Zeir was the owner and operator of the Vault, Sharn's nightclub and gambling house, with locations in the Upper and Lower City, and the Mother Prophet of [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/). A daughter of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and half-sister to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Blair](/people/blair/) and Eden, she married [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/), a soft-spoken yet unmistakably imposing Valenar high elf who was a vampire, and built her congregation out of the captives he kept in the residence beneath her club. When the Inevitables came through the Vault she followed her husband to the rooftop pool deck, where Esther Crona stabbed her through the chest.

No written description of Faith survives; the only likeness is her portrait, beside her husband. Her cultists went masked. She did not.

**The Vault** is two establishments and one operation. The Vault Underground sits in the Upper City, a club and gambling house; the Vault Below is carved into the old sub-levels behind guarded doors in the Lantern District. Both were hers. Both ran ordinary staff — bartenders, dancers, servers, bouncers — none of whom appear to have been involved in or aware of anything beneath them. The Empire licensed her, the elite drank in her rooms, and the entrance to her private residence was a door in her own office on the club's lowest level.

What Sharn saw was Esther Crona's glamorous half-sister running the city's best nightclub. What was true is that Faith Zeir was the Mother Prophet of [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/), a small silent congregation of masked cultists who worshipped her nephew; that the residence beneath her club held a ritual room, a kitchen and a cell off it called the Pantry; that captives were drugged, isolated and used by her husband and by the wealthy men he entertained; and that the entire cult was assembled out of the survivors. Every masked cultist had once been "Father" Malrik Zeir's cattle, personally groomed and reshaped by the Mother Prophet herself. She did not recruit believers. She manufactured them out of the people her husband had already broken.

She kept a very small circle and every member of it was family or leverage. Her husband [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/), the soft-spoken Valenar high elf who turned out to be a vampire. Her brother-in-law [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/), Head of Transportation and one of the primary recruiters bringing victims into the residence. The wealthy men from across the continent who traded captives and treated her home as a private den. And below all of them, [the Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/), a sewer gang her husband was quietly paying for bodies.

**Bloodline:** Faith is a daughter of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), half-sister to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), to [Blair](/people/blair/) and to Eden. Sister Nora confirmed the relation years ago while studying Embrace at the University of Newham. The child her cult called Ember was her own sister's son.

She believes [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) is a prophesied figure, marked by the divine, and that the mark on his face is scripture. She believes a person can be taken apart and rebuilt into a believer, because she has done it repeatedly and it has worked every time. She believes her husband's appetite is a sacrament and not a crime.

In the club she was a host. In the residence she was a priest and an interrogator. She questioned [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) personally in the chambers beneath the Vault Below, with her cultists in attendance, and made him watch Malrik feed — the ordinary technique of a woman who had learned that terror does most of the reshaping and doctrine only has to finish it. The memory-extraction ritual attempted on him in those rooms failed, which is the only reason he could still tell anyone about it. She never lifted the mask on the operation to anyone above her; the Empire's own file on her says nightclub owner.

Faith was born to a Grand Duke who did not raise her, one of four daughters scattered across Khorvaire by [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and reunited only on paper by a Black Dove's genealogical study. Where [Blair](/people/blair/) took to the sea and Esther was sold into a Korth manor, Faith married into Valenar money and Imperial infrastructure. The Malrik's family ties extend into imperial infrastructure through his younger half-brother, Hallorn d'Lyrandar of Aundair, the newly appointed Head of Transportation and son of Supreme Admiral Vex Lyrandar, giving the Zeirs a quiet but potent reach into both Sharn's underworld and its political machinery. When Esther rose to Lord Commander, Faith's position became untouchable; when her sister's Iron Veil swept the Lower City, her club was not searched. Somewhere in that stretch of impunity she stopped being a woman with a cult and became the cult's prophet, and the recruiters — wealthy, foreign and emphatically not believers — kept the Pantry stocked while she did it.

**Right now:** Faith Zeir is dead. Her sister killed her, on a rooftop pool deck, with a single thrust through the chest, in front of the child her congregation had spent years calling divine. What remains of the Children of Ember has not been heard from, and the Underhive has asked the Council of Sharn for legal ownership of the Vault Below.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Mother Prophet of the Children of Ember; owner and operator of the Vault Underground and the Vault Below; connected by marriage to House Lyrandar; a supply node of Project E.D.E.N.

**Holdings:** the Vault Underground, Upper City · the Vault Below, Lantern District · the Zeir private residence beneath the Upper City club, with its ritual room, kitchen, Pantry and rooftop pool deck · Faith's office, the only door between them.

**Items:** correspondence between Malrik and Hallorn concerning Umbra and Project E.D.E.N., taken from the chambers beneath the Vault Below · notes on the failed ritual performed on Grady Marsh · a note recording Mayor Henry Heinrick's membership of [The Velvet Table Society](/organizations/the-velvet-table-society/) · a receipt, in effect, for captives bought from the Drain Snakes.

**The Reshaping:** Faith's one demonstrated art, and the only reason the Children of Ember existed. She took her husband's surviving cattle — drugged, isolated, and already convinced no one was coming — and made them into a congregation that would put on a mask and hold down the next one. Every cultist the Inevitables fought had been through it. So had the frightened young woman who told them so, tied to a chair outside an Upper City safe house, and who was still trying to explain it when they walked away.

## The Night the Vault Fell

The Inevitables came in through the front with a VIP pass Hallorn had handed them himself, and [Lorian](/people/lorian/) — who had been kept in the Pantry — walked them down. They found her cultists mid-ceremony over [Whole Foods](/people/whole-foods/), Calcifer's nanny, bound and terrified in the ritual room, and cut them down. Upstairs the recruiters attacked, Hallorn confronted them, [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) was chained and dragged away, and Malrik fled to the roof with both Gemma and Calcifer. Faith went after her husband.

[Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) killed Malrik outright on the pool deck; the vampire's mist escaped into his coffin but the body was destroyed. And then Esther Crona came down out of the sky, saw her son in danger, saw Gemma in chains and the Mother Prophet running, and stabbed her sister through the chest in a single decisive strike. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) delivered the final blow as she collapsed. Faith Zeir died without being told what she was being killed for.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to be the prophet of a god she had a blood claim on.

**Likes:** the club at full capacity · masks on everyone but herself · her husband's appetite · wealthy men from far away · a convert who has stopped arguing · the word "Mother" · Ember · doors that only open from her office.

**Dislikes:** questions from the floor · anyone who breached her sanctum · captives who kept a name · her sister's soldiers on her street · her sister.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** she mistook total control over broken people for being believed.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown.
