**Player character (Patrick) · Contractor to Temple Holdings LLC · Scholar of the Infernal Machines · Remained in the deep past**

*Also known as:* Pimm · Pym · Willem

**Pim** is a player character of the Temple Holdings LLC chronicle, played by Patrick, who also played [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/). He joined the party in [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) as a contractor to [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), and became its scholar of the Infernal Machines, which he named the eldritch machines. He had criminal contacts in Suzail and made the party allies of the Zhentarim, dug up the grave of [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/), studied [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) with Lex, and was the Crown's primary witness at the trial of [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/). His writings covered the walls of the citadel after Kara's Wish. He stayed in the deep past, in a hidden refuge with Chronocypher and the Changelings of the Moon.

## Arrival

Dave, 2026-09-14: "Pim was a PC played by PAtrick" (sic). Pim arrived at the Halloween episode at Whitehorn. Patrick, that night: "I mean, Him wants to figure out how that machine works. But also, Pim just got here", correcting "him=pim". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-21)</small> Dave promised the party "more information from Pim’s studies" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-23)</small>, and Patrick set out his goal: "Pim wants all the machines." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-08)</small>

He is not a member of the company. When Revan's player asked how Pim had "rights in temple holdings llc AND or a diadem", Dave answered: "yo he doesn't, he's a contractor and has a loaner diadem". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-02)</small> See [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/).

After the Waterdeep session, JL reported that "Pimm mentioned going to a Theymarsh and fomenting rebellion so we could have our own new kingdom to house future Infanta". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-12-08)</small> Dave: "It's a great time for you guys to chart some independent course like this, so I encourage this." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-12-09)</small>

## Suzail and the Zhentarim

In downtime before the crisis in Cormyr, Pim went to the city "and starts talking to his rich art/artifact collector contacts to try and figure out if there are sympathetic rich people who can help put Zanzibar back in power", and Dave allowed it: "ok, so we'll have some info gathering". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-02)</small>

In Suzail, "Revan and Pim may also have contacts here, smugglers who regularly bring contraband Sembian Whiskey into the palace through the waterway", and "Revan and Pim’s contacts bring them" to the Bareback Saloon, where "A Zent bigwig named Emery Grim holds court". <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small> Pim, Zanzibar and Stonecypher visited a fortune teller who told them the story of the three brothers and gave each a message; Pim's was "Don't read it". <small>(Nico's report. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-21)</small> See [the Three Brothers](/lore/the-three-brothers/).

While the others stormed Castle Danzig, "Pim, meanwhile, delivered the Amulet of Kai Ryssdal to Emory Grim and visited a fortune teller who told a story of three brothers." <small>(Arc III Part VIII.)</small> Patrick's account of that downtime:

> We handled that after y'all left. Pim returned the crown jewel to Emory, who now quite likes Pim and says she "owes him one."

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-26)</small>

Afterwards "Emory Grim and the Xentharim of the central continent are now allied with Pim and therefore the party." <small>(Arc III Part VIII.)</small> Before the Battle of the River Lis, Dave told the players he had "always considered the Zents totally up for grabs - for you or St Cloud", and that "You three plus Pim are their closest allies in earth". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

Patrick later wrote: "Fun fact: Pim's Criminal Contacts are actually what allowed us to waltz right into the castle and completely bungle the situation in Suzail". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-18)</small>

## The eldritch machines

Pim is the party's authority on the Infernal Machines. Dave, answering the players' questions about the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/): "Pim is an expert, Kara can always find research, the Lexicon knows what it knows and the War Wizards have one and have been studying it". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small> The same day:

> No designs for the Obliviator have ever been found, although since the “awakening” of motherstone, scholars like Pim and Gimble theorized the machine was already built, and the city and its unique moonstone formations were merely the perfect place to turn it on

> One last thing I would add is that pim believes a better name for them would be the “eldritch machines”

> the machines predate the First Age of Infanta by millennia, Pim and Gimble place them as originating before any other knows artifacts and long before the creation date believed in most faiths

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small> See [Flex Gimble](/people/flex-gimble/). After the Second Battle of Motherstone Dave added that "People in Motherstone and those who have read Flex Gimble and Pim’s scholarship know about the Obliviator and what it likely does". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-29)</small>

## The buried heart

At Motherstone, "The mass grave where Carlysle St. Cloud was buried has been excavated. Pim’s gruesome efforts found a tiny hole, about the size of a human heart, burrowed down and down and down." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small> JL asked, "Pim dug up Carlyle’s bones only to find a hole through the center of the earth. Right?", and Dave answered, "That’s right". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-05)</small>

When the Anachron came to Motherstone, "Pim has spent much of his time studying the new device, comparing it with the Soul Syphon and the Obliviator. They are absolutely made by the same people with the same magics. With Lex’s help, has recorded the runes over every surface of the object and attempted to decode its writing." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small>

## The Second Battle of Motherstone and the trial

Pim was one of the four who saw the Infanta Una go down the pit beneath Motherstone: "the only witnesses to the infanta jumping down were Pim, Roland, Zan and Rev". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small> See [Una](/people/una/). Of Roland's betrayal, "All he or anyone remembers is him going to the control panel and jumping in the invading ships then throwing Pim down the well". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small>

At the trial, "Magnus will need to prepare his defense, the Crown will call Pim as their primary witness so get ready for an oscar worthy performance of political righteousness". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small> JL's recap after the verdict: "Roland not guilty. Terrorists attacked the trial, proving that Motherstone is very vulnerable without the Lexicon. Joseph and Pim are working to build a new one." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-22)</small> In Arc IV, "Pim and Magnus are working with Lex on the Anachron" aboard [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/). <small>(Arc IV Part V.)</small>

Patrick gave Pim's full name: "Full name would actually be Willem". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-25)</small>

## Starfall and the deep past

Pim sailed with the party in Arc V. Dave, after a session in space: "Poor Pim once again not present for the anthropological discovery of the epoch". Patrick: "Same shit as always. Back to staring at the boat walls". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-04-26)</small>

After [Kara](/people/kara/)'s Wish that the Moon had been moved in the past, "Pim’s writings cover the walls and describe their success in moving the moon." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.)</small> Dave's recap: "you soaked in Pims life’s work and heard the call of the Nether for the first time". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-08-08)</small>

By the Time of Troubles he was no longer with the party. Dave: "you don’t know much about them and what they even think really, and Pim is gone". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> His map of Netheril survived: "Pim’s map left in the cave showed all their flying enclaves / And they’re on the master campaign map". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Dave listed him among the characters still in the deep past: "Chronocypher, XIII, Pim, Karametra, Drefanmetra and Revan, and Roland". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> The quest board for the deep past read "Rlyeh, get the Obliviator, meet back with Cypher and Pim". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small>

[Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/), split in time, remained "in the deep past as Chronocypher in a hidden refuge with Pim and the Changelings of the Moon". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-12)</small>

## At the table

Patrick's Arc V sheet for Pim: 70 hit points, AC 14, passive Perception 12. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-06-13)</small> In February 2019 Patrick joked about "a sort of gene-splicing machine in Motherstone that Pim and Magnus trip and fall into, making Magpim, a smooth-talking archaeologist with weird deer spells", and the table called the pair "Magpim" for a time. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>
