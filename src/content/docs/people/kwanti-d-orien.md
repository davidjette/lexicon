**Human · Leader of House Orien · Dragonmarked house of transportation · Level Unknown · Dead, found impaled in lower Sharn**

*Also known as:* **d'Orien**, as the record mostly calls him.

Kwanti d'Orien is the leader of **House Orien**, the Dragonmarked house of transportation, and one of the public leaders of Sharnian society and business. His house runs the largest network of lightning rail lines and engines on the continent and is adding to its portfolio every day, and he has made more explicit alliance with the Empire than other Dragonmarked houses, attempting to leverage the vast might and scale of the Emperor's vision to advance his own transportation Empire. House Orien was represented at the **Interoperability Summit at Sharn**, convened to settle rail standards across the Empire's borders, where Lord Grey's committee killed the gnomes' demand for higher gauge rails and where the Crimson Sun ran a mole hunt among three members of the Imperial Assembly. No physical description of him is recorded, and neither is his birth or his elevation to the leadership of the house.

## The house

**d'Orien's house runs the largest network of Lightning Rail lines and engines on the continent, and is adding to their portfolio every day.** The lightning rail is what makes the Pax Imperium a single country rather than a map: it carries the Empire's soldiers, its trade, its bells under Assembly seal, and — on at least one famous occasion — its enemies. Every strategic question in Khorvaire that involves distance runs through House Orien, and House Orien runs through Kwanti d'Orien.

## Public and private

**In public** he is one of Sharn's civic leaders: a businessman at the head of a great house, sitting on the platforms and in the boxes where the city's commercial life is conducted. **In private** he has made a bet no other Dragonmarked house leader has made in the open. **d'Orien has made more explicit alliance with the Empire than other Dragonmarked houses, attempting to leverage the vast might and scale of the Emperor's vision to advance his own transportation Empire.** Where House Sivis was banned for what it knew and House Lyrandar sent an Admiral to command the Emperor's fleets, Orien simply signed on.

## Competence and circle

d'Orien's counterparts are the men who set the terms his rails run on: [Lord Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/) at the Imperial Trade Federation, whose industrial policy determines what Orien can build, and House Lyrandar, whose airships are the only competing form of Imperial long-distance transport. His house was represented at the Interoperability Summit at Sharn alongside Lyrandar and the trade authorities of Eastern Sharn. No heirloom or personal item of his is recorded.

## Beliefs

He believes the Emperor's vision is large enough to carry his own inside it — the record's phrase is that he is *attempting to leverage the vast might and scale of the Emperor's vision to advance his own transportation Empire*. Nothing else of what he believes is recorded.

## On the job

d'Orien expands. The record's phrase is *adding to their portfolio every day*, and it is written about a man in the middle of a war. He does not appear in any account of fighting, plotting or spying.

## Origin

His birth, his elevation to the leadership of House Orien, his mark, and how the house's alliance with the Empire was struck are not described in any surviving source.

## The gauge question

The **Interoperability Summit at Sharn** was convened to settle rail standards across the Empire's borders, and House Orien sent representatives. Before it opened, the Council of Mayors at Thronehold instructed its delegate to *negotiate for standards which reinforce investments the Imperial rail system*, and Lord Grey's committee killed the gnomes' demand for higher gauge rails outright — *"if Sharn and other border cities are going to function then its time to put the notion to rest."* A wider gauge would have made the border cities' rolling stock incompatible with the Imperial network. Rejecting it locked the continent onto Orien's track.

That summit is also where the Crimson Sun ran its mole hunt. See [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/): three members of the Imperial Assembly were under suspicion, and representatives of Houses Orien and Lyrandar were in the room throughout.

At the summit Kwanti pushed for his own standards and for Orien's centrality in the Empire's infrastructure. Orien won the gauge. [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) then persuaded the summit to centralise that power in the Empire instead of relying on the houses, and by Dave's account Kwanti would hate LeBeefe for it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-31)</small>

## Death

Kwanti d'Orien was dead by the time of the Sharn arc. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-15)</small> A BioTec internal research file on [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) lists House Orien as the former head of transportation, with its leadership decimated. Kwanti was found impaled in lower Sharn, in circumstances the file calls "not consistent with random violence." Members of his family are missing, and Orien's numbers keep falling through disappearances nobody has explained. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-01-02)</small> Before he died, by the file's internal records, Kwanti had started inquiring into "restricted Embra shipments", and the collapse of his house has given House Lyrandar a monopoly on transportation. According to Nico, his entire family was killed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-09-11)</small>
