**Autognome · The Red Eye · The Starsong Awakens · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* 9 · the Red Eye · Negen Zanzibar · the Masked Lord

**Nine** is the Red Eye, and a companion of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/) in [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), who went with them through the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/). He is Barrias's "son", who took Barrias's old nom de guerre, the Red Eye, and led the robot resistance into open hostility with the Sovereignty and an obsession with the banned and vanished Lexicon; that obsession brought him to [Necis](/places/necis/), trying to commune with it again. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-the-red-eye.md)</small> He lived for years in the age of [the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/) as Negen Zanzibar, and believed it his destiny to create the Lexicon anew. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Background

Before *The Starsong Awakens* began, Dave pictured Barrias, fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, as possibly alone except for Seven, Eight and Nine, out on the periphery on a ramshackle pirate ship, collecting salvage to survive and to repair his ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-04)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

According to Nico, Barrias and Seven were in the sphinx temple going through time doors with Nine, with the ship *Pussyfoot* parked outside. Dave recalled that the last door took them to [the five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/); Nico, that it showed the final fight on the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small> Every door in the Sphinx Tomb could be entered. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-07)</small>

Dave, who played Barrias, saw the arc as centred on Nine and on how he set out to fix everything bad that happened after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, with his father and brother eventually bringing him home. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small> His father and brother are not named.

## The Red Eye

"Red Eye" began as the name of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) at the beginnings of the robot resistance. The Red Eye whom [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) and his Bureau partner hunted in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) was not Barrias but Nine, Barrias's "son", who had taken the name. As the Red Eye, Nine led [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/) much further, into active hostility with the Sovereignty and an obsession with the Lexicon, banned and vanished somewhere out in space, which is why he came to Necis trying to commune with it again. It was the big reveal of the game. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-the-red-eye.md)</small>

## Aboard the ship

Nine sailed with Barrias, Se7en, Eight and the Company Esper Lamp on the crew's ship, where he minded the topaz dragon egg from [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/)'s stash and noticed when it began to look "soft-boiled". <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The pocket dimension

Nine and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) spent six months together in a pocket dimension, gathering food, hunting game and building shelters against the elements. Nine shared his plans and hopes with Kol. He was possessed with an almost religious fervour that his destiny was to rewrite the past by creating the Lexicon and sending it into the past, filled with understanding and compassion, to erase the mistakes of the past. He told Kol stories of his father and brothers; some nights he sang by the campfire, and some nights the two sat in silent contemplation of the cosmos. At the end of it he told Kol he had had a dream about him. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> He also tended [Paytra](/people/paytra/) when she woke, weak, in a darkened room: "You shouldn't try to move. You're still too weak. In time, you will heal." <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Negen Zanzibar

Through the doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/) Nine reached Punis in the age of the Five Zanzibars and stayed for years. He became a masked lord of Waterdeep and laid claim to the Zanzibar heritage, all to find the Lexicon, an aspect of which was grafted onto General Varkas's airship. As **Negen Zanzibar**, the People's Champion, he led thieves, peasants and former slaves, until [Varkas](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) captured him and held him in the castle dungeon. He wanted only to be freed, because he knew the Lexicon was on the airship, and would give anything to speak to it and fulfil his destiny. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> JL named him among the five Zanzibars as "the mysterious Negen Zanzibar (an outlaw folk hero whose identity was shrouded in mystery until he was revealed to be an autognome)". <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-17)</small>

The Lexicon's aspect on the airship told [Se7en](/people/se7en/) it had met Se7en's brother, who was also its son, and who said he was also its father, destined to create it. It did not remember a father, and did not want that brother to be its father. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The Sphinx Tomb

In the Sphinx Tomb Nine told Barrias he was the wisest man he had ever known, and asked whether he truly believed his mission foolhardy: birthing a new Lexicon, a better Lexicon, to right the wrongs of the past. He reached forward for an embrace with a blade in his hand, aimed between Barrias's ribs, and fought to reach the door marked Justice. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

At the end of the universe, behind the last door before the seventh, Nine and several of his time-travelling selves stood among the mind flayers of the Grand Design, which he had recognised for what it was. The travellers killed them; the last Nine they killed held the last piece of the NO FU TU RE key. In his dying moments Nine appeared pathetic and apologetic. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> What Nine and Barrias said to each other at the end is not recorded. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>
