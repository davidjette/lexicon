**Giff · Wizard (Order of Scribes) · Magus ex Machina, SSJ Zanzibar · Dead**

*Also known as:* Nilpferd · Chief Arcanist

**Dr. Nilpferd** was a Giff, a hippo-folk wizard of the Order of Scribes and the Magus ex Machina of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by JL. Informally called the ship's Chief Arcanist, he became the superior of Cadet [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) during *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, and in the fifteen years before *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* he died peacefully in his sleep, survived by a daughter cloned from him, Schnuptfabak.

## Light of Xaryxis

Nilpferd joined the crew in January 2023, introduced as the Zanzibar's Chief Arcanist, a wizard of the Order of Scribes, and Carlos Vega's new boss. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> The head of the Order of Scribes and its chief instructor is Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/) of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) network, whose recent work, and the business of many officers of the order, is finding a way to overcome [Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/) to allow interstellar communication and teleportation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

His title on the Zanzibar's crew roster is Magus ex Machina; "Chief Arcanist" is his informal title within the Order of Scribes. <small>(crew roster, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27; 2023-01-22)</small>

Nilpferd is a hippo, a Giff; JL, who played him, had no miniature for him. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small> His portrait shows a purple hippopotamus-headed figure in robes, and Dave remarked on its human hands. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> In December 2023 Dave pictured him as an elephantfolk, and described the scroll wizard as the ship's computer specialist. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-15)</small>

As everyone on the Zanzibar knew, Nilpferd was a ninety-year-old wizard of the Order of Scribes, and it was said there was not a spell he did not know. At the Battle of [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) he commanded a battalion of casters; in old age he took an advisory role aboard the ship. He stood over six feet tall but stoop-shouldered, weighed about nine hundred pounds and moved slowly, though his reflexes stayed sharp. He was mostly deaf and read lips, and wore bifocals on his enormous nose for his poor eyesight. He dressed in the traditional blue and silver robe of an Astral Command arcanist, reaching to his feet, and carried a staff of petrified wood headed with a faintly glowing purple gem. He was rarely seen about the ship, but when he left his quarters everyone could hear him coming. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

When Carlos was moved to the arcane division as a janitor, Nilpferd welcomed him with a warning in Elvish, *oobi snegoobi*, "watch your ass", since there were magics in the division that could destroy planets, and with the three rules every wise arcanist follows: be curious, do good, wash your hands. He put him under Ensign [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

When a Chingwa's *wish* began to fill the ship with gold and mushrooms, Carlos woke Nilpferd from an astral projection with a scroll of *dispel magic* from his own cabinet. Nilpferd scolded them, warned that casting *wish* to stop a wish could undo reality itself, and sent them into the Astral Plane to find his master, the only caster he knew with a tenth-level spell to end it. He went to the deep-space station [the Cube](/places/the-cube/) with Barrias and Caprica to find the legal expert [MacFlecknoe](/people/macflecknoe/). See [the Chingwa's wish](/history/the-chingwas-wish/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session; Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

## Death

Dr. Nilpferd died peacefully in his sleep after a prolonged illness, surrounded by loved ones. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
