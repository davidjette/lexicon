**Elf-lich (*baelnorn*) · Archmage · House Gallidann, then Queen Maab · Destroyed at Motherstone · Later present as a remnant and an ally**

*Also known as:* Calix · the Betrayer · <small>(the strange, undead, extraordinarily old elf calling himself the Masked Prophet in the rewritten past matches his description)</small>

**Calix the Betrayer** was an elf-lich (*baelnorn*) and archmage, brother of **Eldamir** of House Gallidann, and the architect of the **Forgetting** at Motherstone. In the age the elves call the **Heavenfall** he demanded the first Infanta, **Elistrada**, as his pupil and was refused; he stored parts of his soul throughout the smaller islands, allied with **Queen Maab**, and built the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) together with [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, from Netherese magic and technology. After Stone and **Elistrada** founded Motherstone he fought beside them and beside Eldamir in the war that obliviated the Netherese. He then planned to obliviate Motherstone and frame Brightmantle and the dwarvish people for his betrayal, and buried a terminal-throne beneath the city intending to dissolve his mind into the crystal and replace the Lexicon with himself.

Stone pre-empted him and threw himself into the Obliviator, and the act drove Calix and the Gallidan elves insane; Calix and Eldamir were cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other on the beach, and Calix, immortal, survived as a lich, mad, no longer knowing how to find his phylactery. He survived Motherstone: the party, having cured the Lexicon, reached the vault where his hacked throne and phylactery were hidden, and emerged from the phylactery ten years later to find him at the young Empress's side. He is present afterwards as a remnant, as the Masked Prophet in the rewritten past, and as an ally at the muster of [Arc VII](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/).

## Bearing

Calix is a *baelnorn*: an elf who refused the grave and willingly turned himself undead. The record fixes a sigil — his mark is emblazoned on the iron blood seal in the Motherstone dungeon, and again, centuries and planes away, throughout the walls of the Old Man's House in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). In the rewritten past of [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) a very strange elf — undead, extraordinarily old — calling himself the **Masked Prophet** moves among the elves, and he matches the description.

## House Gallidann, and the first Infanta

In the age the elves call the **Heavenfall**, Calix was the brother of **Eldamir**, founder of House Gallidann. Where Eldamir built a house, Calix "moved into solitary study of magic on a nearby archipelago." When a human child showed mastery of elemental magic — the first Infanta, **Elistrada**, the goddess [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) reborn — Calix demanded the child as his pupil. Eldamir refused. The two of them were later cast out of the forgotten city and slew each other on the beach, after the Forgetting had driven Calix and the Gallidan elves insane; Calix, immortal, survived as a lich, mad, and no longer knowing how to find his phylactery.

Only Eldamir stayed dead. Calix stored parts of his soul throughout the smaller islands in hopes of extending his life beyond even an elf's, allied with **Queen Maab**, goddess of air and darkness, and rose again and again to seize the island's power.

## The Forgetting

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Motherstone was built around the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), a mind grown inside the world's largest perfect moonstone; Calix opened the **Gate Ring**, a stable abyssal vortex, to make the city a data vacuum pulling the echoes of all information in creation toward itself. Then he buried his own terminal-throne to *replace the Lexicon with himself* — to dissolve his mind entirely into the crystal and become one with the Motherstones.

Calix built the Lexicon together with [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/), the Brightmantle Infanta, from Netherese magic and technology, and planned to obliviate Motherstone and frame Brightmantle and the dwarvish people for his betrayal. Stone pre-empted him: rather than let his wife [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/) believe he had turned on her, he threw himself into the Obliviator so that she would forget he had ever existed. That act made everything about Motherstone cursed and obliviated knowledge, and drove Calix and the Gallidan elves insane.

He did it to outlast the one thing he fears. His remnant says so in the words of an ancient drama:

> My sin is the Forgetting.\
> Time is the only recompense.\
> If I live until I know myself again\
> I will live forever.

<small>— Calix, in his phylactery beneath Motherstone</small>

He has forgotten *himself* along with everything else: he seeks, but knows not what for. [Mara Mageblood](/people/mara-mageblood/) read the same truth off the last page of a cursed Orcish tome — *"a buried throne for a living god, for even he fears the Leviathan."* The override that locked every other terminal out of the Obliviator's reset was placed on the Fifth of Leaffall, 49, by a single user: Calix.

He also altered his vault and throne so that time passed slower inside and faster outside: the world would forget him quickly, and time would spin off into the future while his consciousness stayed unaware of the doom Toril faced from the Leviathan, which terrified him more than anything. The chamber was never used.

## The lullaby

The whole of it survives only in a nursery song that Mara Mageblood's mother could not sing without losing herself, copied into the journal the heroes carried out of the mountain — and its last line is his name.

> When all the world's inside your mind, forgetting is oblivion,\
> so never sing this song again of — Calix the Betrayer.

## Death

Once the party had cured the Lexicon, Calix was able to locate the city, and he attacked them. They found his hacked throne and his phylactery hidden in his vault, in a chamber that had never been used. When the heroes emerge from the phylactery ten years on, the young Empress who retakes Motherstone introduces the advisor who made her victory possible: Calix. His crown would have locked her into a deadly trance on the Throne and let her divine power be hijacked to destroy the island. The thing that undoes him is the music box *the Infanta's Dream*, made by Brightmantle, whose maker's memory the heroes drew back out of the Obliviator before he could erase it again. By the [Age of the Infanta](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) Calix is dead, and the Company spends years failing to unlock the Calix puzzle in the broken Lexicon.

## The remnant and the ally

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> In the rewritten past he walks as the Masked Prophet, guiding the elves toward ruin with a plan to end the rule of gods and men in one stroke and restore the Moon. And at the muster in [Arc VII](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/) he stands with **Lex** to lay out the final intelligence — because he hears the **Call of Cthulhu** himself, and turns. It is Calix who exposes how [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) deceived the Netherese queens into serving the Leviathan, warping their understanding so they would bring all the magic of their world to bear against their own existence. The moon elves who ride out at the end ride with Elistrae, Eldamir — and Calix.

## Beliefs

He believes knowledge is the only immortality, and that a mind poured into a crystal outlives a body that merely refuses to die. He believes the Leviathan is coming and that being forgotten is the only wall against a god who devours by remembering. He believes he will know himself again.

**Organizations / Groups:** House Gallidann (by blood, and against it) · the court of Queen Maab · the Forsworn, by long alliance

**Holdings:** the buried throne beneath Motherstone · a phylactery held in a pocket of frozen time, ten years deep · fragments of soul scattered across the lesser isles of the Moonshae

**Items:** the blood seal in the Motherstone dungeon, marked with his sigil and openable only with the blood of a dwarf. The Gate Ring above the city — his vortex, still turning.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Eldamir** — his sister, founder of House Gallidann. They killed each other; only Calix came back.
- **Elistrada / Elistrae** — the child he demanded as a pupil, and the goddess he later betrayed.
- **Queen Maab** — his patron, "the elvish goddess of air and darkness."
- **Tylerjynex** — the copper dragon he beguiled and banished to a dark abyss, then kept blinded and slumped atop a hidden temple.
- **The Lexicon** — the mind he tried to replace with his own, and broke instead.
