**Half-illithid · Imperial comptroller · In LeBeefe's service · Unknown**

*Also known as:* Greenesh Hughes · Comptroller Hughes

**Greenish Hughes** was the Imperial comptroller assigned to oversee the transportation hub at [Leef](/places/leef/) and the town around it, and a half-illithid in the service of Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). She commanded a cohort of 480 soldiers and lived inside the hub's main building, but she had no real authority there: she was under the control of a team of Inquisition thugs. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Her files

In [Leef Ep 5](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/) the trio searched her files and found scrolls among them. Dave summed up one of them as "An alternative royal line" and "House of Vol will fall". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## The guard post

At an Imperial guard post on the way into the dwarflands, the party heard distant screaming. Greenish Hughes was there with a few Inquisition men, using LeBeefe's technology to experiment on dwarves and turn them into aberrations. Inside was an abducted bard, Grimpledink, who seemed entirely normal and was slowly turning into a mind flayer. She carried a Ring of Spell Storing holding a teleport to her teleportation circle in the hub. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The night train

On the night train to [Newham](/places/newham/), Greenish Hughes and two half-illithids teleported in by a circle on the floor of the carriage and attacked the trio, while she talked about the powerful and masculine LeBeefe. Halfway through the fight the Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) landed his airship on the train. In Newham, LeBeefe's chief inspector later came to the trio's door asking about the murder on the train. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>
