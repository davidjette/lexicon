> Scrunt is going to create some problems for the party until he forms bonds with you and learns your expectations. He’s got a chip on his shoulder and thrives in chaos.

<small>— JL, introducing his character. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small>

**Kobold · Rogue (arcane trickster) · First mate of the Sea Ghost · [Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/) · Dead**

*Also known as:* Scrunt · Mr Scrunt

**Scrunt Pissfoot** is a kobold arcane trickster, a member of the Rising Dawn, and the player character of JL in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/). Escaped from a prison ship, he washed ashore near [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) chained to [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/), and with her and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) formed [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/). He was first mate of [the Sea Ghost](/items/the-sea-ghost/), talked the party peacefully into [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/), and wore the Rising Dawn's neck tattoo. He died in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/) in September 2022.

## Description and abilities

Scrunt is a kobold. JL, in December 2021: "Scrunt and Lolo should have a kobolds night out to get to know each other." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-22)</small>

He is an arcane trickster rogue. Nico, relaying JL: "Jeff said Scrunt is gonna be a arcane trickster so he’ll have a lot of useful utility spells". JL: "Scrunt is also super versatile in terms of ranged or melee". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> By 2022 he could also fight with the sword [Whelm](/items/whelm/). Dave: "Scrunt can use Whelm cause he’s a fighter". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

JL on the table's alignment quizzes: "We took allignment quizzes and Scrunt is chaotic evil". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> JL again, in October 2021: "Get used to it. Scrunt has itchy stealing fingers." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16)</small>

## The prologue

JL's plan for the first session of the second party: "Gabs and Scrunt wash up on shore chained together." On 2021-08-20 the two, escaped from a prison ship, hid in a cemetery and found Talia Weathervien, later Dolores, breaking into a mausoleum. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15, 2021-08-20)</small>

## Saltmarsh

In *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh*, Scrunt worked out that Salmor had had the haunted house cleared by a priest. Dave: "Scrunt was right that Salmor had the house cleared BT a priest because of the spooks". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small> On 2021-09-18 his owl familiar was killed ("Sorry I killed your owl", Dave), and he took the pseudodragon Bimz as an advanced familiar ("Bimms is my new best friend", JL). Dave: "Bimz has a 10 intelligence, he's almost as smart as Scrunt". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The same day Dave named him first mate of the Sea Ghost: "Mr Scrunt is first mate, captain Hellwood and ships chaplain sister Dolores". The party's ledger lists him as "Scrunt - First Mate". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18; `sources/documents/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-neptunes-rejects-ledger.md`)</small>

## Dunwater

On 2021-10-09 the party fought sahuagin at sea. Dave: "Those sahaugin almost got you Scrunt". Scrunt then brought the party into Dunwater, the lizardfolk stronghold, as far as Queen Okathent. Nico: "Scrunt got us through it without violence". Dave: "But what the adventure doesn’t count on us “monstrous” PCs like Scrunt, this was literally the first adventure in D&D where killing the monsters leads to failure". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

The next session, on 2021-10-15, the table called "The Scrunt Episode". The party stole the boss's things, Gabriella intimidated their hosts, Dolores failed to lie her way out, and Irhtos jailed her. Dave: "“The Scrunt Episode” was good stuff". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16, 2021-10-17)</small>

## Skerrin

In the ambush at the Weathervein tomb on 2021-11-12, Scrunt's poison arrow helped save the party. Nico: "Having scrunts poison arrow and gabbys disarming saved us". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small> JL, in character, on [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/), who had set the ambush: "I don’t want money. I want revenge". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

On Gabriella's honeymoon on the frontier in early 2022, Scrunt went missing. Dave: "But I don’t know what happened to Scrunt!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-25)</small> The party killed Skerrin on 2022-03-12.

## The Rising Dawn

Scrunt belongs to the Rising Dawn. In JL's kobold one-shot of 2022-07-14 he went into the dungeon with Dave's kobold Bullseye and Nico's kobold Charlee, all recruits of the order. Nico: "Scrunt will be with us too, he’s an arcane trickster" and "I think we are recruits to the RD". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-12, 2022-07-13)</small>

In White Plume Mountain the party took the three weapons of the Weathervein treasure. Dave: "I guess for your sake Scrunt the Dawn excepts to take all three and put them in storage now that we crippled their two main foes for them the soggies and the brotherhood", and "And the Dawn/Scrunt want v different outcomes than the swords". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14, 2022-08-19)</small>

His Rising Dawn neck tattoo, as Nico ruled in play: "btw Scrunt Rising Dawn Neck Tattoo = +2 to Dmg to any Scarlet Brotherhood Assassin." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-07)</small>

## Death in White Plume Mountain

On 2022-09-09 an invisible stalker singled Scrunt out. Dave, the next day, on the "Super intense room and the invisble stalker targeting Scrunt". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-10)</small> On 2022-09-16 Scrunt died in the mountain. Nico: "Nah we did good! One of our crew died and we got through it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

Dave on Gabriella: "I decided during that game that the death of Scrunt was very impactful for Gabriella and she resolved to overcoming the negative influence of blackrazor and getting home". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24)</small>

Scrunt died in White Plume Mountain. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-nico-docs-questions.md)</small>

## Later life

Dave, in 2025: "At this point Scrunt probably has a whole village of kobold descendants". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small>
