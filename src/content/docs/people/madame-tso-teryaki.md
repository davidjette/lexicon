**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Matriarch of the Teryaki · The Teryaki clan · Alive**

*Also known as:* Madame Tso

Madame Tso Teryaki is the duergar matriarch of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan and mistress of the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/) beneath the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/). The Teryaki descend from the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) of the Mror Holds, and she brought them south to consecrate the mountain's dead and take the hold back, her brother [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) running the clearing parties. She received the **Inevitables** in her hall, recognised her late husband's Belt of Dwarvenkind at [Eric's](/people/eric-the-cleric/) waist and closed the audience at weapons' point, then granted them one cold night of hospitality on the strength of a returned mourning-braid and a correctly recited marriage-kinship. She gave them the fullest account of the mountain anyone has — the elves of Aerenal, the hammering of Shavat, the making of the bells and the mass graves — and set the terms that sent them to the bottom of it: the remains of [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) and of [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), in exchange for hospitality, the knowledge of the bells and access to Corn's folio. Told that the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) had been chained and bled for three years, she answered that he would renew the clan's pact, and refused to leave the mountain.

## Physical description

A deep dwarf who holds a throne room without sitting on the throne. She is a widow, and she knows her late husband's belt on sight across a dark hall full of armed strangers. She keeps a locked chest and a private door, and she shuts the latter in people's faces without raising her voice.

## The clan

The [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) are descendants of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) out of the Mror Holds, and Madame Tso brought them south to consecrate the dead of the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) and take the hold back. Her brother [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) runs the clearing parties. She runs everything else.

## Public and private

In public she is a matriarch under formal law: she hears petitions, she grants or withholds the hospitality of Rak'tafur by the night, and she says exactly what a guest's standing is. When the **Inevitables** walked into her hall wearing Imperial faces, she opened with *"I am Madame Tso Teryaki. This is my family's home. Who are you?"* and, on seeing the Belt of Dwarvenkind at [Eric's](/people/eric-the-cleric/) waist, closed the audience at weapons' point: *"You trespass on my family's hold. You desecrate our honoured dead. And you steal my late husband's belt."*

In private she is triaging a disaster with a camp of fourteen bedrolls and no army. She refused to discuss a missing child with strangers, told his mother's grief to stay inside the clan, and stated flatly that there was no way down - all while knowing how dangerous below was.

## The history she keeps

Madame Tso gave the party the fullest account of the mountain anyone has: the elves of Aerenal who came to marvel at the work and stayed to enslave the workers; **Shavat** hammered thin as paper and layered until it holds *millions of imperceptible shapes and circuits of golden wire*, enough to cast vast and devastating spells across distance, like sound; the retaken mine set to making a bell, then several, then hundreds; tens of thousands of kin slaughtered and left in mass graves; and the clan's return, to consecrate the dead and take this place back over.

Then she closed the door on everything else:

> I will not let go of the secrets of the dwarves so that you can replicate her horrors. You robbed from my dead. You do not have the right.

## Beliefs

She believes the clan's honoured dead outrank any living stranger's mission, however urgent that stranger says it is.

She believes the secrets of Shavat should die with her people rather than be lent to anyone who promises to use them well.

She believes a pact is a permanent instrument, and that if her ancestors bound the mountain to a dragon then the mountain can be bound to him again.

## Conduct

She granted one cold night of hospitality on the strength of a returned mourning-braid and a correctly recited marriage-kinship - *"Do not overstay your welcome, grave robbers"* - gave the dead outrider's name and offered his bunk in the same breath, and afterwards, when [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) carried [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) home through the middle of her camp, she returned her husband's belt to Eric with her own hands and blessed the party's claim on the folio, the horn and everything else. When Eric told the whole camp aloud that a child had died at his party's feet and been brought back burned, she put a hand on the mother's shoulder, turned to him, said thank you, and led them away.

## The offer

It was Madame Tso and her brother who set the terms that sent the party to the bottom of the mountain: bring back the remains of Sedda and of [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), and the kin will give them anything - hospitality, the knowledge of the bells, and access to Corn's folio, the complete record of the forging of Shavat. Half of that errand was completed. The king is still on the hoard, and the clan has never been told where he lies.

## Right now

Told that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) was enslaved with a bell chained round his neck and drained of blood for three years, exactly as her own people were enslaved to cast the bells, she answered:

> So he'll renew our pact.

Argued at, and asked to bless the mountain as a tomb and leave, she answered *"Why would we leave? We've just now recovered it,"* and *"We must renew our pact with the one who gave this to us."* The kin are still in her hall. Only [Bofric](/people/bofric/) left.
