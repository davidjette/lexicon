**Changeling · Warlock 3 · Propulsion engineer, SSJ Zanzibar · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Irina Malikowski · Irina Malikovski · Irena · Irene

**Irina Malikovsky** is a changeling warlock and the propulsion engineer of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), created and written by Dave and played by Nico from April 2024. Raised among the changelings of Dolphin 9, she found a crashed Astral Command pilot and, through a wish granted by the genie [Razomel](/people/razomel/), took the dead pilot's name and life, binding herself to take Razomel's place after her death. Aboard the Zanzibar during *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* she was a witness to [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/)'s innocence, was saved by him, in May 2024 moved with [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) to take the ship, and in the finale cast a Wish of her own.

## Background

Irina was born to a colony of changelings who had migrated from Faerun to a distant world called Dolphin 9, and was raised as a child of a collective identity community named Dolphlundgren. She discovered a crashed spacecraft whose pilot survived only long enough to tell her story, and took up a dream of leaving her home planet to travel the stars. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small>

The pilot carried a magic bottle holding the genie Razomel, who told Irina she could have any wish and all the magic he could grant if she agreed to set him free. The offer was a trick: accepting it bound Irina to Razomel, cursed to take his place in servitude after her death. She accepted, and wished to live the life of the pilot she had watched die and to explore the universe. Razomel gave her the knowledge and identity of the dead Astral Command pilot, including her name, Irina Malikowski, and she rejoined the pilot's squadron as if the crash had never happened. Razomel, a being made of all the souls who had ever served the cursed vessel, among them the real Irina, now dead, grants her magical powers and commands her eventual fate. Since then she has settled happily into her new life and role. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small> Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) knows her true story. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

On the crew roster of the Zanzibar she is Ensign Irina Malikovsky, Propulsion Engineer. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

In January 2023, when an engineer was killed in a blast and Carlos Vega was suspected, [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) tossed crew members' lockers, which drew Irina into the case. She became a witness to Carlos's innocence. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> By July 2023 Carlos had saved her life, and she was recovering. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small>

Irina and Carlos were both servants of Razomel. In April 2024 Carlos used his third wish and became a djinn bound in a bottle, and Razomel went free. When the Zanzibar reached Sigil, Irina was aboard with [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> Her 2024 player called her Carlos's follower. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

In the session of 10 May 2024 the party walked into the white light, and Mordecai and Irina set out to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small>

The finale of 31 May 2024 had two wish effects. Carlos's earlier third wish scattered the crew all over the place without warning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small> Irina cast the final Wish, by her player's account without any idea of what she was doing. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Her fate after the finale is unknown.

## Abilities and equipment

In December 2022 Irina was a full caster with proficiency in vehicles. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small> In April 2024 she was a second-level changeling warlock with a genie pact, and reached third level after the session of 26 April. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-26; 2024-04-27)</small> Her 2024 player pictured her eldritch blast as a fire whip. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-10)</small>
