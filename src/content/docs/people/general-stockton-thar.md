**Species unrecorded · Dark Druid · Imperial General, The Iron Veil · Western Front · Status unconfirmed**

*Also known as:* General Stockton Thar · Stockton

**General Stockton Thar** is an Imperial general of [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/), commander of its ground forces on the western front, and a Dark Druid native to the **Eldeen Reaches** rather than to Karrnath. He is thought to bear the Mark of Finding. From Fort Crona he commanded the combined force of soldiers and Iron Veil casters who held **Crona's Wall** against the Uruk-Hoth Republic. At the opening of POSÉ he told Lord Commander [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) to her face that the Wall was draining the land of warmth and was sent back to hold it together; he stood beside her on the deck of the **Black Veil** above the Ziggurat in Sharn Ep 10. The Wall collapsed at Korth Ep 1, letting Orc forces into Dark Druid territory, and he appears in no episode record after that.

General Stockton Thar commands the Iron Veil's ground forces with a reputation forged in brutality and fear. A native of the Eldeen Reaches, he is not merely an Imperial officer but a Dark Druid himself, steeped in the shadowed traditions of his homeland. His connection to the eldritch powers of the Reaches lends him a grim authority, and whispers claim that his strategies are guided as much by druidic rites as by military doctrine.

## Physical description

An Imperial general out of the **Eldeen Reaches**, and one of the very few senior Imperials whose authority does not come from a uniform. He is thought to bear the **Mark of Finding**, a rare dragonmark that grants uncanny abilities to locate people and objects. He is a **Dark Druid**, steeped in the shadowed traditions of his homeland, and whispers claim his strategies are guided as much by druidic rites as by military doctrine. <small>No description of his face, colouring or heraldry survives in the recovered files — only that soldiers under his command both fear and respect him.</small>

## The western command

Thar's forces to the west are bolstered by [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) soldiers and casters. These high-level wizards are stationed out of **Fort Crona** and along the massive barrier known as **Crona's Wall**, these spellcasters maintain the mythal-like force wall that holds and pushes back the **Uruk-Hoth Republic**. Stockton's command of this combined force of soldiers and casters has made him the lynchpin of the Empire's western campaign, ensuring that the Iron Veil continues to push its enemies back.

He is thought to bear the Mark of Finding, a rare dragonmark that grants uncanny abilities to locate people and objects. This gift, combined with his ruthless command style, has made him indispensable in the Empire's war against the Uruk-Hoth Republic.

To the soldiers under his command, Stockton Thar is both feared and respected — a general whose cruelty is matched only by his results. To the Empire, he remains a necessary weapon, a man whose dark heritage, dragonmark, and mastery of druidic and arcane forces make him uniquely suited to hunt down enemies wherever they hide.

## Public and private

Publicly he is the Empire's grimmest success — a general whose cruelty is matched only by his results, holding a continental front with a wall of force and a garrison of wizards. Privately he is the only man on record who told [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) to her face that her Wall was breaking the world, and he did it at a restaurant opening in a city he did not command.

The land tells him things. He reports imbalance in the deep spirits, a frozen Lake Galifar and a Wall that steals warmth, and he reports them to a Lord Commander who does not care and is not asking. She sent him back to hold it together.

## Competence and circle

He argued with Esther Crona in public at the opening of POSÉ, and he stood on the deck of her airship **The Black Veil** above the Ziggurat as she prepared to move on the Cogs. He tracks the Lhazaar pirate traffic feeding the Uruk-Hoth Republic.

## The Dark Druid

An Imperial general is not supposed to be a druid, and a druid of the Eldeen Reaches is not supposed to serve Karrnath. Thar is both, and the Empire's own western campaign is fought against Orc forces pushing into *Dark Druid territory* — his own people's ground.

## Beliefs

He believes the deep spirits of the lands whisper of imbalance. He believes the Wall steals warmth from the land — and he believes it enough to say so to the woman who built it. He believes the Lhazaar pirates are worth taking seriously because the Republic pays well and loyalty follows money.

## Conduct in role

He holds ground and he finds people. The Mark of Finding, in the hands of a man commanding a garrison of high-level casters, makes hiding from the western command a technical problem rather than a matter of luck. When he raised the Wall's cost with the Lord Commander, he was told to hold it together.

## Origin

A native of the **Eldeen Reaches**, not of Karrnath, and a Dark Druid before he was ever an Imperial officer. <small>How a druid of the Reaches came into Imperial service, and when, is not recorded.</small> What is documented is the result: an Empire that could not otherwise have held its western border acquired a commander who could read the land it was fighting over, and gave him Fort Crona, a wall that carries the Crona name, and a garrison of wizards whose entire function is to keep a mythal-like barrier standing. He built the western campaign on that and became indispensable to it.

## The Ziggurat

Sharn Ep 10. A crystallizer screen in the Underhive tavern flickered to life showing a live broadcast of the Lower City. Above the Ziggurat hovered Esther Crona's airship, the Black Veil. Esther stepped onto the deck alongside General Stockton Thar, who began casting a spell as the crowd below panicked. The screens abruptly cut to black. <small>What he cast, and on what, is not recorded. Whatever it was, it happened over the largest civilian district in the Lower City and the Empire cut the feed rather than let it be seen. He was in the Cogs with a large drill and a frontline Iron Veil detachment at around the same time.</small>

## Crona's Wall

Korth Ep 1, three years later. **Crona's Wall collapses, allowing Orc forces to push into Dark Druid territory in the Eldeen Reaches.** The Wall he warned about, on the front he was the lynchpin of, in the country he came from.

<small>The general himself appears in no episode record after that line. Whether Stockton Thar died holding the Wall, withdrew with what was left of the western command, or went home to the Reaches ahead of the Orcs is not established. He is one of the very few senior Imperials whose disposition the Inevitables have never confirmed either way.</small>

## Right now

The Wall is down and the Uruk-Hoth Republic is in the Eldeen Reaches. The Hundreds and the Silver Flame are fighting E.D.E.N. constructs across the continent and food shortages are worsening in Sharn. Nobody has reported seeing General Stockton Thar since, and nobody has reported that he is dead.

**Organizations / Groups:** [The Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) — commander of ground forces · the Imperial western command · the Dark Druids of the Eldeen Reaches.

**Holdings:** **Fort Crona** and the garrison of Iron Veil casters along **Crona's Wall** — the mythal-like force barrier holding back the Uruk-Hoth Republic, collapsed at Korth Ep 1.
