**Species unrecorded · Cleric of the Silver Flame · Zilspar Farm 02 · Missing**

<small>Also known as: Donna</small>

Donna is a 59-year-old cleric of the Silver Flame and the resident healer of **Zilspar Farm 02**, the Breland farmstead outside Sharn that served as the operational heart of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/). She remained at the farm while [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and her paladins travelled Khorvaire on missions for the Flame, tending the wounded and keeping the rebellion's quiet operations running beneath the business of the farm, and she held Sister Nora of the Black Doves for months in a concealed underground cell sealed with a Private Sanctum ritual. She nursed [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) and [Lorian](/people/lorian/) after the Vault Below, and had the guest house made up after an Iron Veil patrol was killed on the farm. Zilspar Farm 02 has since been burned to the ground with people boarded up inside and Zero-One taken alive; Donna is not among the dead in the tower field and has not been found.

## Physical description

Fifty-nine years old, with vivid red curling hair to the shoulder and blue eyes set in a lined, kind face that smiles first. She wears heavy oatmeal linen robes with a broad shawl gathered at the throat, over a working harness of leather straps hung with pouches, tool loops and a wooden holy symbol; a silver pendant hangs at her breast and a plain ring is on her finger. She is almost always carrying an open leather-bound book with quills and slips of paper pushed into it. Her hands are calloused from work and her robes are often dusted with flour or soil.

## Zilspar Farm 02

Zilspar Farm 02 is a working farmstead in the Breland countryside outside Sharn and the operational heart of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/). Beneath the ordinary business of produce runs and farm security lie a hidden teleportation circle, a private Fulcrum room, a guest house, and a concealed underground cell that can be sealed with a Private Sanctum ritual. While [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and her paladins travel Khorvaire on missions for the Flame, Donna remains behind as the farm's steady heart, keeping the quiet operations running beneath the surface of daily farm life.

## Public and private

To the farmers she is more than a healer: she is a motherly presence, a voice of calm authority, and the person they instinctively turn to when something goes wrong. Her smile carries the warmth of someone who has raised half the farm's children at one point or another. She also keeps the farm's prison. She led three armed strangers to a concealed underground cell and sealed a bound Black Dove cleric into it with a Private Sanctum ritual, and then went back to the kitchen. She tends the wounded, comforts the frightened, and keeps a prisoner.

In practice she is the entire civilian infrastructure of Fulcrum's stronghold: healer, quartermaster, gaoler, midwife to the survivors of other people's operations. When an Iron Veil patrol descended on the farm and dragged families from their homes, the farmers who fought were people she had raised. When the fight was over the bodies were burned and the skiff dismantled, and the trio came back to the cell and to her.

## Competence and circle

Her circle is the farm and the people brought to it. Zero-One trusts her implicitly, enough to leave her in charge of the farm's spiritual and practical wellbeing whenever she departs. She nursed the captives brought back from the Vault Below — [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/), foggy from drugs and a failed memory ritual, and [Lorian](/people/lorian/), who arrived hot-headed and full of vengeance and left a paladin. Though she rarely leaves the farm she is deeply respected within the Silver Flame.

**The prisoner:** Sister Nora of the Black Doves spent months in Donna's cell. Donna is the one who reported that she was refusing to eat.

## Beliefs

She believes the wounded come first and are asked questions second. She believes a farm that keeps working is the best cover a rebellion can have. She believes she is where she is meant to be, and has never once asked to travel with the paladins.

## Origin

Donna's life is rooted firmly in the soil of Zilspar Farm 02, and where she came from before it is not recorded. Fifty-nine years, most of them apparently on that ground, tending the wounded and frightened while the oldest rebellion on the continent conducted its business through her kitchen.

> "She is the Flame that stays lit when the rest of the world is dark."

## The Patrol

An Iron Veil patrol of four soldiers and a Veil Hound came down on the farm in the night, harassing sleeping workers and dragging families out of their homes. The trio and three farm security fought and killed all of them. The farmers banded together immediately to erase the evidence, burning the bodies and dismantling the skiff, and by dawn Zilspar Farm 02 was again a farm. Donna had the guest house made up.

## Right now

Zilspar Farm has been burned to the ground with people boarded up inside, and the incendiaries were brought in after the fighting was over. Zero-One has been taken alive. There is no sign of Donna among the days-old dead in the tower field, and none of her anywhere else.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to keep the farm, and everyone on it, whole.

**Likes:** the farm's children · flour on her sleeves · Zero-One coming home · patients who eat · the guest house made up · the Flame.

**Dislikes:** Iron Veil patrols · prisoners who refuse food · leaving the farm · being asked to choose between the wounded.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** she keeps a prisoner in a hole under the ground and has made her peace with it.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown.

**Social — Contacts & Relations**

- **Zero-One (Fema Nolan)** — leaves the farm in her charge whenever she travels. Taken alive.
- **Sister Nora** — her prisoner for months; refused to eat. Executed after the trials in Sharn.
- **Grady Marsh** — nursed him back from drugs and a failed memory ritual. Missing.
- **Lorian** — tended him after the Vault Below and told the trio he was ready to fight. Alive.
- **Izaak** — Zero-One's son. Missing since Zilspar; Bantide Moro insists he is alive.
- **The Inevitables** — housed, healed and quartered them repeatedly. Alive.

**Organizations / Groups:** cleric of the Silver Flame; Fulcrum, resident at Zilspar Farm 02.

**Holdings:** Zilspar Farm 02's guest house, kitchen and infirmary · the concealed underground cell and its Private Sanctum · the farm itself, in Zero-One's absence — all destroyed.

**Items:** a wooden holy symbol of the Silver Flame on a leather harness · a silver pendant · an open leather-bound book, kept full of loose paper.

**The Private Sanctum:** Donna's contribution to the war, and one of the few reasons Fulcrum survived as long as it did. She can seal a room against divination, teleportation and scrying, and she used it on the concealed cell beneath the farm to hold a senior Black Dove cleric — Uriel's own suspected eyes and ears in Sharn — for months, entirely undetected, on a farm the Empire drove patrols past regularly.
