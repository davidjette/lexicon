**Species unrecorded · Head of Arcane Eye Security, Sharn Division · The Iron Veil · Executed**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

**Hillary Heinrick** was the head of Arcane Eye Security, Sharn Division, commanding the Empire's citywide surveillance network from within the Veil Building, and the wife of [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), Mayor of Sharn. She ran the ArcEye network for roughly a decade, archiving the city's streets into a restricted catalogue beneath the Sharn Library, and kept the original ArcEye Crystallizer prototype at her home. One recording her network made — the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born on the penthouse balcony — was carried out of her service by a retired surveillance wizard, extracted by the Inevitables and broadcast across the city, and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) died on that balcony the same night. In Sharn Ep 19 Hillary Heinrick was taken into custody, tried, found guilty and executed.

> "His wife, Hillary Heinrick, is equally formidable: a poised, calculating operator who commands the Arcane Eye surveillance network, Sharn division, from within the Veil building."\
> \
> — rebel Key Figures dossier, compiled before the rising

## Physical description

The dossier gives "poised, calculating" and no more. The Heinrick family is one of Sharn's most carefully curated public faces — polished, powerful, and quietly feared. <small>No physical particulars survive in the recovered files.</small>

## The Arcane Eye Security Network

Implemented roughly a decade ago as part of the Empire's expanded internal surveillance initiative, under the operational control of [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/), Sharn Division. **Head of Security: Hillary Heinrick.**

ArcEyes drift along streets, alleys and transit routes in continuous patrol cycles. They are cast, maintained and remotely operated by government employees stationed within the Veil Building — low-level wizards trained specifically in Arcane Eye protocols, capable of extended-duration scrying, coordinated patrol routing and multi-eye monitoring. Each one archives visual and auditory data; patrol footage is recorded with an **ArcEye Crystallizer** and transferred to the Sharn Library, catalogued in restricted archives accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorised Imperial officials. The same network doubles as Sharn's public information system, pushing state-approved messages onto every display screen in the city.

The standing rebel assessment was blunt: assume all major streets, public squares and transit hubs are under continuous observation.

## Public and private

Publicly she is a Sharn society fixture — the Mayor's wife, present at galas, at home with the family of the city's most photographed household. Privately she is a poised, calculating operator who commands the Arcane Eye surveillance network, Sharn division, from within the Veil Building, and her husband is the man who decides what the city is told. Between the two of them, one household held both the record of everything Sharn did and the story Sharn was told about it.

## Competence and circle

Her husband [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) was the Empire's Chief Diplomat, sole survivor of the purge of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/), and Mayor of Sharn. Their daughter, eight-year-old [Chelsea](/people/chelsea-heinrick/), is the lone soft edge in the household, often seen playing with [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) during official events. The anchor **Candice Kurt** maintained personal ties to the Heinricks and was regularly at their private gatherings. She reported, through the Veil, to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) at the top of the same building.

## The prototype

Rebel intelligence held that Hillary Heinrick kept **the original ArcEye Crystallizer prototype in her own home** — one of only three known locations where such a device could be obtained, alongside the ArcEye HQ archive stations and, as it turned out, the back room of a chip parlour owned by a serial murderer. <small>Without a Crystallizer there is no extraction of Grady Marsh's memory, no recording of Calcifer's birth, and no broadcast on the balcony. The Inevitables took theirs off Edwin Graves' corpse instead.</small>

## Conduct in role

She does not appear. Her instrument is a fleet of drifting eyes and a wall of screens, and in nineteen episodes of the Sharn arc she is never once in a room with the party. She is present through the checkpoints, the raids, and the citywide "coordinated terrorist attack" broadcast that put a silver dragonborn, a red-haired tiefling and a human man in short-shorts on every crystal screen in the city.

## Origin

<small>No record of her birth, family or rise survives in the recovered files.</small> What is documented is the post itself: head of the Arcane Eye Security Network's Sharn Division, a network implemented nearly a decade ago, whose archive under the Sharn Library catalogues what the city does, indexed and restricted. Her husband was the last surviving member of a murdered government, kept alive as Mayor. She worked in the same tower as the woman who had killed his twelve colleagues.

## The one recording she failed to keep

The memory that ended the Empire in Sharn came out of her own network. An ArcEye recorded the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born on the penthouse balcony — [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) raising a hand over the newborn in a sickly green glow, the vein-like lines forming on his cheek, the freeze when Esther screamed, and the glowing diamond at the base of Uriel's skull. A retired ArcEye surveillance wizard named Grady Marsh carried the memory of it. The Inevitables extracted it with a stolen Crystallizer and put it on every screen in Sharn using a pre-ArcEye Orien relay her network could not block.

Esther Crona died on that balcony that night.

## Trial and execution

Sharn Ep 19. With the Lord Commander dead, the Mayor dead and the Iron Veil's pact dismantled, Nest and Fulcrum operatives secured the Veil Building and took high-ranking officials into custody — **including Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka**. Trials began immediately. **Sister Nora, Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka were found guilty and executed**, along with other high-level Iron Veil officials.

<small>She was executed at the end of the Sharn arc, three years before the Korth arc opens.</small>

## Right now

She is dead, executed by a provisional government sitting in the penthouse above her old office. Her husband had been killed by the Lord Commander shortly before. Her network survived her: the ArcEyes still drift, the Crystallizer archive under the Sharn Library still holds a decade of restricted footage, and it is the rebellion's now. Her eight-year-old daughter appears in no record after the trials.

**Organizations / Groups:** [The Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/), Sharn Division — Head of Arcane Eye Security · the Heinrick household.

**Holdings:** The Arcane Eye Security Network, Sharn. The ArcEye operators' floors of the Veil Building. The restricted Crystallizer archive in the Sharn Library. A private residence holding the original prototype.

**Items:** The original **ArcEye Crystallizer prototype**, kept at her home. Never recovered by the party — they took a different one off a dead man in the Lower City.

**The Network:** roughly a decade of continuous archived surveillance of an entire city, indexed and restricted. It caught the Empress committing the crime that brought the Empire down in Sharn — and never held the recording, because the wizard on the eye did not file it.
