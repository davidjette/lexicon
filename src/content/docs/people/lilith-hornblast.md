**Warforged · 'Leader in exile' of the East Brelish Parliament · Imperial-aligned · Level Unknown · Alive at last record**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

Lilith Hornblast is a [Warforged](/lore/warforged/) and one of the 'leaders in exile' of the East Brelish Parliament, a sham government in exile of the Mournlands. She is **a severe and devoted representative of Warforged aligned with the Empire, though she sees an independent, Imperial aligned state as the prefered outcome for the Warforged**, and she pursues it from inside the Empire's institutions by negotiation. Her frame, plating, sigils and voice are not described in any source.

**The body she sits in.** The East Brelish Parliament is a government in exile for a country that is a dead land. The Mournland is where the Last War ended <small>(published Eberron canon)</small> and it has no living population to represent — only [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s Hundreds, entrenched in the Glass Plateau; the Cogborn and Warforged of Gorgonhorn on its border; and the E.D.E.N. constructs the Empire built to fight them. A parliament in exile from that has no constituency, no territory and no army.

**In public** Hornblast is a leader in exile of a parliament the record calls a sham. **In private** her position is genuinely held. She is asking for an independent, Imperially aligned state.

**Competence and circle.** Hornblast's circle is the Imperial political apparatus: parliaments in exile, assemblies, functions. She does not appear in any account of fighting, tunnelling or evacuating. Her Warforged counterparts are not colleagues but rivals for the same future — Locke Pierce, who has a nation, an army, an airship fleet and a doomsday device; and Preacher, who has a tunnel network and refused to leave Sharn at all. Her heirloom is **Unknown**; no item of hers is recorded.

She believes an independent, Imperially aligned state is the preferred outcome for the Warforged. Nothing else of what she believes is recorded.

**On the job** Hornblast is described in two words: *severe* and *devoted*.

**Origin.** **Unknown.** Where she was forged, where she served in the Last War, how she came to East Breland, and how she was seated in a parliament in exile are not recorded.

## The other Warforged futures

Hornblast's position sits alongside two others, because the Warforged of Khorvaire have been offered three and are living out the other two.

| Position | Held by | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| An independent, Imperially-aligned Warforged state | **Lilith Hornblast**, East Brelish Parliament | Recognition, from inside the Empire's institutions, by negotiation |
| An armed nation in the Mournland | **Locke Pierce**, the Hundreds, Mt. Silicon | Recognition, or the Ruby Nova and nobody at all |
| No state; stay and protect the people left behind | **Preacher**, R.U.S.T., beneath Sharn | Evacuation, survival, and no claim on any city |

Locke's answer arrived while Hornblast was still negotiating: he demonstrated a controlled recreation of the Mourning, took the skies over Sharn in exchange for feeding a starving city, and told the world that if the bells are rung he will end it first and only his kind will survive. What Lilith Hornblast makes of that is not recorded.
