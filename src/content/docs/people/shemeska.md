**Demon · Casino owner · [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/), Lady's Ward, [Sigil](/places/sigil/)**

Shemeska is a demon who owns [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/), a casino in the Lady's Ward of [Sigil](/places/sigil/). She is seeking the cards of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/), and she hired the fallen angel [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) to hunt down the party and find them. After the party woke in [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/) and fought gangsters in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), they were caught up in her schemes: she staked the ringer that [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) beat at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/), and from then on the party set out to find the cards while trying to outrun her and Jeremiah. In [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) the Grille brothers, drivers hired for the heist at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/), named her as their client.

## Fortune's Wheel

Fortune's Wheel is the casino Shemeska owns in the Lady's Ward. Visitors come through a portal network used by a troupe of clowns around the city, the Mouse Trappers, and are brought through the back halls to the casino floor and then to her suite. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The ringer at the Grindhouse

When [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/) entered Battlebeast in a prize fight at the Grindhouse, another party had entered a ringer, a malfunctioning iron golem controlled from the crowd, and placed a large bet on it. That party was Shemeska, who "would be very upset if this traced back to SG." <small>(DM notes)</small> Her fix was foiled when Battlebeast won. Sharegrave also knows that Shemeska will pay for any information about the tattooed strangers, and keeps it to himself. <small>(DM notes)</small>

After the fight, the owner of Fortune's Wheel was among those asking Sharegrave for Battlebeast to take more prize fights. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The tattooed strangers

News of the party's tattoos made Shemeska furious with [Pharod](/people/pharod/) and Jeremiah, and she reached out to get a closer look at the strangers: "Tell me your story". <small>(DM notes)</small>

She is seeking the Deck, and had Jeremiah search for its cards and kill to find them. Having heard a whisper that the party had come out of the Mortuary, she sent Jeremiah to get [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) to stop them, then changed her mind. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Her offer

What Shemeska tells the party about the cards: <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "…world changing artifact, sought after by many powerful beings over many centuries. I confess I would very much desire to own some my self, and in fact I am … in possession of two, and I know the owners of another three.
>
> All of us who own cards understand the grave danger they can pose and take extraordinary measures… If you ever become owners of one yourself, you will have to do the same."

> "…it means someone dangerous is pursuing them and thinks you can be of use to their effort. It's in your interest to find out why, and possibly find the cards before they do.."

> "I will help you find your murderer."

> "If you find a card for me, I will pay you ten thousand gold.\
> If you bring me two, I will give you One hundred thousand.\
> For three - any Wish I can grant you.

## The Blade of Woe

Sir Guy Standing lost a Ring of Jumping and the Blade of Woe to [Maverick](/people/maverick/). Maverick sold the Blade of Woe to Shemeska, and Guy Standing, furious, holds a blood vendetta against him. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Tiffany House and the Grille brothers

Shemeska sent Jeremiah with a robbery crew to stage a heist at Tiffany House as cover for stealing a card. <small>(DM notes)</small> Jeremiah and a goon, disguised as waiters, went after the RUIN card; Jeremiah fought [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/), and the party resisted him and stole the card. See [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).

The drivers of that crew, the Grille brothers, were taken hostage by the party. At [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/) [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) intimidated one of them into giving up his bosses, "proving that Jeremiah is in league with Shemeska." <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small> Bonefish Grille's account:

> "Please dont tell her I told you. But it was in fortune's wheel. Shemeska was the client, she wanted some kind of CARD\
> — Bonefish Grille <small>(DM notes)</small>
