**Species unrecorded · Head nurse and rebellion surgeon · Fulcrum · Alive — officially a suicide in custody**

*Also known as:* the Nightingale · the Condemned · Banteed Moro (as heard in court)

Bantide Moro is a head nurse of the Church of the Silver Flame and the surgeon of the rebel network Fulcrum, condemned at [Korth](/places/korth/) and officially recorded as a suicide in custody. For twenty years she ran one of the Silver Flame's civilian hospitals inside the Karrnathi Empire while working for Izaak under the codename the Nightingale, holding a cover post as an Imperial medical records keeper that let her move casualty paperwork to hide the rebellion's wounded; she performed the operation that fitted Izaak's prosthetic leg. After Izaak disappeared and Fulcrum was dismantled across the continent, she was tried on ten counts including high treason, prosecuted by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) wearing the face of her own elder sister, the Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), on evidence consisting of twenty years of letters home, inflated casualty reports and a written assessment that the Battle of Anduin was lost for want of supplies. The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital charges and granted the state one further month. She escaped by stopping her own heart in her cell, was pronounced dead and dropped down the prison's corpse chute, and is now hiding, disguised, in her dead sister's apartment on the lower plaza of Korth.

## Physical description

A clear family resemblance to her elder sister [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) — unmistakably the same blood, and not a double; the likeness is close enough that two people who knew Deirdre's face by heart recognised it across a courtroom. When the Inevitables found her she was starved, dehydrated and chained to a wall, and the exhaustion in her was days deep rather than an injury. She carries no visible mark, badge or heraldry of any kind: her whole professional life has been spent looking like an Imperial clerk. <small>Height, colouring and age are not on the record.</small>

## Origin

She was born to the Moro family and rebelled against it. She ran away to join what the Empire's own file calls a weird religion that was anti-war — the Silver Flame — and made a career of nursing inside an Empire that does not believe in the merciful kind. She wrote home for the whole of it. Ordinary letters: relationships, the move to Anduin for work, complaints about the haircuts, jokes about Imperial ritual and hierarchy and the whole apparatus of belonging, an admitted love of orcish music and dance. Every one of them was addressed to a beloved sister. Every one of them was eventually entered into evidence at her capital trial as proof of treasonous intent, alongside the inflated casualty reports and the written assessment that the Battle of Anduin was lost for want of supplies and that surrender was acceptable.

## Fulcrum, and the hospital

The Silver Flame keeps civilian hospitals inside the Karrnathi Empire, which is the sort of arrangement that survives only as long as nobody looks hard at it. Bantide ran one of them as head nurse. Beneath and alongside that work sat the rebellion's entire medical capability: not a field service but a standing one, supplying everything from surgery to revivification to people who could not be admitted anywhere, under a cover job that let her move casualty paperwork around to hide them. Fulcrum, the rebel network she served, has since been dismantled across the continent as though the Empire obtained a list of every name on it.

## The clerk and the surgeon

Officially, Bantide Moro is an **Imperial medical records keeper**: casualty reporting, inventory, staffing and logistics, latterly in the Anduin conflict area. It is a dull, respectable, entirely checkable post, and it is why the Empire's case against her is built out of paperwork.

Actually, she has **worked for Izaak for twenty years**. She is the rebellion's surgeon. She performed the operation that fitted Izaak's prosthetic leg. The two lives were the same job done twice — the woman who files the casualty report is the woman who decides which casualties get filed — and she ran it for two decades without being caught. She was caught in the end not by police work but by proximity: *"being that close to the Emperor is why your suspicion was laid on you."*

## The Nightingale

Her codename in the rebellion is **the Nightingale**. Twenty years of it, and the Empire prosecuting her for adultery, idolatry and a love of orcish music never once wrote the word down.

## The Condemned

The state charged her on ten counts: falsifying Imperial records, false claims of defeat of the Imperial army at Anduin, suspected sabotage and false orders to surrender, embezzlement of medical supplies and Imperial wartime resources, conspiracy to defraud official records, perjury, adultery, idolatry, high treason, and sodomy. Under [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) sabotage, espionage and embezzlement each carry death; falsifying records alone carries a term equivalent to twenty-five human years.

She stood in irons at the centre of eight judges, watched her own sister walk in to prosecute her, and threw herself on the mercy of the court — stating that regardless of the truth of the accusations, she was guilty of having failed the Emperor. Then the advocate stopped being an advocate and spoke as [kin](/lore/kin-and-patron/). The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims, granted the state one further month, and remanded her to the local garrison. She managed to thank her sister and apologise to her as she was removed.

## What broke her

Not the rescue. The truth. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) told her plainly, in front of the Imperial Red Guard, that he agreed her sister had betrayed her; that the woman in front of her was not Deirdre, because he had helped kill Deirdre himself; that he was the Chancellor of Sharn; and that the Emperor is not a man but a dragon. The Red Guard did not react to any of it. She asked for one thing — a holy symbol of the Silver Flame — and when [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) told her outright that she was not her sister, her face went from vindication to grief.

> "You want to win. More than anything, you have to win. That's why I know what happened out there was fake. The real Deirdre would never, ever fail."

## Conduct in the cell

Unfooled, and expensive to lie to. She read the courtroom rescue instantly as a ruse staged to make her trust and talk, refused food, refused to owe anybody anything, and identified the exact flaw in the performance: the real Deirdre had to win above everything else and would never simply have failed. She then told her sister's face to leave and never speak for her again. Everything the Inevitables got from her, they got by telling her the truth first.

> "Get out of here. Leave me alone. And don't speak up for me next time. I don't want to owe you anything."

## What she gave them

Safe in the apartment, she told them the rest. Fulcrum has not merely lost Zilspar Farm; it is being dismantled across the whole continent, people pulled out of every posting on invented charges, executions running for days, as though the Empire had obtained the full list of names. All of it began after **Izaak disappeared**. She refuses to believe he informed and insists he is still alive. When she learned that **Zero-One** had been taken outside Sharn by Eden units, she wept.

Then, too exhausted to travel and too wanted to be seen, she gave them the one thing she had left: an **Imperial listening post on the east side of the city, beside [the Hall of Submission](/places/the-hall-of-submission/)**, holding the records that would explain what happened to Izaak and to Zero-One.

She laughed when Eric told her the Emperor was a dragon, squinted at him, and said there might be a brighter morning after all. She has a forgery kit, twenty years of tradecraft, and was left behind in Korth carrying the fanfare of the dawn.

## Beliefs

She believes her sister Deirdre assembled the evidence that condemned her, and that Deirdre would have done it well.

She believes Izaak is alive and did not inform on anyone, and she says so without qualification while sitting in the ruins of his network.

She believes she is guilty of having failed the Emperor, whatever else she is or is not guilty of — and said so aloud to the panel before anyone spoke for her.

## Competence and circle

She keeps a **forgery kit** and knows how to use it. She recognises rebel cant on sight and knows better than to answer it in front of guards. Her circle is Izaak, whom she will not hear a word against, and the ghost of a sister she wrote to for twenty years. She now carries [Sir Dario Argentino's](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) personal prayer book of the Silver Flame — the only thing she asked anyone for, surrendered through the bars of her cell, and the only possession she owns that the Empire did not issue her.

## Right now

She is invisible, disguised, and hiding in her dead sister's apartment on the lower plaza of [Korth](/places/korth/), days away from full strength and too wanted to be seen. Officially she committed suicide in custody. The state has one month to reassemble a case against a condemned it believes is dead, and the only people who know otherwise have taken a boat to the Ivory Lazaret.

**Organizations / Groups:** Fulcrum (medical support, twenty years) · the Church of the Silver Flame (head nurse, civilian hospital) · the Imperial medical records service (cover post, Anduin theatre)

**Holdings:** None. Her hiding place belongs to the woman she is accused of writing to.

**Items:** A forgery kit, carried out of custody. · Sir Dario Argentino's personal prayer book of the Silver Flame, passed through the bars of her cell by [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and used as the beacon that found her body in the yard.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

Keeping the rebellion's wounded alive, and owing nothing to anyone.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Orcish music
- Orcish dance
- The Silver Flame
- Writing long letters home
- Izaak
- Being right about people
- Surgery
- Anti-war religion, the weirder the better

**Dislikes:**

- Imperial haircuts
- Imperial ritual
- Imperial hierarchy
- The whole apparatus of belonging
- Owing anybody anything
- Being handled
- Prison rations, which she refused
- Her own family, until she ran away from it
- Being lied to by people wearing faces she loves

**Vices & Personality flaws**

She would rather starve in irons than accept a favour she suspects, and she nearly did.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Deirdre Moro** — elder sister, Star Advocate of the Ministry of Law, and the recipient of twenty years of letters. Bantide believes Deirdre assembled the evidence against her. Dead, killed by the Inevitables before Bantide ever learned of it.
- **Izaak** — her handler and the reason for the last twenty years. She fitted his prosthetic leg. Missing; she insists he is alive.
- **Gemma Corso** — wore her sister's face, prosecuted her, saved her, and told her the truth last. Alive; the two are now allies.
- **Eric** — Chancellor of Sharn; wore Graham Cracker's face, and broke the impasse by confessing everything through the bars. Alive.
- **Sir Dario Argentino** — surrendered his personal prayer book to her and carried her body out of the yard. Alive.
- **Graham Cracker** — her sister's paralegal, well enough known to her that she recognised him in the cell and remarked that she thought he was gay. Dead.
- **Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin** — the officer who handed her sister a redacted brief against her as a loyalty test. Alive, hostile, and still expecting a conviction.
- **Zero-One** — a fellow name on the same list. Taken alive by Eden units; the news made her cry.

**Hobbies & Pets**

Orcish music and dance — entered into the record of a capital trial as evidence of treasonous intent.

**Feign Death:** Her one recorded casting, and the escape that took her out of Imperial custody. Having been left a holy symbol she could track and a hearing she was never going to survive, she stopped her own heart in the cell, was pronounced dead, bagged, dropped down the corpse chute behind the prison and stacked with the Empire's reusable dead — then sent one message out from inside the spell: *"Go to the corpse chute. I am not dead. Save me. One hour."* Medicine and Arcana both confirmed the body was hers and apparently dead. A *dispel magic* over Dario's shoulder ended it.
