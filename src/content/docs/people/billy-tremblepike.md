**Transportation Secretary of the metro Karnathi state · Lifelong bureaucrat · Level Unknown · Alive at last record**

*Also known as:* none recorded.

Billy Tremblepike is the Transportation Secretary of the metro Karnathi state, a lifelong bureaucrat and a hanger-on of the Karnathi court. He held a meaningless Deputy position until his four direct superiors died in a Rebel ambush, and was promoted by **sheer incompetency** into the Secretaryship. He was in the audience at the Grand Theatre in Sharn when the orchestra became demonic apparitions and much of the audience was killed, and was one of four men carried out alive. His appearance is not described in any source.

**The office.** Transportation Secretary of the metro Karnathi state is a serious portfolio in an Empire whose coherence is its rail network. It sits directly under the Interoperability Summit's remit — the standards fight over rail gauge, over what runs where, over which cities remain connected. It also sits over the lightning rail lines running east out of Sharn toward the capital: the same lines a Fulcrum crew boarded to take a bell, and the same network House Orien is adding to every day. It is a job that decides where soldiers and freight can go.

**In public** Tremblepike is the Secretary: a man with a portfolio, invited to the theatre and the summit, seated among the leadership of the Empire's largest city. **In private** he is a hanger-on who was moved up because he was harmless, and everyone in the Karnathi court knows it. **Sheer incompetency** is the record's own word, and the promotion was not despite it.

**The four superiors.** Tremblepike held a meaningless Deputy position. Four people stood between him and the Secretaryship. All four died in a rebel ambush — an event the record calls **mysterious** and does not explain — and a man of no ability inherited control of Karnathi transportation. Whether the ambush was rebel work, Imperial housecleaning, or something arranged around Tremblepike without his knowledge is **Unknown**.

He believes proximity to the Karnathi court is a career. He believes the four deaths were an ambush and nothing more, or has decided to. Nothing else of what Billy Tremblepike believes is recorded.

**On the job** Tremblepike attends. He is on the invitation lists, in the boxes, at the summit functions, and there is no record of him deciding anything.

**Origin.** A lifetime in the Karnathi bureaucracy and a lifetime hanging on at the Karnathi court. Nothing earlier is recorded: no birthplace, no family, no species is stated for him in any source.

## The Grand Theatre

Tremblepike was in the audience at the Grand Theatre in Sharn when the conductor Krespo turned on the house and the orchestra became dozens of demonic apparitions. Much of the audience was killed. He was not: **the party escaped with Heighrick, Goldenhew, Tremblepike and Sominyard.** Four Crimson Sun agents pulled him out of a massacre while running a mole hunt against two of the other three men they were carrying. See [City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/).
