**Half-dragon · Son of Uriel Qualanthri and the Crimson Sun · Born in the Sept of Bones · Last seen in the fire that destroyed the Ivory Lazaret ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

**The Heir of Vol** was the son of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), who was the dragon [Viagra](/people/viagra/), the Crimson Sun. He was the child the Qualanthri had bred toward: a half-dragon of the line of [Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) who was to carry the Mark of Death and inherit the Empire. He was born in the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/) in [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/).

## The plan

[E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) meant to breed his own line with the Crimson Sun and get a half-dragon with the Mark of Death who would also inherit the throne. Uriel bore the Emperor daughter after daughter, and every one was counted a failure. The gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/), who heard the plan discussed for years, said what the son would be: *"A half-dragon, half-elf with the Mark of Death would mean the end of all things."* He had fought in the war that destroyed the first such child, thousands of years ago.

E'noch gathered the purest elves of his own house and the other houses of Vol into the Sisters of the Blood, to be brides of the Dragon Emperor and of this heir after him. Uriel was the first of them. She had a plan of her own for the child, which went beyond E'noch's. She meant to supplant her son: to take his power into herself and live joined to him for ever as an undead mother, with the unborn child as her phylactery. E'noch was angry at it and was ready to abandon her if he could have the heir without her.

[Kitsune](/people/kitsune/), Gemma Corso's patron, wanted the child for himself and made Gemma swear to bring it to him unharmed.

## In the womb

Uriel carried him long past his time. In the Emperor's chamber, in labour, she insisted that he was not ready. Over Nos Matre, with her belly glowing through her robes, she told Eric that she kept her son warm. When Gemma fired a wand into her belly she cried out to the child that he could not leave her behind.

The Emperor was destroyed by fire in his bed in [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/), while Uriel was in labour beside him. The unborn child was already his heir, and from that moment the claim to the Empire was his.

Uriel's magic was bound to the child. If he were killed or taken from her while she carried him, it would leave her.

## The Sept of Bones

Uriel was beheaded in the forecourt of Nos Matre and sank through the stone. She lost the child on the way down, and in the Sept, among the dead of two thousand years, he took life as an undead dragon.

When Gemma and Eric broke open the doors of the Sept, the child was outside his mother. He lay in a womb that had split and come away from her, joined to her by a cord: a skeletal figure with a dragon's wings, wet and pulsing, already the size of a house and growing as they watched.

> He's sleeping. He's perfect, isn't he? He has his father's teeth.
> — Uriel

He could not move by himself. Uriel towed him on the cord. While the cord joined them he was a part of her. He could be hurt and killed like any other creature, and his death would have taken her magic with it. He breathed black fire, which melted her own bone guards, and he bit and clawed at whatever came near. When he was hurt she put herself in the way, and when she was hurt she drew his strength back into herself: *"Forgive me, baby. Mother needs you back inside her again."*

She had kept him in so that she could have his destiny for herself. Near the end she said so: *"My grandfather was right. I am a vessel. My being is for him."*

[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) came to the Sept to kill Uriel. Uriel told her that the two of them were to have been mothers of the same godchild, and then bled her over the pit. The Heir opened his mouth like a chick, ate what he could pull out of her, and swallowed the rest of her whole.

## Cut free

Lalo Kitsune, the fox Gemma summoned, cut the cord and claimed the child for his master. Uriel's magic left her at once.

> What have you done? He isn't mine anymore. He isn't anyone's.
> — Uriel

Free of her, he grew faster. He took the shape of a thing half human and half dragon, with three hearts beating in his chest, and drew bones and his mother's remains into himself. The fox looked at him and changed his mind. Gemma cut him with the Bright Dawn, the fox stabbed him, and Eric burned him with sunlight. Every wound closed harder and thicker than before and left him stronger. The army of bones in the Sept fell down, because he was drinking the power that had held it up.

Nothing the party had could kill him. Once he was cut from his mother, every wound he took made him larger and stronger than he had been before it. If he was brought down he stood up again, and he grew with every moment he was free. That was why he had been wanted, and why the war against the first of his kind cost what it did. It was also why [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) had held that the Empress must be killed before the child came.

## The fire

The party fled. The Heir pushed up through the hill under the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), enormous, feeding on the power of [the Polygon](/items/the-polygon/). A ship of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) dropped a glowing red gem into the storm over the valley. It was a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/), the weapon Locke Pierce had built against the Empress and her bells, and it was his answer to the Heir.

## Fate

When the fire cleared, the Lazaret and the Heir were gone. He was not seen again.
