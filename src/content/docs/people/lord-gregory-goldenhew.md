**Human · General Secretary of the Imperial Trade Federation · Member of the Imperial Assembly · Level Unknown · Dead by the Sharn arc**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

**Lord Gregory Goldenhew** is a human member of the Imperial Assembly and the General Secretary of the **Imperial Trade Federation**, the body through which the Empire's productive capacity is allocated. He commanded logistics for the Empire in the Last War and was a businessman his whole life afterwards, harnessing the Empire's productive resources on behalf of the Imperial war machine and his own fortunes, and he maintains supreme power over Imperial industrial policy. When top secret Assembly information that the Crimson Sun had deliberately falsified, concerning the Interoperability Summit at Sharn, was leaked to rebel sources, Goldenhew was one of the three Assembly members attending that summit and one of three suspects; he has never been publicly cleared. He appears in no episode of the Sharn or Korth arcs.

No source describes him physically. What every source describes instead is his temperament, and it is unanimous: **he is considered a mean and vindictive man.**

**The office.** The **Imperial Trade Federation** is where the Empire's productive capacity is allocated. It has a signal corps of its own — the Warforged rogue Fournier served in it, casting *sending* at will — and it sends representatives to the standards summits where the physical form of Imperial infrastructure is decided.

**In public** Goldenhew is a businessman and a public servant: a lifelong commercial man, now the Assembly's authority over industry. **In private** the two careers were never separate. **A businessman his whole life after commanding logistics for the Empire in the war, Goldenhew has harnessed the vast productive resources on behalf of the Imperial war machine and his own fortunes.** **He maintains supreme power over Imperial industrial policy.**

**Competence and circle.** Goldenhew commanded logistics for the Empire in the war, and became a businessman after it. His circle is the Imperial Assembly — Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), Lord Albert Spear — and the Dragonmarked houses whose output he directs, chiefly House Orien under [Kwanti d'Orien](/people/kwanti-d-orien/). No heirloom of his is recorded.

**The suspicion.** Top secret information available only to the Imperial Assembly was leaked to rebel sources — information the Crimson Sun had deliberately falsified, concerning the Interoperability Summit at Sharn. Three Assembly members were attending that summit, and Goldenhew was one of them. **Hayman Maw** sent four of the Empire's most effective agents to establish which of the three was leaking, and to render, interrogate and neutralise them before the summit closed. Goldenhew has never been publicly cleared.

**Beliefs.** What Goldenhew believes is not recorded.

**On the job** Goldenhew holds supreme power over Imperial industrial policy, and he attends the summits that set standards.

**Origin.** A businessman his whole life, and before that the Empire's logistics command in the Last War. Nothing earlier is recorded: no birthplace, no family, no house.

## The Grand Theatre

Goldenhew was in the audience at the Grand Theatre in Sharn on the night the conductor Krespo turned the orchestra into demonic apparitions and slaughtered much of the house. He got out alive, and he got out with the four Imperial agents who were there to investigate him: **the party escaped with Heighrick, Goldenhew, Tremblepike and Sominyard.** He owed [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) his life, and the Unforeseen were under orders to neutralise him if the evidence pointed his way. See [City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/).

**Right now** Lord Gregory Goldenhew is dead. He held supreme power over Imperial industrial policy in Campaign 1, appears in no episode of the Sharn or Korth arcs, and had died by the time of the Sharn arc, as had Lord Albert Spear, Cob Cornwell, John C. LeBeefe and Kwanti d'Orien. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-15)</small>
