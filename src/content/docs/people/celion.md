> I want to meet you. This way is so limiting in what I can tell you. Meet me in Paenis, where this all began. Sit in our corner booth. Ill be there at dawn. Youll see me, Ill be in silver.

<small>— Celion, on his Ally Card; the same words appear in Arc III, Parts IX–XI. Sic on "Ill" and "Youll", which are the card's own spelling.</small>

**Human wizard · The Arcaneum · Lieutenant to Archmage Valen · Killed by the party, then a clone; ally at the Egg**

*Also known as:* Celion the Great · Celion the Broken Cocked <small>(the Heroes' Hill rhyme)</small>

Celion the Great is a human wizard of the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/), lieutenant to Archmage **Valen**, and [Kara](/people/kara/)'s ex-boyfriend from her time at the Arcaneum. The party killed him; he persisted as a clone suspended in liquid in Valen's sanctum, and from there reached Kara in a dream and then through a mirror in Paenis, warning her against the activation of [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) and naming the reason [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) was killed. While he was reforming, [Drefan](/people/drefan/) cast Dream on him, which deformed his body and left him tank-bound in that loop. He helped the party from the tanks on the tenth level of the Arcaneum. He appears as an ally at the Egg. In another loop, the setting of Dave's original Punis game, he lies in the Arcaneum in the tomb of "Celion the Great".

## The original Punis game

The original Punis game was a one-shot Dave ran, set in another loop. Its party were young adults: **Zanzibar the Younger**, a child of the main-loop [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) in a loop that is never explained; a **Kara** from a different loop, or a clone, unaware of the wider campaign; **Akira the Red**; and **Maximillien the Younger**. They broke into the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/) and found the tomb of Celion the Great.

> the "original Punis game" was a one shot I ran where Zanzibar the Younger (child of the Zanzibar main PC in some loop unexplained) and Kara (a different Kara loop/clone unaware of the wider campaign) and Akira the Red (and Maximillien the Younger) are young adults, and find themselves breaking in to the Arcaneum where they see the tomb of "Celion the Great" and they withdraw his "weiner bone".

<small>— Dave (sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small>

In 2019 Dave referred back to that game when the party settled with Celion after Valen's fall: "Those of us from the original Punis game know that promise erodes after his death and canonization". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

## Kara's ex-boyfriend

About a year after the one-shot, Dave introduced Celion into the main plot loop as [Kara](/people/kara/)'s ex-boyfriend.

## How the party encountered him

He reaches Kara first in a dream at the Shadowhigh Inn, lying "on a bed of canvas bags of grain and goose down, dressed in white linen, his shirt open and his arm, chest, neck and stomach all tattoos with intricate magic runes and lettering." He asks her to meet him in Paenis, in their corner booth. When she goes, there is a mirror hanging over the booth, and "a closer look shows Celion sitting in the mirror as if he were sitting in the booth." He takes out a [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). <small>(Arc III, Parts IX–XI, "Arcaneum.")</small>

He apologises for their history in the same breath as he warns her: "I should never have said anything to the Headmaster about your dragon heritage. I was a teacher's pet and a suck up and a bastard and I'm sorry."

## What he was speaking from

The party had already killed him — "You had blown me up" — and he is speaking from a clone. They had traded the Amulet of Kai Ryssdahl for information on how to break into Castle Danzig, "which you used to assassinate Valen and Celion and almost King Danzig himself". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-08-11)</small> Valen had the college's leaders' clones moved out of the Necromancy wing and into his own sanctum, "along with his clone and his second in command, suspended in liquid." Celion has "some control" of his powers, "but if he finds out I know the truth and that I am not on board, he will destroy me."

Valen's own account of what happened to him afterwards: "He is clever, and he kept his sojourns into your mind and your bed secret from me, until one night he had a nightmare. 'Help!' he yelled, 'Help me Kara, I'm drowning! Don't leave me!' Too bad, too. Interrupted his final regrowth, and you can see what it did to him." Celion's head then has "a noticeable balloon at his right temple, his jaw is malformed, and his right arm and shoulder are twisted and hunched. He twitches slightly in unconscious pain."

## The clone and Drefan's Dream

In the plot in which the party killed him, Celion was brought back as a clone. Valen and Kara had clones as well, and Celion made Kara's "out of longing". While he was reforming, Drefan cast Dream on him. Dave:

> Drefan casts Dream on him while he is reforming, deforming his body and making him tank-bound in that loop forever.

<small>— Dave (sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small>

## In the tanks on the tenth level

Celion helped the party from inside the Arcaneum, in the tanks on the 10th level of the college. [Valen](/people/valen/), also in a tank until the final battle, had the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) there, and Kara's clone. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small>

## The testimony

Celion states that St. Cloud gave the Anachron to the Arcaneum deliberately because he could not turn it on himself, and that the Arcaneum's own attempts to activate it had failed for years. He warns: "You have to stop them from activating the Anachron. They can useiti to break time and open a wound in the world so great that nothing will survive." <small>(Sic on "useiti.")</small>

He also supplies the reason Zanzibar was targeted: "Zanzibar sought to subvert the War Wizard's efforts to contain the Infanta, so naturally he had to die." <small>(Celion to Kara, quoted on Zanzibar's own Ally Card.)</small>

## How he became an ally

Not recorded. He was Valen's lieutenant, then the party's enemy, then their informant against Valen from inside a vat — "I did everything I could to surveil and subvert him and those I suspected of collaborating with St. Cloud" — and he is on a card at the end.

## After Valen

In the fight against Valen at the Arcaneum Celion "landed a couple late hits once you exahausted all of Valens power, don’t get it twisted", and for his work he inherited the Arcaneum. Dave on the settlement the party reached with him: "he’ll return the palantir to the king and provide appropriate access and coordination with the civilian govt", and "Those of us from the original Punis game know that promise erodes after his death and canonization but Zan is right that he is a humbler more deferent archmage". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

Before [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/), Dave: "He was conducting weapons research for this very moment". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

Celion gave [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) a ring. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-30)</small> Dave on Celion's build: "He’s basically Zanzibar with high level spells", a War Wizard, "it’s a whole subclass". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small>
