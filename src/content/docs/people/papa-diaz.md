**Priest · Brother · Saltmarsh**

**Also known as:** Brother "Burnface"

**Papa Diaz**, known as **Brother "Burnface"**, is a priest of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) who holds back the Drowned, the undead rising from the desecrated temple of Neptune under the sea. [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) followed him to the shore, where he performed a rite to turn back the Drowned. His words at [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/), in Latin and in English, call on his brothers to dive for the corpse of the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/).

## Physical description

He bears burns from radiant damage, and keeps fish in his pockets. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Against the Drowned

Papa Diaz "Stopped the zombies every night." He is "Drowning himself asleep every night." <small>(DM notes)</small> BattleBeast and Lenore followed him to the shore, where he performed a rite to turn back the **Drowned**, undead coming from the desecrated temple under the water. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## At the Blackfish

With the ship taking on water, Papa Diaz refuses to take a rope to climb up, and points at the ocean, exclaiming: <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "Omnes vos! Fratres!\
> in fluctus ruere oportet,\
> ut corpus Aquei, seraphi submersi, eruatis.\
> Iacet ille sub mari in sepulcrō\
> Dominae Lacrimarum, violatus atque profanatus.\
> Nefarium quid emissum\
> est ex hoc scelere:\
> turbate, obsecro, ne mundus hominum\
> a putrido cadavere devoretur..."
>
> TUTTAVETTI!\
> TUTTAVETTI TUTTI!

<small>(Papa Diaz, DM notes)</small>

In English:

> "All of you! Brothers! We must hurl ourselves\
> into the waves, to recover\
> the corpse of Aqueus, a drowned seraph,\
> He lies beneath the sea, upon the\
> grave of the Lady of Tears, violated and profaned.
>
> From this crime\
> something vile has been unleashed\
> disrupt it, I beg you, lest the world of men\
> be consumed by the rotting dead..!
>
> DIVE!

<small>(Papa Diaz, DM notes)</small>
