**Human · Paladin of the Silver Flame · Leader of Fulcrum · Taken alive**

<small>Also known as: Fema Nolan · Zero-One · Zero‑One</small>

**Zero-One**, born **Fema Nolan**, is a human Paladin of the Silver Flame and the founder and current leader of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/). Born and raised in Thrane, she was a healer and a respected paladin before she helped found Fulcrum alongside [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) and, concealed from every record, [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/). After Stamos was killed she undertook an ancient Silver Flame rite that strips away a name and a past, emerging as Zero-One, the first woman in centuries to do so. She led Fulcrum from the Zilspar farmsteads outside Sharn with her eldest son [Izaak](/people/izaak/) and a cell of loyal guards, resurrected [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) in a rebel base beyond the walls and embedded a diamond in her skull, and was taken alive by Eden units when Zilspar Farm was burned.

Fema Nolan is one of the founding members of the rebel group known as Fulcrum. Born and raised in Thrane, she was once a respected Paladin of the Silver Flame, known for her compassion, discipline, and fierce devotion to protecting the innocent. She is the mother of half-siblings Izaak and Zero of Sharn.

After the tragic death of her companion and Fulcrum founder John Stamos, she undertook an ancient and nearly forgotten Silver Flame rite that strips away one's name and past, emerging as Zero-One, the first woman in centuries to undergo the transformation. Today, Zero-One serves as the current leader of Fulcrum, operating primarily out of the rebel stronghold in Zilspar alongside her eldest son, Izaak, and a cell of loyal guards. Though she maintains a presence in Zilspar, Zero-One is known to vanish for long stretches on missions of her own, traveling across Khorvaire with a select group of personal knights. Her rare appearances, unwavering resolve, and mythic reputation make her one of the most enigmatic and revered figures in the rebellion.

**Status:** Alive. <small>Her page predates Zilspar; the Korth record has her taken alive by Eden units.</small>

## Physical description

In her portrait on the Rebel Factions page she is standing in a wheat field at the end of the day, in a plain cream linen dress with wide sleeves, and she is not armed. Black hair, unbound, falls in waves past her waist. Dark eyes, weathered olive skin, deep lines at the mouth. The paladin who travels Khorvaire with a personal guard of knights is not in the portrait.

## Fulcrum

[Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) is one of the oldest rebel groups in existence, its legacy stretching back beyond the current era of Imperial dominance. Based in the Breland farmsteads outside Sharn with its stronghold centred in Zilspar, it grew from farmers, rangers and dissidents who resisted Imperial expansion into the countryside; its name reflects its role as a pivotal axis of resistance, a balancing force between rural defiance and the Empire's urban dominance. Zero-One is its current leader and one of its four founders, alongside [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) and, concealed from every record, [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/).

## Origin

Born and raised in Thrane, Fema Nolan was once a respected Paladin of the Silver Flame, known for her compassion, discipline and fierce devotion to protecting the innocent, and she was a healer before she was anything else. She helped found Fulcrum with an actor, a furniture trader and a chef who was never named. She lost Stamos to an Imperial ambush on a lightning rail train and stripped away her own name in response. Since then she has led the oldest rebellion on the continent from a farm, run Silver Flame paladins as a standing force, opened her house to trafficking survivors, and gone out repeatedly on errands she has never explained to anyone — including, once, into the sewers of Sharn.

**The rite:** after the death of her companion and fellow founder John Stamos, Fema Nolan undertook an ancient and nearly forgotten Silver Flame rite that strips away one's name and past. She emerged as Zero-One — the first woman in centuries to undergo the transformation, and the origin of a tradition her own daughter would later enter.

## Public and private

To the farms of Zilspar she is the woman who comes back. To the wider rebellion she is a myth: her rare appearances, unwavering resolve and mythic reputation make her one of the most enigmatic and revered figures in the resistance, and she is known to vanish for long stretches on missions of her own. Years ago, in the sewers beneath Sharn, [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) was killed by a mind flayer. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) carried the body out to a rebel base beyond the walls, and Fema Nolan resurrected her — and embedded a diamond in her skull. The Empress of the Empire the rebellion is fighting is alive because Fulcrum's leader brought her back.

She admitted it herself, to the rebellion, in the Fulcrum room at Zilspar Farm 02, in the same session in which the Arcane Eye recording caught that same diamond glowing at the base of Uriel's skull while she stood over a newborn.

## Competence and circle

Her circle is her order and her blood. She keeps a cell of loyal guards at Zilspar and travels with a select group of personal knights. Her eldest son [Izaak](/people/izaak/) runs Fulcrum's line into Sharn; her daughter [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) took the same rite of name-stripping a generation later and serves the Flame in the city. She leaves the farm's spiritual and practical wellbeing to [Donna](/people/donna/) whenever she departs, and trusts her implicitly.

## In command

In command she is spare. She calls a meeting in a sealed room, brings the people who need to be there through a teleportation circle, and lets the person with the confession make it. She took [Lorian](/people/lorian/) — trafficked, drugged, half-destroyed and full of vengeance — and guided him toward the Flame rather than away from his anger, and he came out of it a paladin.

## The Confession Room

She was alone in the Zilspar safe-house room when [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) were brought in, her paladins on the door, and she waited while Jessica, Digma and Richard Blaze came through the circle. She let Richard speak, supported and clarified where it was needed, and did not argue when two of the three refused to believe that Esther Crona could be saved.

## The Diamond

After the party watched Grady Marsh's recovered memory — Uriel raising a hand over a newborn in a sickly green glow, the vein-like lines forming, the freeze at Esther's scream, and the glowing diamond at the base of the Empress's skull as she turned away — Zero-One told the room that the diamond was hers. She had put it there.

## The Council of Sharn

After Esther Crona fell, Zero-One and her Silver Flame paladins took over the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up its clerics. In Nico's notes, Digma Beeve told her and Jessica Grimpledink that Richard Blaze and Calcifer were safe at the farm. At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn she led the Silver Flame delegation with two paladin guards, beside [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and [Donna](/people/donna/), and the Flame asked for the hospital as a healing centre, the Black Doves executed and outlawed, a Flame-led cleansing of the Zeir residence and the Black Veil crash site, citywide cremation, sanctuary for refugees and orphans, and a permanent council seat. Blair's demand for a ban on Silver Flame patrols in the Underhive was one Nico noted Zero-One would oppose. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 19)</small>

> She guided him toward the Silver Flame, urging him to let purpose, not hatred, shape his path.

## Right now

Zilspar Farm has been burned to the ground with people boarded up inside, and Zero-One has been taken alive by Eden units. Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent as though the Empire had obtained a list of every name, and the executions ran for days. [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/)'s standing instruction is that if they cannot save her, they must not let her be taken.

## Beliefs

She believes purpose and not hatred should shape a survivor's path, and says so to every broken person brought to her farm. She believes the Silver Flame is a discipline rather than a comfort. She believes that what she did in that sewer was right, and has never asked anyone to agree with her.

**Organizations / Groups:** founder and current leader of Fulcrum; Paladin of the Silver Flame; commander of a personal guard of knights.

**Holdings:** Zilspar Farm 02 and the Zilspar farmsteads, with their teleportation circle, private Fulcrum room and Private Sanctum cell — all destroyed.

**Items:** none recorded by name. The diamond she embedded is in Uriel Qualanthri's skull.

**The Rite of the Nameless:** Zero-One's own working. It strips away a name and a past and leaves the Flame in their place. She is the first woman in centuries to have survived it, and by doing so she reopened it: the codename Zero has since become a rite of passage among certain women of the faith, and her daughter carries it in Sharn while an elderly Tabaxi carries it in Leef.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to protect the innocent, and to finish the thing she started before the Empire finishes her.

**Likes:** the Zilspar wheat at evening · Donna · her children · survivors who choose purpose · plain linen · a room that seals · the old rites nobody else will perform.

**Dislikes:** being revered · questions about the sewer · her own name.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** she made an irreversible decision alone in a sewer and has carried it alone ever since.
