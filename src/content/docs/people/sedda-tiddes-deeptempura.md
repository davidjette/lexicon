**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Princess and heir of the Deeptempura · Undead, then laid to rest · Dead**

*Also known as:* Sedda Deeptempura - Sedda Tiddes - the Ringer

Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura was the daughter and heir of [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), last king of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/), and the leader with him of the uprising in the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/). Caught breaking protocol and stealing supplies, she was punished with a waste detail in the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/), where [the Speaker](/species/the-bloom/) — calling itself the Mayor — spoke to her for months and talked her into ringing the Thirteenth Casting with an improvised hammer. Everyone in the foundry died and rose again; Sedda rose lucid, and kept the rebellion's supply cache tidy for three years waiting for an extraction that never came. The Inevitables found her, took her confession, and she gave them her father's horn and hammer before asking to be ended. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) gave her the rites of the Silver Flame and took her head, and she was consecrated by her full name and title in the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/).

## Physical description

When the **Inevitables** found her she had been three years dead in acid air, and the decay had taken most of her, but not the parts that mattered: both eyes were still there, and most of the jaw. She moves like a woman doing inventory, not like a corpse - checking boxes, looking into barrels, moving things back and forth in a cache she stocked while she was alive. She speaks clearly, at conversational volume, in the register of somebody who has been expecting visitors for a long time.

## The hold she was born to

Sedda was the daughter and heir of King Corn, last king of the Deeptempura. The House of Kaius bought the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) about a decade ago; her inheritance came with an Imperial overseer, a slave register in the [Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/)'s cabinet, and a work duty.

## Public and private

On paper she was labour. By her own account she was caught *"breaking protocol with my meetings"* and stealing provisions and equipment, and her duty was changed as punishment: carting waste down into the Glitterhame, which had been off limits since the fungal bloom began.

In truth the meetings were an uprising and the stolen provisions were its supply line. She and her father passed encoded messages under the eyes of their own overseers and secretly forged a handheld horn intended to blunt the bells. The punishment detail put her, alone and daily, in the one cavern in the mountain where the Speaker could reach her.

## The Speaker

Down in the bloom she heard a voice that gave her tremendous ideas and appeared to know the movements of every Imperial guard and administrator in the complex. It called itself the Mayor. It spoke to her for months, until she could hear it all the time. She told it about her father's horn and what he thought it might do. *"It encouraged me."*

The plan it gave her was simple and it was not her father's: ring the bell, and the ringing would kill the Sisters and the Imperials and leave her people safe. Afterwards, when the Lord Mayor took the throne, the foundry would be hers.

## Beliefs

She believed that ringing the bell would kill the Doves and the Imperials and leave her people safe.

She believed she would be given the foundry when the Lord Mayor took the throne.

She believed, for three years after her own death, that someone was coming to collect her, and she kept the supply cache tidy against their arrival.

## The night she rang it

The resistance was discovered. Sedda was taken below - but not before she reached the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/). She rang the Thirteenth Casting with an **improvised hammer**: a rock, not the proper one. Then she went straight down the shaft as arranged, and waited.

Everyone in the foundry died. The enslaved dwarves and the workers came back up as the dead. She came back too - lucid, which the Black Dove undead upstairs are not; the party's reading is that she was carrying the bloom's spores when she died. Sister Calvane's suite was sealed and she stayed in it. [Overseer Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/) kept his shift. The Trust filed it as an accident, and its tally says roughly 97% of the dwarvish workforce died in it.

The whole sequence was later shown to [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) as a vision, at the moment his own resistance to the same voice broke: Sedda, spore-infected, hearing exactly what he hears, being urged to ring the bell, and doing it.

## The extraction team

Three years later she looked up from her barrels at three strangers in Imperial disguise and said the thing she had been rehearsing:

> Are you here? Are you finally here? It did not work as the Lord Mayor described. It was horrible. Much worse than he said.

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) took the con and ran it - the Lord Mayor's extraction team, come at last - and Sedda gave her everything: the meetings, the punishment detail, the Speaker, the months of whispering, the plan, and the confession.

> Who rang the bell? - I did. I rang it and came straight down here like the plan was. And I'm still here.

It came apart when Eric said out loud that the man was dead. She had already looked at him and felt the thing between them - the same voice in two heads - and she worked out the rest by herself, in front of everyone.

> Oh God, I'm undead. This is horrible. My people - they've been destroyed. You have to stop this from happening everywhere.

## What she gave them

Dying a second time, she handed over the two keys to the entire bell problem:

- **The horn.** *"My father's horn. It is in his study. There's a secret panel below the rug in the centre of the room."* Play the fanfare of dawn on a horn built to Corn's design, she said, and any bard with enough talent can drown out the bells.
- **The hammer.** *"My father's hammer can crack anything made of black Kyber. If you find that, you could also destroy the bells."* The kin later gave it its name: **KNELL**.

Then she asked for the only thing she wanted. *"But I must ask, if you favour - end this suffering. I am less than myself."* Sir Dario Argentino gave her the rites of the Silver Flame - *"I commit you to the hands of your maker. And I absolve you of all the sins for which you are truly repentant. May the flame have mercy on us all"* - and took her head.

## Consecration

Dario carried her remains three hundred feet up the chain ladder and through the middle of the camp in the Great Hall of Rak'tafur, so that the whole clan came out to see it. He laid her on the altar of the shrine they had cleansed that morning, beside an unknown soldier, and began the prayer in Dwarvish; [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) crossed the floor and finished it with him. They consecrated her by her full name and her full title: **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, princess and heir**.

It repaired everything. [Madame Tso Teryaki](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/) returned the Belt of Dwarvenkind she had confiscated from grave robbers and blessed the party's claim to the folio, the horn, and all they had carried out.

## Right now

Sedda is at rest in the shrine, second on a death warrant signed KAIUS III. Her father is still on the hoard. The voice that talked her into it is still talking, and it is talking to Eric now.
