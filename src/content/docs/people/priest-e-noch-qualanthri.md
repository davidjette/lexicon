**High Elf · Wizard, necromancer and vampire · House Qualanthri · Destroyed in his coffin under the Ivory Lazaret, Korth Ep 20**

**Also known as:** Enoch · Priest E'noch · *the Stranger*, at the court of Karrnath · *the mysterious stranger* of the Imperial record · an elvish shaman called E'noch, in the King of Galifar's records · [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s brother, by Varyn's own last words.

**Priest E'noch Qualanthri** was a high elf wizard and necromancer of House Qualanthri, and the architect of much of what happened on Khorvaire over several centuries. With Varyn Crona he offered the King of Galifar a cure for his dying heir, and used the child as a vessel for the essence of the dragon [Viagra](/people/viagra/), called the Crimson Sun. He worked the forbidden [Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) rites of the Eternal Guardian at the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), took the Rite of Cain and killed his sire, and made the child, grown into [Kaius](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), a true vampire on his own blood. His purpose was to breed his own bloodline with the Crimson Sun and produce a half-dragon bearing the Mark of Death who was also Heir to the Empire. He is the grandfather of Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) destroyed him in his coffin with the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/) in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/).

## Physical description

No likeness of him survived in the Imperial record. When the party found him he was a slight robed figure with red eyes who turned his back on them. Beaten in that form, he rose from the blood on the floor as a long-eared, bat-winged creature with skin nearly ebony. In his coffin he lay with his arms crossed on his chest, very well preserved.

## The goal

Qualanthri, Crona and Duin are cadet houses of the [House of Vol](/organizations/house-vol/), and their elders had kept a mortal secret for millennia: the essence of the dragon who fathered Erandis Vol's line, reduced to a tiny fraction in an artifact after the Purge, held against the day Erandis could be brought back and the Mark of Death could come out of the shadows. In E'noch's generation the plan was put into effect, and he was its author. He meant to raise the dragon and to mate it with the House of Vol again, and with his own line in particular.

His granddaughter Saturnia describes the dream as her people hold it:

> The dream of many in my house and other houses like it are to all go back to this distant past where our kind mated with a dragon. And even though I thought it was a fantasy, it seems my grandfather has made it come true.

The dragon [Aurorix](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/), held in his lair for years, heard him talk of little else: *"The plot to breed with the Dragon Emperor is well known to me. E'noch is obsessed with it. That's all he talks about."* The child he wanted would carry the blood of Qualanthri and of the Crimson Sun, bear the mark, and inherit the Empire.

## The cure for the heir

The Imperial record has him as a priest said to serve the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/), whose prayers over the newborn were unfamiliar to those present, and the court called him the Stranger. The King of Galifar's own records have him as an elvish shaman whose treatments of the ailing prince succeeded, until the records abruptly stop. He wrote his own account in a very old high Elvish, in letters meant for someone in Aerenal and never sent, which the party found behind the cover of a book in the Imperial Archive of the Westhouse. They are unsigned, and they are his:

::::letters
:::letter{leaf="I"}
Crona and I offered the king of Galifar a cure for his ailing heir. We told the king some of what would follow, including that his son would become very powerful and effectively immortal. We did not tell him that his child would be replaced, killed, and turned into a vessel for the dragon.
:::

:::letter{leaf="II"}
The child has woken with the full consciousness of itself as the dragon.
:::
::::

## Crona

E'noch and Varyn Crona were distant relatives through the House of Vol and brothers in the plan. He went to Aerenal and came back with Varyn, whom he declared the chosen guardian of the newborn prince, and Varyn entered the pact knowingly and willingly.

The rite began in Korth and needed to be sealed somewhere with "ancient fire", so he took Varyn to Sharn, to the Forge of Binding in the Dhakaani tunnels. Renn Tal, an archivist of the Old Histories Wing, later deciphered the puzzle door and explained what the chamber was for: **Varyn Crona and E'noch Qualanthri used the Forge as part of the Eternal Guardian ritual, drawing on forbidden Vol rites.** Varyn forged the blade while E'noch layered Vol magic over the binding and anchored Varyn's blood and soul to the weapon. The letters give the reason:

::::letters
:::letter{leaf="III"}
To commit itself to a human body, the Crimson Sun will invest its power in a binding ritual with a terrific sword carried by Crona. This will invest a large piece of His Majesty's power. While it is weak, I am worried this will compound Crona's power and influence.
:::
::::

The worry was sound. The sword took a great part of the dragon's power while the dragon was weak, and Varyn, who carried it, became head Paladin to Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III, the Emperor's closest friend and adviser, and Master of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/), the secret service that bears the dragon's name. E'noch stayed in the background. Together the two of them devised what led to [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/), when Varyn brought the bleeding sun down over Cyre with that sword.

The [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) turned on them, opposed the plan and had Varyn assassinated. [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), who deposed that Council and took the Empire, were in some ways the fulfilment of what Crona and Qualanthri had set out to do, and Uriel among them knew it.

## The Rite of Cain

At some point in those centuries he made himself a vampire.

::::letters
:::letter{leaf="IV"}
I have stayed in the background. In my own time, I have undergone the rite of Cain to expend my life and experiment with true blood magic. I know you will not like this decision, but it is the right one. I have killed my sire, and I am immortal. I do not enslave others freely, but I have experimented with my blood. And I have developed a new form of it, which can be taken by mortals and expend their life more quickly, speeding them toward undeath and in control.
:::
::::

[Embrace](/items/embrace/) was first discovered through his experiments and those of his students, and from there it reached the market. His clan stayed small and secret, and it never left the lair.

## The turning of the Emperor

The dragon had expected to shed the human body in time and take its full form as an ancient dragon. That did not happen, and the failure gave E'noch his hold on it.

::::letters
:::letter{leaf="V"}
The Dragon Emperor understands that his transformation is not going to happen, and that he might live and die as Kaius, a little longer lived than most mortals. He has asked Crona and I for a solution. I have put one forward. He knows the risks. He would be my thrall, unless I feed him in turn. He has made the terms very clear. Crona will bind me and hold his sword at my throat, until His Majesty rises, comes and drinks of me. I will be true to my word. But even as a true vampire, he will be obsessed with my blood.
:::
::::

Varyn held the sword at his throat through the night, and E'noch kept his word and fed the Emperor when he rose. The Emperor became a full true vampire on E'noch's blood, and he has craved it ever since. Aurorix told the party the rest: *"E'noch turned him into a vampire to make him immortal, and his dragon blood was inflamed by this. But vampires have weaknesses, weaknesses that E'noch and his spawn have exploited."*

## The Sisters of the Blood

E'noch gathered the most capable pure-blooded elves of his house and of the other houses of Vol into the Sisters of the Blood, caretakers of the Emperor and his wife and future brides of the Dragon Emperor and his heir. Their final initiation is to meet him. Saturnia, who was cast out of Aerenal when he sent for her, knew what that meant: *"He wants to drink all of my blood and turn me into his slave, or preserve me as a virgin for my cousin's child to mate with, so that future emperor half-dragons can be Qualanthris."*

Uriel, his granddaughter, was the star of that design, and she has spent a decade bearing the Emperor daughters when the plan requires a son. He had others ready. Saturnia again: *"I've been brought here to be a broodmare for this line. My cousin, she sits in the throne now, but I think if my grandfather had his way, she would step aside or die. She has failed in her mission."* Aurorix watched him bring his grandchildren into the blood pool chamber, do unspeakable things to them, and tell them they were next in line once his granddaughter died or failed for the final time.

## The blood

Behind the double doors of his lair a round pool of blood stood under a wall of soaked rock, with Aurorix half sunk in it, paralysed and exhausted, and vats around him. For years they mixed the gold dragon's blood with that of mortals into the potion that sustains the Emperor. It was one source among several: the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) had been an earlier one, bled for three years under the Korramont. Aurorix's account of what it was for:

> They sustain the Emperor. But his coma, it is a lie. They have the Emperor, who is no mortal, in torpor. E'noch turned him into a vampire to make him immortal, and his dragon blood was inflamed by this. But vampires have weaknesses, weaknesses that E'noch and his spawn have exploited. So they sustain him in this vile blood potion, but they steal his essence. If the Crimson Sun were to dawn again, it would mean the end of all things. They have done the world a favor by entrapping that member of my kin. His true name is Viagra.

## Public and private

The Empire's record of him is one line: *a ritual performed by a mysterious stranger known as Priest E'noch*. Renn Tal's research found missing pages in Imperial-approved tomes, altered chronologies, and references to subterranean structures beneath Sharn that should not exist. Inquiries into Varyn Crona trigger Iron Veil surveillance; Renn had to go into hiding for making them. The man Varyn bound himself beside has no portrait and no dates.

## The Echo in the Forge

The Inevitables reached the Forge of Binding with Renn Tal, past a mind flayer and infected Cogborn. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) performed the Rite of Recall. Varyn's Echo manifested and repeated fragments of his final moments rather than fighting with intent, and its last words were these:

> "…we'll bleed the sun, eternal night.\
> Our bloodline shall reign…\
> As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine.\
> E'noch… Qualanthri… my brother…"

The Echo shattered into red-black light and hung in the air like blood splatter, and the Forge went dark. Moments later a drill came through the ceiling and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) dropped onto it and walked toward the extinguished Forge. Blair pulled the party out through a hidden passage.

## The dinner party

A button under a dragon skull in the morgue of the Lazaret's hospital opens a wall on stairs to a gate far older than the hospital. In [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/) the party came through it with Saturnia, broke in on a feast of his household, and found him at the blood pool with Lady Ophelia Qualanthri and Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn. He had Saturnia on a table behind him.

> Lady Ophelia, please make sure our guest is unharmed. Trillo, please destroy the dragonborn. Please save the woman for me.

He fought as the wizard first. He blighted Gemma (*"Do you think my granddaughter learned about disease on her own? We're all doctors in this family."*), covered the room in a *circle of death*, drained her to the floor with his touch, called six spawn out of the iron maidens, and found her invisible across the room with a *finger of death*. Eric's turning burned his spawn and did not touch him.

> So you've uncovered our entire vampire conspiracy. Congratulations. But we've known you were coming, Gemma Corso.

> This is ridiculous! We've been planning this for centuries!

[Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) woke Aurorix and put Palefire through E'noch, and the blood on the floor boiled up into the master vampire.

> I have worked across the centuries, arranged dynasties, destroyed entire kingdoms. I have wrought hell on this continent. Only for you to come and defile my moment of glory. You will be but drops in the pond of blood in which I bathe my anus.

The dragon crashed into him and breathed fire in his face, the Bright Dawn burst his leg open down to the old bones, and Eric drove his dagger upward and unseamed him from below. He went to mist. In the room of coffins Gemma found him, spoke, and drove in the sword he and Varyn had made:

> Your eternal nightfall will be interrupted by the Bright Dawn.

Black smoke filled the room, a red light in his eyes flashed white, and he went to ash.

## Right now

E'noch Qualanthri is destroyed, along with Lady Ophelia, Trillo Dwyn and his household. The Forge of Binding is dark. Uriel, his granddaughter, is in labour above the lair. Aurorix is free and means to finish the work: *"We have to finish destroying the Qualanthris. And the Dwyns. And the Cronas."*

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation** — To breed the blood of Qualanthri with the Crimson Sun and put the result on the throne of the Empire.

**Beliefs** — That the elves of his line are the rightful masters of the continent. *"You are a failure. Your race is nothing. We are your superiors. We are your masters."*

**Likes & Dislikes** — Blood magic, and the long view. He disliked failure in his granddaughter.

**Vices & Personality flaws** — Obsession, by the testimony of the dragon who listened to it for years. He kept his word to the letter when it served him.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **[Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)** — his brother in the plan and a distant relative through the House of Vol; bound by him as the Eternal Guardian. Assassinated; his Echo destroyed at the Forge.
- **[Emperor Kaius](/people/emperor-caius-iii/)** — the vessel he made and the vampire he turned, who craves his blood.
- **[Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)** — his granddaughter, the Empress.
- **Saturnia** — his granddaughter, a neophyte Sister of the Blood, who helped the party destroy him.
- **Lady Ophelia Qualanthri** and **Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn** — his household. Destroyed with him.
- **Aurorix** — the gold dragon he kept in the blood pool for years.
- **[Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/)** — the archivist who reconstructed what he did at the Forge.

**Hobbies & Pets** — None recorded.
