**Species unrecorded · Priest · The Forge of Binding, beneath Sharn · Status unknown — last named by a dying echo**

**Also known as:** Enoch · *the mysterious stranger* of the Imperial record · [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s brother, by Varyn's own last words.

Priest E'noch Qualanthri is the priest who, with Varyn Crona, performed the **Eternal Guardian** ritual at the **Forge of Binding** in the Dhakaani tunnels beneath Sharn, drawing on forbidden [Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) rites. The ritual bound Varyn to Emperor Kaius as sworn protector from birth and made him head Paladin to Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III across centuries. The Imperial record names him only as *a mysterious stranger*; Varyn's echo in the Forge named him brother. He shares a surname with [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), Empress of Khorvaire. He has never been located, dated, or shown to be dead.

## Physical description

None recorded. No likeness, age or description of him exists in any form.

## The rite he performed

Beneath Sharn, in the ancient Dhakaani tunnels, sits the Forge of Binding — a pre-Galifar ritual structure that Imperial-approved histories do not admit exists. Renn Tal, an archivist of the Old Histories Wing trained in pre-Galifar ritual history and Dhakaani archaeology, deciphered the puzzle door and explained what the chamber was for: **Varyn Crona and E'noch Qualanthri once used the Forge as part of the Eternal Guardian ritual, drawing on forbidden Vol rites.** The ritual bound Varyn to Emperor Kaius as sworn protector from birth, and made him head Paladin to Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III across centuries. Her notes indicate the binding may be reversible, and that a counter-rite would require a priest to anchor the invocation and a skilled forge-worker to operate the ancient mechanisms.

## Public and private

The Empire's record of him is one line: *a ritual performed by a mysterious stranger known as Priest E'noch*. Renn Tal's research found missing pages in Imperial-approved tomes, altered chronologies, and references to subterranean structures beneath Sharn that should not exist. Inquiries into Varyn Crona trigger Iron Veil surveillance; Renn had to go into hiding for making them.

## Competence and circle

He drew on forbidden Vol rites and made them hold, on a Dhakaani forge, in a working that survived centuries and three Emperors. His known associate is Varyn Crona, head of the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) and of the Council of 13 — and, by Varyn's dying account, his brother.

## The surname

Qualanthri is an uncommon name; the only other bearer of it on record is the Empress — an Aereni, a Cleric of the Grave who bears the Mark of Death, descends from House Vol, and has spent a decade building a machine out of an old elvish working she did not invent. A priest of that name performed a Vol rite under Sharn long before her. No relationship between them is documented.

## Conduct in role

He performed the Eternal Guardian ritual and appears in the Imperial record only as *a mysterious stranger*. The man he bound is celebrated in marble; the man who bound him has no portrait and no dates.

## Origin

Where E'noch came from, when he arrived, what order he served and where he went are not recorded. The [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/) has priests; the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) have priests; the Aereni have their own. Which, if any, was his is not recorded. He had access to a Dhakaani forge under Sharn, the confidence of the Emperor's sworn protector, and command of forbidden rites.

## The Echo in the Forge

The Inevitables reached the Forge of Binding with Renn Tal, past a mind flayer and infected Cogborn. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) performed the Rite of Recall. Varyn's Echo manifested and repeated fragments of his final moments rather than fighting with intent, and its last words were these:

> "…we'll bleed the sun, eternal night.\
> Our bloodline shall reign…\
> As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine.\
> E'noch… Qualanthri… my brother…"

The Echo shattered into red-black light and hung in the air like blood splatter, and the Forge went dark. Moments later a drill came through the ceiling and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) dropped onto it and walked toward the extinguished Forge. Blair pulled the party out through a hidden passage.

<small>The Empire has spent three years bleeding a dragon; the Doves' liturgy names their project the Eternal Nightfall; the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/) promises a dragon reborn. Varyn Crona said all four things in one breath, and then said his brother's name.</small>

## Right now

E'noch Qualanthri has never been located, dated, or shown to be dead. The Forge of Binding is dark and in Esther Crona's hands as of that night; Esther is dead; Renn Tal survived, is in Sharn, and still believes the Eternal Guardian binding can be undone by a priest and a forge-worker. The party now travels with a runesmith.
