**Duergar (deep dwarf) · Outrider of the mourning-party · The Teryaki clan · Dead**

**Sesame Tofu** was a duergar outrider for the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) mourning-party coming down into the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/), and he died carrying its prayers. He was found in a pocket cave off the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), grey-skinned and weeks dead, curled around a stomach wound, carrying thirty-five gold, a wax rubbing of the crest of [House Moo Goo Gai Pan](/organizations/house-moo-goo-gai-pan/), and a **mourning-braid** — white hair bound in ribboned Dwarvish prayers for the souls and the crafts of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) people. The **Inevitables** carried the braid to his clan, who named him in return.

## Getting his name back

Nobody in the Inevitables knew who he was. What they had was a dead body, a braid, and a crest they could not read. First contact with the clan in the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/) went to the edge of violence over stolen grave-goods, and what pulled it back was [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) handing the braid over.

> It's Sesame Tofu's. Thank you for returning this... you can sleep in Sesame Tofu's bunk. You won't be needing it. - Madame Tso Teryaki

The clan named him, thanked them for it, and gave them his bed.

## Right now

No record says his body was moved from the pocket cave. His braid went home. His bunk in the camp has been slept in once, by an armed stranger in an Imperial disguise, with the clan's permission.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki mourning-party; House Moo Goo Gai Pan, by the crest he carried.

**Items:** A mourning-braid, returned. A wax crest rubbing, still in the party's possession.
