**Sea elf · Ranger · Boatswain of the Sea Ghost · [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) · Dead**

*Also known as:* Oceanus

Oshi, whose full name is Oceanus, was a sea elf ranger and the boatswain of the [*Sea Ghost*](/items/the-sea-ghost/), the ship of the party Neptune's Rejects in the campaign [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/). He joined the crew when the party won the ship, asking for a full share, and Dave ran him as a level-1 party member. He was left in charge of the ship when the party went ashore, and was built to be their guide to [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/). He died on 2021-11-12, the night of the ambush at the Weathervein tomb in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/). His people live under the sea, and returning his body to them stayed on the party's list of quests.

## Joining the crew

When the party took the *Sea Ghost* on 2021-09-18, Dave set out the crew: "MacGruber and Oceanus as navigator and boatswain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The party ledger lists "Oshi - Boatswain" among the *Sea Ghost*'s officers, with Captain Hellwood, [Scrunt](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) as first mate, Dolores as chaplain and McGrubber as navigator. <small>(Neptune's Rejects ledger)</small>

Dave built him as a level-1 character: "He wants a full share to join, including xp", and "He can be our 4th party member". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> The party agreed to leave him in charge of the ship ashore. Dave: "And we leave Oceanus in charge of Sea Ghost when we’re gone there". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

The next day Nico reworked his figure: "I changed our boy Oceanus aka Oshi a bit", and Dave took up the name: "Oshi". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-19)</small>

## Ranger of the sea

Dave: "Yeah he’s a sea elf", in leather armor, "But like swim suit level armor". He carries a trident: "He actually rocks a trident". He does not use a ranged weapon, and "Has shit dex". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

His favored terrain is the ocean. Dave: "Oceanus as a ranger with ocean as his territory is super useful": his group cannot become lost except by magical means, difficult terrain does not slow them, he forages twice as much food, and when tracking he learns the exact number of creatures. "He also speaks draconian", and "His favored enemies are lizardfolk and sauhagin". Dave rolled his personality, and Nico summed up the result as "Sensitive and honorable". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

By October 2021 he had 271 XP, 29 short of the next level, at which "he gains fighting style". Dave: "He’s already helpful on natural stuff", and "I mean he’s built to be the guide for dunwater basically". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

## Death

Oshi died in the session "Salvage Operation – Part I" on 2021-11-12, in which Skerrin Wavechaser's [Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) assassins ambushed the party at the Weathervein tomb. Dave, the next morning: "rip oshi what a session". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> See [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/).

Nico said of her character: "Oshi might be the first person she was close with that has died." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> When Dave listed what followed that night, the blown-up building and the open executions, Nico answered: "For Oshi". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small>

## His body

His people live underwater, and nobody in the party knew where. Dave:

> i love the idea of bringing oshi's body back too, but we don't know exaclt how to find their home so we'd need a quest and a plan to go underwater

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-19)</small>

The quest stayed open. When Dave introduced [Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/), he called him "a potential party npc like Oshi". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> Dave's list of open quests at the end of 2021 began "1) Bring back Oshi’s body". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small> In February 2022, as Gabriella's honeymoon wound down, Dave named it again as "the first loose end to return Oshi’s body to his people". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-25)</small>

## Farewell

After the session of 2022-06-21, when Dave took back the book quests from JL, he signed off: "Great sesh guys RIP OSHI 4 EVA". Nico answered "OSHI 4 EVA", and JL "Poor little weirdo". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-22)</small> Oshi died once, in November 2021, and the line was his farewell.
