**Half sister of Arthur Els · Mother of Mordred · Time of Troubles · Reformed**

*Also known as:* Morgana · La Fay

**Morgana La Fay** is the half sister of Arthur Els of Camelot, in the age of the Time of Troubles. She trapped [Merlin](/people/steve-merlin/) in a tree, stole his power to bewitch Arthur, and bore an incestuous son, **Mordred**, in a ritual that bound the soul of the God of Mercy and Judgement into him, so that Mordred became the Jesu, the Infanta of Judgment. With Mordred she allied with the dwarves of Mithral Hall. The party arrived in [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) charged to destroy Mordred and La Fay and free Merlin; they went to Mirar and reached Morgana in the session of 2020-11-07. By the Winter Solstice she was reformed and helped [Kara](/people/kara/) and the three Weird Sisters cast the Charm of Making to unbind Merlin, and she pledged herself to the party with the Weird Sisters and the High Elves at the muster of [Arc VII](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/).

## Camelot

Nimue of Avalon gave **Uther Els** her sword Excalibur, Ironbane, and set Merlin to watch his line; Uther drove the sword into a stone, and **Arthur Els** grew to take his place in the legend. The Time of Troubles record continues:

> But the further treachery of men took root. His half sister, Morgana La Fay, took advantage of the infidelity of Arthur’s queen and closest knight Lancelot to drive a stake through Camelot. She tricked Merlin into a trap, imprisoning him in a tree on the banks of a bubbling brook. She then stole his power to bewitch Arthur and bear an incestuous child in a ritual which bound the soul of the God of Mercy and Judgement into her son, Mordred. Mordred would grow to become the Jesu, the Infanta of Judgment.
>
> Morgana and Mordred spread rumors of a Holy Grail which caught the blood of the last incarnation of mercy, and that it could rejuvenate the kingdom and defeat Mordred. They made alliance with Infanta-less dwarves of Mithral Hall, submitting that all Gods are in fact one – Moradin, the creator God, and that Jesu was his son, born on Earth to bring deliverance to believers and hellfire for heretics.

<small>— Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.</small>

The party's objectives, as the arc set them: "Destroy Mordred and La Fay", "Free Merlin", and "Ally with the Elves of Avalon and meet with Brightmantle". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.)</small> See [King Arthur and his Knights](/organizations/king-arthur-and-his-knights/), [Nimue and the Elves of Avalon](/organizations/nimue-and-the-elves-of-avalon/) and [the Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/).

In the previous loop Mordred held Ironbane. Dave, of the party's loop: "the Waning One and Valen have one less Bane (mordred + iron bane) than they did last time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-22)</small> See [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), [Valen](/people/valen/) and [the Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/). By the time [Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/), as Peter Glans, gave the party clues against their enemies, Mordred is listed among them as dead. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>

## Mirar

After Camelot, Kara's player Jenny wrote: "Seeing Morgana would be cool if she could help us free Merlin. He would be a good Ally." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-09-26)</small> Dave offered "Netheril, Cormanthyr, Mirar, Mithral Hall" as the next destinations, [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) went back to Motherstone with [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/), and the rest of the party chose a zombie-killing mission: "You have selected Mirar". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-04)</small> After the first session there Dave wrote that "inside the castle will be a bit more challenging than just hack and slash". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-10)</small>

Inside was a dungeon of paired rooms. Dave: "All rooms have a numbered twin, when you exit a room you end up exiting it’s twin in the same direction, with a couple exceptions". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-24)</small> The next morning Dave noted that he "should have had Morgana in an interior lair snatching people as they came in instead of the transformed exit idea". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-10-25)</small>

Dave: "Tomorrow we get Morgana and meet back up with Magnus and Brightmantle". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-11-06)</small> The session awarded 1,500 experience each, and [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/)'s player Nico called it a "Great game!!! Lots of lore info." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-11-07)</small>

## The Solstice rite

Kara travelled to Avalon to learn the Charm of Making and unwind "Morgana’s spell which sealed Merlin in his tree". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VIII.)</small> On the night of the rite:

> The Winter Solstice is here, and the night has arrived to cast the sacred Charm of Making and attempt to unbind Merlin from the Oak where Morgana imprisoned him. Morgana herself is reformed and helping with the effort, along with the three Weird sisters, who with Kara will perform the rite.

<small>— Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VIII.</small> The Charm of Making is a tenth-level ritual with five casters arranged in a pentagram. Merlin was freed; his pledge at the muster of Arc VII is "You got me outta that dang tree!"

## The muster

At Brightmantle's funeral, the allies of every age pledged themselves to the party. Morgana's pledge is given jointly:

> The Weird Sisters, Morgana, and the High Elves – “You restored life to our forest, saved our daughter from the brink, and are the true bearers of Ironbane. We are with you to the end.”

<small>— Arc VII, Leviathan I.</small> Ironbane is Excalibur, which [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) melted into his revolvers.
