**Collector · Master of the corpse-pickers · Ragpicker Square, [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Alive**

Pharod is a collector and scavenger of the dead who lives in Ragpicker Square in [Sigil](/places/sigil/)'s [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), master of the corpse-pickers who carry bodies to [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/). His pickers collected the party's bodies and dumped them there. He holds the whole corpse-collecting racket, with [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) enforcing it for a cut, and disposes of murder victims for killers all over the city, among them [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/). The party saw Pharod with the handsome man.

## The collector of Ragpicker Square

The Dustman bookkeeper Dhall, in the Mortuary, pointed the party to him:

> "Pharod is a collector, a scavenger of the dead. He resides in Ragpicker Square. If anyone knows how you came to be here, it is him."

<small>(Dhall, DM notes)</small>

Ragpicker Square is where "small crowds of hunched, rag clad humanoids swarm carts of dead bodies and seeming refuse, fighting and bidding on the sad spoils." <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The corpse-pickers

The corpse collectors take bodies and deposit them in the Mortuary for junk. Pharod is their master, and is paid by the [Dustmen](/organizations/the-dustmen/), who also offer to buy corpses. The pickers are hard to find, and they did pick up and dump the party. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Outside the Mortuary, just after the party escaped it, a corpse-picker came to them with "these yours?" Non-verbal, it managed only "I'M PHARODS" and "FOR PHAROD". <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## The racket

Pharod has a lock on the whole racket, and Rotten William enforces it for a cut. He deals with murderers all over the city to collect and dispose of their victims, "in the thousands", extraplanar victims included. <small>(DM notes)</small> [Prez](/people/prez/), a prisoner in Rotten William's hideout, overheard Pharod accepting bodies for disposal with no questions asked, in a deal Rotten William had arranged. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Pharod took payment from Jeremiah to dispose of bodies with no questions, and Jeremiah is now threatening him. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party saw Pharod together with the handsome man. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small> The party know that Jeremiah is the handsome man with the glowing sword who killed all of them but Heyu. <small>(Dave)</small>

Pharod is plotting the death of the party, and his men and Rotten William's are climbing the Hive to ambush them. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Enemies

[Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/), the thieves' guild boss of the Catwalk, hates Pharod and wants "something, anything, to give me an edge over that lying cheat..." <small>(DM notes)</small> His thug Ratbone hates both Rotten William and Pharod. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave believes that Rotten William and Pharod are being paid by someone to kill and disrupt his gang. <small>(DM notes)</small>

On hearing of the party's tattoos, [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) is immediately furious at Pharod and Jeremiah. <small>(DM notes)</small>
