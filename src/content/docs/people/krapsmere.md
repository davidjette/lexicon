**Lord of Cormyr · Commander of [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) · [Suzail](/places/suzail/)**

*Also known as:* Lord Krapsmere · Crapshmer · Crapsmere

**Lord Krapsmere** is the commander of the Purple Dragons, the army of King Glenn Danzig of Cormyr, in the Age of the Infanta. In the Dales War, twenty years before that age, he slew the Witch Queen of Cormanthor. On the night of [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/) he believed the party, and he was sent against the campus of [the War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) with orders to kill every War Wizard there. At the end of the Infanta Crisis he led columns of Purple Dragons into [the Egg](/places/the-egg/).

## The Dales War

Of the commander of the Purple Dragons: "It was he that slew the Witch Queen of Cormanthor in the Dales War just twenty years ago, an act that the elves will no doubt seek to avenge." <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small> See [Myth Drannor](/places/myth-drannor/).

## Suzail

At the Citadel of the Purple Dragons the knights drilled "as if they will ride to battle tomorrow." Their commander "believes both the Empire and the elves of Myth Drannor to be a mortal threat", and military plans in the Citadel "show preparations for frontal assault on Myth Drannor." <small>(Arc III Part VII, Suzail.)</small>

## The coup

On Candlenights Eve the party stormed Castle Danzig, killed the War Wizards' archmagi and turned the king into a snail. Dave: "the king's military commander and the king's daughter believe you". Then: "Krapsmere was sent to attack the War Wizard campus and kill all war wizards on site, a bloody and potential devastating task for the purple dragons" / "essentiall, the army was ordered to kill the air force on sight". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16)</small> See [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) and [Kara](/people/kara/).

"Lord Krapsmere has ordered the arrest of all War Wizards in Suzail." He calls them by the pejorative "robes". He reported fierce fighting in the Wizards' intelligence office in the Citadel, with twenty dead before the combatants were subdued; most of the War Wizards had fled to the Arcaneum, and at least twenty more had gathered at their campus in the east of the city. At Castle Danzig "Lord Krapsmere barks orders to the gathered NCOs who are tasked with crisis management." <small>(Arc III Part VIII, War Wizards.)</small>

The players planned around him. In Nico's plan for the morning after, the false king and Zanzibar would call for "Crapshmer", and "Only Crapshmer would know that Zanzibar and the King are leaving". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-25)</small>

## The Arcaneum

Before the assault on [the Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/), Dave: "Krapsmere says local PK units observe no activity around the Arcaneum and assume the wizards are using magic to get to-and-from and staying hidden." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-15)</small> Inside the school, JL, who played Zanzibar, proposed to "bring Crapsmere and the Purple Dragons inside". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-22)</small>

While the party prepared at Cormanthyr, the elves were still coming to terms with the fact that Cormyr and the Purple Dragons, their former enemies, had secured their Infanta. "Calls for the head of Lord Krapsmere, the killer of the their former monarch are heard, and Krapsmere stays out of sight because of this." <small>(Sic. Arc III Part IX, Arcaneum.)</small>

## The Egg

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> At the Crack in the side of the Egg the allied host entered group by group: "One by one, the columns of Purple Dragons led by Lord Krapsmere and each protected by a Warmage in a bubble of brilliant magic, tredge into the acid meat of the egg and into the darkness." <small>(Sic. Arc VIII, notes for a final encounter.)</small> See [Arc VIII, End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).
