**Squire and quest knight · Sworn to Roland Deschain · Chief apostle of Cypher, Istus and Eloise · Alive**

*Also known as:* St. Percival · Saint Percy · St. Perceval · Parsifal · Percy · Perceval

**Percival** is a young would-be knight of the Time of Troubles who swore himself squire to [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) and travelled with the party through the last arcs of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), run at the table by JL as a sidekick. He joined against his mother's wishes on the road to Camelot, rose past tenth level, and was killed and frozen by the Netherese sages; [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) raised him. At the platinum temple he stood as the last line of defence between Tiamat and [Karametra](/people/karametra/)'s ritual. He survived the final battle with almost his whole life obliviated and his faith intact, and became the chief apostle of the church of Cypher, Istus and Eloise and the founder of the faith that becomes [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/).

## Parsifal

In the Time of Troubles the party scried [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) approaching a village with Calix and Eldamir, teleported near him, and met Parsifal. He introduced them to his mother, who admitted privately that his whole family had been killed on a quest as knights, and forbade him to follow the party to Camelot. He disobeyed, and joined the party as they headed into the Waste Forest. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part I.)</small>

In the forest he "tags along and asks interminable questions" ("Do you serve a godking?", "What is magic?", "Have you ever killed anyone?") and wants to train. When the party found dozens of shallow graves, he explained that the entire land is filled with bodies killed in the Infanta Wars. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part II.)</small>

He swore himself squire to Roland. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-08-08)</small> See [Arc VI — Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) and [King Arthur and his Knights](/organizations/king-arthur-and-his-knights/). Of the Arthurian quest, Dave later wrote: "this subplot yielded three huge allies + Percival just cause I was geeking out on excalibur at that moment". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-09)</small>

## Squire and sidekick

JL ran Percy as a sidekick and put experience into him: in October 2021 he was about 1,000 XP short of tenth level, and Dave wrote "Percival is almost 10th level wow". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) got him armour; Dave: "I think Zan did get armor for Percy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-01)</small> He was outfitted by Camelot with half plate, and used Zanzibar's hammer two-handed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-11)</small>

The archmage [Valen](/people/valen/), blinded through the whole fight, never used his Dark Matter combo, which Dave said "would have killed Percy for sure". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-27)</small> In December 2021 JL reported: "My boy Percy got to level 10. 20 AC. And he’s riding a rhinoceros." Dave: "That’s a serious sidekick". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-04)</small>

Before one departure from camp, Sue told Zanzibar that he wanted to be a knight, and to follow Zanzibar and Percy. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> Back in Motherstone, [Zebulon](/people/zebulon/) "sees the devotion of Zanzibar, and Percival, and the rest of your party" and falls into a deep bow. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles, "The Chronicle".)</small>

## Death and raising

Percival was killed by the Netherese sages and frozen. JL wrote that Zanzibar "would want to kill the sages with his bare hands after they killed his boy". Dave: "Oh no Percy………" / "If you hurry you can revivify him I think" / "But you might have to defrost him first". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-19)</small>

As the party took St. Cloud's ship and piloted it away from the rising Egg, "Zanzibar clutches Percival’s frozen corpse, which Magnus sets about thawing and restoring." When he was raised, he was a eunuch. <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I, "The Escape".)</small> Dave: "You also stole St Clouds ship which is pretty good, and you raised Perceval". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-04-09)</small> See [Roland's Cube](/items/rolands-cube/) and [Arc VII — Leviathan](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/).

## The platinum temple

When the party came back to the deep past to help Karametra cast Move Planet, they defended her platinum temple at [Dragon Mountain](/places/dragon-mountain/) against the Tarrasque, dragons and Tiamat. In the Tarrasque fight Percy went inside the temple, and JL, by then estimating him at about twelfth level, wrote: "I love when Percy can be utilized. He’s my boy." Dave: "He’s still in there and is technically the last line of defense between Tiamat and Karametra’s ritual". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small> See [Arc V — Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/).

The next morning Dave wrote:

> percival is the only NPC pet/sidekick/squire to actually survive a major quest I've ever seen, and he's the literally teh archetypical squire and quest knight, and now he's forever chaste
>
> I wondered if you'd leave him in Antiquity but now he's on the journey, a full party member

<small>(Sic on "teh". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-18)</small>

## St. Percival

Percival survived the battle, but almost his whole life was obliviated. His faith survived. He became the chief apostle of the church of Cypher, Istus and Eloise, and the founder of the faith that becomes the Sororia, "and much more". <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c17-c21.md)</small> See [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) and [the Cypherian Pontificate](/organizations/the-cypherian-pontificate/).

The Heroes' Hill rhyme of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) remembers him:

> In deeper waters, Lords in masks were 4 and 21. / Saint Percy for an honest man did search, yet found he none

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-31)</small> An earlier variant of the rhyme, JL's riddle, gives the line as "100 days St. Perceval searched for an honest man, yet he found none." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-09-14)</small>
