**Cogborn · Engineer · RUST inner circle · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Misty</small>

**Misty Kross** is a Cogborn engineer of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/)'s inner circle, and with her husband [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/) she runs Misty's Pawn Shop in the City Center of Sharn's Lower City — the faction's primary safehouse, meeting point and covert access route into the undercity. Displaced as a child by early Imperial crackdowns, she was taken in by [Preacher](/people/preacher/), grew up navigating the tunnels, learned to repair machinery older than Sharn itself, and became one of R.U.S.T.'s most trusted technical minds. She is pregnant, and Preacher's determination to evacuate the Cogborn intensified the moment he learned. The Cogborn are now out of Sharn, and the shop and the tunnel beneath it are still standing.

## Physical description

Short, wiry and heavily marked. Her hair is cut short and dyed a hard red; her eyes are pale blue, and pale tattooed lines run out from beneath them across both cheekbones. Her right arm is a full articulated prosthetic of steel and brass from the shoulder down, and the left arm and her bare midriff are covered in flowing blue-grey tattoo work. She wears an olive tactical plate-carrier cropped at the ribs over nothing much, brown padded work trousers strapped with tool pouches at both thighs, and heavy brown boots; a graffiti-print jacket is usually in her hand rather than on her. She is pregnant.

## The shop

**Misty's Pawn Shop** stands among the street-level shops across from the Ziggurat in the City Center. It is R.U.S.T.'s primary safehouse, its meeting point, and its covert access route into the undercity: beneath the shop floor is a hidden tunnel running down into the abandoned Sub-Transit control room where Preacher holds the network together. Misty and Kriss built the disguise and maintain the entrance.

Kriss, the son of Karen and Martin Kross, married into R.U.S.T.'s inner circle and helped Misty transform the pawn shop into a disguised operations hub. Together they maintain the hidden rail-line entrance beneath their shop, coordinate the movement of Cogborn families into the tunnels, and manage the flow of information between the Lower City and R.U.S.T.'s deeper hideouts. Kriss handles logistics and security, while Misty repairs mechanisms and keeps the shop's facade convincingly mundane. Their shop is the first refuge for those fleeing Iron Veil raids.

To the street she is a pawnbroker with a temper and a mechanical arm. She is also one of R.U.S.T.'s most trusted technical minds, capable of repairing machinery older than Sharn itself, and she coordinates the movement of Cogborn families into the tunnels while keeping the shop's facade convincingly mundane. She was guarded and suspicious when three strangers came asking about her father-in-law, and stayed so until [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/) walked them into the shop himself.

Preacher took her in as a child after her own family was displaced by early Imperial crackdowns, and raised her while the rest of his people fled the city; she grew up navigating the tunnels. Kriss married into R.U.S.T.'s inner circle and helped her convert the shop. DJ Rust runs the Lower City end of the same network. And [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) came to her through him — Misty immediately grasped what a Dhakaani-literate archivist was worth to a people hiding in Dhakaani tunnels, and it was Misty who brought her to Preacher.

**The clock:** Misty is pregnant. Preacher's determination to move the Cogborn out of Sharn intensified the moment he learned.

She believes a hiding place is only as good as the thing built over it. She believes the Cogborn will not be saved by anyone above ground. She believes her child will not be born in the Cogs.

She repairs the mechanisms, maintains the hidden rail-line entrance, keeps the shop's inventory and takings plausible, and manages the flow of information between the Lower City and R.U.S.T.'s deeper hideouts while Kriss handles logistics and security. She patrols the tunnels herself — the party found her out on a route near the ancient chamber and she took them straight to Preacher — and she is fiercely loyal to Preacher and deeply embedded in the Cogborn community.

Misty was a Cogborn child whose family was displaced by the earliest Imperial crackdowns on the district, and she was taken in by a Warforged who had refused an exile decree and gone underground. She was raised in the tunnels, learned machinery from a machine, and came up as one of the very few people in Sharn who could keep pre-Imperial infrastructure running. When BioTec agents came asking after Martin Kross, she and Kriss said nothing useful. When the Iron Veil began flooding the Cogs, their shop became the first refuge for families fleeing raids.

**Right now:** the Cogborn are out of Sharn and R.U.S.T. holds a seat in the new city — Warforged citizenship, the arrest of the Iron Veil engineers, and a Department of Infrastructure, all carried at the first Council of Sharn. The shop is still standing, and the tunnel beneath it still runs down into the dark.

**Organizations / Groups:** R.U.S.T. inner circle; the Kross family of the Lower City.

**Holdings:** Misty's Pawn Shop, City Center, Lower City · the hidden rail-line entrance beneath it · the routes she patrols in the undercity.

**Items:** a full articulated prosthetic right arm · the shop's stock, kept convincingly mundane.

**Old Machines:** Misty's specialism. She repairs mechanisms older than the modern city — sub-transit gear, sealed doors, rail-line lifts — and keeps a hidden entrance operating in the middle of a district under occupation without a single maintenance record existing anywhere.

## The Tunnel Beneath the Shop

After Breaker's Pit, Misty and DJ Rust escorted the party to the shop, where they reunited with Kriss and Renn Tal. Then Kriss and Misty revealed the hidden tunnel, and led the group deep into the abandoned Lightning Rail Sub-Transit control room beneath Sharn. That is where the trio met Preacher, and where they were told the truth of the crisis and the price [Blair](/people/blair/) had set for the Cogborn's passage.
