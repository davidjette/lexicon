**Astral elf · Druid 12 (Circle of Stars) · First Arcanist and Spelljammer · [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) · Dead**

*Also known as:* Lt. Caprica · the singer of stars

**Caprica** is an astral elf druid of the Circle of Stars, played by Nico, who served as pilot and science officer of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). Born in the constellation Capricorn, she graduated first in her class from the Academy and chose the Zanzibar as her best chance to find [the star song](/lore/the-starsong/), a call she heard from the stars. She took command of the ship to save Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) during the mind flayer crisis, led the command crew to [Necis](/places/necis/) by following the star song, and walked with Jim and [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) on the [Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/). She died at the end of *Light of Xaryxis* in finding the answer to the star song, spoken to by an avatar of Cypher. Fifteen years later, in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the Company sought to resurrect her, and her body was recovered.

## Background

Caprica was born in the constellation Capricorn in the Astral Plane, and by her player's account is about seven hundred years old. She studied constellations and made star maps, and while studying heard a call from a constellation. Her people would not pursue it, so she joined Astral Command in the hope of following it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Dave named the call the star song, and left its source a mystery. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10, 2022-10-18)</small> Nico first conceived her in April 2022 as a kalashtar druid of the Circle of Stars with an anthropologist background, and made her an astral elf that September. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-28, 2022-09-29)</small>

Astral elves range from indifferent to hostile toward Astral Command, and an astral elf officer is rare. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Caprica was a non-traditional student at the Academy, many hundreds of years older than her classmates, and her abilities in science and flying intimidated students and professors alike. Despite distrust of letting a possible double agent of a distant astral elvish empire near a Sovereign Spelljammer, her record earned her the offer of first or second helm on almost any vessel. The prologue continues: "You stunned the Admiralty and the Academy but selecting the open helm on the SSJ Zanzibar, currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere." <small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> She chose it because it was the assignment closest to the constellation Capricorn, her best chance to encounter the star song, and a ship with a top science crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

Six months after graduating she was ordered to a colonial waystation on the unsettled world of [Tralfamadore III](/places/tralfamadore-iii/), because no one aboard the Zanzibar could operate its Major Helm and the ship was confined to that system. She spent two months of Jamtime on a science vessel studying the Capricorn constellation and everything she could read about the Zanzibar. When she arrived the ship was nowhere to be found. After a week alone she went north with a science team to collect a sample of the falling stars reported there, and on the way back received a message that the captain would collect her in a small craft from the village of Northspire, at a tavern called the Loose Rooster. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### Aboard the Zanzibar

Caprica reached Northspire in the first session, on 2022-10-21, on a one-line message from a potentially drunk Captain Smallberries a month late to collect her. Astral elves nearly killed the party twice. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19, 2022-10-22)</small>

She is the Zanzibar's new "Spelljammer", the pilot whose spell slots power the ship through space, and its new science officer, with half the bridge deck hers and her quarters those of presumed first mate. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10, 2022-10-17)</small> The crew roster lists her as **FIRST ARCANIST & SPELLJAMMER, Lt. Caprica**, in the Arcane Science department. <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> A space cat named Orion lives in her room. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-17)</small> Her secret item doubles as a Necklace of Fireballs with 1d6 charges. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

In the [mind flayer crisis](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) of November 2022, Caprica took command, saved the captain, and earned the respect and admiration of the crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small> Her player summed up the fight from her side as Caprica being eaten and dying in one hit. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small>

In January 2023 Dave improvised a Sea Goat starry form for her, a Capricorn ram with a melee charge and a swim speed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small> The season premiere of 2023-06-09 was almost all Jim and Caprica. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-09)</small>

### Necis and the pilgrimage

In December 2023 Jim, Caprica and Sandy were on the remote planet of Necis 1. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22)</small> In January 2024 the three set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command, which Dave foretold would make them the greatest officers in its thousand-year history, whether they lived or died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> Crossing the sea of grass by windwheel, Caprica fought as the group's blaster, beside Sandy as buffer and near-tank and Jim as control and utility, against a CR 8 semi-hydra. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-10)</small>

Necis 1 is a world erased from [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). Jim and his crew were deposited there because Caprica followed the star song and brought them along; Dave described her as having upended their world and set them off on their own. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> At [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) the pilgrims saw the future, with an army of shrikes and the spirits of Cypher, and found people hung on the tree of pain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

### Death

In the finale of 2024-05-31, against the Shrike, Caprica died in finding the answer to the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-03)</small> An avatar of Cypher, existing in that distant future, spoke to her in the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small> The next day the table hoped she had survived: Dave pointed to a shuttle left on the Zanzibar and suggested someone might have rescued her off screen. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-03)</small> His epitaph for her named her the singer of stars, whose song rings through the phlogiston forever. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-03)</small>

After the finale the Lexicon was known to have fled [Sigil](/places/sigil/) to escape the Starsong. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

In *The Starsong Awakens*, set fifteen years later and run by JL, Caprica is long dead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> The Sovereignty wants to make the monsters of the Vodoni Empire a scapegoat for the awakened Starsong. The Company knows that would disrupt trade, and wants to resurrect Caprica to help find the Starsong's true source. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small> [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/) told her brother [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) of a "star scream" related to Caprica. <small>(Barrias Ruby-Eye's character sheet)</small>

### The Company dossier

The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier lists Caprica (Elf, Astral) with the status deceased and her location unknown. It states that Lt. Caprica was last seen at the helm of the SSJ Zanzibar, spelljamming the vessel to the Eye of Doom during the [Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), that the probability of her survival is calculated at zero, and that her remains have never been recovered. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> Dave's account places the climax of Starfall at [Necis](/places/necis/). <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small>

According to the dossier, Astral Command seized the wreckage of the Zanzibar and stored it in a top-secret hangar in Realmspace, and Company agents have been denied access to it; its entry on the Zanzibar places the wreck in a high-security facility in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) and reports that data aboard may lead to Caprica's remains. The dossier notes her "extensive and intimate experience" with the Starsong, records that the Company's Board of Directors has deemed her remains a vital asset, and marks clerics, necromancers and casters of resurrection magic for special attention. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> It names [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), and the command crew of the SSJ Vanguard, [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/), as of interest to the Board because of their service with her. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

### The search

In January 2025 Barrias and [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) went back to Picon to continue the search for her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-18)</small> The Company dossier states that her remains were never recovered; by May 2025 the Bureau agents [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) had brought Caprica's damaged body back to the living ship [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/). <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small> Dave, as a player, came to regard the search for Caprica as a red herring. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small> Whether she was resurrected is unknown.

## Abilities and equipment

Caprica's sheet makes her a Druid 12 of the Circle of Stars with the Astral Drifter background and 106,000 XP. Her spell save DC is 17 and her spell attack +9, with Wisdom as her spellcasting ability. <small>(character sheet)</small>

- **Starry Form:** she can spend a use of Wild Shape to take on a starry form in the Archer, Chalice or Dragon constellation, shedding light for ten minutes. <small>(character sheet)</small> Dave also improvised a Sea Goat form for her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small>
- **Astral elf traits:** Starlight Step, a bonus-action teleport of 30 feet four times per long rest; Astral Trance, which replaces sleep with four hours of conscious meditation; Astral Fire; darkvision; Fey Ancestry. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Feats:** Eldritch Adept, taking Beast Speech, and Magic Initiate (Cleric), with *spare the dying* and *sacred flame*. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Cantrips** include *druidcraft*, *thorn whip*, *mold earth*, *create bonfire* and *light*, the last cast through her Scroller. <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:** Elven Chain, a [Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), shield, scimitar, longbow, druidic focus, healer's kit and a potion of greater healing. <small>(character sheet)</small>

- **[Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/):** crewmates who searched for her at Picon fifteen years later.
