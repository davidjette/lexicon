**Human · Druidess · Envoy of the Eldeen Confederacy · Level Unknown · Alive at last record**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

June Gloomstalker is a middle-aged human druidess, sent as envoy from the dark druid coven currently branding themselves as the **Eldeen Confederacy**. The coven is also called the **Children of Winter** and worships a thing called **the Gloaming**; its ruler, **Ivanka Riefenstahl**, took the title Queen after approaching the Unforeseen aboard a ship out of Greenheart and striking a deal — the party would put an alliance to the Imperial Council, and in return the Children of Winter would be confirmed as the rulers of the Eldeen Reaches. [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) carried it to Korth and were rewarded for it, and six months later Esther returned with Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) to finalise the treaty. Years later, with the Empire fighting on every front, Crona's Wall collapsed and Orc forces pushed into Dark Druid territory in the Reaches. Her face, her colouring, her circle and her name before the coven took her are not recorded.

## The coven

The dark druids of the Eldeen Reaches are also called the **Children of Winter**, and they worship a thing called **the Gloaming**. Their ruler took the title **Queen**: Ivanka Riefenstahl, who approached the Unforeseen aboard a ship out of Greenheart and struck a deal — the party would put an alliance to the Imperial Council, and in return the Children of Winter would be confirmed as the rulers of the Eldeen Reaches with Ivanka as its Queen. [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) carried it to Korth and were rewarded for it, and six months later Esther returned with Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) to finalise the treaty. In the throne room the Emperor's own consort listed it among the Unforeseen's achievements: *"you've secured a powerful ally to the West in the Eldeen Reaches."*

## Public and private

**In public** Gloomstalker is a diplomat: an envoy carrying the credentials of a recognised regional government called the Eldeen Confederacy, and that name is a costume the coven put on after the treaty. **In private** the body she represents is a dark druid coven that worships the Gloaming, was installed in power by an Imperial alliance, and is now the West's confirmed authority because four Crimson Sun agents thought it was a good trade.

## Origin

Middle-aged, human, a druidess, and sent. Her birth, her coming to the coven, her part in the Last War and her selection as envoy are not recorded, and neither is what she personally believes.

## What the alliance became

The Eldeen alliance was one of the Unforeseen's showpiece achievements and it did not hold. Years later, with the Empire fighting on every front, **Crona's Wall collapsed** and Orc forces pushed into Dark Druid territory in the Eldeen Reaches. The Confederacy that four Imperial agents installed is now the frontier where the Empire's western defence is failing.
