**Weathervein · Alchemist · Keraptis of White Plume Mountain · Dead**

*Also known as:* PW · Keraptis · Pengua Liquidius Weathervein · "Weathervein" Penguinus

**Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein**, called **PW**, is a dead alchemist of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/) whose bones lay in the Weathervein Tomb beneath the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), and who was also Keraptis, the master of [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/). His journal is a master work on the philosopher's stone. He "made lots of horcruxes", and is in the book that bound [Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/) to [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/). In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) took his bones and receipts from the tomb; the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) would have used Salmor's body to bring him back, and the party later considered murdering [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/) to raise him. His corpse was never raised. The corrupted natures of the three weapons of the Weathervein treasure hoard, [Whelm](/items/whelm/), Wave and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/), came from Keraptis. He is the Weathervein who, in [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/)'s telling of the dynasty, "attempted to bottle the secrets that made their family strong and accidentally destroyed everything." <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-open-questions.md)</small>

## PW, the skeletal alchemist

In the module the skeletal alchemist is a boss. Dave made him a dead questgiver: "The skeletal alchemist is supposed to be a boss fight btw, I made it into a dead questgiver bc I was inspired by the zombie priest and Gale from BG3", "Gale’s puzzle when he dies", "PW". Nico, the same day: "I’ll need diamonds eventually, but we should get some for p.w." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-12)</small>

His journal passed to Dolores. Gabriella Hellwood took up alchemy from it. Dave: "the PW journal is literally a master work on the philosopher's stone so if I borrow it from Dolores I can test up and learn the advanced stuff bit by bit". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13)</small>

The Weathervein Tomb held "Bones of PW", "Receipts signed by PW", an "Alchemist's Weight" and "Alchemical Equip", which Neptune's Rejects had taken by 2021-09-18. <small>(Neptune's Rejects ledger; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> See [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/) for the full inventory.

Dave on a manor on the Saltmarsh coast: "it's PW's house, owned by Salmoor, formerly Sanbalet's smuggling hideout", and "PW is an interesting guy we haven't tracked down much". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-03)</small>

## Bringing him back

In the session of 2021-12-23 Dolores passed a Charisma save against Vivica. Dave: "But gambled a lot on that one CHA save because Vivica would have ended Salmor and used his body to bring back PW". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small>

After Dolores recovered the sword [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), Dave wrote: "We haven’t touched the PW stuff, maybe it’s time to bring him back". Nico's list of the arc ahead included "treasure hunting, PW, and the dwarfs". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

On 2022-04-23 Nico wrote that PW "is gonna need some fresh royal blood me thinks", and Dave named him: "Penguinus". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>

## Keraptis

On 2022-07-23, before the party went into White Plume Mountain, Dave posted a picture captioned "Penguinus “keraptis” Weathervein". Nico: "Yeah, that make sense". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small> The same day Dave posted Keraptis's riddle, the verse of the published module *White Plume Mountain*, which opens and closes:

> Search ye far or search ye near / You’ll find no trace of the three / Unless you follow instructions clear / For the weapons abide with me.
>
> I care not, former owners brave / What heroes you seek to hire. / Though mighty, I’ll make each one my slave / Or send him to the fire.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

By the end of the session of 2022-08-13 the party held the three weapons. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> Dave: "All three weapons have true natures we can try to bring our, and their corrupted natures from Keraptis". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> On 2022-09-23 Dave had a "Keraptis Indoctrination Center" prepared to run, and the party went to [the Eldabrin](/lore/the-eldabrin/) instead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small>

## The horcruxes

Dave, on 2022-09-16, after naming Whelm "The one Weathervein that didn’t consent to being bound into ancient artifacts from the treasure hoard":

> But “Weathervein” Penguinus himself made lots of horcruxes

> Because he’s in the book too and he has his corpse itself you guys never raised

He added that the party had considered murdering Anders Solmor to raise him. Nico: "Nah, Delores likes pirate Anders". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

The book is the holy book whose voice worked on Dolores beside Vivica's, and which told Brother Guido Meatball that he had no choice but to work for her. Dave: "It’s not just vivica now / It’s also my boy in the book". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small>

## Names

Dave listed "Pengua Liquidius Weathervein" among four Weathervein names on 2021-11-19. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-19)</small> He answered Nico's "Pw" with "Penguinus" on 2022-04-23, captioned him "Penguinus “keraptis” Weathervein" on 2022-07-23, and called him "“Weathervein” Penguinus" on 2022-09-16.
