**God · Trickster · Court of Ao · Alive**

*Also known as:* Ensign Hermes

**Hermes** is a trickster god of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), created and played by JL. In *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* he posed as an ensign of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) to put three of its junior crew on trial for their civilization at the Citadel, and in the finale of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* he took [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) as his ward at the court of Ao, to stop mortals from undoing time.

## The ensign

Hermes first appeared as an ensign in Astral Command uniform, a white-skinned human with curly brown hair, dealing cards at a game of twenty-one below decks. He asked the others how they had come to join Astral Command, then told them his name really was Hermes, that he was not a mortal, and that his people had been curious about Astral Command for some time. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

> "What gives you people the right to travel hither and thither through wild space as you please??? The arrogance is astounding. You're like children tampering in the domain of gods."
>
> <small>— Hermes, to Mordecai, Se7en and Forbes.</small>

Most of his people wanted to crush that civilization on principle; Hermes proposed a trial instead. See [the Citadel trial](/history/the-citadel-trial/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small> Struck by the party, he would pretend to be dying and then tell them to try harder. At the summit he wore a toga and a golden laurel. The book *Last Days of the Old Gods*, found at the Citadel, names him among the younger gods who overthrew and devoured their parents: "Hermes the trickster god". <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

JL posted his portrait the next day as a god "who could easily pop up again and make further mischief in some future quest". Dave suggested that someone could swear fealty to him and become his warlock; JL: "Or a cleric!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>

## The court of Ao

In the last sessions of *The Starsong Awakens* Carlos Vega was brought before a court of twelve imperious figures on a lush mountain high among the clouds, presided over by Ao, a white-bearded man in a toga who called for order with a polished stone. Ao judged that Carlos was neither a god nor a mortal man, and that he had entered the forbidden place, "the alpha and the omega… the place where all life begins and ends". Hermes spun round in his chair, claimed to know "Korbos or something", and asked that the accused serve as his ward on a mission of great importance: the mortals below were attempting to undo time. Ao agreed. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

> "Stick with me Korbos… and you'll be a god in no time."
>
> <small>— Hermes, to Carlos Vega.</small>

## The temple of the Ungod

Floating invisibly with Carlos above [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/), Hermes explained what it was: the temple of a very cheeky god, "a god-no-god", whom the gods call the Ungod, an ancient and powerful knowledge god who refused Ao's call to Mount Celestia and was for a while very popular with mortals before he abandoned them. By Ao's decree no god may set foot inside it. Each of its doors opens onto a different point in the Ungod's evolution, from infancy to deathbed, and mortals had trampled inside. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

Hermes locked the party in. They could not step backwards to escape, and had to find the seventh door, whose key the temple's master had lost: a broken chain of letters on moonstone coins reading NO FU TU RE. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>
