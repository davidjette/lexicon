**Duergar (deep dwarf) · kin of the Teryaki camp · The Teryaki clan · Alive**

Wonton is a young duergar of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan and the only person in the mountain who arrives on a **giant spider**. He was first met in a corridor of the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) with a small group of duergar under [General Tso](/people/general-tso/), searching the sealed rooms for the fallen remains of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) so that they could be given proper burial rites. He had his ears boxed by his own people after speaking against the **Inevitables**, and he remains in the camp at [Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/).

## The ears

After the Inevitables cleared the sealed Black Dove shrine - [Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/) and his risen ogres - and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) left roughly 280 gold of dwarven grave-jewels on the altar body and walked out to tell the kin they were there, Wonton had something to say about the strangers. His own people boxed his ears for it:

> Shut your hole, Wonton. You were so scared you wouldn't even go near this door. These people came in here and cleared it out in five minutes.

The camp was opening one haunted room a day at the time, and the party had cleared the level in an afternoon.

## Right now

Still in the camp at Rak'tafur. He is among the kin who stayed when the party warned the clan that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) had claimed the mountain and urged them to evacuate.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki clan; General Tso's clearing parties.

**Hobbies & Pets:** A giant spider, ridden. Unnamed in any record.
