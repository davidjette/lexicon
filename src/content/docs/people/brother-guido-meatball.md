**Celestial warlock · Level 5 · Ally of [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/)**

*Also known as:* Meatball · brother francesco meatbaldi

Brother Guido Meatball is a celestial warlock devoted to a mysterious holy book, and an ally of the party Neptune's Rejects in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh, run by Dave. He serves the book and seeks to bring it back together. He met the party at a bar in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), traveled with them on JL's "jazz dungeons" session, and went with them to Abbey Isle, where the voice in the book told him he had no choice but to work for [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/), and where Dolores tried to kill him. He carried the Atlas, served the party as a healer, and was with them at the Saltmarsh finale.

## Arrival

Meatball came into play in the session of 2021-11-27. Dave: "I originally intended for Brother Guido Meatball to be wandering around elsewhere but we posted up at the bar so I just had him come in". He was a warlock build Dave had made for himself, adjusted for the campaign: "celestial warlock devoted to a mysterious holy book". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-28)</small> Dave called it "the second warlock build I’ve made after St Cloud". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-12)</small>

Before JL's session of 2021-12-03, Dave said Meatball "can hang with us or hang around bugging other people to help him", and "he's a potential party npc like Oshi if we want him". He went along, and JL wrote afterward: "I forgot we were with Meatball!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> See [Oshi](/people/oshi/).

## The book

Nico, who played Dolores, asked what his deal was: a warlock of Neptune, "Or of a Celestial of Neptune, like another weathervien?", and offered her own reading: "The tome is a tome of a weathervien. An angel of Neptune". Dave answered:

> He serves the book, and is dedicated to bringing it back together / And it told him to come here and check this abbey / You guys have an original page of it you know lol

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-12)</small>

Dolores kept the party's page of the book a secret from him. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-12)</small>

## Abbey Isle

Dave rebuilt the Abbey Isle dungeon for the session of 2021-12-16. It was tuned for four level-5 characters, and Dave put the party "with meatball" at "5.75 plus magic items". Nico counted herself and Meatball as the party's part-time healers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-15)</small>

In the abbey the voice in the book spoke. Dave, afterward: "It’s also my boy in the book / Who told Meatball he had no choice but to work for you". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small> Dolores tried to kill him there. Dave: "Man It’s gunna be awkward after you did attempted murder on meatball". Nico: "I didn’t MEAN to lol". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-18)</small> Dave, before the next session: "The book may have told meatball to obey you but you also return to a Saltmarsh in the throes of an inquisition". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-22)</small>

Dave had linked the Atlas of Endless Horizons after the Abbey Isle session. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small> At the end of the month he told the party, "you’ve still got meatball around with the Atlas". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>

## With the party

Dave said he had made Meatball "as a low risk healer npc". Nico: "Nah… meatball is cool. Dolores won’t kill meatball", and "He is kinda an employee". Dave: "he's only lv 5 but he's useful!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

In the ship combat of 2022-04-22 Dolores fell to 2 hit points, "So was meatball". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small> Dave posted his sheet before the session of 2022-06-30, in which the party killed Salmor <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small>, and at the end of the Saltmarsh finale wrote: "Meatball will take darkness, problem solved". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

## Voice

In 2025, working out the voice for [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), Dave wrote: "if I make him TOO italian he'll bleed into Papa Dia and Meatball". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18)</small> See [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/).

**Items:** the book, which he serves; the Atlas.
