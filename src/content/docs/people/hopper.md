**Shopkeeper · Hopper's Magic Emporium, Sub-line District · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Hopper</small>

**Hopper** is the proprietor of **Hopper's Magic Emporium**, a neighbourhood magic shop in the Sub-line District of Sharn's Lower City stocking home-security crystals and ritual incense. His shop and the automated arcade next door both belonged to Dr. Edwin Graves, the Cog Collector; when [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) killed Graves and recovered his property deeds, Hopper's landlord became the rebellion. Told as much, he was thrilled at the chance to expand and struck a major trade deal on the spot, providing supplies, magic items and bulk goods explicitly intended to support the rebellion, and three years later he is still doing it. No physical description of him is recorded, and his beliefs are not recorded.

## The Sub-line District

The Sub-line District is built atop the ruins of Lightning Rail Sub-Transit Station 1, its old concourses and platforms transformed into Station One Market, and around that market sit the small businesses: the Kross Roads Corner Store, The Arcade, and Hopper's Magic Emporium. Hopper is a working retailer of the Lower City rather than a power in it, and has never pretended otherwise.

## The trade deal

He supplies the rebellion out of the shop, and it is a supply line the Empire never thought to close. He cast Summon Dragon into Eric's Ring of Spell Storing on the shared understanding that a true name summons that dragon for one hour and then frees it, which the party intended to spend on Valtrex. He is also the person who told the party about [Candy Crush](/people/candy-crush/), the lawyer acting for the previous deed holder.
