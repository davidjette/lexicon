**Human · Nobleman · Head of city planning and development, the Imperial Council · Dead**

*Also known as:* Lord Albert Spear · Chief Imperial Architect Albert Spear

Albert Spear was a human nobleman, a member of [the Imperial Council / Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) as head of city planning and development, and the Emperor's chief architect and urban planner. He granted the town of New Ham the Imperial Charter that made it the city of [Newham](/places/newham/), and he was one of the three Assembly members at the Interoperability Summit in [Sharn](/places/sharn/) whom [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) suspected of leaking to the rebellion. An Allip showed him the portal to Xoriat beneath Sharn; he tried to defect to Zilargo and shared the portal's existence with the Rebellion, and he reached the [Temple of the Closed Circle](/places/temple-of-the-closed-circle/) with the rebel group [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) at the same time as [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), who killed them all. He was dead by the time of the Sharn arc.

## The Imperial Council

Spear sat among the thirteen heads of the Imperial government, who meet in a tower in [Korth](/places/korth/) called the Imperial Assembly. The Council's roster lists him as:

> "Lord Albert Spear - Human Nobleman: Head of city planning and development"

He was the Emperor's chief architect and urban planner, and was reportedly a favourite of the Emperor "because of his vision for the grand infrastructure of the new Empire."

## The charter of Newham

In the six months after [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) met the Council in Korth, Spear granted New Ham its charter. <small>([Episode 6](/sessions/episode-6-aandb-6-months-later-the-tablet-dm-nico/))</small>

> "Imperial council member, Lord Albert Spear who is head of City planning and development granted New Ham an Imperial Charter to grow from a town into a city. Now known as Newham, the gateway to the East."

The city grew fast. Merchant caravans, train cars of construction equipment and labourers, and airships of arcane builders came in droves; high-end stores and restaurants moved in from the capital; the Lightning Rail Tavern was expanded into a hotel for travellers to the new [Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/); and the river Dark was redirected into a man-made lake named Lake LaBeef after the city's first mayor, [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/).

## The Interoperability Summit

Spear was one of three Assembly members named by Hayman Maw when the Crimson Sun sent the Unforeseen to the Interoperability Summit in Sharn to find a mole. <small>([City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/))</small>

> "The assembly members attending that Summit are Lord Gregory Goldenhew, Lord Albert Spear, and Minister Henry Heighrick."

The Unforeseen were ordered to discover which of the three were leaking to rebel forces, establish the evidence and, on confirmation, render, interrogate and neutralise them before the summit ended. The allegiance chart of the Central Khorvaire Transit Interoperability Summit places Spear among its chairs with [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) and [Kwanti d'Orien](/people/kwanti-d-orien/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-15)</small> The operation's second night gathered all three suspects at a symphony in the Grand Theatre. <small>([City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/))</small>

## The Temple of the Closed Circle

Spear learned of the portal to Xoriat beneath Sharn from an Allip, a spectre of a person burdened with a great secret who is compelled to show it to mortals before passing on. The Allip inspired him to propose an alternative energy source for Sharn's transportation network, in the hope of preventing further war with the Rebellion and a potential apocalypse. In desperation he attempted to defect to Zilargo, and shared the existence of the portal and more with the Rebellion.

He and [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) found the Temple of the Closed Circle at the same time as John C. LeBeefe and the Unforeseen, who killed them all and destroyed the mind flayers occupying the Temple.

## Death

Nico, the Sharn arc's DM, counted Spear among the Summit figures who are dead: "For sure, Spear, Goldenhew, Cornwell, LeBeefe, and d'Orien are dead". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-15)</small>
