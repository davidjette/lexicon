**Elf · Paralegal to Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) · Ministry of Law, Korth · Dead — killed by the Inevitables**

*Also known as:* Graham · (worn as a cover identity by [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/))

Graham Cracker was an elf paralegal to the Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) at the Ministry of Law in [Korth](/places/korth/), an orphan of the old town who found a post at the Ministry. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) killed him alongside Deirdre in Deirdre's own apartment, disposed of both bodies in a Bag of Holding, and burned them at the bottom of the bridge with the orc dead. The preliminary material he had carried to the apartment before he was killed told the Inevitables about a ministry-wide directive opening mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties, and about the pending assignment sending Deirdre to the Summer Palace to seize the records of the Grand Lodge. His face is now worn in Korth as a cover identity by [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/).

## Physical description

A younger elf, dressed for the Ministry. Worn by somebody else, his face passed a Force Captain, a forger, a courtroom and a jailer, and was recognised by a prisoner who had known him personally. No independent description of him is recorded.

## Origin

An orphan of old-town [Korth](/places/korth/) who found a post at the Ministry of Law. Nothing else of his life is recorded.

## The Ministry of Law

Below every Star Advocate of the Ministry sits somebody who carries the paperwork. Preliminary material, briefs, directives, the docket — all of it moves through hands like Graham's before it reaches the advocate, which is how a directive for a ministry-wide purge came to be sitting in a paralegal's notes.

## The clerk and the leak

Publicly, Graham Cracker was an unremarkable young functionary: an orphan, a Korthian old-town boy, and somebody's assistant. Nothing private about him is recorded.

What he did to the Empire, he did after he was dead. It was the **preliminary material he had carried to Deirdre's apartment** before he was killed that told the Inevitables about the ministry-wide directive opening mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties, and about the pending assignment sending Deirdre to the Summer Palace to seize the records of the Grand Lodge. Both of those pages set the entire episode in motion.

## Beliefs

Unknown. No record of his opinions on anything survives.

## Conduct in role

Sufficiently competent to be trusted with a Star Advocate's preliminary material, and ordinary enough that a stranger wearing his face drew no comment at the Ministry of Law. The impersonation's only near-failure came from someone who actually knew him: [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/), mid-jailbreak, looking straight at Eric wearing his face.

> "I thought you were gay, Graham."

## Right now

Dead. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) killed him alongside Deirdre Moro in Deirdre's own apartment, disposed of both bodies in a Bag of Holding, and burned them at the bottom of the bridge with the orc dead. His face is currently prosecuting cases and visiting forgers in Korth.
