**Khoravar · Engineer / Pilot · House Lyrandar · Imperial Head of Transportation · Dead**

*Also known as:* Hallorn d'Lyrandar of Aundair

**Hallorn d'Lyrandar** was a Khoravar engineer and pilot of House Lyrandar and the Empire's Head of Transportation. Born in Aundair to [Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) and half-brother to [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) of Valenar, he bore the Dragonmark of Storm, ran the development and production of airships for the family monopoly and for [the Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/), and was celebrated as an engineer, a racer and a playboy. He also moved **Umbra** across Khorvaire for the Zeirs and recruited victims for Malrik's cellar. He died at The Vault Underground in Sharn Ep 17, and three years later Sister Maelis Dorn's accusation over his death at the Summit at Thronehold broke the peace of Khorvaire.

## Physical description

Young, brilliant and endlessly charismatic, and dressed to be looked at. He was Khoravar — half-elf, half-human — born in Aundair, and he bore the **Dragonmark of Storm**, often called the Dragonmark of Flight. No further description of him is recorded.

## House Lyrandar

The world-famous House of Lyrandar holds the Empire's airship monopoly, and held it through two men. His father, Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, commanded the Empire's war fleets by sea and sky. Hallorn ran the yards that built them.

The Dragonmark of Storm gave him extraordinary skill as a pilot and designer, and carried him to oversight of the development and production of airships for the family's monopoly and for the Iron Veil. His fortune earned him the reputation of being the richest man in Khorvaire.

## Public and private

Publicly he was a daredevil and a bon vivant, racing horses, airships, sailboats and even dragons for sport, and one of the better big-game hunters in the world. His exploits made him a fixture of high society and a favourite subject of gossip.

Privately he built airships for the Iron Veil, corresponded with his half-brother Malrik Zeir of Valenar about **Umbra** and its connection to **Project EDEN**, and had BioTec fabricate a teleportation chip as a gift for Malrik's wife [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/) — a gift with a tracker in it.

## Competence and circle

He called the Lord Commander by her first name. He was half-brother to **Malrik Zeir**, the Valenar high elf who kept blood cattle beneath his own nightclub, which tied Hallorn to two of the most influential bloodlines in Khorvaire. His entry to that world was a VIP pass, handed out personally.

## Origin

Born in Aundair to Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, and half-brother to Malrik Zeir of Valenar. Bearing the Dragonmark of Storm, he took the development and production of airships for the family monopoly, then took the same work for the Iron Veil, and was named the Empire's new **Head of Transportation**. He was also, by then, transporting Umbra across Khorvaire for the Zeirs and recruiting victims for Malrik's cellar: the rebellion's file names him one of the primary recruiters and the man who brought Lorian to Sharn.

## The chip

The teleportation chip in [Gemma's](/people/gemma-corso/) head was crafted by BioTec for Hallorn as a gift for Faith. When it was stolen he had it tracked, and its teleportation pings bounced across the city — including under his brother's club, The Vault Below, the night it was broken into. He noted it pinging outside the city a great deal, and only when the trio teleported to their Upper City safe house did his people finally pinpoint its exact location. He walked up to their door, told them so, left them VIP passes to Malrik's club saying Malrik *"invites"* them, and reminded Gemma the chip was never truly hers. Minutes later the Children of Ember seized her in the street and cut it out.

## Conduct in role

At the opening of POSÉ he complimented Esther Crona's restaurant, complimented her command of the room, and was cut off mid-flourish and asked where her shipments from Valenar were — *"Your brother has no issue supplying, but the master of flight can't deliver?"* He blamed the Khraal. She told him the Khraal-controlled lands would become one more scar of Mourning if necessary.

## The Vault Underground

Sharn Ep 17. The Inevitables used his VIP pass to reach the lower levels of Malrik's club, and the evening came apart on the way down: cultists in a ritual room, recruiters attacking from multiple angles, Gemma chained and dragged away, [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) trapped in a bedroom. Hallorn confronted them. Malrik joined the fight. By the time [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) killed Malrik on the rooftop pool deck and Esther descended out of the night to finish the Mother Prophet, Hallorn d'Lyrandar was dead.

## The accusation at Thronehold

Three years later, at the Summit at Thronehold, **Sister Maelis Dorn** of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) confronted his father in the great hall and publicly accused [Chancellor Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) of being responsible for Hallorn d'Lyrandar's death. Moments later Vex began to rise into the air, wrapped in lightning, and the peace of Khorvaire ended. On the deck of Locke Pierce's airship the next day, the Admiral's fury was fed by three things at once: the Emperor lay comatose, [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) had deceived him, and the man who had killed his son was standing in front of him.

Unknown. No source records what Hallorn believed.

## Right now

Hallorn is three years dead and his father is dead behind him, killed on an airship deck over the water. House Lyrandar's two-man bridge between fleet and infrastructure is gone entirely, and the Empire's skies are being carved up by Locke Pierce's Hundreds. The Head of Transportation's chair has no recorded occupant.

**Organizations / Groups:** House Lyrandar · the Imperial Ministry of Transportation · contractor to the Iron Veil · patron of the upper-city Vault.

**Holdings:** The Lyrandar airship yards and the family monopoly's production lines. The reputation, if not the audited fact, of the largest fortune in Khorvaire.

**Items:** A BioTec teleportation chip commissioned as a gift for Faith Zeir — stolen, tracked across Sharn, torn out of Gemma Corso's skull by Children of Ember cultists and destroyed on the spot. VIP passes to The Vault Underground, handed to the people who would kill his brother with them.

**The Mark of Storm:** the Dragonmark of Flight. It made him an extraordinary pilot and an extraordinary designer. His father bore the same mark.
