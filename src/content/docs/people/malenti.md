**Medium humanoid · Spellcaster · Ghosts of Twatmarsh · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Malento <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

**Malenti** is a spellcaster whom the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) met in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) during [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), in the session of 2022-06-30 in which they killed Salmor. JL, who ran that session, posted her stat block the next morning. At the Saltmarsh finale of July 2022 she offered to take the party with Dwight to parley with Sekolah, and they refused. Had they accepted, Dave said, they would have invaded Saltmarsh and been at open war with [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/).

## After Salmor

JL: "in case ur curious (or if it comes up) malenti's stats". Dave: "Dang she’s pretty nasty with sleep at will, we gotta watch out for her". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

Nico wanted to question her: "Interested in talking more with Malenti and the ant assassin (antman) and see what’s up with the money exchange with th council members". Dave: "Yes interrogating Malento will be good, I wonder what we can do there". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

## The offer

Dave, after the session of 2022-07-07: "Yeah what a game, and how different it would have been if you took Malenti up on her offer". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-08)</small>

> Can’t get over how different the outcome would be if you went with malenti and Dwight to parlay with Sekolah, hardbread stays with the party probably leaves with a shark god boon and stays in the party and you launch an invasion of Saltmarsh and Anders dies in the battle

<small>— Dave, 2022-07-10</small>

Nico: "We did good and the right thing." Going with Malenti's plan, she wrote, the party would have "got back to Saltmarsh and scrunts there as it’s being attacked / flooded". Dave: "a real RPG moment, because yes we would have been in open war w the Rising Dawn". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

## Capabilities

From the stat block JL posted: <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

- A medium humanoid with a swim speed, resistant to cold and poison and immune to being charmed or frightened.
- She speaks Common, Elvish, Infernal and Sahuagin.
- **Amphibiousness:** "She can breathe in or out of water but needs to return to water every 72 hours to avoid suffocating".
- **Charming Appearance:** any non-sahuagin creature that starts its turn near her may be charmed by her for a minute.
- **Spellcasting:** *sleep* and *calm emotions* at will; *bless*, *minor illusion*, *mage armor*, *misty step* and *cure wounds* three times a day; *dimension door*, *heal* and *raise dead* once a day.
- **Magic resistance.**
- She fights with a shortsword and a longbow that deal psychic damage.
