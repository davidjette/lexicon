**Bear-man · Husband of Queen B · Underhive seat on the Council of Sharn · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Berenstain</small>

Berenstain is a bear-man, one of the husbands of [Blair](/people/blair/), and the holder of the Underhive's permanent seat on the Council of Sharn. He worked through an entire night to clear the collapsed tunnel that had sealed the Cogborn into the Pit, and at the first meeting of the Council of Sharn the Underhive was granted the autonomy, the property and the council seat it asked for, making him the first non-human, non-Cogborn figure the new Sharn seated in government. He is described only as a bear-man, and as courteous by those who have met him.

## The Underhive

[The Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) is a sovereign settlement beneath the Lower City, ruled by Blair through bargains and defended by her Drones. Her husbands form her inner circle, each contributing to its infrastructure and security, and Berenstain's contribution is the earth itself: the tunnels, the diggings, the collapsed passages, and the physical shape of a country made out of somebody else's abandoned sewer. He stands among Blair's other husbands in her chambers. To the surface he is a rumour about the Queen's strange court.

## The Cogborn

Berenstain worked through an entire night alongside [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) and [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/) to clear the collapsed tunnel that sealed the Cogborn into the Pit, opening the path to them and making the evacuation possible. Before that he sought the party out personally to thank them for helping the Cogborn — a courtesy nobody in the Underhive owed anyone — and gave them a gift.

> Berenstain thanked the group for helping the Cogborn and gifted them enchanted gloves with the move earth cantrip.

## The council seat

At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn the Underhive requested autonomy, legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below, and a permanent council seat for Berenstain. It got them. Underhive Drones joined the new Department of Infrastructure while maintaining their subterranean autonomy, and Berenstain holds the seat.

## Right now

Berenstain sits on the Council of Sharn as the Underhive's permanent representative, in a city whose Department of Infrastructure is largely staffed by his wife's Drones, three years after the government he sits in tried to arrest her.

**Dislikes:** cave-ins · the Iron Veil in the tunnels · being spoken about rather than to.
