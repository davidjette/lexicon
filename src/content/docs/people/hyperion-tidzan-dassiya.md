**Kalashtar · Sorcerer 13 / Cleric 5 · Vox Sovereign, later Captain · [Children of Hyperion](/organizations/the-children-of-hyperion/) · Alive**

*Also known as:* Sandy · Hyperion Titara · Lt. Hyperion Titdzan-Dassiya · Lt. Cmdr. Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya · Cpt Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya · Capt. Sandy Titzandassia · Holy Captain Tidzan-Dassiya Ultra Hyperion

**Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya** is a kalashtar sorcerer and cleric of the Peace domain, played by Dave, a Lesser Consular of [the Children of Hyperion](/organizations/the-children-of-hyperion/) who served as diplomat and Vox Sovereign of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). Born possessed by an aberrant quori, she joined the Zanzibar as its counselor and communications officer, warned [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) that she would burn him with the wrath of [Hyperion](/lore/hyperion/), and walked with [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) and [Caprica](/people/caprica/) on the [Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) across [Necis](/places/necis/), carrying the memory of what happened there to the Hyperion collective. Her rank rose from lieutenant to lieutenant commander to captain. Fifteen years later, in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, she stands with the Hyperion-run [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) against the party, as Commanding Officer of the SSJ Vanguard.

## Background

Sandy was born possessed with an aberrant quori, and "her cheerful demeanor belies a streak of horror buried in her mind." She is on a mission for peace. <small>(character sheet)</small>

She is a Hyperion: a priestess possessed with a dual soul shared with her god Hyperion, a powerful alien consciousness from the realm of dreams. Her people are missionaries of peace who volunteer as translators and spies on ships' crews. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24)</small> Hyperion is an extraplanar entity with a benevolent church. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> It took over a race of psionically enabled humans on Eberron long ago, and inhabits them and all their children as a collective set of memories and personality; when they are close they can meld minds, which updates the nodes of the network. It is lawful good, and in the Eberron cosmology comes from the dream realm of horrors, which it was escaping. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

The Children of Hyperion are an interstellar temple that professes to represent mediation and peaceful coexistence across the systems. Violence is forbidden in their domains, and many respect that sanctuary. Their members believe themselves possessed by ancient creatures called quori, which guide them and dictate a moral code that makes them diplomats, arbiters, judges and lawful soldiers. The Consular are missionaries of peace who volunteer for service on vessels and bases, mainly as interpreters, negotiators and spies, spreading their knowledge and code as they go. Sandy is a Lesser Consular. <small>(character sheet)</small>

### Name

Dave introduced her in July 2022 as **Hyperion Titara**, and used that name again in October. She was **Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya** by 2022-10-16, and Lt. Hyperion Titdzan-Dassiya, called Sandy, on 2022-10-18. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24, 2022-10-09, 2022-10-16, 2022-10-18)</small> Her sheets name her Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya (Sandy). <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

Dave first built Sandy as a Sorcerer 1 / Peace Cleric 2, the ship's counselor and resident diplomat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24, 2022-10-09)</small> By October 2022 she was a lieutenant and the Zanzibar's Communications Officer and Diplomatic Attaché. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small> The crew roster lists her as **VOX SOVEREIGN, Lt. Cmdr. Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya**, in the Diplomatic department. <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> White is the uniform colour of communications and diplomatic officers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-28)</small>

### Carlos Vega

After the [mind flayer crisis](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) of November 2022, Sandy brought Carlos Vega to security to issue his civilian credentials. She was curt with him: he was permitted on the Engineering and Lounge Decks only, could use no fire-based weapons or tools, and was to limit his Astralex access to matters of importance. Then her eyes glowed an eerie whitish grey. [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) had told her the mind flayer left a mark on Carlos, and she had witnessed the curse and heard its demands. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

### Missions

In May 2023, for the assault on a derelict ship in Doomspace, Dave gave Sandy an armored spacesuit. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-19)</small> Against Zodar her *hold person* failed, because Zodar is not humanoid. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-27)</small>

### The pilgrimage

In December 2023 Sandy, Jim and Caprica were on the remote planet of Necis 1. In January 2024 the three set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command, which Dave foretold would make them the greatest officers in its thousand-year history, whether they lived or died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22, 2024-01-06)</small> Crossing the sea of grass by windwheel, Sandy fought as a buff machine and almost a tank, beside Caprica as blaster and Jim as control and utility, against a CR 8 semi-hydra. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-10)</small>

In the finale of 2024-05-31 Sandy had her best session of the campaign. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> She carried the memory of what happened on Necis, to the Consul and at [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/), so the Hyperion entity knows everything that happened to her there. She does not know what became of her crewmates, because [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) scattered everyone without warning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

### After the Zanzibar

After the finale Dave considered Sandy's course. She would be gunning for Admiral, and in a Sovereignty without the Lexicon the Hyperion entity would have new relevance. He thought she would ally with Starweaver to enforce the end of artificial intelligence, perhaps fanatically, because of what had been done to her best friends and her home, the Zanzibar, and that she and the rest of Hyperion might want to search for her lost crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01, 2024-06-09)</small> He also suggested that a Hyperion long apart from others forms close bonds that are assimilated into the whole when she melds again, turning a specific love into a general love for all, so that Sandy would partly disappear each time she melded; by then she had been away from Sigil for about ten years. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-10)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

In *The Starsong Awakens*, which JL ran fifteen years later, Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Sandy is a captain, and stands with Astral Command against the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09, 2024-12-13)</small>

In November 2024 Dave, as a player, looked for a way to bring Sandy over to the party's side, holding that she was still in there somewhere. Another player warned that if that failed it would be dangerous to bring her near other Sovereignty ships, because she would connect to other Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09)</small> Dave's portrait of her from that week is titled "Holy Captain Tidzan-Dassiya Ultra Hyperion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10)</small>

### The SSJ Vanguard

The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier names Capt. Sandy Titzandassia as Commanding Officer of the SSJ Vanguard, an [Istus-class clipper](/items/the-istus-cruiser/), with Lt. Commander [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) as Executive Officer. The dossier lists the Vanguard as active, its location unknown, with rumours of its top-secret destination ranging from Realmspace to deep within [the Vodoni Empire](/organizations/the-vodoni-empire/). Its command crew is of special interest to the Board because both officers served with [Caprica](/people/caprica/) on the SSJ Zanzibar. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> Astral Command introduced the Istus class after the [Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) and the decommissioning of the Zarus-class battle cruisers, as light, fast and agile vessels. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier; New ships)</small>

## Appearance

Sandy has white braids, piercing eyes and clear skin, and is of average height. <small>(character sheet)</small> Dave's model for her look was Jennifer Coolidge as Mother Harishka of the Bene Gesserit in *Dune*, dressed in white. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-16)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Her *Light of Xaryxis* sheet makes her a Sorcerer 5 of the Aberrant Mind and Cleric 5 of the Peace domain, with the Acolyte background and 64,000 XP. She has 57 hit points and AC 18, casts at spell save DC 17 and attack +9, and has the Linguist feat. She speaks Common, Deep Speech, Draconic, Dwarvish, Elvish, Gith, Goblin and Quori. <small>(character sheet)</small> Her features include Telepathic Speech, the psionic spells of the Aberrant Mind, Quickened and Subtle Spell, Emboldening Bond, Balm of Peace, and the kalashtar's Dual Mind, Mental Discipline and Mind Link. <small>(character sheet)</small>

Her captain's sheet of December 2024 makes her a Sorcerer 13 (Aberrant Mind) / Cleric 5 (Peace) with 265,000 XP and 97 hit points, casting at spell save DC 19 and attack +11, with telepathy to 180 feet. Her strongest spells include *dominate person*, *mass suggestion*, *globe of invulnerability* and *plane shift*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-13)</small>

**Items:** Cloak of Displacement, Charming Lipstick, a [Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), a spacesuit, a crystal and an amulet, with vestments, a censer and incense. <small>(character sheet; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-13)</small>
