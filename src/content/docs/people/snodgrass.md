**Goliath · Ranger · Tunnel scout and Cogborn protector · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Snodgrass</small>

Snodgrass is a goliath ranger of the Cogs and one of the most dependable guides in Sharn's undercity. Raised among Cogborn families and hardened by years spent navigating collapsing infrastructure, he learned early how to move through sewer lines, abandoned sub-transit routes, and forgotten maintenance shafts with an ease few can match. He now lives out of a blocked drainpipe near the Cogborn encampments, keeping watch over families forced underground by the Iron Veil's occupation.

He has a memory for tunnel layouts and a talent for tracking movement through the undercity. Snodgrass hunts sewer gators and tunnel beasts to feed Cogborn families, and escorts vulnerable groups through gang-controlled stretches like those held by the Drain Snakes.

Though not formally part of RUST, Snodgrass is valued for his reliability, stealth, and deep knowledge of the tunnels. Preacher trusts him to scout routes, confirm safe passages, and detect Imperial patrols long before they reach the lower levels.

**Status:** Alive.

## Physical description

Enormous, thick-armed and permanently wet to the knee. His face is broad and hard-lined under a heavy brow, with a short dark goatee and a scar-notched cheek; his hair is cropped close beneath an incongruous red cap set with a green feather, which he wears in a sewer and has never explained. He fights in a scaled green jerkin under a shaggy fur mantle and a fur kilt, with a plated greave on one leg and thigh-high boots. A belt of teeth and tusks — sewer gator, mostly — hangs at his waist beside a curved sabre and a coiled rope. In his right hand is a heavy notched machete he keeps for the water. <small>(Appearance taken from his portrait on the Rebel Factions page.)</small>

## The undercity

The undercity is not one place. It is a stack of failures: sewer lines, storm drains, maintenance shafts, abandoned sub-transit routes, collapsed Cogs tunnels and, beneath all of it, Dhakaani work older than the city. Nobody administers it. Gangs like [the Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) hold stretches of it by force, giant crocodiles and rat swarms hold others, and the people who most need to move through it are the ones least able to.

The surface knows him, if at all, as a large man living rough in a blocked drainpipe near the Cogborn encampments. He is a ranger with a memory for tunnel layouts and a talent for tracking movement through the undercity, he hunts sewer gators and tunnel beasts to feed Cogborn families, and [Preacher](/people/preacher/) trusts him to scout routes, confirm safe passages and detect Imperial patrols long before they reach the lower levels. He is not formally part of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) He does the work anyway.

## Origin

Snodgrass grew up in a district built around a narrow waterway and surrounded by aging factories, in improvised homes welded onto pipes and vents, among people who did the most dangerous work in Sharn for the least money. The tunnels were where children went when the factories were working and where families went when the Iron Veil came, and he learned them both ways. By the time the Cogs were being emptied he had spent years living out of a blocked drainpipe near the encampments, keeping watch over families forced underground, hunting to feed them, and walking groups around the gangs.

He was raised among Cogborn families and hardened by years spent navigating collapsing infrastructure, and he learned early how to move through sewer lines, abandoned sub-transit routes and forgotten maintenance shafts with an ease few can match. He works closest with [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/), who keeps a stall in the Gear Street Markets and was waiting there with him the morning the party came down.

## Conduct in the field

On the move he is the guide and the rearguard at once: he takes point through gang-controlled stretches, escorts the vulnerable, and is the one who says whether a passage is clear. He fought crocodiles in a flooded sewer chamber, cleared the collapsed tunnel to the Pit alongside [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/) and Doppler through a whole night, and walked the entire Cogborn community down the Underhive river and past a dead stone golem to the mountain tunnel. He speaks rarely.

## Gear Street, at Dawn

He and Doppler Klink were waiting together at Doppler's market stall the morning the party crossed the Grand Aqueduct in disguise. From there he took them down into the aqueduct's lower levels toward the Underhive, and the first thing they met was a Drain Snake patrol wearing BioTec Security colours.

## The Cog Collector

The bodies had been turning up around Gear Street Market, the east side of the aqueduct and the Low Line Ward for nearly four years, and then had stopped turning up at all. When a wrapped Cogborn body fell from a rising cargo skylift onto Gear Street at dawn — the first in almost a year — Snodgrass stayed behind in a city his people were leaving to find out who was doing it. The answer was a polite man in a clean shop who walked past them every day.

**The thing he stayed for:** when the Cogborn walked out of Sharn, Snodgrass did not go with them. He chose to remain in the city, determined to help solve the mystery of the Cog Collector and to see justice done for his people — and he was standing in the room when the party opened the back of Circuit City and found the cages.

## Right now

The Cogborn are out. Snodgrass eventually left Sharn with the rescued survivors of Circuit City, heading for Gorgonhorn — and returned: three years later he was among the surviving R.U.S.T. names the Inevitables called on when [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) took a child and a hostage to the Veil Building, and he was in the sky over the city with the rest of them.

## Beliefs

He believes a route is safe when he has walked it and not before. He believes feeding people is scouting's other half. He believes his people are owed a name for what was done to them.

**Organizations / Groups:** none formally. Attached to R.U.S.T., trusted by Preacher, and permanently in the service of the Cogborn.

**Holdings:** a blocked drainpipe near the Cogborn encampments · the undercity routes he has walked and confirmed · hunting grounds in the sewer lines.

**Items:** a heavy notched machete · a curved sabre · a belt of gator teeth and tusks · a red feathered cap.

**The Map in His Head:** Snodgrass carries the layout of the undercity — sewer lines, storm runs, maintenance shafts, dead sub-transit routes, the gang boundaries and the flood levels — and can read movement through it. He guided the party from Gear Street down the aqueduct to the Underhive door, then to the Drain Snakes' northern lair, then out through the Spine with an entire displaced population behind him.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to get his people through the tunnels alive, and to find out who was taking them.

**Likes:** a route walked twice · Doppler's stall · gator hunting · quiet water · Cogborn families · the red cap.

**Dislikes:** the Drain Snakes · Iron Veil patrols in the lower levels · being asked to hurry · the Cog Collector.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** he will stay behind alone rather than leave a thing unfinished.
