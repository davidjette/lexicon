**Aereni High Elf · Cleric-Inquisitor of the Black Doves · The Empire · Alive, whereabouts unknown**

**Also known as:** Sister-Inquisitor Maelis Dorn · Maylas Dorne · *the young one, the Inquisitor* · *M. D.* on foundry escort papers.

Sister Maelis Dorn is an Aereni high elf, Sister-Inquisitor of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and apprentice to Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), at whose veiling she stood. She carries the **Chorus Ledger**, the registry of which house holds which bell and on which consecration-day, and keeps a circuit from Korth east and south by private coach to the Ivory Lazaret northeast of Qarth. She escorted the Original away from the Korranberg yard under the dove seal, and sealed two doors in that same foundry after the Thirteenth Casting rang and killed everyone in it. At a veiling in the Korth house she recited the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/) under seal of initiation to a lay penitent who was a NEST agent, and at the Thronehold Summit she attended in the Empress's place and named [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) responsible for [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/)'s death in front of the boy's father. She is alive and her whereabouts are unknown.

## Physical description

Aereni, young-faced, with a spine like a drawn bow. She is never without the courier-case chained at her side, and her seal is a dove pressed in white wax. Her hands are the only bare hands in Khorvaire that have been seen laid flat on a live [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) casting.

> "She is the only person I saw touch it bare-handed." — Agent FOXTALE, NEST debrief K-KORTH

## The order she carries

The Black Doves are an ancient and isolated order from Aerenal, drawn from a few clans within the Aerenai, bound to tend the dead and dying on battlefields and to eradicate any undead that remain. All races and nations make way for them. Since the Empress rose, the order has become the staff of the [Houses of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/) — the seized [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/) cathedrals and hospitals — and the sole body permitted to consecrate a casting. Foundry personnel are excluded from consecration and reminded that curiosity is a breach of contract. Dorn is the order's inquisitor: not its administrator, not its abbess, but the officer who arrives, inspects, seals, records, and leaves.

## Public and private

In the square at Korth she preaches mercy and the crowds love her. The Korth house defers to her absolutely while she belongs to no one in it. Inside the walls she inspects the bell. When she recites the Litany at a veiling she counts the word **thirteen** off on her fingers. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), who stood in the choir of witnesses at that veiling with her face working correctly, wrote afterward that she had seen the domestic version of Uriel's will and that it was worse than the public one.

## Competence and circle

She reports to Uriel Qualanthri and to nobody else; the sisters say she goes *to the Mother's house to report*. A talkative novice told Gemma, first-voice, that Dorn stood at the Empress's own veiling. Her working relationships are with the foundry Trust at Korranberg, whose escort papers name her, and with the houses on her circuit. Her one recorded friendship is a manufactured one: she took a grieving lay penitent of the Host into her confidence, judged her a worthy initiate, and whispered her the forbidden poem. That penitent was a NEST agent.

## Origin

She is Aereni, and an Inquisitor of an order drawn from only a few clans within the Aerenai, which narrows her birth to a handful of houses on a continent nobody in Khorvaire has visited. Her clan, her parents, her age, her arrival in Khorvaire and her entry into the Black Doves are not recorded. The earliest fixed point in her life is secondhand and comes from a talkative novice: **Dorn stood at the Empress's own veiling.** Everything else about her begins after that — the seal, the circuit, the ledger, the inspections.

## The Veiling at Korth

Gemma Corso entered the Korth house as a lay penitent and stayed eleven days. On the ninth, Dorn led the veiling of a novice and recited the Litany under seal of initiation, and Gemma took it verbatim from her mouth:

> come the night / the bells will toll / thirteen / the dead will rise / to serve the living / and the dragon / will be reborn

Dorn believed she was speaking to a worthy initiate. She was speaking to the Nest.

## Thronehold

Three years later, at the Summit called by Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar to end the civil war, the Empress did not attend. Dorn attended in her place, as the Black Doves' representative, and was the only expression of Imperial will in the hall. During the final assembly, as Eric finished a speech calling for the Empire to be outlawed, she confronted the Admiral and publicly accused Eric of killing his son. Moments later Vex began to rise into the air, wrapped in crackling lightning. The Black Dove sisters left the hall immediately — before the masked Imperial guards, all of them undead and loyal to Uriel rather than to Vex, turned on the Admiral and on everyone else in the room. Vex called down his warships on the Summit grounds. Delegates and civilians died in the streets. Uriel had no physical presence at Thronehold.

## The Sealed Doors under the Korramont

Three years dead, the Korranberg Foundry gave up her name twice. A door at the end of the living quarters had been sealed with a Black Dove prayer ritual — a muslin sheet covered in protective scripture, meticulously sewn into the wood frame — signed by Sister Maelis Dorn. Behind it a creature calling itself [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/) claimed three years of confinement and said it had heard *not a bell but a horn*, then became a winged demon and died in the hall. Deeper in the suite was the real Calvane, undead and near-vampiric, and Calvane's library, which held the consecration rite entire. A second seal in the same hand closed the shrine where Overseer Velen Marsk was still on shift and where the death warrant signed by Emperor Kaius III lay in his satchel, with most of the names struck through and the drains caked in three-year-old blood.

Both seals were placed *after* the bell rang. Dorn walked into a foundry full of murdered dwarves, closed two doors on what was inside them, and walked back out.

The shrine door, too, was sealed with a Black Dove prayer-cloth signed by Sister Maelis Dorn. Behind it Marsk kept two risen ogres, and the bones of hundreds lay heaped at the altar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-09)</small>

## Secret

She is the only person on the continent who can be placed at both ends of the machine. She escorted the Original away from the Korranberg yard under the dove seal after the tuning references were taken, destination withheld from the schedule; and she sealed the doors at that same foundry after the Thirteenth Casting rang and killed everyone in it. She came back to the mountain *after* the disaster and is presumed to have performed the rite on the Thirteenth Casting herself.

## Conduct in role

She keeps a circuit: Korth, then east and south by private coach under escort, gone between nineteen and twenty-six days, then Korth again. She inspects, she consecrates, she seals, and she writes it down. At Thronehold she never drew a weapon. Everyone in Korth knows where Sister Maelis preaches; none of them knows where she goes for the other three weeks.

## Right now

Dorn is alive and her whereabouts are unknown. Her circuit's far end is the hospital in the mountains northeast of Qarth, and the Chorus Ledger goes up that road with her. She still believes Gemma Corso is a grieving girl with a gift for the work. The Inevitables are travelling to the Summer Palace and the Ivory Lazaret on a Ministry of Law warrant, and the ninth great casting hangs in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace itself.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation** — To finish the count.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:** the dawn washing-liturgy · white cloths · silence during the offices · the sung hours · the weight of the courier-case · penitents who grieve properly · a receipt under seal · being deferred to without asking · the number thirteen · preaching in the square.

**Dislikes:** rope · clappers · christenings · being asked why the bell does not ring · novices who touch the metal · foundrymen's curiosity · dockets that survive the brazier · anyone standing between her and a door she has sealed · Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar's temper, which she nonetheless used.

**Vices & Personality flaws** — She took a lay penitent into her confidence as a worthy initiate and recited the Litany to her under seal; the penitent was a NEST agent.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Empress Uriel Qualanthri** — her mistress. Dorn stood at her veiling and is her will when she does not choose to appear. Current.
- **Gemma Corso** — believes her a promising initiate named among the penitents of the Korth house. Gemma considers her a friend and intends to take the Ledger off her. Current, and false on one side.
- **Sister Calvane** — sealed behind Dorn's own writ under the Korramont. Dead twice over.
- **Overseer Velen Marsk** — shrine sealed by her hand after his death warrant was executed. Destroyed by the Inevitables.
- **Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar** — accused Eric in front of him at Thronehold and let his dragonmark do the rest. Killed on Locke Pierce's airship.
- **Eric the Cleric, Chancellor of Sharn** — she named him a murderer in open assembly.
- **The Korranberg Foundry Trust** — escorted the Original away from their yard under her seal; the Trust petitioned that no further reference work be sent to them.

**Hobbies & Pets** — None recorded.

**Items:** **the Chorus Ledger** — a bound registry in grey leather, dove seal in white wax, chained to a courier-case that never leaves her reach or her cell. A novice called it *the mother-book of the Houses*: which house, which bell, which consecration-day. Gemma had one look at it closed and none at it open. It travels under that seal. · The dove seal itself, seen by a muleteer on escort papers going up the Qarth road month after month, and matched by him to the woman who preaches in Korth.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Black Doves (Sister-Inquisitor) · the Houses of Mercy (inspecting officer) · the Empire, as the Black Doves' delegate at Thronehold.

**Holdings:** the cathedral-house at Korth, in practice if not in title · her cell there · standing at the Ivory Lazaret, northeast of Qarth, which is where her circuit ends.

**Word of Condemnation:** at Thronehold she spoke once, in a room of delegates, and named Eric responsible for Hallorn d'Lyrandar's death in front of the boy's father.
