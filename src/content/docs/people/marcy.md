**Mountain Dwarf · Bard, Level 3 · Unaffiliated · Chaotic Good · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* "Marcy" <small>— the label on the character sheet's file. The sheet's own character-name field is blank.</small>

**Marcy** is a mountain dwarf bard recorded on a complete character sheet dated the fifteenth of March: rolled up, equipped, and given a personality and a spell list. The name at the top of the file is the player's label. The character was never named in the world, and the sheet does not state a gender.

Age, height, weight, eyes, hair, skin, deity, appearance, backstory and allies are all empty on the sheet, and the name occurs in none of the seventy-two converted campaign documents. She is not among the eight champions knighted at Harloch — that roll is Kara, Roland, Yoshi, Denver, Fulori, Drefan, Viceroy Couch and Brother Magnus — and she cannot be matched to Denver, who the record treats as male and as Yoshi's trainee.

## What the sheet says

Chaotic Good mountain dwarf, entertainer background, 900 experience. AC 13, 18 hit points, a 25-foot stride, proficiency +2. STR 12 · DEX 14 · CON 10 · INT 12 · WIS 13 · CHA 15. Darkvision, Dwarven Resilience, Stonecunning, Dwarven Armour Training. Bardic Inspiration d6, Jack of All Trades, Song of Rest, Cutting Words <small>(a College of Lore feature; the college itself is not named on the sheet)</small>, Expertise.

The skill spread is a performer's, not an adventurer's: **Performance +6** and **Sleight of Hand +6** at expertise, then Acrobatics, Persuasion and Stealth at +4. She speaks Common and Dwarvish and plays bagpipes, flute, horn and lyre; she also has brewer's supplies and a disguise kit.

**Spells** (DC 12): Thunderclap and Vicious Mockery; Feather Fall, Healing Word, Speak with Animals; Invisibility, Phantasmal Force, Blindness/Deafness.

## Beliefs

She believes that when she performs she makes the world better than it was — the stated ideal, filed under *Beauty*. She believes nobody stays angry at her or around her for long, because she can defuse any amount of tension. She believes she wants to be famous, whatever it takes, and lists that same appetite as her flaw a line later.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation:** Fame.

**Likes & Dislikes:** Likes: performing · costumes · brewing · defusing a room. Dislikes: Unknown.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** "I'll do anything to win fame and renown."

**Items:** leather armour, a dagger, a rapier, a bedroll, five candles, **three costumes**, five days' rations, a waterskin, a disguise kit, bagpipes and a flute. Fifteen gold. Under Treasure, one entry: **"a love letter."** No magic items. Nobody is recorded as having written the letter.
