> This is the group chat for the epic D&D quest which started on the Evening Star and the tragic death of Brownsleeves.

<small>— Dave, opening the Temporal Holdings chat. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-07-06)</small>

**Halfling · Passenger of the *Evening Star* · The Short King of the Moon · Warlock of the Waning One · Dead**

*Also known as:* Francois Jean Pedro Brownsleeves · the Short King · Brownsleevesmetra

**Brownsleeves** is a halfling passenger of the *Evening Star* and JL's character at the opening of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), who died when the ship went down. A Brownsleeves from another loop of time came to the Moon in the deep past, ruled part of it as the Short King and taught its people science, and met the party there in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/). When the party came back to the deep past a second time, the Short King had been seduced by Drefanmetra, [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), and had become his warlock. He joined the party, JL played him in the finale, and he died in the final round of the fight against Cthulhu. The Starfall era has a resort on Selune, the moon of Toril, called [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/).

## The Evening Star

The *Evening Star* was the flagship privateer of the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/), under Captain [Ysmir St. Cloud](/people/ysmir-st-cloud/), and the chronicle opens aboard her. See [Arc I — Hope in Exile](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/). Brownsleeves died in her wreck, and Ysmir St. Cloud's body "went down with Brownsleeves" on the *Evening Star*. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-05)</small>

After the ship and her captain disappeared, the Company's Special Projects department blamed a group of people previously unknown to the Company: [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/), [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), [Drefan](/people/drefan/) Gallidan, [Yoshi](/people/yoshi/), [Kara](/people/kara/) and Brownsleeves. The Company's archive lists him as:

> Francois Jean Pedro Brownsleeves, halfling commoner
>
> No information beyond his name on the manifest.

<small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, SCTC Archives.)</small>

The table marked the voyage's anniversaries. On the second, Jenny, who played Kara, wrote: "Happy two year anniversary of the final voyage of the Evening Star, the deaths of Ismear Saint Cloud and Brownsleeves, the awakening of Silverbane, and the dawn of the Age of Infantas." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-08)</small> On the fourth, Dave: "It is the fourth anniversary of the voyage of the Evening Star, rest in piece Brownsleeves". <small>(Sic on "piece". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-09)</small>

## The Short King

In the deep past, before the Starfall, the party reached [the Moon](/places/the-moon/), a forest world of primitive proto-eladrin who could not communicate by words. What examples they had came from others: "Brownsleeves, St. Cloud, the Dragons, and the Aboleth." Brownsleeves, the Short King, had ruled a small portion of the Moon since his arrival. Rather than enjoy a life of luxury, he spent all his time teaching his subjects "the basics of science and good living that he can remember". When he saw the party he was overjoyed and wept, because he had seen them all die, and he wanted to know whether they knew how to get back. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part I.)</small> See [the Lunar Fae](/organizations/lunar-fae/).

He told them that his own gang had split up when they arrived in the past:

- [Kara](/people/kara/), [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/), [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/), Stump and [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) went "to steal the power to move the moon into the path of the Leviathan and deflect it back into space";
- [Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), Cypher, [Yoshi](/people/yoshi/) and [Stoth](/people/stoth-youngglade/) went to find the Aboleth city and stop St. Cloud;
- [Drefan](/people/drefan/) and Brownsleeves came to warn the moon people to relocate. When Drefan saw what would be destroyed he refused, and left to stop Kara and the others who meant to move the moon. None of them ever returned. Brownsleeves stayed, since the Earth would be destroyed by the blast and he might have a better chance to survive on the Moon.

<small>(Arc V, Starfall Part I.)</small>

The party's own [Stoth](/people/stoth-youngglade/) came back from the deep past. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-open-questions.md)</small>

Dave's summary of what the party knew afterwards included that "Brownsleeves' party split up to go to Rlyeh and Dragon Mtn". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-11)</small> Patrick, who played Magnus, suspected him: "I’m not fully sure that brown sleeves isn’t a dox if some kind, but I might just be paranoid". JL answered: "Brownsleeves ain't no fugazi". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-04-25)</small>

### Another loop

The Short King is Brownsleeves from another loop. Dave: "Technically this isn’t Brownsleeves it’s Brownsleevesmetra or some other loop". In that loop Brownsleeves survived the *Evening Star* and was "along for the whole ride". Kara and St. Cloud were partners there (see [Karametra](/people/karametra/)), and "Like wise Drefan and Brownsleeves see the paradise of the moon and change their minds too". <small>(Sic on "Like wise". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

## The return to the Moon

When the party came back to the deep past a second time, to help [Karametra](/people/karametra/) move the moon and so fulfil the Wish Kara cast on arriving in Antiquity, they went to the Moon again. Had they gone there before the ritual, Dave said, the Short King "would have demanded you not to move the moon and he would have stowed away and sabotaged your ship to prevent it", but "because you zapped there so quickly no chance for him to stop you". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

This time the Short King had been seduced by Drefanmetra, [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), and was a warlock of the Waning One (Archfey). He joined the party.

JL built him as a rogue and a warlock with a pact with Drefanmetra. Dave: "That is awesome, follows the waning one". His crown is moonwood: "Moonwood" (Dave), "Petrified moon wood" (JL). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

His pact weapon is **Pewterthorn**. Asked whether Brownsleeves had "a bodega version of Silverbane", Dave answered "I think yes but probably without the Drefan rune". The blade was fashioned from a great sword into a rapier, and Dave named it: "Calling it Pewterthorn". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small> Later he added: "Pewterthorn can definitely WAX". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-06-23)</small> See [Waxing](/items/the-bane-blades/) and [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/).

## Death

JL played Brownsleeves in the finale at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and he died in the final round of the fight against Cthulhu. See [Arc VIII — End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## Brownsleeve City

In the Starfall era, Brownsleeve City is a sunny beachside resort community on Selune, the moon of Toril, where rookie agents of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) hunted illegal artificial intelligence in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. See [Brownsleeve City Nights](/history/brownsleeve-city-nights/).
