**Lawyer · Counsel to the previous deed holder · Alive · Unresolved**

<small>Also known as: Candy Crush</small>

Candy Crush is a lawyer, recorded only as a large man, retained to represent the previous holder of the deeds to [Hopper](/people/hopper/)'s Magic Emporium and the Arcade next door. He was named to the party by Hopper at the Emporium, in the same conversation in which they revealed they now held both deeds: there is a lawyer, a large man named Candy Crush, who would represent the previous deed holder. That deed holder was Dr. Edwin Graves, who is dead, and whose property portfolio passed to the people who killed him. Candy Crush has never appeared on screen and has made no recorded claim.

## The estate

The previous deed holder was Dr. Edwin Graves — publicly the owner of Circuit City Chip Enhancement Parlor, privately the secret owner of BioTec through shell companies and inherited assets including the estate of [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/), and the Cog Collector who abducted and dismembered Cogborn for Project E.D.E.N. Graves is dead. His property portfolio — the Emporium, the Arcade, the trio's own apartment building and more besides — passed to the people who killed him, in the sense that they walked out of his laboratory holding the deeds.

That leaves a man whose professional obligation is to a dead serial murderer's estate, with a client list assembled through shell companies, holding the legal claim to a substantial part of the Lower City. Nobody has met him. Nobody has served him. Nobody has asked what else was in the portfolio.

> While there you also learned about the lawyer, a large man named Candy Crush, who would represent the previous deed holder.
