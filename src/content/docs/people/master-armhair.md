**Half-elf · Loremaster of the Hall of Records · Adviser to the Imperial court on dragon lore · Alive, departing for the Korramont**

*Also known as:* Armhair

**Master Armhair** is an elderly half-elf loremaster of [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/) in Korth and an adviser to the Imperial court on dragon lore. An orphan of old-town Korth, he assembled the closest thing in existence to a complete family tree of all Eberron's dragons, traced back to the progenitors; the Empire seized the manuscript, and he rebuilt the work from contemporaneous accounts and courtiers' journals and hid the new copy in the Hall of Records itself, shelved under a false index as a treatise on the mating habits of the creatures of the Underdark. From it he argues that a great dragon called **Viagra** came to King Jarret Ilwenarn of Karrnath with a bargain the king accepted, that the sick child of the Queen's Lullaby, Prince Kaius, died and was replaced, and that **Emperor Kaius III is that dragon** and the same being as Kaius I. He gave the Inevitables the index number, asked them to preserve the manuscript, and is leaving for the **Korramont** to find **Valtrex** and finish his book.

## Physical description

Elderly, and unmistakably an old town boy the moment he opens his mouth — the Korthish accent is thick and he has never lost it. He goes nervous and quiet around Imperial officers and does not hide it well. <small>No other description is on the record.</small>

## The Hall of Records

The Hall of Records has been a library, a university and a wizard's tower across a very long history, and is now the Empire's holding place for its most controlled material. It is a resource for several different hierarchies at once, which is why scholars frequent it and loiter in it at all hours; it is also the building from which **every record naming a specific dragon has been removed by order**. Armhair works there.

## The adviser and the heretic

Publicly, Master Armhair advises the Emperor and his court on matters of dragon lore when he is required to. Privately, he has assembled the closest thing in existence to a complete family tree of all Eberron's dragons, traced back to the progenitors. His original manuscript was seized — *"My life's work led to questions the Empire did not want asked"* — and he simply kept learning, and hid the new copy in the Hall of Records itself under the false index. He handed the Inevitables the index number and asked them to preserve it.

## Competence and circle

His method is contemporaneous accounts and courtiers' journals since scrubbed from the archives, and he is honest about its limits: the Emperor's rumoured illness **does not fit his hypothesis**, and he says so rather than explaining it away. His one companion is [Jelton Ahn](/people/jelton-ahn/), a Leonin scholar who appointed himself Armhair's protector against instruction — *"He insisted on it. I told him not to come."* Armhair is an orphan and has no family the record names.

## The thesis

A great dragon called Viagra — the most powerful known, said to have toyed with the forces that govern reality — vanished from the scrolls long before the elves came to Khorvaire, and Armhair has traced it well past that vanishing. He believes it came to **King Jarret Ilwenarn** of Karrnath with a bargain the king accepted; that the sick child of the Queen's Lullaby, **Prince Kaius**, died and was replaced; and that **Emperor Kaius III is that dragon**, and is the same being as Kaius I. He names it a blasphemy of the highest order and invites his listeners to have him executed for it.

> "This is a blasphemy of the highest order, of course, and you could have me executed."

## Beliefs

He believes the Emperor of Karrnath is an ancestral dragon wearing three generations of the same face.

He believes a scholar's obligation is to finish the book, and that dying on the way to finishing it is an acceptable cost.

He believes the people who freed Valtrex should come to him **before telling anyone else** what they learn next.

## Conduct in role

He advises the court accurately and volunteers nothing. He conducts his real work in false indexes and midnight meetings on the lower plaza, and when a Star Advocate of the Ministry of Law told him she had stood in front of a black dragon and spoken with it, he tested her with what he could verify before handing over anything of his own. Then he handed over all of it at once, to strangers.

## What he told them about black dragons

- **None sighted in over 430 years** by written record.
- **Three** descended from the First, all presumed destroyed.
- All went underground when Khyber made the Manifest Zones, and were trapped as the caverns closed.
- **Valtrex** was the youngest and smallest, and therefore the only one able to move through the narrow ways — *"He would have had to travel in and out of Hell."*
- **Dragon blood** is *"an incredibly potent alchemical ingredient... capable of quite a bit more than mortal blood."*

He had narrowed the Korramont sighting to those three before [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) confirmed which one it was.

## The manuscript

Retrieved from the index he gave them, it contained a family tree of Eberron's dragons traced to the progenitors, an identification of **Valtrex's father**, and an extended thread running through the **Mark of Death**, the birth of **Erandis Vol** and this same ancestral dragon — confirming Renn Tal's earlier suspicion about Vol, the Eternal Guardian and Kaius. Set against the Litany's promise that *the dragon will be reborn*, the Inevitables concluded that [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) is trying to bear a son by a dragon, and that Valtrex, bled for three years, does not know Viagra exists and believes the heirs are his.

## Origin

An orphan of old-town Korth who became a loremaster of its greatest archive and rose to advise the court of an Empire on the subject of dragons. Somewhere in the middle of that career he assembled a genealogy that reached back to the progenitors, and the questions it raised were the wrong questions. The Empire took the manuscript. It did not take him, and it did not stop him, and the years since have gone into rebuilding the work from courtiers' journals in a building where the relevant shelves have been emptied.

## Right now

He is leaving for the Korramont to find Valtrex and finish his book, and he fully expects to die doing it. *"This is my only chance to finish my book, then who cares?"* He carries the **Crown of Seven Stars** as a gift to the dragon from the three who freed him, with instructions to bargain rather than fight, a warning about the fungal infestation, and the route to the black lake. He was told never to wear it. He was not told the rest.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Hall of Records, Korth (loremaster) · adviser to the Imperial court on dragon lore

**Holdings:** A false index number in the Hall of Records, behind which stands the only complete dragon genealogy in existence.

**Items:** The **Crown of Seven Stars** — ancient infernal work in a rare ore, taken from Valtrex's hoard beneath the Korramont, carried by Armhair as a gift to its previous owner. It is cursed: handling it reveals the bearer's location to its infernal creator, wearing it permits scrying, and attunement summons the devil to offer a pact that cannot safely be refused. · His reconstructed manuscript, hidden in the stacks under a treatise on the mating habits of creatures of the Underdark.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

Finishing the book.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Contemporaneous accounts
- Courtiers' journals
- False indexes
- Being asked a question he has spent his life answering
- Firsthand witnesses to dragons
- Jelton Ahn, whom he told not to come

**Dislikes:**

- Imperial officers
- Records removed by order
- Being made to advise the court
- Explaining away inconvenient evidence, including his own
- Being protected

**Vices & Personality flaws**

He will trade the most dangerous secret in the Empire to anyone who can prove they have seen a dragon.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Jelton Ahn** — Leonin scholar, self-appointed protector, and a genuine historian in his own right. Alive; leaving Korth.
- **Gemma Corso** — met him as Star Advocate Deirdre Moro and admitted to having spoken with a black dragon. Alive.
- **Valtrex** — the subject he is walking into a mountain to interview. Alive, freed, and claiming the Korramont.
- **The Imperial court** — consults him on dragon lore. It also confiscated his life's work.
- **A half-elf colleague** — overheard in the Hall of Records hunting information on the Korramont and its dwarves. Not pursued.

**Hobbies & Pets**

Dragon genealogy, pursued to the point of capital heresy.
