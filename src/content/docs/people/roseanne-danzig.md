**Princess of Cormyr · Heir of King Glenn Danzig · Wife of Zanzibar**

*Also known as:* Rosanne · Rosanne Conner-Barr Danzig · Roseanna · Princess of Paenis · the princess

Roseanne Danzig is a princess of Cormyr, the eldest daughter of King Glenn Danzig and the heir he named to his throne. During [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/), with the king turned into a snail, [Kara](/people/kara/) impersonated him and persuaded Roseanne to marry [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), to join the thrones of Cormyr and [Punis](/places/punis/). When the king was turned back he offered Zanzibar her hand anyway. Before [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) the party weighed bringing her near the fighting, where Zanzibar's paladin power depended on her nearness. After the battle Zanzibar married her, and when the king fell ill Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr.

## The king's heir

Roseanne is one of King Glenn Danzig's children, who were in the castle for Candlenights with the Duchess of Marsember "entertaining the royal children for the holidays". <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small> Asked who was next in line if the king died, Dave answered: "His oldest daughter Rosanne Conner-Barr Danzig first of her name". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-21)</small> See [Suzail](/places/suzail/).

## Betrothed in the coup

The party stormed Castle Danzig on Candlenights Eve and turned the king into a snail. Dave the next morning: "Well, you nearly killed yourselves and the king and his kids and somehow orchestrated a fragile coup to make Zanzibar king of cormyr." He announced that the next session "will start with a wedding", "Between Zanzibar of Punis and Roseanne Danzig, Princess of Paenis". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-06)</small> Before it, he set the scene: "the action will start immediately after the conversation between Kara-as-King and his daughter, princess Roseanne, where she agreed to marry Zanzibar the next morning". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16)</small>

> Kara has impersonated the king and convinced his daughter, Roseanne, that for his bravery, and as a matter of political strategy, Roseanne is to marry Crown Prince Zanzibar to unite the thrones of Suzail and Punis under a single family.

<small>— Arc III Part VIII</small>

Of Roseanne herself:

> Princess Roseanne is savvy was raised for this very occasion. She does not see through Kara’s disguise and is genuinely interested in furthering her place to take the throne as Queen of Men and to control an infanta herself. She might be convinced of the value of cooperation with elves and dwarves if brought to Motherstone, or successfully persuaded by someone she trusts. She is genuinely attracted to Zanzibar, who reminds her of her father. She distrusts the War Wizards as well.

<small>— Arc III Part VIII. Sic.</small>

## The succession

While the players debated whether Zanzibar or Roseanne would rule, Dave set out the law:

> The king has named Roseanne his heir, Zanzibar marrying her would combine the thrones of Cormyr and Punis in the next generation
>
> Zanzibar can obviously claim the throne if he wants and if Roseanne makes him king that would also be legal
>
> Or he can serve as prince regent to Roseanne’s Queen

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24</small>

Kara's player Jenny asked, "Why do y'all think Zani would be king and not Roseanne queen? The queen of england's husband is a duke." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small> The wedding did not take place during the coup. After the crisis Stonecypher's player Nico proposed securing the Arcaneum first: "Once that's taken care of and the area is safe, if Roseanne and Zanzibar still want to get married we can have a wedding!!" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-30)</small>

Dave later summarised the plot: the party had used intelligence on breaking into Castle Danzig "to assassinate Valen and Celion and almost King Danzig himself, using a forced marriage for Zanzibar to assume the Cormyrean throne". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-08-11)</small>

## The king's offer

When King Glenn Danzig was turned back from being a snail, he saw why Zanzibar had held the coup, appreciated his "rooting out the evil of Valen and his tainting by the Leviathan", and offered Roseanne's hand in marriage anyway. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-punis-and-roseanne.md)</small> See [Valen](/people/valen/).

## The River Lis

Before the battle JL, who played Zanzibar, reported that "Danzig wasn’t keen on bringing Roseanne to the battle." Dave: "I wonder what she has to say about it". JL: "If I can get Roseanne to come we will have our power back. But she’d be so vulnerable." <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

A month later Dave asked the players, "You thinking you'll put the princess and Tico in a tiny hut for the battle?" Nico asked how close she needed to be for Zanzibar to get his powers:

> I’d estimate it drops off a bit, so within 1/4 mile gets you level 7+ spells, and it drops off a level per mile from there
>
> and remember, it's not just Zan's powers, it's half the Purple Dragons as well.

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22</small>

Dave counted "1000 paladin of Zarus out there" on the allied side. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small> See [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) and [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/).

## Marriage

After the Battle of the River Lis, Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne of Cormyr. Later the king fell ill, and Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr after the wedding. After the Infanta War he founded the Empire and set his imperial capital in Suzail. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-punis-and-roseanne.md)</small> See [House Zanzibar](/organizations/house-zanzibar/) and [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/).

Roseanne is the mother of Zanzibar's child **Zara**, an Infanta. Of the Infanta on the field at the River Lis Dave wrote: "Tico and Mora at a minimum, likely Zara in utero". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> Before the battle JL, who played Zanzibar, planned to "convince my preggo girlfriend to come to an almost unwinnable battle". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small>

Years after the campaign, trying to recall a name from another game, Dave offered "Rosanne". Nico: "Wasn’t that Zanzibars wife’s name?" Dave: "Oh right". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small>
