**Human noble · Merchant prince · Saltmarsh, Oerth · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/)**

*Also known as:* Aubrick

Aubreck Drallion is a human merchant prince who once counted himself among the richest folk of his city, with a trade fleet of hundreds of ships. He sold off much of his wealth for property deeds and promissory notes to monopolize a spice trade in the southern jungles, and sent them south aboard his flagship, the *Emperor of the Waves*, which vanished in a storm and left him an ordinary merchant. When the ship was sighted adrift as a ghost ship, he hired the *Soul of Winter* and, through his butler Vertheg, the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) to board it and bring back the box that held his fortune. The party recovered the box and did not double-cross him, and by January 2022 he was expected to be voted a seat on the council of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/).

## Description

Aubreck is a male human noble who has seen better days. His mansion is large but run down, and his clothes have not been fashionable for at least a year. He keeps his iron-gray hair pulled back in a short ponytail, and his mustache is neatly tended. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## The fall of a merchant prince

Aubreck's trade fleet was one of the largest and most prosperous of its kind, with hundreds of ships under his banner carrying goods across the world. Years ago his contacts in the southern jungles reported that he could monopolize trade in rare spices and herbs with heavy investment there. He sold off many of his assets, converted the proceeds to property deeds and promissory notes, and secured that portable wealth aboard the *Emperor of the Waves*, the foremost ship in his fleet. A storm separated the ship from its escort and it was never heard from again, and Aubreck was reduced to the lifestyle of an ordinary merchant. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

> The true fate of Emperor of the Waves is known only to a few.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## Salvagers wanted

Fifteen days before he approached the party, Aubreck received word that the *Emperor of the Waves* had been spotted adrift in the southern sea, apparently a ghost ship, about five days out from land. He spent a sizable part of his savings to hire a ship, the *Soul of Winter*, and a crew, and charged his half-orc butler **Vertheg** with finding adventurers to board the derelict. Vertheg is tall, lanky and awkward, with a single tusk jutting from his lower jaw and warty skin that contrasts with his fine, neat clothing, and he often carries out Aubreck's quasi-legal dealings. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

Aubreck was one of three people who approached the party at the end of the council meeting where they announced the capture of the [*Stinking Albatross*](/items/the-stinking-albatross/); the others were [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/) and [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/). He offered them 10 percent, 10,000 gp, to recover a magic box of promissory notes and property deeds worth 100,000 gp, with 200 gp in advance. The box is made of shiny blue metal and packed in a wooden crate branded with a large letter A. It opens only with a password known to Aubreck. The notes are drawn on a lawful good temple that pays them out only after the original owner of the assets gives permission. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

For the mission he put gear aboard: "studded leather armor for any character who wants it, two light crossbows with twenty bolts each, five 50-foot coils of rope, two grappling hooks, twelve torches, two healer’s kits, six vials of antitoxin, two potions of healing, and two sacks for carrying treasure out of the ship." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-19)</small>

## The box

The party boarded the *Emperor of the Waves* on 2021-11-19. Nico: "we did out thing and got the box and killed the baddies. Only thing we didn’t do was check the ship". Dave: "Ship had a few trinkets but you didn’t miss much, more spiders". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

Before handing it over the party argued over what to do with it. Dave described its contents as "notes and deeds which includes his families whole fortune and land", "Including the Goat", and weighed the choice: "Giving it to Drallion makes him rich again and a huge ally", while "Trying to take it makes us at Salmor level + the church + the navy and makes drallion an enemy". [Scrunt](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/)'s player JL wanted to hold it for ransom; Nico wanted to "look into this guys past and family", asking "What if he’s actually bad news and could possibly be trouble for us and what we want to do if he has more power?" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

They gave it back. Dave, after the session of 2021-11-27: "Sorry it was a short sesh but still fun and could have gone different if you decided to double cross Drallion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-28)</small>

## Keledek's debt and the council

Dave's list of open quests at the end of 2021 included "3) Get Drallion to clear Keledek’s debt". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small> JL took it up to run <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-27)</small>, and on 2022-01-07 the party succeeded. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small>

In the same session Gellen Primewater, by JL's account as DM, was "assuming the council will vote Dolores and Aubrick seats." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-07)</small> Nico reported that Dolores had left Drallion at 1 hit point with *shocking grasp*. Dave said Gabriella resented that she had "risked her life to save that guy's fortune" without thanks, and called her "a bully to punks like drallion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small> See [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/).

**Holdings:** a large, run-down mansion; the deeds and notes in the blue metal box; the *Emperor of the Waves*.
