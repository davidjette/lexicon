**Lightfoot Halfling · Rogue · Temple Holdings LLC · Chaotic Good · Alive**

*Also known as:* Fulori Splitheal <small>(the spelling on her own backstory, and the joke the other servants made of it)</small> · Fulori

**Fulori Splitheel** is a Lightfoot Halfling rogue of Temple Holdings LLC, the halfling who came to Snowdown looking for stolen children and found a stolen goddess instead. She was enslaved as a small child herself — thrown into a sale "to sweeten the pot," because a halfling was too weak to be worth buying for labour — and everything she is good at, she learned in a house where she was property. Set to mind the master's four children, she was taught to read, write, forge, fight and ride by them; when a Good Man's crowd killed the master, she forged the papers that got the children aboard a ship, and lost them to two men with torches on the road. She appears in [Arc I](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/) and in no later arc.

## Appearance

Sixty-four years old, four feet even, forty-four pounds. Light blue eyes, dark brown hair, light skin.

## Bearing and arms

AC 13, 10 hit points, a 25-foot stride. DEX 16 and nothing else above 13. **Expertise in Stealth (+7)**, plus Sleight of Hand and Acrobatics at +5, Lucky, Brave, Halfling Nimbleness and Naturally Stealthy. She speaks Common and Halfling. Her tool proficiencies are thieves' tools, a playing card set, and a **forgery kit**.

## Origin

She was raised in the servants' hall by the older staff, who kept her as a kind of mascot. She learned to keep a straight face, to move silently changing chamber pots, and to pilfer just enough to eat. The name itself is a servants' joke, made at her expense, and she kept it.

Promoted to child-minder for the master's four children — three girls and a boy — she got, sideways, the education a slave was not supposed to have:

> The girls, young ladies in training, taught me to read and write, how to tell secrets and how to use them. I learned how to form letters and showed something of a knack for copying hand writing and duplicating documents. On particularly nice days notes might show up, in the masters hand, that excused all of the children from lessons…\
> \
> The boy taught me to fight, to ride and the value of honor.

Then the Good Men came. Her own account is precise about what they are: "In times of trouble there often arise **Good Men**, charismatic leaders with questionable motives and the ability to whip people into a frenzy for their own purposes." One of them pointed, a crowd rushed a coach in a market town, the master was killed and his wife taken. The son fought his way clear and led them straight home.

Fulori forged documents to pass the children off as common merchants and get them aboard a ship out of the lower harbour. Looking back from the road, she saw the house burning; she learned later that most of the servants were killed and the survivors sold. And then, in the dark, on the run:

> I told the children to wait while I scouted ahead… Suddenly I heard shouting and turned to the torches of two men standing over the children. My heart froze and flew back to them

Her backstory breaks off mid-sentence. What happened between that night and her arrival on Snowdown is not recorded.

## Public and private

Publicly she is the party's quiet professional: she watches Leftfork Farm from the woods, makes contact with two halfling servants gathering wood, and reports that "Good Men are everywhere around here, and the presence of dark elf slaves here doesn't bode well for the residents of the house." Privately every step of it is a search. Her wards are not at Leftfork. They may be with the Forsworn, who are responsible for dozens of kidnappings across Snowdown — who "sometimes use the children in their ghastly rituals," and other times "brutalize the children and turn them into fanatics who infiltrate their homes and assassinate their parents."

## Signature capability — reading the document

The scroll taken from the dead courier at Harloch is meant for Thaddeus Shor and is strangely brief, with the words *"Forsworn Activity on the Rise. Beware"* bizarrely centred on the page. Fulori is the one who looks at the layout rather than the text and concludes that a longer message is hidden there in invisible ink — the thread that exposes the sale of Leftfork and the Company's brokerage of it.

## Beliefs

She believes freedom is the most important thing and is willing to break the law to help those who are enslaved — the ideal written on her sheet. She believes she has "a natural disdain for masters of any type," and hides it behind a cool demeanour. She believes history is best left in the past and some mistakes cannot be undone.

## Right now

At the close of Arc I, Fulori, [Yoshi](/people/yoshi/) and [Couch](/people/cuthbert-couch/) race back to Leftfork and find they have made it in time — the green glowing orb of magic still surrounds the village, so Untari and the Infanta are still safe inside. The Bladesingers intercept them on the approach.

Fulori appears only in Hope in Exile, and is not named in Motherstone or any later arc. Her wards were never found.

**Organizations / Groups:** Temple Holdings LLC · the Lusmundi, named so and one of the Champions of the Evening Star at the Conclave at Leftfork

**Holdings:** the Old Temple at Harloch, offered to the company by the Jarl for 25,000 platinum <small>(Hope in Exile, Part VI; the purchase itself is not recorded)</small>

**Items:** thieves' tools, a forgery kit, a playing card set. Her sheet lists no coin, no treasure and no magic items.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation:** To find the four children she was minding when the torches came.

**Likes & Dislikes:** Likes: locks · cards · a document with something hidden in it. Dislikes: masters of any type · Good Men · being asked to bluff.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** "As a lock pick, look out, hustler or thief I'm among the best… just don't ask me to bluff. (I have a tick when I'm lying)" <small>(the Flaws field of her sheet)</small>

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **The master's four children** — three girls and a boy; her wards, her family, and the reason she is on Snowdown. Whereabouts unresolved.
- **The boy** — taught her to fight, ride and value honour; killed one attacker and wounded another escaping the market town.
- **The Forsworn** — the most likely holders of her wards, and the enemy of the whole arc.
- **Yoshi** and **Cuthbert Couch** — her partners on the run back to Leftfork.
- **Untari** — the enslaved elven chief at Leftfork, whose people the party frees.

**Hobbies & Pets:** cards.
