**Human wizard · Blackstaff of Waterdeep · Missing; ally at the Egg**

**Khelben Arunsun**, the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, is a human wizard and one of the "oldest and wisest of spellcasters and engineers" [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) petitioned when she set out to repair the broken [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) — alongside the archmagi of the Arcaneum, the Grand Wizard of Thay, the arcanagnomes of Greygarden and the forgemasters of the Sword Mountains. <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part II.)</small> He was last seen at the Yawning Portal the night he talked to [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), went into Undermountain, and is held there by the Drow. He appears as an ally at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/).

## Disappearance

> No can find Blackstaff. Asking around the Yawning Portal, he was last seen the night he talked to St. Cloud.

<small>— the Khelben Arunsun Ally Card.</small>

Billy Zeps "knows that he hopped into Undermountain late while no one else was looking," and Durnan knows "he's gone into Undermountain to look into reports of the Drow overrunning it" — and has "sold Blackstaff's location out to them." **The Blackstaff is held by the Drow in Undermountain.** <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV. That is the same Undermountain the male Drow rebels had taken, and beneath which [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) lies in the third epoch.)</small>

How he left Undermountain, and how he came to be on a card at the end, is not recorded. [Holden Tudyx](/people/holden-tudyx/) and the Blackstaff's fellow archmagi also stopped being available after meeting [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/).

## At the Egg

**White — Channel Forces: "Creature w/i 60 ft., has +4 to spell attacks or -4 to saving throws against spells until end of its next turn."**

**Black — Absolution: "Dispel or reverse any spell or magical effect affecting up to 10 creatures within 60 ft."**
