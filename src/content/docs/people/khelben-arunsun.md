**Human wizard · Blackstaff of Waterdeep · Missing; ally at the Egg**

**Khelben Arunsun**, the Blackstaff of Waterdeep, is a human wizard and one of the "oldest and wisest of spellcasters and engineers" [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) petitioned when she set out to repair the broken [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) — alongside the archmagi of the Arcaneum, the Grand Wizard of Thay, the arcanagnomes of Greygarden and the forgemasters of the Sword Mountains. <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part II.)</small> He was last seen at the Yawning Portal the night he talked to [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), went into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), and is held there by the Drow. He appears as an ally at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/).

## Disappearance

> No can find Blackstaff. Asking around the Yawning Portal, he was last seen the night he talked to St. Cloud.

<small>— the Khelben Arunsun Ally Card.</small>

Billy Zeps "knows that he hopped into Undermountain late while no one else was looking," and Durnan knows "he's gone into Undermountain to look into reports of the Drow overrunning it" — and has "sold Blackstaff's location out to them." **The Blackstaff is held by the Drow in Undermountain.** <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV. That is the same Undermountain the male Drow rebels had taken, and beneath which [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) lies in the third epoch.)</small>

He went down the hole right before Obus revealed himself in the Winking Depths arc, and following his trail took the party straight to the 23rd level of Undermountain, where there was access to the Egg. Dave: "But Blackstaff knew the way down". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

How he left Undermountain, and how he came to be on a card at the end, is not recorded. [Holden Tudyx](/people/holden-tudyx/) and the Blackstaff's fellow archmagi also stopped being available after meeting [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/).

## Across the ages

Blackstaff was present in the Time of Troubles, the era of the building of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small> The party's quest list for that era included "Netheril, find Blackstaff". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) came back through time on an [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) "using a dark crystal provided by Blackstaff". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small> Blackstaff sent Zanzibar back in time with Max to stop St. Cloud, and that Zanzibar is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Dave on the staff itself:

> The black crystal at the end of Blackstaff’s iconic staff was the crystal he procured for you which you never came to retrieve, because St Cloud killed you in the deep past. He kept it his whole multi thousand year life until he gave it to Zanzibar to go back to the deep past to save you. He didn’t save the old you, but met with the next you and went forward in time and ended up meeting him and getting the crystal as planned.

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-07-11)</small>

He was "like 2000 years old", and "His whole race was obliviated at one point". He was able to "figure out where the egg was on his own", and after the obliviation he "obviously has advantages in being able to remember or somewhat understand what maybe happened". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small>

**Black — Absolution: "Dispel or reverse any spell or magical effect affecting up to 10 creatures within 60 ft."**
