**Species unknown · Rogue · Leader of the Holhertz Expedition · Alive**

**Bud Holhertz** is a rogue and the leader of a rival adventuring party that failed to recover the **BALANCE** card from [the Tomb of St. Magnus](/places/the-tomb-of-st-magnus/). A Company seeker named **Lexi** hired the expedition, then teleported away on arrival and left it to its fate; only Bud and Lou Sassle got away. In [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), outside [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/), he shot [Lenore](/people/lenore/) with a crossbow from behind, taking her for Lexi, stole her gold and left her dying.

## Physical description

A rogue with a scraggly mustache, black hair and bad teeth. <small>(The Grub & Grog narrative)</small>

## The Holhertz Expedition

Bud's party were the **Salad Triplets**, Biggs, Wedge and Cobb, a fighter, a barbarian and a ranger, and **Lou Sassle**, a monk. They were hired by a [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) seeker named Lexi, who had a map to a secret entrance to the Tomb of St. Magnus and offered them 5,000 gold each, with miscellaneous treasure and access to the Company portals. On arrival the Salad brothers were killed by a monster, Lexi teleported away and left the party to its fate, and Lou and Bud escaped. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Outside the Grub + Grog

On the rainy night of the incident at the Grub + Grog, Lenore was walking with her steel defender **Johnny** from the carriage house to the inn door when an armored thug who seemed to recognize her stopped her. She was shot by crossbow from behind, poisoned, paralyzed and dying. The thug bashed Johnny to bits, but not before Johnny let out an alarm. The rogue who had shot her came close to her face as she lay dying and sneered at her, thinking she was someone named "Lexi" who had wronged him and his gang. He stole her gold and left her dying. [Maverick](/people/maverick/) ran out, revived her and stopped her bleeding, and Ard Farkle tended her in the inn. <small>(The Grub & Grog narrative)</small>

"Holhertz ambushed and nearly killed Lenore." <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>
