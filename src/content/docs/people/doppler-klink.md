**Cogborn · Tinkerer · Last War veteran · Alive, departed Sharn**

<small>Also known as: Doppler Klank</small>

**Doppler Klink** is a Cogborn tinkerer, a veteran of the Last War, and the proprietor of **Doppler Klink's Arcane Gear**, a stall in the Gear Street Markets of Sharn's Lower City. Born and raised in the Cogs, he survived the hazardous factory lines long before he ever saw a battlefield, served through the Last War, and came home to set up the stall, where he repairs arcane devices, builds custom wands and sleeps in the back room when business is slow. He never formally joined [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) but supported [Preacher](/people/preacher/)'s efforts for years, and he armed the party: the wand he built [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) broke a Mind Flayer's concentration, and the one he built [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was put into [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/). When the Cogborn were guided out of the city through the Spine he closed the shop and travelled with them. Three years later he is among the surviving R.U.S.T. names the Inevitables call on, and is making Horns.

## Physical description

An old man with long grey hair pushed back off a deeply lined face, a grey moustache and short beard, and pale eyes that have been squinting at small work for a long time. He sits on a wooden stool in his own stall in a battered dark leather coat over a work shirt, with armoured fingerless gauntlets and a plated greave strapped over one leg and heavy boots beneath. A blue arcane charge glows between his hands — the wand he is presently building — and a mechanical device with a blue lens is mounted at his shoulder. Around him: toolboxes, cable coils, sandbags, a brazier and hanging market lanterns.

## Origin

Doppler Klink was a factory child in the Cogs before he was a soldier. He had already survived the industrial lines that killed most of his generation before anyone handed him a weapon. He served through the Last War, came home to a district that was slowly being taken apart, and set up the stall. For years he was the Gear Street tinkerer — the man who kept the neighbourhood's implants and devices working — while the Iron Veil seized the factories, replaced Cogborn labour with soldiers, and drove families into the tunnels. He supported Preacher through all of it and never joined anything.

## Gear Street

Gear Street is the Lower City's busiest commercial artery, a crowded stretch of shops, food stalls, scrap vendors and arcane tinkerers serving the residents of the Cogs and the Lowline Ward. Doppler Klink's Arcane Gear is one of its fixtures: a cramped stall where he repairs arcane devices, builds custom wands, and sleeps in the back room when business is slow.

He works closest with [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/), the other Cogs veteran nobody enrolled, and the two of them were waiting together at the stall the morning the party came down to cross into the undercity.

## Under Sharn

He fought crocodiles in a flooded sewer, waited out the Drain Snakes' lair in an Underhive tavern rather than pretend to be a soldier, and then spent an entire night with Snodgrass and [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/) clearing a collapsed tunnel with their hands so that a trapped population could walk out.

## Leaving

When the Cogborn community was finally guided through the Spine, down the Underhive river, past the dead stone golem and out to the mountain tunnel, Doppler made the difficult decision to leave Sharn and travel with his people. He had lived in the Cogs his entire life. He had a shop, a street and a name on it. He closed it.

**Right now:** Doppler left Sharn with the Cogborn moving through the Spine toward the mountain tunnel and Gorgonhorn. He did not stay gone: three years later he is among the surviving R.U.S.T. names the Inevitables call on, he built each of the party a wand of magic missile at cost, and he then got to work making Horns.

## Beliefs

He believes a thing is worth what it costs him to make, which is why he builds for the rebellion at cost. He believes his people come before the city. He believes he has fixed enough of other men's wars.

> "Fix what's broken, improvise what's missing, and stay alive by staying useful."

**Organizations / Groups:** none formally. Cogborn community; long-time quiet supporter of R.U.S.T.
