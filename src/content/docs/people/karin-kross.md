**Species unrecorded · Shopkeeper, Kross Roads Corner Store · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Karin · Karen Kross (variant spelling in the record)</small>

Karin Kross is the shopkeeper of the **Kross Roads Corner Store**, a small general shop in the Sub-line District of Sharn's Lower City, and the ex-wife of the BioTec technician [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/). When new neighbours came in to stock up she told them that Martin had turned up the previous morning claiming he was running from BioTec, that BioTec agents had come by shortly after asking whether she had seen him, and that her son [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/) and his wife [Misty](/people/misty-kross/) had been questioned too. That put the party onto the trail of the man whose keycard had opened the BioTec labs, and it was the Nest's only lead on him. No likeness of her survives, and no description beyond the counter she stands behind.

The shop stands across the street from a long-boarded-up apartment that three foreigners reclaimed from a pair of ghosts. She sells household goods to a neighbourhood that lives among the bones of an old rail system.

She is the ordinary end of an extraordinary family. Her ex-husband Martin works at BioTec, or did; her son Kriss and his wife Misty own the pawn shop up the road that is the front door of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) Whether she knows is not recorded.
