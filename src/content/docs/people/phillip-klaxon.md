**Minor noble · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Father of [Darius Klaxon](/people/darius-klaxon/)**

Phillip Klaxon is a minor noble of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) and the father of [Darius Klaxon](/people/darius-klaxon/), whom he is working to raise to prominence. He married a Darkcember, and their son is heir to [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/). He sent the protocol droid [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) to tutor Darius, dealt with [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) for the RUIN card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/), and planned to put Darius on the Lords' Council by ruining Beef Wellington. After the heist at Tiffany House, Wellington was ruined and the card was gone. Klaxon came with Griddy to [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), where Nicollo betrayed the party and claimed to be in Klaxon's service, and Griddy took RUIN back.

## Description

**Appearance:** "A middle aged black man, a minor noble, like Reginald Val Johnson if he were in Game of Thrones." <small>(Character roster.)</small>

## Family and ambitions

Klaxon married a Darkcember; their son Darius is heir to Tiffany House. He wants his son to become the Panther and take the Panther Mask, and sent Nicollo to Darius as a tutor for leadership and cunning. He has dealt with Griddy Champagne for RUIN, and intends to use the card on a Masked Lord. <small>(DM notes)</small>

In the power plays of Waterdeep he is a "STRIVING MINOR NOBLE elevating his son to prominence, bound to service of an Archdevil." His allies are Griddy and Asmodeus; his enemies include Dyksterhaus; his plot is to ruin Wellington; the threat he faces is failure to satisfy Asmodeus and Griddy; and he desires "prominence, wealth, earthly glory". <small>(DM notes)</small> He is also using [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Tiffany House

At Tiffany House Klaxon told Nicollo about his plan to place Darius on the Lords' Council by ruining Beef Wellington; Darius did not want this. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party witnessed Griddy seducing Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN, before [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) surprised them. Griddy and Jeremiah fought, the party resisted him and stole the card, and Nicollo convinced Klaxon and Griddy that he was on their side and that Jeremiah had taken it.

Afterwards Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep, Wellington was ruined, and the Lords' meeting was four days off. Klaxon was happy, but scared to have lost the card. See [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).

## Fort Maximillien

While [Maverick](/people/maverick/) was being questioned in the upper bastion of Fort Maximillien, General Varkus opened the door, followed by Phillip Klaxon and Griddy Champagne, come to recover RUIN. Klaxon "seems furious". Varkus demanded to know the true reason for the party's attack, and whether they were working with his enemies; Maverick explained himself well, "causing KLAXON to fume - don't listen to him! he is conspiring against us!" <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small> The question Klaxon and Griddy brought for Maverick: "if you're not with Jeremiah and Shemeska, then who are you?" <small>(DM notes)</small>

Nicollo was then brought in and betrayed the party, saying he was in the service of Klaxon, as he had claimed, and had tried to take the card the moment he found out [Lenore](/people/lenore/) had it. Griddy picked up the RUIN card from the desk and slipped it into her bra, and Varkus sentenced Maverick to hang at dawn. <small>(Post-session narrative.)</small>
