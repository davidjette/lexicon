**Robot · Artificer (Artillerist) · The Beholder War · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* 1 · the artillery droid

**1.0** is an artillerist robot built from the parts of a downed ship during [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), in the Season 3 flashback of [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), played by Nico. 1.0 joined the group stranded on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) with [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/), and was taken by [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) when they left the group to recover [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/).

## The Beholder War

In the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, Jim was stranded on Negroni Prime with Barrias, Tillamook and a dozen civilians. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> 1.0 is built from parts of the downed ship. Its gun had been mounted on the ship; among the stranded, only Jim, Barrias and Tillamook carried guns, and everyone else was a civilian. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-15)</small>

In the episode of 15 March 2024, the last of a three-episode flashback, Katrina and Horgus abandoned Jim's group to recover the Demoncore. According to Nico, they left the group with no transportation and no way to communicate, and took the artillery droid with them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> What became of 1.0 is unknown.

## Abilities and equipment

1.0 is an artificer of the Artillerist specialisation with a little cannon: a hand axe with the returning weapon infusion, the enhanced defense infusion, and an eldritch cannon it can create once per short rest. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-15)</small>
