**Dwarf · Wardress and keeper of records · The Imperial Counting House · Level Unknown · Alive, beaten, at the pilgrim camp**

Ilma Vitt is the wardress and keeper of records at [the Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/) in the valley of [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/). She is a grey-haired dwarf of about sixty with ink stains up to her elbows. She trained [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), signed him into the Hall of Records when he was given his first post, and wrote the assessment that got him the job: *"a mediocre accountant with an unusual eye."* <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The reconciliation

Vitt kept a private reconciliation of every probate the Counting House handled, in a cipher of her own. She gave it to [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), whom she took for a real Star Advocate, telling her she was the first person from the Ministry to come up the hill in two years. The Director and his whole staff, she said, were enriching themselves on the property of people who die in the hospital, on donations, and on war profiteering laundered through the house, and they should hang for it. The Lodge's books would complete the chain.

> You can't trust the Director.

The key was eight letters, what you do when you really love someone. Reading over Eric's shoulder as he tried MARRIAGE, she noticed his handwriting: *"You cross your sevens. That's very unusual. Where did you learn accounting?"* The key was MULTIPLY. Then she walked out and locked the Advocate's party in her office. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## Beaten

Director [Aurel Kesk](/people/aurel-kesk/) returned with troopers holding Vitt and accused the Advocate of ordering her out of her own office without a warrant. Eric judged that she was under duress. Kesk's men dragged her into his office and beat her until Deirdre demanded her as a witness. Vitt told the party they had to get out at once, and went to the pilgrim camp, where she sleeps most nights, to meet them there. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>
