**Wood Elf · Druid · Temple Holdings LLC / House Gallidann / the Lusmundii · Level 5 (sheet) · Alive**

*Also known as:* Drefan of Gallidan · <small>(the Mundi, seeing Silverbane, genuflected and thought "that Drefan may be their king")</small>

**Drefan** is a wood elf druid sworn to [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/) and a member of the [Lusmundii](/organizations/the-lusmundii/), the druidic cult of Elistrae, and he bears the moonblade [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/). The chronicle first names him when the Moongate sets the shipwreck survivors down on Snowdown; he came ashore at Leftfork Farm, found his own people enslaved under the Shor clan, and took Silverbane out of Daenan Gallidan's sarcophagus aboard the *Evening Star*; at Bask Falls Barrow the spectral echo of [Star Quendi](/people/star-quendi/) blessed his inheritance of it on the condition that he pledge to live and die for her house. In the crystal city the Mundi saw the blade and took him for their king. Hidden aboard as a cloud of mist, he seized the helm of the Company's fleeing flagship the **[Excelsior](/items/the-arielle/)** at Caer Westphal, and the captured ship was rechristened the **Arielle**. He carries Silverbane into the muster at Motherstone as one of five sibling blades.

**Silverbane and House Gallidan.** Drefan is a **Gallidan**, and he carries [Silverbane](/items/the-bane-blades/) — the wood elves' shard of Perfect moonstone. He took it from **Daenan Gallidan's sarcophagus**, which [Ysmir St. Cloud](/people/ysmir-st-cloud/) was smuggling in the Deep Storage of the *Evening Star*, beneath forty starving wood-elf slaves. [Star Quendi](/people/star-quendi/) then blessed his inheritance of the blade at Bask Falls Barrow, which opened another **Master Rune** on it.

## Bearing and arms

No portrait of Drefan survives in the session record. The sheet gives a wood elf druid of the fifth level, Chaotic Good, acolyte-raised: AC 13, 35 hit points, a 35-foot stride, darkvision to sixty feet, immune to magical sleep by Fey Ancestry. His wisdom carries him (WIS 16) and his charisma does not (CHA 8). He speaks Common, Druidic, Dwarvish, Elvish and Sylvan, moves unseen in light cover by Mask of the Wild, carries a herbalism kit, and the sheet lists his weapon simply as **Moonsword** — annotated, in Dave's hand, *Silver Bane*. Under Flaws the sheet says only: *"Cares too much."*

## Origin

The chronicle names Drefan for the first time when the Moongate sets the shipwreck survivors down on Snowdown, the elves' own home island and the seat of the cult; by then the cultists already "look to Drefan for guidance and resolution" over the dwarf, the human, the wizard and the dragonskin. Where he was before that is not recorded. What is recorded is that he comes ashore at Leftfork Farm and finds his own people — **Untari** and her two daughters among them — enslaved under the Shor clan, Untari held by a shackle that electrocuted anyone who touched it and put her in a coma; and that the road from Leftfork runs through Bask Falls, where his house is buried and where the sword is waiting. A later Company file calls him "Drefan of Gallidan … the heir to House Gallidan, a long dead house of elven royalty from before the Time of Troubles."

## The Lusmundii and the house he is sworn to

House Gallidann is the elven line that held the island of Snowdown — anciently **Wain Gallidan** — since Eldamir founded it in the Heavenfall, and the [Lusmundii](/organizations/the-lusmundii/), "Light of the World," are the druidic cult of Elistrae that grew up around the goddess reborn. Where the rest of the company are strangers to that faith, Drefan belongs to its history.

## Public and private

In public the blade makes him a symbol before he has done anything to earn it. Among the white moon-elves of the [Mundi](/organizations/the-mundi/) in the crystal city, the elves see Silverbane and genuflect, think that Drefan may be their king, and ask him whether he has come to claim the rightful throne of Motherstone. Privately the same court, under King **Chad**, is soon working against him: when the Mundi sour on their guests, Chad's method is to turn Drefan against [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), sowing distrust through the man the moon-elves have decided to flatter.

## Competence and circle

He is the company's infiltrator, and his signature feat is theft on a scale nobody else attempts. When the heroes raid the Company's fleeing flagship at Caer Westphal, **Drefan, hidden aboard as a cloud of mist, seizes the helm** of the **Excelsior**. The captured ship — a sail-less elemental marvel that flies, rolls and crashes back into the sea — is rechristened the **Arielle** and flown home over the mountain. Every arc after Motherstone travels on a vessel Drefan stole.

## Signature capability — the sword that remembers

Silverbane is a witness with a persistent memory and blank spaces in it, and Drefan is the man it talks to. Beneath Motherstone it confesses the shame it guards: after the Forgetting, Eldamir and House Gallidann blamed the dwarves and waged war on them, a **genocide**, "capturing entire populations and leading them still alive into their tombs" — and only afterward did Eldamir discover Calix's plot and kill him, dying by his hand in the same stroke. In the rewritten past of [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), away from the Oblivia that erased the age, the blade recovers the rest.

> I remember these times much better now… much of this age was erased from history, which for you is written record but for me, is empty spaces spoiling my persistent memory. But now that I've seen myself as I was in this time — with Eldamir — I remember what it was like.

<small>— Silverbane, Arc VI</small>

## The Master Runes

A Master Rune on Silverbane is opened by a previous owner's blessing. Star Quendi's blessing at Bask Falls Barrow opened one, and each of the blade's other previous owners blessed Drefan with their rune's power **except Daenan**, whom the party never went back to contact. Drefan then added his own rune, his capstone power: *"if you strike a creature with this bow and it has fewer than 100 hit points, it dies."* <small>(sic)</small>

## The Waning One

**Drefanmetra**, the **[Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/)**, is a version of Drefan from another arc. He vowed to stop [the moon](/places/the-moon/) being moved, which would have saved the home of the elves and doomed Toril, and he failed. He went forward in time as the Waning One to preempt [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), and organised the **[Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/)**, the remaining heads of each elvish house, to steal the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) for themselves "and send him back to try again". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> "The Banefae United by Drefanmetra attack the maze and enslaved TJ to get into the Penteract at the heart of Despondos." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

The Waning One fought the party alongside **[Valen](/people/valen/)** and [Carl's Jr.](/organizations/carls-jr-and-the-drow/) at Deadfall Peak. The party charmed him <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> and imprisoned him in his own sword <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-04)</small>. He is held in Silverbane, which Drefan carries, and Drefan lets him out occasionally "for carnal purposes". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## Right now

Drefan carries Silverbane into the muster at Motherstone as one of five sibling blades — Silverbane, Ebonbane, Ironbane, Glassbane, Copperbane — nearly reunited for the first time since the age they cannot remember, and named in Zebulon's vision as five blades piercing an opened eye.

## Beliefs

He believes an oath to a house is a debt payable with a life, which is the exact price Star Quendi named before she gave him the sword. He believes the blade's memory is worth more than the record his own people kept, since the record was edited and the blade was not. He believes in going in first, alone, as mist.

**Organizations / Groups:** Temple Holdings LLC · House Gallidann · the Lusmundii, the Cult of Elistrae

**Holdings:** the Old Temple at Harloch, bought jointly by the company

**Items:** **Silverbane**, a living moonblade of House Gallidann, taken from Daenan Gallidan's sarcophagus aboard the *Evening Star* and blessed to him by the spectral echo of [Star Quendi](/people/star-quendi/) at Bask Falls Barrow on the condition that he pledge to live and die for her house. Its previous bearers are in the barrow; the blade itself remembers them. It carries a Master Rune for each owner who blessed him, and a last one he added himself. The Waning One is imprisoned in it.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Silverbane** — his moonblade, and a witness in its own right. Awake.
- **Star Quendi** — granted him the right to bear it, in exchange for his life. Ascended, in Arboria.
- **King Chad of the Mundi** — mistook him for a king, then tried to use him against Magnus.
- **Brother Magnus** — the companion Chad tried to turn him against. Alive.
- **Drefanmetra, the Waning One** — a version of Drefan from another arc. Imprisoned in Silverbane, which Drefan carries.
- **Untari** — the enslaved elven chief freed at Leftfork; her Bladesingers fight beside the company to the end.
