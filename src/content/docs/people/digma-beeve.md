**Halfling · Furniture trader · Founder of Fulcrum · Comptroller of the Republic of Talenta · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Digma</small>

**Digma Beeve** is a halfling furniture trader out of the City of Newham, a veteran of the Last War, a founding member of the rebel group [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/), and the acting Comptroller of the Republic of Talenta. He founded Fulcrum over two decades ago alongside Fema Nolan and John Stamos and, in secret, Richard Blaze, and his trade routes were the network's first infrastructure. A year before the Sharn arc he became a trusted ally of the resistance group known as the "Heroes of Leef" — Gemma, Eric and Dario — helping them rally Newham's citizens against its corrupt mayor, the former member of The Unforeseen Sorcerer Grand Duke John C. LeBeefe. LeBeefe was killed and the city then collapsed into ruin, and Digma went east with the survivors to found the Republic of Talenta out of that evacuation. He serves as its acting Comptroller while Eric is stationed in Sharn.

## Description

An old halfling, bald over the crown with a fringe of grey and a neat grey moustache, and a smile that arrives before he says anything. He wears a long rust-brown leather coat over a dark green tunic, belted at the waist, with a beaded stone necklace at the throat and worn brown boots. In one hand he holds a small dark leather-bound book — a ledger, not a spellbook — the way another man would hold a weapon. He is short, warm, entirely unthreatening, and has been running goods and people past Imperial checkpoints for over twenty years. No written description of him survives; the particulars above are taken from his portrait.

[Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) is one of the oldest rebel groups in existence, grown from the farmers, rangers and dissidents of the Breland countryside, with its stronghold centred in Zilspar. Digma is one of its founding members, standing alongside [Fema Nolan](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) and, in secret, [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/). It was formed over two decades ago, and his business routes were its first infrastructure.

To the Empire he is a furniture trader from Newham and a veteran of the Last War: a licensed merchant with a long, boring paper trail. What is true is that those trade routes doubled as discreet channels for moving messages, supplies, food and people through Fulcrum's formative years, and that the boring paper trail was the whole point. He built his reputation and then spent it, slowly, over decades, on a rebellion that never once appeared in his accounts.

His oldest and most consequential relationship began in a Newham kitchen. He was the first person to treat Richard Blaze as a man rather than as property; he whispered of rebellion and Richard listened, and that friendship became the seed of Fulcrum. Their partnership was the heart of the network's early survival: one man with vision, another with trust, both willing to risk everything to turn whispered rebellion into a living resistance. He also holds the eastern connections — Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke was his old ally.

**The secret he has carried longest:** Richard Blaze's. He kept it through Stamos's murder, through Richard's marriage to the Lord Commander of the Imperial Military, and through eight years of a child growing up in the Veil Building.

He believes a cover that has held for twenty years is worth more than any victory that would burn it. He believes people are moved the same way freight is: routes, papers, timing. He believes the story told about an event afterwards decides what the event was.

In practice he is the rebellion's fixer and its bookkeeper at once. A year before the Sharn arc he became a trusted ally of the resistance group known as the Heroes of Leef, helping [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) rally Newham's citizens against its corrupt mayor — introducing them to a network of anti-LeBeefe cells hidden throughout the metropolis and uniting those disparate factions into a coordinated resistance strong enough to challenge his rule. He arrives, he introduces, he steps back, and afterwards the paperwork is in order.

Digma Beeve came out of the City of Newham with a trade and a war behind him, and turned both into a resistance. For over twenty years he ran furniture and rebellion along the same roads. He watched Newham fall — the city he came from, destroyed in the otherworldly explosion that made the New Mourning — and he went east with the survivors. Townsfolk, Newham refugees and swamp-dwarf tribes forged the Republic of Talenta out of that evacuation, and Digma now serves as its acting Comptroller while Eric is stationed in Sharn.

**Right now:** Digma is Comptroller of a nation and the eastern rebellion's line to the Mror Holds — it was he who relayed Princess Orange Chicken's letter about the lost Deeptempura forge-clan and the Teryaki mourning-party, which the party connected to the foundry at Korranberg. He is also the author of the lie that keeps Richard Blaze and [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) alive.

**Organizations / Groups:** founding member of Fulcrum; acting Comptroller of [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/); veteran of the Last War.

**Holdings:** a furniture trading business and its routes, out of Newham · the Republic's ledgers · the Republic's line to the Mror Holds.

**Items:** a small dark leather-bound ledger, carried everywhere · a beaded stone necklace.

**The Cover Story:** Digma's real specialism, exercised most consequentially in the hour after the Empire fell in Sharn. He went straight to Richard and Calcifer, spoke privately, and then told the party what the record would say: that Faith and [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) ambushed Richard's private airship during the kidnapping, killing Richard, Calcifer, and two of Esther's Honor Guards. With [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) already dead, there was nobody left to contradict it. Then he left to coordinate the narrative. It has held for three years.

## Newham

It was Digma who made the fall of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) possible — not by fighting, but by knowing which cells existed in which districts and being trusted by all of them. The assault succeeded and LeBeefe was killed. The city then collapsed into ruin and a vast region around it became corrupted, toxic and uninhabitable. Digma went east with the survivors.

> "Digma provided cover and contacts, while Richard built the infrastructure."

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to keep the network alive, and to make sure that what survives is written down correctly.

**Likes:** Richard Blaze · a clean ledger · trade routes · Rourke · the Mror Holds · introductions · a story that will hold.

**Dislikes:** Grand Dukes · improvisation in front of witnesses · anyone who wants credit · the New Mourning.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** he decides what people will be told before he asks them what they want said.

**Hobbies & Pets:** Unknown. He keeps books.

**Status:** Alive.
