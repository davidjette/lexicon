**Monkey · Necromancer · Science department, Astral Command · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Ensign Horgus · Horg

**Horgus Simianis** is a monkey necromancer, brain surgeon and quantum biologist of the science department of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), played by Dave. An Academy contemporary of [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) and [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/), he and Katrina served a secret program built around [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/). During [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/) they left Jim's group stranded on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) to recover it, and they returned in the final season of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*.

## Background

Horgus began as a level-one officer candidate, Ensign Horgus. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-03-31)</small> He was placed in the science department. His official training is as a brain surgeon and quantum biologist, which makes him formally a necromancer. He is old for a monkey and searching for a way to live longer. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-08)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### The Academy

Horgus appeared with young Jim Smallberries and Katrina McQuaid in the Academy flashbacks of April 2023. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01, 2023-04-04)</small> Blastimov, aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), knew Katrina's and Horgus's secret. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-22)</small>

### The race

During the race of April 2023 Horgus hacked the minilex on the Bachman shuttle. The other racers made their Arcana checks to resist the hack and threw it off with spells. The hack was meant to make them spin out while the core was taken, and then to be reversed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-29)</small>

### The Beholder War

Katrina and Horgus returned in the third season, in flashbacks to Jim's origin and the Beholder War. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> Horgus is a necromancer and Katrina a horizon walker, and both trained to support a secret program that was used only once. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> The first use of the Demoncore weapons system left Jim stranded on Negroni Prime with [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/) and a dozen civilians in the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small>

Recovering the Demoncore took priority over saving the stranded group. Nico, who played Katrina, recalled that she and Horgus left the group with no transportation and no way to communicate, and took its artillery droid, [1.0](/people/1-0/). Jim's memories were changed afterwards with a potion of modify memory. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

Dave described the divergence of Katrina and Horgus's story from Jim's as mirroring the divergence of Astral Command from its righteous path, and as the cause of Jim's disillusionment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

### The finale

Before the finale of *Light of Xaryxis*, Horgus upgraded his own barrier tattoo and Katrina's. In Dave's ruling Jim's matching tattoo was magically upgraded as well, probably without his knowledge. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Horgus has four hands, and as a pilot compares to the best thri-kreen pilots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-08)</small>
