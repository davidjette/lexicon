**Leonin · Scholar and historian · Self-appointed protector of [Master Armhair](/people/master-armhair/) · Alive, leaving Korth**

*Also known as:* no other name is recorded.

Jelton Ahn is a Leonin scholar and historian of the [Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/) in [Korth](/places/korth/), and the self-appointed protector of Master Armhair. He met the Inevitables once, at midnight on the lower plaza, identified the **Crown of Seven Stars** on sight as ancient infernal work made by his own people, and gave them a scroll of true resurrection rather than accept the treasure for nothing. He is arranging forged passports to move his wife out of Korth to Kabara, and is leaving with Armhair for the Korramont.

## Physical description

A Leonin, which is not a common sight even in a capital that has learned to look at nothing. He carries a very nice dagger and is bad at concealing it — the first thing the Inevitables noticed about him, before he had said a word. <small>The whole acquaintance lasted one midnight meeting on the lower plaza.</small>

## The scholars of the Hall

The Hall of Records draws scholars because it is where the writing is, and keeps them because everything else has been taken out of it. They work and loiter at all hours, hold their conversations at a volume they think is safe, and quietly arrange the things the Empire has made impossible — forged passports on the third level, false indexes in the stacks, a colleague who will hear a heresy out to the end. Jelton Ahn is one of them, and he is the reason the most dangerous scholar in Korth has not been walking home alone.

## A protector nobody asked for

Publicly he is Armhair's companion. Armhair's own account of the arrangement is flat and slightly embarrassed: *"He insisted on it. I told him not to come."*

He is also a genuine historian in his own right, and demonstrated it in about four seconds. Handed the **Crown of Seven Stars**, he identified it on sight as ancient, made of a rare infernal ore, and said *"I believe it belonged to us"* — recognising the work of the mountain's own people, which is more than the party had managed with the thing in their possession for weeks. He warned Armhair not to touch it.

## Competence and circle

His circle is one elderly half-elf and one wife. He wore a small gold case on a leather cord around his own neck, bound by a thin glass ring, and gave it away to strangers within an hour of meeting them because he would not accept a treasure for nothing.

## The passports

He is trying to get his **wife** out of Korth to **Kabara** on forged papers, through the same non-binary dwarf clerk on the third level of the Hall of Records who papered [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) as Sky Rizzy. He told the party it was a holiday. Nobody believed him and nobody said so.

## Beliefs

He believes the Crown of Seven Stars is the work of his own people and belongs to them.

He believes a treasure of that value cannot be accepted without recompense, and paid on the spot rather than carry the debt.

He believes Armhair should not be walking into a dragon's mountain alone, and is going to lose that argument.

## Conduct in role

Honourable to a fault, in a city where that is a survivable trait only if you are also boring. He guards a heretic, smuggles his wife out under a lie about a holiday, and gives away a resurrection to square a debt nobody was collecting.

> "You cannot provide a treasure of that value for no recompense."

## Origin

Unknown. He is Leonin, he studies history seriously enough to date infernal metalwork by eye, and he identifies the Crown's makers as his own people — *us* — which places his line somewhere in the mountain's past. How he came to Korth and how he met Armhair are not recorded.

## Right now

He is arranging forged passports and getting his wife out to Kabara. Armhair is leaving for the Korramont expecting to die, and Jelton insisted on coming.
