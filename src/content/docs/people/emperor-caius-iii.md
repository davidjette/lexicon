**Species disputed · Emperor of the Pax Imperium · House of Kaius · Comatose**

*Also known as:* Kaius III · Kaius III (as the death warrants are signed) · the Emperor · *and, if Master Armhair is right,* Kaius II, Kaius I, Prince Kaius, and a dragon named **Viagra**.

**Emperor Kaius III** is the Emperor of the Pax Imperium and head of the House of Kaius, seated at the Great Imperial Palace in Korth. He has not sat on the Throne of Bones in ten years: he lies comatose in the palace while his Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) bears his daughters and rules the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), the E.D.E.N. undead and the bells in his name, his Lord Commander murdered his entire [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) with his apparent blessing, and death warrants continue to issue over his signature. Two accounts of what he is are held by the Inevitables and neither has been confirmed: the loremaster Master Armhair holds that he is an ancient dragon named Viagra who replaced the dying Prince Kaius and has since reigned as Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III; the dead necromancer John C. LeBeefe told [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) that he is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun, kept as a vessel and fed dragon blood.

## Physical description

No description of him has been recorded in ten years. The nearest thing to a description in the party's possession is a dream: [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/)'s omen in the Korranberg dark, in which an emaciated old man bearing the Emperor's face stumbled into a marble foyer carrying an infant, blood pouring from his chest, and collapsed into a gold-lined fountain until the water ran red across the floor toward her feet. His face is on coins and statuary across a continent. What wears it has not been observed in ten years.

## The Throne of Bones

The Great Imperial Palace stands at the southern end of the Great Plaza in Korth, far beyond the parade throne, and is theoretically the seat of the Emperor's power. **He has not sat on the Throne of Bones in ten years.** Bureaucrats and nobles still work there, dealing with intermediaries of the court. He keeps a residence in the building. Attached to the palace stands a tower — a [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) cloister and convent with a bell at its top, the ninth great casting, a Master bell delivered and consecrated under the dove seal. A giant [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) symbol is carved into marble in the throne room. Warrants still issue in his name and are still signed.

## Public and private

Publicly the Empire does not say the Emperor is ill. It says he is present. At the opening of POSÉ, [Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) asked [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) directly whether the Emperor was well enough to attend the Summit, and got the whole of Imperial policy on the subject in two sentences — including a pronoun nobody in the room remarked on.

Privately the Empire is a machine running on a signature. The man in the palace is comatose. His Empress bears his children — fourteen daughters, many sets of twins, all under twelve years old — and rules the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), the **E.D.E.N.** undead and the bells in his name. His Lord Commander murdered his entire [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) with his apparent blessing. His courts condemn on the doctrine that submission to the court *is* submission to him. Nothing in Karrnath needs him awake.

## Competence and circle

[Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) was appointed his sworn protector from birth under the title **Eternal Guardian**, and served as head Paladin to Kaius I, Kaius II and Kaius III — an appointment made at a cradle and honoured across three reigns. **Fanta Rhodes**, a cloaked figure of unrecorded race, sits on the Imperial Council as the **Hand of Kaius III**. [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), the covert agent who leads the Blood of Vol, is in hiding in the Imperial Palace where she remains consort to the Emperor. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) performed necromantic sex magic in front of Gabriel Duin and Emperor Kaius III, raising a ghoul from the corpse of the rebel singer [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/). Whatever the Emperor is, that was staged for him.

## The cult that worships him

An Imperial report on [the Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) describes a cult of humans who believe they share an ancestral kinship with the Emperor which they regard as next to divine, and who believe the Emperor is secretly fighting a global cabal from behind the scenes. The Empire's own internal memos on the same cult begin: *"The cult is not entirely wrong, in that the Emperor knows of their cult and secretly agrees with their assertion of his divine blood and his fatherhood over all of Eberron. He has on multiple occasions leaked information through communique and rumor to support their conspiracies and even encourage terrorist acts against his enemies."*

## TWO ACCOUNTS OF HIS NATURE

The Inevitables hold two independent explanations of what the Emperor actually is. They came from different continents' worth of research, and they do not agree. Neither has been confirmed.

### THE ACCOUNT OF MASTER ARMHAIR — THE DRAGON VIAGRA

Delivered at midnight in the lower plaza of Korth by an elderly half-elf loremaster of the Hall of Records, in the presence of the Leonin scholar Jelton Ahn, as the summary of his life's work. Armhair named it a blasphemy of the highest order and invited the party to have him executed for it.

A great dragon called **Viagra**, the most powerful known and said to have toyed with the forces that govern reality, vanished from the record long before the elves came to Khorvaire. Armhair traced evidence of it well past that disappearance. He believes it came to **King Jarret Ilwenarn** of Karrnath with a bargain the king accepted; that the sick child **Prince Kaius** died and was replaced; and that **Kaius III is that dragon, and is the same being as Kaius I**. His method was contemporaneous accounts and courtiers' journals since scrubbed from the archives — including unusual inconsistencies in the funeral accounts of Kaius I. His manuscript further runs a thread through the **Mark of Death**, the birth of **Erandis Vol**, and this same dragon.

**He named his own weakest point.** Armhair was candid that the Emperor's reported illness does not fit his hypothesis. A dragon of that magnitude has no business lying comatose for ten years.

### THE ACCOUNT OF JOHN C. LEBEEFE — THE VAMPIRE VESSEL

Delivered as a voice in [Eric's](/people/eric-the-cleric/) head on the way out of the Korranberg mountain, in the same register that had spent four sessions trying to make him ring a bell. [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) is dead, and was a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and lies.

LeBeefe's lingering voice told Eric that **Emperor Kaius is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun**, kept alive as a vessel and fed dragon blood to strengthen his bloodline. The dragon **Valtrex** had, hours earlier, confirmed the mechanism from his own side of it: the Empire built a rig to harpoon, restrain and drain him, bled him for three years, and shipped the blood out by rail. Master Armhair, asked in a different city about a different question, noted that dragon blood is an alchemical reagent of extraordinary power.

This account accounts for the coma, the rail shipments, the three-year bleeding operation, and the Empire keeping a mad dragon alive under a mountain rather than killing him. Its source is a dead necromancer speaking to a man he is trying to corrupt.

### WHERE THE TWO ACCOUNTS TOUCH

Both run through the Crimson Sun and both run through Vol. Valtrex testified that Erandis Vol bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun; Armhair's manuscript ties the Mark of Death and Vol's birth to the dragon he believes sits the throne; LeBeefe's version makes the Emperor a descendant of the Crimson Sun by blood. Set against the Litany's promise that *"the dragon will be reborn,"* the Inevitables concluded that Uriel is trying to bear a son by a dragon — which is why she keeps bearing daughters, why the Emperor is kept comatose but alive, and why Valtrex was bled for three years. <small>Valtrex does not know Viagra exists. He believes Uriel's heirs are his.</small>

## Beliefs

<small>From the Imperial record.</small> He believes his blood is divine and that he is father to all of Eberron. He believes his enemies are best undone by rumour placed in the right ears, and has leaked to a conspiracy cult to have terrorist acts committed on his behalf. He believes — or the courts that stand in for him do — that every subject is guilty of something, and that a corpse still owes the state a second service.

## Conduct in role

He signs. In a sealed Black Dove shrine under the Korramont, in the satchel of a three-years-dead Overseer named **Velen Marsk**, the Inevitables found a death warrant signed by **Emperor Kaius III** ordering the execution of **Corn Deeptempura**, **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura**, **Sister Calvane**, and every dwarf in the foundry — ending with Marsk himself. Most of the names were crossed out. The drains were thick with old blood. The order is on Imperial letterhead, signed in the name of a man who has not been conscious in ten years.

## Origin

The official history is dynastic and unremarkable: Kaius I, Kaius II, Kaius III, a line of Karrnathi kings become Emperors of the Pax Imperium, served across all three reigns by the same High Elf paladin under a title granted at the cradle. Priest **E'noch Qualanthri** performed a Sacred Rite over the newborn Kaius. The House of Kaius bought the Korramont roughly ten or eleven years ago — the mountain under which a black dragon was chained, collared and bled.

Armhair's reconstruction replaces the dynasty with one continuous occupant. A dragon approached King Jarret Ilwenarn with a bargain; the sick prince of the Queen's Lullaby died and was replaced; the thing that took his place became Kaius I, then Kaius II, then Kaius III. Every record naming a specific dragon has since been removed from the Hall of Records by order, and Armhair's original manuscript was confiscated because his work led to questions the Empire did not want asked. <small>He hid a copy under a false index and gave the party the number. He then left for the Korramont to find Valtrex and finish the book, expecting to die doing it.</small>

## The heart in the box

Gemma Corso's warlock pact is not hers. Her adoptive father, Jane Corso, agreed to bring the archfey **Kitsune** *the heart of Emperor Kaius* in exchange for freedom and power. When Jane died, the pact passed to Gemma. Kitsune gave her a velvet-lined pewter heart-box for the task, and when she came out of the fey-dream her armour had been re-lined in the same velvet. She has told no one. <small>She is currently travelling to the Summer Palace on Imperial credentials.</small>

## Right now

He is comatose in the Great Imperial Palace in Korth, ten years into an illness the Empire will not name, on the far side of a throne room floor carved with the sigil of the Crimson Sun. The Empress is upriver at the Ivory Lazaret waiting for a son. Ten bells remain, at least one of them a Master casting in the tower attached to his own palace. And a warlock with an empty pewter box has just been handed sanctioned Imperial paperwork to walk up to his household and take an inventory.

**Organizations / Groups:** The House of Kaius · [The Imperial Council / Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) (thirteen heads of government under him) · object of worship of [the Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/).

**Holdings:** The Great Imperial Palace and the Throne of Bones, Korth · the Summer Palace and the Ivory Lazaret upriver · the Korramont, purchased roughly a decade ago · the Pax Imperium.

**Items:** Death warrants bearing his signature, in circulation and being executed three years after he last woke. One is in the party's possession.

**The Signature:** the Empire's actual instrument of rule. It condemns clans, opens mass prosecutions and orders genocides in a hand nobody has watched move in a decade. Karrnathi law holds that the court stands in for the Emperor — so in practice the signature does not require him to exist.
