**Duergar (deep dwarf) · kin of the Teryaki camp · The Teryaki clan · Alive**

Crab Rangoon is a duergar of the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) clan and one of the surviving kin in the camp at [Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/), beneath the Korramont. He gave the **Inevitables** the account of the dwarves who had gone down to the black dragon under the mountain and not come back, and he is among the kin who stayed in the camp when the party warned them that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) had claimed the Korramont.

## The conversation

Fresh from finding the Thirteenth Casting in the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/), the party came back into the hall and Crab Rangoon told them the rest of it: that some of the dwarves had ventured down into a vast cavern where the black dragon **Vuldrach** - now known as [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) - nested with the treasures of the [Deep Tempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/). None of them had returned. With the pact between the clan and the dragon long broken, the kin feared the worst, and were right to.

His account established that the dragon of the stair reliefs was still alive under the mountain, that the clan's own treasure was down there with him, and that the Teryaki had already lost people trying to reach it. The party's work at the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) followed from it.

## Right now

Still in the camp. The kin refused to evacuate when the party warned them that Valtrex was free and coming for them, and only [Bofric](/people/bofric/) left. Crab Rangoon is among the kin who stayed.

His trade, family and age are not recorded. The clan is small enough that everyone in it does several jobs.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Teryaki clan.
