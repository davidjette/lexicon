**Valenar elf · Knight and commander · Director of the Valenar rail system · Level Unknown · Alive at last record**

*Also known as:* None recorded.

Yewdress Olba is a Valenar knight and commander, placed in charge of the development of the Valenar rail system. The posting makes a Valenar officer responsible for building the line that connects the Valenar homeland to House Orien's Imperial network, whose standards an Interoperability Summit at Sharn was convened to settle. After the City of Knives the Empire declared a no-go zone over a substantial portion of the Dragon Isles and declined to enforce its will in those waters, and the Valenar are angry at the resulting decline in sea trade. No description of Olba survives — not face, age, colouring, armour or blade.

**The assignment.** The Valenar are elves whose entire social order is built on martial achievement, and their armies came into the Last War hard enough that the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) embedded themselves among their elven kin to keep up with the casualties. What the Empire did with a knight and commander of that people was hand over the building of a railway. The lightning rail is the Pax Imperium's circulatory system: House Orien under [Kwanti d'Orien](/people/kwanti-d-orien/) runs the largest network on the continent and adds to it daily, and an Interoperability Summit was convened at Sharn to settle the standards every new line must meet. A Valenar rail system, developed under a Valenar commander, is Valenar territory made permanently reachable by Imperial rolling stock.

**In public** Olba is a decorated commander entrusted with the modernisation of the Valenar homeland. **In private** nothing is recorded. Whether Olba understands the assignment as honour, exile or occupation is **Unknown**.

What Yewdress Olba believes is not recorded.

**Origin.** Valenar, and a knighthood. **Unknown** beyond that: no birthplace, no date, no house, no campaign record, no account of how the railway came to be handed over.

## The waters go dark

One event in the record bears directly on Olba's posting. After the City of Knives, the Empire declared a **no-go zone** over a substantial portion of the Dragon Isles, following continued out-of-control piracy in the area. The Empire seems to have declined to enforce its will in those waters, and **the Valenar elves are angry for the decline in trade**.

That is the Valenar's commercial position collapsing on the sea lanes at the same moment their overland trade is being rebuilt as an Imperial rail spur under an Imperial-appointed commander. Whether the timing was arranged is **Unknown**. After the no-go zone, whatever the Valenar sell has to go out on Yewdress Olba's rails.
