**Autognome · Artificer 15 (Artillerist) · Brewer · [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Seven · 7

**Se7en** is an autognome artificer, played by Nico, built by [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) as the seventh of his robots. They served below decks aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), brewing booze for the ship and its bar. Fifteen years later, after the Sovereignty outlawed artificial intelligence and autognomes with it, Se7en returned beside Barrias in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, rebuilt as an artillerist, and travelled through the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/) with Barrias and [Nine](/people/nine/).

## Background

Se7en is a robot built by Barrias, and the name comes from being the seventh. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Units one to six came before. Se7en's player suggested those had failed because they were programmed with high Charisma, and Dave that Barrias "maybe" keeps trying to build an automated brewing unit and the units keep gaining sentience. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small> Se7en has high Intelligence and a Charisma modifier of -1, and Dave suggested that Se7en might have no emotions. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small>

Se7en's Scroller is built in. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small> Se7en has no bed, and plugs into a wall. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

Se7en joined the crew as a level-1 artificer who specialises in brewing, probably with engine fuel. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Se7en supplies [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/), the beholder who runs the bar aboard the Zanzibar, with booze, and Dave called them Luigi's best friend on that account. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> Se7en's player described Se7en as liking to hang out with everyone and brew booze. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27, 2022-12-09)</small>

At level 1 Se7en carried a built-in pistol, used *shocking grasp* up close and a dart that could be poisoned, had AC 17 from an autognome's casing, and was proficient with vehicles and able to pilot. Se7en's player planned to make Se7en an Alchemist at level 3, to keep the brewing and chemistry theme. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small>

Se7en was one of the level-1 crew in the Below Decks episode of 2022-12-09, with [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) and [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-07, 2022-12-09)</small> Se7en keeps a pig, Mr Hamburger. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small> Se7en reached level 3 after the Academy flashback of 2023-04-01. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-01)</small>

At the end of *Light of Xaryxis*, by their player's account, Se7en had the best ending of the crew, serving their maker drinks on a beach. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Planning the sequel, JL counted Se7en as the one character of Nico's still in the mix. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-05)</small> Dave pictured Barrias, fifteen years on, alone on the periphery except for Seven, Eight and Nine. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-04)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

*The Starsong Awakens*, which JL ran, is set fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*. A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the [Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) outlawed all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty. JL's premise was explicit: "This includes warforged and autognomes." The Act led to a violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market in robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

Se7en and Barrias returned at level 12, each with a feat learned in the fifteen years between, in one of the arc's two storylines. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01, 2024-07-10)</small> Se7en's player rebuilt Se7en as an Artillerist: with half plate, a shield and infusions, AC 23, boots of flying, the *shield* and *fireball* spells, an arcane firearm, a movable eldritch cannon on a bonus action, and a spell-storing item that allies can use. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-12)</small>

By July 2024 Se7en was predicting the future again. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-27)</small> On 2024-08-09 Se7en and Barrias slaughtered the diners and worked through riddles, and on 2024-08-23 each earned 4,600 XP in a session at a prison. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-09, 2024-08-24)</small>

By May 2025 Se7en and Barrias were in the Sphinx Tomb with Nine, going through its time doors, with the ship *Pussyfoot* parked outside. Dave recalled the last door as a visit to the Five Zanzibars, and Se7en's player recalled it as the final fight on the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small> Dave, as a player, described the arc as centred on Nine, who set out to fix everything bad that happened after Tannhauser Gate, and on his father and brother eventually bringing him home. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small>

The climax of Starfall came at [Necis](/places/necis/), at the end of both *Light of Xaryxis* and *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Se7en's sheet makes them a Small autognome construct, an Artificer 15 of the Artillerist specialty with the Izzet Engineer background and 172,595 XP. They have 123 hit points, AC 20, a walking and a flying speed of 30 feet, spell save DC 18 and spell attack +10, and speak Common, Dwarvish and Goblin. <small>(character sheet)</small>

- **Autognome traits:** Armored Casing, Built for Success, Healing Machine, Mechanical Nature (no need to eat, drink or breathe; immune to disease; resistant to poison) and Sentry's Rest, six hours inert but conscious in place of sleep. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Artillerist features:** Eldritch Cannon (flamethrower, force ballista or protector), Explosive Cannon, Arcane Firearm and Fortified Position. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Artificer features:** Flash of Genius, Spell-Storing Item, Magic Item Savant and Tool Expertise. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Feat:** Magic Initiate (Wizard). <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:** Alchemy Jug, Wand of the War Mage +1, Winged Boots, Cloak of Protection, Knave's Eye Patch, Universal Solvent, half plate, shield, a potion of healing, and brewer's supplies, alchemist's supplies, smith's, tinker's and thieves' tools. <small>(character sheet)</small>
