**Ghost · Daughter of Count Crestus Weathervein · Princess of Clouds · House Weathervein · The Weathervein Tomb, Saltmarsh**

*Also known as:* Vivica Amphibiana · Vivica A

**Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein** is the ghost of the daughter of [Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/) and his wife Amphibiana, of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/). When the mob came for her father he left her in the family tomb beneath the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), where he buried her and her mother. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), on 2021-11-12, the blood of the house's heir [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) raised her ghost; she told Dolores the fall of the house, gave her the [Ring of Shooting Stars](/items/ring-of-shooting-stars/) and charged her to find the Tempest Sword, [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/). Her ghost stayed with Dolores through the ring. A wizard of Saltmarsh's shot tower later removed the ring's curse, leaving "just the risk of possession". She is the Princess of Clouds. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-open-questions.md)</small>

## In the Weathervein Tomb

On the night [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) led the party into an ambush at the Weathervein Tomb, Dolores entered the tomb of her ancestor, "her presence and the blood dripping from her wound causes a weeping girl to appear over the casket." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, *Salvage Operation – Part I*)</small> Dave told Nico that Skerrin "may not have known about the other room opened with your blood". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

The ghost turned, surprised, and asked what the party was doing there:

> I am Vivica Amphibiana, my father is Count Cresto Octopailus Weathervein, prince of the sea and master of the coves. I am here waiting for him to return, so that we may leave this place and return to the land of the Endless Horizon. There, we will be one again, with the Storm, and leave this coil to the brine and flotsam.
>
> My family, the guardians of the Atlas, those who walk upon the water, wielders of the Shooting Stars and the Tempest Blade, our very fatherhood, all wasted for the vanity of a single generation.
>
> My uncles were fools, they squandered our riches and our fame. When my father knew the mob would come for him, he left me here, to be safe. They murdered my mother while she hunted, he buried her anon, along with her father’s sword. Her brothers were to blame. They weakened our stock, our seed. They siphoned off our wealth in gold and blood. And now look at our sorry house. I have nothing left to build.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

Dave, the next day: "Count Cresto Octopailias buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter Vivica Amphibiana in that tomb, with his own tomb above as the entrance", and "Vivica’s two uncles / Cresto’s brothers in law she blamed for their house’s downfall". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## Her vow and her charge

> But I will never give up. I will take my place above the Horizon and guide the sailors home to everlasting domination of their foes. I will strike terror into every man with ears or eyes and tear his heart from his chest to load my cannonade. I will rip down the sky and pour fear and death into the graves until the flood and burst with the afflicted. The Weathervein will turn toward north and our blood will once again purify both land and sea….
>
> You are Weathervein, an angel of the Storm. You are the sky, the sea, the heaven’s fury and the Land’s End. You blood is lightning, your cries are thunder. You inherit the wind. You command the water and the sand. Take my ring, it is the signet of the Princess of Clouds and a gift from my father Count Crestus. You must recover your birthright. Find the Tempest Sword. It is the key to my uncle’s foolish game…

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

Dolores spoke part of the vow aloud herself. Dave: "You uttered the part from “I will never give up” to “purity land and sea”". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave had put "lots of clues/hooks in Vivica’s speech, and Skerrin’s too". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## Through the ring

After the ambush the party blew up a building and executed six members of a secret society. Dave to Nico: "people witnessed your full and terrible power and the rebirth of your house. Vivica’s Ghost remains quite present to you through the ring, and is ecstatic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Of [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/)'s box of deeds, Dave wrote "we could get back the whole house of Weathervein in one fell swoop" and "I know what Vivica wants you to do". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small>

Nico, of her character: "Right now Dolores see’s Vivica as her bestie who can get a little dramatic at times and can be a bad influence." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-12)</small> By the Abbey Isle sessions Vivica was one of two voices at work on Dolores. Dave: "It’s not just vivica now / It’s also my boy in the book", the book that told Brother Guido [Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/) he had no choice but to work for Dolores. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small>

In the session of 2021-12-23 Dolores passed a Charisma save against her. Dave: "But gambled a lot on that one CHA save because Vivica would have ended Salmor and used his body to bring back PW", PW being [Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/). The save DCs climb from Vivica to the book to the sword: "The book’s save Dc is higher, and the swords even higher still". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small> Nico: "Vivica is a menace." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small>

On 2021-12-26 the shot-tower wizard removed the ring's curse. Dave: "Insta-attune ring that powerful is as good as having it attuned, just the risk of possession, but at least it’s not 24 hours a day". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>

When Dolores recovered Weathervein on 2022-03-12, Dave reposted Vivica's vow and charge. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12)</small>

## Names

Dave first posted her as "Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein" during the tomb session, and again among four Weathervein names on 2021-11-19. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12, 2021-11-19)</small> When Nico called her "Vivica A", Dave supplied "Amphibiana". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-22)</small>
