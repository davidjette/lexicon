**Gnome · Arcanist and warrior · House Sivis (banned) · Notaries Guild · Level Unknown · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Herr Bomba** — his nom-de-guerre in the defence of Sharn.

**Torralyn d'Sivis** is a three-hundred-year-old gnomish arcanist and warrior, an elder of the banned House Sivis, and head of the Sivis-dominated Notaries Guild of Sharn, whose agents form the basis for all contract enforcement and financial markets in Sharn and beyond. He was instrumental in the defence of Sharn against the Empire, operating under the nom-de-guerre **Herr Bomba**, and is rumoured to have brought down the North Warrens on nearly half a million Imperial soldiers in the final year of the war. He denies any connection with the resistance activities of the war.

## Physical description

No description of him survives — not his height, his colouring, his dress or his mark.

**The house.** **Torralyn is an elder of House Sivis, the banned gnomish Dragonmarked house of spies, clerks, and bureacrats.** Banned means driven out of Zilargo and out of Imperial administration; membership is grounds for arrest. What it has not meant is irrelevance. **He denies any connection with the resistance activities during the war and heads the Sivis-dominated Notaries Guild where his agents form the basis for all contract enforcement and financial markets in Sharn and beyond.** The Empire outlawed the house of clerks and then left the clerks holding every contract in the city.

**In public** Torralyn is a notary: an old gnome at the head of a guild of witnesses and seals, whose agents underwrite the enforceability of commercial life across Sharn and outward from it. He denies the war record flatly and without elaboration. **In private** the denial is the only account he gives. Which version is true is not confirmed.

> "He denies any connection with the resistance activities during the war."

**Competence and circle.** An arcanist and a warrior both, three centuries deep in each. His counterpart is [Channa Devir](/people/channa-devir/), the other Sivis elder still operating openly in Sharn, who holds the Speakers Guild while he holds the Notaries Guild — the two halves of what the house used to do, split between two people the Empire has not been able to remove. His guild agents are his real circle, and they are everywhere money changes hands.

**The claim.** **It is rumored that between bombs and building collapses, his effort claim the lives of nearly half a million Imperial Soldiers in the collapse of the North Warrens in the final year of the war. (Many suspect that this defeat led to the Emperor's decision to trigger the Mourning to end the war entirely.)**

He believes a contract witnessed and sealed is more durable than an army, which is what the Notaries Guild is. He believes the house was banned for what it knows and not for what it did. He believes, or says he believes, that he had nothing to do with the North Warrens.

**On the job** Torralyn runs an institution rather than a faction. His agents do not report intelligence; they notarise instruments. He is old enough to have outlasted several Imperial administrations. He does not answer the Herr Bomba question and has never been made to.

**Origin.** **Unknown.** Three hundred years are recorded as a figure, not a life. His birth, his training as an arcanist, his elevation among the elders of House Sivis, and how he came to be in Sharn when the Empire arrived at its walls are not recorded. The war record begins and ends with the North Warrens and a nom-de-guerre.

**Right now** Torralyn d'Sivis heads the Notaries Guild in a Sharn that has just thrown the Iron Veil out of its streets. Every contract, debt and title in the liberated city runs through his agents.

- The Notaries Guild

- The name Herr Bomba, spoken to his face
