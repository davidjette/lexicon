**Faerie · Monk of the Astral Way · Galley Master, SSJ Zanzibar · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Kili · Private Kili Tiddleywink

**Kili Tiddleywink** is a faerie monk of the Astral Way and the ship's cook of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), a non-player character built by Dave for *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). The ship's crew roster lists him as Private Kili Tiddleywink, Galley Master.

## Background

Kili is the ship's cook, a faerie Monk of the Astral Way. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-14)</small> Dave described him as very little but able to manifest giant arms in a fight, and as kind of a scumbag. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-05-02)</small>

## Service aboard the Zanzibar

The crew roster of January 2023 gives Kili the rank of Private, with the post of Galley Master. <small>(crew roster, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> Before the roster he was described as a civilian. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-26)</small> <small>(crew roster, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>
