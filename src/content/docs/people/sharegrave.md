**Crime boss · Leader of the thieves' guild on the Catwalk · [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Alive**

Sharegrave is the leader of a thieves' guild headquartered on [the Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/) in the [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), a lifelong resident of the city whose gang burgles and resells goods across the Hive and beyond. His man Ratbone was the first thug the party fought after escaping [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/). The party killed his courier Fieldy Smuts, rescued his thief [Prez](/people/prez/) from [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/)'s hideout, and took Rotten William's contract to kill him. Trapped by his gang, they were instead put to work: [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) fought for him at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/) and beat the rigged golem staked against him, and afterwards Sharegrave paid them, kept them in his debt, and sent them with Prez to [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/).

## The gang

Sharegrave's gang works the streets of the Hive Ward. His thug Ratbone shakes down berks and shops, and warns strangers off with "Get lost Berk! This is Sharegrave's turf!" His debt collector Mochai works near the Smoldering Corpse. <small>(DM notes)</small> Much of the gang on the Catwalk itself is Tabaxi: thugs, pickpockets, and a grey tabby quartermaster with a white mane who runs the workshop. <small>(DM notes)</small>

He hates [Pharod](/people/pharod/), the collector of Ragpicker Square, and wants "something, anything, to give me an edge over that lying cheat..." <small>(DM notes)</small> He is at war with [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/), who has been killing his men. He believes Rotten William and Pharod are being paid by someone to kill and disrupt his gang, and he worries that the murder racket will bring a crackdown on the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Catwalk

The gang's headquarters is strung between two Hive tenements, buildings 4 and 5: an oversized, ballroom-scale balcony cut into building 5 faces another across the way, and between them stretches "a patchwork of planks, beams, ropes, pulleys, and support scaffolding, all supporting a ramshackle Catwalk across the 100 yard span of air." <small>(DM notes)</small> Its five levels are:

- **Level 1, the Grand Balcony.** Humanoid and Tabaxi peasants sort garbage while armed Tabaxi thugs watch from beside a rope-pulled lift. A key to Sharegrave's lair is tucked into a small hole.
- **Level 2, Intake.** Tabaxi thugs break down packages, open chests, clean weapons and count copper. A huge net hangs over the central hole, under a ceiling with a seam that opens. The climb up is "not built for non-Cats."
- **Level 3, the Workshop.** A warehouse and transfer shop full of emptied crates, dismantled carriages and siege weapons, armour and dry goods. Its floor holds a pull trap that drops intruders thirty feet into the net.
- **Level 4, the Barracks.** Cots, beds and humanoid-sized climbing trees and scratching posts, with a locked door on a platform above behind a fine rug.
- **Level 5, the Lair.** Sharegrave's private quarters.

> Sharegrave's quarters are very fine and private, with only a couple vents letting light and air into the den. Upholstered seating and fine wood work adorn every part of the room, multiple fine works of art and trinkets decorate the mantles and walls, and many gold and brass featured chests are neatly arranged in corners or beside the wardrobe. A four poster bed hides a cat bed and there are scratches on the posts.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## Fieldy Smuts and the cannonball

During their first days in Sigil the party killed Fieldy Smuts, a smuggler who had offered them a package to carry across town. "Sharegrave hated that", and wanted the cannonball back. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small> The shipment was a cannonball-sized metal sphere, "smooth, dense, always a little damp", on its way to a goods trader and arcanist, Fat Mike. Sharegrave suspects it is a portkey. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Rotten William's contract

In [the Spillway](/places/the-spillway/) the party invaded Rotten William's hideout, rescued Prez, and made a deal with Rotten William to kill Sharegrave with a wand. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small> Rotten William told them the wand would paralyse Sharegrave and track them; in fact it held a single charge of *fireball* and a *command* to cast it in Sharegrave's presence. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Sharegrave had thought Prez dead, and on his return suspected him of turning coat. He knew the party had been sent by Rotten William, meant to interrogate and kill them, and told his gang to act normally and lure them into a trap. <small>(DM notes)</small> Once he had them, his counter-offer was to double-cross Rotten William and kill him, for 200 gold each. <small>(DM notes)</small> The plan to kill Sharegrave was dropped from the party's list of quests.

## Sharegrave's soliloquy

With the party trapped again, Battlebeast bound for the arena and [Barold](/people/barold-bonds/) and [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) headed for the Gatehouse, Sharegrave addressed them:

> "I trust you have gotten your rebellion against your circumstances out of your system. As far as I can tell, you are stranded in a hostile city with no friends coming to rescue you, some of you are on the run, from the devil or from yourselves, and you've been abandoned by a whim of fate to fend for yourself and obtain some absolution or at least a theatrical end."
>
> "You, my good feline, are not so unknown are you? I have many friends around the cosmos, many in the gladiator business, and in my time I've been to the Arena of Moons."
>
> "...but then I saw you fight my men I knew you were the BattleBeast I'd saw kill eight Autacks with a broken flagpole. You lost me a thousand gold that night..."
>
> "But today you are going to make it up to me. There is a prize fight every tenday at a local blood pit called the GRINDHOUSE"

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Grindhouse

The Grindhouse is a no-holds-barred fighting arena in the Hive Ward run by a bookie called Scam Likely, with a prize fight every tenday. Sharegrave entered Battlebeast in a prize fight worth 2,000 gold, three rounds, and briefed the party:

> "Scam sets odds but also approves the fighter. I happen to know another party who has entered a Ringer, and placed a rather large bet to entice action from the crowd.
>
> My sources have also told me that this ringer is not such a sure thing - a malfunctioning iron golem that's controlled from somewhere in the crowd.

<small>(DM notes)</small> Battlebeast would fight and win because the rest of the party would discreetly take out the golem's operator. Rotten William had agents in the crowd, and the other "party", [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/), would be very upset if the fix traced back to Sharegrave. Prez directed the effort. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The ringer was defeated. The party returned to the Hive to rest and restock, and Sharegrave paid Battlebeast 250 gold and the others 100 each:

> "I'm giving this to you to be nice, so you can have some walking around money. You still owe me.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## Fortune's Wheel

Sharegrave told the party their performance had caused a big stir. Others had come calling to get Battlebeast into more prize fights, among them the party he knew had staked the ringer: the owner of a Lady's Ward casino called [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/). <small>(DM notes)</small> He sent Prez with them to deal with Eaton the acrobat, who had the "Cabinet of Mysteries", and the party reached the casino by portal. <small>(DM notes)</small> At the party's next downtime, Sharegrave and Prez were among the contacts they could return to. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Mammon and the Skeleton Key

Sharegrave burgles and delivers wealth to **Mammon** through a portal. In return he receives uncanny luck and long life, keeps his soul, and is immune to disease and poison. <small>(DM notes)</small> He also knows that Shemeska will pay for any information about the party's tattoos, and does not reveal it to them; instead he sends Prez to show them how to escape the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Skeleton Key:** Sharegrave can cast *knock* silently once a day and picks locks without effort. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Arms:** A rapier and a hand crossbow, both poisoned. He can call "Protect me!" to turn an attack onto an ally. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Views:** He dislikes the [Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/) and dismisses [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/) as a fake plague engineered by the Company to control the city. <small>(DM notes)</small>
