> Asmodeus is a fallen celestial, in this world, he would not have fallen yet or even be distinguishable from the greater fabric of divine creation that fills the world right now

<small>— Dave, on the deep past before Starfall. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-09)</small>

**Archdevil · Ruler of the Nine Hells · Fallen celestial · Defeated at the threshold of the Egg**

*Also known as:* the Prince of Darkness · the archdevil

**Asmodeus** is an archdevil of the Nine Hells and a fallen celestial. With [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) he conspired to trap the soul of the Raven Queen in a mortal child, [Marcus](/people/marcus/); St. Cloud handed him the judgement seat of the dead at [Dis](/places/dis/), he hoarded the souls of the dead, and the gods, cut off from their followers, were born into mortal children as the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/). In the Ages of the Infanta his cults worked through the Horseking of Whitehorn, the [Von Lampes](/organizations/the-von-lampes/) of [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) and the noble houses of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), and St. Cloud's plan to summon him into the heart of Waterdeep was foiled by the party. Revan Darkcember inherited a debt to him with Tiffany House. At the end of the [Infanta Crisis](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/), [Una](/people/una/) summoned him to Waterdeep and the party defeated him at the threshold of the portal to [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). The world remembers instead that Zanzibar and the Purple Dragons defeated him. After the Oblivia he still rules the Hells.

## Nature

Dave, after the party escaped his trap in 2023: "the Asmodeus is Satan and he is a tricky guy". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-04-23)</small> In the deep past before Starfall, "we are in a time before the gods of this world were born", and he had not yet fallen. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-09)</small> JL, who played Zanzibar, wrote that "Zanzibar wanted to find Asmodeus before he turned evil and win him over to our side", and Dave answered, "I think you should try it at least". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-09)</small>

His servant [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) is one of those who followed him out of heaven. Dave: "Gritty Champagne = Garidriel and Erinyes, which is a type of demon that is actually a fallen angel who followed Asmodeus who also left the heavens". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-06)</small>

His son is [Steve Merlin](/people/steve-merlin/). The elves of the Summer Court at Avalon, "desperate to break their curse of infertility and isolation from their divine aspect of the moon", made a bargain "to produce the child of a fiend – Asmodeus – and a mortal woman – and create a sorcerer who could look backward into time". <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VIII.)</small>

## The seat of the dead

The seat at Dis, where the souls of the dead are judged and sent on, has always been contested. In the chronicle's present the Raven Queen held it as a neutral arbiter between the gods and the devils. Asmodeus and St. Cloud conspired to trap her soul in a mortal, Marcus. With the arbiter gone, souls could not reach the outer realms, Asmodeus hoarded them, and the gods had to descend into the world to be near their followers. That is the cause of the Infanta Crisis. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-10)</small>

The Lady of Pain named the deed in Sigil:

> Alphalpha Beginnigin and Wainwright St. Cloud are one in the same, surely you know that. He destroyed me and let Asmodeus take the land of the dead. He’s doing the same thing here.

<small>— [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/). (Arc IV Part V.)</small>

During the crisis Asmodeus was in the Shadowfell. Dave, laying out the party's options: "There’s always Asmodeus in the shadowfell". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> In 2020 the list read "2) Go to Tiffany House and defeat the Asmodeans / 3) Go to the Shadowfell and defeat Asmodeus". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small>

## His cults

**Whitehorn.** "The Khal and the Gravedigger worship Asmodeus, the archdevil of the Nine Hells, and believe that they are destined to rule over the end times." A priest told the Khal that his child "was destined to become Death itself"; after the birth the child "was brought to the priest of Asmodeus, who placed in an arcane device, and bound to it the soul of the God of Death." <small>(Sic. Arc III Part IV.)</small> That child is Marcus.

**The Arcaneum's intelligence.** The War Wizards' file on St. Cloud records that his changeling Team "has notably been in the company of the cult of Asmodeus – one power thought to be behind the new Troubles – and in cohort with other unsavory elements who want to do harm to the peoples of the world. If Asmodeus has control of the Empire, gods help us all." The chairman of the Company "is suspected of being an agent of Asmodeus or some other extraplanar power." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> Inside the Arcaneum, a vision: "The party sees Asmodeus rise over Faerun, colliding with a gigantic nude Elistrae, devastating the land and breaking the sky. Then the oceans swell, and they both look over in fear as a wave washes over the Earth." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

**Waterdeep.** Von Lampe performed a ritual in his villa "with four other Asmodeus worshippers. Asmodeus speaks to them, and remind him his twins’ souls are on the line. Von Lampe pledges to give him the 999 souls plus the infanta by dawn. Asmodeus orders the other four cultists to murder him if he fails." <small>(Sic. Arc III Part VI.)</small> The Von Lampes' penthouse at the Black Diamond in Zhentil Keep hid a ritual room "with black velvet, satanic symbols and statues, a large pentacle in the center of the room, a Supreme Scroll of Gate (Shadowfell), and an amulet-sized pedestal." The Von Lampes had sacrificed their eldest son and their middle child "to become demons", and "Their youngest son was slated to die to bring the Army of Hell through the Gate from the Shadowfell." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small> In the vault of Waterdeep, Allo Vera greeted [Obi](/people/obi/): "Hello Obi. We thought the Asmodeans got you. I’m so sorry." <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small>

## The Amulet of Kai Ryssdall

The party went to an auction in Waterdeep because something St. Cloud wanted was for sale: the Amulet of Kai Ryssdall, a gem big enough to cast Greater Gate, and "a key part of his plan to summon Asmodeus into the heart of Waterdeep". The Von Lampes, owners of Tiffany House and bound by a pact with Asmodeus, were there for the Amulet too. Zebediah reached the Amulet in the vault below, and Stonecypher used the Vault Wand to void the Von Lampes. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c1-c10.md)</small> Dave on why that mattered: "disappearing them in stead of killing them put Asmodeus of your tail for a long time", "bc if you kill them go to hell!" <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> After the Arcaneum and the heist, Dave told the players, "you've foiled Asmodeus and know more about some of the prime movers of this whole antagonism." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-05)</small>

## Tiffany House and Revan

[Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) took Tiffany House, and its old debt to Asmodeus, when he assumed the lordship of Marsember, and his line is saddled with that debt forever. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-authority-factol-tiffany.md)</small> Dave: "Which includes Tiffany House and an old debt to Asmodeus which Revan assumed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-25)</small> Among Revan's contract negotiations: "Asmodeus: owns his soul and will demand the amulet and zarus". He was confronted by an erinyes, "who also serves as her housecarl and spy for Asmodeus. If killed, she returns to the House." <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small> Violet and Gritty Champagne are aliases of that one devil, Griddy, "the bounded servant" of "the pactbound owner of Tiffany House". <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small>

After the raid on the Old Man's House, a captured Dox was held by the party. Dave: "I guess he’ll make a deal with Gritty for his life to serve Asmodeus and Darkcember", "He’s convinced Asmodeus has the best chance to reach the egg first so serving him will have the same outcome as serving Cthulhu himself, since they will become one". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> Kara's player later asked the table to deal with "the issue of Asmodeus-worshippers usurping Revan’s control of TSCTC". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-21)</small> See [the Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/).

## The trap

In the final arc Asmodeus set a trap for the party. Dave: "The gang managed to escape a trap set by Asmodeus yesterday", and "both Revan and Zanzibar turned down offers from the devil to have everything they ever wanted which was a nice surprise". JL reported that "Asmodeus said she’s on Toril somewhere as his servant", of Revan's sister, [Sable](/people/sable-the-cyclone/). Dave: "It’s all culminating in Waterdeep". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-04-23)</small> The campaign checklist of 2022 had already listed "deal with Una/Spiderbros/Asmodeus?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-03-19)</small>

In the history the party did not change, "in the “present” era Asmodeus comes to Waterdeep and there is a massive final battle of the Infanta". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> Dave suggested that once back in the present the party could "muster the Angels to head off Asmodeus but win this time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small>

## At the threshold of the Egg

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Una, who had invaded [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/) with the Spiderbros, summoned Asmodeus to Waterdeep. The party killed Una, but not before the summoning, and a portal was used for him to step down toward the Egg. The party defeated him at the threshold of the portal from the tavern to the crack in the Egg, which they were defending to keep Una and any other Infanta out. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-five-zanzibars-and-asmodeus.md)</small>

At the Crack in the Egg, "A massive greater gate begins to open wide, and from inside you see the gleam of a massive gem glowing green and sparkling red as it rends a hole from here to the Shadowfell. You see the horns of Asmoedeus, then his snout, and the top of his hammer and his fist, but then no more. The head and swordarm of the Prince of Darkness plops out into the blood drenches floor of the Rift". Then the Lady of Pain rises, carrying the severed head, with Marcus on her shoulder: "We have completed our task, and returned Death to its natural rule." <small>(Sic. End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small> See [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## What the world remembers

The Egg and the final battle were obliviated. Only the Asmodean side of the war is remembered: Asmodeus came to kill the Infanta and establish dominion over Toril and was betrayed by St. Cloud, who is remembered as an ally of the devil. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-10)</small> The world remembers that troops on the surface led by [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) and [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) chiefly defeated the archdevil and sent him back, killing St. Cloud and ending the Infanta Crisis. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-five-zanzibars-and-asmodeus.md)</small> [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) stands on the ruins of the tavern where he fell, and [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/) dates its founding from his defeat. See [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

## After the Oblivia

In the order that followed Cypher's Wish, Dave described "the Raven Queen disbursing souls according to their allegiances, and Asmodeus still in charge of the hells". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small> Dis, the fallen seat he had taken, was flung into the Hells. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

In the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), Revan's line still carries the debt, and [Darius Klaxon](/people/darius-klaxon/) bears it; Griddy remains Asmodeus's servant, and the Klaxons' pact runs through her. See [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/). The party suspect that [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) is allied with Asmodeus, because he seems allied with the Klaxons.
