**Species unknown · Vessel of a Cypher Infanta · Starfall · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Rachel/cypher

**Rachel** is the vessel in whom an [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) of [Cypher](/people/stonecypher/) was instantiated. Whenever [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) succeeds in recovering the truth about its own origin, it is possible that Cypher is remembered again too, and that is why a Cypher Infanta was instantiated in Rachel, and why in the distant future an avatar of Cypher existed as well and spoke to [Caprica](/people/caprica/) in [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

In [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), during the Shrike plot, a Rachel and Sol had two months left together. The crew's choices were to bring them along to find [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), to bring them back to [Sigil](/places/sigil/) in case of some last-minute intervention, or to make them comfortable somewhere for their last two months. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-21)</small> What became of them is unknown, as is whether this Rachel is the vessel of the Cypher Infanta.

After Caprica was lost at the end of the arc, Dave, who ran it, raised the possibility that Rachel, as "Rachel/cypher", had rescued her off screen in a shuttle left aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-03)</small> In [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), set fifteen years later, Caprica is long dead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
