**Firbolg · Ranger 4 · Exozoologist, SSJ Zanzibar · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Forbes · Ensign Forbes

Ensign **Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp** is a firbolg ranger and the exozoologist of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), played by Dave. A beast master and alien animal expert on the ship's science staff, he was one of the first-level crew who played cards below decks in December 2022, during *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/).

## Background

Forbes is the ship's science ranger and an expert on alien animals. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small> He is a firbolg ranger science officer who talks like Stallone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small> His character sheet gives his background as Outlander. <small>(character sheet)</small> His birthplace and age are unknown.

## Light of Xaryxis

On the crew roster of the Zanzibar he is Ensign Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp, Exozoologist. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

The session of 9 December 2022 was a card game among the first-level crew below decks while the ship was spelljamming across the cosmos. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small> Forbes was played that night as the melee member of the group, a beast master exozoologist, alongside [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-07; 2022-12-08; 2022-12-09)</small>

Had Se7en been told to turn his pig Mr Hamburger over to the science department, Forbes would have told him to let the pig stay in its habitat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

His character sheet makes him a fourth-level ranger, a Beast Master with a primal companion, a Beast of the Land. His fighting style is Druidic Warrior, which gives him the cantrips *primal savagery* and *druidcraft*, and his favoured enemies are beasts. He fights with a two-handed weapon called the Big Stick, a thrown weapon called the Little Stick, and a longbow, and wears a breastplate. He carries a Scroller and a Mediscroller whose charges cast *cure wounds*, *lesser restoration* and *mass cure wounds*, and a hunting trap. He is proficient with the lyre and speaks Common, Elvish, Giant, Gnomish, Goblin, Primordial and Sylvan. As a firbolg he can speak with beasts and plants and turn briefly invisible. <small>(character sheet)</small> He has no proficiency with vehicles and is not a full caster. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small>

His primal beast can take almost any form, and before he reached third level he was to find a semi-immortal, shapeshifting alien species to become it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-09)</small>
