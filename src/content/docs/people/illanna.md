**Elf · Head of the queen's guard · Prosecutor at Roland's trial · Alive**

*Also known as:* Ilana <small>(the spelling used in Arc III and Arc IV)</small>

**Illanna** is an elf, head of the guard of [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) and a member of the Council of Forgetting. She appears from the first arc, escorting the heroes through the mythal and into the longhouse; at the Council she sat with Kara, Magnus, Drefan, Roland, Yoshi, Untari, Crookshanks, Chad, Bridgett and the Lexicon on the question of whether to hide Motherstone from the world or open it; and in Arc IV she and Untari urged the queen to abandon Motherstone and retreat to the Gildergreen. She prosecuted [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) at the trial that could have ended with his execution by [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), and lost.

<small>On the name: the record carries **Illanna**, **Ilana** in Arc III and Arc IV, and **ILLANNA** in Arc I. All three spellings stand.</small>

## In the record

She is there from the first arc: "Illanna escorts the heroes through the mythal and into the longhouse." <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile, Part VII.)</small>

She sits on the **Council of Forgetting**, the council that had to decide whether to tune the Obliviator to hide Motherstone and Snowdown from the world entirely or open the city — alongside Kara, Magnus, Drefan, Roland, Yoshi, Untari, Crookshanks, Chad, Bridgett and the Lexicon. The session notes summarise her in two words: **"Ilana – strong forgotten."** <small>(Arc III, Interlude.)</small>

The same notes record that "Ilana has been radicalized by the Forsworn and Calix, but is still the…" — the line is cut off in the surviving document. <small>(Arc III, Interlude.)</small>

In Arc IV, "nervous about the invitation of such powerful adversaries onto the island, Ilana and Untari have urged the queen to retreat to the Gildergreen, to abandon Motherstone and to hide with her most loyal followers until the end of the troubles." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part II.)</small>

## The trial

At the Second Battle of Motherstone Roland touched [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/), and [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) mind-controlled him through it; Roland destroyed the Lexicon and let the Company into Motherstone. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-30)</small> The party captured [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/) in [Roland's Cube](/items/rolands-cube/) and threw him and the Palantir into the Obliviator, after which nobody could remember Carlysle or the Palantir. Motherstone remembers only that Roland betrayed it. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small> The fanatic druids of Motherstone demanded his execution. Illanna prosecuted; [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) defended; Elistrae, [Marcus](/people/marcus/) and [Obi](/people/obi/) presided. Magnus swayed the Infanta and Roland was spared.

The Crown called [Pim](/people/pim/) as its primary witness. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small> Most of the witnesses against Roland ended up helping his case. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-23)</small>
