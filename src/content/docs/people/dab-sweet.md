**Trail boss · Frontier caravan · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · Status unknown**

**Dab Sweet** is an aging adventurer and the trail boss of the frontier caravan that the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) rode with on Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon, in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh, run by JL. He had a secret arrangement with the pink elves that broke down while the party rode with him, and afterwards he and his wife Pinky came home with the party to [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), where they took rooms at the Snapping Line. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The caravan

The caravan travelled with a large herd of giant toads as beasts of burden. Dab rode point, and set the party to ride flank and drag and to rustle the toads when they strayed. Pink elves attacked the caravan, burning carts and going after the civilians. One of the caravan, Legate Alorcus, a former cooper who held a deed to land in [Hardby](/places/hardby/), suspected the party of working with the elves. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> Dave's view of the leg: "I can hang in the Oregon trail more I think it’s a great setting". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-18)</small>

Also riding with the caravan was a teamster named Willy, who was [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) in disguise. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## The pink elves

Dab asked the party to back him at a parley with Zacharius, prince of the pink elves, "a hard man. But not without reason." On the ride he asked them pointed questions: their biggest regret, what they would do differently, and whether they believed the stories of the Shadowfell and Avernus. His own secret was a deal with the elves: they robbed his caravans with his consent and split the money. At the meeting they learned that Zacharius was dead, killed by his son Xavier, who would honor no earlier agreement: "Leave this place, Sweet. And take your dogs with you." <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Pinky**: his wife.
- **Zacharius** and **Xavier**: princes of the pink elves; the father his partner, the son his enemy.
- **Legate Alorcus**: a suspicious traveller in his caravan.
