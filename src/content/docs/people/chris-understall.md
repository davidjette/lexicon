**Cogborn · Elder of the trapped community · Alive**

<small>Also known as: Chris Understall · the elder in the Pit</small>

**Chris Understall** is a Cogborn elder, one of the people of the Cogs, the industrial slum of Sharn's Lower City. When the Cogs were flooded with troops and the district emptied, the community fled beneath it into a collapsing tunnel pocket called **the Pit**, and Understall was the elder on the far side of the cave-in that sealed it, holding a trapped population together while [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) negotiated a way out. [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/), [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) and [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/) opened the tunnel in a single night, and he led his people out through the Spine, down the Underhive river and out to the mountain tunnel. No description of him survives.

## The Pit

The Cogborn live in improvised structures welded onto the sides of factories and did the work that kept Sharn's machinery turning until the Iron Veil replaced them with soldiers. The Pit was sealed off by a cave-in with the Iron Veil above and gang-held sewers on every other side.

The tunnel was opened in a single night by Berenstain, Snodgrass and Doppler Klink working together, and the first thing on the other side was Chris Understall. Plans were made for the community to move once the way was confirmed safe. They moved: through the Spine, down the Underhive river, past a dead stone golem and out to the mountain tunnel, where [Preacher](/people/preacher/) and the RUST Runners led them in song.

> That night, Berenstain, Snodgrass, and Doppler worked together to clear the collapsed tunnel, opening a path to the Cogborn and reuniting with the Cogborn elder Chris Understall.

## Right now

The Cogborn are on the road to Gorgonhorn, and back in Sharn a Department of Infrastructure exists to restore the Cogs they left.
