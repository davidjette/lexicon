**Born Cogborn · Performer and Lower City courier · RUST · Alive**

<small>Also known as: DJ Rust · Rust</small>

**DJ Rust** is a charismatic Lower City performer of Sharn and a courier for [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), whose music career doubles as cover for his work within the city's underground networks. Born to a Cogborn family in the Cogs but raised in the Lantern District, he moves easily between both worlds, giving him access to communities the Empire rarely sees coming; his crowded shows in taverns and street venues allow him to pass coded messages, schedule covert handoffs and move information without attracting Imperial attention.

Long before the Iron Veil's occupation intensified he was already a familiar presence at Cogborn gatherings and vigils, and he was the first to recognise that [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/)'s research into Dhakaani tunnels and the Eternal Guardian ritual could be helpful to the Cogborn. He brought her into the Lower City network and saw that she reached people who could protect her; through him she met [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) and eventually [Preacher](/people/preacher/). Within R.U.S.T. he serves as a trusted courier and cultural anchor, coordinating with the Kross family, guiding newcomers through the Lower City, and using his performances to mask the movement of Cogborn families toward hidden tunnel entrances.

## Physical description

On stage he performs bare-chested, wiry and hard-muscled, with long twisted locs falling past his shoulders and a few strands bound in purple thread. He wears thin wire-rimmed spectacles that ought to look wrong on him and do not, a thin goatee, and a single heavy gold chain. He works close to the microphone with his whole face — the likeness the rebellion keeps of him is mid-verse, brows down, mouth wide, one fist choking the mic stand. <small>His likeness is recorded on the Rebel Factions page; no written description of him is recorded.</small>

## The two nightlives

The Lower City has two nightlives that barely touch. The Cogs hold vigils, wakes and gatherings; the Lantern District glows all night with late-night restaurants, cramped bars and small venues, and money moves through it. DJ Rust plays both, which gives him a professional reason to be in a different crowded room every night. He grew up between the two: one is an industrial slum of welded shanties clinging to factory walls, the other is paper lanterns and neon over late-night restaurants. He became a performer, and the performing became the cover. His instincts, mobility and loyalty make him indispensable to R.U.S.T.'s survival, and unlike almost everyone else in the network he does his work in public, on a stage, with his name on it.

He works closest with Misty and [Kriss Kross](/people/kriss-kross/), answers to Preacher, and is the first contact for anyone the network has not vetted. Breaker's Pit, the underground arena inside the Ziggurat, is where he can be found when he is working. In the field his method is charm as tradecraft: he collects a newcomer at a public event, walks them somewhere else while talking continuously, and arrives with them somewhere they could not have found alone. He escorted the party from Breaker's Pit to Misty's Pawn Shop after [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/)'s fight, in front of a crowd that had just watched three wanted terrorists win money.

## Breaker's Pit

Renn Tal came down out of the Sharn Library asking Cogborn elders about Dhakaani tunnels and sewer-line schematics, and every instinct in the Lower City said Imperial informant. DJ Rust decided she was not a spy but a woman wandering into danger, and brought her into the network instead of letting her disappear. Through him she met Misty; through Misty she reached Preacher; through Preacher the rebellion found the Forge of Binding. When she needed to reach the party after the library she did not give them an address — she told them to find DJ Rust at Breaker's Pit. He was there, he collected them after the bout, and he walked them into the network.

> "He recognized she was not a spy but a scholar wandering into danger."

## Right now

The Cogborn are out of Sharn and the district he grew up in is being rebuilt by a Department of Infrastructure that did not exist a year ago. He is one of the surviving R.U.S.T. names the rebellion still calls on.

**Organizations / Groups:** R.U.S.T., as courier and cultural anchor; the Lower City performance circuit.

**Holdings:** no property recorded. He works the taverns and street venues of the Lantern District and Breaker's Pit inside the Ziggurat.

**Items:** a heavy gold chain · wire-rimmed spectacles · a microphone he does not own.

**The Set List:** Rust's actual instrument. A crowded show is a scheduling device: coded messages go out from the stage, handoffs happen while the room is watching him, and Cogborn families move toward tunnel entrances under the noise of a performance the Iron Veil has no reason to raid.

**Personality Characteristics**

**Motivation:** to keep his people moving, and to keep them from being forgotten while they move.

**Likes:** a full room · Cogborn vigils · the Lantern District after dark · the gold chain · people who can be vouched for · walking someone somewhere while talking.

**Dislikes:** empty venues · informants · being asked to stop performing for security reasons · silence in the Cogs.

**Vices & Personality flaws:** his cover requires him to be the most visible man in a resistance network, and he enjoys it.
