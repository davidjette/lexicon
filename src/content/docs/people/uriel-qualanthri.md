**Female High Elf · Cleric of the Grave (in disguise as a Cleric of Light) · [The Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) · Holy Empress of the Pax Imperium · **Alive****

**Also known as:** Uri · the Holy Empress · the White Death · the white queen · the Dragon Mother · Empress Uriel Qualanthri · the Penitent Empress Uriel I

---

Holy Empress Uriel Qualanthri is a female high elf war cleric of Light from Aerenal, one of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and Holy Empress of the Pax Imperium. She came to Eberron against the wishes of her parents, elders in Aerenal, served the Pax Imperium as a conscripted combat medic, and at some point developed the Dragonmark of Death, which marks her as a descendant of the House of Vol. She founded a hospital in the old thieves' den at New Ham that grew into the University of Newham department she still heads, U.N.D.E.A.D., and a frontier hospital in the Talenta Plains raised after the Last War in answer to a pestilence she was herself spreading. Killed by a mind flayer in the sewers of Sharn, she was carried out by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and resurrected by the Fulcrum founder Fema Nolan, who embedded a diamond in her skull. She then discovered that her own commander in the Black Doves, [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), was the Emperor's secret consort, killed her, took her place at the bedside, and put Kaius III into a permanent coma by a blasphemous ritual; the Emperor has not sat the Throne of Bones in ten years and Uriel has ruled in his name for all of them. She has fourteen daughters by him, sired with blood taken from the collared black dragon Valtrex, purged the Black Dove leadership by witch hunt, inducted the loyal remainder into a fertility cult at the Ivory Lazaret, and intends to ring the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). To the rebellion she is the White Death, and Esther Crona's dying command was her death.

Tall and gleaming in black and silver armor and shield, bearing a sable dove on argent field, with a glimmering black-hilted longsword, an elderwood longbow, a tight black leather quiver with expertly crafted arrows on their hip, and a twisting and growing draconic mark between her navel and her loins. Uriel’s eyes are gold and shine with judgement.

Uriel is a war cleric of Light from Aerenal, one of the "Black Doves" who carry the grim duty of tending to the dead and dying on the field of battle and disposing of any undead that remain. All races and nations alike make way for these healer knights who tend to appear only when armies are likely to suffer immense casualties. Their order was quick to come to the aid of the Valenar in their adventures in the Last War, and embedded themselves among their elven kin, making themselves known throughout Khorvaire for their brand of mercy. The Black Doves are an isolated order made of only a few clans within the Aerenai, but they are shown respect and deference by other elves in matters of healing and death.

Uri is strikingly tall and lithe, strong, and good looking, though her dark demeanor and solemn dress give off a severe and unapproachable air, except for when she turns on the light priest glow and welcomes the wounded and the weary into unsuspecting peril. In private, he personality is flippant and openly racist against non-Elves, and her lust for sex and death leaks out of every sneer and off-hand comment. She frequently displays a shallow understanding of strategy and risk, having been coddled and encouraged for her natural talent and beauty with little consequence for cruelty or failure. Her respectable and austere uniform serves to hide her true nature as a rash, perverted psychopath who wants to conquer life for herself.

Uri is a gifted acolyte of Aerenai, a talented combat medic, and a warrior with a seeming singular drive to impress and dominate. She only makes friends with other elite warriors and casters, though she has been rumored to drink and fight with some of the rougher crowd whenever passing through a human or dwarven town. Her longsword belonged to their grandmother, who lives among the Undying Court on Aerenal.

Uri is branded with the Dragonmark of Death, a bloodline and power most believe was wiped out long ago by an alliance of elves and dragons to wipe out the aberrant House of Vol. Uri is a descendent of that House and an eager follower of the Erandis Vol, the half-dragon Queen of Death who remains in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house.

Uri believes that all but elvish life is cheap, and that the undead will ravage the kingdoms of man until the wilds are restored. She believes she can be a part of this by mating herself with a dragon emperor and bearing a Sovereign-like child that will command life and death at once. She believes that any elves who stand against her, including the Undying Court, are unworthy of their immortality and must have it taken from them.

As a conscripted combat medic for the Pax Imperium, Uri is an angel of death on the battlefield, saving the lives of those they consider worthy and either quiet or viciously ending those they do not. They are careful not to reveal their cruel nature to outsiders and even other Black Doves, and they frequently cross the line into murder when it suits them.

Uriel came to Eberron against the wishes of her parents, who are elders in Aerenal and did notthink the high elves should get involved in the Great War. Uriel wanted to kill and use magic and got to do plenty of that. At some point, Uriel developed the Mark of Death. Haven’t filled in that detail yet of how or when. Either way, it means Uriel descends from the House of Vol, which was eradicated by humans, giants and “good” elves thousands of years ago. She believes the Vol bloodline is superior and should rule all of Eberron as an undead wonderland.

Uriel is older than Kaius I, and was a neophyte when [Priest E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) and [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) hatched their plan. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-eberron-open-questions.md)</small> The age of 140 in her entry dates from the founding of the Unforeseen.

In the short term, Uriel is working on death sex magic to be ready for the opportunity to carry the child of an evil undead dragon. She aims to expose and command the Black Doves and turn them into her own personal army of Necromantic warrior priests, on behalf of her future husband Kaius. <small>(That entry was written at the founding of the Unforeseen.)</small>

## From founder to antagonist

Four Imperial operatives stood in the ruined village of Arden Crest and swore to work together and grow in power. Every one of them kept that oath and none of them kept it with the others. The party that pulled the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) out of the Greenbluff mines, held the Talenta Plains Express against a carriage full of rebels, and knelt on the marble of the throne room in Korth is the same party the Inevitables now have standing orders to kill. Uriel rose highest of the four.

She discovered that her own commander in the Black Doves, [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), was the Emperor’s secret consort and a rebel spy who intended to marry the Dragon Emperor and bear his child herself. Uriel proved the superior necromancer and the superior concubine, killed Duin, and took her place at the bedside. A blasphemous ritual put Kaius III into a permanent coma, from which he can do nothing but affirm her mandate and father her children at her whim. The Emperor has not sat the Throne of Bones in ten years. Uriel has ruled in his name for all of them.

After Esther Crona killed the Council and the Emperor took power, "he was also subdued by Uriel and made into a broodstallion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small> The Emperor was given out to be in treatment for "a very serious illness needing special care 24/7", which he came down with after the High Council "was exposed as traitors and executed and hunted down". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Uriel moved him out of Korth, "away from factions who had other aims", and so has "waning influence on politics but more and more monopoly on the emperor himself and the people". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## U.N.D.E.A.D.

The Hospital that Uriel founded in Newham has grown and thrived into a medical research and educational facility. She heads the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases AKA, U.N.D.E.A.D. There she continues to research the undead and discovers that the drug [Embrace](/items/embrace/) when used on F.Lighter slows down the decomposing process.

Six months after Uriel and Esther met the council in Korth, Uriel had buried herself in her studies. [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) kept her distance from Newham, and so from Uriel, appearing only at the groundbreaking ceremony for the university's expansion. The sex magic Uriel had used on **Flighter** <small>(also written F.Lighter)</small> left her a loyal ghoul. Since she could not openly keep a ghoul at the hospital, she patched up the head wound he received in death, and found that applying Embrace to him once a week prevents him from decomposing. It works on him alone: her experiments with Embrace on fresh corpses failed. People around the university whisper that the quiet, handsome soldier must be her lover, and on occasion she has caught him masturbating over her when they are alone. Flighter follows only Uriel's orders. He fights as a ghoul, still uses the greatsword he carried in life, is still proficient in navigation, and can speak a handful of words. Months of laboratory work made Uriel proficient with a herbalism kit. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 6)</small>

## The throne room

On the carriage ride through the Blade Desert to Arden Crest, Uriel daydreamed of her work at the university and her experiments with sex magic, thinking "I need to find more Embrace!", and imagined herself pregnant, walking nude through city streets with hordes of undead under her command behind her, [Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) among them. A sense of dread broke in. The sun darkened as blood pooled over it, a hooded figure beside her said "Seven years!", and she woke with a jolt beside Locke, Esther and LeBeefe, all of them as horrified and confused as she was. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 9)</small>

In the throne room at Korth the hooded figure dropped her robes and stood revealed as Gabrielle Duin, seven years a spy inside the rebellion until the Unforeseen burned her cell's work on the island. She forgave the four, and warned Uriel: "don’t let my forgiveness fool you Uriel, don’t play your silly games with me. Study the undead all you wish but get in my way and you will become another body to add to my collection." She then opened the Crimson Sun carved in the floor, raised a platform bearing fifteen charred rebel bodies and a hooded woman in chains, and led the woman to Uriel: "A gift, for you dear Uriel." The woman was [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/). <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 9)</small>

## Blood work

After the Rekkenmark burned, Chancellor Magnolia Steele — in the last act of her office, before she stepped down and no replacement was named — permitted Uriel and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) to move Dr. Marble’s research in autoanimata and necrocryptography to the Rekkenmark East facilities, with supplemental support from the UN-DEAD. Uriel had already taken samples from Marble’s body. <small>(Dave's Campaign 1 DM notes, City of Knives)</small>

Her own work centred on the magical nature of blood. She synthesised a small store of Embrace from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble, and concluded from it that the sole source of Embrace is the blood of vampires. She then set out to collect vampires she could enslave and harvest at scale. Everything the Empire has since done with Embrace, with the Children of Ember, and with the umbra pipeline that fed Project E.D.E.N. runs downstream of that single downtime experiment. <small>(Dave's Campaign 1 DM notes, City of Knives)</small>

## The diamond

On a visit to Sharn some years ago Uriel was killed by a mind flayer in the city's sewers. Esther Crona carried her out to a rebel base beyond the walls, where a founder of Fulcrum named Fema Nolan resurrected her and embedded a diamond in her skull. Nolan, who now goes by Zero-One and leads Fulcrum, admitted this to the rebellion herself. The diamond was later caught on an Arcane Eye recording, glowing at the base of Uriel's skull.

## Calcifer

Uriel delivered Esther Crona's son on the penthouse balcony. The recovered memory of that night — her hand raised over the newborn in a sickly green glow, dark vein-like lines forming on his cheek, and the freeze when Esther screamed — was broadcast across the whole of Sharn during the rising. It was what finally turned Esther against her.

Asked what the Empress would do about a messiah Esther might already have borne, Dave answered: "Kill it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-31)</small>

Esther Crona's dying command, carried on every screen in the city, was to kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri. The Inevitables have been moving toward the Ivory Lazaret ever since.

## The Ivory Lazaret

Uriel forswore battle and founded the Ivory Lazaret: a hospital, a retreat and a cloister, built around the comatose body of Kaius III and never left. Its threshold carries an Elvish lintel inscription — *None may enter who come bearing harm* — that is a binding ward and not a blessing. Petitioners kneel in the vestibule in uncanny stillness and never move. Nuns drift between rows of motionless patients singing an endless lullaby, and the patients are undead, and so are most of the nuns.

The loyal remainder of the Black Doves were inducted here into a fertility cult: a corrupted nunnery obsessed with mothering the dragon bloodline and birthing a race of half-dragons meant to lead Uriel’s undead armies across Khorvaire, so that Uriel and her sisters can return to Aerenal victorious. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/)’s lead places the building in defensible mountains northeast of Qarth, on the Summer Palace grounds upriver of Korth, reachable by a boat that sails twice a day.

Her public persona, after she founded and entered this monastery to heal the Emperor, is **the Penitent Empress Uriel I**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> The place is "a huge medical spa for him, attended by Uriel’s acolytes" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>, and "a temple to her and the emperors living corpse, where the sick are healed and sent home unawares". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

After killing Gabrielle Duin, Uriel led a purge of the Black Doves and turned most of the order into her convent. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> Dave: "So the Black Doves which were a truly holy order when she took them from Duin, are now a vehicle for the Blood of Vol and Emerald Claw, she has completely corrupted them" / "So she can’t purge them all". The elves of Aerenal are divided over her taking Eberron, depending on whether they are elven supremacists. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Her hospitals across the land let her convent spread and control her secret undead armies under the protection of their reputation, with no obvious guard beyond a few paladins at each, where Esther kept inquisitors in force everywhere. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> She uses "a refined version of Embrace to embed undead servants all over society and in large numbers in the army". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Black Dove battle clerics still assisted Esther's Iron Veil, "the best place to recruit for the true Army". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> The Empire's leaders have hollowed out its institutions for their own purposes and ride them into the ground, each trying to get the upper hand on the others. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## The Daughters

She has fourteen daughters by the Emperor, many of them sets of twins, all of them under twelve years old, and not one of them a son. In the nursery they play under yearly portraits in which every girl is the spitting image of her mother; they hum in unison and finish each other’s sentences, and they are far more perceptive than children of that age have any business being. The rebellion calls them the Daughters of Vol and has fought them in the field as undead E.D.E.N. units under Uriel’s command.

They are not, it turns out, the fruit of a reawakened Vol bloodline. The dragon half was harvested. For three years the Empire kept the black dragon Valtrex collared and bled beneath the Korramont and shipped the blood out by rail, and Valtrex — freed, sane, and testifying — states plainly that Uriel used his stolen blood to sire her heirs. By his account he is their father.

Dave: "she has had many miscarriages from failed attempts but has the emperor comatose so keeps making new ones" / "She’s also brought some to term which she considers failures also and they are all female children half dragon demon kids" / "And they live near her in the palace". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

## The Scion

As the Penitent Empress, Uriel was already pregnant with **the Scion**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Her cult's purpose was the dragon's blood, used to make a dragon spawn that she would carry or raise. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> The doctrine she brings to the faith of the Sovereign Host is "Installing her self + unborn son as the eight Sovereign of course" <small>(sic)</small>: a pregnant god of life and death, the reincarnation of Erandis Vol, "birthing a race marked with the dragonmark of Death and ascending to rule the Host as its sole primateur". She secretly means never to deliver the son. Dave: "She secretly plans to never bear her child the heir and dragon incarnate, instead to siphon its power into her own, turning it into a living phalactery" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Both Valtrex and LeBeefe connect the dragon blood she took to "her scheme to birth the next dragon messiah". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## Eternal Nightfall

Sister Nora of the Black Doves, taken in a cellar in Sharn and questioned, gave up three things: Project E.D.E.N., the names of Esther Crona’s sisters, and Uriel’s doctrine of the Eternal Nightfall. Nora was executed after the rising and left no further account of it. What the Inevitables have since assembled is the shape of the thing rather than its text: the dead rise everywhere, and stay risen, and the wilds take back the kingdoms of man exactly as Uriel has said in writing that she wants.

## The bells

The Fantanya Nyel — the Hell’s Bells — are real, there are thirteen Master castings and hundreds of parish bells beneath them, and Uriel intends to ring them. That is Locke Pierce’s finding and the whole basis of his ultimatum. Fulcrum’s decade-long bell-counting operation and a partially burned foundry schedule put the ringing on an appointed night; the Inevitables’ own reading of the dossiers concludes she is waiting on a single event, most likely the birth of a male heir. Ten bells remain. At least one of them is a Master, and it is in Korth.

Uriel does not leave the Emperor’s side. She waits on a son, and every daughter is a failure she has to be patient about. Her undead guards turned on Supreme Admiral Vex d’Lyrandar at the Thronehold Summit on her order, which is how the last chance at peace died. And a Ministry of Law warrant is currently carrying three people with her name on their orders straight onto her grounds.

**Vices & Personality flaws**

A rash, perverted psychopath under an austere uniform. She has a shallow understanding of strategy and risk, and has never once been made to pay for cruelty or failure.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Emperor Kaius III** — her husband, her patient and her prisoner. She put him in the coma herself. Comatose; father of her fourteen daughters; reported by Locke Pierce’s dossier and by LeBeefe’s lingering voice to be a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun, kept alive as a vessel and fed dragon blood.
- **[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)** — fellow founder of the Unforeseen, godmother to Esther’s son, and the woman who carried Uriel’s corpse out of the Sharn sewers. Dead. Her last command was Uriel’s death.
- **[Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/)** — her commander in the Black Doves, the Emperor’s consort before her, and the woman she killed for the bedside. Officially missing; blame placed on Lady Glow.
- **[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)** — fellow founder, now her declared enemy, holding a weaponised recreation of the Mourning he has promised to use on the world if she rings first. Alive, and in the skies over Sharn.
- **[John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)** — fellow founder. Dead at Newham. Still audible.
- **Sister Nora** — Black Dove of the Sanctum of the Last Mercy in Sharn, and widely and correctly believed to be Uriel’s eyes in that city. Executed after the Sharn rising.
- **Sister-Inquisitor Maelis Dorn** — Black Dove of the Light out of the Lazaret, and the only expression of Uriel’s will at the Thronehold Summit. Alive.
- **Fema Nolan, called Zero-One** — the Silver Flame paladin who resurrected her and set the diamond in her skull, and who now leads Fulcrum against her. Taken alive at Zilspar Farm.

**Hobbies & Pets**

None recorded. Her downtime is laboratory work on the magical nature of blood, and a standing intention to acquire vampires she can keep.
