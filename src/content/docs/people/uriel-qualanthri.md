**Female High Elf · Cleric of the Grave (in disguise as a Cleric of Light) · [The Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) · Holy Empress of the Pax Imperium · **Alive****

**Also known as:** Uri · the Holy Empress · the White Death · the white queen · the Dragon Mother · Empress Uriel Qualanthri

---

Holy Empress Uriel Qualanthri is a female high elf war cleric of Light from Aerenal, one of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and Holy Empress of the Pax Imperium. She came to Eberron against the wishes of her parents, elders in Aerenal, served the Pax Imperium as a conscripted combat medic, and at some point developed the Dragonmark of Death, which marks her as a descendant of the House of Vol. She founded a hospital in the old thieves' den at New Ham that grew into the University of Newham department she still heads, U.N.D.E.A.D., and a frontier hospital in the Talenta Plains raised after the Last War in answer to a pestilence she was herself spreading. Killed by a mind flayer in the sewers of Sharn, she was carried out by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and resurrected by the Fulcrum founder Fema Nolan, who embedded a diamond in her skull. She then discovered that her own commander in the Black Doves, [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), was the Emperor's secret consort, killed her, took her place at the bedside, and put Kaius III into a permanent coma by a blasphemous ritual; the Emperor has not sat the Throne of Bones in ten years and Uriel has ruled in his name for all of them. She has fourteen daughters by him, sired with blood taken from the collared black dragon Valtrex, purged the Black Dove leadership by witch hunt, inducted the loyal remainder into a fertility cult at the Ivory Lazaret, and intends to ring the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). To the rebellion she is the White Death, and Esther Crona's dying command was her death.

Tall and gleaming in black and silver armor and shield, bearing a sable dove on argent field, with a glimmering black-hilted longsword, an elderwood longbow, a tight black leather quiver with expertly crafted arrows on their hip, and a twisting and growing draconic mark between her navel and her loins. Uriel’s eyes are gold and shine with judgement.

Uriel is a war cleric of Light from Aerenal, one of the "Black Doves" who carry the grim duty of tending to the dead and dying on the field of battle and disposing of any undead that remain. All races and nations alike make way for these healer knights who tend to appear only when armies are likely to suffer immense casualties. Their order was quick to come to the aid of the Valenar in their adventures in the Last War, and embedded themselves among their elven kin, making themselves known throughout Khorvaire for their brand of mercy. The Black Doves are an isolated order made of only a few clans within the Aerenai, but they are shown respect and deference by other elves in matters of healing and death.

Uri is strikingly tall and lithe, strong, and good looking, though her dark demeanor and solemn dress give off a severe and unapproachable air, except for when she turns on the light priest glow and welcomes the wounded and the weary into unsuspecting peril. In private, he personality is flippant and openly racist against non-Elves, and her lust for sex and death leaks out of every sneer and off-hand comment. She frequently displays a shallow understanding of strategy and risk, having been coddled and encouraged for her natural talent and beauty with little consequence for cruelty or failure. Her respectable and austere uniform serves to hide her true nature as a rash, perverted psychopath who wants to conquer life for herself.

Uri is a gifted acolyte of Aerenai, a talented combat medic, and a warrior with a seeming singular drive to impress and dominate. She only makes friends with other elite warriors and casters, though she has been rumored to drink and fight with some of the rougher crowd whenever passing through a human or dwarven town. Her longsword belonged to their grandmother, who lives among the Undying Court on Aerenal.

Uri is branded with the Dragonmark of Death, a bloodline and power most believe was wiped out long ago by an alliance of elves and dragons to wipe out the aberrant House of Vol. Uri is a descendent of that House and an eager follower of the Erandis Vol, the half-dragon Queen of Death who remains in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house.

Uri believes that all but elvish life is cheap, and that the undead will ravage the kingdoms of man until the wilds are restored. She believes she can be a part of this by mating herself with a dragon emperor and bearing a Sovereign-like child that will command life and death at once. She believes that any elves who stand against her, including the Undying Court, are unworthy of their immortality and must have it taken from them.

As a conscripted combat medic for the Pax Imperium, Uri is an angel of death on the battlefield, saving the lives of those they consider worthy and either quiet or viciously ending those they do not. They are careful not to reveal their cruel nature to outsiders and even other Black Doves, and they frequently cross the line into murder when it suits them.

Uriel came to Eberron against the wishes of her parents, who are elders in Aerenal and did notthink the high elves should get involved in the Great War. Uriel wanted to kill and use magic and got to do plenty of that. At some point, Uriel developed the Mark of Death. Haven’t filled in that detail yet of how or when. Either way, it means Uriel descends from the House of Vol, which was eradicated by humans, giants and “good” elves thousands of years ago. It’s leader, Erandis Vol, was the Queen of Death, a half dragon half elf that ruled over undeath. She’s Uriel’s ancestor. Uriel wants to be like her mother and fuck Kaius and give birth to the next Dragon/Elf demigod. She believes Kaius is a dragon in disguise, and may also be her relative from the same line. She believes the Vol bloodline is superior and should rule all of Eberron as an undead wonderland.

In the short term, Uriel is working on death sex magic to be ready for the opportunity to carry the child of an evil undead dragon. She aims to expose and command the Black Doves and turn them into her own personal army of Necromantic warrior priests, on behalf of her future husband Kaius. <small>(That entry was written at the founding of the Unforeseen.)</small>

## From founder to antagonist

Four Imperial operatives stood in the ruined village of Arden Crest and swore to work together and grow in power. Every one of them kept that oath and none of them kept it with the others. The party that pulled the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) out of the Greenbluff mines, held the Talenta Plains Express against a carriage full of rebels, and knelt on the marble of the throne room in Korth is the same party the Inevitables now have standing orders to kill. Uriel rose highest of the four.

She discovered that her own commander in the Black Doves, [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), was the Emperor’s secret consort and a rebel spy who intended to marry the Dragon Emperor and bear his child herself. Uriel proved the superior necromancer and the superior concubine, killed Duin, and took her place at the bedside. A blasphemous ritual put Kaius III into a permanent coma, from which he can do nothing but affirm her mandate and father her children at her whim. The Emperor has not sat the Throne of Bones in ten years. Uriel has ruled in his name for all of them.

## U.N.D.E.A.D.

The Hospital that Uriel founded in Newham has grown and thrived into a medical research and educational facility. She heads the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases AKA, U.N.D.E.A.D. There she continues to research the undead and discovers that the drug Embrace when used on F.Lighter slows down the decomposing process.

## Blood work

After the Rekkenmark burned, Chancellor Magnolia Steele — in the last act of her office, before she stepped down and no replacement was named — permitted Uriel and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) to move Dr. Marble’s research in autoanimata and necrocryptography to the Rekkenmark East facilities, with supplemental support from the UN-DEAD. Uriel had already taken samples from Marble’s body.

Her own work centred on the magical nature of blood. She synthesised a small store of Embrace from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble, and concluded from it that the sole source of Embrace is the blood of vampires. She then set out to collect vampires she could enslave and harvest at scale. Everything the Empire has since done with Embrace, with the Children of Ember, and with the umbra pipeline that fed Project E.D.E.N. runs downstream of that single downtime experiment.

## The White Death

To the Empire she is the Holy Empress. To the rebellion she is the White Death.

## The diamond

On a visit to Sharn some years ago Uriel was killed by a mind flayer in the city's sewers. Esther Crona carried her out to a rebel base beyond the walls, where a founder of Fulcrum named Fema Nolan resurrected her and embedded a diamond in her skull. Nolan, who now goes by Zero-One and leads Fulcrum, admitted this to the rebellion herself. The diamond was later caught on an Arcane Eye recording, glowing at the base of Uriel's skull.

<small>Zero-One has since been taken alive at Zilspar Farm.</small>

## Calcifer

Uriel delivered Esther Crona's son on the penthouse balcony. The recovered memory of that night — her hand raised over the newborn in a sickly green glow, dark vein-like lines forming on his cheek, and the freeze when Esther screamed — was broadcast across the whole of Sharn during the rising. It was what finally turned Esther against her.

Esther Crona's dying command, carried on every screen in the city, was to kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri. The Inevitables have been moving toward the Ivory Lazaret ever since.

## The Ivory Lazaret

Uriel forswore battle and founded the Ivory Lazaret: a hospital, a retreat and a cloister, built around the comatose body of Kaius III and never left. Its threshold carries an Elvish lintel inscription — *None may enter who come bearing harm* — that is a binding ward and not a blessing. Petitioners kneel in the vestibule in uncanny stillness and never move. Nuns drift between rows of motionless patients singing an endless lullaby, and the patients are undead, and so are most of the nuns.

The loyal remainder of the Black Doves were inducted here into a fertility cult: a corrupted nunnery obsessed with mothering the dragon bloodline and birthing a race of half-dragons meant to lead Uriel’s undead armies across Khorvaire, so that Uriel and her sisters can return to Aerenal victorious. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/)’s lead places the building in defensible mountains northeast of Qarth, on the Summer Palace grounds upriver of Korth, reachable by a boat that sails twice a day.

## The Daughters

She has fourteen daughters by the Emperor, many of them sets of twins, all of them under twelve years old, and not one of them a son. In the nursery they play under yearly portraits in which every girl is the spitting image of her mother; they hum in unison and finish each other’s sentences, and they are far more perceptive than children of that age have any business being. The rebellion calls them the Daughters of Vol and has fought them in the field as undead E.D.E.N. units under Uriel’s command.

They are not, it turns out, the fruit of a reawakened Vol bloodline. The dragon half was harvested. For three years the Empire kept the black dragon Valtrex collared and bled beneath the Korramont and shipped the blood out by rail, and Valtrex — freed, sane, and testifying — states plainly that Uriel used his stolen blood to sire her heirs. By his account he is their father.

## Eternal Nightfall

Sister Nora of the Black Doves, taken in a cellar in Sharn and questioned, gave up three things: Project E.D.E.N., the names of Esther Crona’s sisters, and Uriel’s doctrine of the Eternal Nightfall. Nora was executed after the rising and left no further account of it. What the Inevitables have since assembled is the shape of the thing rather than its text: the dead rise everywhere, and stay risen, and the wilds take back the kingdoms of man exactly as Uriel has said in writing that she wants.

## The bells

The Fantanya Nyel — the Hell’s Bells — are real, there are thirteen Master castings and hundreds of parish bells beneath them, and Uriel intends to ring them. That is Locke Pierce’s finding and the whole basis of his ultimatum. Fulcrum’s decade-long bell-counting operation and a partially burned foundry schedule put the ringing on an appointed night; the Inevitables’ own reading of the dossiers concludes she is waiting on a single event, most likely the birth of a male heir. Ten bells remain. At least one of them is a Master, and it is in Korth.

## Right Now

Uriel does not leave the Emperor’s side. She waits on a son, and every daughter is a failure she has to be patient about. Her undead guards turned on Supreme Admiral Vex d’Lyrandar at the Thronehold Summit on her order, which is how the last chance at peace died. And a Ministry of Law warrant is currently carrying three people with her name on their orders straight onto her grounds.
