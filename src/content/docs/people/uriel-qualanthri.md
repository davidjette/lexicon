**Female High Elf · Cleric of the Grave (in disguise as a Cleric of Light) · [The Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) · Holy Empress of the Pax Imperium · Dead ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

**Also known as:** Uri · the Holy Empress · the White Death · the white queen · the Dragon Mother · Empress Uriel Qualanthri · the Penitent Empress Uriel I

---

Holy Empress **Uriel Qualanthri** was a high elf cleric from Aerenal, a combat medic of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), and a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). For ten years she ruled the Pax Imperium in the name of [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/). She bore the Dragonmark of Death, which marked her as a descendant of the House of Vol, and she passed as a cleric of Light while she served to further her evil ends. To the Empire she was the Holy Empress, to the rebellion she was the White Death. She was one of the four founders of the Unforeseen the last enemy of the Inevitables.

She came to Khorvaire against the wishes of her parents and served the Empire as a conscripted medic in the Last War. She founded a hospital at New Ham that grew into the university department she headed for the rest of her life, U.N.D.E.A.D. She was killed by a mind flayer under Sharn and raised by [Fema Nolan](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/), who set a diamond in her skull in place of her brain. She killed her commander, [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), took Duin's place beside the Emperor, married him, and held him in torpor with a rod through his heart. At the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) she turned the Black Doves into a convent of her own, bore the Emperor fourteen daughters, and carried a son, the [Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/), far past his time. She meant to ring the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) and raise the dead of Khorvaire.

[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s dying command was to kill her, and [the Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) carried it out. In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) she rang the Original over Nos Matre, was beheaded in its forecourt, and was destroyed in the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/) beneath it, on the day her son was born.

## Appearance and character

Tall and gleaming in black and silver armor and shield, bearing a sable dove on argent field, with a glimmering black-hilted longsword, an elderwood longbow, a tight black leather quiver with expertly crafted arrows on her hip, and a twisting and growing draconic mark between her navel and her loins. Uriel’s eyes were gold and shone with judgement.

![Uriel Qualanthri in the black and silver armour of the Black Doves](/images/uploads/uriel-qualanthri-black-dove-og-photo-musotos4.webp)
*Uriel Qualanthri as a Black Dove, in the original portrait.*

The Black Doves carried the grim duty of tending to the dead and dying on the field of battle and disposing of any undead that remained. All races and nations alike made way for these healer knights, who tended to appear only when armies were likely to suffer immense casualties. Their order was quick to come to the aid of the Valenar in their adventures in the Last War, and embedded themselves among their elven kin, making themselves known throughout Khorvaire for their brand of mercy. The Black Doves were an isolated order made of only a few clans within the Aerenai, but they were shown respect and deference by other elves in matters of healing and death.

Uri was strikingly tall and lithe, strong, and good looking, though her dark demeanor and solemn dress gave off a severe and unapproachable air, except for when she turned on the light priest glow and welcomed the wounded and the weary into unsuspecting peril. In private, her personality was flippant and openly racist against non-Elves, and her lust for sex and death leaked out of every sneer and off-hand comment. She frequently displayed a shallow understanding of strategy and risk, having been coddled and encouraged for her natural talent and beauty with little consequence for cruelty or failure. Her respectable and austere uniform served to hide her true nature as a rash, perverted psychopath who wanted to conquer life for herself.

Uri was a gifted acolyte of Aerenal, a talented combat medic, and a warrior with a seeming singular drive to impress and dominate. She only made friends with other elite warriors and casters, though she was rumored to drink and fight with some of the rougher crowd whenever passing through a human or dwarven town. Her longsword belonged to her grandmother, who lived among the Undying Court on Aerenal.

As a conscripted combat medic for the Pax Imperium, Uri was an angel of death on the battlefield, saving the lives of those she considered worthy and either quietly or viciously ending those she did not. She was careful not to reveal her cruel nature to outsiders and even other Black Doves, and she frequently crossed the line into murder when it suited her.

## Beliefs and ambition

Uriel came to Khorvaire against the wishes of her parents, who were elders in Aerenal and did not think the high elves should get involved in the Great War. She wanted to kill and use magic, and got to do plenty of that.

At some point she developed the Dragonmark of Death. How and when was never established. Most believed the mark and its bloodline had been wiped out long ago, when an alliance of elves and dragons destroyed the aberrant House of Vol. Uriel was a descendant of that House and an eager follower of Erandis Vol, the half-dragon Queen of Death, who had been in hiding since the calamity that destroyed her house.

She believed that all but elvish life was cheap, and that the undead would ravage the kingdoms of man until the wilds were restored. She believed she could be a part of this by mating with a dragon emperor and bearing a Sovereign-like child that would command life and death at once. She believed that Kaius was a dragon in disguise, and possibly her relative from the same line. She believed the Vol bloodline was superior and should rule all of Eberron as an undead wonderland, and that any elves who stood against her, including the Undying Court, were unworthy of their immortality and must have it taken from them.

When the Unforeseen were founded, her short-term work was death sex magic, to be ready for the opportunity to carry the child of an evil undead dragon. She aimed to expose and command the Black Doves and turn them into her own personal army of necromantic warrior priests, on behalf of her future husband Kaius.

## With the Unforeseen

Four Imperial operatives stood in the ruined village of Arden Crest and swore to work together and grow in power. Every one of them kept that oath and none of them kept it with the others. They pulled the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) out of the Greenbluff mines, held the Talenta Plains Express against a carriage full of rebels, and knelt on the marble of the throne room in Korth. A generation later the Inevitables had standing orders to kill them. Uriel rose highest of the four.

![The four founders of the Unforeseen](/images/uploads/the-unforeseen-john-c-lebeefe-esther-cro-mu3h0twc.webp)

### U.N.D.E.A.D.

The hospital that Uriel founded in the old thieves' den at New Ham grew into a medical research and teaching facility. She headed the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases, or U.N.D.E.A.D. There she researched the undead.

Six months after Uriel and Esther met the council in Korth, Uriel had buried herself in her studies. [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) kept her distance from Newham, and so from Uriel, appearing only at the groundbreaking ceremony for the university's expansion. The sex magic Uriel had used on **Flighter** <small>(also written F.Lighter)</small>, a dead Imperial soldier, left her a loyal ghoul. Since she could not openly keep a ghoul at the hospital, she patched up the head wound he received in death, and found that applying the drug [Embrace](/items/embrace/) to him once a week prevented him from decomposing. It worked on him alone: her experiments with Embrace on fresh corpses failed. People around the university whispered that the quiet, handsome soldier must be her lover, and on occasion she caught him masturbating over her when they were alone. Flighter followed only Uriel's orders. He fought as a ghoul, still used the greatsword he carried in life, was still proficient in navigation, and could speak a handful of words. Months of laboratory work made Uriel proficient with a herbalism kit. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 6)</small>

### The throne room

On the carriage ride through the Blade Desert to Arden Crest, Uriel daydreamed of her work at the university and her experiments with sex magic, thinking "I need to find more Embrace!", and imagined herself pregnant, walking nude through city streets with hordes of undead under her command behind her, [Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) among them. A sense of dread broke in. The sun darkened as blood pooled over it, a hooded figure beside her said "Seven years!", and she woke with a jolt beside Locke, Esther and LeBeefe, all of them as horrified and confused as she was. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 9)</small>

In the throne room at Korth the hooded figure dropped her robes and stood revealed as Gabrielle Duin, seven years a spy inside the rebellion until the Unforeseen burned her cell's work on the island. She forgave the four, and warned Uriel:

> I trained you well Uriel, perhaps too well. I do find it….. cute, that you believe your mark means anything, or perhaps Vol blood runs through your veins as it does mine, but don’t let my forgiveness fool you Uriel, don’t play your silly games with me. Study the undead all you wish but get in my way and you will become another body to add to my collection.\
> — Gabrielle Duin, to Uriel, in the throne room at Korth

She then opened the Crimson Sun carved in the floor, raised a platform bearing fifteen charred rebel bodies and a hooded woman in chains, and led the woman to Uriel: "A gift, for you dear Uriel." The woman was [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), a known rebel and famous singer. Uriel performed her sex magic on the corpse in front of Duin and the Emperor and raised Lady Glow as a ghoul. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 9)</small>

### Blood work

After the Rekkenmark burned, Chancellor Magnolia Steele permitted Uriel and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) to move Dr. Marble’s research in autoanimata and necrocryptography to the Rekkenmark East facilities, with supplemental support from U.N.D.E.A.D. It was the last act of Steele's office before she stepped down, and no replacement was named. Uriel had already taken samples from Marble’s body. <small>(Dave's Campaign 1 DM notes, City of Knives)</small>

Her own work centred on the magical nature of blood. She synthesised a small store of Embrace from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble, and concluded from it that the sole source of Embrace is the blood of vampires. She then set out to collect vampires she could enslave and harvest at scale. Everything the Empire later did with Embrace, with the Children of Ember, and with the umbra pipeline that fed Project E.D.E.N. followed from that single downtime experiment. <small>(Dave's Campaign 1 DM notes, City of Knives)</small>

## The diamond

On a visit to Sharn Uriel had her brain sucked out by a mind flayer in a hidden temple below the city. Esther Crona carried her out to a rebel base beyond the walls, where Fema Nolan, then a nurse in the rebel camp, resurrected her and replaced her brain with a massive diamond. The diamond, together with the Headband of Intellect she wore, turned Uriel from a lustful fanatic into an archgenius. Nolan went on to found Fulcrum and led it against her as [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/), and admitted the resurrection to the rebellion herself. The diamond was later caught on an Arcane Eye recording, glowing at the base of Uriel's skull.

![Esther Crona carries Uriel's body into the rebel camp, where Fema Nolan reaches for her as LeBeefe and Locke Pierce look on](/images/uploads/esther-uriel-lebeefe-locke-and-fema-nola-muxjg6ke.webp)
*Esther carries Uriel to Fema Nolan in the rebel camp.*

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) Zero-One said that when she first raised Uriel, Uriel's brain was missing, and that she put a gem in its place *"which had raised her from the dead by the light of the Flame."* She believed she had given Uriel the mind behind everything that followed. Uriel could raise the dead and had done it everywhere, but in Zero-One's account there were levels of lifelike animation that still escaped her. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

## Empress

Uriel discovered that her own commander in the Black Doves, [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), was the Emperor’s secret consort and a rebel spy who intended to marry the Dragon Emperor and bear his child herself. Uriel proved the superior necromancer and the superior concubine, killed Duin, and took her place at the bedside. Duin was officially missing, and the blame was placed on Lady Glow.

The Empire was told that a blasphemous ritual had left Kaius III in a permanent coma, from which he could do nothing but affirm her mandate and father her children. The coma was a lie, told even to the highest of her own sisters, and Esther Crona believed it when she died. Uriel had driven a rod of petrified wood through the Emperor's heart. He was a vampire, and the rod held him in torpor and left her able to breed with him. He was fed dragon blood through a line in his arm, and she performed rites of sex and blood over him to conceive the dragon heir of Death. He did not sit the Throne of Bones again, and she ruled in his name for ten years.

After Esther Crona killed the Council and the Emperor took power, "he was also subdued by Uriel and made into a broodstallion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small> The Emperor was given out to be in treatment for "a very serious illness needing special care 24/7", which he came down with after the High Council "was exposed as traitors and executed and hunted down". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Uriel moved him out of Korth, "away from factions who had other aims", and so had "waning influence on politics but more and more monopoly on the emperor himself and the people". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

### The wedding

Tavern talk in Leef, in the year of the Leef / Newham arc, was that the Emperor had married a priestess of the Black Doves named Uriel, that his wedding gift to the Empire had been the abolition of slavery, and that he had fallen sick and had not been seen since his wedding day. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) was always secretly in love with Uriel, and was hurt when Uriel married the Emperor. Uriel's wedding gift to Esther was the undead bodies of the Council, to serve Esther as her Death Knights. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small>

## The Ivory Lazaret

Uriel forswore battle and founded the Ivory Lazaret: a hospital, a retreat and a cloister, built around the body of Kaius III on the Summer Palace grounds upriver of Korth. Its threshold carried an Elvish lintel inscription, *None may enter who come bearing harm*, which was a binding ward and no blessing. Petitioners knelt in the vestibule in uncanny stillness and never moved. Nuns drifted between rows of motionless patients singing an endless lullaby. The patients were undead, and so were most of the nuns.

Her public persona, after she founded and entered this monastery to heal the Emperor, was **the Penitent Empress Uriel I**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> The place was "a huge medical spa for him, attended by Uriel’s acolytes" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>, and "a temple to her and the emperors living corpse, where the sick are healed and sent home unawares". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

After killing Gabrielle Duin, Uriel purged the Black Dove leadership by witch hunt and turned most of the order into her convent. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> The loyal remainder were inducted at the Lazaret into a fertility cult: a corrupted nunnery obsessed with mothering the dragon bloodline and birthing a race of half-dragons meant to lead Uriel’s undead armies across Khorvaire, so that Uriel and her sisters could return to Aerenal victorious. Dave: "So the Black Doves which were a truly holy order when she took them from Duin, are now a vehicle for the Blood of Vol and Emerald Claw, she has completely corrupted them" / "So she can’t purge them all". The elves of Aerenal were divided over her taking Eberron, depending on whether they were elven supremacists. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

Her hospitals across the land let her convent spread and control her secret undead armies under the protection of their reputation, with no obvious guard beyond a few paladins at each, where Esther kept inquisitors in force everywhere. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> She used "a refined version of Embrace to embed undead servants all over society and in large numbers in the army". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Black Dove battle clerics still assisted Esther's Iron Veil, "the best place to recruit for the true Army". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> The Empire's leaders hollowed out its institutions for their own purposes and rode them into the ground, each trying to get the upper hand on the others. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

### The Stairs of Her Mercy

The Stairs of Her Mercy rose in white arcs out of the Plaza of the Martyrs toward a colossal statue of Uriel in her Black Dove habit. The statue stood at the apex altar with shield and imperial crown, over a fountain at her feet. In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/) the party climbed the stairs disguised as Black Doves, and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) spat into the fountain.

![The Stairs of Her Mercy](/images/album/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-the-stairs-of-her-mercy.webp)

At the Grand Lodge a gambler calling himself Joey Monica said the Empress came down her stairs every couple of months, and had last done so nine or ten months before; his companion said she was over two hundred years old. Pilgrims in the plaza said the Empress healed in person but touched only five or six people, and nobody could predict which. By [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) the pilgrims in the valley were saying that the Empress might soon have her baby.

### The Polygon

In the forecourt of Nos Matre [the Polygon](/items/the-polygon/) floated in the air, tended by sisters night and day. [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) examined it with *detect magic* and judged it a store of immense power that could cast spells, and Uriel's focus. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

![The floating object over the forecourt](/images/uploads/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-the-mukkg76s.webp)
*The floating object over the forecourt of Nos Matre.*

Her power came from the millions of dead buried under the Lazaret's hill. The Polygon was fed by them and stored their power for her. It was her projection and mind stone, through which she could speak and be seen anywhere in the valley, and for as long as it stood it restored her as she fought.

## The Daughters

She had fourteen daughters by the Emperor, many of them sets of twins, all of them under twelve years old, and never a son. In the nursery they played under yearly portraits in which every girl was the spitting image of her mother. They hummed in unison and finished each other’s sentences, and they were far more perceptive than children of that age have any business being. The rebellion called them the Daughters of Vol and fought them in the field as undead E.D.E.N. units under Uriel’s command.

Dave: "she has had many miscarriages from failed attempts but has the emperor comatose so keeps making new ones" / "She’s also brought some to term which she considers failures also and they are all female children half dragon demon kids" / "And they live near her in the palace". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

The dragon half of them was harvested. For three years the Empire kept the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) collared and bled beneath the Korramont and shipped the blood out by rail, and Valtrex, once freed, stated plainly that Uriel had used his stolen blood to sire her heirs. By his account he was their father. The gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/), bled for years under the Lazaret, called the youngest girls his brood, because they had been made from his blood.

In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) the party came through the daughters' hall in the Summer Palace. All the girls died there except Drakiel, who was eleven, and the baby Ysabel. Their cousin Saturnia took the two of them across the river, bound for Aerenal.

## The Scion

As the Penitent Empress, Uriel was already pregnant with **the Scion**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Her cult's purpose was the dragon's blood, used to make a dragon spawn that she would carry or raise. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> The doctrine she brought to the faith of the Sovereign Host was "Installing her self + unborn son as the eight Sovereign of course" <small>(sic)</small>: a pregnant god of life and death, the reincarnation of Erandis Vol, "birthing a race marked with the dragonmark of Death and ascending to rule the Host as its sole primateur". She secretly meant never to deliver the son. Dave: "She secretly plans to never bear her child the heir and dragon incarnate, instead to siphon its power into her own, turning it into a living phalactery" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Both Valtrex and LeBeefe connected the dragon blood she took to "her scheme to birth the next dragon messiah". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

The son was her grandfather's design. [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) meant to breed his own line with the Crimson Sun a second time and produce a half-dragon who bore the Mark of Death and was heir to the Empire, and Uriel took up her part in it eagerly. She then went further than the plan allowed. She meant to supplant her own son, take his power for herself, and live joined to the unborn child for ever as an undead mother, a lich with the child for her phylactery. That was why she carried him so far past his time. It angered E'noch, who had gathered the purest elves of the houses of Vol into the Sisters of the Blood as brides for the Dragon Emperor and for the heir, and to whom Uriel was only the first of them. He held that she had failed and told his other grandchildren they were next in line. He was ready to abandon her if he could have the child without her.

Her magic was bound up with the child she carried. If he were killed or taken from her, it would leave her.

## The Eternal Nightfall

Sister Nora of the Black Doves, taken in a cellar in Sharn and questioned, gave up three things: Project E.D.E.N., the names of Esther Crona’s sisters, and Uriel’s doctrine of the Eternal Nightfall. Nora was executed after the rising and left no further account of it. What the Inevitables assembled afterwards was the outline of the doctrine and never its text: the dead rise everywhere, and stay risen, and the wilds take back the kingdoms of man, as Uriel had said in writing that she wanted.

The instrument was the Fantanya Nyel, the Hell’s Bells. There were thirteen Master castings and hundreds of parish bells beneath them, and Uriel intended to ring them. That was Locke Pierce’s finding and the whole basis of his ultimatum.

> Stop Uriel before she rings the bells. If you fail, I will end this world before she can, and only my kind will survive it.\
> — Locke Pierce, at Mt. Silicon

![A Fantanya Nyel in its shipping crate](/images/uploads/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel-mu0t8j70.webp)
*A Fantanya Nyel, one of the Hell's Bells.*

Fulcrum’s decade-long bell-counting operation and a partially burned foundry schedule put the ringing on an appointed night. The Inevitables’ own reading of the dossiers concluded that she was waiting on a single event, most likely the birth of a male heir. They were right. She rang the mother bell, the Original, when her labour could no longer be held back. At last count ten other bells remained, at least one of them a Master casting in Korth, and what became of them when the Original rang was never learned.

## Fate

### The road to the Lazaret

For ten years Uriel kept to the valley of the Ivory Lazaret, close to the Emperor, trying for a son. She counted each daughter a failure.

Three years after the fall of Sharn, the Empire called a peace summit at Thronehold. The masked Imperial guards at the summit were Uriel's undead. On her order they turned on Supreme Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/), who had convened it, and the summit ended in [a massacre](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/). It was the last chance of peace between the Empire and its enemies.

The Inevitables then went to Korth to kill her. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) took the identity of Deirdre Moro, a Star Advocate of the Ministry of Law whose credentials gave clearance to the Ivory Lazaret, with Eric as her paralegal and Dario as her bodyguard. They entered the Lazaret as novices, killed the Black Dove leadership in the Westhouse in [Korth Ep 19](/sessions/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse/), and destroyed E'noch under the hospital in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/). In [Korth Ep 16](/sessions/korth-ep-16-orientation-day/) Uriel's sisters forced Zero-One to raise Esther Crona under the Lazaret by the rite that had once raised Uriel.

### The Summer Palace

In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) the party found her in labour on a bed beside the Emperor's, insisting the child was not ready and demanding Esther. She had murdered Archbishop Pom Pilot of Arawai in the Emperor's garden that afternoon. Eric's fireball set the Emperor's bed alight, and Gemma drove the Bright Dawn into her belly. The fire destroyed the Emperor. It did not stop her, because the child in her womb was already his heir.

> You think killing my husband is going to stop me?

Then she tore the transfusion line out of her arm.

> Fine. Then if it's time, then it's time. He's coming. The night begins.

She vanished in a flash of white light, and the bell began to ring. The party last saw her that day flying for the bell in white robes with her scythe, Mourning Dove, joined to the Polygon by a line of purple light, as the dead dug themselves out of the burial ground below. Her head sister, Sister Marshall, had been killed beside her. <small>(Korth Ep 21)</small>

### Nos Matre

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) she rang the Original from the belfry of Nos Matre. The Original was the mother bell, from which every other bell of the chorus was copied. Its ringing raised the dead for a mile around, drove the living who heard it mad, and put every undead thing in earshot under her hand, whoever had made it.

Dario broke the bell with Knell, and the tower fell. Uriel fought the party in the air, grown to more than a woman's size, with a contraction taking her every few moments. In the forecourt she went on holding the dead, and [Eden](/people/eden/), through the Polygon.

> The dead are under my control, with or without the bell.

Gemma brought her to the ground and Dario took off her head. The beheading did not end her. Her labour had come, and it drew her remains down through the paving to the dead under the hill. She lost the child on the way down, and he took life in the Sept.

### The Sept of Bones

She went on in the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/) under the hill, half spirit, her head apart from her body, joined by a cord to the child she had borne, the [Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/). While the cord held he was a part of her, and her magic depended on him. She could draw his strength into herself to mend her wounds. She had kept him in too long, meaning to have his destiny for herself, and before the end she said that her grandfather had been right and that she was a vessel.

Esther Crona came down to kill her. Uriel stunned her, bled her and fed her to the child. Then Kitsune's fox cut the cord, and her magic left her at once.

> It's not fair. I deserve to be queen. I deserve to be happy. You took that from me.

Eric the Cleric destroyed her with a mace of disruption. The diamond was still in her skull.

### Afterwards

The Heir of Vol, cut from her, grew until he broke the surface under Nos Matre. A ship of Locke Pierce's Hundreds dropped a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) on the valley. When the fire cleared, the Ivory Lazaret, Nos Matre, the Summer Palace grounds and the Heir were gone.

**Status:** Dead. Destroyed in the Sept of Bones.

---

**Organizations / Groups:** Uriel was a member of the humanitarian medical organisation [The Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). She purged the Dove leadership by witch hunt and inducted the loyal remainder into a fertility cult.

**Holdings:** The Ivory Lazaret, the hospital-retreat and cloister built around the Emperor’s incapacitated flesh, and the Summer Palace grounds upriver of Korth, both destroyed with the valley on the day she died. The old thieves’ den in New Ham that she seized and turned into a hospital, which grew into the university department she headed until her death. The frontier hospital in the Talenta Plains, raised after the Last War in answer to a pestilence she was herself spreading. A massive statue of her above the Skyport in Sharn, destroyed by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)’s airship on the last night of the rising.

**Items:** Uriel had two necromantic "black boxes". One, found in New Ham, allowed her to create undead. The second was found in a mechanical undead knight in an uncovered tomb outside Arden Crest, and was damaged during battle. See [Black Box](/items/black-box/). From the journey to Arden Crest she took a **Heart of Commanding Dust Devil**, the heart of an ancient mummy that summons a dust devil when its command word is spoken, once between dawns, and a jar of **Restorative Ointment** holding ten doses, each of which restores 2d8 + 2 hit points, ends poisoning and cures any disease. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 9)</small> As Empress she carried the scythe Mourning Dove and wore a Headband of Intellect.

**Sex Magic:** Uriel performed necromantic sex magic on the corpse of a dead Imperial soldier to raise it, and created the ghoul Flighter. She later performed the act in front of Gabrielle Duin and Emperor Kaius III, creating a ghoul from the corpse of [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/).

**Combat:** Before the final sessions Dave put Uriel at CR 16 and said she would be accompanied. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-12)</small> In the Unforeseen one of her tactics was *hold person* or *command* followed by *inflict wounds*, for advantage and critical hits. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-24)</small>

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation**

To bear the dragon heir, ring the bells, and rule an undead Eberron as the mother of the God of Death. Her original entry closed with the line: "The future empress and mother of the God of Death."

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:**

- Erandis Vol
- Elvish life, and only elvish life
- Sex
- Death
- Embrace, and corpses that do not decompose
- Black and silver
- Being told she was beautiful and talented
- Elite warriors and casters (she would not befriend anyone else)
- Drinking and brawling in human or dwarven towns while passing through
- Statues of herself
- Her own research logs

**Dislikes:**

- Non-elves
- Gabrielle Duin
- The Undying Court
- Her parents, and their politics
- Decomposition
- Consequences
- Being outranked by a Black Dove
- Fourteen daughters

**Vices & Personality flaws**

A rash, perverted psychopath under an austere uniform. She had a shallow understanding of strategy and risk, and until her last day she was never made to pay for cruelty or failure.

**Hobbies & Pets**

None recorded. Her downtime was laboratory work on the magical nature of blood, and a standing intention to acquire vampires she could keep.
