**Infanta of Luck · Ally at the Egg · Fate not recorded**

**Tami** is the **Infanta of Luck**, and one of the allies drafted as a card for the final battle at the Egg. Her card is one of two in the set of 41 with no flavour text. Nothing else about her is recorded, and she appears under this name in no session document.

The record does establish an **Infanta of Fortune**, who leads the Calimshites and holds Memnon at the time of Arc VI. <small>([Zebulon](/people/zebulon/), Arc VI Part IX: "Memnon was under the control of the Calimshites, who are led by the Infanta of Fortune.")</small> Whether Tami and the Infanta of Fortune are the same person is **not stated anywhere**.

## At the Egg

**White — Luck Manipulation: "Cast Silvery Barbs as a reaction once per round, but it does not consume your reaction."**

**Black — Twist Fate: "Target creature who died this round regains half its hit points, another creature you choose takes that damage (necrotic)."**

## Status

Not recorded.
