**Child · son of the Lord Commander · officially deceased, in fact alive**

<small>Also known as: Calcifer Blaze · Calsifer · Ember</small>

Calcifer Crona-Blaze is the eight-year-old son of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), Lord Commander of the Imperial Military and Director of the Iron Veil, and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), the Empire's favourite chef and a founder of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/). He was born on the penthouse balcony at the summit of the Veil Building and delivered by his godmother, Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), who marked his face in the minute after his birth. He was raised inside the Veil Building under constant guard, worshipped in absentia by [the Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) as **Ember**, and became the single piece of evidence the rebellion needed once [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s memory of the birth came out of the Crystallizer. The memory was broadcast over every screen in Sharn on the night of the rising, with Calcifer standing on the balcony where it had happened. He was declared dead the following morning and taken out of the city by his father.

Down the left side of his face and neck runs a black, vein-like birthmark, the colour of ink under skin, branching from the cheekbone toward the collar. He is closely watched. He plays with Chelsea Heinrick, the Mayor's daughter, who is the same age. When the Arcane Eye recording was finally broadcast, every citizen of Sharn learned that the mark was not a birthmark at all — it formed on his face in the minute after he was born, under a woman's hand.

He was born into the most fortified household in the Lower and Upper City combined: the penthouse at the summit of the Veil Building, above the government offices, above the Arcane Eye control rooms, above a high-security prison for political dissidents. Every adult who has ever had authority over him has been running a different operation through him.

To Sharn he was the Lord Commander's sheltered son, a marked and slightly unlucky child glimpsed at galas. Privately he was three separate secrets. To the Empire he was proof of Esther's loyalty and an object of the Empress's personal interest. To the Children of Ember he was **Ember** — a prophesied figure whose mark was scripture, worshipped by a masked congregation in a ritual room beneath his aunt's nightclub, in a cult run by his mother's own half-sister. And to the rebellion he was, from the moment Grady Marsh's memory came out of the Crystallizer, the single piece of evidence that could break the Empire's grip on the city. He knew about none of it.

His household is his circle. His nanny [Whole Foods](/people/whole-foods/) raised him day to day and was taken by the cult and bound on a ritual floor for the crime of being between them and him. His father's staff, his mother's Honor Guards — four masked armoured figures who turned out to be the undead Council of 13 — and the Empress who delivered him and visits. His only recorded friendship is with another eight-year-old whose mother ran the Empire's surveillance network and whose father died on the same balcony he was standing on.

**The mark:** Uriel Qualanthri delivered him on the penthouse balcony while his mother laboured alone. The Arcane Eye caught what followed. Uriel examined the newborn, then raised her hand over his face with cold deliberation; a sickly green glow spread from her fingertips; his breathing faltered and stopped; the dark vein-like lines formed across his cheek. At Esther's scream she froze, the glow faded, and the baby gasped and began crying as if nothing had happened. As she turned away the lens caught a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull.

He believes his mother is coming back, which nobody has yet corrected. He believes his father's cooking is the ordinary business of the family. He believes the mark on his face is simply how he looks.

**Rebel file.** Calcifer Blaze, age 8, is rarely observed outside Imperial compounds and is monitored by dedicated personnel at all times. Behavioral notes indicate a bright, sheltered child with no confirmed signs of indoctrination or early training; however, the possibility remains under review.

He behaves like a sheltered eight-year-old under constant guard, because that is what he is. On the balcony at the end, with the whole city watching on every screen, he ran into his father's arms. He was in the room for his aunt's death, his uncle's death, the Mayor's death and his mother's; his father cast Darkness over the bedroom door so that he would not see the last of them.

**Origin.** Calcifer was born eight years ago on the penthouse balcony of the Veil Building, an event no Arcane Eye report ever legally recorded because the officer who saw it wandered off his patrol route and chose not to file. He grew up in the tower his mother ruled the city from, monitored constantly, insulated completely — a child raised inside a surveillance apparatus that was pointed everywhere except at him. His father spent those eight years running a rebellion out of a restaurant supply chain and never once used the boy as cover. His mother spent them terrified of a thing she had seen on her own balcony and could not prove. Then a retired wizard got drunk in a sake bar in the Lantern District and started telling the story, and every arrangement that had held Calcifer's life in place came apart.

## The Broadcast

The memory was extracted at Zilspar Farm 02 with an ArcEye Crystallizer Lens taken out of Circuit City, and watched in the private Fulcrum room. Three copies were made. [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) kept one. The trio held the others and argued about whether to show a city the worst minute of a child's life.

They showed it. [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) played it from the EBT-7 on the penthouse balcony, live, over every screen in Sharn, with Calcifer standing on that same balcony. It shattered Esther Crona's composure, ended the Empire's hold on the city inside a night, and made the boy's face the most recognisable image in Khorvaire — which is precisely why he had to be declared dead the next morning.

> "They believe in and worship Ember — Calcifer, whom they consider a prophesied figure."

**Right now:** Calcifer Blaze is officially dead — killed, the story goes, when [Faith](/people/faith/) and [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) ambushed his father's private airship during the kidnapping. [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) built the lie in the hour after the rising and it has held. Father and son were teleported out to Zilspar Farm before anyone else reached the penthouse. Zilspar Farm no longer exists.

**Holdings:** the penthouse nursery at the summit of the Veil Building, now the Chancellor's residence · the Blaze lake house outside Sharn · a place at Zilspar Farm 02, since destroyed.

**Items:** none recorded. The most valuable object associated with him is a memory taken out of another man's head.

**The Mark:** whatever Uriel Qualanthri did to him in his first minute is not understood by anyone living. It killed him briefly and then let him go. It left a visible sign the Empress's own cult read as prophecy. The Empress has fourteen daughters by the Emperor and no son, and the rebellion's working theory is that she is waiting on an heir.
