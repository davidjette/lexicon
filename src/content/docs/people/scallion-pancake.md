**Duergar (deep dwarf) · of the Deeptempura · House Deeptempura · Dead**

Scallion Pancake was a duergar of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/), one of the fallen recovered from the sealed living quarters of the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) three years after his death. Questioned by [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) under *speak with dead* as part of his own clan's burial rites, he gave the **Inevitables** the account of [King Corn](/people/king-corn/)'s uprising and named [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) as the dwarf who rang the bell.

## The rite

Scallion Pancake was recovered in the room-by-room clearance of the foundry living quarters that the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) were carrying out so that the dead of the Deeptempura could be given proper burial rites. Part of that rite is asking. General Tso cast *speak with dead* on him and let the Inevitables put the questions.

## What he said

From his testimony, and from the documents recovered in the rooms cleared alongside him, the picture assembled for the first time:

- King Corn had a daughter, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura.
- Father and daughter, enslaved by the Empire in their own home, had been communicating in **encoded messages** and planning an uprising.
- They were secretly forging a **handheld horn** intended to counteract or resist the bell's effects.
- The resistance was discovered. Sedda was taken below into the cavern where the dragon dwells - but not before she rang the bell.
- It was Sedda who rang it, three years ago, and the ringing is what turned the enslaved dwarves and the workers into the dead.

The party afterwards recovered the Speaker, the improvised hammer and the horn beneath the study floor, each of which the account had pointed them to.

## Right now

Recovered by his clan for burial rites, in a mountain now claimed by a dragon. No record says whether the rite was completed.

**Organizations / Groups:** The Deeptempura clan; the foundry workforce, as the Trust's property.
