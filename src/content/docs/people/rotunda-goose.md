**Parliamentarian · Survivor of Thronehold · The Grand Lodge · Alive**

**Rotunda Goose** is a parliamentarian who attended the summit at [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/) and survived it without her left hand and an ear. She travelled to the Summer Palace on the river barge from Korth and stays at [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), where she is looking for a way out of the Empire.

> Full price. Full price, after what I gave them.

## Thronehold

At the Thronehold Summit she spoke with [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and Lorian. <small>([Korth Ep 1](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/))</small> She survived the summit without her left hand and an ear. <small>([Korth Ep 14](/sessions/korth-ep-14-the-listening-post/))</small>

## The river barge

She was aboard the river barge from Korth to the Summer Palace, where she was seen in conversation with [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/). <small>([Korth Ep 14](/sessions/korth-ep-14-the-listening-post/))</small>

## The Grand Lodge

[Korth Ep 17](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/). On the Saturday night she was holding court by the fire in the Lodge's ballroom, wounded and not dressed for the hunt. She said there was no safe passage out of the Empire any more, and that all her money could not buy one. She greeted the Advocate Deirdre Moro, who was [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) in disguise, and presented Lord Aldric and Lady Sabine Varenne, Karrnathi lords. Of the Advocate's ambitions she said, *"Not that any of that matters these days, but it'll matter in the aftermath. We're all planning for what comes next."* When Deirdre said she would like to visit the library, Goose told her this was the night they bring people through it. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

## Right now

She is at the Grand Lodge and has not found her passage out. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>
