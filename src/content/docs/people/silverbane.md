**Intelligent bastard sword (moonblade) · Level 15 · Neutral Good · House Gallidann · Awake, and borne by Drefan**

*Also known as:* Feybane <small>(its dormant name, before it was recognised)</small> · the Moonsword · "all the Silverbanes across all time"

**Silverbane** is the sentient [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) blade of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/), an intelligent bastard sword that has served the house for three thousand years and retains a persistent memory of the ages the **Forgetting** erased. Its four previous owners were **Eldamir**, **Star Quendi**, **Renos** and **Daenan**; it was found dormant under the name **Feybane** in the deep hold of the slaver *Evening Star*, interred with the *baelnorn* Daenan, taken from his sarcophagus, and then blessed to [Drefan](/people/drefan/) by the spirit of Star Quendi at Bask Falls Barrow, who opened another Master Rune on it. Its siblings are **Onyxbane**, **Ironbane** (Excalibur), **Glassbane** and **Copperbane**. It seeks the advancement of elvenkind and elven ideals, and grants its powers as it chooses to protect the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/).

## The blade itself

A versatile bastard sword, +1 to attack and damage, 1d10+1 in one hand and 1d12+1 in two. It has INT 12, WIS 10, CHA 12 and darkvision to 120 feet. Its forte is adorned with an image of the goddess [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), and House Gallidann's rune still glows on the steel.

A line of runes decorates the blade, **one rune for each wielder it has had** — and for each such rune, one ability. It communicates by transmitting emotions, sending a tingling sensation through the wielder's hand; more explicitly, it speaks through visions or dreams when the wielder is in a trance or asleep. In the party's shared dreams it appears in humanoid form, and in one of them it catches an enormous woman-child in its arms as she finishes her dance, then puts a hand on Drefan's shoulder and nods. Eldamir's words to Drefan when she laid the blade on his shoulder: "you carry the heart of your people."

## The Master Runes

House Gallidann has died out. When Silverbane realises this, it sadly pledges to find a new master — and it will not simply attune. It urges any new holder to **seek the blessing of each of its previous owners** and to learn the old ritual of attunement. **A Master Rune is opened by a previous owner's blessing**, and as each owner gives her blessing a new power awakens.

- **Eldamir** — *Luna*: an extra 1d6 radiant damage to fey and lycanthropes on a hit.
- **Star Quendi** — *Sentinel*: the blade glows faintly when fey or lycanthropes are within 120 feet.
- **Renos** — *Moonbeam*: cast Moonbeam twice per day.
- **Daenan** — *Dominate*: cast Dominate Monster twice per day, fey at disadvantage.

<small>Source discrepancy, preserved not resolved: the blade's dormant **Feybane** record assigns the rune-powers differently — Eldamir grants the flat +1, Star Quendi the radiant damage, Renos the Sentinel glow, and Daenan an *Illusion* power that changes the weapon's appearance rather than Dominate Monster. Which mapping is current is not settled.</small>

The awakening carries a price. A moonblade that rejects a claimant inflicts 1d6 negative levels — death, if they exceed the claimant's own level. Acceptance costs 5,000 experience and adds a new rune to the line.

Drefan came by the blade in two steps. It was **taken from Daenan Gallidan's sarcophagus** aboard the *Evening Star*; then, at Bask Falls Barrow, **Star Quendi blessed his inheritance of it and opened another Master Rune** on the sword. Each of its previous owners blessed him with their rune's power **except Daenan**, whom the party never went back to contact, so Daenan's rune stands unopened.

Drefan then **added his own rune**, his capstone power: *"if you strike a creature with this bow and it has fewer than 100 hit points, it dies."* <small>(sic)</small>

## What it wants

Silverbane seeks the advancement of elvenkind and elven ideals. **Courage, loyalty, beauty, music and life** are all part of its purpose. It will also grant its powers, as it chooses, to protect the Infanta. If it has a flaw, it is overconfidence: once it has decided on an owner it believes only that person should wield it, even if the owner falls short of those ideals.

## The confession

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Silverbane remembers the slaying of [Calix](/people/calix/), because it did it itself. Eldamir and Calix stood on the beach as boats landed all around them; Calix tried to blind Eldamir with fire and poison, and Eldamir ran into battle with her eyes closed, following Silverbane's lead into her brother's heart. They both fell dead on that spot — but Calix had buried part of his soul elsewhere.

It remembers killing a great many more than Calix. In Eldamir's hands it slew hosts of dwarves, orcs, dragonkin and men. After the Betrayal the elves of Snowdown poured out their wrath on every other race in the Moonshae, and Silverbane was at the front. Pressed, it cannot remember the nature of the Betrayal at all — only that the Infanta's heart was broken and Eldamir thirsted for revenge against her brother.

The worst of it comes at the blood seal beneath Motherstone, which glows when the blade is brought near:

> After the Forgetting of Motherstone, Eldamir blamed the dwarves and pledged to rid them from the Moonshae Isles and the world… When they were gone, they led a genocide, capturing entire populations and leading them still alive into their tombs.

And then, because the seal demands it, **Silverbane must take the blood of a dwarf to open it** — the sword that led the genocide, asked for one more dwarf.

## Memory across the Oblivia

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Reunited with its siblings Onyxbane, Ironbane and Glassbane in the rewritten past of the Time of Troubles, away from the [Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) that erased that age, the blades find that their memories and intellects are sharper in each other's presence, and that being in that age makes them more powerful than ever. Silverbane finally recovers the founding:

> I remember these times much better now. You have to forgive me, but much of this age was erased from history, which for you is written record but for me, is empty spaces spoiling my persistent memory. But now that I've seen myself as I was in this time — with Eldamir — I remember what it was like. Elistrae came to us in the body of a human girl, and the elders did not know what to do, at first. They tried to exorcise her, summon her out of the child, but it was no use. The elvish god had fused to a mortal soul in order to be born and spread her grace.

The recovered memory also carries Elistrae's forbidden love for an avatar of a dwarven god, and Eldamir and Calix "moments away from drowning their entire landing camp in the Sea of Swords" before the goddess stayed their hand and gave them [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/).

The blades also meet an older, corrupted version of it riding with the Masked Prophet toward the destruction of dwarves and men. Silverbane's response is immediate — *"NOT me, to be clear…"* — and Ebonbane agrees: "That's right. This Silver was haggard, much older and totally jaded."

## How it is treated

The sword outranks the man carrying it. Among the [Mundi](/organizations/the-mundi/) of the crystal city, the moon-elves see Silverbane and genuflect; they bow before the blade as their lord and ask Drefan whether he has come to claim the rightful throne of Motherstone. When their king Chad demands executions, the record notes he will back down *"if commanded by Silverbane or Drefan"* — the sword listed first. By the seventh arc the peoples of every age hail the heroes as the rightful bearers of all five blades: *"you bear all the Silverbanes across all time."*

## Right now

At Brightmantle's funeral the Moon Elves, with Elistrae, Eldamir and Calix, give the acclamation: *"You bear all the Silverbanes across all time. You are the great hunter, the Deadfall Raining, the Waning and the Waxing One."* Five blades, in Zebulon's vision, are about to be driven into a single opened Eye.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **Drefan** — its current bearer, who pledged to live and die for House Gallidann to get it.
- **Eldamir** — first rune. Founder of the house; died killing her own brother, following the blade's lead with her eyes shut.
- **Star Quendi** — second rune. Greeted the sword "as an old friend and wept" when its new bearer came to her barrow.
- **Renos** and **Daenan** — third and fourth runes; Quendi's son and grandson. Daenan's tomb is where the blade was found, dormant, as Feybane, and Daenan is the one owner who never blessed Drefan.
- **Drefan's own rune** — the last on the line, added after the blessings: a creature struck with fewer than 100 hit points dies.
- **Onyxbane · Ironbane · Glassbane · Copperbane** — its siblings. Sharper together.
- **Elistrae** — whose image is on its forte, and whose protection overrides its loyalty to any owner.
