**Archfey · Warlock patron of Gemma Corso · Active**

*Also known as:* Katsune

**Kitsune** is an archfey and the warlock patron of [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/). He was first the patron of her adoptive father, Jane Corso, whom he met while Jane was a soldier in the Last War, and he showed himself to Gemma only after Jane died during the fighting in Leef and Newham. On the road to Korth he told her that Jane had promised him the heart of [Emperor Caius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) in exchange for freedom and power, and that the debt had passed to her, and he gave her a velvet-lined box to carry it in. On the river boat to [the Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/) he told her that the heart of the Emperor was a riddle, and that what he wants is the Emperor's child.

## Appearance

By Nico's account he calls himself Kitsune, and has shown himself to Gemma "looking like a fox, a male human magician, and small fox-humanoid". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-06)</small> He came to her through fog in a fey-dream dance on the road to Korth; in a later dream he sat on a chaise in a white toga; in the last he wore the robes and amulet of the Archbishop of Arawai, a passenger on the boat to the Summer Palace.

## Jane Corso

By Nico's account, Kitsune was the patron of Jane Corso <small>(also written Croso)</small>, Gemma's adoptive father, and met him during the Last War, when Jane was a soldier. Jane knew the Colonel from the same war. Jane taught Gemma to cast some magic as a child, pulling a sword from thin air among it, and called her his little fox. Kitsune never really showed himself to Gemma until after Jane was killed, in the fighting in Leef and Newham. Gemma did not know whether Jane had made a pact with him during the war, or on what terms. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-06; 2026-07-07)</small>

## The bargain

Before the trio left Sharn for Korth, Gemma meditated and made contact with Kitsune, who appeared through the fog in a fey-dream dance. He told her the truth of her pact: Jane had agreed to bring him the Emperor's heart in exchange for freedom and power, and when Jane died the pact passed to Gemma. He gave her a velvet-lined pewter heart-box for the task. When she came back from the fog, her fey-crafted armour had been enhanced and its interior lined in velvet like the box. She kept the revelation to herself. <small>([Korth Ep 4](/sessions/korth-ep-4-road-to-korth/))</small>

Gemma's armour carries charges that summon **Lalo Kitsune**, a fey fox. In the Glitterhame the fox, drawn fresh from those charges, dropped a cube of darkness on the risen skeletons in the pool. <small>([Korth Ep 7](/sessions/korth-ep-7-the-glitterhame-and-the-grave-robbers/))</small>

## Counsel beneath the Korramont

At the Mountain Door, after Eric's augury came back weal and woe, Gemma contacted her patron and heard the warning *"Never negotiate with an insane dragon."* <small>([Korth Ep 6](/sessions/korth-ep-6-the-long-night-at-the-mountain-door/))</small> Kitsune also told Gemma, in Dave's words, to "think less literally about what is the heart of the emperor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-15)</small>

On the Grand Stair, in a talk about faith around the fire, Gemma named her patron to Dario and Eric as Kitsune. Dave, as DM, attached a standing warning to him: he has to lie. <small>([Korth Ep 7](/sessions/korth-ep-7-the-glitterhame-and-the-grave-robbers/))</small>

## The fountain

In camp beneath the Korramont, Gemma dreamed of a marble foyer with a grand fountain, gold and murals, and Kitsune sitting on a chaise in a white toga. He regarded her, said nothing, and looked pointedly at the far wall. The doors burst open and an old man with a scraggly beard, starved, with blood pouring from his chest, came out holding an infant. He fell into the fountain, the water turned red, spilled across the white floor and reached her feet, and she woke. The old man resembled the Emperor. She did not see the child's sex. <small>([Korth Ep 9](/sessions/korth-ep-9-the-death-warrant-and-the-ringer/))</small>

## The boat

On the night the trio reached the Grand Lodge, Gemma spent her free casting of *contact other plane* going to sleep, and dreamed of the boat again, empty except for herself and Kitsune. He wore the Archbishop of Arawai's robes and amulet and was looking at the water. He made a holy sign over her, ironically, and asked whether she had come to become a nun. She told him she had come to retrieve his gift. He told her she was close, and that if she did not hurry she would have to steal it screaming. Asked what becomes of the bargain if she dies, he allowed that it was never an immediate binding agreement, and that it settled in somewhere around the eighth or ninth person she killed with the power he gave her.

He then explained himself, which he had never done. He has no children. He told her that when you become a father your heart is said to be living outside your body, and that he wants that, with one particular child. He means to take it out of this world entirely, and she would not have to see it again for a very long time.

He corrected the terms. The heart of the Emperor was a riddle, and the box she had carried for the heart of Caius was for the wrong thing: what he wants is the Emperor himself, the child, alive or dead. What the elf witch has dug into, he said, is some of the oldest magic there is. She has found a way to sire a child from the bones of the earth, from the seed of existence; what she has taken out of that broken wreck of a creature is very rare, and she will protect it at all costs.

Gemma told him that what she does is for some semblance of normalcy in the world, and not for him, and that handing him a child powerful enough to destroy them would be handing another bomb to another ally, like [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). He answered that he knows most of what she does and does not pay attention to all of it, just as not everything he does is for her. When she summed up her career, setting out to avenge her father and killing a great many people she judged to be bad, he turned from the rail, asked whether she thought they had even started yet, and told her not to call him if what she meant to give him was crybaby nonsense. His last words before the boat rocked into fog were that the time is almost near, and that she must get him what he wants before she rings the bell. The thing he had been steering Gemma toward was the Emperor's unborn heir. <small>([Korth Ep 14](/sessions/korth-ep-14-the-listening-post/))</small>

> "Never negotiate with an insane dragon."

<small>— Kitsune, to Gemma at the Mountain Door.</small>
