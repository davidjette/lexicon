**Thri-kreen · Fighter 1 / Wizard 2 · Squadron leader, SSJ Zanzibar · Dead**

*Also known as:* Kreen (callsign) · Cmdr Chad "Kreen" Mantis · Lt. Cmdr. Chad "Kreen" Mantis

Lieutenant Commander **Chad "Kreen" Mantis** is a thri-kreen fighter and bladesinger, the top fighter ace aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) and its Squadron Leader in [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), a character run by the DM and played by Dave. Passed over for the ship's helm, he flew small craft for [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) through *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, and died in May 2024, in the closing episodes of the arc.

## Background

He was built as a third-level character, the top fighter ace on board, who was passed over for the helm position. A small-craft jammer with the callsign "Kreen", he can fly a small fighter but cannot handle the Zanzibar's Major Helm, and is a little bitter about it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small> He gives [Caprica](/people/caprica/), the ship's spelljammer, pilot-to-pilot shade. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> His character sheet gives his background as Wildspacer and his faith as Astral Command. <small>(character sheet)</small>

He held the rank of Commander when he was created. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small> Commander stands as his earlier rank; from the crew roster of January 2023 onward he is a Lieutenant Commander. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

On the crew roster of the Zanzibar he is the Squadron Leader, Lt. Cmdr. Chad "Kreen" Mantis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small> Although he is an arcana-trained officer, he reports to the enlisted security chief [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/), because he is a bladesinger and the security chief commands the ship's support craft. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-13)</small> He wears security colours, and is usually in a flight suit. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-14)</small>

In April 2024, after [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/)'s third wish sent the Zanzibar to Sigil, Chad Mantis was aboard the ship with [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

He died in the session of 2 May 2024, which picked up from a cliffhanger assassination. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-02; 2024-05-03)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

He is a fighter and bladesinger who throws knives and flies jets. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> His character sheet makes him a first-level fighter with the Thrown Weapon Fighting style and a second-level wizard of Bladesinging. He carries six daggers and a shortsword, and wears an Armored Flight Suit and Astral Command Pilot Wings. He carries a [Scroller](/items/the-scroller/), is proficient with navigator's tools and space vehicles, and speaks Common and Thri-kreen, with thri-kreen telepathy. <small>(character sheet)</small>

He has six arms. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> The thri-kreen of his character sheet has one pair of secondary arms below the primary pair. <small>(character sheet)</small>
