**Species unrecorded · Director · The Imperial Counting House · Level Unknown · Alive**

Aurel Kesk is the Director of [the Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/), the Department of the Exchequer's office in the valley of [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/). By his own account he has not been up the Stairs of Her Mercy or to the palace in more than a year, and he has never seen the Emperor. By [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/)'s account he and his whole staff are enriching themselves on the property of the dead and on war profiteering. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## General Thar

[General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) cornered him in his own hall with officers, dark druids and death troopers and demanded to be taken to the Emperor. Kesk answered:

> General, I count money. I haven't been up the stairs or to the palace in more than a year and I've never seen the Emperor. Nobody can open that door except for the sisters.

Thar's druids handed him a tightly rolled parchment before the delegation left, and several death troopers stayed behind to watch him. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The Star Advocate

Kesk greeted [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) with *"The Ministry sends nobody up this hill for two years. Then sends a Star Advocate. Should I be worried or flattered?"* and passed her to his Sub-Auditor, Corvan Bleek. When Vitt handed her the evidence, Kesk came back with troopers holding Vitt and accused the Advocate of acting without a warrant. Shown a scroll passed off as a warrant signed by the Magister, he said, *"I can't believe that Merkin, of all people, would pull this."* His men beat Vitt in his office until Deirdre demanded her as a witness. He let Vitt go, told the Advocate that [Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/) could forget the favour Kesk had planned for him, warned her that there are more important people than he who will be upset if she holds too hard to the letter of the law, and offered to make her cooperation very worth her while. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>
