**Cleric of the Black Doves · Sanctum of the Last Mercy, Sharn · The Empire · DEAD — tried and executed, Sharn Ep 19**

**Also known as:** the eyes and ears of Empress Uriel.

Sister Nora was a senior cleric of the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) order, representing the Sanctum of the Last Mercy at the Black Dove Hospital in Sharn, and the observer sent to the city by Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). She is first attested at the University of Newham studying the drug Embrace, where she established the blood relation between [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and her half-sisters; in Sharn she performed the necromantic rites over [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/)'s corpses and carried a BioTec obedience chip in her skull that stopped her speaking one name. She was abducted from the opening of the restaurant POSÉ, held in a cell at Zilspar Farm 02 and interrogated until she gave up Project E.D.E.N., Esther's sisters and the doctrine of the Eternal Nightfall; [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) cut the chip out and the name behind it was Dr. Edwin Graves. When Sharn rose she was tried and executed alongside Hillary Heinrick and [Dr. Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), and cremated under the Silver Flame's directive.

## Physical description

A senior cleric of the Black Dove order in the habit of the Sanctum of the Last Mercy, polite and quiet, the sort of presence a city stops noticing. In the side of her head near her ear sat a BioTec obedience chip. It made her nose bleed and dropped her to the floor whenever she came near a forbidden name, and it was cut out of her in a cell at Zilspar Farm 02 by Sir Dario Argentino.

## The order she served

The Black Doves themselves are an ancient and isolated order from Aerenal, bound to the grim duty of tending to the dead and dying on battlefields and eradicating any undead that remain. During the Last War, the Black Doves were quick to aid the Valenar, embedding themselves among their elven kin and earning renown throughout Khorvaire for their austere brand of mercy. Uriel Qualanthri, the Empress, is herself a cleric of Light from Aerenal and one of the Black Doves.

The Black Dove Hospital in Sharn's Upper City is one of the [Houses of Mercy](/organizations/the-houses-of-mercy/), and the one with an unfinished bell tower.

## Public and private

Around Sharn, Sister Nora is known as a polite and quiet presence, yet it is widely suspected that she serves as the eyes and ears of Empress Uriel, a representative and trusted liaison sent to observe the city and its shifting powers. Her connection to Uriel elevates the standing of the Black Doves within the Empire, ensuring that figures like Sister Nora are treated with deference in matters of healing, death, and spiritual authority. The suspicion was correct. What was not suspected is how much of the Empire's machinery ran through her hands: she performed the necromantic rites over Project E.D.E.N.'s corpses, she had established the blood relation between Esther Crona and her half-sisters years earlier while studying Embrace at the University of Newham, and she knew the phrase **the Eternal Nightfall** and what it meant. She said it aloud once, at POSÉ, which is why the Nest went after her.

## Competence and circle

She moved between the hospital, BioTec and the Veil Building, and was chipped by the same hand that chipped BioTec's own employees. She was close enough to Esther Crona that when Esther's youngest half-sister [Eden](/people/eden/) died, Esther came to Nora in desperation — and the result was EDN-00, the prototype of Project E.D.E.N. Her associates in Sharn were Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka, and she was executed with both of them.

## Secret

She was not the top of anything. The chip in her skull proves it: somebody senior enough to own BioTec's implant supply thought Sister Nora was a leak risk and installed a block she could not speak past. She died holding the Empress's confidence and somebody else's leash.

## Beliefs

She believes the Eternal Nightfall is a mercy and that a world remade in undeath ends suffering rather than extending it. She believes Eden's resurrection was a miracle even though it failed. She believes a soul can be put beyond the Empress's reach, which is the closest thing to a limit she ever set on Uriel.

## Conduct in role

Quiet, deferential, unhurried, and present at every event that mattered. She hid by being clergy: nobody searches a nun's rounds, nobody asks a hospital why bodies arrive at night. Under interrogation she gave nothing until magic and exhaustion cracked her, then gave a great deal — and hit the wall of the chip at exactly the question that mattered most. When she stopped cooperating at Zilspar, she did it by refusing food.

## Origin

Her birth, her clan and her arrival in Khorvaire are not recorded. She is first attested at the University of Newham, studying the drug Embrace, in the same years that Uriel Qualanthri's hospital there grew into the research and educational facility that houses U.N.D.E.A.D. It was at Newham that she confirmed the blood relation between Esther Crona, [Faith](/people/faith/), [Blair](/people/blair/) and Eden. By the time of the Sharn arc she was senior enough to represent the Sanctum of the Last Mercy, to speak for the order at civic functions, and to be the Empress's chosen observer in the Empire's second city.

## The Abduction at POSÉ

At the opening of [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s restaurant POSÉ, a Cogborn protest outside erupted into chaos and shots fired from within the crowd killed the news anchor Candice Kurt on the balcony. In the panic the trio turned invisible and abducted Sister Nora, escaping by a hidden elevator route revealed by Izaak. They teleported her unconscious to Zilspar Farm 02, where Donna led them to a concealed underground cell and sealed it with a Private Sanctum ritual. Her disappearance closed the checkpoints between the upper and lower city and sent Iron Veil raids sweeping the Cogborn neighbourhoods.

## The Cell at Zilspar

Bound and gagged, she was first questioned under Zone of Truth in yes-or-no answers. Later, in the cellar, under magical pressure, her conditioning fractured and she talked: Project E.D.E.N. — hybrid undead-Warforged soldiers built from dead Iron Veil soldiers and abducted Cogborn, Warforged components, Umbra, and Black Dove necromantic rites, deployed into the Mournlands to fight the Hundreds and mine Red Kyber. Eden as EDN-00, *"a miracle, yes but an incomplete one, a soul even Uriel may not reach."* The Eternal Nightfall. Esther's sisters.

Then they asked who supplied the bodies. She grabbed the side of her head near her ear, bled from the nose and collapsed. Dario cut the chip out. The name behind it was Dr. Edwin Graves, the Cog Collector, and finding him ended the body-supply pipeline. Afterward Nora refused to eat, and the question of what to do with her sat open on the Nest's mission board: keep her, execute her as a political message, or something else.

> "She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her…but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes but an incomplete one, a soul even Uriel may not reach."

## Trial and Execution

When Sharn rose, [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) paladins under Zero-One seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up the clergy, and inside they found a large golden bell crated in an unfinished tower — a replica of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). Trials began immediately. Sister Nora was found guilty and executed alongside Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka and other high-level Iron Veil officials, and the Flame's cremation directive was enacted across the city. The live Key Figures register still lists her as active; she was executed in Sharn Ep 19.

## Right now

She is ash, by her enemies' own ordinance. What she said in that cellar is still the spine of the case against the Black Doves: the rites are the order's own work, the doctrine has a name, and the Empress's reach has at least one edge to it.

## Personality Characteristics

**Motivation** — Service to the Empress, and to a mercy that does not end at death.

**Likes & Dislikes**

**Likes:** the Sanctum's rounds · quiet corridors · deference · the study of Embrace · being trusted with names · civic functions where she can watch the room · the Eternal Nightfall · the word *miracle*.

**Dislikes:** being asked who supplies the bodies · Zone of Truth · the chip behind her ear · Cogborn protests · food, once she had decided to stop · the Silver Flame.

**Vices & Personality flaws** — She said "the Eternal Nightfall" out loud at a party.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations**

- **Empress Uriel Qualanthri** — her superior and the reason for her standing in Sharn. Uriel did not come for her.
- **Esther Crona** — came to Nora when Eden died; the result was EDN-00. Dead.
- **Dr. Edwin Graves** — chipped her so she could not speak his name. Dead.
- **Dr. Joanne Menka** — BioTec's head Hybrid Specialist, her opposite number on the science side. Executed with her.
- **Hillary Heinrick** — commanded the Arcane Eye network in Sharn. Executed with her.
- **Sister S** — a tie at the Sanctum of the Last Mercy the Nest identified as emotional leverage. Never used.
- **Donna** — the Silver Flame cleric who kept her cell and reported that she was refusing to eat.

**Hobbies & Pets** — None recorded.
