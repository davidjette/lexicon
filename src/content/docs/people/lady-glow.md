**Singer and entertainer · Rebel agent · Level Unknown · Dead, and standing**

*Also known as:* none recorded.

**Lady Glow** is a famous singer and entertainer known throughout Khorvaire, and a rebel agent. Her cover broke when a rebel crew infiltrated an Imperial Lightningrail train in hopes of stealing the Hell's Bell "Fantanya Nyel"; she escaped the battle, and the Imperial media named her a rebel and terrorist and blamed her for the death of the actor [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), who was aboard the same train and was also a rebel agent. The Unforeseen found her again at a rebel base in the Dragon Watch Isles, and she escaped that raid with [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/). Duin later delivered her in chains to [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) in the throne room at Korth, where Uriel killed her and raised her as a ghoul.

No source describes her face, her height or her colouring. The last living description of her is from the throne room floor at Korth: a woman in chains with a hood over her head, shivering with fright, standing in a ring of fifteen charred rebel corpses.

**In public** she was one of the most famous entertainers on the continent, in the same tier of celebrity as the actor John Stamos, and there is no evidence anyone suspected either of them of anything. **In private** both were rebels, and their fame was the operational asset — a singer's touring schedule is a reason to be on any train, in any city, in any room, and nobody asks a second question. **Lady Glow was discovered to be a rebel when a rebel crew infiltrated an imperial Lightningrail train in hopes to steal the Hell's Bell "Fantanya Nyel".**

**During a long and dangerous battle on the train against Imperial solders and agents, (including Esther, Uriel, Locke and LeBeefe) Lady Glow was able to escape.** She is one of very few people who ever got away from [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) at full strength.

**The Imperial media reported that Lady Glow was a rebel and terrorist and was responsible for the attack on the Lightningrail. She was also blamed for the death of beloved actor John Stamos who too was on the train and also a rebel agent.** Stamos was in fact killed by [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). The Empire knew that, erected a statue to Stamos in Korth as a fallen national icon, and hung his murder on the woman who had been working alongside him.

Beliefs: Unknown. No words of hers are recorded.

**Origin.** **Unknown.** Where Lady Glow was born, what her name was before it was Lady Glow, when she joined the rebellion, and who recruited her are not recorded.

## The Dragon Watch Isles

**Esther, Uriel, Locke and LeBeefe later found and fought Lady Glow in a rebel base in the Dragon Watch Isles. She escaped the attack with Gabriel Duin.** Duin was an Imperial officer seven years into a cover; whether Glow knew it is not recorded. It was that raid — the burning of the island and the work on it — that ended Duin's infiltration and set everything after it in motion.

## The gift

In the throne room at Korth, Duin waved a hand over the Crimson Sun carved into the marble floor and the symbol opened, raising a stone platform out of the ground. In the middle of it stood a chained, hooded woman; around her lay the fifteen charred bodies of the rebels the Unforeseen had killed and burned. Duin led her over, unhooded her, and said: *"A gift, for you dear Uriel. No hard feelings."*

**Lady glow was later "gifted" to Uriel Qualanthri in the throne room of Emperor Kaius III by Gabriel Duin. Uriel then preceded to kill Lady Glow and perform necromatic sex magic on her corpse creating a ghoul.**

## Three accounts of what happened to her

Three accounts of what became of her disagree.

| Source | Account |
|---|---|
| **Imperial public reporting**, issued after the City of Knives | Gabrielle Duin is missing and Lady Glow is blamed for her assassination. Glow was morbidly injured during her arrest and has been sentenced to live out the remainder of her life in medical confinement at Newham Hospital. |
| **The throne room record** | Uriel killed her in front of the Emperor and raised her as a ghoul on the spot. |
| **Fulcrum's own file on the bells**, written a decade or more later | "Uriel Qualanthri killed Lady Glow and stood her back up as a music box. It sings at the Emperor's bedside, they say. The sweetest voice in the world." |

The two rebel-side accounts agree with each other. The Imperial account confines her to Newham Hospital on a charge for the assassination of a woman who was not assassinated, and Duin is missing rather than dead.

## Right now

Lady Glow is a ghoul. She is, by the rebellion's account, kept at the Emperor's bedside and made to sing. Officially she is a patient at Newham Hospital serving a life sentence. She has never been produced.
