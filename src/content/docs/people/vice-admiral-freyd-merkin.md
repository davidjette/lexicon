**Species unrecorded · Vice Admiral · The Ministry of Law, Korth · Alive, hostile**

*Also known as:* Vice Admiral Merkin

**Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin** is a senior officer of the Imperial legal service at [the Ministry of Law](/organizations/the-ministry-of-law/) in Korth, where he runs the Star Advocates and the mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties. In Korth Ep 13 he handed his best prosecutor, Star Advocate Deirdre Moro, a fully redacted capital brief against her own sister **Bantide Moro** as a loyalty test; when the advocate set down her role and bore witness as kin instead, he intervened to prosecute from the floor himself, was forced to concede on the record that bearing witness for kin *"is her right,"* and lost the case. He then handed her the Grand Lodge assignment anyway, with a Monday deadline and a bounty attached to failure. The woman holding those sanctioned Imperial credentials is [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) of the Inevitables.

## Physical description

<small>No description of Merkin is recorded. He has been met once in person, outside a courtroom in the Ministry of Law, by a woman wearing somebody else's face.</small> What is documented is the register: clipped, personal, and entirely comfortable delivering a threat and a compliment in the same breath.

## The Ministry of Law

The Ministry of Law sits northeast of the Great Plaza in Korth, next door to the Hall of Records, with a separate side entrance for officers and upper floors full of advocates. Its High Court for capital cases sits on an upper level overlooked by a balcony holding an **Iron Maiden** — a device that kills the condemned while leaving the corpse intact for reuse. Its jail is in the northeast corner of the same complex. Merkin runs Star Advocates out of it, and Star Advocates prosecute crimes against records.

Karrnathi justice descends from the old Galifar courts and was genuinely reformist when it split off, creating a judicial bureaucracy notionally independent of the throne. It does not seek truth; it seeks atonement. There is no defence counsel and no discovery — only a panel of judges whose stated function is to interrogate the prosecution. Merkin's origin, service history and route to the Ministry are not recorded.

## Public and private

Publicly he is a senior officer of the Imperial legal service running the mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties — a ministry-wide directive with executions expected over the following weeks. Privately he is a talent-spotter running one prosecutor very hard, and he is himself being run. *"My betters are expecting an update on the Grand Lodge situation."*

He suspects his own star. He knows about her "little jaunts" and does not believe she works from home. He set the Summer Palace deadline at Monday, on a Friday morning, with a bounty attached to failure.

## Competence and circle

His method is the loyalty test, and he does not warn you it is one. He handed his best prosecutor a fully redacted brief — name and identifying details struck out throughout — against her own sister, and sat back to see what she would do with it. When she broke script and testified as kin instead, he intervened personally to prosecute from the floor, and was forced to concede on the record that bearing witness for kin *"is her right."* He conceded it through locked teeth and then gave her the Summer Palace assignment anyway.

## Beliefs

He believes an officer's allegiance to the Empire should be tested where it hurts most, and arranged exactly that. He believes his prosecutor is too credulous — that she believes too much, thinks everything is for the good of the Emperor, and does not understand the contravening forces at work. He believes that wriggling out of a trap without holding a grudge is a killer instinct worth cultivating.

## Conduct in role

He does not appear until he is needed and then he is unavoidable. The first contact is a folder pushed under a door with no date on it, on Imperial letterhead, reading only **"Where are you?"** The second is an ambush outside the High Court at dawn, opening with a threat of court-martial for dereliction. The third is the assignment, handed over in person, in the corridor, immediately after the defeat — because the defeat has not changed the schedule and the boat leaves that night.

## The Bantide Moro case

Korth Ep 13. The brief demanded the prosecution of an Imperial medical records keeper for falsifying documents, espionage, sabotage and embezzlement. In Karrnathi law she was simply **"the condemned,"** guilt presumed. The supporting material covered inflated casualty, supply and staffing reports from the Anduin theatre, a written assessment that the Battle of Anduin would be lost for want of supplies and that surrender was acceptable, personal letters mocking Imperial duty and hierarchy, and a documented love of orcish music and dance. Every letter was addressed to a beloved sister.

The condemned turned to face the advocate and was unmistakably Deirdre Moro's sister. Her name was Bantide Moro. The charges were read without expression — falsifying records, false claims of defeat at Anduin, sabotage, false orders to surrender, embezzlement, conspiracy, perjury, adultery, idolatry, high treason and sodomy — and several judges registered admiration at the clarity of the delivery.

Then the advocate set down the advocate's role and took up the kin's, and Merkin lost. The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims, granted the state one additional month to produce evidence, and remanded the condemned to the local garrison.

> "I gave you the chance to do anything... instead you're going to throw your lot in with her? I guess I expected more out of you."\
> \
> "You're too credulous, Moro. You believe too much. You think everything is for the good of the Emperor. You have to understand that there are contravening forces you don't understand."\
> \
> "You've managed to wriggle out of it, and you don't seem to hold any grudge. It's almost as if you considered it fair play, and that's the instinct I'm trying to cultivate. It's a killer instinct."

## The Grand Lodge assignment

He told her she had spent her only chip, that Bantide would never leave that cell or recover her post — and then handed her the file in person. Travel to the **Summer Palace**, the **Ivory Lazaret** and the surrounding grounds; seize all records of guests, names and dates from the **Grand Lodge**; arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates found there. The Grand Lodge is a former guild hall used as a gathering place by non-Imperial dignitaries who need to influence the Imperial seat without serving it directly, and the Empire suspects dealings there with Lhazaar pirates. The file contained a likeness of [Captain Joseph L. Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), the Dread Pirate.

The deadline was Monday. It was Friday morning. Failure to return carried a bounty. The advocate he issued it to is Gemma Corso of the Inevitables, and the Empress is at the end of that river.

## Right now

Merkin is alive, in Korth, expecting a report on the Grand Lodge by Monday from an advocate he does not trust, on behalf of betters he will not name. The condemned he was owed is officially a suicide and is currently hidden in his own star prosecutor's apartment. Somewhere above him, Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent as though the Empire had obtained a list of every name.

**Organizations / Groups:** [The Ministry of Law](/organizations/the-ministry-of-law/), Korth · the Imperial officer corps (Vice Admiral).

**Holdings:** The Star Advocates of the Ministry of Law and the mass-prosecution docket against everyone who reported casualties.
