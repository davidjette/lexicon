**Gnome · Wizard · House Sivis (banned) · Speakers Guild · Likely leader of the Rebel Alliance · Level Unknown · Alive**

*Also known as:* none recorded beyond her office, Secretary of the Speakers Guild.

Channa Devir is a four-hundred-year-old gnome wizard, an elder of the banned House Sivis, Secretary of the Speakers Guild of Sharn and one of the most respected public officials in the city. She is one of the few known former members of House Sivis to have left Sharn and Zilargo without arrest, and she brokers compromise between landowners, businesses and Imperial forces to keep peace and trade running in Sharn. She is also a likely leader of the Rebel Alliance. The Crimson Sun has had her marked for some time and has not acted; the Emperor has not moved against her. Her face, height, colouring and dress are not described in any source.

**The house.** **House Sivis** is the gnomish Dragonmarked house of spies, clerks and bureaucrats, and it is banned — its members driven out of Zilargo and out of Imperial administration, membership itself grounds for arrest. **She is one of the few known "former" members of House Sivis have left Sharn/Zhilargo without arrest, probably because of her fame and popularity among the well to do of the city.** The **Speakers Guild** she runs is what the Empire has instead of a functioning civil accord in Sharn: the body through which landowners, businesses and Imperial forces are made to agree on something in writing.

**In public** Devir is the compromise itself. **She is respected by both separatist and collaborationist factions with Sharn and is frequently seen aiding in compromise between land owners, businesses and even Imperial forces to ensure peace and trade within the city.** Nobody in the city's commercial or political leadership can afford to be the person who made Channa Devir stop returning calls. **In private** she is on the other side of the war entirely. **Channa is a four-hundred year old Gnome wizard and a likely leader of the Rebel Alliance.** The neutrality that makes her indispensable to Sharn is the same neutrality that puts her in every room where something is decided.

**Competence and circle.** A wizard with four centuries of practice and a career built entirely out of other people's need for a trustworthy intermediary. Her natural counterpart is [Torralyn d'Sivis](/people/torralyn-d-sivis/), the other elder of the banned house still standing in Sharn, who heads the Notaries Guild — between the two of them, Sivis gnomes still write and still enforce the terms of nearly everything the city does. Her heirloom, if she has one, is unrecorded.

**The secret.** **She has been a target of the Crimson Sun for some time, but she is suspected to have 'death proof' spells which may provide leverage for her, that if she were to die, awful secrets would be automatically revealed. For now, the Emperor has not moved against her.** She has not hidden. She has arranged matters so that killing her is more expensive than tolerating her, and then continued going to meetings.

> "For now, the Emperor has not moved against her."

She believes a city that is still trading is a city that has not yet lost. She believes an agreement written down and witnessed outlives the people who signed it, which is the doctrine of her banned house. She believes the Empire will kill her the moment the arithmetic changes, and has arranged for the arithmetic not to change.

**On the job** Devir works by convening. She does not appear to take sides in the room, which is why both sides keep inviting her, and she is present for the initial position, the concession and the final text of every significant compromise struck in Sharn. An intelligence apparatus can surveil a rebel. It cannot easily surveil the woman the collaborators themselves asked to chair the meeting.

**Origin.** **Unknown.** Four hundred years of life are recorded as a number and not as a biography. Her birthplace in Zilargo, the date she took the Sivis mark, her part in the Last War, how she came to Sharn, and how she left the house's territory without being arrested for it are not recorded.

**Right now** Channa Devir is Secretary of the Speakers Guild in a city that has thrown off the Iron Veil, and she is a likely leader of the Rebel Alliance in a rebellion that has just won its first city and now has to govern one. The Emperor still has not moved against her.

### Personality Characteristics

**Motivation:** keep Sharn trading, keep Sharn talking, and outlive the Empire.

**Likes:**

- Agreements in writing
- Being invited by both sides
- The Speakers Guild
- The well-to-do of Sharn, who protect her without meaning to
- Peace and trade within the city
- Contingencies that work while she sleeps

**Dislikes:**

- The ban on House Sivis
- The Crimson Sun
- Being asked which side she is on
- Arrests without paperwork

**Vices & Personality flaws:** Unknown. Her public conduct has no recorded blemish.
