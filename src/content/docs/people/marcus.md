> Find proof Alphalpha Beginnigain and Wainwright St. Cloud are somehow the same person, then bring me to Sigil to prove it to my sister. She will help us find and destroy St. Cloud, restore my place at the heart of death, and end this Time of Troubles.

<small>— Marcus's Ally Card. The same instruction is given to the party in Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.</small>

**Human · Non-binary · Infanta of Death · the reincarnation of the Raven Queen · Living — Death restored to its rule**

*Also known as:* the Raven Queen · the Infanta of Death · Astra or Aurora Saharel, one of the twin queens of Netheril · "the death child" <small>(the Company crew's name for the target of their search)</small>

**Marcus** is the child who carries the soul of the **Raven Queen**, the **Infanta of Death**, and the twin of the **Lady of Pain**. Asmodeus and [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) conspired to trap the Raven Queen's soul in a mortal, and Marcus is that mortal; with the Seat of Death and Judgment vacant, Asmodeus hoarded the dead and the gods descended into mortal children. She was hidden among the orphans of the Gravedigger's tomb in **the Ride**, where the Horseking **Khal Unger** and his devil-worshipping Gravedigger tortured a princess once a year, on her birthday, trying to summon a god that was in the room but not in the girl. She made the dead kill them and ran, and the party, St. Cloud's men and child-traffickers all converged on the Citadel of the Raven looking for her. She appeared afterwards in the bank vault at Waterdeep with [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) and Obi, set the party the task of proving that Alphalpha Beginnigain and St. Cloud were one man, and was carried into [Sigil](/places/sigil/), where her sister banished her to Despondos. The two returned together at the last battle, after the severed head of Asmodeus, with Death restored to its natural rule and the named dead walking out of the land of the Dead behind them.

## Why she exists

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small>

Marcus is not an accident of reincarnation. Marcus is **where the Raven Queen was imprisoned**.

The Raven Queen held the Seat of Death and Judgment at the Heart of the [City of Dis](/places/dis/), as a neutral arbiter between the gods and the devils, controlling the flow of souls onward to the outer realms. **Asmodeus and St. Cloud conspired to trap her soul in a mortal** — and the mortal is Marcus. With the Seat vacant, Asmodeus hoarded the dead, the gods began to starve, and they descended into mortal children to stay near their followers. **That is the Infanta crisis, and Marcus is its cause and its cure.**

It also reframes the tomb in the Ride below. The Horseking and his Gravedigger were torturing a princess once a year to wake a god, and they were not wrong that a god was there to be found — they were looking in the wrong child, for a god somebody else had already put in a box.

## Who she is

The Raven Queen and the Lady of Pain are the twin queens of Netheril — **Astra and Aurora Saharel** — which is why the record calls the Lady of Pain and Marcus sisters in the literal sense.

Marcus is non-binary, and both pronouns appear in the sources: the Ride testimony uses *he* ("He hated being a human locked in the tomb"), the Winking Depths uses *she* ("as if suggesting she show mercy"), and the finale calls her the Lady of Pain's *sister*. *She* is the primary, because she is the reincarnation of the Raven Queen.

Marcus's own account of how the two of them came to hold Sigil, spoken through the raven Poe:

> My sister and I came to Sigil with the sage, after the Second Fall. We took the Banefae's home while they were feeding our people into oblivion. By the time they returned, the cities were ours. We cast them into Despondos where all of them perished. The sage had lied to us for a century, so we threw him in as well and gave his house to the poor. He caused both the First and Second Fall. Now he will trigger the Third.

<small>— Marcus, Arc IV, [The Infernal Machines](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/), Part V. The sage is [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/).</small>

## Appearance

The only description in the record is from the final battle, riding on her sister's shoulder: "a slight, pale and dark haired child, and on their shoulder, a jet black raven." <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

## The tomb in the Ride

The Ride is a remote valley of horse farmers with a trading post called **Whitehorn**. The Gravedigger there worshipped the devil, he and the Khal worshipped him together, and they performed a ritual on the princess every year to try to summon him. The testimony that corrects them comes from a boy who knew Marcus:

> But they're wrong, she isn't the devil. It's Marcus. Marcus is one of them, it's him that has the Goddess of Death inside him. The Raven Queen. He hated watching them torture her every year on her birthday. And this time he had enough. He made the dead kill them and ran away. Marcus was always the Raven Queen. He hated being a human locked in the tomb. The Gravedigger didn't know, but he knew Marcus was bad and kept him in the tomb and didn't let him come ot.

<small>— Arc III, The Age of the Infanta, Part IV. Quoted from the session notes, which are in bullet form; sic on "didn't let him come ot." The Company was searching for the same figure and called it "the death child."</small>

Leaving the tomb was the first time she had ever left it, and the note adds that if she is still alive she is probably lost. The party, St. Cloud's men and child-traffickers all converge on the Citadel of the Raven looking for her.

## In the vault at Waterdeep

Marcus appears in the bank vault alongside Elistrae and Obi — the record calls the three of them "the other two infanta" from Obi's point of view. When Allo Vera speaks, "Marcus raises two fingers and says 'silence,' and suddenly a black thread appears at Allo Vera's mouth, and blood begins to stream from her lips as it weaves around them, sealing her mouth closed." Obi then "puts a hand on Marcus' wrist, as if suggesting she show mercy to Vera."

Marcus frees Vera's mouth and puts the charge to her directly: "You betrayed your God to Wainwright St. Cloud and a deceived a child into marching into his certain death. What do you have to say for yourself?" <small>(Sic on "and a deceived a child." The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small>

When Vera and Piergerion talk Obi into returning to Waterdeep, Elistrae says "No…" and Marcus says "Don't—". Obi disappears.

## What she asks the party to do

> Enter the Old Man's house and destroy the Dox collective. Find proof that the Alphalpha Beginnigain and Wainwright St. Cloud are somehow the same person, then bring me to Sigil to prove it to my sister. She will help us find and destroy St. Cloud, restore my place at the heart of death, and end this Time of Troubles.

<small>— Marcus, Arc IV Part V. Sic on "Alphalpha Beginnigain," which the Lady of Pain renders "Alphalpha Beginnigin."</small>

The party can bring her into Sigil through the portals in the living area. The record notes that Marcus demands it.

## Her sister

The Lady of Pain appears the moment Marcus enters the city:

> Alphalpha Beginnigin and Wainwright St. Cloud are one in the same, surely you know that. He destroyed me and let Asmodeus take the land of the dead. He's doing the same thing here. Those demons tearing the city apart will bring the Demogorgon here to rule in your stead. Then one of them, or one of the mortal gods on earth will find the egg and bring us to the end.\
> \
> Do not destroy these fools. It's their destiny to destroy the Traveler. That's why we failed. We were never meant to be.\
> \
> Help me take the Shadowfell and unbreak the world.

<small>— the Lady of Pain, Arc IV Part V. "One in the same" is the source's wording and is quoted exactly.</small>

She then absolves the party, purges the city of demons, and banishes her sister to Despondos with the line "Seek your throne in the maze."

## The rule of Death restored

They arrive together at the last battle, after the severed head of Asmodeus. Marcus speaks for both of them:

> We have completed our task, and returned Death to its natural rule. But we could not ignore your cause again. So we have come to join your fight, and we have brought some of your friends.

The record notes that "Marcus smiles for the first time you have ever seen." The friends stepping out of the land of the Dead behind them are Obi, Jeggings, Holden Tudyx, **the Orcs who served [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/)**, and a sheepish **Flex Gimble**, "barely recognizable as such, his face long since replaced by a bronze mask and his limbs replaced with six mechanical multiarticulated prosthesis." <small>(Sic on "prosthesis." End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small>

The sisters were at [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) twice. They came once as Marcus and [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/) from the present, and once as [the Saharel twins](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/), young women of Netheril, from Antiquity in the second loop. The party went back in time and recruited allies for the Egg; some of those allies were allies, and some enemies, in their own timeline's future.

## Social

**Contacts & Relations:**

- **The Lady of Pain** — her twin, ruler of [Sigil](/places/sigil/). Banished her to Despondos; killed Asmodeus with her afterwards.
- **Asmodeus** — took the land of the dead. Beheaded.
- **Wainwright St. Cloud** — named by both sisters as the sage who destroyed the Lady of Pain and let Asmodeus take Death.
- **Obi** — a fellow Infanta. Stayed her hand in the vault at Waterdeep; returned to Waterdeep against her objection; came back out of the land of the Dead at the end.
- **Elistrae** — a fellow Infanta, present with her in the vault.
- **Khal Unger** and the **Gravedigger** — the Horseking and priest who tortured a girl each year looking for her. Killed by the dead she raised.
- **The princess of the Ride** — the child they mistook for her, tortured yearly on that mistake.

**Hobbies & Pets:** a jet black raven, Poe, which rides on her shoulder and speaks with her voice.
