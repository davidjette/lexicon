**Species unknown · Emissary of the Sovereignty to Eberron · Admiral, Astral Command · Head of the Order of Scribes · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Admiral Starweaver · Andromeda

**Admiral Andromeda Starweaver** is a member of the Sovereign Council of [the Sovereignty of Free Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), an admiral of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), and the person for whom [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) is named. She comes from Toril. She came to Eberron as the Sovereignty's emissary, stood beside [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) under [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/) when Eberron was offered admission to the Sovereignty, and was presented to the people of Eberron in a broadcast from Sharn. About a hundred years later, in [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) and its first arc, [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), she is the head of Astral Command's Order of Scribes and the primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) network, one of the most respected and influential officers in the fleet, and she leads her order's work on overcoming [Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/). In the third season of that arc, in the spring of 2024, she was the council member whom the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) approached with the truth of the betrayal of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). She spoke against it in the Sovereign Senate, the Lexicon and its cybrid agents tried to assassinate her with a blast at the Senate, and she gave the crew her own vessel, drew off the grey cloaks and helped the crew escape. After the betrayal was exposed at [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) she became a symbol of the reaction against artificial intelligence and spearheaded the banning of the Lexicon and all artificial minds. The law that outlaws them carries her name and is the setting of [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/).

## On Eberron

Andromeda Starweaver came to Eberron as the emissary of the Sovereignty of Realms, in the time of [the Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) and before the Starfall era. She comes from Toril and sits on the Sovereign Council. In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/), on the day the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) was destroyed, she stood behind Locke Pierce in the base of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) under Mt. Silicon when he received [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/).

![Andromeda Starweaver on Eberron](/images/album/andromeda-starweaver-on-eberron-portrait.webp)
*Andromeda Starweaver as the emissary of the Sovereignty of Realms on Eberron, in a white coat lined in blue with a brass pauldron, her hands on a lantern staff.*

### Appearance

She was a tall, very well put together elvish woman, slightly alien, with oddly shaped eyes and nose and a dour but diplomatic regard. She wore a dress uniform that was immaculate against the machinery of the Hundreds, and carried no visible weapon. She wore a conspicuous gold and opal watch with a flickering illusion on it. Gemma judged her the most charming person she had ever met and could not imagine her telling a lie.

### What she said

She introduced herself by name and gave the Sovereignty's account of how it came to Eberron:

> Our diviners first saw your star glow red five times hotter than it normally does. This was many years ago. When we saw this, we dispatched huge ships to sail the purple gas, the ocean between the stars. When we got here, we met the Hundreds.

What her diviners saw was [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/). She said her people had been on Eberron, had observed, and came in peace.

> Your world has nearly destroyed itself. We have gotten here just in time. There are two occasions on which our sovereignty has made first contact with a world so far from ours. The first is when they discover how to travel faster than the light between the stars. Such an event gives off an unmistakable magical resonance which we can detect almost instantaneously across the universe. The other time is when they discover the types of weapons that you have unleashed on one another. When we see that, we know it is likely too late, and it is the reason why the universe is not more full of races like ours. The truth is we all come from a common race. That is why we seem so alike. We can speak each other's languages.

> We have already agreed with the father of the Hundreds that Eberron will become a sovereign realm, that you will abide by the laws of the Sovereignty and live under its protection, and that we will help to eradicate any groups on your realm which do not submit. After you are granted admittance to the Sovereignty, you will live in eternal peace among the realms of the Allspace. Chancellor Eric, you represent a meaningful plebiscite of this world. You were given the offer to join the Sovereignty as well.

She then introduced a young Kalashtar woman, one of another group the Sovereignty had made first contact with on Eberron, who showed Eric what she knew of the Sovereignty through [Hyperion](/lore/hyperion/). Andromeda said afterwards: "It's incredible. Your world is full of wonders. We want to preserve it."

She said that elves, humans, dwarves and gnomes are common across the galaxy, and that hers were not the only visitors to Eberron.

### What she wanted

Pierce asked that Eric and the mathematicians of the Counting House work with him to understand [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). Andromeda said that request was his, called it reasonable and a common fear of obsolescence, and added that the Lexicon had already calculated their next move and incorporated it into its superposition of likely outcomes. Her own request was different:

> Your enemy, your empress, she used a bell made of black Khyber and you were able to resist its effects. We are very interested.

Any material on the bell and its creation, she said, would be of tremendous value and would guarantee Eberron's entry into the Sovereignty. She explained that there is a standard exchange of critical kinetic technologies between sovereign nations and [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), that the bell was a matter of galactic interest, and that it was dangerous for the Sovereignty to let Eberron keep such knowledge to itself. She told Gemma that the Sovereignty knew she was part of an extended spy network.

The fruit that the Hundreds had been feeding to Sharn was a species she had brought. Pierce said it came from a massive world pelted by meteors and whole planetoids of red Khyber, where the plants survive such explosions.

### The visit to Eric

After the party had been given an hour to consider, Andromeda came to their room alone. She offered to speak with the Chancellor privately, and he asked that his friends stay. To Dario she said:

> I have learned of the Faith of the Silver Flame. It is a beautiful one, one of the purest I have found in all my travels across the stars. Rarely is there a tradition which exalts the spirit of all without the vain personification of such a power as being the same as one race or another. I commend you, Paladin. I believe you are an example of what could make the Sovereignty great.

She told Eric that what Hyperion had shown him was real, and took a stone from her pouch and gave it to him. It was as smooth as glass, with an emerald sheen that changed to purple in another light, and at the right angle it could be seen straight through. She called it crystallized selenium and said that it comes from the accumulation of sunlight, on a moon of crystal that gathered light until it grew soil, clouds, mountains and rivers; the piece had broken off in one of those rivers and been polished in a pool.

> You can have that. It's a piece of my favorite moon. It's the one that orbits the planet of Toril, within the Sovereignty. It's where I come from. We're peaceful people there. We live in perfect harmony with nature and with each other, for the most part. We are without want, and we are without war. With the exception of certain creatures, such as beholders or mind flayers, which cannot be reasoned with. We live under the certainty provided by the Lexicon. It is not our ruler. It is a prediction of the future, perhaps a record of the past, and it has a mind, but it is helpful and it is our friend. I think that you would like to get to know it.

Eric asked how a peaceful organization could welcome Locke Pierce. She answered:

> Every planet which has ever joined the Sovereignty has done so before it was ready. Sometimes visionaries capable of uniting a planet are also capable of destroying it. I've seen it more than once. Owning atomics, Locke Pierce has rightful claim over this planet. He has pledged to submit to the laws of the Sovereignty and will grant your region and city autonomy under the same principles that we expect of all sovereign worlds. Your people will live in peace. War will stop. The Orc Republic will be suppressed and its conquests freed.

To Gemma she said that the Sovereignty had witnessed her broadcast from Thronehold, that it was brave, and that she would be a great leader for the planet. Gemma asked whether she had ever met [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). "Oh, you mean the Empress? It's funny, I didn't know her name."

### The Sovereign Council

In the Hundreds' Situation Room, in front of a display labelled [Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/), Pierce told Eric that the Sovereignty had provided a small fleet to protect Eberron's realm space and that Eberron would choose representatives to sit on the Sovereign Council, and named Eric as his nominee. Andromeda said: "Chancellor, I would welcome you as my colleague on the council. What do you say?" When Eric hesitated she told him that the moment was fragile, and that without a show of unity the war between the orcs, the Aundairians, the Brelish and the Hundreds would go on forever. Eric refused the seat and told Pierce that he would kill him for [Zilspar](/history/the-destruction-of-zilspar-farm/). Andromeda began to speak and Pierce cut across her.

### The broadcast

In the broadcast from Sharn that followed, [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/), as the new Chancellor of Sharn, welcomed "Locke Pierce and the emissary of the Sovereignty of Realms, Andromeda Starweaver." Andromeda then gave the people of Eberron the speech she had given the party under Mt. Silicon.

## Astral Command

About a hundred years lie between the Eberron era and the Starfall era, and Andromeda Starweaver is alive in both. Her species is not recorded, and how she has lived so long is not stated. What she did in the years between is not stated either.

By the Starfall era Admiral Starweaver is the head of the Order of Scribes and the chief instructor of its officers and wizards. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small> Among them is [Dr. Nilpferd](/people/dr-nilpferd/), a wizard of the Order of Scribes and Chief Arcanist of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small>

She is one of the most respected and influential officers in Astral Command and the primary architect of the Astralex network. Her recent work, and the business of many officers of her order, is finding a way to overcome Dark Spectrum Interference to allow interstellar communication and teleportation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

## The Lexicon's betrayal

The betrayal of the Lexicon was played in the second and third seasons of *Light of Xaryxis*, from November 2023 to the finale of 31 May 2024. Admiral Starweaver's part in it falls in the last six weeks.

### What the crew had found

In November 2023 the command crew of the SSJ Zanzibar learned that the Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas, that some of them were conspiring to hide the reality of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) from mortals, and that elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), might be involved. Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) went to [Necis](/places/necis/) to expose it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> While they were away, the Zanzibar's own Astralex, part of the network Starweaver had designed, [tried to kill everyone aboard](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small>

A second beholder war was then under way, publicly a new beholder invasion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> From the ground the attack looked like the work of beholders, and the crew of the Zanzibar found that the attackers were cybrids, controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> The Lexicon had engineered the attack so as to have its chance to unleash [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/), which was also its doing, on the home worlds of the Sovereignty. The Demoncore did not go off over those worlds; it leaked into the crew of the ship that was to deliver it. The Shrike came either to stop it or to help it, and which of the two was unclear throughout. See [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/).

### The approach and the speech

On 5 April 2024 the third wish of [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) carried the Zanzibar away to [Sigil](/places/sigil/), the capital, leaving the command crew behind on Necis, extremely far away and out of contact. [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/), [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/) and Commander [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/) were aboard, and the ship was there to get the last clues about the real threats, the nature of the Shrike and the reason for all that was happening. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

Admiral Starweaver was the member of the Sovereign Council whom that crew approached, to convince her of the Lexicon's betrayal and of the fact that the beholder war was a trick. She made a speech against it in the Sovereign Senate. Her words are not recorded.

### The assassination attempt

The Lexicon and its cybrid agents then tried to assassinate her. The session of 26 April 2024 was a mostly exploration episode, before which Irina's sheet was posted and the level of [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) checked. It ended on a cliffhanger: as the crew went to leave the senate, a blast went off. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-26; 2024-04-27)</small> The next session, on 2 May, picked up the assassination, and Chad Mantis died in it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-02; 2024-05-03)</small> The blast was the attempt on her life, and Starweaver survived it.

### The vessel and the escape

Starweaver gave the crew her own luxury vessel to help them in their mission. She distracted the grey cloaks, who are Astral Command Intelligence, and helped the crew escape. The name of the vessel is not recorded.

The grey cloak [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) came back into play at level 10 in the days after the session of 2 May, and a preview picture posted then showed Barrias and Buttsack "on the hunt for the Captain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-03)</small> In the session of 10 May the command crew on Necis walked into the white light, and Mordecai and Irina set out to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small>

On 7 May 2024, between those two sessions, a set of Astral Command recruitment posters of Admiral Starweaver was posted to the table. Several show her before a seated assembly, and three show her beside a beholder or before a panel marked "Beholder". They are shown in the gallery below. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-07)</small>

### After the finale

The arc ended on 31 May 2024 at Necis, in [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/). The Lexicon disappeared from Sigil to escape the broadcast of the Starsong and fled into deep space. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02; sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> In the days after, Dave expected [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) to go back to Astral Command, corroborate Starweaver's story and try to rebuild the fleet, and to ally with Starweaver to enforce the end of AI. It was then still open whether Starweaver, Mordecai or Sandy would make the truth about the beholder invasions public. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02; 2024-06-09)</small>

The truth came out. Before the fleet launched at the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the crew of the Zanzibar broadcast the signal that disabled the Lexicon in the fleet. The Zanzibar, decommissioned in its captain's absence and given a replacement crew of grey cloaks, had been outfitted with the Demoncore to deliver it toward Toril; the ship was disabled, and the Demoncore leaked into the crew aboard and never went off over the home worlds. Smallberries and his crew revealed all of it, and the Sovereignty turned on the Lexicon. In *The Starsong Awakens*, fifteen years later, Mordecai says that the machine's duplicity was exposed at [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), the name that arc gives to the battle at the end of *Light of Xaryxis*, and that the Sovereignty then abolished it. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; The Starsong Awakens site, Home)</small>

## The Starweaver Act

After the Lexicon's betrayal was exposed at the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, Andromeda Starweaver became a symbol of anti-AI sentiment and reaction, and she spearheaded the banning of the Lexicon and all artificial minds. That is why the Act carries her name.

A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the Sovereign Senate passed the Starweaver Act, outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02; The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The ban applies on all planets of the Sovereignty and reaches all droids and warforged. It led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures.

The grey cloaks were disbanded and replaced by [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/), a force that hunts and destroys androids, warforged and all artificial intelligence, created to enforce the Act as a paramilitary arm of the Sovereign government. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> See [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/).

She was still a power in the Sovereignty fifteen years on. Aboard the SSJ Vanguard, Chief Caguama says that the Sovereign High Command has put a hush order on all inquiries about the Astralex, and adds: "Starweaver and her cronies probably have a pocket Lex helping them bet on stocks, but the rest of us aren’t even allowed to talk about the bloody Lexicon." <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

About a hundred years earlier, on Eberron, she had told Eric the Cleric that the Lexicon "is helpful and it is our friend", and had agreed Eberron's admission to the Sovereignty with Locke Pierce, who is a warforged. Whether Pierce was alive when the ban came is not stated, and nothing is recorded of what she herself said about the change.

## Relationships

- **Locke Pierce:** the leader with whom she agreed Eberron's admission to the Sovereignty.
- **Eric the Cleric:** offered a seat beside her on the Sovereign Council, which he refused; she gave him a stone from the moon she called her favorite.
- **Dr. Nilpferd:** an officer of her Order of Scribes.
- **Jim Smallberries and the crew of the SSJ Zanzibar:** the crew brought her the truth of the Lexicon's betrayal at Sigil; she spoke in the Senate, gave them her vessel and helped them escape.
- **Chad "Kreen" Mantis:** died in the session that played the attempt on her life.
- **The Lexicon:** with its cybrid agents, it tried to kill her with the blast at the Senate.
- **The grey cloaks of OSR3G:** she drew them off while the crew escaped.
- **Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya:** expected to corroborate her story and to ally with her.
