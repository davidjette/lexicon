**Human · Rogue 5 / Warlock 1 / Wizard 3 · Cadet · [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Cadet Carlos Vega · Senor Vega · Genie Carlos

**Carlos Vega** is a human rogue, genie-pact warlock and bladesinger, played by Nico, who came aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). He went down into a mind flayer's lair at his own suggestion and was marked by it, was given civilian credentials, enlisted as a cadet, and was later reassigned to security. He saved the life of [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/), a fellow servant of the genie [Razomel](/people/razomel/). With the Zanzibar breaking apart, he made his third wish and was bound as a djinn in a bottle; that wish scattered the crew. He spent the next fifteen years in the bottle carried by [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/), and by November 2024 was back in play in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, returning with [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) to Picon to search for [Caprica](/people/caprica/).

## Background

His player first pitched him in October 2022 as the "man in the brig": a handsome man who had snuck aboard the last time the ship docked at a space station to resupply, and was only caught once it was already in deep space. She pictured him as a rogue or a swords bard. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> His first AI portrait was captioned Carlos Vega. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-20)</small>

By his player's account, Carlos was stuck on a rock and was down for anything, trying to gain the captain's trust so he could get off it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-14)</small> He reached level 4 in November 2022. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-06)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### The mind flayer's mark

Going down into the lair of the [mind flayer on the rock](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/) was Carlos's "idea". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small> The mind flayer, small fry but a good foreshadow in Dave's words, got the better of the party as they wandered into his lair. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-14)</small>

Afterwards [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) brought Carlos to security to issue his civilian credentials. He was permitted on the Engineering and Lounge Decks only, could use no fire-based weapons or tools, and was to limit his Astralex access to matters of importance. [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) had told Sandy the mind flayer left a mark on him. Her eyes glowing whitish grey, she said: "If I find that you are harboring a mindflayer spawn aboard this ship I will burn you with wrath of all of Hyperion." <small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

### Cadet

In January 2023, in an adaptation of the *Star Trek* episode "The Drumhead", an engineer was killed in a blast and Carlos was suspected. Irina Malikovsky was drawn in when Buttsack had crew lockers tossed, and she witnessed for Carlos's innocence. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> The same week [Dr. Nilpferd](/people/dr-nilpferd/), the Zanzibar's Magus ex Machina and a wizard of the Order of Scribes, became Carlos's new boss. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> The crew roster lists **Cadet Carlos Vega** among the Civilians and Guests. <small>(Desktop/D&D/Starfall TNG/970900243884499.pdf; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

In May 2023 Carlos won the party the human faction by cheating at cards; sleight of hand is his best skill. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-15)</small> In June 2023 he became a bladesinger. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-03)</small> The season premiere of 2023-06-09 reassigned him to a security post, and his new uniform matched the colours of [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/). Dave called him a bladesinger with a Walkman. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-10, 2023-06-14)</small>

### Irina and Razomel

Carlos saved Irina Malikovsky's life. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small> Irina is a changeling who, after finding a crashed spacecraft, accepted a wish from the genie Razomel, bound in the dead pilot's magic bottle, and took the pilot's life and identity; in exchange she must take Razomel's place in servitude after her death. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-01)</small> Carlos and Irina were both servants of Razomel. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

In August 2023 Carlos took up the Cloak of the Void, which Dave liked because Carlos could use it like a matador and throw people into the void. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-12)</small>

### The third wish

In December 2023 the Zanzibar's own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard, leaving the ship adrift in the Astral Sea while the captain and command crew were far away. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> In January 2024 Carlos, Buttsack and Mordecai were aboard the helpless Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-26)</small>

In April 2024, with the ship breaking apart, Carlos made his third wish, and the Zanzibar left the command crew behind and reached [Sigil](/places/sigil/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> Once his pact was laid out, he used both of its wishes within 24 hours. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> The third wish made him a djinn. Anyone he grants wishes to is cursed, since his wishes are monkey-paw wishes, and he can grant them only to those who accept his pact and so set out on the road to freeing him. He can try to free himself by binding someone bad, though giving bad people wishes leads to bad outcomes. Razomel is free, able to go wherever he wants. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

His bottle went to Buttsack, who made his Investigation roll and so knows that Carlos is inside. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> Dave teased that Carlos would one day be purified by the planes of fire and turned into a fire elemental, and his player pictured him as an eldritch fire djinn. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06, 2024-04-20)</small>

### The finale

Carlos's wish scattered the crew of the Zanzibar all over the place without warning, so that Sandy did not know what became of her crewmates. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small> At the finale of 2024-05-31 Irina cast a final Wish. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Dave judged that all the wishes had a major impact on how everything went down. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Fifteen years later Buttsack, paralysed after a sportsball career with the Khorvaire Kestrals, had retreated to an isolated Githzerai temple in the outer planes to focus on inner peace, meditation "and the true meaning of the mysterious bottle he carries with him." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Carlos spent those years in the bottle. By November 2024 he was back in play. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-22)</small> How he came out of the bottle is unknown.

The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier lists Carlos Vega as deceased, and reports that Buttsack "entertains fantasies of communing with the spirit of his crewmate from the SSJ Zanzibar". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

In January 2025 Carlos and Barrias Ruby-Eye, an unlikely pairing in JL's words, went back to Picon to continue the search for Caprica. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-18)</small>

## Abilities and equipment

Carlos's sheet makes him a variant human Rogue 5 (Soulknife) / Warlock 1 / Wizard 3 (Bladesinging) with a custom background and 49,000 XP. He has 54 hit points and AC 17, and speaks Common, Deep Speech, Elvish, Infernal and Thieves' Cant. <small>(character sheet)</small>

- **Soulknife:** psychic blades and psionic energy dice. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Warlock:** the sheet names his patron as the Genie, of the efreeti kind, with Genie's Wrath and Bottled Respite. Dave named Carlos a servant of Razomel. <small>(character sheet; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>
- **Bladesinger:** Bladesong, four times per long rest. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **Feats:** Telekinetic, with an invisible *mage hand*, and Fighting Initiate (Two-Weapon Fighting). <small>(character sheet)</small>

**Items:** Cloak of the Void, Wand of Winter, Arcane Grimoire +1, Goggles of Night and a [Scroller](/items/the-scroller/); studded leather, two scimitars, a dagger and a light crossbow; a lute, fine clothes, and disguise, forgery and thieves' tools. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Personality

His sheet gives his traits as "I am always calm, no matter what the situation. I never raise my voice or let my emotions control me." and "The first thing I do in a new place is note the locations of everything valuable—or where such things could be hidden." <small>(character sheet)</small> His player described him as chaotic and spontaneous. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>
