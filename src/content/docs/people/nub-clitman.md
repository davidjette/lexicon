**Person · [Arc IV, The Infernal Machines](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/) · Dead**

*Also known as:* Nub · the mysterious Nub

**Nub Clitman** was a person the party sought during their time in [Sigil](/places/sigil/) in the Age of the Infanta. Roland's mother Gabrielle left a note in her journal to ask him about Starfall. At the end of Arc IV he ran in on [Pim](/people/pim/) and Brother Magnus aboard the party's ship, failed to deceive them, and was killed by [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/).

## In Sigil

Among the notes at the end of Gabrielle's journal, which [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) found beside him at Deschain & Son, is the line: "Ask Nub about Starfall and whatevers down there W wants". <small>(Sic. Arc IV Part IV.)</small>

The party wanted to find him. JL, who played Zanzibar: "We need to find Toots Gildebrand about fixing Lex. I wouldn’t mind finding Nub Clitman also." Dave: "Ah yes the mysterious Nub". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-20)</small>

## At the ship

At the Arc IV finale, "Magnus, Pim, Zanzibar and Maximillian are on the ship. Zanzibar and Maximillian are reviewing intelligence about the situation in Cormanthor, and Pim and Magnus are working with Lex on the Anachron when Nub Clitman runs in. “I’ve got it!” he cries! “I’ve figured it out!”" Three rounds in, the clock strikes nine again: "Nub changed the clock". <small>(Arc IV Part V, "At the Ship.")</small> See [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) and [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/).

Among Dave's "huge moments from last game that changed a lot" was "nub crit failing his deception roll". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-10)</small>

## Death

Zanzibar killed him. LaMont, who played Revan: "He killed nub clitman". Dave: "Oh that’s right lol". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>

<small>**The name.** JL offered "Nub Clitman" to the table as an NPC name, and again in the list of names Dave asked for. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-22; 2019-07-17)</small>
