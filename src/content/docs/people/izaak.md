**Species unrecorded · Ranger and Artificer · Fulcrum liaison to the Nest · Missing, presumed dead**

<small>Also known as: Izzak</small>

**Izaak** is a ranger and artificer of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) and, for over nine years, the rebellion's liaison between the Breland farmsteads at Zilspar and [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) in Sharn's Upper City. He patrolled the farmlands between Sharn and Zilspar as a ranger of the Sharn City Watch, left the service when the Watch was absorbed into the Iron Veil, and joined the farmstead rebels nearly a decade ago. He is the eldest son of Fulcrum's founder [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and half-brother to [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/), the other end of his line. He recruited the Inevitables at the Astra Bar and implanted their Ghost Chips himself. He then left on a Fulcrum mission recorded only as redacted and vanished; Zilspar has since been destroyed, his mother taken alive, and he is presumed dead.

## Physical description

A dark-skinned man in a broad brown felt hat and round tinted glasses, close-cropped beard, a mustard scarf bunched at the throat. He wears grey field fatigues under a plate carrier hung with pouches, cargo shorts, and heavy boots. Below the left knee is a black prosthetic leg, fitted by the rebellion's own surgeon. A gold ring on one hand, a small hoop in one ear, and arms folded in every likeness anyone has taken of him.

## Origin

Izaak was a ranger of the Breland farmlands under the Sharn City Watch, in the years when the Watch still meant local law rather than Imperial security. He watched the Empire close its hand on the countryside, watched the Watch itself absorbed into the Iron Veil, and left. He joined the farmstead rebels nearly ten years ago and embedded himself in Fulcrum, a group long tied to Sharn's underground struggles. Somewhere in those years he lost a leg; the head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital, codename the Nightingale, performed the operation that fitted the prosthetic. He became the rebellion's chip man, and spent over nine years running messages between a farm and a buried railway station.

## The liaison

Fulcrum holds the Breland farmsteads and Zilspar; the Nest holds a rebuilt transit station under a park in the Upper City. For over nine years Izaak has served as the vital liaison between his half-sister Zero of Sharn and the Fulcrum rebels in Zilspar, maintaining a covert line of communication that bridges the distance between the city's highest terraces and the farms outside its walls. He works it partly through a shared familiar, a falcon linked to his sister.

Unlike his half-sister he does not follow the Silver Flame, and he is trusted by both the Nest and Fulcrum without belonging entirely to either. He is respected for his experience, resourcefulness, and ability to navigate both city and countryside with ease.

## Bloodline

His mother is Zero-One, founder and leader of Fulcrum, once the paladin Fema Nolan. He is her eldest, and lives at Zilspar alongside her and a cell of loyal guards. Zero of Sharn is his half-sister.

## The Astra Bar

Newly arrived in Sharn, the trio were instructed to meet a rebel operative during a quiet meeting at the Astra Bar in the Upper City. Their first assignment came from him: infiltrate the corporate labs of BioTec and steal a prototype known only as the Ghost Chip. He also gave them the way into the Nest — a hidden puzzle in Skyroot Park, solved at the end of a night that had very nearly killed all three of them.

He met three unvetted foreigners over a quiet drink, gave them a target and an entry method, and did not tell them anything they did not need. Afterwards he implanted their Ghost Chips himself, arranged the Vilspar produce-delivery cover, flew back into the Lower City with them, and revealed a hidden elevator route out of a collapsing gala only at the moment it was needed.

## The Redacted Mission

The last public entry against his name on the Nest's board reads, in full: *Ongoing: redacted Fulcrum mission.* Nobody outside Fulcrum's leadership was told what it was. Then Fulcrum began to die.

**Right now:** Izaak is missing. Zilspar Farm has been destroyed and his mother taken alive by Eden units; [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) reports him presumed dead. Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent as though the Empire had a list of every name, and it began after Izaak vanished. The Nightingale believes he is alive.

> "His independence makes him a trusted figure among The Nest and Fulcrum."

**Organizations / Groups:** Fulcrum; formerly the Sharn City Watch; permanent liaison to the Nest.

**Holdings:** quarters at Zilspar alongside his mother's guard; the farmland routes between Sharn and Zilspar, which he knows better than the patrols do.

**Items:** a black prosthetic left leg, fitted by the Nightingale · a shared familiar, a falcon linked to his half-sister · the Ghost Chips he implanted in [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/).

**Chipwork:** Izaak's trade and the rebellion's most valuable technical service. He embeds and improves chip enhancements — false identity, teleportation, communication — in a city where the same technology is used by BioTec to stop people saying a name. Every Ghost Chip that walked the Inevitables past an Iron Veil checkpoint was his work.
