**Dwarf · Princess of the swamp tribes · The Mror Holds · Alive**

*Also known as:* **Orangemeet** · Oranjhiken

**Princess Orange Chicken** is a young dwarven leader of the swamp tribes of [the Mror Holds](/places/the-mror-holds/), the granddaughter of the shaman [Yellameet](/people/yellameet/), and the wife of [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/). She was one of the local defenders at [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), and she is the mother of their child, Red Dragon Shrimp.

## The alliance

JL introduced her at the table as **Orangemeet**, with a portrait, in [Leef Ep 6](/sessions/leef-ep-6-the-battle-of-leef/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-05)</small> Her grandfather's third condition for an alliance between the dwarves and the rebels was a meaningful alliance by marriage with her, and Dario was engaged to her. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> She fought among the defenders of Leef alongside Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke and Zero of Leef, and married Dario. When the survivors of Newham fled east, the townsfolk, the refugees and her swamp-dwarf tribes forged [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/) together.

## Red Dragon Shrimp

On the morning after the trio came home from Newham, she embraced Dario and told him she was pregnant. On the day she went into labour, [Colonel Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/) died in his sleep. The child is **Red Dragon Shrimp**. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

When Dario's downtime mission to the dwarves won only an offer of materiel and funds, and no outright alliance, Dave wrote that "this would be devastating to Dario but Orange Chicken would not be suprised" <small>(sic)</small>, and she answered with a proverb: an alliance of unrelated dwarves is "more rare than a hairless bridegroom". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small>

## Since

It was she who wrote to [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) about the lost Deeptempura forge-clan and the Teryaki mourning-party, which the party connected to the foundry at Korranberg. Years after the wedding, Dario talked his way into a night's hospitality at Rak'tafur by naming Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long in the right order. <small>([Korth Ep 7](/sessions/korth-ep-7-the-glitterhame-and-the-grave-robbers/))</small>
