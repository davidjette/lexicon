**Human · Sorcerer · Master of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) · Member of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) · Killed in the purge of the Council**

*Also known as:* Haymen Maw · Hamen Maw

**Hayman Maw** is a human sorcerer who became Master of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) after the assassination of [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), and who held the seat on the [Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) that the Master holds by right. He was the handler of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) during the mole hunt at the Interoperability Summit in Sharn.

## Master of the Crimson Sun

Varyn Crona was murdered outside Fairhaven, and Maw inherited the Master's chair and its Council seat. The Council roster lists him as "Human Sorcerer: Current head of the Crimson Sun." Varyn had openly disliked him, and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), Varyn's apprentice and the order's High Inquisitor, disliked him as well.

## City of Knives

Maw collected [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) by carriage and brought him to a Crimson Sun safehouse disguised as an exclusive hotel near the diplomatic district of Sharn, where [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/), Esther Crona and [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) were waiting. He briefed the four with Fanta Rhodes beside him, and Rhodes said nothing throughout. <small>([City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/))</small>

> "There is a mole on the Imperial Assembly."

Deliberately false intelligence about the summit, available only to the Imperial Assembly, had reached rebel sources. Maw named the three Assembly members attending the summit as the suspects: [Lord Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/), [Lord Albert Spear](/people/albert-spear/) and Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/). He sent the Unforeseen to establish which of the three was leaking.

## His terms to Locke Pierce

Pierce had downloaded State Secret material naming the killer of Esther's master and could not decrypt it. Maw allowed him to keep working at Rekkenmark East under Esther's supervision on two conditions: he was to notify Maw the instant the records cracked, and he was to say nothing to Esther if anything about Varyn Crona turned up. Had Pierce run, Maw would have come for him in person with four Crimson Sun agents and a squad of city guards.

## The purge

Evidence later connected members of the Council of 13 to Varyn's assassination. With the leave of [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), Esther Crona hunted down the Council and their immediate families and killed every member except Henry Heinrick. She then dissolved the Crimson Sun.
