**Human sailor · Boatswain and Chief Engineer of the *Arielle* · Died; ally at the Egg as undead**

Jeggings is a human sailor, Boatswain and Chief Engineer of the **Arielle**. He served aboard [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/)'s personal vessel and stayed with the ship when the party took it and renamed it, and his debrief gave the party their first account of St. Cloud's movements — the hunt for the child in the horse valley, the low cruises over the Aunuroch Desert, and the failed rescue on the Dragon Coast. He died, and returned out of the land of the Dead as an undead ally at the Egg.

## How the party encountered him

As crew. Jeggings "doesn't know who built the Arielle, and St. Cloud is the only one who knows how everything works. He has trained Jeggings and others on various components, though, and Jeggings can likely serve as the Boatswain and Chief Engineer." <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

## What he knew

Jeggings was "not privy to the purpose of most missions, especially since most are just to facilitate the personal and business travels of St. Cloud, who mostly flies between cities and parties and meetings and events." Three missions struck him as strange:

- ferrying "an armed force wearing not uniforms to a remote valley full of backward horse farmers, looking for what the crew nicknamed **'the death child'**" — that is the hunt for [Marcus](/people/marcus/);
- "quite a few slow, low cruises over the Aunuroch Desert, as if St. Cloud enjoyed long cruises through dusty hot windstorms, or was looking for something" — the Anauroch is where the Company found the Netherese designs and the Palantir;
- "an extended mission, nearly a month, in the Dragon Coast… a rescue mission of sorts, and as far as Jeggings knows, it was mostly a failure. But they did bring a few broken soldiers and a child on board, and brought them to Waterdeep."

He also reports that "St. Cloud left the boat to meet with people in the city and did not return, is presumed dead."

## How he became an ally

He returns out of the land of the Dead at the final battle, second of the named dead the Raven Queen brings. <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small> His card type is **Undead Human Sailor** and his mana cost is a single blue — the cheapest ally in the set.

## At the Egg

**White — Stab:** "Deal 5 piercing to a cr. in melee range."

**Black — Scramble the Core:** "Four elementals appear momentarily and deal 10d8 each of fire, cold, lightning and bludgeoning damage to all creatures within 90 ft."

<small>His white action is the weakest in the set and his black action one of the strongest. St. Cloud scrambled the core of the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) at Starfall.</small>

## Status

Already dead when he arrives. Whether his card was sacrificed is not recorded.
