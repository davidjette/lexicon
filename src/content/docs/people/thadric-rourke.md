**Half-elf · Colonel, retired · The rebels of Leef · Dead**

*Also known as:* **The Colonel**

**Colonel Thadric Rourke** was a retired half-elf colonel and veteran of the Last War who ran a small band of rebels out of the rail-town of [Leef](/places/leef/). He welcomed [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) to the rebellion, gave the trio their first jobs, fought beside them at [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), and sent them west to [Newham](/places/newham/) and his man there, [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/). He died peacefully in his sleep at Leef the year after the fall of Newham. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The Colonel

An old half-elf with a scar across his face, in a musty old military uniform whose epaulettes still carry a colonel's stripes. He kept a corner table in the Cat & Biscuits tavern in Leef, where the drinkers talked about battles of the Last War won and lost long ago, and he kept an owlbear named Gimpy. He had lost his wife and his son to the Empire after fighting its war, and he commanded a small army of goons and soldiers that kept the Imperials busy with smash-and-grab jobs along the lightning rail. Rebels of his in Leef included Moose and Jordan Huckleby. He knew Gemma's father, Jane Corso, from the war. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

He saw the rebellion in plain terms:

> "There are 2 sides, the empire and us. And the empire means LeBeefe. Grand Duke LeBeefe."

<small>— Colonel Thadric Rourke, in Leef.</small>

## The rebellion at Leef

When Imperial guards came into the Cat & Biscuits looking for Gemma, the Colonel fought beside the newcomers and then sent them out the back. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

> "You, go. We'll cover for ya. If any more come in here looking, we never saw ya. … And if you're ever looking for work, you come see me. You're pretty handy in a fight. And we can always use more fighters. Welcome to the rebellion."

<small>— Colonel Thadric Rourke, at the Cat & Biscuits.</small>

After Gemma's father was killed, he offered the pair a choice: two clean papers of transit to Aundair that night, or a fight. The fight was the raid on the Imperial stockyard, where a supply train had unloaded 135 crates marked MILITARY SUPPLIES NEWHAM. His next job was the old temple of Dol Arrah, where the Black Doves kept a listening station, for the price of its input switch; in its basement the trio found [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/). Zero, the Silver Flame's agent in Leef, thought him a well-intentioned fool and a thorn in her side. On the night before the Battle of Leef he and three of his rebels were arrested in the raids. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## After Newham

The Colonel pointed the trio west at LeBeefe himself; Digma Beeve was his man in Newham. When they came home from the ruins of Newham, Gemma raised a glass with him at the bar of the Cat & Biscuits to the fall of the city. He brought them the intelligence one of his crew intercepted on the New Mourning: Rekkenmark scientists had found the ground would not be habitable for a hundred years. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

He died peacefully in his sleep on the day Princess [Orange Chicken](/people/orange-chicken/) went into labour. Zero performed the ceremonial consecration of the body. Digma stepped forward to give the eulogy and was overcome, and Gemma gave it. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>
