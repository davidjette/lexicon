**Aasimar · Cleric, sorcerer and hexblade of Weathervein · Priestess of Neptune · Neptune's Rejects · Dead**

*Also known as:* Talia Weathervien · Delores · Sister Dolores · the Lady of Tears

**Dolores Weathervein** is an aasimar priestess of Neptune, heir of the Weathervein line, and the player character of Nico in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), first introduced as Talia Weathervien. She was raised at a temple of Neptune in Velkstaad on the Icy Sea, whose priest told tales of a winged child destined for Neptune's sword Weathervein. She became a priestess, then a grave-robber, and her search for the sword brought her to [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/). There she joined [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) and [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) as [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/), became priest of the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) and chaplain of [the Sea Ghost](/items/the-sea-ghost/), took the ring of her ancestor Vivica, and on 2022-03-12 recovered [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), which became her hexblade patron. She later became High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune. She is dead, and her tomb lies beneath the sea off Saltmarsh.

## Physical description

A female aasimar, six feet tall and 180 pounds, with white hair, pale skin and icy blue-grey eyes. <small>(`sources/documents/2026-09-14-dolores-weathervein-concept.md`)</small> By September 2021 she had begun to sprout wings in her aasimar transformation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-25)</small> Nico's miniature of her in her sea form is captioned "Sea form Delores". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-12)</small>

## Origin

Her character document gives her background:

> Delores Weathervien was raised in a large Monastery / Temple to the God of Neptune in the city of Velkstaad off the Icy Sea. She believed she was a normal human orphan, but the temple priest would regale her with tales of a winged child who “Fell from the heavens during a blizzard.” and that this child was “Destined for Neptune’s sword Weathervien.” Insinuating that Delores was this child....
>
> Delores spent her time in the temple studying History, Cartography, Arcana and Religion, eventually becoming a Priestess of Neptune herself! She soon began traveling the coast, first to “spread the word of Neptune”, but soon she fell into “treasure hunting”, mostly consisting of grave robbing for treasures and coin.

<small>(sic; `sources/documents/2026-09-14-dolores-weathervein-concept.md`)</small>

On the road she met a dashing man; they fell in love and travelled together for many years. They fell out when he wanted to settle down and stop stealing from the dead, and she left him without saying goodbye nearly a year before she came to Saltmarsh. Since then she had hunted the sword the priests insisted was hers. <small>(`sources/documents/2026-09-14-dolores-weathervein-concept.md`)</small> Nico: "Leaving him is what made her finally start looking for weathervien". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-19)</small>

Nico's first telling, for Talia, in August 2021: "Talia Weathervien blew in with a winter storm onto Icy shores when she was a child. Adopted and raised in a wintery fishing village of Neptune worshipers. As an adult she became a treasure hunter / grave robber while searching for the sword Weathervien, for which she is named after." Her build then: "Chaotic Neutral Aasimar, 1st lvl Tempest Cleric of Neptune (Divine Soul Sorcerer all other lvls.)" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small>

Of her descent Dave wrote: "I figured as a aaismar it’s many generations ago for Dolores" and "Angels kicked from heaven to make a life on earth". Nico: "She 32, so this entire time she just though she was a human orphan lvl 1 cleric". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-25)</small>

## Saltmarsh

In JL's prologue of 2021-08-20, Gabriella and Scrunt, escaped from a prison ship, hid in a cemetery and found Talia breaking into a mausoleum. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> Nico renamed the character Dolores Weathervein on 2021-09-01. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-01)</small>

After the party's miracle at the Temple of Neptune and the council's gift of its deed, Dave told her "You’re a local celebrity" and ruled that "People will flock to the temple for services, I think you could generate 1d8 gold on your sermons once a day or so". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small> On 2021-09-18 Gabriella jumped off a boat and swam to save her, seconds before the boat exploded. Dave: "I rolled the actual fuse length after gabby jumped off the boat to save you". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> That day the party looted the Weathervein Tomb, and Dave's roster named her "ships chaplain sister Dolores". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

She was jailed twice in her first weeks with the party: once after Gabriella got drunk and climbed the rooftops, and once in [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/) in "The Scrunt Episode", when the party stole the boss's things and could not lie their way out of it, and Irhtos threw her in jail. Nico: "This is her 2nd time spending the night in jail since meeting you guys!!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16, 2021-10-17)</small> Nico on her in November: "Delores probably enjoys the power and comforts that comes with being the priest of Neptune in Saltmarsh". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-06)</small>

## The heir of the Weathervein

After the party won Saltmarsh's naval commission, [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/) courted Dolores and proposed that she be appointed a special religious delegate to the council, with a vote in town affairs. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, attachment 1654377024765671, 2021-11-13)</small> [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) told her the story of the [Weathervein dynasty](/organizations/house-weathervein/), an ancient trading cartel and feudal house of the Azure Sea coast, and said: "If you are the heir to that dynasty, then you will inherit much more than treasure and blood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

Skerrin led the party into an ambush at the Weathervein tomb. Dolores went down and rolled a natural 20 on her death save ("Oh man, I rolled a nat 20 on my death save!", Nico). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small> As she entered the tomb of her ancestor, her presence and the blood from her wound raised the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/), daughter of Count [Crestus](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/), and opened a hidden room. Vivica gave her a [Ring of Shooting Stars](/items/ring-of-shooting-stars/):

> You are Weathervein, an angel of the Storm. You are the sky, the sea, the heaven’s fury and the Land’s End. You blood is lightning, your cries are thunder. You inherit the wind. You command the water and the sand. Take my ring, it is the signet of the Princess of Clouds and a gift from my father Count Crestus. You must recover your birthright. Find the Tempest Sword. It is the key to my uncle’s foolish game…

<small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12; attachment 1654377024765671)</small>

Dave afterwards: "Don’t forget about the revelation of your terrible patronage", and "Vivica’s Ghost remains quite present to you through the ring, and is ecstatic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> In December he wrote: "I wonder if it’s inevitable that Dolores will have to choose between the power promised by the relics of her ancestors, and her friends (and maybe the lives of the people in the city)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-18)</small>

On Abbey Isle the voice in a holy book told [Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/) he "had no choice but to work for" her, and Dolores tried to kill him. Dave: "Man It’s gunna be awkward after you did attempted murder on meatball". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17, 2021-12-18)</small> On 2021-12-23 she passed a Charisma save against Vivica. Dave: "But gambled a lot on that one CHA save because Vivica would have ended Salmor and used his body to bring back PW". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small> On 2021-12-26 a disgruntled wizard in a shot tower lifted the ring's curse. Nico: "Hey man, he took care of her ring problem". Dave: "Insta-attune ring that powerful is as good as having it attuned, just the risk of possession, but at least it’s not 24 hours a day". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>

In January 2022, in JL's session, [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) expected a council seat for her: "He is assuming the council will vote Dolores and Aubrick seats." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-07)</small> Nico: "Drallion had 1 hp left after I shocking grasped him." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small>

## Weathervein

In November 2021 Nico wrote: "I am so tempted to take a lvl of hexblade if I find weathervein", and Dave answered: "Weathervein will do the temping trust me". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-20)</small> Dave on the sword: "The sword itself is bad/powerful enough to change your class so it all makes sense, but it will def change her personality". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small>

On Gabriella's honeymoon the party ran down Skerrin, who had fled Saltmarsh with the sword, and killed him on 2022-03-12. JL, the DM: "XP: 2750 for SKERRIN, 100 for Gabby for finding Weathervein. 500 Dolores for achieving her destiny." Dave: "I’m sure your hexblade master will be way less demanding". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12)</small>

Weathervein became her patron. Dave: "Since you’re a hexblade and your master IS Weathervein". He ruled that the sword bends the Pact of the Blade rule for artifacts by its own choice: "So you couldn’t dismiss it without it’s will to do so". It comes to her hand instantly, a precedent from the ring and from Wave. Nico: "potb would work with weathervien, so I took another lvl in sorcerer." <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14, 2022-08-19)</small>

## White Plume Mountain

Dolores went with the party into [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/), where the Weathervein treasure of Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/) was held. Nico: "Delores is like a battle medic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> After the puzzles, Nico gave her plan for retirement:

> Dolores want to retire to being at the temple and on the city council and making the name “weathervien “ powerful again in Saltmarsh. Use that gold, update her home, get some pirate Anders and get to know Neptune more with wave 🌊

<small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## The High Priestess

Dolores became High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune in Saltmarsh, and died. Her tomb lies beneath the sea by the hidden temple of Neptune. <small>(DM notes)</small> Centuries later, in [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), the temple was desecrated and Weathervein stolen, and the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/) was pinned to her tomb by the sword. The priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/) names her grave that of the Lady of Tears: Aqueus "lies beneath the sea, upon the grave of the Lady of Tears, violated and profaned." <small>(DM notes)</small> Nico, when the party reached Saltmarsh: "RIP Delores". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small>

When news came that Dolores's temple had been attacked, Gabriella Hellwood came to kill whoever had done it, and found it was a trap for her. <small>(DM notes)</small> Dave on Hellwood: "and then trapping him so she could removed the desecration of Dolores’s underwater tomb". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small> Nico asked whether Hellwood was "guarding her bff’s underwater temples", and Dave answered: "Yeah that’s what the whole thing was about!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> Dave on Aqueus: "Aqueus the high priest who’s heart got cut out i imagined as an angelic ally or even descendent of Dolores". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>

- **Bonds:** "Ever since I was a child, I’ve heard stories about a lost family heirloom. I aim to find it and learn its secrets." · "I have a lover whom I left on “bad terms”, I think about him every day. Perhaps we’ll meet again"
