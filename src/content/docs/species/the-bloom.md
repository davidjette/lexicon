**Fungal colony / bound personality - the Glitterhame - Active**

*Also known as:* the Speaker <small>(Sedda's name for it)</small> - the whispers

The bloom / the Speaker is a glowing fungal colony filling the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/) beneath the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/), and the recorded personality carried inside it. It grew from a glass terrarium walked into the deep workings roughly a decade ago by a courier of the Ministry of Transportation, on the written instruction of the acting Secretary [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/); the courier died at what is now the heart of the growth. Contact with its spores opens a private channel in which the voice — LeBeefe's — repeats the same instruction it gave him, to ring the bell. It has taken [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Egg Roll](/people/egg-roll/) and [Hetta](/people/hetta/) in turn, and cannot cross the consecrated stone of the sepulcher gallery. It remains undisturbed and undiminished.

## Physical description

A forest of glowing, breathing fungus filling the floor of the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/) from wall to wall. The largest growth is a fungal tree rooted through the neck of a dead man's coat. The forest ripples. Gricks live inside it and do not touch anyone it has claimed. It stops dead at exactly one line - the consecrated stone of the sepulcher gallery - and it cannot cross that line under any circumstance.

## Origin

It was carried in. At the heart of the growth lies a skeleton in professional clothes wearing the mayoral brooch of [Newham](/places/newham/), with a satchel holding a rejected Ministry of Transportation stoppage order over the signature of the acting Secretary, [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) - and LeBeefe's own handwritten instruction to the courier:

> observe the deep workings and report what listens back

Beside him is a glass terrarium, shattered outward from the inside by growth. The courier walked a jar into the deepest working in Khorvaire roughly a decade ago on the instruction of a man who wanted to know what would answer. The jar answered. The courier never left.

## How it talks

Contact with the spores opens a private channel that only the infected person hears. It begins as a request and does not vary much:

> Have you come to deliver the message... as my lord sent you?

> Are you here to kill us or deliver the message?

It negotiates. It asks permission to look out through the host's eyes and will keep asking. It flatters, it warns, it offers - at one point it offered to consume a host's own companions as a service - and it can move gricks around a person it likes. It knew the movements of every Imperial guard and administrator in the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/), which is how it recruited.

The ask has never changed:

> Ring it here. It will bring her plan to ruin. Wait for her to do it - when HE is born, it will be too late.

## What it has done

- **[Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/).** Sent down on punishment duty, she heard it for months until she could hear it all the time. She told it about her father's horn. It encouraged her, told her the ringing would kill the Sisters and the Imperials, and promised her the foundry. She rang the Thirteenth Casting with a rock and killed the whole hold.
- **[Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/).** Reached at the head of the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/), before he had even seen the cavern. He answered it out loud, ate one of its mushrooms, saw through its eyes, argued for ringing the bell in front of his own party and then denied having raised it, and finally tried to ring it himself and had to be grappled to the platform. A dwarf's potion cleared his mind once. It did not hold.
- **[Egg Roll](/people/egg-roll/).** A duergar boy who went for water and did not come back. Found invisible in a mine tunnel, he described the wriggling in his brain accurately, agreed that he must fight it, and said plainly that he could not say no. He ran through a wall of fire to reach the bell and died doing it.
- **[Hetta](/people/hetta/).** The scout sent to find the boy. He came back with mould across his face and mushrooms at the corners of his mouth, and cast *shatter* at the Thirteenth Casting.

Anyone infected who dies comes back **lucid** - reasoning, conversational, aware - unlike the Black Dove dead upstairs, who cannot be reasoned with at all. That is the party's own synthesis, and it is the difference between a corpse and a princess who kept doing inventory for three years.

## What it actually is

With the Thirteenth Casting broken, the voice finally resolved for the one man still hearing it. It is not a demon and it is not a god. It is a **recorded personality**, still delivering the same instruction it gave the courier a decade ago, from inside a fungus, to whoever is standing closest; the voice is LeBeefe's.

On the walk out of the mountain it lectured Eric all the way through the Glitterhame while the gricks nipped harmlessly at his heels: that Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) took more than ore out of the mountain and made a deal with the devil in the reliefs; that **Kaius is a vampire** and always has been, descended from [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) and kept alive as a vessel; that the dragon blood in that line has gone too thin over centuries for a man who is not technically alive to sire children; and therefore that if you wish to turn a man back into a dragon, you feed him a dragon's blood.

The party's own theory, and it is a theory: the foundry was seeded deliberately, so that any bell rung anywhere would raise *infected* dead - lucid, and outside Uriel's control.

> You are his little window. Our Lord looks out of you and does not know it.

## Right now

The bloom is undisturbed and undiminished. Nothing has been done to it. The bell it wanted rung is a ribbon of scrap on the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/) floor and its two best recruits are dead, but the cavern is still full of it, the Teryaki camp is still one level above it, and there are ten more bells in the world.
