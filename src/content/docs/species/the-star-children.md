**Spawn of Cthulhu · Builders of Ry'leh in Revan's vision · Depicted throughout the city**

*Also known as:* the spawn of Cthulu <small>(sic)</small>

The star children are, in the vision [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) had on reading the book at [Refuge](/places/refuge/), the spawn of Cthulhu. In the story a gentle voice sang to him, some of them broke free of their father's mind, mined the stars for their stones, learned over millions of generations to travel the dark and to cloak themselves from him, and came at last to a world of giant reptiles and dragon gods. There they stayed in the sea, built the city of Ry'leh, and set about replacing their aging bodies, until Cthulhu found them again. The vision is one of the many tellings of where [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) and the Infernal Machines came from. R'lyeh itself is an ancient city of the aboleth, built around the dormant Zoth and his ship.

## Revan's vision

Dave told the vision to Revan's player LaMont after the session at Refuge, where play ran in a repeating loop:

> Revan, this last loop through I did not deal with what you remember from reading the book. The moment you set eyes on the type inside the book, I described the black tentacles that reached out and seemingly took over your body.  But your mind went elsewhere. It delved deep into the pages of text, into a suffocating black ink of water, deep below where the sand and coast give way into unfathomable depths.  Your mind traveled into the lost corpse city of Ry'leh. where dead Cthulu lies dreaming.

<small>— Dave. Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30</small>

## The song of the city

> A gentle voice sings the story of the city to you, of the star children, the spawn of Cthulu, followed him through the infinite blackness, dreaming of worlds to consume. Some broke free of his all encompassing mind, and found their way into the astral depths, the soupy darkness where stars are born and crushed to death. They mined the stars for their stones, and over millions of generations devised methods to travel the blackness, and to cloak their presence.  For Cthulu demands of all its children that he help him come to be, but if Cthulu could not remember them, then they would be free to forget him as well.
>
> Eventually, they found their way to the other side, in a world of material certainty, populated only by giant reptiles and their dragon gods. The star children stayed there, in the sea, and built the city of Ry'leh, and set about to replace their aging bodies. But Cthulu cannot forget forever, and while he searched and search for his traitorous spawn, a message called to him from the depths, below a cloak of thinnest air, he would find them again, destroy them utterly and impregnate their world to start it all again.

<small>— Dave. Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30</small>

## The city

What stayed with Revan were "the visions of the city, with buildings with walls and roads that meet at impossible angles, unrecognizable forms in the shadows, twisted and incomprehensible architecture leading your mind into spaces with no exit." A glimpse of "a tiny fraction of his body, the Egg" sent him into madness, and he put "a burning hole in Barold's brain" and went "searching for the ones who try to stop you every time". The city was "all built from a slightly glowing green stone, all beneath the ocean, in the darkness, built for the star children, who you see depicted throughout the city." Dave posted three pictures of them. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30)</small> See [the Egg](/places/the-egg/).

## Cthulhu's rebellious children

In May 2020, after the party reached the deep past, Dave set out what they knew: "the Obliviator has been deactivated, allowing the Leviathan to remember the Earth and come for his rebellious children." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-11)</small> See [the Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/).

## The aboleth

On seeing the pictures, LaMont said the party had met one before: "Shit we’ve encountered one of those before. It took control of Kara". Kara's player Jenny: "The star children are Aboleth (aboleths?)" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30)</small> Four months later, when the fish apes charmed Stonecypher, Dave asked Jenny, "yeah Kara remember when you were 'enslaved' by the aboleth?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-29)</small> See [Kara](/people/kara/).

## The Star Child

At the end of time, in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/), the goddess June told Chronocypher of the gods and the father they fled:

> The gods of this realm are the split soul of the Star Child, whose name was Zoth-Ommog. We left our father somewhere deep in the dark, and fled him across all of time. He consumes all worlds in his pursuit of us.

<small>— June. Arc V, Starfall Part VI</small>
