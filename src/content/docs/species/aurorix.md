**Gold dragon · Prisoner of E'noch Qualanthri · Ally of the Inevitables · Dead ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

**Aurorix** was a gold dragon whom [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) kept for years in a pool of blood under the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), bleeding him to make the potion that sustained [Emperor Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/). The Inevitables freed him in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/). He fought beside them through the Summer Palace, carried them to the bell at Nos Matre, and was killed there by the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/), his enemy of six thousand years.

## The pool

Behind the double doors of E'noch's lair, a round pool of blood stood under a wall of soaked rock. Aurorix lay half sunk in it, paralysed and exhausted, with vats around him. His blood was mixed with the blood of mortals into the concoction that kept the Emperor in his torpor. He heard everything said in that room for years. He watched E'noch bring his grandchildren into the chamber and tell them they were next in line once his granddaughter died or failed for the last time.

[Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) woke him during the fight with the vampires. He took a human shape afterwards, a yellow-haired young man, and later passed as a green-robed sister. He said he had been drinking his own blood for years and that it would take a great deal to bring him back to his full form.

## What he told the party

Aurorix named the Emperor to the party as [Viagra](/people/viagra/), the Crimson Sun. He had been there the first time Viagra was killed: *"It was not easy. An entire nation of giants fell in the effort. The elvish people were almost wiped out. Most dragons were wiped out."* He said Viagra made Vol, and that E'noch had turned the Emperor into a vampire and held him on dragon blood while his essence was stolen.

He told them about the Sept of Bones, the temple of an old god of death and renewal buried under the hill. He said that the houses of Vol keep the Mark of Death secret and that all their scions bear it, and he held that every child of that breeding had to die: *"A half-dragon, half-elf with the Mark of Death would mean the end of all things."* He wanted Saturnia dead for her mark. Gemma refused him.

## The Summer Palace

In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) he crossed the Emperor's gardens with the party. In the daughters' hall he called the youngest girls his brood, because they had been made from his blood, and he killed them. In the Emperor's chamber he ran into Uriel's guardians shouting Viagra's name and breathed fire over the burning bed: *"The world came together to destroy you once, Viagra. We will not let you rise again."* When Uriel fled to ring the bell he offered to take the party away from the valley by ship. When they would not go, he took his true shape and carried them.

## Nos Matre

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) he flew the party at the bell with very little strength left. The Original's second toll overcame him. He fell out of the sky, and the Choir of Novices stabbed him to death on the terrazzo. Eric the Cleric called him back with a diamond after Uriel had been beheaded.

> You've saved me, Priest of Maths, and you have saved us all.

He told them that Uriel could not be dead, and that she must be in the Sept of Bones. A few moments later Eric summoned Valtrex to help.

> No. You have summoned my mortal enemy. You fool. Get off of me.

The two dragons went for each other's throats and took no more part in the battle. Valtrex killed him.

## Fate

Aurorix died at Nos Matre. His body was in the valley when it was destroyed.
