**Elven people · The Astral Sea · Indifferent to hostile toward [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) · Extant**

**Astral elves** are an elven people of the Astral Sea, a vast race spread across many star systems, mostly organised and ranging from indifferent to hostile toward [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/). They are among the enemies of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) and the antagonists of [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). The astral elf druid [Caprica](/people/caprica/) left the traditional life of her people to join Astral Command and became pilot of the SSJ Zanzibar; astral elves nearly killed her and her companions twice in their first session, and an astral elf honor guard stood at the climax of the first season.

## Society

Astral elves are a vast race spread across many systems, all of whom range from indifferent to hostile toward Astral Command. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> They are mostly organised and unfriendly. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Their racial politics are not uniform: there are different factions and different types. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-29)</small> Dave compared them to Spock, and to Star Trek's Romulans and Vulcans. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-29; 2022-10-10)</small>

Many astral elves are star druids who live the traditional way of their people. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> A distant Astral Elvish empire exists. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

## Ships

The **Star Moth** is the signature ship of the astral elves. Astral elves will attack non-elves who fly one. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

## Relations with Astral Command

Astral elf officers in Astral Command are rarer than officers of the races found on terrestrial planets. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> The prologue to Caprica's first session describes the distrust that met her:

> "Despite the distrust of allowing a potential double agent of some distant Astral Elvish empire to gain access to a Sovereign Spelljammer, due to your exemplary record and reputations, you were offered 1st or 2nd Helm on basically any vessel you wanted."
>
> <small>— Prologue to Session 0/1 (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

In the first session of *Light of Xaryxis*, played 2022-10-21, astral elves nearly killed the party twice. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-22)</small> At the climax of the first season, on the "Infanta Sigil" map, the party faced an astral elf honor guard and Xeleth. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-02)</small>

## Notable astral elves

- [Caprica](/people/caprica/), a druid of the Circle of Stars with the Astral Drifter background, pilot and science officer of the SSJ Zanzibar <small>(character sheet)</small>
