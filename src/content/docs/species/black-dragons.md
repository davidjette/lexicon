**Species · Khyber and the deep places · No specimen recorded in over four hundred and thirty years**

**Black dragons** are a chromatic dragon species of Khyber and the deep places. Three descend from the First, all driven underground when Khyber formed the Manifest Zones and all three presumed destroyed; no black dragon has been recorded in over four hundred and thirty years, and all named dragon records have been removed from the Imperial archive at Korth. The youngest, **Valtrex**, was the only one small enough to move through the narrow ways. He held a treaty with the Deep Tempura Clan under the Korramont for generations, was collared with a bell and bled for his blood by the Empire for three years in a flooded cavern beneath the Korranberg Foundry, and was freed by the Inevitables, who left him circling the peak and claiming the mountain as his domain. What the Inevitables know of the species comes from two sittings: one with Valtrex chained at the black lake, and one with the loremaster Master Armhair in the censored Hall of Records at Korth.

## THE HALL OF RECORDS AT KORTH — MASTER ARMHAIR'S ACCOUNT

An elderly half-elf loremaster of the Hall, approached by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) after she overheard scholars discussing a black dragon over the Korramont. His account, given to a stranger who had admitted to standing before one and speaking with it:

- **No black dragon has been recorded in over four centuries.** Four hundred and thirty years and more.
- **All named dragon records have been removed from the archive.** Not lost — removed.
- **Three black dragons descend from the First**, all presumed destroyed.
- All were **driven underground when Khyber formed the Manifest Zones**.
- **Valtrex, the youngest, was the only one small enough to move through the narrow ways.**
- **Dragon blood is an alchemical reagent of extraordinary power.** Armhair added this unasked.

<small>Armhair's confiscated manuscript, recovered under a false index, carries a dragon family tree traced back to the progenitors and an identification of Valtrex's father — a thread that runs on to the [Mark of Death](/lore/the-mark-of-death-and-the-dragonmarks/), [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/)'s birth, and the vanished progenitor he calls [Viagra](/people/viagra/). He set out for the Korramont to finish the book, expecting to die there.</small>

## THE DWARVEN RELIEFS — RAK'TAFÛR, UNDER THE KORRAMONT

Masterwork carvings in the stair chamber of the Deep Tempura Clan record the other half of the history: King Corn discovering the Glitterhame, forging infernal ore under the guidance of a horned devil, and later **striking a peace treaty with the black dragon Valtrex**. Later panels show Corn bringing him tribute, and the arrangement held for generations. Then the Empire came and enslaved the clan in their own halls; by Valtrex's account the duergar let the Empire into his lair, and by doing so violated the pact.

<small>**Valtrex** is the canon name. Published recaps and the reliefs' earlier transcription carry **Vuldrach**, which remains valid as historical record.</small>

## THE ANIMAL — VALTREX AT THE BLACK LAKE

Found alive, airborne, insane and **wearing a bell locked around his neck** in the flooded cavern beneath the foundry. On the far platform stood an Imperial rig built to harpoon, restrain and drain him: the Empire had been bleeding the dragon for three years and shipping the blood out by rail. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) teleported onto his back and shattered the collar with an adamantine mace; freed, Valtrex destroyed the rig himself, and the madness lifted with the bell.

What the species is capable of, observed: flight in an enclosed cavern; acid, in water that smelled of it and stone that reeked; drag marks described as *large and reptilian*; *speak with dead* cast on the crowned corpse atop his own hoard; fluent negotiation in Draconic; and a hoard that included the Crown of Seven Stars and the black-Khyber warhammer KNELL. He demanded total domain over the mountain and the servitude of the Teryaki clan, took a Ring of Lesser Wish for the hammer and the crown, destroyed the original bell moulds himself, and ordered the Inevitables never to return.

## HIS TESTIMONY

Given freely, once he was no longer insane, and it is the single richest lore payload in the archive:

- The elvish houses that fled after the purge of [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/) remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline.
- **Erandis Vol bore a child by a dragon called the Crimson Sun.**
- [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) has used his stolen blood to somehow sire her own heirs — **which makes him their father**.
- The Crown of Seven Stars represents seven pledges made to a devil whose name cannot be spoken.

<small>"The Crimson Sun" names a dragon here and the Empire's secret service elsewhere. Both usages are live; the connection is unresolved and is preserved rather than settled. Gemma's patron had warned her the day before: *"Never negotiate with an insane dragon."*</small>

## Habitat and Range

Driven underground when Khyber formed the Manifest Zones, and confined since to the deep places — the narrow ways, and the flooded cavern beneath the Korramont. Valtrex nested on the Deep Tempura treasure beneath the Korranberg Foundry for as long as the clan's records run, and the dwarves who went down to him after the pact broke did not come back. He has since taken the surface: he circles the peak and claims the Korramont as his domain, and the Teryaki kin who refused to evacuate remain there under him, hoping to renew the pact.

## Interaction With Other Species

**Dwarves:** treaty, then centuries of peace, then a broken pact and a slaughter neither side caused. **Elves:** the old war against the dragons produced arcane harmonics — the elvish weapon Bofric identified in King Corn's melody, which suppresses a bell's influence entirely while it plays. **The Empire:** a collar, a harpoon rig, and three years of blood on the rail. **The Inevitables:** freed him, armed themselves out of his hoard, warned an entire clan to run, and left when he told them to.
