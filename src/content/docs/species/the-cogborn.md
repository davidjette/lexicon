**The industrial underclass of Sharn's Lower City · Homeland: The Cogs · Displaced · Evacuated to Gorgonhorn**

*Also known as:* the Cogborn · the Cogs (of their people, collectively) · "the base" (their own chant)

The Cogborn are the industrial underclass of Sharn's Lower City: generations of workers who built and ran the city's industrial heart in the Cogs, lived and worked in harsh conditions beneath its infrastructure, and were counted by the rebellion as a people distinct from both the Warforged and the human sympathisers who joined them. Over the years that followed the outlawing of the Warforged, the Iron Veil's seizure of the factories and the disappearances worked by the Cog Collector, the community was driven out of its jobs, its neighbourhoods and its homes, and was finally evacuated through the Underhive to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/). <small>What the Cogborn are as a species is not stated in any source. The Gear Street Markets that serve them sell [chip implants and grafts](/lore/implants-and-chip-enhancement/); whether the Cogborn are defined by them is not recorded.</small>

## The Cogs

The Cogs form the industrial slum of the Lower City, southwest, built around a narrow waterway and ringed by ageing factories and processing plants. Smoke, steam and machine noise fill the air at all hours. Cogborn homes are improvised structures welded directly onto the sides of the factories — stacked shanties, patched platforms and rooms clinging to pipes, vents and maintenance walkways. The neighbourhood is dense, dangerous, and absolutely vital to Sharn's mechanical heartbeat.

Their commerce is Gear Street Markets to the south: scrap vendors, food stalls, arcane tinkerers, and the largest building on the street, **Circuit City**. Their arena is Breaker's Pit under the Ziggurat. Their clinic is Graft & Gear. Their aqueduct is a boundary, a landmark, and a way out.

Most Cogborn work in the factories and water plants, live on the southwest side of the Lower City in shacks built on top of their workplaces, and are human, though other races live among them. Years of smoke, steam and metal dust have given many of them milky white eyes, an adaptation to the harsh, low-light environment. Accidents are frequent and missing limbs are common, and most Cogborn who lose a limb cannot afford a prosthetic; [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) has a prosthetic arm. Younger Cogborn have been able to make lives outside the neighbourhood. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-22)</small>

## The Collapse

Cogborn life in Sharn was destroyed in stages, and each stage has a name.

**The Warforged decree.** When the Lord Commander outlawed all Warforged in Sharn, most fled toward Gorgonhorn or the Mournlands. The Warforged had shouldered the most dangerous industrial tasks — the ones that kill flesh-and-blood workers — and their removal put those jobs back onto Cogborn bodies already weakened by decades of hazardous work and malnutrition. One Warforged stayed: **Preacher**, who had been raised in the Cogs and considered the Cogborn family, and who went into the undercity with his adopted daughter **Misty** rather than leave.

**The Iron Veil takeover.** The Veil seized the factories and replaced Cogborn labour with trained soldiers. Workers were pushed out of their jobs, then their neighbourhoods, then their homes; many fled beneath the Cogs, where collapsing tunnels and abandoned sub-transit lines became the last refuge.

**The disappearances.** For nearly four years Cogborn went missing across the Lower City; early victims were found dumped around Gear Street Market, the east side of the Aqueduct and the Lowline Ward, and later the killings became pure vanishings. The Cogborn gave the killer a name — the **Cog Collector** — and had no idea it was Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), the polite shopkeeper on Gear Street who owned the largest implant parlour there, taking labourers off their own streets, experimenting on them, harvesting their organs, and shipping their [Umbra](/items/umbra/)-prepped bodies up a private skylift into [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/).

**The riot.** A peaceful Upper City protest became a deadly riot when an explosion tore through the crowd. R.U.S.T. lost twenty-one members; the Cogborn community lost forty-three civilians — workers, elders, parents, children. The Iron Veil's retaliation was immediate and used the chaos as justification to flood the district with soldiers.

**The broadcast.** Every screen in Sharn turned red, and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) executed Cogborn prisoners live from the deck of her warship, in armour, so that the whole city would understand what dissent cost.

## What They Said About It

On the grand staircase outside POSÉ, while the Empire's officers drank inside, the crowd was chanting:

> **Leader:** "We are the Cogs!"\
> **Crowd:** "We are the base!"\
> **Leader:** "Bodies gone missing!"\
> **Crowd:** "Name every face!"\
> **Leader:** "Where are our people?"\
> **Crowd:** "Gone without trace!"

Shots came from inside the protest and killed the news anchor Candice Kurt on the balcony. The official story blamed a group of protesters from the Cogs.

## R.U.S.T.

What began as scattered acts of survival — smuggling families through forgotten tunnels, hiding fugitives in abandoned rail lines — became a network of Cogborn, Warforged and human sympathisers. R.U.S.T. was never a revolutionary movement. Preacher is explicit about it: he does not seek to liberate Sharn, he seeks to save the Cogborn still trapped beneath it.

- **Preacher** — Warforged, founder, raised in the Cogs. The last of his kind in the city.
- **Misty Kross** — Cogborn engineer, Preacher's adopted daughter, pregnant; keeps the pawn-shop hub and repairs machinery older than Sharn.
- **Kriss Kross** — logistics and security; married into the network. Son of [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/).
- **DJ Rust** — Cogborn family, raised in the Lantern District; passes coded messages through his shows.
- **Doppler Klink** — Cogborn tinkerer and Last War veteran, Gear Street stall. Left Sharn with his people.
- **Snodgrass** — goliath ranger raised among Cogborn; hunts sewer gators to feed families and guides them through the tunnels.
- **Chris Understall** — Cogborn elder, found on the far side of a cave-in.

## The Exodus

With the Cogs overrun by the Iron Veil, the Cogborn hid in the tunnels beneath the district, but both tunnels out were blocked: one on purpose, because of "dangerous stuff on the other side", and one by a cave-in. Either could lead to the Underhive if Blair allowed it. The community had lost many of its capable fighters in the riot, and many of those left were sick and weak. Getting them out of the city was R.U.S.T.'s first priority. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-03)</small>

The route out was assembled piece by piece and it ran through everything the Empire had abandoned. [Blair](/people/blair/), Queen of the Underhive, agreed to open her routes in exchange for the clearing of the Drain Snakes and a secured passage toward the mountain pass. Berenstain, Snodgrass and Doppler dug out a collapsed tunnel. The Inevitables cleared an ancient chamber where Blair's previous suitors had vanished.

Then the community walked: through the **Spine**, down the underhive river, through the ancient chamber past a defeated stone golem, and out to R.U.S.T. and the mountain tunnel. Preacher and the RUST Runners met them with song and handed out prayer sheets, praying to the love of something called **Lexicon**. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) teleported in once the people were clear, thanked the party, and gave each of them a RUST communication necklace. Doppler Klink chose to leave Sharn and go with his people.

Their destination is [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/), a Cogborn and Warforged stronghold on the border of the Mournlands, where under Locke Pierce a blended society has begun to take shape — strong enough to heal, grow, and one day resist the Empire on its own terms.

After the exodus the Cogborn's biggest fight is against the [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units east of Gorgonhorn in the Mournlands, which [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) mainly fend off. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-31)</small>

## What Remained

Six severely ill Cogborn were pulled out of the cages behind Circuit City and handed to the RUST Runners. Others never got out at all: in the flooded chambers beneath the Cogs, on the way to the Forge of Binding, the party fought **infected Cogborn** alongside a young aboleth and, deeper still, a mind flayer. Those were people, once, and nobody has said whose.
