**Black dragon (elder) - Lord of the Korramont - Unaligned to any faction - Alive**

*Also known as:* Vuldrach (the older spelling, carved on the Grand Stair and used in the published record) - Voldrak - Vondrak - Valtrex the Putrid

**Valtrex** is an elder black dragon, the youngest of the three descended from the First and the only one small enough to move through the narrow ways. He held a peace treaty with the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) under [the Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) for generations, until the duergar let the Empire into his lair; the Empire locked a bell around his neck, which kept him insane, and bled him on a harpoon rig above [the black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) for three years, shipping the blood out by rail. The Inevitables shattered the collar, and he destroyed the rig himself, gave them his testimony on the blood of Vol and the Crown of Seven Stars, broke the original bell moulds, and ordered them never to return. He now circles the peak as **Valtrex the Putrid** and claims the Korramont as his domain.

## Physical description

An elder black dragon, old and frail, with one eye gashed open, a horn snapped off, wings torn through, and a line of ballista scars running the length of his flank. He can still fly, and he covers eighty feet a round when he does. His breath is acid and his lair raises the water at his will. For three years he wore a bell on a sealed chain around his neck, heavy enough to weigh his head down and locked so that he could not reach it.

## What the record says about his kind

No black dragon has been recorded anywhere in Khorvaire in over four centuries. All named dragon records have been removed from the Imperial archives. The loremaster **Master Armhair**, in the Hall of Records at Korth, gives the surviving version: three black dragons descend from the First, all three presumed destroyed, all driven underground when Khyber formed the Manifest Zones. Valtrex was the youngest, and the only one small enough to move through the narrow ways. Armhair's confiscated manuscript names his father. Dragon blood, Armhair adds without being asked, is an alchemical reagent of extraordinary power.

## The treaty

The right-hand relief on the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/) shows [King Corn](/people/king-corn/) standing before a coiled drake with his arms open: a peace treaty between the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) and the dragon under their mountain. Panels further along show Corn bringing him tribute. The arrangement held for generations. Valtrex's position on how it ended is unambiguous: the duergar let the Empire into his lair, and by doing so they violated the pact.

## Three years on the rig

On the far platform above the [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) the Empire built a machine, in the same pulley-and-scaffold idiom as the bell rigs upstairs: a harpoon, enormous kettles, and a frame sized precisely to hold a dragon's wings still. The bell on his neck kept him insane and kept him roaring; the rig took his blood; the rail that officially never existed took the blood out of the mountain. The Sisters ran it, and he heard them talk while they worked - about the Empress's fruitfulness, among other things.

His only warning to the world arrived before anyone met him, through [Gemma Corso's](/people/gemma-corso/) patron: *"Never negotiate with an insane dragon."*

## The collar

He ambushed the party out of the dark at the lake, opening with a vitriolic sphere and the line *"Long down the ringing of the bell, the queen of death has come. We are hopeless."* What blunted him was not damage. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) stood on a broken dwarven bridge in front of a black dragon and played the fanfare of dawn, and the dragon lost a contest of will to a runesmith with a horn. Valtrex, landing to kill him: *"Stop that infernal racket. I hate jazz."*

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) misty-stepped onto his back and brought an adamantine mace down on the chain. It exploded in white light and the bell fell to the floor and rang as it landed. Then the dragon understood what had happened to him.

> How dare you? What have you done? How did you do this? You've stopped the ringing.

He bellowed, and then he began to laugh with relief. He flew to the harpoon rig and destroyed it himself - acid over the kettles and the frame, and the rest smashed by hand - and then reached down into the acid pool where [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was submerged and said *"Give me your hand,"* and pulled him out.

## The bargain

Once sane, he opened negotiations by naming his own terms unprompted: the descendants of the oath-breakers will serve him, and he asked the party to lead the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/)'s most revered and powerful down to him under a guise of peace, so that he could consume them and enslave the rest. Nobody accepted. Nobody refused either.

Dario spoke to him in Draconic, laid a **Ring of Lesser Wish** on the hoard, and lifted **KNELL** out of the crowned corpse's arms along with the **Crown of Seven Stars**. The hammer and the crown came back borrowed, and Valtrex was clear whose property they were: *"That hammer doesn't belong to King Corn. It belongs to me. So does his crown."*

Dave's account of how the trade was won: "Corn gave you his blessing when he spoke with dead because you helped his ghost", and "freeing him made him go from hostile to indifferent, and then good persuasion rolls plus the trade plus the endorsement of Corns ghost let him part with the hammer". On what the party gained: "And you added a potentially ally I suppose" / "A lot more powerful than a bunch of useless duergar". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## The testimony

Given one chance to ask him anything, the party got the deepest piece of lore anyone in the campaign has yet been handed:

- The elvish houses that fled to Khorvaire after the purge of House Vol - the Qualanthri among them - remained obsessed with the blood of Vol.
- **Erandis Vol mated with a dragon.** Its true name is unknown; some have called it [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/). The child of that union, half dragon and half elf, bore the apex Mark of Death. The house was destroyed for it - but not before it tried to destroy all dragonkind.
- The Sisters drained him for three years and shipped it out on its own rail line, and he heard them speak of the Empress's fruitfulness. His conclusion: *"If she is using my blood to create these heirs, then I am their father."*
- *"I will destroy those children if you do not. This is my prerogative. It is the Dova way."*
- On the crown: *"Corn made seven pledges to that devil. Be warned."* On the devil's name: *"Speaking his name is to summon him."*

Then he broke open the lead-lined case that held the original bell moulds and kicked them into the acid.

> I will never give mortals the power of these bells. They were designed purely to deter us and destroy our empires.

## Beliefs

He believes the bells are an elvish weapon built to break dragon empires, and that no mortal should ever hold one.

He believes the Teryaki inherited their ancestors' broken oath along with their ancestors' hold, and that the debt is collectable in persons.

He believes Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s heirs are his own get, and that killing them is a father's right rather than a favour to anyone.

## Conduct

He speaks Draconic and resents being addressed in anything else - *"You dare speak to me in anything but the tongue of the Dovah"* - hoards jealously, and issues warnings rather than threats: *"Touch nothing else or I will swallow you and shit you out."* Roughly ten thousand gold and a quantity of silver ingots were left untouched on the hoard, and so was the crowned corpse.

## Right now

Valtrex is free, sane, hostile and above ground. The party's last sight of him was from the treeline: a black dragon circling the peak of the Korramont on torn wings, declaring the domain of **Valtrex the Putrid**. Scholars in the Hall of Records at Korth are already discussing a black dragon over the mountain, four centuries after the last one was recorded. Master Armhair has gone up there with a cursed crown as a gift, expecting to die. *"Do not return. This is now my domain."*

**Organizations / Groups:** None. Party to the broken Deeptempura treaty; a potential ally against the Ivory Lazaret and an evil black dragon, both of which are true at once.

**Holdings:** The [black lake](/places/the-black-lake/) and its hoard; [the Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) entire, by declaration.

**Items:** King Corn's crowned remains; a Ring of Lesser Wish; the destroyed bell moulds, at the bottom of the acid.
