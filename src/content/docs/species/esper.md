**Race · *The Starsong Awakens* · Psychic · Silver-eyed**

The **Esper** are a race of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, easily recognised by their shining silver eyes and watchful gaze, whose innate psychic powers of telepathy, telekinesis and precognition make them valuable allies and dangerous enemies throughout Wildspace, the Astral Sea and beyond. Their origins are unknown. [The Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) breeds and clones Esper at [the Garden](/places/the-garden/), a facility on Lathander VII in Clusterspace, which has grown and trained more than 1,500 of them. A very rare potion, the Elixir of Esper Tears, grants the psychic power of the Esper to whoever drinks it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race; Company dossier; New magic items)</small>

JL published the Esper as a playable race on his campaign site in June 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-08)</small>

## Origins

The origins of the Esper are shrouded in mystery. Some claim they are mutated elves; others believe they were born from the phlogiston itself. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race)</small>

## Description

Esper are about the same size and build as elves. They have shining silver eyes, four fingers on each hand and four toes on each foot. All Esper are intersex, and many choose to present as male or female. They are sterile and cannot reproduce sexually. Esper young and old are found across the galaxy, yet they do not seem to age naturally. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race)</small>

## Abilities

Esper are deeply attuned to the psionic energy that surrounds and penetrates all things, and they see in darkness and dim light out to 60 feet, though only in shades of grey. They resist psychic damage and are hard to take over with mind control magic. Every Esper knows three cantrips, *friends*, *message* and *mind sliver*, and casts them through the intellect alone, with no words, gestures or materials; after casting one, an Esper needs no food or drink for a day. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race)</small>

As player characters, Esper are Medium, walk 30 feet, choose where their ability score increases fall, and speak, read and write Common and two other languages. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race)</small>

## The Garden

*Main article: [The Garden](/places/the-garden/)*

The Company built Lathander VII, once known as the Ice Hell of the Lathander system, into a breeding and cloning facility for Esper, called the Garden. In the Company dossier the Garden has grown and trained more than 1,500 Esper "to aid and support our Companywide efforts". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## Elixir of Esper Tears

The Elixir of Esper Tears is a very rare potion, clear and watery, that grants the psychic power of the Esper. For one minute after drinking it, the drinker has advantage on Intelligence-based ability checks, among them Arcana, History, Investigation, Nature and Religion, and on all saving throws. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New magic items)</small> The campaign site spells it "Elixer".
