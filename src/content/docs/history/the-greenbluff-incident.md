**Mining disaster and relic recovery · Greenbluff camp, outside Newham · about ten years before the Inevitables**

The **Greenbluff incident** is the mining disaster and relic recovery at the Greenbluff camp outside Newham, about ten years before the Inevitables. Empire-owned slaves died in a deep gallery and every survivor went mad in the same shape, describing a sound in the workings that shook the roof down. [The Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) attributed the incident to an ancient relic and sent a retrieval party — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — who fought the risen dead and a rebel column, uncovered a buried chamber at the bottom of the mine and carried the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) out of it. The bell went east by lightning rail under Assembly seal. The camp has been shut since.

Greenbluff was a played-out mining camp and is now a place where nothing roots. Everything that follows in the Empire's history — the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/), the Houses of Mercy, the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) bell network, the Eternal Nightfall — begins in one buried room at the bottom of that mine. Three accounts of the incident survive. They agree on the facts and differ entirely on what the facts meant.

## ASSEMBLY DISPATCH — THE INCIDENT

An incident took place in the mine taking the lives of a number of Empire-owned slaves. The survivors were recorded as having gone mad. Every one of them, mad in the same shape: they described a sound, deep in the workings, that shook the walls violently enough to bring down the gallery roof.

[The Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) concluded that the incident related to an ancient relic last attested in that region, and moved with a speed that tells you they already knew what they were looking for. Hamen Maw of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) ordered a retrieval party: the orphaned squire [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), the Black Dove acolyte [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), and Magistrate [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). They were to meet an Ancient Relic Specialist sent up from the Rekkenmark: [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/).

## FIELD REPORT — THE DESCENT

In the mine the group fought the risen dead, and then a rebel column that had come for the bell before them. Beneath both they uncovered a buried chamber that had held the thing in silence for centuries. They took it.

The legend attached to the artifact runs so: a Celestial Queen buried the bell centuries ago. Its craftsmen were ancient elves, who fused the bell from an infernal metal. It emitted horrific tones that woke the dead, and those who stood close to the ringing went mad. The Queen did not destroy it.

## NOTES FROM THE PROGENITOR

Locke Pierce, writing years later from Mt. Silicon under the title Progenitor, is the only participant to have set the day down as a beginning rather than an assignment.

> I first stood in a room with Uriel Qualanthri at the bottom of the Greenbluff mine, and we carried the Fatanya Nyel out of it together. Every catastrophe in this file walked out of that room with us.

His judgement on the Queen is the coldest line in the file: *The Queen did not destroy it. I have come to believe she could not.* By his assessment the Greenbluff bell is the only true Fantanya Nyel; everything hanging in a House of Mercy tonight is a copy cast in the last decade at the Empress's expense, and the copies do not need to be strong. They only need to resonate the mother bell's call. He suspects there are hundreds.

## THE GROUND AFTERWARD

Fulcrum kept a scrap from a miner who was there, and has kept it since the beginning.

> Camp's been shut since the madness in the deep gallery. Ground's still wrong — nothing roots. Whatever they took out of there in my father's time went east on the rail under Imperial seal, and men in fine coats came up from Korth to ride with it. There's no bell at Greenbluff now. Greenbluff is where the bell is FROM.

## WHAT THE ROOM PRODUCED

The four who went down were strangers with separate commissions. They came up with a shared secret and, later, a name for themselves. [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) was formally sworn later, at Arden Crest, but Greenbluff is where the four of them were first in a room together with something worth betraying each other for.

The bell went east by lightning rail under Assembly seal. It did not arrive quietly — see [the lightning rail ambush](/history/the-lightning-rail-ambush/). About ten years afterward, in a shipping crate inside the Black Dove hospital in Sharn, the rebellion found a replica of it and finally understood what the last decade of church-building had been for.

**Dating:** Greenbluff falls at the first and second sessions of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) campaign, about ten years before the Inevitables. The written sources spread wider: the Fulcrum cover letter puts Greenbluff at **twenty years ago**; the Council of Sharn records the Unforeseen's Greenbluff recovery as **thirteen years** before the fall of Sharn, which by the same reckoning is roughly sixteen years before the present. Both figures stand in the sources as written. <small>(In the master chronology the Eberron campaigns — The Unforeseen, Sharn and Korth — sit after the Infantaverse and Dead Man's Hand era and before Starfall: The Next Generation.)</small>
