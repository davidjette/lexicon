**Plot · Campaign 1, The Unforeseen · Sharn, the Interoperability Summit · Crimson Sun operation, four nights and three days**

*Also known as:* **"The Trains Will Run On Time"** <small>(the heading of the Campaign 1 record)</small> · continued in [City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/).

City of Knives is a [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) operation of Campaign 1, The Unforeseen, run over four nights and three days at the Interoperability Summit in Sharn. It opens in the basement of the Rekkenmark, where [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) has downloaded State Secret material naming the killer of [Esther Crona's](/people/esther-crona/) master and cannot decrypt it, and continues through a period of downtime at Rekkenmark East. Hayman Maw and Fanta Rhodes then brief the four of them at a Crimson Sun safehouse in Sharn: top secret and deliberately false summit intelligence available only to the Imperial Assembly has been leaked to rebel sources, and the suspects are Lord Gregory Goldenhew, Lord Albert Spear and Minister Henry Heighrick. The Unforeseen are to investigate, expose and neutralise the mole before the summit ends. The operation continues in [City of Knives, Pt II](/lore/city-of-knives-pt-ii/).

## The standoff before it

The operation begins in the basement of the **Rekkenmark**, with [Esther](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and [Locke](/people/locke-pierce/) in a standoff over what Locke has just done. He has downloaded something. He knows who killed Esther's master and cannot decrypt it, because the information sits behind State Secret encryption and only an Assembly member or higher holds the phrase that opens it.

Every member of the Assembly and the higher Imperial command holds a **personal encryption key** to their files on the **Rekkenmark mainframe**. The key must be delivered precisely and in the owner's own voice. To accept one, Locke must enter a *login state* in which he is incapacitated and can present the encrypted material to the permissioned user. Only the highest-ranking officers and top-secret projects are worthy of record on the mainframe — but much of the intelligence the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) collects is stored there for analysis.

Locke then established that **with every personal encryption key he collects, he unlocks more and more of the Empire's secrets — secrets nobody else can reach.** From that point the party's standing quest ran through the Imperial Assembly one member at a time.

<small>Uriel takes samples from Dr. Marble's body on the way out.</small>

## Commendation, or condemnation

The Empire's response to Locke depends entirely on whether he admits what he did. If he discloses the download, he is declared **Imperial property** and ordered into full seclusion for processing — enforced, if he runs, by four Crimson Sun agents, **Hayman Maw** himself, and a squad of city guards. If he submits and convinces Maw he can be trusted, he is allowed to proceed at **Rekkenmark East** under Esther Crona's supervision. Maw's instruction in that case is exact: notify him the instant the records crack, and say **absolutely nothing to Esther** if anything about [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) surfaces.

If Locke lies successfully, or simply never mentions it, the team is commended for stopping Marble and the undead in the Rekkenmark, ordered to keep the incident top secret, and told to report to their commanders in the morning.

## The public reporting

What Khorvaire was told afterwards, item by item:

- **Magnolia Steele** steps down from the Imperial Assembly and as Chancellor of the Rekkenmark. No replacement is named for either position.
- **Gabrielle Duin is missing**, and blame for her assassination is placed on [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/). Glow was morbidly injured during her arrest and has been sentenced to live out the remainder of her life in medical confinement at Newham Hospital. <small>(Rebel-side accounts of what became of Lady Glow do not match this.)</small>
- At least a dozen other [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) commanders and associates are found killed or reported missing over the following weeks. Some are believed to have fled to join the Rebellion.
- The Empire publicly acknowledges armed rebels inside the Mournlands, and dismisses them as minor local warlords disputing paltry resources, beyond the worry of any Imperial citizen.
- A **no-go zone** is declared over a substantial portion of the Dragon Isles after continued out-of-control piracy. The Empire has not enforced its will in those waters, and the Valenar elves are furious at the collapse in trade.

## Downtime

<small>From the Campaign 1 DM document. Locke's findings were gated by investigation rolls: 15 for the Imperial report, 20 for the internal memos, 25 for Project Godseed and 30 for Duin's letters. Items marked "a quest for another day" were prep.</small>

Before his resignation, Chancellor Steele permitted Locke and Uriel to relocate **Dr. Marble's research in autoanimata and necrocryptography** to Rekkenmark East with supplemental support from the **UN-DEAD** <small>(so written in the Campaign 1 record; Uriel's Newham department is the U.N.D.E.A.D.)</small> Together they analysed the nature of **D4AMB**, the history of the Warforged, and the composition of **Embrace**.

**Uriel** worked on the magical nature of blood. She synthesised a small store of Embrace from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble, which led her to theorise that **the sole source of Embrace is the blood of vampires**. She set out to collect vampires she could enslave and harvest for an ample supply.

**Locke** decoded Imperial encryption. The files on the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) sat at a lower encryption level than the Imperial secrets and he cracked several: the Imperial report on the cult, the internal memos admitting the Emperor agrees with it, **Project Godseed**, and the **personal letters of Gabrielle Duin**.

**Esther** trained with her sword and heard nothing from it for weeks. Then one day, in the instant she let it go, it took her.

> Crona stands before you, scowling in pain. The walls disappear and a storm sky floods your perception, cracks and bolts all around. He points out into the darkness. A burgeoning cloud expands at a geometric rate, engulfing the horizon. You hear the tone of a bell, as clear as day. Your ears pop from the sound, it's excrutiating. Crona's mouth fills with blood. His neck opens and spills it on the floor. You slip in its warmth and fall. It smears your hands and you touch your face. You look up. The sky has no rain, only thunder and heat. The cloud washes over you. You smell death.

The sword clangs to the floor with blood on its edge. In the mirror her face is still covered in fresh blood, and there is a heavy trickle from a gash the blade opened along a vein in her hand. She bandages it with a medical kit stolen from Uriel's office weeks earlier and sips a healing potion. It tastes like lavender. It reminds her of Varyn again. She looks in the mirror. He isn't there.

**Richard Blaze** comes by for dinner one night and mentions he is moving to Sharn for a while, opening a new yakitori concept called *"Blaze on a Stick"*.

**LeBeefe**, if he assisted Uriel, could work with her on capturing a vampire to make a batch of Embrace for the Tabby PC network: "Possibly a quest for another day." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-15)</small>

## The trains will run on time

[LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) attends the annual Council of Mayors at Thronehold — a largely ceremonial meeting that gives him time to visit the city's famous brothels and little else. At the end of his one compulsory committee session a gavel comes down and Lord Grey announces that LeBeefe will represent the Eastern Provinces at the **Interoperability Summit at Sharn** next month, with instructions to negotiate standards that reinforce Imperial rail investment. The committee rejects the gnomes' demand for higher gauge rails outright: if Sharn and the other border cities are to function, the notion is to be put to rest. Meeting adjourned.

Later that day a carriage pulls up to LeBeefe on his way home from a restaurant with **Hayman Maw** inside. Maw takes him to a Crimson Sun safehouse disguised as an exclusive hotel near the diplomatic district, through magical fields that scan for surveillance and teleportation magic. Locke, Esther and Uriel are already there. All four have been offered a 2,000 gold advance for expenses. Waiting with them in the lounge are **Chase Wexler**, a half-elven Crimson Sun initiate; **Politus deSmack** of the Imperial diplomatic service; and **Fournier**, a Warforged of the Trade Federation signal corps who can cast *sending* at will.

**Fanta Rhodes** and Hayman Maw emerge. Maw speaks. Rhodes says nothing at all.

> "There is a mole on the Imperial Assembly. We know this because top secret information available only to the Assembly was leaked to rebel sources last week. This information was purposefully false, and it regarded the upcoming Interoperability Summit at Sharn. The assembly members attending that Summit are Lord Gregory Goldenhew, Lord Albert Spear, and Minister Henry Heighrick." — Hayman Maw

Also attending: representatives of Houses [Orien](/people/kwanti-d-orien/) and Lyrandar, and the local governments and trade authorities of Eastern Sharn. The four of them are to discover which and how many of the Assembly members at the summit are leaking to the rebels, establish evidence, and — on confirmation — **render, interrogate and neutralise them before the summit ends**. They are assigned by Imperial decree to Station Chief **Fred Walker**, a changeling bard of the fourteenth level, who will supply the resources. The mission is top secret; they are authorised to use any means to keep it so; and if captured or killed, all knowledge of the operation will be disavowed. Success pays 10,000 gold on top of the advance.

**Four nights, three days.** Operations run out of Tradefair Station, a warehouse and materials depot serving as Crimson Sun headquarters. Dark clouds over Sharn. Looks like rain.
