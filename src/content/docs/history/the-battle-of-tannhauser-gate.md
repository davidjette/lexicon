**Battle · [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) · Fifteen years before *The Starsong Awakens* · Location unknown**

*Also known as:* Tannhauser Gate · the crisis at Tannhauser Gate

The **Battle of Tannhauser Gate** was a battle of the [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) era, fought fifteen years before the events of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. In the Company dossier, [Caprica](/people/caprica/) was last seen during the battle at the helm of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), spelljamming the ship to the Eye of Doom, and [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) afterwards seized its wreck. [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/) was held as the battle's traitor, [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) was awarded the Medal of Valorous Service and declined it, and hairs of [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) were recovered after it. A year later [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/). Reforms that followed the battle made Admiral [Timothee Buckets](/people/timothee-buckets/) Sovereign Supreme, Astral Command introduced its Istus-class vessels, and Company artificers first detected the sonic disruption called the T.U.N.E.

## Opening narration

JL opened *The Starsong Awakens* with a narration, marked on his campaign site to be read in the voice of Stellan Skarsgård. It counts fifteen years since the crew of the Zanzibar solved the riddle of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), and fifteen years since the battle:

> It's been 15 years since the crew of the Zanzibar solved the riddle of the Shrike… 15 years since the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, where the line that separates good from evil was exposed. But who cared to notice? And what has changed in those 15 years? Many good people died that day, and many wicked people prospered. Such is the way of things. But nothing ever stays the same.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Home</small>

## The battle

The Company dossier, which Barrias Ruby-Eye's sister [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/) gives the crew, records the battle in several of its entries. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small>

**Caprica.** Lt. Caprica was last seen at the helm of the SSJ Zanzibar, spelljamming the vessel to the Eye of Doom during the battle. The Company calculates the probability of her survival at zero and lists her as deceased. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The Eye of Doom is the vortex left in place of the sun of [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-04)</small>

**Large Luigi.** The dossier names Large Luigi, prisoner LL9807, as "the traitor of the Battle of Tannhauser Gate". Held as an enemy combatant without protection under the Negroni Accords, he has awaited trial for high treason and espionage for fifteen years at the [Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center](/places/the-katrina-mcquaid-memorial-prison/). <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> In JL's account of the old crew fifteen years on, Luigi is prisoner LL849, held in a high-security [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/) facility and awaiting trial for treason and crimes against the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

**Buttsack.** Following the battle Buttsack was awarded the Medal of Valorous Service, and declined it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

**Horgus Simianis.** Hadozee hairs were recovered after the battle, belonging to "the legendary deceased Astral Command hero Horgus Simiani" <small>(sic)</small>. They were crafted into the Wand of Horgus Hairs, a very rare wand that lets a spellcaster attuned to it cast *animate dead* without material components, three times between dawns. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New magic items)</small>

## Aftermath

### The wreck of the Zanzibar

Astral Command seized the wreckage of the Zanzibar and stored it in a top-secret hangar in Realmspace. Company agents have been denied access, and attempts at infiltration have yielded little. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The dossier's entry on the ship lists the Zanzibar, a Zarus cruiser, as decommissioned, housed in a high-security facility in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) on the moon of Toril near the "Faerunian Experience Museum & Family Theme Park". Company agents suggest the ship may hold data leading to Caprica's remains, though Astral Command may have recovered or inadvertently destroyed it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

The dossier states that Caprica's remains have never been recovered. Citing her "extensive and intimate experience" with the earlier sonic disturbance known as [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/), the Company's Board of Directors has deemed her remains a vital asset, and the dossier calls for the special attention of clerics, necromancers and casters of resurrection magic. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> In Nico's account of play in May 2025, [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) brought Caprica's body back to [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

### The Starweaver Act

A year after the battle the Starweaver Act was passed in the Sovereignty, outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included. It led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market for robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> In the Company dossier, the Sovereign Senate's passage of the Act made the possession or dissemination of artificial intelligence within Sovereign territory a crime carrying a penalty of up to 1,000 years in prison, and [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) was created as a paramilitary arm of the Sovereign government to enforce it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

### The Sovereign Supreme

Radical reforms of the Sovereign government "following the crisis at Tannhauser Gate" resulted in the appointment of Astral Command Admiral Timothee Buckets as supreme ruler, with a broad range of powers over the military, the legislature and the judiciary. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

### The Istus class

Astral Command introduced the Istus-class vessel after the battle, built for stealth exploration, extreme speed and easy manoeuvrability. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The new line followed the decommissioning of the heavy Zarus-class battle cruisers: light, fast and agile ships whose thin hulls are protected by Arcane Artillery that casts spells on the vessel. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships)</small> One of them, the SSJ Vanguard, is commanded by Capt. Sandy Titzandassia, with Lt. Commander [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) as executive officer, both of whom served with Caprica on the Zanzibar. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> See [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) and [the Istus Cruiser](/items/the-istus-cruiser/).

### The T.U.N.E.

Company artificers first detected the Temporarily Unknown Noise Energy (T.U.N.E.) following the battle. Barely perceptible at first, it grew louder and more unstable over fifteen years, and is now audible to the unaided ear within 500 lightyears of the border of [the Vodoni Empire](/organizations/the-vodoni-empire/). The sound can destroy phlogiston, making interstellar travel and communication extremely difficult. Sovereign leadership has implicated the Vodoni Empire. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## Relation to the end of Light of Xaryxis

*[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, the first arc of Starfall, ended with a finale played on 31 May 2024, in which the crew fought the Shrike, the Zanzibar took critical damage, Caprica died, a Wish was cast and the Lexicon fled Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31; 2024-06-01; 2024-06-02)</small> See [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/). JL proposed *The Starsong Awakens* the next day, set fifteen years later, and dated the Starweaver Act to a year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-02)</small>

Dave's account places the climax of Starfall at [Necis](/places/necis/), the destroyed planet where the Time Tombs stood and the Shrike originated, at the end of *Light of Xaryxis* and of *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> The Company dossier places Caprica's last sighting at the helm of the Zanzibar during the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, bound for the Eye of Doom. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## Legacy

Dave, as a player, wrote that *The Starsong Awakens* is "centered on [Nine](/people/nine/) and how he set out to fix everything bad that happened after Tannhauser Gate". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small>
