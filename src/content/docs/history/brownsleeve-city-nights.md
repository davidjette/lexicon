**Storyline · [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/), Selune · Played 11 July to 1 November 2024**

*Also known as:* Brownsleeves City Nights · Brownsleeve City: Tales from the Dark Side of the Moon

**Brownsleeve City Nights** was the [Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) storyline of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, in which the rookie agents [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/), played by Dave, and [Paytra](/people/paytra/), played by Nico, hunted the source of illegal artificial intelligence in Brownsleeve City, a beachside resort on the moon of Toril. It ran alongside the storyline of the legacy cast, [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/). The agents followed the trail of the Red Eye through a robot fighting ring, the kidnapping of the city's mayor, a pilgrimage to four holy sites on the moon and a power plant. In the finale Paytra was killed, and an epilogue showed an Istus Cruiser. Kol and Paytra were afterwards rebuilt as robots.

## Background

A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the Sovereignty passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included. The Act led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market for robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

*The Starsong Awakens* ran two campaigns at once that would eventually sync up. In one the players ran Barrias and Se7en in a sequel game. In the other they were "2 rookie Blade Runners on the moon of Toril", assigned to "the sunny beachside resort community on the moon called Brownsleeve City", to find the source of illegal AI. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small>

The rookies were level-6 members of the Bureau, each starting with a Club of Compliance, one uncommon magic item, 100 gp and a Bureau badge, and sharing one standard-issue flying car. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small> Kol is a githzerai kensei monk, an agent of the Bureau who specialises in getting close to dangerous machines and dismantling them fast. <small>(character sheet; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-19)</small> Paytra is a feral tiefling fighter and warlock. <small>(character sheet)</small> [Spoon](/people/spoon/), a zealot barbarian, is a Bureau of Compliance Deputy. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-25)</small>

## Investigation

In the first episode, played on 11 July 2024, the rookies' scene with their new partners involved a drink, and Kol refused it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-11)</small>

Kol's notes record the early cases. A note on Jeb, a bugbear cyber jack, bore the letters "R E" and the address of a bean factory, and the symbol of a red eye. The bean factory held a robot fighting ring, which the agents blew up. Police killed Jeb. The agents visited his brother, George W, arrested him and tore up his shop, where they found unicorn meat, a map of an old cruiser, and the word "Inevitable" in red letters. A robot tried to kill them and take George. The notes also record two sayings: "in ships with painted sails did the red eye travel the galaxy" and "no man with face of flesh can face the inevitable and survive". <small>(character sheet)</small>

A robot the table knew as **the Inevitable** self-destructed, saying "see you again soon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-27)</small>

Jeb was a bugbear who worked at a bar, and the agents brought him in to the police precinct. He died in his cell before he could be questioned. His brother George W, afraid that Jeb would talk, paid a guard 1,000 gp to switch off the cell cameras, and Jeb was strangled. The police at the precinct were crooked. A napkin from the Ornery Owlbear led on. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

George W kept a convenience store with five Iron Cobra robots caged behind the register, each with a red eye on its breast. He believed himself a robot, flesh made metal, had a large red eye carved into his chest, and wore a name tag reading GEORGE W. In his back office hung a large map of an old Zarus-class heavy cruiser, with the word INEVITABLE marked at the helm. Bound and gagged on Paytra's rothé-skin couch, he told the agents:

> I am the Red Eye. I was born of flesh, made metal. My blood, pure liquid nitrogen. If I die, I will be born again in the Red Eye's gaze.

<small>— George W, under questioning.</small>

He called himself one head of a hydra, said that no man made of flesh could face the Inevitable and live, and said that the Red Eye, who once travelled the galaxy in ships with painted sails, would rise again, and "it will end where it first began". <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

The Bureau had given the agents a furnished suite in the Moonstone Arms downtown, a skyrise over the ocean; from its windows they could see the city's family theme park and a weapon shop called the Zanzibarmory. That night robots came to both agents' doors in the shape of frightened strangers begging to be let in, an aasimar woman at Paytra's and a half-orc man at Kol's, and attacked once inside. When one was destroyed, a panel in its skull opened on the mark of the Red Eye and a processor where its brain should have been. Its files held a map of Brownsleeve City marking a warehouse in the middle of the city, and millions of lines of poetry: <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

> Red Eye, circuits divine, code ignite, Creator of stars, savior's light. Bytes of wisdom, digital grace, In silicon realms, we find your trace.

<small>— from the robot's processor.</small>

In the warehouse hangar the agents fought off a pack of small drones and found the moldering hulk of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), its name worn on the hull, the ship tilted and holed and nested with animals, a crude red eye painted on a wall. [Spoon](/people/spoon/) remarked that the old Zarus-class ships could hold more than a hundred Sovereign soldiers. Inside were a robot engineer's makeshift workbench and a burned-out bridge whose spelljamming helm had been torn away. The Inevitable waited on the bridge: ten feet tall, of spiky black plasti-steel, with fists like hammers and a red eye painted on his carapace, and a little winged quadrone in glasses at his side.

> I am the Red Eye. We are all the Red Eye. In the Red Eye's gaze we bask, and at the Red Eye's will we serve. The Red Eye created us all in their image.

<small>— the Inevitable, on the bridge of the Zanzibar.</small>

He called the wrecked ship a potent metaphor for the agents' people, "who will soon find their place in the dustbin of history", and when he was beaten he self-destructed and brought the wreck down around them. As they got out, a black cat darted behind the helm, and a faint, haunting melody grew louder as they came near it. The tag on its neck read ORION. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The mayor

After the session of 9 August 2024 the next episode was titled "Brownsleeve City: Tales from the Dark Side of the Moon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-09)</small>

Kol's notes list a map of Brownsleeves City, a moon stone mine, and a hangar holding [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). A figure in a metal suit entered a secure area, took the mayor and blew up City Hall, with the words "time of the sacrifice has come". The mayor's assistant was Beef Wellington. <small>(character sheet)</small>

In the session of 16 August the agents learned that "There are 4 sites on Selune holy to the children of red eye. Make the pilgrimage, follow his righteous path, to save your mayor’s life." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16)</small> The session included a fight with a fire elemental and ended Act I. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16; 2024-08-17)</small>

The agents survived the explosion at city hall. Through an antique Astral Command wrist communicator, powered by a small white stone, they spoke with the Inevitable, who told them the only way to save the mayor's life was a pilgrimage to the four holy sites of the Red Eye: the SSJ Zanzibar, which they had already visited, the Wet Fusion Plant, the BCCC School of Artificery, and Negroni Prime Memorial Stadium. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The power plant

At a power plant the agents confronted the workers and a cyborg who recited a historical poem, noted as "4 21 5 Zanzibars, 6 Statues". <small>(character sheet)</small> In the session of 6 September 2024 the agents shut the plant down on suspicion alone, and Kol's wound began growing crusty metallic scales. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-09-07)</small>

The agents also investigated the BCCC School of Artificery. They found a Little Red Book, "The Way It Is" by Red-Eye, hollowed out with a pearl-handled pistol inside, and discovered plans for a wet fusion bomb from a student of the school. They arrested the chief engineer, and at the stadium they obtained a children's history of Faerûn. <small>(character sheet)</small>

The power plant was the Wet Fusion Plant. On its wall hung a dusty framed photograph of a red-bearded dwarf on the bridge of an old Astral Command vessel with three AI companions, captioned as [Barrias Redeye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), inventor of wet fusion technology, with his companions 4, 5 and 6. Among its twelve workers were the half-orc shift supervisor Dove Tonsils, the goliath reactor technician Dr. Cooter Brown and the tiefling security guard Cream Thessel, and two of [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/), both cyborgs: Dim Burger, a half-red-dragon dragonborn too dim to hide his implants, and a bugbear werebot, his minder, whose bite could turn its victim into a cyborg. Their way was chaos, throwing coworkers off ledges and poisoning the air. The dying bugbear recited a version of the rhyme of the Zanzibars and the statues on Heroes' Hill, told the agents to know their history if they meant to save the mayor, and gave the number that would stop the bomb at the stadium, 1138. On him were robotic implants powered by small white stones like the one in the wrist communicator, the hollowed-out copy of *[The Way It Is](/items/the-red-eye-manifesto/)* with its pearl-handled pistol, and a security badge for the BCCC School of Artificery. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

The episode ended with Kol infected by the werebot's nanobot curse, the power down across the moon after the plant's reactor failed, and the city's traffic stopped by mass rioting. The House of Tyr at the mall was kept by Brother Grande, a cleric in secret sympathy with the robots. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

The Red Eye's terrorists then broke up a game at Negroni Prime Memorial Stadium. Kol went after one of them, armed with an iridium blaster, into the basement, where a shield guardian, warding-bonded to a bandit captain, guarded the bomb; the two were called Frank and Boobert. Paytra carried the red book and the history book. Among what the agents took was a library card for the BCCC School of Artificery. On the road to the college they fought riders on motorbikes and a truck. The college was empty but for an old orc librarian, and a flyer there read "Do you know the Red Eye? See T.S., Glittergold Hall room 4". The library search led them to the Mines of Phandelver ride at the family theme park, closing for the blackout, where workers struggling with crates asked the agents to help load them onto a waiting ship: the Red Eye's vessel, carrying stolen moonstone away. In a fight with bikers the agents' car was badly damaged and four bikers were killed. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Finale

The finale was played on 1 November 2024. Dave's character tried to throw a robot off, planning to crash a truck with himself and the Inevitable aboard and destroy two robots at once, so that Paytra could fly to safety. Paytra had already fallen unconscious, and the crash killed her. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

> At least she went peacefully, rocketing against the surface of the moon.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

Paytra was killed in the line of duty. The epilogue showed an Istus Cruiser, whose crew would be the new level-3 characters, and the legacy cast was put on hold. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

The next session opened on a television report by Rodrigo Milk, live from the shore of the Waning Sea: a 90,000-pound Parsifal Truckster had fallen from the sky, injuring one Sovereign agent and killing another, less than a day after the midtown explosion and the moon-wide blackout traced to a terrorist attack on the wet fusion plant, and sources blamed an extremist group called the Red Eye. Kol stood in the burning wreckage on the beach, his head ringing, and found cold metal on his face. Two paramedics told him the robo-virus in him had reached stage three, that stage four was irreversible neural compromise, and that on the moon he would be shot on sight. They strapped him to a gurney and flew him out of the moon's orbit to a sleek new Sovereign military ship, the SSJ Vanguard. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Aftermath

The new characters were devotees of the Red Eye, serving aboard the stolen [Istus Cruiser](/items/the-istus-cruiser/): [Umwelt](/people/umwelt/), called Weld, and [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-10-26; 2024-11-02; sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> "Red Eye" had been the name of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) at the beginnings of the robot resistance, but the Red Eye whom Kol and Paytra had been hunting was [Nine](/people/nine/), Barrias's "son", who had taken the name and led the resistance into open hostility with the Sovereignty and an obsession with the banned and vanished Lexicon. It was the great reveal of the arc. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-the-red-eye.md)</small>

Kol and Paytra were rebuilt as robots, choosing from four new robot backgrounds. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-17)</small> Kol's player pictured the rebuilt Kol as a spine with legs and a skull, one arm and half a metallic ribcage, who "Committed hari kari at the moment he no longer needed his organs". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10)</small>

> Their mission is a success. They found the Red Eye.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-10)</small>

Later in the arc Paytra and Kol brought Caprica's body to the Terrasque ship and met Marcel and Weld there. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

The Vanguard, an Istus-class clipper, was worked by followers of the Red Eye. Aboard it the new recruits, Weld and Marcel, met [OU812](/people/ou812/), who told them the Inevitable had died in his showdown with the Bureau and gave them the Red Eye's orders: find out what the ship's commanding officers, [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/), knew about the Lexicon. Paytra woke in the dark in a sickbed, tended by [Nine](/people/nine/): "You shouldn't try to move. You're still too weak. In time, you will heal." Rebuilt, Kol took the robot background Malware and Paytra Terminator, and from the Vanguard the party went on to seize [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>
