**Chronology · From the deep past to the Starfall era · Dale Reckoning, table years and intervals**

*Also known as:* the timeline

The **chronology** of the shared continuity is a span of about 65 million years, from the deep past to the [Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) of the Starfall era. Its events are dated in three ways that do not agree with one another: Dale Reckoning (DR) in the handouts of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), round years Dave gave at the table, such as "like 1900" for the present of the Ages, and intervals in years between one era and the next. The intervals are firmer than the year numbers, and the Dale Reckoning dates in the handouts stand.

In order, the eras are the deep past; Antiquity and the rise of Netheril; the first Time of Troubles; the Ages of the Infanta, which are the present of the second Time of Troubles; the future of Dark Zanzibar; [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) and [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), five hundred years after the Ages; the Eberron campaigns, far after Dead Man's Hand; and [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), some five thousand years after the Ages.

## The eras

1. **The deep past.** About 65 million years ago: "deep past is about 65 million years ago." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small> Dave's handwritten [Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/) begins its line at "40,000,000 B.C.", and in 2020 he called the same moment "40,000,000 years in the past". <small>(Infanta Timeline, page 2; Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-22)</small> The two figures are one moment: "same thing, use 65M to evoke the dinosaur asteroid timeframe". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> The party rode [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) there in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/). A perfect [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) kept out of sunlight "for about 50 million years" becomes a Perfect Dark stone <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>, and the one the party took out of [the Tudyx Cube](/items/the-tudyx-cube/) had been inside it "for a presumed 65 million years". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

2. **Antiquity and Netheril.** On the Infanta Timeline the rise of the Netherese, when the Grey Sage gains the trust of "two sorceror twins who rule a small kingdom inland" (sic), comes between prehistory and the first Time of Troubles. <small>(Infanta Timeline, page 1)</small> The twins are [Aurora and Astra Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/), and the Grey Sage of Antiquity is the party's own [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). [Chronocypher](/people/stonecypher/) was incarnated in Antiquity as Eloise, the Infanta of Time, during the first Infanta Crisis. Dave pairs this age with the next as "antiquity/time of troubles". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small> After the fall of Netheril, "St Cloud has to wait another 1000 years to try again…" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

3. **The first Time of Troubles.** "first tome of troubles = like 800" (sic). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> It is the setting of [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), when, on the Infanta Timeline, the gods "bourne themselves on Earth" (sic). Dave described the era to the party as holding the building of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), [Blackstaff](/people/khelben-arunsun/), "the forgotten Netherese including the Twin Queens", "the proto origins of Cormyr and the Dale Reckoning". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small> From "like 800" to "like 1900" is about eleven hundred years, which agrees with St. Cloud's wait of "another 1000 years". <small>With the party in that age, Dave also put the future "thousands of years from now not millions". (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>

4. **The Ages of the Infanta: the present.** "second time of troubles (present) = like 1900". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> On the Infanta Timeline the second Time of Troubles is marked "(present)" and opens with Wainwright St. Cloud's birth in Baldur's Gate. The handouts date the same years in Dale Reckoning. [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) runs from 1481 to 1492 DR. The executive memos in the St. Cloud Trading Company's archive, recovered in [Arc IV](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/), run from "5th Last Seed, 1460 DR (approx. 30 years ago)" to "10th Evening Star, 1491 DR (this year)", and St. Cloud's memo on [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) that led to the breach of [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) is dated 28th Midyear 1461 DR. <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives)</small> <small>Two memos in the same archive give the same age for different years: "8th, Sun’s Dusk, 1480 DR (approx. 17 years ago)" and "6th Evening Star DR 1474, (17 years ago)". (Arc IV, SCTC Archives)</small> The Ages end with the defeat of Asmodeus and [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/)'s founding of [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

5. **Kara's ten-year skip.** Time ran slower inside Calix's vault in [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/), and when the party emerged from the phylactery ten years had passed; the Infanta of the Moon was ten years old. [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) is set ten years after Motherstone. In 2018 Dave dated those years for [Kara](/people/kara/): "she just effectively time traveled 10 years forward from like 1860 - 1870". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2018-11-21)</small> <small>The 2018 table years put the end of the skip at "like 1870"; in 2021 Dave put the present at "like 1900". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2018-11-21; 2021-08-26)</small>

6. **Future Zanzibar, to about 2100.** "future Zan lives all the way to 2100 before coming back". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> Zanzibar was left behind when the party left [Refuge](/places/refuge/) for the past. In that loop he went forward to fight beside the sea elves against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and there Blackstaff sent him back in time, with Max, to stop St. Cloud: this is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/#dark-zanzibar). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

7. **Dead Man's Hand, five hundred years after the Ages.** Dead Man's Hand is set five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta, and opens with "500 years ago, the universe faced a Crisis." <small>(Dead Man's Hand, opening)</small> The Cormyrean Empire was founded five hundred years before it, and in [Sigil](/places/sigil/) [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/) date the Ordo Trium to the aftermath of the Infanta Crisis, "500 years ago". <small>(DM notes)</small> In between, [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) headed [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) "until his disappearance about 200 years after the events of the campaign". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-13)</small> Of the emperors before the war, Dave said: "the previous line of Zanzibars resisted that for 500 years because Zanny wasn’t about to let Revan take over his part of the universe". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> The campaign takes place during the beginnings of the War of the Five Zanzibars, which broke out five hundred years after the founding of the Empire. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> <small>Planning the campaign in January 2024, Dave first put it "hundreds of years since the Infanta Wars", then "Maybe a thousand years or two", and said "every power for a thousand years claimed his throne". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-18; 2024-01-21)</small>

8. **The Infanta Crises.** The first and second [Infanta Crises](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/) "were on the order of 100 years" each. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> The first fell in Antiquity. The Infanta Crisis ended with the founding of the Empire, five hundred years before Dead Man's Hand; the five hundred years are the time since the Crisis.

9. **The Eberron campaigns.** The Eberron plots fall far after the events of the Infantaverse and Dead Man's Hand, and before Starfall. <small>(See [The Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/).)</small> Within Eberron:
   - [The Greenbluff incident](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/), at the opening of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), about ten years before the Inevitables. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-10. Fulcrum's bell file gives roughly twenty years ago; the Council of Sharn record gives thirteen years before the fall of Sharn.)</small>
   - The [Leef](/places/leef/) and [Newham](/places/newham/) arc, where [the Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) formed.
   - One year.
   - The [Sharn](/places/sharn/) arc: "Sharn Arc was weeks of action". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> <small>Other accounts of the Sharn rising give "roughly two years". (See [Sharn](/places/sharn/).)</small>
   - Three years: "3 years between Sharn and Thronehold/Korth". <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>
   - [The Thronehold massacre](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/) and the [Korth](/places/korth/) arc.

   By the Starfall era the Sovereignty of Realms spans Toril, Eberron, Oerth and Krynn. <small>(See [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/).)</small>

10. **The Sovereignty and Starfall.** The War of the Five Zanzibars was a critical collapse of the continent before a dark time preceding the ascendance of the Sovereignty. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Dave's figures for the era, all counted from the Ages:
    - Realmspace as the home world of the spacefaring order, "2,000 years in the future post Infanta verse". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), with a "thousand year history" by the time of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small>
    - Starfall, some five thousand years after the Ages: "5,000 years in the future her heirs would still be in charge of a unified Realmspace". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - "the great melting of the 6700s", after which Fort Zanzibar is surrounded by ocean. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>
    - [The SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/): "Flagship from 500 years ago". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - [The Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/): "it takes ten thousand years for it to get to where it is". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>
    - [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), set fifteen years after [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
    - [The Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), passed "a year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate". <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

    <small>Realmspace's "2,000 years" and Starfall's "5,000 years" are separate figures from the same evening, and the Lexicon's ten thousand years is longer than either. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2023-12-02)</small>

## The calendars

### Dale Reckoning

Dale Reckoning is the calendar of the handouts of the Ages of the Infanta. The present of the Ages is 1491 DR, the year the St. Cloud Trading Company's last memo in Arc IV calls "this year", and the chronicle as a whole runs 1481–1492 DR. The ten years of the vault fall between the chronicle's opening year and that memo. The oldest Dale Reckoning date in a handout is the *Ars Calixia* of [Calix](/people/calix/), penned "circa 100 DR". <small>([O-3255](/items/o-3255/), a Dead Man's Hand handout)</small> Dave placed the "proto origins" of the Dale Reckoning in the first Time of Troubles. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small>

The Dale Reckoning dates in the handouts stand.

### Table years

Dave's round years are a separate count, given at the table with "like":

> I think it’a first tome of troubles = like 800, second time of troubles (present) = like 1900, and future Zan lives all the way to 2100 before coming back

<small>— Dave. Sic on "it’a" and "tome". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small>

The same count gives "like 1860 - 1870" for the ten-year skip <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2018-11-21)</small> and "the 6700s" for the great melting <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>. The table years and Dale Reckoning have no fixed conversion: the present is "like 1900" at the table and 1491 DR in the handouts. On the table count, five thousand years after "like 1900" falls after the melting of the 6700s.

### Intervals

The intervals between eras are firmer than either kind of year number. Where a year number and an interval disagree, the interval holds, and where a table year and a Dale Reckoning date disagree, the Dale Reckoning date holds.

| Interval | Length |
|---|---|
| The deep past to the present | About 65 million years |
| The first Time of Troubles to the second | About 1,000 to 1,100 years |
| Calix's vault, Arc II to Arc III | Ten years |
| Each Infanta Crisis | On the order of a hundred years |
| The Ages to Revan's disappearance | About 200 years |
| The Ages to Dead Man's Hand and the War of the Five Zanzibars | 500 years |
| Greenbluff to the Inevitables | About ten years |
| Leef to Sharn | One year |
| The Sharn arc | Weeks |
| Sharn to Thronehold and Korth | Three years |
| The Ages to the spacefaring Realmspace | About 2,000 years |
| The Ages to Starfall | About 5,000 years |
| *Light of Xaryxis* to *The Starsong Awakens* | Fifteen years |
| The Battle of Tannhauser Gate to the Starweaver Act | One year |
