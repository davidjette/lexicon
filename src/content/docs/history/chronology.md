**Chronology · From the deep past to the Starfall era · Dale Reckoning, table years and intervals**

*Also known as:* the timeline

The **chronology** of the shared continuity is a span of about 65 million years, from the deep past to the [Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) of the Starfall era. The handouts of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) date events in Dale Reckoning (DR). At the table Dave gave round years, such as "like 1900" for the present of the Ages, and most later eras are measured in years from the Ages.

In order, the eras are the deep past; Antiquity and the rise of Netheril; the first Time of Troubles; the Ages of the Infanta, which are the present of the second Time of Troubles; the future of Dark Zanzibar; [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) and [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), five hundred years after the Ages; the Eberron campaigns; and [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), some five thousand years after the Ages.

## The eras

1. **The deep past, about 65 million years ago.** <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10; Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small> The party rode [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) there in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/). A perfect [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) kept out of sunlight "for about 50 million years" becomes a Perfect Dark stone <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>, and the one the party took out of [the Tudyx Cube](/items/the-tudyx-cube/) had been inside it for some 65 million years. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small>

2. **Antiquity and Netheril.** On Dave's handwritten [Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/) the rise of the Netherese, when the Grey Sage gains the trust of "two sorceror twins who rule a small kingdom inland" (sic), comes between prehistory and the first Time of Troubles. <small>(Infanta Timeline, page 1)</small> The twins are [Aurora and Astra Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/), and the Grey Sage of Antiquity is the party's own [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). [Elistrada](/people/elistrae/), the first Infanta, lived in this age, and [Chronocypher](/people/stonecypher/) was incarnated in it as Eloise, the Infanta of Time, during the first Infanta Crisis. Dave pairs this age with the next as "antiquity/time of troubles". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small> After the fall of Netheril, "St Cloud has to wait another 1000 years to try again…" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

3. **The first Time of Troubles, about 800.** "first tome of troubles = like 800" (sic). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> It is the setting of [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), when, on the Infanta Timeline, the gods "bourne themselves on Earth" (sic). Dave described the era to the party as holding the building of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), [Blackstaff](/people/khelben-arunsun/), "the forgotten Netherese including the Twin Queens", and "the proto origins of Cormyr and the Dale Reckoning". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small> About eleven hundred years separate it from the present, St. Cloud's wait of "another 1000 years". With the party in that age, Dave put the future "thousands of years from now not millions". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>

4. **The Ages of the Infanta, the present, about 1900.** "second time of troubles (present) = like 1900". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> On the Infanta Timeline the second Time of Troubles is marked "(present)" and opens with Wainwright St. Cloud's birth in Baldur's Gate. In the handouts [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) runs from 1481 to 1492 DR. The executive memos in the St. Cloud Trading Company's archive, recovered in [Arc IV](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/), run from "5th Last Seed, 1460 DR (approx. 30 years ago)" to "10th Evening Star, 1491 DR (this year)", and St. Cloud's memo on [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) that led to the breach of [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) is dated 28th Midyear 1461 DR. <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives)</small> [Ella](/people/elistrae/) is the Infanta of the Moon of this age. The Ages end with the defeat of Asmodeus and [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/)'s founding of [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/). <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-five-zanzibars-and-asmodeus.md)</small>

5. **The ten years in Calix's vault.** Time ran slower inside Calix's vault in [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/), and when the party emerged from the phylactery ten years had passed and Ella was ten years old. [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) is set ten years after Motherstone. In 2018 Dave dated those years for [Kara](/people/kara/): "she just effectively time traveled 10 years forward from like 1860 - 1870". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2018-11-21)</small>

6. **Dark Zanzibar's future, to about 2100.** "future Zan lives all the way to 2100 before coming back". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small> Zanzibar was left behind when the party left [Refuge](/places/refuge/) for the past. In that loop he went forward to fight beside the sea elves against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and there Blackstaff sent him back in time, with Max, to stop St. Cloud. This Zanzibar is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/). <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small>

7. **The Infanta Crises.** The first and second [Infanta Crises](/lore/the-infanta-crisis/) each lasted on the order of a hundred years. The first fell in Antiquity, and the second ended with the founding of the Empire. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-five-zanzibars-and-asmodeus.md)</small>

8. **Dead Man's Hand, five hundred years after the Ages.** Dead Man's Hand opens with "500 years ago, the universe faced a Crisis." <small>(Dead Man's Hand, opening)</small> In [Sigil](/places/sigil/), [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/) date the Ordo Trium to the aftermath of the Infanta Crisis, "500 years ago". <small>(DM notes)</small> In between, [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) headed [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) "until his disappearance about 200 years after the events of the campaign". <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-13-dead-mans-hand-company.md)</small> Of the emperors before the war, Dave said: "the previous line of Zanzibars resisted that for 500 years because Zanny wasn’t about to let Revan take over his part of the universe". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> The campaign takes place at the beginning of the War of the Five Zanzibars, which broke out five hundred years after the founding of the Empire. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-and-the-war.md)</small>

9. **The Eberron campaigns.** The Eberron campaigns fall after Dead Man's Hand and before Starfall. <small>(See [The Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/).)</small> Within Eberron:
   - [The Greenbluff incident](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/), at the opening of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), about ten years before the Inevitables and thirteen years before the fall of Sharn. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 19)</small>
   - The [Leef](/places/leef/) and [Newham](/places/newham/) arc, where [the Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) formed.
   - A year between Leef and Sharn.
   - The [Sharn](/places/sharn/) arc, weeks of action.
   - Three years between Sharn and Thronehold. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-batch.md)</small>
   - [The Thronehold massacre](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/) and the [Korth](/places/korth/) arc.

   By the Starfall era the Sovereignty of Realms spans Toril, Eberron, Oerth and Krynn. <small>(See [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/).)</small>

10. **The Sovereignty and Starfall.** The War of the Five Zanzibars was a critical collapse of the continent, followed by a dark age before the rise of the Sovereignty. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-zanzibar-and-the-war.md)</small> Dave's figures for the era, counted from the Ages:
    - Realmspace as the home world of the spacefaring order, "2,000 years in the future post Infanta verse". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), with a "thousand year history" by the time of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small>
    - Starfall, some five thousand years after the Ages: "5,000 years in the future her heirs would still be in charge of a unified Realmspace". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - "the great melting of the 6700s", after which Fort Zanzibar is surrounded by ocean. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>
    - [The SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/): "Flagship from 500 years ago". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>
    - [The Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/): "it takes ten thousand years for it to get to where it is". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>
    - [*The Starsong Awakens*](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), set fifteen years after [*Light of Xaryxis*](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
    - [The Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), passed "a year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate". <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## The calendars

### Dale Reckoning

Dale Reckoning is the calendar of the handouts of the Ages of the Infanta. The present of the Ages is 1491 DR, the year the St. Cloud Trading Company's last memo in Arc IV calls "this year", and the chronicle as a whole runs 1481–1492 DR. The oldest Dale Reckoning date in a handout is the *Ars Calixia* of [Calix](/people/calix/), penned "circa 100 DR". <small>([O-3255](/items/o-3255/), a Dead Man's Hand handout)</small> Dave placed the "proto origins" of the Dale Reckoning in the first Time of Troubles. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-07-25)</small>

### Table years

At the table Dave gave round years with "like":

> I think it’a first tome of troubles = like 800, second time of troubles (present) = like 1900, and future Zan lives all the way to 2100 before coming back

<small>— Dave. Sic on "it’a" and "tome". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small>

The same count gives "like 1860 - 1870" for the ten years in the vault <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2018-11-21)</small> and "the 6700s" for the great melting <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>. On this count the present is about 1900, where the handouts give 1491 DR.
