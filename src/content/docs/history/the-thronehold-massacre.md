**Peace summit, destroyed · Thronehold · Korth Ep 1–2 · Three years after the fall of Sharn**

The Thronehold Massacre is the destruction of the peace summit held at Thronehold three years after the fall of Sharn, in Korth Ep 1–2. Nearly every major faction on the continent sent delegates; the Empire's masked guards at the summit were undead belonging to [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), and they turned on Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, who had called the summit. Vex called down his own warships on the summit grounds, killing delegates and civilians, and was himself killed in flight south by [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) over the northern Mournlands.

Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) called it. He declared it was time to negotiate an end to the civil war, and the call was controversial on every side: the Silver Flame and Zero-One opposed it, NEST saw propaganda value in it, and the Emperor's spokespeople denounced the gathering as illegitimate. Nearly every major faction sent representatives anyway.

Khorvaire was three years into fracture. Crona's Wall had collapsed and orc forces were pushing into Dark Druid territory in the Eldeen Reaches; the Hundreds and the Silver Flame were locked in fighting against E.D.E.N. constructs; food was running short in Sharn.

## THE DELEGATIONS

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) came as Chancellor of Sharn with [Lorian](/people/lorian/) and with [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) in disguise, representing Sharn and Leef; [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was still deployed on the Thrane front. Orc tribes, [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and Aundair all sent delegates. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) hinted at "new growth" in the Mournlands and had already sent Eric a Cyrean Red Pomegranate — a fruit believed extinct since [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/), arriving fresh and oversized. Lorian established, quietly, that every masked Empire guard at the summit was undead.

## THE SPEECH AND THE ACCUSATION

In the final assembly in the great hall, Eric delivered a speech calling for the Empire to be outlawed. As the delegates reacted, Sister [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), attending for the Black Doves, confronted Vex and publicly accused Eric of being responsible for the death of his son [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/).

Vex began to rise into the air, lightning crawling across his body. Through the arceye cameras carrying the summit across the continent, his voice said:

> Kill Cancellor Eric for the Empire.

## THE TRAP

The masked guards surged forward — and turned on Vex. They were not his. They were [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s, and they attacked him and everyone in their path. The Black Dove sisters were already leaving the building. Vex, screaming of her betrayal, called down his own warships and ordered them to fire on the summit grounds. Explosions tore through the surrounding buildings and killed delegates and civilians.

The Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/)' airship had just arrived at Thronehold. When the bombardment began he turned it around and left the island entirely.

## THE FLIGHT

Eric, Lorian and Gemma ran with Locke Pierce and the Hundreds through smoke and debris and misty-stepped aboard a rising ship. Vex, wrapped in lightning and rage, threw himself into the sky and clung to the hull. They fought him on the open deck all the way south, and Gemma finally drove **the Bright Dawn** up his spine as the ship crossed into the northern Mournlands. His storm died with him.

## WHAT THE CONTINENT SAW

The arceye feeds showed the Chancellor of Sharn rejecting the Empire, the Admiral exploding into fury, the summit collapsing into slaughter, and then, from the same direction, a red dome blooming silently over the Mournlands and taking the remnants of Vex's fleet with it.

To the free states, the last chance for peace had died and a new weapon had arrived. They named it **the Last Mourning**. See [the Eternal Nightfall](/lore/the-eternal-nightfall/) for what it was built to pre-empt.

**Result:** the peace summit destroyed; Vex d'Lyrandar killed; the Empire's undead exposed at the negotiating table; Locke Pierce's Ruby Nova demonstrated · **Dead:** Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, delegates and civilians
