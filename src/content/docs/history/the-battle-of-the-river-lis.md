**Mass battle · The River Lis, eastern border of Cormanthor · Temple Holdings LLC, between Arc IV and Arc V**

*Also known as:* the Battle of Elmwood · the Battle of River Lis

The **Battle of the River Lis** was a mass battle fought on the River Lis, on the eastern border of [Cormanthor](/places/myth-drannor/), between the army of the Infanta **[Mora](/people/mora/)**, called the **Moradinate**, and an allied host of Cormyr, [Punis](/places/punis/) and the elves. Mora's dwarves and orcs had devastated every kingdom to the east and sacked Mulmaster, and the river was the last chokepoint before their dreadnoughts entered the West. The allies mustered at Elmwood. [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/), [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) and [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) fought as commissioned officers of [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/), and Roland commanded a war machine called **The 24-Hour News**. The party "stopped the cities". After the battle Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne of Cormyr and left the party, and the chronicle turned to [Arc V — Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/).

## Mora's march

After the party's war with the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/), "orcs are laying waste to Thay". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> After Roland's trial, Zanzibar's player JL reported that Moradin-worshipping dwarves across the world had announced they would side with the marauding orcs, "because they think the Orc’s infanta is Moradin." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-22)</small> That autumn Dave summed up the Infanta: "Zaria is an embryo, Obi is hiding in Waterdeep, Elistrae is losing it and Marcus wants you all to defy the Lady of Pain so she can go to Sigil, Mora is marching an orc army to your gates". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-19)</small> By October Zanzibar was close to a way "to bring a host of angels to Cormyr to stop the Orc invasion of Cormanthor." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-01)</small>

The army is the **Moradinate**, and "the dwarves and orcs have lined up behind Mora". Each kingdom to the east "has had to face the Moradinate alone and have been devastated, it’s scorched earth behind the dreadnoughts". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> Dave on the dreadnoughts:

> They will be approaching in massive armored tanks the size of city blocks, but enough to Ford the river and filled with troops and spellcasters, and presumably at least one Infanta

<small>— Sic. Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-17</small>

## The field

> Mora's army has sacked Mulmaster and are using rubble from the dynamited mountains and city walls to send endless missile fire across River Lis into Cormanthor\
> \
> Elmwood is the most forward securable position and is the muster for allied forces

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21</small>

The battlefield lies on the eastern border of Cormanthor, just west of the ruins of Impiltur. Zhentil Keep is just across the sea. The allies were to engage the Moradinate as it crossed the river, "the last chokepoint before their dreadnoughts enter the West". Holding the crossing cut both ways: "If you can stop their crossing of the river, you can stall them ona small enough place to possibly annihilate them from above or within, but the same is true for your armies". <small>(Sic on "ona". Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

The Lis was the eastern front. [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) was "the second front, by way of under mountain". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> See [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/).

### Infanta on both sides

"There will be Infanta deployed on the battlefield on both sides regardless", Dave told the players: "Tico and Mora at a minimum, likely Zara in utero". [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), also called Tico, was near the front under the protection of the elves. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> A month later Dave counted "two Infanta on one side and at least one on the other, each with enormous fanatical armies." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small>

Divine power at the Lis depended on distance. Zanzibar's paladin magic depended on keeping the princess near the fighting: "within 1/4 mile gets you level 7+ spells, and it drops off a level per mile from there", and "it's not just Zan's powers, it's half the Purple Dragons as well. Likewise, the Mora and Tico being near the battle is essential to their followers". The allies had "1000 paladin of Zarus out there". An Infanta also draws power from followers nearby, "so a well attended Infanta will be more powerful than one who is alone". A retreat by teleport meant "either fleeing with the Infanta leaving the injured to perish, or to stay and defend the Infanta if the position is overrun". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small>

## Raising the host

Revan, Zanzibar and Stonecypher were "enlisted and compelled to fight", and were told the major fighting was "a few weeks away", with "Enough time to get the angels and cormyrian forced to Lis, and local allies or those with flight". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> Dave on the alliance the party brought to the river:

> You have a really impressive force to start with, it’s true. You guys worked hard to line up entire kingdoms behind you and made choices to expand your allies instead of fighting more enemies, so you have most of humanity and elven kind behind you, the dwarves and orcs have lined up behind Mora

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21</small>

- **The War Wizards.** "This their jam, they’re 100% on board". [Celion](/people/celion/) "was conducting weapons research for this very moment". See [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/).
- **The Purple Dragons.** Also committed, with the alliance with the elves "sound", "that’s the peace you arranged and then you delivered the Infanta". Cormyr's lorded gentry are committed to arms and their serfs muster to defend their holds, "But the PDs are the standing army". The paladins of Zarus from Punis "are all in the PDs, probably 1/2 the officer corps".
- **[The Zhentarim](/organizations/the-zhentarim/).** "When planning this entire campaign I always considered the Zents totally up for grabs - for you or St Cloud. Arc after arc you guys have made major moves to help them all over the world. You three plus Pim are their closest allies in earth". Their strongest contact was Jonathan Silverhand, "a top 3 Zent" who "owes you the city of WD to a Certain extent".

<small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

The battle opened with the party courting [Obi](/people/obi/), while [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) and [Drefan](/people/drefan/) tried to bring [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) into the fight. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-17)</small> The party arrived "just as the majority of the angelic refugees are being suited up for the imminent attack from Mora’s Army crossing the river". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small>

When Roland joined the four, Dave gave them their commissions:

> As landed gentry in Cormyr and Punis, you four are commissioned officers in the Purple Dragons so I'm tasking you with command of units in the battle.

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19</small>

Stonecypher briefly served as a general in Zanzibar's army at the battle. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-12)</small>

## Order of battle

The army was the one the party's alliances had earned. "You’re getting the army I build for you", Dave said, "Based on the allies you’ve made along the way / And frankly, you have made quite a few". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small>

| Arm | Units |
|---|---|
| Infantry | Purple Dragon Guard · Punis Royal Guard · Angelic Conscripts |
| Cavalry | Purple Dragon Force · Cormanthryian Bladesingers |
| Archers | Cormanthryian Arcane Archers · Purple Dragon Artillery |
| Airborne | War Wizards · Angelic Guardians |
| Reserves | Zentarim Corsairs? · Obi? · Elistrae? |

<small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small> Nobody came from [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/): asked "Anyone from motherstone?", Dave answered "not yet".

Among the units:

- **Purple Dragon Guard.** Human veteran super-heavy infantry; Courageous and Martial; can reroll a Morale check once per battle and inflicts two casualties on a larger target.
- **Punis Royal Guard.** Human elite heavy infantry, an Honor Guard that cannot be diminished and has advantage on morale.
- **Angelic Conscripts.** Celestial green light levies, Eternal and impossible to horrify; their Song puts attacks on the unit at disadvantage for a turn.
- **Purple Dragon Force.** Human elite heavy cavalry; its Charge inflicts two casualties on a success, and its Lay on Hands restores a casualty to any unit.

<small>(River Lis Units)</small>

### The 24-Hour News

Roland commanded a special unit through his diadem, **The 24-Hour News**:

> Built from specs stolen from St. Cloud with help from the War Wizards and your gunsmithing expertise, you and Lex can build this in time for the battle.

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19</small>

"the Fabricate spell is nuts, btw. it only takes a few wizards a few days to make something like this if they have a skilled craftsman with them". LaMont, who played Revan, on seeing it: "Sweet mother of mayhem. You have become death". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small>

## The battle

The heroes fought with "intelligence and a special mission that will coincide with the wider apocalyptic battle and decide its ultimate outcome, if you can beat the clock. Failure will be deadly." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-22)</small> The battle was played on 2019-12-22 with the simplified warfare rules from *Strongholds & Followers*, mixing scales so that both command and hero actions counted. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21, 2019-12-19)</small>

Dave's word on the result, that evening: "you stopped the cities". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-12-22)</small>
## Aftermath

[Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/) opens "on the aftermath of the battle with a flying child adorned in luxurious black and silver robes, appearing over the battlefield about 500 feet away from your boat above the clouds". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-12-22)</small>

After the Battle of the River Lis, Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne of Cormyr, and after the wedding he was crowned king of Cormyr. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> He did not go with the party into the deep past: "he left after the River Lis and you guys went off with Zeb!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small>

## In Elven lore

The Heroes' Hill rhyme, a later folk telling, counts the dead at a million:

> A million died at River Lis, so says the Elven lore.\
> Celion the Broken Cocked he killed a million more.

<small>— The Heroes' Hill rhyme. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-31</small>
