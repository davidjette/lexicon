**Campaign Arc · Temple Holdings LLC, Arc I · The Moonshae Isles, off the Sword Coast · Complete**

*Opens on:* the privateer *Evening Star* · *Ends at:* Harloch, on Snowdown · *Leads into:* [Arc II — Motherstone](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/)

Arc I — Hope in Exile is the founding arc of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), played across the Moonshae Isles off the Sword Coast. It opens at Baldur's Gate aboard the privateer *Evening Star*, flagship of the Sword Coast Trading Company, and the wreck of that ship leaves a band of strangers as the guardians of an infant carried in secret by the druidic cult of the Lusmundii. The survivors cross the islands of the Moonshae pursued by the Empire, by the Forsworn and by the messengers of Queen Maab; they free the enslaved elves of Leftfork Farm, recover the Infanta from a binding rite at Bask Falls Barrow, and reach the walled town of Harloch, where they buy the derelict Old Temple and take the name Temple Holdings, LLC. The arc ends with Kara and Brother Magnus descending into a buried power station beneath Snowy Mountain on the trail of the Motherstone.

The arc's opening text sets the era:

> In this era of crumbling empires, chaos lurks just past the reach of firelight, and bides its time before it reconquers everything. Can the civilized races push back the Wild, or should they even try?

## The premise

In that age of fading order, the druidic cult of the [Lusmundii](/organizations/the-lusmundii/) — "Light of the World" — carries an infant in secret through hostile waters. They believe the child will turn the tide of history against all kingdoms, laws and cultivation, and return the world to nature. The strangers who become her protectors are — Drefan aside — not elves and do not share her cult's faith. They are simply the ones who happen to be aboard the wrong ship at the right moment.

## Part I — Fall of an Evening Star

The chronicle opens at Baldur's Gate, aboard a vessel of staggering scale:

> It is a great sailing ship with nearly a dozen decks. Four huge masts carrying seemingly fifty sails, some as big as blankets, others as big as clouds. The Evening Star, under the command of Captain Ysmer St. Cloud, is the flagship privateer of the Sword Coast Trading Company, the largest imperial charter company in the Western sea and beyond.

Her master, Captain **Ysmir St. Cloud**, is "a darkly greedy man" whose celebrated thirty-year safe passage hides a darker trade. He is a slaver, and the ship's uncanny luck is bought with the suffering of his **Navigator** — an enslaved dark-elf wizard kept compliant by enchanted rum, prized for his star-magic and his **Quicksilver Sextant**, which "reveals the direction of any known object, place or person. The path it suggests will always avoid all storms and pirates."

The player characters board as passengers, stowaways or prisoners. The decks teem with intrigue — a mutiny brewing among the crew under First Mate **Hroldan Fark**; the dark-elf rogue **Raylin** and the disguised assassin **Delia**, each circling a mysterious prisoner; and, in the deep hold, two secrets the Company would kill to keep. In St. Cloud's own cabin lie papers describing a sarcophagus and "the image of a naked woman holding a sword silhouetted by the Moon," beneath a single cryptic line: *"To keep this, you must first give it to me."*

### What lay in the deep hold

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Below the cargo, in Deep Storage, St. Cloud smuggled **forty starving wood-elf slaves** and the sarcophagus of **Daenan Gallidann** — "the well-preserved corpse of a centuries-dead elven prince who worshipped Elistraee the Dark Maiden." **Silverbane was in that sarcophagus.** [Drefan](/people/drefan/) is a Gallidan, and he takes his house's shard of Perfect moonstone out of it. <small>(See [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).)</small> Daenan is a *baelnorn*, an elven lich; the record says the baelnorn "willingly turned themselves undead in order to stay on Earth and fight against the tide of man," guarding his family's forest lands until an earthquake sealed him in his tomb. Interred with him are three relics that echo through the whole chronicle:

- **Feybane**, a dormant Moonblade of the House of Gallidann — +1 bastard sword, +3 against spriggans, fey and lycanthropes, attunable only by those of chaotic faith who worship Elistraee;
- the **Moonstone Crown**, "a circlet that once per day can emit a blinding light";
- the **Flask of Starfire**, "a flask of white light" that, with a prayer to Elistraee, "can bring a dying body fully back to life."

Daenan "will awaken if either his sword, crown or potion are removed."

Three plots run at once — break out the prisoner, uncover the Navigator's secret, and join (or stop) the mutiny — and all three converge below decks. The arc's first chapter ends in catastrophe.

## Part II — The Greater Storm

Ysmir St. Cloud is dead and the *Evening Star* is lost with most aboard her — her riches, her crew and her unliving cargo sinking into the Sea of Swords. The only survivors crowd two lifeboats kept afloat by a passenger's magic: a handful of sailors, freed slaves, the party, and the infant.

Driven off course, they make landfall on a small, fey-touched island and step straight into an old tragedy in miniature. The island belongs, by inheritance, to the green hag **Sycorax**, long ago slain and bound within a lightning-struck tree; her deformed son **Calamer**; and the air-spirit **Leira** — son and spirit alike enslaved by the exiled wizard **Fortuno**, deposed Duke of Gorha, who rules from a tower with his daughter **Andira** and hunts the **moonstone heart** buried beneath the island. The island's cast — the drowned-and-bound sorceress, the enslaved air-spirit, the monstrous son, the magician-duke deposed by his family and his sheltered daughter — follows Shakespeare's *Tempest*.

By the chapter's end Fortuno and Andira are dead, his tower torn apart by tempest winds, and Sycorax is freed. In gratitude — or in payment — she opens a **Moongate**, a shimmering white bridge arcing over the sea, and sends the survivors on to the next island.

## Part III — Snowdown

The Moongate sets them down on [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/) (anciently **Wain Gallidan**), the elves' own home island and the seat of the Cult of [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), the Lusmundii. Here the chronicle's protagonists are named for the first time: [Drefan](/people/drefan/), the elf sworn to House Gallidann who comes to bear the moonblade [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/); the wizard-merchant [Kara](/people/kara/); [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/), who hunts the bloodline of St. Cloud; [Yoshi](/people/yoshi/) and his companion **Denver**; the dwarf [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/); and [Fulori](/people/fulori-splitheel/), searching for kidnapped wards of her own.

They come ashore at **Leftfork Farm**, worked by aging drow slaves under **Thaddeus Shor** and his impossibly old father **Amadeus**. The Shors' unnatural long life is stolen: a shard of moonstone, forged into an iron shackle, drains the life of the enslaved elven chief **Untari** and her daughters, and a moonstone **heartstone** hidden in a nearby cave commands a crumbling golem. To free Untari's people, the heroes fight through the goblin warren of **Heartstone Cave** to the moonstone core itself.

## Part IV — The Good Men

Victory is soured at once. Returning to camp, the party finds the Infanta gone, carried off by the wet-nurse Yoshi had trusted with her. Kara fixes the child's bearing with the Sextant, and the chase runs west — past the burning plantation of **Grenphal** and the rescued viceroy **Couch** — to **Bask Falls Barrow**, the ancestral tomb of House Gallidann, where the Forsworn and their dark-fey patrons work a terrible rite. The barrow's walls are a history in stone.

### What the barrow remembers — the first Infanta

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> The Hall of Stories records the deep history of the Infantas. In the age the elves call **the Heavenfall** — the Time of Troubles — a great cosmic mist descended and cut the gods off from the world:

> Although a mystery at the time, it is now known that the mist was conjured by Sylvanus, the highest god of elves, to conceal his plans to invade the Underdark and bring his daughter Lolth to heel. His plan failed.

Severed from their creations, the gods warred and some fell to earth. On this island the first [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) was born: the cast-out moon-goddess **Elistrae** — innocent sister of Lolth — entered an unborn human child through its mother's prayers and grew into the living goddess-queen **Elistrada**. Her guardian, the elf-noble [Star Quendi](/people/star-quendi/), daughter of House-founder **Eldamir**, raised her, ruled in her name, and "now sits immortal in the throne forests of **Arboria**, in the deepest heart of the Feywild." Quendi's son **Renos**, and his son **Daenan**, kept the island after her. Eldamir's brother [Calix](/people/calix-the-betrayer/) refused the grave — he became a *baelnorn*, stored fragments of his soul across the lesser isles, allied with **Queen Maab**, goddess of air and darkness, and rose again and again to seize the island's power.

The infant the heroes are guarding is **Elistrae reborn**, and the rite at Bask Falls is an attempt to bind Star Quendi's spirit into a mortal child so that Maab's faction can rule the Moonshae as Elistrada once did.

The rite is broken — the returned hag Sycorax and the spectral cat-fiend **Greymalkin** turn on one another over the prize — and the Infanta is recovered unharmed. The heroes learn the cost of harming her: the **Cries of Elistrae**, screams that "shake the Earth, wracking the entire island and bringing the walls of Harloch to the ground." Before they leave they may commune with the echo of Star Quendi, who blesses them and names their charge:

> Who are you, champions? Who are you, champions of Elistrae and the Moonshae Isles? Tell me your names so I may sing songs of you among the gildergreens in throne forests of Arboria… But she mustn't be raised as a god. She must be a child, living beneath the Gildergreen.

## Part V — Harloch

Carrying the Infanta back toward the cult, the party reaches the walled town of **Harloch** on a day of festival — and into the arms of the Empire. Commander **Carlysle St. Cloud**, brother of the captain they drowned, has arrived with a fresh garrison, claiming a great victory over the Forsworn that the heroes know to be a lie. A letter taken from an Imperial courier reveals the deeper scheme: Leftfork Farm and all its people have been **sold** to a buyer who means to mine its moonstone, with the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) brokering the deal. Beneath the town's derelict **Old Temple** lies a crypt, the **Ghost of Aldawan**, and the **Gauntlet of Aldawan**; the festival erupts into an assassination attempt, and the chapter scatters into a dozen threads as the messengers of Queen Maab begin to stalk the party openly.

## Part VI — Where the Hearth Is

The garrison marches out and leaves Harloch undefended; the Jarl, already saved once by the party from St. Cloud, begs them to make the town their home and offers to sell them the Old Temple. Buying it, the heroes give themselves a base, a refuge for the Cult of Elistrae — and a name. **They become "Temple Holdings, LLC,"** the holding that gives the whole campaign its title. A new thread opens here: **Fhastine Mageblood**, the half-elf apothecary of *The Dram*, keeps a journal she cannot read and a moon-marked riddle of a missing mother — a clue pointing past Harloch toward the [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/).

## Into Arc II

The final session splits the party. While Yoshi, Fulori and Couch race back to shelter the Infanta and her cult at Leftfork — now hunted by the elf-lich Calix himself — Kara and Brother Magnus follow Fhastine's clue. A weathered dragonborn healer recognises the two symbols from the journal and leads them to a mine beneath **Snowy Mountain**, where a dead explorer left a letter for her daughter:

> I have found its belly, but in breaking it I have undone myself… I've come so close to understanding. I've written it all down for you, but I fear you won't be able to read it for many many centuries. If you find me, please, push on and find the mother stone.

After the shelter at Leftfork, the party returned the Infanta to safety at the Gildergreen and went on into Motherstone. Dave's recap on the eve of the second arc: "you guys return the infanta to safety at the Gildergreen and then wandered through ancient titanic pipes into the hidden city of Motherstone." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-01-26)</small> Couch reached Motherstone later, through a side adventure with Yoshi. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-04-23)</small>

### What was buried under the mountain

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> Climbing the borehole for what feels like a mile, the explorers break through a blasted steel casing into something impossible — "a huge, metal tunnel… at least five stories high and perfectly round," a turbine hall, and at last a **Power Station**:

> Ascending from the hatch, you step up into a building so huge and ancient, but somehow far more advanced than any you have seen before. Blue light flows up through tiny visible conduits in the stone, humming from the turbines below. The light flows into man-sized pedestals throughout the room. On some of these pedestals, pale blue moonstones rest and glow in the energy.

Guarded by clockwork spiders, guardians and a centurion, this buried engine — and the hunt for the Motherstone the dead elf died seeking — carries directly into [Arc II — Motherstone](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/).
