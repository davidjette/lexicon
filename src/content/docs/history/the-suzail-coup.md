**Coup d'état · Castle Danzig, [Suzail](/places/suzail/) · Candlenights Eve, Age of the Infanta**

*Also known as:* the coup

The **Suzail Coup** was the seizure of Castle Danzig in [Suzail](/places/suzail/), capital of Cormyr, on Candlenights Eve in the Age of the Infanta, when the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) killed the archmagi of the [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) and turned King Glenn Danzig into a snail. [Kara](/people/kara/), disguised as the king, persuaded Princess Roseanne to marry [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), and Lord Krapsmere sent [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) against the War Wizards. In the crisis that followed an Infanta self-destructed and brought a tower down on the party, Zanzibar died and was pulled back, and the party carried the nature Infanta, [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), to Cormanthor. The king, turned back from a snail, offered Roseanne's hand to Zanzibar anyway, and after [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) Zanzibar married her and was crowned king of Cormyr.

## Before the coup

The party reached Suzail during Candlenights. King Glenn Danzig believed Zanzibar dead, "perishing in his mission to find an infanta for Cormyr to call their own", and with the Obus Infanta awake and the elves and orcs threatening he had turned to the War Wizards for counsel. They urged him to invade the Dalelands and "take the center in order to stand a chance against Waterdeep". The Citadel held plans for a frontal assault on [Myth Drannor](/places/myth-drannor/), and the War Wizards wanted [Una](/people/una/) back "to conduct a world war". The Duchess of Marsember, in the castle entertaining the royal children for the holidays, distrusted the War Wizards and wanted to avoid open war with the elves. <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small>

Dave tied the crisis to the first choice the party made in the Age of the Infanta: "Our first game we decided whether to openly engage with the nations or keep Motherstone a secret. This is the outcome of having a secret team of superheroes trying to affect the affairs of man on the DL!" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16)</small> Patrick, who played [Pim](/people/pim/), credited "Pim's Criminal Contacts" with getting the party "right into the castle". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-18)</small>

## The night in Castle Danzig

Dave, the morning after:

> Well, you nearly killed yourselves and the king and his kids and somehow orchestrated a fragile coup to make Zanzibar king of cormyr.

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-06</small>

The party stormed Castle Danzig, killed the archmagi and turned the king into a snail, leaving blood and smoke in the throne room. Kara impersonated the king and convinced Roseanne that she was to marry Crown Prince Zanzibar "to unite the thrones of Suzail and Punis under a single family". The Duchess of Marsember was last seen being whisked away from the fight by a War Wizard. The bodies of the dead wizards, dragged to the courtyard, "have turned inert and stiff". Explosions had torn through Candlenights Eve, and word was spreading that the royal family had been harmed. <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small> Revan's player LaMont: "Stabbing his prone body is my career accomplishment." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>

The same night Pim met Emery Grim of the Zentarim, and a fortune teller told the party the tale of [the Three Brothers](/lore/the-three-brothers/). Gordon Lightfoot expected the party at the Shrine of Elistrae in twenty-four hours. <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small>

The spell would hold: "He’s a snail and it’s for like 8 hours so don’t get cocky". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-08)</small>

## A matter of hours

> you'll have a matter of hours before word gets out that there was a coup and people start believing whether or not it was the war wizards or someone else who did it, and whether the king is real or not. and news flash, the wizards will be working against you in that fact and they have a campus in the city

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16</small>

The king's military commander and his daughter believed the party. The War Wizards who escaped knew the story was false, and "the duchess knows you're there and probably cares very much if you squish her nephew". The duchess did not know about the snail, or that Kara was not the king. Krapsmere was sent "to attack the War Wizard campus and kill all war wizards on site, a bloody and potential devastating task for the purple dragons": "essentiall, the army was ordered to kill the air force on sight". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16)</small> LaMont, who played Revan: "Hahaha that’s 2 civil wars in a row. Why can’t we just sneak ANYWHERE!"

Krapsmere ordered the arrest of every War Wizard in Suzail, calling them "robes". Fighting in the Wizards' intelligence office in the Citadel left twenty dead. Most of the War Wizards fled to the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/), and at least twenty more gathered at the campus in the east of the city, behind forcefields. <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small> Many of Cormyr's paladins and knights believed the War Wizards had attempted a coup, "because they had to fight and kill the guys you mind controlled - however - the sudden appearance of the thought dead Zanzibar and this crew of highly capable adventurers leaves some doubt as to what happened." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-21)</small>

### The succession

Roseanne is the king's eldest daughter, "Rosanne Conner-Barr Danzig first of her name". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-21)</small> Dave on the options:

> The king has named Roseanne his heir, Zanzibar marrying her would combine the thrones of Cormyr and Punis in the next generation\
> Zanzibar can obviously claim the throne if he wants and if Roseanne makes him king that would also be legal\
> Or he can serve as prince regent to Roseanne’s Queen

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24</small>

Roseanne is "savvy" and was "raised for this very occasion"; she is "genuinely interested in furthering her place to take the throne as Queen of Men and to control an infanta herself", and distrustful of the War Wizards. <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small>

## The crisis

The next session opened after Kara's talk with Roseanne, with the king still a snail and everyone back in the throne room. "You have not rested", Dave told the players, "But neither have your enemies". It was run in real time, with each character's task and travel distance tracked. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>

The War Wizards' research summary, found on their campus, described their Infanta: "Infanta can be controlled by subjecting them to psychological torment. This sets their default nature to self destruct, we consider this a safety feature." <small>(Arc III, VIII Handouts)</small> Their nature Infanta, a six-year-old Sembian boy called Tiko, had been "the victim of intense experimentation by the War Wizards". <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small>

> FYI Roland you very nearly were killed when the Infanta in the room next to you self destructed and blew you and Drefan 300 ft away and collapsed that whole tower on your friends. Everyone survived though, except that Infanta :(\
> But you got the nature Infanta to Cormanthor and won the day

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-27</small>

Zanzibar died in the fighting. Dave: "I'm obsessed with zanzibar dying, seeing the shadowfell and the city of judgment on fire, and then getting ripped back to the world and called a hero". Of the whole session: "that one was gruesome. but you guys stopped them from destroying the entire city." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> The identity of the Infanta who self-destructed is not recorded.

## Aftermath

The party was left to "come to terms with the elves of Myth Drannor" and to decide whether to storm the Arcaneum. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-27)</small> They did, and after his exile at the hands of the Arcaneum Zanzibar returned to [Punis](/places/punis/) and was crowned King of Punis. The Duchess of Marsember was killed, and Zanzibar gave Marsember to [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) as his hold, and it became Darkcember. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

When King Glenn Danzig was turned back from a snail, he saw why Zanzibar had held the coup, appreciated his "rooting out the evil of Valen and his tainting by the Leviathan", and offered Roseanne's hand in marriage anyway. Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne after the Battle of the River Lis. Later the king fell ill, and Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr after the wedding. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>
