**Protest, bombing and massacre · Upper City, Sharn · Sharn Ep 2, and the nights before it**

The **Cogborn protest, explosion and riot** was a peaceful [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) march in the Upper City of Sharn that an explosion tore apart, on the night of the grand opening of [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s restaurant [POSÉ](/places/pose/) (Sharn Ep 2). Twenty-one members of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) and forty-three Cogborn civilians were killed; shots from within the crowd struck the balcony and killed the news anchor [Candice Kurt](/people/candice-kurt/), whose death led the Empire's coverage. The Iron Veil answered overnight with raids, searches, forced removals and checkpoints dividing the upper city from the lower, and that same night [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) executed Cogborn prisoners live on every screen in Sharn. Who set the explosion has never been established. The Inevitables were inside POSÉ throughout.

## R.U.S.T. CASUALTY LEDGER

A peaceful protest in the Upper City erupted into a deadly riot when an explosion tore through the crowd. [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) lost twenty-one members. The [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) community lost forty-three civilians: workers, elders, parents, children.

The Iron Veil's retaliation was swift and merciless. Raids, searches and forced removals intensified overnight. Checkpoints went up dividing the upper city from the lower. Soldiers flooded the district. It accelerated the collapse of Cogborn life in Sharn and left R.U.S.T. with only one option: escape.

## SHARN NEWS NETWORK — THE POSÉ ATTACK

The Empire's account is about a different casualty. As the crowd surges up the grand staircase, shots ring out **from within the protesters**, striking the balcony and killing celebrity news anchor [Candice Kurt](/people/candice-kurt/). The official story is that she was assassinated by a group of protesters from the Cogs. Broadcasts covering the attack, the riot and the tightening of Iron Veil security ran on every crystal screen in the city for days afterward.

Two things about that account do not sit still. The shots came from inside a crowd that had come to protest peacefully and was already being torn apart by a bomb. And the death that led the coverage was a news anchor's, not the forty-three.

## THE EXPLOSION

No source in the rebellion's possession names who set it. R.U.S.T.'s own record says only that an explosion tore through the crowd, and that the Iron Veil used the chaos as justification. Whether the bomb was rebel, Imperial or something else has never been established, and the people best placed to investigate spent that night abducting [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the Black Doves through a hidden elevator route and did not go back.

## THE EXECUTIONS

That same night, every screen in Sharn turned red. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), clad in full armor atop her Imperial warship, executes Cogborn prisoners live for the entire city to witness.

> The message is unmistakable: dissent will be crushed.

It was the first time the Inevitables saw her.

## WHAT IT SET IN MOTION

The Cogborn were driven under the city and sealed into a collapsing tunnel pocket. Getting them out required a deal with [Blair](/people/blair/) of the Underhive, the clearing of the Drain Snakes, an ancient chamber full of spiders and a stone golem, and finally the long walk through the Spine to Gorgonhorn. At [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/), R.U.S.T. came to the table with three demands: Warforged citizenship, the arrest of the Iron Veil engineers, and a new infrastructure department. By nightfall Warforged forces were offering troops and engineers to restore the Cogs and a new Department of Infrastructure existed.
