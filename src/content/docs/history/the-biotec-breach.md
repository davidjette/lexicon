**Corporate infiltration · BioTec Sharn facility, Upper Central District · Sharn Ep 1**

The BioTec Breach was the infiltration of the [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) facility in Sharn's Upper Central District by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), their first job for the rebellion, recorded in Sharn Ep 1. Briefed by the rebel operative [Izaak](/people/izaak/) at The Astra Bar, they used a keycard lifted from the junior enhancement technician [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) to reach a restricted laboratory, where a panicked scientist unleashed three undead-Warforged hybrid units and gave the rebellion its first sight of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) They carried out the Ghost Chip and a folder of encrypted data, solved a hidden puzzle in Skyroot Park that opened the way into [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/), and each took a Ghost Chip implant from Izaak.

Newly arrived in Sharn, [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) were told to meet the rebel operative [Izaak](/people/izaak/) at The Astra Bar in the upper city. Their assignment: infiltrate the corporate labs of [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) and steal a prototype known only as the Ghost Chip. They had come to Sharn a year after the Leef / Newham fighting. The Sharn arc that began here lasted weeks: by Sharn Ep 16 they had been in the city nearly two weeks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-05-08)</small> The Korth arc followed three years after the fall of Sharn.

## THE TARGET

BioTec presents publicly as a medical research institute. In fact it is a covert development arm for the Empire, supplying the Iron Veil and military intelligence with prototype devices and classified enhancements. Its Sharn facility carries no Arcane Eye security inside the building — an absence considered deliberate, and read by the rebellion as proof of research that could not risk being recorded. Exterior patrols still ran.

The Ghost Chip itself is an implant module that makes its wearer undetectable to ArcEye surveillance, creating a persistent blind spot around the implanted individual. The decision to exclude ArcEyes from the facility had been taken by the lead scientist on the project, Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), citing data contamination risks.

## HOW THEY GOT IN

Groundwork had been laid by [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/), who spent weeks of surveillance and strategic intoxication on a junior enhancement technician named [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) at The Astra after his shifts. Kross gave up the Ghost Chip's function, Menka's name, and the existence of a restricted laboratory door off the East lab that he had never been permitted through.

The trio befriended Kross while he was drunk, escorted him home, and lifted his keycard.

## WHAT CAME OUT OF THE RESTRICTED DOOR

The break-in spiralled the moment private security found them. A panicked scientist unleashed three horrific undead-Warforged hybrid units, never before seen — corpse skin, mechanical limbs, masked faces hiding features that were coming apart.

They barely survived it. This is the rebellion's first sight of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/): a hybridisation programme under Menka, fusing humanoid bodies with Warforged cybernetics to produce obedient, tireless troops. Three years later there were enough of them to be thrown in waves against a Cyrean portal in the Mournlands held by the Silver Flame, and eight were still standing at the ruins of [Zilspar Farm](/history/the-destruction-of-zilspar-farm/) when the Inevitables landed there.

## WHAT THEY CARRIED OUT

The Ghost Chip, and a folder of encrypted high-level data. Following Izaak's instructions they solved a hidden puzzle in Skyroot Park, which opened the way into [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/), where they met Zero of Sharn and slept while the city did.

The folder, redacted down at the Nest, produced the name **Project EDEN** — and Izaak then implanted a Ghost Chip in each of them, which is how three foreigners moved through Sharn for the weeks of action that followed, up to the fall of the city.

## THE TAIL

Martin Kross went missing and turned up eventually as [Blair](/people/blair/)'s prisoner in the Underhive, a control chip in his skull; when [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) cut it out he could finally say the name of BioTec's true private owner. Edwin. [Dr. Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) — the Cog Collector, who had been abducting Cogborn labourers for over four years and shipping their prepared bodies to the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) hospital.

> Their break-in quickly spirals out of control when private security confronts them and a panicked scientist accidentally unleashes three horrific undead warforged hybrid units never before seen.

**Result:** Ghost Chip and encrypted files taken · Project EDEN exposed · Martin Kross burned · the trio inducted into the Nest
