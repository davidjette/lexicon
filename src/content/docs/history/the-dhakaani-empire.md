**Goblinoid empire · Western Khorvaire · c. 16,000 to 5,000 years ago · Fallen**

*Also known as:* the Empire of Dhakaan · the Age of Dhakaan (its era)

The Dhakaani Empire, or the Empire of Dhakaan, was a goblinoid nation that dominated most of western Khorvaire from around 16,000 to 5,000 years ago, a span scholars call the Age of Dhakaan. <small>(published Eberron canon)</small> Sharn, Wroat and [Korth](/places/korth/) are built on its ruins. Beneath [Sharn](/places/sharn/) its tunnels run under the sewers and the Cogs, and in them stands its relic the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), where [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) performed the Eternal Guardian ritual. The archivist [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) traced those tunnels for the rebellion, and the Inevitables crossed a Dhakaani chamber in [Sharn Ep 12](/sessions/sharn-ep-12-the-spine-and-the-stone/) and reached the Forge in [Sharn Ep 15](/sessions/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding/).

## The Age of Dhakaan

Nico set out the empire's history during the Sharn arc:

> "The Dhakaani Empire, or the Empire of Dhakaan, was a mighty goblinoid nation that dominated most of the western continent of Khorvaire over a period extending from around 16,000 to 5,000 years ago. This 11,000-year-period is called the Age of Dhakaan by scholars. Numerous cities of the modern age are built atop the ruins of the Empire of Dhakaan, including Sharn, Wroat, and Korth."

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-29)</small>

## The ruins beneath Sharn

The undercity of Sharn is layered sewer lines, storm drains, maintenance shafts, abandoned sub-transit routes and collapsed Cogs tunnels, and beneath them lies Dhakaani work older than the city. The blocked tunnels near the Cogborn's hiding place lead into ancient Dhakaani systems.

**Renn Tal**, an archivist of the Old Histories Wing of the Sharn Library, is trained in pre-Galifar ritual history and Dhakaani archaeology. Her research into the Eternal Guardian took her into the Lower City, where she interviewed Cogborn elders, studied sewer-line schematics and traced forgotten Dhakaani tunnel systems. [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/) brought her into the Lower City network, and [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) put her to deciphering Dhakaani symbols and mapping the ancient tunnel routes.

In [Sharn Ep 12](/sessions/sharn-ep-12-the-spine-and-the-stone/) the trio entered the long-sealed Chamber Room on the route to [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), a webbed hall guarded by four giant spiders and an ancient stone golem. Among the magic items they recovered was an Ioun Stone carrying old Dhakaani runes. The runes on the golem and on the stone matched the writings in Renn's books. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-29)</small>

In [Sharn Ep 15](/sessions/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding/), deep in the Dhakaani tunnels, [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) found he could open a membrane-like door, and stepping through it brought flashes of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)'s voice. A mind flayer and infected Cogborn ambushed the party in the chamber beyond, on the approach to the Forge.

## The Forge of Binding

The Forge of Binding is a Dhakaani relic, used to bind oaths, spirits, weapons and command structures. By the Eternal Guardian writings, E'noch and Varyn went to Sharn and down through its Dhakaani ruins to reach it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-29)</small> There Varyn Crona and E'noch Qualanthri drew on forbidden Vol rites to bind Varyn to Emperor Kaius as his sworn protector from birth. Renn deciphered the inscription on the Forge's puzzle door, and at the Forge Eric performed the Rite of Recall that raised the Echo of Varyn Crona.

## Goblin work in the Empire

Dave: "the crimson sun was forged using old goblin shit, everyone is stealing from some one a thousand years ago". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-13)</small> He later said: "the architects of the Crimson Sun and the Kaius dynasty obviously used every dark art they could find, including goblin smithing, dwarvish and infernal smithing, draconic blood magic, necromancy, artificing, elemental magic, red khyber, black khyber, everything". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small> See [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) and [the Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/).
