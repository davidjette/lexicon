**Pilgrimage · Necis 1 · December 2023 to May 2024**

*Also known as:* the pilgrimage · the Shrike plot

**The Shrike pilgrimage** was the journey of [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), called Sandy, across the planet [Necis](/places/necis/) to [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) in search of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), during the second and third seasons of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. It followed the discovery that parts of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) were hiding the reality of the Shrike from mortals. On Necis 1 the three officers set out on a sojourn that severed them from [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), crossed the sea of grass by windwheel, and reached the Time Tombs, where they saw a vision of an army of shrikes. The pilgrimage led into [the finale of the arc](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/). Its story was adapted from Dan Simmons's novel *Hyperion*.

## Background

The crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) first met the Shrike in a horror episode played on 7 January 2023. It was very hard to damage and very deadly, and the party retreated. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small> After a session in October 2023 Dave asked whether the crew would bring Sol and Rachel to find the Shrike, take them back to Sigil in hope of a last-minute intervention, or make them comfortable for their last two months together. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-21)</small>

In the session of 17 November 2023 the crew learned four things, in Dave's words:

> 1) the lexicon has evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas 2) some are conspiring to hide the reality of the shrike from mortals for some reason 3) elements of astral command may also be involved somehow (the grey cloaks) 4) you are headed to blow the lid off of it at Necis

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

In the same session the **Voodoo Boys** operated on an unconscious member of the party, who came away with an implant, and hacked Jim's brain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> While the command crew was away, the Zanzibar suffered [the Astralex attack](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/).

## Necis 1

On 22 December 2023 play rejoined Jim, Caprica and Sandy "on the remote planet of Necis 1". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22)</small>

In the session of 5 January 2024 the three set out on a sojourn that effectively severed them from Astral Command. Dave said it would make them "the greatest officers of Astral Commands thousand year history" <small>(sic)</small>, "whether you live or die". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small>

## The sea of grass

The pilgrimage set off on 2 February 2024: "we’re off to see the Shrike". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-03)</small> On 9 February the pilgrims crossed the sea of grass aboard a druidic windwheel, the ***Edmund Fitzgerald***, in a multi-day journey played as skill challenges. They passed their saves against poisonous pollen and fought a CR 8 semi-hydra. News reached them of an orbital strike on **Lightfoot**. **Henri** joined the pilgrimage and brought an unexpected perspective to it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-09; 2024-02-10)</small>

The session of 16 February ended the season with the pilgrims about to reach the Shrike. Play then jumped back in time to the flashback of [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-17)</small>

## Cybrids

When play returned to the present in April 2024, [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/)'s third wish took the Zanzibar to [Sigil](/places/sigil/), and the pilgrims were left extremely far away and out of contact. Seen from the ground, an attack looked like the work of beholders, but the attackers were found to be cybrids, controlled by Lexicon entities. Jim and his crew had gone off as an independent force, all brought to a world that had been erased from the Lexicon, because Caprica followed [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/) and brought them along. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## The Time Tombs

On 12 April 2024 the pilgrims entered the Time Tombs, a dungeon crawl of two or three rooms at a time between rests, against snakes and sandworms. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-12; 2024-04-13)</small>

In the session of 19 April Dave read verse in play, ending with the lines "I was lost, but saved." and "I am a translator." The name **Zith'ra** was given in play, and each player earned 2,500 XP. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-19; 2024-04-20)</small> The verse of the pilgrimage includes [*Martin's Final Cantos*](/items/martins-final-cantos/), a poem in three books telling of a forgotten goddess who slew a leviathan and vanished from every mortal mind. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-20)</small> Dave ruled that the deeds of [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/), the true nature of Cthulhu and the origins of the eldritch machines had been erased from memory and "are now cursed information which only madmen and poets ever understand". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

The pilgrims had innocents with them whom they would not leave behind, so they did not delve too deep into the palace, and waited. "The air of death is palpable." They saw the future: the army of shrikes and "the approximational spirits of cypher". They also found people on **the tree of pain**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

## The white light

On 10 May 2024 the party walked into the white light, while [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) and [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/) were set to try to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small> [The finale](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/) followed on 31 May. Necis, where the Time Tombs stood and the Shrike originated, is where the climax of Starfall took place. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small>
