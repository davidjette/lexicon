**Battle and finale · [Necis](/places/necis/) · Played 31 May 2024**

*Also known as:* the season finale

**The wreck of the Zanzibar** was the finale of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, played on 31 May 2024, in which [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) took critical damage in the fight against [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/). [Caprica](/people/caprica/) died finding the answer to [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/), [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/) cast the final Wish, the crew was scattered, and [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) fled [Sigil](/places/sigil/) and the Starsong broadcast into deep space. The finale closed the arc for the crew of Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/). The climax of Starfall took place at Necis.

## Background

The final episodes followed [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/). A session in late April 2024 ended with a blast as the party left the senate, and the next, played on 2 May, picked up with an assassination in which Commander [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/) was killed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27; 2024-05-02; 2024-05-03)</small> On 10 May the party walked into the white light, and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) and Irina were set to try to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small> The penultimate session was played on 24 May.

## The battle

Before the finale [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) received a barrier tattoo. Jim had one as well; when [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) upgraded his own and Katrina's, Jim's upgraded with them, raising his armour class from 13 to 17. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small> Each player ran at least three characters, and Dave ran five player characters as well as the monsters. The Shrike was played from the Shrike (Hyperion) stat block, and a turtle ship took part. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

The Shrike had been taken over by a mortal man to thwart a mortal woman who was enacting the Lexicon's will. It was drawn there by the Starsong and merged with it "in heart of their time bridge" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

The Zanzibar took critical damage, and so much happened at the end that it closed the campaign's first arc. All the wishes had a major impact on how everything went. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Two wishes are recorded. [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/)'s third wish, made in April 2024, had "snapped everyone all over the place with no warning". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06; 2024-06-09)</small> At the finale Irina cast the final Wish, and in the words of her player "had no idea what she was doing". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Caprica's player said "Th Zanzibar and the Stonecypher got torn apart together" <small>(sic)</small>, referring to [the SSJ Stonecypher](/items/the-ssj-stonecypher/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

[Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), called Sandy, had her best session of the campaign. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## Caprica

Caprica died in finding the answer to the Starsong. Her player did not expect it, and said that Caprica "probably wasn’t expecting that finding the answer to the star song would kill her, but she got her answers". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-03)</small> An avatar of Cypher spoke to her in the Starsong. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small> In the days after the finale Dave held out hope that she was alive: a shuttle was left on the Zanzibar, and Rachel, or Cypher, might have rescued her off screen. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-03)</small> He captioned an image of her:

> Do not weep for the singer of stars, her song rings through the phlo forever

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-03)</small>

## The Lexicon

At the end of the finale the Anachron, or the Lexicon, disappeared from Sigil to avoid the broadcast and escaped to the depths of space, where it could continue to exist. Dave's expectation was that before it could be exposed for trying to betray humanity, it fled into deep space "to become a hostile AI empire far away", with the caveat "Unless it doesn’t idk I’m not in charge". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> People would know that it fled Sigil to escape the Starsong, and it is unknown whether they knew it had been the villain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## Location

Necis, a destroyed planet whose remnants are where the Time Tombs stood and the Shrike originated, is where the climax of Starfall happened at the end of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> Before the finale the Zanzibar had reached Sigil, carried there by Carlos Vega's third wish, and at the end the Lexicon disappeared from Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06; 2024-06-02)</small>

## Aftermath

Sandy carried the memory of what happened on Necis and at the Time Tombs. Because Carlos had scattered everyone without warning, she did not know what had happened to her fellow crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small> Of Jim's story, Dave wrote that he "lost many of his friends, his ship, and ended back up where he started". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> Caprica's player gave [Se7en](/people/se7en/) "the best ending, serving his maker drinks on a beach". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>

*[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* is set fifteen years later. By then Caprica is long dead, and the Company wants to resurrect her to help find the true source of the awakened Starsong. Jim lives under the name Charles Largeberries on a small mining asteroid, wanted for dereliction of duty and destruction of Astral Command property. [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) carries "the mysterious bottle" with him, and Carlos, bound as a djinn, is back in play by November 2024. Katrina McQuaid is dead, and a prison bears her name: the Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-11; 2024-06-12; 2024-11-22)</small> In that arc [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and Se7en saw "the final fight on the Zanzibar" through one of the time doors of the Sphinx Tomb. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>
