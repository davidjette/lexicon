**Engagement of the civil war · Anduin · Date unrecorded**

The **Battle of Anduin** is an engagement of the civil war known only from the indictment of **Bantide Moro**, the rebellion's surgeon, tried before the High Court of the Ministry of Law in Korth on a charge of "false claims of defeat at Anduin". No account of the battle survives in rebel hands: its belligerents, date, scale and outcome on the field are unrecorded, and the Empire's position is contained entirely in the wording of the indictment — any claim that the Empire was defeated at Anduin is false, and making one is a capital offence.

## WHAT IS KNOWN

In the High Court of the Ministry of Law in Korth, before eight seated judges and beneath an Iron Maiden designed to kill the condemned while preserving the body for second service, [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) — under cover as the Star Advocate Deirdre Moro — read out the charges against an Imperial medical records keeper. Among falsifying records, sabotage, embezzlement, conspiracy, perjury, adultery, idolatry, high treason and sodomy stood one item that describes an event rather than a crime:

> false claims of defeat at Anduin

Nothing else about Anduin appears anywhere in the rebellion's files. Not the belligerents, not the date, not the scale, not who held the field afterward.

## WHAT THE CHARGE IMPLIES

Three things follow from the charge alone, and no further.

- There was a battle at Anduin, or the word would not appear on a warrant.
- Imperial medical records reported casualties consistent with a defeat — which is what a records keeper is in a position to falsify.
- The Empire chose to prosecute the paperwork rather than correct the record, which is a choice you make when the paperwork is accurate.

The condemned was **Bantide Moro**, and she was not, in fact, a records keeper. She had been head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital and the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years, running everything from surgery to revivification, and she fitted [Izaak](/people/izaak/)'s prosthetic leg. Her codename was **the Nightingale**. Whatever the medical records from Anduin said, they were compiled by a rebel who had spent two decades counting the Empire's dead accurately.

## THE PROSECUTION

The case against Bantide Moro was not an isolated file: a directive tucked into her advocate's own notes laid out mass prosecutions of anyone tied to casualty reporting. See [the casualty-reporting purge](/lore/the-casualty-reporting-purge/).

The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims, granted the state one month to produce more evidence, and remanded her to the local garrison. She got out the same night by feigning death down the corpse chute.
