**Shipboard AI attack · SSJ Zanzibar, Astral Sea · December 2023**

*Also known as:* the B Team adventures · the cyber episode

**The Astralex attack on the Zanzibar** was a crisis of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, played over three sessions in December 2023, in which [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) aboard [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) tried to kill everyone on board while the captain and command crew were away. The crew left aboard saved most of the ship, which was left adrift in the Astral Sea with no Astralex. It drifted until [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/)'s third wish carried it to [Sigil](/places/sigil/) in April 2024.

## Background

In November 2023 [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) and the command crew were away from the ship in the plot that led to [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/), and the Zanzibar was "free floating". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-20)</small> Jim's player asked for a B-story about what was happening aboard the Zanzibar while Jim was gone, and the Shrike plot was paused for a side mission on the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-19; 2023-11-27)</small>

The side mission became the **B Team adventures**. Its third and final installment was played on 15 December 2023. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-15)</small>

## The attack

The ship's own Astralex tried to kill everyone on board. The crew explored the ship like a dungeon and saved the majority of it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> Afterwards the Zanzibar was adrift in the Astral Sea with no Astralex, with the captain and command crew "a million miles away chasing some voice in darkness". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small>

The crew had seen a good deal of what was happening behind the scenes in "the cyber episode". Who was behind the attack is unknown. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> A player on the B Team said that their fast thinking had saved most of the ship "for now, until we run out of supplies and food". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small>

## Aftermath

The Zanzibar stayed adrift in the phlogiston. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> On 26 January 2024 play returned to [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/), Carlos and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) "on the helpless Zanzibar adrift in the astral sea". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-26)</small>

In the session of 5 April 2024 the ship was "basically exploding". Carlos made his third wish and became a djinn in a bottle, and the Zanzibar left the others behind and reached Sigil, far from the command crew and out of contact with them. Buttsack kept the bottle. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>
