**Arcane catastrophe · The kingdom of Cyre, now the Mournlands · Twenty-one years before the Imperial pageant**

**The Mourning** is the arcane catastrophe that destroyed the kingdom of Cyre in a single day, twenty-one years before the Imperial pageant that commemorates it. Only the [Warforged](/lore/warforged/) survived and [the Last War](/history/the-last-war/) ended. What is left is a poisoned country called the Mournlands, wrapped in thick toxic fog that requires a mask to enter, where the dead do not stay down and something called Mistborn illness spreads through the mist. The Empire attributes the event to Emperor Kaius III and the Crimson Sun, calls it a mercy, and celebrates the anniversary as **the Rejuvenation**; no other party has claimed responsibility for it.

## THE IMPERIAL ACCOUNT

Kaius III stood on a mountaintop above the battle on his Great Eagle, flanked by his loyal thirteen. He raised his greatsword and split the darkened clouds to reveal a great **Crimson Sun**. He pointed the sword back down at the battle, and the sun flashed bright and hot upon the land of Cyre.

The Empire's telling makes this a mercy that ended a hundred years of slaughter, and the survivors a congregation. The anniversary is a celebration. Its name is **the Rejuvenation**. See [A Bard's Tale](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/) for the full performed text.

## THE PROGENITOR'S ACCOUNT

[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) does not tell it as a story at all. He built one.

Inside Mt. Silicon he showed the Inevitables a weaponised catastrophe: a controlled recreation of the Mourning itself, held as a final option to wipe out everything before [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) could turn the world into an undead empire. He spoke of the Crimson Sun and the power it had once unleashed, and he set it off. A silent red dome bloomed over the battlefield the party had just been lifted from, pushed past the Mournland border, and was visible from Thronehold.

He calls it **the Ruby Nova**. His terms were plain: stop Uriel before she rings the bells, or he ends the world first, and only his kind survive it.

> Only the Warforged survived.

<small>— the pageant, on the first Mourning. It is also Pierce's design specification for the second.</small>

## THE THIRD MOURNING

There has already been another. When [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) killed Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) in [Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/), a large otherworldly explosion collapsed the city and corrupted the region into a toxic uninhabitable waste. The survivors named it **New Mourning**. Nobody has explained that one either.

Three catastrophes, then, of the same shape and no agreed mechanism: Cyre, Newham, and the Ruby Nova. Only the third has a named author, and he built it by studying the first.

## SOMETHING IS CHANGING IN THERE

Before the Thronehold Summit, Locke Pierce sent [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) a **Cyrean Red Pomegranate** — a fruit believed extinct since the Mourning — and it arrived large and fresh. He spoke of "new growth" in the Mournlands. Whether it is an offering, a warning or a sign of the land healing is unclear. Gemma refused to eat it, took one for study at Zilspar Farm, and later handed it to Jessica Grimpledink in Sharn for study.

The Mournlands also remain a battlefield. [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) hybrid units and undead press against Silver Flame lines around a great Cyrean portal, and every paladin who falls there rises again within moments and joins the other side.
