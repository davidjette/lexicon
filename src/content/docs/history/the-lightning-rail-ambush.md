**Rebel operation, failed · The Talenta Plains Express, between Newham and Korth · Campaign 1**

The **lightning rail ambush** was a failed rebel operation aboard the Talenta Plains Express between Newham and Korth in the first campaign, mounted by [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) to take the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) off the train. The bell stayed in Imperial hands. The actor [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), exposed as a rebel, was killed; the singer [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), exposed with him, escaped, and the Imperial media announced that she had murdered him. A statue to Stamos was raised in Korth. Lady Glow was taken later, handed to [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) in the throne room of Emperor Kaius III, and killed and raised as a ghoul.

After the [Greenbluff recovery](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/), [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) was tasked with returning the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) to the Empire by rail. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) were hired to secure the train and eliminate any threats. Men in fine coats came up from Korth to ride with the freight. The bell travelled under Assembly seal.

## IMPERIAL FIELD REPORT

Soon after the train left New Ham the group discovered that it was swarming with rebels. After a long and dangerous battle the group, with the help of some passengers and imperial soldiers, were able to defeat the rebels and make sure the Bell remained in the hands of the Empire.

The report also records incidental details. This is the first time the four met the up-and-coming restaurateur [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), and by Pierce's account it is also where Blaze and Esther Crona first met — the marriage, the penthouse and [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) all trace back to a fight on a train over a bell. Father White, a priest of the Sovereign Host, and his paladins fought alongside the Imperial party. The rebels had an airship, and their hideout in the Shadow Mount Forest was located from the wreckage.

## THE IMPERIAL BROADCAST

[Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), a famous singer known throughout Khorvaire, was exposed as a rebel during the battle and escaped the train. [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), a famous actor known throughout Khorvaire, was exposed as a rebel and did not.

The Imperial media announced that Lady Glow was a rebel and a terrorist, that she was responsible for the attack on the lightning rail, and that she had murdered John Stamos. A statue commemorating his life and talent was raised in Korth. The Empire mourned him publicly, as one of its own.

## FULCRUM'S ACCOUNT

The rebellion's version is the cover letter of a [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) courier packet delivered to the Chancellor of Sharn, signed only "I", and it is shorter.

> Fulcrum boarded that train to take it and we failed. That's where John Stamos died. The Empire told everyone that Lady Glow had murdered him, that she was a rebel. She was, of course, but so was he.

Pierce's own file is blunter still: *We killed John Stamos and blamed it on Lady Glow.* The record of Stamos names his killer as John C. LeBeefe, and notes that LeBeefe kept the man's eyeballs.

## WHAT BECAME OF LADY GLOW

She got clear of the train and stayed clear for a while. The four found and fought her again at a rebel base in the Dragon Watch Isles, and she escaped that too, in the company of [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) — who was at that time still running a seven-year infiltration of the rebellion on the Emperor's behalf.

An Imperial bulletin from the [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/) period reports that Duin had gone missing, that Glow was blamed for assassinating her, and that Glow was gravely injured during her arrest and sentenced to see out her life in medical confinement at Newham Hospital. That hospital was Uriel's.

What is certain is the end. In the throne room of Emperor Kaius III, Gabriel Duin unhooded a chained woman and handed her to Uriel Qualanthri as a gift. Uriel killed her in front of the Emperor and performed necromantic sex magic on the corpse, raising her as a ghoul. Fulcrum's cover letter finishes the thought:

> Uriel Qualanthri killed Lady Glow and stood her back up as a music box. It sings at the Emperor's bedside, they say. The sweetest voice in the world. Stamos died for this bell.

**Dating:** Greenbluff, and the rail journey that followed it, fall about ten years before the Inevitables. Fulcrum's bell file places the ambush at **twenty years ago** and ties it directly to Greenbluff; the rebel-faction record says Stamos was killed **over a decade ago**. The bell file is the more detailed of the two, and both figures stand as written.
