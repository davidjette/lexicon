**Citywide rising · Sharn · Sharn Ep 18 · Three years before the Korth arc**

The **fall of Sharn** is the citywide rising of Sharn Ep 18, three years before the Korth arc, in which [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) was killed on the balcony of her penthouse at the Veil Building and the Empire was expelled from the city. It began as a rescue, went citywide when [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) triggered [the broadcast](/lore/the-sharn-broadcast-override/) that showed the city what [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) had done to Esther's newborn son, and ended with Esther dying of her wounds after voiding the Iron Veil's pact citywide, ordering Uriel's death, and hurling her own airship into Uriel's statue above the Skyport. By dawn Sharn was free, leaderless and on the brink of collapse.

It began as a rescue. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) had descended on the Zeir rooftop the moment [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) fell, killed the Mother Prophet with a single strike, seized her son [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) and the chained [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), and lifted off into the night. [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) broadcast a full city lockdown. [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) told [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) where she would go: the penthouse at the Veil Building.

## THE BALCONY

They fought through the Iron Veil at the door. Inside, Eric saw the LeBeefe Eye sitting on the fireplace mantel and refused to pick it up. On the balcony stood Esther, her son, her two Honor Guards, Henry Heinrick, and Gemma — unchained now and terrified.

Richard tried to reason with her. Calcifer ran into his arms. Esther accused Richard of bringing her killers into her home.

Then Dario triggered [the broadcast](/lore/the-sharn-broadcast-override/): his pre-recorded message, and immediately behind it the reclaimed memory of the night Calcifer was born. The whole city watched [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) raise a hand over a newborn, mark him, still his breathing, and freeze only at his mother's scream.

The memory shattered Esther's composure. In a rage she revealed the true faces of her Honor Guards — undead members of the Council of 13 — and killed Henry Heinrick.

## THE FIGHT

Eric turned one of the undead guards and forced it from the fight; the other went off the balcony. Esther dropped all three heroes to nothing, repeatedly, and killed Dario's mount. Richard held Calcifer and used subtle sorcery to heal and bolster the trio without being seen to do it.

Halfway through, the sky above Sharn filled. Nest fighters, Leef soldiers, Silver Flame paladins, RUST runners, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s Hundreds, [Blair](/people/blair/) and her Underhive, and the pirate fleet of [the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) rose against the Iron Veil on every screen in the city. The rebellion had begun.

Dario struck her with the magic missile wand [Doppler](/people/doppler-klink/) had built him. Richard pulled Calcifer into his bedroom and cast darkness so the child would not see what came next.

## HER LAST TWO SENTENCES

Bleeding out and beaten, Esther Crona made her voice carry magically across the whole of Sharn.

> Your pact is void.

Every Iron Veil soldier in the city felt their pact collapse in the same instant. Longsword pact weapons vanished from their hands. Warlock spells evaporated. An occupying army was disarmed by its own commander in a single breath.

Then:

> … Kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri…

With the last of her strength she seized her own airship, the Black Veil, telekinetically and hurled it into the enormous statue of Uriel standing above the Skyport. The explosion destroyed the symbol of Imperial power over the city. She fell from the balcony and landed at the base of the Veil Building.

## WHAT IT LEFT

Dario broadcast her corpse live and declared that the Empire would fall. Iron Veil soldiers surrendered in droves; some fled; a small number vanished and became the seed of a hidden Iron Veil remnant. By dawn Sharn was free, leaderless, wounded and on the brink of collapse — which is the problem [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/) convened to solve the same day.

Esther was **thirty-one** when she died. She had been nineteen at Greenbluff and twenty-nine when the trio first arrived in her city; the Sharn arc spans about two in-world years and all three ages are correct.

Her dying command is still an open order. The Inevitables have been moving toward the Ivory Lazaret ever since.

**Result:** Lord Commander and Mayor killed; the Iron Veil pact voided citywide; the Empire expelled from Sharn · **Dead:** Esther Crona, Henry Heinrick · **Honor Guards:** one turned and fled, one knocked from the balcony
