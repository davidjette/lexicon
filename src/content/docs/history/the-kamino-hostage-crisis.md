**Hostage-taking · *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* · 30 December 2022 · DM: JL**

*Also known as:* the Die Hard Xmas episode · the Life Day party

**The Kamino hostage crisis** was an episode of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, run by JL on 30 December 2022, in which [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and a few friends from [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) went to his sister [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/)'s Life Day party in the Crystal Tower on [Kamino](/places/kamino/), and a war party of astral-elf militants calling themselves the Radiant Visage took the guests hostage. JL later called it "the Die Hard Xmas episode". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small>

## Background

Nine days before, JL had asked who Barrias's sweetheart, lost love or old flame would be. Dave answered that the one who got away was "a pretty Giff lass who's father was too rich to let her go off with a grease monkey like Barry" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-21)</small>

While the Zanzibar travelled in deep space a transmission arrived from Halle inviting Barrias and a couple of friends to her party. Life Day is an intergalactic celebration, kept in many systems, for sharing joy and cheer with loved ones, and Kamino lay only a few quadrants from the ship's position. Barrias's former love managed the cloning facility there. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## The tower

On arrival their identities were checked, their weapons taken, and they were assigned a guide, the gnomish Cadet Byron Yellowater, who ferried them to the Crystal Tower. The sign out front forbade droids, automatons and robots. The party was on the third floor, the last guests left in the building, where a slick gnome was hitting on Barrias's old love and made sure the visitors knew she was the big boss. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

Then the elevator dinged and twelve astral-elf knights stepped out with glimmering swords. Their leader, a mean-looking elf with a deep irradiated scar glowing pink on his face, fired an iridium blaster into the air.

> "We are the Radiant Visage. And you are all now our hostages. This tower belongs to the Astral Elves. You have stolen our birthright."
>
> <small>— the leader of the Radiant Visage, who demanded payment in harlocks or in blood.</small>

<small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

The tower's floors held more of the militants and their hirelings: a gnoll pack lord asleep with an iridium blaster, a wereboar and astral-elf scouts, an astral-elf knight with a pet phase spider, a skinny minotaur lookout on the roof, and a thief working at the lock of the basement vault. Memos in the building told of forty-seven astral elves imprisoned for terrorist threats against Kamino Corp, and of the Radiant Visage as a group of astral elves threatening Kaminoan authority and talking of an elven insurrection against the galaxy. The roof was rigged to explode with a hundred vials of alchemist's fire. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## Aftermath

The next morning Dave wrote, "now I know why you asked about my old flame", and posted pictures of the night, one with a Santa outfit added. The episode introduced the Iridium Complicator, a +1 musket whose bullets are rare and farmed from meteors. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-31)</small> How the siege ended is not recorded.
