**Staged robbery · [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/), [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · The day of the incident at the Grub + Grog**

**The Tiffany House heist** was the robbery staged at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) in [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) by [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) and a hired crew, who came disguised as waiters for the **RUIN** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). The party were at the house, where [General Peterbilt Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) spoke against the Zanzibar and [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) had set [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) to ruin Beef Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN. Jeremiah surprised them. He and Griddy, both fallen angels, faced each other, fought, and the party resisted him and stole the card. [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) convinced Klaxon and Griddy that Jeremiah had taken it. Wellington was ruined, and Phillip's son [Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/) agreed to his place in Waterdeep. The party drove away with two of the crew as hostages, to [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/).

## Arrival in Waterdeep

The way into Waterdeep opens inside a well-appointed foyer high in **Blackstaff Tower**, where several mages look up quizzically at the arrivals, who all have questions about their point of origin and whether they carry magic items, which can be sold there for four times their normal price. Security escorts newcomers out of the Tower and issues each a **VISA**, a 24-hour voucher to be in the city, with a warning not to violate it. To protect the Mage's Guild, all extraplanar entities within the city walls are monitored. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Nicollo, then Mach V, has an innate sense of the direction of his owner's delivery address, and can follow it directly. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The City of Splendors has lost its lustre. Shops and parks are closed, people are cagey and hooded, and knights on horseback patrol the streets, stopping passersby to ask for identification. The **Lady's Circle** is a large plaza with, among other things, a large teleportation circle where plane tickets can be used. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**The Barker.** A man stands on an apple cart proclaiming the liberty of Waterdeep from continental rule, and calls for the Unmasked Lord to evict the [Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) and win independence from the Zanzibar. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**The Zanzibar** is the Emperor of Cormyr and Faerun. Immediately after the Ages of the Infanta, [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), crowned king of Cormyr and with his forces in the old imperial capital of Waterdeep, declared himself Emperor of Faerun and consolidated control of the continent, mostly peacefully. He set his imperial capital in Suzail. From then on the Emperor has been called the Zanzibar. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## At Tiffany House

On approach the party saw mounted knights in violet plumed helmets. Inside, they:

- saw General Varkus deliver an incendiary speech against the Zanzibar;
- heard Phillip Klaxon tell Mach V of his plan to place Darius on the Lords' Council by ruining **Beef Wellington**;
- witnessed Griddy seducing Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN;
- saw clues in a tapestry, and items: a pen and a scroll.

They also came away with three things: Darius does not want this; Phillip is using Varkus; and "Tiffany House's owners have been doomed to Hell." <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>

## Jeremiah

Then Jeremiah surprised them. <small>(DM notes, session record)</small> The party know him as the handsome man with the glowing sword who killed all of them but Heyu, and [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/), hired by [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) to hunt them down and find the cards. In Dave's account, "Jeremiah and some goon had staged a heist disguised as waiters to steal the card as well."

Jeremiah knows Griddy. The party know her as **Garidriel**, and the two of them are fallen angels at different stages of their fall from grace. <small>(DM notes)</small> What they said to each other:

> **Jeremiah:** Garidriel, you are lost. Moriel will face justice once and for all.
>
> **Griddy:** Jeremiah, I heard you'll be joining us soon in the Hells! What could they possibly have tempted you with to cause your fall from Grace?
>
> **Jeremiah:** I am not tempted. I am resolved to cleanse your master from all realities.
>
> **Griddy:** You know my master will not stand for this intrusion on his personal estate, nor the murder of his more useful servants. Now that I think of it, I don't believe your heavenly masters will appreciate the breach of custom much themselves. There is a delicate balance that keeps our old enmities from scorching this plane and others into ash.
>
> **Jeremiah:** Let justice be done, though the heavens fall.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

"Griddy and Jeremiah fought and the party resisted him." <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## The card

The party stole RUIN. Nicollo "convinced Klaxxon and Griddy that he was on their side but that Jeremiah had taken the card." <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14; sic on Klaxxon)</small>

Afterwards Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep and Wellington was ruined, with four days left until the Lords' meeting. Phillip Klaxon was happy, but scared to have lost the card. Griddy was suspicious, but demanded that they steal the card back from Jeremiah. <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>

## That night

The party drove through the rain to the Grub + Grog in the Sea Ward with two hostages, and with "the very large cast iron contraption with chimes and hammers and gears which made the hyponotic music used to pull off the heist" <small>(sic)</small>. The hostages were the **Grille brothers**, drivers: **Bonefish**, the nervous one, and **Big Macaroni**, resolute but bargaining. Shadowhigh and Maverick talked them into a bag of holding; in a room at the inn Nicollo intimidated Macaroni into giving up his bosses, proving that Jeremiah was in league with Shemeska, then freed and paid the brothers to join the party and asked them to call him The Prince. What followed is [the incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/). <small>(The Grub & Grog narrative)</small>

At [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) after the arrests, [Sir Merriam Wordsworker](/people/sir-merriam-wordsworker/) asked [Maverick](/people/maverick/) why he had been spotted at Tiffany House earlier that day, with the RUIN card peeking out of a loosely wrapped deck on the desk. Varkus came in with Phillip Klaxon and Griddy Champagne and demanded the true reason for the attack, and whether the party worked with his enemies; Klaxon fumed that Maverick was conspiring against them. Then Nicollo was brought in and betrayed the party: he had never meant to steal the RUIN card, he said, he was in Klaxon's service, and the moment he found out [Lenore](/people/lenore/) had the card he killed her and tried to take it. Griddy picked the card up from the desk and slipped it into her bra, and Varkus sentenced Maverick to hang at dawn. <small>(Fort Maximillien narrative)</small>
