**Insurrection · Leef, eastern Khorvaire · Before the Sharn arc**

The liberation of Leef was an insurrection that broke the Imperial garrison at Leef, the last eastern stop on the lightning rail, before the Sharn arc. It is the engagement at which [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) first met, fighting alongside the town's defenders; the three were afterwards titled the Heroes of Leef, and Dario married the dwarven leader Princess Orange Chicken. Leef later absorbed the refugees of [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/) and became the seed of [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/). The liberation was the Leef half of the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/).

## THE TOWN

Leef was nothing: the final eastern stop on the rail, a desert outpost with no reason to be defended. That changed when Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) expanded his empire eastward. He built an airship yard there and flooded the town with illithid city guards and Iron Veil soldiers. The town acquired a garrison, an occupation and a purpose it had not asked for.

## WHO WAS THERE

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) arrived in the town separately and met there.

Gemma and Dario came in by train. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24)</small> By Dave's account, on arriving in Leef they were "immediately roped into a terrorist act before meeting our allies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-21)</small> Eric joined the two of them after the attack on the train station. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-22; 2025-08-30)</small>

**The local defenders:**

- **Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke** — a hardened veteran of the Last War, and the one who afterwards pointed the trio west at LeBeefe himself.
- **Zero of Leef** — an elderly Tabaxi follower of the Silver Flame.
- **Princess Orange Chicken** — a young dwarven leader from the swamp tribes of the Mror Holds. She later married Dario. Their child is Red Dragon Shrimp.

## THE RISING

Together they liberated Leef.

**Homecoming.** Gemma came home to her adoptive father, Jane Corso, at his junkyard in Leef, with Imperial guards already looking for a tiefling of her description. Colonel [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/) helped the pair fight off the guards who came for her at the Cat & Biscuits tavern, and two Inquisition agents killed Jane at the junkyard and tried to frame Gemma for his death. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The train station.** Gemma and Dario attacked the town's train station, blew up shipments and killed guards; Gemma ran from the explosion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22; 2025-09-27)</small> Dario ran into hostile fire and almost died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> Nico later observed that "Some may say The hero’s of Leaf also caused an act of terrorism when we attacked the train station". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

The job was the Colonel's. A supply train had come in on the lightning rail marked MILITARY SUPPLIES NEWHAM and unloaded 135 crates into the stockyard, and since Leef was the last stop on the line, everything bound east for the war on the dwarves came through it. Rourke offered the pair papers of transit to Aundair or a fight, and they chose the fight: they went in disguised, took what they could carry and blew up the rest. Dario was knocked out and Gemma dragged him clear of the blast; he came away with a longsword of vengeance, and she with gloves of thievery. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The temple of Dol Arrah.** The Colonel's next job was the abandoned [temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/), whose basement was a Black Dove listening station; he wanted its input switch. Zero, an old Tabaxi woman begging for alms on the way, knew Dario for one of the Flame and told him the treasure in the temple might not be the treasure he sought. In the basement the party found Black Dove priests, their undead and, locked in a cage and Feebleminded, the Imperial auditor [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/). Dario spared one of the Doves there, Luz Fayana, <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small> and a surviving Dove offered an alliance against LeBeefe, calling the caged auditor, who bore the Imperial mark of audit, the key to LeBeefe's fortress. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The belly of the beast.** With Eric, they found that "LeBeefe is attempting to build a mind flayer army. He’s attempting to take the Dwarflands. And he’s doing it with Esther’s troops!" (JL). They beheaded prisoners. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

**The swamp.** In a swamp, the party hallucinated. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small>

**The dwarves.** The Colonel sent them east to carry a gift to the dwarves and make them allies. At an Imperial guard post on the way, Comptroller [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/) and Inquisition agents were using LeBeefe's technology to turn captured dwarves into aberrations, and held an abducted bard, Grimpledink, the father of [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/), who was slowly turning into a mind flayer; he joined the party. After the battle, as he turned, he asked them to kill him and made them promise to kill LeBeefe: "you put a blade in his belly and you tell him Grimpledink sent ya." <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small> In the swamp a fox caught in a trap spoke to Gemma, and ran off saying "Knew your father I did": it was [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/). The dwarves' shaman [Yellameet](/people/yellameet/) sent them up his tree of wisdom for a fruit before he would treat with them, and then set his terms, among them a marriage alliance with his granddaughter. Dario was engaged to her, and came back towards Leef with a small army of 35 dwarves behind him. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The Battle of Leef.** On the night before, soldiers went door to door arresting people and took the Colonel and three rebels. LeBeefe brought in a giant siege engine, a Thresher, to tear a path through Leef straight into the Dwarflands, and an admiral to command the troops from a large airship, and Greenish Hughes had the Colonel. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> The fighting came to a head in the Battle of Leef. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06 to 2025-09-12)</small>

The victory was won and the Colonel came through it alive. The town titled the three strangers who did it **the Heroes of Leef**, and they celebrated with Orange Chicken and the dwarves and set up a workshop of their own, the Falcons' Workshop. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> Dario carried the Mror Holds connection out of it in the form of a wife, and that kinship has mattered since: years later [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) could talk his way out of a drawn-weapons standoff in a reclaimed dwarven hall by invoking Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long.

## WHAT IT BECAME

At Rourke's urging the trio went west to Newham to finish the job at its source. That campaign killed LeBeefe and destroyed the city — see [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/) — and sent a flood of refugees east along the rail to the only free town on the line.

Leef absorbed them. Townsfolk, Newham refugees and the swamp-dwarf tribes forged [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/) out of the wreck, with [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) as acting Comptroller. It stands between New Mourning to the west and the allied Mror Holds to the east, fortified and largely beyond the reach of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s forces, and it is the centre of eastern resistance. Its delegation sat at the Council of Sharn asking for alliance and stability, and travelled to Thronehold with the Chancellor.

> Once a dusty desert outpost known only as the last eastern stop on the lightning rail, Leef changed forever.
