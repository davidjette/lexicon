**Dungeon crossing · Level 3 of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/) to [Skullport](/places/skullport/) · After the STAR card · Reached Skullport**

**The Road to Skullport** was the party's crossing of the third level of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), from the stair below the Arcane Chambers to the gate of [Skullport](/places/skullport/), carrying the Company's book [O-3255](/items/o-3255/) to its rendezvous. [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/), [Barold Bonds](/people/barold-bonds/), [Maverick](/people/maverick/), [Lenore](/people/lenore/) with her steel defender Johnny, [Heyu](/people/heyu/) and [Tripp Sandhill](/people/tripp-sandhill/) passed through a dwarven tomb and an earthquake-ruined underground town, killed nine giant spiders and the drow of House Wendy's who held its kitchen, and freed the bugbear and goblins they had enslaved. Two of the goblins, **Waddle** and **Snood**, chose freedom and came along. BattleBeast talked down a dozen hobgoblins, the party made camp in the long tunnel to Skullport, and at the water's edge a kuo-toa gave Maverick a **cloak of the manta ray**. After a fight with merrows in the harbour, the Xanathar Guild's duergar took a toll and let them into the city.

## Down to the third level

The party left the Arcane Chambers with O-3255 and the STAR card and went down a long winding stair that ended heading south. The air grew damp, mossy and fishy, and the water was fresh, which told them a river ran on this level. The Company's letter sent them to Skullport, reached from the third level, to hand the book to their contact at the standard rendezvous, a tavern on the city's lower level called **the Flagon and the Dragon**. A good history check told them Skullport is a rogue city beneath the city, built where a great underground river meets the underground sea of the Winking Depths and held by the Xanathar Guild. Tripp was coming for his fee: the book was to earn them a thousand gold.

Those who had been hanged at [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) had lost everything they carried. Maverick's portal key, won gambling at the casino, was gone with the rest.

## The dwarven tomb and the ruined town

Heyu led with a light on her wand, tapping the walls as she went. The first halls were a catacomb, dusty and hung with cobwebs despite the damp, smelling of old decay and burned incense. The construction and the incense marked it as a dwarven tomb of the faith of Moradin. The dwarves of that faith held a monotheism in which [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) was a heresy, possibly because the obliviation of Brightmantle made the worship of lesser gods look like heresy or madness. Its Infanta was [Mora](/people/mora/), who allied with the orcs and was fought at [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/).

Beyond the tomb was a rough-hewn cavern thirty feet high holding an ancient underground settlement of low stone buildings, their roofs mostly caved in and their insides picked clean. An earthquake long ago had collapsed whole sections and cut the town in two. Webs hung between every building, and one room held recently opened egg sacs. BattleBeast, leading in the dark under Heyu's *foresight*, heard three giant spiders coming. In the fight Barold's wild magic filled the air around him with illusory butterflies and flower petals. A spider webbed BattleBeast, who slashed himself free and tore apart the one he had stunned.

The party split for a few minutes. BattleBeast and Tripp met two more spiders, which fled up the walls. Heyu then opened a door on three more, which dropped from the ceiling, poisoned her and climbed back out of reach. Barold's cleave hit two at once, and Heyu's *shocking grasp* burst one across the wall. BattleBeast ran the last one down, put his fist through it and beat it against a wall until it came apart. Nine spiders were dead.

## The kitchen of House Wendy's

A smell of meat stew led them to a kitchen where a bugbear was chained to the wall and three goblins, shackled together by the ankles, were cooking under three male drow guards. A fourth drow was in the water closet, and Maverick opened the door on him. When the party asked the way to Skullport, the drow drew their weapons. BattleBeast punched one to death and Barold broke another's skull with his bat. A third drow dropped magical darkness over the room, in which Lenore collided with a goblin and knocked the stew across the floor, and ran for the door, where Johnny clotheslined him and Tripp finished him with *true strike* and a dagger. The drow from the latrine answered with his sword in hand and escaped into a second darkness. The male drow owned nothing but their light chain shirts, and Barold took one.

The freed bugbear offered to take them to his boss, **Azrock**, who would know the way. This side of the ruined town had been Azrock's until the drow came, a house led by a red-headed priestess who call themselves **House Wendy's**. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-dead-mans-hand-skullport.md)</small> The prisoners had been caught stealing supplies, and the drow had drained one of them, Blinky, for days with spider venom. The party let them arm themselves with the drow's hand crossbows and rapiers and declined to join their war. On the way south they passed the river bank, where rafts were moored, and went through a transplanted dwarven hall canted slightly out of line with the rock around it. A goblin stepped on a pressure plate and was crushed by a falling block, and the party found the remaining plates and walked the others around them.

## Waddle and Snood

At a fork the guides showed the ways on: a hole only a goblin could fit through, toward Azrock; the river, which would swallow them and pass them out onto the ground or into the water; and a long tunnel south to Skullport, with worse things on it. The goblins expected to be beaten for having been captured. The beatings, they said, were what kept goblins motivated; otherwise they would just sit around all day smoking, drinking, laughing and having babies. Maverick promised that if they came along and made it out, they could sit under their own fig trees, and two of them came.

Before the bugbear went back alone, the goblins let slip that Azrock was born blind, can somehow see, and has lost the magic dagger that makes him so strong. The party told the bugbear about the dead bugbears they had found earlier with sunken, empty eye sockets. He said those were Xanathar's, and that the Xanathar can take a creature's brain out and put in another, and hurried off to warn Azrock that some of his own bugbears might be spies. The goblins who stayed are brothers, **Waddle** and **Snood**.

A dozen hobgoblins, alerted by the bugbear, came out of the western cave to stop the party. BattleBeast told their captain that the entrails of their enemies were strewn through everything to the north, and that he could smell it on BattleBeast's claws. The captain asked whether they had come to take the territory, and wanted help against the drow; BattleBeast refused. When the captain demanded the goblins back, Waddle answered for himself that they were free and thinking men: *"If you prick us, do we not bleed? If you poison us, do we not die?"* The captain let them pass, and told Waddle and Snood never to come back.

The tunnel ran a couple of miles, in and out beside the river. The party made a long rest in it, and on watch saw patrols of hobgoblins and bugbears pass in both directions without finding them.

## Skullport

The party came in by Beggar's Rest Pass onto a view of the whole city, on three levels, around Skull Island, the Xanathar Guild's fortress where the river Sargauth runs into the underground sea. Hundreds of duergar and humans in dirty dark armour garrisoned the island under the Xanathar's banners, with ballistae on the walls and thirteen flameskulls circling the city at different heights.

A hungry kuo-toa squatted at the water's edge. The party gave it a cantaloupe from the drow kitchen. It dived, and came back with a wet, leathery thing like a manta ray's wing, with a hood and a tail: a **cloak of the manta ray**, which lets its wearer breathe underwater and swim fast. Maverick put it on and swam out to find a way across, and BattleBeast held his breath and went after him. In the dark water two scavenger merrows attacked them with harpoons and bites, and Tripp dived in to help. Maverick trapped one merrow in an illusion of an electrified cage, and BattleBeast killed it and leapt back up onto the dock. The other harpooned Maverick in the leg and vanished into the deep.

Duergar on the towers shouted that there was no swimming and no pedestrian access that way. A rowboat brought two human thugs and a duergar lieutenant, who wanted the Guild's cut of any business done in Skullport. The party's business was at the Flagon and the Dragon, and they paid him one gold coin. He ferried them into the island harbour, past the Tower of the Seven Woes. After a light pat-down, they were let through the main gate onto the Arkstone Bridge into the city.

## Revelations

- **House Wendy's** is the drow house holding the northern half of the ruined town on Level 3. It is led by a red-headed priestess, and its male drow own nothing.
- **Azrock**, a bugbear chief, held that half of the town before the drow came. He was born blind, somehow sees, and has lost his magic dagger.
- The bugbears found dead with empty eye sockets belong to the **Xanathar**, who is said to replace creatures' brains.
- **Tripp Sandhill is fatemarked, with the STAR card.** He, too, woke up with the tattoo. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-dead-mans-hand-skullport.md)</small>
- **Heyu's human form** comes from the Circlet of Human Perfection, taken from the leader of the Goblin Bazaar on Level 2. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-dead-mans-hand-skullport.md)</small>
- **Griddy Champagne's sisters** are Sandy Rawlins and Dusty Grapes.
