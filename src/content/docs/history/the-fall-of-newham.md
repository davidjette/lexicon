**Urban rising and arcane catastrophe · Newham City · Before the Sharn arc**

The fall of Newham was an urban rising and arcane catastrophe that destroyed the city of [Newham](/places/newham/) before the Sharn arc. Sent west by Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke after [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) welded the city's scattered anti-LeBeefe cells into one resistance and killed Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). An otherworldly explosion then collapsed the city and corrupted the region around it, which the survivors named New Mourning; they evacuated east to Leef, where they became part of [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/). The rising was the Newham half of the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/).

## THE CITY

Newham was the Empire's proudest new thing. It began as a village called New Ham, took an Imperial Charter from Lord Albert Spear, and inside a few years had a casino, a remodelled lightning-rail hotel, a redirected river turned into a lake named for its first mayor, a university built out of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s hospital, and a Rekkenmark annexe with [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) as its director. It was called the gateway to the East. Nothing lives there now.

## THE GRAND DUKE

[John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) had been Newham's first mayor and had risen to Grand Duke, carrying the Empire eastward with an airship yard, illithid city guards and Iron Veil garrisons. He was a sorcerer of unknown powers and a founding member of [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). The lake carried his name.

After [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke urged [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) west to confront the architect of the whole eastern arrangement in his own city.

## THE RISING

In Newham they met [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/), Rourke's old ally, a halfling furniture dealer, a Last War veteran, a founder of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) and the first person ever to treat [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) as a man rather than as property. Digma opened his book: a network of anti-LeBeefe cells scattered across the metropolis, none of them talking to each other.

When people in Newham think of "the rebels", by JL's account, they are most likely to think of the **St. Stamos cultists** and their actions: a radical cell whose war cry is "St. Stamos". Soon after the trio reached the city a theater was bombed, and a singer with element stones in her torso was not saved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small> Of the rebel groups the trio worked to win over, three of four became allies, vampires among them; the fourth, possible defectors from the military base, had not yet been approached. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

With his guidance the trio welded those cells into a single coordinated resistance, strong enough to move on the Grand Duke directly. The assault succeeded. LeBeefe was killed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-05)</small>

## THE EXPLOSION

Then the city ended. A large otherworldly explosion collapsed Newham into ruin and corrupted a vast region around it: toxic, uninhabitable, and closed. What detonated is not recorded, nor whether it was LeBeefe's doing or his death's.

The survivors did not stay to find out. They evacuated east en masse, to Leef.

## NEW MOURNING

They named the wasteland **New Mourning**. The Empire had declared that the Mourning was the Emperor's mercy and renamed its anniversary [the Rejuvenation](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/); the survivors of the second one used the Empire's own discarded word for their new republic's western border.

Out of the evacuation came [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/): the townsfolk of Leef, the Newham refugees and the swamp-dwarf tribes of the Mror Holds, forged into a nation sitting between the corrupted waste to the west and allied dwarves to the east. Digma Beeve is its acting Comptroller. It is one of the few places in Khorvaire that was ever beyond [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s reach.

## THE MAN WHO WOULD NOT STAY DEAD

LeBeefe died at Newham and has not stopped talking. Beneath Sharn, at [the Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), Eric took only half damage from psychic attacks and heard a tune from LeBeefe's Black Palace. In the Korranberg Glitterhame the dead courier at the heart of the fungal bloom carried a rejected Ministry requisition **signed by John C. LeBeefe**, instructing him to observe the deep workings and report what listens back. The voice that kept urging Eric to ring the bell reminded him of LeBeefe. On the way out of the mountain that voice told him the Emperor is a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun.

> Their assault succeeded, and LeBeefe was killed, but the victory came at a catastrophic cost.

**Result:** Grand Duke killed; Newham destroyed; the region corrupted into New Mourning; the Republic of Talenta founded from the survivors
