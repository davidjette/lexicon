**Continental war of succession · Khorvaire · A hundred years, ended twenty-one years before the Imperial pageant was written**

The Last War is the hundred-year war of succession fought across Khorvaire between the five successor kingdoms of Galifar. It began when King Jarot divided the empire among his five children, who were unable to live in peace and fought to claim it whole; it ravaged the continent, took the life of Kaius I, and ended twenty-one years before the Imperial pageant was written, with the destruction of Cyre in the event the Empire calls the Rejuvenation and everyone else calls [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/). Out of it came the Pax Imperium under Kaius III, the [Warforged](/lore/warforged/) nation in the Mournlands, the Silver Flame's outlawing, and the veterans who staff every rebellion on the continent.

## THE INHERITANCE

The Imperial account, performed as [A Bard's Tale](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/), gives the origin. Centuries ago a king named Galifar united the five nations of Khorvaire into a single empire. His descendant King Jarot had five children, and on his death gave them each a kingdom:

- **Breland** — where the lightning rail originates.
- **Aundair** — fertile fields that feed the nations, and fine wines.
- **Thrane** — lush forest and natural waterways.
- **Cyre** — the strongest scientific and manufacturing power of the five.
- **Karrnath** — the first human nation established on the soil of Khorvaire, ruled by the great Kaius the First.

Jarot's children were unable to live in peace and fought to claim the empire as their own. A hundred years of war ravaged the lands, took the life of Kaius I, and brought Khorvaire to ruin.

## THE HOUSES OF AERENAL

Dave has named the elvish houses of Aerenal "that for one reason or another made their way to Khorvaire to dominate here" as Duin, Qualanthri and Crona, and has said of them: "they could have been responsible for the Last War and the collapse of Galifar itself". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## THE WARFORGED

Cyre came closest to winning it, because Cyre built soldiers. The Warforged — mechanical, souled, tireless — were made to fight this war, and they were still fighting it when the war ended in a way none of them had accounted for.

That origin is contested inside the Empire's own institutions. At the Rekkenmark, Professor Marble believed ancient dwarves had used necromancy to put souls into machines, and that this is how the Cyrans made Warforged; she was researching the connection when she accidentally raised the undead that overran the academy. DR4MB, the three-hundred-year-old Cyran machine built to decommission Warforged, was taken by Karrnath two years before the war ended.

## THE ENDING

The Empire says it ended on a mountaintop. Kaius III, on his Great Eagle and flanked by his loyal thirteen, raised his greatsword, split the clouds to reveal a Crimson Sun, pointed the sword at the battle below, and the sun flashed hot upon the land of Cyre. Only the Warforged survived. They worshipped him as their saviour, and he gifted the Mournlands to the Souled Machines.

The Empire's word for that day is **the Rejuvenation**. Everyone else's word is the Mourning.

## WHAT THE WAR LEFT RUNNING

The war never really stopped; it changed name and shape. The Pax Imperium that came out of it holds the continent under an Emperor who has not sat the Throne of Bones in ten years. Its rebellions — [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) founded over twenty years ago, [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/), [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/), [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/) — are staffed by its veterans and their children. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) leads the Warforged nation in the Mournlands the Emperor supposedly gifted them, and has built a device that reproduces the Crimson Sun deliberately.

And when [a summit was finally called at Thronehold](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/) to end the civil war that replaced it, the Empress filled the hall with undead and burned the negotiation down.

## AFTER THRONEHOLD

In Dave's account of the war after the Summit: "The war has boiled over and is overrunning the imperial core, threatening the capital and triggering the endgame". "The Uruk republic is on the verge of conquering Aundair, and the empire is on its back feet protecting Karnath" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>
