**Massacre and arson · Zilspar Farm 02, the Breland farmsteads outside Sharn · Korth Ep 12**

The destruction of Zilspar Farm is the massacre and arson that ended Zilspar Farm 02, [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/)'s stronghold among the Breland farmsteads outside Sharn, reported to the Inevitables in Korth Ep 12. Eight [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units held the ground when the party flew out to it; the buildings had been burned over people boarded up inside after the fighting was over. Zero-One was taken alive, [Izaak](/people/izaak/) was presumed dead, and Sharn lost the farm that fed it.

Zilspar was where everyone went when the city stopped being survivable. It was [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/)'s stronghold, a working farm with a concealed underground cell and a private Fulcrum room. [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) and [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) were hidden there after [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/) declared them dead. Sister Nora was interrogated there; [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s memory of Calcifer's birth was extracted there. Donna, a Silver Flame cleric of fifty-nine, ran it. It also fed Sharn.

## THE NEWS

The Inevitables came back from the Korramont carrying **KNELL**, the horn and the folio, and walked into [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) delivering the worst news of the war. Zilspar Farm had been destroyed. Zero-One had been taken alive. [Izaak](/people/izaak/) was presumed dead.

> Zilspar Farm has been destroyed, Zero-One taken alive, and Izzak presumed dead.

A dossier from the Hundreds confirmed no survivors.

## THE SCENE

They flew out anyway. They landed wide and were found immediately: eight [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units still on the ground, and not the things the party had fled at level five. They self-heal. They survive on undead fortitude. They articulate on impossible second elbows. They burst into lightning when they die. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) killed one on top of an antenna and the discharge electrified the whole tower field for the rest of the fight; [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) sheathed Pale Fire and used KNELL purely to shove them clear before they detonated, and threw Eric bodily into the brush to save him. All eight fell.

The wreckage records the sequence. **Many E.D.E.N. soldiers died defending the farm** — the farm fought and hurt them. Incendiaries were brought in afterward, and the buildings were burned over people who had been boarded up inside.

## WHO IS NOT ACCOUNTED FOR

No sign of Zero-One, Izaak, Donna or Grady Marsh. Among days-old dead they found exactly one living person: a farm hand's boy who gave his name as **Noot Gunray**. Dario refused to let him be questioned. They flew home with the orphan intending to hand him to someone who could raise him.

## THE ARITHMETIC AFTERWARD

Flying home, the Chancellor put it together. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) had offered food. The farm was destroyed days later. Pierce then offered more food, at the price of military and logistical access to Sharn's airspace for the airships of the Hundreds — and Eric granted it that same afternoon. Any one of those hulls could carry a bomb like the Ruby Nova. By the following morning a capital ship had settled out of the fog over Sharn and was handing out fruit in orderly lines.

Eric told [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) he had handed Pierce the keys, that he knew it was a trick, and that he only hoped they would survive Korth in time to take it back.

Whether Pierce knew about Zilspar and gave it away to the Empire, or someone else betrayed Fulcrum, is unresolved. Jessica had already asked Gemma privately whether Eric — hearing a voice he had hidden from everyone — was how the Empire found the farm. See [the rolling-up of Fulcrum](/lore/the-rolling-up-of-fulcrum/).

## WHAT IT COST SHARN

With the farm gone and no fuel in the city, [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) put Sharn a month from severe food shortages; the Hundreds calculated forty-seven days from famine.
