**Magical accident and away mission · *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* · 2023 · DM: JL**

*Also known as:* The Trouble with Wishes · The Lost Session

**The Chingwa's wish** was a two-part story of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, run by JL in 2023. While [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Dr. Nilpferd](/people/dr-nilpferd/) went aboard [the Cube](/places/the-cube/) to find the legal expert [MacFlecknoe](/people/macflecknoe/), a [Chingwa](/lore/the-chingwa/) in the Zanzibar's arcane laboratory granted [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) his favourite thing, and the wish began to fill [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) with gold and mushrooms. Carlos and [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/) were sent into the Astral Plane to find a lich who could undo it. On the station, MacFlecknoe was found and the crystal dragon Oxyl's orb was taken. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

## The orders

The Zanzibar approached DS-K7, the deep-space station called Little Sigil for its many portals and its many residents from Sigil. The Astralex relayed orders from Astral Command to the captain: stop at the station and seek out MacFlecknoe, a reclusive expert in intergalactic law. Nilpferd, Barrias and Caprica took the *Minnow* to dock with the station, though the Astralex could not tell them how to find MacFlecknoe or even what he looked like. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

## The arcane division

For his help after the engine explosion aboard the ship, Carlos was reassigned by the captain to the arcane sector and promoted from busboy to janitor, reporting to Ensign Forbes and serving under the Chief Arcanist, Dr. Nilpferd. JL had introduced Nilpferd to the players as "Carlos' new boss". <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22)</small> Nilpferd welcomed him to the world of magics with a warning in Elvish, *oobi snegoobi*, "watch your ass", and three rules that all wise arcanists follow:

> 1. Be curious
> 2. Do good
> 3. Wash your hands
>
> <small>— Dr. Nilpferd, to Carlos Vega.</small>

<small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

The arcane division held carnivorous plants that snapped at passers-by, molds, mosses and mushrooms of every colour, glowing crystals, shelves of jars and bins of curiosities, and small cages of chittering, barking, humming alien animals. In a fishbowl-like terrarium on the counter sat a Chingwa, pressing its hands to the glass. It spoke directly into Carlos's mind and asked what his favourite thing was, something he could touch. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

## The wish

The Chingwa cast *wish* to please them. Carlos's favourite thing was gold; Forbes's was mushrooms. Both began to fill the ship at an alarming rate, and the vessel approached its capacity. Unable to stop it, Forbes and Carlos turned to Nilpferd, who was deep in astral projection in his quarters. Carlos found his cabinet of scrolls and cast *dispel magic* to bring him out of it. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

Nilpferd told them that nothing in the arcane is more dangerous than a wish spell, except casting *wish* to undo one, which could split the ship and rip the galaxy apart. The only way to stop a wish once cast was a tenth-level spell, which only an epic magus could command: his own master. He took their hands and sent them into the Astral Plane to find her and ask her to cast *Superior Wish*. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

In the Astral Plane they floated in the clouds above a great tree, tethered by spectral cords to a pool of silvery fluid. On a nearby asteroid stood a village of hostile but harmless kobolds with Chicago accents. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## Little Sigil

Aboard the station, residents resented the presence of Astral Command. A signpost in hundreds of languages pointed to the market, the temple, the fighting pit and the library, and gangsters, priests, gladiators and psionic sorcerers alike would only tell the crew how to find MacFlecknoe in return for a service: each group wanted to know how a gnome named Malorcus Donk kept getting the better of them. Donk's secret was that he sneaked into the chamber of the crystal dragon Oxyl and rubbed its orb for luck. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

Through a back-alley arrangement with thugs at a lasagna stand, Barrias and Caprica were told to follow a gnome who would lead them to a treasure in Oxyl's possession; if they could claim it, the lasagna gang would tell them where to find MacFlecknoe. They tracked and caught the gnome, who promised to lead them to Oxyl's lair, and followed him into the sewers. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small> MacFlecknoe was found, and the dragon's orb was taken. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

## The session of 11 August 2023

The story was picked up again after a long break. On 4 August 2023 JL said he would need to find his notes to prep "the session I was thinking about back in June", and he ran it on 11 August. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-04; 2023-08-11)</small>

> Barrias ends this session unsure if the lich actually cast that wish and saved Tillamook.
>
> <small>— JL (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-11)</small>
