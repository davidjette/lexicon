**Campaign arc · [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) · DM Dave · Three seasons, 2022–2024 · SSJ Zanzibar**

*Also known as:* LoX · the Xaryxis mission · Starfall TNG (the second season)

***Light of Xaryxis*** is the first arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), run by Dave in three seasons from October 2022 to May 2024, in which the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) went from [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) to the Time Tombs of [Necis](/places/necis/). Dave adapted the published adventure of the same name for an Astral Command crew, played its opening chapters and then went off the book. The first season brought the pilot [Caprica](/people/caprica/) aboard, met [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) and ended with a climax in Sigil. In the second, which Dave called "Starfall TNG", Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) faced retirement, the Lexicon was revealed as a hidden society of conflicting AIs, and the command crew left Astral Command on a pilgrimage across Necis while the Zanzibar drifted. The third opened with a flashback to the first [Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/) and ended in [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/): the ship critically damaged, Caprica dead, the crew scattered by wishes and [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) fled from Sigil. JL ran several guest episodes. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24; 2022-11-10; 2023-07-20; 2024-06-02)</small>

## Background

Dave chose the published adventure as the campaign's first arc and reworked it around a crew of Astral Command officers, which the party are not in the book. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24; 2022-10-27)</small> The SSJ Zanzibar is a Sovereign Spelljammer and a flagship of five hundred years before. It has a rare Major Helm, and with no one aboard able to fly it the ship had been confined to one system, T-Space, limping on a backup Minor Helm with [F.L.A.P.](/people/f-l-a-p/) as autopilot. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10; 2022-10-11)</small>

In the prologue Caprica, an astral elf who had graduated first in her class from the Academy, stunned the Admiralty by choosing the open helm on the Zanzibar, "currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> She chose it as her best chance to find the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small> She was ordered to a colonial waystation on the unsettled world of [Tralfamadore III](/places/tralfamadore-iii/), joined a science team chasing reports of falling stars on its northern continent, and was told the captain would collect her from the Loose Rooster tavern in the village of Northspire. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

## Season 1 (2022–2023)

### Tralfamadore III and the Rock of Bral

The first session, on 21 October 2022, was a double episode. It opened on level-1 regulars of Northspire who knew nothing of space travel, then followed Caprica and Smallberries; astral elves came close to killing the party twice. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19; 2022-10-22)</small> By 27 October the crew had completed the first three chapters of the adventure and reached level 6. In the book the party follow Sartell to Bral to meet Commodore Krux of the Second Wind; here the Rock of Bral became the last major settlement on the edge of the Sovereignty, to which the crew return for civilisation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> The crew played a split session on Bral before leaving for Doomspace. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-05)</small>

On 10 November the arc went off the book with the first flight of the Zanzibar, a mystery episode in which the crew outran an enemy. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-10)</small>

### The mind flayer on the rock

*Main article: [The Mind Flayer on the Rock](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/)*

Over three sessions in November 2022 a mind flayer in a lair on a rock infested members of the crew. Going down into the lair was the idea of [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/), then a civilian. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small> [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) used his monk's training to overpower the captain, Ensign [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) performed surgery that stopped ceremorphosis in the infested crew, and Caprica took command and saved the captain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-19; 2022-11-27)</small> Afterwards [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) issued Carlos civilian credentials, restricted him to the Engineering and Lounge Decks, and warned him that she had witnessed the mind flayer's curse on him. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

### Below Decks, the paradise world and the Shrike

On 9 December the level-1 crew, among them Mordecai, [Se7en](/people/se7en/) and [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/), had an episode below decks, which JL had planned as a card game. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-07; 2022-12-10)</small> On a small paradise world a week later Jim ordered the Zanzibar to leave, but he passed on his duress code, so the crew defied the order and turned hostile to rescue the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-18)</small> On 30 December JL ran an episode about the old flame of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), which introduced the Iridium Complicator. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-31)</small>

On 7 January 2023 Dave ran a horror episode in which the crew first met the Shrike. It proved very hard to damage and very deadly, and the party retreated. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small>

### The Drumhead

The episode of 20 January 2023 adapted "The Drumhead" from *Star Trek: The Next Generation*. It grew out of a thread between Smallberries and [Princess Xedalli](/people/princess-xedalli/). An engineer was killed in a blast and Carlos was suspected; [Irina Malikovsky](/people/irina-malikovsky/), pulled in when [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) searched the crew's lockers, witnessed for his innocence. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-21; 2023-01-22)</small> A week later Dave issued the crew roster of the Zanzibar, on which Carlos is a cadet. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-27)</small>

### The Academy and the race

At JL's request the players rolled level-1 officer candidates, and on 1 April 2023 JL ran an Academy flashback with the young Smallberries, [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/). In it Smallberries was a one-star captain and Katrina a two-star captain, technically the captain of the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-03-31; 2023-04-01)</small> JL's next episode brought the Borgias into the game. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-08)</small> Many of the characters did not want the Xaryxis mission to go to waste, and Jim refused to let it fail. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-14)</small> Blastimov came aboard the Zanzibar knowing Katrina's and Horgus's secret. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-22)</small>

In the race of 28 April Horgus hacked the minilex of the Bachman shuttle, meaning the crew to spin out while one of the hackers got in and out with the core; the crew resisted the hack with Arcana and threw it off with spells. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-29)</small>

### Doomspace and Sigil

On 12 May Carlos won the party the human faction by cheating at cards. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-15)</small> Before leaving [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/), the crew went back aboard the derelict [SSJ Stonecypher](/items/the-ssj-stonecypher/), full of undead, to recover a helm for the thri-kreen captain, without which his people would not come with them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-19)</small> On 26 May they came close to defeat against Zodar, who was immune to being frightened and was not humanoid. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-27)</small>

The season's climax on 2 June 2023 was played in Sigil, with an astral elf honour guard and Xeleth, and ended with Commander Vael established as a recurring enemy after the crew's triumph. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-02; 2023-06-03)</small>

## Season 2, "Starfall TNG" (2023–2024)

### Retirement and the prophet

The season premiere on 9 June 2023 was a character episode, almost all Jim and Caprica, with loose ends to tie up before the long return home. Jim took hard news that raised the prospect of his retirement, creating tension between Astral Command and the party, and Carlos was reassigned to security. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-09; 2023-06-10)</small> After a long fight in which the crew took most of the best items from a hoard, the session of 23 June closed what the table called the Mind Fucker Saga. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-17; 2023-06-22)</small> In that session a helm was commanded with an Orcish invocation to Cypher:

> Cypher's path be our guide, his wheel our protection. Forward through the astral sea, in the name of Cypher!
>
> <small>— the English of the invocation. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-23)</small>

No sessions were played in July. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-20)</small> In August JL ran an episode that ended with Barrias unsure whether a lich had cast the wish that saved [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/), the boy Jim had believed dead at Negroni Prime. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-11; 2024-03-08)</small> The crew then held a memorial for [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) and returned to the Zanzibar with the prophet, Tillamook, who had become the prophet of terrorists following the Beholder great mother. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-18; 2023-08-25; 2024-03-08)</small>

### The Lexicon and the B Team

The session of 17 November 2023 set out the season's plot. The Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas; some of them were conspiring to hide the reality of the Shrike from mortals; elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, might be involved; and the crew were headed for Necis to expose it. The Voodoo Boys operated on one of Nico's characters and hacked Jim's brain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

*Main article: [The Astralex Attack on the Zanzibar](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/)*

With the command crew away, JL asked for a story aboard the Zanzibar, and Dave ran a three-part B Team side mission. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-20)</small> In its finale on 15 December the ship's own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard. The crew saved most of the ship, which was left adrift in the Astral Sea with no Astralex, while the captain and command crew were a million miles away. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small>

### The pilgrimage

*Main article: [The Shrike Pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/)*

On 22 December 2023 the story rejoined Jim, Caprica and Sandy on the remote planet of Necis 1. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22)</small> The pilgrimage followed Simmons's *Hyperion*, with Lightfoot in the place of Keats on the map. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-23)</small> In January 2024 the three set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command, while Buttsack, Carlos and Mordecai waited on the helpless Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06; 2024-01-26)</small> The pilgrimage set off to see the Shrike on 2 February, and a week later crossed the sea of grass aboard the druidic windwheel *Edmund Fitzgerald*, fought a semi-hydra, survived poisonous pollen, and the story took a tragic turn with the orbital strike on [Lightfoot City](/places/lightfoot-city/). A pilgrim named Henri joined them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-03; 2024-02-10)</small> The season ended on 16 February, as the pilgrims were about to reach the Shrike. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-17)</small>

## Season 3 (2024)

### The Beholder War

*Main article: [The Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/)*

The third season opened on 23 February 2024 with a flashback to Jim's origin, bringing back Katrina and Horgus, to learn the nature of the Beholder War while a second war was under way in the present. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> In the flashback the junior characters were the flight crew of the SSJ Balduran, with Jim in hypersleep training for the mobile infantry, when a beholder ship crashed deliberately into Neverwinter and the whole Academy was sent to war. The war centred on Negroni Prime, an outpost planet of the Sovereignty where beholders lived, and the Beholder Lords were largely wiped out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

The episode ended with Jim stranded on Negroni Prime with Barrias, Tillamook and a dozen civilians in the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, and with the first use of the Demoncore, the weapons system whose later containment breach aboard the Stonecypher turned its whole crew undead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> In the last episode of the flashback the robot [1.0](/people/1-0/), built from parts of the downed ship, joined the group, and Katrina and Horgus abandoned Jim's group to recover [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/), taking 1.0 with them. Jim's memories were changed with a potion of modify memory after he last saw them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-15; 2024-03-16)</small>

### Sigil and the Time Tombs

Back in the present on 5 April, with the Zanzibar close to destruction, Carlos made his third wish and was bound as a djinn in a bottle, which Buttsack carries knowing Carlos is inside. The Zanzibar reached Sigil, far out of contact with the command crew. On Necis the crew had found that the attackers who looked like beholders were cybrids controlled by Lexicon entities, and Jim and his officers had become an independent force on a world erased from the Lexicon, brought there by Caprica following the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

The command crew entered [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) on 12 April, a dungeon crawl of snakes and sandworms. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-13)</small> On 19 April verse was read aloud in the tombs, including the line "I am a translator.", and a being called Zith'ra was named; the next day Dave posted [Martin's Final Cantos](/items/martins-final-cantos/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-19; 2024-04-20)</small> The pilgrims kept the innocents with them and did not go deep into the palace, saw a vision of the future with the army of shrikes and the approximational spirits of Cypher, and found people on the tree of pain. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> An exploration episode ended with a blast as the crew left the senate. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>

### The finale

*Main article: [The Wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/)*

In the next session, picking up the cliffhanger, Commander [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/) was killed in an assassination. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-03)</small> On 10 May the crew walked into the white light, and Mordecai and Irina set out to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small> The finale on 31 May pitted the crew against the Shrike, with a turtle ship on the board; each player ran at least three characters, and Dave ran five and the monsters. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

The Zanzibar took critical damage, and all the wishes shaped how events went. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> The arc's end turned on two wish effects: Carlos's earlier third wish, which had scattered the crew without warning, and a final Wish cast by Irina, who by Nico's account had no idea what she was doing. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-09)</small> Caprica died in finding the answer to the star song; in the days after, Dave hoped she had been rescued off screen. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01; 2024-06-03)</small> The Anachron/Lexicon disappeared from Sigil to avoid the broadcast and fled into deep space. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Sandy carried the memory of what happened on Necis, to the Consul and at the Time Tombs, but did not know where her crewmates had gone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small> The climax took place at Necis. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> JL's material for *The Starsong Awakens* names the battle at the end of the arc [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Home)</small>

Dave had not meant the finale to end the campaign. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> In his summary of Jim's story, Jim was betrayed by his closest friends and his government, lifted from rock bottom by Caprica's crew, stopped a great evil and was nearly retired for it, went rogue to find a greater one, passed through the veil, lost many of his friends and his ship, and ended back where he started. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> The story continued fifteen years later in [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/).
