**Genocide · The Korranberg Foundry, beneath the Korramont · Three years ago, the same year Sharn fell**

The Korranberg Massacre was the killing of the enslaved Deeptempura dwarves at the Korranberg Foundry beneath the Korramont, three years ago, the same year Sharn fell. King Corn and his daughter Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura were discovered planning a rising; Sedda rang the Thirteenth Casting before she was taken below, and the sound drove the slave halls mad. Overseer Velen Marsk and his undead ogres then finished the count under a writ of warrant signed Kaius III, which ordered the execution of Corn, Sedda, Sister Calvane, every dwarf in the foundry, and Marsk himself. Tens of thousands of dwarves died. The black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) was collared and bled by rail for the three years that followed, and the clan's descendants, led by Madame Tso Teryaki, returned to bury the dead and reclaim the halls of Rak'tafûr.

Korranberg is a one-road mining town in the foothills of the King's Forest. Above ground the locals will not talk about the *incident* at the foundry three years ago. Below ground there are thousands of dead dwarves, an Imperial death warrant with most of the names crossed out, and drains still caked with three-year-old blood.

## THE CLAN

The mountain belonged to the **Deeptempura** clan — deep dwarves, conquered and enslaved by the Empire years ago and set to work in their own hold. King Corn had found the Glitterhame and learned to forge the infernal ore under the guidance of a horned devil, and had made peace with the black dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) who nested in the lake below. That ore is **Shavat**, Black Khyber, formed where Shavarath touches the mortal world. It is what the [Hell's Bells](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) are made of.

The clan matriarch's account is one sentence long: the history of Shavat, the forging of hundreds of bells, and the slaughter of tens of thousands of dwarves.

## THE UPRISING THAT DID NOT HAPPEN

King Corn and his daughter **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura**, enslaved in their own home, had been communicating in code and planning a rising. They were also secretly forging a handheld horn meant to resist the bell's effects. They were discovered. Sedda was taken below toward the dragon's cavern — but not before she rang the Thirteenth Casting.

She did not decide that alone. For months something in the Glitterhame's fungal bloom had whispered to her, and she rang the bell on the Speaker's instructions, with a rock rather than the proper hammer, believing it would kill the Imperials and leave her the foundry. Found three years later in the black water at the bottom of the chasm, decayed and lucid, she confessed the whole of it and asked to be ended.

## THE TOLL

The Thirteenth Casting was tuned but never consecrated. It was enough. The slave halls went mad. Caskets in the sepulcher gallery still hold undead sealed inside since the night it rang; the halls of the living quarters had to be cleared room by room, three years on, by a Silver Flame templar and a dwarven burial party.

In one cistern the party found a writhing mass of bones barely held together. On a nearby wall, in chalk, a child had drawn a bell with dwarves covering their ears.

## THE WARRANT

Behind a Black Dove prayer seal signed by Sister [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), Overseer **Velen Marsk** was still on shift three years dead, still ordering his undead ogres to finish the count and exterminate the dwarves. In his satchel was a writ of warrant signed **Kaius III**: the execution of Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, Sister Calvane, and every dwarf in the foundry, ending with Marsk himself.

Most of the names were crossed out.

The madness did not do all of it. The bell emptied the halls; Overseer Marsk's undead ogres walked those halls afterward to finish the count. The rail tunnel behind the casting is collapsed. Above them the Trust's yard went on burning its own dockets on a brazier by the gate every night, exactly as it had before.

## WHAT WAS DONE WITH THE DRAGON

Valtrex, whose pact with the clan predated all of this, was collared, harpooned and bled. The Empire built a rig on the lake platform to restrain and drain him and shipped the blood out by rail for three years. He believes — and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s fourteen daughters are the evidence he offers — that his stolen blood was used to sire her heirs, and that he is their father.

## WHAT SURVIVED

Descendants of the clan returned to the mountain to bury their dead and reclaim the halls of Rak'tafûr, led by Madame Tso Teryaki and her brother General Tso. From that camp the Inevitables took the three things that make [breaking the bells](/lore/the-breaking-of-the-bells/) possible at all: King Corn's lead-bound folio of forging mathematics, his brass horn and the fanfare of dawn, and **KNELL**, his black-Khyber warhammer.

Sedda was given last rites by [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and consecrated beside the unknown soldier by her own kin. King Corn was found at last in the dragon's hoard, crowned, and named the *white queen* as the author of the massacre.
