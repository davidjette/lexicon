**Trial · *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* · 9 December 2022 · DM: JL**

*Also known as:* Below Decks · the Below Decks episode

**The Citadel trial** was an episode of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, run by JL on 9 December 2022, in which three junior crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/), [Se7en](/people/se7en/) and [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/), sat down to a card game below decks with an ensign named [Hermes](/people/hermes/) and found themselves on trial for the whole of their civilization. Hermes, a god, carried them to the Citadel, the last remnant of an earlier universe, and set them to reach its summit unscathed within twelve hours. How he judged them is not recorded.

## Below decks

The Zanzibar was hurtling through deep space with [Caprica](/people/caprica/) at the helm and the captain in sick bay, recovering from near-fatal wounds. In the lower decks the crew had begun to cheer for Mordecai, who had saved the captain's life. To celebrate his return, Se7en offered a potent drink of rocket fuel, fermented bean juice and grape skins, which tasted like poison and promised no hangover. Mordecai, Se7en, Forbes and an ensign named Hermes slipped into a secret hangout behind the engine room, and Hermes dealt cards for a game of twenty-one. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

The table remembered it as the Below Decks episode. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-09)</small> JL compared it the next day to an episode of *Star Trek: The Next Generation*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>

## The trial

Hermes asked how the others had come to join Astral Command, then told them he was not in Astral Command and they were no longer on the Zanzibar. He was not a mortal. His people had long been curious about [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), and most of them wanted to crush its civilization on principle for travelling wild space as it pleased, "like children tampering in the domain of gods". Hermes had proposed a trial instead, and the three had been chosen at random to stand for their civilization, as perfectly ordinary examples of it. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

The card table now stood on an asteroid, beside a magnificent old tower and the fallen head of a colossal statue. Hermes named it the Citadel: all that was left of the last universe, where the old gods had fought their last battle with the new and been conquered and devoured by their children. If the three reached its highest point unscathed within twelve hours, their civilization would have a reprieve from destruction. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

## The climb

On the way up the three met:

- **Space guppies**, which swam through the air from a pond to attack them, and a weeping [Chingwa](/lore/the-chingwa/), a tiny space pixie with hair of crackling white electricity who spoke nonsense words and gave a charm to those who showed her kindness.
- **The skeleton of a goliath** in ancient metallic armour, picked over by two balloon goons.
- **A space swine**, with wings like an angel's, caught by the foot in a bramble. It became Se7en's pig, **Mr Hamburger**, and the ship's cook [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/) was soon planning to make gabagool of it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small>
- **A grey scavver** circling the tower.
- **Jubjub, the golden goblin**, who had come to the Citadel looking for treasure for medicine for his sick wife and been abandoned there by pirates. He asked whether they had a ship and, finding they had none, drew a blaster to take their scrollers.
- **Two abyssal wretches** on the second floor, and a poisonous snake on the third.

<small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

In a corner of Jubjub's camp lay a book in Deep Speech, *Last Days of the Old Gods*. It told how the elder gods were defeated in a great war by their children, who overthrew and ate them and took their divinity, built a new universe for themselves, and left one shred of their parents' universe intact, the Citadel, deep in the Temporal Plane. Among the younger gods it named were Chauntea and Waukeen of Toril, the Nine Sovereigns of Eberron, and Hermes the trickster. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>

The last door carried three runes in Deep Speech, STRENGTH, MERCY and CUNNING. At the top Hermes appeared in a toga with a golden laurel on his brow, congratulated them, and asked whether it had not all been a little too easy. A copper dragon wyrmling coated in shimmering quicksilver guarded the final door. Beyond it Hermes applauded, reminded them that they had been told to arrive unscathed, and asked why their civilization should not be eliminated. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small> His verdict is not recorded. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

## Aftermath

JL posted Hermes's portrait the next day as a god "who could easily pop up again and make further mischief in some future quest". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-10)</small> He did, in the finale of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*.
