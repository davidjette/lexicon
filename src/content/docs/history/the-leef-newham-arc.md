**Campaign arc · *Unforeseen Strikes Back* · DM: JL · Played 11 July to 12 December 2025**

**The Leef / Newham Arc** was the first arc of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, the Eberron campaign that followed *The Unforeseen*, and was run by JL from 11 July to 12 December 2025 over twenty sessions, numbered Leef Ep 0 to 19. Its party was [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), played by Nico; [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), played by Dave; and, from 29 August 2025, [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), played by JL. The characters began at level 1 and reached about level 6. The arc opened in the rail-town of [Leef](/places/leef/), where the fighting came to a head in the Battle of Leef, moved west to [Newham](/places/newham/), where Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) was killed, and closed with JL's "year long episode" of downtime missions. One in-world year later the trio went to Sharn, where the [Sharn arc](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) began. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07; 2025-08-30; 2025-09-07; 2025-09-25; 2025-12-05; 2025-12-14; sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-batch.md)</small>

## Background

JL proposed the campaign in June 2025 at level 1, with the idea "I’ll start us in Newham to face LeBeefe." Play began at Leef. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>

The three characters came to it from different places:

- **Gemma Corso**, in Nico's description an infernal tiefling warlock with the wayfarer background. She grew up in Newham working at her adoptive father's junkyard, where he taught her "how to call forth a blade", left Newham years before to travel, and came home after escaping an arrest. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07; 2025-06-08)</small> Nico later said that her adoptive father Jane's patron, the archfey Kitsune, showed himself to Gemma only after Jane died, in Leef and Newham. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-07-06)</small>
- **Sir Dario Argentino**, "Squire Templar of the Silver Flame", a silver dragonborn paladin from a jungle near Q'barra, of clan Dawnmantle. He travelled under the cover of a journeyman smith called Flintscale. <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07; 2025-06-26; 2025-07-13)</small>
- **Eric the Cleric**, in JL's description "a tax attorney sent by the empire to investigate irregularities in LeBeefe’s accounting", a human who "Serves Oghma, god of maths" and "probably grew up in the Depths of Sharn". JL said the party would meet him "around level 3 maybe", as "part of the rebel storyline". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07; 2025-07-22)</small>

JL, as DM, set out the world the party entered. [The Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) is outlawed: "Membership is punishable by death. They’re also one of the more powerful rebel factions." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24)</small> His "stuff everyone knows" for the map of Khorvaire: "the goblins and gnomes in the south have allied and declared an independent state", "the orcs in the west have created a sovereign state", and "directly to our south is the elven homeland of valinar, where a strong rebel contingency is being persecuted." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> The currency of the Grand Duchy of Newham is the LeBeefe. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## Leef

Session zero, on 11 July 2025, came with *[A Bard's Tale](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/)*, the Imperial telling of the Mourning performed by Hamilton Connor and his acting troupe. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-11)</small>

The first played session followed on 18 July. Dario gave the Silver Flame's pass phrase, "Long night?" / "Means a brighter day.", and decapitated the guard Morlo Gint in front of a scrapyard; see [Leef Ep 1 — The Scrapyard](/sessions/leef-ep-1-the-scrapyard/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18; 2025-07-19)</small> Dave later recalled that on reaching Leef the party "got immediately roped into a terrorist act before meeting our allies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-21)</small>

In [Leef Ep 4 — The Train Station](/sessions/leef-ep-4-the-train-station/) (22 August) Gemma ran from the explosion at the train station, and Dario spared a Black Dove later named Luz Fayana. In Nico's account the heroes of Leef "attacked the train station and blew up their shipments and killed guards". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22; 2025-09-27; 2025-10-19)</small>

In [Leef Ep 5 — The Belly of the Beast](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/) (29 August) Eric joined the party, and they learned that LeBeefe was building a mind-flayer army to take the Dwarflands with [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s troops. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> By 1 September Dario was level 5. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-01)</small>

The sessions of 5, 6 and 12 September covered a swamp and the Battle of Leef. In Nico's words, "We were definitely hallucinating in the swamp". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small> The session of 6 September stopped in the middle of the battle, and JL planned to resume it on 12 September "to (possibly?) conclude the first arc of the campaign." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-07)</small> See [Leef Ep 6 — The Battle of Leef](/sessions/leef-ep-6-the-battle-of-leef/) and [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/).

## Newham

"Forward to Newham with the 3 amigos," JL wrote before the session of 26 September. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-25)</small>

The first Newham session, [Leef Ep 10 — The Theater Bombing](/sessions/leef-ep-10-the-theater-bombing/), included a theater bombing. A singer with element stones in her torso was not saved. JL, as DM: "When people in Newham think of “the rebels” they’re most likely to think of the St. Stamos cultists and their actions." The party gained a home base in the city, and an orc actress offered another lead. The party knew of one teleportation circle, at the train. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

In [Leef Ep 14 — The Rebel Allies](/sessions/leef-ep-14-the-rebel-allies/) (7 November) Dario's *protection from evil and good* "worked like a charm" in the fight, and afterwards Dave wrote "we're 3/4 for making allies from these rebels"; Nico added "we have vampi allies!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

In [Leef Ep 16 — The Mega Heist](/sessions/leef-ep-16-the-mega-heist/) (21 November) the party pulled off a heist, and Inspector LeGranouille joined them. Dave wrote that LeGranouille "saw us roll in and knew immediately we were good necessary trouble", and that LeBeefe's troops were "all mind flayers". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-22)</small> LeGranouille was LeBeefe in disguise, following with the party the whole time, and his turn was a ruse. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-legranouille-is-lebeefe.md)</small>

LeBeefe was killed in [Leef Ep 18 — The Death of LeBeefe](/sessions/leef-ep-18-the-death-of-lebeefe/) on 5 December 2025. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-05)</small> See [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/).

## The year long episode

The arc's last session, [Leef Ep 19 — The Year-Long Episode](/sessions/leef-ep-19-the-year-long-episode/), was played on 12 December 2025. Beforehand JL set homework: "Each of you will be given a downtime mission. Dario to the Dwarflands, Gemma to Breeland." Each player was to say what the mission was, what complications arose, and, since "You weren’t 100% successful", what intel they gained about the Empire. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07)</small>

Dario went to seek a full alliance with the dwarves for a breakaway union from the Empire. The dwarves declined to join the rebellion outright and offered potential support in materiel and funds. His wife, Princess Orange Chicken, was unsurprised, and answered with a proverb that an alliance of unrelated dwarves is "more rare than a hairless bridegroom". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small> Gemma's mission for the rebellion during the downtime year carried a complication, and she came out of that year with a teleportation chip found on [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/). <small>(Nico, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-05-09)</small>

JL summed up the result: "We are now firmly established as heroes of the realm, and we have serious roots in Talenta." Dave listed what Dario would leave behind if he went to Sharn: "wife and kid, commander of the rebel forces, ambassador to the dwarves". JL gave Dario the rank of "Field Marshall of Talenta", and Eric's dragonmark lets him cast *phantom steed*, with "a speed of 100 ft per round". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>

## After the arc

One in-world year passed between the end of the arc and the Sharn arc, which covered weeks of action; three more years passed before Thronehold and Korth. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-batch.md)</small>

In that year [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) sent Gemma on a mission to [Zilspar](/places/zilspar/), where she found information on Cob Cornwell about the opening of [POSÉ](/places/pose/) and the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/). Nico, as DM of the Sharn arc, said of the rebels there: "They saw what you all did for Leef and brought you into their ranks because you do good work!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-21)</small> Before the first Sharn session, "Digma hands you all a sealed dossier, passed from a trusted rebel contact in Sharn." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small> [Sharn Ep 1 — Welcome to Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) was played on 20 December 2025, with the party at level 6.

## Sessions

- **Leef Ep 0** (11 July 2025): session zero, with *A Bard's Tale*.
- **[Leef Ep 1 — The Scrapyard](/sessions/leef-ep-1-the-scrapyard/)** (18 July 2025): the Silver Flame pass phrase; Dario decapitates the guard Morlo Gint in front of the scrapyard.
- **Leef Ep 2** (8 August 2025): the pass phrase again, as "Long night, means a brighter dawn".
- **Leef Ep 3** (15 August 2025): a session in Leef.
- **[Leef Ep 4 — The Train Station](/sessions/leef-ep-4-the-train-station/)** (22 August 2025): the train-station explosion; a Black Dove spared, later named Luz Fayana; Dario nearly dies.
- **[Leef Ep 5 — The Belly of the Beast](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/)** (29 August 2025): LeBeefe's mind-flayer army and the Dwarflands; prisoners beheaded; Eric's first session; a scroll from Greenish Hughes' files.
- **[Leef Ep 6 — The Battle of Leef](/sessions/leef-ep-6-the-battle-of-leef/)** (5 September 2025): an opening crawl; Orangemeet; hallucinations in the swamp.
- **Leef Ep 7** (6 September 2025): the Battle of Leef; the session stops in the middle of the battle.
- **Leef Ep 8** (12 September 2025): the Battle of Leef resumes.
- **Leef Ep 9** (19 September 2025): Dario undercover.
- **[Leef Ep 10 — The Theater Bombing](/sessions/leef-ep-10-the-theater-bombing/)** (26 September 2025): the first Newham session; the St. Stamos cultists' theater bombing; the singer with element stones; the orc actress; a home base.
- **Leef Ep 11** (3 October 2025): a session in Newham.
- **Leef Ep 12** (10 October 2025): a session in Newham.
- **Leef Ep 13** (24 October 2025): a session in Newham.
- **[Leef Ep 14 — The Rebel Allies](/sessions/leef-ep-14-the-rebel-allies/)** (7 November 2025): *protection from evil and good* wins the fight; three of four rebel groups allied, vampires among them.
- **Leef Ep 15** (14 November 2025): a session in Newham.
- **[Leef Ep 16 — The Mega Heist](/sessions/leef-ep-16-the-mega-heist/)** (21 November 2025): the mega heist; Inspector LeGranouille, LeBeefe in disguise, joins.
- **Leef Ep 17** (28 November 2025): a session in Newham.
- **[Leef Ep 18 — The Death of LeBeefe](/sessions/leef-ep-18-the-death-of-lebeefe/)** (5 December 2025): the attempt on LeBeefe; he dies.
- **[Leef Ep 19 — The Year-Long Episode](/sessions/leef-ep-19-the-year-long-episode/)** (12 December 2025): downtime missions, Dario to the Dwarflands and Gemma to Breland; the trio rooted in Talenta.
