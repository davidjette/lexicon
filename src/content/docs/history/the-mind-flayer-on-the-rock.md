**Mind flayer infestation · *Light of Xaryxis* · November 2022**

*Also known as:* the sewer team · triple open brain surgery · the Mordecai Method

**The mind flayer on the rock** was a crisis in the first season of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, played over three sessions in November 2022, in which a mind flayer infested members of the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) after they entered its lair. [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) had suggested going down there. [Dr. Glup](/people/dr-glup/) overpowered the captain, [Caprica](/people/caprica/) took command and saved him, and Ensign [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) stopped the ceremorphosis of the infested crew in surgery. Carlos came aboard the Zanzibar afterwards as a civilian, marked by the mind flayer.

## Background

Carlos Vega wanted to get off the rock and was trying to gain the captain's trust to do it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-14)</small> Going down into the mind flayer's lair was his idea. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small>

The crew members who went down were called the "sewer team" at the table. Aboard the Zanzibar, only the security chief [Butsac](/people/buttsack/), and possibly Dr. Glup, knew much about mind flayers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-16)</small>

## The infestation

The plot ran to a third session. In the second, played on 18 November 2022, Dr. Glup, the ship's plasmoid monk and chief medical officer, used his monk abilities to overpower the captain. That session ended with "triple open brain surgery" still to come. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-19; character sheet)</small>

The third session was played on 26 November 2022. The party came close to a total party kill and held together. Caprica took command, saved the captain, and earned the respect and admiration of the crew. Mordecai, a warlock of the Celestial serving in the medical corps, stopped the ceremorphosis of the infested crew. His technique is called **the Mordecai Method**, and Dave wrote that it "will someday be taught in medical schools for how to stop ceremorphosis". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small> Caprica's player remembered the session this way: "Caprica got ate and died in one hit lol. We did it though. Morti saved those infested fools!!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small>

The mind flayer was "small fry but a good foreshadow", and it got the better of the crew as they wandered into its lair. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-14)</small>

## Aftermath

[Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), called Sandy, brought Carlos to security to issue his civilian credentials. She restricted him to the Engineering and Lounge Decks, barred him from fire-based weapons and tools, and told him to limit his Astralex access to matters of importance. Then her eyes glowed an eerie whitish grey:

> "Doctor Glup told me that the mindflayer left a mark on you. I witnessed this curse and heard it’s demands. If I find that you are harboring a mindflayer spawn aboard this ship I will burn you with wrath of all of Hyperion."

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26; sic)</small>

She welcomed him aboard "as the guest of the Galactic Soveriegnty of Freespaces" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

Caprica's player later recalled that the crew had found an abandoned nautiloid, and that Caprica "prob has ptsd from the last time she was on one". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-14)</small>
