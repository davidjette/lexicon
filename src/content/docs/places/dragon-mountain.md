**Mountain · The deep past · Seat of Tiamat · Site of the platinum temple**

*Also known as:* Dragon Mt. · Dragon Mtn

**Dragon Mountain** is a mountain of the deep past and the seat of **Tiamat**, guarded by hundreds of dragons. When the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) first reached the deep past in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/), [Brownsleeves](/people/brownsleeves/)'s party from another loop had split between [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) and Dragon Mountain, and Kay, the Traveler's wife, had gone there to have Tiamat move the Moon. That sorceress is [Karametra](/people/karametra/), who seduced Bahamut and had the dragons build a platinum temple as the spell component for a casting of **Move Planet**, to push [the Moon](/places/the-moon/) into the path of [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). In the final arc the party returned to the deep past and defended the temple while she cast the spell, against a tarrasque, some dragons and then Tiamat herself, and they succeeded. Tiamat was killed there and re-formed in Hell.

## The mountain

In the session record of Arc V, Tiamat holds the top of the mountain. It is "Guarded by hundreds of dragons", and the Tarrasque, summoned by Tiamat, approaches from the jungle, spiraling up the mountain and forcing the party's progress through "at least two sessions of dragon lairs". "This mountain is made of Earth and not plant, and conjuration magic is impossible any higher up the mountain." Above two dragon lairs, the peak is the Ice Lair of Tiamat, where the Abolethi Courtesan stands. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

When the party reached the deep past Dave named their "main locations" as "The Moon by way of the plane of air and a vertical protofeywild, fucking Dragon Mountain, and an underwater interstellar mindslaver city". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-17)</small>

## The dragons

At Dragon Mountain three of Tiamat's [chromatic dragons](/organizations/chromatic-elder-dragons/) confronted the party. **Nefarion**, a black dragon, returned "with the flayed body and skin of Drefan"; **Naga**, a blue, and **Marduk**, a red, argued with him:

> They are here to do the same as the Abolethi courtesan, to convince her gloriousness to destroy the Moon.

<small>— Naga. (Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

> SILENCE, they understand our tongue. They can duplicate themselves, there are likely thousands upon thousands. We face a deluge of these walking fish, fortelling doom from the stars with just enough foresight to convince her gloriousness that all of us will eventually betray her. Destroy these, and then we must execute our plan and turn the Tarrasque back toward the jungle.

<small>— Nefarion. Sic on "fortelling". (Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

The dragons had already gone to war under the sea. Dave: "the dragons went into the ocean to try to kill the aboleth and destroy Zoth, this is because they believed Zoth was calling the Leviathan". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> "the dragons fight each other and then lose a battle against the aboleth". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-11)</small> [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) had brought them against the aboleth to reach Zoth's ship; see [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) and [the Metallic Elder Dragons](/organizations/metallic-elder-dragons/).

## Arc V: the first visit

The party's first online session, on 21 March 2020, turned on whether to go to Dragon Mountain. JL, who played Zanzibar's twin Zeb in that arc, proposed "seeking out the good dragon king" or "tricking the evil dragon queen somehow into thinking we are hot shit", and then: "We need to roll into Dragon Mt. like we own the place. We aren’t here to make a deal, we are here to dictate terms." Dave answered, "you guys ARE time travelers with a message of certain doom", and posted a picture captioned "<3 tiamat". JL: "These maniacs only respect authority. Obey us or perish." Roland's player added "Dref should pee on them". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-03-21)</small>

The Short King on [the Moon](/places/the-moon/) told the party how his own party had split on arriving in the past: Roland, Revan, Stump and Zanzibar went "to steal the power to move the moon into the path of the Leviathan and deflect it back into space", and Magnus, Cypher, Yoshi and Stoth went to the Aboleth city. "None of them ever returned." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Parts II–IV.)</small>

After [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/) shot the Traveler, Dave, answering for the corpse, gave the party the line "“She went to Tiamat to move the moon”". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-04)</small> He set out what they knew: "Others have gone to Dragon Mtn to somehow move the moon in its way and crack the egg before it reaches the earth." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-03)</small> A week later: "You know "Brownsleeves' party split up to go to Rlyeh and Dragon Mtn, and that the dead "St. Cloud's Kara" went to Dragon Mtn as well." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-11)</small>

## The platinum temple

**Move Planet** appears among the Netherese epic spells of the eleventh tier: "Move Planet: (Evocation) - Encyclopedia Arcana page 13". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small>

Dave's quest board for the deep past, posted in December 2021, read: "Dragon Mountain, help Karametra and bring Tiamat into the team, stop the dragon war and lead them into space". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> Kara had cast a Wish on arriving in Antiquity that the Moon had been moved, and in the final arc the party came back to the deep past a second time to fulfil it. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small>

Karametra seduced **Bahamut** and had the dragons build a platinum temple, and she cast Move Planet inside it. The party defended the temple against a tarrasque, some dragons, and then Tiamat herself. Dave: "They succeeded if you can believe it." <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small>

## The Tarrasque

The Tarrasque was fought in September 2022. JL, now playing [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/): "We sliced thru Le Terrasque like velveeta. Give us a fucking challenge!!!!!!! I wanna die historic." Dave: "Tarrasque was disappointing honestly, but next one is an all out dragon ambush with Tiamat, it doesn’t get any bigger than this while staying anywhere near the book". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small>

JL credited Zanzibar, "such a tough motherfucker", and [Percy](/people/percival/), who "was used effectively to go inside the temple and allow us all to see inside thru DM narration". Dave: "It’s true, you were killing that from the inside one or the other", and of Percy: "He’s still in there and is technically the last line of defense between Tiamat and Karametra’s ritual". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small>

## Tiamat

In the dragon ambush that followed, the party killed Tiamat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small> Dave had set out her fate a year before: "Tiamat and her forces are destroyed before the egg arrives and she is lost in the abyss until she reconstitutes in Hell". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> Her brood afterwards stood with the party, and the Chromatic Elder Dragons are allies at the Egg, where their card's white action is "Tiamats Voice" <small>(sic)</small>.

Drefanmetra, [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), was nearly a fourth fight. Dave: "I almost had him ambush you after you killed Tiamat but I thought that was too many battles in one place". The party reached the temple before the Short King could act: "because you zapped there so quickly no chance for him to stop you". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The jungle:** the Tarrasque's approach, spiraling up the mountain.
- **The dragon lairs:** two lairs on the way to the summit.
- **The Ice Lair of Tiamat:** the peak.
- **The platinum temple:** built by the dragons as the spell component of Karametra's Move Planet, and held by the party while she cast it.
