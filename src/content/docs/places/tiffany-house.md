**Urban mansion · [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Heir: Darius Klaxon, then Maverick · Standing**

**Tiffany House** is an urban mansion in [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), whose owner traditionally sits on the Lords' Council. [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) married into the Darkcember family, and their son [Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/) is heir to the house; after the Klaxons, the heir is [Maverick](/people/maverick/), a distant descendant of [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/). [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) serves as its housecarl. Under the Cormyrean occupation [General Peterbilt Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) courted Waterdeep's old houses there, and on the day of the incident at the Grub + Grog it was the scene of [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/), in which [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) and a hired crew came for the **RUIN** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/) and the party took the card instead.

## The house

Tiffany House is an impeccably maintained urban mansion in the classic style, "the gem of the street its on" <small>(sic)</small>, with large pitched roofs and stonework carved from onyx. Traditionally its owner sits on the Lords' Council, though history has been unkind to the owners for centuries. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Among the things the party saw inside were clues in a tapestry, a pen and a scroll. <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>

Tiffany House stood five hundred years earlier, in the Ages of the Infanta, when it figured in Revan Darkcember's contract negotiations in Waterdeep. <small>(Arc IV, Winking Depths, Part IV)</small>

## The heirs

**Phillip Klaxon**, a minor noble, married a Darkcember, and their son was heir to Tiffany House. Phillip wants his son to become **the Panther**, and dealt with Griddy Champagne for the RUIN card, which he intended to use on a Masked Lord. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Darius**, who bears the names Klaxon, Darkcember and Montevideo, is a shareholder in the Company and the heir of a line that includes Tiffany House. His father sent [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) to him as a tutor in leadership and cunning, wanting Darius to take the Panther mask. His pact with Asmodeus, made through Griddy Champagne, "is unbearable for him," and he sought the RUIN card to rid himself of it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Maverick**, the drow bard prince, is a distant descendant of Revan Darkcember and, after the Klaxons, heir to Tiffany House. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

What the party learned on their visit: Darius does not want the place his father is arranging for him; Phillip is using Varkus; and "Tiffany House's owners have been doomed to Hell." <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>

## Griddy Champagne

Griddy Champagne is housecarl at Tiffany House. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party know her to be **Garidriel**, a fallen angel. The RUIN card had belonged to [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/), whose family discovered moonstone on their land and used it to travel and buy treasures, among them many magic shoes and garments and a magic playing card with a picture of a crumbling sword and crown. He lost the card to a thief, "a beautiful seductress who robbed him": Griddy Champagne. <small>(DM notes)</small> She obtained RUIN for both Phillip and Darius, intending to play them off each other. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Under the occupation

General Varkus raised funds at Tiffany House among Waterdeep's old families.

On the day the party came, mounted knights in violet plumed helmets stood outside, and Varkus delivered an incendiary speech against the Zanzibar, the Emperor of Cormyr and Faerun. <small>(DM notes, session record)</small>

## The heist

Phillip Klaxon told Nicollo, then going by Mach V, of his plan to place Darius on the Lords' Council by ruining **Beef Wellington**, and the party watched Griddy seduce Wellington with a false deck stacked with RUIN. Jeremiah surprised them. He and a goon, disguised as waiters, had staged a heist to steal the card; Griddy and Jeremiah fought, the party resisted him, and the party stole RUIN. Afterwards Wellington was ruined, Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep, and the Lords' meeting was four days off. See [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).

That night, at Fort Maximillien, [Sir Merriam Wordsworker](/people/sir-merriam-wordsworker/) asked Maverick why he had been spotted at Tiffany House earlier that day.
