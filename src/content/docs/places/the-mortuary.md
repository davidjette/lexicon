**Faction headquarters · [The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Dustmen · Standing**

The Mortuary is the headquarters of [the Dustmen](/organizations/the-dustmen/) in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), the faction whose role is to manage the dead. The city's corpse collectors bring bodies to it, and the dead arrive down a long chute. The [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) party woke there, each tattooed with a [fatemark](/lore/fatemarks/) and each last remembering being murdered by a handsome man with a glowing sword, [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/). They escaped into the ward and were jumped by thugs in the street outside.

## The Dustmen

The Dustmen, also called the Heralds of Dust and the Dusters, are the faction "Who believe Everyone is Dead". Their factol is **Skall**, their aligned plane is Hades, and their members include corpse-collectors and undead. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "We Dustmen believe that life is an Illusion, a false existence. True Death is the final release from the Cycle of pain and suffering."
>
> <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Graham Cracker**, a kind Dustman, has information on the corpse-pickers. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## How the dead arrive

The corpse collectors take bodies and deposit them at the Mortuary. [Pharod](/people/pharod/) has a lock on the whole racket, and [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) enforces it for a cut; Pharod deals with murderers all over the city to collect and dispose of their victims, in the thousands, extraplanar victims included. <small>(DM notes)</small> [Prez](/people/prez/) overheard Pharod accepting bodies for disposal with no questions asked, in a deal arranged by Rotten William. <small>(DM notes)</small> The corpse-pickers are hard to find, and it was corpse-pickers who picked up the party's bodies and dumped them there. The Dustmen offer to buy the corpses of the party. <small>(DM notes)</small>

[Heyu](/people/heyu/) came to the Mortuary for the spirit of [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/), who worshipped Cypher and hired her to retrieve an object from his corpse there. Near it she saw a corpse collector leaving a drop chute with an empty cart, and Rotten William's thugs threw her into the corpse chute. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Dhall

**Dhall** is the Dustman bookkeeper in the Mortuary, and can be persuaded to explain how the party came to be there. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "You arrived as we all do, a tumble down a long chute and then a heavy thud."
>
> "An ancient hand bade the Caretakers return you to this Torment."
>
> "You are marked by fate, it is not for me to strike thy name from the Book of the Living."
>
> "Pharod is a collector, a scavenger of the dead. He resides in Ragpicker Square. If anyone knows how you came to be here, it is him."
>
> — Dhall <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The escape

The party escaped the Mortuary with four questions: who killed them, who raised them, why they were there, and where they were. In the street outside they were jumped by thugs, two of whom died, took Ratbone into custody, and met a corpse-picker working for Pharod.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The corpse chute:** the long drop by which the dead arrive, "a tumble down a long chute and then a heavy thud."
- **The street outside:** Enya's beat, where Rotten William's thugs work near the Mortuary and where the party was jumped.
