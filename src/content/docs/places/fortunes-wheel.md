**Casino · The Lady's Ward, [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Owned by Shemeska · Standing**

Fortune's Wheel is a casino in the Lady's Ward of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), owned by the demon [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/). Visitors reach it by portal, among them the portal network of the Mouse Trappers, a troupe of clowns. Its owner staked the iron golem ringer that [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) defeated in a prize fight at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/), and its patrons have included [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/).

## Access

One portal to Fortune's Wheel is the Jester's cabinet of treats; another portal, a book of tiny monsters, leads out of it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The Mouse Trappers use a portal network around the city that reaches the casino. **Extreme Dill Massengill** receives visitors who come through it and takes a payment of 50 gold. Visitors are brought through the back halls to the casino floor, and then to the Suite. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Entertainment

Extreme Dill Massengill performs at the casino with **Adele Nazeem** and **Urethra Franklin**. <small>(DM notes)</small> **Eaton Fire**, an acrobat, is in possession of the "Cabinet of Mysteries", which bears the symbol of the Jester; [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s errand at the casino is for [Prez](/people/prez/) to deal with Eaton. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Prize fighting is offered at Fortune's Wheel as well as at the Grindhouse. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## History

Shemeska staked a ringer, a malfunctioning iron golem controlled from the crowd, in the prize fight at the Grindhouse in which Sharegrave had entered Battlebeast. Sharegrave knew Shemeska would be very upset if any interference were traced back to him. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party defeated the ringer.

By the account of Bonefish Grille, one of the drivers hired for [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/), Shemeska engaged that crew at Fortune's Wheel. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Patrons

Bumpkin Countrymile was invited to stay at Fortune's Wheel and is losing his family's moonstone fortune at its gambling tables; the casino does not know he is almost broke. He will sell his plane ticket home to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Sir Guy Standing** lost a Ring of Jumping and a Blade of Woe to [Maverick](/people/maverick/), and is furious that Maverick sold the Blade of Woe to Shemeska. He has a blood vendetta against Maverick, means to hire [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) to murder him and recover the items, and may be found at Fortune's Wheel. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Layout

- **The casino floor:** the gambling tables.
- **The back halls:** the route by which the Mouse Trappers' visitors are brought in.
- **The Suite:** where visitors are taken after the casino floor.
