**Casino · The Lady's Ward, [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Owned by Shemeska · Standing**

Fortune's Wheel is a casino in the Lady's Ward of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), owned by [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/), a demon. It is reached by portal, among them the portal network of the Mouse Trappers, a troupe of clowns. Its owner staked the golem ringer that [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) fought at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/), and its guests have included [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/). By the account of the driver Bonefish, it was at Fortune's Wheel that Shemeska hired the crew that robbed the party at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/).

## The way in

One portal to Fortune's Wheel is the Jester's cabinet of treats; another portal, a book of tiny monsters, leads out of it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The Mouse Trappers, a troupe of clowns, use a portal network around the city that reaches the casino. **Extreme Dill Massengill** receives visitors who come through it and takes a 50-gold payment, and performs at the casino with **Adele Nazeem** and **Urethra Franklin**. Visitors are brought through the back halls to the casino floor, and then to the Suite. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Eaton and the Cabinet of Mysteries

**Eaton Fire**, an acrobat, is in possession of the "Cabinet of Mysteries", which bears the symbol of the Jester. [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s errand at the casino is for [Prez](/people/prez/) to deal with Eaton. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Grindhouse ringer

The owner of Fortune's Wheel staked a ringer in the prize fight at the Grindhouse that Sharegrave had entered Battlebeast in: a malfunctioning iron golem controlled from the crowd. Sharegrave knew Shemeska would be very upset if any interference were traced back to him. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party defeated the ringer. Prize fighting is open to the party at Fortune's Wheel as well as at the Grindhouse. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Tiffany House

Shemeska hired [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) to hunt the party and find the cards, and at Tiffany House Jeremiah and a goon, disguised as waiters, staged a heist to steal the RUIN card (see [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/)). The Grille brothers, the drivers hired for the job, name their employers:

> "It was a tall blond guy with some bad scars, people called him **Jeremiah**. Rudest most arrogant prick I ever met. He hired us to rob the party while he got something from the house."
>
> — a Grille brother <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "Please dont tell her I told you. But it was in fortune's wheel. Shemeska was the client, she wanted some kind of **CARD**
>
> — Bonefish <small>(DM notes)</small>

At [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/) the party held the two Grille brothers, Bonefish and Big Macaroni, and [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) intimidated them into giving up their bosses, proving that Jeremiah was in league with Shemeska.

## Guests and rivals

Bumpkin Countrymile was invited to stay at Fortune's Wheel, and is losing a moonstone fortune at its gambling tables; the casino does not know he is almost broke. He will sell his plane ticket home to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Sir Guy Standing** lost a Ring of Jumping and a Blade of Woe to [Maverick](/people/maverick/), and is furious that Maverick sold the Blade of Woe to Shemeska. He has a blood vendetta against Maverick, means to hire [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) to murder him and get the items back, and may be found at Fortune's Wheel. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Places of Interest:**

- **The casino floor:** the gambling tables, where Bumpkin Countrymile is losing his family's moonstone fortune.
- **The back halls:** the route by which the Mouse Trappers' visitors are brought in.
- **The Suite:** where visitors are taken after the casino floor.
