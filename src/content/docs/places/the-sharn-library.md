**Library and archive · [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/), [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) · Restricted ArcEye archive · Standing**

*Also known as:* The City Library · the Grand Sharn Library

The Sharn Library is the public library of the GoldSky Neighborhood in Sharn's Upper City, described in the city's own literature as compact yet invaluable. It holds family lockboxes and an Old Histories Wing, and behind the public stacks it houses the restricted archive of the entire **Arcane Eye** network, ten years of patrol footage accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorised Imperial officials.

## The archive

Every ArcEye in [Sharn](/places/sharn/) records what it sees and hears. Patrol footage is captured on an **ArcEye Crystallizer** and transferred here, where it is catalogued and stored in restricted archives accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorised Imperial officials. The network was implemented about ten years ago; the archive goes back that far.

## The protocol

The library also runs a standing order. **Renn Tal**, an archivist from the Old Histories Wing specialising in [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and [the Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), warned three visitors quietly that the library was not safe and told them that if they needed to talk further they should find DJ Rust at [Breaker's Pit](/places/breakers-pit/). A male librarian named Peter then confirmed with her that per protocol he was to contact the Iron Veil if anyone enquired about Varyn Crona — and moments later the Iron Veil arrived.

The soldiers confronted the visitors outside and demanded they come along. The visitors killed them and teleported out. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) herself arrived at the library shortly after, questioned witnesses and left, which is why Renn Tal has been in hiding ever since.

## Present day

The library held the Grimpledink family's lockbox, and the key to it was one of Jessica's first gifts to the Inevitables. Renn Tal has not returned to it; she went into hiding in the Lower City and has stayed with the rebellion since. What became of the restricted archive after the Iron Veil dissolved is not recorded.
