**Sigil**, the **City of Doors**, is "the most central city on the most central point of all of existence" — a city of spires "perched atop an impossibly tall formation," ruled by the **Lady of Pain**. It is also, less famously, the corporate headquarters of the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/). As the outer planes collapsed it filled with refugee angels and demons and became a deportation apparatus; it was here that the heroes broke the Company's interplanar empire and the Lady of Pain burned the Old Man's House; and five hundred years later [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) opens in the city's Mortuary.

## The city

> Portals to other worlds and even the depths of hell are tucked into windows, doorways, potholes, and street signs. Each of these portals requires a key or phrase to activate them, and these secrets are the currency of this city.

Sigil's economy runs on access, and the Sword Coast Trading Company, built on planar shipping, made the city its headquarters.

In the history in which the [Drow](/organizations/carls-jr-and-the-drow/) split from the elves, Starfall Tower "was more or less empty for hundreds of years" as "Sigil was built around it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

Dave described the city of the Ages: "In the Infanta days, it was a cosmic crossroads ruled by an inscrutable but ultimately neutral demigod, its culture was a salad of influences from refugees and denizens from all the planes, but where gods were forbidden from entry. St. Cloud took advantage of its lassez faire rules and central location to build a time and space spanning organization." <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-10)</small> The Outlands around the spire are a wasteland, very distant from Sigil "because the top of the tower may as well be another plane". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-10)</small>

In Dave's words, "the city is designed like a dungeon", and parts of it stay closed until the right areas are completed. Portals "are linear magical objects, sometimes one way sometimes two or three way, interplanar portals are much more rare. But it’s a mathematical certainty that there at least one from Sigil to Suzail". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-20)</small> Outside the city gates lie the Red Bazaar, the ports and St. Cloud Tower; inside are **Old Town** and "the various enclaves of the city". The party beat the pit fiend Willy Fistergash in the Red Bazaar and bribed a criminal contact of [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/)'s "to sneak under a hole in the wall into the city proper". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-08-22)</small> A portal from Sigil leads to [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-08-23)</small>

## A drowning refuge

As the gods vanish into mortal [Infantas](/lore/the-infanta/), the outer planes collapse, and their angels and demons flood into the City of Doors. The cube-shaped **Modrons** check every refugee's papers and herd the rest back toward dying demiplanes, until **Port Somewhere** "is now an industrial interplanar deportation machine."

## The Lady of Pain

Sigil's ruler proves to be the sister of [Marcus](/people/marcus/) — the Raven Queen. The Lady of Pain and the Raven Queen are the twin queens of Netheril, Astra and Aurora Saharel; Marcus is the reincarnation of the Raven Queen. <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V has the Lady of Pain "banish her sister to Despondos" with the words "Seek your throne in the maze"; at the last battle "The Lady of Paid and her sister Marcus, the Raven Queen float before the stern of your ship" — End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter; sic on "Paid".)</small>

## Where the Company broke

It is in Sigil that the heroes break the Company's interplanar empire, and it is the Lady of Pain who burns the Old Man's House. Afterwards the Company is "crippled and scattered, leaving assets and employees up for grabs."

In the same fighting "the demon riot tears the western half of the city apart and the lady of pain vaporizes it as well", and Port Somewhere was destroyed. JL, who played Zanzibar: "Shit man. We have taken out St Cloud’s entire operation on the prime material plane in Waterdeep and now his base in the outer planes." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>

## The peak of Sigil

In the Time of Troubles the party crossed [Despondos](/places/despondos/), and "finally the party confronted Valen, Carl’s Jr. and The Waning One at the peak of Sigil, and Anti-Drefan transformed into a gargantuan fae dragon, and the Bane Blades finally awakened". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-10)</small> See [Valen](/people/valen/), [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/) and [the Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).

## After the Oblivia

When the Mammon Machine was undone at the end of the Ages, it warped the space around it, "sending Dis into the Hells and twisting the area around Sigil into a great Torus, the donut shape it has in planescape". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> In the order that followed, Sigil was "remade into a torus and sealed off from airships, with the Lady of Pain reinstated to keep it a neutral zone". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small> See [the City of Dis](/places/dis/), [the Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) and [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

## Five hundred years later

Dead Man's Hand begins here, five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta. Its heroes wake in Sigil's **Mortuary**, tattooed with fate marks and each remembering their own murder. They fight gangsters in the **Hive Ward**, and they cross **Shemeska**, a demon who owns a casino in the city.

In that age worship of gods or devils is taboo in Sigil, and temples never grow too large for fear of the Lady of Pain; the one exception is [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), the priestly sisterhood of Cypher, who keep [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) at the heart of the High Courts. <small>(DM notes)</small> [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/), a plague of the Hive Ward, was cured when the party closed a portal to a plague dimension that had opened in a drain in the Spillway.

## The Starfall era

In the time of [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), "the leadership of the Sovereignty is heavily advised by the Lexicon whose main node is in Sigil, overseen by what emerged from the church of Cypher". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-03)</small> Dave wrote that Sigil's "planar centrality led to its establishment as the capital of the known universe, with a Pax Léxica taking over and aligning the local good pantheons with the alliance of material planes, so I see it more like a Babylon, or an Istanbul". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-10)</small> See [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/).

## Places of Interest

**Port Somewhere:** the main port, and the deportation apparatus that has grown over it.\
**St. Cloud Tower:** the Company's headquarters, dominating the port, "almost all the staff dressed and looking exactly like Wainwright St. Cloud."\
**The Old Man's House:** behind a portrait in the Tower's executive suite, the millennia-old palace of **Wainwright St. Cloud**, emblazoned throughout with the sigil of **Calix**. Burned to ash by the Lady of Pain.\
**The Spire:** the impossibly tall formation the city sits upon.\
**Starfall Tower:** the tower Sigil was built around.\
**The Red Bazaar:** outside the city gates, where the party beat the pit fiend Willy Fistergash.\
**Old Town:** the city proper inside the gates, with its enclaves.\
**[The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/):** honeycomb tenements, corpse markets and gang turf, where the Dead Man's Hand party woke and fought their first battles.\
**[The Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/):** the Dustmen's headquarters in the Hive Ward, where the dead arrive down a long chute.\
**[The Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/):** Sharegrave's thieves' guild headquarters, strung across the span between two Hive buildings.\
**[The Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/):** a no-holds-barred fighting arena in the Hive Ward, with a prize fight every tenday.\
**[Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/):** Shemeska's casino in the Lady's Ward.\
**[Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/):** the towering cathedral of the Sororia, in which no god is depicted.\
**[The High Courts of Sigil](/places/the-high-courts-of-sigil/):** the seat of the city's legal and bureaucratic power, where Justiciars hear most cases and the Lexicon renders final judgment.\
**The Clerk's Ward:** home to a jump portal provided by the Company, [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/). <small>(DM notes)</small>
