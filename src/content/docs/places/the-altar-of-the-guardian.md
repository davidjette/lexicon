**Landmark · Southwest [Korth](/places/korth/), beside the barracks · Tomb of [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) · Former seat of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/)**

The Altar of the Guardian is a monument in southwest Korth, standing beside the primary barracks of the Karrnathi army, and it holds the **tomb of Varyn Crona** — High Elf, member of the Imperial Council, and leader of the Crimson Sun. It was the centrepiece and seat of that order while the order was active. Varyn Crona was murdered outside Fairhaven by a masked man believed to be a rebel leader; [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) carried his body back to the capital, and the Altar is where the Empire put him.

## The barracks quarter

Across a large bridge west of [the Great Plaza](/places/the-great-plaza/), the primary barracks of the Karrnathi army sit inside the city walls. The military is such a large share of Korth's population that the barracks mess is effectively open to the public, which makes it the one reliable civic amenity in a city with almost no commerce — and a very poor neighbourhood in which to be a criminal.

## The order it belonged to

The Crimson Sun was the Empire's secret service, and Varyn Crona ran it until his death. Esther Crona lived alone in the Varyn manor afterward, believing his soul had passed into his sword. She is dead now too, on a balcony in Sharn, thirty-one years old and Lord Commander of a city that no longer answers to the Empire.

<small>The Crimson Sun is also the name of a dragon that mated with Erandis Vol. Both usages are in play; the connection between them is unresolved.</small>

## The compound next door

A **secret military compound** stands immediately beside the Altar. It carries heavy Imperial presence and its purpose is unknown — one of three such buildings in Korth, alongside the unnamed hall near [the Hall of Submission](/places/the-hall-of-submission/) and the forbidden building in the far east. No record available to the Inevitables says what is inside any of them, and they have not gone to look.
