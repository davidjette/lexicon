**Subterranean lake and dragon lair - the bottom of the Korramont**

The **Black Lake** is the subterranean lake at the bottom of the Korramont, three hundred feet below the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) floor, and the declared lair of the dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/). On its far platform the Empire built a rig for capturing, restraining and bleeding him, and ran it for three years, shipping the blood out on its own rail line and keeping the dragon compliant with a bell chained around his neck. When the collar was broken Valtrex destroyed the rig himself and forbade anyone to come back.

## The descent

Down a chain-and-metal ladder through air that gets wetter the whole way, the mountain ends in water. The chasm stream lands in white noise and spray and settles into a pool that churns and then goes black. There is no ceiling above the lake itself. The water smells of stomach acid, and the descent has been described by people making it as going down into the bile and the bowels of some great creature.

## The shore

A shingle bank runs north from the foot of the ladder, past drag marks that read *large and reptilian*. A ruined but crossable dwarven bridge spans the water north; below it the stream splits, some west, some east. A second, eaten bridge further out will collapse under three hundred pounds of concentrated load, so it is crossed one at a time.

To the west of the ladder's foot is a cache of purloined supplies - months or years of stolen provisions and equipment, now old trash. It was the staging ground of the foundry uprising, and [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) was still keeping inventory on it three years after she died.

## The lake

Train track runs along the crumbling south ridge - the rail that officially never existed, coming down this far. A huge collapsed wooden platform sits to the west. Two islands stand out in the dark, the further one larger.

On the near island lie the hoard and, at the top of it, a corpse wearing a crown: [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), folded around the black-Khyber warhammer his people call **KNELL**. Roughly ten thousand gold and a quantity of silver ingots surround him, and the crown on his head is the **Crown of Seven Stars**, which is a devil's receipt.

## The rig

On the far platform stands the thing that explains the whole mountain. It is built in exactly the same idiom as the bell scaffolds upstairs - pulleys, timber, iron - and fitted with a **harpoon**. Beside it are enormous kettles, and a frame sized precisely to hold a dragon's wings still.

It is a machine for capturing, restraining and bleeding [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/). The Empire ran it for three years and shipped the blood out on its own rail line, and it kept the dragon compliant by chaining a bell around his neck so that he never stopped hearing it. It is also the party's answer to the question they kept asking upstairs about why the Empire marched prisoners down instead of killing them where they stood.

When his collar was broken, Valtrex destroyed the rig himself - acid over the kettles and the frame, and legendary blows for what survived that.

## Present day

The lake is a dragon's declared lair and he has forbidden anyone to come back.

> Do not return. This is now my domain. - Valtrex

The original bell moulds are somewhere on its bottom, kicked into the acid by the dragon rather than surrendered to mortals. When the Thirteenth Casting rang for the last time the dead in the lake rose and climbed the shaft, and when they were turned and driven back down it, he destroyed them.

King Corn's body is still on the hoard. The [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) were never told where he lies.

<small>Two of the kin's scouts, Fried Tofu and Moo Goo Gai Pan, were still alive down here when the party came back the second time - legless, their bodies melted away by acid, screaming for their mothers. A lair action raised the water and they drowned in the fumes.</small>
