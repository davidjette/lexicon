**Tunnel · beneath [Sharn](/places/sharn/), within [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) · Evacuation route · Open**

The Spine is an ancient hidden tunnel running from the Underhive toward the caved-in passage where the Cogborn were trapped. [Blair](/people/blair/) revealed it herself, after the Drain Snakes were cleared and she had decided the bargain would be kept.

## Clearing it

Two obstacles stood between the Spine and a functioning evacuation route: a collapsed tunnel, and an ancient sealed door. The collapse was cleared in a night by Berenstain, Snodgrass and Doppler Klink working together, opening a path through to the Cogborn and to their elder **Chris Understall**. Beyond the sealed door lay the **Chamber Room**, a vast webbed hall guarded by four giant spiders and an ancient stone golem — the place, by Blair's own account, where past suitors had vanished trying to impress her. It was cleared the next morning.

## The march

The Cogborn community was then walked out of Sharn along it: through the Spine, down the Underhive river, through the ancient Chamber Room, past the corpses of the golem and the spiders, and directly to the R.U.S.T. post and the mountain tunnel beyond, where the RUST Runners led them in song and handed out prayer sheets. They kept walking toward [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/).
