**City · Under the sea, near the Egg · Seat of the Atlas kings · In two eras: ancient, and ruined**

*Also known as:* Ancient Atlantis · Modern (Ruined) Atlantis

**Atlantis** is the undersea city of [the sea elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/), whose kings are all named Atlas. It stands near [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and it exists in two eras of the Temple Holdings LLC chronicle. In Antiquity its king was Atlas II, and the party teleported there during [the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/). The Bane blades told the party that when the Netherese were defeated the Egg crashed back into the ocean and destroyed the city. In the Renaissance, the party's present, Atlas III and Atlas IV are of Atlantis, and [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) fought beside the sea elves against the Drow in the Winking Depths and helped to found the city of Atlantis itself. The runes of Zanzibar's sword Glassbane come from the kings of Atlantis.

## The Atlas kings

Every king of Atlantis is named Atlas. Dave: "all from kings of Atlantis (all named Atlas)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

Dave's quest board for the final arc places them by era:

> In this time: /  / - Netheril, find Blackstaff, confront St Cloud/Alphalpha and the Queens, take the Obliviator / - Atlantis, find Atlas II, enter the Egg

> In the Renaissance: /  / - Atlantis, find Atlas III and IV, defeat the spider bros, enter the egg

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small>

Atlas II is the Atlas of Antiquity. Atlas III and Atlas IV are of the Renaissance, the party's present. **Atlas IV** is "the Triton prince you met in the winking depth". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> Dave on his father: "Since that guy was just a prince when you met him". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> See [the Winking Depths](/places/the-winking-depths/).

Atlas and his people are called both tritons and sea elves; the two names are interchangeable. The first Atlas is remembered in a rune on Glassbane.

## Antiquity

After the party charmed [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/) and killed Valen in the Time of Troubles, Dave told them: "you know Calix and Atlas have returned to their Infanta to lead attacks on Netherese cities and the Egg itself." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> See [Calix](/people/calix-the-betrayer/) and [Netheril](/places/netheril/).

The threads still open that November included "Netheril, Atlantis in two eras, the deep past and the Dragons". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-05)</small> The next session was to include "going to Atlantis now and the future". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-11-20)</small>

In January 2022 the party reached Atlantis in one session. Dave: "These simplified infanta adventures are working for me on these minor chapters" and "No reason to spend two sessions just approaching Atlantis". Nico: "I realized after the game, because we ended up teleporting to Atlantis with glass and we could have done so from motherstone, no need for the ship lol". Dave: "Absolutely no need". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-15)</small>

When the party took the Netherese ship from St. Cloud in [Arc VII](/history/arc-vii-leviathan/), its paper maps were "mostly concerned with location and surrounding geology of the Egg, the huge cleft in its side, and the location of likely dangers such as the mind flayer colonies near the Cleft, the city of Atlantis, and the not too distant city of Motherstone." <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small> See [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/).

At [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s funeral the sea elves pledged themselves to the party:

> You saved us from the mindflayers and defeated the Waning One. You are the true bearers of Glassbane. We are with you to the end.

<small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

## The fall of Atlantis

Dave's outline of the first loop's Antiquity: "Meanwhile the Atlantans also largely fail, but Brightmantle succeeds". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

The [Bane blades](/items/the-bane-blades/) gave the party the account of the city's end. Dave: "the banes have told you that the Egg rises from the sea as the Sea Elves attempt to enter it, and then the defeat of the Netherese causes it to crash back into the ocean and destroy Atlantis". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small>

## The Renaissance

While the party planned for [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/), JL, who played Zanzibar, proposed "Maybe while we are in WD Zan can return the sword to the Atlantans???", and later "How could we disrupt a possible Una/Mora alliance? Maybe with the Atlantans??" <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

At the start of [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/), the first option Dave put to the party was "go into the sea and look for Atlantis/The Egg". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small> The party went to [Refuge](/places/refuge/) instead. In 2021 Dave wrote:

> The entire ocean wing of the Infanta campaign has been left unplayed but I love that it’s still very much a part of the story - from the beginning they went to Harloch instead of the Merglen, you all didn’t follow the spiderbros and meet up with Atlas III (or even find out who he was) Zanzibar’s story intersects and merges but only in backstory, and now who knows if you’ll see Atlantis in this one

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small>

He proposed a quest line across both eras: "It seems like Ancient Atlantis -> Modern (Ruined) Atlantis is a great quest line that could do and it’s pretty brilliant since you can basically get a jump on the Spider Bros by getting the Sea Elves moving toward the Egg to head off Una". Among the allies the party could raise in its own time for the Egg: "the combined forces of the West, war wizards, motherstone, Atlantis, anyone else you can muster". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> See [Una](/people/una/).

In the loop in which Zanzibar was left behind when the party left Refuge for the past, he fought beside the sea elves and Atlas IV against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach the Egg, with his steward [Maximillien](/people/maximillien/). That Zanzibar is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/). Zanzibar also helped to found the city of Atlantis itself.

## Glassbane's runes

Glassbane is the sea elves' Bane blade, a shard of Perfect moonstone, carried by Zanzibar. Its runes are the blessings of the kings of Atlantis. Dave: "Glassbane got one from Atlas himself on the moon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small> The level of storm sorcerer it grants "comes from the blessing of Atlas IV". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> Dave on the full set:

> Yeah glassbane should have 4 runes like the others - all from kings of Atlantis (all named Atlas), the one I think we skipped is the FATHER of the sea elf you met in the winning depths

<small>(Sic on "winning". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

- **The first Atlas:** "+2 intelligent  Greatsword plus Mental Load".
- **The last Atlas:** "1 level of storm sorc".
- **The last Atlas's father:** "Immunity to Cold and 2d6 extra cold damage on attacks w Glassbane".
- **The Atlas of Antiquity:** "The last rune is from the Atlas of antiquity here in this time".

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Zanzibar's own rune is set out at [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/).
