**Island city and neutral ground · Khorvaire · Site of the Peace Summit · Ruined and bombarded**

Thronehold is an island city of Khorvaire and the continent's neutral ground, the place where the powers of Khorvaire meet when they intend to stop killing each other <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>. It has a great hall large enough for every faction on the continent to send a delegation to, and it is close enough to the northern tip of the Mournlands that what happens at the border can be seen from it. In Campaign 1 it hosted the annual Council of Mayors, the largely ceremonial meeting at which Lord Grey's committee assigned John C. LeBeefe to the Interoperability Summit at Sharn. Three years after the fall of Sharn it hosted Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar's Peace Summit, which ended in a massacre and a bombardment by Vex's own warships.

## The Summit

Three years after the fall of Sharn, Supreme Admiral **Vex d'Lyrandar** called a Summit at Thronehold and declared it was time to negotiate an end to the civil war. His push for peace was controversial — the Silver Flame and Zero-One opposed it, NEST saw propaganda value, and the Emperor's spokespeople denounced the gathering as illegitimate — and nearly every major faction sent representatives anyway: the orc tribes, the Hundreds, the Black Doves, Aundair, Leef, Sharn.

The Summit was presented as "a neutral attempt to end the civil war by priests and scholars of the [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/)." Vex was the Empire's convener under orders: "He did not want to offer a truce to the rebellion or the Uruk/hundreds, but was ordered to enter negotiations, but prepared for an ambush." The Emperor's spokespeople called "the Rebels in Sharn and Talenta traitors and deathbound but leaving room for terms with the larger forces approaching his capital", and rumour had the Emperor considering a personal appearance. <small>(Dave; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06; 2026-06-19)</small>

The delegates spent the afternoon negotiating, mapping intentions, and being watched: ArcEye cameras broadcast the whole Summit live across Khorvaire. [Lorian](/people/lorian/) determined that the masked Empire guards in attendance were all undead.

## The massacre

In the final assembly [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), Chancellor of Sharn, delivered a speech calling for the Empire to be outlawed. Sister **Maelis Dorn**, attending for the Black Doves, publicly accused him of the death of Hallorn d'Lyrandar in front of Hallorn's father. Vex rose into the air, wrapped in lightning, and ordered his own broadcast across the continent: *"Kill Chancellor Eric for the Empire."*

The masked guards then turned on Vex. They had never been his — they were [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s, and the trap was hers. Vex, screaming of her betrayal, called down his warships and ordered them to fire on the Summit grounds. Explosions ripped through the surrounding buildings and killed delegates and civilians. The Black Dove sisters had already left. The Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/)'s airship arrived, saw the rooftops burning, turned and fled the island.

Locke Pierce and the Hundreds ran for their vessels. Three passengers misty-stepped aboard one of them as it lifted, and Vex went up after the ship with them.

## Present day

The Summit grounds and the buildings around them were left in ruins, and the whole continent watched it happen on the Empire's own cameras. From the island the border of the Mournlands is visible — including the red dome of Locke Pierce's **Ruby Nova** blooming across the horizon and taking the remnants of Vex's fleet with it. To the free states, that was the moment a new weapon called **the Last Mourning** became the true end of the war.

> "Kill Chancellor Eric for the Empire."\
> — Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, broadcast live across Khorvaire
