**World-tree · The true nature of Toril · Dead**

*Also known as:* Ydryssl · Ydrissl · Toril · the Forgotten Realms (its aftermath)

**Ydryssyl** is a great tree that grew from a **godseed** in the Elemental Chaos, and the true nature of **Toril**: the world is **the body of the dead tree**, and the Forgotten Realms is what was left after it was killed.

## Who was here, in order

1. **Ydryssyl** grows from the godseed. The [Moon](/lore/moonstone/) forms from crystalized starlight in orbit with it.
2. **Dragons evolve here as native creatures.** They are the only ones who are from here. <small>(Which is what the [Metallic Elder Dragons](/organizations/metallic-elder-dragons/) mean: "Dragons were the rulers of the material plane. Even after the Aboleth arrived, they could only hold the seas. The skies belonged to Bahamut.")</small>
3. Fae evolve on Ydryssyl and the Moon. The **Banefae** hunt Changelings and cross into Ydryssyl.
4. **The Aboleth and Zoth arrive through a portal through the plane of water**, fleeing [Cthulhu](/lore/the-leviathan/) in the deep realm. They are refugees.
5. The Aboleth **inject Elemental Magic into apes and creatures, forming the mortal races**, and build [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) around the dormant Zoth and his ship.
6. **Cthulhu follows.**

## The death of the tree

**Cthulhu is in many eggs, which roam endlessly.** One of them eventually meets Ydryssyl, destroys it, and kills it.

[The Egg](/places/the-egg/) is one of many, and the others are still moving. June's account at the end of time describes the process from the far side: "Once the evening sky was bright with stars, but he swallowed nearly every one. These forgotten realms lie somewhere in the deepest past, erased by his incessant and unstoppable hunger." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part VI.)</small>

The [timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/) records what the impact left behind: the Moon cut off from Earth, the Feywild and Shadowfell split off, the Banefae exiled to become the Elves, and Ydryssyl itself destroyed — **becoming the Spire, which is [Sigil](/places/sigil/)**.

## Afterwards

The gods of the realm are "the split soul of the Star Child, whose name was Zoth-Ommog," and so are mortal souls. Everything living on the corpse of the tree is a fragment of the passenger who was fleeing the thing that killed it. <small>(Arc V Part VI.)</small>

At the end of time, Chronocypher sees it whole: "the craft around you falls away, revealing the immense and graceful curve of Ydryssl, its branches arching into space." <small>(Sic. Arc V, Starfall Part VI.)</small>
