**Principality · Beside the Kingdom of Cormyr · Seat of King Zanzibar · Drowned in the Starfall era**

*Also known as:* the Principality of Punis and Paenis · the kingdom of Punis · Paenis · Punae · Panae

**Punis** is a human principality beside the Kingdom of Cormyr, formally the **Principality of Punis and Paenis**, and the birthright of [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/). Its coin is the Gold Zan, and its paladins serve Zarus. The archmage [Valen](/people/valen/) founded the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/) just outside it in Antiquity, and the school has been its rival ever since. In the Age of the Infanta Zanzibar's father, the king, died, and after the party's war with the Arcaneum Zanzibar returned from exile and was crowned King of Punis. He later married Princess Roseanne of Cormyr, and in the Empire he founded, Fort Zanzibar stands at Punis in Cormyr. By the Starfall era, after the great melting of the 6700s, Punis and Paenis are "only a drowned memory".

## Antiquity: Panae and the Arcaneum

In the Time of Troubles the land appears as **Punae** or **Panae**. The party meant to go to "Punae, Cormanthyr and Netheril at some point" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-11-08)</small>, and going to Cormanthyr and Panae meant dealing with Valen, the elves of Cormanthyr and Copperbane. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-11-22)</small> Valen was known among the humans to have established "a temple of sorts in the hills of Panae" not far from Camp Vengeance. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII)</small> Camp Vengeance, "the future site of Fort Zanzibar", was where the party broke up a fight between the human settlers and the elven natives. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-05-27)</small> See [Settlers of the Dale](/organizations/settlers-of-the-dale/).

Valen "betrayed the Cormanthryians against the men of the Dale and in exchange was granted the land to build the Arcaneum". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2020-07-26)</small> Dave:

> Valen is the original villain of the Pubis world, he created the Arcaneum and the permanent rivalry with the kingdom of Punis\
> His cult is what you destroyed in the very first session with Zan the Younger

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23. Sic on "Pubis".</small>

## The Age of the Infanta

The *Evening Star* sailed from Punis; its old officer Commodore Beekins had been aboard "since it left Punis" and meant to ride it all the way back. <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile Part I)</small>

The high priest of Punis sent Zanzibar, its crown prince, to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) to ask [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) about his vision. "This town’s ancient temple is crumbling". <small>(Arc III, Interlude)</small> While Zanzibar was in [Suzail](/places/suzail/), news came that his father had died: "The king finally succumbed to his injuries last night in the presence of his wife, your brothers, and a priest of Zarus", with his brother Seppy to be crowned the day after the funeral. <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small> The party had chosen Punis as their next destination, "the Principality of Punis and Paenis to recapture @Zanzibar’s birthright". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-01)</small>

In [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/), Roseanne of Cormyr agreed to marry Zanzibar "to unite the thrones of Suzail and Punis under a single family". <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small> Dave: "Zanzibar marrying her would combine the thrones of Cormyr and Punis in the next generation". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>

### The Arcaneum

The Arcaneum is the headquarters of Cormyr's [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/), about two days' ride from Suzail. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small> "The Arcaneum is in Punis, so going home for Zanzibar means going by the Arcaneum". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-30)</small> Dave described it:

> There is a large ten-sided building just outside of Punis which is the physical location of the school. The inside is a large forum surrounded by function rooms and a handful of administrative offices. Inside a secure area is a crystal ball which serves as a portal to the rest of the school, which is set up on a series of local demiplanes, one for each School of magic practiced in the college, plus one for the Archmage and his work.

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-15</small>

A year before the party came, [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) wrote to the Company: "Please make arrangements for a visit to the Arcaneum at Punis." <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives, 3rd Rain's Hand 1490 DR)</small> After Valen's defeat [Celion](/people/celion/) inherited the school: "And for his work he inherited cia/hogwarts". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

### King of Punis

After his exile at the hands of the Arcaneum, Zanzibar returned to Punis and was crowned King of Punis. He named [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) and [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) Thanes and gave them holds; Revan's was Marsember, which became Darkcember. He named [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) his general and promoted [Maximillien](/people/maximillien/) to Steward. Seppy is a footnote, and Zanzibar's twin, Zebediah, was disinherited. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> See [House Zanzibar](/organizations/house-zanzibar/). Dave on the thanes' gifts: "your thane packages from Punis include 500 Gold Zans and assorted jewelry and trinkets worth another 100". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-31)</small>

JL, who played both twins, gave Zebediah's story: "Zeb was banished for being a juvenile delinquent. He went to Waterdeep and fell in with a bad crowd." And of the kingdom: "He’s definitely gay but that’s accepted in Punis. Zarus-worshippers are racist but not everyone in Punis is racist." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small> The heist that followed began in Punis, "since it's closer to Xenthil Keep and the hook flows through Punis". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

The Punisean crown had recovered the Baneblade **Glassbane** in a war, and Zanzibar carries it as King of Punis. See [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).

When Zanzibar's player proposed that Punis take in refugees from [Sigil](/places/sigil/), Dave suggested "letters of passage maybe? That refugees could use to pass through to Punis?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-20)</small> When the same player floated a constitutional monarchy for Punis, Dave warned: "A sudden influx of immigrants may have political impacts as well". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-19)</small>

### The River Lis

The **Punis Royal Guard**, human elite heavy infantry and an honour guard, stood in the allied line at [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/). The party fought there as Purple Dragon officers "As landed gentry in Cormyr and Punis", and the paladins of Zarus from Punis "are all in the PDs, probably 1/2 the officer corps". <small>(River Lis Units; Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21, 2019-12-19)</small> After the battle Zanzibar married Princess Roseanne of Cormyr. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## The Empire and after

Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr after his wedding, and after the Ages of the Infanta he founded [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Fort Zanzibar is at Punis in Cormyr. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

In the Starfall era the starship [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) flies above Fort Zanzibar, "which is now surrounded by oceans after the great melting of the 6700s":

> Punis and Paenus only a drowned memory

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10</small>

## Places of Interest

- **Paenis** — the second half of the principality. [Una](/people/una/)'s parents "own the mill in Coxswain, in the west of Paenis" <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small>; when she vanished from Motherstone, most people there believed she had been sent home to Paenis <small>(Arc III Part XII)</small>. The Arcaneum's file names [Kara](/people/kara/) "Kara of Paenis", and Celion asked her to "Meet me in Paenis, where this all began. Sit in our corner booth." <small>(Arc III, VIII Handouts; Parts IX–XI)</small> Zanzibar's player JL reported that Roland was made "Baron of Wyvernstead, a haunted country manor in Paenis." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-06-22)</small>
- **The Arcaneum** — the ten-sided school of the War Wizards, just outside Punis.
- **The temple of Punis** — the town's ancient temple, crumbling as divine magic failed. <small>(Arc III, Interlude)</small>
- **Wicktit's** — a bar in Punis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2019-01-01)</small>
- **The Temple of Olsat** — recently excavated near Punis, with a dungeon of pattern-recognition and analogy puzzles buried beneath it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-02-14)</small>
- **Fort Zanzibar** — raised on the site of Camp Vengeance, and ringed by ocean in the Starfall era.

The musician Gordon Lightfoot, who played the Sheepskin Tavern in Suzail, was called "the Lutist of Punis" by Kara's player Jenny. <small>(Arc III Part VII; Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>
