**Star system · *Light of Xaryxis* · Crystal sphere shattered**

**Doomspace** is a star system once enclosed by the primordials in a crystal sphere, which the gods shattered, and whose sun is gone, leaving a spiraling vortex called the Eye of Doom. The crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) set out for Doomspace from [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. Before leaving the system they returned to the derelict [SSJ Stonecypher](/items/the-ssj-stonecypher/), whose crew had been turned undead by a breach in its Demoncore, to recover a helm for a thri-kreen captain.

## History

Thousands of years ago a war between the gods and the primordials ended with every god banished from Doomspace. The primordials then encased the system in a crystal sphere that kept the gods out. After eons the gods found a way to shatter the sphere, though no one knows how, and its destruction filled the outermost region of Doomspace with crystalline shards as big as asteroids. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-04)</small>

What happened next is speculation. Some say the gods appeared before the leaders of Fyreen and Malas and demanded worship, and when they were rebuked vented their fury by collapsing the sun, leaving behind the Eye of Doom. Those who deny the gods exist turn to ancient myths: perhaps the sun was slain by a primordial foe in the form of a comet, or perhaps the sun was the shell of a great dragon's egg that finally hatched, "and the so-called Eye of Doom is the hatchling’s hungry maw." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-04)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

After their stay at Bral the crew of the Zanzibar set off for Doomspace. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-05)</small>

In Doomspace lies the SSJ Stonecypher, a derelict full of undead. The crew had already been aboard once, meeting a hodgepodge of undead, among them vampiric mist, ogre zombies and zombies, and had not found everything on the ship. Before leaving Doomspace they went back to recover a helm for a thri-kreen captain, whose people would otherwise not come with the Zanzibar. [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) was given an armored spacesuit for the assault. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-19)</small>

The ship's plight began with [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/). That weapons system, first used in [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), later went wrong on the Stonecypher, where a containment breach turned the entire crew undead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The Eye of Doom:** the spiraling vortex left in place of the sun.
- **Fyreen and Malas:** whose leaders, some say, refused the gods' demand for worship.
- **The shard field:** crystalline shards as big as asteroids in the outermost region, the remains of the crystal sphere.
- **The SSJ Stonecypher:** a derelict ship whose entire crew turned undead.
