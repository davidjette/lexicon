**Walled town · [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/) · Founded by Gregor Har · Ruled by a Jarl**

*Also known as:* Harlock

**Harloch** is a walled town on the island of [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/), founded by Gregor Har and ruled by a Jarl. The party reached it at the end of [Arc I, Hope in Exile](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/), on the day of Har's Feast, when Commander [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/) arrived with an Imperial garrison. After the garrison marched out, the Jarl begged the party to make Harloch their home and offered them the Old Temple, and the party's company, [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/), was based there. In later years the party owned half of the First Harloch Trust, and the [Bank of Harloch](/organizations/the-bank-of-harloch/) persisted well into the future, giving the Sovereign currency its nickname, "Harlochs".

## Founding

The town's patron is Gregor Har, "the founder of Harloch and patron of the city". Each year at dusk on Har's Feast the people drink from the Jarl's cup "in honor of the common man who founded Harloch". The heirs of the founder, Gretel and Monsor Har, keep a small barley farm in the corner of the town, loathe the feast, and speak ill of the Jarl. <small>(Hope in Exile, Part V.)</small>

## Har's Feast

A letter taken from an Imperial courier offered Thaddeus Shor a letter of credit for thirty thousand platinum gilders, "payable upon presentation of the deed to Leftfork Farm and all of its inhabitants to the Charter House in Harloch", and added: "We have a ship for you in Harloch." <small>(Hope in Exile, Part V.)</small>

The party arrived at dusk on the day of Har's Feast. The East Gate was closed and "A huge section of the wall has recently collapsed." Carlysle St. Cloud addressed some thirty fresh troops before the Jarl's Longhouse, and Jarl Ricard Weftain made him the guest of honour, to dine in the founder's chair. At the toast St. Cloud told the hall that the Forsworn would return, "And Harloch’s walls can no longer save it", and offered the Company's credit to buy out any who would leave. Assassins then set upon the Commander and the Jarl. <small>(Hope in Exile, Part V.)</small> See [Arc I](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/).

## Where the Hearth Is

The garrison later marched out through the South Gate, leaving the town undefended. The Jarl, whom the party had already saved from St. Cloud, came to them:

> I implore you to make Harloch your home. Please, be our champions and protect us when you can.

He offered them the Old Temple: "The buyer of this temple has yet to deliver funds. It can be you for the same price: 25,000 platinum." The town was deep in debt to the Trading Company, and the Jarl meant to use the money to repair the walls and "hire a sovereign army from the sons of Harloch". <small>(Hope in Exile, Part VI.)</small>

At the close of the arc, "At Harloch, half of Temple Holdings, LLC tends to their physical and psychological wounds." <small>(Hope in Exile, Part VII.)</small> Dave, in 2021: "from the beginning they went to Harloch instead of the Merglen". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small>

## The Company's file

The Sword Coast Trading Company's archive records what befell Carlysle St. Cloud there: "While investigating an earthquake in the town of Harloch, an incident occurred which led to his grievous wounding. His remains were sequestered by Special Projects." <small>(Arc IV, the SCTC Archives.)</small> See [the Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/).

## The First Harloch Trust

When the party took their airship, Dave offered them a policy with the First Harloch Trust, "started by the girl you guys saved from the brothel. You invest half your money in her bank and she's got plenty of leverage now to pay on a policy". He reminded them: "you are effectively 50% owners of the First Harloch Trust which you bankrolled with 1/2 of your holdings, so you are major shareholders in the second of two banks in the whole world", and "the 1st Harloch Trust has charter houses in many major cities". A home for the ship, "either in Harlock or in Motherstone", would cost another 50,000 gold "for an airdock capable of repairing the boat". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> See [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/).

The players had their own plans for the town. Patrick, who played Magnus, collected "that Gem Encrusted bowl that I commissioned back in Harloch", and Jenny, who played Kara, thought of "my team at the magic shop back in Harloch". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> For [Dref's Kids](/organizations/drefans-kids/) Jenny proposed: "Orphanage in Harloch is the  only ethical solution." <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>

## The Bank of Harloch and the Harlochs

The Bank of Harloch and its holding company, Temple Holdings, persist well into the future; the company was rebranded Temporal Holdings after the end of the game, and the Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs" because of the bank. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> Asked whether "the Harlock bank" was still a major economic concern, Dave answered: "The galactic currency could be Harlochs" / "They invented a magical blockchain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> See [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) and [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/).

## Places of Interest

**The Old Temple:** built ages ago to a god or goddess long forgotten; used as a palace, a gathering place and a market, and by the party's arrival a boarded-up warren for the destitute. In its bare gardens stands a broken statue of which only narrow obsidian elven legs and feet remain.\
**The Jarl's Longhouse:** facing the Old Temple across the main square; its hall has a throne made from the antlers of giant bucks.\
**The Charter House:** the Sword Coast Trading Company's business office, kept by Master Ben and his half-orc bodyguard Donan, with high walls and enchantments.\
**The warehouses:** the Company's stores on the waterfront, guarded by the Imperial Navy, with an auction block for slaves.\
**Isold's Metalwares:** the smithy of Isold and her wife Trenk.\
**The Dram:** the apothecary of the half-elf Fhastine Mageblood.\
**Harstead:** the farm of the founder's heirs.\
**The fish market:** where an old drow named Black Charlie keeps the biggest stand.\
**The docks:** to the west; the halfling fisherman Josa moors behind the south tower.

<small>(Hope in Exile, Parts V and VI.)</small>
