**Region and dwarven nation · eastern Khorvaire · Allied to the Republic of Talenta · Standing**

The Mror Holds are the dwarven nation east of [Leef](/places/leef/) <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>, allied to the Republic of Talenta. They sit on the far side of the Republic from [New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/), and the Republic exists in part because their swamp tribes were willing to fight in the west.

## The swamp tribes

Among the defenders who liberated Leef from Grand Duke [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)'s occupation was **Princess Orange Chicken**, a young dwarven leader out of the Mror swamp tribes. When the survivors of Newham fled east, it was the townsfolk, the refugees and those swamp-dwarf tribes together who forged the Republic of Talenta. Princess Orange Chicken is now the wife of [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/); their child is called Red Dragon Shrimp.

## The clans and their memory

It was **Chief Yellowmeat** in the Mror Holds who received the descendants of the **Deep Tempura Clan** when they announced they were returning to their ancestral mines beneath [the Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) — the same mountain, and the same abandoned foundry, that the Inevitables were riding toward at that moment. The mourners of the Teryaki line came out of the Mror Holds to walk back into a mine full of their own murdered kin.

Old kinship claims still function here as currency. In the Great Hall of Rak'tafûr, a knight speaking Dwarvish invoked Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long, and that was enough to turn drawn weapons into one cold night of hospitality.

## Present day

On learning that the [bells](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) were cast from an unknown planar ore, the Inevitables reached out to the dwarves of the Mror Holds, in the hope that ancient knowledge of ore and forging could reveal a weakness in the bell-metal. The answer came from a clan still living beside the forge.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The court of Chief Yellowmeat:** where the Deep Tempura descendants announced their return.
- **The swamp tribes:** the dwarven communities who marched west and helped found a republic.
