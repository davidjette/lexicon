**Town · The Outlands · Home of [Heyu](/people/heyu/)**

**Also known as:** Plague-Mort <small>(published Planescape canon)</small>

**Plaguemort** is a town of the Outlands and the home of [Heyu](/people/heyu/), who ran a private-eye business there for petitioner souls. When the soul of the murdered [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/) crossed the Outlands to Plaguemort, the divine will of Cypher sent Heyu to [Sigil](/places/sigil/) to help him, and she joined the fatemarked party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/). Her family lives in Plaguemort; her husband is Vel Crow, a human.

In published Planescape canon Plague-Mort is the gate-town of the Outlands that opens onto the Abyss. <small>(published Planescape canon)</small>

## Heyu's business

In Plaguemort Heyu ran a business for petitioner souls. Dave:

> She came to Sigil because the soul of Feng Milosh crossed the outlands to Plaguemort, and she ran a business for petitioner souls to give her their last coins to deliver messages or uncover truths about their deaths, like a private eye, and then she felt the divine will of Cypher telling her to go to Sigil and help this man.

<small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-heyu-and-feng.md)</small>

Feng Milosh, a spirit and petitioner, had worshipped Cypher but was headed to the Abyss for his acts in life. He hired Heyu to recover an heirloom from his corpse in [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/) of Sigil and return it to his daughter, [Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/). <small>(DM notes)</small> The heirloom was the **FATES** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). <small>(Dave)</small> Heyu travelled to Sigil through a portal known to a Cypherian monk in a distant town, borrowing the gold to use it, and the journey took a month. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Heyu's family

In September 2025 Dave wrote: "So everyone has their personal plots although heyu’s is complete - is she going home now??" / "It’s good money adventuring" / "Could move your family into a better neighborhood of plaguemort". JL, who plays Heyu, answered: "Yeah if Heyu has a bastion in Sigil she could relocate her family. I think unless something comes up she would want to reunite with her family." Dave: "Your research hireling could be your hubby". JL: "That could be fun. My hubs is Vel Crow, human." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-21)</small>

Among the visions of the Vision Table at [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/) is "HEYU'S FAMILY BURNED", given with "a feeling that this is because they didn't recover the cards." <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Deals with devils

JL, asking of [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) "how evil is Mach 5? He seems eager to throw his lot in with the Asmodians, no?", wrote of Heyu: "Heyu is from Plaguemort, familiar with making deals with devils. But she’s still interested in how eager Mach 5 was to serve that master." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small>
