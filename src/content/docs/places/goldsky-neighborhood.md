**District · [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) · Sharn's wealthiest quarter · Standing**

The GoldSky Neighborhood is the district of Sharn's upper terraces where the city's elite officials and dignitaries live. Luxury mansions, high-end apartments and elevated shopping terraces stack up the slope of the district, and from the rooftop of the [Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/) you can see all of it laid out at once, with the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/) and the [Black Dove Hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/) on the horizon behind it.

## What is in it

Within the GoldSky Neighborhood lie several of the city's most notable establishments, including the renowned **Sharn Theater** and its adjoining hotel, the Black Dove operated [Sanctum of the Last Mercy Hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/), and the compact yet invaluable [City Library](/places/the-sharn-library/).

It also carries [the Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/), the largest tower in the city — a busy commercial and residential hub, filled with shops, apartments, and constant civilian movement, with the small **GoldSky Iron Veil Precinct Station** at its base and one of the most widely viewed ArcEye feeds in the city mounted directly above it. [Hillary Heinrick](/people/hillary-heinrick/), head of ArcEye Security, kept her home in the neighborhood, and the original prototype ArcEye Crystallizer in it.

## Present day

Wealth here was Imperial wealth. When the Iron Veil's pact broke, the Silver Flame seized the hospital, high-ranking officials were taken into custody across the district, and crowds pulled down statues of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) in the terraces. The mansions are still there and a good number of them are now empty.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/):** the largest tower in the city, and the most-watched view in Sharn.
- **Sharn Theater:** the city's great house.
- **[Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/):** connected directly to the Sharn Theater, and one of the busiest civilian towers in the Upper City.
- **[Sanctum of the Last Mercy](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/):** the Black Dove hospital.
- **[The Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/):** small, well-kept, and holding every ArcEye recording ever taken.
- **The GoldSky Iron Veil Precinct Station:** a small precinct house at the foot of the great tower.
