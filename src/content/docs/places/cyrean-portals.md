**Portals · [the Mournland](/places/the-mournland/), the dead kingdom of Cyre · Military objectives · Contested**

*Also known as:* the Cyrean portal

Cyrean Portals are permanent portals left throughout the old kingdom of Cyre, now [the Mournland](/places/the-mournland/), which can be used to move troops and materiel around the Mournlands; whoever captures one can deploy across the region. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small> In [Korth Ep 3](/sessions/korth-ep-3-the-last-mourning/) [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s airship found a small group of [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) paladins holding a massive Cyrean portal in the northern Mournlands against undead and [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) hybrid units, with [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) among the defenders. The trio evacuated the survivors, and Locke then detonated the [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) over the portal site as a demonstration.

## Remnants of Cyre

Cyre was destroyed in [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/), which ended [the Last War](/history/the-last-war/) and left the kingdom a poisoned country of toxic fog. Dave described the portals after Korth Ep 3:

> "they’re remnants from the kingdom of Cyre, they’re are permanent portals through out the kingdom that can be used to move troops and material around the mournlands"

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

He added: "Capturing them means being able to deploy around". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

## The portal in Korth Ep 3

In the three years after the fall of Sharn, Dario joined the Dawn Crusade, "leading a cavalry of knights into the most brutal and hopeless fighting against the Empire in the kingdom of Thrane". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-06)</small>

After the Thronehold Summit collapsed, Locke's airship crossed into the northern tip of the Mournlands. Through the fog [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) saw a flaming sword cutting through the mist, and Locke dove the ship toward it. Below, Silver Flame paladins were holding the portal against waves of undead and E.D.E.N. hybrids. Dario was among them, wounded and still fighting. Asked whether he had been defending the portal or trying to close it, Dave answered: "He was defending it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

Locke hovered the ship over the battlefield. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and Dario held the line long enough to evacuate the survivors, and every paladin who fell rose moments later as undead and joined the enemy. The trio brought the last surviving soldiers aboard before the horde overwhelmed the position. Dario's wound carried the signs of Mistborn illness.

## The Ruby Nova

At Mt. Silicon, Locke's control-chamber screens showed the battlefield where the paladins had just been rescued, and he made it the target of his demonstration. The blast engulfed the portal site, pushed past the Mournland border and was visible from Thronehold, destroying the remnants of Vex d'Lyrandar's airships near the border. Dave placed it on "the army and the portal you guys fled from", "basically right on the area around thronehold", and said "that whole area you fled would be affected, if not destroyed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small> On its distance from Thronehold he said: "the purpose was to demonstrate the weapon, so it was close enough to see it up close but not enough to kill the witnesses, not immediately at least". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>
