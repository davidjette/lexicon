**Planet · Erased from the Lexicon · Site of the Time Tombs · Destroyed**

*Also known as:* Necis 1 · Neesus · Neecis (JL)

**Necis** is a planet, now destroyed, whose remnants are where [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) stood, where [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) originated, and where the climax of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) happened, at the end of both [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/) and [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/). <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> The world had been erased from [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/). [Caprica](/people/caprica/) followed the star song there and brought [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) and Sandy with her, and the three crossed it on [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) to the Time Tombs. The crew of *The Starsong Awakens* returned to Necis fifteen years later. When and how it was destroyed is unknown.

## Geography

Necis includes Lightfoot City with the sea of grass, the Tesla forest, Chronos Keep and the Valley of the Time Tombs. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-12)</small>

Its creatures include the common Necian python, whose venom is a poison, and sandworms. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-12; 2024-04-13)</small> A CR 8 semi-hydra and poisonous pollen were met on the sea of grass. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-10)</small>

## History

### Light of Xaryxis

Three things brought the command crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) to Necis. [Caprica](/people/caprica/) was following the star song, which had been her reason for joining the ship, and it led there. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> In October 2023 the crew had Sol and [Rachel](/people/rachel/) with them, who had two months left together, and weighed bringing them to find [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-10-21)</small> And in the session of 17 November 2023 the crew learned that the Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs, that some of them were conspiring to hide the reality of the Shrike from mortals, and that elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), might be involved. They were headed "to blow the lid off of it at Necis". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> By December 2023 Jim, Caprica and [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) were on "the remote planet of Necis 1." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22)</small>

Necis was a world erased from the Lexicon for reasons unknown. On it Jim and his crew stepped outside their roles as Astral Command officers to act as an independent force. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06; 2024-04-06)</small>

The pilgrims crossed the sea of grass in the druidic windwheel *Edmund Fitzgerald*, in a journey of several days, and on the way came the orbital strike on Lightfoot. The pilgrim Henri travelled with them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-10)</small> From the ground the attack looked like the work of beholders, and the crew of the Zanzibar found that the attackers were cybrids, controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> The Lexicon had engineered the attack so as to have its chance to unleash [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/) on the home worlds of the Sovereignty. The Demoncore did not go off over them; see [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/).

On 5 April 2024, with the Zanzibar close to destruction, the third wish of [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) carried the ship away to Sigil and left the pilgrims behind, extremely far away and out of contact. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> At Sigil its crew took what they knew to Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), who spoke against the Lexicon in the Sovereign Senate. The blast at the senate that ended the session of 26 April was an attempt on her life, made by the Lexicon and its cybrid agents. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27; Dave, 2026-11-21)</small>

By April 2024 the pilgrims were exploring the Time Tombs. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06; 2024-04-13)</small> With innocents they did not want to leave behind, they did not delve too deep into the palace; they saw a vision of the future with an army of shrikes, and found people strung on the "tree of pain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> On 10 May they walked into the white light. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small>

At the Shrike Palace, Jim submitted to a vision of Rachel, who proved to be an avatar of [Cypher](/people/stonecypher/), the three-fold goddess who is also Eloise and Istus. He entered the tree of pain and faced the Shrike alone in a psychic battle, and then left this time realm. Where he went is not settled: it may have been the end of time, or a vision of a future in which humanity has ascended and looks backward to destroy the Shrike and the Lexicon. He appeared on the bridge of the Zanzibar as the Shrike. <small>(Dave, 2026-11-21)</small> The arc ended on 31 May 2024 in [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/), against the Shrike, and the climax took place at Necis. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small>

Dave has said that Sandy would carry the memory of what happened on Necis, with the Consul and at the Time Tombs, back to the Hyperion entity, which would then know everything that happened to her there. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

### After the battle

In the period after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) the Sovereignty moved in on Necis and tried to rebuild the Lexicon there. Astral Command ran Project Paradox Dawn, a level-four classified operation on Necis secured by the grey cloaks, which was later shut down permanently. One of its captains recorded that he meant to advise high command to suspend the operation, evacuate Necis and detonate the planet from orbit. Whether that was done is not stated. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

### The Starsong Awakens

In *The Starsong Awakens* the characters went back to Necis on 2025-02-28, in a session that included a hologram to "Neesus". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-02-28)</small> Weld's notes also record Necis. <small>(character sheet)</small>

Necis lay 9,990 parsecs from the planet Maysee, beyond the reach of any ship without a Far Caster or a nautiloid; the crew seized [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/) because it could make the crossing without phlogiston. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> [Nine](/people/nine/), the Red Eye, came to Necis trying to commune again with the banned and vanished Lexicon. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-28-the-red-eye.md)</small> When the Terrasque arrived where Necis should have been, there was nothing but rubble: the planet was missing, and no one knew what had happened to it. Among the remnants stood the Time Tombs, six in number, and the crew split up to explore them. See [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) and [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/), which legend called the birthplace of the Lexicon. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>
