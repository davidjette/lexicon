**Lake · western Khorvaire · Frozen solid · Standing**

Lake Galifar is the great inland lake of western Khorvaire <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>. During the Empire's western campaign its surface froze solid, which General Stockton Thar reported to the Lord Commander as an effect of [Crona's Wall](/places/cronas-wall/) draining the warmth out of the land. The freeze was still listed among the standing facts of the conflict at the last Nest briefing before the fall of Sharn. The Wall has since collapsed.

## The freeze

At the opening of [POSÉ](/places/pose/), General **Stockton Thar** — an Eldeen Reaches native, a Dark Druid, and the officer holding the western front together — reported to the Lord Commander that the lake's surface had frozen solid the previous week. He tied it directly to the Wall: the barrier was draining warmth out of the land, and the deep spirits of the country were whispering of imbalance.

[Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) told him to hold it together and find his control.

## Present day

By the last Nest briefing before the fall of Sharn, Crona's Wall freezing Lake Galifar was listed alongside the worsening war in the West and rising Red Khyber activity as one of the standing facts of the conflict. The Wall has since collapsed. What the lake has done since is not recorded.
