**Region · eastern Khorvaire, west of Leef · Corrupted wasteland · Uninhabitable**

*Also known as:* The New Mourning

**New Mourning** is a toxic, corrupted and uninhabitable region of eastern Khorvaire, west of [Leef](/places/leef/): what is left of [Newham](/places/newham/) and the country around it. Newham City was held by Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), its first mayor, until a coordinated resistance rising killed him, after which a massive otherworldly explosion collapsed Newham into ruin and poisoned a vast region around it. The survivors evacuated east to Leef and founded the **Republic of Talenta**.

## How it was made

Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) — first mayor of Newham, aberrant-minded sorcerer, and by the end slowly transforming into something otherworldly — carried the Empire eastward from the outpost of [Leef](/places/leef/), raising an airship yard, illithid city guards and the Iron Veil there. Three operatives who had just liberated Leef followed his trail west to Newham City, met **Digma Beeve**, and with his guidance welded a scattering of anti-LeBeefe cells into a single coordinated resistance.

The rising succeeded. LeBeefe was killed. And then a massive otherworldly explosion collapsed the city into ruin and corrupted a vast region around it: toxic, poisoned, and dead to settlement. The gateway to the East became the second wound in the map of Khorvaire.

## After

The survivors fled east, evacuating en masse to Leef. There the townsfolk, the Newham refugees and the swamp-dwarf tribes out of [the Mror Holds](/places/the-mror-holds/) forged a new nation, the **Republic of Talenta**, which now sits between the corrupted wasteland of New Mourning to the west and the allied Mror Holds to the east. It is one of the few places in Khorvaire that was largely beyond the reach of Esther Crona's imperial forces.

## Present day

Nothing lives in New Mourning. Everything that made Newham — the lake named after its first mayor, the university, the casino, the remodelled hotel, the Rekkenmark annex — is under it.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The ruin of Newham City:** collapsed in the blast, buried in whatever the blast left behind.
- **[Greenbluff](/places/greenbluff/):** the played-out mining camp outside Newham where the first bell came out of the ground; whether it lies inside the corrupted region is not recorded.
- **Lake LeBeefe:** the man-made lake cut from the river Dark and named for the city's first mayor.
