**Thieves' guild headquarters · [The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Sharegrave's gang · Standing**

The Catwalk is the headquarters of [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s thieves' guild in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/): a ramshackle catwalk of planks, beams, ropes, pulleys and scaffolding strung across the hundred-yard span between two Hive buildings, with five levels running from a grand balcony to Sharegrave's lair. From it the guild burgles and resells large amounts of goods inside the Hive and some outside. The [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) party came there under a deal with [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) to kill Sharegrave, were trapped, and left in Sharegrave's service, with [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) bound for a prize fight at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/).

## Sharegrave's guild

Sharegrave is a lifelong resident of Sigil who keeps his headquarters on the Catwalk and many hideouts around the city. He is an enemy of Rotten William, believes that Rotten William and [Pharod](/people/pharod/) are being paid by someone to kill and disrupt his gang, and worries that the murder racket will bring a crackdown on the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small> Most of the gang seen on the Catwalk are Tabaxi. His man Ratbone knows the thieves' cant signal that gets a visitor invited up. <small>(DM notes)</small>

[Prez](/people/prez/), a tom cat, is a failed conman and mediocre burglar who owes Sharegrave 600 gold. Sharegrave sent him to spy on Rotten William; he was discovered and captured, and overheard Pharod accepting bodies for disposal. He is about 85% loyal to Sharegrave but would help the party, and "Knows more than he lets on". <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave had thought Prez dead, and now suspects him of turning coat. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The party at the Catwalk

In the Spillway the party rescued Prez and struck a deal with Rotten William to kill Sharegrave. Rotten William gave them a wand, and said it would paralyse Sharegrave and track them. The wand held one charge of fireball, with a command to cast it in Sharegrave's presence. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave knew the party had been sent by Rotten William, meant to interrogate and kill them, and told his gang to act normal and lure them into a trap. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party were trapped. Their severe injuries went unhealed, except Battlebeast's; Battlebeast was sent to the arena, and [Barold](/people/barold-bonds/) and [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) headed for the Gatehouse.

> "I trust you have gotten your rebellion against your circumstances out of your system. As far as I can tell, you are stranded in a hostile city with no friends coming to rescue you, some of you are on the run, from the devil or from yourselves, and you've been abandoned by a whim of fate to fend for yourself and obtain some absolution or at least a theatrical end."
>
> — Sharegrave <small>(DM notes)</small>

Sharegrave's errands for the party were to bring him Fieldy Smuts's cannonball, and to double-cross Rotten William and kill him for 200 gold each. He knows that [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) will pay for any information about the party's tattoos, and does not reveal it; he sends Prez to show the party how to escape the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party left with the cannonball errand and the double-cross of Rotten William among their outstanding quests, and the plan to kill Sharegrave with the wand abandoned.

## The five levels

### Level 1: the Grand Balcony

> An oversized, ball-room scale balcony cut into the Hive bldg 5, facing another similar balcony across the way in bldg 4. Between the two buildings above you are stretched a patchwork of planks, beams, ropes, pulleys, and support scaffolding, all supporting a ramshackle Catwalk across the 100 yard span of air.
>
> A few humanoid and Tabaxi peasants linger, and a few armed Tabaxi thugs watch from near a rope-pulled lift with a few boxes on it.

Two Tabaxi thugs watch, peasants sort through garbage, and more Tabaxi watch from above. A stash of 50 gold and a healing potion is hidden here, and a key to Sharegrave's lair is tucked in a small hole. <small>(DM notes)</small>

### Level 2: Intake

> Many Tabaxi thugs and … linger and work, breaking down packages, opening chests, cleaning weapons, and counting copper. They stare a bit at the party until Prez waves them off.

A Tabaxi pickpocket greets Prez: "Hey Prez who are your pig friends? Make sure they wipe their feet. Can't have berks tracking shit all over..." <small>(DM notes)</small> A huge net is suspended above the central hole, with a ceiling above the net, and a seam in the ceiling suggests it opens. The climb up is "not built for non-Cats." <small>(DM notes)</small>

### Level 3: the Workshop

> A wide open warehouse and transfer shop filled with emptied crates, chests, wardrobes, dismantled carriages and siege weapons, and stacks of armor, weapons, dry goods, and other equipment. A quartermaster, a grey Tabby with a white mane, orders thugs around, and brings devices and small jewelry boxes to a seated lockpick wearing an eyeloop. He grunts at the party and ignores them.

The thugs ask visitors to examine bodies and treasure in the centre of the room, where a pull trap drops them thirty feet into the net below. Anyone who avoids it faces eight thugs fighting to subdue, with spring traps overhead, dust and smoke, rope arrows and slings. <small>(DM notes)</small>

### Level 4: the Barracks

> A few Tabaxi sit at a small table playing Dice, near a series of cots, beds and humanoid sized climbing trees and platforms held up by scratching posts. Many trunks are pushed against the walls and random items belonging to the gang are tucked under beds and set on chairs.
>
> A platform above has an evidently locked door and a fine rug in front of the door.

### Level 5: the Lair

> Sharegrave's quarters are very fine and private, with only a couple vents letting light and air into the den. Upholstered seating and fine wood work adorn every part of the room, multiple fine works of art and trinkets decorate the mantles and walls, and many gold and brass featured chests are neatly arranged in corners or beside the wardrobe. A four poster bed hides a cat bed and there are scratches on the posts.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The Grand Balcony:** a ballroom-scale balcony cut into Hive building 5, with a rope-pulled lift and a hidden key to the lair.
- **Intake:** where packages are broken down and copper counted, over a central hole hung with a huge net.
- **The Workshop:** a warehouse of stolen goods, a Tabaxi quartermaster, and a pull trap into the net.
- **The Barracks:** cots, climbing trees and scratching posts, below a locked door on a platform.
- **The Lair:** Sharegrave's fine private quarters, with a cat bed hidden in a four-poster.
