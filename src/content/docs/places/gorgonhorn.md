**Settlement · western border of the Mournlands · Warforged stronghold · Standing**

**Gorgonhorn** is a Cogborn and Warforged stronghold on the western border of the Mournlands, held by R.U.S.T. and the Hundreds, and it is where the Warforged went when the Empire made being Warforged illegal. Under the leadership of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) a blended society of Cogborn and Warforged has begun to take shape there: a community strong enough to heal, grow, and one day resist the Empire on its own terms.

## The exile

When the Lord Commander [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) outlawed all Warforged in [Sharn](/places/sharn/), most fled to Gorgonhorn and the Mournlands. **Preacher**, raised in the Cogs and the last Warforged to remain in the city, stayed hidden with his family while the rest of his people escaped — and later came back up into the tunnels not to fight for Sharn but to save the Cogborn who had been left behind. His long-term vision is to lead them safely to Gorgonhorn and unite them with Locke Pierce's Hundreds in the Mournlands, where a blended Cogborn–Warforged society is beginning to take root. <small>— of Preacher, in the faction record.</small>

## The exodus

The route out ran under the city, not through it: down through [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), along [the Spine](/places/the-spine/), down the Underhive river, through a long-sealed chamber where a stone golem and four giant spiders had been waiting, to R.U.S.T. and the mountain tunnel, and out into the mountain pass.

At the crossing the RUST Runners led the people in song and handed out prayer sheets, praying to the love of something called **Lexicon**. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) teleported in once the crowd had cleared, thanked the party, and gave each of them a RUST communication necklace. The majority of the Cogborn of Sharn walked to Gorgonhorn that night. **Doppler Klink** went with them; **Martin Kross** and his son went during the same exodus.

## Present day

Gorgonhorn sits on the border of the most poisoned land on the continent and is held by the two organisations best suited to living there: the Warforged who do not breathe, and the resistance network that grew up in tunnels. R.U.S.T.'s demands at the founding of the Council of Sharn — Warforged citizenship, the arrest of the Iron Veil's engineers, and a new infrastructure department — were made by people who had somewhere else to go if the answer was no.

- **The mountain tunnel:** where Preacher and the RUST Runners gathered the people, and sang, before the crossing.
