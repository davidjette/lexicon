**Shop · [Gear Street Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/), [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) · Chip-implant parlour · ABANDONED**

Circuit City Chip Enhancement Parlor was a chip-implant parlour on Gear Street in Sharn's Lower City, the largest building on the street, known for chip implants and refurbished [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) equipment and advertised on pamphlets left in Lower City apartments. Its owner, [Doctor Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), used the shop as the entry point to a hidden laboratory behind it, where abducted Cogborn were held and their bodies fed into BioTec's research. Graves died in that back room when investigators traced a Cogborn death to the shop, and the building has stood empty since.

## The back room

A Divination ritual pointed the investigators here after a Cogborn body fell from a service lift. Inside, the owner took a suspicious interest in their implants, locked the front door and disappeared into the back. Hidden behind Circuit City, they found a laboratory of Horrors, with a large cage of abducted Cogborn, medical tables, monstrous experiments, and a corpse-delivery system feeding BioTec's research.

The owner was [Doctor Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/). He was the **Cog Collector**, and he was secretly the private owner of BioTec, of Circuit City, of the Arcade beside [Hopper's](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/), and of the apartment building his hunters were living in. He died in his own back room; the confrontation exposed the full extent of his crimes and severed the central link between BioTec, the Cogborn disappearances, and Project EDEN. Recovered from it were bank records, property deeds, eight hundred gold, an **ArcEye Crystallizer Lens** — the device that would later pull the truth out of Grady Marsh's head — and six severely ill Cogborn, handed off to RUST Runners.

## The skylift

The building's private, covered rooftop houses the **cargo skylift** Graves used to transport Cogborn bodies to the [Black Dove Hospital in the Upper City](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/). The skylift is still programmed to travel between Circuit City and the Hospital. The structure also contains a hidden back exit that leads into [the Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/), offering a discreet approach or escape route.

## Present day

Now empty, the building sits close enough to the factory that parts of its main floor and roof may fall within the EBT-7 radius of the Lower City's permanent **ArcEye** camera on the Cogs factory roof. Together, these features make Circuit City one of the most strategically advantageous positions near the Ziggurat camera — an abandoned implant shop that is one of the best-placed buildings in [Sharn](/places/sharn/).
