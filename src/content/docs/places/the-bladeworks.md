**Forge floor - the Korranberg Foundry, inside the Korramont**

The Bladeworks is the room where the **Fantanya Nyel** were actually made. It is reached from the [Great Hall of Rak'tafur](/places/the-great-hall-of-rak-tafur/) past a young sentry and a warning, across a gap where a bridge used to be, into sulphur and heat: the lava flows are still glowing years after the works went dark.

## What is on the floor

Beside the cold melt, several small castings sit inverted where they were left. Tools, weapons and ingots lie scattered across the floor, all of them **Shavat** - the same planar ore as the bells. To one side, on scaffolding, over a train cart and beneath a rope-and-pulley system, hung an enormous black bell. Behind it, the rail tunnel that once carried finished consignments out of the mountain has collapsed in on itself.

That bell is the **Thirteenth Casting** - the last great casting, thirteen of thirteen, which the [Trust](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/)'s own schedule records as *in the yard, tuning not yet taken*, waiting on a reference the House would not send again before the appointed night. It never left. Seen from directly underneath, the inside of it carries impossibly intricate circular patterns that resolve into a single arcane geometry.

## The two ringings

**Three years ago**, [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) came into this room during the uprising and struck the Thirteenth with a rock. It killed everyone in the foundry and raised most of them.

**And once more, on the last night.** [Hetta](/people/hetta/), the Teryaki scout sent out after the missing boy, came back with mould across his face and mushrooms forming at the corners of his mouth, called out an old Dwarvish word for cave-in, and cast *shatter* at the bell. The toll took everyone: the party, Hetta, every kin in the next hall, and the risen dead climbing the shaft from the lake below, all pulled into one mind and one thirst.

## The breaking

What ended it was a runesmith who did not run. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) reached the ladder with zombies coming up behind him, turned round, and played the fanfare of dawn instead, and the confusion lifted off the room. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) cast faerie fire blind above his own head so that a bell nobody could see through the smoke would still be outlined. And [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) swung **KNELL**.

The Thirteenth Casting did not ring. It cracked, lost its symmetry, folded in on itself and collapsed into a jagged warped ribbon of metal across the floor. The recoil threw Dario backwards and deafened him and Bofric until they had rested.

> Then let us be rid of it.

Later the same evening, while the party argued with the matriarch in the next hall about evacuation, the clang and twist of metal kept echoing out of the Bladeworks: Dario walking bell to bell, smashing every remaining small casting in the foundry, getting better at anticipating the recoil each time.

## Present day

A forge floor covered in ruined metal. Every casting in it is destroyed, the original moulds are at the bottom of the acid lake below, and the rail out is collapsed. There is nothing left here to make a bell with, and nothing left here to make one from.
