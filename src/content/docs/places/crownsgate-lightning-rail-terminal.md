**Rail terminal · Directly outside Sharn's city gates · Operational, under Imperial patrol**

*Also known as:* Crownsgate

The Crownsgate Lightning Rail Terminal is a sprawling transit hub directly outside Sharn's city gates, and the primary point of arrival and departure for long-distance rail travel across Kornath <small>(sic, in the source; Khorvaire)</small>. It is patrolled by Imperial [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) soldiers. Nine years ago the city dismantled the Lightning Rail Sub-Transit network the terminal had fed; its abandoned tunnels became the ground the rebellion and the displaced [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) used.

## The lost subway beneath the city

Nine years ago, Sharn dismantled its once-famous Lightning Rail Sub-Transit, a network of miniature rail lines that snaked through the city's levels and connected dozens of towers. The system was a marvel of Orien-industrial engineering, but a series of catastrophic events led to decommissioning. Remnants of the old sub-rail tunnels still exist throughout the city.

The [Sub-line District](/places/sub-line-district/) is built atop the ruins of Sub-Transit Station 1, its old concourses turned into Station One Market. The [EBT-7](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) the party used to seize every screen in Sharn was the old network's own communication relay — one carried at each station hub and in each train car. And when the Iron Veil pushed the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) out of the Cogs, it was the collapsing tunnels and abandoned sub-transit lines that became their last refuge.

## Places of Interest

**The main concourse** — arrival and departure for all long-distance rail across Kornath.

**The Iron Veil patrol** — the hub is patrolled by Imperial Iron Veil soldiers.

**The Sharn gates** — the main Lower City gates stand in City Center, opening out toward the world beyond Sharn.
