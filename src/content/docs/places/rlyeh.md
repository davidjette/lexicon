**Sunken city · Ancient Aboleth capital · Built around the dormant Zoth and his ship**

*Also known as:* Rlyeh · Ry'leh

R'lyeh is "an eldritch ancient city of Aboleths with impossible geometry," built around the dormant **Zoth** and his ship: the aboleth raised their capital around a sleeping god and the vessel he arrived in. It is not Cthulhu's city. Every device the chronicle calls an Infernal Machine came off that vessel, and it is where [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) returns in every loop to summon Cthulhu and escape into the future. The party travelled there to stop him from using the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) to do so, and did not stop him.

## The loop

**He does this over and over.** Each loop he reaches R'lyeh, summons, and goes forward — and "forms of the party try to stop him every time (or help him in some cases.)" <small>([Karametra](/people/karametra/) is one of the cases where a version of the party helped him, until she changed her mind.)</small>

In the loop of [Brownsleeves](/people/brownsleeves/), the Short King, his party "split up to go to Rlyeh and Dragon Mtn". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-11)</small> That loop's Magnus died at R'lyeh with that loop's Stonecypher. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-16)</small>

In the party's own loop, [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/) killed an earlier St. Cloud there, the Traveler, "the one who was the grey sage “last” time". Their own St. Cloud watched it: "He spied on you throughout the deep past to ambush you as you killed a previous st cloud at rlyeh then zipped forward". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09, 2022-07-03)</small>

Max's sacrifice opened the gates of R'lyeh. [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/) and Max entered the city just before the party's first time in the deep past, at the cost of Max's life, and freed Chronocypher from the Zoth matrix. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23; Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small> Dave's later quest board for the deep past read: "Rlyeh, get the Obliviator, meet back with Cypher and Pim (stop the dragon war?)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small>

## The Infernal Machines

Everything the chronicle calls an **Infernal Machine** came off that vessel. The [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) is half of it; the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) were parts of it.

## What St. Cloud did there

By the time the party followed him back, [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) had already:

- **brought the dragons against the aboleth**, to get down to the ship on the ocean floor — see [Metallic](/organizations/metallic-elder-dragons/) and [Chromatic Elder Dragons](/organizations/chromatic-elder-dragons/);
- **deactivated the Obliviator**, then a part of the ship;
- **taken the Anachron**, then a part of the ship;
- and **flown the ship into space to meet Cthulhu**, then travelled forward in time with it, ahead of the impact.

## In the session record

The Traveler "can lead the party through 'Rlyeh" to free the slave races from the Aboleth and the syphon matrix, and to try to stop St. Cloud's sabotage of the joined mammon-obliviator-anachron. Survivors "can exit Rlyeh and witness the first impact, which comes in a number of days." <small>(Arc V, [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/), Parts II–VI. The record writes it both "Rlyeh" and "'Rlyeh".)</small>

## Revan's vision

The Infernal Machines and the city they came from have many false tellings. One is the vision [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) had on reading the book, in which his mind "traveled into the lost corpse city of Ry'leh. where dead Cthulu lies dreaming." A gentle voice sang him the story of the [star children](/species/the-star-children/), spawn of Cthulhu who broke free of his mind and "found their way to the other side, in a world of material certainty, populated only by giant reptiles and their dragon gods. The star children stayed there, in the sea, and built the city of Ry'leh, and set about to replace their aging bodies." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30)</small>

In the vision the city had "buildings with walls and roads that meet at impossible angles, unrecognizable forms in the shadows, twisted and incomprehensible architecture leading your mind into spaces with no exit", "all built from a slightly glowing green stone, all beneath the ocean, in the darkness, built for the star children, who you see depicted throughout the city." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30)</small>
