**Tower · [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/), [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) · The largest tower in Sharn · Standing**

The Grand GoldSky Tower is the largest tower in [Sharn](/places/sharn/): a busy commercial and residential hub filled with shops, apartments and constant civilian movement, with stacked walkways and balcony levels running up its flanks and skybuses passing up, down and diagonally between it and the neighbouring districts. It is visible from the garden terrace of [the Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/) across the city.

## The most-watched view in the city

High on the tower, mounted directly above the small **GoldSky Iron Veil Precinct Station**, sits one of Sharn's four permanent **ArcEye** cameras. It is fixed and non-rotating, and it captures the Skyport and its air-traffic lanes, the Veil Building rising in the distance, and [POSÉ](/places/pose/) and the surrounding towers, apartments and businesses.

This feed is iconic across the city. Citizens use it to track airship movement and weather, and — routinely — to check whether [Esther](/people/esther-crona/)'s airship, the **Black Veil**, is currently in Sharn. It is the most widely viewed ArcEye feed there is.

## As a target

High risk. The camera sits directly over a Veil precinct house and the tower is crowded, so suspicious behaviour is noticed quickly. Against that: the stacked walkways and balcony levels allow vertical manoeuvring, an override can be attempted from a level above or below the precinct's elevation so long as the device is within range, and the frequent skybuses briefly pass within that range as moving platforms.
