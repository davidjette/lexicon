**District · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), south · Commercial artery · Standing**

*Also known as:* Gear St. Markets · Gear Street

The Gear Street Markets are the busiest commercial artery of Sharn's Lower City, a crowded stretch of shops, food stalls, scrap vendors and arcane tinkerers where locals eat, trade and haggle beneath flickering lights and rusted signage. They serve chiefly the residents of [The Cogs](/places/the-cogs/) and [the Lowline Ward](/places/lowline-ward/). Early victims of the Cog Collector were dumped around the market, and the investigation into the killings began on the street before it reached [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) at the end of it.

## The stalls

Notable storefronts include **Doppler Klink's Arcane Gear** <small>(Doppler Klank's on the gazetteer)</small> — a cramped stall where [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/), a Cogborn veteran of the Last War, repairs arcane devices, builds custom wands, and sleeps in the back room when business is slow — and the largest building on the street, [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/), known for chip implants and refurbished BioTec equipment.

## The bodies

Early victims of the Cog Collector were found dumped around Gear Street Market. Then, at dawn one morning, a wrapped Cogborn body fell from a cargo skylift onto Gear Street Market, dropping as the lift was rising toward the Upper City — the first missing Cogborn body to appear in almost a year. The investigation into the **Cog Collector** began here before it walked through Circuit City's front door. Nobody on the street had failed to notice that people were disappearing; the protest chant that had gone up in the Upper City weeks earlier was already the district's own.

## Present day

The street is close enough to the great factory in the Cogs that the Lower City's permanent **ArcEye** camera can be reached from the market side without entering Veil property at all, which made Gear Street the most viable ground in Sharn for hijacking the Empire's broadcast. Patrols move along both the ground-level streets and the elevated catwalks above; crowds make stealth difficult and make disappearing easy.

Doppler Klink left with his people during the Cogborn exodus, came back, and now builds wands at cost for anyone who intends to use them on the Empire.

**Places of Interest:**

- **Doppler Klink's Arcane Gear:** repairs, custom wands, and the best-informed stall on the street.
- **[Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/):** the largest building on Gear Street; chip implants, refurbished BioTec equipment, and worse.
- **The cargo skylift:** Circuit City's private rooftop lift, still programmed to run between the shop and the Black Dove Hospital; it dropped a corpse on the market.
