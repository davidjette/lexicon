**Underground port city · Beneath [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), on the river Sargauth · Held by the Xanathar Guild · The party's rendezvous**

**Also known as:** the Port of Shadow

**Skullport** is a rogue city beneath [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), built in an immense cavern where the underground river Sargauth runs into an underground sea, and held by the Xanathar Guild from its fortress on Skull Island. It is reached from the third level of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/) by the river and by five meandering passages, and from the sea caves that run down from Waterdeep's shore. The Company's standard rendezvous there is the Flagon and the Dragon, a tavern on the lower level of the town, and the party came to the city on [the Road to Skullport](/history/the-road-to-skullport/) to deliver the Company's book [O-3255](/items/o-3255/).

## The city

The town stands on three levels, a lower, a middle and a top level, and a stone bridge joins its southern end to **Skull Island**, around which the sluggish river Sargauth flows. The Port of Shadow has been held, claimed or occupied over the years by duergar, drow, dwarves, crazed wizards, slavers and pirates. It is now under the sway of the Xanathar, a beholder crime lord, who has turned it into a haven for the Guild. <small>(Dave, at the table, 2026-09-26)</small>

Atop Skull Island stands a fortress garrisoned by hundreds of the Xanathar's minions, duergar and humans in dirty dark armour, under the Xanathar's banners and symbol. Huge ballistae stand along its walls, and thirteen flameskulls fly around the outside of the city at different heights, burning, so they can be seen from far off. Steel augers are installed along the mouth of each harbour on the island and can be raised or lowered on rusty iron chains from winches in the towers to either side; raised, they pierce the hulls of passing ships and sink them. Around the harbour stand barracks for about a hundred soldiers and thugs, and a great tower called **the Tower of the Seven Woes**. The main gate on the far side leads onto **the Arkstone Bridge** into the city; the middle of the bridge is rigged to collapse. <small>(Dave, at the table, 2026-09-26)</small>

The Guild takes a cut of every piece of business done in Skullport. Swimming across to the island is not permitted, and there is no pedestrian access from the landing at Beggar's Rest Pass; a duergar lieutenant rows out to collect the toll and ferries paying visitors across. <small>(session summary)</small>

The party speculated that the Xanathar might hold one of the cards of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/); nothing in play has shown it.

## The approach

The tunnel from Level 3 of Undermountain comes out at **Beggar's Rest Pass**, a couple of miles of passage running in and out beside the river, patrolled by hobgoblins and bugbears. From its mouth the whole city can be seen across the water. An old dock creaks at the water's edge with no boats at it, and a hungry kuo-toa squats there waiting for food. The water below is dark and holds scavenger merrows, which pick over the wrecked ships. <small>(session summary)</small>

## The party in Skullport

The party reached the city with O-3255, fed the kuo-toa and got a **cloak of the manta ray** from it for [Maverick](/people/maverick/), and fought two merrows in the harbour. The duergar lieutenant took one gold coin from them, ferried them into the Skull Island harbour past the Tower of the Seven Woes, had them lightly patted down, and let them through the main gate onto the Arkstone Bridge. They made for the Flagon and the Dragon on the lower level. <small>(session summary)</small>

## Places of Interest

**Skull Island:** the Xanathar Guild's fortress in the river.\
**The Tower of the Seven Woes:** on the island, by the harbour.\
**The island harbours:** closed by steel augers on chains.\
**The Arkstone Bridge:** from the island's main gate into the city, rigged to collapse at its middle.\
**The Flagon and the Dragon:** a tavern on the lower level, the Company's standard rendezvous.\
**Beggar's Rest Pass:** the tunnel from Undermountain's third level.
