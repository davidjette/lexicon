**Maze dimension · The Shadowfell · Holds the City of Dis and the Penteract**

*Also known as:* the Maze of Despondos · the Mazes of Despondos · the maze

**Despondos** is a maze dimension, "a great labyrinth in the Shadowfell", made in antiquity by the copper dragon [Tylerjynex](/people/tylerjinex/) to encase the Seat of Judgement, the corpse of Thanatos and the Soul Syphon. The places nest: Despondos holds [the City of Dis](/places/dis/), and Dis holds [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/). The [Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/), united by [Drefanmetra](/people/the-waning-one/), attacked the maze and enslaved Tylerjynex to reach the Penteract. The Queens of [Netheril](/places/netheril/) and the Grey Sage defeated the Banefae and cast them in; the machines were hidden there, and the Queens and the Grey Sage were themselves trapped inside for centuries until the sage, the Old Man, escaped. In Netheril's era the souls of the dead marched through Despondos to be fed into the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/). In the Time of Troubles the party entered it through "the gate foretold" and fought Valen, Carl's Jr. and the Waning One at the peak of Sigil, which is Deadfall Peak.

## The making

"Tylerjinex made the maze dimension of Despondos to encase the Seat of Judgement, The corpse of Thanatos, and the Soul Syphon." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> Inside it he built the Penteract, with the god of puzzles, as an impossible labyrinth that the dead can pass because they have an eternity, and to keep the Banefae and the Netherese from the Seat.

Before the Oblivia the machines of death lay at the centre: "It used to be that the Mammon Machine and the Soul Syphon were are the heart of the Penteract, located at Sigil/Dis inside Despondos, a great labyrinth in the Shadowfell." <small>(Sic on "were are". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>

## The road of the dead

The maze is the road the souls of the dead walk. "It’s power drew apostolic souls from the dead through the maze and into the syphon, where Thanatos (and later the Raven Queen) divided them into their heavens and hells." <small>(Sic on "It’s". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> An in-world account from the Dead Man's Hand era has the souls wander "sometimes for millennia, through the Mazes of Despondos, through the streets of Sigil and Gate Towns at the borders of the Outlands". See [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/).

Netheril ran on that road. "All the souls which March through Despondos get fed into the mammon machine and obliviated and it powers their society." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-07-06)</small>

## The Banefae attack

In antiquity the Banefae came for the Seat. "The Banefae United by Drefanmetra attack the maze and enslaved TJ to get into the Penteract at the heart of Despondos." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> Tylerjynex ended in thrall to [Calix](/people/calix/), who "banished him to some dark abyss"; the abyss was his own maze.

## The Queens and the Grey Sage

The Queens of Netheril, [Astra and Aurora Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/), and the Grey Sage, [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), went into Despondos in turn:

1. As allies the Queens and the sage defeated the Banefae: "They are confronted by the Queens and Grey Sage and defeated." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> The Queens' own account: "We cast them into Despondos where all of them perished." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small>
2. The machines were hidden in Despondos. <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).)</small>
3. [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s collapse shut all three in: "Last loop Brightmantle did the same thing as you, collapsed the city and sent the mammon machine into the desert, it trapped the Queens and Grey Sage in Despondos for centuries." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-03)</small>
4. The Queens turned on the sage: "The sage had lied to us for a century, so we threw him in as well and gave his house to the poor." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small>
5. The Queens became the Ladies of Pain and Death, and the Old Man escaped Despondos. <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).)</small>

## The key

A [Bane blade](/items/the-bane-blades/) opens the way in. The Sword Coast Trading Company's memo on [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) asks: "Where is the vault located, on this plane or elsewhere? If it’s on the Shadowfell is it in the Maze of Despondos? How can they access Despondos without a baneblade, or do they have one?" <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

## Marcus's throne

In Sigil [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/) absolved the party, purged the city of demons, and banished her sister [Marcus](/people/marcus/) to Despondos with the words "Seek your throne in the maze." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines, Part V.)</small> Her Ally Card's black action is **Lord of Despondos**: "Cast Maze without concentration."

## Copperbane

**Copperbane**, family sword of the Tudix, "is not lost, but is hidden in Despondos." Peter Glans, who is Tylerjynex, "left it in a block of stone just outside the Penteract and walked away", to torment Valen and protect it forever. <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small> The rhyme of the sword calls it "a moonshard of copper concealed in a maze". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-01-16)</small>

## The party in the maze

In the [Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) the party followed Valen in:

> you all entered the labrynthian dimension of Despondos through the gate foretold, Valen and co had already gone in ahead of you, the party moved very fast, ignoring enemies and exploration and rushing from coppermark to mark, stepping sideways into the material plane where they hopped narrow grass pillars pitched steeply toward the sky. You fought and subdued an undead magma titan trapped in the maze, and you spared some wounded Drow as you avoided attachment by wandering ghosts, finally the party confronted Valen, Carl’s Jr. and The Waning One at the peak of Sigil, and Anti-Drefan transformed into a gargantuan fae dragon, and the Bane Blades finally awakened

<small>— Dave's recap, sic on "labrynthian" and "attachment". (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-10)</small>

"Not even one of you wandered into the maze," Dave told the table after the first of the two sessions. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-08)</small> In the second the party charmed [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), won over Carl's Jr. and [the Drow](/organizations/carls-jr-and-the-drow/), and left Valen in a puddle of acid. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small>

Before the final invasion of Netheril, Tylerjynex reported from the peak of Sigil that "From inside Despondos and the Shadowfell, he can see the Penteract cracking and bucking at the force of some internal pressure." <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small>

## After the Oblivia

When Cypher obliviated the eldritch machines and Cthulhu, the loss of the Mammon Machine remade the region around its heart: "The sudden obligation of the mammon machine would have been enough to warp the space around it, sending Dis into the Hells and twisting the area around Sigil into a great Torus, the donut shape it has in planescape." <small>(Sic on "obligation". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> See [the City of Dis](/places/dis/) and [Sigil](/places/sigil/).
