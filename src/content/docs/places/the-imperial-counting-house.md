**Imperial treasury office · Plaza of the Martyrs, below the Ivory Lazaret · Department of the Exchequer · Standing**

The Imperial Counting House is the Empire's treasury office in the valley of [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), standing on the Plaza of the Martyrs beside the foot of the Stairs of Her Mercy. It collects the valley's taxes, distributes its resources, and counts and confirms the Emperor's personal take, which is large, because he buys a great deal of luxury goods for himself and for the valley, all of which is his property except [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/). A posting here is a very senior assignment among the Empire's auditors. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## History

The building was a hospital, the **Hospital for the Weary**. The Empire took it over as an office when the Emperor built his Summer Palace in the valley, and the Empress then undertook the immense work of building a new hospital as part of the cathedral on the hill. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The building

It is run by the Department of the Exchequer, the department [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) served in as an auditor. Imperial paladins stand at the single front entrance. Inside, a public registrar's desk stands to the left and stacks of hazardous-materials containers to the right, among rows of clerks. A long back corridor, where death troopers lounge along the south wall, passes a commissary used by civilian and Imperial staff alike and doors marked **Director**, **Treasury** and **Records**, the last with the nameplate of Wardress I. Vitt. Imperial officers visiting the valley are normally quartered here, in officers' quarters kept because the valley is so remote. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## Staff

- **[Aurel Kesk](/people/aurel-kesk/)**, the Director.
- **Corvan Bleek**, Sub-Auditor, who was in Eric's intake at the Exchequer twenty years ago.
- **[Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/)**, wardress and keeper of the records, who trained Eric.
- Clerics of Matt Matheny among the clerical staff, who complain that the probate line has grown so long the doors had to be shut, and that they are being made accomplices to their superiors' corruption. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The embezzlement

By Ilma Vitt's private reconciliation, the Director and his staff are enriching themselves on the property of people who die in the Lazaret, on donations, and on war profiteering laundered through the house. Patients consign their bodies to the Emperor and their belongings to the church to pay for their care. Gunpowder, textiles, building materials, precious metals, artillery and ships are donated to the church and sold out the door at bargain prices. During Korth Ep 15 a man in a swashbuckler's coat paid Bleek a bag of platinum for a load of sealed containers, which went by carriage to the back of the Grand Lodge. Vitt's ledger is half the proof; the Lodge's books are the other half. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## In the Korth arc

In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/), [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) cornered Director Kesk in the main hall and demanded to be taken to the Emperor. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), as the Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), came to work her case here, received Vitt's reconciliation, and took Vitt out of the Director's hands when his men beat her. The Counting House has been openly hostile to Deirdre since.
