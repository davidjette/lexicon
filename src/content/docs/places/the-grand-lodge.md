**Guild hall · the Summer Palace grounds, near the Ivory Lazaret · Gathering place for non-Imperial dignitaries · Destroyed ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

The Grand Lodge was a former guild hall on the grounds of [the Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/), distinctly Masonic in flavour, which served as a gathering place for non-Imperial dignitaries and passers-through: people who need to influence the Imperial seat without directly serving the Emperor. The Ministry of Law ordered its records seized on the suspicion that Lhazaar pirates come and go from it, and that order is the Inevitables' authorised route onto the Summer Palace grounds.

## The assignment

Vice Admiral **Freyd Merkin** of the Ministry of Law has directed a Star Advocate to travel to the Summer Palace, [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) and the surrounding grounds, and to secure **all records of the Grand Lodge** — guests, names, dates and related information — and to arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates found there.

The Empire's stated suspicion is that there are dealings at the Lodge with **Lhazaar pirates**. Merkin's own words on it were plain: *"I want evidence that the pirates are in and out of that place."* The file includes a likeness of Captain [Joseph L. Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), the Dread Pirate, husband of Blair of the Underhive, and a former fling of the advocate now carrying the file.

## What is really going on

The Lhazaar pirates have been running supplies to the Uruk-Hoth Republic for years, which General Stockton Thar raised with the Lord Commander at [POSÉ](/places/pose/) and got told that opportunists sell their loyalty to whoever pays.

The Inevitables' reading is that whatever is actually happening at the Grand Lodge, [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) is behind it.

## The route to the Lazaret

The assignment is the party's authorised route to the Ivory Lazaret and therefore to striking distance of the Empress, and it is the reason [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) argued against throwing the Bantide Moro trial at all: *"We're not here to free one saboteur."*

The deadline is Monday. Failure to return carries a bounty.

## The hunting lodge

The Grand Lodge is an old hunting lodge, older than the Summer Palace, and has no Imperial affiliation. Its members are the very rich. They include **Brillo Myers**, a textile magnate, and **Craig**, the construction magnate who built the great war memorial and cemetery and a voting member of the membership committee. [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/) was a member, and his portrait hangs among the Fieldmasters on the grand stair. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

The members hunt at night and sleep by day, and their talk of hares and crossbows is a cant for business. A servant named **Bimples** looks after the Fieldmaster's Suite, where the Inevitables stayed in [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/). In the upstairs gaming room members play random walk, in which a mechanical egg wanders the table until it knocks down some number of pins. There a man calling himself **Joey Monica** asked the Star Advocate for help with "quite a few millions of barrels of barley" stuck in a customs affair, moving out of "certain war-damaged areas". <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

On the grand stair two men passed a purse between them, and a coin that fell from it was minted in the [Uruk-Hoth Republic](/organizations/the-uruk-hoth/). Sealed hazardous-materials containers paid for at [the Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/) go by carriage to the Lodge and around the back of the house. The Lodge will not take pilgrims at any price. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

On the Saturday night of [Korth Ep 17](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/) the hunters gathered under a full moon. The newest Fieldmaster's portrait on the grand stair has no name under it and shows a lean woman in her forties. Rotunda Goose held court in the ballroom with Lord Aldric and Lady Sabine Varenne and said that everyone there was planning for what comes next. She told the Star Advocate that this was the night the Lodge brings people through the library. Most of the King's Forest lies north of the river and is closed to hunting, and the Lodge holds a grandfathered licence under which only its members may hunt there. In the Hunters' Salon, reached through the boardroom, the quartermaster Cousin Felcher took a handful of platinum bars from a demon in hunting dress. The hunter Fora Dollar, a member sponsored by a lord whose life he saved, hired himself to the Advocate there to hunt a great red bird. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) the Lodge would not admit Deirdre Moro to the library, because it is for members only, but Bimples offered its lending service. The book that came back carried Joseph Roberts's seal. A sliding bookcase at the end of the old library opens on a guard room, where visitors are blindfolded with black silk, and beyond it on the room where the Fieldmaster, the woman in the newest unnamed portrait on the stair, kept the Lodge's books. Roberts told Gemma that the Lodge carried grandfathered safe harbours: so many of the Emperor's cousins had been members that it was kept exempt from certain reporting and licensing requirements, and its boats travelled the river without stopping for every taxing authority. That morning he sold the Lodge to a buyer he would not name. His men carried gold, jewels and papers out through the back, the Fieldmaster left with the ledgers, and by noon things were being taken down. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

**Places of Interest:**

- **The guest records:** names, dates and arrivals; the object of the entire assignment.
- **The lodge hall:** the old guild room where dignitaries who do not serve the Emperor meet the people who do.
- **The grand stair:** hung with portraits of the Fieldmasters, Cob Cornwell's among them.
- **The Fieldmaster's Suite:** a guest suite looked after by the servant Bimples.
- **The gaming room:** upstairs, where members play random walk.
- **The ballroom:** a long hall carpeted in deep red, with a bar along one wall and a fireplace.
- **The Hunters' Salon:** east of the ballroom through the boardroom, where the serious hunters drink.
- **The library:** where people are brought through on Saturday nights.

## The end of the Lodge

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) the valley of the Summer Palace was destroyed by a weapon dropped from a ship of the Hundreds. Joseph Roberts had sold the Lodge before then. He would not name the buyer, and said only that he knew how to time these things and that the buyer had wanted it for a teardown.

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) Roberts introduced the Lodge's last Fieldmaster by name on his hideaway island: **Vanessa Redwing**, Master Huntress, a half-elf, and his wife. He told Gemma that the portal in the Lodge's library was the way goods and fleeing Imperials had left the valley of the Lazaret, where teleporting was impossible, and that it was one of a network across the continent. He gave Gemma the ledger of the Grand Lodge, with all his dealings in it, including the orc coin from sales of salvage and war material. He left with Redwing and most of the money from the sale, and again would not say who had bought the Lodge. <small>(Korth Ep 23)</small>
