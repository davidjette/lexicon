**Guild hall · the Summer Palace grounds, near the Ivory Lazaret · Gathering place for non-Imperial dignitaries · Standing**

The Grand Lodge is a former guild hall on the grounds of [the Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/), distinctly Masonic in flavour, now serving as a gathering place for non-Imperial dignitaries and passers-through: people who need to influence the Imperial seat without directly serving the Emperor. The Ministry of Law has ordered its records seized on the suspicion that Lhazaar pirates come and go from it, and that order is the Inevitables' authorised route onto the Summer Palace grounds.

## The assignment

Vice Admiral **Freyd Merkin** of the Ministry of Law has directed a Star Advocate to travel to the Summer Palace, [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) and the surrounding grounds, and to secure **all records of the Grand Lodge** — guests, names, dates and related information — and to arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates found there.

The Empire's stated suspicion is that there are dealings at the Lodge with **Lhazaar pirates**. Merkin's own words on it were plain: *"I want evidence that the pirates are in and out of that place."* The file includes a likeness of Captain [Joseph L. Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), the Dread Pirate, husband of Blair of the Underhive, and a former fling of the advocate now carrying the file.

## What is really going on

The Lhazaar pirates have been running supplies to the Uruk-Hoth Republic for years, which General Stockton Thar raised with the Lord Commander at [POSÉ](/places/pose/) and got told that opportunists sell their loyalty to whoever pays.

The Inevitables' reading is that whatever is actually happening at the Grand Lodge, [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) is behind it.

## The route to the Lazaret

The assignment is the party's authorised route to the Ivory Lazaret and therefore to striking distance of the Empress, and it is the reason [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) argued against throwing the Bantide Moro trial at all: *"We're not here to free one saboteur."*

The deadline is Monday. Failure to return carries a bounty.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The guest records:** names, dates and arrivals; the object of the entire assignment.
- **The lodge hall:** the old guild room where dignitaries who do not serve the Emperor meet the people who do.
