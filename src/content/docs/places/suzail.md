**City · Capital of the Kingdom of Cormyr · Seat of the Purple Dragons · Later the imperial capital of the Cormyrean Empire**

*Also known as:* the City of Song

**Suzail** is the capital of the Kingdom of Cormyr, a port on the Sea of Stars known as the City of Song, and the headquarters of [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/). In the Age of the Infanta the [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) kept a campus there, where they held Infanta and ran a magical stock farm on the power of one of them. During the festival of Candlenights the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) turned King Glenn Danzig into a snail in his own castle, the night of [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/), and the crisis that followed brought down a tower on the party. [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) later married the king's daughter [Roseanne](/people/roseanne-danzig/) and was crowned king of Cormyr, and after founding [the Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/) he set his imperial capital in Suzail.

## The city

Cormyr is "the most powerful central power in the loose affiliation of human kingdoms in the center of the continent", and Suzail is the headquarters of the Purple Dragons, "an elite corps of knights loyal to King Danzig and his royal line". Their officer class is the War Wizards, whose war-magic academy, the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/), stands in the neighbouring kingdom of [Punis](/places/punis/), about two days' ride away. <small>(Arc III Part VII; Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-24)</small>

> Suzail is a large city, though not as large as Waterdeep, and it remains an old fashioned place, with older buildings, reserved gardens, and practical public spaces.

<small>— Arc III Part VII, "The City of Song"</small>

Danzig Palace, home of King Glenn Danzig and his family, dominates the centre of the city. The Citadel of the Purple Dragons holds the southeast corner, where the knights garrison. The Elvish Quarter lies in the west, near Manor Street where the richest citizens live. The east is cluttered with slums and battered workshops, and the south is the Port of Suzail, the largest trading centre on the Sea of Stars. <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small>

Suzail has portals. Dave: "portals are linear magical objects, sometimes one way sometimes two or three way, interplanar portals are much more rare. But it’s a mathematical certainty that there at least one from Sigil to Suzail". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-07-20)</small> Nichole, who played [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/), planned to carry the snail king out through the portal at a pet shop in the city that leads to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-25)</small>

## The War Wizards in Suzail

The War Wizards' research summary, found at their campus, records their Infanta programme there. It had been "three years since we found Una right in our own backyard"; they had taken five Infanta, and three were killed. Of the nature Infanta: "Only recently did we find Sylvanus, the nature god, and through our work we have quadrupled the output of our key magical stock farm here in Suzail." They built the Tempus shrine "to test our theory about the proximity of infanta and were proven correct", and the elves of the city knew their own shrines had woken. <small>(Arc III, VIII Handouts)</small>

[Una](/people/una/) was taken from the campus tower by "A group of professional thieves and burglars" who "slipped out without detection", without using magic. The War Wizards tracked them to the Charter House of the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) near the docks, and from there to the platform for "the doomed train ride to Baldur’s Gate". They could not enter the Charter House, and believed "the inside has been transformed into a pocket dimension or similar demi-verse". <small>(Arc III, VIII Handouts)</small> See [The Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/).

## Candlenights

The party of Temple Holdings LLC came to Suzail during Candlenights, "a ten day polytheistic celebration of the winter solstice and the turning of the year", when nearly every building is hung with lights, garlands, wreaths, antlers, flowers, berries and pine. The city was tense. The knights expected war, a fire was racing through a slum in the east, and the Royal Docks were choked with warships called home from all over the Sea of Stars. The Purple Dragons had ransacked and confiscated the Company's holdings in the port. <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small> A fortune teller in the South Market told the party the tale of [the Three Brothers](/lore/the-three-brothers/).

On Candlenights Eve the party stormed Castle Danzig, killed the War Wizards' archmagi and turned the king into a snail, and Lord [Krapsmere](/people/krapsmere/) sent the Purple Dragons against the War Wizard campus. Dave: "you'll have a matter of hours before word gets out that there was a coup". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-16)</small> In the crisis that followed an Infanta self-destructed and "collapsed that whole tower on your friends", and the party got the nature Infanta, [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), out to Cormanthor. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-27)</small> Dave, the next day: "you guys stopped them from destroying the entire city." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-28)</small> The full account is [The Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/).

## The imperial capital

When King Glenn Danzig was turned back from a snail, he offered Roseanne's hand to Zanzibar. After [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) Zanzibar married her; the king fell ill, and Zanzibar was crowned king of Cormyr after the wedding. In the aftermath of the Infanta War he founded the Empire and set his imperial capital in Suzail. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) was the old imperial capital. The emperors of his line, among them [Zanzibar the Penitent](/people/zanzibar-the-penitent/) and [Zanzibar William the Melancholy](/people/zanzibar-william-the-melancholy/), rule from Suzail.

## Places of Interest

- **Castle Danzig** (Danzig Palace) — the royal seat at the centre of the city, and the scene of the coup.
- **The Citadel of the Purple Dragons** — the knights' garrison in the southeast corner, with the War Wizards' intelligence office inside it.
- **The War Wizards campus** — a double-turreted tower over academic buildings, a farm and orchard of rare magical plants, a greenhouse, laboratories and dormitories; Una lived in the top turret. <small>(Arc III Part VIII)</small>
- **The Royal Docks** — the commercial port, choked with military vessels at Candlenights.
- **The North Market** — fine goods, imports and clothes, with shops farther into town.
- **The South Market** — produce and livestock, and weapons, poisons, ruffians and fortune tellers, run by nomads who leave before the gates close.
- **The Port of Suzail** — the largest trading centre on the Sea of Stars, where the Company's Charter House stands.
- **The Temple of Obus** — suddenly reawakened, and besieged by the wounded, ill and dying.
- **The Abbey of Tempus** — a modest abbey on the War Wizards campus, full of divine energy.
- **The Shrine to Zarus** — all but abandoned, its temple crumbling.
- **The Shrine to Elistrae** — frequented by the Elvish Quarter and passing Harpers, and active.
- **The Garden of Sylvanus** — a hidden shrine that sprang to life and grew the streets around it thick with moss and flowers.
- **The Shrine of Astlan** — the Beastlord's shrine, dormant and infested with cats.
- **The Nightgate Inn** — a cheap inn outside the walls, kept by an old halfling named Greg who hates criminals although his inn is full of them.
- **The Bareback Saloon** — a tavern near the South Market secretly owned by [the Zhentarim](/organizations/the-zhentarim/), where Emery Grim holds court.
- **The Sheepskin Tavern** — the liveliest inn in the city and the unofficial headquarters of the Harpers, where Gordon Lightfoot plays.

<small>(Arc III Part VII, unless noted)</small>
