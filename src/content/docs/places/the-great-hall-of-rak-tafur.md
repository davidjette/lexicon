**Throne hall - the seat of the Deeptempura, beneath the Korramont**

*Also known as:* Rak'tafûr

The Great Hall of Rak'tafur is the throne hall of the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/) beneath the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/), the ceremonial heart of the hold and the last piece of it still in dwarven hands. Ten columns carved as giants and drakes hold up its roof, and at the end of it, on a dais, stands the clan's throne. It is now held by the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/), who came from the Mror Holds to consecrate the dead of their cousins and take the hold back, and who camp around the dais rather than on it.

## Getting in

The hall sits behind two obstacles. First the **Iron Door** - smith-marked, riveted, without a keyhole, reading KNOCK AND BE KNOWN. It opens to *shave and a haircut*, written out in Dwarvish on a leather scrap tucked behind a shield statue, and as it swings it says the names of whoever is standing outside it. Then a statue gate: blue tile, two pressure plates and an axe trap that still works, both plates jammed with somebody's abandoned thieves' tools, both iron-bound doors false. The real way through is a floor passage that opens under the wall when the knock is given.

## The camp

Fourteen bedrolls under a shielded truce-lamp. A field-shrine built out of recovered tokens. An old dwarf sitting with a roll, writing names onto it. The [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) came from the Mror Holds to consecrate the dead of their cousins and to take the hold back, and they have been clearing the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) level one room at a time so that the fallen can be given proper rites. They were managing less than a room a day.

The matriarch [Madame Tso Teryaki](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/) holds the hall. Her brother [General Tso](/people/general-tso/) runs the clearing parties. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) the runesmith reads the clan shorthand.

## Hospitality

Rak'tafur has a formal law of hospitality and it can be begged for in Dwarvish. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) begged it, proving kinship by marriage through Orange Chicken, Yellow Meat, and the court of King Six-Foot-Six-Inch-Long, and received exactly one cold night of it, with the terms stated plainly: *"Do not overstay your welcome, grave robbers."*

The party had walked in wearing Imperial faces, wearing the matriarch's dead husband's belt, and carrying five belts out of the clan's own sepulchers. The one thing that bought them any goodwill at all was a returned mourning-braid, which gave the dead outrider on the stair his name: [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/). Standing was only fully repaired much later, when Dario carried [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/)'s remains through the middle of this camp and laid her on the shrine altar.

## Present day

The bell has rung once more and been broken. Every kin in the hall was caught in the toll. The party told the clan plainly that [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) intends to claim the mountain and urged them to leave, and were refused twice:

> Why would we leave? We've just now recovered it. - Madame Tso Teryaki

> We cannot leave. This is the purpose of our clan. We have given so much. - General Tso

Only Bofric walked out. The hall remains a camp under a mountain Valtrex has claimed.

## Places of Interest

- **The throne and dais** - unoccupied. The Teryaki camp around it, not on it.
- **The council room** - behind the throne through an unlocked iron door: a table of black Khyber cut from a single slab, and a stone door carved as a dwarf's face.
- **The foundry door** - past a young sentry and a warning, out into sulphur and heat and the still-glowing lava flows, with the [Bladeworks](/places/the-bladeworks/) across a gap where a bridge used to be.
- **The shrine** - cleansed of [Velen Marsk](/people/overseer-velen-marsk/) and his ogres, and now the resting place of an unknown soldier and of the princess and heir.
- **The living quarters corridor** - sealed doors, undead behind them, opened one at a time.
