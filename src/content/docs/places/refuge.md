**Settlement · Site of the Mammon Machine's portal · Held in a repeating hour**

Refuge is a settlement and the site of [the Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/)'s portal, and one of the three places where time is broken. The **Infanta of Time** incarnated near the machine in order to protect it from [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) and **encased it in a one-hour bubble that repeated over and over**, and it went on repeating until the party arrived to free her and try to stop him. They did not stop him at Refuge; they followed him through the portal and further back, to [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/).

## The repeating hour

The party dreams of it afterwards: "Couch — **The clock tower of Refuge** — striking noon and collapsing." <small>(Arc V, [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/), Part III, "Dreaming." That is the hour, seen from inside it.)</small>

<small>"Long before Refuge, this gang of witches, thieves, and outlaws has managed to…" — the line in Arc V Parts I–II is cut off, and dates the town as older than the events here.</small>

## One of the three broken moments

At the end of time, the dying goddess June shows Chronocypher "three places where time is broken, where the Traveler shattered the Forgetting":

> The moment you just left\
> \
> The moment you left Refuge\
> \
> And then. Here Cthulhu is first awakened from his long slumber. Here the Axis of Time passes through the heart of the world. Here we are born on Earth again. And so shall you be.

<small>(Arc V, Starfall Part VI.)</small>
