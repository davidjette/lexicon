**Settlement · Site of the Mammon Machine's portal · Held in a repeating hour**

Refuge is a settlement and the site of [the Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/)'s portal, and one of the three places where time is broken. The [Infanta of Time](/people/the-infanta-of-time/), **June**, incarnated near the machine in order to protect it from [St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) and **encased it in a one-hour bubble that repeated over and over**, and it went on repeating until the party arrived to free her and try to stop him. They did not stop him at Refuge; they followed him through the portal and further back, to [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/). When the party returned to the "present", Chronocypher took June's place at Refuge.

## The repeating hour

The party dreams of it afterwards: "Couch — **The clock tower of Refuge** — striking noon and collapsing." <small>(Arc V, [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/), Part III, "Dreaming.")</small>

<small>"Long before Refuge, this gang of witches, thieves, and outlaws has managed to…" — the line in Arc V Parts I–II is cut off, and dates the town as older than the events here.</small>

## The party at Refuge

Refuge is a desert mining town with a diamond mine. After the party went near the mine, the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) began "working in new ways". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-13)</small> Among the party's choices for the start of Arc V, Dave listed "Go to the diamond mine at Refuge and look for St Cloud/Mammon Machine". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small> Asking the table for names for the town's people before the session of 2020-01-26, he wrote, "Remember we’re in a desert mining town". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-26)</small> There the party found St. Cloud "inside a time bubble" and followed him into the deep past. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-17)</small>

## One of the three broken moments

At the end of time, the dying goddess June shows Chronocypher "three places where time is broken, where the Traveler shattered the Forgetting":

> The moment you just left\
> \
> The moment you left Refuge\
> \
> And then. Here Cthulhu is first awakened from his long slumber. Here the Axis of Time passes through the heart of the world. Here we are born on Earth again. And so shall you be.

<small>(Arc V, Starfall Part VI.)</small>

## Chronocypher's return

In the Time of Troubles the party's Anachron at Motherstone, loaded with a Perfect Dark crystal, was "capable of going to Refuge In The pResent, the Dark Future, or the deep past". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

When the party returned to the "present", [Chronocypher](/people/stonecypher/) replaced June at Refuge and lived through the arc there again as the child June. At the last moment, instead of dying, she was blown free of Refuge and rescued by the party, who appeared to have just left for the past and came back at level 20 or higher from the back half of the campaign.
