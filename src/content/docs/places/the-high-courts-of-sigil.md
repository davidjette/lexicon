**Courts of law · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Seat of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/)**

**The High Courts of Sigil** are the courts at the heart of legal and bureaucratic power in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). The public assume that the judgments of the High Courts are handed down by the various Justiciars, who hear the majority of cases and conflicts. At the heart of the Courts sits [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), a gargantuan construct kept by [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), whose judgment is final for nearly every faction in the city; only factions in [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) and the most anarchic groups, such as the Wreckers, deny that authority.

## The Justiciars

The Justiciars hear the majority of the cases and conflicts brought before the High Courts, and it is to them that the public attribute the Courts' judgments. The Lexicon's existence is not common knowledge. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Lexicon

The Lexicon of the High Courts is a gargantuan construct, an eldritch machine made from an extraterrestrial crystal of hyperdense selenium, of "ORIGINS UNKNOWN". <small>(DM notes)</small> The Sororia are its keepers, and describe it as "a tremendously powerful artifact, a magical oracle whose provenance and origin is of constant debate". <small>(DM notes)</small>

> It is assumed by the public that judgments of the High Courts are meeted out by the various Justiciars who hear the majority of cases and conflicts. But at the heart of legal and bureaucratic power of the city sits the Lexicon, whose impartial and far-seeing judgment is high regarded by nearly all the city's factions and presumably the Lady herself.
>
> <small>— DM notes (sic)</small>

The Lady is [the Lady of Pain](/people/the-lady-of-pain/), ruler of Sigil.

## Those who deny its authority

> Only factions in the Company or the most anarchic groups like the Wreckers deny the final authority of a high court judgment rendered by the Lexicon.
>
> <small>— DM notes</small>

The Company, [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), works on understanding and attempting to duplicate the Lexicon. <small>(DM notes)</small> In the years in which Dead Man's Hand is set, the Company, the Church of Cypher and many others are trying, behind the scenes, to create more Lexicons, to copy the original, or to avoid domination by it.

## Places of Interest

**The Lexicon:** the gargantuan selenium-crystal construct at the heart of the Courts, kept by the Sororia.\
**The Justiciars' courts:** where the majority of the city's cases and conflicts are heard.
