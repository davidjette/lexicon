**Tower · [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/), [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Iron Veil headquarters · Now the seat of Sharn's provisional government**

*Also known as:* The Veil Tower

The Veil Building is a towering monolith in the Upper City: a brutalist arcane-tech structure and the physical fact of the Empire's authority in Sharn. It houses government offices, **Arcane Eye Security**, and a high-security prison for political dissidents and rebel sympathisers. Airbus Passes are sold at the Veil Building. Iron Veil headquarters until the rising, it is now the seat of Sharn's provisional government.

## The floors

**The Garden Floor** is a mid-tower open-air terrace reserved exclusively for Veil personnel and government employees: a manicured garden and quiet seating, a secluded courtyard hidden inside the tower's own structure, and one of Sharn's four permanent **ArcEye** cameras — small, fixed, non-rotating, looking along the terrace walkway with a distant view of the skyline and a partial view of [the Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/). It is the most dangerous camera in the city to reach. The level is not public, it is watched by security checkpoints, ID-locked lifts and internal surveillance wards, and any unauthorised presence triggers an immediate Iron Veil response. It sits directly below the penthouse.

**The ArcEye operators** — low-level government wizards trained in Arcane Eye protocols, who cast, maintain and remotely operate the city's drifting eyes — are stationed inside the building.

**The penthouse** at the summit was [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s: a fortress in the clouds where she and her family resided when in Sharn, with a balcony overlooking the city, a bedroom for her son, and a fireplace mantel on which the **LeBeefe Eye** sat in plain view. From that perch she watched the city and decided who lived, who died and who disappeared.

## The night it changed hands

[Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) led two of the Inevitables here through the city lockdown, believing Esther would retreat to the penthouse with their son. They fought Iron Veil soldiers at the entrance door. On the balcony, a pre-recorded message and a stolen memory went out across every screen in Sharn; Esther revealed her Honour Guards as undead members of the Council of 13 and killed Mayor [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/). She voided the Iron Veil's pact with her last breath, hurled her airship into the statue of [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) above the Skyport, and fell from her own balcony to land at the base of the building.

> "Kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri."\
> — the last order given from the penthouse balcony

## Present day

Nest and Fulcrum operatives secured the Veil Building the same night and took its senior officials into custody. The penthouse is now the seat of the new administration and the residence of [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), Chancellor of Sharn.
