**District · [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Working city · Six neighbourhoods and an aqueduct · Standing**

The Lower City is the working district of Sharn beneath the city's upper towers, built from older infrastructure, stacked neighborhoods, and a network of streets that have been rebuilt and repurposed many times over. It's crowded, noisy, and practical — a place where people work, live, and move through tight spaces lit by lanterns, neon signs, and the glow of machinery. Six neighbourhoods make it up, split north from south by the Grand Aqueduct. After the riot at POSÉ the Iron Veil occupied the Cogs and drove the Cogborn underground; most of them left Sharn through the Underhive for Gorgonhorn, and the district no longer has an occupying army in it.

Several distinct neighborhoods make up the Lower City, each shaped by the industries, transit systems, and communities that once defined them. Old rail stations have become marketplaces, factory zones have turned into dense housing, and former commercial hubs now serve as gathering points for food, trade, and nightlife. [The Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) cuts across the district, dividing the northern neighborhoods from the southern ones while providing water and access routes throughout the area.

## The neighborhoods

**[Lantern District](/places/lantern-district/) — northwest.** Paper lanterns and neon over narrow streets, late-night restaurants and cramped bars, an Air-Pad above the rooftops, and [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/) carved into the old sub-levels.

**[City Center](/places/city-center/) — north.** [The Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/), the residential towers facing it, [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/), and the main Lower City gates opening onto the world beyond Sharn.

**[Sub-line District](/places/sub-line-district/) — northeast.** Built atop the ruins of Lightning Rail Sub-Transit Station 1; the old concourses are now Station One Market, with Kross Roads Corner Store, [Hopper's Magic Emporium](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/) and The Arcade around it, and faded Orien sigils show through the graffiti.

**[The Cogs](/places/the-cogs/) — southwest.** The industrial slum, home of the Cogborn, factories and shanties welded to the sides of factories.

**[Gear Street Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/) — south.** The busiest commercial artery in the Lower City.

**[Lowline Ward](/places/lowline-ward/) — southeast.** The highest residential population in the district, stacked into high-rise towers above the markets.

**Transport.** Airbuses shuttle between towers and levels, Zephyr Pods run the streets and bridges at ground level, and Skyports on reinforced platforms serve long-range airships in the lower districts as well as the upper. Fares, rules and the lost Sub-Transit are on the [Sharn](/places/sharn/) page.

## What is under it

Below the streets run the sewers, the runoff passages off the aqueduct, and the abandoned sub-transit tunnels. Gangs hold stretches of it — the **Drain Snakes** fortified the northern sewer network and blocked all safe movement until they were cleared out. Further down, past a flooded chamber full of crocodiles and a reinforced door, is [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/).

## The occupation and the exodus

After the riot at [POSÉ](/places/pose/) the Iron Veil used the violence as justification to accelerate its occupation of the Cogs. Checkpoints divided upper from lower. Cogborn families were driven underground into a collapsing tunnel pocket called the Pit and cut off from their own neighborhood.

The way out was not up. It was down: through the Underhive, along [the Spine](/places/the-spine/), and out under the mountain to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/). The majority of the Cogborn left Sharn that way. Iron Veil presence in the Lower City later fell back to pre-Esther levels: patrols present, but predictable and spread thin.

## Present day

The Council of Sharn's new Department of Infrastructure is staffed in part by Underhive drones, and Warforged engineers have been offered to restore the Cogs. The Underhive's asking price at the founding council was autonomy, a permanent seat, and legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below. The Lower City no longer has an occupying army in it, and is still the part of Sharn most likely to be hungry.
