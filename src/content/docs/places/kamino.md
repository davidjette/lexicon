**Planet · Cloning corporation · *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* · Active**

**Kamino** is a highly restricted planet, ninety-nine percent water, whose whole surface is run as one corporation devoted to cloning. [Astral elves](/species/astral-elves/) built it as a colony thousands of years ago and abandoned it during their great war with the [beholders](/species/beholders/); industrious gnomes have lived there since and built the cloning operation back up. In *Light of Xaryxis* it was the scene of [the Kamino hostage crisis](/history/the-kamino-hostage-crisis/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## The corporation

Kamino runs on bureaucratic efficiency. Every landing craft is processed through a central hub, where identities are checked, weapons taken and each visitor assigned a guide. Visitors are issued a sending stone, with the warning that many of the older towers are enchanted against magic and some spells will not pass through their walls. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

The manager of its cloning facility, the big boss of the Crystal Tower, is the former love of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-31)</small>

Kamino Corp held forty-seven astral elves on charges of terrorist threats against the corporation. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The Crystal Tower**, the corporation's tower, where the sign out front reads: No droids, automatons or robots allowed. Its basement vault held 640 million harlocks in bearer bonds and priceless astral-elf antiques, figurines and weapons behind a labyrinthine lock. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>
