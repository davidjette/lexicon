**Airship port · Sharn, upper and lower districts · Operational**

*Also known as:* the Skyports

A Skyport is one of Sharn's primary hubs for long-range airship travel, perched on reinforced platforms throughout both the upper and lower districts. The Skyport zones are among the few areas in the city where private flight is legally permitted, and they provide safe approach corridors for incoming and outgoing vessels. Each Skyport maintains extensive docking facilities, cargo platforms, and long-term mooring spaces for private airships, allowing captains to secure their vessels for days or even months at a time. Outside these designated zones, all aerial travel remains strictly restricted under city law.

## The flight prohibition

To ensure safety within the city's dense vertical districts, all private flight, whether by spell or vehicle, is strictly prohibited within restricted airspace, with exceptions granted only to military forces and a select class of authorized elite. Outside the designated flight corridors surrounding the Skyports, unauthorized aerial travel is subject to fines, impoundment, and potential arrest. The Skyports are therefore the only lawful way in or out of Sharn by air for anyone who is neither military nor among the authorized elite.

## Places of Interest

- **The Upper City Skyport** — the city's primary Skyport, a central hub for private and military airship travel. Its air-traffic lanes sit in frame on the most-watched [ArcEye](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/) feed in the city, which citizens use to track airship movement — and to check whether the Black Veil is in port.
- **Docking and cargo platforms** — extensive, and rated for long-term mooring of private vessels.
- **The approach corridors** — the only legal aerial lanes in Sharn.
- **The Lantern District Air-Pad** — perched above the Lower City rooftops, where personal airships and Airbuses descend through the fog.
