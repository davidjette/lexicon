**Stairway and relief gallery - the descent into the Korramont**

The Grand Stair is the masoned descent from the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/) into the [Glitterhame](/places/the-glitterhame/), the spine of the hold, and the gallery in which the clan's official history is cut in stone. It runs from the stair room behind the Door down into the hold, and it was built to be climbed: every tread, every landing and every carving is oriented for someone coming *up* out of the hold to the door above.

## The descent

It is wet, slick and unlit, with a rill crossing partway down that has taken more than one party member off their feet. The walls are Shavat, and Shavat has acoustics: struck armour rings the stone like a drum and the sound carries the length of the shaft. That woke a stirge colony on the party's way down, and it is the reason nothing goes down this stair quietly.

Off one landing is a pocket cave where a grey-skinned duergar lay weeks dead, curled around a stomach wound, carrying thirty-five gold, a mourning-braid of white hair wrapped in ribboned Dwarvish prayers *for the souls and the crafts of the Deeptempura people*, and a wax rubbing of the crest of [House Moo Goo Gai Pan](/organizations/house-moo-goo-gai-pan/). His name turned out to be [Sesame Tofu](/people/sesame-tofu/), and it took returning the braid to the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) to learn it. Further down, four grells hunt in pairs among the stalactites; the cavern floor beneath them is littered with small eaten skeletons and ruined provisions of the same vintage as the mourner.

## The reliefs

Masterwork carvings flank the head of the stair. They are the formal record of the two bargains that made the hold, and the party read them before they had met anyone who could argue about them.

**Left wall.** A crowd of dwarves led by a king wearing a crown of **seven stars** - the same figure as the Warden Statue at the Door - descending into a mountain of polished black ore. The text names [King Corn of the Deeptempura](/people/king-corn/) and the discovery of **the Glitterhame**, *a cavern full of infernal ore*. At the wall's end the king stands before a huge winged, horned devil that points at an anvil: a bargain for the art of forging Shavat, carved by its own beneficiaries as a triumph.

**Right wall.** Dwarves at anvils bearing swords and axes, and at the end a drake coiled around an anvil with the king before it, arms open - a peace treaty with the black dragon then called **Vuldrach**, and now known as [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/).

A third detail went unread until the party was on the way out with the crown in a bag. In the panel where the seven-fingered devil hands King Corn the Crown of Seven Stars, the king's hands are drawn wearing symbolic golden shackles - and they are drawn that way in that panel and in every panel after it. Further along the same hallway, Corn brings tribute to the dragon.

## Present day

The gate at the top is broken off its pintle. The stair is open from the Mountain Door all the way down, and the way out of the mountain now runs past these walls in both directions. The stirges are dead and the grells are dead. The whispering that started at the stair head - *"Deliver the message"* - came from the bloom below, and the bloom is still there.

<small>A bone map drawn in blood by [Motha](/people/motha/), dying, marks the stair, the pocket cave and the high cavern, and nothing else.</small>
