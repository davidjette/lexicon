**District · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), north · Commercial and civic core · Standing**

City Center is the commercial and civic district at the north side of Sharn's [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), built around [the Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/), a stepped commercial complex whose interior levels are filled with markets and essential services. The main Lower City gates stand here, and the district's plaza is where produce deliveries arrive from the farms. Three operatives under Zilspar Farm 02 produce-delivery cover first entered the Lower City through it, and at the founding of the Council of Sharn the Underhive claimed the Ziggurat as its own.

## Inside the Ziggurat

Inside are the **Scorching Blaze** fast eatery, the **FoundMe Foundry**, the **Graft & Gear** implant clinic, and the underground arena known as [Breaker's Pit](/places/breakers-pit/).

## Across the plaza

Across from the Ziggurat sit several residential towers and a row of street-level shops, including the well-known [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/). It was here, after a produce delivery to the plaza, that three newcomers asked locals about housing and learned of an abandoned apartment near the Kross Roads Corner Store in the neighbouring [Sub-line District](/places/sub-line-district/).

## The gates

The main Lower City gates are located here, opening out toward the world beyond Sharn and toward the **Crownsgate Lightning Rail Terminal** outside the walls.

## Present day

At the founding of the Council of Sharn the Underhive asked for legal ownership of the Ziggurat outright, along with the Vault Below and a permanent council seat for Berenstain. The markets inside it kept trading through the entire rising.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/):** markets, services, an eatery, a foundry, an implant clinic and a fighting pit.
- **[Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/):** pawnbroking above, a hidden tunnel below.
- **The main Lower City gates:** the way out of Sharn by road and rail.
