**Frontier town · Oerth · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/)**

**Hardby** is a frontier town on Oerth, reached by the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) at the end of the caravan leg of Gabriella Hellwood's honeymoon, in the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh, run by JL. From Hardby they sailed home to [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

## The town

Six pink elves hung from a gibbet at the edge of town. Everyone was nervous of elf attack, and someone had painted DEATH TO ZACHARIUS, after the prince of the pink elves, on a wall. One tavern bore the sign of the Sea Princes, and a wanted poster offered a thousand gold, posted by the Sea Princes, for a female elf pirate: Captain Cassandra of the ship *Sea Cow*. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> Nico, before the voyage: "If we meet the boat on hardby then we may have seafaring encounters." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

## The voyage home

On the sail back the party passed sirens calling ships onto jagged rocks and weathered a storm. Then the *Sea Cow* attacked and tried to board them. Captain Cassandra fought with a crocodile beside her, among a crew that included a troll in a pirate's cap with a giant owl for a parrot. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> Cassandra was probably killed. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small> The *Sea Cow*'s hold included caged, trained blood hawks <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>, and a week after the fight Nico was talking about "blood hawks". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-29)</small>
