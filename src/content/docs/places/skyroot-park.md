**Park · [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/), [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Suspended parkland · Standing**

Skyroot Park is an expansive suspended parkland in the Upper City of Sharn, a city block of greenery and leisure high above the metropolis and a rare open, growing thing in a city otherwise made of towers, catwalks and machinery. Beneath it lies the abandoned **lightning rail Station 5**, dismantled nine years ago after catastrophic failures and rebuilt by the survivors into [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/), the clandestine stronghold of Sharn's rebel faction. A hidden puzzle in the park is the entrance to it.

## What is under it

Station 5 was once part of Sharn's famed Lightning Rail Sub-Transit network, dismantled nine years ago after catastrophic failures. The rebels seized upon this forgotten infrastructure, transforming the station and its surrounding tunnels into a secure base of operations. [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) was on duty at Station 5 when the detonations occurred nine years ago; after the city blamed the Sub-Transit Union, she and a small group of survivors retreated into the sealed tunnels beneath Skyroot Park and rebuilt Station 5 into what it is now.

## The puzzle

Hidden somewhere in the park is a puzzle. Solve it and it grants access to the Nest. Having stolen the **Ghost Chip** and a folder of encrypted BioTec data, three newcomers to the city followed instructions from the rebel operative [Izaak](/people/izaak/), solved the puzzle, and met [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) on their first night in Sharn.

## Present day

The park is still a park, and its second function has not been advertised.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The hidden puzzle:** the entrance mechanism to the Nest.
- **[The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/):** the Sharn rebel stronghold in the old Station 5 beneath the park.
