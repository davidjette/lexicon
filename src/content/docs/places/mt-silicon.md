**Fortified stronghold · The Mournland · [The Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) · Seat of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)**

**Mt. Silicon** is the fortified stronghold of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), the warforged nation of [the Mournland](/places/the-mournland/), carved into a mountainside in the dead kingdom of Cyre. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) leads the Hundreds from it. He brought [the Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) there from his airship over the Mournlands, armed them for the Mournlands and demonstrated the [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) from its control chamber. It was in the base under the mountain, after the destruction of the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), that he and [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), emissary of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), offered Eberron's admission to the Sovereignty.

## The stronghold

Mt. Silicon is a fortified warforged hold carved into a mountainside. A giant warforged guards its gate. Behind its doors is a control chamber walled in screens and filled with the Hundreds, whom Locke calls his children.

## The Ruby Nova

In [Korth Ep 3](/sessions/korth-ep-3-the-last-mourning/) Locke Pierce landed the Inevitables at Mt. Silicon after the fight aboard his airship over the Mournlands. In the control chamber he showed them his countermeasure to the ringing of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/), a controlled recreation of [the Mourning](/history/the-mourning/), and detonated it over the northern Mournlands. The chamber's screens showed the battlefield he had made its target. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) stayed at Mt. Silicon to recover while Locke took [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) up to the bridge. Still inside the mountain, in [Korth Ep 4](/sessions/korth-ep-4-road-to-korth/), the trio took Locke's parting gifts and teleported to Korth.

## The Kalashtar

Among the people held at Mt. Silicon were Kalashtar orphans from Breland. After [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/) the Hundreds classified them as an experiment and brought them to the mountain. They were treated humanely while the Hundreds tried to isolate the personality they shared, which calls itself [Hyperion](/lore/hyperion/).

## The Sovereignty

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) the stone Pierce had given Gemma brought the Inevitables into the base under Mt. Silicon. Pierce stood in front of them alone and without guards while the room worked around him. On the big board were red circles over the Lazaret valley and, very close to it, over Korth, and many tracked objects leaving Sharn. Andromeda Starweaver stood behind him. Extraterrestrials of many kinds worked among the Hundreds in a common, well-kept military uniform.

The party was given an hour in the residences the base keeps for humanoids. The lanterns in the corridor then changed colour to lead them past charging stations and a shooting range to the Situation Room, where the big screen was labelled in Gnomish "[Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/)" with a version number and showed the space between Eberron and one of its moons. There Pierce named Eric as his nominee to the Sovereign Council. Eric refused, and told Pierce that he would kill him for [Zilspar](/history/the-destruction-of-zilspar-farm/). Around the room the warforged stood up and their batteries began to charge. Pierce let the three leave by the stone.
