# The Forge of Binding

**Dungeon · Dhakaani depths beneath [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Ritual forge · EXTINGUISHED**

The Forge of Binding is an ancient ritual site buried deep within the Dhakaani ruins beneath Sharn. A relic of the [Dhakaani Empire](/history/the-dhakaani-empire/), it was used by [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and **[E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/)** to perform the Eternal Guardian ritual, binding Varyn's blood and soul to the weapon he forged there. The ritual established Varyn as the Eternal Guardian and created the supernatural connection that would endure through his death, eventually allowing his presence to remain within the sword inherited by [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/).

The Forge was also the site of the **Rite of Recall**, the counter-rite that destroyed Varyn's Echo and severed the binding. Its power went out during the ritual, and the weapon once known as the Crimson Sun was transformed into crystalline White Khyber — Siberys. Now carried by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) as **[Bright Dawn (Crimson Sun Unbound)](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/)**, the sword is the most tangible remnant of what happened within the Forge.

## The Relic

The Forge's purpose is described in the Eternal Guardian writings: it can bind **oaths, spirits, weapons and command structures**. Its power reaches beyond ordinary enchantment, allowing a ritual to establish a connection between a person, a weapon and the forces that govern life and death. The full extent of the Forge's capabilities remains unknown, but the rites performed there left consequences that survived for years beyond their creation.

Varyn and E'noch descended through the Dhakaani ruins beneath Sharn to perform the Eternal Guardian ritual. Varyn knowingly and willingly entered the pact, binding his blood and soul to the sword he forged from Black Khyber. E'noch layered forbidden Vol magic into the rite, introducing a form of magic later identified by [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) as capable of calling, binding and unbinding souls. The precise purpose of E'noch's additional work, and the ultimate design behind the ritual, remain unresolved.

The Forge's significance was not understood when the party first encountered evidence of its workings. A stone golem fought in an ancient chamber along the Cogborn's route out of Sharn, along with an Ioun Stone recovered from that encounter, bore runes matching the writings Renn had been studying in her books. Those markings connected the ancient site and its guardians to the same body of knowledge that would eventually lead the party to the Forge.

## The Way Down

The entrance to the Forge lay behind a boarded-up sewer door near the Underhive tunnels off [the Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/). Beyond the boards, the route descended through a series of dangerous chambers and concealed passages into the ancient depths beneath Sharn.

The descent began in a rat-infested entryway, followed by a flooded chamber occupied by infected Cogborn and a young aboleth. Beyond it, an alien-looking corridor crawled with more infected creatures. Deeper inside, a membrane-like door could be opened only by certain hands, leading to a chamber where a mind flayer and its thralls waited. Past these chambers stood a massive puzzle door whose inscription had to be deciphered before the party could proceed.

![Sharn Ep 15 — The Forge of Binding - Dario, Eric, Renn and Gemma at the Forge of Binding door.png](/images/uploads/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding-dario-e-mv1l178u.webp)
*Dario, Eric, Renn and Gemma at the Forge of Binding door*

The route was more than a passage through forgotten ruins. Its barriers, inhabitants and inscriptions guarded a place whose workings had remained hidden beneath the city, despite the lasting consequences of the ritual performed there.

## The Rite of Recall

Inside the Forge, Renn Tal explained how the site had been used and helped the party understand the ritual that bound Varyn to his sword. Eric the Cleric performed the **Rite of Recall**, while Sir Dario Argentino bound elemental cold into his weapon. The rite called forth **Varyn's Echo**, a manifestation of the Eternal Guardian that repeated fragments of his final moments rather than engaging the party with deliberate intent.

![Sharn Ep 15 — The Forge of Binding - Dario, Eric and Gemma at the forge of Binding with the Echo of Vayrn Crona.png](/images/uploads/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding-dario-e-mv1l3oy7.webp)
*Dario, Eric and Gemma at the forge of Binding with the Echo of Vayrn Crona*

Among the Echo's final words were fragments that suggested a far larger design:

> "...we'll bleed the sun, eternal night.
> Our bloodline shall reign...
> As the blood is the power, and the blood flows through me, the power is mine.
> E'noch... Qualanthri... my brother..."

The Echo's reference to E'noch as *my brother* raised further questions about the relationship between the two men, the nature of their pact and the purpose of the bloodline mentioned in the fragment. Whether the word was literal or symbolic remains unknown. The Echo offered no further explanation.

![Sharn Ep 15 — The Forge of Binding - Dario, Eric and Gemma at the forge of Binding with the Echo of Vayrn Crona shatering.png](/images/uploads/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding-dario-e-mv1l2nm6.webp)
*Dario, Eric and Gemma at the forge of Binding with the Echo of Vayrn Crona shatering*

Its form then shattered into red-black light, hanging in the air like blood splatter before fading. At that moment, **the Forge went dark**. The binding that had preserved Varyn's presence within his sword was broken, and the ancient ritual site was extinguished.

The consequences were immediately visible in the weapon. The red drained from the Crimson Sun, and its material crystallized into White Khyber — Siberys. The blade was no longer the Red Khyber weapon associated with the Mourning, and Varyn's Echo could no longer be heard or felt through it. The sword's history continued, but its supernatural connection to the Eternal Guardian had ended.

## The Drill

The party had little time to consider what the Rite of Recall had accomplished. Moments after the Echo shattered, the ceiling gave way as a massive drill broke through from above. It had been driven down from the great factory in [the Cogs](/places/the-cogs/), where Esther had gone with General Stockton Thar and a company of frontline Iron Veil.

The drill crashed into the Forge, and Esther dropped onto it before striding toward the extinguished ritual site. [Blair](/people/blair/) burst out of a hidden tunnel and pulled the party into an escape passage that led back to the Underhive vault. The drill broke through as the party fled, bringing Esther into the chamber just as the Forge's power had been extinguished.

![Sharn Ep 15 — The Forge of Binding - Esther Crona ontop a Drill inside the Forge of Binding holding the unbound Crimson Sun.png](/images/uploads/sharn-ep-15-the-forge-of-binding-esther-mu3earr0.webp)

Esther arrived too late to witness Varyn's Echo being destroyed. She found the ritual site dark and the sword transformed. The presence she had relied upon for years—the voice she believed was Varyn, guiding and training her through the blade—was gone. Whatever she had sought beneath Sharn, the result was not the reunion or confirmation she may have expected.


*Esther Crona arrives at the Forge of Binding atop the drill, holding the unbound Crimson Sun.*

## What Esther Found

At sunrise the following day, Esther spoke with [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/). She told him that she had been searching for something deep beneath the city and had finally found it. Once she did, she said, it was as though a veil had been lifted. She then left for [Crona's Wall](/places/cronas-wall/).

Her words offered little explanation of what she had sought or what she believed she had accomplished. The Forge's power was gone, Varyn's Echo had been destroyed, and the weapon's transformation had severed the connection through which Esther had heard him. Whether the lifting of the veil referred to the loss of that connection, the revelation of something she had been pursuing, or another realization remains uncertain.

The Forge's destruction did not resolve the mysteries surrounding Varyn and E'noch. The meaning of the Echo's final words, the purpose of the Eternal Guardian ritual and the larger design behind the binding remain unanswered.

## Right Now

**The Forge of Binding is extinguished.** The Rite of Recall destroyed Varyn's Echo and severed the supernatural binding that connected him to the sword. The drill's arrival followed moments later, and Esther reached the chamber after the ritual's conclusion. The site no longer functions as it did when Varyn and E'noch performed the Eternal Guardian ritual.

The weapon survived. Once the Crimson Sun, forged from Black Khyber and transformed into Red Khyber through the Mourning, it crystallized into White Khyber — Siberys when the binding was broken. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) now wields it as her pact blade under its new name, [Bright Dawn (Crimson Sun Unbound)](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/).

The Forge's power is gone, but its legacy remains in the sword, in Esther's lost connection to Varyn, and in the unanswered questions surrounding E'noch Qualanthri and the ritual they performed beneath Sharn.
