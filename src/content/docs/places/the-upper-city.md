**District · [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Governmental, corporate and elite quarter · Standing**

*Also known as:* The upper terraces

The Upper City is the governmental, corporate and elite quarter of Sharn, the pinnacle of the city's political, economic and cultural power, defined by its commanding institutions and an immaculate skyline. It holds the Veil Building, the Skyport, BioTec's Sharn facility, Skyroot Park and the GoldSky Neighborhood. Iron Veil checkpoints were installed between the upper and lower halves of the city after the riot at POSÉ, and on the night the Empire fell in Sharn the district took the damage.

## The institutions

At its core rises the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/), seat of military authority and governance. Corporate headquarters, whose influence shapes much of the city's technological and arcane advancement, is also found in the Upper City; [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/)'s Sharn facility is located in the Upper Central District. The district hosts Sharn's primary **Skyport**, a central hub for private and military airship travel, while the expansive [Skyroot Park](/places/skyroot-park/) provides a rare sweep of greenery suspended high above the metropolis.

Prominent social venues such as [The Vault: Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/), a nightclub and gambling house, contribute to the district's reputation for prestige and exclusivity. The quieter [Astra Bar](/places/the-astra-bar/) serves BioTec's staff after their shifts.

## GoldSky and the Bazaar

Dominating the upper terraces, the [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/) houses Sharn's elite officials and dignitaries, and with them the renowned **Sharn Theater** and its adjoining [hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/), the Black Dove-operated [Sanctum of the Last Mercy Hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/), and the compact but invaluable [City Library](/places/the-sharn-library/). The district is further distinguished by **The Bazaar**, a major commercial center and market complex that houses [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s celebrated restaurant, *Eat My Blaze Too*.

## Checkpoints

The division between upper and lower is not only architectural. After the riot at [POSÉ](/places/pose/), Iron Veil checkpoints were installed between the two halves of the city and raids swept the Cogborn neighbourhoods below in search of suspects.

## Present day

On the night the Empire fell in Sharn, the Upper City took the damage: the statue of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) above the Skyport was destroyed by Esther Crona's own airship, the [Black Dove Hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/) was seized by Silver Flame paladins, and the Veil Building was taken by Nest and Fulcrum operatives. Crowds tore Uriel's statues down across the district. The towers still stand.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/):** the Iron Veil's brutalist monolith, now the Chancellor's seat.
- **The Skyport:** the city's primary hub for private and military airship travel, and one of the few places private flight is legal. <small>See the transport section of [Sharn](/places/sharn/).</small>
- **[BioTec](/organizations/biotec/):** the Imperial research contractor's Sharn facility, in the Upper Central District.
- **[Skyroot Park](/places/skyroot-park/):** suspended parkland, and a puzzle that opens onto the Nest.
- **[GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/):** mansions, terraces, and the wealthiest addresses in Sharn.
- **The Bazaar:** the district's market complex and the home of *Eat My Blaze Too*.
- **[The Astra Bar](/places/the-astra-bar/):** a quiet upper-city bar, and the site of the rebellion's recruitment of the Inevitables.
- **[POSÉ](/places/pose/):** Richard Blaze's newest restaurant, and the site of the riot.
- **The Conduit Exchange:** the shop that was holding the EBT-7.
