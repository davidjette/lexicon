**Fortified gate complex - the surface entrance to the Korramont**

The Mountain Door is the dwarves' front door, built when the front door mattered: a masoned gatehouse set into the mountainside at the head of a switchback road, with slit galleries flanking the approach so that anyone knocking is being shot at from two sides. Inside, guard rooms, barracks, storage, a founder's statue, and a rift hall where the floor simply stops and a rope bridge used to carry the corridor across.

## Chronicle

It was Deeptempura work and it was busy work; the old bones crammed into the corners of the guard rooms are years dead, and they predate the orcs by a long way. When the foundry closed the Door was abandoned with everything else.

About five months before the **Inevitables** arrived, a roughly twenty-strong **Uruk-Hoth** recon regiment under War-Chief [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/) moved in and made it an observation post, watching troop movements in and out of Korth and intercepting communications. They pushed down the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/) once, met the things below, and came back - in Braga's own phrase - retching and talking about whispers. Then they blocked the stair with rock and dirt and stayed upstairs.

> You'd be a fool to unblock it. I'm safe in here - unless you intend to unlock this door. - Braga Kul

## The assault, and the long night

The party came up the road in Imperial disguise and took the Door head-on. Archers in the slit galleries; the main door barred; a fireball in the barracks that set every bale of bedding alight and filled the corridor with smoke; an orc woman cutting the bridge ropes as she died. In the camp melee War-Chief Braga Kul stepped over [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and cut his throat, and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) opened the top of his skull with a dagger and revivified him through a diamond that grew tenfold and filled the cavity. Dario came back one eye short, with the diamond still in the socket.

The following night the regiment's scout party came home, and the barred front door opened from the inside - one of them had eaten a charm of lesser polymorph, come through an arrow slit as a mouse, and lifted the bar. That fight burned the rope bridge away entirely, anchor lines and all, and finished the regiment. Every corpse in the complex was burned, faces destroyed first, and dropped into the chasm, so that Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) could never raise any of them.

## Present day

Cleared, emptied, and burned out. The rift can no longer be crossed without magic or fresh rope, so the party's own route out of the mountain ran around through the mourners' quarters instead, with skeletons rattling out of every tucked-in space behind them.

## Places of Interest

- **The slit galleries** - archer nooks flanking the approach. The slits pass nothing larger than a mouse, which turned out to matter.
- **The Warden Statue** - the clan founder, crowned with seven stars, the same figure as the king on the stair reliefs. Table canon of the mountain: a coin offered to the statue buys one hour of immunity to poison. It has been bought, and it has run out mid-fight.
- **The rift hall** - a chasm across the corridor. The rope bridge is gone, burned rather than cut.
- **The barracks** - orc-occupied, fireballed, and later blown up from the inside by a war-caster with a black-powder charge who said *"I'd die for the people"* first.
- **The cells** - where Braga Kul was held, and where he died with a rock in his fist.
- **The trophy door** - hung with a skull that is no natural creature, and resembles the one-eyed tentacled things the party fought outside Leef years ago. It has never been identified.
- **The stair room** - behind a dwarven iron gate with the key snapped off in the lock. Scattered dwarvish dead three to four years old, killed reaching it from the inside, and the relief carvings that begin the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/).
