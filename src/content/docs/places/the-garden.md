**Breeding and cloning facility · Lathander VII, Clusterspace · [The Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) · Active**

*Also known as:* Lathander VII · the Ice Hell of the Lathander system

**The Garden** is a breeding and cloning facility for the [Esper](/species/esper/), built by [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) on the planet Lathander VII in Clusterspace. The planet was once known as the Ice Hell of the Lathander system. The Company built the desolate world into a "Garden", a place of life and growth, and by the time of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* it has grown and trained more than 1,500 Esper to support the Company's efforts. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## History

Lathander VII was previously known as the Ice Hell of the Lathander system. The Company built the planet into a "Garden", in its own words "a place of life and growth". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> When the facility was founded is unknown.

The Garden appears in the Company dossier that [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/) gives the crew at the start of *The Starsong Awakens*, where it is listed as active. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11; The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small> The dossier, written by the Company, describes the facility's work:

> A breeding and cloning facility for Esper, the Garden has grown and trained more than 1,500 Esper to aid and support our Companywide efforts.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier</small>

## Location

The Garden lies in Clusterspace. The Company dossier calls Clusterspace an ideal location for such a facility. Originally of mind flayer design, it is easily navigable from outside the crystal sphere and virtually impossible to escape. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## The Esper

The Esper are a race easily recognised by their shining silver eyes, with innate psychic powers of telepathy, telekinesis and precognition. Their origins are "shrouded in mystery". <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New playable race)</small> See [Esper](/species/esper/).
