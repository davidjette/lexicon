**Tomb · A hidden entrance on a Moonshae isle · Holds the BALANCE card · Heavily guarded**

**The Tomb of St. Magnus** is a heavily guarded tomb, reached by a hidden entrance on an isle of the Moonshae, that holds the **BALANCE** card of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). It is protected by the Order of St. Magnus, the Harmonium Seraphim, by [the Lusmundii](/organizations/the-lusmundii/), and by traps and magic. A Company seeker named **Lexi**, who had a map to a secret entrance, hired [Bud Holhertz](/people/bud-holhertz/)'s expedition to raid it; a monster inside killed the crew, Lexi teleported away, and only Bud and Lou Sassle escaped. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Guardians

The tomb is protected by:

- **the Order of St. Magnus**, the Harmonium Seraphim;
- **the Lusmundii**, "Priests of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/)";
- traps and magic.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

[Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/), a cleric of [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) of the Knowledge Domain and of [the Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/), hates the Cult of St. Magnus, the Harmonium. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## What is inside

The BALANCE card is in the tomb. Treasures believed to be hidden there include a helm and armor, and perhaps a [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/). A monster is inside. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Holhertz Expedition

A person mistakable for [Lenore](/people/lenore/), named Lexi, a seeker for the [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), hired Bud Holhertz and his crew to raid the tomb. She had a map to a secret entrance and offered them 5,000 gold each, with miscellaneous treasure and access to the Company portals. The crew were the Salad Triplets, Biggs, Wedge and Cobb, and the monk Lou Sassle. On arrival the Salad brothers were killed by a monster, Lexi teleported away and left the party to its fate, and Lou and Bud escaped. <small>(DM notes)</small>

In Bud's own telling, he "had to watch Biggs burned alive with green fire, Wedge get crushed by some titanic Ogre with Two heads while Cobb drowned in his acid vomit," and getting home cost "thousands and months," and the lives of "quite a few PEACEKEEPERS to get out of the Moonshae." <small>(DM notes)</small>
