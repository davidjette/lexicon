**Dungeon · beneath [Sharn](/places/sharn/) · Xoriat portal site · Cleared, portal incomplete**

The Temple of the Closed Circle is a foul and ancient temple buried deep below the city of Sharn, now accessible only through a secret entrance in a flower shop, hidden behind a wardrobe. It contains a portal to **Xoriat**, the Far Realm, which is incomplete and requires several more 'tablets' to open it wide enough for Xoriat to invade the city. Maintained by priests who were slaves to the mind flayer elder brains who once ruled Sharn and Eberron, the temple was rediscovered and resurrected by the Cult of the Closed Circle, who brought a Ulitharid through the portal. The Unforeseen reached it at the same time as the Chief Imperial Architect and the rebel group Fulcrum, killed them all and destroyed the mind flayers; the tablets were not recovered.

## The Cult

Maintained by priests who were slaves to the mind flayer elder brains who once ruled Sharn and Eberron, it was discovered and resurrected by the **Cult of the Closed Circle**, who helped bring a Ulitharid to Eberron.

The Ulitharid, known only as **"The Brain,"** sought to consume enough brains to establish a colony in Sharn and eventually rule the city. The Oblex and Star Spawn Manglers in the Temple were also brought through the portal, in order to terrorize and control the cultists.

## The Allip and the Architect

Imperial Assembly member and Chief Imperial Architect Albert Spear became aware of the portal via an Allips — a spectre of a person burdened with a great secret, who is compelled to show it to mortals before passing to the other side. The Allips inspired him to propose an alternative energy source for Sharn's transportation network, in the hopes that further war with the Rebellion and potential apocalypse could be prevented. In desperation, he attempted to defect to Zhilargo and shared the existence of the portal and more with the Rebellion.

## The Unforeseen

[John C. LaBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) was likewise tempted to find the Temple. He and [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) found the Temple at the same time as Spear and the Rebellion group known as **"Fulcrum"**, killed them all, and destroyed the mind flayers who had occupied the Temple and built the cult around them.

The Ulitharid killed Dr. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and consumed her brain. She was shortly resurrected by **Fema Nolan**.

<small>The 'tablets' were not immediately recovered after the battle that killed the mind flayers. Other factions may or may not know about these tablets and recover them before the Unforeseen can return. They may also be in the party's possession and would be a great find for the Emperor.</small>

## Afterward

Two of the temple's threads outlived it. The woman who resurrected Uriel in that chamber is now known as **Zero-One**, and she has since admitted that she was Fema Nolan, and that she is the reason for the glowing diamond embedded at the base of Uriel's skull — the one the ArcEye caught on the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born.

And the mind flayers were never entirely gone from beneath Sharn. One was still waiting years later in the Dhakaani depths on the approach to [the Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), with a chamber of infected Cogborn thralls around it.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The flower shop:** the only surviving entrance, behind a wardrobe.
- **The portal chamber:** the incomplete gate to Xoriat.
- **The priests' halls:** maintained by priests who were slaves to the mind flayer elder brains.
