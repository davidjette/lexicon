**Moon of Toril · A living fae world in the deep past · Struck by the Egg at the Starfall · Selune in the Starfall era**

*Also known as:* the moon · the dead moon · Selune

**The Moon** is the moon of Toril. In the deep past it was a living world of forest and natural moonstone, home to proto-eladrin hunters, the fae and the [Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/), and the party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) reached it in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/) by way of the Plane of Air and a vertical protofeywild. [Brownsleeves](/people/brownsleeves/) from another loop ruled a small part of it as the Short King. [Karametra](/people/karametra/) had the dragons of [Dragon Mountain](/places/dragon-mountain/) build a platinum temple and cast Move Planet to push the Moon into the path of [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and the party defended the temple while she cast it. Before the impact the party saved the moon people, and evacuees from the Moon were sheltered in a time temple beneath the sea. The Egg struck the Moon at the Starfall; [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) and [the Bane blades](/items/the-bane-blades/) are shards of it. Afterwards it was a dead moon. By the era of [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) it is Selune, where [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) stands.

## Before the Starfall

The Moon forms from crystalized starlight, in orbit with Ydryssl, and the fae evolve on Ydryssl and on the Moon. <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/))</small> The session record of Arc V describes it:

> Primitive proto-eladrin live hunter gatherer lives on the Moon, which is a lush deciduous forest environment with frigid poles, covered in natural moonstone and inherently magical (and destructive of the sanity of non-elf sentient mortals when too close).

<small>(Arc V, Starfall Parts I–IV. Sic on "live hunter gatherer lives".)</small>

The moon people cannot communicate by words, only by hand symbols, drawings and example, and have no sophisticated culture or centralized leadership. Their examples and possible words come from others: Brownsleeves, St. Cloud, the dragons and the Aboleth. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part I.)</small>

On the Moon the party met **Ewe**, a nude faerie who copies the appearance of everyone she meets and is an expert at fighting dragons. She joined the party and led them into a moongate. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Parts I–IV.)</small> They woke in a glowing pool, and looked up at the dark side of the World Tree:

> Large, leafy trees glisten in the light emitting from the pools of water and the starlight above. The Sun is behind the Earth now, so the Moon is dim. But now the heroes can see the true nature of the Moonstone – moonlight emits from every grain of sand, every pebble, and every smudge of dirt.

<small>(Arc V, Starfall Part IV.)</small>

Everyone but [Drefan](/people/drefan/), and to a lesser extent [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/), felt the wooziness of dreaming, and some began to fall into hysterical madness. In the hunting grounds a Lunar Stag drank at the pool, and the Banefae **Galidan, Tudiks, Viven, Carls and Atlas** were hunting [unicorns](/organizations/lunar-unicorns/). They are pre-language hunters who stand out from the others of their kind by adopting names and specific forms, and by making real objects with fine furs, wools and precious metals. They took the party for dragons in humanoid form. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part IV.)</small> Beyond a waterfall lay the Short Kingdom, a slope of steaming pools where the [Lunar Fae](/organizations/lunar-fae/) sat with their twins and triplets, and an onsen bath house at the end of it.

## The Short King

**Brownsleeves**, the Short King, has ruled a small portion of the Moon since his arrival, and spends his time teaching his subjects the basics of science and good living that he can remember. When he saw the party he wept, because he had seen them all die. His own party had split when they arrived in the past: Roland, Revan, Stump and Zanzibar went "to steal the power to move the moon into the path of the Leviathan and deflect it back into space"; Magnus, Cypher, Yoshi and Stoth went to find the Aboleth city and stop St. Cloud; Drefan and Brownsleeves came to warn the moon people to relocate. When Drefan saw what would be destroyed he refused, and left to stop the others. "None of them ever returned." Brownsleeves stayed. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Parts II–IV.)</small> Dave summed up what the party knew: "Brownsleeves' party split up to go to Rlyeh and Dragon Mtn". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-11)</small>

The Short King is Brownsleeves from another loop: "Technically this isn’t Brownsleeves it’s Brownsleevesmetra or some other loop." In his reality he survived the *Evening Star*, and "Drefan and Brownsleeves see the paradise of the moon and change their minds". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small> That Drefan is **Drefanmetra**, [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/), who vowed to stop the Moon being moved, "saving the home of the elves and dooming Toril on its own", failed, and went forward in time to become the Waning One. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-oral-histories-cast.md)</small>

## Reaching the Moon

Before the arc the table argued about going. Roland's player wrote "Fuck it. We’re going to the moon." in November 2019. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small> When the party reached the deep past Dave listed their "main locations" as "The Moon by way of the plane of air and a vertical protofeywild, fucking Dragon Mountain, and an underwater interstellar mindslaver city", and noted that they had not brought "the spacesuits I gave you for a potential trip to the moon". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-17)</small> When Kara's player suggested the terrestrial options, JL answered: "What? Let’s fuckin die on the moon." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-24)</small>

In a session of April 2020 a single spell chained steam damage through twelve elementals and a stag. Dave: "the damage math of this particular effect might have actually blown a crater in the moon". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-04-25)</small>

From a moon pool not far from the Traveler's house, Ewe led the party aboard the Arielle, and Lex teleported the ship on, three hops, to a coral archipelago in an immense ocean "with the moon shining above". <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part V.)</small> See [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/) and [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/).

## Moving the Moon

The man in the shack was the Traveler, a [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) of an earlier loop, and his wife Kay had left him. Dave, answering for his corpse: "“She went to Tiamat to move the moon”". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-04)</small> If she failed, "Ydrissyl won’t just die, it will be obliterated and everything will be gone, not just the moon, leaving a chance to defeat Cthulhu in the future". <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part IV.)</small> Dave told the party that "Others have gone to Dragon Mtn to somehow move the moon in its way and crack the egg before it reaches the earth." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-03)</small> Tiamat's chromatic dragons said the "Abolethi courtesan" had come "to convince her gloriousness to destroy the Moon". <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part II.)</small>

The Banefae had a plan of their own, to "unite the bane blades into the Fatherstone and use it to trap the power of the Netherese and destroy all mortal souls in a rite to undo time and restore the Moon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

On arriving in Antiquity [Kara](/people/kara/) cast a Wish that the Moon had been moved (see [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/)). Dave's quest board for the deep past read "Moon, Rescue the elves" and "Dragon Mountain, help Karametra and bring Tiamat into the team, stop the dragon war and lead them into space". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> In the final arc the party came back to the deep past a second time to fulfil the Wish. Karametra seduced Bahamut and had the dragons build a platinum temple as the spell component for **Move Planet**, and while she cast it the party fought a tarrasque, some dragons and then Tiamat herself, and succeeded. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-ages-rulings-a1-a30.md)</small> The party went on to the Moon a second time, where the Short King, now a warlock of the Waning One, joined them. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small> Dave: "If you had gone to the moon first he would have demanded you not to move the moon and he would have stowed away and sabotaged your ship to prevent it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

## The moon people saved

Dave put the choice to the table before the session of 12 November 2022: "Will you guys want to save the Banefae and the other fae on the Moon before it’s impact with the Egg or are you skipping that side quest to go straight to the Obliviator, or do you want to split up and try both at the same time?" He added, "I think you have a week till Starfall". <small>(Sic on "it’s". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-06)</small>

> I suppose in this cosmology we are in a time before the gods of this world were born, before magic and reality was split open by the Starfall and blood of Zoth distributed into the mortal races.

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-09)</small>

In the session of 3 December 2022 [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/) left a simulacrum, Schmagnus, "casting Temple of the Gods everyday and to expand a permanent time vault at the bottom of the ocean", "Protecting all the evacuees from the moon and some perfect moonstones". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-12-03)</small> Dave announced the next session with "Yes tomm we save the moon people". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-01-13)</small> Kara's player captioned a photo of it "Battling the space cock". Afterwards JL asked "What happened to the bunnies Dref saved? Can we train them as battle bunnies?", and Kara's player answered "If we drop them in the time temple they should have some downtime to train". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-01-14, 2023-01-15)</small> The Lunar Fae stood with the party at the Egg.

## The Starfall

The Egg, the Evening Star, struck the Moon in the deep past, and in that epoch the party attacked the Egg in orbit of Toril after the impact. The Starfall "gave the moon it's divot and caused the majority of the plane's moonstone to fall from the sky". <small>(Sic. [Moonstone](/lore/moonstone/).)</small> Dave on the Moonshae Isles: "geology tells us the moonshae archipelago was caused by a great impact with lunar material, both volcanic and lunar material makes them up." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-13)</small> The Infanta Timeline has the Moon cut off from Earth, the Feywild and the Shadowfell split off, and the Banefae exiled to become the elves.

The Bane blades are shards of Perfect moonstone carried by the Banefae. The rune page for their Waxing power calls them "moonblades cut from A pristine shard of the primordial moon". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-09)</small> Glassbane took a rune "from Atlas himself on the moon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small> [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) is one of them.

In Antiquity [Zebulon the Meek](/people/zebulon/) had a vision of the party: "I watched the moon explode and form its rings". <small>(Arc VI Part IX.)</small>

## The dead moon

After the Starfall the Moon was a dead moon. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-15)</small>

## Selune

By the Starfall era the Moon is Selune, a moon of Toril. Brownsleeve City is a sunny beachside resort community there, with a moonstone mine and a hangar holding [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01; Kol Hanaka's character sheet)</small> Four sites on Selune are holy to [the children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The hunting grounds:** a glowing pool where the party woke, the Lunar Stag, and the Banefae at their hunt.
- **The Short Kingdom:** Brownsleeves's realm of steaming pools below a waterfall, with a small shack and an onsen bath house in the style of the halflings of Blesstown.
- **Moon pools and moongates:** Ewe led the party through a moongate, and from a moon pool the Arielle was teleported on toward R'lyeh.
- **The Traveler's shack:** in the far forest, where the Traveler lived with a decorative box and a single gun.
- **Brownsleeve City:** a resort on Selune in the Starfall era.
