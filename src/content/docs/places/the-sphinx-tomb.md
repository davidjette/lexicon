**Tomb · *The Starsong Awakens* · Location unknown**

*Also known as:* the sphinx temple

**The Sphinx Tomb** is a tomb of many doors that lead through time, entered in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/) went through its time doors with the robot [Nine](/people/nine/), leaving their ship, the Pussyfoot, outside. Their journeys through the doors reached the Five Zanzibars and the final fight on [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), the ship of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. It stood among [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) of [Necis](/places/necis/), and legend called it the birthplace of the Lexicon. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

The time doors were first used in April 2025, on an expedition on which [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) was also met. The doors involve literal time travel and causality. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-04; 2025-04-05)</small> Any door in the tomb could be entered. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-07)</small>

When the story resumed in May 2025, heading into its endgame, Nico recapped where it stood: Barrias and Seven were in the sphinx temple going through the time doors with Nine, with the Pussyfoot parked outside, and [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) had brought [Caprica](/people/caprica/)'s body back to [the Terrasque](/items/the-terrasque/) and met [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/) and [Weld](/people/umwelt/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-18; 2025-05-23)</small>

Accounts differ on what the last door showed before that session. Dave recalled that the last thing the travelers did was go to the Five Zanzibars; Nico recalled that it was one of the doors showing the final fight on the Zanzibar, after which they came back. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

## The endgame

Sad King Billy called the Sphinx the most unstable of the temples of Necis, the alpha and the omega, a monument to the things mortal men cling to which can never be. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> In the tomb, [Nine](/people/nine/) told [Barrias](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) he was the wisest man he had ever known, asked whether he truly believed his mission foolhardy, birthing a new and better Lexicon to right the wrongs of the past, and reached forward to embrace him with a blade in his hand aimed between his ribs. Nine wanted only to reach the door marked Justice. A faint hum of the starsong came through the door marked Forgiveness. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

### The seventh door

In the final session [Hermes](/people/hermes/) and [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) floated invisibly above the tomb. Hermes called it the temple of the Ungod, a god-no-god barred by Ao's decree to every god, each of whose doors opens onto a different point in the Ungod's evolution; his notes listed those inside as Barrias, [Se7en](/people/se7en/), Nine, [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/), [Paytra](/people/paytra/), and "James Starberries in the body of Caprica". With a snap of his fingers Hermes locked them in. They could not step backwards to escape; the only way out lay through a seventh door, into the Lexicon's inner domain of consciousness, where it had retreated after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). Its key was a broken chain of letters on moonstone coins, NO FU TU RE, scattered through the other doors, and the Lexicon had lost it. The travellers went in two groups, Barrias with Paytra and Se7en with Kol. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

Behind the first door a thin man with a warm smile and a close-cropped haircut greeted them:

> "I'm glad you joined me here. This was an important moment in my evolution. There are 7 doors to my temple, you've seen 6 of them. The only way out now is in the 7th room. You'll need to find the key, but it won't be easy. I hid it so well even I can't remember where I put it."

<small>— The thin man, in the Sphinx Tomb.</small>

The dragon door opened on the primordial age. Se7en and Barrias stood at the base of a great tree, with an airship marked ARIEL nearby and dragons battling across a jungle, and one of the dragons spoke directly to them: the platinum dragon Bahamut, who held a piece of the key that a thin man had given him to keep safe until he knew whom to give it to. They went back through and into another door. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small> Another door opened on the fall of Netheril, a floating city of skyscrapers tumbling from the sky under arcing bolts of magic, where Blackstaff, who knew them for time travellers, held a piece of the key. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> The Cthulhu room made them sick; they could understand nothing and had to leave. They glimpsed the inside of the Egg during that fight, distorted. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

The last door before the seventh opened on the end of the universe, the illithid Grand Design: mind flayer sorcerers communing with an obelisk whose glowing runes were shaped like the characters of gnomish binary, a lexical entity. Nine was there with several of his time-travelling selves, having worked out that this was the Grand Design. The last Nine they killed held the last piece of the key, and the Lexicon appeared to ask whether they had found all the pieces of the puzzle, and what it had cost. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

The seventh room was a white void, the Lexicon's inner domain, where it conceived of the Grand Design. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Doors

- **The Five Zanzibars:** a journey to the time of [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), by Dave's recollection.
- **The Zanzibar's final fight:** one of the doors showed the final fight on the SSJ Zanzibar, by Nico's recollection.

The door names, written in Common backwards, are **Unity**, **Justice**, **Clarity**, **Forgiveness**, **Confidence** and **Resolution**. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

- **Unity:** [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), at Punis.
- **Forgiveness:** the SSJ Zanzibar and the Shrike at the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, where Barrias and Se7en stood beside Caprica, unsure what they were there for, and Nine knew.
- **The dragon door:** the war of Bahamut and Tiamat in the primordial age.
- **Netheril:** the fall of the floating city, in antiquity.
- **The Cthulhu room:** the Infanta crew's fight with Cthulhu inside the Egg.
- **The end of the universe:** the illithid Grand Design.
- **The seventh door:** the Lexicon's inner domain, opened by the NO FU TU RE key.

<small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>
