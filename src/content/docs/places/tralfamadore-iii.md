**Planet · Trafalmadorespace · *Light of Xaryxis* · Unsettled**

*Also known as:* Trafalmadore III

**Tralfamadore III** is an unsettled world with a colonial waystation, in the system called Trafalmadorespace, or T-Space. The SSJ Zanzibar, which had no one aboard able to operate its Major Helm, was confined to T-Space, so [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) ordered its new helm officer [Caprica](/people/caprica/) to report to the waystation on Tralfamadore III to be picked up. When the ship failed to appear, Caprica joined a science team hunting falling stars on the planet's northern continent, and was then summoned to the seaside village of Northspire to meet [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/). *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* opens there.

## Geography

Tralfamadore III is an unsettled world. It has a colonial waystation and landing site, and the village of Northspire. At the time Caprica arrived, reports of falling stars on its northern continent had captivated the planet. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

### Northspire

Northspire is a small seaside village with docks, where a space galleon rests in the water, and a tavern across the village from the docks, the Loose Rooster. The village has no technology and is entirely old-fashioned. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

## History

Caprica, who had graduated first in her class from the Academy six months before, chose the open helm on [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), "currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere", and was ordered to report to Tralfamadore III, the closest she could get to the ship. She spent two months of Jamtime aboard a scientific vessel bound for the planet, studying the Capricorn constellation and everything she could read about the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

When she arrived the Zanzibar was nowhere to be found. Her fellow passengers left at once for the northern continent. After signalling the ship and waiting a week alone, Caprica went north to help the science team collect a sample of the falling stars; the team found no specimens. While hiking back to the colonial landing site, she received a message from the Zanzibar that the captain would soon arrive in a small craft to collect her from the Loose Rooster in Northspire. There was no further message. So Caprica came to Northspire on "a one line message from a potentially drunk Captain Smallberries, who was supposed to retrieve you for a tour of duty a month ago." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

The first episode of *Light of Xaryxis*, a double episode, opened cold on a band of level-1 regulars and then followed Caprica into the village. [Astral elves](/species/astral-elves/) nearly killed the party twice during it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19; 2022-10-22)</small> Dave's regular, a level-1 tiefling paladin, was from Northspire and knew nothing of space travel. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The colonial waystation:** Caprica's reporting post, with a landing site for the science team.
- **The northern continent:** the scene of the falling stars.
- **Northspire:** a seaside village with docks and a space galleon in the water.
- **The Loose Rooster:** the Northspire tavern where Caprica was told to wait for the captain.
