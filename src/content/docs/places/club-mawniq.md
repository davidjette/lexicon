**Night club · Newham, later Leef · Relocated**

**Club Mawniq** was a night club in [Newham](/places/newham/) kept by two vampires who had broken with the Blood of Vol, the brother Morg and his sister Nips. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) worked there before she left the city. In the rising against Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) the club's vampires became allies of Gemma, [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), and after [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/) they reopened at [Leef](/places/leef/) as a 24-hour massage parlour. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The club

Entry to the club was by password. The dancer Diamond, Gemma's friend, worked there, and the bouncer was named Unc. Gemma left on bad terms with the house. Nico, who plays her: "The vampires at Club Mawniq owe me money", and "Gemma skipped town and never got her last paycheck". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

Morg and Nips were friendly and willing to help, but the club had fallen on hard times. They were no longer the only vampires in town. A week before, an airship from Aundair had brought in a young, headstrong vampire named Gary, whose magic was strong and bewitching and who was making a meal of the club's regulars; he had not yet learned that it is more delicious to drain your prey through exploitation, and it was no longer safe for the pair to hunt at night. Gary came to the club's door with music, a whole band of guitar, banjo, fife and voices behind him, trying to charm his way in by impersonating people the listeners had lost. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

After the fight at the club [Luz Fayana](/people/luz-fayana/) came to the trio with dossiers on three disaffected Inquisition soldiers. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> The fight came in Newham before [Leef Ep 14](/sessions/leef-ep-14-the-rebel-allies/), by which the vampires were allies; Nico: "we have vampi allies!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

## At Leef

The vampires were among the refugees from Newham who made new lives at Leef, where they opened a 24-hour massage parlour. When Eric came home from his mission to Khraal, he and his hobgoblin companion Fronque had an appointment there for a couples massage. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Known people:** Morg and Nips, owners · Diamond, a dancer · Unc, the bouncer · Gary, a rival vampire from Aundair.
