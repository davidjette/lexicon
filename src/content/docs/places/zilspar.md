**Farming settlement · Breland, outside Sharn · Fulcrum stronghold · DESTROYED**

*Also known as:* Zispire · Zilspar Farms · Zispire Farms · 

Zilspar was farmland: a belt of Breland <small>("Breeland" in the rebel-factions page)</small> farmsteads outside [Sharn](/places/sharn/) that fed the city, sent its produce in on delivery runs, and organised itself as the **Farm-Table Co-operative**. It was also the stronghold of **Fulcrum**, the oldest rebel network on the continent, and it was where three people from Sharn went whenever they needed a night of sleep nobody was watching. Three years after the Sharn rising a hundred E.D.E.N. troops came across the farms and laid them flat, burning the buildings over the people inside; one survivor was found.

The actor [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) was born here.

## The Co-operative as cover

In Nico's notes for Sharn Ep 2, Gemma's Zilspar agricultural cover became the cover for the whole trio in Sharn. Their badges for the opening of [Posé](/places/pose/) read "Farm-to-Table Fresh" and "Zilspar Co-operative", under the names Hermione Granger, Harry Potter and Ronaldo Weasley. [Izaak](/people/izaak/), who is from Vilspar and had been working with Gemma for months, was the only rebel allowed to be seen in public with them, because all four passed as co-op employees who travel, work and live between Zilspar and Sharn. It was nothing unusual for [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) to make trips to the Zilspar farms for his restaurants. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

After the abduction of Sister Nora the Nest advised moving her out of the city to the Zilspar farms, and the trio carried her to Farm 02 in a bag of holding. Nico's first plan for revealing Richard Blaze as a founder of Fulcrum was also set at the farms, with Fema Nolan, Digma Beeve and Jessica Grimpledink meeting the trio in a private house. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

## Farm 02

**Zilspar Farm 02** was the operational heart. **Donna**, a 59-year-old Silver Flame cleric, ran it as its motherly anchor. Beneath the buildings lay a concealed underground cell — sealed with a Private Sanctum ritual, and used to hold **Sister Nora** of the Black Doves until her trial — and a private Fulcrum room where the memory of [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/)'s birth was pulled out of Grady Marsh's head with an ArcEye Crystallizer Lens.

A hidden teleportation circle connected the farm to [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), Jessica Grimpledink, Digma Beeve and Zero-One. Rescued captives were nursed here. Lorian trained here. Almost every turning point of the Sharn rising was decided in one of its rooms.

## The patrol

The farm's one open battle came early: four Iron Veil soldiers and a Veil Hound descended on it at night, harassing sleeping workers and dragging families out of their homes. They were killed to the last man by three visitors and three farm security. The farmers then burned the bodies, dismantled the skiff, and erased every trace by morning.

## The end

Three years later, a hundred **E.D.E.N.** troops came across the farms and laid them flat. Zero-One was taken alive; Izaak was presumed dead. When the Inevitables flew out days later, they found the buildings burned and the dead unburied — bodies opened by blades, and a family killed while they waited for the bus. The evidence showed that many E.D.E.N. soldiers had died defending the farm before incendiaries were brought in afterward to burn the buildings over people boarded up inside.

Eight E.D.E.N. units were still standing in the tower field. All eight were destroyed. Among the days-old dead, one survivor was found: a farm hand's boy who gave his name as **Noot Gunray**.

No sign was ever found of Zero-One, Izaak, Donna or Grady Marsh.

## Present day

Zilspar's destruction is the reason Sharn is a month from famine, and the reason the Chancellor handed Locke Pierce the keys to the city's airspace in exchange for bread. The Hundreds' own dossier confirms no survivors.

**Places of Interest:**

- **Zilspar Farm 02:** Donna's farm, the guest house, the sealed cell and the Fulcrum room.
- **The teleportation circle:** the hidden link between the farm and the leadership of the rebellion.
- **The tower field:** the antenna field where the last eight E.D.E.N. units were destroyed.
