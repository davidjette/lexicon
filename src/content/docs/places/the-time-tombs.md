**Tomb complex · Valley of the Time Tombs, Necis · Goal of the Shrike pilgrimage · Destroyed with Necis**

*Also known as:* the time tombs · the Valley of the Time Tombs

**The Time Tombs** are tombs in the Valley of the Time Tombs on [Necis](/places/necis/), the planet where [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) originated. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> In [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) made [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) there in 2024, exploring them room by room and seeing a vision of an army of shrikes. Necis is now destroyed, and the tombs stood in what are now its remnants.

## Location

The Valley of the Time Tombs lies on Necis, which also holds Lightfoot City with the sea of grass, the Tesla forest and Chronos Keep. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-12)</small>

## History

### The Shrike pilgrimage

The command crew's pilgrimage set out in February 2024 to see the Shrike. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-03)</small> The season ended as the pilgrims were about to reach it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-17)</small> After a three-episode flashback to the Beholder War, the pilgrimage resumed at the Time Tombs. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

On 2024-04-12 the pilgrims explored the tombs as a dungeon crawl, clearing two or three rooms before resting. They fought snakes and sandworms, and met the poison of the common Necian python. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-12; 2024-04-13)</small>

On 2024-04-19 verse was read aloud in the tombs, closing with the line "I am a translator." The name Zith'ra was given in the same session; what Zith'ra is is unknown. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-19; 2024-04-20)</small> The next day [Martin's Final Cantos](/items/martins-final-cantos/) was posted in full. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-20)</small>

The pilgrims had innocents with them whom they did not want to leave behind, so they did not delve too deep into the palace. They saw the future with an army of shrikes, and found a number of people on the "tree of pain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> Dave later described the tree of pain as strung with undying mortals who showed a glimmer of remembering what had been erased, and said it could be removing those memories from reality to protect reality, harvesting them to discover the truth, or both. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

### Aftermath

Dave has said that Sandy would carry the memory of what happened on Necis, with the Consul and at the Time Tombs, to the Hyperion entity. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>
