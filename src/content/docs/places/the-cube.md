**Deep-space station · Independent state · *Light of Xaryxis*, *The Starsong Awakens***

*Also known as:* Little Sigil · DS-K7 · DS-7

**The Cube** is a deep-space station of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), nicknamed Little Sigil for its many portals to destinations throughout the multiverse and for its many residents from [Sigil](/places/sigil/). Once a colony of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), it later liberated itself under the rule of the ancient crystal dragon Oxyl and became an independent state and a nexus of illicit trade. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> Not to be confused with [Roland's Cube](/items/rolands-cube/).

## Light of Xaryxis

In *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* the station was DS-K7, and residents resented the presence of Astral Command. The crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) came there on orders to find the legal expert [MacFlecknoe](/people/macflecknoe/), who had lived aboard for over two hundred years. A signpost at the dock pointed in hundreds of languages to the market (gangsters), the temple (priests), the fighting pit (gladiators) and the library (psionic sorcerers). Oxyl already kept its hoard aboard, and a gnome named Malorcus Donk stayed a step ahead of every faction on the station by rubbing the dragon's orb for luck. He lived at 432½ West Quasar Street, next to the Orcish deli. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes; Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small> The crew found MacFlecknoe and took the orb. See [the Chingwa's wish](/history/the-chingwas-wish/). <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

By *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, fifteen years later, the station had freed itself from Sovereign control under Oxyl and was known as a nexus for all manner of illicit trade. [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/) knew it well, though they had not been there in a few years. They went there looking for an arcane artillery, a magic item that lets a spelljamming vessel cloak itself. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> Barrias's sheet records 1,000 gold of his Ragnar XII stash going "to Tiberius at the Cube". <small>(character sheet)</small>
