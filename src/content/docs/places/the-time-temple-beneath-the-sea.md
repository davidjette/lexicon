**Temple and permanent time vault · The bottom of the ocean, in the deep past · Kept by Schmagnus · Shelters the Moon's evacuees**

*Also known as:* the time temple · the permanent time vault

The Time Temple Beneath the Sea is a temple and permanent time vault at the bottom of the ocean, made in the deep past during the final arc of the Ages of the Infanta. The party of [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) made a simulacrum of [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), called Schmagnus, and left him beneath the sea to cast Temple of the Gods every day and expand the vault. It protects the evacuees from [the Moon](/places/the-moon/) and a store of [perfect moonstones](/lore/moonstone/). Magnus used Divine Intervention on two days running: once to make Schmagnus "effectively a real person", and once to make the Slow inside the temple permanent.

## The idea

A time temple first came up in the Time of Troubles, as a way home. Kara's player Jenny proposed sending [Percival](/people/percival/) back in time to bury perfect moonstones and wait for them: "We put Percy in a time temple with some perfect moonstones in a box, and a time machine. He waits for 123 years and then comes back". She added, "Dave and I did the math earlier today (but that doesn’t necessarily endorse this plan) and 123 years in a time temple will do the trick." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10)</small>

## The Moon's evacuees

Before the session of 12 November 2022 Dave put a choice to the table: "Will you guys want to save the Banefae and the other fae on the Moon before it’s impact with the Egg or are you skipping that side quest to go straight to the Obliviator, or do you want to split up and try both at the same time?" <small>(Sic on "it’s". Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-06)</small> See [the Lunar Fae](/organizations/lunar-fae/) and [the Banefae](/organizations/the-banefae/).

## Schmagnus

The temple was made at the session of 3 December 2022. Zanzibar's player JL reported it to the chat as it happened:

> We cloned Magnus. Named him Schmagnus. You’re welcome.

<small>— JL. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-12-03</small>

JL went on: "And then we abandoned Schmagnus in a time temple beneath the sea with a time-travel alternate version of Zanzibar called Schmanzibar." Dave corrected the first part:

> Technically a Simulacrum which will spend an eternity casting Temple of the Gods everyday and to expand a permanent time vault at the bottom of the ocean
>
> Protecting all the evacuees from the moon and some perfect moonstones

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-12-03</small>

## The two Divine Interventions

Dave's summary the next morning, for a player who had missed it:

> Main thing I think you missed was @Magnus using Divine Intervention to give the Schmagnus simulacrum effectively a real person (so that he can regain spell slots so he can cast Temple of the Gods everyday for a year) and the next day again to make the Slow in the temple spell permanent. I know you’re used to that not working all the time but this time is worked great.

<small>— Dave. Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-12-04</small>

The same message ends: "Also Kara cast Wish. Big magics". That is the Wish [Kara](/people/kara/) cast on arriving in Antiquity, that the Moon had been moved, which the party fulfilled on their second trip to the deep past. It is a different Wish from the one she cast in the Time of Troubles in 2020 (see [Arc VI](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/)).

Later that day he added how the first request was granted: "It occurred to me after that a divine being wouldn’t want Schmagnus to be some automoton with no soul, so to grant Magnus’s request they would give Schmagnus a soul, so that he can perform the spell as normal". <small>(Sic on "automoton". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-04)</small>

## After

Dave announced the following session with "Yes tomm we save the moon people". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-01-13)</small> After it, JL asked "What happened to the bunnies Dref saved? Can we train them as battle bunnies?", and Dave answered "Yes". Jenny suggested, "If we drop them in the time temple they should have some downtime to train", and JL: "Damn. Now we really have to do it. Schmanzibar and Schmagnus can arm them and teach them the way of the blade." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-01-15)</small> See [Drefan](/people/drefan/).

## The table's theory

On the day the temple was made JL offered an origin for a later people: "The moon men + Schmagnus and Schmanzibar might be the original Atlanteans. Schmagnus and Schmanzibar are their Remus and Romulus." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-12-03)</small>
