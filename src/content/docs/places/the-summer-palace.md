**Imperial retreat · upriver from Korth · Grounds of the Ivory Lazaret · Standing**

The Summer Palace is the Empire's retreat upriver of [Korth](/places/korth/), and the address on the travel papers of anyone permitted to approach [the Holy Empress](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). It is where the court actually sits, because the Empress is there and the Emperor's body is there with her.

## Getting there

A river runs close to Korth, and passenger boats depart from it. The vessel to the Summer Palace sails **twice a day**.

## The grounds

The estate holds [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) and [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), along with the surrounding grounds. A **Star Advocate** of the Ministry of Law carries due-diligence powers and weights-and-measures inspection rights at every port of entry in the Empire — and, with them, standing clearance to the Summer Palace and the Lazaret both.

Seen from the south window of the Grand Lodge, across half a mile of grassland, the palace shows a landing pad with a small, highly advanced craft sitting on it, a lighthouse with its own dock, and the great statue of Kaius I looking west into the gardens. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The Emperor's convalescence

The Emperor's body lies here. Uriel moved him out of Korth, "away from factions who had other aims", and "The hospital at the Summer Palace is a temple to her and the emperors living corpse, where the sick are healed and sent home unawares". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> The Emperor was given out to be in treatment for "a very serious illness needing special care 24/7", and the palace is "a huge medical spa for him, attended by Uriel’s acolytes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Entering it calls for disguises. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-12)</small>

The Empress and her high sisterhood tend the ailing Emperor here and carry his commands. In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/), [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) demanded to deliver his report to the Emperor in person. At the foot of the Stairs of Her Mercy a high sister, a very tall woman in black wearing a red mask at the head of a procession of sisters in red, told him the Emperor was very ill but had agreed to meet him in his garden on the morrow. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## Present day

Vice Admiral **Freyd Merkin** handed exactly that assignment to a woman wearing a dead advocate's face: travel to the Summer Palace, the Ivory Lazaret and the surrounding grounds; secure all records of the Grand Lodge; arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates. The deadline was Monday. It was Friday morning. Failure to return carries a bounty on her head.

The boat sails tonight.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/):** a former guild hall, now a gathering place for non-Imperial dignitaries.
- **The river landing:** the boat from Korth, twice a day.
