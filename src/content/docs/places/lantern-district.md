**District · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), northwest · Nightlife quarter · Standing**

The Lantern District is the nightlife quarter of the northwest Lower City, and it glows long after the rest of the Lower City sleeps. Paper lanterns and neon signs hang over narrow streets lined with late-night restaurants, cramped bars, and small shops. The district's **Air-Pad** sits perched above the rooftops, where personal airships and Airbuses descend through swirling fog. Travelers drift between eateries, the **Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar**, and the **Nightwick Motel** tucked between apartment towers. Beneath the district, hidden behind guarded doors, lies [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/), a notorious club and gaming house carved into the old sub-levels.

## Above and below

Above the street the district is hospitality. The sake bar is where regulars are noticed when they stop coming in, and where a Nest informant overheard a drunken tale: an old man named Grady Marsh bragged about his time working at ArcEye HQ, recalling the night eight years ago when he "didn't legally file a surveillance report." [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/), born to a Cogborn family in the Cogs but raised in the Lantern District, moves easily between both worlds.

Beneath it lies the Vault. A retired ArcEye wizard named **Grady Marsh** stopped appearing at the sake bar; the search for him ran through his apartment, where there was blood on the floor and a matchbook advertising the Vault; and the search ended in cells under the dance floor, with two starved prisoners kept as blood cattle beside him.

## Present day

The lanterns still burn all night. The cells below the Vault were emptied, the Children of Ember cultists who kept them were killed, and the district's other trade goes on largely as before.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The Air-Pad:** a rooftop landing platform for personal airships and Airbuses.
- **Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar:** a small bar whose regulars are the district's early-warning system; Grady Marsh's local.
- **The Nightwick Motel:** cheap rooms between two apartment towers.
- **[The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/):** a club and gaming house in the old sub-levels, with a great deal beneath it.
