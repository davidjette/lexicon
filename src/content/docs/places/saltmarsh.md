**Place · Seaside town · Oerth · Temple of Neptune desecrated**

**Saltmarsh** (OERTH - Saltmarsh) is a seaside town on Oerth, the world of Greyhawk, joined to [Sigil](/places/sigil/) by a gateway that runs from a toilet seat in the Smoldering Corpse, a bar in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), to a fountain in the courtyard of its Temple of Neptune. The temple was desecrated, its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein is dead, and its holy relic [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/) was stolen; the town's authorities suspected the pirate captain [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) and offered a bounty for her death. The party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) came through the gateway from Sigil. BattleBeast and Lenore followed the priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/) to the shore, the party saved Rake Ashley from the water, and at the whirlpool over the hidden temple of Neptune they killed beasts and devils and found the corpse of the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/) on its desecrated altar. They then fought [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) aboard Hellwood's ship.

## The gateway

The Sigil end is a toilet seat in the Smoldering Corpse, under the sign of **Euryale**, the Medusa. The Saltmarsh end is the fountain in the Temple Courtyard, which shows "Pericles beheading Medusa". It is the first entry on Sigil's list of portkeys, "Tattoo / SC Toilet", which leads to Greyhawk, and the party went through it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The desecration of the temple

> Neptune's sept is defiled, and its holy relic "Weathervein" is stolen. Authorities suspect Hellwood.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

The local authorities want Hellwood dead and offer a bounty. Wanted posters of [Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/) and the rest of the Blackfish's crew hang around the temple and the town square. The High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune, **Dolores Weathervein**, is dead. The town's two open questions are who desecrated the Temple of Neptune, and the return of Weathervein and the curing of the desecration. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Corpses rise from the nearby sea. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## What investigation turned up

Investigation, perception and religious lore in the temple turned up the following. <small>(DM notes)</small>

- The missing artifact is tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore.
- The desecration was violent and fast, and there were no witnesses.
- There was no gunpowder, and no evidence that it was pirates.
- A temple lies under the water nearby, and there is evidence the attackers sought it.
- A painting of angels "at sea, sky, and stars waiting to come back down".
- In the library, brine wreaths, for water breathing.

## Papa Diaz and the Drowned

The priest Papa Diaz, Brother "Burnface", "Stopped the zombies every night." [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) followed him to the shore, where he performed a rite to turn back the **Drowned**, undead coming from the desecrated temple under the water. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Xendros and Rake Ashley

Xendros keeps a shop whose wares include a Helm of Telepathy at 900 gold, an 8th-level scroll of *antipathy/sympathy* at 15,000 gold, a Potion of Invisibility at 300 gold, a 5th-level spell scroll of *cone of cold* at 4,000 gold and a spell scroll of *toll the dead* at 100 gold. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Rake Ashley** fell in the water, and the party saved her; she was to ferry them to find the **Blackfish**. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Beneath the whirlpool

The party traveled to the whirlpool over the hidden temple of Neptune, killed beasts and devils, and saw the desecration of the altar with Aqueus's corpse. <small>(DM notes)</small> What followed, on and beneath Hellwood's ship, is [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## The Company and Saltmarsh

The symbol of the Company's Metallurgy, Ores, Oxides and Nano Materials Department is a moon. When Lenore is called in to report on Buttsniv City and Saltmarsh, a Company employee wearing it is to approach her and invite her to a secret meeting. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Places of Interest

**The Temple of Neptune:** Neptune's sept, defiled; its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein dead and its relic Weathervein stolen.\
**The Temple Courtyard:** the fountain of Pericles beheading Medusa, the far end of the gateway from the Smoldering Corpse.\
**The town square:** wanted posters of Keisha Milosh and the Blackfish's crew.\
**Xendros's shop:** helms, potions and spell scrolls.\
**The shore:** where Papa Diaz performs his rite against the Drowned.\
**The whirlpool:** over the hidden temple of Neptune.\
**The tomb of Dolores Weathervein:** on the sea floor, where Aqueus lies pinned by Weathervein.\
**The Blackfish:** Gabriella Hellwood's ship.

**Cards:** Celestial, Star and Temple, each reversed; Ship; FATES. <small>(DM notes)</small>
