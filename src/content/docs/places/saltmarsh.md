**Place · Seaside town · Oerth · Temple of Neptune desecrated**

**Saltmarsh** (OERTH - Saltmarsh) is a seaside town on Oerth, the world of Greyhawk, joined to [Sigil](/places/sigil/) by a gateway that runs from a toilet seat in the Smoldering Corpse, a bar in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), to a fountain in the courtyard of its [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/). A few hundred years before Dead Man's Hand it was the home port of [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/): the council deeded them the temple and granted them a naval commission, and they broke a secret society, fought the town's power brokers and saw off the sahuagin. Their priest [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) later became the temple's High Priestess. The temple was desecrated, its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein is dead, and its holy relic [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/) was stolen; the town's authorities suspected the pirate captain [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) and offered a bounty for her death. The party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) came through the gateway from Sigil. BattleBeast and Lenore followed the priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/) to the shore, the party saved Rake Ashley from the water, and at the whirlpool over the hidden temple of Neptune they killed beasts and devils and found the corpse of the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/) on its desecrated altar. They then fought [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) aboard Hellwood's ship.

## Saltmarsh in Ghosts of Twatmarsh

In Ghosts of Twatmarsh, the table's campaign on *Ghosts of Saltmarsh*, the town lies on the coast of the Azure Sea, in reach of the Sea Princes <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>, with Keoland and Hardby in the players' talk of its politics and voyages. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15; 2022-03-13)</small> Dave on its law: "unarmed assault is punishable by 3 - 30 days and a fine of up to 500 gold or so", and "Brandishing a weapon is 3-10 years and they send you to the county capital for that". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-10)</small> The dwarves keep a temple to Moradin in the town. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13)</small> Xendros, who keeps a shop there, is a woman in the Twatmarsh telling <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-11)</small>; JL spelled the name "xhendross". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small>

### Neptune's Rejects and the Daughters of Neptune

Dave named the party on 2021-09-10: "Yes now our band is tight - a holy woman who’s actually a grave robber, and her paladin and pet who are actually a pirate and a dragonrat" / "Neptune’s Rejects". The name was set at the table a week later: "You named the group Neptune's Rejects." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-10; 2021-09-17)</small> Nico joked in 2022 that they could raid the warehouse and "become Neptune’s Pirate’s". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30)</small>

The council gave the party the deed to the Temple of Neptune. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small> After they captured the *[Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/)* they stormed a council meeting to demand a commission for the naval protection of Saltmarsh Harbor; the council approved it, and "the crowd is mingling and admiring its new tiny navy and its protection by the Daughters of Neptune." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> The Daughters of Neptune is the town's name for the party as its navy. Dave listed what followed: "we sailed into town with gunboats, had an attempt on no our lives, and immediately blew up a building and publicly executed four members of the community" / "Six!", "Members of a secret society which we devastated in a single night". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> The party then demanded 20% of the town's public revenue. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small> After the voyage to Abbey Isle they returned to "a Saltmarsh in the throes of an inquisition". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-22)</small>

### The power brokers

Dave on the town's politics: the sahuagin were "the main book plot", and the real threat was the town's power brokers. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-15)</small> Those the party dealt with include Eda, who was "bringing the old ways back to Saltmarsh" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>; the smuggler Primewater, whose daughter Gabriella Hellwood married; Copperlocks and Firebrand, the "military guy"; Keledek, whose debt the party won; the merchant prince [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/), whom the council might confirm to a seat <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small>; [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>; and two men of the Solmor family.

**[Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/)** wooed Dolores Weathervein and suggested she be appointed a special religious delegate to the council, with a vote in town affairs. He compared her to his mother, Petra, blamed the Sea Princes for her death, and hoped Neptune would support an independent Saltmarsh "dedicated to freedom and trade". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> **Salmor** owned PW's house; Dave: "it's PW's house, owned by Salmoor, formerly Sanbalet's smuggling hideout". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-03)</small>. In January 2022 "Salmor is gone and they want to forfeit his stuff" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08)</small>; that April Dave said "Salmor is here to stop the Weathervein from rising again" and "now the Salmoors own the land around here". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small> The party sneaked back into Saltmarsh and killed Salmor at the end of June 2022. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-30; 2022-07-01)</small> Anders survived the finale: Dave "rolled him up", and "Anders wants to be your little cabin boy"; Nico: "And he has his daddy’s ring." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small> Dave later called him "Dread Pirate Anders". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

In the sahuagin attack the party saved the town's dwarves and the imperial mining operation. Dave: "The people we really saved were the dwarves and imperial mining operation". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01)</small>

### The two Weathervein tombs

Two tombs of the [Weathervein line](/organizations/house-weathervein/) lie at Saltmarsh. The **Weathervein tomb** beneath the Temple of Neptune, on land, is the family tomb of [Count Crestus](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/): "Count Cresto Octopailias buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter Vivica Amphibiana in that tomb, with his own tomb above as the entrance". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Skerrin Wavechaser told Dolores that the gravedigger Cujo believed there was more to the tomb, "that there have been reports of hauntings below the temple." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> The tomb of **Dolores Weathervein** herself lies under the sea, by the hidden temple of Neptune; it is also called "the Weathervein Crypt", and Papa Diaz calls it "the grave of the Lady of Tears". <small>(DM notes)</small> Dolores was Nico's player character in Ghosts of Twatmarsh and the ship's chaplain of Neptune's Rejects <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>, and she died as High Priestess centuries before Dead Man's Hand.

## The gateway

The Sigil end is a toilet seat in the Smoldering Corpse, under the sign of **Euryale**, the Medusa. The Saltmarsh end is the fountain in the Temple Courtyard, which shows "Pericles beheading Medusa". It is the first entry on Sigil's list of portkeys, "Tattoo / SC Toilet", which leads to Greyhawk, and the party went through it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The desecration of the temple

> Neptune's sept is defiled, and its holy relic "Weathervein" is stolen. Authorities suspect Hellwood.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

The local authorities want Hellwood dead and offer a bounty. Wanted posters of [Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/) and the rest of the Blackfish's crew hang around the temple and the town square. The High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune, **Dolores Weathervein**, is dead. The town's two open questions are who desecrated the Temple of Neptune, and the return of Weathervein and the curing of the desecration. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Corpses rise from the nearby sea. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## What investigation turned up

Investigation, perception and religious lore in the temple turned up the following. <small>(DM notes)</small>

- The missing artifact is tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore.
- The desecration was violent and fast, and there were no witnesses.
- There was no gunpowder, and no evidence that it was pirates.
- A temple lies under the water nearby, and there is evidence the attackers sought it.
- A painting of angels "at sea, sky, and stars waiting to come back down".
- In the library, brine wreaths, for water breathing.

## Papa Diaz and the Drowned

The priest Papa Diaz, Brother "Burnface", "Stopped the zombies every night." [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) followed him to the shore, where he performed a rite to turn back the **Drowned**, undead coming from the desecrated temple under the water. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Xendros and Rake Ashley

Xendros keeps a shop whose wares include a Helm of Telepathy at 900 gold, an 8th-level scroll of *antipathy/sympathy* at 15,000 gold, a Potion of Invisibility at 300 gold, a 5th-level spell scroll of *cone of cold* at 4,000 gold and a spell scroll of *toll the dead* at 100 gold. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Rake Ashley** fell in the water, and the party saved her; she was to ferry them to find the **Blackfish**. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Beneath the whirlpool

The party traveled to the whirlpool over the hidden temple of Neptune, killed beasts and devils, and saw the desecration of the altar with Aqueus's corpse. <small>(DM notes)</small> What followed, on and beneath Hellwood's ship, is [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## The Company and Saltmarsh

The symbol of the Company's Metallurgy, Ores, Oxides and Nano Materials Department is a moon. When Lenore is called in to report on Buttsniv City and Saltmarsh, a Company employee wearing it is to approach her and invite her to a secret meeting. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Places of Interest

**The Temple of Neptune:** Neptune's sept, defiled; its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein dead and its relic Weathervein stolen.\
**The Temple Courtyard:** the fountain of Pericles beheading Medusa, the far end of the gateway from the Smoldering Corpse.\
**The town square:** wanted posters of Keisha Milosh and the Blackfish's crew.\
**Xendros's shop:** helms, potions and spell scrolls.\
**The shore:** where Papa Diaz performs his rite against the Drowned.\
**The whirlpool:** over the hidden temple of Neptune.\
**The Weathervein tomb:** beneath the Temple of Neptune, the tomb of Count Crestus, Amphibiana and [Vivica](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/).\
**The dwarven temple:** the dwarves' temple to Moradin.\
**The tomb of Dolores Weathervein:** on the sea floor, where Aqueus lies pinned by Weathervein.\
**The Blackfish:** Gabriella Hellwood's ship.

**Cards:** Celestial, Star and Temple, each reversed; Ship; FATES. <small>(DM notes)</small>
