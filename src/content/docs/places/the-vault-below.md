**Nightclub and gaming house · [Lantern District](/places/lantern-district/), [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) · Children of Ember sanctum · Cleared**

*Also known as:* The Vault Bellow (as the Sharn Ep 16 summary spells it)

The Vault Below is a notorious nightclub and gaming house carved into the old sub-levels beneath the Lantern District, hidden behind guarded doors. On the dance floor it is a pulsing, industrial nightclub with signature specialty drinks, each with its own uncanny knack for sharpening a drinker's skills. [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/), Esther Crona's half-sister, is owner and operator of The Vault, Sharn's infamous nightclub and gambling house; locations in the upper and lower city. This is the lower one, the sister establishment of the Upper City's [Vault Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/). Beneath its dance floor the Inevitables found [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) chambers, prisoners kept as blood cattle, and the documents that opened the Umbra conspiracy; the club's other trade ended with Faith and [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/).

## Below the dance floor

A guarded door takes a whispered password and grants access to the restricted levels beneath the club. Below the dance floor the trail ran through [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) cultists into hidden chambers holding the retired ArcEye wizard [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/), shaken but alive, and two emaciated prisoners kept as **blood cattle** for [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/)'s feeding. One of them was [Lorian](/people/lorian/), who spent months drugged, exploited, and moved between the Zeir penthouse and The Vault Below; the trio found him in a holding cell beneath the lower-city club, one of the few captives still alive.

The documents scattered in those chambers opened the whole conspiracy: correspondence between Malrik and his half-brother [Hallorn](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) about Umbra and its connection to **Project EDEN**; mention of [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) joining "The Velvet Table Society," which meets at the upper-city Vault; and notes on a ritual attempted on Grady Marsh to extract what he had seen.

A concealed trapdoor from the lower chambers opens into the undercity tunnels.

## Present day

Four cultists fell and the prisoners were freed. With Malrik and Faith Zeir dead and the Children of Ember destroyed at the Vault Underground, the Vault Below's other trade ended with them. At the founding of the Council of Sharn the Underhive's delegation asked for legal ownership of the building.
