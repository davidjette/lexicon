**Commercial complex · [City Center](/places/city-center/), [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) · Markets and arena · Standing**

The Ziggurat is a stepped commercial complex at the north side of the Lower City, and one of the most recognisable structures in [Sharn](/places/sharn/). Its interior levels are filled with markets and essential services, and the Ziggurat plaza is where the Zilspar farm delivery to Richard Blaze's restaurant was made. The Lower City's single permanent ArcEye camera is fixed on the building, and the city watched [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s airship come to a stop above it. At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn the Underhive asked for legal ownership of the building outright.

## What is inside

**Scorching Blaze** — a fast eatery. <small>(The Sharn Ep 4 summary places "Richard Blaze restaurant" in the Ziggurat plaza; the city guide names only the Scorching Blaze here. No source identifies the two as the same establishment.)</small>

**The FoundMe Foundry**.

**Graft &amp;amp; Gear** — an implant clinic.

**[Breaker's Pit](/places/breakers-pit/)** — the underground fighting arena beneath the markets.

## The Black Veil over the Ziggurat

The Lower City's single permanent **ArcEye** camera is fixed on this building from a factory roof in [the Cogs](/places/the-cogs/), with [the Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) in the foreground. So when Esther Crona's airship, the **Black Veil**, came to a stop directly above the Ziggurat and she stepped onto the deck beside General Stockton Thar while he began to cast, the whole city watched it happen. Then the crystallizer screens cut to black.

## Present day

At the first meeting of the Council of Sharn, the Underhive's delegation asked for autonomy, a permanent council seat for Berenstain, and legal ownership of the Ziggurat and [the Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/).
