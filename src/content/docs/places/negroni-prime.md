**Outpost planet · [The Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) · *Light of Xaryxis* · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* Negroni · the Planet of Galactic Peace

**Negroni Prime** is an outpost planet of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) where [beholders](/species/beholders/) had a presence, and the center of the first [Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/). Beyond it in the Astral Sea lay a neighborhood of planes ruled by individual Beholder Lords, who banded together to attack the Sovereignty and were largely wiped out. In the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), the boy [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/) and a dozen civilians were stranded on the planet. Years later, in the present of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, a memorial was held for Negroni Prime.

## The Beholder War

Beholders were thought to be reclusive and isolated, their conflicts mostly between individuals, so their banding together against the Sovereignty came as a surprise. The war centered on Negroni Prime. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> A beholder ship kamikazed into Neverwinter, and the whole Astral Command Academy was sent to war. Jim traveled aboard the SSJ Balduran in hypersleep, in training for the mobile infantry, while the ship's flight crew served their first assignment. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

The Demoncore weapons system was first used in the war. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> After the Black Hole Sun, Jim was stranded on Negroni Prime with Barrias, Tillamook and a dozen civilians. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> Only Jim, Barrias and Tillamook carried guns; the rest were civilians. The group also had the artillery robot [1.0](/people/1-0/), built from the parts of a downed ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-15)</small>

[Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/), who served a secret program behind [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/), parted from Jim's group to recover the weapon; by Nico's account they left the group with no transport and no way to communicate, and took 1.0 with them. By Dave's account Katrina could have saved the stranded people, but recovering the Demoncore mattered more. Jim's memories of what happened were later changed with a potion of modify memory. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> How long Jim and Barrias remained on Negroni Prime is unknown. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

Jim believed Tillamook had died at Negroni Prime, but the boy survived and later became the prophet of terrorists following the Beholder great mother; the party brought him to Limbo. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small> Barrias was later left unsure whether a lich had cast the wish that saved Tillamook. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-11)</small>

Negroni Prime had been a farming planet near the Beholder Warlord Enclave, and the battle there was the final showdown of Astral Command's decades-long war with the Beholder Warlords. Magic of unimaginable power was used in it, leaving the once fertile planet all but uninhabitable. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small>

In the nightmare in which [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) later relived it, he stood as a young warrant officer in verdant farmland beside Captain [Warwick Blastimov](/people/warwick-blastimov/), a young Private Smallberries and the boy Private [Tillamook](/people/tillamook/). The beholders had mind-controlled the entire 76th division, and a thousand Astral Command soldiers of every people charged their own lines, flanked by a dozen beholder commanders scorching the fields with eye rays. A beholder in a silver circlet paralyzed Barrias with a ray, and he could only watch as another carried Tillamook away in its eye stalks, crying for help. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small>

A second beholder war was under way in the present of *Light of Xaryxis* when the story of the first was told. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-24)</small> From the ground its attacks looked like the work of beholders, but the Zanzibar's crew found the attackers were cybrids controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## Memorial

A memorial for Negroni Prime was held during *Light of Xaryxis*, after being set aside in an earlier episode. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-18)</small>

Admiral [Timothee Buckets](/people/timothee-buckets/) invited Barrias, Captain Smallberries and [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/) as survivors of the battle to the unveiling of a memorial on the anniversary, planned by the Sovereignty in conjunction with the Beholder Collective. Luigi was not much impressed; Jim was offended that anyone would want a memorial for a slaughter. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small> The board for the session was called Paradise. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-08-18)</small>

## The Planet of Galactic Peace

After the war Negroni Prime was renamed the Planet of Galactic Peace and set up as a neutral zone where refugees from all worlds could find safe haven. No weapons of any kind are allowed on the planet, and no starship with warp capability may enter its orbit. The Beholder Collective and Astral Command spend billions of harlocks a year terraforming it, since most of the land is irradiated and toxic. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small>

Only one small region remains habitable, **Paradise City**, protected by an astral bubble. It is ruled by the Triumvirate, one human, one astral elf and one beholder, for the three main forces that sacrificed so much in the war: Commander Sweat E. Blankets, a toxic creep in an Astral Command uniform; Senator Symone Shortgrass, an older astral elf; and Lord P'stram-ee, a very, very old beholder who can hardly keep his eye open. The three are racist, sexist and corrupt, and spend the terraforming money on their own luxury. The city's people are poor and angry, many of them marked by slight mutations, and resent that their families are left outside the bubble to suffer the radiation. Outside the bubble stands a giant obsidian tablet, twenty feet tall and a mile wide, inscribed with the names of those who died in the battle. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Barrias' Nightmare session)</small>
