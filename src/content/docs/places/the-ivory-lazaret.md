**Hospital and cloister · the mountains upriver of Korth, on the Qarth road · Seat of the Holy Empress · Destroyed ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

*Also known as:* The Ivory Lazarette · the white hospital in the mountains

The Ivory Lazaret was the white hospital in the mountains where the Holy Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) kept the Emperor. It stood with the [Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/) upriver of [Korth](/places/korth/), in defensible mountains northeast of Qarth, and it was the place the Inevitables had been moving toward since [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s last command was to kill the Empress.

## The Emperor

The Emperor is given out as comatose, and Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar's fury at Thronehold was fed by the belief. The coma is a lie: he is held in torpor and sustained on a potion of blood. <small>(Korth Ep 20)</small> Uriel, a cleric of Light from Aerenal and one of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), has borne him fourteen daughters, many sets of twins, all under twelve years old — and the Inevitables' reading of the bell dossiers is that she is waiting for a specific event, possibly the birth of a male heir, before the bells are rung. Fulcrum's cover letter to the Chancellor adds one rumour about the bedside: that [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), killed by Uriel and "stood back up as a music box", sings there, the sweetest voice in the world.

<small>Gemma's lead, from her time undercover in Korth, places the Lazarette in defensible mountains northeast of Qarth.</small>

## The Emperor's convalescence

The Lazaret is "The Emperor's convalescence and seat of the White Death". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> The Emperor was given out to be in treatment for "a very serious illness needing special care 24/7", which he came down with after the High Council "was exposed as traitors and executed and hunted down", and the place is "a huge medical spa for him, attended by Uriel’s acolytes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Uriel moved him out of Korth, "away from factions who had other aims". The hospital "is a temple to her and the emperors living corpse, where the sick are healed and sent home unawares". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Uriel's public persona, after she founded and entered this monastery to heal the Emperor, is **the Penitent Empress Uriel I**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>

The Lazaret takes in patients seeking care, which offers a potential way in: it will treat the wounded. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

## The dove seal

Fulcrum's best intelligence on the place came from a muleteer working the high road out of Qarth: wool-wrapped freight going up the mountain, always at night, always under Sister escort, and the escort papers stamped with a wax seal shaped like a dove — the same seal carried by the young Inquisitor who preaches in Korth. Whatever lives at the top of that road, the dove seal goes home to it.

## Right now

The Ivory Lazaret no longer stands. At last count ten [bells](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) remained to be broken, at least one of them a Master casting hanging in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace, and it is not yet known what became of them when the Original rang.

In [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/) [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) told Gemma that the polyhedron over the cathedral is a new device. Smaller ones went up outside some powerful headquarters before it, and since this one appeared, messages in the valley have gone missing or arrived changed, and people have been arrested and followed. The Lazaret itself is sealed with *private sanctum*. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) the Ivory Lazaret was destroyed. The Empress rang the Original from the belfry of Nos Matre, and the dead of the hill climbed out of the ground. Dario broke the bell with Knell and the tower fell. The Polygon in the forecourt carried Uriel's will over the dead, opened to show a perfect darkness inside, and was struck while the party fought below in the Sept of Bones. Uriel was destroyed there. The Heir of Vol, cut from her, grew until it broke the surface under the building. A ship of the Hundreds dropped a glowing red gem into the storm over the valley. It was a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/), Locke Pierce's weapon. When the fire cleared, the Lazaret, Nos Matre and the buildings of the valley were gone.

## The Stairs of Her Mercy

In [Korth Ep 15](/sessions/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy/) the party was set down in the Plaza of the Martyrs, whose stones are carved with tens of thousands of names. The plaza is a market for everything from holy water and blankets to building stone and furniture, and the [Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/) stands on it. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

The Stairs of Her Mercy, formerly the Sovereign Steps, rise out of the plaza in white arcs. At their foot a queue of petitioners begs sisters in green habits to let them through, past white guards who stand head and shoulders over the crowd. Each landing is an altar once dedicated to a god of the Sovereign Host and rededicated to the Mother, and at the apex altar a colossal statue of Uriel in her Black Dove habit stands with shield and imperial crown over a fountain at her feet. By old tradition the military is not allowed up the stairs, and Imperial power stops at the edge of the hill. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

On Relic Row the twins Bran Flakes and Frosted Flakes sell a place on the list within a day for 3,500 gp: 2,500 to them and their supplier, and 1,000 to be left on the altars on the way up. They also sell water from the Fountain of Mercy at a silver a flask. Frosted places the Lazaret in the western nave of Nos Matre, with the Tomb of Kaius I in front of it, a garden with an orrery on the east side, and stairs south to the Imperial gardens. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

At the top of the stairs is the forecourt of Nos Matre. A floating object hangs in the middle of it, sharp-edged and swirling grey, pink, white and sometimes black, and four sisters stand one to a side staring up at it without moving. Just inside the doors of the nave stands a great fountain of glittering pale blue water. The Tomb of Kaius I in the forecourt carries abjuration magic. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## The eternal flame

An eternal flame burns at the back of the altar. The guide who leads novices through Nos Matre calls it out as "part of the original structure built on this hill more than two millennia ago, demonstrating the deep history of this site". <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

The flame stood on the tip of a dark spired tower that ran down into the hill. Under a paving slab beside it, cut steps and a ladder led down to the Council's crypt and the Sept of Bones, the older structure the guide was speaking of without knowing it.

## The Tomb of Kaius I

The Tomb of Kaius I stands in the forecourt of Nos Matre, before the western nave, and carries abjuration magic. It bears a prominent inscription in two languages: <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

> Nányë i carnë elen i síla lómissë.\n> Vályë tarë saptanyassë ar níta.\n> Uin tassë; uin firnë.
>
> Zu’u los fin sahqo fil tol viin ko vulon.\n> Ni kriist ahst dii qoth ahrk luv.\n> Zu’u ni los til; zu’u drey ni dir.

![The Tomb of Kaius I](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-tomb-of-kaius-i.webp)
*The Tomb of Kaius I.*

The first three lines are Elvish and the second three are Draconic, and they say the same thing: *I am the crimson star that shines in the night. Do not stand at my grave and weep. I am not there; I did not die.*

The tomb was a stone effigy on a draped bier, far more weathered than anything near it, and there was no body in it. Kaius I was the same being as Kaius III, and he never rested there or anywhere else. Under the bier a shaft with an iron ladder went down into the lair of [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/), where it came out over the fountain. When Eric asked by divination how the Emperor could be killed, the answer he was given was this inscription.

## Under the hill

The hospital worked. The Fountain of Mercy healed real wounds in front of the crowd, and the sisters in green were real healers. The same order took the strongest, the most beautiful and the most gifted of the Empire's dead and dying down under the hill and turned them into soldiers loyal to the Empress. The pilgrims saw only the first of these.

A button under a dragon skull in the hospital's morgue opens a wall on stairs to a gate far older than the hospital. Behind it was the lair of [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) and his household, destroyed in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/): a dining hall, cells, a room of coffins standing in soil, a fountain under a shaft that runs up beneath the Tomb of Kaius I, and the pool of blood where the gold dragon Aurorix was bled for the Emperor's potion.

A paved tunnel from the lair runs out against the Sept of Bones, an ancient stone structure buried in the soil, with dull chanting inside it and green light behind iron bars clogged with skulls. It lies almost directly under the Polygon. Aurorix told the party what it is:

> A very, very long time ago, long before most of your kind ever came to this place, a people inhabited this forest. They worshipped an ancient god. It was a god of death, yes, but also a god of renewal, of recycling, of taking from what was lost what could still be gained. They built a sept here before there was a hill. A great sept of bones stood on this peninsula. Over the years it was buried, more and more bodies, buried in ceremonial graves all around this holy site, until people forgot that the temple was even here. They built another one on top of it. People think that one's ancient. But these fools, if they've dug up that place, are meddling in magics which they can never control or understand.

## The dead and the ways down

The millions of dead buried under the hill are the source of Uriel's power. The Polygon in the forecourt of Nos Matre is fed by them and stores their power for her, and it is also her projection and mind stone: while it stands she can speak and be seen anywhere in the valley, and it restores her when she is hurt. It listens as well. Smaller ones were raised outside a few powerful headquarters before it, and since it went up, magical messages sent into or out of the valley (*sending*, sending stones, animal messengers) go missing or arrive changed, and their senders are followed and arrested. When the bell rang in [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/), tens of thousands of the dead began to dig themselves out of the burial ground, and Uriel flew for the bell joined to the Polygon by a line of purple light.

The Sept has more than one way in. Under a paving slab beside the eternal flame, cut steps and a ladder go down to the Council's crypt, the entrance to the Sept. A hidden stair in the grotto behind the Summer Palace goes 200 feet down under the hill to the same crypt, and a tunnel runs on from the crypt to the vampires' lair. There is another way in under the statue of Uriel at the top of the Stairs of Her Mercy, where the statue animates, and the shaft from the Tomb of Kaius I runs down into the lair.

The crypt is a brick vault walled from floor to ceiling with stone coffins stacked on their sides. Twelve black coffins with gold nameplates lie on the floor. They hold the members of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) whom [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) hunted down; there are twelve because [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) survived the purge and his body was never brought here. A thirteenth casket stands open and empty. It was Esther's, and she lay in it for years, until Zero-One was captured and Esther was taken up to the slab. Bones spill from a break in the wall beside a stair that turns once and opens on the Sept, past a tall window packed with skulls and hooded statues holding lamps.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/):** the Imperial residence upriver of Korth, on whose grounds the Lazaret stands.
- **[The Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/):** the former guild hall beside the Lazaret where non-Imperial dignitaries gather, suspected of dealings with Lhazaar pirates.
- **The Qarth road:** the high road up the mountain, worked at night by wool-wrapped freight under Sister escort.
- **The Plaza of the Martyrs:** the market square at the foot of the hill, its stones carved with tens of thousands of names.
- **[The Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/):** on the Plaza of the Martyrs.
- **The Stairs of Her Mercy:** formerly the Sovereign Steps, the altar-landings climbing from the plaza to Nos Matre.
- **Nos Matre:** at the top of the stairs, with the floating object in its forecourt, the pale blue fountain inside its nave, and the Lazaret in its western nave.
- **The Tomb of Kaius I:** in the forecourt of Nos Matre, before the western nave, inscribed in two languages.
- **The eternal flame:** at the back of the altar, part of the original structure on the hill.
