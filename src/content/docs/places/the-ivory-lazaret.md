**Hospital, cathedral and cloister · A river valley upriver of Korth · Seat of the Holy Empress · Destroyed ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

*Also known as:* The Ivory Lazarette · the white hospital in the mountains

The Ivory Lazaret was the hospital of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) in the west wing of Nos Matre, the cathedral on the hill of a river valley upriver of [Korth](/places/korth/), and the name was used for the whole valley. A [sept of bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/) stood on the site before there was a hill, and two thousand years of burials raised the hill over it. A temple of the Sovereign Host was built on top, and under the kings of Galifar the valley became an Imperial resort with a hunting lodge that grew into the [Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/). The Holy Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) moved the Emperor there, took the cathedral for her order, and turned the hill into a hospital for pilgrims above ground and a workshop of undead soldiers below it. The Inevitables came by the night boat from Korth after [Korth Ep 14](/sessions/korth-ep-14-the-listening-post/) and spent eight sessions in the valley. In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) the Empress rang the Original from the belfry, she and her heir were fought under the hill, and a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/) dropped by the Hundreds burned the valley away.

## What the Inevitables knew before they arrived

The Lazaret had been the place the Inevitables were moving toward since [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s last command was to kill the Empress. None of them had been inside it. What they had was the following.

**The Emperor's convalescence.** The Lazaret is "The Emperor's convalescence and seat of the White Death". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> The Emperor was given out to be in treatment for "a very serious illness needing special care 24/7", which he came down with after the High Council "was exposed as traitors and executed and hunted down", and the place is "a huge medical spa for him, attended by Uriel’s acolytes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small> Uriel moved him out of Korth, "away from factions who had other aims". The hospital "is a temple to her and the emperors living corpse, where the sick are healed and sent home unawares". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Uriel's public persona, after she founded and entered this monastery to heal the Emperor, is **the Penitent Empress Uriel I**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>

**The Emperor and the daughters.** The Emperor was given out as comatose, and Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar's fury at Thronehold was fed by the belief. Uriel, a cleric of Light from Aerenal and one of the Black Doves, had borne him fourteen daughters, many sets of twins, all under twelve years old, and the Inevitables' reading of the bell dossiers was that she was waiting for a specific event, possibly the birth of a male heir, before the bells were rung.

**The rumour of Lady Glow.** Fulcrum's cover letter to the Chancellor added one rumour about the bedside: that [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), killed by Uriel and "stood back up as a music box", sang there, the sweetest voice in the world.

**The dove seal.** Fulcrum's best intelligence on the place came from a muleteer working the high road out of Qarth: wool-wrapped freight going up the mountain, always at night, always under Sister escort, and the escort papers stamped with a wax seal shaped like a dove, the same seal carried by the young Inquisitor who preaches in Korth. Whatever lived at the top of that road, the dove seal went home to it.

**Gemma's lead.** Gemma's lead, from her time undercover in Korth, placed the Lazarette in defensible mountains northeast of Qarth.

**A way in.** The Lazaret took in patients seeking care, which offered a potential way in: it would treat the wounded. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-27)</small>

> "When you go up the Qarth road, and you will, if there is anything left of her in that holy place, you give her quiet."\
> — the cover letter of the Fulcrum bell file, signed "I"

## The valley

The Lazaret stood in a broad, fertile valley with a single hill rising from the middle of it. The river ran along the west side, with a stone quay where the night boat from Korth landed its passengers. The buildings stood in a line from north to south: the quay and the town, Nos Matre on the crown of the hill, and behind the hill the Westhouse, the Emperor's Gardens and the Summer Palace. Across the river to the north lay the King's Forest.

![The valley from the river, looking north](/images/maps/77-the-ivory-lazaret-valley-map-looking-north.webp)
*The valley from the river, looking north: the Westhouse, the Emperor's Gardens and Heatherhill below Nos Matre.*

![The valley looking south](/images/maps/78-the-ivory-lazaret-valley-map-looking-south.webp)
*The valley looking south: the Grand Lodge, the Imperial Counting House, the Stairs of Her Mercy and the plaza.*

Nobody could teleport into the valley, and everyone came by boat. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small> By old tradition the military was not allowed up the stairs, and Imperial power stopped at the edge of the hill. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small> Above that line the sisters kept order with the white guard, soldiers in white cloaks who stood head and shoulders over the crowd and did not speak. They were undead.

The sisters' habits marked their work. The green were healers, many of them carrying the holy symbols of the Sovereign Host and older native gods. The black were the novices and the order's officers. The Sisters of the Light wore white, the Sisters of the Bone welcomed the dead, and the Sisters of the Blood, in red, tended the Emperor. <small>(Korth Ep 15, Korth Ep 16)</small>

## The town below the hill

The night boat landed at the quay, where white guards stood along the stone facing the water. A road climbed from it to the Plaza of the Martyrs, whose paving was cut with tens of thousands of names. The plaza was a market for everything from holy water and blankets to building stone and furniture, and on Relic Row the twins Bran Flakes and Frosted Flakes sold a place on the stairs' list within a day for 3,500 gp: 2,500 to them and their supplier, and 1,000 to be left on the altars on the way up. They also sold water from the Fountain of Mercy at a silver a flask. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

![The quay](/images/maps/86-the-ivory-lazaret-the-quay.webp)
*The stone quay where the night boat landed.*

![The Counting House, the Plaza of the Martyrs and the foot of the stairs](/images/maps/87-the-ivory-lazaret-counting-house-plaza-of-the-martyrs-and-the-stairs.webp)
*The Imperial Counting House, the Plaza of the Martyrs and the foot of the Stairs of Her Mercy.*

The [Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/) stood on the plaza. Patients consigned their bodies to the Emperor and their belongings to the church, and the Counting House sold that property, the church's donations and whole shipments of war material out of its door at bargain prices. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small> All of it went to the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), the old hunting lodge on the river, where [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) ran his trade from behind the library and paid in Uruk-Hoth gold. Roberts sold the Lodge and left the valley in [Korth Ep 18](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/). He told Gemma: "There's no one in the Lazaret to save. This isn't Leef. This whole place should go."

North of the hill was the pilgrim camp, sixty or eighty pilgrims of many faiths with no Doves among them. The followers of Matt Matheny kept a small camp at the back of it, where [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) were taken in after the slab room, and where [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/) and the staff who fled the Counting House gathered. The Plaza Band learned [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/) there. <small>(Korth Ep 18, Korth Ep 19)</small>

## The Stairs of Her Mercy

The Stairs of Her Mercy, formerly the Sovereign Steps, rose out of the plaza in white arcs. At their foot a queue of petitioners begged sisters in green habits to let them through, past the white guards. Each landing was an altar once dedicated to a god of the Sovereign Host and rededicated to the Mother. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

![The Stairs of Her Mercy](/images/maps/88-the-ivory-lazaret-the-stairs-of-her-mercy.webp)
*The Stairs of Her Mercy, climbing the hill from the plaza toward Nos Matre.*

At the apex altar a colossal statue of Uriel in her Black Dove habit stood with shield and imperial crown over a fountain at her feet. It carried a plaque, *URIEL I*, with Elvish beneath: *By her mercy are we kept pure.* A sister on the landing said it was a wedding gift from the people of Aerenal to the Empress and the people of Karrnath, placed nearly thirteen years before. The landing was laid over older stone, and the statue stood on its own two stone feet on a patterned platform that was heavily scuffed. <small>(Korth Ep 19)</small> The statue could move, and under it was one of the ways down to the Sept of Bones.

The Empress came down her stairs every couple of months to heal in person, and touched only five or six people. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small>

## Nos Matre

Nos Matre, the Cathedral of the Mother, stood on the crown of the hill: a cruciform building of white stone under domes of green copper, with two wings and one tall tower. Its name, Our Mother, came from the Sovereign who claimed that shape and name. [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) remembered it, ten or twelve years before, as an interfaith temple open to everyone. Under the Doves a mass was sung in its great worship hall in a priestly High Elven that prayed openly for the eternal night. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small> The east wing was the altar and the west wing was the hospital.

![Nos Matre with the Polygon in its forecourt](/images/maps/97-nos-matre-the-cathedral-of-the-mother.webp)
*Nos Matre on the crown of the hill, with the Polygon floating in its forecourt.*

**The Polygon.** [The Polygon](/items/the-polygon/) hung in the middle of the forecourt, sharp-edged and swirling grey, pink, white and sometimes black, and four sisters stood one to a side staring up at it without moving. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small> It was fed by the millions of dead under the hill and stored their power for Uriel. It was also her projection and mind stone: while it stood she could speak and be seen anywhere in the valley, and it restored her when she was hurt. It listened as well. Roberts told Gemma that smaller ones had gone up outside some powerful headquarters before it, and that since this one appeared, messages in the valley had gone missing or arrived changed, and people had been arrested and followed. The Lazaret itself was sealed with *private sanctum*. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small> Uriel said of the Polygon in the Sept: "Ask LeBeefe. I stole it from him." <small>(Korth Ep 22)</small>

**The Threshold of Mercy.** Just inside the doors of the nave stood a great fountain of glittering pale blue water, the Fountain of Mercy. A sister told the crowd that the Mother of Mercy had transformed it, and that it healed any wound and gave a full night's rest. The party watched an old man carried up the hill have his face lowered into the water. He stood up healed, and a sister walked him off into the hospital wing alone. Eric's *detect magic* found a tremendous weight of necromancy in the water. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small> It was the fountain of Gemma's vision with [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/), where it ran with blood. A matching fountain stood in the hall of the Westhouse, and a drink from either gave a long rest. <small>(Korth Ep 19)</small>

![The fountain court of the nave](/images/maps/104-nos-matre-the-nave-fountain.webp)
*The fountain court of the nave of Nos Matre.*

**The belfry.** The Original hung in the tower. It was the mother bell from which every other [bell](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) of the chorus was copied. In [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/) a battle sister gave the standing order: "They will not step foot on Nos Matre. Because according to the plan, the instant that they do, we ring that bell."

### The Tomb of Kaius I

The Tomb of Kaius I stood in the forecourt of Nos Matre, before the western nave, and carried abjuration magic. It bore a prominent inscription in two languages: <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

> Nányë i carnë elen i síla lómissë.\n> Vályë tarë saptanyassë ar níta.\n> Uin tassë; uin firnë.
>
> Zu’u los fin sahqo fil tol viin ko vulon.\n> Ni kriist ahst dii qoth ahrk luv.\n> Zu’u ni los til; zu’u drey ni dir.

![The Tomb of Kaius I](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-tomb-of-kaius-i.webp)
*The Tomb of Kaius I.*

The first three lines are Elvish and the second three are Draconic, and they say the same thing: *I am the crimson star that shines in the night. Do not stand at my grave and weep. I am not there; I did not die.*

The tomb was a stone effigy on a draped bier, far more weathered than anything near it, and there was no body in it. Kaius I was the same being as [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), and he never rested there or anywhere else. A hole about an inch wide was drilled through one of the O's of the inscription, smooth and as old as the stone. <small>(Korth Ep 19)</small> Under the bier a shaft with an iron ladder went down into the lair of [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/), where it came out over the fountain. When Eric asked by divination how the Emperor could be killed, the answer he was given was this inscription. <small>(Korth Ep 18)</small>

### The eternal flame

Behind the altar the terrace ran out to a garden at the edge of the hill, with a brass orrery and an eternal flame on a tall iron stand. The guide who led novices through Nos Matre called the flame out as "part of the original structure built on this hill more than two millennia ago, demonstrating the deep history of this site". <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

The flame stood on the tip of a dark spired tower that ran down into the hill. Under a paving slab beside it, cut steps and a ladder led down to the Council's crypt and the Sept of Bones, the older structure the guide was speaking of without knowing it. From the same terrace the rear steps went down the south side of the hill to the Emperor's Gardens.

## The Lazaret

The west wing of Nos Matre was the hospital the pilgrims were allowed to see. Its trauma ward was a former church turned into a triage hall, with blood in the grout. The old man from the fountain already had a bed there when the novices' tour came through, and his forehead had been opened to bleed his humours. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

![The lazarette in the west transept](/images/maps/109-nos-matre-west-transept-the-lazarette.webp)
*The lazarette in the west transept of Nos Matre, with its rows of beds.*

A stair climbed from the ward to the Director's tower room, where Director [Lus Fayina](/people/luz-fayana/) received novices before the laid-out corpse of a very large knight and told them he had given his life for the Empire and would give his death as well. The room held medical books, fine instruments, and [Umbra](/items/umbra/) in purple-blue ampules packed in Black Dove healing kits, ready to inject into the living to prepare them for death. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

## The hospital under the hill

The hospital worked. The Fountain of Mercy healed real wounds in front of the crowd, and the sisters in green were real healers. The same order took the strongest, the most beautiful and the most gifted of the Empire's dead and dying down under the hill and turned them into soldiers loyal to the Empress. The pilgrims saw only the first of these.

Beneath the tower a stair with rails for stretchers went down eighty feet through three floors cut into the hill. The party walked all three as novices in [Korth Ep 16](/sessions/korth-ep-16-orientation-day/).

![Level One of the hospital under the hill](/images/maps/117-the-hospital-under-the-hill-level-one.webp)
*Level One, which received the novices and the dying.*

**The first floor** had a reception like a college dining hall, where sisters fitted the novices for robes and a sister with a slate asked each the same three questions: where were you born, who was your mother's mother, and have you ever been pregnant. Elves were asked a fourth, in Elvish: on what boat did your family travel from Aerenal? Beyond it was the last ward, a row of private cells for patients whose cases were hopeless but whose bodies were strong enough for *the process of second life*.

**The second floor** was where the dying were turned into soldiers. In a workroom undead sisters fitted bodies with Imperial armour. Lus Fayina told the novices: "These are the sorrowful sisters. You will join their order only if you are among the most elite at the time of your death." On the transformation floor the strongest bodies spent their final moments in cells. [Donna](/people/donna/), the Silver Flame cleric from Zilspar, lay in one of them. Beyond were finished undead soldiers, steeped in Umbra and dressed in the red-and-white armour of the Imperial Guard, under Sister Mary Christmas.

![Level Two of the hospital under the hill](/images/maps/119-the-hospital-under-the-hill-level-two.webp)
*Level Two, where the dying were turned into soldiers.*

**The third floor** held the classroom and the slab room, a surgical amphitheatre with an observation gallery. There Zero-One, held in irons, was forced to raise Esther Crona, and [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/) made her finish the rite. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small> Esther was resurrected as a living woman by the rite that had raised Uriel, with her heart taken out. She was kept strapped to a table in a room of Sorrowful Sisters, a glowing red ruby wired in where her heart should be, and the heart stood in a jar in the same room. <small>(Korth Ep 20)</small> [Eden](/people/eden/) freed her on the last day. <small>(Korth Ep 22)</small>

![Level Three of the hospital under the hill](/images/maps/121-the-hospital-under-the-hill-level-three.webp)
*Level Three, with the classroom and the slab room.*

**The concourse.** From the bottom floor an underground concourse ran south under the hill to the Westhouse. It was a station and market run by sisters and civilians, and sisters raised the bloated corpse of a giant there in public. <small>(Korth Ep 16)</small>

![The concourse under the hill](/images/maps/126-the-concourse-under-the-hill.webp)
*The concourse that ran south under the hill from the hospital to the Westhouse.*

### The vampires' lair

Beyond Esther's room was the morgue, with a crematorium and a dragon skull. A button in the floor there woke three vampire spawn in the coffins and opened a wall on stairs to a gate far older than the hospital. Behind it was the lair of E'noch Qualanthri, Uriel's grandfather, and his household: a dining hall, cells of starving spawn, a room of coffins standing in soil, a white alabaster fountain under a shaft set with skulls that ran up beneath the Tomb of Kaius I, and the pool of blood where the gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/) was bled for the Emperor's potion. The party destroyed E'noch, Lady Ophelia, Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn and the rest in their coffins and freed Aurorix in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/).

![The lair of E'noch Qualanthri's brood](/images/maps/150-the-vampire-lair-overview.webp)
*The lair below the hospital: halls of dark brick over black water, with the dining hall, the cells and the study.*

### The Sept of Bones

A paved tunnel from the lair ran out against [the Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/), an ancient stone structure buried in the soil, with dull chanting inside it and green light behind iron bars clogged with skulls. It lay almost directly under the Polygon. Aurorix told the party what it was:

> A very, very long time ago, long before most of your kind ever came to this place, a people inhabited this forest. They worshipped an ancient god. It was a god of death, yes, but also a god of renewal, of recycling, of taking from what was lost what could still be gained. They built a sept here before there was a hill. A great sept of bones stood on this peninsula. Over the years it was buried, more and more bodies, buried in ceremonial graves all around this holy site, until people forgot that the temple was even here. They built another one on top of it. People think that one's ancient. But these fools, if they've dug up that place, are meddling in magics which they can never control or understand.

The millions of dead buried around it were the source of Uriel's power. The Sept had more than one way in. Under a paving slab beside the eternal flame, cut steps and a ladder went down to the Council's crypt, the entrance to the Sept. A hidden stair in the grotto behind the Summer Palace went 200 feet down under the hill to the same crypt, and a tunnel ran on from the crypt to the vampires' lair. There was another way in under the statue of Uriel at the top of the Stairs of Her Mercy, and the shaft from the Tomb of Kaius I ran down into the lair. Gemma and Eric used none of them. They went straight down through the earth by *dimension door*. <small>(Korth Ep 22)</small>

The crypt was a vault of funerary urns, with sarcophagi of many ages mortared into its walls. Twelve black coffins held the members of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) whom Esther Crona hunted down; there were twelve because [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) survived the purge and his body was never brought there. A thirteenth casket stood open and empty. It was Esther's, and she lay in it for years, until Zero-One was captured and Esther was taken up to the slab.

![The Council's crypt](/images/maps/156-the-sept-of-bones-the-councils-crypt.webp)
*The Council's crypt, with bones spilling down its stair.*

![The Sept of Bones](/images/maps/158-the-sept-of-bones.webp)
*The Sept of Bones: stairs, altars and dark spires over a floor of piled bones.*

## The Westhouse

The Westhouse was the green-domed house on the bluff south-west of the cathedral, with a small harbour below it. It had been the wing of the palace where the royal family lived, as grand as Versailles, and it was the headquarters and academy of the Black Doves. <small>(Korth Ep 16, Korth Ep 19)</small>

![The Westhouse and the Kaius Triumphal Arch](/images/maps/127-the-westhouse-with-the-kaius-triumphal-arch.webp)
*The Kaius Triumphal Arch and the green-domed Westhouse.*

A fountain in its hall matched the one in the nave, inscribed *None may harm those who come bearing*. Upstairs was a classroom of veiled elven sisters, where the lesson went past the Liturgy of Her Mercy into rites of the Blood of Vol, and the small, sparse office of High Inquisitor Maelis Dorn. The Imperial Archive had been the Emperor's own office and record room before the Doves took the house. It held titles, treaties, births and deaths, catalogues of military hardware, maps, and files on the Doves and the Emperor, and behind the leather cover of a book the party found E'noch's unsent letters to Aerenal on how the Crimson Sun was put into the infant heir of Galifar. <small>(Korth Ep 19)</small>

In [Korth Ep 19](/sessions/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse/) the party, lodged there as General Thar and his druids, killed Maelis Dorn, Lus Fayina, two Sister Mary Christmases and [Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/) in the Westhouse and left it burning.

## The Emperor's Gardens

A walled formal garden lay on the level ground between the Westhouse and the Summer Palace, entered under the Kaius Triumphal Arch, a gate of dark stone with a great statue of Kaius I above it. White knights stood under the arch. The gardens were unnaturally lush for the climate, kept alive by magic. <small>(Korth Ep 19, Korth Ep 21)</small>

![The Emperor's Gardens](/images/maps/132-the-emperors-gardens.webp)
*The walled garden between the Westhouse and the Summer Palace.*

The Emperor was said to have agreed to meet [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) there. The party killed Thar in the King's Forest and kept the appointment wearing his face, and Lus Fayina turned them away: the Emperor was in surgery, and the Empress was with child. <small>(Korth Ep 17, Korth Ep 19)</small>

In a walled corner of the gardens a cherry tree stood under a statue of Lathander the Dawn Lord, fashioned to look like Kaius III. Uriel murdered Pom Pilot, Archbishop of Arawai, under that tree, and he rose as a revenant wight. <small>(Korth Ep 21)</small>

The rod that held the Emperor in torpor spilled that torpor over the palace and its grounds. Saturnia, the young Sister of the Blood who helped the party, said that anyone who set foot there without the anointing fell asleep "like an innocent babe, long enough for one of the guards to come over and cut your throat." The undead were spared, and so was anyone of the Emperor's or the Qualanthri blood, and anyone marked on the brow with the salve of dragon blood. <small>(Korth Ep 19, Korth Ep 21)</small>

## The Summer Palace

[The Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/), Heatherhill, stood at the south end of the line: a hunting lodge of the days of Galifar, turned mostly into a hospice. From the Grand Lodge, across half a mile of grass, it showed a landing pad with a small, highly advanced craft on it, a lighthouse with its own dock, and the statue of Kaius I. <small>(Korth Ep 15)</small> The Emperor lay in the upper bedchamber with a gold-tipped stake of petrified wood through his heart, and the Empress's daughters lived in a wing of their own. In [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/) the party fought through the daughters' hall to the bedchamber and burned the Emperor on his bed, which destroyed him. The palace sat in its own ward against teleportation, inside the one that covered the valley.

## The King's Forest

Most of the King's Forest lay north of the river and was closed to hunting, except to members of the Grand Lodge under a grandfathered licence. Thar camped on a peak above a waterfall there with dark druids of the Eldeen, and the party killed him on it. Eden came to the same peak at dawn with her soldiers and told the woman she took for Thar that there were twenty Eden units in the valley below. <small>(Korth Ep 17)</small>

## The last day

In [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/) a force with cannon landed on the beaches near the pilgrims' camp, the hospital was locked down, and the Empress went into labour. When the Emperor burned in [Korth Ep 21](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/), Uriel left the palace by magic and rang the Original. Tens of thousands of the dead dug themselves out of the burial ground, and she flew for the bell joined to the Polygon by a line of purple light.

In [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/) Dario broke the bell with [Knell](/items/knell/) and the tower fell. No more dead rose after that. The Polygon carried Uriel's will over the dead already risen and over Eden, and opened to show a perfect darkness inside. Dario took the Empress's head in the forecourt, and she sank through the stones to the Sept of Bones, where Gemma and Eric fought her and Eric destroyed her. On the surface Dario defended Matheny's people and Zero-One on the hillside, and [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) cleared the dead around them with acid. The Polygon was struck, and the power of the dead stopped rising out of the Sept.

[The Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/), cut from his mother, grew until he broke the surface under the building. Joseph Roberts brought the airship *Revenge* in over the cathedral roof and took off the party, Zero-One, Ilma Vitt and what was left of Matheny's people. A ship of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) dropped a glowing red gem into the storm over the valley. It was a Ruby Nova, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s weapon.

## Aftermath

When the fire cleared, the Lazaret, Nos Matre and the buildings of the valley were gone, with the Heir in the middle of the fire. At last report nobody aboard the *Revenge* knew what became of Eden or the Plaza Band. At last count ten bells remained to be broken, at least one of them a Master casting hanging in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace, and what became of them when the Original rang was never learned.

In [Korth Ep 23](/sessions/korth-ep-23-the-father-of-the-hundreds/) Locke Pierce told the Inevitables that the song was being played in every city and the bells were being brought down. Wherever the bells had rung the dead rose for a few minutes and had to be put down. The board in the Hundreds' base under [Mt. Silicon](/places/mt-silicon/) showed red circles over the Lazaret valley and over [Korth](/places/korth/), which was destroyed as well. Pierce said that killing Uriel had held back the Heir's growth long enough for the Hundreds "to get a good shot", and that because the bells had started ringing everyone understood why it had to be done. The portal in the library of the Grand Lodge had been the way goods and fleeing Imperials left the valley, where teleporting was impossible.

- **The hospital under the hill:** three floors cut into the hill, where the dying were made into soldiers. <small>[Map](/maps/117-the-hospital-under-the-hill-level-one/)</small>
- **The vampires' lair:** the household of E'noch Qualanthri, behind the hospital morgue. <small>[Map](/maps/150-the-vampire-lair-overview/)</small>
- **[The Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/):** the buried temple under the hill, entered through the Council's crypt. <small>[Map](/maps/158-the-sept-of-bones/)</small>
- **The Westhouse:** the old royal wing on the bluff above its harbour, headquarters of the Black Doves. <small>[Map](/maps/128-the-westhouse-from-the-harbour/)</small>
- **The Emperor's Gardens:** the walled garden behind the Kaius Triumphal Arch, between the Westhouse and the palace. <small>[Map](/maps/132-the-emperors-gardens/)</small>
- **[The Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/):** Heatherhill, the hunting lodge where the Emperor lay staked. <small>[Map](/maps/133-heatherhill-the-summer-palace/)</small>
- **The King's Forest:** across the river to the north, where General Thar died.
- **The Qarth road:** the high road up the mountain, worked at night by wool-wrapped freight under Sister escort.
