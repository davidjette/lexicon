**Hospital · [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/), [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) · Black Dove foundation · SEIZED**

*Also known as:* The Black Dove Hospital · Sanctum of the Last Mercy Hospital

The Sanctum of the Last Mercy is Sharn's Black Dove-operated hospital, standing in the GoldSky Neighborhood among the mansions and shopping terraces, visible on the horizon from the roof of [the Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/). The [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) are an ancient isolated Aerenal order who tend the dead and dying and destroy undead, and the Sanctum is the public face of their work in the city.

## What came in the back

The cargo skylift on the roof of [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) in the Lower City ran directly to this hospital, and it carried Cogborn bodies. Doctor Edwin Graves supplied them; the Sanctum received them. When the Inevitables put the question of who supplied the bodies to the captive **Sister Nora**, she seized with pain, clutched the side of her head and bled from the nose: a BioTec-implanted mental block, preventing her from naming the supplier at all.

## The seizure

On the night the Iron Veil's pact broke, Zero-One and Silver Flame paladins seized the Black Dove Hospital and rounded up the Black Dove clergy. During the takeover, the Nest and the Silver Flame uncovered two things in the building: a large golden bell inside a shipping crate, and an **unfinished bell tower**.

The artifact was identified as a replica of the ancient [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) — a relic crafted by ancient elves from infernal metal, associated with raising the dead and inducing madness. A similar bell had been uncovered about ten years earlier by the Unforeseen in the [Greenbluff](/places/greenbluff/) mines outside Newham. <small>(The Sharn Ep 19 summary says thirteen years; Fulcrum's bell file says "twenty years ago" and the rebel-factions page "over a decade ago".)</small>

Sister Nora was tried and executed. At the founding Council of Sharn the Silver Flame pushed to outlaw the Black Doves and enforce citywide cremation, and got both.

## Present day

Three years on, the hospital is where Zero of Sharn convened a representative of every faith on the continent to hear a dwarf play the **fanfare of the Dawn**, and where the assembled clergy began teaching what they named the **Horn of Mourning**, congregation by congregation. Dario broke the crated bell in the unfinished tower above it — packed in straw by people too frightened to touch it — clearing the muffling himself and swinging with the words *"may our children forgive us."*
