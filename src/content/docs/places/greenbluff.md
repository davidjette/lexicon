**Mining camp · hills outside Newham, eastern Khorvaire · Abandoned · Inside New Mourning**

*Also known as:* the Greenbluff mines

Greenbluff is an abandoned mining camp in the hills outside [Newham](/places/newham/) in eastern Khorvaire, played out and shut since an incident in its deep gallery. That incident killed a number of Empire-owned slaves and left the survivors mad in identical fashion; the Empire moved on it immediately, and what was carried up out of the shaft was the original [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/), the Hell's Bell, recovered under Assembly seal by four people who between them would spend the next decade taking the continent apart. The bell went east by lightning rail toward [Korth](/places/korth/), and [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) died in the attempt to take it off the train. Newham is gone now and a vast region around it is the corrupted [New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/).

## The camp

What remains is a shut camp, a collapsed gallery, and ground that is still wrong. Nothing roots there. A miner's statement, kept by Fulcrum since the beginning of its bell file, records:

> "Camp's been shut since the madness in the deep gallery. Ground's still wrong, nothing roots. Whatever they took out of there in my father's time went east on the rail under Imperial seal, and men in fine coats came up from Korth to ride with it. There's no bell at Greenbluff now. Greenbluff is where the bell is FROM."

## What came out of it

The incident in the deep gallery killed a number of Empire-owned slaves and left the survivors mad in identical fashion. The Empire moved on it immediately, and what was carried up out of the shaft was the original Fantanya Nyel, recovered under Assembly seal. See [The Greenbluff Incident](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/).

The bell went east by lightning rail toward Korth. Somewhere between Newham and the capital a party boarded the train to take it and failed; John Stamos died in that attempt, and the Empire blamed [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) for it.

<small>**Dating:** Greenbluff was the first and second sessions of The Unforeseen campaign, about ten years before the Inevitables. Fulcrum's cover letter dates it "twenty years ago"; the Sharn Ep 19 summary puts the bell recovery thirteen years before the fall of Sharn; the rebel-factions file has John Stamos dying on the rail "over a decade ago". In the master chronology the Eberron campaigns sit after the Infantaverse and Dead Man's Hand era and before Starfall: The Next Generation.</small>

## Present day

Newham is gone, and a vast region around it is now the corrupted New Mourning; whether the camp outside the city lies inside that region is not stated in any source. About ten years after the bell recovery <small>(thirteen years, in the Sharn Ep 19 summary)</small>, a golden replica of the same relic was found in a shipping crate under an unfinished bell tower at Sharn's [Black Dove hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/), and identified as a replica of the bell the Unforeseen had uncovered in the Greenbluff mines.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The deep gallery:** its roof brought down by a sound the survivors could all describe.
- **The buried chamber:** the sealed room that held the bell in silence for centuries.
- **The rail:** the line the bell left on, east under Imperial seal, with men in fine coats up from Korth riding with it.
