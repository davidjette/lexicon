**Temple · Saltmarsh, Oerth · Neptune · Desecrated, its relic stolen**

*Also known as:* Neptune's sept <small>(DM notes)</small> · the church <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-12)</small>

The **Temple of Neptune** is the temple of the sea god Neptune in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), on Oerth, with the Weathervein Tomb beneath it. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), Saltmarsh's council gave the temple's deed to the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/). They took 900 gold in donations toward its repair, emptied the Weathervein Tomb, and were ambushed there by Brotherhood assassins, where [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) met the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/). Weathervein, the blade Dolores recovered, afterwards became the temple's holy relic. A few hundred years later, in the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), the temple was desecrated, its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein long dead, and the relic [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/) stolen. The town blamed [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/); Dave later wrote that [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) had tricked her into taking the blame.

## Neptune's Rejects and the deed

In the session of 2021-09-11 the party "went to the temple to get holy stuff" and "Performed a miracle at the temple". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-11)</small> Dave asked the next day "why someone gave it away for free". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-12)</small>

When Nico reasoned that Skerrin and Anders had given the party the deed, Dave answered: "The whole council did that". He added: "It’s politically popular to support the local religious fervor at least yeah" and "And the temple was a burden". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

After fines the party took in 900 gold in donations, given "with peoples expectation that it will fix the Temple"; "800 of that was from One Anonymous Donor". Dave ruled that people would flock to the temple, and that Dolores "could generate 1d8 gold on your sermons once a day or so". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small> Dave: "Easy to find you at the temple / But it’s a defensible position". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

## The restoration

A graveyard lies right beside the temple, and the temple has catacombs. <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13, 2021-09-17)</small> Dave posted a map of the restored temple: "if you actually spend the money on the restoration this is what you can get, although I imagine a small necropolis surrounding it on the stone foundation though". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

A fresco in the temple told the Weathervein story. Dave: "the fresco in the temple was basically retelling the story Jeff did". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

The party planned to hire sailors who would "fix the temple when we’re docked and crew the ship when we travel". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> Nico's list for the work: "Temple, carpenters to fix roof / walls. Build of fix one room home for Wellgar and fix attic office". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

**Wellgar Brinehanded** is the temple's cleric. <small>(Neptune's Rejects ledger)</small> Nico wanted to "keep good Ol’ Wellgar around", and Dave agreed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-13)</small> Dave suggested that Dolores ordain him: "He knows guidance" / "So he’s a level 0 priest"; Nico: "he’s the man we leave to run the church". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

## The Weathervein Tomb

The Weathervein Tomb lies below the temple. It is the family tomb of [Count Cresto Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/), "prince of the sea and master of the coves". Dave: "Count Cresto Octopailias buried his wife Amphibiana and his daughter Vivica Amphibiana in that tomb, with his own tomb above as the entrance". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12, 2021-11-13)</small>

The party emptied it in September 2021; its contents are listed below. The PW whose bones and receipts lay there is [Penguinus](/people/penguinus-weathervein/). <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18; 2022-04-23)</small>

On the night of the council meeting at which the party announced the capture of [the Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/), [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) gave Dolores a clue to the whereabouts of Weathervein: "Cujo (gravedigger) admitted to me that he believed there was more to the Weathervein tomb, that there have been reports of hauntings below the temple." He led the party to the tomb at night and ambushed them there with Brotherhood assassins. <small>(Dave's session notes, "Salvage Operation – Part I". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave: "Skerrin knew there was no sword in there, he knew he could watch you and ambush you in secret. He may not have known about the other room opened with your blood". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> The party's ally [Oshi](/people/oshi/) was killed in that fight. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, 2021-11-18)</small> See [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/).

When Dolores entered the tomb, "her presence and the blood dripping from her wound causes a weeping girl to appear over the casket": the ghost of Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein. Vivica gave Dolores her ring, "the signet of the Princess of Clouds and a gift from my father Count Crestus", and charged her: "You must recover your birthright. Find the Tempest Sword." Count Crestus's own tomb held a Ring of Water Walking. The stonework shows a "fresco of heavenly storm angels … ruling the seas", and an image of the Atlas of Endless Horizons, "a map to heaven, an heirloom of the fam". <small>(Dave's session notes. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12, 2021-11-13)</small>

## The church in Saltmarsh

By January 2022 the smuggler [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) sought allies against the church. JL, running the session: "Gellen is eager for allies to oppose the rising tide of religious fervor. "He’s scared of the church and their reign of terror even though he’s a sworn enemy of the brotherhood." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-07)</small>

## The relic and the High Priestess

Weathervein became the holy relic of the temple, and Dolores Weathervein its High Priestess. By the time of Dead Man's Hand she was dead, and her own tomb lay beneath the sea, by a hidden temple of Neptune under a whirlpool. <small>(DM notes)</small> See [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/).

## The desecration

> Neptune's sept is defiled, and its holy relic "Weathervein" is stolen. Authorities suspect Hellwood.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

The desecration was violent and fast, with no witnesses, no gunpowder and no evidence that it was pirates. The investigation found that the missing artifact is tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore, that the attackers sought the temple under the water nearby, a painting of angels "at sea, sky, and stars waiting to come back down", and brine wreaths in the library. Wanted posters of the Blackfish's crew hang around the temple. <small>(DM notes)</small> The fountain in the Temple Courtyard, which shows "Pericles beheading Medusa", is the Saltmarsh end of the gateway from the Smoldering Corpse in [Sigil](/places/sigil/)'s [Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), and the party of Dead Man's Hand came through it. <small>(DM notes)</small>

Of Hellwood, Dave said in 2025: "But it’s been a few hundred years….". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small> He later set out what had happened: "Goodface wanted the card, killed Milosz looking for it, figured Hellwood still had it, desecrated the tomb to draw her out, Hellwood used the ship as bait (she didn’t have it) so she could try and save the temple and close the abyss". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> And: "Hellwood’s fate as the Dunwater Horror, tricked by Jeremiah into taking the blame for the Temple of Neptune". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small> Goodface is Jeremiah. What followed on the sea floor is [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/); see also [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/) and [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/).

## Places of Interest

**The Temple Courtyard:** the fountain of Pericles beheading Medusa, the Saltmarsh end of the gateway from the Smoldering Corpse.\
**The catacombs:** beneath the temple.\
**The Weathervein Tomb:** below the temple, entered through Count Crestus's tomb, with a further room opened by Dolores's blood.\
**The graveyard:** right beside the temple, where Dave imagined a small necropolis.\
**The library:** brine wreaths, for water breathing.
