**Landmark · South side of [Korth](/places/korth/) · Open to the public**

The Hall of Submission is a public building on the south side of [Korth](/places/korth/) where the very old go **willingly** to cross over to the other side and begin their [second service to the Emperor](/lore/second-service/). It is also where the dead are brought by others and transformed into that service. Officers wait outside with troop transports to collect the converts. It is one of the busiest buildings in the imperial capital and one of the quietest, and there is no line outside it that anyone is forced to join.

## What it is like

Open, public and well attended. The queue is elderly. Nobody is coerced in any way a visitor can see. It is one of the better places in Korth to hold a conversation you do not want overheard, for the simple reason that **almost no one in earshot is alive**.

## What stands beside it

- **The Listening Post** — an Imperial intelligence office immediately beside the Hall, on the east side of the city. [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) named it as the place holding the records on Izaak, on Zero-One, and on how Fulcrum was rolled up across the continent. It is the Inevitables' stated next objective inside the walls.
- **An unnamed building** with heavy Imperial presence, very close to [Deirdre Moro's](/people/deirdre-moro/) residence. Contents unknown.

## Karrnathi law and the pledge

Under [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) a death sentence specifies both the duration of the pain to be inflicted and the length of service owed afterward in undeath — *unless the condemned has already pledged themselves to the Emperor*. The Hall of Submission is where that pledge is made, in daylight, by people who are not condemned of anything. The same system's other intake is a corpse chute behind the prison.
