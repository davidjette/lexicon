**Landmark · Ceremonial heart of [Korth](/places/korth/) · Owned outright by the Empire**

*Also known as:* the Parade Throne (of its stair and throne structure)

The **Great Plaza** is the ceremonial centre of [Korth](/places/korth/), the oldest and largest of the ancient forums and plazas the imperial capital was built out of, and the space every other important building in the city faces. A grand stair rises at its heart to a **ceremonial throne** where the Emperor once sat for parades and events. It has been a very long time. There is no doorway *through* the throne — to cross from the upper plaza to the lower you pass **underneath the throne structure**, and everyone in Korth does it several times a day.

## The upper plaza

Ceremonial, immaculate and mostly empty. It is overlooked by the towers of the centre, all of them Empire-owned and converted into housing that you rent from the Empire or are assigned as part of your post, one shared bathroom to a floor. It rains here. Huge **steam vats** stand in the open near the ministries, generating and storing the pressurised steam that runs the city, and the hiss under the paving is constant.

Stormtroopers under a Force Captain hold a checkpoint in the corner of the plaza and stop anyone with a visible weapon. [The Imperial Blackguards and Mercenary Act](/lore/the-imperial-blackguards-and-mercenary-act/) is enforced there.

> "Deirdre, it would require quite an authority to bring an armed unenlisted into the center of the city. So I ask you again, who are you?"

## The lower plaza

Below and beyond the parade stair, toward the palace, and the one part of the core that belongs to ordinary people. It holds a few public services and sits near the station of the decommissioned lightning rail subway. It is somewhere for humans to exist away from the ceremony of the rest of the city — younger people gather here, and people having lunch. At night it empties out.

Its public administration building serves as a great rotunda and town hall, and it is where **rations are distributed** to Karrnathi peasants, in queues, under guard. The corruption is visible if you stand there long enough: a man leaving with more than his share, guards who took payment for it. It is the only seam the Inevitables found in the whole machine of Korth.

## The crossing

The Inevitables crossed the Great Plaza on their first night in Korth, at eight in the evening, in the rain, and got stopped at the checkpoint — [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was armed and unregistered, and the Force Captain wanted to see what a Ministry advocate would ask him. They were waved through with a written reprimand and a warning that smuggling weapons into the city is punishable by death, and they went north to read the law. They crossed it again at midnight to meet a loremaster on the lower plaza and be told the Emperor is a dragon.

## Around the plaza

- **North** — [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/), directly across, a giant building.
- **Northeast** — the Ministry of Law, with [the High Court](/places/the-high-court/) and the jail.
- **West** — a large bridge across to the barracks and [the Altar of the Guardian](/places/the-altar-of-the-guardian/).
- **South** — far beyond the throne, the Great Imperial Palace.
- **The statue of [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/)** — the Empire raised it in the capital to commemorate his life and talent after putting it about that rebels killed him. <small>Where in the city it stands is not recorded.</small>
