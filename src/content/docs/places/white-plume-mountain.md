**Dungeon · Oerth · Hold of Keraptis · The Weathervein treasure · Completed**

**Also known as:** White Plume Mtn; WPM; the feathered mound

**White Plume Mountain** is a dungeon on Oerth and the hiding place of the Weathervein treasure: the three weapons **Wave**, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/), into which members of the Weathervein line, [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/), were bound. Its master was Keraptis, whom Dave named [Penguinus](/people/penguinus-weathervein/) "keraptis" Weathervein. After the Saltmarsh finale of the [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) campaign, the party **[Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/)** went into the mountain. By August 2022 they held all three weapons. In September they finished its puzzles, lost [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/), and went on to the temple of [the Eldabrin](/lore/the-eldabrin/).

## The Weathervein treasure

Dave proposed the dungeon for the campaign in March 2022: "I also love the idea of White Plume Mountain being the dungeon with the Weathervein treasure, and that the dwarves found it and extracted The artifacts, and that we have to bring them back in". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> The party already carried one such weapon, the sword [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/), which is based on Wave. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-19)</small>

When the party reached level 10 at the Saltmarsh finale, Dave listed their leads as "the mine / WPM / Eldabrin and then Scarlett Brotherhood (the Devil lives?)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

## Keraptis's poem

Dave posted an image captioned "Penguinus “keraptis” Weathervein", and the same day the riddle poem of Keraptis: <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

> Search ye far or search ye near\
> You’ll find no trace of the three\
> Unless you follow instructions clear\
> For the weapons abide with me.
>
> North past forest, farm and furrow\
> You must go to the feathered mound\
> Then down away from the sun you’ll burrow\
> Forget life, forget light, forget sound.
>
> To rescue Wave, you must do battle\
> With the Beast in the Boiling Bubble\
> Crost cavern vast, where chain-links rattle\
> Lies Whelm, past water-spouts double.
>
> Blackrazor yet remains to be won\
> Underneath inverted ziggurat.\
> That garnered, think not that you’re done\
> For now you’ll find you are caught
>
> I care not, former owners brave\
> What heroes you seek to hire.\
> Though mighty, I’ll make each one my slave\
> Or send him to the fire.

<small>(Keraptis; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

## The descent

Dave ran the dungeon, and [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) went through it as an NPC whom the other players directed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

In the session of 2022-08-13 the party cleared a little under two-thirds of the dungeon in about two and a half hours of in-game time, without clearing the Wave hall. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> They crossed a frictionless room by making ice, and would have to cross it again on the way out. By the next day they held Wave, Whelm and Blackrazor. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> By Dave's account [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/), Scrunt's order, expected to take all three weapons into storage, since the party had crippled the Dawn's two main foes, the sahuagin and [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

The party rested in an Oni's luxury cell, where Blackrazor tried to make Gabriella attune to it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-19)</small>

On 2022-09-09 the party fought through a room where an invisible stalker targeted Scrunt, who was in gaseous form. Dave called it a "Super intense room". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-10)</small>

On 2022-09-16 the party finished the dungeon's puzzles. A vampire charmed Gabriella, invisible stalkers were sent against Scrunt, and Scrunt died. Whelm told the party exactly what to do. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16; 2022-09-24)</small> Dave, after the session:

> But we legit survived those puzzles which could have killed any of us, and we have 12 hours to maybe get back out, or maybe we disappear for three months into the underdark and no one comes out of white plume mountain

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

He described the party as having "actually completed white plume mountain and dove deeper into its worst case scenario". By his account all three weapons have true natures, and natures corrupted by Keraptis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## Leaving the mountain

Dave had called the dungeon "just the beginning of the mystery of eldabrin". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> On 2022-09-23, with twelve hours left before the door closed, the party went to the Eldabrin. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small>

After Scrunt's death Gabriella resolved to overcome Blackrazor's influence and get home, and the party had put the swords away by the time they reached the Eldabrin. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24)</small> Dave expected Gabriella to retire from adventuring "If we get out of the Underdark intact". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## Places of Interest

- **The Boiling Bubble:** where, by Keraptis's poem, the Beast guards Wave.
- **The Wave hall:** left uncleared by the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>
- **The vast cavern:** "where chain-links rattle", with Whelm past two water-spouts, by the poem.
- **The inverted ziggurat:** under which, by the poem, Blackrazor lay.
- **The frictionless room:** disables certain spells, levitate among them; the party crossed it on ice. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>
- **The Oni's luxury cell:** where the party rested. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-19)</small>
- **The door:** set to close twelve hours after the party finished the puzzles. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small>

**Hazards:** resting in the mountain calls for exhaustion rolls. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>
