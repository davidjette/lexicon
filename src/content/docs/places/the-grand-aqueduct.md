**Waterway · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), central spine · Boundary, landmark and back road · Standing**

The Grand Aqueduct is a wide, concrete-lined waterway that cuts horizontally across the Lower City of Sharn, sitting lower than the surrounding neighbourhoods. It channels water through a long recessed trench, its sloped sides marked with old stonework, drainage grates and maintenance walkways running along the edges. It serves as both a boundary and a landmark, dividing the northern neighbourhoods from the southern ones while providing water and access routes throughout the district, and the service tunnels that branch off it carry most of the district's hidden traffic.

## The network beneath

Access points, service tunnels and runoff passages connect to the aqueduct at irregular intervals, forming a network of shadowed routes beneath the district. Almost every hidden thing in the Lower City eventually touches it: the movable panel behind a fireplace in a boarded apartment in [City Center](/places/city-center/); the discreet back exit of [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/); the boarded sewer door that turned out to be the way into [the Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/); and the long descent toward [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/).

When [Queen B](/people/blair/) invited three strangers to come and find her, the only direction she gave was that the aqueduct would be their first landmark. Blair's Drones found [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) wandering the Grand Aqueduct after his disappearance and brought him in for questioning; early victims of the Cog Collector were found dumped along its east side.

Years ago, after the outlawing of the Warforged in Sharn, a young Tiefling woman discovered [Preacher](/people/preacher/)'s hidden cell in the Lower City. Preacher chased her through drainage tunnels, and in the struggle she fell from the aqueduct's upper walkway, becoming impaled on the machinery below. He believed he had killed her. She was [Eden](/people/eden/), the Lord Commander's sister.

## What lives in it

Its lower levels were patrolled by the **Drain Snakes**, a gang that fortified the northern sewer network and blocked all safe movement through it until they were wiped out. Below the aqueduct proper, a massive drain pipe descends into a flooded sewer chamber where giant crocodiles and rat swarms hunt, and past that lies a reinforced door and the threshold of the Underhive.

One dead Drain Snake was found wearing a **BioTec Security** jacket, with a dead BioTec agent lying nearby, badge intact. Two more BioTec agents were later found floating in the flooded chamber. Nobody has satisfactorily explained what they were doing down there.

## Present day

With the Drain Snakes gone the aqueduct routes are the safest they have been in years, which chiefly benefits the Underhive drones now working in Sharn's new Department of Infrastructure — the same people who used the tunnels to move an entire population out of the city.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The maintenance walkways:** the aqueduct's edge paths, and the fastest unwatched route across the Lower City.
- **The great drain pipe:** the descent from the aqueduct's lower levels toward the undercity.
- **The flooded chamber:** crocodiles, rat swarms, and the reinforced door beyond.
- **The boarded sewer door:** the entrance to the Dhakaani tunnels and the Forge of Binding.
