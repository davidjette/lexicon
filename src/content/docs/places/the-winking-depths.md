**Underground region · Below the Deep Crystal of Motherstone, toward the Egg · Mind flayers, aboleth and Drow · An arc the party teleported out of**

*Also known as:* the winking depth · the Winking Depths arc

**The Winking Depths** are the depths below the Deep Crystal of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), which lie toward [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), and the name of the Temple Holdings LLC arc Dave built there. The party teleported out of that arc and went to [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), where they played a political intrigue game instead. In Waterdeep they put the Zhentarim agent Jonathan Silverhand in as Unmasked Lord, [Obi](/people/obi/) revealed himself, and [Blackstaff](/people/khelben-arunsun/) went down into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/); following his trail later took the party to the 23rd level of Undermountain and access to the Egg. The war in the Depths was fought after the party left [Refuge](/places/refuge/) for the past. [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/), left behind, fought beside Max, Atlas IV and the sea elves against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach the Egg, and there Blackstaff sent him back in time as [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/). In the time of the quest male Drow rebels held Undermountain and the Winking Depths.

## Below the Deep Crystal

After the end of [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/), Patrick, who played [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/), wrote "Now very curious what's in the Winking Depths and the Snowy Mountain Mine". Dave: "The Winking Depths are what you would have fallen into if you didn’t catch the roots falling out of the Deep Crystal". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-07-01)</small>

Just as the party cured [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), [Flex Gimble](/people/flex-gimble/) shrank them with a shrink ray and used a flood spell to push them down to the bottom of the crystal, which opened over the Winking Depths. Tiny, they caught the roots of the Eldergreen and climbed into the world of the worms and beetles. The opening lies toward the Egg; the party did not know it.

## The arc

The party teleported out of the Winking Depths arc Dave had built and went to Waterdeep, where they played a political intrigue game instead. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> In the Winking Depths arc they met the sea-elf prince Atlas IV, "the Triton prince you met in the winking depth". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> See [Atlas and the Sea Elves](/organizations/atlas-and-the-sea-elves/) and [Atlantis](/places/atlantis/).

In 2021 Dave wrote, "It’s making me realize that I really left mine flayers as a whole out of this campaign even though the big/bad basically is one" <small>(sic on "mine")</small>, and "But that cause you guys didn’t actually play the Winking Depths". Nico: "Ha true, I got us the fuck outa there lol" and "But then we had a cool city / political game". Dave: "For sure, plus that quest is still out there if you can get back to the Present". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small>

While the party planned for [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/), JL, who played Zanzibar, wrote "I’m angling for a return to the Winking Depths, friends." LaMont, who played [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/): "Down and back again. “A winking depths novel”". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small>

## Waterdeep

In a bank vault in Waterdeep, Piergerion the Younger and Allo Vera stepped in with Zanzibar, Maximillian and Laereli behind them, and found Obi with [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) and [Marcus](/people/marcus/). Elistrae put Max, [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/) and Zanzibar into visions of the two priests' memories of [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) and the Imperium, and at the end Obi disappeared. <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small> The full scene is at [Obi](/people/obi/).

In the same chapter Revan negotiated his contracts. Asmodeus "owns his soul and will demand the amulet and zarus", and Revan was confronted by Violet, an erinyes who served as a housecarl and as a spy for Asmodeus. <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small> See [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) and [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/).

Blackstaff was gone, last seen the night he talked to St. Cloud. Billy Zeps knew he had hopped into Undermountain late while no one else was looking; Durnan knew he had gone in to look into reports of the Drow overrunning it, and had sold Blackstaff's location to the Drow, who held him in Undermountain. <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small>

Two pretenders came for the city's summit meeting. Billy Zeps wanted help smuggling Jonathan into the city, "where he will take the throne and declare it a free city under the Lord Alliance again". Mortimer was approaching by [Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) boat from Neverwinter to declare his Imperium, and Dox was all over town arranging a show of support for him. <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small>

Dave: "You guys put a zhentarim agent in as unmasked lord during the winking depths" and "Jonathan Silverhand". JL: "Yeah we were thinking of our immediate needs and not the geopolitical and historical ramifications." Dave: "Yeah ofc you were ousting the false infanta". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-09)</small> Before the River Lis Dave told the party, "You have a strong contact in Jonathan Silverhand" and "He’s a top 3 Zent and he owes you the city of WD to a Certain extent". <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> See [the Zhentarim](/organizations/the-zhentarim/) and [the Lords of Waterdeep](/organizations/lords-of-waterdeep/).

## The war in the Depths

The war Zanzibar fought with Max and the sea elves came after the party left Refuge for the past. Zanzibar was left behind, and in that loop he went forward to fight beside the sea elves against the Drow and the mind flayers to reach the Egg. Once there, Blackstaff sent him back in time, with Max, to stop St. Cloud. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> That Zanzibar is [Dark Zanzibar](/people/dark-zanzibar/), and Max is [Maximillien](/people/maximillien/), who gave his life at [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/).

Atlas IV fought beside them: "the Triton prince you met in the winking depth and who become the third wheel to Max + Zan in the offscreen future war VS the spiderbros". <small>(Sic on "become". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-09)</small> Fighting with the water elves and Prince Atlas against the Drow in the Winking Depths is how Zanzibar earned Glassbane by deed. See [the Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/).

In the history the party did not change, Dave wrote, "in the “present” era Asmodeus comes to Waterdeep and there is a massive final battle of the Infanta, followed by a prolonged underground and undersea campaign to capture the Egg, which Una and the driders win". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> See [Una](/people/una/). A month earlier, on whether Zanzibar and Cypher might travel back to the "present" to fight Una and the spider bros, Dave wrote, "Really should include Revan in this as it’s basically Winking Depths 3, descent to the Egg, and he has a lot of unfinished business in that time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-20)</small>

## In Antiquity

In [the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) the party came near the Depths in another age. Dave: "you’re deep underwater and a quick jaunt from the Egg and the Winking Depths where Mindflayers and Aboleth are spewing out and the Netherese are up to something". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-02-13)</small>

## At the Egg

At the final battle the party attacked the Egg from three epochs. In the time of the quest the Egg lay far below Waterdeep, guarded by countless mind flayers and sought by the male Drow rebels who had taken Undermountain and the Winking Depths. See [The Ally Cards](/lore/the-ally-cards/) and [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).
