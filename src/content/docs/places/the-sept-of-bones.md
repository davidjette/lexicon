**Buried temple · Under the Ivory Lazaret · Older than the hill · Destroyed with the valley ([Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/))**

**The Sept of Bones** was an ancient temple buried under the hill of the [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), almost directly below [the Polygon](/items/the-polygon/). The millions of dead laid around it over the centuries were the source of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s power. She made her last stand there in [Korth Ep 22](/sessions/korth-ep-22-the-reckoning-of-the-mother/), and [the Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/) was born there.

## What Aurorix said

The gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/) told the party what it was, in [Korth Ep 20](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/):

> A very, very long time ago, long before most of your kind ever came to this place, a people inhabited this forest. They worshipped an ancient god. It was a god of death, yes, but also a god of renewal, of recycling, of taking from what was lost what could still be gained. They built a sept here before there was a hill. A great sept of bones stood on this peninsula. Over the years it was buried, more and more bodies, buried in ceremonial graves all around this holy site, until people forgot that the temple was even here. They built another one on top of it. People think that one's ancient. But these fools, if they've dug up that place, are meddling in magics which they can never control or understand.

## The ways in

A paved tunnel runs from the lair of [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) to the outside of the Sept, where the party heard dull chanting and saw green light behind iron bars clogged with skulls. Under a paving slab beside the eternal flame at the master altar of Nos Matre, cut steps and a ladder go down to the Council's crypt. A hidden stair in the grotto behind the Summer Palace goes down under the hill to the same crypt. There is another way under the statue of Uriel at the head of the Stairs of Her Mercy, and the shaft under the Tomb of Kaius I comes down in the lair.

Gemma and Eric used none of them. They went straight down through the earth by *dimension door* from the ritual garden.

## The crypt

The crypt is the entrance to the Sept. It lies at the foot of an ancient stone stair under the terrazzo of the ritual garden, a vault of funerary urns with sarcophagi of many ages mortared into its walls. Twelve black coffins no more than thirteen years old hold the members of the [Council of 13](/organizations/the-council-of-13/) whom [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) hunted down. One more casket stands open and empty. It was Esther's. In the crypt Gemma's sword, the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/), turned red for a moment and felt angry in her hand.

The roof of the crypt opens to the sky on a mechanism. It ground open during the fight and let in daylight and rain.

Two huge doors lead from the crypt into the Sept. Gemma broke them in with an adamantine mace.

## The Sept

Inside is a steep-walled chamber with altars, pedestals for sacrifice, and stairs rising to the four quarters. Every flat surface lies five to ten feet deep in skulls, bones and pieces of bone. Undying flames burn white and green. The power of the dead rose from the heaps as a faint purple light, up through the ceiling toward the Polygon.

The floor of bones gave up guards for Uriel as long as she held it, great things built of bone with glaives in their hands.

## The last stand

Uriel came here when her body sank through the paving of the forecourt above. She fought Gemma and Eric on the stair, half spirit, joined by a cord to her son. Esther Crona came down to kill her and was fed to the child over the pit. Dario came down with the news that the Polygon was going to blow. Kitsune's fox cut the cord, and Eric the Cleric destroyed the Empress on the stair.

When the Heir was cut free he drank the power that had held the bones up, and the army in the Sept fell down. He broke out through the hill above.

## Right now

The valley above the Sept was destroyed by the weapon the Hundreds dropped on it. What is left under the ground is not known.
