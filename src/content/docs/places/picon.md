**Moon · The Feywild · Home of [Caprica](/people/caprica/)'s family · Blighted by the song**

**Picon** is a moon in the Feywild, the home of [Caprica](/people/caprica/)'s family, in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). A sonic disturbance has left it scarcely habitable. In January 2025 [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) went back to Picon to continue the search for Caprica, and climbed its Crystalline Volcano to an elven computer that gave them the way to [Necis](/places/necis/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-01-18; 2025-01-25)</small> <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Description

Picon was a small village world on the sea, rich with fish, rustic but technologically very advanced. A sonic disturbance has since rendered it scarcely habitable, and it is largely abandoned, except for a few dangerous fey creatures and a few elves who have begun to mutate from prolonged exposure to the song: they are going bald, losing their memory, and gaining psionic powers. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## Caprica's family

Caprica's father and mother live on Picon with her sisters, Aralon and Sagittaron. Her father insisted he had no daughter named Caprica; he had named his daughter Capricornelius. The family told the searchers to climb to the highest point on the moon and seek the wisdom of Elistraee on the Crystalline Volcano, and her father gave them the Mace of Elistraee, a +2 mace that lets its wielder cast *Otto's irresistible dance*. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New magic items)</small>

## The Crystalline Volcano

The climb was a hero's journey. On the way the searchers saved a youngling, who proved to be a green hag with two boggles, and had a vision of their purpose: Caprica at the helm of the Zanzibar, Caprica fighting the Shrike, and Caprica floating in a tank, armless and legless like the Venus de Milo. The narrow, winding path is swept by falling rocks and littered with dead elves carrying prayer scrolls to Elistraee; a banshee and an awakened tree fought them there. They wore headphones of their own devising against the song. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

A wounded young elf girl named Luna stumbled from the crags asking for help and warning of evil fey who preyed on travellers. She was a night hag. She could tell Carlos was not who he said he was, and tried to turn the pair against each other, telling Carlos that Barrias would use all his wishes for himself and telling Barrias that Carlos was "not a man, he's a creature beyond understanding"; exposed, she summoned giant frogs. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

At a shrine to Elistraee covered in lunar runes and elvish script, an inscription read: "Those who would seek the wisdom of the goddess must face the trials. Yours have already begun." It held very old coins: a Torilian coin with the crest of the Moonshae Isles, an Oerthian coin with the face of an old Lord of Greyhawk, and a gold torus of Sigilian jink, beside an elven skeleton holding a carved rod, a Rod of Security. Beyond it, a rock bridge crossed a river of lava from the summit, guarded by fire elementals. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

**The Righteous Pass** is a narrow pass between two fifty-foot statues, a unicorn and a fairy queen, staring at each other; only the righteous may pass, and those who walk through are separated. The statues judged Barrias ("you abandoned your child") and Carlos ("you are slothful and vain"). In his trial of empathy the unicorn took Barrias to [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) after the battle, among thousands of wounded calling for aid, Jim, Luigi, Caprica, Tillamook and Se7en among them, with a troop transport ready to depart nearby and another on a ridge ahead, and asked whether he would choose efficiency or empathy. In Carlos's trial of selflessness a fairy wearing the face of Irina took him back into his bottle and accused him of loving only himself, and three Carloses from different times came against him: the original, a wizard and a genie. [Se7en](/people/se7en/) could not enter, having failed the trials. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-28-starfall-jl-nico-answers.md)</small>

At the summit, where the tune grew loudest and visions of Necis floated through the hallucinogenic vapours of the volcano, waited the Corrupted Feylord of Sound, the twisted husk of a bald, grey, coughing female elf:

> "You seek the wisdom of Elistraee? I am Elistraee… or rather I will be once I have killed her for abandoning her children."

<small>— The Feylord of Sound, on the Crystalline Volcano.</small>

<small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## LS-TR3

Inside the volcano stands LS-TR3, a massive supercomputer of elven construction connected to a vast multi-dimensional network, with a huge terminal of star maps to every point in the elven universe; Caprica's name is among them. The searchers hacked it for the star map while mechanical wolves, goblins, ogres, iron cobras and a mechanical giant came against Barrias at the terminal, until the computer shut down its defenders and gave them the hologram to Necis. The crew also came away with a Rod of Security and a Hat of Many Spells. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> A short session on 28 February 2025 returned the crew to Necis with the hologram to "Neesus". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-02-28)</small>
