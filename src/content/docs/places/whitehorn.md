**Trading post · The Ride · Arc III, The Age of the Infanta**

**Whitehorn** is a small trading post in the Ride, a northern valley north of Zhentil Keep and east of the Anauroch Desert, where the Eraka horsemen of Khal Unger keep order. It was the last stop of [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/)'s ship before Snowdown, and the Company left a team there to search for the Infanta of Death, [Marcus](/people/marcus/), who had fled the tomb beneath its temple. In [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/) the party followed the ship's records to Whitehorn, fought the dead there and freed enslaved children. They returned to defeat zombies, devils, a necromancer and earthquakes, and extracted the [Soul Syphon](/items/the-soul-syphon/) from the tomb.

## The Ride

The Ride is a cold grass plain separated from civilization by the Dragonspine Mountains. It is patrolled and dominated by the mounted Eraka hordesmen under the command of **Khal Unger**, the Horseking, whose men keep minimal order at major crossings like Whitehorn and mostly push out any other gangs or powers that intrude on their territory. <small>(Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part IV.)</small>

Whitehorn is a neutral place between the steppe barbarians who control the valley, "mostly a place for ore and loot mined form the local mountains to find their way into the pockets of thugs and villains." <small>(Sic on "form". Arc III Part III.)</small> The settlement itself:

> Whitehorn is a motley encampment littered with rickety stalls and rows and rows of neatly erected tents, with the smell of horses and sheep in full bloom, even in the dead of winter.

<small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

At the very end of the road north stands a small temple for the consecration of the dead, with a handful of more permanent structures around it, among them the longhouse where the Horseking eats, sleeps and drinks. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

## St. Cloud's ship

The ship's navigation records showed that the *Excelsior*, later [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/), was moored at Zhentil Keep, made a short trip to Whitehorn, returned to Waterdeep, and then made its final journey to Snowdown. <small>(Arc III Part III.)</small> Whitehorn lies more than a thousand miles from Snowdown, and Lex told the party the trip would take a little over three days at the Arielle's highest speed. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small> Dave described the destination as "the last known berth of St Cloud’s ship where they dropped off a search and capture team looking for the Infanta of Death". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-08)</small>

## The Horseking's daughter

When the party arrived there were no children in sight; they had been tucked away. There was word that the king was hurt and his daughter, the future queen, dead or missing, and mounted warriors surrounded the longhouse on high alert. The commoners said the princess had been possessed and then stolen by the devil, and they were terrified because the dead had come to life to take her. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

The Khalisi, the Khal's wife, told a different story. The Khal and the Gravekeeper of the temple worship Asmodeus. When the Khal first took power he dreamed that his son would ride a white horse and every man alive would tremble at the sound of its hooves, and a priest told him his child was destined to become Death itself. At the birth of the Khal's first child the priest placed the baby in an arcane device and bound to it the soul of the God of Death. When the ritual did not work, they repeated it every year on the anniversary of her birth. This time they went too far, and the dead bodies of centuries of Khals and Khalisis ambushed them and stole her child. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

The orphans the Gravedigger kept in the tomb knew otherwise: the God of Death had been inside Marcus, one of them, who made the dead kill and ran away. See [Marcus](/people/marcus/) and [Drefan's Kids](/organizations/drefans-kids/). Marcus was bound for the Citadel of the Raven, which is occupied by [the Zhentarim](/organizations/the-zhentarim/). <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

## The first visit

The party reached Whitehorn in Dave's "special Halloween episode". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-08)</small> [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) turned into a zombie from his wounds; [Stoth](/people/stoth-youngglade/) subdued him, tied him up and ran for help; [Kara](/people/kara/) flew the ship in and nuked the other zombies; and [Drefan](/people/drefan/) cured him. Afterwards fruit tasted bad to Revan and made him nauseous. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-21)</small>

The Horseking's daughter was safe, and did not want to go back. Dave: "The poor Khal and his wife have no idea their child is safe" / "Yeah she didn’t want to go back at all". A machine stood in the temple's tomb, and removing it meant the party would "probably have to dynamite the tomb to get that thing out". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-21)</small> Dave named it "The Soul Syphon at Whitehorn". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-23)</small>

Drefan and Roland both freed bands of enslaved children, and Dave suggested the next session open "with an extraction back at whitehorn". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>

## The return

Dave offered the party a return "to retrieve the Soul Syphon/rescue Khalisi"; [Pim](/people/pim/)'s player voted aye because "Pim wants all the machines." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-08)</small> The party attacked Whitehorn, fought a Horned Devil and a Chain Devil, cast Planar Binding on the Chain Devil and interrogated it, and extracted the Soul Syphon. <small>(Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part V.)</small> Dave, after the session:

> Frankly I'm impressed you could thoroughly defeat 12 zombies, 2 devils, a 15th lv necromancer and multiple earthquakes but you did it. Lots of information this time, I can't even summarize everything you know. But you did discover the eldritch origin of the soul syphon, some combination of water elemental magick and magic from the far realm (deep), and you know the empire's plans to invade Amn in the morning.

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-11)</small>

## Places of interest

- **The temple:** a small temple for the consecration of the dead at the end of the road north, kept by the Gravekeeper.
- **The tomb:** beneath the temple, reached in its deeper parts by tiny access tunnels; home of the Gravedigger's orphans and of the Soul Syphon.
- **The longhouse:** where the Horseking eats, sleeps and drinks.
- **The encampment:** stalls and tents for the ore and loot trade of the valley.
