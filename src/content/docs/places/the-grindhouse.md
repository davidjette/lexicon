**Fighting arena · [The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Operated by Scam Likely · Standing**

The Grindhouse is a brutal, no-holds-barred fighting arena in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), operated by a bookie named **Scam Likely**. It takes its name from the grinding of steel gears embedded in its walls, part of a defunct factory, and it holds a prize fight every tenday. In one of those fights [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/), entered by [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/), defeated a ringer staked by [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/), the owner of [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/).

## Description

> A brutal, no-holds-barred fighting arena in the Hive Ward with a reputation for chaos + … Operated by a … bookie named Scam Likely, its name comes from the grinding … of steel gears embedded in the walls, part of a deadly defunct factory which occassionally rotates and shifts creating hazards in the the floor. A huge cage over the arena can be lowered to trap the fighter…
>
> The structure is a dark, industrial monstrosity - smoky air filled with the acrid smell of burning oil, blood stains of every color soaked and rusted into the flooring. Chains hang from the ceiling, rattling endlessly from unknown movements. Some claim the building is formerly a piece of Carceri, brought to Sigil for some foul industrial purpose.
>
> <small>(DM notes; sic)</small>

## Rules

The Grindhouse's only rule is not to interfere directly with a fight or you will **join it**. Scam Likely sets the odds and also approves the fighters. Anyone who causes a disruption is removed to the cellar or thrown into the arena. <small>(DM notes)</small>

A prize fight is held every tenday. Prize fighting is also offered at Fortune's Wheel. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## History

### Battlebeast's prize fight

After the party were trapped on [the Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/), Sharegrave sent Battlebeast to the arena. Sharegrave has friends in the gladiator business, has been to the Arena of Moons, and recognised Battlebeast when he saw him fight Sharegrave's men. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "But today you are going to make it up to me. There is a prize fight every tenday at a local blood pit called the GRINDHOUSE"
>
> "Scam sets odds but also approves the fighter. I happen to know another party who has entered a **Ringer**, and placed a rather large bet to entice action from the crowd.
>
> My sources have also told me that this ringer is not such a sure thing - a malfunctioning iron golem that's controlled from somewhere in the crowd. … You have no chance against it otherwise."
>
> — Sharegrave <small>(DM notes)</small>

Battlebeast was entered for a prize of 2,000 gold, to fight three rounds. The other party behind the ringer was Shemeska, who would be very upset if any interference were traced back to Sharegrave; [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) had agents in the crowd; and the golem's operator was a wizard or other caster somewhere nearby. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The ringer was a malfunctioning iron golem in damaged plating, moving in jerks. Its failing core threw off lightning, or made it strike harder at a cost to itself, and it fought with crushing slams and lurching swipes. <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party won the fight and defeated the ringer, foiling Shemeska's fix. They returned to the Hive, rested, restocked and were paid: 250 gold to Battlebeast and 100 gold to each of the others.

> "I'm giving this to you to be nice, so you can have some walking around money. You still owe me.
>
> — Sharegrave <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Layout

- **The arena floor:** blood-soaked and rusted, over defunct factory gears that rotate and shift beneath the fighters.
- **The cage:** a huge cage over the arena that can be lowered to trap the fighters.
- **The ceiling chains:** rattling endlessly from unknown movements.
- **The cellar:** where anyone who disrupts a fight is taken.
