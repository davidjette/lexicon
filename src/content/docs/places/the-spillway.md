**Dungeon · [The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Rotten William's hideout · Plague portal closed**

**The Spillway** is a dungeon in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), a place of sewers, and the hideout and lair of the extortionist [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/). The party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) first raided his hideout there to rescue [Prez](/people/prez/). They returned to storm his lair, chased him down into the sewers of the Spillway Depths, and in the Sanitizing Room killed him and the plague demon Gorelach and Gunt, saved the Bleaker intern Craig and closed a portal to a plague dimension that had opened in a drain and was poisoning the water. Closing it cured [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/). [Barold Bonds](/people/barold-bonds/) died in the fight and was reincarnated as a tiefling.

## Rotten William's hideout

From their camp in the Hive the party heard that Rotten William was in the Spillway, and saw a cranium rat running north-west toward it. They invaded his hideout there, found evidence of a fight with Tabaxi, saw cranium rats, and rescued Prez, a thief in [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s gang. On that visit they also saw [Pharod](/people/pharod/) with the handsome man, [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/). <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

There they made a deal with Rotten William to kill Sharegrave. He gave them a wand which he said would paralyse Sharegrave and track them; it held a single charge of *fireball* and a *command* to cast it in Sharegrave's presence. <small>(DM notes)</small> See [the Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/).

## The missing cleric

[Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/), a healer of [the Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/), had lost her assistant Craig for three weeks. Her leads on the cause of Cageblight pointed to the sewers under the Spillway, which she could not get close to. <small>(DM notes)</small> Among the party's outstanding errands after the Grindhouse were to double-cross Rotten William and kill him, to find a missing cleric in the Spillway, and to find the cause of Cageblight. <small>(DM notes)</small>

On the day of a session in March 2025, Dave offered the players a choice: "Do you wanna do the spillway, stay in the lady’s ward/sigil, or go back to hive ward to clear out the spillway" / "I mean go to another plane like Faerun or etc". He described the dungeon:

> The Spillway is a dungeon with at least one boss fight, leaving sigil to go to a new plane will start a new arc and explore someone’s murder and backstory, you could spend time in sigil outside the hive ward too, my suspicion is that’s the #3 priority but if people’s characters need to check boxes, we could do quick breakouts too

And: "You guys are essentially at the max level for the Hive now so it’s a good time to clean up that act, but I also get that people want to move on to the main plot so to speak, both will be fun, spillway will be a crunchy dungeon" / "Like it was last time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-03-14)</small>

Nico, who plays [Lenore](/people/lenore/), set out the case for going back: "Finding Diana’s associate basically leads us to the spillway / Rotten William AND possibly finding what’s causing the sickness (finding a cure?) which could put Diana and her healers on our side too." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-03-14)</small>

## The Spillway Depths

The party assaulted Rotten William's lair in the Spillway, "killing everyone except 'Whitey,' who they tortured (lightly) but then escaped." Rotten William had been planning a kidnapping and a murder for hire, and fled into the sewer. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

The party pursued him deeper, past slimes and hazards, and met a giant spider and a colony of cranium rats called **Many-as-One**. They talked their way through, and found Rotten William and his cronies afraid to enter a chamber filled with black tendrils. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## The Sanitizing Room

They cornered Rotten William in the **Sanitizing Room**, where a portal to a plague dimension had opened in the drain and was poisoning the water system. Craig, carrying a scroll of *close portal*, was there:

> Craig had attempted to finish his Master's mission to close it, but in so doing, angered a plague demon. RW ordered his men to stop him, but the party ambushed and killed them and then the Demon.. RW did try to appeal to it to save him, but it held no mercy for him.

<small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

The demon was **Gorelach "the Maw" and Gunt**, the Twin Maw of Plagues, two heads on one body. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party killed it, saved Craig and killed Rotten William, and by closing the portal they cured Cageblight entirely.

Barold died in the fight. Dave:

> Barold died in combat, and I let him roll on my expanded wild magic table (he's a wild magic barb) he rolled super high and was Reincarnated instantly, he rolled Tiefling on a table to decide the race.

<small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

Among the spoils were a Wand of Secrets, an Epee of Temperance, various potions and a ring of invisibility. Diana Rhea gave the party 2,000 gold and poultices, the Gatehouse was opened to them, and the Bleak Cabal became their faction ally. <small>(DM notes, play log)</small>

## Places of Interest

**Rotten William's hideout:** where the party rescued Prez and took the wand meant for Sharegrave.\
**Rotten William's lair:** stormed by the party; only "Whitey" escaped.\
**The Spillway Depths:** sewers of slimes and hazards, a giant spider and the cranium rats of Many-as-One.\
**The chamber of black tendrils:** which Rotten William and his cronies were afraid to enter.\
**The Sanitizing Room:** site of the plague portal in the drain, closed by the party.
