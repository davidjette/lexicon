**Cavern - the ore seam at the heart of the Korramont**

The Glitterhame is a vast cavern of polished black **Shavat** - the planar ore of Shavarath - at the heart of the Korramont. It is named in stone on the [Grand Stair](/places/the-grand-stair/) as the discovery that made the [Deeptempura](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/): *a cavern full of infernal ore*. [King Corn](/people/king-corn/) found it, bargained with a devil for the art of working it, and built a hold and a dynasty on the strength of that bargain. Its floor is now a forest of fungus, and it has been off limits to the [foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/)'s workforce since the growth began.

## Present state

The floor of it is now a forest of glowing, breathing fungus. The [bloom](/species/the-bloom/) fills the cavern from wall to wall and stops dead at exactly one boundary: the consecrated stone of the sepulcher gallery, which it cannot cross. Gricks move through it unbothered and pay no attention to anyone the bloom has claimed. Because the cavern was off limits, [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) was sent down here on punishment duty.

The approach into the cavern is a gallery of everlasting flames and luminescent crystal - beautiful, and lined with sealed caskets holding an undead apiece, trapped since the night the bell rang.

## The heart of the growth

At the centre of the bloom, under the largest fungal tree, lies a skeleton in professional clothes wearing the mayoral brooch of [Newham](/places/newham/). The tree is rooted through the collar of his coat. His satchel holds a rejected Ministry of Transportation stoppage order over the signature of the acting Secretary, [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), and LeBeefe's handwritten instruction to the courier:

> observe the deep workings and report what listens back

Beside him lies a glass terrarium, burst outward from the inside by growth. That is the seed of every whisper in the mountain. A courier walked it down here roughly a decade ago, died, and the thing in the jar ate the cavern.

## What it does to people

Contact with the spores opens a conversation. [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) answered it aloud at the threshold of the gallery and never fully stopped: the bloom looked out through his eyes, walked him to the courier's body, offered to consume his friends, and asked for one thing over and over - *"Ring it here. When HE is born, it will be too late."* Sedda had exactly the same conversation three years earlier and did what it asked.

The party's reading is that anyone infected who dies comes back lucid, which is how a princess spent three years sorting barrels at the bottom of the world and could still hold a conversation about it.

## Places of Interest

- **The sepulcher gallery** - the masoned approach: everlasting flames, crystal light, two living wreaths of ceremonial belts, and the sealed sarcophagus of [Queen Pu-Pu Platter Deeptempura](/people/queen-pu-pu-platter-deeptempura/), which will not open for strength, for plant growth, or for fire. Consecrated ground, and the only safe footing in the cavern.
- **The kin's trail** - a rope line, a cold camp and mushroom "fish" drying on cords, left by the [Teryaki](/organizations/the-teryaki-clan/) on their way in.
- **The falls** - a masoned stair descending beside the water to a lower chamber: a pool of blind white fish, a dozen skeletons in three-year-rusted Imperial slave shackles, and a cistern whose hatch opens onto a pile of bones packed so tightly they can barely constitute themselves.
- **The chalk drawing** - low on a wall at a child's height: a bell, and stick-figure dwarves with their hands over their ears.
- **The Iron Door** - smith-marked, riveted, no keyhole, reading KNOCK AND BE KNOWN. It opens to *shave and a haircut*, and in the creak of opening it says the names of the people knocking.
- **The crevice and the drainage tunnel** - a narrow vertical crack in the rock wall, squeezed single-file, climbing into a forgotten drain that gets hotter and more sulphurous the further it goes. An **Alkilith** had torn a permanent doorway to the Abyss open in the wall there - most likely the route by which a succubus reached Sister Calvane's suite. The demon is destroyed and the portal snapped shut with it.
