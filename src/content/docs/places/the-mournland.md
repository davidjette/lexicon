**Region · the dead kingdom of Cyre, central Khorvaire · Toxic and undead-haunted · Held by the Hundreds**

*Also known as:* The Mournlands · Cyre · the Glass Plateau (the region within it held by the Hundreds)

The Mournland is the dead kingdom of Cyre, a region of central Khorvaire held by [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and **the Hundreds**. Beneath an airship it churns with thick, poisonous fog that will kill an unmasked traveller, and inside the fog the dead do not stay down: at the Cyrean portal every fallen paladin rose moments later as undead, so that the battle grew the enemy's numbers while it was being fought.

## The Glass Plateau and Mt. Silicon

The **Glass Plateau** is the part of the Mournland that matters strategically, and it belongs to Locke Pierce and the Hundreds. Their stronghold is **Mt. Silicon**, a fortified Warforged hold carved into a mountainside, with a vast control chamber full of screens and of the Warforged Locke calls his children.

The plateau is also what BioTec and Project E.D.E.N. were mining for: E.D.E.N. retrieved materials from the Glass Plateau for testing, and the Hundreds kept their distance from those operations — "not without incident."

## The Ruby Nova

In the control chamber at Mt. Silicon, Locke Pierce showed the Inevitables his countermeasure to the ringing of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/): a weaponised catastrophe, a controlled recreation of the Mourning itself, meant to wipe out everything before [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) can turn the world into an undead empire. He named it the **Ruby Nova**, and demonstrated it on the Cyrean portal site the Silver Flame survivors had just been evacuated from. A silent red dome bloomed across the horizon, engulfed the portal, pushed past the Mournland border and destroyed the remnants of Vex d'Lyrandar's fleet. It was visible from [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/).

To the free states the weapon was immediately dubbed **the Last Mourning**.

## Something is changing

A **Cyrean Red Pomegranate** — a fruit believed extinct since the Mourning — arrived in Sharn as a private gift from Locke Pierce, large and fresh. It suggests something within the Mournlands is changing. Locke's own phrasing for it was "new growth."

## Travelling there

Survival requires specialised masks against the toxic air. The Hundreds issue upgraded breathing devices that generate an **Air Bubble**; each is inscribed in ancient gnomish with the words *Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers*. Wounds taken in the mist carry **Mistborn illness**, a curse that spreads with whatever force is raising the dead where they fall.

**Places of Interest:**

- **Mt. Silicon:** the fortified Warforged stronghold of the Hundreds, carved into a mountainside.
- **The Glass Plateau:** the Hundreds' territory, and E.D.E.N.'s quarry.
- **The Cyrean portal:** a massive portal held for a time by Silver Flame paladins, and the Ruby Nova's first target.
- **[Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/):** the R.U.S.T. and Hundreds settlement on the western border.
