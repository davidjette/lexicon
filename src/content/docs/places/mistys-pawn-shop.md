**Shop · [City Center](/places/city-center/), [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) · Pawnbroker · Standing**

Misty's Pawn Shop is a street-level pawnbroker's in the City Center of Sharn's Lower City, across from [the Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/), run by **Kriss Kross** and his wife **Misty**. Beneath it a hidden tunnel runs down into the abandoned Lightning Rail Sub-Transit control room that serves as the headquarters of **R.U.S.T.**

## The family

Kriss is the son of **Karin Kross**, who keeps the Kross Roads Corner Store nearby, and of her ex-husband, the BioTec technician **Martin Kross**. When Martin ran from BioTec he came to the shop, told them he planned to hide in the aqueduct, and asked Kriss to meet him there; when Kriss went, Martin was gone. BioTec agents came asking questions afterwards, and the couple were guarded and suspicious with anyone who asked more.

Misty is also **Preacher**'s daughter — raised by the last Warforged left in Sharn while the rest of his people fled to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/).

## The tunnel

It was through the door beneath the shop that the Inevitables were brought to meet Preacher, learn that the Cogborn were trapped and running out of time, and hear Blair's price for letting them out.

It is also where **Renn Tal** was hidden after [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) came to the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/) looking for whoever had been asking about [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/).
