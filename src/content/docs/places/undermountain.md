**Dungeon · Far below [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Halaster Blackcloak's collection · Level 1 and Level 2, the Arcane Chambers**

**Also known as:** the Yawning Portal (its entrance at Fort Maximillien)

**Undermountain** is a dungeon far below [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), reached from [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/) through a giant trash hole which "happened to be the 'Yawning Portal' of Undermountain Fame." The Company's file on [O-3255](/items/o-3255/) holds that tomes and items stolen from the library of the House of Wonder at the direction of [Halaster Blackcloak](/people/halaster-blackcloak/) found their way into it, and on its second level Halaster has assembled relics of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) as an exhibit. On the night of [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/), [Lenore](/people/lenore/), [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Heyu](/people/heyu/) escaped into it through a portal in the hole; at dawn the bodies of the hanged [Maverick](/people/maverick/), [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) and [Barold Bonds](/people/barold-bonds/) fell after them, and [Tripp Sandhill](/people/tripp-sandhill/), a player character played by Patrick, raised the fallen. With [Rodriel](/people/rodriel/), who had also escaped capture at the fort, the party worked through Level 1 and descended into Level 2, the Arcane Chambers.

## The Yawning Portal

Inside Fort Maximillien a door leads outside to a huge pit in the ground, "with no bottom in sight, its edges littered with TRASH." Among Waterdeep's places of interest, Fort Max is listed with its portal: "TRASH HOLE." <small>(DM notes, IMG_3009)</small>

During the breakout Lenore used a spell to open a portal, horizontal and far below the ground inside the pit, and she, BattleBeast and Heyu jumped in. [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) stood on the edge, then turned back and gave himself up to the guards. The three hit the hard smooth stone of an underground dungeon in pitch black, in a room with only two exits and old rusty shields lining the walls, covered in obscene graffiti. They had no idea what was in the darkness. Of the party, only Heyu, Lenore, BattleBeast and Rodriel were not captured at the fort.

Undermountain suppresses every form of teleportation magic into or out of the dungeon except Halaster's gates, and the portal Lenore opened in the trash hole was one of them. Lenore has a great edge in finding and opening them. <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-08)</small>

After dawn they crouched under a translucent sheltering dome that Heyu raised from her wand, while Lenore fitted on some slinky black leather armor Heyu had saved from a previous defeated foe. Then three bodies fell onto the dome: Shadowhigh, Maverick and Barold. Half of the party had landed in the hole dead, betrayed by Nicollo, "their erstwhile companion." They were "lucky to meet **Tripp Sandhill** who raised their fallen (for a hefty prize)." <small>(DM notes, IMG_3100)</small>

## Level 1

Tripp Sandhill warned the party about the "vampires" (undertakers). The party encountered a bugbear with an intellect devourer and killed it, and killed wandering goblins. They helped Tripp get revenge for the death of his party, recovered the mission file for O-3255, and met an old man who was the bandit camp's prisoner. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3100, IMG_3105)</small>

### The Undertakers

The Undertakers are the "vampires" of Tripp's warning, and they are not vampires. Their bosses are "UKTARL", who is **Ricardo Spurtz**, and "HARRIA", who is **Iwanna B. Horny**, "now a **real** FLESH GOLEM." The Undertakers demand ten gold a person, want the bugbears killed, and keep a trapped secret door rigged with a spring spear trap. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3080, IMG_3100)</small>

### Worg's Eye Outpost

Worg's Eye Outpost is held by goblins and bugbears, with intellect devourers among them, and they are fully aware of the party's presence. They survived an air elemental with moderate casualties, down to fifteen goblins and three bugbears or intellect devourers, and their goblins track the party from about 180 to 240 feet away. One of the outpost's goblins carries the **Golden Castanets**, an uncommon bardic instrument that grants a bonus to spell attacks and spell save DCs and, once a day, restores a use of Bardic Inspiration. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3105, IMG_3107)</small>

### Soggy Blankets

[Soggy Blankets](/people/soggy-blankets/) is the old man lying in wet blankets with a chain around his neck. He pops up, pulls the chain off and immediately starts resetting traps: the secret door trap, the fallen chandelier and the spear trap. He speaks in riddles, and he walked away from the party. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3101, IMG_3106, IMG_3107)</small>

Soggy Blankets is Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Mage, in disguise. He knew where the book O-3255 was, and he walked away during the party's fight with the shadows. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>

### What the party took from Level 1

- **The mission file for O-3255.**
- **The Bronze Visage.**
- **A circlet of blasting,** taken by Heyu. It casts *scorching ray* once between long rests.
- **Directions** to "Ars Calix (Bookcase in office Level 2)".

<small>(DM notes, IMG_3105, IMG_3107, IMG_3108)</small>

## Halaster Blackcloak's collecting

The O-3255 file records that the tome passed through the library of the House of Wonder in Waterdeep before disappearing in a robbery around 1651 DR, and that recent findings lead the Company to believe the theft was at the direction of Halaster Blackcloak, and that the missing tomes and items found their way into Undermountain. Of Halaster himself it says:

> Blackcloak is almost certainly aware of the nature of O-3255 and has likely isolated it from other books, symbols, and vulnerable media, and from himself and his person, though his reputation as a "Mad" mage may indicate exposure to Oblivia, both known and unknown to the Company.

<small>— O-3255</small>

## Level 2, the Arcane Chambers

The party descended to Level 2, the Arcane Chambers, and encountered the **Goblin Bazaar**. They freed its slaves, causing a riot, killed its leader and took his **Circlet of Human Perfection**. A revenant, **Halleth Carter**, came with the party to kill his friends, and the party killed the revenant's friends, the priest and the dwarf. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3107, IMG_3108)</small>

The Arcane Chambers have a Gate, three Trials, a Guardian and a Fate <small>(DM notes, IMG_3108)</small>:

**Gate:** Aberration\
**Trials:** Inverted Medusa (Euryale), inverted Ring, inverted Tomb\
**Guardian:** Inverted Temple\
**Fate:** Star

### The Mind Flayer Door

The Mind Flayer Door carries "frescoes of dwarven ceiling twisted with tentacles." <small>(DM notes, IMG_3108)</small>

### Motherstone Hall

Motherstone Hall is Level 2, rooms 23 to 26.

> Segments of Motherstone architecture, bas relief, tombs, statuary, and relics stolen by Halaster over the centuries, and assembled as a kind of exhibit and "model" for understanding and admiring the ancient city. Built before any obliviation was lifted on Brightmantle, so constructed with profound mystery around the origin of the city.

<small>(DM notes, IMG_3109. See [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/).)</small>

In the hall's large chamber, 23B, lies the **Sleeping Fomorian**. Beyond a way marked "GATE & RING" is a long hall of **wedding frescoes**, marked "(opens)" and "(Place rings on finger)", with an altar at its far end. Below it, under the word "DOWNFALL", a long corridor of alcoves runs through room 25, marked for the Medusa, where stone will "unpetrify creatures." Elsewhere in the hall stands a **faceless crone statue**. **Nadia the Unbent** is there with five thugs and a bugbear. Room 26 is an **elven tomb**, the tomb of **Daenan Gallidan**, and it is empty except for the STAR card. <small>(DM notes, IMG_3108, IMG_3109)</small>

Daenan Gallidan is the elven prince of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/) whose sarcophagus, with [Silverbane](/people/silverbane/) inside it, was carried in the hold of the *Evening Star* in [Hope in Exile](/history/arc-i-hope-in-exile/).

## In the Ages of the Infanta

Five hundred years before Dead Man's Hand, male Drow rebels had taken Undermountain and the Winking Depths. [Khelben Arunsun](/people/khelben-arunsun/), the Blackstaff, went into Undermountain and was held there by the Drow. In the time of that quest, one of the three epochs from which the party attacked [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), the Egg lay far below Waterdeep, reached past countless mindflayers and those Drow. <small>(See [the Ally Cards](/lore/the-ally-cards/).)</small>

The Blackstaff went down just before Obus revealed himself in the Winking Depths, and following his trail took the party straight to the 23rd level, where there was access to the Egg. Halaster's personal sanctum was in close proximity to the Egg; the party went through it at the end of that quest, in a rush, and Halaster was not there. <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-06-20)</small>
