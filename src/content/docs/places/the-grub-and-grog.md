**Inn and tavern · Sea Ward, [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Proprietor Ard Farkle · Allied with the Harpers · Standing**

**Also known as:** Ard Farkle's, Ard Farkle's Grub & Grog

The Grub + Grog is an inn, tavern and carriage house in the Sea Ward of [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), kept by the kenku Ard Farkle and allied with the Harpers, whose dark third floor hides a Harper safe house known to those in the know as the Crow's Nest. On the rainy night of [the Tiffany House Heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/) the party pulled into its carriage house with two captured drivers and took a room. The night ended in [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/): [Lenore](/people/lenore/) was ambushed and nearly killed between the carriage house and the inn door, the party attacked [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/) at a [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/) sales meeting in the inn's converted barn, and most of them were arrested by [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/).

## The inn

The inn takes up a small block in the Sea Ward, surrounded by unusually wide streets which give it a sense of separation from the rest of the city. It is a three-story stone-and-timber building with a detached carriage house and a converted barn now used for weddings and events. On a rainy night it is lit by lantern and firelight, and the light escapes its many doors and windows on every floor but the top one, which is dark. A weathervane with a behatted crow crests the roof.

It is a very old and popular inn in an older corner of the Sea Ward, "a local haunt where the more frugal denizens of this noble district have their nightly revels." <small>(DM notes)</small>

Its signature drink is **Moonstorm Punch**, sold under the line "GET A STIFF PUNCH IN THE SEA WARD". <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Ard Farkle

The proprietor, Ard Farkle, is a crow-man kenku. He works behind the bar himself and directs a big staff there. His magnanimous disposition makes him popular even as an obvious outsider to Waterdeep. <small>(Session narrative; DM notes.)</small> When Lenore was brought in wounded on the night of the incident, Ard tended to her. The house is allied with the Harpers. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The Crow's Nest

The dark top floor under the crow weathervane is a Harper hideaway, known secretly as the "Crow's Nest". Room 301 is the safe house, and the secret symbol on its door reads "SANCTUARY". <small>(DM notes)</small> The Harpers there include Tenderbottom, a spy, and Eggplant Toothpaste, an initiate. <small>(DM notes)</small>

On the night of the incident the Crow's Nest held Tenderbottom, an orc ranger maimed by [Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/)'s troops <small>(DM notes)</small> and dying. A halfling sat with the orc, the two of them talking about a tragedy in the farmlands. A lithe blonde elf ranger slipped in and sat with Tenderbottom in their final moments, and swore that the people would know what had happened in the farmlands.

## The 1st of Istus

On the 1st of Istus every drink bought at the bar comes with a token. Ard Farkle's greeting sets the rule:

> It is the 1st of Istus so every purchase of a drink gets a token. The rule is you must keep your token hidden and secret and only reveal it to another guest at the same time they reveal their own.

<small>(DM notes)</small> Three guests whose tokens match colours win the door prize, a **Gleaming Steel Arm Piece**: a magic armoured arm-piece worn as an accessory, worth about 2,500 gold, secretly charmed to restrain its wearer if they attempt to harm an innocent, together with a year of free drinks for all three. <small>(DM notes)</small> On the night of the incident [Maverick](/people/maverick/) introduced himself to Ard, got a white token with his drink, and heard that he could win the arm-piece, a magic accessory he wanted.

## The rooms

**Room 101** is an empty room holding noble's clothes, luggage, a dagger with dried blood on it and evidence that its guest left quickly. <small>(DM notes)</small> On the night of the incident [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/), [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Heyu](/people/heyu/) found a trail of blood leading from one of the inn's rooms, and inside a bloodied dagger and an open letter under the seal of the Arcaneum, the mysterious and powerful mages' guild. The letter said only: "WE KNOW".

**Room 102** holds an old silver-framed mirror: look into it, and your face changes as you imagine it. The room is haunted by a ghost. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Room 301** is the Crow's Nest.

## The night of the incident

The party arrived with mounted knights posted at the other three corners of the square and pulled into the carriage house, where a goblin stable hand in a wide hat waved them in zealously. Lenore paid him five gold, to his great joy. They took a room and questioned their two prisoners, the Grille brothers, there. That evening a smaller but much nicer carriage stood near the inn while an attendant unloaded cardboard boxes; it was Rex Bedsheets's, and the attendant was his footman. The Rexium sales meeting was held in the converted barn event room, where about ten people watched the footman's pitch before Rex came on stage. The fight that followed began in the barn and ran out into the streets; afterwards Heyu stood among the traumatised citizens in the barn and the carnage left by Shadowhigh's bullets. For the full account, see [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/).

## Places of Interest

**The Main Bar:** Ard Farkle's post, with a big staff, the token game and the Moonstorm Punch.\
**The Carriage House:** detached from the inn, with hay for the horses and a goblin stable hand in a wide hat.\
**The Converted Barn:** the event room, used for weddings and events, and for Rexium's sales meeting.\
**Room 101:** the empty room with the bloodied dagger and the Arcaneum letter, "WE KNOW".\
**Room 102:** the silver-framed mirror and the ghost.\
**The Crow's Nest:** Room 301, the Harper safe house on the dark top floor, marked "SANCTUARY".

**Patrons:** three young adventurers who have taken a job from ap Dyksterhaus, the Unmasked Lord, to protect a diplomat from Calimshan who fears arrest by Varkus's forces. <small>(DM notes)</small>
