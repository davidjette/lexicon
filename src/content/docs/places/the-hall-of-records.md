**Landmark · The Ministry of Records · North side of [Korth](/places/korth/), across [the Great Plaza](/places/the-great-plaza/) · Access restricted**

*Also known as:* the Ministry of Records · the Hall

The **Hall of Records** is the Ministry of Records of the Empire: a giant building directly north of [the Great Plaza](/places/the-great-plaza/) in [Korth](/places/korth/), and one of the oldest institutions in the oldest of the human capitals. It has been a **library**, a **university** and a **wizard's tower** at different points in a very long history, and the architecture still carries all three arguments at once.

It is **not** a general library. The Rekkenmark and others are far larger. Korth's Hall exists to hold the **most controlled material** in the Empire: sensitive government documents, secrets, licences, records of the Emperor's ancestors, subjects of great magical knowledge. A serious scholar comes here to prove why he *hasn't* seen a thing. Access is restricted, and it is nonetheless a public building serving several hierarchies at once.

## What is not in it

It has been **emptied out and backfilled with fluff**. The legal section — in the capital of a kingdom whose courts are its proudest export — is down to a handful of tomes, the most elementary descriptions of the Karrnathi constitution and the basics of [Karrnathi jurisprudence](/lore/karrnathi-law/).

More pointedly: **any record naming a specific dragon has been removed by order.**

## The real holdings

> "The secret of the Hall of Records is not so much the records themselves, but the people here."

Scholars work and loiter here at all hours, because it is where the writing is. It is nice to hear the Korthish accent in the stacks. On the **second level** they sit reading and gossiping, and it was there that the Inevitables overheard scholars discussing a black dragon sighted over the Korramont — and found [Master Armhair](/people/master-armhair/).

On the **third level** sits a non-binary dwarf clerk who forges **identity papers and weapon registrations** for a shakedown or a bribe, writing a description into a book, drawing a portrait onto the page with a wand, and stamping an official seal on it. This is where [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) became **Sky Rizzy** and where every weapon he carried was logged on Form 7-168 except KNELL. [Jelton Ahn](/people/jelton-ahn/) uses the same clerk to move his wife to Kabara. The clerk loathes her.

Somewhere in the stacks, shelved under a false index as a treatise on the **mating habits of the creatures of the Underdark**, sits the only complete family tree of Eberron's dragons — hidden in the Empire's own archive by the man the Empire consults about dragons.

## Beneath it

[Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) lived in a bunker deep beneath this building for nearly three years. He was an Imperial auditor before that, in the department that catches people falsifying records and stealing Imperial goods — the people [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) then prosecuted. He walked back into the Hall wearing a dead paralegal's face.

## One night in the Hall

Between eight in the evening and midnight, three people with stolen faces read the entire surviving legal code of Karrnath, learned the procedure of a capital trial well enough to run one at dawn, discovered that sabotage and espionage and embezzlement all carry death, met the Empire's own dragon-scholar and heard him name the Emperor a dragon, bought forged papers upstairs, and were handed the index number of a heresy.
