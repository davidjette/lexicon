**Shop · [Sub-line District](/places/sub-line-district/), [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) · Magic shop · Standing and expanding**

Hopper's Magic Emporium is a magic shop in the Sub-line District of Sharn's Lower City, selling home-security crystals and ritual incense over the counter. It sits among the small businesses tucked between clusters of residential towers around Station One Market, next door to **The Arcade**. Both buildings were secretly owned by **Doctor Edwin Graves**, and when Graves was killed at [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) the deeds passed to the people who killed him; Hopper has supplied the rebellion ever since.

## The deal

When the new deed holders told Hopper he had a new landlord and that the Arcade next door was available, Hopper was thrilled at the chance to expand and struck a major trade deal on the spot: supplies, magic items and bulk goods, intended to support the rebellion. The previous deed holder's lawyer, a large man named **Candy Crush**, remains a loose end.

## What Hopper has done since

Eric picked up incense for future rituals here the morning the Cog Collector was found. Three years on, Hopper cast **Summon Dragon** into a Ring of Spell Storing on the understanding that a true name summons that dragon for one hour and then frees it — a favour the Inevitables intend to spend on a black dragon under a mountain.
