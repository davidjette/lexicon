**District · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), southeast · Residential · Standing**

The **Lowline Ward** is the residential district of the southeast [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), rising above [Gear St. Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/), and it contains the highest residential population in the Lower City. The district is packed with clusters of high-rise apartment towers, along with a small business park and an urgent care center that serves the surrounding neighborhoods. It's quieter than the markets below, but no less crowded, with the steady hum of daily life filling its narrow walkways and stacked living spaces. It is where the people who work Gear Street and the factories of [the Cogs](/places/the-cogs/) sleep.

## The Cog Collector's dumping ground

For nearly four years, Cogborn were disappearing across the Lower City. Early victims' bodies were found dumped around Gear Street Market, the east side of the Aqueduct, and throughout the Low Line Ward, their bodies showing signs of experimentation and tampering. As the disappearances increased, fewer bodies were recovered, until the killings became pure vanishings with no remains left behind. The killer was [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), working out of [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) on the street below.

## Present day

The ward's towers are as full as they ever were. The bodies stopped appearing when Graves died.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The apartment tower clusters:** the densest residential blocks in the Lower City.
- **The business park:** a small commercial cluster serving the surrounding towers.
- **The urgent care center:** serves the surrounding neighborhoods.
