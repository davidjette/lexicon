**One-road mining town - foothills of the King's Forest, beneath the Korramont**

**Korranberg** is a one-road mining town in the foothills of the King's Forest beneath the Korramont, and a company town whose company shut down three years ago and never left. One road in, one road out, a dry fountain in the middle of it, and a freight yard at the end with a brazier burning and six idle guards standing round it. There is no inn. There is a boarding house, kept by a widow named **Marest**, who will answer questions at a fixed price in gold and stop the moment the questions get interesting.

## Chronicle

The town exists because of the mine and the [Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/) in the mountain above it. When the House of Kaius bought the [Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) about a decade ago it rebuilt the freight rail up to the works; when the foundry closed the rail was **purposefully removed rather than destroyed**, leaving a cut through the countryside that appears on old maps and on no current one. Marest's version is the official version, and the whole town says it the same way: *"took apart a long time ago when they closed the foundry and the mine."* There was an accident. She will not be tricked into more.

> Of course you understand there is no rail, and there never was a rail. - Austin, Korranberg Foundry Trust

Everyone in Korranberg is wary and nobody will speak openly about the incident at the foundry three years ago. Ask twice and the conversation ends. Beyond the wariness, the town's live complaints are ordinary ones: orc activity in the hills, night freight that was never on any schedule, and a teamster's-tale reputation among Fulcrum's sources for being the yard where the foundry man burns his own paperwork at the gate while you watch.

## Present day

The town has now had two impossible days in a row. The marshals spent an entire day taking the heads off bodies that keep climbing out of the dump behind the freight yard - the bell rang again, three hundred feet under the mountain, and this is what that looks like from below. And somebody saw a dragon on the Korramont.

The Trust clerk, who has met the Imperial lawyer Deirdre Moro twice now and has no idea she is [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), let the party sleep in the [Trust office](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/) for a five-gold tip. They took fifty pounds of the Trust's records out with them in a bag of holding, and teleported to Sharn before he came back in the morning.

## Places of Interest

- **The dry fountain** - the middle of the one road, and the town's only ornament.
- **The freight yard** - a brazier, six idle guards, and the gate where dockets are burned nightly per standing instruction. The dump behind it is currently producing bodies.
- **[The Trust office](/organizations/the-korranberg-foundry-trust/)** - crates of records, one clerk, and no records at all for the last three years.
- **Marest's boarding house** - the closest thing to lodging. Gold per question.
- **The old rail cut** - freight-gauge, rails lifted, running out of town and into the King's Forest.
- **The mine road** - forested switchbacks up the mountain to the [Mountain Door](/places/the-mountain-door/). Abandoned, and until recently held by orcs.

**Notable residents:** Marest, the widow at the boarding house. Austin, the Trust's clerk - first job, keen on his rating, and the reason the rebellion now has seven years of freight schedules. **Pal** and **Audrey**, local scavengers taken prisoner by the orcs in the mountain and freed by a woman in a fox mask; they owe her a meal in town, any time.
