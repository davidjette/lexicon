**Ward · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Gang territory · Standing**

The Hive Ward is a ward of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), a district of honeycomb tenements, corpse markets and gang turf, and the place where the [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) party woke in [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/) and fought their first battles. Its streets are worked by thugs in the pay of two rival gangs, [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/)'s thieves' guild on [the Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/) and [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/)'s extortionists, and through the party's time there it was afflicted by [Cageblight](/lore/cageblight/). The party escaped the Mortuary, ended the Venus in Furs affair in Ragpicker's Square, killed the smuggler Fieldy Smuts, fell into Sharegrave's service and fought for him at [the Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/), and in [the Spillway](/places/the-spillway/) put an end to both Rotten William and the plague.

## Heyu's way in

[Heyu](/people/heyu/) came to the ward on an errand for the spirit of [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/), who hired her to retrieve an object from his corpse at the Mortuary. The tout she hired to lead her there would not enter the Hive Ward, and she found her way alone. She saw a corpse collector leaving a drop chute with an empty cart, and was ambushed by Hive thugs working for Rotten William, led by Enya, who took her spellbook and threw her into the corpse chute. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Outside the Mortuary

The party escaped the Mortuary and were jumped by thugs in the street outside. Two of the thugs died, one escaped, and the party took **Ratbone** into custody. A corpse-picker came for the dead and, without speaking, let the party know it was [Pharod](/people/pharod/)'s. It was here that the party first learned about Cageblight.

Ratbone is Sharegrave's man, not Rotten William's. He shakes down berks and shops, hates Rotten William and Pharod, knows that Sharegrave lives on the Catwalk and the thieves' cant signal that gets a visitor invited there, and remembers Enya from around the Night Market. <small>(DM notes)</small> Heyu's spellbook was recovered.

## The Venus in Furs affair

Severin, a sensible-seeming man who speaks softly, frequents Ragpicker's Square looking for adventurers to help him satisfy his mistress, **Vanda**, whom he says is an incarnation of Leviathan. He convinced Vanda to treat him like a slave, worships her as a goddess, and drafted a detailed contract outlining his servitude, which she agreed to. Vanda has taken a more aggressive lover, **Alex Papadopolis**, who humiliates Severin. <small>(DM notes)</small> On meeting the party Severin tells them "you are perfect!" <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party took the errand, which led to Hive #11. Vanda contacts "the **LADY**" dressed in the Lady of Pain's garb, a blasphemy that brings the dabus. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> "Severin you pathetic little worm, who are these people?"
>
> "SILENCE! I shall prepare to contact, the **LADY**"
>
> — Vanda <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party resisted Vanda and killed her. The dabus mazed Severin, and the party captured Alex Papadopolis. They took Severin's apartment in the Hive as their camp.

## The first rest

From the camp the party heard that Rotten William was in the Spillway, and saw a cranium rat running north-west toward it. They killed **Fieldy Smuts**, a smuggler and burglar; Sharegrave hated that, and wants the cannonball back. Fieldy's shipment was a cannonball-sized metal sphere, "smooth, dense, always a little damp", on its way to Fat Mike, and Sharegrave suspects it is a portkey. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party also heard that Enya works for Rotten William and is something in the Night Market.

## The Spillway

The party invaded Rotten William's hideout in the Spillway. They saw evidence of a fight with Tabaxi, rescued [Prez](/people/prez/), a thief in Sharegrave's gang, and saw cranium rats. They made a deal with Rotten William to kill Sharegrave with a wand he said would paralyse him, and they saw Pharod with the handsome man, [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/).

## Sharegrave's service

The deal took the party to the Catwalk, where they were trapped. [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) was sent to fight at the Grindhouse, while [Barold](/people/barold-bonds/) and [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) headed for the Gatehouse. After the fight the party returned to the Hive, where they rested, restocked and were paid: 250 gold to Battlebeast and 100 to each of the others. Their outstanding errands were to bring the cannonball to Fat Mike, to double-cross Rotten William and kill him, to find a missing cleric in the Spillway, to find the cause of Cageblight, and to learn who killed them.

## The end of Rotten William and Cageblight

The party assaulted Rotten William's lair in the Spillway, killing everyone there except a man called "Whitey", who escaped. Rotten William, who had been planning a kidnapping and murder for hire, fled into the sewer. The party pursued him past a giant spider and the cranium rat colony called Many-as-One, and cornered him in the Sanitizing Room, where a portal to a plague dimension had opened in the drain and was poisoning the water system. Craig, the missing Bleaker intern, had tried to finish his master's mission and close it, and had angered a plague demon. The party killed the plague demon, Gorelach and Gunt, saved Craig, closed the portal and killed Rotten William. Closing the portal cured Cageblight, and the [Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/) became the party's faction ally.

## Cageblight

Cageblight was a pandemic afflicting the Hive Ward, falling hardest on the poor, the destitute and workers: grey skin, coughing, or madness. Remedy hawkers worked the streets, from sunlight vendors to sellers of snake oil and charms, alongside overworked clerics. <small>(DM notes)</small> Sharegrave dismissed it as a fake plague engineered by the Company to control the city. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The streets

Thugs are "ever present and dangerous" <small>(DM notes)</small>. In Ragpicker's Square, Rotten William's thugs harass the storyteller Reekwind: "go on and die so we can sell your corpse!" <small>(DM notes)</small>

> Small crowds of hunched, rag clad humanoids swarm carts of dead bodies and seeming refuse, fighting and bidding on the sad spoils. Thugs look on. RATS infest the corners
>
> — Ragpicker's Square <small>(DM notes)</small>

The square's vendors sell rations, torches, blood charms, simple weapons, ragged cloaks, rope and lockpicks; for more, scavenger's kits, alchemist's fire, antitoxin, a cursed cranium rat tail, a Dustman shroud, a fake Cageblight cure and an unknown portal key made of a fingerbone; and for 300, a ring of protection. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> A series of honey-comb-shaped tenement building just up from the ground, their compartment pores house countless people, most have covered the opening of their cell with cloth or planks. Wooden bridges span the distance between buildings on the upper floors, with small structures built precariously over an immense drop.
>
> — The Hive <small>(DM notes; sic)</small>

## The medusa toilet

The toilet in the Smoldering Corpse Bar, marked with a medusa, is a portal. It opens at the fountain in the courtyard of the [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) on Oerth, a fountain of "Pericles beheading Medusa". The ward's list of portkeys gives it as "Tattoo / SC Toilet → GREYHAWK". It was the party's first portal. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/):** the Dustmen's house of the dead, where the party woke.
- **Ragpicker's Square:** rag-clad crowds bid on carts of the dead; Pharod resides here, and Sharegrave works it.
- **The Smoldering Corpse Bar:** a bar whose medusa toilet is a portal to Saltmarsh; Mochai the debt collector works near it.
- **The Marketplace:** Rotten William's extortionists work in and around it.
- **The Night Market:** where Enya is something.
- **The Hive:** honeycomb tenements joined by wooden bridges over an immense drop; Severin's apartment there was the party's camp.
- **The Southwestern Hive:** Fieldy Smuts's ground.
- **[The Catwalk](/places/the-catwalk/):** Sharegrave's headquarters, strung between two Hive buildings.
- **[The Grindhouse](/places/the-grindhouse/):** a no-holds-barred fighting arena with a prize fight every tenday.

**People of the Ward:**

- **[Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/):** leader of the thieves' guild on the Catwalk. "Bring me something, anything, to give me an edge over that lying cheat..." <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **[Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/):** leader of a group of extortionists in and around the Marketplace. "Pay up or I'll make an example of you right here..." <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **[Pharod](/people/pharod/):** collector and scavenger of the dead, living in Ragpicker's Square.
- **[Prez](/people/prez/):** a thief in Sharegrave's gang, rescued from the Spillway.
- **Ratbone:** Sharegrave's thug, taken into custody outside the Mortuary. "Get lost Berk! This is Sharegrave's turf!" <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **Enya:** Rotten William's thug near the Mortuary, who took Heyu's spellbook. "What do you want Berk? asking for trouble? Hand over your junk." <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **Fieldy Smuts:** smuggler and burglar of the Southwestern Hive, killed by the party. "This package needs to get across town, no questions asked" <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **Mochai:** a debt collector for Sharegrave near the Smoldering Corpse, who will hire muscle.
- **Reekwind:** a vagabond storyteller stricken with Cageblight and harassed by thugs. "Story for a coin, sir?" <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **Mourns-for-Trees:** "I fear for the last trees in this place." "Cageblight affects all living things" <small>(DM notes)</small>
- **Severin:** Vanda's devotee, who recruits adventurers in Ragpicker's Square; mazed by the dabus.
- **Vanda:** Severin's mistress, killed by the party.
- **Alex Papadopolis:** Vanda's aggressive lover, who humiliates Severin; captured by the party.
- **Fat Mike:** an efreeti collector who does not haggle, the destination of Fieldy's cannonball, for which the price is 1,200 gold.
- **Fell:** a tattoo artist, who told the party of their marks, "It's not one of mine. It's a **Fatemark**." A [fatemark](/lore/fatemarks/) is a tattoo found on people whom Istus has chosen to fulfill a duty, a destiny tied to a constellation.
