**Place · Lizardfolk stronghold · Oerth · Ruled by Queen Okathent**

**Dunwater** is a lizardfolk stronghold on Oerth, ruled by **Queen Okathent**, and the setting of "Danger at Dunwater", the third adventure of *Ghosts of Saltmarsh*. In October 2021 the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), after a sahuagin attack at sea, entered Dunwater without violence, led by their kobold [Scrunt](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/), and reached the queen. In the next session the party stole "the bosses things" and Irhtos jailed [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/). The party returned to Dunwater in June 2022 to pick up the book quests. Centuries later [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) became the Dunwater Horror.

## The book adventure

Dave's list of the *Ghosts of Saltmarsh* adventures gives "Danger at Dunwater - four to six 3rd level". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-11)</small>

## Entering Dunwater

The party's sea elf ally [Oshi](/people/oshi/) was built for the place. Dave: "I mean he’s built to be the guide for dunwater basically". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

In the session of 2021-10-09 sahuagin almost killed Scrunt at sea; Nico: "Scrunt got us through it without violence, which is what I’m sure he wanted". Dave, after the session: "you’ve seen just the path from the north entrance to where the queen was" / "So we can explore all of dunwater next time and see if we can earn the trust of Queen Okathent". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

Dave on the adventure's design: "this was literally the first adventure in D&D where killing the monsters leads to failure" / "Like there really are so many lizards that if you attack you either die or run away" / "And you fail the mission". Nico: "We have a common enemy with them and they are not hostile to salt marsh". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>

## The Scrunt Episode

Dave named the session of 2021-10-15 “The Scrunt Episode”, in which Dolores "got to have dinner and get arrested". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-16)</small> Nico: "we just stole the bosses things and couldn't lie our way out of it". Dave: "Gabrielle successfully intimidated them but you didn’t successfully deceive them" / "So irhtos just threw you in jail to deal with later". Nico: "we still want them on "our side" or at least not hostile with Saltmarsh if possible"; Dave: "We def will get rewarded more if they are friend and not indifferent and we fail if they are foe". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-17)</small>

## Return in 2022

Dave, before the session of 2022-06-29: "We’re headed to Dunwater to pick up the book quests". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-29)</small>

## The Dunwater Horror

In the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), Gabriella Hellwood, Dave's character in Neptune's Rejects, is the Dunwater Horror, also called the Horror of Dunwater and Ladybeard. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-oral-histories-cast.md)</small> At [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) the Dunwater Horror clashed with the demon lord Dagon at a portal on the sea floor off Saltmarsh. <small>(DM notes)</small> Afterwards Dave wrote: "Now I guess there’s a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> And later: "Hellwood’s fate as the Dunwater Horror, tricked by Jeremiah into taking the blame for the Temple of Neptune". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small> See [the Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/).
