**Hotel · [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/), [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) · Hospitality hub · Standing**

The Grand Sharn Hotel is a major hospitality hub in the GoldSky Neighborhood of Sharn's Upper City, connected directly to the **Sharn Theater**. It attracts a constant flow of travellers, tourists, theatre-goers and long-term guests, which makes it one of the busiest civilian towers in the Upper City. One of Sharn's four permanent **ArcEye** cameras sits on its rooftop, and is one of the few ArcEye nodes that rotates.

## The rotating eye

On its rooftop sits one of Sharn's four permanent **ArcEye** cameras, and it is one of the few ArcEye nodes that rotates, cycling slowly between two panoramic views.

**Facing left:** the roof of the Sharn Theater in the foreground, the GoldSky Neighborhood beyond it — Sharn's wealthiest district, its luxury mansions, high-end apartments and elevated shopping terraces — and [the Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/) and [the Black Dove Hospital](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/) on the horizon.

**Facing right:** the theatre roof again, the oldest Upper City district beyond it, and glimpses of [the Veil Tower](/places/the-veil-building/) and the Skyport.

## As a target

For anyone intending to seize the Empire's broadcast, the Grand Sharn is moderately dangerous — not because of the Iron Veil, whose presence here is low, but because of civilian traffic. Guests, tourists and theatre patrons move through it at all hours and odd behaviour stands out fast. The rooftop is accessible to hotel guests only and is monitored for guest safety. The obvious approach is also the most comfortable one: rent a room on a high floor close enough to the roof, and blend in as exactly what everyone else in the lobby is.
