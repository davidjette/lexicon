**Temple · Leef · Abandoned; a Black Dove listening station · Desecrated**

**The temple of Dol Arrah** is a small temple of the sun goddess beside the lightning rail station at [Leef](/places/leef/), with a simple village around it. By order of the Emperor it was abandoned by its worshippers, and it became a refuge for indigents and wayward folk; beneath it [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) kept a listening station, through which they knew of anything that came in or out of the town. Colonel [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/) sent the party to break it, and in its basement they found [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) in a cage. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## The listening station

The Colonel's job was simple: break in, disable the station in the basement, and bring him its input switch, for 300 gold pieces. On the way the party met an old Tabaxi woman begging for alms. She was Zero, the Silver Flame's agent in Leef, and she knew Dario for one of the Flame. She told him the treasure was in the temple, "though it may not be the treasure you seek", and called the Colonel a well-intentioned fool. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Both of the temple's doors were locked and trapped, with homeless people watching the front. The upper temple was empty. In the basement were skeletons, two Black Dove priests and, locked in a cage, Eric the Cleric, an auditor of the Imperial Revue Service who had been arrested on his arrival in the Grand Duchy of Newham and brought to the Doves to test his story; they had imprisoned and tortured him, and he had lost his mind to a *feeblemind* spell. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> Dario spared one of the Doves there, Luz Fayana. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small>

A surviving Dove offered the party an alliance. The Black Doves, she said, were not their enemy: Her August Majesty the Empress Uriel also sought to oppose Grand Duke LeBeefe and rid the Empire of his corruption, and the man in the cage, who bore the Imperial mark of audit, was the key to LeBeefe's fortress. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> The raid was part of [Leef Ep 4](/sessions/leef-ep-4-the-train-station/). Eric joined the party from here, and in his first session as a player, [Leef Ep 5](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/), they learned LeBeefe was building a mind-flayer army. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## Afterwards

In the year after the fall of Newham, Zero, now high priestess of the Flame, came to Dario with a question: the Black Doves had desecrated the holy place on the hill, and a decision had to be made about what was to be done with it. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>
