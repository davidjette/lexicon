**Prison complex · Shadowspace · *The Starsong Awakens* · Active, at maximum capacity**

*Also known as:* the Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison

The **Capt. Katrina McQuaid Memorial Prison & Humane Detention Center** is a massive prison complex in Shadowspace, named for [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and listed in the Company dossier of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)* as active and at maximum capacity. Its inmates include [Large Luigi](/people/large-luigi/), prisoner LL9807, whom the dossier calls "the traitor of the Battle of Tannhauser Gate". He has awaited trial there for fifteen years and serves [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) as an asset and criminal informant. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

## History

The prison appears in the Company dossier that [Halle Barrias](/people/halle-barrias/), sister of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/), gives the crew at the start of *The Starsong Awakens*, fifteen years after *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-11)</small> The prison bears the name of Captain Katrina McQuaid of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), who served in *Light of Xaryxis*. When it was built, and by whom, is unknown.

## Large Luigi

The dossier's entry on the prison describes its best-known inmate:

> This massive prison complex currently houses prisoner LL9807, Large Luigi, the traitor of the Battle of Tannhauser Gate. As an enemy combatant, Luigi has no protection under the Negroni Accords and has been awaiting trial for the crimes of high treason and espionage for 15 years. Luigi is currently a Company asset and criminal informant.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier</small>

In JL's account of where the old crew of the SSJ Zanzibar are fifteen years on, Luigi is known only as prisoner LL849 and has spent those years in a high-security OSR3G facility, awaiting trial for treason and crimes against [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), which officially disavows all knowledge of his whereabouts. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
