**Island · Dwarven silver mine · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/)**

**Ratcatcher** is a small island off the coast of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) with a dwarven silver-mining outpost, the mining operation that made the town rich, run by the dwarven magnate Manistrad Copperlocks. In the campaign Ghosts of Twatmarsh the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) sailed there when the dwarves found something strange in the mine and someone said the word "duergar". <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: 1st session with Scrunt, Dolores and Gabriella)</small> JL ran the dwarf-clan session on 2021-10-01. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01)</small>

## Three commissions

Each of Saltmarsh's factions sent the party with a different aim. [Anders Solmor](/people/anders-solmor/) wanted the dwarves helped. [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/) wanted the duergar found and brought round to working with the pirates against the mine. [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) wanted them to use the chaos to kill Copperlocks's second in command, the foreman Hawaiian Coffee, who had been pushing Sergeant Eliander Fireborn to investigate the recent murders. All three warned them to watch out for the gorillas. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: 1st session with Scrunt, Dolores and Gabriella)</small>

## The mine

Sahuagin attacked on the way, and apes on the beach. At the mine the guards told the party there had been an incident: Hawaiian Coffee had disappeared, and Copperlocks was on her way to supervise a formal investigation. The deputy foremen, Billy and Butterface, took orders from Copperlocks alone and told them to leave. Snooping turned up a memo from Butterface to Coffee: "EVERYTHING GOING ACCORDING TO PLAN. U.D. PENETRATION EXPECTED WITHIN THE WEEK. EXPECT ACQUISITION OF ITEM WITHIN 30 DAYS." Beyond a cordon lay a hole a hundred feet deep, with signs of a struggle at its edge. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: 1st session with Scrunt, Dolores and Gabriella)</small> The party found Hawaiian Coffee dead. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## The duergar

In the dark below, the duergar, hostile to any outsider, lived in a few small huts under a xarrorn chief. The xarrorn claimed that the outland dwarves were liars, after duergar necromancy rather than silver; the claim is unconfirmed. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: 1st session with Scrunt, Dolores and Gabriella; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
