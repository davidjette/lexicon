**Town · eastern Khorvaire · Refounded as the Republic of Talenta · Standing**

*Also known as:* Leaf

Leef is a town in eastern Khorvaire, the last eastern stop on the lightning rail, refounded after the collapse of Newham as the **Republic of Talenta**. It was a dusty desert outpost until Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) expanded his empire eastward, constructing an airship yard and flooding the town with illithid city guards and Iron Veil soldiers. It was liberated by the three adventurers who met there, and became a fortified rebel stronghold beyond the reach of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s imperial forces.

## The liberation

It was in Leef that [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) met for the first time. Together the trio liberated the town with the help of local defenders: **Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke**, a hardened veteran of the Last War; **Zero of Leef**, an elderly tabaxi follower of the Silver Flame; and **Princess Orange Chicken**, a young dwarven leader from the swamp tribes of [the Mror Holds](/places/the-mror-holds/). The victory earned the three of them the title **The Heroes of Leef**.

Gemma and Dario came in by train, and by Dave's account were "immediately roped into a terrorist act before meeting our allies": they attacked the train station, blew up shipments and killed guards. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24; 2025-08-22; 2025-09-27; 2025-12-21)</small> The party later hallucinated in a swamp, <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small> and the fighting came to a head in the Battle of Leef. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06 to 2025-09-12)</small> The full account is at [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), the Leef half of the [Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/).

The town takes its name from its station, formally the John C. LeBeefe Imperial Aerodome and Lightning Rail Hub, most of whose letters have fallen off the sign. Before the rising Leef was the hub, an outskirts of a bar, a junkyard and a few shacks, and a small temple of Dol Arrah beside the rail line with a village around it. The hub was run by Comptroller [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/), the Imperial officer set over the town, who commanded a cohort of 480 soldiers and lived in the hub's main building, though a team of Inquisition agents held the real power. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The trio's first ally was found at the **Cat & Biscuits**, a tavern of old drunks in dusty clothes kept by a halfling barkeep with a freedwoman's tattoo on her neck. Its talk ran to the Last War, the price of taxes and whether the Newham Falcons, the sportsball team, had a chance in the new season, and every few sentences someone said "gods-damned LeBeefe, curse his soul". There Colonel [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/) took the party's side against Imperial guards and told them, "Welcome to the rebellion." <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

It was Rourke who then sent them west, to confront the architect of Newham's oppression in his own city.

## The Republic

When [Newham](/places/newham/) collapsed and the region around it became [New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/), the survivors fled east and evacuated en masse to Leef. There the townsfolk, the Newham refugees and the swamp-dwarf tribes forged a new nation: **the Republic of Talenta** <small>("formally known as Leef", per the rebel-factions page)</small>, positioned between the corrupted wasteland to the west and the allied Mror Holds to the east. It became a fortified rebel stronghold and one of the few places in Khorvaire largely beyond the reach of Esther Crona's imperial forces.

By Dave's account the nation is "Talenta" or the "talenta republic", while "the town can stay Leef but long term we'll probably rename it after whichever one of us dies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

## The year after Newham

In the year after the fall of Newham the rail stop became a capital. [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) was elected comptroller, the only name on the ballot, and the old lightning rail depot became the meeting hall where the township's business is debated and its rulings made; see [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/). <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The trio's base, **the Falcons' Workshop**, grew from a smithy and a storehouse into a small bastion: Dario added a barracks for up to twelve fighters, Gemma an armory, and Eric a humble fane to Matt Matheny. Its hands were Reggie, a tiefling, at the storehouse; Moo Goo, a dwarf, and Fill, a human, at the smithy; Merry, an orc, at the barracks; Pina Colada, a dwarf, at the sanctuary; and at the armory Wizmo, the autognome that Jane Corso built, who spent the year trying to make the Falcon, the party's small airship, fly again. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Refugees from Newham made new lives in the town. Bunzo the Magician set up shop with his cat; the vampires of [Club Mawniq](/places/club-mawniq/) opened a 24-hour massage parlour; Deuce Loosely's rogues started a thriving smuggling ring; and six orcs who had fought in the assault on LeBeefe's fortress stayed on. Dwarves from the east, halflings from the plains and weary adventurers passed through, most with no money, some with a little to invest, all with rumours. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Gemma spent a month with her old admirer Benjen turning the arid outskirts into farmland, and the town gained a silo. The country around Leef is prairie-dog, antelope and bison land, with the occasional dinosaur, but that year strange, unusually aggressive beasts with psionic powers began to come out of [the New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/) onto the outskirts. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Present day

Leef soldiers were among the ships that filled the sky above Sharn on the night the Iron Veil's pact broke, and Leef sent a delegation to the first meeting of the Council of Sharn seeking alliance and stability. When the Chancellor of Sharn travelled to the Peace Summit at [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/), he went as a representative of Sharn and of Leef both.

**Places of Interest:**

- **The airship yard:** built under LeBeefe's occupation.
- **The lightning rail terminus:** the last eastern stop on the line, formally the John C. LeBeefe Imperial Aerodome and Lightning Rail Hub.
- **The Cat & Biscuits:** the tavern where the Colonel drank.
- **The Corso junkyard:** Jane Corso's yard on the outskirts.
- **[The temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/):** the abandoned temple on the hill, once a Black Dove listening station.
- **The Falcons' Workshop:** the trio's workshop, storehouse, smithy, barracks, armory and sanctuary.
- **The meeting hall:** the old lightning rail depot, seat of the township's government.
