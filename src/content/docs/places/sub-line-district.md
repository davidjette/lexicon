**District · [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), northeast · Market and residential quarter · Standing**

The **Sub-line District** is the market and residential quarter of the northeast [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/), built atop the ruins of **Lightning Rail Sub-Transit Station 1** and a bustling hub of everyday life. The old concourses and platforms have been transformed into **Station One Market**, a sprawling food court and marketplace filled with vendors, steam and neon signage.

## The bones of the old system

The district still carries the bones of the old rail system, wide walkways, repurposed conduits, and faded Orien sigils beneath layers of graffiti. Nine years after the Sub-Transit was decommissioned, its infrastructure is simply the floor people shop on.

It is also still a functioning piece of machinery, if you know where the doors are. Beneath [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/) a hidden tunnel runs down into the abandoned **Sub-Transit control room**, which for years was the headquarters of **R.U.S.T.** — Resistance Under Sharn's Tyranny — and of Preacher, the last Warforged left in the city.

## Above ground

Surrounding the market are small businesses like **Kross Roads Corner Store**, [Hopper's Magic Emporium](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/), and **The Arcade**, all tucked between clusters of residential towers. The Arcade turned out, along with a great deal else in the Lower City, to have been quietly owned by Doctor [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/).

Kross Roads is run by [Karin Kross](/people/karin-kross/). Across the street from it stands a long-boarded apartment whose previous tenants, Tony and Tidy Danza, had been making wine on-site and had prepaid a six-year lease with the owner E.G.; they were still in residence as ghosts when the unit was reopened. Behind the fireplace is a hidden movable panel that leads into a sewer tunnel connected to [the Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/). The apartment building, it later emerged, belonged to Edwin Graves too.

## Present day

The tunnels beneath the Ziggurat and Sub-Line District were held by the **Drain Snakes**, who had fortified the northern sewer network and blocked all safe movement, until the gang was destroyed and the Cogborn evacuation route opened. Station One Market kept trading throughout.

**Places of Interest:**

- **Station One Market:** a food court and marketplace built into a dead rail station.
- **[Hopper's Magic Emporium](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/):** the district's magic shop.
- **The Arcade:** Edwin Graves's automated arcade, next door to Hopper's.
- **Kross Roads Corner Store:** Karin Kross's shop; household goods, and a family the Empire kept asking questions about.
- **The boarded apartment:** across from Kross Roads; a wine cellar, two ghosts, and a panel behind the fireplace.
- **The Sub-Transit control room:** R.U.S.T.'s headquarters, reached through a pawn shop.
