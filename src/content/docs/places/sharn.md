**City · Breland · Vertical metropolis · Formerly Imperial, now self-governing · Standing**

*Also known as:* the City of Towers <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>

Sharn is a city built upward. Where other capitals sprawl, Sharn stacks: towers on towers, bridges strung between them, whole neighborhoods hanging in the air above older neighborhoods that were themselves built on the roofs of neighborhoods older still. Nothing in it was designed. Everything in it was added. The result is a metropolis with no ground floor, only a bottom, and beneath the bottom somebody else's far older city.

## What lies underneath

Before there were towers there were tunnels. The rock under Sharn is threaded with ancient **Dhakaani** workings, and beneath those, older and fouler things. The [Temple of the Closed Circle](/places/temple-of-the-closed-circle/) was raised and maintained by priests who were slaves to the mind flayer elder brains that once ruled Sharn and Eberron entire; it holds an incomplete portal to Xoriat, the Far Realm, and it is reached today through a wardrobe in a flower shop.

Deeper still lies the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/), where [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and E'noch Qualanthri performed the Eternal Guardian ritual on forbidden Vol rites. The city spent its whole recorded history sitting on top of these places without knowing it, and the Empire went digging for one of them with a massive drill.

## The vertical city

Sharn divides cleanly into two halves that barely speak to one another.

The [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) is the pinnacle of the city's political, economic and cultural power: the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/), the corporate towers, the principal **Skyport**, and the [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/) where the officials and dignitaries live. Its skyline is immaculate because keeping it immaculate is policy.

The [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) sits beneath Sharn's upper towers, built from older infrastructure, stacked neighborhoods, and a network of streets that have been rebuilt and repurposed many times over. It's crowded, noisy, and practical — a place where people work, live, and move through tight spaces lit by lanterns, neon signs, and the glow of machinery. Old rail stations have become marketplaces, factory zones have turned into dense housing, and former commercial hubs now serve as gathering points for food, trade, and nightlife. [The Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) cuts across the district, dividing the northern neighborhoods from the southern ones.

Beneath the Lower City is the undercity: sewer lines, drain tunnels, abandoned sub-transit routes, and [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), a whole subterranean settlement with its own queen, its own tavern and its own law.

## Sharn's Modern Transportation Network

Sharn's transportation network is anchored by three primary systems: long-range airships that dock at elevated Skyports throughout both the upper and lower city, efficient Airbuses that shuttle citizens between towers and levels, and compact Zephyr Pods that navigate streets and bridges at ground level.

To ensure safety within the city's dense vertical districts, all private flight, whether by spell or vehicle, is strictly prohibited within restricted airspace, with exceptions granted only to military forces and a select class of authorized elite. Outside the designated flight corridors surrounding the Skyports, unauthorized aerial travel is subject to fines, impoundment, and potential arrest.

**Skyport:** Sharn's Skyports serve as the city's primary hubs for long-range airship travel, perched on reinforced platforms throughout both the upper and lower districts. These zones are among the few areas in the city where private flight is legally permitted, providing safe approach corridors for incoming and outgoing vessels. Each Skyport maintains extensive docking facilities, cargo platforms, and long-term mooring spaces for private airships, allowing captains to secure their vessels for days or even months at a time. Outside these designated Skyport zones, all aerial travel remains strictly restricted under city law. The Upper City's primary Skyport is a central hub for private and military airship travel; the colossal statue of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) stood above it until Sharn Ep 18.

**Airbus:** Airbuses function as large, open-sided levitating platforms powered by bound air elementals, gliding smoothly along fixed sky lanes between towers and districts. They are the most common mid-tier public transit option. Fast, reliable, and capable of carrying dozens of passengers at once. A single ride typically **costs 2–5 silver**, depending on distance and district, making them accessible to most working citizens. Riders can also purchase Airbus Passes at the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/) or any destination terminal, including a **monthly unlimited-ride pass for 10 gold**, favored by commuters and tower-hoppers who rely on the system daily. Frequent skybuses travel up, down, and diagonally between the [Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/) and nearby districts.

**Zephyr Pod:** Zephyr Pods are compact, single-rider ground vehicles powered by contained air-elemental turbines, giving them a smooth, whisper-light glide across Sharn's bridges and lower-level streets. Designed for quick personal travel, easy to unlock, easy to park, and perfect for short-range hops between districts. Pods are magically speed-governed to a maximum travel speed of **60 feet per round**, ensuring safety in crowded urban lanes. **Rentals typically cost 2 silver per hour**, with **day passes available for 2 gold** for frequent errand-runners and couriers.

**The Lightning Rail Hub Beyond the Gates:** Directly outside Sharn's city gates lies the **Crownsgate Lightning Rail Terminal**, a sprawling transit hub that serves as the primary point of arrival and departure for long-distance rail travel across Kornath <small>(sic, on the gazetteer)</small>. The hub is patrolled by Imperial Iron Veil soldiers.

## The Lost Lightning Rail Subway

Nine years ago, Sharn dismantled its once-famous Lightning Rail Sub-Transit, a network of miniature rail lines that snaked through the city's levels and connected dozens of towers. The system was a marvel of Orien-industrial engineering, but a series of catastrophic events led to decommissioning.

In its place, Sharn adopted the current dual-system of Airbuses and Zephyr Pods, which proved more flexible, safer within the vertical maze of the city, and far easier to reroute as districts expanded or shifted. Remnants of the old sub-rail tunnels still exist throughout the city.

So does **Sub-Transit Station 1**, buried under the [Sub-line District](/places/sub-line-district/) and used for years as the control room of a resistance cell, and **Station 5** beneath [Skyroot Park](/places/skyroot-park/), rebuilt by survivors of the collapse into the Nest. So, occasionally, is Orien hardware nobody bothered to collect, including the **EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node**, which turned out to be the one device in the city capable of seizing the Empire's own eyes.

## The eyes

The [Arcane Eye Security Network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/), commonly referred to as **ArcEyes**, was implemented nearly a decade ago as part of the Empire's expanded internal surveillance initiative. In Sharn, ArcEyes are visible throughout major districts, drifting along streets, alleys, and transit routes in continuous patrol cycles. Their presence is considered a standard component of urban security, though their true operational capacity remains classified. They are cast, maintained and remotely operated by low-level government wizards inside the Veil Building, trained specifically in Arcane Eye protocols: extended-duration scrying, coordinated patrol routing, multi-eye monitoring.

Every ArcEye archives what it sees and hears. Footage is recorded on an **ArcEye Crystallizer** and shipped to the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), where it is catalogued in restricted archives open only to Iron Veil personnel and authorised Imperial officials. The same network carries the city's public information feed, pushing state-approved images, alerts and directives to display screens across Sharn in real time. Assume all major streets, public squares and transit hubs are watched.

There are exactly **four permanent ArcEye cameras**: the Veil Building garden floor, the [Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/), the roof of the [Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/), and a factory roof in [The Cogs](/places/the-cogs/) overlooking [the Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/).

## Under the Iron Veil

When [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) assumed command of the Empire's military, the Sharn City Watch was dissolved and absorbed into her elite force: [The Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/). What was once a patchwork of local law enforcement became a unified, militarized presence; disciplined, brutal, and loyal only to Esther. Their black armor and crimson insignia dominated the city's skyline and streets. Patrols moved in tight formations, propaganda screens echoed their creed, and citizens knew better than to run or resist. The Iron Veil made a pact; they didn't just enforce law, they enforced loyalty.

Their headquarters was the Veil Building, a brutalist arcane-tech monolith holding government offices, Arcane Eye Security and a high-security prison for political dissidents. At its summit was Esther's penthouse, from which she watched the city and decided who lived, who died, and who disappeared.

## The rising

Over roughly two years a rebel network — the Nest, Fulcrum, R.U.S.T., the Silver Flame, the Underhive, the Hundreds — worked the city from beneath while three operatives worked it from inside. The Cogborn were evacuated through the Underhive to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/). The Cog Collector was killed in [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/). The trafficking network beneath [the Vault Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/) was destroyed.

It ended on a balcony at the top of the Veil Building. A recovered memory of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born was broadcast across every screen in Sharn through the EBT-7; Esther Crona was killed; and with the last of her strength she voided the Iron Veil's pact and hurled her own airship, the **Black Veil**, into the colossal statue of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) above the Skyport. Pact longswords vanished from soldiers' hands across the whole city in the same instant. By morning Sharn was free, leaderless, wounded and very close to collapse.

## The Council of Sharn

A provisional government formed within a day. Leef, NEST, the Silver Flame, R.U.S.T., the Hundreds and the Underhive each sent representatives to the penthouse, and the meeting became the first sitting of the **Council of Sharn**. Trials began immediately and several of the Empire's officials in the city were executed. The Silver Flame's citywide cremation directive was enacted, and when they came for Esther Crona's body it was already gone from the morgue; witnesses described two elven women carrying a corpse out during the chaos.

[Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) was appointed **Chancellor of Sharn** and took residence in Esther's former penthouse, which is now the seat of the administration. Warforged offered troops and engineers to restore the Cogs. Underhive drones joined a new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy.

## Right now

Three years on, the free city is hungry. The destruction of [Zilspar Farm](/places/zilspar/) left Sharn a month from severe food shortages, and it was already out of fuel; Nest engineers now bleed the **Eye of Corruption**'s rising output off as furnace fire, and Renn Tal has declared the Eye the property of Sharn. Three warforged under Himmie Joffa offered to feed a city they calculated was forty-seven days from famine; the price was military and logistical access to Sharn's airspace for the airships of the Hundreds. The Chancellor granted it, knowing it was a trick, and hopes only to survive Korth in time to take it back. A capital ship now settles out of the fog to hand out fruit in orderly lines — Locke Pierce has taken the skies of Sharn.

**Places of Interest:**

- **[The Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/):** Iron Veil headquarters, government offices, political prison, and the penthouse that is now the Chancellor's seat.
- **[GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/):** the wealthiest district in the city, residence of officials and dignitaries.
- **[Skyroot Park](/places/skyroot-park/):** a rare sweep of greenery suspended high above the metropolis, and the door to the Nest.
- **[The Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/):** compact, invaluable, and the restricted archive of every ArcEye recording ever made.
- **[Sanctum of the Last Mercy](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/):** the Black Dove hospital, seized by the Silver Flame on the night the city turned.
- **[The Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/):** the stepped commercial complex at the heart of the Lower City, markets above and a fighting pit below.
- **[Gear Street Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/):** the Lower City's busiest commercial artery.
- **[The Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/):** a recessed concrete waterway that is also a district boundary and a shadowed back road.
- **[The Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/):** a sovereign settlement beneath the sewers, ruled by [Blair](/people/blair/).
- **[The Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/):** Dhakaani depths, a puzzle door, and the place the Eternal Guardian ritual was made.
- **[Temple of the Closed Circle](/places/temple-of-the-closed-circle/):** an incomplete portal to the Far Realm, behind a wardrobe, behind a flower shop.
- **The Nest:** Jessica Grimpledink's fortified Upper City command centre, entered by solving a puzzle in Skyroot Park.
- **The Bazaar:** the Upper City's commercial complex, home to [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s *Eat My Blaze Too*.
- **Sharn Theater:** the city's great house, attached to the [Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/).
- **The Conduit Exchange:** the Upper City shop that held the EBT-7 until the Nest sent someone to collect it.
