**Elven city · The forest of Cormanthor · Seat of the elves of Cormanthyr**

*Also known as:* Cormanthyr, the realm · Cormanthor, the forest

**Myth Drannor** is an elven city behind a mythal in the forest of **Cormanthor**, the seat of the old elven realm of **Cormanthyr**. In Antiquity its queen, Alen Tudyx, the "Lode Star of Myth Drannor", married Peter Glans and was murdered by her brother [Valen](/people/valen/), who had betrayed the Cormanthyrians to the men of the Dale; the city withdrew into mist to fight the encroaching humans. Twenty years before the Age of the Infanta, Lord Krapsmere of Cormyr killed its Witch Queen in the Dales War. In that age the party carried the Infanta of Nature there from Suzail and left him in its keeping, its bladesingers and archers fought the Moradinate at the River Lis, and its elves went down into the Egg with the allied host. Five centuries later Princess Zythara fled there, rekindled its mythal and rose as the Witch Queen of Myth Drannor.

## Names

**Cormanthor** is the forest and **Cormanthyr** the old elven realm; both names are in use. Its people are the Cormanthyrians, also written Cormanthyreans.

## Antiquity

In the Time of Troubles the young druids of Myth Drannor told the party the history of "their absent King by marriage, a human named Peter Glans, their Queen ALEN TUDIX, now dead, and her brother VALEN." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII)</small> Alen Tudyx, "Lode Star of Myth Drannor", was queen of the Cormanthyrean elves. She married Peter Glans, who is the copper dragon [Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/), mainly to care for her adopted child, the Infanta of the God of Puzzles. She died mysteriously; her brother Valen murdered her, wanting her Baneblade, Copperbane. Glans "ran off with Copperbane after she mysteriously died", and Valen "also absconded with many of their clans magic objects." <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-05-27)</small> See [The Bane Blades](/items/the-bane-blades/) and [Holden Tudyx](/people/holden-tudyx/).

Valen "betrayed the Cormanthryians against the men of the Dale and in exchange was granted the land to build the Arcaneum". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2020-07-26)</small> See [Punis](/places/punis/) and [Settlers of the Dale](/organizations/settlers-of-the-dale/).

> Myth Drannor is now cloaked in the mists and fighting the encroaching human presence.

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-05-27</small>

At Fort Vengeance the party parted with "the Sun Elves from Myth Drannor". [Calix](/people/calix/) and Eldamir asked to be told when the party found Myth Drannor. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> The session of 2021-08-21 included a scene at Myth Drannor; Dave posted "the scene at myth drannor" the next day. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-08-22)</small>

## The Dales War

In the Dales War, twenty years before the Age of the Infanta, Lord Krapsmere of [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) slew the Witch Queen of Cormanthor, "an act that the elves will no doubt seek to avenge." <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small>

## The Age of the Infanta

Cormyr treated the elves as a mortal threat. In Suzail at Candlenights, the Citadel of the Purple Dragons held plans for a frontal assault on Myth Drannor, and the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) reported that "The elves of Myth Drannor in the forest kingdom of Cormanthor are believed to have found and secured the Infanta of Sylvanus, God of Nature." <small>(Arc III Part VII)</small> The [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) held that Infanta at Suzail, and planned "to release the Sylvanus infanta to allies of the Cormanthyrian elves to provide a common threat and keep Cormyr independent. When the Sylvanus infanta self-destructs, we can overrun Cormanthyr and take the center." <small>(Arc III, VIII Handouts)</small>

After [the Suzail Coup](/history/the-suzail-coup/) the party carried the boy, [Teo](/people/teo-infanta-of-nature/), out of Suzail: "you got the nature Infanta to Cormanthor and won the day". They were left to "come to terms with the elves of Myth Drannor." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-27)</small> Dave: "Cormanthyr is a full power on its own right and could field a full army." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-06)</small> [Holden Tudyx](/people/holden-tudyx/) kept a sanctuary in Cormanthyr, which his Word of Recall could reach across the planes. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-19)</small>

The elves of Cormanthyr, in the party's debt, gave them shelter before the assault on the [Arcaneum](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/). They were shocked that Cormyr and the Purple Dragons, their former enemies, had secured their Infanta, and there were calls for the head of Lord Krapsmere, the killer of their former monarch. <small>(Arc III, Parts IX–XI)</small>

The party let the elves keep the Infanta:

> It's important to know that you guys relented to the Cormanthyreans for them to keep their infanta in mythdrannor\
> it means Sylvanus worshipers not in Myth Drannor cannot use their holy magics

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01</small>

"once an infanta is born, that god's magic only works in their proximity". When Zanzibar's player JL predicted "a massive immigration crisis" for the elves, Dave answered "yes Zan they very well might". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

After the Arcaneum fell the elves opened the city to the party, with one exception:

> All of you are also free to come and go through Myth Drannor as long as you behave. Except for Zanzibar, who they feel it would be inappropriate to allow inside the mythal

<small>— Dave, Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-31</small>

## The River Lis

Mora's orcs invaded Cormanthor. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-01)</small> At the Lis, on Cormanthor's eastern border, the Moradinate sent "endless missile fire across River Lis into Cormanthor", and Teo, called Tico, was near the front, protected by the elves. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-10-21)</small> The allied army at [the Battle of the River Lis](/history/the-battle-of-the-river-lis/) included the **Cormanthryian Bladesingers** as cavalry and the **Cormanthryian Arcane Archers**. <small>(Oral Histories: Battle of River Lis, 2019-12-19)</small>

## The Egg

At the Crack in the side of [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), the allied host went group by group into the wound. "Elves of Myth Drannor see their plant clothes whither and proceed naked into the folds of brain", beside the Purple Dragons under Lord Krapsmere, Untari's Bladesingers of Motherstone, the Watch of Waterdeep and the armies of Amn. <small>(Sic on "whither". Arc VIII, notes for a final encounter)</small> See [Arc VIII — End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## The Witch Queen of Myth Drannor

In the reign of [Zanzibar William the Melancholy](/people/zanzibar-william-the-melancholy/), the Emperor's cousin Princess **Zythara**, a prodigy of the Arcaneum, was stripped of her titles for defying ecclesiastical oversight of arcane study. She fled to Myth Drannor, rekindled its mythal, and rose against him as the Witch Queen of Myth Drannor, one of the five Zanzibars of [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/). See [The Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/).
