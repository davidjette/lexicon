**City · eastern Khorvaire · The gateway to the East · DESTROYED**

*Also known as:* New Ham · Newham City

**Newham** was a city in eastern Khorvaire, the gateway to the East, destroyed in the rising against Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). It began as a small village named New Ham. Imperial council member Lord Albert Spear, head of City planning and development, granted New Ham an Imperial Charter to grow from a village into a city. LeBeefe was its first mayor and later its Grand Duke; when three operatives of the Inevitables took the city with its own resistance cells and killed him, a massive otherworldly explosion collapsed Newham into ruin and left the region around it corrupted and uninhabitable — [the New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/). The survivors evacuated east to [Leef](/places/leef/) and founded the Republic of Talenta.

Things moved fast in Newham: caravans of merchants, train cars full of construction equipment and laborers, and airships with arcane builders came in droves. New homes were built and high end stores and restaurants moved in, coming directly from the capital. The Lightning Rail Tavern was expanded into a glorious Hotel to accommodate all the new travelers to Newham's new Casino, [The Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/). The river Dark which flows near Newham was redirected and turned into a man-made lake, named Lake LaBeefe after the City's first Mayor, [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/).

## The Catacombs

Construction on the east side of town uncovered a series of ancient passageways seeming to lead away from the city. The first of 4 [ancient tablets](/items/ancient-stone-tablets-4/) was discovered deep within catacombs, guarded by ancient mechanical lizards and an ancient, sick silver dragon. [Esther](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Locke](/people/locke-pierce/) and [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) found the tablet and returned it to the Empire.

## The Grand Duke

John C. LeBeefe did not stay a mayor. He was made **Grand Duke** and carried the Empire eastward from the outpost of [Leef](/places/leef/), raising an airship yard, illithid city guards and the Iron Veil there — and by the end he was slowly transforming into something otherworldly, ruling Newham with anti-LeBeefe rebel cells hidden throughout the metropolis.

## The rising and New Mourning

Three operatives — [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) — liberated Leef and then travelled west, at Colonel Thadric Rourke's urging, to confront the architect of Newham's oppression in his own city. There they met **Digma Beeve**, Rourke's old ally, who introduced them to the network of anti-LeBeefe cells hidden throughout the metropolis and helped them weld those cells into a single coordinated resistance.

The assault succeeded and LeBeefe was killed. The victory came at a catastrophic cost: a massive otherworldly explosion collapsed the city into ruin, and a vast region around Newham became corrupted, toxic and uninhabitable — [the New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/).

The survivors fled east, evacuating en masse to Leef. There the townsfolk, the Newham refugees and the swamp-dwarf tribes of [the Mror Holds](/places/the-mror-holds/) forged a new nation: **the Republic of Talenta**, which stands between the wasteland to the west and the Mror Holds to the east, and which remains one of the few places in Khorvaire largely beyond the reach of the Empire.

## Present day

Newham is not a city any more. The manor, the hotel, the university, the academy annex and the lake all lie under the New Mourning; the mining camp at [Greenbluff](/places/greenbluff/) outside the city may lie with them. The city was raised, chartered and destroyed inside a single lifetime.

## Places of Interest

**[The Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/):** A newly built casino in the city of Newham.

**LeBeefe Manor:** Home of Mayor John C. LeBeefe.

**Lightning-rail Hotel:** Newly remodeled hotel with theater and restaurant.

**God Save the Blaze:** Pub restaurant / Club style services a hip younger collage crowd. Own and ran by Chef [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/).

**University of Newham:** The Hospital that Uriel founded has grown and thrived into a medical research and educational facility. Uriel heads the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases AKA, U.N.D.E.A.D.

**The Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Anex:** Established during the cities development. Locke Pierce has a semi-permanent post in Newham as Director.
