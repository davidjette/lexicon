**Memory extraction and archival device · BioTec manufacture · Standard equipment of ArcEye Surveillance · One lens recovered from Circuit City, Sharn Ep 13**

*Also known as:* Crystallizer · ArcEye Crystallizer Lens · crystallizer screen (colloquial, for the public displays it feeds)

The **ArcEye Crystallizer** is a memory extraction and archival device manufactured by [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) and carried as standard equipment by ArcEye Surveillance, which uses it to record the patrol footage of the [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/) and archive it in the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/). It will also extract and stabilise a full memory from a living mind. One lens was recovered from [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) in Sharn Ep 13, taken to Zilspar Farm 02 and used on Grady Marsh; the crystal it produced was later fed into the [EBT-7](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) and pushed onto every screen in Sharn.

## Function

Every ArcEye in Sharn archives visual and auditory data, and it is the Crystallizer that records the patrol footage. What it captures is then transferred to the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), catalogued, and stored in restricted archives accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorised Imperial officials. The [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/) would be a scrying service without it; with it, the network is an archive that never forgets.

It does the same work on a living mind. A Crystallizer can safely extract and stabilise a full memory from a person — which is a very different proposition from the crude ritual attempts made without one. Grady Marsh was subjected to a failed memory-extraction ritual beneath the Vault Below and came out of it drugged, foggy and only able to offer fragments. He insists that only an ArcEye Crystallizer can safely extract and stabilize the full memory, and he was right.

## Where to Find One

The mission board listed three possible locations, in descending order of survivability:

- **Black-market dealers.** [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/) deals in chip implants and old BioTec equipment, they may have one. It did.
- **Hillary Heinrick's home** in the GoldSky Neighborhood. The head of ArcEye Security keeps the *original prototype* there — a gift from Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/).
- **ArcEye HQ**, which has them at every archive station. Not recommended. Highly dangerous.

<small>The prototype was a personal gift from BioTec's head Hybrid Specialist to the head of ArcEye Security.</small>

## The Lens from Circuit City

The Inevitables did not go to Circuit City for a Crystallizer. They went there hunting the Cog Collector, guided by a Divination ritual, and found Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) and a backroom full of cages. Among the evidence recovered from the wreckage — bank records, property deeds, 800 gold, six severely ill [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) — was an **ArcEye Crystallizer Lens**.

It was taken to Zilspar Farm 02 and used on Grady Marsh.

## What Came Out of Grady Marsh

Eight years earlier, Grady had wandered off his ArcEye patrol route, looked into the Lord Commander's penthouse, and never legally filed the report. The extraction was witnessed by [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), Jessica Grimpledink, Zero-One and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/).

The feed showed [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) collapsing into sudden labour on her penthouse balcony while [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) stood motionless and watched. Uriel delivered the child, examined him closely, and then raised her hand over the newborn's face. A sickly green glow spread from her fingertips; the baby's breathing faltered and stopped; dark vein-like lines formed across his cheek — the mark that would later be called his birthmark. At Esther's scream for help, Uriel froze. The glow faded, and the baby gasped and cried as though nothing had happened. As Uriel turned away, the ArcEye caught a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull.

The memory ends with Uriel lifting her gaze and looking directly at the unseen observer.

That crystal was later fed into the EBT-7 and pushed onto every screen in Sharn.
