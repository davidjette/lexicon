**Portable arcano-analog message relay · House Orien · Lightning Rail Sub-Transit System · Discontinued · Recovered by the Inevitables, Sharn Ep 16**

*Also known as:* EBT-7 · the Sharn Broadcast Override · "the device"

The EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node is a portable arcano-analog relay device developed by House Orien during the operation of Sharn's Lightning Rail Sub-Transit System. Before the network collapsed, the EBT-7 served as the primary communication tool for the entire Sub-Transit line.

It is a relay built for a train system that no longer exists, and the only known means of overriding the Empire's surveillance grid in Sharn. It was recovered by the Inevitables in Sharn Ep 16 and used from the Veil Building penthouse to broadcast the memory of Calcifer's birth across the city.

## What It Was Built For

Each of Sharn's Sub-Transit station hubs carried one, and every Sub-Transit train car was equipped with its own unit. These devices were used to relay:

- travel updates
- delay and reroute notices
- tunnel and weather alerts
- emergency announcements
- station-to-train communication
- visual broadcasts on Orien Crystallizer screens to travelers

The EBT-7 predates all modern scry-tech, including ArcEye surveillance systems and the Sharn News Network. Because the Sub-Transit rails ran through stone, metal, and magically shielded tunnels, the EBT-7 used **arcano-analog resonance** to propagate signals where modern scry-tech could not — a signal that does not care about wards because it was never designed around them.

Only a small number of EBT-7 were created and they were discontinued after the Sub-Transit shutdown, making this device one of the rarest remaining pieces of pre-ArcEye Orien communication technology in the city.

## How It Works & Mission Use

The EBT-7 is a portable Orien arcano-analog repeater designed to capture a visual signal and rebroadcast it. When connected to a visual source, such as a memory crystal reader, or using its built-in camera, the device converts that image into a short-range analog broadcast.

When brought within \~100 ft of a permanent ArcEye camera, its analog signal can overpower the ArcEye receiver, forcing it to mirror the EBT-7's feed citywide. This creates a brief, unavoidable public broadcast. The [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/) was built after the EBT-7 and was never shielded against it.

**Technical Summary**

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Signal Type** | Orien analog resonance |
| **Range** | \~100 ft for ArcEye override |
| **Broadcast Duration** | Up to 5 minutes |
| **Power** | Orien arcano-battery |
| **Inputs** | Memory crystal reader, sending-stone visual feed, scry-camera, built-in camera |

## Mission Application

To use the EBT-7, you must reach one of Sharn's four permanent ArcEye camera locations and activate the device within range. Once activated, the EBT-7 will override the camera and broadcast:

- a live feed
- a recorded memory chip
- or both, as long as the 5-minute broadcast window remains active

> The broadcast cannot be stopped once initiated, and the transmission location, based on which ArcEye is targeted, will be exposed. All live feeds are permanently recorded.

Read as three rules:

- **It cannot be stopped once started.** There is no abort. Whatever goes out, goes out for the full window.
- **It exposes you.** The transmission location is revealed by which ArcEye is being overridden. The Empire will not know who you are, but it will know exactly where you are standing.
- **It is being recorded while you use it.** All live feeds are permanently recorded. The broadcast is evidence against the broadcaster by design.

A memory extracted with an [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) can be fed straight into it.

## The Four Permanent ArcEye Camera Sites

An override requires reaching one of exactly four permanent ArcEye camera locations in Sharn. The briefing on each follows.

### 1. Veil Building: Garden Floor Permanent ArcEye Camera

*Restricted Upper-City Terrace Feed*

This permanent ArcEye camera is mounted on the Garden Floor of the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/), a mid-tower open-air terrace reserved exclusively for Veil personnel and government employees. The level features a manicured garden and quiet seating areas, forming a secluded courtyard hidden within the tower's structure.

The camera is small, fixed, and non-rotating. Its feed captures the terrace walkway and garden, with distant views of the Upper City skyline, including a partial view of the [Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/), the largest tower in the city.

**Access & Approach Notes**

This is the most dangerous ArcEye camera to access.

- The Garden Floor is not public. Access is restricted to Veil officers, government staff, and authorized personnel.
- The level is monitored by security checkpoints, ID-locked lifts, and internal surveillance wards.
- Any unauthorized presence triggers immediate Iron Veil response.
- It is located directly below the Crona / Blaze penthouse.

### 2. Grand GoldSky Tower: Upper-City Skyline Camera

*Most-Viewed ArcEye Feed in Sharn*

This permanent ArcEye camera is mounted high on the [Grand GoldSky Tower](/places/grand-goldsky-tower/), positioned directly above the small GoldSky Iron Veil Precinct Station. It is one of the most widely viewed ArcEye feeds in the city, offering a sweeping, elevated look across the older Upper-City district.

The camera is fixed and non-rotating, capturing:

- the Skyport and its air-traffic lanes
- the Veil Building rising in the distance
- [POSÉ](/places/pose/) and surrounding towers, apartment and businesses

This feed is iconic across Sharn and is frequently used by citizens to track airship movement, weather patterns, and general Upper-City activity. It is commonly used by citizens to check whether Esther's airship, the Black Veil, is currently in the city.

**Access & Approach Notes**

This is a high-risk ArcEye camera due to its location above a Veil precinct and the constant foot traffic around the tower.

**Primary Dangers**

- The camera sits directly over the GoldSky Iron Veil Precinct Station
- The Grand GoldSky Tower is a busy commercial and residential hub, filled with shops, apartments, and constant civilian movement.
- Suspicious behavior stands out quickly in such a crowded, high-visibility environment.

**Possible Advantages**

- Multiple stacked walkways and balcony levels allow for vertical maneuvering.
- The override could be attempted from a level above or below the precinct's elevation, as long as the EBT-7 is within range.
- Frequent skybuses travel up, down, and diagonally between the tower and nearby districts, offering moving platforms that briefly pass within the override distance.

### 3. Grand Sharn Hotel: Rotating Upper-City Camera

*Dual-View ArcEye Feed (Left/Right Rotation)*

This permanent ArcEye camera is mounted on the rooftop of the [Grand Sharn Hotel](/places/grand-sharn-hotel/), a major hospitality hub connected directly to the Sharn Theater. The hotel attracts a constant flow of travelers, tourists, theater-goers, and long-term guests, making it one of the busiest civilian towers in the Upper City.

The camera is one of the few ArcEye nodes that slowly rotates left and right, cycling between two distinct panoramic views.

**Left-Facing View**

- the roof of the Sharn Theater in the foreground
- the [GoldSky Neighborhood](/places/goldsky-neighborhood/) in the distance — Sharn's wealthiest district
- luxury mansions, high-end apartments, and elevated shopping terraces
- the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/) and the Black Dove Hospital on the horizon

**Right-Facing View**

- the Sharn Theater roof again in the foreground
- the oldest Upper-City district beyond it
- glimpses of the Veil Tower and Skyport

**Access & Approach Notes**

This camera is moderately dangerous, not because of Veil presence, but because of the tower's constant civilian traffic.

**Primary Dangers**

- The Grand Sharn Hotel is a high-traffic civilian tower with tourists, travelers, theater patrons, and long-term guests moving at all hours.
- Suspicious behavior stands out quickly in such a crowded, social environment.
- The rooftop is accessible to hotel guest only and is monitored for guest safety.

**Possible Advantages**

- The hotel has low Iron Veil presence, making it far less dangerous than Veil-controlled towers.
- A room could be rented on a high floor close enough to the rooftop for an EBT-7 override.
- The hotel's public nature could allow you to blend in easily as guests, tourists, or theater-goers.

### 4. The Lower City: Ziggurat Camera

*Lower-City Fixed ArcEye Feed*

This permanent ArcEye camera is mounted on the roof of the largest factory in [The Cogs](/places/the-cogs/), the same industrial complex Esther entered with [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), Frontline Iron Veil and a large drill. The camera has a single, fixed angle and does not rotate. Its feed is an elevated view of the Lower City's central [Ziggurat](/places/the-ziggurat/), one of the most recognizable structures in the district with a partial view of the [Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) in the foreground.

**Access & Approach Notes**

This may be the safest option for an EBT-7 broadcast depending on timing and approach.

**Current Conditions**

- Iron Veil presence in the Lower City has returned to pre-Esther levels.
- The factories and The Cogs are still Veil-run and patrolled, but not under the extreme lockdown seen during Esther's descent.
- Patrols are present, but predictable and spread thin across the district.

**Primary Dangers**

- The streets around [Gear Street Market](/places/gear-street-markets/) are crowded, making stealth tricky.
- Patrols move along both ground-level streets and elevated catwalks.
- The factory itself is Veil-controlled, so entering the building is risky.

**Possible Advantages (Significant)**

This location offers multiple strong approach routes, making it potentially the safest ArcEye node:

- The camera is positioned on the factory roof, which can be reached without entering the building by using exterior ladders, catwalks, or maintenance platforms.
- Because the camera is so close to Gear Street Market, getting within the \~100 ft override range is possible from the market side, without ever touching the factory.

**[Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/):** Now empty the building sits close enough to the factory that parts of its main floor and roof may fall within the EBT-7 radius. The building's private, covered rooftop houses the cargo skylift Graves used to transport [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) bodies to the Black Dove Hospital in the Upper City. The skylift is still programmed to travel between Circuit City and the Hospital. The structure also contains a hidden back exit that leads into the Aqueduct, offering a discreet approach or escape route. Together, these features make Circuit City one of the most strategically advantageous positions near the Ziggurat camera.

**Summary:** While The Cogs are patrolled and Veil-controlled, the combination of exterior access, market proximity, and the abandoned Circuit City roof makes this camera one of the most viable and least risky targets for an EBT-7 broadcast, especially compared to the Veil-restricted Upper-City nodes.

## Recovery

Jessica Grimpledink described it to the Inevitables as a salvaged device capable of hijacking Sharn's ArcEye live feeds and broadcasting whatever they chose across the entire city, and sent them to an Upper City shop to collect it. They came back with it into an ambush: Children of Ember cultists took [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) in the street and tore the teleportation chip out of her skull. The trio killed two, took the third alive, and got the EBT-7 inside.

At the Nest, Jessica briefed them on the war in the west, Crona's Wall freezing Lake Galifar, rising [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) activity, and the looming Sharn Broadcast Override. In the end the broadcast went out — Grady Marsh's memory of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born, playing on every screen in Sharn, with [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s hand over a newborn's face — and the city rose. <small>It was used from the Veil Building penthouse itself, above the Garden Floor camera: see [The Sharn Broadcast Override](/lore/the-sharn-broadcast-override/).</small>
