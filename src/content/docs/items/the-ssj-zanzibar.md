**Sovereign Spelljammer · Astral Command · Flagship of five hundred years before · Critically damaged**

*Also known as:* S.S.J. Zanzibar · the Zanzibar · Zanny · Zan · Zanzo

**The SSJ Zanzibar** is a Sovereign Spelljammer of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), the fleet of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), and the ship of [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), named for the Emperors of Faerun, each of whom is called the Zanzibar after [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/). A flagship of five hundred years before, it languished in a single system for want of a pilot for its Major Helm until [Caprica](/people/caprica/) took its open helm. Under [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) it flew the missions of [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/). While the command crew were away, bound for [Necis](/places/necis/), its own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard and left it adrift; a wish carried it to [Sigil](/places/sigil/), and it took critical damage in the finale of the arc, in which Caprica died.

## Name

After the Ages quest, Zanzibar, crowned king of Cormyr, declared himself Emperor of Faerun, and every Emperor after him was called the Zanzibar. The Spelljammer craft of Starfall took the same name. <small>(CANON.md 5ac)</small> Like the Karametra, the Balduran and the Excelsior, it is one of the Astral Command ships that bear names from the Ages of the Infanta. A statue of Zanzibar, "Big Z", stands aboard as the ship's namesake. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

## History

### Before Light of Xaryxis

Starfall is set some five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta, and the Zanzibar was a flagship five hundred years before its events. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Dave's image of the ship in flight shows it above Fort Zanzibar, surrounded by oceans after the great melting of the 6700s, with Punis and Paenus only a drowned memory. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

The Zanzibar's Major Helm is very rare and needs a rare pilot, who spends spell slots to bend space and jam at a million miles an hour; most ships carry minor helms of limited range. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10; 2022-10-11)</small> With no one aboard able to operate it, the ship limped on a backup minor helm with [F.L.A.P.](/people/f-l-a-p/) as autopilot, confined to Trafalmadorespace, "T-Space". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11; 2022-10-19)</small> In that time it grew complacent and put down roots at its port. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Dave described it as a misfit ship in an unimportant and dangerous sector, so remote that its crew worked under old rules. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

JL proposed, and Dave approved, that the Zanzibar's sister ship is the **Karametra**, presumed lost for ten years. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

### Light of Xaryxis

Caprica, first in her class at the Academy, was offered the first or second helm of almost any vessel and chose the open helm on the Zanzibar, "currently floating between nowhere and absolutely nowhere." The captain was to collect her from the Loose Rooster, a tavern in the village of Northspire on Tralfamadore III. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small>

A list of ship's systems had to power up before the first flight of the Zanzibar on 2022-11-10, the first mission off the book of the published adventure, in which the crew outran an enemy. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-10; 2022-11-13)</small> In December 2022 the ship ignored Jim's order to leave, because he had given them his duress code. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-18)</small>

While Jim, Caprica and Sandy were away from the ship in November 2023, the Zanzibar was free-floating. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-20)</small> In the "B Team" episodes its Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard, leaving the ship "adrift in the astral sea with no Astralex". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> Nico's account is that the crew's fast thinking saved most of the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> Buttsack, Carlos Vega and Mordecai were aboard the helpless ship in January 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-26)</small>

In April 2024, with the ship close to exploding, [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) made his third wish. The Zanzibar arrived in Sigil, extremely far from the command crew and out of contact with them. Carlos, bound as a djinn in a bottle, was aboard with Buttsack, and Irina Malikovsky was aboard with Chad Mantis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small> After the party walked into the white light, Mordecai and Irina moved to take the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-11)</small>

In the finale of *Light of Xaryxis* on 2024-05-31, against the Shrike, the Zanzibar took critical damage and Caprica died. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Nico's comment on the finale has the Zanzibar and [the SSJ Stonecypher](/items/the-ssj-stonecypher/) torn apart together. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> A shuttle was left aboard. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-03)</small> Dave has called the Zanzibar the home of Sandy, its diplomat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

### The Starsong Awakens

In *The Starsong Awakens*, fifteen years later, Kol Hanaka's notes from Brownsleeve City record "hangar - SSJ Zanzibar". <small>(character sheet)</small> Barrias Ruby-Eye, Se7en and Nine went through the time doors of the Sphinx Tomb; Dave recalled that the last door took them to [the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), and Nico that it showed the final fight on the Zanzibar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

## Layout

Dave described the ship as basically a small town, whose facilities fail when the story requires it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-17)</small> Its fittings are as follows. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-15; 2022-10-17; 2022-10-18; 2022-10-19)</small>

- **Bridge:** the captain's chair and main viewscreen; the captain's quarters and ready room open off it.
- **Captain's private hangar:** a sub-bridge room holding the captain's personal craft, which fits two comfortably and three tightly.
- **Shuttle hangar:** with a retractable emergency door.
- **Ten Forward:** the ship's bar.
- **Brig:** built into the cargo hold.
- **Wardroom:** with a secret emergency arms locker.
- **Science room:** sharing storage with sickbay.
- **Jeffries tubes:** access tunnels that connect through the ship, and a secret room near the engine.
- **Lounge:** at the top of the ship.
- **Engineering:** elemental engines with contrails and flames.
- **Armament and rigging:** the Particle Ballista and Plasma Mangonel, gunner units, escape pods, retractable laser sails and vertical sails.
