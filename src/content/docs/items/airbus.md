**Public transit vehicle · Sharn · In service**

*Also known as:* Airbuses · skybuses <small>(the form used in the EBT-7 briefing)</small>

The Airbus is a Sharn public transit vehicle: a large, open-sided levitating platform powered by a bound air elemental, gliding along fixed sky lanes between towers and districts. Nine years ago Sharn dismantled its once-famous Lightning Rail Sub-Transit and adopted in its place the current dual system of Airbuses and [Zephyr Pods](/items/zephyr-pod/), which proved more flexible, safer within the vertical maze of the city, and far easier to reroute as districts expanded or shifted. The Airbus is now the most common mid-tier public transit option in the city, fast, reliable, and capable of carrying dozens of passengers at once.

## Place in the city

Airbuses sit inside Sharn's flight law: private flight is prohibited in restricted airspace, so for most citizens the fixed sky lanes are the way to move between levels.

## Fares

A single ride typically **costs 2-5 silver**, depending on distance and district, making them accessible to most working citizens. Riders can also purchase Airbus Passes at [the Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/) or any destination terminal, including a **monthly unlimited-ride pass for 10 gold**, favored by commuters and tower-hoppers who rely on the system daily.

## Tactical note

The [EBT-7](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) briefing records that frequent skybuses travel up, down, and diagonally between the Grand GoldSky Tower and nearby districts, offering moving platforms that briefly pass within override distance of a permanent ArcEye camera. <small>The briefing uses "skybus"; the city gazetteer uses "Airbus."</small>
