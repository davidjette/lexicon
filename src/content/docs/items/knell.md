**Warhammer · Black Khyber (Shavat) · Royal arm of the Deeptempura · Older than the bells · Believed unique · Carried by Sir Dario Argentino**

*Also known as:* Knell · King Corn's hammer · the hammer that made them · the black-Kyber hammer

**KNELL** is a warhammer of [black Khyber — Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/), the personal royal arm of King Corn Deeptempura and older than the bells cast from the same metal. It was recovered from King Corn's crowned corpse in the hoard of the dragon Valtrex, on the island in the black lake beneath the Korramont, named by the surviving Teryaki kin, and is carried by [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/). It is believed unique, and it is the only thing found that will destroy a bell: a struck bell does not ring but gives a tremendous cracking sound, a rift almost splitting the body in two, and then the metal folds in on itself under something like a magnetic force — symmetry lost, collapsing into a twisted ribbon across the ground.

## The hammer

KNELL is heavy even for a paladin under a potion of stone giant strength. It carries a violent recoil: the swing that broke [the Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/) threw its bearer's hand back and deafened both him and the dwarf standing near him for hours. Breaking a Master is loud in a way that costs.

Dave gave its properties at the table as "+2 and adamantine and destroys magical structures like force cage". Under the revised Dual Wielder rules Dario cannot fight with KNELL and **Pale Fire** together: "So Dario has to decide whether to use Knell or Pale Fire". Swapping between them takes an hour, "To attune". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-27)</small>

The runesmith Bofric is precise about why it matters, and about what the horn cannot do:

> It can only maybe drown out the sound as long as you play it. To break the bell, you would need to break it with the hammer that made it.

## How it was named

The Inevitables did not find it first; they were told about it. Three years dead and still lucid in the shallows beneath the Korranberg Foundry, **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura** — the woman who rang the bell — gave [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) two things before she asked to be ended: where her father's horn was hidden, and that her father's hammer could crack anything made of black Kyber.

It was the surviving Teryaki kin, back at the camp in the Great Hall, who supplied the name. **KNELL.** Sedda had not called it that. The kin did.

The name and the hammer's whereabouts came in [Korth Ep 9](/sessions/korth-ep-9-the-death-warrant-and-the-ringer/); the hammer stayed where it was: "the hammer that can break the bells lies somewhere below, in the dark, with Vuldrach." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-09)</small>

## Recovery

It was on the island in the black lake, in the hoard of the dragon **Valtrex**, folded into the arms of King Corn's crowned corpse. Valtrex, newly freed from an Imperial collar and three years of being bled, was unambiguous about who owned it:

> That hammer doesn't belong to King Corn. It belongs to me. So does his crown.

[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) negotiated in Draconic, laid a **Ring of Lesser Wish** on the hoard, and lifted KNELL and [the Crown of Seven Stars](/items/the-crown-of-seven-stars/) out of it. King Corn's remains stayed on the hoard. The kin were never told where he was.

This was [Korth Ep 11](/sessions/korth-ep-11-the-domain-of-valtrex/). Dave's account of how the trade was won: "freeing him made him go from hostile to indifferent, and then good persuasion rolls plus the trade plus the endorsement of Corns ghost let him part with the hammer". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-22)</small>

## On-screen work

- **The collar-bell** — Valtrex's neck-bell, struck as a demonstration at Gemma's request. The first bell KNELL ever broke on screen.
- **The Thirteenth Casting** — broken after Hetta rang it and the toll put every living thing in the level into one mind. Dario spent his last channel divinity to make the hammer a sacred weapon, rolled a natural 1, and swung again.
- **The Bladeworks** — every remaining casting standing in the foundry, destroyed bell by bell in one walk.
- **The hospital tower, Sharn** — a great casting crated in straw in an unfinished tower. Dario cleared the packing himself and struck with *“may our children forgive us.”*
- **The temple of Boldrei, Lower City** — the strike brought the tower down through the floor into the building below and buried him. The crowd Gemma had just pacified turned on them.

It has also been used as a blunt instrument of ordinary war: at the ruin of Zilspar Farm, Dario sheathed **Pale Fire** and took up KNELL purely to shove Eden units clear of the party before they detonated.

## What was learned about it afterwards

Research in Sharn established three findings that govern the whole campaign against the chorus:

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Breaking a bell notifies no one** | No word of a destroyed casting travels back to Uriel Qualanthri. The bells can be taken down quietly, one at a time. |
| **Horns can be reproduced** | The method of Corn's horn is recoverable from the folio. Players can be trained. |
| **KNELL cannot** | There is very likely no second KNELL in the world. |

The rebellion can mask the Noise anywhere it can put a horn-player, and it can *end* a bell only where it can put one specific hammer in one specific pair of hands.

## Right now

Dario carries it, has weapon mastery with warhammers, and is one-eyed with a revivification diamond in the socket. In Korth he had every weapon he owns logged with a forged registration under the alias **Sky Rizzy** — *every weapon except KNELL*. It is not on any Imperial list. By his own count, given to a room holding a representative of every faith on the continent, he has broken three bells and will break every one. **Ten remain**, at least one a Master hanging in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace itself.
