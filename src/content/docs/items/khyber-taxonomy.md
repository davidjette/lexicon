**Material classification · Three named varieties · Delivered as lore by Sir Dario Argentino · Contested at the margins**

*Also known as:* Kyber · Khyber · black Khyber / Shavat · red Khyber · white Khyber / Siberys

The Khyber taxonomy is the classification of the three named varieties of Khyber ore — black Khyber or Shavat, red Khyber, and white Khyber or Siberys — as delivered by Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/). Khyber is not a term of art. It is a common word, the way *iron* is a common word, and people are imprecise when talking about metallurgy. It is also the name of one of the three progenitor dragons, encased in the body of Eberron, and the ore is called Khyber out of old myths that it is his body, his blood, or his outer scales. Which of those three the myths mean has never been settled.

## BLACK KHYBER — SHAVAT

The bell-metal. **Shavat**, black Khyber: a mythic planar ore formed where **Shavarath** — the plane of eternal battle — touches the mortal world. It was identified by name beneath the Korramont, where the black ore walls of the Grand Stair amplified every misstep.

Its properties, as established on screen:

- Tempered and shaped by a dwarven method itself likely derived from something infernal, the resulting body vibrates in sympathy with the arcane forces governing life and death. This is what makes [the Black Noise](/lore/the-black-noise/) possible.
- It can be worked into ordinary arms and tools. The Bladeworks floor was littered with weapons, tools and ingots of it, and the party took what they could carry.
- [KNELL](/items/knell/) is made of it. So is every bell it breaks. The hammer and the target are the same substance.
- [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/)'s theory of the foundry: the enslaved dwarves were transmuting black Khyber into a *more complex crystalline form*, able to take imprinted arcane sigils dictated by the elves who enslaved them.

The seams are, in the assessment of Locke Pierce's metallurgists, **the hardest and oldest known rock in Eberron**. The Korranberg mountain was one. Its molds are now at the bottom of an acid lake and nothing is cast from it again.

## RED KHYBER

Something else entirely — *"something we've seen used to horrible ends."*

Red Khyber is the Empire's industrial obsession. BioTec's E.D.E.N. units, hybrid undead-warforged soldiers built through BioTec, Umbra and Black Dove rites, were made to survive the Mournland for two purposes: to fight the Hundreds entrenched in the Glass Plateau, and to **mine red Khyber** out of the irradiated waste. Rising red Khyber activity was on the rebellion's threat board in Sharn years before anyone in the party had heard the word Shavat.

**The Bright Dawn** — Gemma Corso's sword — used to be red Khyber, and is now crystalline. Dario has turned it over twice and not liked what he found: *"I remember when you drew this sword. I thought it might be cursed — that it might curse you the way Crona's sword cursed her. But this… no. It couldn't. Could it?"*

**The live question.** Jessica Grimpledink, working with Bofric, determined that [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s wedding gift is **black Khyber and red Khyber together**. She stopped seeing a cursed heirloom and started seeing a prototype weapon, and is now fixated on it. Nobody has said what an alloy of the two does. Nobody knows.

## WHITE KHYBER — SIBERYS

The rocks that fall from the sky: the shed scales of **Siberys**, the shards of the moon, white Khyber.

Dario corrects this one himself, immediately, to **moonstone** — *"because people are imprecise when talking about metallurgy."*
