**Document · Lead-bound · Deeptempura clan shorthand · A Tome of Understanding · In the hands of the Inevitables · Partially translated**

*Also known as:* the folio · King Corn's folio · the lead-bound folio

**Corn's folio** is the lead-bound working record of [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), the dwarven king enslaved in his own hold and made to build the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). It holds the plates of the first bell's casting with its tempering and hammering methods, harmonic mathematics, drawings of horns, and on its last page the notes that became [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/); it is also a Tome of Understanding on metallurgy and leadership. Corn hid it under the floor of his study in the Korranberg Foundry, where the Inevitables recovered it three years after his death together with [the horn](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/). It is now in Korth with the party, with roughly one section translated by the runesmith Bofric.

## How this was obtained

Three years dead in the shallows beneath the Korranberg Foundry, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura told [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) where her father had hidden his work, and then asked to be killed. A *locate object* ritual afterwards delivered the joke at the party's expense: the cache was three hundred feet straight up, in the study they had already searched and walked out of. Under the rug: a sliding panel, a stair, and a spell-sealed chest that opened to a key Gemma was already carrying. The folio and the horn were inside.

## THE PLATES — THE CASTING OF THE FIRST BELL

Drawings of bells, and of the casting of the first bell. Tempering and hammering methods. Mathematical figures which [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) recognised on sight as harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms. This is the manufacturing record of [the Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) as a physical object: how it is brought to temper, and what shape makes it sing the way it sings.

The man who wrote it built the machine.

## THE MARGINS

Crowded with question marks. One word is legible — **"wounding"** — written beside a picture of a big hammer striking a bell. It is the idea that became [KNELL](/items/knell/)'s entire function, written by the smith who had the hammer in his own hall the whole time.

Further in: drawings of **horns**, worked against harmonic-resonance mathematics, trying — in Eric's reading — *"to solve some type of problem related to the common symmetries of multiple waveforms."*

## THE LAST PAGE

In plain Common, not clan shorthand, in a shaking hand:

> I made their key. Someone must learn to make its answer sing.

Beneath it, a simple set of notes — the fugue, expanded in the sheet music taken from the same room. That is [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/).

## THE TREATISE

The folio is also a **Tome of Understanding** — a genuine work of instruction, not merely a workbook. Its subject is metallurgy and leadership, and it leans throughout on the metaphor of mixing alloys as the ingredients of a strong civilisation. Read properly it grants a permanent increase in Wisdom, to a maximum of 30, over forty-eight hours of study across sixty days.

<small>A dwarven king enslaved in his own hold, writing a book about how you blend a people into something that holds under load, in the margins of the drawings for the weapon his captors made him build.</small>

## BOFRIC'S TRANSLATION

The runesmith **Bofric** reads Corn's shorthand and estimates about a week for the whole book. He has so far finished exactly one section, *because it interested him*: the song the king spent his final days perfecting, enraptured with a muse. Twenty-four bars with a short eight-bar bridge, very dwarven in style. He played it on a whistle, then on the king's own horn, and lifted the bell's compulsion out of Eric's mind entirely.

He named the art [arcane harmonics](/items/arcane-harmonics/) — procured by the elves, mastered only by the dwarves, a secret weapon of the elves' war against the dragons. He is bent, milk-eyed on the left, fingers stained with lampblack, and not a combatant. He came anyway.

## CROSS-REFERENCE — KORRANBERG FOUNDRY TRUST, PRIVATE SCHEDULE OF WORKS

The Empire's own account of the same manufacture reads differently. The Trust's schedule records the Original as *"Not of this foundry, and the Trust will thank the House to record that it never was,"* received once, under guard, *"for the taking of tuning references only — our masters worked in wax and never with hammers, per the terms."*

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **The Trust says** | It only ever copied. It took wax references. It never worked the Original. |
| **The folio says** | There are plates here of *the casting of the first bell*, with tempering and hammering methods. |
| **The reconciliation** | None offered. Either the Trust is disowning work its enslaved dwarves did, or Corn drew a casting he was never present for. |

The Trust also burned its own dockets nightly on a brazier kept at the gate for the purpose. Corn's folio was bound in lead and buried under a floor.

## WHAT THE BOOK IS WORTH

- **It makes horns reproducible.** Research in Sharn established the method of Corn's horn from these pages. Doppler Klink is building them.
- **It makes the fanfare teachable.** Notes on the last page, arrangement in the sheet music, translation by Bofric, and the clergy of Sharn carrying it congregation by congregation.
- **It does not make a second KNELL.** Research concluded the hammer is likely unique. The folio names the wound and cannot make the blade.

## Right now

The folio is in Korth with the party, under Imperial cover, alongside KNELL and the horn. Bofric is out of the mountain — the only person known to read Corn's shorthand. Roughly one section of the book has been translated. The rest of it is still, functionally, the Empire's manufacturing secret sitting in a rebel's bag.
