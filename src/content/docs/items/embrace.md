**Drug · Circulated in New Cyre and Newham · Studied at the University of Newham · In Imperial use**

Embrace is a drug that makes the person taking it seem undead. The party of [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) found it in heavy circulation in New Cyre, where Father White was taking it, and the gang leader Taby PC cut [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) in on moving it through New Ham. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) then found that applying it once a week kept her ghoul Flighter from decomposing, though it failed on fresh corpses; she synthesised a small store from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble and theorised that its sole source is the blood of vampires. As Empress she uses a refined version to embed undead servants throughout society and the army. Six bottles of it were among the goods the Inevitables took from the [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) in Sharn.

## New Cyre

The Unforeseen went to the refugee camp and town of New Cyre to learn whether Father White was a rebel. <small>([Episode 4](/sessions/episode-4-aandb-new-cyre-dm-lamont/))</small>

> A Drug named "Embrace" was in heavy rotation in town and the leader of the gang "Booty Salad," Taby PC, made a deal with LeBeefe to move Embrace through New Ham.

> It was discovered that Father White was taking the drug Embrace, causing him to seem undead.

## Flighter and U.N.D.E.A.D.

By Episode 6 Uriel headed the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases, U.N.D.E.A.D. The sex magic she had used on Flighter had left her a loyal ghoul, and she could not openly keep a ghoul at the hospital. She patched up the head wound he received in death, and experimented on him with Embrace. <small>([Episode 6](/sessions/episode-6-aandb-6-months-later-the-tablet-dm-nico/))</small>

> "she discovered that applying the drug ”Embrace” on him once a week prevents him from decomposing. This only seems to work on him so far, as experiments on fresh corpses with Embrace have failed."

People around the university took Flighter, a quiet, handsome soldier, for her lover. He followed only Uriel's orders, fought with the greatsword he had used in life, and kept his skill at navigation and a handful of words.

[Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) also studied Embrace at the University of Newham, and there established the blood relation between [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and her half-sisters.

In a carriage crossing the Blade Desert after the party's return from the Dragon Watch Isles, Uriel thought to herself: "I need to find more Embrace!" <small>([Episode 9](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/))</small>

## Rekkenmark East

Chancellor Steele permitted Locke Pierce and Uriel to move Dr. Marble's research in autoanimata and necrocryptography to the Rekkenmark East facilities, where they analysed, among other things, the composition of Embrace. <small>([City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/))</small>

> "Uriel’s primary work centers on the magical nature of blood. She is able to synthesize a small store of Embrace from the heart and liver of Dr. Marble, leading her to theorize that the sole source of Embrace is the blood of vampires. She sets out to collect Vampires who she can enslave and harvest for an ample supply."

## The Empress's undead

Dave, on Uriel as Empress: "She’s also definitely using a refined version of Embrace to embed undead servants all over society and in large numbers in the army, like order 66". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small> Asked by JL who needs the Black Doves when the Empress has an undead army, he answered: "An undead army that can live and operate in the open with Embrace". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-28)</small>

## Embra

In Nico's notes for the Sharn arc, Embrace is a known street drug that makes its user "appear dead". Ten years before the arc Uriel discovered that the drug, used on corpses, slows their decomposition, and she went on studying its uses on the undead. The main ingredient is made from part of a rare plant that grows only in Valenar, called **Embra**. Nico also writes **Umbra**, a name Dave suggests may derive from Embra. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-nico-docs-questions.md)</small> Malrik Zeir's company harvests it, and Hallorn d'Lyrandar helps ship it from Valenar to Sharn, where it is used by [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) in the study and creation of the [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) hybrid soldiers. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

In the same notes, when Esther Crona's half-sister Eden was resurrected by the Diamond Ritual and the ritual did not go smoothly, Embrace and BioTec were brought in on Esther's orders to make Eden "whole again". Nico's script for the opening of [Posé](/places/pose/) has Esther demanding of Hallorn, "Where are my shipments of Embra from Valenar?", and telling Faith that Eden would stay where she was until Malrik and Hallorn increased "the Embra supply". <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 2)</small>

## The Children of Ember

In [Sharn Ep 6](/sessions/sharn-ep-6-the-vault-below/) the trio broke into the Children of Ember's rooms beneath [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/). Their haul included a Children of Ember tome, robes and four masks, six bottles of Embrace, and eight pounds of raw [Umbra](/items/umbra/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-14)</small>
