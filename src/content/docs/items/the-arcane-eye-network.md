**Imperial surveillance infrastructure · Implemented \~10 years ago · Operational control: the Iron Veil, Sharn Division · Head of Security: Hillary Heinrick · Active throughout the Sharn arc**

*Also known as:* ArcEyes · ArcEye · the Arcane Eye Security Network · the Eyes

The **Arcane Eye Network** (ArcEyes), formally the Arcane Eye Security Network, is the Empire's citywide surveillance grid over Sharn. It was implemented nearly a decade ago as part of the Empire's expanded internal surveillance initiative and is operated by the Iron Veil's Sharn Division from the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/) under Head of Security Hillary Heinrick. ArcEyes are visible throughout the major districts, drifting along streets, alleys and transit routes in continuous patrol cycles; their presence is considered a standard component of urban security, though their true operational capacity remains classified. The same grid carries the Empire's public broadcasts, and every feed it takes is permanently recorded. When Sharn rose, the Veil Building was seized by Nest and Fulcrum operatives, Heinrick was tried and executed, and the network changed hands.

## How They Work

ArcEyes are cast, maintained, and remotely operated by government employees stationed within the [Veil Building](/places/the-veil-building/). These operators are low-level wizards trained specifically in Arcane Eye protocols; their spellcasting ability is limited, but their specialization allows for extended-duration scrying, coordinated patrol routing, and multi-eye monitoring. There are a great many desks.

Each ArcEye is capable of archiving visual and auditory data. Patrol footage is recorded using an [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) and transferred to the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), where it is cataloged and stored in restricted archives accessible only to Iron Veil personnel and authorized Imperial officials. **All live feeds are permanently recorded**; nothing an ArcEye sees is ever discarded.

## The Second Function

The Eyes do not only watch. In addition to surveillance duties, ArcEyes are integrated into the city's public information network. They can transmit images, alerts, and state-approved messages to display screens throughout Sharn, allowing the Empire to broadcast news, directives, and emergency notices in real time. The surveillance grid and the broadcast grid are the same grid.

Sharn has watched itself through this network in both directions. Citizens use the fixed feed on the Grand GoldSky Tower to check whether Esther Crona's airship the **Black Veil** is in the city. Every screen in Sharn turned red the night [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) executed [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) prisoners live from the deck of that warship, so that there would be no misunderstanding about the cost of dissent.

## Standing Assessment for Operatives

The standing assessment issued to operatives reads:

> Assessment: The Arcane Eye Security Network represents one of the Empire's most pervasive surveillance tools. Operatives should assume that all major streets, public squares, and transit hubs in Sharn are under continuous observation. Avoiding detection requires deliberate route planning, magical interference, or movement through unmonitored lower-city sectors.

In practice the rebellion used all three. Route planning through the Grand Aqueduct and the collapsed sub-transit lines. Movement through the Cogs and the Underhive, where coverage thins and the Empire has never much cared what it cannot see. And magical interference in the form of the only purpose-built countermeasure that exists: the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/), which was BioTec's own product, stolen out of BioTec's own building.

## The Blind Spots

The network has three known blind spots.

**The BioTec building.** The Empire's own research contractor runs no ArcEyes inside its walls. Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/) made that call personally, citing "data contamination risks." This absence is considered deliberate and unusual, suggesting the presence of sensitive research that cannot risk ArcEye recording or oversight. Exterior patrols of [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) still occur.

**The Ghost Chip.** An implant that disrupts the scry-signature the Eyes rely on, generating a persistent blind spot around the wearer — invisible and inaudible to ArcEyes. It was built by BioTec, the Empire's own research contractor.

**The retired officer.** Grady Marsh wandered off his patrol route eight years ago, looked into the Lord Commander's penthouse, and did not legally file the surveillance report. That single unfiled document is the only reason anyone alive knows what happened at the birth of [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/).

## What the Network Was Made to Give Up

Grady's memory was extracted with a Crystallizer lens at Zilspar Farm and viewed by [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), Jessica Grimpledink, Zero-One and [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/). The ArcEye feed showed Esther collapsing into labour on the penthouse balcony while [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) stood and watched; Uriel delivering the child, examining him, and raising a hand over his face in a sickly green glow until his breathing stopped and dark vein-like lines spread across his cheek. At Esther's scream the glow faded and the baby cried. As Uriel turned away the Eye caught a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull — and then Uriel lifted her gaze and looked directly at the unseen observer.

<small>That last is the only recorded instance of an ArcEye being noticed by the thing it was watching.</small>

## Override

The network has exactly one known technical vulnerability, and it is older than the network. The [EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/), a House Orien relay from the defunct Lightning Rail Sub-Transit system, predates all modern scry-tech; brought within roughly a hundred feet of a permanent ArcEye camera, its analog signal overpowers the receiver and forces the Eye to mirror it citywide for up to five minutes. There are four permanent camera sites in Sharn, and the whole of the Sharn Broadcast Override plan turned on reaching one of them.

## Fate

When Sharn rose, Nest and Fulcrum operatives secured the Veil Building. Hillary Heinrick, who had commanded the Arcane Eye network from inside it, was taken into custody, tried and executed alongside Dr. Menka and Sister Nora. The screens stayed on. They had simply changed hands.
