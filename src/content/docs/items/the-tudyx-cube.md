**Item · Puzzle box · Invented by Dae, the Infanta of Puzzles · Named for House Tudyx of Cormanthor**

*Also known as:* the Tudix Cube

The **Tudyx Cube** is a puzzle box of a design invented in the first age of the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/) by Dae, the Infanta of Puzzles, and produced in quantity by Dae's adoptive mother, Queen [Alen Tudyx](/people/alen-tudyx/) of Cormanthor. The boxes became popular in Cormanthor and took the name of her house. One of them, a silver-filigreed box that [Kara](/people/kara/) took from the quarters of [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) aboard the [Arielle](/items/the-arielle/), carried a perfect moonstone through about 65 million years and gave the party the Perfect Dark stone that lets [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) travel in time. Another, probably one of the originals, was given to [Heyu](/people/heyu/) in [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/). <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small>

## Description

What is inside a Tudyx Cube cannot be reached from the outside by any means. Its complex puzzle and its materials generate a demiplane, and the only way into that demiplane is to solve the puzzle. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small> The cube hides its contents from magic. In the DM's notes for Dead Man's Hand it is drawn as a cube within a cube, with an arrow to Brother Montegue's magic word, "STONE". <small>(DM notes)</small> The one aboard the Arielle was a fine silver-filigreed box that could not be opened, and nothing the party tried could discover what was inside it. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small>

## Origin

In the first age of the Infanta, Peter Glans, who is the copper dragon [Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/), and his wife Alen Tudyx adopted Dae, the Infanta of Puzzles, and raised them. Alen was queen of Cormanthor and [Myth Drannor](/places/myth-drannor/) and the sister of the Archmage [Valen](/people/valen/). Peter had bought a puzzle box in his past, and he showed it to Dae. Dae then invented a box that was more difficult and more surprising. The Queen had it produced in quantity, it became a popular item in Cormanthor, and it was known as a Tudyx cube after the name of her house. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small>

[The Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) is a grander form of the same idea. Peter Glans and Dae made it as a five-dimensional demiplane encasing the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) and the [Soul Syphon](/items/the-soul-syphon/) at the heart of [Dis](/places/dis/), to keep the Infanta from reaching them and to end the wars over the power of Death. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small>

At [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s funeral Tylerjinex counted the cube among what the party had done for him: they "left me with the greatest gift I'd ever received (the Tudix Cube)". <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small> An earlier note says he named Kara his heir "after seeing her again and remembering her and having been obsessed with the TUDIX cube." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>

## St. Cloud's cube

### Found aboard the Arielle

The party first met the cube with no idea of its purpose or design. In the first episode of [the Age of the Infanta](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/), [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/), newly enthroned at [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), set out to expel the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) from the rest of [Snowdown](/places/snowdown/). A report came in that St. Cloud's personal ship was in the harbour at Caer Westphal, and the party took the ship in a running battle that ended in the air above the port. The St. Cloud they mortally wounded turned grey and featureless as he died: he was a changeling, one of a management team of changelings who all wore St. Cloud's face. See [the Arielle](/items/the-arielle/) for the battle. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small>

The ship was St. Cloud's own, and his personal quarters held many of his belongings, kept for posterity. One was the silver-filigreed box. Kara took it and put it in her inventory, where it stayed for at least three years of real play. She did not know then that it was a Tudyx Cube. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small> Dave described the box at the time as "sealed by a mysterious force", and it was the same box that St. Cloud had demanded [Roland](/people/roland-deschain/)'s father open. Roland's father refused, and St. Cloud and his brothers killed him. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small>

### The same box in the deep past

In the deep past Roland shot the St. Cloud known as the Traveler, husband of [Karametra](/people/karametra/), and the party took his Tudyx Cube. It was unlocked and empty. Questioned after his death, the Traveler said: "We kept our wedding rings and flowers from the wedding in the box, now it’s empty". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-05-04)</small>

This was the box from the Arielle, at an earlier point in its own history. The party dropped a perfect moonstone into it and closed the lid, which fixed in advance what the box in Kara's inventory contained. They then opened that box, which had never opened for them before, and found a Perfect Dark crystal inside, locked away for about 65 million years. St. Cloud may have lost the box in the Starfall. He found it again a very long time later and could no longer open it. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> Dave, at the table:

> you basically passed yourself something through time, it's a schroedinger's box

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small>

The Anachron needs a Perfect Dark moonstone to travel in time, and this was the stone that powered it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03; Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-dead-mans-hand-breakout.md)</small> See [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/).

## Heyu's cube

In Dead Man's Hand, [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/), Factol of the Faceless, gave a Tudyx Cube to Heyu at [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/) in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). It was not designed for the Deck of Many Things, but a Tudyx Cube has the right size and features to hold it. Heyu's cube is probably one of the originals, passed on by the Factol of [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/) for that purpose. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-10-08-tudyx-cube-spine.md)</small> He told her: "You bear the mark of the Mother of Mysteries and her sisters. You were meant to bear it, and to hold the Cards. Find them, for her; return this piece of her holy Memory." With the charge he gave her the cube. <small>(DM notes; Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-dead-mans-hand-breakout.md)</small>
