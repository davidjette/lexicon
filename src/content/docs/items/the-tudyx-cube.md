**Item · Puzzle box · Made to store the Deck of Many Things · Held by [Heyu](/people/heyu/)**

The **Tudyx Cube** is a puzzle box intended to store [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). It is the same kind of item that [Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/) and Alen Tudyx discovered and began producing with the help of the Infanta of Puzzles. In [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/), Factol of the Faceless, gave one to Heyu at [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/) in [Sigil](/places/sigil/), after charging her to find the Cards.

## Description

The cube hides its contents from magic. It is drawn as a cube within a cube, with an arrow to Brother Montegue's magic word, "STONE". <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The first cubes

Tylerjinex and Alen Tudyx discovered these puzzle boxes and began producing them with the help of the Infanta of Puzzles. One of them was used to store a Perfect Moonstone, which later powered [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), passing over multiple ages of the world when it was brought back by St. Cloud and the party. <small>(Dave)</small>

## Heyu's cube

At Our Lady of Mysteries Brother Montegue told Heyu: "You bear the mark of the Mother of Mysteries and her sisters. You were meant to bear it, and to hold the Cards. Find them, for her; return this piece of her holy Memory." With the charge he gave her the cube. <small>(DM notes)</small>
