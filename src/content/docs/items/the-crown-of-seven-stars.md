**Regalia · Ancient infernal work in a rare infernal ore · Cursed · Seven pledges to an unnameable devil · Given to Master Armhair to carry to the Korramont**

*Also known as:* King Corn's crown · the devil's gift

The **Crown of Seven Stars** is the crown of the Deeptempura, ancient work in a rare infernal ore, and the token of seven pledges **King Corn** made to a devil whose name cannot be spoken. It sat on his crowned corpse on the black dragon **Valtrex**'s hoard beneath the Korramont for three years until the Inevitables reached the island in the black lake, where [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) lifted it out on loan and the party carried it away unworn. In Korth they gave it to **Master Armhair**, an elderly half-elf loremaster of the Hall of Records who was travelling to the Korramont to find Valtrex, without disclosing that it is cursed. Touching it reveals the holder's location to its infernal creator, wearing it permits scrying on the wearer, and attuning to it summons the devil.

## The crown

The crown of the Deeptempura, worn by **King Corn** and on his crowned corpse when the Inevitables reached the island in the black lake beneath the Korramont. **Jelton Ahn**, a Leonin scholar in Korth, identified it on sight as ancient work in a rare infernal ore, and said he believed it had belonged to his own people.

The dragon **Valtrex**, newly freed and speaking on the record, was blunter about ownership and about what the thing is. It represents **seven pledges made to a devil whose name cannot be spoken**. King Corn made them. The crown is the token of them.

> That hammer doesn't belong to King Corn. It belongs to me. So does his crown.

The masoned relief carvings on the walls of the stair chamber above tell the same story from the dwarven side: King Corn discovering the Glitterhame, **forging infernal ore under the guidance of a horned devil**, and later striking a peace treaty with the black dragon. The pact is recorded in the stonework.

## The curse

Three escalating stages:

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Touching it** | Reveals the holder's location to its infernal creator. |
| **Wearing it** | Permits scrying on the wearer. |
| **Attuning to it** | Summons the devil, who offers a pact that cannot be refused without becoming its target. |

## Recovery

[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) negotiated in Draconic with a dragon who had just destroyed his own harpoon rig, laid a **Ring of Lesser Wish** on the hoard, and lifted [KNELL](/items/knell/) and the crown out of it — borrowed, with a warning attached that the crown was the devil's gift. It went into [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/)'s bag **unworn**, and the party's stated intention was either to destroy it or to keep it away from anyone who would use it.

## What they did with it instead

In Korth they gave it away.

**Master Armhair**, an elderly half-elf loremaster of the Hall of Records, had just handed them his life's heresy — that a great dragon called Viagra came to King Jarret Ilwenarn with a bargain, that the sick child Prince Kaius died and was replaced, and that Kaius III is that dragon and the same being as Kaius I. He intended to travel to the Korramont, find Valtrex, and finish his book, and he expected to die doing it.

The party gave him the Crown of Seven Stars to carry down as a gift to the dragon from the three who freed him. They told him to bargain rather than fight, warned him about the fungal infestation, and described the route to the lake.

**They did not disclose that the crown was cursed**, beyond instructing him never to wear it. Jelton Ahn, refusing to accept a treasure of that value for nothing, gave them a small gold case bound with a glass ring containing a **scroll of true resurrection**.

## Where it is now

In the hands of an old scholar bound for a black dragon to have an argument about a book — carrying an object that tells a nameless devil exactly where it is at every moment. The Inevitables no longer hold it. Nobody has attuned to it. Nobody has counted what the seven pledges were, or whether any of them are still owed.
