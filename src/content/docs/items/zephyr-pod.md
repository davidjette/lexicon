**Personal ground vehicle · Sharn · In service**

*Also known as:* Zephyr Pods · pods

A Zephyr Pod is a compact, single-rider ground vehicle powered by a contained air-elemental turbine, which carries it in a smooth, whisper-light glide across Sharn's bridges and lower-level streets. Pods are designed for quick personal travel, are easy to unlock and easy to park, and serve short-range hops between districts. Nine years ago Sharn dismantled its Lightning Rail Sub-Transit and adopted in its place the dual system of [Airbuses](/items/airbus/) and Zephyr Pods, which proved more flexible, safer within the vertical maze of the city, and far easier to reroute as districts expanded or shifted.

## Place in the transit system

Where the Airbus is the city's mass transit, the pod is its errand vehicle: one rider, ground level, and outside the flight law altogether in a city where unauthorised aerial travel means fines, impoundment, and potential arrest.

## Governance and cost

Pods are magically **speed-governed to a maximum travel speed of 60 feet per round**, for safety in crowded urban lanes.

Rentals typically **cost 2 silver per hour**, with **day passes available for 2 gold** for frequent errand-runners and couriers.
