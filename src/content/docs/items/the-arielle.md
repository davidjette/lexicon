**Elemental airship · Gargantuan vehicle · Formerly the Company flagship *Excelsior* · Taken by Temple Holdings LLC · Crashed in the deep past**

*Also known as:* the Excelsior · the Ariel

**The Arielle** is an elemental airship with a soarwood hull, powered by an Elemental Core that traps elementals. Under the name *Excelsior* it was the flagship of the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) and the personal vessel of [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/). [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) took it at Caer Westphal in [Arc III](/history/arc-iii-age-of-the-infanta/), renamed it, and flew it to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), where [Kara](/people/kara/) took command of the elemental that drives it. Lex, the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), was built into the ship before the Second Battle of Motherstone. It was refitted for underwater travel, carried the party to the Moon and R'lyeh in [Arc V](/history/arc-v-starfall/), and crashed in the deep past, leaving [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) behind with Lex inside it.

## The Excelsior

In the time of the Company the ship was the *Excelsior*, "the flagship of the Company’s fleet". It looked "at first glance to be a regular sailing ship with an important exception – it has no sails. Instead of a mast it seems to have an arching pillar that emerge from the aft and settles over the main deck. It is smaller than the last flagship of the Company, the Evening Star, but has many more refined and advanced features which are visible from the outside." It had four main decks, "a large swiveling cannon" mounted in the centre of the forecastle, and below the passenger cabins a skiff bay whose floor "is entirely a glass dome, revealing the dark sea below." <small>(Arc III Part I.)</small>

Its last voyages, from its navigation records: "The Excelsior was moored at Zhentil Keep, then made a short trip to Whitehorn, and then returned to Waterdeep, before it made its final journey to Snowdown." <small>(Arc III Part III.)</small> Its boatswain [Jeggings](/people/jeggings/) described it as "Wainwright St. Cloud’s personal vessel", and listed its stranger missions: an armed force carried "to a remote valley full of backward horse farmers, looking for what the crew nicknamed 'the death child'", "slow, low cruises over the Aunuroch Desert", and a month-long rescue mission on the Dragon Coast that brought "a few broken soldiers and a child" back to Waterdeep. Jeggings "doesn't know who built the Arielle, and St. Cloud is the only one who knows how everything works." <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

In a vision shown to the party by Allo Vera, "the Arielle, when it was still called Excelsior" sets down in a jungle clearing, and an imperial garrison carries crates and "countless dead and wounded" aboard while St. Cloud stands "with an 8 year-old boy asleep in his arms. It is Obi." <small>(The Winking Depths, Part IV.)</small> See [Obi](/people/obi/).

## The taking of the ship at Caer Westphal

The Company had fallen back to its naval seat at Caer Westphal, and the *Excelsior* was docked there "under the command of Wainwright St. Cloud himself", loading cargo to evacuate the island. [Stoth](/people/stoth-youngglade/) staked out the docks, and [Drefan](/people/drefan/) went aboard as "a cloud of soft white mist". <small>(Arc III Part I.)</small> The cargo on the dock was a trap, with Warforged in the crates. Drefan reached the helm "with a dryad, trying to pilot it and fighting off its crew. The ship has flown in the air, done a full barrel roll, and crashed back down into the water, obliterating the docks." <small>(Arc III Parts I and II.)</small>

Dave's summary for the players who missed the session: "the heroes stormed the ship, encountering four elementals and a legendary arcane golem, slew them all with minimal damage to the ship, only casualties were about 40 sailors and 24 civilians". The party had pushed "the water elemental onto the fire elemental, basically killing 20% of the bad guys instantly before they could ever attack you." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-09-15)</small>

Dave went on: "You guys now have a flying yacht and some navigation records and notes as to where the ship has been. The notes indicate that the ships captain signs documents as St Cloud, but is corresponding with a superior named “WSC” who uses the seal of the Chancellor of Waterdeep". The captain's desk held Wainwright's order to "bring me my ship." Dave set a deadline for the new name: "if you’re changing the name of the ship from Excelsior then you need to christen it by the start of the next session". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-09-15; Arc III Part IV)</small>

The party also found a sealed box aboard. Dave: "you all found the box on the Arielle when you first took the ship, it was sealed by a mysterious force. this was the same box that st cloud demanded that Roland's father open. Roland's father refused and st cloud and his brothers killed him." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> It was a [Tudyx Cube](/items/the-tudyx-cube/), taken from St. Cloud's quarters.

## The Arielle at Motherstone

"The heroes fly the newly christened airship Arielle away from the smoldering docks of Caer Westphal, over the forest, up the mountain and into the city of Motherstone," and it settled beside the brass ring around the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/). "The Arielle is safe here, and now it serves those who would thwart her former masters." <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

The name is shared with the elemental in its core. Kara "commands the elemental who occupies the Airship so she's basically in charge". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-02)</small> Kara's player planned for her to spend downtime "communing with Arielle and coming to terms with the complex power dynamic of their relationship". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-14)</small> Asked whether the ship worked without Arielle, Dave answered:

> No, it’s made of soarwood, so it will stay buoyant in the atmosphere without her but would have no propulsion or ballast

> There’s room for one elemental of each type, though, and more are needed to restore all of its power

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> Casters in the party were strong enough "to summon elementals in the off time and bind them for this purpose."

The same day Dave priced the ship's upkeep: a crew of ten at 1,000 gold, 1,000 gold for each replacement, and a policy with the First Harloch Trust for "liability, causality and pension insurance for crew at a cost of 50,000 Gold", with 50,000 more "for an airdock capable of repairing the boat" in Harloch or Motherstone. The ship "needs all its downtime for repair", and "boats are a money sink in all realities". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-15)</small> The party turned the policy down. Asked a year later whether the ship was insured, Dave replied: "no, you guys declined insurance". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-09-07)</small>

## In service

The Arielle's logs sent the party after the "death child" to Whitehorn, over a thousand miles away; Lex put it "a little over three days" at the ship's highest speed. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small> At Whitehorn, after [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/) turned into a zombie from his wounds, Kara "flew the ship in" against the other zombies and saved him. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-21)</small> Drefan wanted the enslaved children he and Roland freed there to live aboard and maintain the ship, and they became Dref's Kids. Dave at first ruled "You already had a crew, the kids do not want to live on a warship" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-25)</small>, and a year later: "Drefs kids run the boat, they’re not a potion factory". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>

The party rarely took it into battle. During the Arcaneum campaign Dave remarked: "It is funny that you guys have a super squad of elementals and a flying boat that you just don't even use". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-02-15)</small> Magnus set the party's damage record with a spell "against the fire elemental on the Ariel the first time you guys saw them scramble the core". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-04-25)</small>

Its jumps are fuelled by [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/): "four moonstones is like four jumps for the ship". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-11-11)</small>

## Lex and the Anachron

In the Arcaneum, the runes of the Anachron were found to closely resemble "the Arcane Core of the Ariel". <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small> The Company's Arcane Core and the ship's Elemental Core are the same device: "Arcane Core" is the Company's name for it, and "Elemental Core" names what it does.

After the Arcaneum Dave told the players: "Lex is now built into the Arielle, you've foiled Asmodeus and know more about some of the prime movers of this whole antagonism." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-05)</small> In the Second Battle of Motherstone, "as soon as he transfers himself to the ship (about 3 rounds in) he loses all connection to the motherstone Lex, which is now dead", "but he doesn't remember transferring at all". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-28)</small> From then on Lex travelled aboard. In the next arc "Pim and Magnus are working with Lex on the Anachron" on the ship. <small>(Arc IV Part V.)</small> See [Pim](/people/pim/) and [Brother Magnus](/people/brother-magnus/).

## Starfall

At the start of Arc V the party paid for a refit: "scratch $25K off your account and the boat is fixed and optimized for underwater travel". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-13)</small> Kara's portent roll "saved not only the ship last sesh (not jeggings)". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-19)</small>

"The heroes ride aboard the Ariel which arcs ever upward toward the sky, in huge arcs across the face of the World Tree." <small>(Arc V Part I.)</small> Underwater, after it "was beset by slimy fish monkeys", Lex read the magical fields at a moon pool and teleported the whole ship: "Three hops later (4 total power stones are used), the ship appears above a tiny coral archipelago in the middle of an impossible immense ocean with the moon shining above." <small>(Arc V Part V.)</small>

At Starfall St. Cloud scrambled the Anachron's core and fled into a time portal. For those who stayed aboard, "The spacecraft shudders and alarms are sounding everywhere," and "Monitors show the craft is failing and without intervention will fall back into the atmosphere and onto the surface of the massive ocean below you." <small>(Arc V Part VI.)</small> The Arielle crashed in the deep past and left the Anachron behind with Lex inside, where it became [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/).

In the deep past, after [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) shot the Traveler, the party put a perfect moonstone into the Traveler's empty Tudyx Cube and closed the lid. They then opened the box they had taken from St. Cloud's quarters on the Arielle, which had never opened for them before. Now it opened, and a Perfect Dark moonstone came out, having been inside for a presumed 65 million years. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> See [Karametra](/people/karametra/).

## At the Egg

In the notes for the final encounter, "The Arielle approaches a huge fold in the brainflesh of the Egg", Elistrae, Eloise and Tymora raise their hands to part it, and "The ship bucks and cracks as the flesh you move through spasms and rocks the ship". <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small> See [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) and [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/).

## Specification

Dave's stat block for the ship, posted in 2024, gives: Gargantuan vehicle; HP 400 (threshold 10); AC 14; approximate cost 185,000 gp; speed "15 x the # of elementals in core"; a pilot at a control wheel "on the bridge or inside the battle bridge" and a crew of five; a load of "Thirty Medium creatures; twenty tons of cargo"; five decks, "The bridge, the main deck and battle deck, the passenger deck, the crew deck and galley, and the cargo hold and skiff"; and an elemental cannon on the battle deck that "can fire an elemental bolt of any type that is present in the Core". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-06)</small> The Arc III sheet gives the same hull with a weaker cannon. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

> **Elemental Core**
>
> The ship’s primary power source is an arcane contraption called an Elemental Core. It can store (and trap) up to five elementals and use their power to operate and enhance the ship. The propulsion, weapons, and security functions of the ship can all draw upon their power. The ship requires at least one elemental to operate and is more powerful the more elementals it contains.

> **Out of Control**
>
> The airship is made of soarwood, so it is atmosphere-buoyant at most elevations. But control over the airship must be maintained to keep it from floating into things or flipping over. An out-of-control airship moves forward at half speed. Each round, it has a 50% chance of rotating in the air. If an airship strikes an unmovable surface like a mountain or the earth while out of control, it is destroyed.
