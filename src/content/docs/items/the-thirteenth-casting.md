**Unique Artifact · Great casting the thirteenth · Korranberg Foundry Trust · Rung twice · DESTROYED**

*Also known as:* the Thirteenth Bell · great casting the thirteenth

The **Thirteenth Casting** was the last of the thirteen great castings of the Korranberg Foundry Trust, an enormous black bell of [Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/) hung on a scaffold at the Bladeworks deep beneath the Korramont. It never left the yard and was never consecrated. Three years ago **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura**, King Corn's daughter, struck it with a rock on the instruction of a whispering thing in the Glitterhame she called the Speaker, killing tens of thousands of dwarves and raising an entire enslaved workforce as undead. It was rung a second time by the Teryaki scout **Hetta**, who cast *shatter* at it, taking the party, the kin camp and the risen dead into one mind. Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) then broke it with [KNELL](/items/knell/) and destroyed every remaining casting in the Bladeworks.

## The object

An enormous black bell of [Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/), hung on a scaffold over a rail cart by a rope-pulley system at the Bladeworks of the Korranberg Foundry, deep beneath the Korramont. The rail tunnel behind it was collapsed. Around it on the forge floor lay tools, weapons and ingots of the same black ore, and several smaller castings sitting inverted beside cold lava flows.

Its surface carried carved runes; to anyone listening too closely while it was still vibrating from the last ringing, the runes and the vibration overlapped into infinite superimposed words. The master's insignia on it was **stamped upside down** — an old smith's curse, meaning the makers were under duress.

## Its paperwork

The surviving leaf of the Trust's private schedule enters it as the last of the great castings, and the entry is a complaint:

> ¶ 13. Great casting the thirteenth. In the yard. Tuning not yet taken. … casting the thirteenth cannot take its tuning until the reference is consigned to us again, and the House's factor writes that the reference will not travel again before the appointed night. The thirteenth therefore waits on Her Radiance's convenience. The Trust notes for the record that it has met its dates and that the choir wants only what the House itself withholds.

<small>**Contradiction on the record.** The Trust says *tuning not yet taken*. The party's own account of the Bladeworks describes it as *tuned, but never consecrated*. Both statements stand.</small>

## The first ringing

Three years ago, **Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura** — King Corn's daughter, heir of a hold enslaved in its own halls — climbed to it and struck it. She used **a rock**, not the proper hammer. She had been listening for months to a whispering thing in the Glitterhame she called *the Speaker*, which knew every guard's movements and told her that ringing the bell would kill the Doves and the Imperials and leave her people safe.

What it actually did: tens of thousands of dwarves dead, an entire enslaved workforce raised as undead, every casket in the sepulcher gallery holding something sealed inside it ever since, and a child's chalk drawing on a wall of a bell with dwarves covering their ears. Sedda fled below as arranged, to wait for an extraction that never came, and waited three years in the shallows before [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) gave her last rites and took her head.

<small>The Empire's own answer to the disaster is in Overseer Velen Marsk's satchel: a writ of warrant signed by **Emperor Kaius III** ordering the death of Corn Deeptempura, Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, Sister Calvane and every dwarf in the foundry, ending with Marsk himself. Most names crossed out. The drains in that room are caked with three-year-old blood.</small>

## The compulsion

The bell recruited. From the moment the Inevitables reached the Grand Stair, [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) heard a whisper he recognised as [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/)'s voice, telling him to deliver the message and then to ring it. He concealed it for several sessions. He argued the case out loud — *"whoever rings the bell, they control the magic"* — and then denied having raised it. He was tackled and restrained once, purged by a dwarven potion once, and at the Bladeworks he critically failed the save, swallowed the wax meant for his ears, drew his mace and made for the bell before Dario grappled him to the platform and [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) blanketed the scaffold in darkness.

It also killed a child. **Egg Roll**, a duergar boy who had mapped the whole foundry level by hand, ran through a ring of holy fire to reach the bell and died doing it. Eric revivified him, burned and disfigured.

## The second ringing, and the end

Gemma set an alarm twenty feet around the bell and filled the Bladeworks with smoke before the party descended to the lake. The alarm fired. **Hetta** — a Teryaki scout, mould across his face and mushrooms at his lips — was at the casting, and cast *shatter* at it.

The toll took everyone: the party, the kin camp in the next hall, and the risen dead climbing the shaft, all into one mind — a thirst for blood and flesh and an urge to tear one's own skin off. Everyone inside it wounded themselves.

Dario came out of it first, spent his last channel divinity to make [KNELL](/items/knell/) a sacred weapon, swung, and rolled a natural 1 — the bell clanged and the recoil threw his hand back. He swung again and broke it. There was no ring: a tremendous cracking sound, a rift almost splitting it in two, and the metal folding in on itself, symmetry lost, collapsing into a twisted ribbon. He and the runesmith Bofric were deafened for hours.

He then walked bell to bell through the Bladeworks and destroyed every remaining casting standing there. The freed dragon Valtrex destroyed the original molds himself. **Nothing is cast from that mountain again.**

## What it means for the count

Thirteen great castings were commissioned; eleven were delivered; two were in the yard. The thirteenth is broken, and every small casting in the Bladeworks with it, and Dario has since broken two more in Sharn. **Ten bells remain** — at least one of them a Master in Korth, great casting the ninth, hanging in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace itself. The [Original](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) is untouched, and will not travel again before the appointed night.
