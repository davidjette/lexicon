**Cruiser · The Starsong Awakens · Stolen · Crewed by devotees of the Red Eye**

*Also known as:* the stolen Istus Cruiser

**The Istus Cruiser** is a cruiser of [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/) crewed by devotees of the Red Eye, [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/). It first appeared in the epilogue to the finale of [Brownsleeve City Nights](/history/brownsleeve-city-nights/) on 2024-11-01. Its crew include [Weld](/people/umwelt/) and [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/), and Marcel's backstory places it with the robot resistance as a stolen ship. Who it was stolen from is unknown.

Istus-class clippers are light, fast and agile vessels that [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) introduced after the [Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), when it decommissioned its heavy Zarus-class battle cruisers. The SSJ Vanguard is one of them.

## History

JL, as DM, asked Dave and Nico in October 2024 to build new level-3 characters who would each be a devotee of the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-10-26)</small> The finale of Brownsleeve City Nights ended with an epilogue showing an Istus Cruiser, and JL announced that the new characters would be its crew, with Barrias Ruby-Eye and the legacy cast put on hold. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>

## Crew

- **Weld** (Umwelt), played by Dave, a warforged farmer and Wild Heart barbarian, a liberated farming and ranch-hand unit who joined the resistance. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02)</small>
- **Marcel Martel**, played by Nico, a high elf arcane trickster and a once-famous Waterdeep musician and actor. Nico's backstory places him with the resistance aboard "the stolen Istus Cruiser", fighting for robots since Bureau agents dismantled his wife, Robin Martel, under the Starweaver Act. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-07)</small>

## The Istus class

After decommissioning the heavy Zarus-class battle cruisers, among them the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), Astral Command took its fleet in a new direction: a line of light-weight, fast and agile vessels. To compensate for their thin hulls, Istus-Class Clippers carry Arcane Artillery, which casts spells to protect the vessel. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships)</small> The [Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/)'s dossier describes the class as built for stealth exploration, extreme speed and easy maneuverability. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

**Arcane Artillery** is a legendary wondrous item that looks like a giant cannon but is a powerful and precise magical wand weighing two tons. Mounted on a spelljamming vessel and attuned, like a spelljamming helm, by a spellcaster, it casts protective spells on that vessel: *absorb elements*, *haste*, *invisibility*, *protection from energy* and *shield*. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New magic items; New ships)</small>

### The SSJ Vanguard

The Company's dossier lists the **SSJ Vanguard**, an Istus-Class Clipper, as active, its location unknown. Rumours of its top-secret destination range from Realmspace to deep within [the Vodoni Empire](/organizations/the-vodoni-empire/). Its Commanding Officer, Capt. Sandy Titzandassia ([Hyperion "Sandy" Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/)), and its Executive Officer, Lt. Commander [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/), both served with [Caprica](/people/caprica/) on the SSJ Zanzibar, which makes its command crew of special interest to the Company's Board. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>
