**Item · Sentient weapon · Artifact of the Weathervein treasure · A Weathervein bound without consent**

**Whelm** is a sentient weapon and one of the three weapons of the Weathervein treasure, bound with the one member of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/) who did not consent to it. [Keraptis](/people/penguinus-weathervein/) kept it in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/), where the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) won it in August 2022 along with Wave and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/). In September 2022, after the party finished the mountain's puzzles, Whelm told them exactly what to do.

## In White Plume Mountain

Keraptis's riddle poem placed Whelm across a great cavern:

> Crost cavern vast, where chain-links rattle\
> Lies Whelm, past water-spouts double.

<small>(Keraptis; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

The party held Whelm, Wave and Blackrazor by 2022-08-14. [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) could use Whelm because he was a fighter. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> The same morning Dave set out how [the Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/), Scrunt's order, saw the three weapons:

> I guess for your sake Scrunt the Dawn excepts to take all three and put them in storage now that we crippled their two main foes for them the soggies and the brotherhood\
> They begrudgingly acknowledge Weathervein as owning Wave but don’t fully trust her not to use it\
> And who knows with blackrazor, obvs the Brotherhood can’t get it back but what is it\
> Whelm was theirs

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14; "excepts" sic)</small>

## The Weathervein who did not consent

On 2022-09-16, after the party finished the dungeon's puzzles, Whelm spoke to them and told them exactly what to do. Dave called her "the ultimate quest giver of this whole campaign", and identified her as "The one Weathervein that didn’t consent to being bound into ancient artifacts from the treasure hoard". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

By Dave's account all three weapons have true natures, and natures corrupted by Keraptis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> Nico asked whether the spirits could be banished from the weapons, and Dave answered "Maybe!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small> Dave later proposed that the party unlock all three weapons and let them agree to the party's pact, "and we’ll just have to overcome their petty demands in critical moments", and looked ahead to a campaign to "infiltrate a mind flyer colony and rid these artifacts of a sentient curse". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16; 2022-09-17)</small>
