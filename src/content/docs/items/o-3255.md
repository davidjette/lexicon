**Tome · Volumes XIII to XVI of the Ars Calixia · Company classification Epsilon-0 · Lost in Undermountain**

**O-3255** is the Company's reference for a gold and adamantine-bound tome holding volumes XIII to XVI of the *Ars Calixia*, the magnum opus and autobiography of the elvish archmage [Calix](/people/calix/), penned with a magic quill circa 100 DR. Its contents are an [oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/), a self-keeping secret, and [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) believes it holds a higher density of written oblivia than any other tome of the work. It passed through the library of the House of Wonder in [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) and disappeared in a robbery around 1651 DR, which the Company believes was directed by Halaster Blackcloak, and the missing tomes found their way into [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/). The sister volumes of the *Ars Calixia* reside in the personal library of the Chairman of the Board. In Undermountain the party recovered the Company's mission file for O-3255 and obtained directions to the Ars Calixia: a bookcase in an office on Level 2.

> The location of O-3255 is yet unknwon to the Company. Recent evidence suggests it passed through the library of the House of Wonder in Waterdeep before disappearing in a robbery around 1651 DR. Other recent findings lead he Company to believe this theft was at the direction of Halastar Blackcloak, and that the missing tomes and items found their way into Undermountain. Blackcloak is almost certainly aware of the nature of O-3255 and has likely isolated it from other books, symbols, and vulnerable media, and from himself and his person, though his reputation as a "Mad" mage may indicate exposure to Oblivia, both known and unknown to the Company.

>
> Company staff are not to open, read, or otherwise attempt to understand or reveal the contents of O-3255. If encountered, staff are strictly directed to isolate and deposit O-3255 into a closed, secure, water-resistant vessel and return immediately to the department office for immediate containment.

> Description:
>
> O-3255 is a gold and adamantine-bound tome approximately 24 by 18 inches by its cover and 4 inches thick. It is estimated to contain just under 1,000 vellum and heartsblood ink pages, penned with magic quill by the elvish archmage Calix circa 100 DR. It represents Volume XIII - XVI of the ARS CALIXIA, the wizard's magnums opus and autobiography. Its sister volumes I - XII and XVII - XXV reside in the personal library of the Chairman of the Board.
>
> The contents of O-3255 are a self-keeping secret - an "oblivia". O-3255 is possibly the longest known written example of an oblivia in existence. Based on our current knowledge and from research into similar objects in possession of the Company, attempts to read or remember the words written in its pages always fail, leaving the reader with acute mental stress, inability to remember or speak, and in some cases, temporary insanity and psychic trauma. Tests conducted on its sister volumes XIII - XII found frequent examples of unobservable oblivia throughout those books, most of which had gone ignored by scholars for centuries, including many who reproduced the tomes by hand, oblivia and all, with no memory of doing so.
>
> O-3255 is believe to contain a much higher frequency and density of written oblivia than any other tome in the Ars Calixia, which likely led to it becoming lost from the wider collection and out of recorded memory.
