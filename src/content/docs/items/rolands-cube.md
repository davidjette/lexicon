**Wonderous Object · Polarized moonstone · Built by Roland Deschain with the Deepmaker of Motherstone · Three charges · Totally unique**

*Also known as:* the Cube · the banishment cube

**Roland's Cube** is a wonderous object, a perfect cube of polarized moonstone sliced into nine cubes per side, made by [Roland Deschain](/people/roland-deschain/) with the help of the Deepmaker of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). It is detonated or triggered with a separate Key, and it holds four spells. [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/) was captured in it during the Second Battle of Motherstone. In the Time of Troubles, Roland was himself trapped inside it, and the Cube was recovered broken.

## History

- **Its making.** Before the party's session in Punis, Dave recorded that Roland "spent a whole off session doing that and also spent like 25,000 gp" on the Cube. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-01-02)</small>
- **The Second Battle of Motherstone.** Dave's account of the battle's end: "they destroyed two of the ships and stopped the invasion, the last ship jumped back to Sigil with Couch on board, they captured Carlysle in Rolands cube and then threw him and the Palantir into the Obliviator." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small> See [the Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and [the Palantir](/items/the-palantir/).
- **Roland enters the banishment cube.** During [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) Roland went into the Cube's banishment demiplane himself. Dave awarded him 5,000 experience for it and ruled that the award would not be repeated. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-02-19)</small>
- **Roland trapped.** In a battle against [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) during [the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/), Roland became trapped in his own Cube. In the same battle the party took St. Cloud's ship and raised Percival. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-04-09)</small> Dave, the next day: "I did not know whether my plan to get roland would work". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-04-10)</small>
- **Recovered broken.** At the climax of the Time of Troubles, when Zeal rose into orbit and the Grey Sage was captured alive, "Roland’s Cube is in hand, though broken." Dave: "How to recover Roland from the Cube and what do with St Cloud remains to be decided." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-07-17)</small>

## The item document

The text of the Cube's item document, as Dave wrote it and posted it to the players. Under each spell's name the document also gives the standard entry for Banishment, Stinking Cloud, Daylight and Slow.

> **Roland’s Cube**
>
> Wonderous Object *(requires attunement)* 25,000 GP
>
> Casting Time: 1 Action
>
> Range: Touch or Thrown (20/60 ft)
>
> Components: 1000g Dwarven Metal (to repair)
>
> **Origin and Features**
>
> Roland’s Cube is a uniquely perfect cube of polarized moonstone, magically sliced into nine cubes per side, and articulated by arcane mechanisms of a hybrid dwarven/Sigilese design. It is a totally unique object created by Roland Deschain with the help of the Deepmaker of Motherstone.
>
> **Charges**
>
> The Cube has 3 charges. A Half Blast expends 1 charge, a Full Blast expends 2. A Suicide Blast uses all 3 and damages the Cube.  A charge is restored by exposing the Cube to moonlight for four hours. All three charges are restored by leaving the Cube out in the moonlight for 8 consecutive hours.
>
> **Setting and Detonating the Cube**
>
> When you use Roland’s Cube, first you **remove its Key** then you either **throw it or attach it to a surface** (such as a table or a section of floor or wall). The Cube can attach to any rigid or semi-rigid surface (such as a monster’s flesh) but cannot attach to liquid or semi-liquid (such as a flowing ooze). The Cube will stick to a valid surface if thrown from within 60 ft with that intent.
>
> The Cube cannot be deactivated without its **Key**. You decide what triggers the Cube when you deploy it, and you can deactivate or trigger it at any time with the detonator switch on the Key. If you are holding the Key unsecured by a rope or chain while you suffer damage, you must make a Constitution saving throw (DC is the amount of the damage) to keep from dropping it.
>
> You can also set other remote triggers, including touching or trying to disarm the Cube, approaching within a certain distance of it, or seeing or examining it, or manipulating the object on which the Cube is attached. Once the Cube is triggered, this effect ends.
>
> You can further refine the trigger so the spell activates only under certain circumstances or according to physical characteristics (such as height or weight), creature kind (for example, the ward could be set to affect aberrations or drow), or alignment. You can also set conditions for creatures that don’t trigger the Cube, such as those who say a certain password. When you inscribe the Cube, choose **Arcane Demolitions** or **Arcane Reliquary.**
>
> **Arcane Demolitions.** When triggered, the Cube erupts with magical energy in a 20-foot-radius sphere centered on the Cube. The sphere spreads around corners. Each creature in the area must make a Dexterity saving throw. A creature takes 5d8 acid, cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage on a failed saving throw (your choice when you set the Cube), or half as much damage on a successful one. Add an additional 5d6 of the chosen damage type per charge expended when taking this action (Full Blast or Suicide Blast).
>
> **Arcane Reliquary.** You can store a prepared spell of 3rd level or lower in the Cube by casting it as part of setting the Cube and using at least one charge. The spell must target a single creature or an area (or valid creatures within an area near the Cube, if the spell calls for multiple targets). The spell being stored has no immediate effect when cast in this way. When the Cube is triggered, the stored spell is cast. If the spell has a target, it targets the creature that triggered the Cube. If the spell affects an area, the area is centered on the Cube. If the spell summons hostile creatures or creates harmful objects or traps, they appear as close as possible to the Cube and attack it. If the spell requires concentration, it lasts until the end of its full duration. If you spend an extra charge (Full Blast), you can extend the range of your spell by 10x or increase the duration by one order. (round to min, min to hour, hour to day, day to month)
>
> Roland’s Cube contains four spells which can prepared inside the Cube without casting or using a spell slot. It takes one action to dismantle and rearrange the sides of the Cube to prepare a different spell, and a bonus action to set a different Blast Level for the spell with the Key.  The Key must be in the Cube in order to set, move, disarm or change the spell contained within the Cube.
>
> **Roland’s Cube contains these spells:**
>
> **Banishment**
>
> ***When casting Banishment with Roland’s Cube,*** *the demiplane is contained* <ins>*within*</ins> *the Cube.*
>
> *If you expend all three charges (Suicide Blast) targeting on a creature native to this plane, the creature is trapped until you repair the cube and use a charge to let it out, after which it is free.*
>
> *If the Cube moves off the plane in which it first contained a creature, the creature is freed.*
>
> **Stinking Cloud**
>
> **Daylight**
>
> ***When casting Stinking Cloud or Daylight the Cube is always the point of origin.***
>
