**Unique Artifact · Rare · Four known · Two Imperial, two rebel · Undeciphered**

The **Ancient Stone Tablets** are four stone slabs carved in an unknown ancient language which, brought together, are thought to foretell the coming of a great time of change in Eberron. The first was unearthed by construction in [Newham](/places/newham/) and its second half recovered outside the town; a second was found in an ancient tomb in the Blade Desert near the mostly abandoned village of Arden Crest. Both were turned over to the Empire. Dwarfs uncovered the third somewhere in the Ironroot Mountains, and the fourth is held by rebels in a stronghold outside Fairhaven. Emperor Kaius took an interest in the tablets, and [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) were tasked with recovering the remaining two. None of the four has been reported since the first campaign.

## The four

**Tablet 1:**\
Construction in [Newham](/places/newham/) unearthed a broken tablet in an unknown ancient language. The Empire believe this may be part of one of 4 ancient tablets that foretell the coming of a great time of change in Eberron. Emperor Kaius has taken interest in these tablets.

[Esther](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Locke](/people/locke-pierce/) and [LaBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) are tasked with finding the other half of this tablet, which they uncover outside of [Newham](/places/newham/) . It is turned over to the Empire.

**Tablet 2:**\
A second tablet is found in an ancient tomb deep in the Blade Desert near the mostly abandoned village of Arden Crest . It is turned over to the Empire.

**Tablet 3:**\
Dwarfs uncovered the 3rd tablet somewhere in the Ironroot Mountains .

**Tablets 4:**\
Known to be in the hands of rebels in a rebel stronghold outside of Fairhaven . Where the tablet was uncovered is unknown.

Tablets 1 and 2 were guarded by mechanical creatures. Unknown if the other 2 also were.

## The tasking

The Unforeseen crew has been tasked to find the remaining two tablets. Per [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/):

> The ancient tablets themselves cannot be decoded until we have all 4. The rebels too were after these tablets, and I believe they have been found by more powerful rebel forces than I was ever involved in. One was found by the dwarfs to the East in the Ironroot Mountains. Last I knew they planned to move it to Rebel territory in Zilargo, if it has happened yet, I do not know.. Another is being kept in Aundair at a rebel stronghold believed to be somewhere near Fairhaven.

## Significance

When all 4 are together they can foretell the coming of a great time of change in Eberron.

## What became of them

None of the four has been reported since the first campaign. The tablets belong to that campaign and to [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) — the party that later became the Empire's architects. In the decade-plus since, no dossier or seized document has mentioned a stone tablet again.
