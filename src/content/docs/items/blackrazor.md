**Item · Greatsword · Sentient artifact of the Weathervein treasure · Crestus bound within · Guarded near Saltmarsh**

**Blackrazor** is a sentient greatsword and one of the three weapons of the Weathervein treasure, with [Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/) of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/) inside it. [Keraptis](/people/penguinus-weathervein/) kept it in [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/), "Underneath inverted ziggurat", where the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) won it in August 2022. [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) carried it through the rest of the dungeon and resisted its influence. A few hundred years later, in the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), it lies in a lair near [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) guarded by Hellwood herself, a dying demikraken.

## In White Plume Mountain

Keraptis's riddle poem placed the sword last among the three weapons:

> Blackrazor yet remains to be won\
> Underneath inverted ziggurat.\
> That garnered, think not that you’re done\
> For now you’ll find you are caught

<small>(Keraptis; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

The party held Blackrazor, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and Wave by 2022-08-14. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> By Dave's account all three weapons have true natures and natures corrupted by Keraptis, and Blackrazor is evil even apart from that corruption. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

## Its will

Asked whether Gabriella would resist the sword if it began telling her what to do, Dave answered:

> It wants to end the world one soul at a time so I don’t think so? It’s like a super devil

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

Blackrazor stands against Gabriella's patron, [Hafgufa](/lore/hafgufa/):

> Blackrazor is a competitor to that, at least with Crestus inside it. Could be it could be brought to serve hoff goofa in which case who knows, but otherwise, it is a very dangerous item to all of us

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

Dave judged it "worse than Weathervein probably", the sword of the same family. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small> For Gabriella to bond it through a warlock's pact, "I would have to get Blackrazor or such to also serve Hoff goofa or something", or "switch to hexblade and serve the sword". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

Gabriella could not use Blackrazor unattuned, and while the party rested in the Oni's luxury cell the sword tried to make her attune to it. Wave and [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/) come to the hand at the sword's will, and by Dave's ruling "blackrazor should be able to do that at its will to". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-19)</small>

## Gabriella Hellwood and the sword

After the party finished the dungeon's puzzles, Dave proposed that they unlock all three weapons and let them agree to the party's pact, "and we’ll just have to overcome their petty demands in critical moments". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> He planned a ninth fighter level for Gabriella so that she could use Indomitable "whenever blackrazor tries to force me into killing someone". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small> He also wrote that "Killing an elder brain with blackrazor is pretty final bossy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

On 2022-09-23 Dave posted an image of Blackrazor "held by a female pirate". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small> The next morning he set down how Gabriella had carried it:

> Gabriella was absolutely ready to hear the Eldabrin’s message and spent the last two sessions of White Plume resisting it with saves and stuff as basically an NPC\
> I decided during that game that the death of Scrunt was very impactful for Gabriella and she resolved to overcoming the negative influence of blackrazor and getting home\
> And since we put the swords away by the time we got to Eldabrin she wasn’t hearing it, but if Dolores had given into Weathervein or if Gabriella lost some of those saves,\
> Could have gone differently.

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24)</small>

## The demikraken's lair

Hellwood's pact made her an eldritch horror, the Dunwater Horror that fought Dagon at [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/), and kept her alive "a few hundred years". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31; 2026-03-07)</small> After the Dead Man's Hand session in which Hellwood gave her last words through [Maverick](/people/maverick/)'s quill, Dave wrote: "She’s lucky she didn’t get consumed by blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-21)</small> The next day:

> Now I guess there’s a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small>

Asked by Nico whether Hellwood was guarding the underwater temples of her friend [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/), Dave answered: "Yeah that’s what the whole thing was about!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small>

**Inspiration:** JL suggested Elric's cursed sword Stormbringer as an inspiration for Weathervein. Dave: "That’s probably the inspiration for blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-05-14)</small>
