**Implant enhancement module · BioTec prototype · Anti-surveillance countermeasure · Project lead: Dr. Joanne Menka · Prototype stolen, Sharn Ep 1**

*Also known as:* the Ghost Chip · "the prototype" (BioTec letterhead)

The Ghost Chip is a small implant enhancement module that makes the person carrying it invisible and inaudible to the [Arcane Eye network](/items/the-arcane-eye-network/). It was developed by [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/), an Imperial contractor, under project lead Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), and no other device of its kind is on record. The rebellion learned of the prototype from a recovered BioTec letterhead document, confirmed it through the technician [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), and stole it from the BioTec labs in Sharn Ep 1; the first three chips fitted to living people were fitted to the rebel operatives [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) at the Nest.

## What It Does

The Ghost Chip is an implant enhancement module designed to make the user undetectable to Arcane Eye surveillance systems (ArcEyes). When installed you can use an Action to turn on the Ghost chip. When turned on, the chip disrupts the scry-signature ArcEyes rely on, by creating a persistent blind spot around the implanted individual. (Wearer is invisible and can't be heard by ArcEyes.) Free to turn off. Once per long rest. The effect is not concealment from people; it conceals the wearer from the ArcEyes alone.

## Provenance

A BioTec letterhead document recovered by operative **Foxtail** during a [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) lightning rail infiltration mission referenced a prototype device labeled "Ghost Chip." The document indicated the prototype was nearing completion and entering final testing. That was all the rebellion had: a name on a page. <small>The rebel roster spells the alias Foxtale and lists it as Gemma Corso's. Whether the lightning-rail operative was the same person is not recorded.</small>

Following this lead, [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) conducted a covert investigation. She tracked [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), a junior enhancement-technician at BioTec, to an upper city bar, [The Astra](/places/the-astra-bar/), after he got off of work. After several weeks of surveillance and strategic intoxication, Kross revealed additional details to Zero not present in the recovered document. According to Kross:

- The Ghost Chip is an implant enhancement module designed to make the user undetectable to Arcane Eye surveillance systems (ArcEyes).
- The project is overseen by Lead Scientist Joanne Menka, a senior researcher with full Imperial clearance. The decision to exclude ArcEyes from the facility was made by Menka herself, citing "data contamination risks."
- Kross also mentioned a restricted laboratory door off the East lab he's never had access to / does not know what work is being done in there.

## The Theft

Newly arrived in Sharn, [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) were handed the job at their first meeting with the rebel operative Izaak: get inside BioTec and steal the prototype. They befriended Kross while he was drunk, walked him home, and lifted his keycard.

The break-in went wrong immediately. Private security confronted them, a panicking scientist opened a containment door, and three undead-warforged hybrid units nobody outside the programme had ever seen came out of it. The trio barely survived. They left with the Ghost Chip and a folder of encrypted high-level data, and that folder is where the name **Project EDEN** first came from.

The cost, as reported by Menka to the Lord Commander at POSÉ the following night: two hired guards and one senior scientist dead, **Unit 0.3** found beside the scientist's body having executed a full force pulse at shutdown, Kross missing, and the prototype gone.

> **Esther:** "And the units?"\
> **Menka (whispering):** "Unit 0.3 was discovered beside the scientist's body… Someone knew exactly where to strike. The Ghost Chip is missing, And Kross…"\
> **Esther:** "Clean the mess and find Kross."

## Issue to the Inevitables

At the Nest, after Zero helped them redact and crack the stolen files, [Izaak](/people/izaak/) implanted each of the three with Ghost Chips. From that night on they moved through a surveilled city with a hole cut around them.

## The Teleportation Chip

Gemma also carried a **teleportation chip** in her head — BioTec hardware, crafted for [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) as a gift to [Faith](/people/faith/), and stolen. Whether it was one of the chips Izaak implanted at the Nest or came out of the BioTec labs on the first night is not recorded. It worked — it pulled a whole rescued group out of the undercity and back to Zilspar Farm 02 in one jump — and it was trackable the entire time. When it was stolen, Hallorn had it tracked. Its teleportation pings had bounced across the city, including under his brother's club, The Vault Below, the night it was broken into; it was shown pinging outside the city quite a lot; and only when the trio teleported to the Upper City safe house did his people finally pinpoint its exact location.

> "The chip was never truly hers."\
> — Hallorn d'Lyrandar

Children of Ember cultists seized Gemma in the street, restrained her, tore the chip out and destroyed it on the spot. The trio killed two of them and dragged the third inside for questioning.

By Nico's account, Gemma found the teleportation chip in the year before Sharn, on her secret mission for the rebellion: it was "found on Cobb Cornwell, labeled as a gift from Hallorn to Faith". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-05-09)</small> <small>(Cobb is a variant of [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/).)</small>
