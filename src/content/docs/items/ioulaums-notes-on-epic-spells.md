**Document · A clue in the Chronicle · Three tiers of epic spells · Two versions, 2021 and 2022**

*Also known as:* Notes on Epic Spells

Ioulaum's Notes on Epic Spells is a document from [the Time of Troubles](/history/arc-vi-time-of-troubles/) that lists epic spells by tier, with the book and page where each can be found, and marks with a star the ones "necessary for enclavement". It closes with an Orcish symbol, *avatara*, and an order to bring anything bearing it to [Zeal](/places/zeal/). It could be found in [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/), and the party missed it there when they fled with Lex. Dave gave it to the players in October 2021, and in March 2022 posted a second version from which Move Planet had been removed. **Move Planet** is the spell [Karametra](/people/karametra/) cast to put the moon in the path of the Egg.

## In the Chronicle

In July 2021 Dave asked the players about it:

> Did I share with you all a item/clue labeled "Ioulaum’s Notes on Epic Spells"
>
> you would have found it when exploring The Chronicle

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-31</small>

Revan's player LaMont did not remember it. Dave: "Hmm, well it seems that's something you all missed since you decided to flee with Lex instead of facing down the Sages". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-07-31)</small> The same day, asked whether the party had "discovered anything about “epic spells” yet", Stonecypher's player Nico answered "No epic spells", and Dave, going through his notes, found "there was a big clue/item to raid that you guys didn't get cause you bailed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-07-31)</small> See [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) and [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/).

## The first version

Dave posted the document on 23 October 2021, at the session where the party charmed [the Waning One](/people/the-waning-one/) and [Valen](/people/valen/) died in acid. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-10-23)</small> The same evening he told the players that the epic spells included "Spheresail to make Spelljammer Helms and Breach Crystal Sphere to travel through the interstellar medium", and that "A lot of these epic spells drain levels/Xp from the caster lol". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> See [Spelljamming](/lore/spelljamming/).

## Epic magic

Dave on the cost: "10th level magic is multi-speller caster shit btw", and "it also requires sacrificing levels, so you may actually end up using your spare levels for casting epic spells". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03)</small> The next day: "The epic magic you guys talked about for terraforming the moon or making an island is real, the Netherese have it". His quest board for the era listed "Motherstone, finish clone statuary, learn epic magic?" and "Avalon, learn Charm of Making, free Merlin". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-04)</small> See [Netheril](/places/netheril/) and [Steve Merlin](/people/steve-merlin/).

## The second version, and the scroll of Move Mountain

Dave posted the document again on 19 March 2022. It is the same text, except that tier XI has two spells:

> **XI**
>
> - Worldweave: (Alteration)  - Elven, Unknown, possibly known as the ‘Charm of Making’?
> - Breach / Seal Crystal Sphere: (Alteration) reversible - Encyclopedia Arcana page 11

<small>— Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-03-19</small>

At that session the party interrupted a Netherese ritual and took one of the starred spells:

> One of our most dangerous battles yet I think and still not over - you did interrupt the ritual despite their confidence and damaged the likelihood their plans  will succeed, you recover a “scroll” of Move Mountain, yet Another epic level spell in your arsenal of tools to orchestrate your endgame. You downed two Netherese archmages and a few aquanauts - 2000 xp

<small>— Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-03-19</small>

## Move Planet and the Charm of Making

Karametra had the dragons build a platinum temple as the component for a casting of Move Planet, to push the moon into the Egg's path, and the party defended the temple while she cast it. See [Karametra](/people/karametra/) and [Dragon Mountain](/places/dragon-mountain/). Kara learned the Charm of Making in Avalon to free Merlin from his oak. <small>([Kara](/people/kara/).)</small> The black action on Karametra's Ally Card is "Charm of Making: Cast the Wish spell."
