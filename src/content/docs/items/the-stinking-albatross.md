**Ship · Galley · Captured by Neptune's Rejects · Ghosts of Twatmarsh**

The **Stinking Albatross** is a galley captured by the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) in November 2021, in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/). The party stormed a meeting of [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/)'s town council to announce the capture and demand a commission for the naval protection of Saltmarsh Harbor, and the council voted to approve the expenditure. <small>(Dave's session notes, "Salvage Operation – Part I". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-11, 2021-11-13)</small> That night the crowd admired "its new tiny navy and its protection by the Daughters of Neptune". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>
