**Musical instrument · Coiled brass · King Corn's prototype · One original, more now being made · Carried by the Inevitables**

*Also known as:* Corn's horn · King Corn's horn · the Horn of Morning · the answer to the bell

The **Horn of Mourning** is a coiled brass musical instrument, King Corn Deeptempura's own unornamented prototype, which when played with [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/) suppresses the influence of the bells entirely for as long as it sounds. It was found in a cache beneath the floor of Corn's secret study under the Korramont, first played by the Teryaki runesmith **Bofric**, who used it to clear the compulsion of [the Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/) out of [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s mind, and named at a meeting [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) convened in Sharn, after which the clergy of the continent began teaching the tune congregation by congregation. The bells require Shavat and a foundry working under the Empire's draft; the horn can be made by any competent brass-worker with the right drawings, and the drawings survived. The original is carried by the Inevitables.

## Description

Coiled brass, unornamented, an unusual asymmetrical shape, **two mouthpieces**, and smith-marked with King Corn Deeptempura's own mark. It was found wrapped in oilcloth in a cache beneath the floor of his secret study, under the rug, behind a sliding panel, down a hidden stair, inside a spell-sealed chest that opened to a key the party was already carrying. **Brass — not Kyber.**

It is a prototype.

## What it does

Played with [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/), a horn of Corn's design suppresses the influence of the bells entirely for as long as it sounds. The first demonstration was clinical: [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) had been under the compulsion of the Speaker in the Glitterhame for days and had twice tried to ring [the Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/) with his own hands. Bofric played the tune on a whistle and the compulsion *softened*. Gemma produced the horn; Bofric recognised the king's smith-mark on it, leaned into it, and played the same tune. The sound of the bell left Eric's mind entirely.

Bofric named the art [arcane harmonics](/items/arcane-harmonics/) and was immediately honest about the limit:

> It can only maybe drown out the sound as long as you play it. To break the bell, you would need to break it with the hammer that made it.

**Limits:** it masks the [Black Noise](/lore/the-black-noise/); it does not break a bell; and it stops the moment the player stops.

## The name

The name was given in Sharn, at a meeting [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) convened at the hospital with a representative of every faith on the continent in the room. Bofric put the horn to his lips and played, and the clergy took the tune away with them to teach congregation by congregation. They named it **the Horn of Mourning**.

<small>One session recap renders it the *Horn of Morning*. Both spellings are in the record.</small>

## Reproduction

The horn stopped being a relic the week the party got back to Sharn. Research established the method of its making; [Corn's folio](/items/corn-s-folio/) carries drawings of horns worked against harmonic-resonance mathematics, trying to solve a problem in the common symmetries of multiple waveforms. **Doppler Klink** built wands of magic missile for the party at cost and then got to work making Horns. Between the original, the notes and one dwarf who reads Corn's shorthand, the rebellion can now manufacture instruments and train players for them.

**Known players:**

- **Bofric** — Teryaki runesmith, bent, milk-eyed on the left, fingers stained with lampblack. Translated the song out of the folio and played it first. Not a combatant, and says so.
- [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) — took proficiency in Performance and in the horn as an instrument, making her the party's second player.
- **The clergy of Sharn** — learning it congregation by congregation.

<small>The party's own first answer to "who plays it" was [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) — the most famous bard alive, and believed dead. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s charisma was judged wrong for it.</small>

## Right now

The original is in Korth with the Inevitables, alongside [KNELL](/items/knell/) and the folio, while they work an Imperial cover toward the Summer Palace and the Ivory Lazaret. An outstanding item on their own list is to teach the fanfare and the horn's construction to someone who will remain behind — because if the chorus starts while all three of them are inside the Empire, the horns are what the continent has.
