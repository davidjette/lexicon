**Item · Instrument of capital punishment · Installed on the balcony of [the High Court](/places/the-high-court/), Ministry of Law, [Korth](/places/korth/)**

The **Iron Maiden** is an instrument of capital punishment installed on the balcony of [the High Court](/places/the-high-court/) of the Ministry of Law in [Korth](/places/korth/). It kills the condemned while preserving the body intact for [second service](/lore/second-service/), satisfying both durations a Karrnathi death sentence specifies. It stands one level up, in plain view of anyone standing before the panel of judges. The one proceeding recorded beneath it, the trial of [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/), ended in a remand to the garrison, and the Maiden went unused.

## What it does

The Iron Maiden **kills the condemned while preserving the body intact for reuse**.

Under [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) a death sentence specifies two durations: how long the pain of the killing is to last, and how long the condemned owes the Emperor afterward in undeath. The Maiden satisfies both — a killing of specified duration that leaves a corpse fit for [second service](/lore/second-service/).

## Where it sits

The High Court is reached by a set of stairs from the ground floor of the Ministry of Law, past Imperial guards who do very little. Beyond the panel and the condemned, the balcony with the Maiden inside is in plain view of the whole chamber. Behind the same complex, in the northeast corner, the jail's corpse chute empties into a yard where the dead are stacked for collection. The route from the balcony to the stack is short.

## Under it

On the morning [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) stood condemned on ten charges, the Maiden was where she was going. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) read the charges beneath it, then abandoned the advocate's role and testified as kin. The panel found the documentation insufficient and remanded the condemned to the garrison instead.

Bantide reached the corpse chute anyway before the day was out, under her own power, having cast *feign death* in her cell. The Empire recorded her a suicide in custody. The Maiden went unused.
