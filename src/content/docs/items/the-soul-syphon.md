**Infernal Machine · Traps and transfers souls · Many exist · Destroyed at the end of the Infanta**

*Also known as:* the Soul Syphon at Whitehorn

**The Soul Syphon** is one of [the Infernal Machines](/lore/the-infernal-machines/), a device that entraps souls and can carry them into other bodies. Many exist. In the deep past the Grey Sage and the twin queens of Netheril found one and used it to capture and drain the god of death, and later carried it to [Sigil](/places/sigil/); it came to rest at the heart of [the Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) in [Despondos](/places/despondos/), where it drew the souls of the dead to be judged. In the present age the Old Man used a Soul Syphon to trade souls with [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/), and St. Cloud used one to trap the Raven Queen. [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) extracted a Soul Syphon from the tomb at [Whitehorn](/places/whitehorn/) in Arc III, and at [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/) the party found thousands of them on the sea floor. When Cypher obliviated the Eldritch Machines and Cthulhu, the Soul Syphon was destroyed.

## Origin

The Infernal Machines are parts of Zoth's craft and of R'lyeh, scattered over deep time, and their origin is told many ways. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c1-c10.md)</small> After the raid on Whitehorn Dave told the party: "you did discover the eldritch origin of the soul syphon, some combination of water elemental magick and magic from the far realm (deep)". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-11)</small> The Company's file on the machines holds that they were "built by beings who traveled the border between the Deep Realms of the Astral Sea and the Elemental Plane of Water." <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

## Netheril and the god of death

Dave's handwritten Infanta Timeline records the Grey Sage promising the twin queens of Netheril that they would rule Toril "if they help him locate and use the Eldritch Machines. They find the Soul Syphon and use it to capture and drain the power of the god of death." The god of death, named there as Thanatos, was drained; souls ceased to pass on to heaven, and the gods began to be born on earth. After Netheril's defeat the Grey Sage and the queens took the Soul Syphon and the Anachron to Sigil, and the machines were hidden in Despondos. See [The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/) and [Astra and Aurora Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/).

## At the heart of the Penteract

[Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/) built the maze around it: "Tylerjinex made the maze dimension of Despondos to encase the Seat of Judgement, The corpse of Thanatos, and the Soul Syphon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

Dave on what it did there:

> It used to be that the Mammon Machine and the Soul Syphon were are the heart of the Penteract, located at Sigil/Dis inside Despondos, a great labyrinth in the Shadowfell

> It’s power drew apostolic souls from the dead through the maze and into the syphon, where Thanatos (and later the Raven Queen) divided them into their heavens and hells

<small>— Sic on "were are" and "It’s". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> See [the City of Dis](/places/dis/).

## St. Cloud and the Raven Queen

Three steps in the Infanta Timeline's column for the present age turn on the machine: "Old Man uses Soul Syphon to trade souls w/ WSC"; "Use Revan's father to get other soul syphon + anachron"; and "Use SS to trap Raven Queen." <small>(The Infanta Timeline, in Dave's hand.)</small> In the first, the Old Man is the previous loop's St. Cloud; in the last, the Raven Queen was trapped in a mortal child. See [the Three Brothers](/lore/the-three-brothers/), [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/) and [Marcus](/people/marcus/).

The players pieced part of this together in Arc III. JL, who played Zanzibar: "We know St Cloud used one underground beneath Anorak Mts (I think?) to suck the Goddess of Death’s soul out of the heavens and into the body of a human." Dave: "Yes that’s one". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

## Whitehorn

At [Whitehorn](/places/whitehorn/), a trading post in the Ride, the Khalisi told the party that a priest of Asmodeus had placed the Khal's newborn child "in an arcane device, and bound to it the soul of the God of Death", and repeated the ritual every year on the child's birthday. <small>(Arc III Part IV.)</small>

A machine stood in the tomb beneath the temple. Pim's player, Patrick, wanted to "take it home with us and figure out how it works in laboratory conditions", and Dave warned that they would "probably have to dynamite the tomb to get that thing out". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-21)</small> Two days later Dave listed the party's options, first among them "The Soul Syphon at Whitehorn - go back and study? Retrieve?" <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-10-23)</small> On 2018-11-08 he offered the return "to retrieve the Soul Syphon/rescue Khalisi?", and Patrick answered: "Pim wants all the machines." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-08)</small>

The party attacked Whitehorn, "fought a Horned Devil, a Chain Devil (cast planar binding and interrogated it) and extracted the soul syphon." <small>(Arc III, The Age of the Infanta Part V: Soul Syphon.)</small> Dave, after the session:

> Frankly I'm impressed you could thoroughly defeat 12 zombies, 2 devils, a 15th lv necromancer and multiple earthquakes but you did it.  Lots of information this time, I can't even summarize everything you know.  But you did discover the eldritch origin of the soul syphon, some combination of water elemental magick and magic from the far realm (deep), and you know the empire's plans to invade Amn in the morning.

<small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-11)</small>

At [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/), when the Anachron arrived, "Pim has spent much of his time studying the new device, comparing it with the Soul Syphon and the Obliviator. They are absolutely made by the same people with the same magics." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small> See [Pim](/people/pim/).

## The Company's file

The Sword Coast Trading Company's Special Projects department studied the machine. From its file <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives)</small>:

- "Special Projects declassified an object of mysterious origin about a room in size with a giant block with a prolate focal point aimed downward, suspended above a humanoid-sized stone slab rounded like a bed."
- "Special Projects disclosed that many Soul Syphons exist in multiple planes, and that it had previous demonstrated the power to entrap immensely powerful souls and even transport and imprison those souls in other bodies across the universe." <small>(Sic on "previous".)</small>
- "The first priority give to R&D was to miniaturize the device for use on humanoids. This required significantly less power than the extradimensional power of the original machine, and the prototype developed was sequestered by Special Projects." <small>(Sic on "give".)</small>
- "The second priority was to use the Soul Syphon to remove the souls of the Infanta and contain them inside of Perfect Moonstones. This projects saw multiple failures." <small>(Sic on "This projects".)</small>

See [the Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) and [Moonstone](/lore/moonstone/).

## R'lyeh

At [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) the party flew into a whirlpool in the deep past. On the sea floor were glowing pods, "each with a bear, or giant lizard, or rodent, or cat, and boring through a hole in their brain stem, a needle attached to the end of a Soul Syphon. Thousands upon thousands are arrayed across the ocean floor, incapacitated and penetrated by this eldritch contraption. At the ends of a handful of them, you see the carcasses of dead Aboleths, killed in the act of transferring their consciousness into the body of a primitive mammal or reptile." A chain of explosions had destroyed hundreds of the Syphons, and a handful of the animals swam free. <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part V.)</small>

Inside the ship at R'lyeh, "Conspicuous are giant Soul Syphon apparatus with ape-like creatures plugged in through their brains." St. Cloud, found there with the Anachron, told the party: "I’ve freed the races enslaved by the abominations. They will go on to form every mortal race, except for yours, young Gallidan." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part V.)</small>

## Destroyed

When Cypher obliviated the Eldritch Machines and Cthulhu at the end of the Infanta, the mechanisms of death were remade. Dave: "Souls would now be free to roam more along the lines of natural forces, and the influence of the eldritch machines disappear". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> In the order that followed there was "no obliviator in existence and with the soul siphon and mammon machine destroyed, the natural convection of apostolic souls create balance between the gods and devils", with the Raven Queen "disbursing souls according to their allegiances". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>
