**Mind flayer nautiloid · Stolen from [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) · *The Starsong Awakens***

**The Pussyfoot** is an ancient mind flayer nautiloid in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the second arc of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). It lay beneath the streets of the Rock of Bral until the Eberron Corps of Engineers dug it out for a museum of astro-history, and the crew of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) stole it. [Buttsack](/people/buttsack/) named it after his mother. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## History

The nautiloid was lodged beneath the city streets of Bral, found by the ruling aarakocra and sold to Eberron, whose Corps of Engineers were exhuming it to bring it to their planet for display. By the time the crew came for it, it was almost fully excavated and visible from the surface, held by haughty archaeologists and some dusty illithid tadpoles. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, The Rock of Bral)</small> <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

The crew wanted it because only a nautiloid could reach Buttsack in his Githzerai community on the Plane of Elemental Chaos. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small> Barrias's notes record that the crew stole the nautiloid along with robots and Grover Stevens. <small>(Barrias Ruby-Eye's character sheet)</small> After the crew found Buttsack in Root Hold, he named it the Pussyfoot, after his mother. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

Later, while Barrias and [Se7en](/people/se7en/) went through the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/) with [Nine](/people/nine/), the Pussyfoot was parked outside. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>
