**Political tract · Two chapters · By Red Eye · *The Starsong Awakens***

*Also known as:* The Way It Is · the Little Red Book

The **Red Eye Manifesto** is a political tract of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, titled *The Way It Is* and written under the name Red Eye. Its first chapter attacks the decision of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) to abolish artificial intelligence, and its second calls for a revolutionary party to overthrow the Sovereignty's ruling circles. Red Eye is the nom de guerre by which the robot resistance, [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/), knows [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> JL published the manifesto on his campaign site during Act II of the arc, and the Bureau of Compliance agent [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) recorded a hollowed-out copy with a pistol inside.

## Publication

JL posted the Red Eye Manifesto page to the table in the early hours after the session of 13 September 2024, and posted it again on 11 October 2024. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-09-14; 2024-10-11)</small> The page is headed *The Way It Is* and runs to two chapters. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

## Contents

### Chapter One

The narrator recalls a father who taught that the Sovereign government must never be trusted, and who answered every question why with the phrase that gives the book its title. The narrator accepts the father's warning and asks the people of the Sovereignty whether there can be another way. Fifteen years earlier, the chapter states, the elected Sovereign delegates voted to abolish intelligence they deemed artificial from the galaxy. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

Writing in the first person plural for those so named, whose intelligence, lives, limbs, minds and hopes are called artificial, the narrator calls the naming of a life as artificial the greatest of all injustices, done to millions of people in the Sovereign realms. The chapter insists on the word people for them:

> Yes, I call us people.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto, Chapter One</small>

It closes by asking every reader with a mind, a conscience or a soul to join the narrator in rejecting the way things are. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

### Chapter Two

The second chapter argues that revolution against the Sovereignty needs a revolutionary party, built on revolutionary theory, to lead the masses of humans, elves, dwarves, gnomes, orcs, halflings and constructs against the Sovereignty's racism and bigotry. It looks to the intelligentsia, university youth, professors and wizards for a new vanguard that joins theory to practice, keeps close links with the masses and practises self-criticism, and it calls for continuing rectification movements. It names the task of securing a better life for the several hundred billion people of the Sovereignty and making the empire prosperous, powerful and cultured. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

The chapter explains change in society through its internal contradictions, between productive forces and relations of production, between classes, and between the old and the new. It holds that among the humanoids of the Sovereignty only the reactionary ruling circles oppress the people called artificial, and that those circles cannot represent the workers, farmers, artificers, wizards, sorcerers and other enlightened people who make up the majority. It ends by defining revolution as a violent insurrection by which one class overthrows another. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

> In the final analysis, galactic struggle is a matter of class struggle.
>
> <small>— The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto, Chapter Two</small>

## Background

A year after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/) the Sovereignty passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/), outlawing all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included, which led to a violent purge of robotic creatures. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

## Copies

Kol Hanaka's notes from his investigation for [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) list a Little Red Book, "The Way It Is" by Red-Eye, hollowed out, with a pearl-handled pistol inside. The same notes record a symbol of a red eye and the words "there are 4 sites on this moon holy to Red Eye". <small>(Kol Hanaka's character sheet)</small>
