**Document · Grey leather · Dove seal in white wax · Chained to a courier-case · Carried by Sister-Inquisitor Maelis Dorn · Seen closed, never open**

*Also known as:* the mother-book of the Houses · the ledger

**The Chorus Ledger** is a bound registry in grey leather, sealed with a dove in white wax and chained to a courier-case, carried by Sister-Inquisitor **Maelis Dorn** of the Black Doves. A novice of the Korth house called it *the mother-book of the Houses* — which house, which bell, which consecration-day — and it is the only complete record of the chorus in existence: the Korranberg Foundry Trust knows only what it poured, and [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/)'s decade-long census is guesswork. NEST agent **FOXTALE** — [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) — spent eleven days inside the Korth cloister as a lay penitent of the Host and got *one look at it closed and none at it open*, which is the closest anyone hostile has come. The Ledger is unrecovered, and travels with Dorn on a circuit that FOXTALE traced to the Ivory Lazaret.

## NEST FIELD DEBRIEF K-KORTH — SOURCE: FOXTALE

Paragraph six, verbatim in substance: a bound registry that arrives and departs with Dorn. Grey leather, dove seal in white wax, chained to a courier-case that never leaves her reach or her cell. A novice called it **the mother-book of the Houses** — *which house, which bell, which consecration-day.*

FOXTALE's assessment of its bearer: **Sister-Inquisitor Maelis Dorn** is Aereni, young-faced, spine like a drawn bow. The Korth house defers to her absolutely and she belongs to no one in it. She preaches mercy in the square and the crowds love her; inside the walls she inspects the bell the way an armorer inspects a blade before a duel. She is *the only person FOXTALE saw touch it bare-handed*. The sisters say Dorn stood at the Empress's own veiling. She is [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/)'s will when Uriel does not choose to appear.

## KORRANBERG FOUNDRY TRUST — PRIVATE SCHEDULE OF WORKS

The Trust kept its own book and burned it nightly on a brazier at the gate. One leaf survived, by clerical error, and it explains precisely why the Ledger is the only complete record in existence:

- **Great castings** are entered on the Trust's schedule — thirteen commissioned, eleven delivered, two in the yard.
- **Parish castings** number three hundred and forty-one delivered to the Houses to date.
- **Small consignments** are *"kept separately by the Sisters and not by this office."*
- **Consecration** is performed by the Sisters alone; foundry personnel are excluded and reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft.

So the foundry knows what it poured and does not know what became of it. The Sisters know both. Every delivered entry ends *receipt under the dove seal*.

The Trust's schedule records the Original returned to Her Radiance's keeping under escort **Sister-Inquisitor M. D.**, destination withheld from the schedule.

## THE FULCRUM BELL FILE

The rebellion's own attempt at the same census, assembled by a network that has been counting bells for over a decade. It is fourteen scraps in different hands behind one cover letter signed **"— I"**, and it is honest about what it is: *"Each scrap in this packet describes a church bell, I've stood under three of them myself."*

Fulcrum's file is a decade of brave work by a cook, a lamplighter, a porter, a midwife, a carter, a gravedigger, a purser, a teamster, a tinker, a miner and a muleteer — undated scraps, third-hand scraps, scraps that may or may not describe the same bell — and it is *still* guesswork.

The Ledger is an accounting book.

## HB-CHORUS-13 — DIRECTORATE OF RELIC INTELLIGENCE

[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s dossier supplies the reason the count cannot simply be inferred from thirteen. The copies are weaker than the Original and do not need to be strong; they only need to resonate the mother bell's call. *"This means there can be many many more than thirteen. I suspect there are hundreds."*

Which makes the Ledger the only object in the world that could tell the rebellion how many horns it needs and where to put them.

## MOVEMENT — WHERE THE BOOK GOES

Dorn keeps a circuit. Korth, then east and south by private coach under escort, gone between nineteen and twenty-six days, then Korth again. The sisters say she goes **to the Mother's house to report**.

FOXTALE cross-walked that against gate logs and against a muleteer's scrap in the Fulcrum material — wool-wrapped freight going up the Qarth road at night under Sister escort, the escort papers sealed with a dove, *the same seal the muleteer had seen on the young Inquisitor who preaches in Korth*. Her conclusion, stated as certainty: the far end of the circuit is the white hospital in the mountains northeast of Qarth. **The Ivory Lazaret.**

> The dove seal goes up that road and comes back with her. Whatever the mother-book is written for lives at the top of it.

## STATUS

Unrecovered. Dorn signed the prayer ritual that sealed Sister Calvane's door in the Korranberg Foundry after the bell rang three years ago, and stood as the Black Doves' representative at the Thronehold Summit, where she publicly accused Chancellor Eric of Hallorn d'Lyrandar's death; her whereabouts since the Summit are unconfirmed. She is also, awkwardly, the friend [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) made while wearing another face, and the woman who recited [the Litany](/lore/the-litany/) to her at a veiling while counting the *thirteen* on her fingers like a checklist.

FOXTALE's request for tasking is still open, and still the shortest route to the book:

> I can hold the penitent cover and I can get closer to Dorn — she has begun to notice my grief approvingly, which is the door. Authorize the approach and I will travel in her shadow to the mountains myself.
