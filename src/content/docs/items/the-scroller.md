**Standard-issue device · Astral Command · Charged scroll-preparer · Communicator and tricorder**

*Also known as:* Arcalex Scroller · sector variants: mediscroller, arcanoscroller

**The Scroller** is the standard-issue device of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), a limited communicator and tricorder that holds spells as charges. Dave introduced it for the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in October 2022 as a rechargeable utility spell scroll, reworked it in January 2023 into a scroll-preparer loaded before each mission, and planned sector-specific versions such as the mediscroller. Several of the Zanzibar's officers carry one.

## Design

The first Scroller had daily charges and a few utility spells, and let its user spend charges in place of spell slots for some of the spells they already knew. It worked like a rechargeable utility spell scroll that had to be plugged in every day. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small>

Scrollers make Starfleet-style basic coordination possible but extremely unreliable, and were issued with the caveat that they can fail at any moment. Dave suggested that when a scroller is involved in big spells such as sending or Leomund's tiny hut, the DM can veto the casting. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small> Lower-powered scrollers with fewer charges were considered for level-one crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-06)</small>

In January 2023 Dave changed the Scroller to work like scrolls: an expanded spell list with six charges, used to download scrolls before a mission, enough for three 2nd-level spells or six castings of a 1st-level spell. Scroll rules apply, so a spell must be on the user's own list. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small> The revised Scroller is a scroll-preparer, charged with scrolls on a long rest and then cast as normal, with sector-specific versions such as mediscrollers and arcanoscrollers to follow as the crew levelled. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-21)</small>

## Users

- **[Se7en](/people/se7en/):** Dave ruled that the autognome's scroller is built in, saving an attunement slot, because Se7en is an artificer. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-08)</small>
- **[Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/):** carries a Scroller and a Mediscroller, attuned to the Mediscroller. <small>(character sheet)</small>
- **[Caprica](/people/caprica/), [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/) and [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/):** each attuned to a Scroller. <small>(character sheets)</small>
- **[Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) and [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/):** each carries a Scroller. <small>(character sheets)</small>

In November 2023 the Voodoo Boys operated on Nico's character and fitted a cyberdeck implant. Dave ruled that the cyberdeck can replace the scroller, since it is a scroller in the head. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>
