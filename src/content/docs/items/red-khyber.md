**Material · Mined from the Glass Plateau, the Mournlands · Extracted by Project E.D.E.N. units · Shipped through BioTec · Purpose unknown**

*Also known as:* Red Kyber · red khyber

Red Khyber is a material mined from the irradiated Glass Plateau in the Mournlands by [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units and shipped through [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/). Mining it is one of E.D.E.N.'s two stated objectives, the other being the war against [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), the Warforged resistance entrenched in the Plateau; the project's corpse pipeline, [Umbra](/items/umbra/), grafts and airships serve both. Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar reported that enough had been retrieved from the Plateau to continue testing "both projects", and the Nest tracked rising Red Khyber activity as the western war worsened. What the Empire does with the material is not recorded.

## Where It Comes From

It is mined out of the irradiated wasteland of the **Glass Plateau**, deep in the cursed Mournlands. The E.D.E.N. units were built for that ground, and the mining is half of their stated purpose. Sister Nora, under a Zone of Truth, gave both objectives in the same breath: fight the Warforged resistance entrenched in the Plateau, and mine Red Khyber from the wasteland.

Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar reported to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) at POSÉ that "EDEN has retrieved enough from the Glass Plateau to continue testing the project, both projects." The Empire runs at least two programmes on the material, and only one of them is known to the rebellion.

## Where It Goes

[Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), once the control chip was out of his skull, listed **Red Khyber shipments** alongside the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/) prototypes and the private owner as the three things he knew about [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/). A junior enhancement-technician in the enchantments department had visibility of the shipments.

The Nest was tracking the flow from the other end. When Jessica Grimpledink briefed the trio inside her Upper City command centre, **rising Red Khyber activity** sat in the same list as the worsening war in the west and Crona's Wall freezing Lake Galifar.

## What It Is For

The Empire's use for Red Khyber is not recorded. Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), asked what it was beneath the Korramont, said only that it was *"something we've seen used to horrible ends."* What is known:

- It supports "both projects," per Vex d'Lyrandar — and only one project has a name the rebellion knows.
- Its activity level rose as the western war worsened.
- It moves through BioTec.

## Khyber, Red and Black

Red Khyber is not the only coloured Khyber on record. In Korth, Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) identified the black ore walls beneath the Korramont as **Shavat**, or Black Khyber — a mythic planar ore formed where Shavarath touches the mortal world. Later, Jessica Grimpledink and Bofric determined that Esther Crona's wedding gift was made of **both black and red Khyber**, and she has since become fixated on it as a prototype weapon.

Whether the wedding gift and the Glass Plateau shipments belong to the same programme has not been established.
