**Item · Ring · Signet of the Princess of Clouds · House Weathervein**

*Also known as:* Vivica's ring · the signet of the Princess of Clouds

**The Ring of Shooting Stars** is a ring of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/), "the signet of the Princess of Clouds", which [Count Crestus Octopailus Weathervein](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/) gave to his daughter [Vivica](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/), the Princess of Clouds. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/), on 2021-11-12, Vivica's ghost gave it to the house's heir [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) in the Weathervein Tomb beneath Saltmarsh's [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/), and charged her to find the Tempest Sword, [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/). Vivica's ghost stayed present to Dolores through the ring. On 2021-12-26 a wizard of Saltmarsh's shot tower removed the ring's curse, after which it attuned as an action and carried "just the risk of possession".

## The signet of the Princess of Clouds

Vivica's family were "the guardians of the Atlas, those who walk upon the water, wielders of the Shooting Stars and the Tempest Blade". Giving the ring to Dolores, she said:

> Take my ring, it is the signet of the Princess of Clouds and a gift from my father Count Crestus. You must recover your birthright. Find the Tempest Sword. It is the key to my uncle’s foolish game…

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>

Dave posted the item at the table that night: "HER RING: Ring of Shooting Stars". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> The next day Nico wrote "That ring is crazy", and Dave answered "Yes indeed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

## Vivica through the ring

Dave to Nico, the morning after the tomb: "Vivica’s Ghost remains quite present to you through the ring, and is ecstatic". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small>

In the session of 2021-12-23 Dolores passed a Charisma save against Vivica. Dave: "But gambled a lot on that one CHA save because Vivica would have ended Salmor and used his body to bring back PW". The saves climb from the ring to the book to the sword: "The book’s save Dc is higher, and the swords even higher still". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-24)</small> See [Penguinus "Keraptis" Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/).

## The curse removed

On 2021-12-26 the party met "a disgruntled wizard" (JL) in Saltmarsh's shot tower. Nico: "he took care of her ring problem, something she can’t do. Dolores is impressed". Dave: "Remove curse has no material components", and:

> Insta-attune ring that powerful is as good as having it attuned, just the risk of possession, but at least it’s not 24 hours a day

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-26)</small>

Before the session of 2022-01-07 Nico wrote that Dolores was going in without her ring. Dave: "only an action to attune.....". Nico: "Don’t tempt me Frodo!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-07)</small>

On 2022-03-13, weighing a warlock level for Gabriella Hellwood, Dave wrote that "between that and say the ring, it woul dbe worth dipping". Nico: "I’m sure Dolores would be fine giving you the ring. Vivica and Gabby may get along better". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small>

## Properties

- It is the Ring of Shooting Stars of published D&D, linked by Dave from D&D Beyond. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small>
- It carried a curse, removed by *remove curse* on 2021-12-26.
- After the curse was lifted it attunes as an action, with the risk of possession by Vivica's ghost.
- Vivica's is the lowest of three save DCs, below the book's and Weathervein's.
