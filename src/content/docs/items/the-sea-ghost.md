**Vehicle · Sailing ship · Neptune's Rejects · Captain Gabriella Hellwood**

**The Sea Ghost** is a sailing ship, the vessel and moveable home of [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) in [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/). The party won it on 2021-09-18, at the end of *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh*, and kept its name. [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) was its captain, [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) its first mate and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/) its chaplain, with [Oshi](/people/oshi/) as boatswain and MacGruber as navigator. It had no weapons when the party took it; the party's ledger later lists two harpoon ballistas and a mangonel aboard, a crew of ten sailors and twelve named hands, and a treasury spent on repairs. Its home port was [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/).

## Winning the ship

The party took the ship on 2021-09-18. Nico asked "Are you re-naming the ship captain?", and Dave answered "Bad luck" and "She’ll remain Sea Ghost". Dave: "It’s honestly the best player home I’ve seen in real dnd"; Nico: "It’s great cause it’s movable". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small> Dave's plan for it that day: "We hire a small village of sailors and their families become part of our faction", and "And we leave Oceanus in charge of Sea Ghost when we’re gone there". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

## Quarters

Dave: "There’s three quarters up top for each of us!", "First mates quarters are down stairs though", and "There’s a boatswains cabin too". Scrunt's pseudodragon familiar Bimz was found aboard, in a room Nico proposed to repair: "the room that we found Bimz should be fixed from fire damage". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

## Weapons

When the ship was won, Dave: "Sea Ghost has no weapons so it can only ram or the crew can shoot a sort of crappy volley", and "Would need to modify the hold pretty heavily to put in guns though". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18, 2021-09-19)</small>

The ledger lists its armament:

> Weapons:\
> 2 harpoon / Ballestas - 120ft / 400ft dis. 3d10 piercing dmg\
> 1 Maganels (small catapults)

<small>(sic; `sources/documents/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-neptunes-rejects-ledger.md`)</small>

That is two harpoon ballistas, each dealing 3d10 piercing damage at a range of 120 or 400 feet, and one mangonel. The *[Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/)*, the galley the party captured, carried the same armament.

## Ship combat

Dave adapted the Saltmarsh ship rules for the Sea Ghost: "the commanding officer uses their action to give commands to the boat/crew, which acts on its turn as a creature", and "if it has enough crew, it take 3 actions, chosen by the commander from a list". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-19)</small>

## At sea

- **Sahuagin.** On 2021-10-09 the party fought sahuagin at sea while the ship took damage. Nico: "made the fight more exciting having to deal with the boat dmg too". Dave, the next day: "Sea Ghost is famous among more than the air breathers". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-10)</small>
- **A small fleet.** With the *Stinking Albatross* captured, Dave wrote on 2021-11-03: "yeah if we don't look out we will become sea princes ourselves and/or responsible for the naval and spiritual defense of Saltmarsh". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-03)</small>
- **Major Dickhead.** The day after Gabriella's wedding, Dave wrote: "I thought I might see Dickhead today but assumed it would be a marine vessel boarding the sea Ghost and forcing me to flee Saltmarsh". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-22)</small>
- **The honeymoon.** On the frontier during Gabriella's honeymoon, Dave proposed: "Maybe we could get word to the Sea Ghost to come get us in the nearest port". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-25)</small>
- **A boss fight.** After the Saltmarsh finale, with Gabriella at Fighter 8 / Fathomless warlock 2, Dave wrote that he "would put her and the Sea Ghost up as a boss fight in any campaign". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>
