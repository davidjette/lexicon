**Ship · The Starsong Awakens · Armed with the Eye of Ao Cannon**

*Also known as:* the Tarrasque ship (Nico)

**The Terrasque** is a ship of [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), armed with the Eye of Ao Cannon. By May 2025 [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) had brought the body of [Caprica](/people/caprica/) back to the ship, where they met up with Marcel Martel and Weld. Its origin and owners are unknown.

## Armament

The **Eye of Ao Cannon** requires an attuned crewmember, who is incapacitated until the end of their next turn after using it. It has two modes. <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-13)</small>

- **Bring Life:** cast instantaneously, it restores 6d8+30 hit points to the Terrasque.
- **Bring Death:** a ranged weapon attack at +19 to hit, with a range of 200/800 feet, that cannot hit targets within 60 feet of the ship, dealing 60 (6d8+30) radiant damage to one target.

## History

While Barrias Ruby-Eye and Se7en were in the sphinx temple going through time doors with Nine, and the Pussyfoot was parked outside, Paytra and Kol brought Caprica's body back to the Terrasque and met up with [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/) and [Weld](/people/umwelt/). <small>(Nico, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>
