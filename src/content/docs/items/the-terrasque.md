**Ship · The Starsong Awakens · Armed with the Eye of Ao Cannon**

*Also known as:* the Tarrasque ship (Nico)

**The Terrasque** is a ship of [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), armed with the Eye of Ao Cannon. By May 2025 [Paytra](/people/paytra/) and [Kol Hanaka](/people/kol-hanaka/) had brought the body of [Caprica](/people/caprica/) back to the ship, where they met up with Marcel Martel and Weld. It was a Terrasque-class warship of the Sovereignty, commanded by the Hyperion Admiral Diodorus, until the followers of the Red Eye seized it in orbit over the planet Maysee. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

Terrasque is also the name of a line of warships that [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) introduced for planetary defence, each roughly the size of a small moon and armed with an Eye of Ao Cannon. Two Terrasque-class warships protect [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/).

## Armament

The **Eye of Ao Cannon** requires an attuned crewmember, who is incapacitated until the end of their next turn after using it. It has two modes. <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-13)</small>

- **Bring Life:** cast instantaneously, it restores 6d8+30 hit points to the Terrasque.
- **Bring Death:** a ranged weapon attack at +19 to hit, with a range of 200/800 feet, that cannot hit targets within 60 feet of the ship, dealing 60 (6d8+30) radiant damage to one target.

The cannon is a legendary wondrous item that must be mounted on a spelljamming vessel, and each use consumes a diamond worth 25 gp. Its description reads: "It is the will of the gods to bring life or death upon mortals. The function of this chair is to harness that power." <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New magic items)</small> The cannon costs 150,000 gp. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships)</small>

## History

While Barrias Ruby-Eye and Se7en were in the sphinx temple going through time doors with Nine, and the Pussyfoot was parked outside, Paytra and Kol brought Caprica's body back to the Terrasque and met up with [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/) and [Weld](/people/umwelt/). <small>(Nico, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-23)</small>

## The heist

The followers of the Red Eye needed the Terrasque because it can travel between galaxies without phlogiston: their destination was the planet Necis, 9,990 parsecs away, which no ship without a Far Caster or a Nautiloid could hope to reach. They planned the heist aboard the SSJ Vanguard, in orbit over Maysee, and it was played on 22 November 2024. What the planners knew of the ship:

- It carries a crew of a thousand, and devastating weapons, and can heal itself with arcane energy.
- Its exhaust port is large enough for an Istus-class vessel to pass through safely, but is full of toxic gas.
- Once every 24 hours a crew shift leaves its command centre almost unmanned for one minute.
- Its life support and artificial gravity are controlled from the command centre.
- Its remote megastructures allow no automation, leaving long stretches of the ship empty of people.
- A docking port on Maysee sends goods and people to and from it three times a day, morning, noon and night.
- Its commander, Admiral Diodorus, is a Hyperion. Hyperion must touch to share knowledge, but dreams pass between them at short range.
- Up to 150 Astral Command crew are aboard, in three rotating shifts of 50, with up to 40 civilians: vendors, advisers and cleaners.
- It has three spelljamming helms. One could break the ship apart and spelljam away, mostly safely, leaving the ship useless. Coordinating the helms requires Hyperion spelljammers.
- The access codes to its lifts and security doors change every twelve hours, and any terminal that detects hacking locks down and delivers an electric shock.

The party's assets were fifteen Red Eye crew working the Vanguard, eight hangers-on, Astral Command gear, two laser ballistae, an arcane cannon, and Captain [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) and Lieutenant Commander [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) in the brig. Nine gave the party gifts before the heist: an enspelled quarterstaff that casts *calm emotions* and an enspelled handaxe that casts *darkness*. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

After the heist the Terrasque jumped to where Necis should have been, and found nothing there but rubble. OU812 explained that the planet was supposed to be there. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

## The Terrasque class

Inspired by legends of Torillian Dreadnought vessels, Astral Command introduced a line of ships that combine the security and firepower of a starbase with "the adroit dexterity of a Star Moth". Roughly the size of a small moon, the Terrasque was built for planetary defence, yet is nimble enough to float on water or land safely on the ground. Its size requires three spelljamming helms to manoeuvre, two aft and one ahead. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, New ships)</small>
