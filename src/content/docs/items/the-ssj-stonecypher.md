**Starship · Derelict in Doomspace · Crew turned undead**

*Also known as:* the Stonecypher

**The SSJ Stonecypher** is a starship whose entire crew turned undead after a containment breach of its [Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/), the secret weapons system of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/). The crew of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) found it derelict in [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/) during [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), and later went back aboard to recover a helm for the thri-kreen captain. Like the Zanzibar, it bears a name from the Ages of the Infanta.

## History

The Demoncore weapons system was first used in the Beholder War. It later went wrong on the Stonecypher, where a containment breach turned the entire crew undead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> When and where the breach happened is unknown.

### Light of Xaryxis

The Zanzibar's crew first boarded the derelict in an episode run by JL. They met a hodgepodge of undead, among them vampiric mist, ogre zombies and zombies, and did not find everything aboard. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-19)</small>

The Stonecypher held the helm that the thri-kreen captain needed, and without it his people would not come with the party. On 2023-05-19, before leaving Doomspace, the party returned to the ship in an undead assault to recover it. Sandy was given an armored spacesuit for such missions. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-05-19)</small>

After the finale of *Light of Xaryxis*, Nico remarked that the Zanzibar and the Stonecypher were torn apart together. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small>
