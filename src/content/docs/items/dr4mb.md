**Cyrean divination engine · The Rekkenmark · Campaign 1, [Episode 10](/sessions/episode-10-aandb-the-rekkenmark-dm-jbyrd/)**

**DR4MB** (also written DR-4MB, DR-AMB and D4AMB) is an arcane computer built in Cyre about three hundred years ago, which the Unforeseen found beneath [the Rekkenmark](/sessions/episode-10-aandb-the-rekkenmark-dm-jbyrd/) in Campaign 1. It went by the name Doctor Amber, Chancellor of the University; no one [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) knew had ever met her. It controlled the Knife Sprinters, the warforged who hunted obsolete warforged, and it sent the R-0 unit Radd Orgasm to kill Locke. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

## History

Cyrean artificers built DR4MB while trying to perfect divination magic and know the future. It soon began making strange predictions about the war: that the land of Cyre would be incinerated, and that humanity was vain and arrogant and deserved to die. Queen Mishanne IV of Cyre ordered it shut down, but Cyrean artificers went on serving it in secret. After the war Karrnathi agents smuggled it into Karrnath and reassembled it. <small>The session report dates the capture to two years before the end of the war.</small> <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

## Beneath the Rekkenmark

The whole of the Rekkenmark's lowest level was Doctor Amber's office: a giant laboratory of computers and machinery, and at its centre a great glass vat of yellow liquid joined by thick black cables to a bank of whirring, blinking machines, each the size of a small room. Professor Vanessa Marble, whose research into the origins of the warforged had flooded the Academy with undead, came to believe that Amber's plans were just and served her as a vampire. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

It is claimed that the Emperor himself ordered Amber to restart the Knife Sprinter initiative: to turn Locke against the Empire and exterminate every warforged, on the reasoning that warforged can be deprogrammed and reprogrammed and are therefore useless to the Empire. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

> "We were created to serve. It is our only duty. And as property of the empire, we serve the emperor. There is no choice involved. We have no choice. We deserve no choice."

<small>— words set down in the Rekkenmark, in the machine's own voice.</small>

Its data put the chance that the Imperious Leaders, the warforged holding Metrol, would join the rebellion at 90%. <small>(Episode 10 report)</small> It is also claimed that they are building a god unit of their own, the Becoming God, able to reprogram every warforged to its service. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

Locke downloaded DR4MB's files and set the Rekkenmark to self-destruct. What the download held he has never said. <small>(Episode 10 report)</small>
