**Weapons system · Astral Command · Secret program · Used once in the Beholder War**

*Also known as:* demoncore

**The Demoncore** is a secret weapons system of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), capable of turning an entire plane undead. It was first used in [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), where [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) served the secret program behind it, and Katrina chose to recover the weapon over saving stranded civilians. A containment breach of the Demoncore later turned the entire crew of [the SSJ Stonecypher](/items/the-ssj-stonecypher/) undead.

## History

### The Beholder War

The Demoncore was first used during the Beholder War, in the flashback episodes of [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/) played in March 2024. In the aftermath of the Black Hole Sun, [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) was left stranded on [Negroni Prime](/places/negroni-prime/) with Barrias Ruby-Eye, Tillamook and a dozen civilians. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small>

Horgus is a necromancer and Katrina a horizon walker, both in support of the secret program, which was used only once. Katrina could have saved the stranded civilians and chose to recover the Demoncore instead. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small> In Nico's account, the two left Jim's group with no transportation and no way to communicate, took their artillery droid, and Katrina gave Jim a sending stone. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

After Jim last saw Katrina and Horgus, his memories were changed with a potion of modify memory. Dave judged that any knowledge Jim gained of the Demoncore was probably erased by [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

Dave called Astral Command's possession of a weapon that can turn an entire plane undead "a horrifying power", and wrote that the divergence of Katrina and Horgus's story from Jim's mirrors the divergence of Astral Command from its righteous path, leads to Jim's disillusionment, and will tie back to the crisis of the present. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-16)</small>

### The SSJ Stonecypher

After its first use, the Demoncore "went wrong on the Stonecypher, where a containment breach led to the entire crew turning undead". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-09)</small> The crew of the SSJ Zanzibar found the ship derelict and overrun with undead in Doomspace.
