**Material · Grown and supplied by Malrik Zeir · Transported across Khorvaire by Hallorn d'Lyrandar · Consumed by Project E.D.E.N. and by the Children of Ember · Sharn supply severed**

Umbra is a cultivated necromantic reagent. It is not mined; it is grown by [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/), a Valenar high elf and a vampire, and moved across Khorvaire by his half-brother [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/), the Empire's Head of Transportation. It is used to prepare corpses for the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/)' necromantic rites, to drug the living kept as blood cattle beneath [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/), and in the infusion that produced [EDN-00](/people/eden/), the first result of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) With both Zeirs and Hallorn d'Lyrandar dead, the Sharn end of the pipeline is severed.

## The Supply Chain

**Malrik Zeir grows and supplies Umbra. [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) transports it across Khorvaire.** The two are half-brothers; Hallorn is the Empire's Head of Transportation, reputed the richest man in Khorvaire, and Malrik is a soft-spoken Valenar high elf married to [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/), who is [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s half-sister.

The Inevitables found the correspondence themselves, in the restricted levels beneath [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/): letters between Malrik and Hallorn about Umbra and its connection to [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), filed among scattered documents and relics in the chambers where Grady Marsh was held.

## Two Uses

**Preparation of the dead.** [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) did not simply ship [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) corpses to the Black Dove hospital; he shipped them *altered and Umbra-prepped*. Sister Nora, under interrogation, described the rites themselves as **Umbra-infused necromantic rites** performed on the bodies by the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/).

**Preparation of the living.** [Lorian](/people/lorian/), rescued from the chambers beneath the Vault Below, described being lured to Sharn, drugged with Umbra, and kept as **"blood cattle"** for Malrik Zeir's feeding. The captives in the cell called the Pantry were kept drugged and isolated.

## EDN-00

Umbra was present at the start of Project E.D.E.N. [Eden](/people/eden/) was resurrected through a failed ritual and an Umbra infusion, and the result — [EDN-00](/people/eden/) — is Nora's "miracle, yes, but an incomplete one."

## The Nest's assessment

The Nest identified Umbra as the choke point. Malrik Zeir's elimination would sever the Umbra supply for Project E.D.E.N., shut down the Children of Ember, and end the trafficking of his victims in one stroke; removing Hallorn would destabilise the distribution network on top of it.

Esther Crona used the same bottleneck. At POSÉ, when Faith begged her to bring their sister home, the answer was a production quota, and Eden was withheld from her family until the Umbra shipments rose:

> **Faith:** "Esther, bring her home, it's eating at her… Bring her home. The project is ready."\
> **Esther:** "She stays until YOUR husband and his slut brother increase supply."

## Status

Both Zeirs are dead, and Hallorn d'Lyrandar with them. The Sharn end of the Umbra pipeline is severed, the Black Dove head priests were captured and their rites disrupted, and the hospital that received the prepped bodies was seized. Whether Umbra grows anywhere outside Malrik's operation is not recorded.
