**Item · Blade · Holy relic of the Temple of Neptune · Stolen**

**Weathervein** is a blade and the holy relic of the Temple of Neptune in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore. It was stolen when the temple was desecrated, and the town's authorities suspected [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/). With it [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) pinned his former comrade, the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/), to the tomb of the High Priestess Dolores Weathervein on the sea floor, piercing the angel's heart outside his body; with Aqueus's desecrated corpse and Weathervein he opened a portal there, through which the demon lord Dagon tries to emerge. Returning Weathervein and curing the desecration is the quest tied to the Blackfish, and to [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## The relic of Neptune's sept

> Neptune's sept is defiled, and its holy relic "Weathervein" is stolen. Authorities suspect Hellwood.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

Investigation in the temple found that the missing artifact is tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore, and that the desecration was violent and fast, with no witnesses. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Dolores Weathervein, the Lady of Tears

**Dolores Weathervein** was the High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune, and is dead. <small>(DM notes)</small> Her tomb lies beneath the sea, and the priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/) names her grave that of the Lady of Tears: Aqueus "lies beneath the sea, upon the grave of the Lady of Tears, violated and profaned." <small>(Papa Diaz, DM notes)</small>

When news came that Dolores's temple had been attacked, Gabriella Hellwood came to kill whoever had done it, and found it was a trap for her. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The tomb and the seraph's heart

Jeremiah betrayed Aqueus in a ritual of desecration, pinning him to the tomb of Dolores by Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small> On the sea floor the tomb gives off a fading radiance, and the impaled corpse of the drowned seraph lies upon it, "His heart pierced outside his body", slain by Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> Party is ascending from the depths, witnessing the desecration of the Weathervein Crypt and the ascent of the winged man w/ glowing sword.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

The portal on the sea floor was opened by Jeremiah with Aqueus's desecrated corpse and Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small> Weathervein must be removed from the angel's disembodied heart. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Quest:** Return Weathervein; cure the desecration.
