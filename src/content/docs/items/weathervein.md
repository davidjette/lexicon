**Item · Blade · Holy relic of the Temple of Neptune · Stolen**

**Also known as:** the Tempest Sword; the Tempest Blade; Weatherbrand

**Weathervein**, called the Tempest Sword, is a blade, the family sword of [House Weathervein](/organizations/house-weathervein/), and the holy relic of [the Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore. In [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) the first party died over it and an assassin took it; [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) fled with it, and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/), the heir of the house, recovered it in 2022 and served it as a hexblade. After her day it was the temple's relic. It was stolen when the temple was desecrated, and the town's authorities suspected [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/). With it [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) pinned his former comrade, the seraph [Aqueus](/people/aqueus/), to the tomb of the High Priestess Dolores Weathervein on the sea floor, piercing the angel's heart outside his body; with Aqueus's desecrated corpse and Weathervein he opened a portal there, through which the demon lord Dagon tries to emerge. Returning Weathervein and curing the desecration is the quest tied to the Blackfish, and to [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## The Tempest Sword of House Weathervein

The name belongs first to the dynasty. In Skerrin Wavechaser's telling, "The Weathervein dynasty was an ancient trading cartel and feudal house in along the coast of the Azure Sea, centuries before the emergence of the Sea Princes." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> The ghost [Vivica](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) Amphibiana Weathervein called her family "the guardians of the Atlas, those who walk upon the water, wielders of the Shooting Stars and the Tempest Blade", and charged Dolores: "You must recover your birthright. Find the Tempest Sword. It is the key to my uncle’s foolish game…" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-12)</small> In 2021 Dave also called it "Weatherbrand". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14)</small>

The sword is a longsword: "Weathervein +1 longsword. Cast lightning lure as a bonus action, regain 1d4 charges when it rains or storms or submerged in sea water for 8 hours." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dolores took it as her patron. Dave: "Since you’re a hexblade and your master IS Weathervein". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

The house's treasure hoard is a separate set of weapons, Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/), held at [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/). Dave: "I also love the idea of White Plume Mountain being the dungeon with the Weathervein treasure, and that the dwarves found it and extracted The artifacts, and that we have to bring them back in". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> Members of the line were bound into those artifacts. Whelm is "The one Weathervein that didn’t consent to being bound into ancient artifacts from the treasure hoard", and "“Weathervein” Penguinus himself made lots of horcruxes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> [Count Crestus](/people/crestus-octopailus-weathervein/) is inside Blackrazor: "Blackrazor is a competitor to that, at least with Crestus inside it." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

## Provenance

1. **Party 1 and the assassin.** The first party of Ghosts of Twatmarsh died over the sword in its first session, in 2021. Dave: "Who took Weatherbrand"; Nico: "That assassin who murdered those nice pirates". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14)</small> Dave later: "I TPKd you guysnover that sword and then you made a character out of looking for it". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small>
2. **Skerrin Wavechaser.** When he led the party into an ambush at the Weathervein tomb, "Skerrin knew there was no sword in there". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13)</small> Dave's recap: "Skerrin fled Aaltmarsh with Weathervein and tried to disappear with it to get away from you". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-23)</small>
3. **Dolores Weathervein.** The party killed Skerrin on the frontier, and Dolores recovered the sword. JL, who ran the session: "XP: 2750 for SKERRIN, 100 for Gabby for finding Weathervein. 500 Dolores for achieving her destiny." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-12)</small>
4. **The Temple of Neptune.** After Dolores's day it was the temple's holy relic.
5. **Jeremiah.** It was stolen in the desecration of the temple, and Jeremiah pinned Aqueus to Dolores's tomb with it. <small>(DM notes)</small>
6. **The party of Dead Man's Hand.** At Saltmarsh every member of the party wanted it. Dave: "Everyone individually salivating over Weathervein was awesome" / "I did not want that sword in this campaign but it would have been my own fault for introducing it". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-21)</small>

## The relic of Neptune's sept

> Neptune's sept is defiled, and its holy relic "Weathervein" is stolen. Authorities suspect Hellwood.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

Investigation in the temple found that the missing artifact is tied to a family of Aasimar rulers of yore, and that the desecration was violent and fast, with no witnesses. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Dolores Weathervein, the Lady of Tears

**Dolores Weathervein** was the High Priestess of the Temple of Neptune, and is dead. <small>(DM notes)</small> In Ghosts of Twatmarsh she was Nico's player character, the priest of Neptune in Saltmarsh who recovered the sword, and she died as High Priestess centuries before Dead Man's Hand. Her tomb lies beneath the sea, and the priest [Papa Diaz](/people/papa-diaz/) names her grave that of the Lady of Tears: Aqueus "lies beneath the sea, upon the grave of the Lady of Tears, violated and profaned." <small>(Papa Diaz, DM notes)</small>

When news came that Dolores's temple had been attacked, Gabriella Hellwood came to kill whoever had done it, and found it was a trap for her. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## The tomb and the seraph's heart

Jeremiah betrayed Aqueus in a ritual of desecration, pinning him to the tomb of Dolores by Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small> On the sea floor the tomb gives off a fading radiance, and the impaled corpse of the drowned seraph lies upon it, "His heart pierced outside his body", slain by Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small>

> Party is ascending from the depths, witnessing the desecration of the Weathervein Crypt and the ascent of the winged man w/ glowing sword.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

The portal on the sea floor was opened by Jeremiah with Aqueus's desecrated corpse and Weathervein. <small>(DM notes)</small> Weathervein must be removed from the angel's disembodied heart. <small>(DM notes)</small>

**Quest:** Return Weathervein; cure the desecration.
