**Discipline · Elvish in origin · Dwarven in mastery · A weapon of the war against the dragons · Named by Bofric of the Teryaki**

**Arcane harmonics** are the discipline underlying both the bells of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) and [the Horn of Mourning](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/): a body of tempered [Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/) shaped to vibrate in sympathy with the arcane forces that govern the boundary between the living and the dead. The art was procured by the elves, mastered only by the dwarves, and used as a secret weapon in the elves' war against the dragons. Its working mathematics survive in [Corn's folio](/items/corn-s-folio/), and it was named by the runesmith **Bofric** of the Teryaki three years into the second war, after he translated King Corn's last song and played it on the king's own horn.

## The naming

Bofric is bent, milk-eyed and lampblack under the fingernails. He had translated exactly one section of [Corn's folio](/items/corn-s-folio/), the section that interested him: King Corn's last song. He played it on a whistle, and [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s compulsion softened. He was handed [the king's own horn](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/), recognised the smith-mark, and played it properly — a natural 20 on Arcana — and the sound of the bell left Eric's mind entirely.

Then he named it: **arcane harmonics**. Procured by the elves and only ever mastered by the dwarves. A secret weapon in the elves' war against the dragons.

## What it is

[The Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) is the discipline pointed at the boundary between the living and the dead: a body of tempered [Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/) shaped to vibrate in sympathy with the arcane forces that govern it, throwing [the Black Noise](/lore/the-black-noise/) across a thousand octaves and a continent. [The fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/) is the same discipline pointed back at the bell.

The working mathematics survive in Corn's folio: figures which Eric recognised on sight as **harmonic frequencies and Fourier transforms**, and drawings of horns worked against harmonic-resonance mathematics, *"trying to solve some type of problem related to the common symmetries of multiple waveforms."*

## The elvish inheritance

The bells are not an Imperial invention and they are not, at bottom, a Karrnathi necromantic programme. They are an **elvish** weapon from the old war against the dragons, revived. The Fantanya Nyel was fused by ancient elves out of infernal metal on the orders of a Celestial Queen. The Empress who has cast hundreds more of them is an elf. The Litany that the sisters recite at a veiling ends with a dragon being reborn.

The elves procured it and the dwarves mastered it.

## The transmutation problem

[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/)'s working theory of the Korranberg Foundry, delivered over Egg Roll's hand-drawn map, is that the enslaved dwarves were **transmuting black Khyber into a more complex crystalline form** — one able to take imprinted arcane sigils dictated by the elves who enslaved them. The ore alone is not the weapon; the ore, the sigils and the temper together are.

He also read the mark on the bell itself: **the master's insignia stamped upside down**. An old smith's curse. It means they were under duress when they made it.

## The two limits

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **The horn's limit** | It masks while it plays, where it is heard, and no further. Bofric was explicit about this in the same breath as the discovery. |
| **The hammer's requirement** | *"To break the bell, you would need to break it with the hammer that made it."* Resonance can be answered by resonance; it can only be *ended* by [KNELL](/items/knell/). |

## Who can practise it

Bofric, who reads Corn's shorthand and estimates a week for the rest of the folio. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), who took performance and the horn as an instrument specifically to be the second. Doppler Klink, who is now building horns from the reconstructed method. And the clergy of Sharn, every faith on the continent, teaching the fanfare congregation by congregation.
