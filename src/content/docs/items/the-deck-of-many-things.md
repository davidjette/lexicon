**Artifact · A deck of magic playing cards · Scattered among many owners · Sought by Shemeska and by the party**

The **Deck of Many Things** is a deck of magic playing cards, each card named and pictured, which [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) calls a "world changing artifact, sought after by many powerful beings over many centuries." Its cards are held by many owners. Shemeska hired [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) to hunt down the party of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) and find the cards, and Jeremiah killed [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/), who had one, and each of the party. Gathering the cards became the party's main quest. They kept Fates from Feng's daughter, stole Ruin at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) and lost it at [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), and saw [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/) turn Throne on Nicollo at [the Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/).

## The cards

Twenty-one numbered cards are named: **Key · Balance · Puzzle · Gem · Rogue · Sage · Fates · Knight · Sun · Moon · Comet · Star · Jester · Throne · Ruin · Donjon · Void · Skull · Flames · Talons · Euryale**. A second set of unnumbered cards runs alongside them: **Beast · Dragon · Elemental · Fool · Maze · Plant · Priest · Tower**.

Rex Bedsheets' Throne is called a fate card. Each of the party's [fatemarks](/lore/fatemarks/) is recorded as a row of cards.

## Shemeska's offer

Shemeska is seeking the Deck, and had Jeremiah search for the cards and kill to find them. Her words to the party:

> I confess I would very much desire to own some my self, and in fact I am … in possession of two, and I know the owners of another three.
>
> All of us who own cards understand the grave danger they can pose and take extraordinary measures to … them. If you ever become owners of one yourself, you will have to do the same.
>
> If you were killed / are connected with / because of the Deck, it means someone dangerous is pursuing them and thinks you can be of use to their effort. It's in your interest to find out why, and possibly find the cards before they do..
>
> I will help you find your murderer.
>
> If you find a card for me, I will pay you ten thousand gold. If you bring me two, I will give you One hundred thousand. For three - any Wish I can grant you.

<small>— Shemeska (DM notes). Sic on "my self" and "do..".</small>

## Fates

Fates belonged to Feng Milosh. Of Feng, aboard the Blackfish: "He was crew here." "Feng won it from Gooch in a game of pink Monty." It "brought him awful good luck, it did, until the end..." <small>(DM notes. Gooch is Graspin Gooch.)</small> Feng's daughter [Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/) wanted nothing to do with it:

> I believe in the will of the Fates. And thats why I dont want anything to do with a magic playing card that lets you predict the future.

<small>— Keisha Milosh (DM notes). Sic on "thats" and "dont".</small>

The party kept Fates from Keisha, and killed Jeremiah, not permanently. <small>(DM notes; see [the Blackfish encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/))</small>

## Jester

At [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/), Jester marks the "Cabinet of Mysteries", the Jester's cabinet of treats, a portal into the casino in the possession of Eaton the acrobat. It is part of a portal network used by a troupe of clowns around the city. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Key

Among her inducements, Shemeska would show the party Key. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Ruin

Ruin bears the picture of a crumbling sword and crown. [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/), whose family discovered moonstone on their land and used it to travel and buy treasures, had it among many magic shoes and garments, and lost it to a thief, a beautiful seductress who robbed him: [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/). <small>(DM notes)</small>

At [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) in Waterdeep the party saw General Varkus deliver an incendiary speech against the Zanzibar; [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) told [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) of his plan to place his son [Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/) on the Lords' Council by ruining Beef Wellington; the party witnessed Griddy seducing Wellington with a false deck stacked with Ruin; and Jeremiah surprised them. <small>(DM notes, session notes)</small> Jeremiah and a goon, disguised as waiters, staged a heist to steal the card. Griddy and Jeremiah fought, the party resisted him, and the party stole Ruin. Nicollo convinced Klaxon and Griddy that he was on their side and that Jeremiah had taken the card. Afterwards Darius agreed to his place in Waterdeep and Wellington was ruined, four days before the Lords' meeting; Klaxon was happy but scared to have lost the card, and Griddy, suspicious, demanded the party steal it back from Jeremiah. <small>(DM notes)</small>

[Lenore](/people/lenore/) was holding Ruin when the Purple Dragons took her to Fort Maximillien after [the incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/). While Sir Merriam Wordsworker questioned [Maverick](/people/maverick/) in the upper bastion, the seized items lay on the desk before him, and peeking out of a loosely wrapped deck was Ruin. General Varkus came in with Phillip Klaxon and Griddy Champagne. Then Nicollo was brought in and betrayed the party: he said he had never meant to steal Ruin, that he was in Klaxon's service, and that the moment he found out Lenore had the card he had killed her and tried to take it. Griddy picked Ruin up from the desk and slipped it into her bra. <small>(Fort Maximillien narrative)</small>

## Balance

Balance is in [the Tomb of St. Magnus](/places/the-tomb-of-st-magnus/), which is heavily guarded, has a hidden entrance on a Moonshae isle to which there is a map, and has a monster inside. [Bud Holhertz](/people/bud-holhertz/)'s rival adventuring party recently failed to recover the card. They were hired by Lexi, a seeker of [the Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) who can be mistaken for Lenore; on arrival the monster killed the Salad triplets, Lexi teleported away, and Holhertz and Lou Sassle escaped. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Throne

At the Grub + Grog, Megan Moron gave Maverick a pamphlet of [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/) which shows Rex Bedsheets on a throne, and in his pocket is Throne. When the party attacked the Rexium sales meeting, Rex fled in his carriage; in its flaming wreckage he pulled Throne from his pocket and dominated Nicollo's mind, forcing him to run back and attack Lenore. Nicollo stabbed her before coming to and shaking off the hypnotizing effect of the card. Rex yelled "they're murderers! thieves!" as the party was led away. <small>(Grub + Grog and Fort Maximillien narratives)</small>

Rex Bedsheets is a debt-bound warlock of a Mummy Lord. While Rex is alive and in possession of Throne, the Mummy Lord is in torpor; if not, the Lord wakes and gains direct control of all wearing the Charms. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Star

In Motherstone Hall, on Level 2 of [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/), where Halaster assembled segments of Motherstone architecture, tombs, statuary and relics stolen over the centuries, the tomb of Daenan Gallidan of [House Gallidann](/organizations/house-gallidann/) is empty except for Star. <small>(DM notes)</small>
