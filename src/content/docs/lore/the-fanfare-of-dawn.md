**Musical work · Twenty-four bars and a short eight-bar bridge · Very dwarven in style · Composed by King Corn Deeptempura · Now being taught across a continent**

*Also known as:* the fanfare of the dawn · the fanfare · Corn's last song · the fugue

The Fanfare of Dawn is a musical work of twenty-four bars with a short eight-bar bridge, very dwarven in style, composed by King Corn Deeptempura in the last days of his life — with his hold enslaved, his daughter under sentence, and the foundry above him casting the instruments of a continental massacre. Played on [a horn of Corn's design](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/) it suppresses the influence of [the Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) completely, within hearing, for as long as it is played. The runesmith [Bofric](/people/bofric/) recovered it from Corn's folio and identified the underlying art as [arcane harmonics](/items/arcane-harmonics/); after he played it at the hospital in Sharn the assembled clergy of the continent took it up and began teaching it congregation by congregation, naming the instrument **the Horn of Mourning**.

## The piece

Twenty-four bars with a short eight-bar bridge, in a very dwarven style.

The notes exist in two places. The last page of [Corn's folio](/items/corn-s-folio/) carries a simple set of them in a shaking hand, beneath the line *"I made their key. Someone must learn to make its answer sing."* The full arrangement is in the sheet music taken from the same study, which the party had already carried out of the room.

## What it does

Played on [a horn of Corn's design](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/), the fanfare suppresses the influence of [the Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) completely, within hearing, for exactly as long as it is being played. A bard with enough talent can drown out the bells.

The first time it was heard, the runesmith Bofric played it on a whistle — no horn, no black metal, nothing but a dwarf and a tune — and the compulsion riding [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s mind *softened*. Then he was handed the king's own horn, recognised the smith-mark on it, leaned in and played it properly, and the sound of the bell left Eric's head entirely.

> It can only maybe drown out the sound as long as you play it. To break the bell, you would need to break it with the hammer that made it.

<small>— Bofric</small>

## What it is

Bofric identified the underlying art as [arcane harmonics](/items/arcane-harmonics/): procured by the elves, mastered only ever by the dwarves, and a secret weapon in the elves' old war against the dragons. The fanfare and the bell are built on the same art.

## The teaching

At the hospital in Sharn, [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) convened a meeting with a representative from every faith on the continent. Bofric played the fanfare. The clergy took it up and began teaching it congregation by congregation, and named the instrument **the Horn of Mourning**. Adamantine in the listeners' pockets hummed along with it.

**Known players:** Bofric · [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), who took proficiency in Performance and in the horn specifically to be the second · and, increasingly, whoever the clergy of Sharn have got to so far.

**Outstanding:** the party's own stated intention to teach the fanfare, and the horn's construction, to someone who will remain behind when they go into the Empire. That has not been done.
