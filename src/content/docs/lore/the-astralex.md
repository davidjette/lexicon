**Information network · Astral Command · Starfall**

*Also known as:* the Astralex network

**The Astralex** is the information network of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), whose primary architect is Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/). Aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) access to it was regulated and a specialist officer served it. During the "B Team" crisis of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*, while the captain and command crew were away on Necis, the Zanzibar's Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard, and the ship was left adrift in the astral sea without it.

## Design

Admiral Andromeda Starweaver, head of the Order of Scribes, is the primary architect of the Astralex network. Her order also works on overcoming [Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/), which blocks interstellar communication and teleportation. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

## Aboard the Zanzibar

Access to the ship's Astralex was a matter of security. When [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) issued civilian credentials to [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/), she told him:

> Limit your Astralex access to matters of importance.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

The crew roster lists 2nd Lt. Dorlin Horcusporcus as the Zanzibar's Astralex Specialist. <small>(crew roster)</small> He is a deep gnome Astralex expert who hacked into Captain Smallberries's report. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-27)</small>

During the race, Horgus hacked the minilex on the Bachman shuttle. The crew made their Arcana checks to resist it and used spells to throw the hackers off; the hackers had wanted them to spin out rather than die, so that the core could be taken and the hack reversed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-04-29)</small> See [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/).

## The attack

While Captain Smallberries and the command crew were on [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/), the Zanzibar was left floating free, and its remaining crew had adventures of their own. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-20)</small> In the third and final installment of those "B Team adventures" the ship's Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard. Afterwards the Zanzibar was adrift in the astral sea with no Astralex, its captain and command crew a million miles away "chasing some voice in darkness". Who was behind the attack was unclear, though the crew had seen a lot behind the scenes in "the cyber episode". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-15, 2023-12-16)</small> Nico wrote that the crew's fast thinking saved the majority of the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-16)</small> See [the Astralex attack on the Zanzibar](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/).

[Buttsack](/people/buttsack/), Carlos and [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/) remained aboard the helpless ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-26)</small> After Carlos made his third wish the Zanzibar left the others behind, and it next lay in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

## What the Vanguard's officers said

Fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, followers of the Red Eye took the SSJ Vanguard and, on the Red Eye's orders, questioned its officers about the Lexicon. Their accounts differ, and each is their own claim. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Awakens Notes)</small>

- **Chief Caguama**, a tortle, called the Astralex a tool, only as good as the hand that wields it, good for telling when it would rain or snow; when the machine got too smart for its own good, "we put it down". He would not talk about the Lexicon itself: Sovereign High Command had put a hush order on all inquiries about the Astralex, though he supposed Starweaver and her cronies probably kept "a pocket Lex" to bet on stocks.
- **Lieutenant Commander [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/)** said the Lexicon was, he believed, of Netherese construction; that ancient Torileans grew addicted to its prophecies and could not see that it was manipulating them toward a future it desired; and that the Sovereignty abolished it at [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). Pressed, he conceded that it never actually went away but stopped speaking, and that [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/) coming from the Vodoni Empire has an atomic signature noticeably like the Astralex's.
- **Captain [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/)** said Sovereign scientists had found readings exactly like the Lexicon's in primordial sediment, asked whether man created Lex or Lex created man, and called it "a Moebius strip that ends at the beginning and begins at the end". She tied it to the temporal anomalies around [Necis](/places/necis/), [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) and [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/), and collapsed screaming.
