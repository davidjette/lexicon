**Planned event · Continent-wide · HAS NOT HAPPENED**

**The Eternal Nightfall** is a planned coordinated necromantic rising across the whole continent, to be rung in with a chorus of [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) hung in the Houses of Mercy. It is the doctrine of Holy Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) sisterhood, and it is named in the sisterhood's own liturgy. It has not happened: the bells are hung, the network is built and the doctrine is recorded, and the Empress is waiting for one condition to be met, which the Inevitables read as the birth of a male heir.

## THE DOCTRINE

The rebellion first heard the name in a cellar at Zilspar Farm, from [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) under interrogation, alongside the truth of Project E.D.E.N. and the identity of [Esther](/people/esther-crona/)'s sisters. She called it **Uriel's doctrine of the Eternal Nightfall**.

Years later, undercover in a Korth church as a high-elf acolyte, [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) befriended Sister [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), who took her for a worthy initiate and whispered the litany:

> come the night\
> the bells will toll\
> thirteen\
> the dead will rise\
> to serve the living\
> and the dragon\
> will be reborn

## THE MACHINE

The intelligence assessment is [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s, filed as HB-CHORUS-13 from the Mt. Silicon archive. [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and the Black Dove sisterhood took the Sovereign Host churches, temples and hospitals and turned them into **Houses of Mercy** staffed by Dove nurses and nuns, and installed a specially manufactured [Hell's Bell](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) in as many of them as possible.

It was paid for with the Emperor's personal wealth and the House of Kaius, and with the seized assets of the Sovereign Host dioceses and abbeys, impounded by Imperial decree. The result is a continent-scale network of medical facilities doubling as laboratories and deployment centres.

Only the [Greenbluff](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/) bell is original. The rest are copies, cast in the last decade, and the copies are weaker — which does not matter, because they only need to resonate the mother bell's call. Pierce suspects there are hundreds.

## WHAT RINGING THEM DOES

The audible toll is incidental. The Fantanya Nyel emit a polyphase arcane emission across a thousand octaves, above and below hearing, which travels continental distances and penetrates hundreds of feet of matter. Pierce calls it **the Black Noise**. His three predictions:

- **Silence does not stop it.** A silence ward, a deafened ear, a sealed vault stop the toll, not the Noise. It propagates through the arcane substrate, not the air. Deep caves and dungeons are no protection. Wax in your ears will let you die without the courtesy of a warning.
- **The living unravel.** Sustained proximity corrodes sanity along the Greenbluff pattern — intrusive tolling, waking dreams of the grave, violent mania, and death, in every documented case self-inflicted or mutual. The mad do not flee the bell. They defend it while they tear off their own skin.
- **The dead wake.** Within the Noise the dead rise where they lie, and they come up listening, ready to serve a sufficiently powerful necromancer keyed into the chorus. There is exactly one necromancer alive with that reach.

The one time a casting has been struck — [Korranberg](/history/the-korranberg-massacre/), three years ago — it emptied a hold of tens of thousands.

## THE CONDITION SHE IS WAITING FOR

Uriel has fourteen daughters by Emperor Kaius, many of them sets of twins, all under twelve. She has no son. The Inevitables' reading of the bell dossiers is that she is waiting for a specific event, most likely the birth of a male heir.

The consecration rite recovered from Sister Calvane's library gives the price: **thirteen Doves, all thirteen planes**, the bells bound to *the unholy blood and the great mother*, and a very dear sacrifice — a living child, or virgin royalty.

The dragon [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/), bled by the Empire for three years, believes Uriel used his stolen blood to sire those heirs and that he is their father. Master Armhair's suppressed manuscript traces the Mark of Death, Erandis Vol's birth and the Emperor himself to the same dragon. Set against the litany's promise that *the dragon will be reborn*, the conclusion the party reached in Korth is that Uriel is trying to bear a son by a dragon — and that the bells are the coronation.

## WHAT STANDS BETWEEN

**KNELL**, the one hammer that cracks a casting. **The horn and the fanfare of dawn**, which mask the Noise while they play, now being taught congregation by congregation as the **Horn of Mourning**. Ten bells remain, at least one of them a Master — the ninth great casting, hanging in a Black Dove tower attached to the Imperial Palace itself. See [the breaking of the bells](/lore/the-breaking-of-the-bells/).

And the road itself. Fulcrum's last scrap is from a muleteer on the Qarth road, northeast, toward the white hospital in the mountains: wool-wrapped freight going up at night under Sister escort, and escort papers sealed with a dove — the same seal worn by the young Inquisitor who preaches in Korth. Whatever lives at the top of that road, the dove seal goes home to it. The Inevitables have been moving toward the Ivory Lazaret since Esther Crona's dying command.

And behind all of it, the Ruby Nova. If the Inevitables fail, Locke Pierce ends the world before Uriel can have it.
