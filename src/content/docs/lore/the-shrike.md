**Creature · Necis · Light of Xaryxis and The Starsong Awakens · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* The Shrike (Hyperion)

**The Shrike** is a deadly and nearly invulnerable being whose origin lay at the Time Tombs of [Necis](/places/necis/), and the object of the pilgrimage made by the command crew of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. The crew met it in a horror episode and retreated. Some of the hidden intelligences of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) conspired to hide its reality from mortals, and Captain Smallberries, Caprica and Sandy crossed Necis 1 to [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) to find it. In the finale of *Light of Xaryxis* Captain Smallberries came to the bridge of the Zanzibar as the Shrike, and the Shrike appeared again in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. Starfall melded into the plot of Dan Simmons's novels *Hyperion* and *The Fall of Hyperion*, in which a creature called the Shrike haunts the Time Tombs. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14; published novels)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### First encounter

The Shrike first appeared in a horror episode, where it was "very very hard to damage and very deadly", and the party made a tactical retreat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small> Where the encounter took place is unknown.

### The conspiracy

By the later part of *Light of Xaryxis* the Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas. Some of them were conspiring to hide the reality of the Shrike from mortals, and elements of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), may also have been involved. The crew set course to expose it at Necis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> Attackers who looked from the ground like beholders proved to be cybrids, controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

### The pilgrimage

[Captain Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) reached the remote planet of Necis 1 and set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22, 2024-01-06)</small> The pilgrimage set off toward the Shrike, and was about to reach it when the story cut back in time to a season of flashbacks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-03, 2024-02-17)</small> On its return the crew sought "the last clues about what the real threats are, the nature of the shrike and the reason why this is all happening". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

During the pilgrimage the crew saw the future "with the army of shrikes and the approximational spirits of cypher", and found a number of people on "the tree of pain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> See [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/).

### The finale

The final session of *Light of Xaryxis* was played with a stat block titled "The Shrike (Hyperion)", alongside a turtle ship. By its end the Zanzibar had taken critical damage. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31, 2024-06-02)</small> See [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/).

The Shrike of the finale was Jim Smallberries. At the Shrike Palace on [Necis](/places/necis/), he submitted to a vision of [Rachel](/people/rachel/), who proved to be an avatar of [Cypher](/people/stonecypher/), the three-fold goddess who is also Eloise and Istus. He entered the tree of pain and faced the Shrike alone in a psychic battle, and then left this time realm. Where he went is not settled: it may have been the end of time, or a vision of a future in which humanity has ascended and looks backward to destroy the Shrike and the Lexicon. He appeared on the bridge of the Zanzibar as the Shrike, at the moment the deployment of [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/) was nearly complete. He fought [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/) and [Horgus Simianis](/people/horgus-simianis/) and killed Katrina, and Horgus teleported away with her body. [Sandy](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) and [Caprica](/people/caprica/) fought the undead crew until the party fled, and Caprica was left to fly the ship into the black hole of [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/). <small>(Dave, 2026-11-21)</small>

## Nature

Dave described the outcome in two lines:

> The shrike turned out to be taken over by a mortal man to thwart another mortal woman who was enacting the lexicons will
>
> Drawn there by the star song and merged with it in heart of their time bridge

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

The man was Jim Smallberries, who faced the Shrike at the Shrike Palace and came to the bridge of the Zanzibar as the Shrike. The line does not name the woman. Aboard the Zanzibar, Jim as the Shrike fought and killed [Katrina McQuaid](/people/katrina-mcquaid/), a grey cloak, on the bridge of the ship that carried the Demoncore. <small>(Dave, 2026-11-21)</small> The star song of the second line is [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/).

The Lexicon engineered the cybrid attack of the arc's present so as to have its chance to unleash [the Demoncore](/items/the-demoncore/) on the home worlds of the Sovereignty. The Shrike came either to stop it or to help it. Which of the two was unclear throughout. The Demoncore did not go off over the home worlds: it leaked into the crew of the Zanzibar, the ship outfitted to deliver it toward Toril. <small>(Dave, 2026-11-21)</small>

**The tree of pain** is strung with undying mortals who showed a glimmer of remembering what was obliviated, whether of Cthulhu, of Cypher's life, or of any of it. Dave wrote that "it could be removing those memories from reality to protect reality or … harvesting them to try and discover the truth, or both". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small> See [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

## The Starsong Awakens

The same stat block, "The Shrike (Hyperion)", was used again in *The Starsong Awakens*, in the session of the doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/), which turned on "literal time travel and causality". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-04, 2025-04-05)</small>

## Player theories

The players offered their own readings during the pilgrimage. Jim's player took *[Martin's Final Cantos](/items/martins-final-cantos/)* to mean that the Shrike is the crew itself, and that Jim is the Shrike. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-20)</small> In the finale Jim came to the bridge of the Zanzibar as the Shrike. Caprica's player suggested that the Shrike might be a "techno obliviator" that impales on the tree of pain those who come too close to the mystery. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>
