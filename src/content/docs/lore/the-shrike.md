**Creature · Necis · Light of Xaryxis and The Starsong Awakens · Status unknown**

*Also known as:* The Shrike (Hyperion)

**The Shrike** is a deadly and nearly invulnerable being whose origin lay at the Time Tombs of [Necis](/places/necis/), and the object of the pilgrimage made by the command crew of the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. The crew met it in a horror episode and retreated. Some of the hidden intelligences of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) conspired to hide its reality from mortals, and Captain Smallberries, Caprica and Sandy crossed Necis 1 to [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) to find it. It figured in the finale of *Light of Xaryxis* and appeared again in *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*. Starfall melded into the plot of Dan Simmons's novels *Hyperion* and *The Fall of Hyperion*, in which a creature called the Shrike haunts the Time Tombs. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14; published novels)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

### First encounter

The Shrike first appeared in a horror episode, where it was "very very hard to damage and very deadly", and the party made a tactical retreat. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-08)</small> Where the encounter took place is unknown.

### The conspiracy

By the later part of *Light of Xaryxis* the Lexicon had evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas. Some of them were conspiring to hide the reality of the Shrike from mortals, and elements of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), the grey cloaks of [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), may also have been involved. The crew set course to expose it at Necis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small>

### The pilgrimage

[Captain Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/), [Caprica](/people/caprica/) and [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/) reached the remote planet of Necis 1 and set out on a sojourn that severed them from Astral Command. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-22, 2024-01-06)</small> The pilgrimage set off toward the Shrike, and was about to reach it when the story cut back in time to a season of flashbacks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-02-03, 2024-02-17)</small> On its return the crew sought "the last clues about what the real threats are, the nature of the shrike and the reason why this is all happening". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

During the pilgrimage the crew saw the future "with the army of shrikes and the approximational spirits of cypher", and found a number of people on "the tree of pain". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> See [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/).

### The finale

The final session of *Light of Xaryxis* was played with a stat block titled "The Shrike (Hyperion)", alongside a turtle ship. By its end the Zanzibar had taken critical damage. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31, 2024-06-02)</small> See [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/).

## Nature

Dave described the outcome in two lines:

> The shrike turned out to be taken over by a mortal man to thwart another mortal woman who was enacting the lexicons will
>
> Drawn there by the star song and merged with it in heart of their time bridge

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

The identities of the man and the woman are unknown. The star song of the second line is [the Starsong](/lore/the-starsong/).

**The tree of pain** is strung with undying mortals who showed a glimmer of remembering what was obliviated, whether of Cthulhu, of Cypher's life, or of any of it. Dave wrote that "it could be removing those memories from reality to protect reality or … harvesting them to try and discover the truth, or both". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small> See [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

## The Starsong Awakens

The same stat block, "The Shrike (Hyperion)", was used again in *The Starsong Awakens*, in the session of the doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/), which turned on "literal time travel and causality". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-04, 2025-04-05)</small>

## Player theories

The players offered their own readings during the pilgrimage. Jim's player took *[Martin's Final Cantos](/items/martins-final-cantos/)* to mean that the Shrike is the crew itself, and that Jim is the Shrike. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-20)</small> Caprica's player suggested that the Shrike might be a "techno obliviator" that impales on the tree of pain those who come too close to the mystery. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small>
