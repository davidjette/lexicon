**Information operation · Sharn, city-wide ArcEye network · Sharn Ep 18**

The **Sharn Broadcast Override** was an information operation carried out by the Inevitables on the night of [the rising](/history/the-fall-of-sharn/), Sharn Ep 18, in which [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) used the [EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) to seize the city's ArcEye feeds from the Veil Building penthouse. Two broadcasts went out in one night: a pre-recorded declaration followed by the reclaimed memory of [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/)'s birth, in which [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) reaches for the newborn; and then, minutes later, a live feed of [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s body at the foot of her own tower, with Dario's declaration that the Empire would fall. Sharn rose that night.

Sharn watched itself constantly. ArcEye cameras fed the crystal screens in every district; the Sharn News Network told the city what it had just seen; [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) had used those same screens to execute Cogborn prisoners live during [the riot](/history/the-cogborn-protest/). The override took that machine off her.

## THE DEVICE

The **EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node** is a portable arcano-analog relay built by House Orien for the Lightning Rail Sub-Transit System. Every station hub carried one and every train car had its own; they relayed delays, reroutes, tunnel alerts, emergency announcements and visual broadcasts to Orien Crystallizer screens. Because the sub-transit rails ran through stone, metal and magically shielded tunnels, the EBT-7 used arcano-analog resonance to propagate where modern scry-tech cannot. It predates ArcEye entirely.

That is the flaw it exploits. Brought within about a hundred feet of a permanent ArcEye camera, the EBT-7's analog signal overpowers the receiver and forces it to mirror the EBT-7's feed citywide. Up to five minutes. It cannot be stopped once started, the transmission's location is exposed by which camera is hit, and every live feed is permanently recorded. Only a small number were ever made and they were discontinued with the sub-transit shutdown.

## HOW IT WAS OBTAINED

[Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) explained the device to the trio at Zilspar Farm and sent them to collect it from an upper-city store, The Conduit Exchange. They picked up the package and walked out into an ambush: a ragged man claiming [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/) wanted a word, then [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) waiting outside their own safehouse, and then the Children of Ember, who seized [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), cut the teleportation chip out of her head and destroyed it on the spot. The EBT-7 came through it intact.

## THE FIRST BROADCAST

On the night of [the rising](/history/the-fall-of-sharn/), having fought through the Iron Veil guard at the door of the Veil Building penthouse, [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) used the EBT-7 to pre-record a message. On the balcony, facing Esther, with [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) nearby and [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) and the Honor Guard watching, he triggered it — and ran the reclaimed memory of Calcifer's birth immediately behind it.

The city saw [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) reach for a newborn, mark him, drain him, and freeze at his mother's scream. It saw the diamond glowing at the base of her skull. It saw the Empress of the Empire, the Holy Empress whose statues stood over the Skyport, do that.

The memory had been pulled out of [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s head with an ArcEye Crystallizer Lens taken from the Cog Collector's laboratory, and the trio had deliberately chosen not to broadcast it when they first watched it. Three copies were made. One went to Jessica for safekeeping. This was the one they held back for.

## THE SECOND BROADCAST

Minutes later Esther was dead at the foot of her own tower. Dario used the EBT-7 again, live, and showed the city her corpse. He declared that the Empire would fall.

It ignited Sharn. Iron Veil soldiers whose pact had just been voided surrendered in droves; others fled; a small number vanished into the shadows and became the first seeds of a hidden Iron Veil remnant. Statues of Uriel came down across the city within hours.

> The broadcast cannot be stopped once initiated, and the transmission location, based on which ArcEye is targeted, will be exposed. All live feeds are permanently recorded.

**The four permanent ArcEye cameras:** the Veil Building Garden Floor, directly below the Crona / Blaze penthouse and the most dangerous ArcEye camera to access · the Grand GoldSky Tower, the most-viewed ArcEye feed in Sharn · the Grand Sharn Hotel rooftop, one of the few ArcEye nodes that rotates · the Lower City Ziggurat camera on the roof of the largest factory in The Cogs, assessed as possibly the safest option. Full access and approach notes for each are on [the EBT-7](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/).
