**Tattoos · Marks of Istus · Borne by the party of Dead Man's Hand**

**Fatemarks** are tattoos found on people whom Istus has chosen to fulfill a duty, a destiny tied to a constellation. The strangers of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), who had lived wholly separate lives and all but one of whom were murdered by [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/), the handsome man with the glowing sword, woke in [the Mortuary](/places/the-mortuary/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/) tattooed with fatemarks. The tattoo artist Fell identified one as a Fatemark, and the marks drew the attention of [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) and of [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/). At [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/) the Factol of the Faceless told [Heyu](/people/heyu/) that she was meant to bear hers and to hold the Cards. Each bearer's fatemark is recorded as a row of cards of [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/).

## Istus

Istus is one of the three Fates. The Ordo Trium, the Order or Rule of Three, holds that its wisdom established itself in the aftermath of the Infanta Crisis, 500 years ago, "when the 3 FATES ISTUS, ELOIS, and CYPHER pulled the world back from the brink." <small>(DM notes. Sic on "ELOIS", Eloise.)</small>

## In the Mortuary

Dhall, a Dustman bookkeeper in the Mortuary, can be persuaded to explain how the marked came to be there:

> An ancient hand bade the Caretakers return you to this Torment.
>
> You are marked by fate, it is not for me to strike thy name from the Book of the Living.
>
> You arrived as we all do, a tumble down a long chute and then a heavy thud.

<small>— Dhall of [the Dustmen](/organizations/the-dustmen/) (DM notes)</small>

## Fell

Fell, a tattoo artist, told the party to come back on a Tuesday morning for consulting, and said of the mark:

> It's not one of mine. It's a Fatemark.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

> TATTOOS found on people who ISTUS has chosen to fulfill a duty, a destiny tied to a constellation.

<small>(DM notes)</small>

## Who wants to know

When Shemeska heard of the tattoos she reached out to get a closer look at the party: "None knew anything after death, where did the Tattoos come from?!" She asked them, "Tell me your story." <small>(DM notes)</small>

Sharegrave knows that Shemeska will owe him for any information regarding the tattoos, and does not reveal it; he sends [Prez](/people/prez/) to show the party how to escape the Hive. <small>(DM notes)</small>

## Our Lady of Mysteries

At Our Lady of Mysteries, the cathedral of [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), Heyu was surrounded by the Faceless, who brought her to the Factol. [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/), Factol of the Faceless, an attractive young Orcish priest "w/BD energy", told her:

> You bear the mark of the Mother of Mysteries and her sisters. You were meant to bear it, and to hold the Cards. Find them, for her; return this piece of her holy Memory.

<small>— Brother Montegue (DM notes)</small>
