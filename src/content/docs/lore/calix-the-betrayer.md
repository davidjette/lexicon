**Calix the Betrayer** is a Cormyrean lullaby about [Calix](/people/calix/), the elf-lich whose betrayal at [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) brought on the Forgetting, [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) that erased the city from the memory of the world. It ends with his name and a warning never to sing the song again. [Mara Mageblood](/people/mara-mageblood/)'s mother sang it to her, and Mara copied all twelve lines into her journal, which [Kara](/people/kara/) recovered beneath Snowy Mountain a century later and the party carried into [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/). Its lines appear again as flavour text on two [Ally Cards](/lore/the-ally-cards/) made for the final session of [Arc VIII](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/): Calix's card, which calls it a Cormyrean lullaby, and Ella's, which calls its opening a Forsworn lullaby.

*For the elf-lich, see [Calix](/people/calix/).*

## In Mara Mageblood's journal

Mara introduces the song on the third page of her journal:

> There is a lullaby i've always found strange. whenever my mother sang it to me she seemed to leave herself when she remembered it, as if it called to her from some forgotten recess of her mind. it occurs to me that this lullaby may be of use on my journey:

The journal's text of the song:

> Our true queen, she saw the boy, atop his shining stallion,\
> and with a sigh of true love's power she gave him all her kingdom.\
> Every boy and every girl, wished to see them dancing.\
> in the hall, atop the trees within their mountain kingdom.\
> But lo, there is a darkness there, that stalks and plots betrayal, and with a lie can all undo the magic of their kingdom.\
> Under where the mushrooms grow, he hides his wicked measures,\
> but hold your breath and close your eyes and he cannot devour you.\
> Every step is seven days, but never stop to wonder,\
> for wondering's fine but not when time is fleeting for our kingdom.\
> When all the world's inside your mind, forgetting is oblivion\
> So never sing this song again of\
> Calix the Betrayer

<small>— Mara Mageblood's Journal (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-09-26)</small>

Directly after it the journal copies an "interesting passage in Glacius' History of the Moonshae Kingdom" that names the nursery rhymes:

> That Calix betrayed the House of Gallidan is certain. Most assume (and the nursery rhymes say) that Calix beguiled the child goddess and tried to woo her into darkness. But stories always frame the Infanta as an eternal child, but she was quite the opposite. She was a human girl who lived and died at a ripe old age. How old was she when Calix betrayed her? This is not recorded.

## On the Ally Cards

Calix's card carries the last four lines, printed as follows:

> "Every step is seven days, but never stop to wonder,\
> for wonderings fine but not when time\
> is fleeting for our kingdom.\
> When all the worlds inside your mind,\
> forgetting is oblivion. So never sing this song again of\
> Calix the Betrayer" - Cormyrean lullaby

The second variant of Ella, Infanta of the Moon's card carries the first two lines as one sentence:

> "Our true queen, she saw the boy, atop his shining stallion, and with a sigh of true love's power she gave him all her kingdom." - Forsworn lullaby

## Differences between the versions

- The journal gives twelve lines. Calix's card gives the last four, and Ella's card the first two.
- Calix's card names the song a Cormyrean lullaby; Ella's card names its opening lines a Forsworn lullaby. The journal gives it no title or origin.
- The journal reads "wondering's" and "world's"; Calix's card reads "wonderings" and "worlds".
- The journal sets "for wondering's fine but not when time is fleeting for our kingdom." as one line; the card breaks it after "time".
- The journal ends "forgetting is oblivion" with no stop and sets "So never sing this song again of" and "Calix the Betrayer" as lines of their own. The card puts a full stop after "oblivion" and runs "So never sing this song again of" on in the same line.
- In the journal the third line ends "dancing." with a full stop, and the fourth line begins in lower case.

## Subject

The song tells of a true queen who gave her kingdom to a boy on a shining stallion, of a darkness that "stalks and plots betrayal" and hides "under where the mushrooms grow", and ends on the name of Calix. Calix demanded the first Infanta, **Elistrada**, the goddess [Elistrae](/people/elistrae/) reborn, as his pupil and was refused; he later betrayed her and [Stone](/people/stone-infanta-of-discovery/) at Motherstone, murdering dozens of Infanta at the peace-treaty meeting, and the Forgetting followed.

Beneath Motherstone, below Calix's blood seal, a spiral staircase "choked with glowing fungus" descends to a domed chamber where anyone who enters must "close their eyes and hold their breath" or fall into the thrall of its magic. <small>(Arc II Part VI.)</small> The party survived that chamber by the lullaby's instruction, "hold your breath and close your eyes". Beyond the chamber lay Calix's vault and phylactery, where time passed slower inside than outside; the party emerged from it ten years in the future.
