**Chronology · From the Godseed to the present · The definitive timeline**

*Also known as:* the Infanta Timeline

**The Infanta Timeline** is the definitive chronology of the [Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/), running from the growth of Ydryssl out of the Godseed to the second Time of Troubles. It survives as two handwritten pages dated June 2020, and it runs **up to the party's first journey back**; everything after the return to Starfall is not on it. <small>(Readings marked (sic?) are uncertain.)</small>

**The timeline is a closed loop, and the document draws it as one.** A single line runs from 40,000,000 B.C. to the present, and a great arc carries [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) from his own birth back to the beginning of it.

<small>(The line's starting point is the deep past, about 65 million years ago. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-12-10; Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c11-c36.md)</small>

## The three eras

| Era | Opens with |
|---|---|
| **Pre-history** | Ydryssl grows from the Godseed |
| **The First Time of Troubles** | The Netherese break Death |
| **The Second Time of Troubles** (the present) | Wainwright St. Cloud is born in Baldur's Gate |

Dave put the first Time of Troubles at "like 800" and the second, the present, at "like 1900". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-26)</small>

## Deep time

- **Ydryssl grows from the Godseed** in the Elemental Chaos.
- The [Moon](/lore/moonstone/) forms from crystalized starlight, in orbit with Ydryssl.
- Dragons form. Fae evolve on Ydryssl and the Moon.
- The **Banefae** hunt Changelings and cross into Ydryssl.
- **The Aboleth inject Elemental Magic into apes and creatures — forming the mortal races.**
- Rogue Aboleth, the children of Cthulhu, arrive at Ydryssl.
- The **Eldritch Machines are activated to make the Leviathan forget Ydryssl forever**.
- [Cthulhu](/lore/the-leviathan/) traverses the lunar orbit in search of the Aboleth.
- **St. Cloud reverses the Eldritch Machines**, bending Cthulhu into the Moon.
- **Ydryssl is destroyed and becomes the Spire — [Sigil](/places/sigil/).**
- The Moon is cut off from Earth. The Feywild and [Shadowfell](/places/dis/) split off. The Banefae are exiled and become the Elves.
- **Cthulhu sleeps, growing to consume Ydryssl.**
- The Banefae build the **Starfall tower** atop the spire, forming Sigil — but **St. Cloud reaches the top of the spire and sets up house before they do**.
- **St. Cloud lives as "the Old Man."**

<small>(The timeline's note that Ydryssl destroyed becomes Sigil sits alongside the account in which Sigil and [Dis](/places/dis/) are both planar disturbances caused by the Egg's impact at Waterdeep. Both stand; neither is merged into the other.)</small>

Tiamat and her forces were destroyed before the Egg arrived, and she was lost in the abyss until she reconstituted in Hell. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-30)</small> By Dave's account the mind flayers came from "Zoth-brain octopi merging with the elder brain in the Egg", ruled the astral plane from Toril for millions of years, and were destroyed by the rise of the red dragons and the Gith. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small>

## The rise of the Netherese

> WSC, now known as the Grey Sage, gains the trust and counsel of two sorceror twins who rule a small kingdom inland. He introduces them to the Leviathan, and promises them they will rule Toril until the end of days, after which the three of them will merge with Cthulu and create a new universe — if they help him locate and use the Eldritch Machines. They find the Soul Syphon and use it to capture and drain the power of the god of death.

<small>— the Infanta Timeline, page 1. Sic on "sorceror" and "Cthulu". The two sorceror twins are [Astra and Aurora Saharel](/people/aurora-and-astra-saharel/).</small>

What follows from that, in four steps:

1. The god of death — named here as **Thanatos** — is captured and drained with the Soul Syphon.
2. **Souls cease to pass onto heaven.**
3. **The gods begin to bourne themselves on Earth.**
4. **Humans begin to become the first true "Gods."**

That is the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/). <small>(The same mechanism appears at the other end of the chain: the Raven Queen is trapped in a mortal, the Seat at Dis is vacated, Asmodeus hoards the dead, and the gods must descend.)</small>

In the same period the Aboleth and Dragon Empires are destroyed in cataclysm, and the slave races — "orc, human, dwarf, goblin, gnome, lizard people, aarakokra, etc." — are killed or freed.

## The First Time of Troubles

- The Netherese increase Cthulhu's power and their own, first by draining the God of Death, then by **activating the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/)** — and are **stopped just before destroying the world**.
- **Death is broken; souls are harvested by Netheril to make a utopia.**
- The Netherese find the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) and the Mammon Machine, and activate them to broadcast Cthulhu into all minds, awaken him, and fly the **Black Omen** <small>(sic?)</small> to Waterdeep.
- **The Allied Gods of [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) attack Netheril and defeat them** — crashing the Black Omen and all the mountains, and **feeding the Netherese into the Obliviator**.
- **The Obliviator is taken to Motherstone.**
- The **Grey Sage and the Queens of Netheril take the Soul Syphon and the Anachron to Sigil.** <small>([Calix](/people/calix/) is boxed and arrowed into this step on the document.)</small>
- The Banefae join the war against Netheril. **The Queens banish the Banefae to Despondos.**
- **The machines are hidden in Despondos.**
- **The Grey Sage is banished to Despondos.**
- **The Queens become the Ladies of Pain and Death.**
- **The Old Man escapes Despondos.**

[Tylerjinex](/people/tylerjinex/) "made the maze dimension of Despondos to encase the Seat of Judgement, The corpse of Thanatos, and the Soul Syphon". The Banefae, united by [Drefanmetra](/people/the-waning-one/), attacked the maze and enslaved Tylerjinex to get into the [Penteract](/places/the-penteract/) at the heart of [Despondos](/places/despondos/). Their plan was "to unite the bane blades into the Fatherstone and use it to trap the power of the Netherese and destroy all mortal souls in a rite to undo time and restore the Moon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

The Queens and the Grey Sage went into Despondos together. As allies they defeated the Banefae: "They are confronted by the Queens and Grey Sage and defeated." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small> In the last loop "Brightmantle did the same thing as you, collapsed the city and sent the mammon machine into the desert, it trapped the Queens and Grey Sage in Despondos for centuries." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-03)</small> The Queens then turned on the sage and threw him deeper, took the seats of Pain and Death, and the Old Man escaped.

Meanwhile "the Atlantans also largely fail, but Brightmantle succeeds". Drefanmetra's efforts still led to the fall of Netheril and [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/)'s victory, "and St Cloud has to wait another 1000 years to try again". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-21)</small>

## The Second Time of Troubles — the present

1. **Wainwright St. Cloud is born in Baldur's Gate.**
2. **The St. Clouds meet the Old Man.**
3. **The Old Man uses the Soul Syphon to trade souls with WSC.**
4. **WSC kills the Old Man and turns his brothers.**
5. **They form an extraplanar empire.**
6. **They use [Revan](/people/revan-darkcember/)'s father to get the other Soul Syphon and the Anachron.** <small>(See [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/).)</small>
7. **They use the Soul Syphon to trap the Raven Queen.** <small>(See [Marcus](/people/marcus/).)</small>
8. **Find ways to trigger the apocalypse.**
9. **Travel back to stop the heroes, and live forever.** <small>(Boxed on the document and labelled **EVENING STAR**. The infinity sign is drawn beside it.)</small>

## The loop closes

Read the columns together and the document states it plainly:

**The Old Man is the previous loop's Wainwright St. Cloud.** He lives as the Old Man in deep time; he is the Grey Sage who raises Netheril; he is banished to Despondos and escapes; he meets the St. Cloud brothers in the present — and the young Wainwright murders him and takes what he has.

Two different St. Clouds are killed. "Grey Sage" is an office that each loop's St. Cloud holds. The young Wainwright kills the Old Man, the immediately previous loop's St. Cloud, in Baldur's Gate. The party killed an older loop's St. Cloud, **the Traveler**, at [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/): "you killed the one who was the grey sage “last” time at Rlyeh." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09)</small> The party's own St. Cloud watched that killing, "to ambush you as you killed a previous st cloud at rlyeh" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-03)</small>, went forward, and became the Grey Sage of Antiquity, who "knows you’re coming, because he’s YOUR St. Cloud." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-09)</small>

Which names the sage of [the Three Brothers](/lore/the-three-brothers/). The fairy tale has "an elderly sage" hire three brothers to carry his library from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep, and the brothers steal the shipment and murder him — and one book survives the fire. **The Black Book that gave Wainwright his Warlock Pact came off his own older self's corpse.**

It also names the sage in [Marcus](/people/marcus/)'s account: "My sister and I came to Sigil with the sage, after the Second Fall… The sage had lied to us for a century, so we threw him in as well."

## What is not on it

The document ends at the party's first journey back. Everything from the return to [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) — the three epochs, the Egg, the final battle and [the Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) — is not yet recorded on it.
