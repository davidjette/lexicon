**Fairy tale · Told across Faerûn · True**

*Also known as:* the story of the three brothers

**The Three Brothers** is a story told to the party by a fortune teller in Suzail. It is also the origin of three of the setting's objects and of the family that drives most of its plot: **Ysmir**, **Carlysle** and [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/).

## The tale, as told

> There were three brothers, cunning merchants, whose cunning ruthlessness led them to success at a young age. One day, an elderly sage hired them to transport his vast library of tomes from Baldur's Gate to Waterdeep. Rather than honor their deal, they stole the shipment and murdered the old man. When they discovered the books were mostly worthless, they burned them.\
> \
> One book survived the fire — a black book, bound in flesh. When they opened it, its spirit spoke to them, warning them if they were willingly burdened by the horrific knowledge kept within, they would receive a gift that fulfilled their dreams and that the secret inside would dictate their fate for all time. The youngest brother wished to be the greatest ship captain in the realm, and so the book gave him a magical sextant that granted him that renown. The second wished to never die, so his heart was turned into amber and bronze, an immortal engine that would pump away even if his body is destroyed. The third and eldest brother wished to own and command the book itself. The book made him its master and it his.\
> \
> These gifts made the brothers rich and powerful, and they achieved their dreams. But the knowledge that the book contained consumed their waking thoughts, dark wisdom so horrible that I dare not speak of it to you. But they never rested for another day, nor forever, as they lived on in an endless world of constant dread.

<small>— a fortune teller in Suzail. Arc III, The Age of the Infanta, Part VII (Suzail) and Part VIII (War Wizards); repeated in the Part VIII handouts.</small>

## Who the sage was

**The elderly sage they murdered was [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) himself, from the previous turn of the loop.**

The master chronology has St. Cloud living in deep time as **the Old Man**, raising Netheril as **the Grey Sage**, being banished to Despondos and escaping — and then, in the present, in three consecutive steps: "The St. Clouds meet the Old Man", "Old Man uses Soul Syphon to trade souls w/ WSC", "Kills old Man + turns WSC's brothers." <small>([The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).)</small>

The library came from Baldur's Gate — where the timeline says Wainwright was born — and the one book that survived the brothers' fire was his own older self's. **His Warlock Pact came off his own corpse.**

## The three gifts

| Brother | Wish | Gift |
|---|---|---|
| **Ysmir**, the youngest | To be the greatest ship captain in the realm | A magical sextant. Captain of the *Evening Star*; the celebrated thirty-year safe passage. |
| **Carlysle**, the second | Never to die | A heart of amber and bronze — "an immortal engine that would pump away even if his body is destroyed." |
| **Wainwright**, the third and eldest | To own and command the book | The Black Book itself. "The book made him its master and it his." |

<small>Ysmir's gift is elsewhere named the **Silver Sextant**, Carlysle's a **mechanical heart** that made him immortal, and Wainwright's **the Black Book which contained knowledge of Cthulhu and gave him his Warlock Pact**. The Arc I session text calls Ysmir's instrument the **Quicksilver Sextant**. Both names stand; neither is corrected.</small>

## What the knowledge was

The fortune teller will not name it. The black book carries knowledge of [Cthulhu](/lore/the-leviathan/), and it is the source of Wainwright's Warlock Pact. The Arcaneum's reading of what such knowledge does to a person: "Those who learn the true nature of this being become consumed with it, and often start on extremely bizarre endeavors to bring it into being. Usually this results in madness, but when a powerful Sorcerer, or Wizard, is under its thrall, it has led to apocalyptic ends." <small>(Celion, Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small>

## How each gift came due

**Ysmir's sextant.** The Quicksilver Sextant "reveals the direction of any known object, place or person. The path it suggests will always avoid all storms and pirates." Ysmir was a slaver, and the *Evening Star*'s luck was worked by an enslaved dark-elf Navigator kept compliant with enchanted rum. Ysmir is the first of the brothers to die. <small>(Arc I, Hope in Exile, Parts I–III.)</small>

**Carlysle's heart.** The engine pumps on *even if his body is destroyed*. The party killed him and buried him, and the heart dug itself out of the grave <small>(see [Carlysle St. Cloud](/people/carlysle-st-cloud/))</small>. At the Second Battle of Motherstone the party captured Carlysle in [Roland's Cube](/items/rolands-cube/) and threw him and the [Palantir](/items/the-palantir/) into the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/). <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-05-25)</small>

**Wainwright's book.** "The book made him its master and it his."

## The trifecta

After Carlysle's obliviation, "a mysterious change to the trifecta of St. Cloud… extended to Wainwright and shaped much of his hatred of the party from then on." <small>(Nobody in Motherstone remembered Carlysle or the Palantir afterwards; the city believed Roland had betrayed it.)</small>

Hearing the tale again later did not bring back the party's memory of Carlysle. Dave: "The Obliviator still clouds everything around Carlysle and it still has erased the Palantir, but now you can put a name to it and Roland can reason and appreciate that he got his revenge". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-10-14)</small>
