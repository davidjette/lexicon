**Ancient magical devices · Parts of Zoth's craft · Studied by the Company's Special Projects · Origins erased from memory**

*Also known as:* the Eldritch Machines · the infernal devices

The **Infernal Machines**, which [Pim](/people/pim/) called the **Eldritch Machines**, are a class of ancient magical devices that are parts of Zoth's craft and of [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/), scattered over deep time. The [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/), the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) and [the Soul Syphon](/items/the-soul-syphon/) are among them, and the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) was made from them. [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) carried them forward from Zoth's ship; the Netherese held them and powered their empire with them; [Brightmantle](/people/brightmantle/) took the Obliviator to [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/) and built the Lexicon from it; and the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) opened a Special Projects department to find and control them. [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) took the Soul Syphon at Whitehorn and the Anachron from the Arcaneum. When Cypher obliviated Cthulhu and the machines at the end of the Infanta, the Obliviator, the Soul Syphon and the Mammon Machine were destroyed, and the origins of all of them became cursed information.

## The name

"Infernal machine" is the older name. At Motherstone the elves of the crystal gardens told the party of the Drek's spider god Ayror, "who controls these infernal machines from deep below these ruins." <small>(Arc II, Motherstone Part I.)</small> The fourth arc of the chronicle is named for them: [The Infernal Machines](/history/arc-iv-the-infernal-machines/).

Pim proposed the other name. Dave, answering the players' questions on the machines: "One last thing I would add is that pim believes a better name for them would be the “eldritch machines”". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

## Zoth's ship

The machines are salvage from the vessel of Zoth-Ommog, which lay dormant on the ocean floor while the aboleth built R'lyeh around it. The Mammon Machine is half of that vessel, and the Obliviator and the Anachron were both parts of it: "The Penteract is a five-dimensional structure surrounding the City of Dis, which is ½ of Zoth's vessel (i.e Mammon Machine)." <small>(Arc VI, Time of Troubles Part VII.)</small>

St. Cloud brought the dragons against the aboleth to reach the ship, deactivated the Obliviator, took the Anachron, and flew the ship into space to meet Cthulhu, travelling forward in time with it ahead of the impact. See [The Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/).

Dave on how that history was kept: "there are many false tellings of the origins of the Infernal/Eldritch machines because of obliviation and fog of war/history. The true nature of them is that they are parts of Zoth's craft/Rlyeh scattered over deep time. But some would know them as Netherese, when they obliviated they had new owners, and so on, only St Cloud had a really fully picture, and briefly, the party." <small>(Sic. Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## The tellings

Each people that held the machines gave its own account of them.

**The Netherese.** In the Company's reading of Netherese texts, the Mammon Machine was "the greatest treasure of that doomed people", with which "they had slain the god of death and captured his essence in the heart of the machine". <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small> A captured Netherese civilian gave the heroes "detailed knowledge about the Netherese, their culture, the role of the Eldritch Machines in creating their power, the religion of the Nether, and their plan to marry the Mammon Machine with the Egg". <small>(Arc VII, Leviathan I.)</small> See [Netheril](/places/netheril/).

**The Company's researchers.** St. Cloud's file on them opens: "The Infernal Machines are much older than human civilization, possibly millions of years old and built by beings who traveled the border between the Deep Realms of the Astral Sea and the Elemental Plane of Water." Of the Mammon Machine, "Toots Gilderbrow asserts that it was used for Deep Realm travel by an immense interstellar vessel." <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

**Flex Gimble and Toots Gilderbrow.** Gilderbrow told the party: "Flex blamed the Infernal Machines, and we set out to find them." Of the Obliviator: "The elves and dwarves who built Motherstone were ingenious, but the Obliviator was not their work." <small>(Arc IV, The Infernal Machines Part III.)</small> See [Flex Gimble](/people/flex-gimble/).

**Pim.** "No designs for the Obliviator have ever been found, although since the “awakening” of motherstone, scholars like Pim and Gimble theorized the machine was already built, and the city and its unique moonstone formations were merely the perfect place to turn it on." And: "the machines predate the First Age of Infanta by millennia, Pim and Gimble place them as originating before any other knows artifacts and long before the creation date believed in most faiths". <small>(Sic. Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

**Dave, to the table.** Asked whether the Lexicon's technology resembled the Netherese technology the party had met, "the train and/or infernal devices", Dave answered: "The Obliviator and the other infernal devices are unique and not netherese". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-08-24)</small>

**Legend.** "The Obvliator is an 'infernal machine', one of four from legend" / "it is incorporated into motherstone, but experts suspect it was built far before that". <small>(Sic. Dave. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-04-01)</small>

## In deep time and Netheril

Dave's handwritten Infanta Timeline has the Eldritch Machines activated to make the Leviathan forget Ydryssl forever, and St. Cloud reversing them to bend Cthulhu into the Moon. As the Grey Sage he promised the twin queens of Netheril that they would rule Toril "if they help him locate and use the Eldritch Machines"; they found the Soul Syphon and drained the god of death with it. The Netherese went on to activate the Mammon Machine, and to find the Obliviator and the Anachron. After their defeat the Obliviator was taken to Motherstone, the Grey Sage and the queens carried the Soul Syphon and the Anachron to [Sigil](/places/sigil/), and the machines were hidden in [Despondos](/places/despondos/). See [The Infanta Timeline](/lore/the-infanta-timeline/).

The orcs of Cypher held machines as well. Dave: "The Netherese won, took the Obliviator and other critical Eldritch artifacts and put every member of the Orcish urban population and intelligentsia into a memory Holocaust, Fed men women and children into the obliviator". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-21)</small> See [the Cypheric Orcs](/organizations/cypheric-orcs/).

## Motherstone

"Brightmantle takes the Obliviator from Netheril to add it to Motherstone" / "It’s what let’s him build lex from the Motherstone". <small>(Sic. Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2020-07-26)</small> Dave tied the machine to the Infanta: "the last time the Infanta were on earth two of them got a married and built motherstone which has one of machines floating over it". By drawing information toward it, the Obliviator "creates the magical fields which carry the Infanta a power across the world", and "It makes the lexicon possible by feeding that information into it". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

## The Company's Special Projects

St. Cloud's search for the machines ran through the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) and its successor, St. Cloud Trading Partners. From his executive memos <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives)</small>:

- **28th Midyear, 1461 DR**, on [House Nimbus](/organizations/house-nimbus/): "Simply destroying them will not help us, they have to give us the Machines." Revan Darkcember's clan had guarded one of the machines in Sigil, and St. Cloud killed them for it. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>
- **18th Frostfall, 1462 DR:** "The recent breaches of security have led to regrettable outcomes. Its unfortunate that the teams had to be liquidated. We are opening a Special Projects department to handle issues related to the Infernal Machines." <small>(Sic.)</small>
- **15th Hearthfire, 1468 DR:** "All executive business is to be conducted through Dox Prime. I will be focusing on Special Projects going forward."
- **3rd Rain's Hand, 1490 DR:** "Special Projects will deliver payload to my ship for delivery to the War Wizards. I will be going myself."
- **10th Evening Star, 1491 DR**, resigning as chief executive: "I will continue to lead Special Projects."

Research and Development was founded to learn about the machines "and how to activate them". St. Cloud made a grant to Himmelberg University to enlist the Sigilese academics Toots Gilderbrow and Flex Gimble in collecting everything known about them, and "The special report they produced was sequestered by Special Projects." The department's file lists five machines: the Obliviator, the Soul Syphon, the Lexicon, the Mammon Machine and the Anachron. <small>(Arc IV, SCTC Archives.)</small>

The payload of 1490 was the Anachron. At the Arcaneum, [Celion](/people/celion/) wrote that St. Cloud "wanted us to activate it, because he didn’t know how", and "Now I know that St. Cloud brought more than just the Infernal Machine." <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI.)</small> Dave told the party in 2019 that "the War Wizards have one and have been studying it". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

## Temple Holdings and the machines

The party's first machine was the Soul Syphon in the tomb at Whitehorn. When Dave offered a return to retrieve it, Patrick, who played Pim, answered: "Pim wants all the machines." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-08)</small> After the raid Dave told them "you did discover the eldritch origin of the soul syphon, some combination of water elemental magick and magic from the far realm (deep)". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2018-11-11)</small> See [The Soul Syphon](/items/the-soul-syphon/).

After the Arcaneum the Anachron came to Motherstone. "Pim has spent much of his time studying the new device, comparing it with the Soul Syphon and the Obliviator. They are absolutely made by the same people with the same magics." The Lexicon found its runes to be "a unity of powerful elemental rune magic and an arcane, Deep Realm derived mathematical logic language." <small>(Arc III Part XII.)</small>

When the players asked where to study the machines, Dave answered: "Pim is an expert, Kara can always find research, the Lexicon knows what it knows and the War Wizards have one and have been studying it". On their connection to the Infanta: "I think you guys are at the forefront of that question, maybe the WW know a little more". <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2019-03-03)</small>

## At R'lyeh

At [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) the party followed St. Cloud to R'lyeh, where the machines were still one apparatus. They found thousands of Soul Syphons across the sea floor, the Obliviator spinning open at the heart of a whirlpool, and St. Cloud, who "tinkers with the infernal machine with the Anachron at its center. He is clearly preparing it for time travel." <small>(Arc V, Starfall Part V.)</small> The session record names the joined device the "mammon-obliviator-anachron". <small>(Arc V, Starfall Parts II–IV.)</small> See [The Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/).

## Obliviated

At the end of the Infanta, Cypher obliviated Cthulhu and the machines with him. Dave: "when Cypher obliviated the eldritch machines and Cthulhu, it would have to remake the mechanisms of death itself, and the area around Sigil/Dis would have completely changed" / "Souls would now be free to roam more along the lines of natural forces, and the influence of the eldritch machines disappear". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> Of the world after: "With no obliviator in existence and with the soul siphon and mammon machine destroyed, the natural convection of apostolic souls create balance between the gods and devils". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-21)</small>

What they had been was erased with them: "the true nature and existence of Cthulhu and the origins of the eldritch machines (including the Anachron/lexicon) were erased from memory and are now cursed information which only madmen and poets ever understand". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-27)</small> See [The Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).

The Lexicon wishes it could know the full story of the machines, and that drives it insane. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-temple-holdings-rulings-c1-c10.md)</small> Dave, on its search: "whenever it is successful at recovering the truth about its origin, it reopens the door for Cthulhu to be summoned again". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

## The machines

- **[The Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/)** — the orb in the bronze ring above Motherstone, which erases what passes into it from every mind.
- **[The Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/)** — half of Zoth's vessel; the greatest treasure of Netheril.
- **[The Anachron](/items/the-anachron/)** — the drive of the Company's airships and of the *Arielle*, and a time machine; home of the Lexicon.
- **[The Soul Syphon](/items/the-soul-syphon/)** — a machine that traps souls and moves them between bodies; many exist.
- **[The Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/)** — the mind of Motherstone, built by Brightmantle from the Obliviator and the city's great crystals.
