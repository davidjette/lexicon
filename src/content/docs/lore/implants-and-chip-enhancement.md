**Arcane-cybernetic augmentation · Commercial, medical and Imperial · Ubiquitous in Sharn · Primary manufacturer: BioTec**

*Also known as:* chips · chip enhancement · implants · grafts · "getting chipped"

**Implants and chip enhancement** are arcane-cybernetic augmentation, commercial, medical and Imperial in use and ubiquitous in Sharn, where the primary manufacturer is [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/). They are sold on the street, fitted in clinics, and worn by dock workers and by councillors. Alongside its medical trade BioTec supplied the Iron Veil and military intelligence with prototype devices and classified enhancements: the [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/), made-to-order teleportation chips, interrogation technology, the warforged hybridisation grafts of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), and obedience-blocking control chips that prevent an implanted person from speaking a particular name. Two of those control chips were cut out on the record, both by Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/).

## The Trade

Chip enhancement is ordinary commerce in the Lower City. The **Graft & Gear** implant clinic operates openly inside the Ziggurat, among the markets and the fast eateries. **Circuit City**, the largest building on Gear Street, was known citywide for chip implants and refurbished [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) equipment, and served the workers of the Cogs and the Lowline Ward. Its owner had a professional reputation, immaculate paperwork and rare inventory.

For the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) this is how a worker keeps working after the factory has taken something off them, and how the job is kept. Circuit City, the market that sold them their hands, was owned by [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), who was collecting their bodies.

## Imperial Applications

BioTec's public face is medical. Its contract work is not. Under Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/), the company advanced the Empire's capabilities in chip enhancement, interrogation technologies and other bio-engineering applications, supplying the Iron Veil and military intelligence with prototype devices and classified enhancements.

- **The [Ghost Chip](/items/the-ghost-chip/)** — an implant that makes the wearer invisible and inaudible to ArcEyes.
- **Teleportation chips** — made to order, and trackable by their pings. [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) commissioned one as a gift for [Faith Zeir](/people/faith/); when it was stolen he had it followed across the city.
- **Interrogation technology** — Menka's department, and the reason BioTec is named in the same breath as the Veil's cells.
- **Hybridisation grafting** — the warforged components of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/), fused with humanoid bodies.
- **Control chips** — see below.

## Control Chips

The obedience-blocking control chip does not compel action. It compels *silence*: the implanted person cannot speak a particular name or disclose a particular involvement. Attempting it hurts.

Dr. [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) implanted them in BioTec employees and in [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), preventing any of them from naming him or admitting his role in anything. Graves owned a corporation, a chip parlour, an arcade and an apartment building, and his own file describes him as so understated it bordered on invisible.

### Two Removals

**Sister Nora, Zilspar Farm 02.** Under a Zone of Truth she gave up Project E.D.E.N., the Eternal Nightfall and the names of Esther's sisters without much trouble. Asked directly who supplied the corpses, she began to seize, clutching the side of her head near the ear as blood ran from her nose, and collapsed. Sir [Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) removed the chip and left her to recover. That extraction is the reason the Cog Collector investigation existed at all.

**Martin Kross, the Underhive.** Held in Blair's chambers, [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) told the trio what he knew about BioTec, the Ghost Chip prototypes and [Red Khyber](/items/red-khyber/) shipments, and said there was a private owner pulling the strings whose name he could not produce. Dario cut the control chip out of his skull. Kross said: **Edwin**.

## Inside Circuit City

The technology is common enough that a citizen's hardware attracts no notice. When Graves took a suspicious interest in the Inevitables' implants inside Circuit City, he locked the front door and disappeared into the back.
