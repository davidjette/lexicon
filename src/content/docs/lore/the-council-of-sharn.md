**Provisional government, trials and executions · The Veil Building penthouse, Sharn · Sharn Ep 19**

The Council of Sharn is the provisional government of Sharn, formed in the Veil Building penthouse on the morning after the rising (Sharn Ep 19) by representatives of six factions, each of which stated its price for joining. The council ordered immediate trials, executed [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/), Hillary Heinrick, Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/) and senior Iron Veil officials, dissolved the Iron Veil and dismantled the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), and by nightfall had appointed [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) Chancellor of Sharn in the dead Lord Commander's penthouse.

By dawn [Sharn was free](/history/the-fall-of-sharn/) and about to starve, riot or be retaken. [Lord Commander Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) was dead at the base of her own tower. Mayor [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) was dead on the balcony. The Iron Veil's pact had been dismantled by its own commander. Nobody was in charge of a city of towers.

## THE COVER STORY

[Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) arrived first and went straight to [Richard](/people/richard-blaze/) and [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/). After a brief private exchange he told the trio that the two of them would teleport to Zilspar Farm before anyone else got there, and proposed the lie that would keep them alive: [Faith](/people/faith/) and [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/) had ambushed Richard's private airship during the kidnapping and killed Richard, Calcifer and two of Esther's Honor Guards. With the Children of Ember already dead, there was nobody left to contradict it. Digma left to coordinate the narrative.

It held. Three years later the Chancellor's own table still names Richard Blaze as the most famous bard alive and believed dead.

## THE ROUND-UP

[Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) came next with the night's ledger. Zero-One and Silver Flame paladins had seized the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) hospital and rounded up its clergy. Mass protests were tearing down statues of [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) across the city. Nest and Fulcrum operatives held the Veil Building. High-ranking officials including Hillary Heinrick and Dr. [Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/) were in custody. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s Hundreds and [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) were collecting Iron Veil soldiers: many fled, many hid, many simply surrendered.

## THE TABLE

Representatives of every major faction came to the penthouse. Jessica opened, credited the trio with preventing Sharn's fall, and framed the council's purpose as stabilisation and continued resistance against the Empire. Each delegation stated its price.

- **Leef** — alliance and stability.
- **NEST** — trials, and rebuilding funds.
- **The Silver Flame** — outlaw the Black Doves, and citywide cremation, enforced.
- **R.U.S.T.** — Warforged citizenship, arrest of the Iron Veil engineers, a new infrastructure department.
- **The Underhive** — autonomy, legal ownership of the Ziggurat and the Vault Below, and a permanent council seat for Berenstain.

It nearly broke in the room. During a dispute [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) ordered [Blair](/people/blair/) arrested — the Queen of the Underhive, whose drones had died fighting the Iron Veil days earlier and whose tunnels had carried the Cogborn out. She was tried later and eventually released.

## THE TRIALS

Trials began immediately. [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/), Hillary Heinrick and Dr. Joanne Menka were found guilty and executed, along with other high-level Iron Veil officials. The Silver Flame's cremation directive was enacted across the city.

## THE BODY

When the Silver Flame came to cremate Esther Crona, her body was gone. Witnesses reported two elven women — believed to be [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) — removing a corpse from the morgue during the chaos. The order she gave with her last breath was to kill Empress Uriel Qualanthri, herself one of the Black Doves, and it was women believed to be Black Doves who took her.

## THE BELL IN THE CRATE

In the seized hospital, the Nest and the Silver Flame found a large golden bell inside a shipping crate, and an unfinished bell tower waiting for it. It was identified as a replica of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) — infernal metal, ancient elvish work, associated with raising the dead and inducing madness — and a similar bell had been dug out of the [Greenbluff mines](/history/the-greenbluff-incident/) about ten years earlier by [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/). <small>(The Sharn Ep 19 summary says thirteen years; Fulcrum's bell file says "twenty years ago" and the rebel-factions page "over a decade ago".)</small>

## THE GOVERNMENT

By nightfall a provisional government existed. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) was appointed Chancellor of Sharn and took residence in Esther's former penthouse, now the seat of the administration. Warforged forces offered troops and engineers to stabilise the city and restore the Cogs. Underhive drones joined the new Department of Infrastructure while keeping their subterranean autonomy. The Iron Veil was dissolved and the Black Doves dismantled.

> Sharn was free but rebuilding had only begun.
