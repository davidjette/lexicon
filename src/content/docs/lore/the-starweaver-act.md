**Law · The Sovereignty of Realms · In force in The Starsong Awakens**

*Also known as:* the Starweaver act

**The Starweaver Act** is a law of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/), passed by the Sovereign Senate a year after [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/), that outlaws all artificial intelligence, warforged and autognomes included. It led to a devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures and created a thriving illicit market for robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> It bears the name of Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/). Under it the agents of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) hunt illegal artificial intelligence, and [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/), the robot resistance, fight for robots. The Act is the setting of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*.

## Background

The Act followed the betrayal of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/), which at the end of *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)* fled Sigil for deep space. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Dave called the Act a "Totally predictable outcome of the Lexicon turning on humanity too", and wrote that people would lose trust in AI: "There would be a Butlerian jihad!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

After the Lexicon's flight, [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) virtually replaced it with [Hyperion](/lore/hyperion/). The Company is most likely pursuing possibly illegal replacements at dark sites outside the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

JL's opening narration for *The Starsong Awakens* places the Battle of Tannhauser Gate fifteen years before the arc, when the crew of the Zanzibar solved the riddle of the Shrike. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Home)</small> See [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). Barrias's player wrote that *The Starsong Awakens* "is centered on Nine and how he set out to fix everything bad that happened after Tannhauser Gate". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-06)</small>

## Provisions

The Company's dossier on [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) records that, following the Sovereign Senate's passage of "The Starweaver Act", the possession or dissemination of artificial intelligence was criminalised within Sovereign territory, carrying a penalty of not more than 1,000 years in prison. The Bureau was created to enforce the new law, with authority to act as a paramilitary arm of the Sovereign government, and it pursues and destroys artificial intelligence and those who seek to perpetuate it. <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Company dossier)</small>

The [Red Eye Manifesto](/items/the-red-eye-manifesto/) describes the vote this way:

> Fifteen years ago, our elected Sovereign delegates voted to abolish intelligence they deemed artificial from the face of the galaxy.
>
> <small>— The Way It Is, Chapter One (The Starsong Awakens site, Red Eye Manifesto)</small>

## Effects

The purge fell on every artificial being, warforged and autognomes included, and a black market in robots grew up beside it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Dave added that cybrids and replicants would be pushed to the edges and hunted down, and that without robots slavery and labor exploitation on the fringes would greatly increase. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Dave also wrote that Sandy, [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), "would def ally with Starweaver and enforce the end of AI, might even become fanatical bc of what they did to her best friends and her home (the Zanzibar)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

Marcel Martel's backstory, as his player wrote it, records one victim: his wife Robin Martel, a well-known and well-liked synth AI politician in Waterdeep, "was taken from their home by Bureau goons and dismantled" once the Act was implemented. Marcel has fought for her and for all robots since. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-07)</small> See [Marcel Martel](/people/marcel-martel/).

## Enforcement

The Bureau of Compliance sends agents after the source of illegal AI. Its rookie agents, whom the table called "Blade Runners", were posted to Brownsleeve City, a sunny beachside resort on the moon of Toril, each with a Club of Compliance and a Bureau badge, and one flying car between them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small> Kol Hanaka's sheet lists him as "Bureau of Compliance - Agent (Blade Runner)". <small>(character sheet)</small> His partner Paytra died in the line of duty on the moon, and Kol and Paytra were later rebuilt as robots, with new robot backgrounds to choose from. The DM wrote of them: "Their mission is a success. They found the Red Eye." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02, 2024-11-10, 2024-11-17)</small>

## Resistance

The robot resistance is devoted to the Red Eye, the nom de guerre of [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> There are four sites on Selune holy to the children of the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-08-16)</small> The crew of the stolen Istus Cruiser are devotees of the Red Eye. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-10-26, 2024-11-02)</small> They include Weld, a liberated warforged farming and ranch-hand unit who joined the resistance, and Marcel Martel, once a famous Waterdeep musician and actor. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-02, 2024-11-07)</small> Weld's sheet gives his faith as "Red Eye Revolutionary". <small>(character sheet)</small> See [Umwelt](/people/umwelt/).
