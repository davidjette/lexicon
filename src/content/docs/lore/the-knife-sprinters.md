**Warforged hunter-killers of Cyre · Controlled by [DR4MB](/items/dr4mb/)**

**The Knife Sprinters** were [warforged](/lore/warforged/) of Cyre programmed to terminate obsolete warforged units, remembered in the session report as past warforged "set out to kill / decommission warforged in Cyer" <small>(sic)</small>. Their emblem is a bronze badge bearing a shield and a dagger. They were controlled by a machine called [DR4MB](/items/dr4mb/). <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

The Knife Sprinter protocol was believed to have ended when Cyre fell. The symbol was old enough that, of the Unforeseen, only Uriel and LeBeefe recognised it when it turned up in the room of the R-0 unit Radd Orgasm, who had tried to kill [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) in Korth with an order for Locke's decommissioning. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

It is claimed that the Emperor ordered DR4MB, as Doctor Amber, to start the Knife Sprinter initiative again and exterminate every warforged. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
