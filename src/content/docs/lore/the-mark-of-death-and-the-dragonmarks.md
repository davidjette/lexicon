**Heritable arcane brands · The dragonmarked houses of Khorvaire · One mark extinct, and two women wearing it**

The **Mark of Death and the Dragonmarks** are the heritable arcane brands carried by particular bloodlines of Khorvaire, granting powers specific to each line and forming the basis of the dragonmarked houses' monopolies. The **Mark of Death** is the mark of [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/), officially extinct: the house was eradicated thousands of years ago by an alliance of elves, humans, giants and dragons raised against it, and its leader [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/) was a half-dragon half-elf who ruled over undeath. Two living women bear the mark — [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), whose own file does not record how or when she developed it, and Gabrielle Duin, consort to the Emperor — and Master Armhair's confiscated manuscript ties the mark, Erandis Vol's birth and a single vanished dragon together.

> "At some point, Uriel developed the Mark of Death. Haven't filled in that detail yet of how or when."

## THE HOUSES' ACCOUNT — WHAT A DRAGONMARK IS

A dragonmark is a heritable arcane brand carried by particular bloodlines, granting powers specific to its line, and it is the basis of the great houses' monopolies. It runs in the blood and cannot be bought. The marks attested in this archive:

- **The Dragonmark of Storm**, often called the Dragonmark of Flight — House Lyrandar. Borne by Supreme Admiral Vex d'Lyrandar, whose mark gave him command of the Empire's sea and airship armada, and by his son [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/), pilot, designer and Head of Transportation.
- **The Mark of Finding** — thought to be borne by General Stockton Thar, Imperial commander of the Iron Veil's ground forces and a Dark Druid of the Eldeen Reaches; a rare mark granting uncanny ability to locate people and objects.
- **The Passage Mark** — carried by [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), who used it to break a grell's grip thirty feet above the floor of the Glitterhame.
- **The Mark of Death** — House Vol. Officially extinct.

## THE ERADICATION

The Mark of Death is a bloodline and power most believe was wiped out long ago by an alliance of elves and dragons, raised to destroy the aberrant [House of Vol](/organizations/house-vol/). House Vol was eradicated by humans, giants and "good" elves thousands of years ago; its leader was [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/), the Queen of Death, a half dragon half elf who ruled over undeath. The mark's offence was the crossing of the elvish and draconic lines.

**It is not closed.** Valtrex, testifying under the Korramont with no reason to flatter anyone: *the elvish houses that fled after the purge of House Vol remain obsessed with Vol's bloodline.*

## THE LIVING BEARERS

- **Uriel Qualanthri** — a twisting and growing draconic mark between her navel and her loins. She is a descendant of the House and an eager follower of Erandis Vol, and the mark is the reason she believes the Vol bloodline should rule all of Eberron as an undead wonderland. How and when she developed it is not recorded anywhere, including in her own file, which says so out loud.
- **Gabrielle (Gabriel) Duin** — claims direct descendance from the House of Vol and bears the Dragonmark of Death. Handler of the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) under the codename Godseed; killed the former Star Prelate of the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) on the fields at Cyre and blamed it on the undead; now in the Imperial Palace as consort to the Emperor.

Two women, both bearing an extinct mark, both inside the Imperial house.

## THE ARMHAIR THREAD

Master Armhair's confiscated manuscript, recovered from the Hall of Records at Korth under a false index, contains a dragon family tree traced back to the progenitors, an identification of Valtrex's father, and **a thread connecting the Mark of Death, Erandis Vol's birth, and the same dragon** — the vanished progenitor he calls [Viagra](/people/viagra/), and whom he claims is the being on the Imperial throne. Renn Tal, the Sharn archivist who traced the ritual [Priest E'noch](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) worked at the Forge of Binding to bind the Eternal Guardian, had reached the same suspicions from the other end of the continent.

If Armhair's thread holds, the Mark of Death is what happens when one particular dragon's blood enters an elvish line — which would mean the alliance of elves and dragons that eradicated House Vol was hunting a dragon's own descendants, and that the dragon in question survived the purge by becoming a king.

<small>Preserved ambiguity: the dragon by which Erandis Vol bore a child is called **the Crimson Sun**. So is the Empire's secret service, whose head was [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/) and whose sigil is carved in marble in the throne room. Both usages are in play and the connection between them is unresolved.</small>

## WHAT THE MARK IS FOR

The consecration rite of the [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/), recovered from [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/)'s library, binds each casting to **"the unholy blood and the great mother"** — thirteen Doves, all thirteen planes, and a very dear sacrifice. The Progenitor's assessment of the network says the risen come up ready to serve *"a sufficiently powerful necromancer keyed in to the chorus,"* and that there is exactly one such necromancer alive.

The rite binds the bells to blood, and the network answers one necromancer.

## The Party's Reading — Inference

Set the mark beside the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/)'s closing promise and the theory the Inevitables reached in Korth follows: Uriel bears the Mark and descends from Vol; Erandis bore a dragon's child; the Litany says the dragon will be reborn; therefore Uriel is attempting to bear a dragon-sired son — which is what fourteen daughters and three years of bleeding Valtrex are for. **This is the party's inference and no recovered document states it.**

Uriel's plan for the son goes further.
