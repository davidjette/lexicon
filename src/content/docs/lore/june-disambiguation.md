**June** is a name borne by an Infanta, by the child Chronocypher became in her place, and by an Eldeen envoy. It may refer to:

- **[June, the Infanta of Time](/people/the-infanta-of-time/)**, the Infanta at Refuge and the incarnation of Istus, the god of Fate and Time, who guarded the Mammon Machine from Wainwright St. Cloud.
- **[June, as lived by Chronocypher](/people/stonecypher/#chronocypher)**: when the party returned to the "present", Chronocypher took June's place at Refuge and lived through that arc again as the child June. See [Stonecypher](/people/stonecypher/).
- **[June Gloomstalker](/people/june-gloomstalker/)**, the human druidess sent as envoy of the dark druid coven that calls itself the Eldeen Confederacy.

*See also:* the Fates, the three-fold god of Time, Fate and Mystery: Eloise, Istus and Cypher.
