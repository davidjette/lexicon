**Religious rite · The Silver Flame · Ancient and nearly forgotten**

**The Silver Flame Name-Stripping Rite** is an ancient and nearly forgotten rite of the [Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/) that strips away a woman's name and her past and leaves an embodiment of the Flame's discipline and sacrifice, taking the codename **Zero**. It is a rite of passage among certain women of the faith and is not undertaken lightly. Fema Nolan took it after the death of her fellow Fulcrum founder [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) and was the first woman in centuries to undergo the transformation; three bearers of the name are recorded — Zero-One, [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) and Zero of Leef. The words, the officiant, the setting and the duration of the rite itself are not recorded.

## THE RITE AS THE FLAME DESCRIBES IT

An ancient and nearly forgotten rite that strips away one's name and past. Those who take the name devote themselves fully to the Flame and live as embodiments of its discipline and sacrifice. The codename **Zero** is what remains. It is a rite of passage among certain women of the faith, and it is not undertaken lightly: Fema Nolan took it after the death of her companion and fellow founder, and was **the first woman in centuries to undergo the transformation**.

## THE NUMBERING

- **Zero-One** — Fema Nolan, born and raised in Thrane, once a respected Paladin of the Silver Flame known for compassion, discipline and fierce devotion to protecting the innocent. Founding member of Fulcrum with Richard Blaze, Digma Beeve and [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/); she took the rite after Stamos's death on the lightning rail and emerged as Zero-One. She now leads Fulcrum out of Zilspar, vanishes for long stretches with a select group of personal knights, and is the mother of Izaak and of [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/).
- **Zero of Sharn** — her daughter; a young human cleric of the Flame, covert agent of the Nest, and, in the Korth arc, the convener of a meeting at the hospital with a representative from Sharn of every faith on the continent.
- **Zero of Leef** — an elderly Tabaxi follower of the Flame, one of the defenders who held Leef alongside Thadric "The Colonel" Rourke and Princess Orange Chicken.

## THE FLAME UNDER THE EMPIRE

The Silver Flame is outlawed by the Empire. Under those conditions a name is a liability, and the Empire's own courts operate a [kin-and-patron](/lore/kin-and-patron/) system in which a condemned woman's spouse, siblings, employer or patron may testify to her value. A woman with no name and no past cannot be leveraged through her family, and her family cannot be leveraged through her.

The **Nightingale** shows what the rite is protecting against. Bantide Moro worked twenty years for Izaak as head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital and the rebellion's surgeon, from surgery to revivification, and kept a codename and a legal identity as an Imperial medical records keeper. When the Empire came for her it did not need to prove sabotage; it prosecuted her in her own name, produced letters addressed to a beloved sister, and put her sister in the courtroom to read the charges.

## WHAT IT COSTS

Not everything is erased, and that is the flaw. Zero-One is *known to be* the mother of Izaak and Zero of Sharn; her lineage is written openly in the rebellion's own record of its factions; the family remains a network and therefore a target. When **Zilspar Farm was destroyed and Zero-One taken alive** by E.D.E.N. units, the Nightingale's report from Korth made the pattern plain: Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent as if the Empire had obtained a list of every name, executions running for days, and all of it beginning after Izaak disappeared. The rite unmakes a name. It cannot unmake a list.

## The Rite and Its Mirror

The Flame is not the only order in Khorvaire with an initiation rite for its women. The [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) veil their novices under seal of initiation and recite the [Litany](/lore/the-litany/) at the veiling, and [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/) stood at the Empress's own veiling. Two sisterhoods, one proscribed and one privileged, on opposite sides of the same war.

> "Those who take the name strip away their former identity, devoting themselves fully to the Flame and living as embodiments of its discipline and sacrifice."
