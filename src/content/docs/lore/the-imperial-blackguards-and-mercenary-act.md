**Concept · Imperial statute · Weapon and bodyguard licensing within the walls of [Korth](/places/korth/)**

*Also known as:* the Blackguards Act · the Mercenary Act

The Imperial Blackguards and Mercenary Act is an Imperial statute governing who may carry a weapon, and who may employ an armed bodyguard, within the walls of [Korth](/places/korth/). It makes smuggling weapons into the city punishable by death, requires an armed unenlisted bodyguard in the city centre to be brought in on substantial authority and to be appropriately licensed and insured, and is administered by form number — 11-B and 62-A for the authority, 7-168 for each registered weapon. Failure to file draws a written official reprimand signed by the responsible officer of authority. It is enforced at checkpoints such as the one on the [Great Plaza](/places/the-great-plaza/), and evaded through a forger on the third level of [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/).

## What it requires

- **Smuggling weapons into the city is punishable by death.**
- An armed, **unenlisted** bodyguard in the centre of the city requires **quite an authority** to bring in at all.
- The bearer must be **appropriately licensed and insured** under the Act.
- Failure to file is met on the spot with a written **official reprimand**, signed by the responsible officer of authority.

## The forms

- **Form 11-B** — filed by the authority under whom the armed unenlisted carries.
- **Form 62-A** — the companion filing; cited in the same breath as 11-B by any officer who stops you.
- **Form 7-168** — registration of a weapon; the clerk writes down each weapon the bearer produces.

Identity papers carry a written description and a portrait drawn directly onto the page with a wand and sealed. A licensed guard is expected to produce all of it on demand at any checkpoint.

## Enforcement

A checkpoint stands at a corner of [the Great Plaza](/places/the-great-plaza/), a small cadre of stormtroopers under a Force Captain. When [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) — wearing [Deirdre Moro's](/people/deirdre-moro/) face — produced Ministry of Law authority for an undocumented bodyguard, the Force Captain had already worked out what she was:

> "You're with the Ministry of Law. Why didn't you say so? I wanted to see what you'd ask me. He is carrying weapons under your authority. You must file Form 11-B. Sixty-two A. He must be appropriately licensed and insured under the Imperial Blackguards and Mercenary Act. I should not have to describe this to you, lawyer."

He wrote the reprimand, took her scribbled signature, and let them pass with the line *"Smuggling weapons into the city is punishable by death, Advocate. Please proceed."*

## How it is beaten

On the third level of [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/) sits a non-binary dwarf clerk who forges identity papers and weapon registrations for a bribe or a shakedown. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was papered there under the name **Sky Rizzy**, with a wand-drawn portrait and an official seal, and every weapon he produced was logged on Form 7-168 — a lance among them.

Every weapon except **KNELL**. The hammer that breaks the bells has never appeared on an Imperial form.
