**Counter-artifact campaign · The Korramont, then Sharn · Korth Ep 11–12, ongoing**

**The breaking of the bells** is the counter-artifact campaign the Inevitables have waged against the chorus of the Fantanya Nyel, begun in the Korramont in Korth Ep 11–12 and continued in Sharn. For a decade [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) had been counting bells and nobody had worked out what to do about one; the answer had been forged by a slave king in the mountain the Empire was casting them in, and buried with him. Three answers came out of the Korramont — the hammer **KNELL**, King Corn's horn and the fanfare of dawn, and the forging folio. With them the campaign has destroyed [the Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/), every cast bell in the Korranberg Bladeworks, the hospital bell in Sharn and the bell of the temple of Boldrei; ten castings remain, at least one of them a Master.

## THE THREE ANSWERS

**KNELL** — King Corn's personal black-Khyber warhammer, older than the bells, capable of cracking any bell ever forged. Recovered from the crowned corpse in [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/)'s hoard and named by the kin. Believed unique.

**The horn and the fanfare of dawn** — Corn's prototype, coiled brass, unornamented, two mouthpieces, carrying his smith-mark. Bofric the clan runesmith translated the song the king spent his final days perfecting and identified it as arcane harmonics, an elvish weapon from the old war against the dragons. Played on a horn of Corn's design, it suppresses a bell's influence entirely — but only while it plays. It masks. It does not break.

**The folio** — lead-bound, the forging mathematics and tempering of the castings, and on its last page: *I made their key. Someone must learn to make its answer sing.*

## THE THIRTEENTH CASTING

It hung on scaffolding over a rail cart in the Bladeworks, tuned and never consecrated, and it had already killed [a hold full of dwarves](/history/the-korranberg-massacre/) once. [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) had been told by a voice to ring it since the Glitterhame and had tried to, twice; [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) grappled him to the platform and [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) blanketed the scaffold in darkness until he came back.

In the end it was not Eric who rang it. Gemma's alarm brought them back to find Hetta, the clan's missing scout, fungus-grown and casting a spell at the casting. The bell rang. Everyone in the foundry, the camp, and the dead in the lake below went into a shared frenzy. The party nearly went with it, snapped free to save Bofric — and Dario broke the Thirteenth Casting with KNELL, deafening himself and Bofric with the blow.

Eric turned the risen dead back down the shaft. Dario then destroyed every remaining cast bell in the Bladeworks. Valtrex, freed, destroyed the original moulds himself.

## THE HOSPITAL BELL AND THE TEMPLE BELL

Back in Sharn, Dario destroyed two more in a single morning.

The first sat crated in the unfinished tower above the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) hospital — the bell found at [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/) — packed in straw by people too frightened to touch it. He cleared the muffling himself and swung with *may our children forgive us*.

The second hung in a small temple of Boldrei in the lower city. Gemma masked herself as Chancellor Eric and promised the crowd outside that nothing would be harmed. Dario's strike brought the entire tower down through the floor into the building below, buried him, and turned the crowd she had just pacified against them. By morning the papers blamed the Chancellor for the temple.

## WHAT THEY LEARNED DOING IT

- Breaking a bell **does not notify** [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). They can keep going.
- The horn can be reproduced — the method is confirmed, and [Doppler](/people/doppler-klink/) is making them.
- KNELL is likely unique. There is one hammer for however many bells there are.
- The bells are not counted in dozens. Locke Pierce's assessment is that every House of Mercy on the continent holds a copy, that copies only need to resonate the mother bell's call, and that there may be hundreds.

## THE CONGREGATION

At the hospital, [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) convened a representative of every faith on the continent. Bofric played the fanfare of the Dawn, and the clergy took up teaching what they named the **Horn of Mourning**, congregation by congregation. Dario told them he had broken three bells and would break every one, and that the woman who rings them must be cut down along with everyone who helped her.
