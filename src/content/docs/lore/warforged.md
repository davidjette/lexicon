**Species · Constructed people of the Last War · Outlawed in Sharn · A nation-in-arms in the Mournland**

*Also known as:* **the Hundreds**, for those who followed Locke Pierce into the Mournland · "Souled Machines", in the Imperial pageant · **Cogborn** is *not* a Warforged word: the Cogborn are the flesh-and-blood labour of Sharn's foundry districts, and the two peoples are constantly confused by Imperial paperwork and never by each other.

Warforged are constructed people, built as soldiers for the Last War <small>(published Eberron canon; the Imperial pageant credits Cyre with creating them)</small> and left standing when it ended. Generations of them worked the most dangerous industrial tasks in the Cogs beneath Sharn until Lord Commander [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) outlawed them in the city; most left for Gorgonhorn or the Mournland, the Iron Veil seized the vacated foundries, and the Cogborn community collapsed behind them. **Preacher** stayed and founded R.U.S.T. in the undercity. In the Mournland, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) has built the Hundreds into an armed Warforged nation at Mt. Silicon, holding the Glass Plateau against E.D.E.N. units and holding the Ruby Nova in reserve, while other Warforged remain aligned with the Empire.

## What they are

Warforged are constructed people: living constructs of wood, stone and metal, made in numbers for the Last War and left standing when it ended <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>. In the Cogs beneath Sharn, generations of Warforged labourers took the most dangerous industrial tasks — the ones that would have killed a flesh-and-blood worker — alongside the Cogborn workers who built the city's industrial heart.

The Empire's own propaganda apparatus records where they sit in its hierarchy of contempt. **Project Godseed**, the Crimson Sun's covert programme to breed radical loyalty among disaffected merchant-class workers and city guardsmen, worked by convincing them they were *better than Warforged, goblins, gnomes, halflings, orcs and dwarves* — a list, in that order, with the Warforged at the head of it. The cult that programme grew into is the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/).

## The decree

Warforged are **outlawed in Sharn**. The order came from the Lord Commander, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), ruling the city for the Empire from the Veil Building. Most of the Warforged in Sharn left — toward Gorgonhorn on the western border of the Mournland, or into the Mournland itself. The Iron Veil moved into the vacated foundries, seized the factories, and replaced Cogborn labour with trained soldiers.

The Cogborn, already weakened by decades of hazardous work and malnutrition, lost the neighbours who had absorbed the worst of the labour. They were pushed out of their jobs, then their neighbourhoods, then their homes, and finally beneath the Cogs into collapsing tunnels and abandoned sub-transit lines. Removing the Warforged from Sharn destroyed the Cogborn as a community.

## Preacher, the last one in the city

One Warforged refused the decree. **Preacher** was raised in the Cogs and shaped by its furnaces, and when the order came he did not go to Gorgonhorn. He vanished into the undercity with his adopted daughter **Misty Kross**, a Cogborn engineer he had taken in as a child, and lived for years in abandoned sub-transit tunnels using his knowledge of forgotten infrastructure to shelter Cogborn fugitives and smuggle families past Iron Veil patrols. That network became **R.U.S.T.** — Resistance Under Sharn's Tyranny.

Preacher's stated aim was not the liberation of Sharn but evacuation: the people the Empire deemed expendable moved out from under the city and delivered to Gorgonhorn, where a blended Cogborn-Warforged society has begun to take root. When the Inevitables cleared the Spine and the Chamber Room, the Cogborn walked out through the mountain tunnel, and Preacher and the RUST Runners led them in song and handed out prayer sheets to something called **Lexicon**. Most of the Cogborn have now left Sharn.

Years ago, after the outlawing, a young Tiefling woman found his hidden cell in the Lower City. He chased her through the drainage tunnels and in the struggle she fell from the aqueduct's upper walkway and was impaled on the machinery below. He believed he had killed her. She is [Eden](/people/eden/), and the Hundreds now fight her under the name **the Blue Devil**.

## The Hundreds

**The Warforged Nation of the Mournland.** Deep in the Glass Plateau, inside the dead land that ended the Last War, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — himself a Warforged, and a founding member of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) — has built an army and calls its members his *children*. Their stronghold is **Mt. Silicon**, a fortress carved into a mountainside around a control chamber of screens. They hold the Glass Plateau against E.D.E.N. units: hybrid undead-Warforged soldiers manufactured by BioTec through Umbra and Black Dove rites specifically to survive the Mournland, fight the Hundreds, and mine Red Kyber out of it. The Empire built a second kind of Warforged to kill the first kind.

The Hundreds are not a client of the rebellion. Locke offers transport, dossiers, breathing devices that generate an air bubble against the Mournland mist, and an emergency channel to his captain. At Mt. Silicon he showed the Inevitables the **Ruby Nova**: a controlled recreation of the Mourning itself, and demonstrated it on a battlefield in front of them, a red dome blooming past the Mournland border and visible from Thronehold. His terms were plain. Stop [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) before she rings the bells; if you fail, he ends the world first, and only his kind survives it.

When Sharn rose, the Hundreds came. They filled the sky alongside the Nest, Leef, the Silver Flame, RUST and the Underhive, and afterward rounded up Iron Veil soldiers through the streets. Later, with the city forty-seven days from famine, three Warforged arrived under **Himmie Joffa** and offered to feed it — in exchange for military and logistical access to Sharn's airspace. Eric granted it, said aloud that he knew it was a trick, and hoped they would survive Korth in time to take the keys back. By morning a capital ship had settled out of the fog and was handing out fruit in orderly lines. **Locke Pierce has taken the skies of Sharn.**

## Warforged of the Empire

Not all of them chose exile. [Lilith Hornblast](/people/lilith-hornblast/) sits as one of the "leaders in exile" of the East Brelish Parliament, the sham Mournland government, and represents Warforged aligned with the Empire — arguing that an independent, Imperially-aligned Warforged state is the best available outcome for her people. **Fournier** of the Trade Federation signal corps, a rogue with *sending* at will, was among those waiting in the lounge of the Crimson Sun safehouse in Thronehold when the Unforeseen were briefed on the mole in the Imperial Assembly. The two Warforged futures — Hornblast's negotiated statehood and Locke's armed nation with a doomsday weapon — have never been reconciled.

## Research on the forged

The Empire has studied its own creations. After the incident at the Rekkenmark, Chancellor Magnolia Steele permitted Locke and Uriel to relocate **Dr. Marble's** research in **autoanimata** and **necrocryptography** to the Rekkenmark East facilities, with supplemental support from U.N.D.E.A.D. Working there, they analysed the nature of **D4AMB**, the history of the Warforged, and the composition of **Embrace**. What that history turned out to be is not recorded. Locke came out of Rekkenmark East able to decrypt Imperial state secrets, and eventually with a nation.
