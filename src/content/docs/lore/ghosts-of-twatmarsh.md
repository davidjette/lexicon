**Campaign · *Ghosts of Saltmarsh* module · Saltmarsh, Oerth · DMs Dave, JL and Nico · August 2021 to October 2022 · Concluded**

*Also known as:* Twatmarsh · marshtwat · the pirate game · the Neptune game

**Ghosts of Twatmarsh** is a Dungeons & Dragons campaign played on the *Ghosts of Saltmarsh* module, set in [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/) on Oerth, with the DM's chair passing between Dave, JL and Nico. Its first party died in its first session, in August 2021, over the sword [Weathervein](/items/weathervein/). The second party, [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/), was [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/), [Scrunt Pissfoot](/people/scrunt-pissfoot/) and [Dolores Weathervein](/people/dolores-weathervein/). They took Saltmarsh's [Temple of Neptune](/places/the-temple-of-neptune/) and the ship [the Sea Ghost](/items/the-sea-ghost/), became the town's navy, recovered Weathervein on Gabriella's honeymoon, and finished the book's quests at level 10 in July 2022. They then went into [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/) after the Weathervein treasure and on to the temple of the [Eldabrin](/lore/the-eldabrin/), and in October 2022 the table moved on to [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). A few hundred years later, [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) walked into the same Saltmarsh.

## The module and the table

Before the first session Dave listed the book's adventures and their levels: *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh* (1st), *Danger at Dunwater* (3rd), *Salvage Operation* (4th), *Isle of the Abbey* (5th), *The Final Enemy* (7th), *Tammeraut's Fate* (9th) and *The Styes* (11th), each for "four to six" characters. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-11)</small> The title "GHOSTS OF TWATMARSH" heads Dave's session document for "Salvage Operation – Part I". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, attachment 1654377024765671, 2021-11-13)</small>

The DM rotated. Dave ran the first party's session, most of the book's quests and White Plume Mountain, and ran Gabriella as a DM-PC while he did. JL ran the second party's prologue, the dwarf-clan session, his "jazz dungeons", Gabriella's engagement, her honeymoon and the hunt for Skerrin, the killing of Salmor, and the kobold one-shot. Nico ran Gabriella's wedding. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15, 2021-10-01, 2022-01-22, 2022-06-21)</small>

The Weathervein story was the DMs' own addition to the book. Nico: "Yeah, anything to do with Weathervien is really outside book stuff." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>

Dave's summary of the campaign's shape:

> Level 1-6 become major figure of Saltmarsh, level 7-10 Gabriella’s honeymoon

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-18)</small>

## The first party

The first party was **Drip**, a druid Dave ran as a DM-PC ("I'll just play drip as a DM PC :D"), **Ditzy**, Nico's paladin of Neptune, and **Shingleberry**, JL's character. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-11, 2021-08-13, 2021-08-15)</small> On 2021-08-13, in *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh*, they died in the Saltmarsh marketplace, and an assassin took the sword. Dave asked "Who took Weatherbrand", and Nico answered "That assassin who murdered those nice pirates". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14)</small>

Dave started the next party "in the exact smae marketplace next to these bodies while the guards push everyone away". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-14)</small> Of Nico's new character he later wrote: "I TPKd you guysnover that sword and then you made a character out of looking for it". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-17)</small> When Ned and his men attacked the second party, Dave wrote: "They tried to whack us" and "Like they whacked shingle berry". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-17)</small>

## The second party

JL's plan for the prologue: "Gabs and Scrunt wash up on shore chained together." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15)</small> On 2021-08-20 Gabriella and Scrunt, escaped from a prison ship, hid in a cemetery and found Nico's character, Talia Weathervien, breaking into a mausoleum. Nico renamed her Dolores Weathervein on 2021-09-01, and the party named itself Neptune's Rejects on 2021-09-17. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-08-15, 2021-09-01, 2021-09-17)</small>

Allies who travelled with them:
- **[Oshi](/people/oshi/)** (Oceanus), a sea elf ranger Dave ran, who joined on 2021-09-18 and died on 2021-11-12. Dave's farewell after the session of 2022-06-21 was "Great sesh guys RIP OSHI 4 EVA". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18, 2021-11-13, 2022-06-22; `sources/dave/2026-09-14-inevitables-rulings-batch.md`)</small>
- **MacGruber**, a deaf sailor, and **Bimz**, Scrunt's pseudodragon familiar. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-18)</small>
- **[Brother Guido Meatball](/people/brother-guido-meatball/)**, a celestial warlock of a mysterious holy book, run by Dave from 2021-11-27. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-28)</small>
- **Hardbread**, played by JL once Dave took back the DM's chair in June 2022. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-21)</small>

## Levels 1 to 6: Saltmarsh

The party played *The Sinister Secret of Saltmarsh* in September 2021. They cleared the haunted house, performed a miracle at the Temple of Neptune, were given the temple's deed by the council, killed Ned, won the *Sea Ghost*, and looted the Weathervein Tomb. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-11, 2021-09-17, 2021-09-18)</small> In October JL ran a session among the dwarves, and in [Dunwater](/places/dunwater/), the lizardfolk stronghold, Scrunt talked the party in without violence as far as Queen Okathent. Dave: "this was literally the first adventure in D&D where killing the monsters leads to failure". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-01, 2021-10-10)</small>

In November the party captured the *[Stinking Albatross](/items/the-stinking-albatross/)*, stormed a council meeting and won a commission as Saltmarsh's navy. In "Salvage Operation – Part I", [Skerrin Wavechaser](/people/skerrin-wavechaser/) led them into an ambush by Brotherhood assassins at the Weathervein tomb. Oshi died there, the ghost of [Vivica Amphibiana Weathervein](/people/vivica-amphibiana-weathervein/) gave Dolores her ring, and the party destroyed the secret society in a single night. They then recovered [Aubreck Drallion](/people/aubreck-drallion/)'s box from the derelict *Emperor of the Waves*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-11-13, 2021-11-20; attachment 1654377024765671)</small> In December came JL's session ("Great sesh jeff jazz dungeons rule", Dave) and Dave's rebuild of Abbey Isle, after which Saltmarsh was "in the throes of an inquisition". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-12-03, 2021-12-22)</small>

In January 2022 Gabriella struck a marriage deal with the smuggler [Gellen Primewater](/people/gellen-primewater/), and on 2022-01-21 she married his daughter, [Garbanzo](/people/garbanzo/), at a wedding with gaming tables. The session ended on a cliffhanger. Dave: "The cliffhanger is big because the main thing about my character before there was anything else about her is that she wanted to kill this guy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-01-08, 2022-01-22, 2025-05-31)</small> As Dave recalls it, the man was Major Francisco Dickhead, the superior who had sent her Marine unit to its death. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-twatmarsh-open-questions-2.md)</small>

## Levels 7 to 10: Gabriella's honeymoon

JL's honeymoon arc took the party to the frontier. Dave: "I can hang in the Oregon trail more I think it’s a great setting". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-18)</small> Scrunt went missing; the party helped a gnome inn for news of Skerrin and searched a caravan for him. On 2022-03-12 they killed Skerrin, and Dolores recovered Weathervein. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-02-25, 2022-03-05, 2022-03-12)</small>

In June 2022 the party declined to fight a Ruby dragon that threatened them, because, Dave wrote, "the idea of Saltmarsh and my house and all my stuff getting gilded into nothing was not worth the risk". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-18)</small> JL then finished his material ("We wrapped up everything that I had planned"), and Dave took back the book's quests, heading for Dunwater. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-06-21, 2022-06-29)</small> On 2022-06-30, in JL's session, the party slipped back into Saltmarsh and killed Salmor. They refused an offer from [Malenti](/people/malenti/), and at the finale on 2022-07-09 they beat the [Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) and reached level 10. Dave: "Level 10 actually feels properly earned in this case, esp since it’s the second party we rolled". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-01, 2022-07-08, 2022-07-10)</small>

## The kobold one-shot

On 2022-07-14 JL ran a one-shot for level-7 kobolds, recruits of the [Rising Dawn](/organizations/the-rising-dawn/): Dave's Bullseye, a kobold criminal archer-for-hire; Nico's Charlee, a battle smith with a steel defender robot dog named Bucket; and Scrunt. JL's rules: "Give your kobies 1 feat and 2 very rare items." and his framing: "This is how the Fascist Dawn weeds out the losers." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-12, 2022-07-13)</small>

## White Plume Mountain

Dave first raised the idea in March 2022: "I also love the idea of White Plume Mountain being the dungeon with the Weathervein treasure, and that the dwarves found it and extracted The artifacts, and that we have to bring them back in". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> After the approach session of 2022-07-21 he posted Keraptis's riddle poem and a portrait of [Penguinus Weathervein](/people/penguinus-weathervein/) captioned "Penguinus “keraptis” Weathervein". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small> In the mountain Gabriella was run as an NPC whom the players directed. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-23)</small>

On 2022-08-13 the party cleared "a little less than 2/3 of the dungeon in about 2.5 hrs of in game time" and held Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/). Dave: "That’s why I thought it fit our Saltmarsh so well cause we already had one of them in our group". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14, 2022-08-19)</small> On 2022-09-16 they finished the mountain's puzzles. A vampire charmed Gabriella, Scrunt died, and Whelm, "The one Weathervein that didn’t consent to being bound into ancient artifacts from the treasure hoard", told them what to do. They had twelve hours to get out. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16, 2022-09-24)</small>

## The Eldabrin

Dave had called White Plume Mountain "just the beginning of the mystery of eldabrin", and after the puzzles he wrote: "Eldabrin has been the secret bad guy since like level 3". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14, 2022-09-16)</small> On 2022-09-23 JL took the chair ("I can do the temple of Eldebrin or not.") and the party went to the Eldabrin. The swords had been put away by then, so Gabriella did not hear its message. Dave: "Gabriella was absolutely ready to hear the Eldabrin’s message". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23, 2022-09-24)</small>

Scrunt was back with the party by 2022-10-07. On 2022-10-14 the party fought a dragon, and the next day Dave wrote: "It’s an honor and a privilege adventuring with you two". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-07, 2022-10-14, 2022-10-15)</small>

## The twatmarshverse

In 2025 the party of Dead Man's Hand came through a gateway from Sigil to Saltmarsh. Dave: "Yeah did you expect to enter the twatmarshverse". Nico: "I did not think we would go to Saltmarsh and be involved with our og crew" and "RIP Delores". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small> They found the Temple of Neptune desecrated, its High Priestess Dolores Weathervein dead and her tomb beneath the sea, and Weathervein stolen. Gabriella Hellwood was captain of the pirate ship Blackfish; she is also Ladybeard and the Horror of Dunwater. <small>(DM notes; `sources/dave/2026-09-14-oral-histories-cast.md`)</small> See [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

Dave on Hellwood by then: "But it’s been a few hundred years…." and "Hellwood only lived this long because she became an eldritch horror from her pact with offgoofa". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31, 2026-03-07)</small> Her patron is [Hafgufa](/lore/hafgufa/). After the party's time in Saltmarsh he wrote: "Now I guess there’s a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor". Nico asked "Is she guarding her bff’s underwater temples?", and Dave answered "Yeah that’s what the whole thing was about!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> Of Scrunt, Dave wrote: "Scrunt I think lived a natural life". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>
