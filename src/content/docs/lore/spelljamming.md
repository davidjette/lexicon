**Magic of space travel · Helms and jam drives · Starfall**

*Also known as:* jamming

**Spelljamming** is the magic by which ships travel between worlds in Starfall: a pilot at a spelljammer helm uses their spell slots to bend space and "jam" at a million miles an hour. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Spelljammer helms and jam drives are among the most valuable things in the universe, and most ships have neither. The [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) carries a Major Helm, which is very rare and needs a rare pilot. With no one aboard able to operate it, the ship languished in a single system until [Caprica](/people/caprica/) joined it as its spelljammer.

## Helms and drives

A spelljammer draws on a caster's spell slots; a lifejammer draws on a victim's soul. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-05-01)</small>

A ship's Jam Drive and spelljammer helm are the source of its power and reach. Without them a ship is restricted mostly to whatever orbit it is in, and in some cases nearby moons, and most ships have none at all. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Spelljammer helms, jam drives and guns are probably the three most valuable things in the universe, "And probably still diamonds for resurrection". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

Helms come in two grades. A Major Helm is very rare and allows much faster, deep-space travel for a large ship; most ships have Minor Helms, which have limited range. The Zanzibar has a Major Helm and a backup minor helm, on which it limped along with its autopilot [F.L.A.P.](/people/f-l-a-p/) at the controls. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small> The fighter ace [Chad "Kreen" Mantis](/people/chad-kreen-mantis/) could fly a small fighter but could not handle the Zanzibar's Major Helm. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-23)</small>

## Pilots

The pilot of a spelljammer is "the pilot, the brain and power of the ship itself", and can jam safely by having the stars memorized. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small>

Caprica graduated first in her class at the Academy and was offered first or second Helm on almost any vessel. She chose the open helm of the Zanzibar, which was confined to Trafalmadorespace, or T-Space, because no one on its crew could operate its Major Helm. To meet it she spent two months of Jamtime aboard a scientific vessel bound for [Tralfamadore III](/places/tralfamadore-iii/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19)</small> On the crew roster she is "Lt. Caprica", First Arcanist and Spelljammer. <small>(crew roster)</small>

An Orcish invocation calls on a helm in the name of [Cypher](/people/stonecypher/). It opens "Gorrukk nashok Cypher, urukk karok ghashra ug skarn", and its translation begins:

> By the eternal march of Cypher, master of time and space, I command the currents of the cosmos. Hear my words, oh helm of old, and carry us to our ordained place.

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-06-23)</small>

## Space and the Phlogiston

Every system is a "space", such as Realmspace, Krynnspace or Greyspace; the word refers to a solar system. Realmspace, Greyspace and Krynnspace are the core systems of [the Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> The Starfall tagline asked "What adventures await between the crystal spheres?" <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

The Phlogiston, "the phlo", is part of Starfall's cosmology, brought back from older Spelljammer material. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11, 2023-04-08)</small> After [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/) attack the Zanzibar was "adrift in the phlo". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-06)</small> Dave's epitaph for Caprica reads "her song rings through the phlo forever". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-03)</small>

## In the Ages of the Infanta

In the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) the epic spells **Spheresail**, used to make spelljammer helms, and **Breach Crystal Sphere**, used to travel through the interstellar medium, were known, and the orcs of that age studied the nature of the Crystal Spheres. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small> Starfall is set some five thousand years after the Ages, after the great melting of the 6700s. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09, 2022-10-10; other figures given at the table: 2,000 years after the Ages for Realmspace as home world, a thousand-year history for Astral Command, and ten thousand years for the Lexicon's own age)</small>

## Ship combat

For ship-to-ship combat Dave set out a rule restored from older editions. Ships move only in straight lines on 50-foot hexes and spend movement to turn: a ship with a speed of 300 feet moves six hexes, and spends two hexes of movement to turn sixty degrees. Weapons fire in set directions, and a ship that enters an enemy's hex can board it. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small>
