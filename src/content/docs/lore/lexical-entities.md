**Artificial minds · Warforged, "Gen 2 synths" · ELEs, "Gen 3" · [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) to [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/)**

**Also known as:** ELEs

**Lexical entities** are artificial minds of the Infantaverse, placed by Dave in a line of generations after the warforged: "So warforged are like Gen 2 synths and ELEs are like Gen 3". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-21)</small> The Johnny unit, a standardized entity of the far future, is one, and knows it. In the age of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta, the spread of artificial intelligence and the origins of [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) are under way, and half of that campaign's party "is or possesses lexical entities": [Lenore](/people/lenore/) and [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) are constructs, [Heyu](/people/heyu/) has a share of the Lex, and there is an "outside chance" that [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) is a construct too. Thousands of years later, in the Sovereignty of [Starfall](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) outlaws all artificial intelligence.

## Generations

Dave set out the generations while the Dead Man's Hand party was being made:

> So warforged are like Gen 2 synths and ELEs are like Gen 3

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-21)</small>

Of the robots of the two ages, he wrote: "robots in the last campaign were much more automatons, now some don’t even know they’re artificial". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

## The Johnny unit

The **Johnny unit** is a standardized lexical entity of the far future. Dave:

> Johnny is a standardized entity from thousands of years in the future, if the Conpany is experimenting with AI souls in warforged in the distant past it doesn’t have to be the same name :)

> Johnny is also aware he is a lexical entity so there’s something different about Lenore the same way Rachel is different from Batty in blade runner

<small>— Sic on "Conpany". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-21)</small>

The name came from the table. When Nico described Lenore, JL answered "Just name her Johnny" and "Maybe she’s the very first iteration of the Johnny unit" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-20)</small>, and Nico suggested "Perhaps Lenore is the name of the female model of a Johnny unit". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-21)</small> Dave answered with the first line quoted above. Lenore's steel defender is also named **Johnny**.

## In Dead Man's Hand

Dave on the age of Dead Man's Hand:

> He distribution of artificial intelligence and the origins of the Lexicon are obvs a big thing that’s happening in this time, robots in the last campaign were much more automatons, now some don’t even know they’re artificial

<small>— Sic on "He". (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

In Sigil in that age a Lexicon sits at the heart of the High Courts, kept by [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), and the Factol of the Sororia is the Lexicon. [Nimbus Corp](/organizations/nimbus-corp/), the Company, works on understanding and attempting to duplicate it. <small>(See [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) and [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/).)</small>

### The party

When the party was made, more than one player proposed an artificial character. Dave: "We could be a whole party of secret androids!!!" <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-24)</small> Nico: "It’s crazy how many of us though of robots / Ai lol" <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-26)</small> Two days later he took stock:

> Heyu has share of lex and both Nik and Lenore are constructs, outside chance that Shadowhigh is too (?) interesting how that aspect of the lore remains present\
> If half the party is or possesses lexical entities

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-26)</small>

**[Lenore](/people/lenore/)** is a construct who looks human and presents as human. Nico's concept for her was a character who "looks human but is actually a war-forge (think Bishop from Aliens, who is own by the company) she would act and tell people she is human". Asked by JL whether she knows she is a robot, Nico answered "Hmm maybe not?", and Dave added: "Modify memory plus disguise self device somewhere in her body". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-28)</small> As an artificer she can heal constructs, but, in Nico's words, "until Lenore knows what she is, couldn’t use it on herself". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-21)</small> The cards of her fatemark begin with **Construct**. In [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) the rival adventurer [Bud Holhertz](/people/bud-holhertz/) took her for **Lexi**, a Company seeker who had abandoned his party at [the Tomb of St. Magnus](/places/the-tomb-of-st-magnus/). Dave to the players: "I’m sure you’ve got theories what this Lexi business  is". Nico's theories were that Lenore is "being “reused” and memory wiped each missions", or that "there is another unit out there that looks just like me and was involved in a moonshay isles mission". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-01-31)</small>

**[Nicollo](/people/nicollo/)**, "Nik", is a protocol droid-style robot, built by a clandestine engineer who modifies protocol droids into tutors for young princes.

**[Heyu](/people/heyu/)** has a share of the Lex. Her arcane focus is a floating moonstone <small>(character roster)</small>, and JL wrote early in the campaign, "Heyu is going to get her moonstone back." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-24)</small> The Factol who received her at [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/) charged her to find the Cards and "return this piece of her holy Memory." <small>(DM notes)</small>

**[Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/)** is the "outside chance" of Dave's list.

## In Starfall

About five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta, in the setting of *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, the Sovereignty of Realms passed [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) a year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate. JL, who ran that arc:

> Here’s the idea: a year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate, the Starweaver Act is passed in the Sovereignty outlawing all artificial intelligence. This includes warforged and autognomes. This leads to devastating and violent purge of robotic creatures. It also creates a thriving illicit market for robots.

Dave replied: "Totally predictable outcome of the Lexicon turning on humanity too". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>
