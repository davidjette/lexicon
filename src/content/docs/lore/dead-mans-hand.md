**Campaign · Five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta · Opens in Sigil · Running**

**Dead Man's Hand** is the present-day campaign of the [Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/), set five hundred years after [the End of the Infanta](/history/arc-viii-end-of-the-infanta/) — which is to say, in the world the [Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) left behind, where the war that saved everything is a hole in the record and the heroes who ended it are a rumour at best.

## How it begins

The campaign opens in [Sigil](/places/sigil/). The party wake in **the Mortuary**, each of them **tattooed with fate marks**. They had lived entirely separate lives, and each one's last memory is the same: being **murdered by a handsome man with a glowing sword**.

They team up. They fight gangsters in the **Hive Ward**. Then they are caught up in the schemes of **Shemeska**, a demon who owns a casino in the city. From there the campaign becomes a race: the party set out to **find the cards**, trying to stay ahead of Shemeska and the killer she has hired, **Jeremiah, a fallen angel**.

Who the man with the glowing sword is, what the fate marks mean, and what the cards are are not recorded.

## The deck

The campaign is named for, and built around, a deck. Twenty-one numbered cards survive in the campaign's own materials, each with a name: **Key · Balance · Puzzle · Gem · Rogue · Sage · Fates · Knight · Sun · Moon · Comet · Star · Jester · Throne · Ruin · Donjon · Void · Skull · Flames · Talons · Euryale**. Alongside them run a second set of unnumbered cards — **Beast · Dragon · Elemental · Fool · Maze · Plant · Priest · Tower**. Each player character is dealt a hand, and those hands are kept: the folder of them is titled, literally, *fate hands*, and the readings are logged.

<small>In [Arc II](/history/arc-ii-motherstone/) the party had to beat Mannemarco, the King of Worms, at his favourite game on the moonstone cube called the Illimat — a card-and-combat duel in which every played card summoned a beast into the arena and every captured card was won in blood. Whether the two decks are related is not recorded.</small>

## The company

The roster, per the campaign's own character list:

- **Battle Beast** — white-maned lion-man gladiator monk. Massive, scarred, unarmed, disciplined. Moves with predator tension.
- **Lenore** — human steampunk artificer. Brass goggles, flying boots, long rifle slung on her back; layered utility gear and an ultra-modern black bob.
- **Shadowhigh** — cowgirl ranger. Leather duster, wide-brim hat, revolver and whip, with a small draconic pet on her shoulder. Sardonic, relaxed but deadly.
- **Maverick** — dark elf bard prince. Dark skin, romantic white hair, silken clothes, rapier at his hip, flamboyant and rakish. "Looks even better when dishevelled."
- **Heyu** — ravenkin wizard and investigator, two and a half feet tall, cloaked and feathered, with a magical floating [moonstone](/lore/moonstone/) focus.
- **N.I.C.O.LL.O Mach VIII LI** — a protocol-droid-style robot in a polished humanoid frame, wearing dirty and torn Italian Renaissance garb, ruffled sleeves and faded brocade. Stands perfectly still unless spoken to.

**Supporting:** **Rodriel**, paladin of Neptune and former ruffian, in sea-stained armour · **Barold Bonds**, a red tiefling barbarian with a spiked baseball bat · **Johnny**, Lenore's hulking steel defender.

## Adversaries

**Shemeska** — a demon, and the owner of a casino in Sigil. **Jeremiah** — a fallen angel, the killer Shemeska hires to run the party down.

**General Varkus** — bald, stern, late fifties, usually in commander's armour; "the very picture of masculine discipline and command." **Phillip Klaxon** — a middle-aged minor noble. **Griddy Champagne** — a beautiful and dangerous woman, secretly a devil in human form, who dresses in black versions of ordinary clothes. Also named, without further detail: **Sir Loin**, **Colonel Angus** and **Sir Merriam Wordsworker**.

## Continuity with the older chronicle

Two threads visibly survive the Forgetting into the present day. Heyu's arcane focus is a floating **moonstone** — the substance the whole of the Ages of the Infanta was fought over, seeded across the world by the Starfall that broke the Moon. And the party's world has firearms, clockwork automata and steampunk artifice as ordinary equipment, which is what the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) was building toward when it learned to ride the Obliviator's leylines and put that power into consumer goods.

## Record

No session record of Dead Man's Hand survives. What exists of the campaign's materials is the deck, the dealt hands, the tarot-reading log, and the roster of player characters.
