**Disease · [The Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Cured**

**Also known as:** Cage Blight

**Cageblight** is a virulent disease that took hold in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/) of [Sigil](/places/sigil/), afflicting the poor, the destitute and workers. It greys the skin, brings hallucinations and a nightmarish fear of containment, and ends in necrosis, respiratory failure and madness. Its source was a portal to a plague dimension that had opened in a drain in the Spillway, poisoning the water system. Hunting [Rotten William](/people/rotten-william/) into the Spillway Depths, the party found the portal, killed the plague demon Gorelach and Gunt, saved the Bleaker intern Craig and closed it. By closing the portal, they cured Cageblight.

## In the Hive Ward

Cageblight was a pandemic in the Hive Ward: grey skin, coughing, or madness. <small>(DM notes)</small> Remedy hawkers worked the streets, from sunlight vendors to sellers of snake oil and charms, alongside overworked clerics. Traditional remedies were herbs, poultices and charms; *remove disease* cured it, but only temporarily for many. Fear of infection ran through the ward, along class divisions. <small>(DM notes)</small>

In Ragpickers Square the vendors sold a fake Cageblight cure, and the storyteller Reekwind, a vagabond, was stricken with it. <small>(DM notes)</small> Mourns-for-Trees, who fears for the last trees in Sigil, warns that "Cageblight affects all living things". <small>(DM notes)</small>

The party learned about Cageblight after they escaped the Mortuary and fought Ratbone in the street.

## The course of the disease

<small>(DM notes)</small>

- **Stage 1, Infection.** Fever, fatigue, itchiness and some coughing. It is caught on contact and incubates over a long rest.
- **Stage 2, Mild.** Ashen skin, severe fatigue and pain; anxiety and trouble focusing. It comes after a week of infection, and from this stage the sufferer is contagious.
- **Stage 3, Severe.** Hallucinations and claustrophobia, "nightmarish fear of containment"; clouded eyes. It comes after two weeks of infection.
- **Stage 4, Terminal.** Necrosis of the limbs, eyes, mouth and ears, respiratory failure, and madness. It comes after three weeks of infection.

## Theories of its cause

The cause of Cageblight was unknown in the Hive Ward. The explanations put forward were unsanitary conditions, its being highly contagious, a vengeful entity such as a faction or demons, and planar contamination. <small>(DM notes)</small>

[The Company](/organizations/nimbus-corp/) was researching cures. [Sharegrave](/people/sharegrave/), who dislikes [the Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/), dismisses Cageblight "as a fake plague engineered by the Company to control the city". <small>(DM notes)</small>

[Diana Rhea](/people/diana-rhea/), a Bleaker cleric of Brightmantle, wants to cure Cageblight. Her assistant Craig had been missing for three weeks, and her leads on the cause pointed to the sewers under the Spillway, which she could not get close to. <small>(DM notes)</small> The party took on finding the missing cleric in the Spillway and the cause of Cageblight as quests. At the Gatehouse they volunteered at night, were detained, disarmed and disrobed, and met Diana Rhea, who gave their belongings back and beseeched them to find Craig.

## The source in the Spillway

The party assaulted Rotten William's lair in the Spillway, killing everyone except "Whitey", whom they tortured lightly and who then escaped. Rotten William had been at work planning a kidnapping and murder for hire, but fled into the sewer. The party pursued him deeper in, avoiding slimes and hazards, and encountered a giant spider and a colony of cranium rats called **Many-as-One**. They talked their way through, and found Rotten William and his cronies afraid to enter a chamber filled with black tendrils.

They cornered Rotten William in the **Sanitizing Room**. A portal to a plague dimension had opened in the drain, poisoning the water system. Craig had attempted to finish his master's mission to close it, and in doing so had angered a plague demon. Rotten William ordered his men to stop Craig; the party ambushed and killed them, and then the demon. Rotten William tried to appeal to the demon to save him, but it held no mercy for him.

The party killed Gorelach and Gunt, saved Craig, and killed Rotten William. [Barold](/people/barold-bonds/) was killed and reincarnated as a tiefling. By closing the portal, they cured Cageblight.

Among what they found were a Wand of Secrets, an Epee of Temperance, various potions and a Ring of Invisibility. Diana Rhea gave them 2000 gold and poultices, the Gatehouse was opened to them, and the Bleak Cabal became their faction ally.

## Gorelach and Gunt

**Gorelach "the Maw" and Gunt**, the Twin Maw of Plagues, are the plague demon of the Spillway: two heads on one body, each active in turn while the other gathers its power. <small>(DM notes)</small>

- **Gorelach** bites and rends, bursts boils that spill maggot spawn, which explode, and, charging, retches and vomits a long line of acid at the last living creature to have hurt it.
- **Gunt** slams and fires snot rockets, raises boils that explode when struck, and, charging, chokes and coughs a pestilent cloud that poisons those caught in it and worsens their Cageblight. The cloud does not affect Gunt.
- Brought low, the demon enters a third phase and adds a fecal spray.
