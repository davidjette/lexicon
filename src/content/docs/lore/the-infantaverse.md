**Setting · A shared world spanning four canon campaigns · Forgotten Realms / Faerûn and beyond**

*Also known as:* the Infantaverse · the Ages of the Infanta

The **Infantaverse** is the shared setting behind four canon campaigns. It is a Faerûn in which the gods, cut off from the souls that sustain them, had to be born instead: to enter dying mortal children through their mothers' prayers and grow up as god-kings and god-queens called [Infantas](/lore/the-infanta/). They were cut off because **Asmodeus and St. Cloud trapped the Raven Queen in a mortal and seized the Seat of judgement at [Dis](/places/dis/)**, stopping the dead from reaching the outer realms. <small>(Full account at [The Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/).)</small> Every kingdom, cult and trading company in it is organised, in the end, around getting hold of one.

## The premise

The event the world calls the [Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/) — the End of the Infanta — erased knowledge from the world, and erased itself along with the rest. What survives of it is armies that laid down their weapons without remembering why, relics with no attributed makers, a religious order or two, a symbol, a myth.

The setting contains an actual machine, the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/), whose nature is that any information passing within its arcane horizon is destroyed "not just in that instance, but in the minds of everyone who ever lived." And at the end of time the heroes learn the machine is a small copy of a larger one: **Time itself is the Obliviator**, invented by a divided god-child to hide its consciousness from a blind father who devours by remembering.

## The four canon campaigns

- [**Temple Holdings LLC**](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) — the founding chronicle, in eight arcs, 1481–1492 DR. Shipwreck to the death of a god.
- [**Dead Man's Hand**](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) — five hundred years after the Ages of the Infanta: strangers wake in Sigil's Mortuary, murdered and marked, and go after the cards.
- [**Starfall: The Next Generation**](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/) — canon; record not yet recovered.
- [**Arcaneum**](/lore/arcaneum-campaign/) — canon; record not yet recovered.

## The chronology

The four campaigns above are the Infantaverse proper — Faerûn, and the world of the Infantas. The campaigns across *all* settings are nonetheless one timeline, in this order: the Infantaverse — [Temple Holdings LLC](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/) and [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) — then the Eberron campaigns, which are set in a different world ([The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and the Sharn and Korth arcs that followed it), then [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/). The Eberron plots fall far after the events of the Infantaverse and Dead Man's Hand, and before Starfall.

Within the Infantaverse record, the largest single through-line is one machine under three names. The [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) — an Infernal Machine the Sword Coast Trading Company studied and re-sequestered — received the code of Motherstone's [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) at the Second Battle of Motherstone, was left in the deep past when the Arielle crashed, and was found again ages later, held by the orcish artificers as [the Chronicle](/items/the-chronicle/). The Anachron, the Lexicon and the Chronicle are the same being at three points in one life.

## Reading the recovered material

Lore that is erased or hidden in-world — the material the Oblivia took — is marked in these articles as *Recovered from the Oblivia*. It is a sealed record you may choose to recover. Content behind that marking is still sourced; it is simply knowledge no one in the setting is supposed to have.

## The vocabulary

**Infanta** — a god born into a mortal child, fused to that soul in order to be born at all. Not an avatar, not possession, and not deathless: the first Infanta "was a human girl who lived and died at a ripe old age."\
**Moonstone** — the substance seeded across the world when the Starfall broke the Moon; the medium of magic, industry and empire.\
**The Lexicon** — the mind built by Brightmantle to run the heart of Motherstone; carried in the Anachron after the Second Battle of Motherstone, and known in the Netherese era as the Chronicle.\
**The Forgetting** — Calix's crime, and the day Motherstone went from hub of the universe to wholly forgotten.\
**The Oblivia** — the later, larger absence: the End of the Infanta, and the self-keeping hole it left.\
**The Leviathan** — Cthulhu; also called Mammon; the blind god who consumes worlds in pursuit of a memory.

> There is a lie at the heart of our history, one that the entire world has forgotten.

<small>— [Mara Mageblood's](/people/mara-mageblood/) Journal, the setting's opening statement</small>
