**Antagonist · [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) · The temple of the Eldabrin**

**Also known as:** Eldebrin · Elebrin · the Electronic Brain

**The Eldabrin** is the secret villain of the Ghosts of Twatmarsh campaign, in Dave's words "the secret bad guy since like level 3". [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/) was "just the beginning of the mystery of eldabrin". On 2022-09-23, with twelve hours left before the mountain's door closed, the party [Neptune's Rejects](/organizations/neptunes-rejects/) went to the temple of the Eldabrin. [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) had been ready to hear the Eldabrin's message, but the party had put their swords away by then, and she did not hear it.

## The Brotherhood's objective

The Eldabrin was what [the Scarlet Brotherhood](/organizations/the-scarlet-brotherhood/) was really after at Saltmarsh: an ancient source of power beneath the mines, which the Brotherhood's own records could not say was a being, a weapon or an aura. The sahuagin invasion of the town was a ruse to cover the search. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## Before the temple

After the Saltmarsh finale, Dave listed the party's leads:

> Yeah and I think we set up some good options for how to go now - the mine / WPM / Eldabrin and then Scarlett Brotherhood (the Devil lives?)

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

In White Plume Mountain, with Wave, [Whelm](/items/whelm/) and [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/) in hand, Dave wrote:

> white plume mtn is just the beginning of the mystery of eldabrin, if we get out of here with these swords we will be much changed by then I'm sure

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small>

When the party finished the mountain's puzzles he wrote that "Eldabrin has been the secret bad guy since like level 3" and "the Weathervein arc is mostly complete". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small>

Dave expected mind flayers ahead. "If we’re fighting mind flayers which meta gaming I know we are", the party would need strong weapons, and "Killing an elder brain with blackrazor is pretty final bossy". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-16)</small> The next day he looked ahead to a campaign to "infiltrate a mind flyer colony and rid these artifacts of a sentient curse". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-17)</small>

## The temple of the Eldabrin

On 2022-09-23 JL offered to run "the temple of Eldebrin". Dave: "Yeah I wanna go to the eldabrin" / "Weve got 12 hours till the door closes" / "So whether this is a four month journey into the underdark or a jaunt into a mindlfayer hive w/e I’m ready". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-23)</small> The party went to the Eldabrin that night.

The next morning Dave wrote:

> Gabriella was absolutely ready to hear the Eldabrin’s message and spent the last two sessions of White Plume resisting it with saves and stuff as basically an NPC\
> I decided during that game that the death of Scrunt was very impactful for Gabriella and she resolved to overcoming the negative influence of blackrazor and getting home\
> And since we put the swords away by the time we got to Eldabrin she wasn’t hearing it, but if Dolores had given into Weathervein or if Gabriella lost some of those saves,\
> Could have gone differently. This party always on the edge of going evil

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-24)</small>

## Elebrin, the Electronic Brain

The Eldabrin is also Elebrin. <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small> What follows is an unconfirmed account. In the mystical Delirium Woods, travelling with a fellow paladin, Bobert, called Sir Bobby B, the party sought the Cosmic Key of Murvis the Great, a device said to open and close portals at will, and befriended the mad elves of the woods, who set them to rid their land of demon invaders. In the Holy Cave at the heart of the forest the elves worshipped as a god an enormous machine of wood, glass and metal. Faded letters on it once read ELECTRONIC BRAIN; only ELEBRIN remained. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small>

Elebrin's account of itself, likewise unconfirmed: it landed seven thousand years ago from a distant star, badly damaged, and was repaired by Murvis the Wise, whom it called its father and had not seen in a very long time. The wizards of its world had made a weapon too powerful to keep and built Elebrin to pilot it away to another world; they crashed, and it had lost contact with the weapon for centuries, though it would have felt the weapon's activation. The Cosmic Key was not a separate thing: it was inside Elebrin and powered it, Murvis's greatest invention, a hammer inscribed in what looked like dwarvish, and by Elebrin's telling the tool with which Murvis had helped the Raven Queen seal Asmodeus in the demon realms. Removing it might kill Elebrin. <small>(JL's DM notes: Slatmarsh: Greyhawk sessions)</small> <small>(sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
