**Campaign · The far end of the chronology · Record partly recovered**

*Also known as:* Starfall TNG · Starfall: TNG

**Starfall: The Next Generation** is a canon campaign set at the far end of the chronology: after the [Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/) chronicle, and after the Eberron campaigns, which themselves fall far after the Infantaverse and before Starfall. It was a long campaign, in which the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) rules a Sovereignty of Realms spanning four worlds and then betrays the mortal races.

## The Sovereignty of Realms

By the Starfall era the [Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) rules a **Sovereignty of Realms** spanning four worlds: **Toril**, **Eberron**, **Oerth** and **Krynn**.

The approach to it is visible from the Eberron side of the timeline, where Eberron is on the verge of first contact with an interplanar civilisation from Toril "largely led by the Lexicon and its many allies and instruments." By Starfall that contact has happened.

Starfall refers to [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/) as a critical collapse of the continent of Faerûn, before a dark time preceding the ascendance of the Sovereignty.

## The Lexicon's betrayal

In the course of the campaign the Lexicon **betrays the mortal races**. The recorded reason is that it was "obsessed with trying to remember the events of the Infanta war."

Those events are what the world was made to forget. At the end of the Infanta war Cypher cast Wish over the head of Cthulhu, ending the crisis and erasing Cthulhu and the Obliviator from memory. The Infantaverse quest went with them, and so did the premise of time travel by the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) — the machine the Lexicon lives in. <small>(See [The Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).)</small>

<small>How the betrayal unfolded, who it fell on, and what the Lexicon did are not recorded.</small>

## What the name inherits

The title points back at [Arc V — Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/): the arc in which the heroes rode the Anachron into the deep past, walked the Moon as a living forest before it broke, and were told at the end of time that Time itself is the Obliviator. The **Starfall** is the event that ends that arc — the black sphere called [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), or the Evening Star, falling toward the world, breaking the Moon and seeding the moonstone of every age that follows. A **Starfall Tower** also stands in Sigil.

<small>Whether the campaign's title refers to that event, that tower, or something else is not recorded.</small>

## Known entities

Figures and records drawn from this campaign's material: Albert Spear, Barrias Red-Eye, Space Guppie, and the episode record Light of Xaryxis: Ep. 3.
