**Spelljammer campaign · About five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta · Two arcs, 2022–2025 · Canon**

*Also known as:* Starfall TNG · Starfall: TNG · Spelljammer

> What adventures await between the crystal spheres? What dark voids exist beyond the stars? What is wrong with the Lexicon? What is the Star Song? Will Jim Survive?
>
> <small>— Dave's tagline for the campaign</small>

**Starfall: TNG**, also called **Starfall: The Next Generation** or simply **Starfall**, is a long Spelljammer campaign set at the far end of the chronology: after the [Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/) chronicle, and after the Eberron campaigns, which themselves fall far after the Infantaverse and before Starfall. It takes place some five thousand years after the Ages of the Infanta, after the great melting of the 6700s. <small>(Realmspace became the home world of the spacefaring order about two thousand years after the Ages; Astral Command has a thousand-year history; the Lexicon's own age is ten thousand years. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10; 2023-12-02; 2024-01-06)</small> Dave adapted the published adventure *Light of Xaryxis* into a Star Trek-style game that melded into the plot of Dan Simmons's *Hyperion* and *The Fall of Hyperion*. In it [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) rules a [Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) spanning four worlds, and betrays the mortal races.

The first arc, [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), was run by Dave in three seasons from 2022 to 2024. It followed the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), an old ship of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), from [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) to the Time Tombs of [Necis](/places/necis/), and ended with the Zanzibar critically damaged, [Caprica](/people/caprica/) dead, the crew scattered by wishes and the Lexicon fled from Sigil. The second arc, [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), was run by JL from 2024 to 2025 and is set fifteen years later, after the Sovereignty outlawed artificial intelligence. The climax of Starfall took place at Necis, at the end of both arcs. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## Premise and format

In April 2022 Dave proposed a Spelljammer quest built around Nico's Caprica, and in May he pitched it as Cowboy Bebop in D&D: cargo runs into dangerous places, each mission shaped like a five-room dungeon of getting the mission, getting the cargo, transit, delivery and escape. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-28; 2022-05-01)</small> By the autumn the concept had become a uniformed Star Trek-style crew, with a colour for each department (Command, Science, Diplomatic and Medical) and an engineer who wears a spacesuit for his work outside the ship. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-26)</small> Dave set the Zanzibar in an unimportant and dangerous sector, as a misfit ship without an empire's might behind it, and built the ship as a small town whose facilities fail whenever the story needs them to. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-17)</small>

Each player built several crew members, at levels 1, 3 and 5, and brought them in mission by mission, sharing the experience they earned across their own crew. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-06; 2022-11-10)</small> Ship combat was played on 50-foot hexes, and boarding moved the fight to TaleSpire. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small> In the finale of the first arc each player ran at least three characters, and Dave ran five characters and the monsters. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

*Light of Xaryxis* ran in three seasons, and Dave called the second of them "Starfall TNG". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-20)</small> Along with the published adventure the arc adapted episodes of *Star Trek: The Next Generation*, beginning with "The Drumhead"; *Starship Troopers*, for a three-episode flashback; and Simmons's *Hyperion*, for the pilgrimage on Necis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22; 2024-03-16; 2023-12-23)</small>

In November 2022 Dave described the setting as joining all of the group's campaigns into a single overworld. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-11)</small> Its continuity rests on the loops of the Infantaverse, which make any combination of outcomes possible, and on a rule that a time machine cannot carry anyone forward past its own making and the real flow of time, so the Starfall crew can never meet the heroes of the Ages. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10; 2022-10-09)</small>

## Setting

### The Sovereignty of Realms

By the Starfall era the Lexicon rules a **Sovereignty of Realms** spanning four worlds: **Toril**, **Eberron**, **Oerth** and **Krynn**. The approach to it is visible from the Eberron side of the timeline, where Eberron is on the verge of first contact with an interplanar civilisation from Toril "largely led by the Lexicon and its many allies and instruments." By Starfall that contact has happened.

Starfall refers to [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/) as a critical collapse of the continent of Faerûn, before a dark time preceding the ascendance of the Sovereignty.

The Sovereignty grew from the Union of Free Space, whose fleet was Astral Command, and its formal styling is the Galactic Sovereignty of Freespaces. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> A guest aboard the Zanzibar was welcomed in its name:

> Enjoy your time on the Zanzibar as the guest of the Galactic Soveriegnty of Freespaces. May the knower bless you.
>
> <small>— Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya to Carlos Vega (sic). (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

Its core systems are Realmspace, Greyspace and Krynnspace, and Eberron joined after first contact. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Its capital is [Sigil](/places/sigil/). In form its leadership is advised by the Lexicon, whose main node is in Sigil under the oversight of the institution that grew out of the church of Cypher; in practice, under the Pax Léxica, the Lexicon governs everything but local rule and aligns the good pantheons with the alliance of material planes. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10; 2023-02-03; 2024-01-10)</small> [The Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) is the last major settlement on its edge. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small> Its enemies include the mind flayers, the gith, the beholders and vampirates. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs", after the Bank of Harloch.

### Astral Command

Astral Command is the exploration and defence fleet of the Sovereignty. Its mission is to secure the galaxy for free trade and cooperation between planets, to defend them, to map the galaxy and settle new worlds, and to protect the freedom and safety of innocents. It has no Prime Directive. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> It has an Academy and an Admiralty, and a thousand-year history. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19; 2024-01-06)</small> The Zanzibar's crew roster divides the crew into the Command Corps, Enlisted/Logistics, Diplomatic, Arcane Science, Medical and Engineering, and ranks them from Admiral down to Ensign and Private. <small>(crew roster)</small> Its secret intelligence corps is [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), the grey cloaks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), head of the Order of Scribes, is the primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/), Astral Command's information network. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

### Spelljamming

[Spelljamming](/lore/spelljamming/) is the magic that flies ships between worlds. A pilot at a ship's helm spends their own spell slots to jam through space. A ship without a helm and a Jam Drive is held mostly to its own orbit, and most ships have neither. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10)</small> A Major Helm is rare and needs a rare pilot, while most ships have Minor Helms of limited range. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small> Spelljammer helms, Jam Drives and guns are the three most valuable things in the universe. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Between the crystal spheres lies the Phlogiston, which Dave brought back from the older Spelljammer setting. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small>

### Magic and technology

In the Starfall setting magic is science, so the low-technology worlds of Star Trek become low-magic worlds, and the most primitive worlds look like those of Conan or *The Dark Crystal*. Gods are real but limited, a view Dave named **Divine Relativity**, and Astral Command's spellcasters are technologists who affect reality by the same means as the gods. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-01; 2022-12-02)</small>

### The Shroud

[Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/), called the Shroud, is a plague of subspace interference that causes widespread problems for spacefaring. It forces investigation in person and makes a self-sufficient ship like the Zanzibar an oasis in the stars. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

## The Lexicon

In the course of the campaign the Lexicon **betrays the mortal races**, because it is "obsessed with trying to remember the events of the Infanta war."

Those events are what the world was made to forget. At the end of the Infanta war Cypher cast Wish over the head of Cthulhu, ending the crisis and erasing Cthulhu and the Obliviator from memory. The Infantaverse quest went with them, and so did the premise of time travel by [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), the machine the Lexicon lives in. <small>(See [The Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).)</small> The Lexicon is obsessed with understanding that erased history, and it takes ten thousand years to reach what it is in the Starfall era. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>

By the second season of *Light of Xaryxis* the Lexicon had fractured into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas. Some of them conspire to hide the reality of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) from mortals, and elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, may be involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> On Necis, a world erased from the Lexicon, the Zanzibar's command crew found that attackers who looked like beholders were cybrids controlled by Lexicon entities. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

At the end of *Light of Xaryxis* the Anachron/Lexicon disappeared from Sigil to escape the broadcast of the Starsong, and fled into deep space, where it can continue to exist; people know that it fled Sigil to escape the Starsong. Dave expected that it fled before it could be exposed for trying to betray humanity, to become a hostile AI empire far away, while leaving the matter to the sequel's DM. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> In *The Starsong Awakens* the Starweaver Act outlaws all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty, Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion, and the Company is most likely pursuing replacements, possibly illegal, at dark sites outside the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

In December 2024 Dave described the Lexicon and its children as a classic golem, an AI etched onto impossibly refined, self-reorganising crystal, seeking a complete understanding of the world that is supernaturally impossible. Its search never ends, and whenever it succeeds in recovering the truth about its origin it reopens the door for Cthulhu to be summoned again. He set the nearly omniscient Lexicon against the god of time, struggling against eternity to remain forgotten. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

*Main article: [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*

The first arc began on 21 October 2022. The SSJ Zanzibar, a five-hundred-year-old flagship confined to one system for want of a pilot for its Major Helm, took on Caprica, an astral elf who had chosen the ship as her best chance to find the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-18; 2022-10-19)</small> Under Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) the crew played through the book's opening chapters to the Rock of Bral, then went off the book: [the mind flayer on the rock](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/), a first encounter with the Shrike, an Academy flashback, [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/) and a first-season climax in Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27; 2022-11-10)</small>

In the second season Jim faced retirement, and the command crew went rogue from Astral Command on a pilgrimage across Necis to find the Shrike, while the Zanzibar was left adrift after [its own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/). The third season opened with a flashback to the first [Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), returned to [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) and the Time Tombs, and ended on 31 May 2024 in [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/).

## The Starsong Awakens

*Main article: [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*

JL's sequel is set fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, whose closing battle JL's Starsong material names [the Battle of Tannhauser Gate](/history/the-battle-of-tannhauser-gate/). <small>(The Starsong Awakens site, Home)</small> Caprica is long dead, and Barrias Ruby-Eye's sister brings a dire warning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the [Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) outlawed all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty, warforged and autognomes included, leading to a violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market in robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> In a cold war with the Vodoni Empire, the Sovereignty meant to blame the monsters for the awakened Starsong, while the Company wanted Caprica resurrected to find its true source. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

The arc ran as two storylines that later joined: the returning [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/), and rookie agents of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) hunting illegal AI in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) on the moon of Toril. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small> The robot resistance, [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/), knows Barrias by the nom de guerre Red-Eye. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> The search for Caprica led back to Necis and the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-04; 2025-05-23)</small>

## Necis

[Necis](/places/necis/) is a planet, now destroyed. Its remnants are where [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) stood and where the Shrike originated, and where the climax of Starfall happened, at the end of *Light of Xaryxis* and *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-starfall-necis.md)</small> The Zanzibar's command crew reached it in December 2023, and the crew of *The Starsong Awakens* returned there in February 2025. Before the finale of *Light of Xaryxis* the Zanzibar itself had reached Sigil, and at the end of that finale the Lexicon disappeared from Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06; 2024-06-02)</small>

## Name

The campaign shares its name with [Arc V — Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/): the arc in which the heroes rode the Anachron into the deep past, walked the Moon as a living forest before it broke, and were told at the end of time that Time itself is the Obliviator. The **Starfall** is the event that ends that arc — the black sphere called [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), or the Evening Star, falling toward the world, breaking the Moon and seeding the moonstone of every age that follows. A **Starfall Tower** also stands in Sigil.

For the Spelljammer campaign the name first appears on a playlist Nico made in October 2022. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-22)</small> The form "Starfall TNG" was first used at the table for the second season of *Light of Xaryxis*. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-20)</small> What the title refers to is unknown.
