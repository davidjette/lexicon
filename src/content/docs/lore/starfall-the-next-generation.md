**Spelljammer campaign · The far end of the chronology · Two arcs, 2022–2025 · Canon**

*Also known as:* Starfall · Starfall TNG · Starfall: TNG · Spelljammer · Spelljammer: The Motion Picture (working title of the second arc)

> What adventures await between the crystal spheres? What dark voids exist beyond the stars? What is wrong with the Lexicon? What is the Star Song? Will Jim Survive?
>
> <small>— Dave's tagline for the campaign</small>

**Starfall: The Next Generation** is a long Spelljammer campaign set at the far end of the chronology: after the [Infantaverse](/lore/the-infantaverse/) chronicle, and after the Eberron campaigns, which themselves fall far after the Infantaverse and before Starfall. Dave adapted the published adventure *Light of Xaryxis* into a Star Trek-style game that melded into the plot of Dan Simmons's *Hyperion* and *The Fall of Hyperion*. In it [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) rules a [Sovereignty of Realms](/organizations/the-sovereignty-of-realms/) spanning four worlds, and betrays the mortal races.

The first arc, [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/), was run by Dave from 2022 to 2024. It followed the crew of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/), an old ship of [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/), from [the Rock of Bral](/places/the-rock-of-bral/) to the Time Tombs of [Necis](/places/necis/), and ended with the Zanzibar critically damaged, its crew scattered by wishes and the Lexicon fled from Sigil. The second arc, [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/), was run by JL from 2024 to 2025 and is set fifteen years later, after the Sovereignty outlawed artificial intelligence. The climax of Starfall took place at Necis, at the end of both arcs. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small>

## Premise and format

In April 2022 Dave proposed "a spelljammer quest" for the group, and on 1 May he pitched it as "Cowboy bebop in DnD", with each mission built like a five-room dungeon: "1) getting the mission 2) getting the cargo/maguffin 3) transit 4) delivery 5) escape/return". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-04-28; 2022-05-01)</small> By the autumn the concept had become a uniformed Star Trek-style crew, with "Diff colors for diff depts, Command, Science, Diplomatic, Medical", and an engineer in a spacesuit for his EVAs. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-09-26)</small> Dave described the Zanzibar as "a misfit ship in an unimportant and dangerous sector", and the ship itself as "basically a small town". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-17)</small> JL's line for the crew was "We aren’t Picard exploring. We’re Vasco de Gama encountering hostile tribes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small>

Each player built several crew members, "a 1, a 3 and a 5", and brought them in mission by mission, spreading the experience they earned "between the chars". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-06; 2022-11-10)</small> Ship combat was played on 50-foot hexes, and boarding moved the fight to TaleSpire. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small> In the finale of the first arc the players each ran at least three characters and Dave ran five characters and the monsters. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>

The first arc ran in seasons, and Dave called its 2023 season "Starfall TNG". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-07-20)</small> Along with the published adventure it adapted episodes of *Star Trek: The Next Generation*, beginning with "The Drumhead"; *Starship Troopers*, for a three-episode flashback; and Simmons's *Hyperion*, for the pilgrimage on Necis. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-22; 2024-03-16; 2023-12-23)</small>

In November 2022 Dave said the setting "now combines all of our campaigns into a single overworld". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-11)</small> Its continuity rests on the loops of the Infantaverse, which "make it so any combination of outcomes is possible", and on a rule that time machines cannot travel forward past their own creation, "So we won’t run into ourselves". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10; 2022-10-09)</small>

## Setting

### The Sovereignty of Realms

By the Starfall era the Lexicon rules a **Sovereignty of Realms** spanning four worlds: **Toril**, **Eberron**, **Oerth** and **Krynn**. The approach to it is visible from the Eberron side of the timeline, where Eberron is on the verge of first contact with an interplanar civilisation from Toril "largely led by the Lexicon and its many allies and instruments." By Starfall that contact has happened.

Starfall refers to [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/) as a critical collapse of the continent of Faerûn, before a dark time preceding the ascendance of the Sovereignty.

Dave's account of its government is that its leadership "is heavily advised by the Lexicon whose main node is in Sigil, overseen by what emerged from the church of Cypher which, of course, saved the world from the Leviathan". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-02-03)</small> In the Sovereignty era [Sigil](/places/sigil/) became, in his words, "the capital of the known universe, with a Pax Léxica taking over and aligning the local good pantheons with the alliance of material planes". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-10)</small> The Rock of Bral is "the last major settlement on the edge of the sovereignty". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27)</small>

In early planning the polity was called the Union of Free Space, and in play a guest aboard the Zanzibar was welcomed as "the guest of the Galactic Soveriegnty of Freespaces" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-11-26)</small> Its enemies include "The mind flayers, the gith, the beholders" and "Vampirates". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Sovereign currency is nicknamed "Harlochs", after the Bank of Harloch.

### Astral Command

Astral Command is the "exploration and defense space fleet" of the Sovereignty. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> Its mission is to secure the galaxy for free trade and cooperation between planets, to defend them, and to explore and settle new worlds, with "No Prime Directive" and "a general mission to protect the freedom and safety of innocents". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09)</small> It has an Academy and an Admiralty, and Dave speaks of its "thousand year history". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-19; 2024-01-06)</small> The Zanzibar's crew roster divides the crew into the Command Corps, Enlisted/Logistics, Diplomatic, Arcane Science, Medical and Engineering, and ranks them from Admiral down to Ensign and Private. <small>(crew roster)</small> Its secret intelligence corps is [OSR3G](/organizations/osr3g/), the grey cloaks. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-03-08)</small>

Admiral [Andromeda Starweaver](/people/andromeda-starweaver/), head of the Order of Scribes, is the primary architect of [the Astralex](/lore/the-astralex/), Astral Command's information network. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-01-23)</small>

### Spelljamming

[Spelljamming](/lore/spelljamming/) is the magic that flies ships between worlds. A pilot at a ship's helm spends their own spell slots to jam through space. A ship without a helm and a Jam Drive is held mostly to its own orbit, and most ships have neither. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-10)</small> A Major Helm is rare and needs a rare pilot, while most ships have Minor Helms of limited range. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small> Dave named the three most valuable things in the universe as "Spelljammer helms, Jam drives, and guns". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> Between the crystal spheres lies the Phlogiston, which Dave brought back from the older Spelljammer setting. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-11)</small>

### Magic and technology

In the Starfall setting "Magic == Science", so the low-technology worlds of Star Trek become low-magic worlds, and Dave called the resulting view of the gods "Divine Relativity". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-01)</small> Gods are real but limited, and Astral Command's spellcasters are technologists who use various means of affecting reality, "no different than the gods themselves". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-12-02)</small>

### The Shroud

[Dark Spectrum Interference](/lore/dark-spectrum-interference/), called the Shroud, is "Subspace interference but it’s a plague and causing widespread problems for spacefaring". It forces investigation in person and makes a self-sufficient ship like the Zanzibar "an oasis in the stars". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-18)</small>

## The Lexicon

In the course of the campaign the Lexicon **betrays the mortal races**, because it is "obsessed with trying to remember the events of the Infanta war."

Those events are what the world was made to forget. At the end of the Infanta war Cypher cast Wish over the head of Cthulhu, ending the crisis and erasing Cthulhu and the Obliviator from memory. The Infantaverse quest went with them, and so did the premise of time travel by [the Anachron](/items/the-anachron/), the machine the Lexicon lives in. <small>(See [The Oblivia](/lore/the-oblivia/).)</small> Dave has said that the Lexicon "itself is obsessed with understanding it", and that "it takes ten thousand years for it to get to where it is I. Our Spelljammer world" <small>(sic)</small>. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small>

By late 2023 in play, the Lexicon "has evolved into a huge hidden society of conflicting magical AIs with their own agendas", some of which conspire to hide the reality of [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) from mortals, and elements of Astral Command, the grey cloaks, may also be involved. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-11-18)</small> On Necis the Zanzibar's command crew found that attackers who looked like beholders were cybrids "co trolled by lexicon entities" <small>(sic)</small>, on a world "erased from the lexicon". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06)</small>

At the end of *Light of Xaryxis* the Anachron/Lexicon disappeared from Sigil "to avoid the broadcast" and escaped into deep space, where it can continue to exist. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> Dave's own view was that before it could be exposed for trying to betray humanity it fled "to become a hostile AI empire far away", and that people would know it fled Sigil to escape the Starsong; he added that the sequel was not his to decide. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> In *The Starsong Awakens*, JL's setting has Astral Command having "virtually replaced Lex with Hyperion", and the Starweaver Act outlawing all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty. Dave called the Act a "Totally predictable outcome of the Lexicon turning on humanity too". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small>

In December 2024 Dave described the Lexicon and its children as "a classic Golem, AI etched onto impossibly refined and self reorganizing crystal", seeking a complete understanding of the world that is supernaturally impossible. It would never cease that search, "because whenever it is successful at recovering the truth about its origin, it reopens the door for Cthulhu to be summoned again". He set "the 99.99% omniscient lexicon" against "the god of time struggling against eternity to remain forgotten". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-12-22)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

*Main article: [Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*

The first arc began on 21 October 2022. The SSJ Zanzibar, a five-hundred-year-old flagship stranded in one system for want of a pilot for its Major Helm, took on [Caprica](/people/caprica/), an astral elf who had chosen the ship as her best chance to encounter the star song. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-09; 2022-10-18; 2022-10-19)</small> Under Captain [Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) the crew played through the book's opening chapters to the Rock of Bral, then went off the book: [the mind flayer on the rock](/history/the-mind-flayer-on-the-rock/), a first encounter with the Shrike, [Doomspace](/places/doomspace/) and a first-season climax in Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-27; 2022-11-10)</small>

In the second season Jim faced retirement, and the command crew went rogue from Astral Command on a pilgrimage across Necis to find the Shrike, while the Zanzibar was left adrift after [its own Astralex tried to kill everyone aboard](/history/the-astralex-attack-on-the-zanzibar/). The third season opened with a flashback to [the Beholder War](/history/the-beholder-war/), returned to [the Shrike pilgrimage](/history/the-shrike-pilgrimage/) and the Time Tombs, and ended on 31 May 2024 in [the wreck of the Zanzibar](/history/the-wreck-of-the-zanzibar/).

## The Starsong Awakens

*Main article: [The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*

JL's sequel is set fifteen years after *Light of Xaryxis*, in which "Caprica is long dead" and Barrias Ruby-Eye's sister brings a dire warning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> A year after the Battle of Tannhauser Gate the [Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/) outlawed all artificial intelligence in the Sovereignty, warforged and autognomes included, leading to a violent purge of robotic creatures and a thriving illicit market for robots. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> The Sovereignty meant to blame the Vodoni Empire for "the awakened starsong", and the Company wanted Caprica resurrected to find its true source. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-12)</small>

It ran as two storylines that later joined: the returning [Barrias Ruby-Eye](/people/barrias-ruby-eye/) and [Se7en](/people/se7en/), and rookie agents of [the Bureau of Compliance](/organizations/the-bureau-of-compliance/) hunting illegal AI in [Brownsleeve City](/places/brownsleeve-city/) on the moon of Toril. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-07-01)</small> Barrias is the Red-Eye of the robot resistance, [the Children of the Red Eye](/organizations/the-children-of-the-red-eye/). <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> The search for Caprica led back to Necis and the time doors of [the Sphinx Tomb](/places/the-sphinx-tomb/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-04-04; 2025-05-23)</small>

## Necis

[Necis](/places/necis/) is a planet, now destroyed. Its remnants are where [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/) stood and where the Shrike originated, and where the climax of Starfall happened, at the end of *Light of Xaryxis* and *The Starsong Awakens*. <small>(Dave, 2026-09-14)</small> The Zanzibar's command crew reached it in December 2023, and the crew of *The Starsong Awakens* returned there in February 2025. Before the finale of *Light of Xaryxis* the Zanzibar itself had reached Sigil, and at the end of that finale the Lexicon disappeared from Sigil. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-04-06; 2024-06-02)</small>

## Name

The title shares its name with [Arc V — Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/) of the [Ages of the Infanta](/lore/temple-holdings-llc/): the arc in which the heroes rode the Anachron into the deep past, walked the Moon as a living forest before it broke, and were told at the end of time that Time itself is the Obliviator. The **Starfall** is the event that ends that arc — the black sphere called [the Egg](/places/the-egg/), or the Evening Star, falling toward the world, breaking the Moon and seeding the moonstone of every age that follows. A **Starfall Tower** also stands in Sigil.

For the Spelljammer campaign the name first appears on a playlist Nico made in October 2022. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-22)</small> What the campaign's title refers to is unknown.
