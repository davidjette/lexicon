**Imperial legal campaign · Korth and the Karrnathi courts · Korth Ep 13, ongoing**

The casualty-reporting purge is an Imperial legal campaign directed by Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin of the Ministry of Law, prosecuting everyone tied to Imperial casualty reporting on capital charges before the Karrnathi courts. The directive was found in a paralegal's old notes, in the apartment of a dead Star Advocate, by the three people wearing that Star Advocate's face. It is being executed now, and the court that hears the cases presumes guilt, allows no defence counsel and carries a death penalty on three of its standard charges.

## THE FIRST FILE

Waiting under Deirdre Moro's door was a folder from Vice Admiral **Freyd Merkin** with two words on it — *Where are you?* — and inside, a redacted brief demanding the prosecution of an Imperial medical records keeper for falsifying documents, espionage, sabotage and embezzlement. The charge sheet included *false claims of defeat at [Anduin](/history/the-battle-of-anduin/)* and *false orders to surrender*. Sabotage, espionage and embezzlement all mean death.

The letters entered in evidence against her were letters she had written to a beloved sister.

## THE COURT THAT CANNOT ACQUIT

Karrnathi justice descends from Galifar and was once reformist. It now seeks atonement rather than truth. Its architecture, learned by the trio in the Ministry of Records the day before they had to work inside it:

- The accused is not the accused. She is **the condemned**. Guilt is presumed.
- There is no defence counsel. There is no discovery.
- Judges interrogate the prosecution; nobody interrogates the evidence.
- The condemned may be innocent of the charge and guilty of something, and absolution requires submission to the court, which stands in for the Emperor.
- In place of a defence there is a kin-and-patron system: spouses, siblings, employers or patrons testify to the condemned's **value**, not to her innocence.

The High Court chamber is overlooked by an Iron Maiden designed to kill the condemned while preserving the body intact for **second service**. The Empire that is prosecuting the casualty returns raises its own dead.

## WHAT THE PURGE IS FOR

Medical records are where a war's real casualty figures live. An empire fighting a continental civil war with undead levies, and telling its citizens it is winning, cannot let accurate returns circulate, and cannot correct them without conceding they existed. It prosecutes the clerks instead.

The second use is quieter. Every prosecution is also a loyalty test of the advocate who brings it. Merkin intercepted Gemma outside the courtroom, threatened court-martial, and framed the case in exactly those terms.

## THE CASE THAT BROKE

The condemned turned to face her prosecutor and was the sister of the woman whose identity Gemma was wearing. Her name was **Bantide Moro**. Gemma read the charges without expression — the judges admired the clarity — and Bantide threw herself on the mercy of the court and confessed to failing the Emperor.

Then Gemma abandoned the advocate's role and testified as kin: that she could not reconcile the charges with the woman she grew up with, that her first assumption had been that Bantide was framed, and that she did not understand where the evidence had come from. Merkin objected and conceded her right to speak as kin. The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims, granted the state one month to produce more, and remanded her.

Merkin's assessment afterward was that she had spent her only chip, that Bantide would never leave that cell or recover her post — and he handed her the Grand Lodge assignment: the [Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/) and the Ivory Lazaret, seize all guest records, arrest any saboteurs or suspected pirates. Deadline Monday. It was Friday morning. Failure to return carried a bounty.

> In Karrnathi law she was simply "the condemned", guilt presumed.

## WHAT BANTIDE WAS

Officially, a medical records keeper. In truth, head nurse of a Silver Flame civilian hospital and the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years — surgery through revivification, and the operation that fitted [Izaak](/people/izaak/)'s prosthetic leg. Codename **the Nightingale**. She escaped the garrison by casting feign death and dropping down the corpse chute, and is now hiding in a dead woman's apartment in the Imperial capital, officially recorded as a suicide.

Her report from inside the machine is the subject of [the rolling-up of Fulcrum](/lore/the-rolling-up-of-fulcrum/).
