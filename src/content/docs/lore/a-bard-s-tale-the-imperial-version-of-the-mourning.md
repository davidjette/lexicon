**Imperial pageant piece · Performed across Khorvaire · Attributed to Hamilton Connor and his acting troupe**

**Also known as:** the Imperial version of the Mourning

**A Bard's Tale: The Imperial Version of the Mourning** is an imperial pageant piece staged as entertainment by Hamilton Connor and his acting troupe, and it is the Empire's authorised account of the Last War, the Mourning and the founding of the Pax Imperium. It is performed on the anniversary of the Mourning — an anniversary the Empire calls **the Rejuvenation** — and it ends on a line of praise for the Emperor. The troupe's text is preserved below as performed, followed by the accounts that do not agree with it.

## A BARD'S TALE — AS PERFORMED

<small>(Hamilton Connor and his acting troupe)</small>

Centuries ago there was a King named Galafar who united the 5 nations of Khorvaire into a mighty empire. King Jarot, a descendant of Galafar, had 5 children; upon his death he gifted them each their own kingdoms; Breeland in which The lightning rail originates. Aundair with its beautiful swaths of fertile fields that supply the nations with food and fine wines. Thrane with it's lush forest and natural waterways. Cyre with its strong scientific and manufacturing power and Karnnath the first human established nations on the soil of Khorvaire which was ruled by the great Kaius the 1st.

Jarot's children were unable to live in peace and fought to claim the empire as their own. A 100 years of war ravaged the lands and took the life of Kaius the 1st, Khorvaire soon fell to ruin. The scientifically advanced people of Cyre came close to winning the war, as they had created the mechanical soldiers known as the warforged to aid them in battle. They were about to conquer when 21 years ago to this day, Kaius the 3rd upon his Great Eagle and flanked by his loyal 13 stood upon a mountain top overlooking a great battle in the lands of Cyre. Kaius the 3rd raised his Greatsword to the sky, splitting the darkened clouds above revealing the morning sun, a great Crimson Sun. He pointed his greatsword back down to the battle and the Crimson Sun flashed bright and hot upon the land of Cyre. Only the Warforged survived. They worshiped him as their savior and he gifted the Mournlands to the Souled Machines. Ending the last war. Today we celebrate the morning sun, The Rejuvenation. Praise Emperor Kaius III.

> Today we celebrate the morning sun, The Rejuvenation. Praise Emperor Kaius III.

<small>— the closing line, as performed</small>

## THE CLAIMS THE TALE MAKES

The tale gives the Empire a founder older than the war — Galifar — so that the Pax Imperium is a restoration and not a conquest. It assigns the hundred years of ruin to Jarot's quarrelling children rather than to any living house. It puts the deadliest event in recorded history in the Emperor's own hand and calls it a mercy: he did not destroy Cyre, he ended the Last War. And it converts the survivors into worshippers. The [Warforged](/lore/warforged/), the only living things left standing in Cyre, are written as a congregation that thanked him for the gift of their own graveyard. In the Empire the anniversary is called **the Rejuvenation**.

## THE ACCOUNT FROM MT. SILICON

Three years after the fall of Sharn, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) took [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) into a control chamber inside the Warforged stronghold at Mt. Silicon and showed them a weapon he had built: a deliberate, controlled recreation of the Mourning itself. He spoke of the Crimson Sun and of the power it had once unleashed, and then he pressed the switch. A red dome bloomed silently across the northern Mournlands, obliterated a portal site and the ships hanging above the border, and was visible from Thronehold. He called it **the Ruby Nova**.

The free states, watching it on the arceye feeds beside the wreck of the Thronehold Summit, named it something else: **the Last Mourning**. Pierce was in the room when the Empire's founding relic came out of the ground, and he understood the Mourning well enough to build a second one that could be aimed.

## THE NAMES THE TALE USES

The troupe's spellings are its own and are preserved above. The Emperor signs himself KAIUS — the death warrant recovered beneath the Korranberg Foundry is signed **Kaius III** — while the ancestral kings of Karrnath are the Kaius line the tale invokes. They include Galafar for Galifar, Karnnath for Karrnath and Breeland for Breland.

The tale also names the Emperor's **loyal 13** on the mountaintop. Thirteen recurs elsewhere in the Empire's business: thirteen [Master bells](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/), thirteen [Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) and thirteen planes in the consecration rite, and the undead members of the Council of 13 who stood as [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s Honor Guard on a balcony in Sharn.
