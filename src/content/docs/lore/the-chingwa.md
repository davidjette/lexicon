**Creature · Fey · *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)***

**The Chingwa** is a kind of tiny, fairy-like space pixie of [Starfall: TNG](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/), with hair made of crackling white electricity. Chingwas speak a language known only to themselves, full of words like "chingwa" and "beenwa", and can speak directly into a mind. Little is known about them; they are harmless and highly magical. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session; Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes)</small>

## Encounters

- **At the Citadel.** A weeping Chingwa met [Mordecai](/people/mordecai/), [Se7en](/people/se7en/) and [Forbes of-the-Smelly-Swamp](/people/forbes-of-the-smelly-swamp/) during [the Citadel trial](/history/the-citadel-trial/). To those who showed her sympathy she offered a small charm, shaped like the first thing they saw with their eyes closed, which vanished at a touch and laid *false life* on them. Rudeness she answered with *guiding bolt*. <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - Hermes 1st appearance session)</small>
- **In the arcane laboratory.** Forbes knew them from that encounter as harmless and quite magical. One was later kept in a terrarium in the arcane division of [the SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/). It asked [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/) what his favourite thing was, and, eager to please, cast *wish*, which began to fill the ship with gold for Carlos and mushrooms for Forbes. See [the Chingwa's wish](/history/the-chingwas-wish/). <small>(JL's DM notes: Starfall Notes - The Trouble with Wishes; Starfall Notes - The Company - Holly Berrias Session)</small>
