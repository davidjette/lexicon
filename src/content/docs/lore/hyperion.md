**Extraplanar entity · Lawful good · The Children of Hyperion · Starfall**

*Also known as:* the Hyperion entity

<small>For the Dan Simmons novels whose plot Starfall melded into, see [Starfall: The Next Generation](/lore/starfall-the-next-generation/).</small>

**Hyperion** is a lawful good extraplanar entity that took over a race of psionically enabled humans on Eberron a very long time ago, and inhabits them and all their children as a collective set of memories and personality. Its church, [the Children of Hyperion](/organizations/the-children-of-hyperion/), sends missionaries of peace to serve on vessels and bases as interpreters, negotiators and spies; [Hyperion Tidzan-Dassiya](/people/hyperion-tidzan-dassiya/), called Sandy, served aboard the [SSJ Zanzibar](/items/the-ssj-zanzibar/) in *[Light of Xaryxis](/history/light-of-xaryxis/)*. After the Lexicon fled at the end of that arc, [Astral Command](/organizations/astral-command/) virtually replaced [the Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/) with Hyperion.

## Nature

Hyperion is an extraplanar entity with a benevolent church. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-10-10)</small> It is a powerful alien consciousness from the realm of dreams; in the Eberron cosmology it comes from the dream realm of horrors, and was escaping that abyss. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24, 2024-06-09)</small>

Its hosts can meld minds with each other when they are close, which lets them update the nodes of the network. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small> Dave has suggested that a Hyperion who spends a long time away from others may form closer relationships, which are assimilated into the whole when it melds with another, so that "that specific love gets turned to a more general love for all". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-10)</small>

A priestess of Hyperion is "possessed with a dual soul shared with her god Hyperion". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-24)</small> Sandy is a kalashtar who speaks Quori, and was "Born possessed with an aberrant Quori". <small>(character sheet)</small> In published Eberron canon the kalashtar are humans bound to spirits called quori from Dal Quor, the plane of dreams. <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>

## The Children of Hyperion

The Children of Hyperion are "An interstellar temple which professes to represent mediation and peaceful coexistence throughout the systems", whose members "believe themselves to be (and likely are) possessed by ancient creatures called Quori". Their Consular volunteer for service on vessels and bases. Sandy holds the rank of Lesser Consular. <small>(character sheet)</small>

## Light of Xaryxis

Sandy served on the Zanzibar as its diplomat. At the close of the mind flayer crisis, when she issued civilian credentials to [Carlos Vega](/people/carlos-vega/), she warned him:

> If I find that you are harboring a mindflayer spawn aboard this ship I will burn you with wrath of all of Hyperion.

<small>(sic) (Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-11-26)</small>

Sandy "would carry the memory of what happened on Necis to the Consul and at the time tombs", so the Hyperion entity would know everything that happened to her on [Necis](/places/necis/) and at [the Time Tombs](/places/the-time-tombs/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-09)</small>

## The Starsong Awakens

Dave foresaw that in a Sovereignty after the Lexicon "the Hyperion entity would have new relevance". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-01)</small> By *[The Starsong Awakens](/history/the-starsong-awakens/)*, fifteen years later, Astral Command has virtually replaced the Lexicon with Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-06-02)</small> See [the Starweaver Act](/lore/the-starweaver-act/).

In *The Starsong Awakens* Sandy, now a captain, stands with Astral Command against the party. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09, 2024-11-10)</small> Barrias's player wanted a way to bring her over to the party's side, and Paytra's player warned that bringing her close to other Sovereignty ships would be dangerous, because she would connect to other Hyperion. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-11-09)</small>

## Name

*Hyperion* is also the title of the Dan Simmons novel whose plot Starfall followed. The stat block for [the Shrike](/lore/the-shrike/) is titled "The Shrike (Hyperion)". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-05-31)</small>
