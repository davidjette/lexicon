**Colossal aberration (Great Old One) · Cthulhu · Mammon · In his long slumber, folded within [the Egg](/places/the-egg/)**

The **Leviathan** is a colossal aberration and Great Old One, a thousand feet tall, also named Cthulhu and Mammon, and the final antagonist of the chronicle. He lies in his long slumber folded inside [the Egg](/places/the-egg/) — the Evening Star that struck the world at Starfall, now a cleft shell not far from [Motherstone](/places/motherstone/). He was reached through the Crack in the Egg and killed by the destruction of his head followed by contact with an [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/).

## The blind devourer

At the end of time the goddess **June** names the truth. The gods of the realm are "the split soul of the Star Child, whose name was **Zoth-Ommog**," who fled their father across all of time:

> My father, Cthulhu, who you call Mammon, or Leviathan, is blind. He remembers all but can see none. He moves by omniscience but he cannot see inside this delicate place we had achieved… He consumes all worlds in his pursuit of us. Once the evening sky was bright with stars, but he swallowed nearly every one.

He devours by *remembering*. His children "created Time itself, to hide those things from him that happen not all at once," and [forgetting is the only defence anyone has](/lore/the-oblivia/). "Our consciousness is that moment out of Time, the delicate world that He cannot penetrate. He can only corrupt it, with fear… He always finds a vessel, one who will Travel on his behalf."

In the vision [Revan Darkcember](/people/revan-darkcember/) had of [R'lyeh](/places/rlyeh/), a gentle voice told the same story from the other side: the [star children](/species/the-star-children/), "the spawn of Cthulu," followed him through the blackness, and some "broke free of his all encompassing mind," crossed the astral depths, and built the city of Ry'leh in the sea of a world of giant reptiles and dragon gods. <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2020-01-30)</small> Dave placed the deep past "in a time before the gods of this world were born, before magic and reality was split open by the Starfall and blood of Zoth distributed into the mortal races." <small>(Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2022-11-09)</small>

## The mind flayers

The mind flayers came from "Zoth-brain octopi merging with the elder brain in the Egg", and for millions of years they "ruled the astral plane from Toril" until the red dragons and the Gith rose to destroy them. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-09-06)</small> Dave: "Zoth is an elder brain, Leviathan is a cosmic elder god, the mother of all mine flayers". <small>(Sic. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

Before the finale the party fought a mind-flayer encounter. Dave, the next day:

> Cthulhu is the big daddy mindflayer

<small>— Dave. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2023-06-04)</small>

## The Call

His chant — *Ph'nglui mglw'nafh Cthulhu R'lyeh wgah'nagl fhtagn* — presses itself into every mind near him, and his **Call** reaches sensitives like **Calix** across the world. Calix's entire crime, the Forgetting that erased a city and a king, was committed to buy time against this: "a buried throne for a living god, for even he fears the Leviathan."

## The Apotheosis

Through **Wainwright St. Cloud**, who "has warped their understanding," the Leviathan deceives the Netherese queens into the **Apotheosis of Man** — marrying the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/) to the Egg — in the belief they are making a "New Star." In truth it would channel the souls of the dead into the Egg until "Cthulhu remerges with the remnants of Zoth, and Time and Consciousness itself give way to total oblivion."

Any member of the party, an Infanta among them, could have betrayed the mission and given Cthulhu what he wants: "to rejoin with all sentient beings and obliterate sequential time itself". <small>(Dave. Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2021-10-23)</small>

## The Anachron

Two attempts to reach him ran through the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/). Archmage **Valen** of the Arcaneum, who could not turn the device on, said why he wanted it: "This magnificent device baffles me. We've tried everything to try and turn it on. We're certain this device will bring us to where and when we have to be to summon her, to summon the Leviathan…" <small>(Arc III Parts IX–XI, Arcaneum. Valen refers to the Leviathan as "her.")</small> At Starfall, the session record names the apparatus St. Cloud sabotages as the **mammon-obliviator-anachron** — the [Mammon Machine](/items/the-mammon-machine/), the [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/) and the Anachron joined — and gives his aim: "to awaken and alert Cthulhu that his children are here" and "to veer toward Ydrissl." <small>(Arc V, [Starfall](/history/arc-v-starfall/), Parts II–IV.)</small>

## How he was killed

Asked for their plan to defeat the Leviathan, two of the party answered:

> I can’t speak for everyone else but I’m going to try and shoot at it.

<small>— Roland. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-06-21)</small>

> I’m going to lecture it about the difference between right and wrong.

<small>— Magnus. (Oral Histories: Temporal Holdings, 2021-06-21)</small>

He is reached through the Crack in the Egg, an "immense, subterranean wound," where geometry confounds and a sea of acid dissolves all but him. He is fought in pieces — Head, Claws, Tentacles, Wings — each regenerating. He dies only when his head is destroyed and then **touched with an [Obliviator](/items/the-obliviator/)**.

The instruction in the encounter notes: "They must reduce the Head to 0 HP and then make contact with it with an Obliviator." "The moment Cthulhu makes contact with the Obliviator, a shockwave of nearly unavoidable negation magic will erupt from Cthulhu in 6 seconds." <small>(End of the Infanta, notes for a final encounter.)</small> The obliviation of the Infantaverse quest by Cypher in this finale is also what removes the premise of time travel by the [Anachron](/items/the-anachron/) from memory for millennia.

Cthulhu was brought to 0 hit points and then erased by Cypher's Wish. Dave: "Cypher killed Cthulhu but also severed the ancient reality that Zod left in the mechanism of death". <small>(Sic on "Zod". Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2023-12-02)</small> What followed was "an all new status quo where the cosmos are orderly in the great wheel model". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2024-01-18)</small>
