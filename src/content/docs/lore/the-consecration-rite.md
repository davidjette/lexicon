**Ritual · Black Dove liturgy · Thirteen officiants, thirteen planes, one sacrifice · Performed on delivery, by the Sisters alone**

*Also known as:* the consecration

The **consecration rite** is the [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) liturgy said over a cast bell on delivery, and it is what makes the bell a [Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). It takes thirteen Doves, the invocation of all thirteen planes, the binding of the bell to *"the unholy blood and the great mother,"* and a very dear sacrifice — a living child, or virgin royalty — and it is performed by the Sisters alone, foundry personnel excluded. Its terms were recovered from the library of Sister Calvane beneath the Korranberg Foundry.

## The terms

A cast bell is not yet a weapon. The Korranberg Foundry Trust could pour [Shavat](/items/khyber-taxonomy/), temper it and tune it, and what came out of the yard was an extremely expensive piece of black metal. It becomes a Fantanya Nyel when thirteen Doves say the rite over it.

The rite was recovered from the library of Sister Calvane in the reclaimed halls beneath the Korranberg Foundry, by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) on a natural 20 on Religion. The party took the whole fifty-pound library out with them.

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Officiants** | **Thirteen Doves.** Sisters of the Black Dove order, and no one else. |
| **Invocation** | **All thirteen planes.** |
| **Binding** | The bell is bound to *"the unholy blood and the great mother."* |
| **Sacrifice** | **A very dear sacrifice — a living child, or virgin royalty.** |

<small>"Thirteen" in this world counts two different things and they are not the same thirteen. There are thirteen *Master* bells — the great castings — and there are the thirteen Doves and thirteen planes of this rite. Three hundred and forty-one parish castings sit beneath the Masters and are consecrated too.</small>

## The practice

The Trust's private schedule is unambiguous about the division of labour: **"All castings consecrated on delivery, by the Sisters alone. Foundry personnel are excluded from consecration and are reminded that curiosity is a breach of the draft."** Each delivered entry in the surviving leaf ends the same way — delivered, consecrated, *receipt under the dove seal*. At Atur the bell was delivered standing and the tower raised over it afterward, at the consignee's expense.

The record of who signs those receipts, house by house and consecration-day by consecration-day, is [the Chorus Ledger](/items/the-chorus-ledger/).

## The arithmetic of the sacrifice

The reading the party works from is the second one. **Virgin royalty** narrows the field considerably. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) has fourteen daughters by Emperor Kaius, many of them sets of twins, all of them under twelve years old. The trio's own conclusion, reviewing the dossiers in Sharn, was that she is waiting for a specific event — **possibly the birth of a male heir**.

Set that against the Litany the Sisters recite at a veiling:

> come the night / the bells will toll / thirteen / the dead will rise / to serve the living / and the dragon / will be reborn

And against the terrarium's instruction in the Glitterhame: *"Ring it here. When HE is born, it will be too late."*

And against [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/)'s dream at the foundry: a marble foyer, a gold-lined fountain, her patron watching from a chaise, and an emaciated old man wearing the Emperor's face stumbling in with an infant and blood pouring from his chest, collapsing into the water until it ran red across the floor toward her feet.

## The unconsecrated one

[The Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/) was never consecrated. It sat in the Korranberg yard waiting on tuning references the Empress would not send, and it still rang loud enough to kill a hold, drive a party of level-eleven adventurers to tear at their own skin, and raise the dead in the lake below.

What the consecration adds to a casting that already does that is not recorded.

## Doves in the record

Two sisters of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) are written into the bell-lore beneath the Mror Holds. **Sister Maelis Dorn** signed the prayer ritual on the muslin sheet that sealed the door — which means she came to the foundry after the bell rang, and probably performed the rite on the casting herself. **Sister Calvane** is named on the Emperor's own death warrant, alongside King Corn and Sedda; her library held the rite; a winged demon was wearing her name behind that sealed door, and her actual corpse was in the bedroom beyond it, standing up and almost vampiric.
