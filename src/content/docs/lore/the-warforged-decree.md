**Imperial edict · Sharn · Years before the rising**

The **Warforged decree** was an Imperial edict by which [Lord Commander Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) outlawed all [Warforged](/lore/warforged/) in the city of Sharn, years before the rising. Most fled toward Gorgonhorn or the Mournlands; one, [Preacher](/people/preacher/), stayed hidden in the undercity, and the smuggling work he began there became [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) With the Warforged gone the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) inherited the labour that had killed flesh-and-blood workers, and were then pushed out of their jobs, their neighbourhoods and their homes by the Iron Veil. The decree was superseded at [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/) in Sharn Ep 19, where R.U.S.T. won Warforged citizenship.

## THE EDICT

The decree's text, its stated justification and its date are not recorded. Its effect was that the Warforged left, and the work they had been doing did not go away.

The Empire's own founding story runs the other way. In [the pageant of the Rejuvenation](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/) only the Warforged survived the Crimson Sun; they worshipped him as their saviour and he gifted the Mournlands to the Souled Machines. In Sharn, one of that Emperor's own commanders made them illegal.

## WHAT IT DID TO THE COGS

The Cogs are the industrial slum beneath the Lower City: factories, processing plants, and Cogborn homes welded directly onto the sides of them. Warforged labourers had always taken the tasks that killed flesh-and-blood workers. With them gone, the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) took those tasks instead — a population already weakened by decades of hazardous work and malnutrition.

Then the Iron Veil seized the factories and replaced Cogborn labour with trained soldiers. The Cogborn were pushed out of their jobs, then their neighbourhoods, then their homes. Many fled beneath the Cogs into collapsing tunnels and abandoned sub-transit lines. The chain that ends with an entire people sealed in a tunnel pocket waiting to be evacuated through [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) begins with this edict.

## THE ONE WHO STAYED

[Preacher](/people/preacher/) was raised in the Cogs and had spent decades on the furnace lines. When the decree came he refused to leave, and vanished into the undercity with his adopted daughter Misty, whom he raised while the rest of his people escaped. For years he sheltered fugitives and smuggled families along routes only he remembered. That work became [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/)

> He has returned to the tunnels not to fight for Sharn, but to save the Cogborn who were left behind.

## REPEALED

At [the Council of Sharn](/lore/the-council-of-sharn/), with the Lord Commander dead and the Iron Veil dissolved, R.U.S.T. brought three demands to the table. The first was Warforged citizenship. Warforged forces offered troops and engineers to stabilise the city and restore the Cogs, and Underhive drones joined the new Department of Infrastructure.
