**Report · Campaign 1, The Unforeseen · The Grand Theatre, Sharn · Report date 15 Oct 2020**

**City of Knives, Pt II** is the Campaign 1 report of the second night of the Crimson Sun's hunt for the mole on the Imperial Assembly, continuing [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/). [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) attended a symphony at the Grand Theatre of Sharn to put the three suspects in one room; the conductor Krespo turned the orchestra into demonic apparitions which slaughtered much of the audience before he destroyed himself; the party escaped with four Assembly and Summit figures; and [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) revealed her identity to Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), who teleported away.

## General Summary

- Party prepared for social event at the Grand Theatre, disguised Esther and Uriel.
- Attended symphony, meeting several members of the Summit and Imperial Assembly.
- During the performance, the conductor Krespo turned on the audience and the musicians turned into dozens of demonic apparitions which slaughtered much of the audience
- The party and several people present stopped Krespo and he destroyed himself in a final act of mass murder
- During the fight, LaBeefe walked in on Minister Henry Heighrick having sex with a girl, Heighrick then erased the event from LaBeefe's memory
- The party escaped with Heighrick, Goldenhew, Tremblepike and Sominyard.
- Esther revealed her identity to Heighrick, who teleported away
- Party proceeded to an after party

## What the night was for

> "There is a mole on the Imperial Assembly." — Hayman Maw, briefing the operation

The Crimson Sun's brief was to find out which members of the Imperial Assembly attending the Interoperability Summit were leaking to the rebellion. The three named suspects were Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) — written *Heighrick* throughout the Campaign 1 record — Lord [Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/), and Lord Albert Spear. The Grand Theatre was where all three could be put in one room without anyone having to ask for a meeting. [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) went in disguised, both of them being faces the Assembly might know.

## Krespo

The conductor turned. The orchestra he was conducting turned with him — the musicians became dozens of demonic apparitions and went into the audience. Much of the audience of the Grand Theatre of Sharn was slaughtered where it sat, in evening dress, during a performance, in front of the assembled political and commercial leadership of the Empire's largest city. The party and several other people present brought Krespo down, and he answered by destroying himself in a final act of mass murder.

Who Krespo was working for is not recorded, nor whether the attack was aimed at the Assembly members in the hall, at the summit itself, or at the mole hunt.

## The room Heinrick was in

In the middle of a massacre, [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) opened the wrong door and found the Empire's Chief Diplomat with a girl. Heinrick did not explain, did not threaten and did not bargain. He reached into LeBeefe's head and took the memory out.

## The escape, and what walked out with them

Four Assembly and Summit figures came out of the Grand Theatre with the party: Heinrick, Goldenhew, [Billy Tremblepike](/people/billy-tremblepike/) and [Filly Sominyard](/people/filly-sominyard/). Two of the four were on the Crimson Sun's suspect list, and all four now owed the party their lives.

Esther revealed her identity to Heinrick — High Inquisitor of the Crimson Sun, in the middle of a covert operation aimed at him — and he teleported away on the spot.

The party proceeded to an after party.
