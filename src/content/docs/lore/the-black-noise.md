**Arcane phenomenon · Polyphase emission · Continental range · Penetrates rock to hundreds of feet · Not stopped by silence**

*Also known as:* the Noise

The Black Noise is the polyphase arcane emission given off by the bells, and the effect by which they drive the living mad and raise the dead. It is named for, and distinct from, the audible ring of a bell: the emission occupies frequency bands above and below hearing as well as within it, and is not stopped by silence. The name is [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s, from the research he carried out on the original artifact — the only known attempt at the thing — over the years since he helped lift it out of the Greenbluff mine himself.

## The emission

[Fantanya Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) emits a polyphase arcane emission which occupies multiple frequency bands across a thousand octaves, both within, above and below human hearing. As a result it can travel continental distances and penetrate almost all matter to a depth of hundreds of feet, making low-lying dungeons and caves no protection from the effects. It would turn graveyards, crypts and tombs into cacophonies of the Black Noise.

The Progenitor's assessment opens *"The ring you would hear if the Bells were rung is incidental."* The measures ordinarily taken against a bell — stopping the clapper, warding the room, blocking the ears, going underground — answer the sound rather than the emission.

## The medium

The ore is raised, in the assessment of Pierce's metallurgists, from seams of the hardest and oldest known rock in Eberron: [Shavat, black Khyber](/items/khyber-taxonomy/), formed where Shavarath touches the mortal world. It is tempered and shaped by a dwarven method itself likely derived from something infernal, and the resulting body vibrates in sympathy with the arcane forces governing life and death.

That sympathy with the arcane forces governing life and death is the mechanism of the emission.

## Three predictions

**Silence does not stop it.** A silence ward, a deafened ear, a sealed vault — these stop the toll, not the Noise. The frequency propagates through the arcane substrate, not the air. *"Wax in your ears will let you die without the courtesy of a warning."*

**The living unravel.** Sustained proximity to an active bell corrodes sanity along the Greenbluff pattern: intrusive tolling, waking dreams of the grave, violent mania, and death — gruesome, and in every documented case self-inflicted or mutual. **The mad do not flee the bell. They defend it while they tear off their own skin.**

**The dead wake.** Within the Noise the dead rise where they lie, and the risen are not wild. They come up listening, ready to serve the will of a sufficiently powerful necromancer keyed in to the chorus. There is exactly one necromancer alive with the reach the network is tuned for, and she breeds the corpse of the Emperor.

## The evidence

|  |  |
|---|---|
| **Greenbluff** | Empire-owned slaves killed in the deep gallery. Every survivor mad in the same shape, describing a sound that shook the walls into a cave-in. The camp is shut and the ground still will not root. |
| **The Korranberg Foundry** | One ringing, three years ago, by a girl with a rock. Tens of thousands of dwarves dead; every casket in the sepulcher gallery holding an undead sealed inside it since; a child's chalk drawing on a wall of a bell with dwarves covering their ears. |
| **The Bladeworks toll** | When Hetta struck the Thirteenth Casting, the party, the kin camp in the next hall and the risen dead climbing the shaft became *one mind* — a thirst for blood and flesh, and an urge to tear one's own skin off. Everyone inside it wounded themselves. |
| **Vathirond** | The graves nearest the House of Mercy do not settle. A gravedigger of thirty years' standing knows how a grave is supposed to sit, and those do not. |
| **Korth cathedral** | A silent bell that is warm at dawn in a Karrnathi winter, and a slow pressure under the crossing at roughly one beat to every four of a heart. The novices call it *the Mother's breathing* and are proud of it. |

## The only known counter

Not silence. **Music.** [The fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/), played on [a horn of King Corn's design](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/), suppresses the influence entirely, and only while it plays. Bofric names the art [arcane harmonics](/items/arcane-harmonics/). Permanent removal requires [KNELL](/items/knell/).

<small>A rebel proposal — a scroll of *Greater Silence*, given to Eric by Renn Tal on the theory that amplified silence across multiple casters could disrupt the resonance — sits against the Progenitor's prediction that silence stops the toll and not the Noise. Eric accepted the scroll.</small>
