The **Infanta Crisis** was the war over the [Infanta](/lore/the-infanta/), gods reborn into mortal children. There were two Infanta Crises, the first and the second, and each lasted on the order of a hundred years. The crisis ended with the defeat of **Asmodeus** in [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/), remembered as the work of troops led by [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) and the [Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/), and with the founding of the [Cormyrean Empire](/organizations/the-cormyrean-empire/) by Zanzibar, as Zanzibar the Elder, first Emperor of Faerûn. The Empire was founded five hundred years before [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/).

## What the war was about

An Infanta is a god reborn into a mortal child. When the gods were shut out of the world, some incarnated themselves in mortal form, and the god-children that resulted "inverted the world with their immense and terrifying sorcery and slavish followings." Every kingdom, cult, corporation and church in Faerûn moved to seize one.

In the Ages of the Infanta, Waterdeep proclaimed a boy Imperator and Pontifex Universalis on the word of the priests of Obus; the [Sword Coast Trading Company](/organizations/sword-coast-trading-company/) compiled profiles on the Infantas of Order, Time, Destiny, Reason, Revelry, Moon, War, Nature, Forge, Death, Sea and Murder; the elves of Myth Drannor secured the Infanta of Sylvanus; orcs were rumoured to hold one at Mordulkin; and an empire mobilised to invade Amn by sea and land, in a move that risked dragging "all the kingdoms of elf, man and dwarf into a continental conflagration."

## Asmodeus

Asmodeus acted through intermediaries. Cult cells operated inside the noble houses of Waterdeep: one nobleman pledged 999 souls and an Infanta by dawn to buy back his twins, with four fellow cultists instructed to murder him if he failed. The Arcaneum's intelligence of the period names "the cult of Asmodeus — one power thought to be behind the new Troubles," with the marginal note: *"If Asmodeus has control of the Empire, gods help the peoples of the world."* A vision recorded in the Arcaneum shows **Asmodeus rise over Faerûn**.

<small>Recovered from the Oblivia.</small> **Una**, who had invaded [Undermountain](/places/undermountain/) with the Spiderbros, summoned Asmodeus to Waterdeep. The party killed Una, but not before she had summoned him, and a portal was used for him to step down toward [the Egg](/places/the-egg/). The party defeated Asmodeus at the threshold of the portal from the tavern to the crack in the Egg, which they were defending to keep Una and any other Infanta out. All the Infanta are drawn to the Egg to merge with Cthulhu and bring about the end, as [Wainwright St. Cloud](/people/wainwright-st-cloud/) designed.

The Egg and the final battle were obliviated, and the battle at the threshold was forgotten with them. The world remembers that troops on the surface led by Zanzibar and the Purple Dragons chiefly defeated the archdevil and sent him back, killing St. Cloud and ending the Infanta Crisis.

The ground where the tavern stood was consecrated, then fortified, then forgotten by everyone except the soldiers garrisoned on top of it. That garrison is [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/).

## What it produced

The Cormyrean Empire was founded on the claim that its founder's blood is holy, with **Zarus** — god of sentinels, conquest and humanity triumphant — as state patron. Its frontier army, the Western Vanguard, is "half crusaders, half occupying force." Under the Empire, Princess Zythara was stripped of her titles for defying ecclesiastical oversight of arcane study.

Zanzibar the Elder is [Zanzibar](/people/zanzibar/) of the Ages of the Infanta. Immediately after the Ages, crowned king of Cormyr and with his forces in the old imperial capital of Waterdeep, he declared himself Emperor of Faerûn and consolidated control of the continent, mostly peacefully, and from then on the Emperor was called "the Zanzibar". Five hundred years later the Empire he founded came to [the War of the Five Zanzibars](/history/the-war-of-the-five-zanzibars/), a critical collapse of the continent.

## The Ordo Trium

In [Sigil](/places/sigil/), [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/) teach the Ordo Trium, the Rule of Three, whose wisdom "established itself in the aftermath of the INFANTA CRISIS, 500 years ago, when the 3 FATES ISTUS, ELOISE, and CYPHER pulled the world back from the brink." <small>(DM notes)</small>
