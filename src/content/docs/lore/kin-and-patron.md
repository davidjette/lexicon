**Concept · Karrnathi legal doctrine · The only substitute for a defence in an Imperial court**

*Also known as:* bearing witness for kin · the character witness · standing

**Kin and Patron** is the doctrine of [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) under which a person of standing may address a sentencing panel and speak to the condemned's *value to society*. Karrnathi law gives the condemned no counsel, no discovery and no right of reply worth the name, because the condemned's job is to admit guilt and seek atonement; since the condemned may not question the evidence against them, **the witness of kin or patronage is the only voice in a Karrnathi courtroom permitted to question the circumstances of the evidence at all.** It was invoked in the [High Court](/places/the-high-court/) at [Korth](/places/korth/) by [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), who broke off her own prosecution to testify as kin for the condemned [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/).

## Who has standing

- **Kin** — a wife, a child, a sibling.
- **An employer** — commonly a wealthy person speaking for a servant or retainer, which is the ordinary use of the doctrine.
- **A patron.**
- **A ward.**

**It is the state that qualifies someone for that role.** For what the code calls the monster races there is no provision at all — they are denied advocates entirely.

## What it is for

The court is asked whether the condemned is worth more to the Empire alive and reordered than dead and in [second service](/lore/second-service/).

## The loophole

The doctrine assumes the witness and the advocate are two different people. It does not appear to have anticipated that they might be the same one.

In the [High Court](/places/the-high-court/) at [Korth](/places/korth/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) — wearing the face of Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), prosecuting a woman who turned out to be Deirdre's own sister — read the charges flawlessly, and then simply **stopped being the advocate**. She said she could not believe the woman she grew up with had done these things, that her first assumption had been a frame, and that she did not understand where the evidence had come from.

Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin stormed the floor to object that the advocate had confused her roles, and in objecting was forced to state the doctrine aloud and concede it: **it is her right.** An advocate of the Ministry of Law who is also the condemned's sister may set down the first role and take up the second, mid-trial, and the state that qualified her for both cannot stop her.

> "Advocate Moro has confused her role as Advocate and is instead here to bear witness for her kin — which is her right."

The panel found the documentation insufficient to sustain the capital claims and granted the state one further month. [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) went back to a cell instead of the balcony.
