**Warlock patron · The Fathomless · The deepest ocean in the Abyss · Patron of Gabriella Hellwood**

**Also known as:** Hoff Goofa; offgoofa

**Hafgufa** is a Fathomless warlock patron, in Dave's words "the unyielding maw of the sea itself, a primeval incarnation of the sailors nightmare and the end of all drowned men". [Gabriella Hellwood](/people/gabriella-hellwood/) made a pact with it during the [Ghosts of Twatmarsh](/lore/ghosts-of-twatmarsh/) campaign, promising to send it deserving souls, and by 2022 was a fighter and warlock of the Fathomless. The pact made her an eldritch horror and kept her alive for a few hundred years, until the time of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), when she was the Warlock Pirate of the Kraken and captain of the *Blackfish*. Her deckhand [Keisha Milosh](/people/keisha-milosh/), daughter of [Feng Milosh](/people/feng-milosh/), told the party that Hafgufa's depths are said to be the deepest ocean in the Abyss.

## The maw of the sea

> Hoff Goofa to me is the unyielding maw of the sea itself, a primeval incarnation of the sailors nightmare and the end of all drowned men, Gabriella made a deal so that she would send it deserving souls

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

## Gabriella Hellwood's pact

Dave described the Fathomless as a patron in March 2022: "The Fathomless as a patron, basically davy jones, you get a bonus action tentacle and water breathing and swim speed". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-03-13)</small> By the Saltmarsh finale Gabriella was "8th fighter 2nd Fathomless". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-10)</small>

JL posted an image of Gabriella captioned "Neptune and Hafgufa fighting for Gabby’s soul". <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-07-18)</small>

In [White Plume Mountain](/places/white-plume-mountain/) Gabriella carried the sword [Blackrazor](/items/blackrazor/). For her to bond a sword through the pact, Dave wrote, "I would have to get Blackrazor or such to also serve Hoff goofa or something". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-14)</small> The next day:

> Blackrazor is a competitor to that, at least with Crestus inside it. Could be it could be brought to serve hoff goofa in which case who knows, but otherwise, it is a very dangerous item to all of us

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2022-08-15)</small>

## The eldritch horror

A few hundred years later, in the time of Dead Man's Hand, Hellwood was still alive. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-05-31)</small> Dave:

> Hellwood only lived this long because she became an eldritch horror from her pact with offgoofa

<small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>

She was then the captain of the pirate ship *Blackfish*, called the Warlock Pirate of the Kraken <small>(DM notes)</small>, and the Dunwater Horror that clashed with the demon lord Dagon beneath her ship at [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/) <small>(DM notes; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>. After that, by Dave's account, she became "a dying demikraken near Saltmarsh who guards a lair with blackrazor". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-22)</small> Nico wrote that "Gabs soul was  haunted by the Goofa". <small>(Nico, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-03-07)</small>

## Keisha Milosh

Keisha Milosh, a deckhand of the *Blackfish* whose father Feng had been crew there, spoke of Hafgufa to the party aboard the sinking *Blackfish*, where she refused her father's FATES card:

> "You spoke to my father?"\
> "Was he going to hell?"\
> "Hafgufa's depths are said to be the deepest ocean in the Abyss. But I dont worship Hafgufa. I believe in the will of the Fates. And thats why I dont want anything to do with a magic playing card that lets you predict the future."

<small>(Keisha Milosh, DM notes)</small>
