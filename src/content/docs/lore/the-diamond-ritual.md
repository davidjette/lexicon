**Resurrection rite · Performed on Uriel Qualanthri and on Eden · Sharn**

*Also known as:* the diamond ritual

The Diamond Ritual is a resurrection rite that leaves a diamond permanently embedded in the body of the person brought back. [Fema Nolan](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/), a founder of [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/), performed it on [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) after a mind flayer killed her beneath [Sharn](/places/sharn/), and the diamond has sat in Uriel's skull ever since. Years later [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), who had seen it done, brought the body of her half-sister [Eden](/people/eden/) to the Black Dove Hospital and described the rite to the clerics. [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) led a modified version with a Khyber-cut diamond set into Eden's sternum in place of her heart, and Eden became EDN-00, the prototype of [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

## Uriel Qualanthri

On a visit to Sharn some years before the Sharn arc, Uriel was killed by a mind flayer in the city's sewers. Esther Crona carried her out to a rebel base beyond the walls, where Fema Nolan resurrected her and embedded a diamond in her skull. <small>(World Anvil, Uriel Qualanthri)</small> The account of [the Temple of the Closed Circle](/places/temple-of-the-closed-circle/) has the temple's Ulitharid kill Dr. Uriel Qualanthri and consume her brain, and Fema Nolan resurrect her shortly afterwards. <small>(World Anvil, Temple of the Closed Circle)</small>

In [Sharn Ep 14](/sessions/sharn-ep-14-the-memory-in-the-sky/) the trio watched [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s recovered ArcEye memory of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born, and as Uriel turned away, the ArcEye caught "a glimpse of a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull." Zero-One then revealed that years ago, when she was known as Fema Nolan, she was the one responsible for resurrecting Uriel and the reason for the diamond. <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small> In [Sharn Ep 18](/sessions/sharn-ep-18-the-fall-of-esther-crona/) [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) broadcast the same memory to the whole city in [the Sharn Broadcast Override](/lore/the-sharn-broadcast-override/). <small>(Sharn site, Episode Summaries)</small>

In Nico's notes Uriel's ritual placed the diamond at the base of the skull, and it was this resurrection that Esther described to the Black Doves when Eden died. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

## Eden

### The incident

In Nico's notes the Eden incident took place four years before the Sharn arc and six months before BioTec Phase I. Eden, a young Tiefling woman and the half-sister of Esther Crona, [Faith](/people/faith/) and [Blair](/people/blair/), was attending college in Sharn when she suffered catastrophic trauma to the sternum. Esther found her and in a panic took her to the Black Dove Hospital in Upper Sharn, where she told the clerics about the diamond ritual she had once seen Fema Nolan perform on Uriel. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

Sister Nora filed the sealed testimony on the incident. Its overview lists:

- Catastrophic sternal trauma
- Complete cessation of vital signs
- Arcane contamination consistent with necrotic exposure
- Esther delivered Eden's body in a state described as "hysterical, blood‑covered, and begging."

<small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

In [Preacher](/people/preacher/)'s account, after the Warforged were outlawed in Sharn a young Tiefling woman discovered his hidden cell in the Lower City; he chased her through the drainage tunnels, and she fell from the upper walkway of [the Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) and was impaled on the machinery below. Esther in desperation came to Sister Nora for help. <small>(Sharn site, Mission Board)</small> A restricted BioTec addendum places the incident at the unregistered wing of the Black Dove Hospital in Upper Sharn. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-01-02)</small>

### The procedure

Nico's notes set the Diamond Ritual out as an emergency resurrection modelled on the Uriel–Fema Nolan event. Its components were:

- a Khyber-cut diamond, size unknown;
- a modified Last Mercy rite;
- a necro-arcane lattice stabilizer;
- direct channelling from three senior Doves.

Uriel's ritual had set the diamond at the base of the skull. Eden's required sternum embedding, the diamond replacing her heart. The recorded outcome: "Eden revived." <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

Sister Nora was the main cleric to perform the ritual, with the help of [Umbra](/items/umbra/) and BioTec technology. Esther Crona, Sister Nora, [Dr. Joanne Menka](/people/dr-joanne-menka/) and two Black Dove clerics were the only people present, and the only ones who know exactly what happened to Eden. The ritual did not go smoothly, and on Esther's orders the drug [Embrace](/items/embrace/) and [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) became involved in "making her whole again". <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

### Afterwards

The BioTec addendum "Foundational Incidents & Prototype History" lists her as **Subject Zero: Eden**, designation EDN-00, status "Active (Unstable)": the first hybridization attempt, from before BioTec was formally involved, whose resurrection set Project E.D.E.N. going. Knowledge of her is limited to Esther Crona, Dr. Joanne Menka and the Black Dove high clergy. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-01-02)</small>

Nico planned [the Sanctum of the Last Mercy](/places/sanctum-of-the-last-mercy/) at the Black Dove Hospital as a place where the trio might find information on the Diamond Ritual, and prepared Sister Nora's interrogation with the note that she would not be open about Eden or about her own part as the main cleric of the ritual. <small>(Nico's DM notes, Episode 3)</small>

In the cellar at Zilspar Farm 02 in [Sharn Ep 7](/sessions/sharn-ep-7-shadow-of-the-past/), Nora revealed that EDN-00 was created from the resurrected body of Eden. She believed Eden's soul was lost:

> "She's more alive than the hybrids that came after her…but not whole. Not like she once was. A miracle, yes but an incomplete one, a soul even Uriel may not reach"

<small>(Sharn site, Mission Board)</small>

Eden serves in the Mournlands as the hybrid the Hundreds call the Blue Devil, deployed by Iron Veil airships. <small>(Sharn site, Mission Board)</small>
