**Species · The Empire's constituency · The Blood of Vol's membership · Neither of those things by consent**

The human species is published Eberron setting material <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>, and Karrnath was, in the Empire's own pageant, "the first human established nation on the soil of Khorvaire". Under the Empire the species became the material of a manufactured supremacy: the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) devised the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) as a covert project to breed radical human support for the throne, handled by an agent codenamed Godseed until the cult outgrew its handler. Humans are recorded on both sides of the war that followed, and slavery of humans is within living memory.

## Project Godseed

The [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) devised the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/) as a covert project to breed radical support among disaffected workers in the merchant class and city guard forces who were susceptible to propaganda that they were **better than Warforged, goblins, gnomes, halflings, orcs and dwarves**. It was seeded with resources and nuggets of information by an unnamed covert agent, codename **Godseed**, embedded inside a civilian organisation, who served as the cult's primary handler until it grew too large to control. Godseed was [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), and the civilian organisation was a humanitarian medical order.

## What the cult believes about being human

From the Imperial report Locke Pierce decrypted at Rekkenmark East: a cult known as the Blood of Vol is filled with humans who believe they share an ancestral kinship with the Emperor, which they regard as next to divine. They believe a dark and sinister cabal of politicians, media figures, financiers and college professors are collaborators in a global plot to commit mass pedophilia and degenerate the core values of society. They believe the Emperor is secretly fighting this cabal from behind the scenes, and that any criticism of him or failure of his reign can be laid at the feet of this deep state — or explained away as intentional, and as setting the stage for a tremendous purge and storm that will wipe the corrupt from the Earth and leave them in control.

The internal memos add that **the cult is not entirely wrong**. The Emperor knows of them, secretly agrees with their assertion of his divine blood and his fatherhood over all of Eberron, and has on multiple occasions leaked information through communiqué and rumour to support their conspiracies and even encourage terrorist acts against his enemies.

## Humans on record

Humans appear on both sides of the conflict. Against the Empire: **Zero of Sharn**, the young human cleric of the Silver Flame who runs the Nest's covert work; [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), described on the Sharn News Network as "a human man in short-shorts" and now Chancellor of Sharn; and Grady Marsh, the retired ArcEye surveillance wizard in his late seventies whose memory of Calcifer's birth broke Esther Crona. For it: [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), human aberrant-mind sorcerer, Grand Duke and founder of the Unforeseen; the human noblemen [Lord Gregory Goldenhew](/people/lord-gregory-goldenhew/), Lord Albert Spear and Minister [Henry Heinrick](/people/henry-heinrick/) of the Imperial Assembly, with the human sorcerer Hayman Maw at the head of the Crimson Sun; [Kwanti d'Orien](/people/kwanti-d-orien/) of the rails; and [June Gloomstalker](/people/june-gloomstalker/), the middle-aged human druidess who speaks for a coven of dark druids.

<small>The species of most figures in the rebellion — Richard Blaze, John Stamos, Fema Nolan, Jessica Grimpledink — is not recorded. Digma Beeve is a halfling.</small>

## Being human under the Empire

Slavery is inside living memory and inside the biographies of people currently in power: [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) was born into it; Richard Blaze was captured in an Imperial raid and sold into kitchen labour in Newham; LeBeefe sold Empire-owned slaves to private investors in the Demon Wastes. The rebel-factions page counts "Cogborn, Warforged, and human sympathizers" as three groups, and the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/) of Sharn's foundry districts are accordingly not counted as human.

The Emperor himself may not be human at all. Two competing in-world accounts survive: one holds him to be the ancestral dragon; the other, recorded by [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), holds him to be a vampire descended from the Crimson Sun. Neither is settled. The cult of humans who claim ancestral kinship with him may be claiming kinship with a dragon, or with a vampire, or with nothing.
