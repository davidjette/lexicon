**Concept · Karrnathi social and legal institution · Administered at [the Hall of Submission](/places/the-hall-of-submission/), [Korth](/places/korth/)**

*Also known as:* crossing over · the second service to the Emperor

A Karrnathi subject serves the Emperor once while alive. **Second service** is the second term, and it begins after death.

It is not framed as a punishment and mostly is not one. The very old walk to the Hall of Submission **willingly**, to cross over to the other side and begin the service. The dead are brought there by others and transformed. Officers wait outside with troop transports to collect the converts. The building is open to the public.

## A society that mandates it

Every institution leans in the same direction until surrender is the only level ground left. **Karrnath is a society that more or less mandates that you give your corpse to the state.**

The pressures are visible from the outside:

- **The army is the city.** Living and undead columns move between the barracks all night, and the undead ranks are filled from somewhere. In [Korth](/places/korth/) the barracks mess is effectively open to the public because the military is so large a share of the population — the Empire feeds you, and the Empire expects the return.
- **The courts price it in.** Under [Karrnathi law](/lore/karrnathi-law/) a death sentence specifies both the duration of the pain to be inflicted and **the length of service owed afterward in undeath** — if the condemned has not already pledged themselves to the Emperor.
- **The machinery is efficient.** Behind the prison at the Ministry of Law a stair descends to a yard where a corpse chute empties the dead into stacks for reuse. [The Iron Maiden](/items/the-iron-maiden/) on the High Court balcony kills the condemned while leaving the body intact, because a damaged body is a wasted one.
- **The origin is imported.** The accepted explanation for Karrnath's military and undead system is that it was learned from the elves of the Undying Court, who came with the Emperor's line and brought official necromancy with them.

## What it is like

The Hall of Submission is one of the pleasanter places in Korth to hold a private conversation, for the reason that almost no one in earshot is alive. The Empire has done something more thorough than compulsion — it has made the surrender of the body into a courtesy, and the refusal of it into rudeness.
