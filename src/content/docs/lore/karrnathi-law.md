**Concept · Jurisprudence of the Kingdom of Karrnath · Administered from the Ministry of Law, [Korth](/places/korth/)**

Karrnathi law is the jurisprudence of the Kingdom of Karrnath, administered from the Ministry of Law at [Korth](/places/korth/). It split off from the old Galifar courts to embody the state rather than the crown, and created a judicial bureaucracy notionally separate from the Emperor. It has no *defendant*: the person in irons before the panel is **the condemned**, so called from the moment the brief is written, because **guilt is the premise rather than the question**. The system seeks atonement rather than truth, has no defence counsel, no discovery and no complex evidentiary rules, scales sentences by race and expected lifespan, and denies advocates entirely to what the code calls the monster races. Its one provision for the accused is the kin and patron witness. Korth's judiciary is at present co-opted by Imperial control, and the case of [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) was fought and adjourned under it.

The surviving legal shelf of [the Hall of Records](/places/the-hall-of-records/) has been emptied and refilled with filler and is down to a handful of volumes. It was read in a single night by two people who needed to lose a capital case by morning.

> "The rule of law isn't what it once was."

## The doctrine

Karrnathi justice **does not seek truth; it seeks atonement.** The elementary texts state the principle without embarrassment: you happen to be accused of this, and while you may be innocent of *it*, you are guilty of something. To win absolution you must submit to the will of the court. Through that submission the wisdom of the court can then pass judgement on the appropriate sentence, or non-sentence, for the crime.

The condemned's role is to seek atonement and more or less to admit guilt; the court's role is to weigh, not to test. And the court is a stand-in for the Emperor — an extension of his wisdom and his mercy. Submitting to it is submitting to him.

The system **effectively mandates that you give your corpse to the state**, and offers the criminal one road back in: see [second service](/lore/second-service/).

## Origins

The Karrnathi system **split off from the old Galifar courts and was revolutionary when it did**. It set out to embody the state rather than the crown, and it created a judicial bureaucracy notionally separate from the Emperor himself.

The panels of judges are mythologised accordingly: virtuous paragons of justice who have made many wise decisions over the years, including **exonerating people later celebrated as heroes of the people**. The books are full of that mythology.

## What a Karrnathi court does not have

- **No defence counsel.** There is no opposing advocate. The state's advocate prosecutes; nobody argues the other side.
- **No discovery.** There is no phase in which evidence is exchanged or the condemned is interrogated by anyone but the state. The state advocate is expected to assemble the case and present all of it.
- **No complex evidentiary rules.** It is, functionally, an arbitrary court of judges.

What it has instead is a **panel** — eight judges sat for [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) — whose stated function is to **interrogate the prosecution's reasoning and decide for themselves**. It is adversarial, but the adversary of the prosecutor is the bench.

**Acquittal is perfectly common.** Getting off is a normal outcome of a functioning atonement system, and the party's plan to save a life by losing a trial rested entirely on that fact.

## Kin and patron — the only substitute for a defence

In place of an advocate for the accused there is the [kin and patron](/lore/kin-and-patron/) system. A person of standing may bear witness to the condemned's *value to society* and thereby sway the wisdom of the court: a wife, a child, a sibling, an employer, a patron, a ward. Because the condemned's own role is to admit guilt, it is this witness alone who can question the circumstances of the evidence.

**It is the state that qualifies who holds that standing.**

## Procedure

- The state's advocate stands before the panel and **reads the charges**.
- The advocate **demonstrates the evidence and distributes it** to the judges.
- The advocate **calls for the sentence**.
- A qualified witness of kin or patronage may speak to the condemned's value.
- The panel deliberates and rules — sentence, or no sentence, or an adjournment granting the state time to produce more.

## Sentencing

**Sabotage, espionage and embezzlement all carry death.** Falsifying Imperial records carries a variable term **equivalent to twenty-five human years**.

**Sentences are scaled by race and expected lifespan.** The term is not a number of years; it is a proportion of the life the state believes you were going to have. An elf and a human convicted of the same falsification do not serve the same sentence, and neither of them is being punished for the same length of time.

**A death sentence specifies two durations.** The first is **how long the pain of the killing is to last**. The second is **how long the condemned owes the Emperor in service afterward, in undeath** — if they have not already pledged themselves to him. [The Iron Maiden](/items/the-iron-maiden/) on the balcony of [the High Court](/places/the-high-court/) exists to satisfy both clauses at once: it kills the condemned while leaving the body intact for use.

## Monster races

For what the code calls the monster races, it sets out the following:

- They receive **much harsher penalties** for the same offences.
- They are **denied advocates entirely** — no kin, no patron, no witness of value.
- Their **methods of execution are laid out specifically so that they can be executed at all**, should they for some reason be formally accused of a crime.

## The state of the law now

Korth is in crisis and its judiciary has been co-opted by Imperial control. The legal section of the Hall of Records has been stripped to first principles and padded with filler. A ministry-wide directive currently opens **mass prosecutions of everyone responsible for reporting casualties**, with executions expected to run for weeks.

## The State v. Bantide Moro

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), wearing the face of Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), read ten charges against the condemned without expression — falsifying Imperial records, false claims of defeat at Anduin, suspected sabotage and false orders to surrender, embezzlement, conspiracy to defraud official records, perjury, adultery, idolatry, high treason, and sodomy. Several of the eight judges registered admiration at the clarity of the delivery.

Then she set the advocate's role down and picked up the other one. She testified as **kin**: that she could not reconcile the charges with the woman she grew up with, that her first assumption had been a frame, and that she did not understand where the evidence had come from. Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin burst in to object and was forced to concede on the record that bearing witness for kin *"is her right."*

The panel deliberated, found the documentation **insufficient to sustain the capital claims**, granted the state one further month to produce evidence, and remanded the condemned to the local garrison.

> "Advocate Moro has confused her role as Advocate and is instead here to bear witness for her kin — which is her right." — Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin
