**Species · Zilargo and Sharn · House Sivis, banned · Long-lived**

The **gnome** is a long-lived humanoid people of Khorvaire whose nation is **Zilargo** <small>(published Eberron canon)</small>. In Sharn they are associated above all with **House Sivis**, the gnomish Dragonmarked house of spies, clerks and bureaucrats, which the Empire has banned. Two gnomes hold high office in the city, both elders of that house and both measuring their careers in centuries: [Channa Devir](/people/channa-devir/), four hundred years old, Secretary of the Speakers Guild, and [Torralyn d'Sivis](/people/torralyn-d-sivis/), three hundred, head of the Notaries Guild and rumoured to have been the wartime saboteur Herr Bomba.

## House Sivis, banned

**House Sivis** is the gnomish Dragonmarked house of spies, clerks and bureaucrats, and it is **banned**. Channa Devir is one of the few known "former" members of the house to have left Sharn or Zilargo without arrest, probably because of her fame and popularity among the well-to-do of the city.

The ban did not make the house's work stop. It made it informal. Torralyn d'Sivis, an elder of the house, now heads the Sivis-dominated **Notaries Guild**, whose agents form the basis for all contract enforcement and financial markets in Sharn and beyond. Channa Devir, another elder, is Secretary of the **Speakers Guild** and the person both separatist and collaborationist factions in Sharn call in to make a compromise stick.

## Herr Bomba

The Empire's grievance against the gnomes is the North Warrens. In the last year of the war, operating under the nom-de-guerre **Herr Bomba**, Torralyn d'Sivis is rumoured — between bombs and building collapses — to have claimed the lives of nearly half a million Imperial soldiers in the collapse of the North Warrens of Sharn. Many suspect that defeat is what led the Emperor to trigger the Mourning and end the war entirely.

Torralyn denies any connection with resistance activity during the war. He is an elder of a banned house running the guild that enforces every contract in the city, and he says he was not there.

## What the Empire says about gnomes

The Crimson Sun wrote it down. **Project Godseed** was devised as a covert programme to breed radical support among disaffected workers in the merchant class and the city guard who were susceptible to propaganda that they were *better than Warforged, goblins, gnomes, halflings, orcs and dwarves*. Gnomes are third on the Empire's own list of peoples it manufactured contempt for. The cult that grew out of that programme is the [Blood of Vol](/organizations/blood-of-vol/), and it is now beyond the control of the people who built it.

The petty version of the same contempt runs through Imperial infrastructure policy. At the Council of Mayors at Thronehold, Lord Grey's committee resolved that *"this committee rejects the Gnomes demand for higher gauge rails and if Sharn and other border cities are going to function then its time to put the notion to rest."* LeBeefe was assigned to represent the Eastern Provinces at that Summit and to negotiate for standards reinforcing Imperial investment in the rail system; the Summit is also the setting of [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/), in which the Unforeseen were sent to find a mole in the Imperial Assembly.

## Death proof

Channa Devir has been a target of the [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) for some time and is still alive. She is suspected of holding **'death proof' spells**: arrangements under which, if she were to die, awful secrets would be automatically revealed. The Emperor has not moved against her.

## Gnomish, the language

The air-bubble breathing devices Locke Pierce issued for travel through the Mournland mist each bear an inscription in ancient gnomish: **Interplanetary Association of Lexical Engineers**. What that organisation was, and what a lexical engineer did, is **Unknown**.
