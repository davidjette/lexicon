**Counter-insurgency sweep · Continent-wide · Ongoing, beginning after Izaak vanished**

The rolling-up of Fulcrum is the continent-wide counter-insurgency sweep in which the Empire dismantled the rebel group [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/), beginning after the disappearance of [Izaak](/people/izaak/) and running through days of mass arrests and executions. It is ongoing.

Fulcrum is one of the oldest rebel groups on the continent: farmers, rangers and dissidents, founded more than twenty years ago by Fema Nolan, [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), and — secretly — [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) with [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/). It survived the [lightning rail](/history/the-lightning-rail-ambush/), the fall of Sharn and three years of open war.

## THE NIGHTINGALE'S REPORT

The account comes from **Bantide Moro**, codename the Nightingale, the rebellion's surgeon for twenty years, delivered in a dead woman's apartment in Korth hours after she escaped her own execution.

Fulcrum is being dismantled across the continent, **as if the Empire had obtained a list of every name**. Executions ran for days.

She also gave the timing:

> All of it began after Izaak disappeared.

## IZAAK

Izaak was a Breland ranger, a skilled artificer, the liaison between [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) and Fulcrum, and the man who implanted the Ghost Chips that let the Inevitables move through Sharn unseen. He is the son of Zero-One and the half-brother of [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/). Bantide fitted his prosthetic leg herself.

He is presumed dead after [the destruction of Zilspar Farm](/history/the-destruction-of-zilspar-farm/). Bantide refuses to believe he informed on anyone and insists he is still alive.

## THE OTHER READINGS

Nobody in the rebellion has settled this, and the competing explanations are all live.

- **Izaak talked, or was made to.** The timing fits exactly.
- **Someone else betrayed Fulcrum.** Bantide's own position, and she knew him for twenty years.
- **The Chancellor is the leak.** [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) asked [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) privately whether [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) — spore-compromised, hearing a voice he had concealed from everyone — was how the Empire found the farm.
- **Locke Pierce sold it.** Eric's own reconstruction: Pierce offered food, the farm was destroyed days later, Pierce then offered more food for the price of access to Sharn's airspace, and it was paid that same afternoon.

## THE LOSSES SO FAR

**Zero-One** (Fema Nolan) — founder, Paladin of the Silver Flame, mother of Izaak and Zero of Sharn, the woman who resurrected [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and put the diamond in her skull. Taken alive by E.D.E.N. units at Zilspar. Jessica's instruction to Gemma was that if they cannot save her, they must not let her be taken.

**Izaak** — vanished, then presumed dead at Zilspar.

**Donna**, the Silver Flame cleric of Zilspar Farm 02, and **Grady Marsh** — no sign of either among the dead. **Bantide Moro** — officially recorded as a suicide. Unnumbered others across the continent, over days of executions.

## WHAT IS LEFT TO PULL ON

Too exhausted to travel and too wanted to be seen, Bantide gave the Inevitables one thing before they left for the Summer Palace: a listening post beside the Hall of Submission, holding records that may explain what happened to Izaak and to Zero-One.
