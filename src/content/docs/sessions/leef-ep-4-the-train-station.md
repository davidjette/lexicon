**Leef arc · Episode 4 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 4 — The Train Station** is the fourth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played on 22 August 2025, it included an explosion at the train station in [Leef](/places/leef/), from which [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) ran. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) spared a member of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), who was later named Luz Fayana, and nearly died after running into hostile fire. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22; 2025-08-23; 2025-10-19)</small>

## Summary

Gemma ran from the explosion at the train station. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22)</small> In Nico's later account, the heroes of Leef "attacked the train station and blew up their shipments and killed guards"; she noted that "Some may say" this was an act of terrorism, and Dave answered, "yeah but civilians is different". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

Dario spared a Black Dove. JL, as DM, asked for her name the same night: "Dario, what is the name of the Black Dove you spared???" Dave offered two, an Elvish name such as Valenya Elenwe if she was "the same type origin as Uriel", or Luz Fayina. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> JL settled it in October: "Luz Fayana is the Black Dove Dario let go". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

Dave wrote the next day that Dario was "too focused on getting a smite" and "ran into hostile fire and AOE", without casting Bless or Protection, "and almost died". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> Gemma used her warlock's fey step healing; Nico wrote that she "was happy to see the warlock fey step / healing step work". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small>

## Revelations

- The Black Dove whom Dario let go is **Luz Fayana**, also written Luz Fayina. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23; 2025-10-19)</small>
- JL, as DM, placed her among the "pro-empire, anti-Uriel factions (like Fayina) who could be friends or foes", who "want imperial reform not the end of empire". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>
