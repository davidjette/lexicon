**Leef arc · Episode 4 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 4 — The Train Station** is the fourth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played on 22 August 2025, it included an explosion at the train station in [Leef](/places/leef/), from which [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) ran, and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) nearly died after running into hostile fire. The same session's second job was a raid on the abandoned [temple of Dol Arrah](/places/the-temple-of-dol-arrah/), a Black Dove listening station, where the party found [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) caged and Dario spared the Black Dove Luz Fayana. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22; 2025-08-23; 2025-10-19)</small>

## Summary

The job came from [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/), who offered the pair a choice: two papers of transit to Aundair that night, or to fight back. A supply train marked MILITARY SUPPLIES NEWHAM had unloaded 135 crates into the stockyard at Leef, the last stop on the line for soldiers heading east against the dwarves. The Colonel's orders were to steal what they could carry and blow up the rest. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Gemma ran from the explosion at the train station. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-22)</small> In Nico's later account, the heroes of Leef "attacked the train station and blew up their shipments and killed guards"; she noted that "Some may say" this was an act of terrorism, and Dave answered, "yeah but civilians is different". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-27)</small>

Dario was incapacitated in the blast, and Gemma dragged him away from it; the pair had gone in disguised, and the city was put on high alert. From the stockyard Dario took a +1 longsword of vengeance, possessed by an aspect of Tira Miron, and Gemma took the gloves of thievery. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Dave wrote the next day that Dario was "too focused on getting a smite" and "ran into hostile fire and AOE", without casting Bless or Protection, "and almost died". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> Gemma used her warlock's fey step healing; Nico wrote that she "was happy to see the warlock fey step / healing step work". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small>

After the train-station raid the Inquisition executed citizens of Leef at random, and Dario became a folk hero. [Zero](/people/zero-of-sharn/), an old blind Tabaxi woman begging for alms and a secret agent of the Silver Flame, found him: "I see the flame in you. Take my hand… can you feel the flame in me?" She told him a treasure lay in the temple of Dol Arrah, "though it may not be the treasure you seek", called the Colonel "a well-intentioned fool", and said that the cancer at the heart of the Empire was the Black Doves. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

> You think you are part of the rebellion? No. You are not part of the rebellion. There will come a time when you will ask yourself what you truly believe in, what you are willing to die for, what you are willing to sacrifice everything for, your friends, your family… and if you search your soul and find an answer… then you will be part of the rebellion.

<small>(Zero, JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The Colonel's side of the job was 300 gold pieces for the input switch of a listening station. The temple of the sun goddess, abandoned by order of the Emperor and become a refuge for indigents, was secretly a listening station of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), devoted to the Empress Uriel. In its basement were skeletons, Black Dove priests and a man locked in a cage. Once the priests were killed another Dove came forward to bargain: the Black Doves, like the rebels, opposed Grand Duke LeBeefe, and the man in the cage "bears the imperial mark of audit. He is the key to LeBeefe's fortress". <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Dario spared an elf of the Black Doves in the temple. JL, as DM, asked for her name the same night: "Dario, what is the name of the Black Dove you spared???" Dave offered two, an Elvish name such as Valenya Elenwe if she was "the same type origin as Uriel", or Luz Fayina. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-23)</small> JL settled it in October: "Luz Fayana is the Black Dove Dario let go", and placed her among the "pro-empire, anti-Uriel factions (like Fayina) who could be friends or foes", who "want imperial reform not the end of empire". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-10-19)</small>

The man in the cage was Eric, an accountant sent by the Imperial Revue Service to investigate the tax accounts of Newham. Imperial soldiers had seized him on arrival and handed him to the Black Doves, who imprisoned and tortured him in the temple basement and cast *feeblemind* on him. His superior was a gnome, Master Clipperfast. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small> Eric entered play as JL's character in the following session, [Leef Ep 5](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/).

## Revelations

- The train-station attack was a raid on the stockyard of Newham military supplies, the Colonel's first job for the pair.
- Dario's longsword is a +1 sword of vengeance possessed by an aspect of Tira Miron.
- The temple of Dol Arrah at Leef was a Black Dove listening station, and Eric had been its prisoner.
- The Black Dove whom Dario spared there is **Luz Fayana**, also written Luz Fayina.
- Zero, the old Tabaxi beggar of Leef, is an agent of the Silver Flame.
