**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 14 · DM: Nichole**

**Sharn Ep 14 — The Memory in the Sky** is the fourteenth session of the Sharn arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Nichole. At [Zilspar Farm](/places/zilspar/) the trio finally extracted [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s memory with the [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) Lens and watched the recording of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born: [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) tried to kill the newborn and stopped only at his mother's scream, and a glowing diamond showed at the base of Uriel's skull. [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) revealed that she, once Fema Nolan, had resurrected Uriel and was the reason for the diamond. The trio chose not to broadcast the memory, made three copies, and went on to break into the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/) beneath Sharn.

> As Uriel turned away, the ArcEye caught a glimpse of a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull.

## Summary

The morning after defeating [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), the trio and [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) regrouped in their lower-city safe house. [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) chose to leave Sharn with the rescued Cogborn, heading toward [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/). With their numbers reduced, the group reviewed everything they had uncovered and decided their next step was clear: return to [Zilspar Farm](/places/zilspar/), reconnect with their allies, and finally extract [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s memory using the [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) Lens.

[Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) teleported the group to the farm. [Donna](/people/donna/) reported that [Sister Nora](/people/sister-nora/) was refusing to eat, and she told them about [Lorian](/people/lorian/) — the hot-headed captive they had rescued from Malrik — and that he had been training with [Izaak](/people/izaak/) and [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) and he is eager to join the rebellion. Soon after, [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) led everyone into the private [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) room for the memory extraction.

Grady's memory was pulled successfully. Donna and Grady stepped out, and Jessica teleported in with Lorian. After Lorian left, the trio waited with Jessica and Zero-One for Richard's arrival, debating what to do with Sister Nora and whether the memory they were about to view should ever be shown publicly. When [Richard](/people/richard-blaze/) arrived, they watched the recording of the night [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) was born — and saw the truth Esther had feared.

The ArcEye feed revealed [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) collapsing into sudden labor on her penthouse balcony while [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) stood motionless, watching. Uriel eventually delivered the baby and examined him closely. Then, with a cold, deliberate motion, she raised her hand over the newborn's face. A sickly green glow spread from her fingertips as the baby's breathing faltered and went still. Dark, vein-like lines formed across his cheek — the mark that would later be known as his "birthmark."

At the sound of Esther's desperate scream for help, Uriel froze. The glow faded. The baby gasped and began crying again as if nothing had happened. As Uriel turned away, the ArcEye caught a glimpse of a glowing diamond embedded at the base of her skull. The memory ended with Uriel lifting her gaze and looking directly at the unseen observer.

Shaken, the group argued over what to do next. Richard revealed he had already arranged to leave Sharn with Calcifer within the next two days, fearing the growing threat of Malrik and Faith's cult, The [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/). Jessica promised that once Richard and his son were safely gone, the Nest, along with Lorian would help the trio investigate and strike at the Zeirs. Though tempted to broadcast the memory to the entire city, the trio ultimately chose to wait. They made three copies and gave one to Jessica for safekeeping.

Zero-One also revealed that years ago, when she was known as Fema Nolan, she was the one responsible for resurrecting Uriel and the reason for the diamond embedded in her skull.

With the memory set aside for now, they turned their attention to the [Forge of Binding](/places/the-forge-of-binding/). Renn believed the entrance lay behind the boarded-up sewer door near the Underhive tunnels. Gemma teleported the group back to the lower city, where they broke through the old boards and descended into the forgotten dungeon beneath Sharn.

They fought through three chambers: a rat-infested entryway, a flooded room filled with infected Cogborn and a young aboleth, and an alien-looking corridor crawling with more infected creatures. During the battle, [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) discovered he was taking only half damage from psychic attacks, experiencing flashes of memory from his time around [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and hearing a familiar tune from LeBeefe's Black Palace.

## Revelations

- **Uriel Qualanthri tried to kill Calcifer at birth**, and stopped only at his mother's scream. The birthmark is the mark of that killing.
- A **glowing diamond** is embedded at the base of Uriel's skull.
- Zero-One was once known as **Fema Nolan**, and she is the one who resurrected Uriel — and the reason for the diamond.
- Uriel looked directly at the unseen observer. She knew she was being recorded.
- [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) takes only half damage from psychic attacks, and the tunnels give him flashes of [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and a tune from LeBeefe's Black Palace.

## Consequences

Three copies of the memory were made and one given to Jessica for safekeeping. Richard revealed he had already arranged to leave Sharn with Calcifer within two days. The trio chose not to broadcast — a decision they reversed four episodes later, on Esther's own balcony, with the city watching.
