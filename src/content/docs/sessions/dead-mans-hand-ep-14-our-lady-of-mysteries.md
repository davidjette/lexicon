**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) and [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about early November 2025 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 14 — Our Lady of Mysteries** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about early November 2025. [Heyu](/people/heyu/) was called before the Factol of the Sororia, and the party left Sigil by plane ticket for occupied Waterdeep, where they came to the gala at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/). The session ended with [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) at the door. What followed is [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/).

## Our Lady of Mysteries

Before the main action, Heyu visited [Our Lady of Mysteries](/places/our-lady-of-mysteries/), the high church of Cypher, her goddess of fate and mysteries. All around the towering church were [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/), the Faceless Sisters, nuns in masks that range from simple veils to solid masks covering the whole face, with tourists and the faithful milling about. The church holds no image of any god or person, only geometric art meant to invoke the infinite complexity of nature and the mind. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Heyu comes to Our Lady of Mysteries](/images/gallery/dmh-mysteries-cathedral.webp)
*Heyu comes to Our Lady of Mysteries.*

She walked to a small altar with a silver candle, a little tray of coins and a sign reading "5g suggested donation". She prayed, and had a vision of [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) turned to stone in terror. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Heyu prays at the small altar with the silver candle](/images/gallery/dmh-mysteries-altar.webp)
*Heyu prays at the small altar with the silver candle.*

Six of the Sororia surrounded her and invited her deeper into the cathedral. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The Faceless Sisters gather around Heyu](/images/gallery/dmh-mysteries-sisters.webp)
*The Faceless Sisters gather around Heyu.*

There she met the Factol, [Brother Montegue](/people/brother-montegue/), an orcish man dressed and styled as a samurai elder. He sat with her over tea and asked her about [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/). He gave her a puzzle box to hold the Deck, and charged her with collecting all the cards in the name of Cypher. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Heyu takes tea with the Factol, the puzzle box between them](/images/gallery/dmh-heyu-and-feng.webp)
*Heyu takes tea with the Factol, the puzzle box between them.*

## The Lady's Circle

[Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), then Mach V, led the party to the Lady's Circle, the largest teleportation circle in Sigil, in the center of a vast public square among hundreds of towers in the Lady's Ward. He held a plane ticket that let him bring everyone through with him, and handed it to the gnome who takes the tickets. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Mach V hands over the plane ticket at the Lady's Circle](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-ladys-circle.webp)
*Mach V hands over the plane ticket at the Lady's Circle.*

## Blackstaff Tower

They appeared in a candlelit room in Blackstaff Tower in Waterdeep, in the evening, and startled an elderly wizard. He handed them papers: visas good for twenty-four hours in the city. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The party arrive in Blackstaff Tower and startle the old wizard](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-blackstaff-tower.webp)
*The party arrive in Blackstaff Tower and startle the old wizard.*

The streets of Waterdeep, once a rich and bustling city, were depressed. Stores were boarded up, people looked dejected, and heavily armored knights with purple banners and plumes patrolled on horseback. It was autumn. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Lenore, Mach V and Rodriel walk the occupied streets of Waterdeep](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-waterdeep-streets.webp)
*Lenore, Mach V and Rodriel walk the occupied streets of Waterdeep.*

## The gala

Tiffany House is a large, old, well-appointed mansion. A black-tie fundraiser was under way in its front courtyard, lit by hundreds of candles and magic lights, behind a low brick wall, a fence and a gate. A mounted knight stood outside, two inattentive knights kept the gate, and a maitre d' took invitations. A band played inside. On a low balcony over the party a head table was set for [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/), [Phillip Klaxon](/people/phillip-klaxon/) and Phillip's son [Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/). <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Mach V and Maverick look over the gala from the street](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-approach.webp)
*Mach V and Maverick look over the gala from the street.*

Mach V and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) said they were only there to deliver something. Lenore and [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) posed as delivery persons and mechanics, their cargo being Mach V and Johnny. By the scullery Lenore showed an attendant a bill of sale, which he read closely, while Shadowhigh eyed the high rooftops for vantage points. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Lenore shows the bill of sale at the scullery door](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-delivery.webp)
*Lenore shows the bill of sale at the scullery door.*

Heyu transformed herself into a halfling noblewoman, and she and [Maverick](/people/maverick/) went in as guests in fine clothes and each took a goblet of brandywine. [Rodriel](/people/rodriel/) stood awkwardly behind them, posing as their bodyguard. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Maverick and Heyu, as a halfling noblewoman, among the guests, with Rodriel behind them](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-guests.webp)
*Maverick and Heyu, as a halfling noblewoman, among the guests, with Rodriel behind them.*

Across the party Shadowhigh saw a beautiful maid flirting with a corpulent cattleman among the guests. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

General Varkus, high commander of the Purple Dragon Knights, was lauded by the guests as he rose behind the head table and raised his arm to silence the crowd before his speech. Phillip Klaxon, a minor lord and a sycophant, and Darius, an unwilling prince, stood on either side of him. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![General Varkus raises his arm for silence, Phillip and Darius beside him](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-varkus-rises.webp)
*General Varkus raises his arm for silence, Phillip and Darius beside him.*

## Inside the house

The party crept through the back halls of the mansion and examined a huge tapestry that lines an entire hall. It holds portraits of the owners of the house arranged in a long family tree, and their looks are of subtle fear and pleading. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The party study the tapestry of the house's owners](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-tapestry.webp)
*The party study the tapestry of the house's owners.*

In the kitchens, busy with dwarves and hobbits cooking and slinging plates, Phillip examined Mach V, who was being delivered to him, for his features and quality. Mach V was stoic and spoke respectfully, and Darius stood by, a little dejected. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Phillip Klaxon looks Mach V over in the kitchens while Darius waits](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-phillip-examines-mach.webp)
*Phillip Klaxon looks Mach V over in the kitchens while Darius waits.*

In Darius's private chambers Mach V and Darius sat at a small table over chess. Mach V was winning, and explained that Darius should take his advice. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Mach V and Darius at chess](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-chess.webp)
*Mach V and Darius at chess.*

Mach V, Lenore, Shadowhigh and Darius then crawled on their bellies past a large four-poster bed in a lantern-lit state room, Darius in the lead. A single high-heeled foot showed what they were sneaking by, and a pair of cowboy boots stood on the floor beside the bed. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Darius leads Mach V, Lenore and Shadowhigh past the bed](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-past-the-bed.webp)
*Darius leads Mach V, Lenore and Shadowhigh past the bed.*

## The waiter

Mach V flung open a door to the hallway. A waiter stood there in a caterer's uniform and apron: strong, beautiful and menacing, with blond hair, a vicious scar across his throat, and the whole top left quarter of his head scarred and hairless. Behind him the people in the tapestry had devil's horns. It was Jeremiah, and he had revenge in his eyes. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Jeremiah, as a waiter, at the door](/images/gallery/dmh-tiffany-jeremiah-at-the-door.webp)
*Jeremiah, as a waiter, at the door.*
