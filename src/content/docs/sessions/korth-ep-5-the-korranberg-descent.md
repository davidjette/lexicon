**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 5 · DM: Dave**

Korth Ep 5 — The Korranberg Descent is the fifth session of the Korth arc of Campaign 2, The Unforeseen Strikes Back, run by Dave. Deirdre Moro's files led the trio to **Korranberg**, a one-road mining town in the foothills of the King's Forest and once home to the [Deeptempura Clan](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/); Digma reported at the same time that Deeptempura descendants were returning to the same mines. Riding out in their disguises, the trio found the town unwilling to speak about an incident at the Foundry three years earlier, and were attacked at the mine entrance by the orc warband of [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/). [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was killed and raised with a diamond set in his skull, losing an eye; Kul was captured, the mine entrance secured, and a carved staircase found leading down.

## Summary

Cleaning the apartment, the trio learned that Deirdre Moro had been a contract lawyer for the Empire — a post granting her and her assistant wide mobility through the Capitol and its territories. Among her files were maps, client lists and old work orders referencing **Korranberg**, a one-road mining town in the foothills of the King's Forest, once home to the [Deeptempura Clan](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/), deep dwarves conquered and enslaved by the Empire years ago. [the Korranberg Foundry](/places/the-korranberg-foundry/), now abandoned, sat connected to old rails and mine networks.

Around the same time, Digma contacted the trio with news from the Mror Holds: descendants of the Deeptempura Clan had recently visited Chief Yellowmeat, stating they were returning to their ancestral mines in Korranberg, suggesting dwarves might already be heading toward the same location the trio was investigating.

The trio rode out of the Capitol in their disguises. The town was quiet and wary; locals refused to speak about an "incident" at the Foundry three years earlier, and rumours of Orc activity circulated. At the mine entrance Orcs sounded a war horn and attacked. The trio cut down nearly twenty of them before [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) was surrounded and struck down by an Orc captain called [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) formed a ring around his body and tore through the rest, knocked the captain unconscious, and Eric filled the deep gash in Dario's skull with a diamond and cast *resurrection*. Dario came back — at the cost of an eye.

## Revelations

- Korranberg was a **Deeptempura** town before the Empire enslaved the clan and took the mines.
- Something happened at the Foundry **three years ago** — the same year Sharn fell — and the townsfolk will not name it.
- Deirdre Moro's credentials give her standing to enter almost any Imperial facility.
- Braga Kul's warband has taken the abandoned tunnels, and the Empire has not reclaimed them.

## Cast

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) · [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) · [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) (killed and raised) · [Braga Kul](/people/braga-kul/) (captured) · Digma · Chief Yellowmeat, in report.

## Consequences

Dario returned from death with a resurrection diamond in his empty socket — the injury he carries for the rest of the arc. As the trio rested, Gemma freed two captives from a nearby cell. She then burned every body they had accumulated, the real [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) and Graham Cracker included, and threw the ash into the canyon. With the mine entrance secured, a carved staircase led down.
