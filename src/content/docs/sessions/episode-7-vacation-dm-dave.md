**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 7 · DM: Dave · Report filed 12 Sep 2020**

**Episode 7 "Vacation" DM: Dave** is the seventh session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, filed 12 Sep 2020. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) invited the party to the coastal town of Aeire in Valenar as bait for the rebels holding [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) and a further tablet; LeBeefe was captured, the party stormed and burned the rebel base in the Dragon Watch Isles, and Duin and [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) escaped.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

[Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) invites LeBeef, Locke and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) to the coastal town of Aeire in Valenar. Once there Uriel springs a surprise on the group, stating that her assistant told her the location of [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) and Rebels plus another possible tablet, so Uriel fed her assistant info to get back to the rebels that LaBeefe would be in Aeire. A group of rebels then capture LeBeefe. Esther, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and Uriel follow them off the coast to a secret rebel base in the Dragon Watch Isles.

Once there LaBeefe, in miniature form, fights his way through the base and the rest of the crew fight their way in, taking out almost all of the people in the base and burning the base down. Gabriel Duin was there, as was [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), but they got away. The group flee by boat and come away with many treasures.

## Revelations

- Before arriving in Aeire, Locke and Esther were stationed at a dig site which is a possible location of another tablet located in the Blade Desert.
- A rebel map, a gnome tablet and Esther's new lightsaber is found among other treasures.
- Gabriel Duin and Lady Glow escape. "Confirming" Duin to be a rebel.
- There was a monk who used a "force" power similar to Esther.
- LeBeefe has a dream that he is somehow connected to the tablets.

> Gabriel Duin and Lady Glow escape. "Confirming" Duin to be a rebel.

## Consequences

Uriel used her own staff as bait and burned a seven-year deep-cover operation to the waterline without knowing it — the grievance [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) brings to the throne room two episodes later. The party left the Dragon Watch Isles rich, with a gnome tablet they were about to lose to a pirate, and with Duin "confirmed" a rebel on evidence that was exactly backwards.
