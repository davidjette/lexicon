**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 6 (a&b) · DM: Nico · Report filed 11 Sep 2020**

LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

Episode 6 (a&b) "6 Months Later / The Tablet" DM: Nico is the sixth session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, run by Nico and reported on 11 Sep 2020. It opens six months after the alliance with the Children of Winter was carried to the council in Korth, with all four player characters installed in offices in the new city of [Newham](/places/newham/), and follows their reunion at the Lightning Rail Hotel on the last day of The Rejuvenation, where Emperor [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) set them to recover four [ancient stone tablets](/items/ancient-stone-tablets-4/). In the passageways beneath Newham they fought ancient mechanical lizards and an old, sick silver dragon, and recovered both halves of the first tablet.

## Summary

Six months have passed since [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) met with the council of 12 in the Imperial Capital Korth, bringing them news of a new found alliance with the Children of Winter, the Dark druids of the Eldeen Reaches. In that time there have been many changes:

**LeBeefe** - New Ham became the bustling city of [Newham](/places/newham/), with [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) as Mayor of the new city. LeBeefe spends most of his free time gambling at the new casino in town [The Clamshell Casino](/places/the-clamshell-casino/) and with his girlfriend [Ova](/people/ova-santoro/).

**Uriel** - The Hospital that Uriel founded in Newham has grown and thrived into a medical research and educational facility. She heads the department known as the University of Newham Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases AKA, U.N.D.E.A.D. There she continues to research the undead and discovers that the drug Embrace when used on F.Lighter slows down the decomposing process.

**Locke** - [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) now has a semi-permanent post in Newham as Director of the Rekkenmark Academy, Newham Anex which consist of Director Lock Pierce, his secretary a young woman named Tessa Moongrove and some dig crew. His group worked on uncovering a series of ancient passageways seeming to lead away from the city which were uncovered during new construction.

**Esther** - Esther Crona spent the first month back in the Eldeen Reaches, traveling with Imperial Council member, [Minister Henry Heighrick](/people/henry-heinrick/), Chief diplomat and head of the ministry of diplomacy. There they finalized the Alliance treaty with the Children of Winter and Dark Queen Ivanka. Back in Karrnath Esther then headed up a team to infiltrate the supposed rebel base in the Shadowmount Forest which was found long abandoned. Then she is left for months without orders to sit in her late masters manor in Korth awaiting assignment. During that time she drinks heavily, talks to her sword and meets with Chef [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/) for sexy times.

Esther is finally given an assignment by Imperial councilor member's Magnolia Steel, Chancellor of the Rekkenmark Academy, and Garth Brooks, Bishop of the Sovereign Host. They sent Esther to meet Uriel, Locke and LeBeef in Newham at the Lightning Rail Hotel on the last day of The Rejuvenation. Their mission; Construction in Newham has unearthed a broken tablet in an ancient language. Believed to be part of one of 4 [ancient stone tablets](/items/ancient-stone-tablets-4/) that foretell the coming of a great time of change in Eberron. Emperor Kaius has taken interest in these tablets and entrusts High Inquisitor, Esther Crona and the team who uncovered the [Fantanya (Hells) Nyel (Bell)](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) to retrieve these tablets.

The first night together the group get drunk at the inn while watching a re-telling of the Mourning, they gamble at the casino and have a run-in with two succubi. The next morning they enter the ancient passageways where the broken tablet was found. There they battle ancient mechanical lizards and a very old and sick silver dragon. They climb out a secret exit to an abandoned shack where they find murdered High Elves killed by odd looking arrows, and they find the other half of the broken tablet.

## Revelations

- 1 of 4 ancient tablets found.
- The Mechanical Lizards are ancient tech never seen before / never recorded. First time seeing robotic intelligence outside [warforged](/lore/warforged/).
- A dragon mark symbol is seen on the floor of the ancient room where the Silver dragon is found.
- LaBeefe's girlfriend Ova has a roommate (name?) who ends up interning for Uriel at the university.
- Between episodes Uriel kills Ova.
- Applying the drug "Embrace" on an undead turned with sex magic once a week prevents it from decomposing.
- The Rejuvenation is a month-long celebration of Emperor [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/) and the anniversary of the end of the Last War. SEE SECTION [A BARD'S TALE](/lore/a-bard-s-tale-the-imperial-version-of-the-mourning/) TO HEAR THE IMPERIAL VERSION OF THE MOURNING.

## Consequences

All four hold office in the one city: a mayor, a hospital director, an academy director and the Emperor's High Inquisitor. The tablets become the party's object of pursuit in place of the bell, and the mechanical lizards are the first sighting of the ancient machine intelligence that returns as the undead knight in [Episode 9 "The Dig Site / Throne Room"](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/).
