**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/), [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about early December 2025 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 15 — The Tiffany House Heist** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about early December 2025. The night of [the Tiffany House heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/), told scene by scene. It follows straight on from [the previous session](/sessions/dead-mans-hand-ep-14-our-lady-of-mysteries/), with [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) at the door.

## The music box

In the front courtyard the bards on stage, a dark-haired woman in amethyst and gold and a man with a lute, unveiled a heavy brass and steel device which they explained was a gnomish retroglockenspielifier: a box-shaped mechanical instrument that plays beautiful, hypnotizing music. As it began to play, the guards and guests were all struck unconscious, the guests falling asleep into their soup. [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/), [Rodriel](/people/rodriel/) and [Heyu](/people/heyu/) shook off its effects. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The bards unveil the retroglockenspielifier](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-the-bards.webp)
*The bards unveil the retroglockenspielifier.*

[Maverick](/people/maverick/) was overcome when the music was unleashed, and hit his head on a table on his way down. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Maverick out cold on the courtyard floor](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-maverick-down.webp)
*Maverick out cold on the courtyard floor.*

## The waiters

The catering staff threw off their aprons and began robbing the unconscious guests, piling jewels and belongings into tablecloths knotted into sacks. One of them noticed Rodriel awake and shouted "get him!" <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![A waiter spots someone awake as the crew strip the sleeping guests](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-waiters-rob-the-guests.webp)
*A waiter spots someone awake as the crew strip the sleeping guests.*

![Battlebeast and Rodriel by the kitchen door](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-here-we-go-again.webp)
*Battlebeast and Rodriel by the kitchen door.*

The bard was the thieves' leader, and stood near the box shouting orders to them. Heyu, invisible, examined the machine on its small wooden stage among the round tables, and the bard did not see her. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Heyu, unseen, examines the music box while the bard gives orders](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-heyu-and-the-machine.webp)
*Heyu, unseen, examines the music box while the bard gives orders.*

The waiters closed with Battlebeast and Rodriel in the courtyard and wounded both severely with dagger strikes to the torso. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The waiters knife Battlebeast and Rodriel](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-courtyard-melee.webp)
*The waiters knife Battlebeast and Rodriel.*

## The two angels

In the house, Jeremiah and [Griddy Champagne](/people/griddy-champagne/) unveiled their wings and angelic weapons: for Jeremiah a glowing white-grey sword, and for Griddy an ebony mace and a rope of entanglement. Her wings are black and feathered, and she fought in ebony armor. Jeremiah lunged for the false deck of many things on the end table beside the bed, where Griddy now stood and the nobleman she had been with cowered in his undergarments. She wound him in the glowing rope, tight around the arms and torso, and pulled him back. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Griddy binds Jeremiah in the rope as he reaches for the deck](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-jeremiah-and-griddy.webp)
*Griddy binds Jeremiah in the rope as he reaches for the deck.*

[Lenore](/people/lenore/) lay nearly dead on the floor after a blow from Jeremiah, with Johnny trying to give her first aid. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Johnny tends Lenore](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-lenore-down.webp)
*Johnny tends Lenore.*

[Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) fired her revolver through the door, a hail of thorns flying behind the shot. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Shadowhigh fires through the door](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-shadowhigh-through-the-door.webp)
*Shadowhigh fires through the door.*

[Darius](/people/darius-klaxon/) grabbed the cards. Jeremiah, still wrapped in the rope, headbutted him from behind, and Darius fell to the floor out cold with the cards scattering. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Jeremiah, still bound, strikes Darius down and the cards scatter](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-darius-struck.webp)
*Jeremiah, still bound, strikes Darius down and the cards scatter.*

## The kitchens and the carriage

Shadowhigh, Lenore, on her feet again and bloodied, and Johnny ran through the kitchens toward the fight in the courtyard, past the rest of the staff lying unconscious. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Shadowhigh, Lenore and Johnny run through the kitchens](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-through-the-kitchens.webp)
*Shadowhigh, Lenore and Johnny run through the kitchens.*

Outside the kitchen door two getaway drivers stood beside an empty horse-drawn carriage in a side alley. Shadowhigh, lying prone in the kitchen doorway, shot the legs out from under both. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Shadowhigh takes aim at the two drivers from the kitchen door](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-the-drivers.webp)
*Shadowhigh takes aim at the two drivers from the kitchen door.*

## RUIN

[Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), then Mach V, confronted the nobleman in his underwear and threatened him, with Darius, awake again, looking on intimidated. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Mach V holds the deck over the cowering noble as Darius watches](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-mach-and-the-noble.webp)
*Mach V holds the deck over the cowering noble as Darius watches.*

The corpulent noble was then strapped to the bed again. Mach V leaned over him holding the false deck and forced him to draw the first card. It was a true, gold-embossed card of RUIN, with the image of a sword through a crown and a flame. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The noble draws RUIN](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-ruin.webp)
*The noble draws RUIN.*

## The end of the fight

In the courtyard Rodriel smote a waiter with holy light while Battlebeast lay wounded and unconscious on the ground. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Rodriel smites a waiter above the fallen Battlebeast](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-rodriel-smites.webp)
*Rodriel smites a waiter above the fallen Battlebeast.*

The last of the robbers fled, and the nobles began to wake. Phillip Klaxon congratulated Mach V, and farther off, near the gate, [General Varkus](/people/peterbilt-varkus/) put a hand on Darius's shoulder. They had succeeded, and had impressed the General. Griddy, in evening wear again, looked on, concerned. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Phillip Klaxon praises Mach V; by the gate, Varkus stands with Darius](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-aftermath.webp)
*Phillip Klaxon praises Mach V; by the gate, Varkus stands with Darius.*

In the aftermath Griddy, in her seductress's dress, gave Battlebeast a long look in a study of the house. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Griddy Champagne and Battlebeast in the study](/images/gallery/dmh-heist-griddy-and-battlebeast.webp)
*Griddy Champagne and Battlebeast in the study.*
