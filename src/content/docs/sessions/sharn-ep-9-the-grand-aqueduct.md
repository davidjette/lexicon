**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 9 · DM: Nichole**

<small>Not to be confused with [Korth Ep 9](/sessions/korth-ep-9-the-death-warrant-and-the-ringer/), or with Campaign 1's [Episode 9](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/). Three sequences, three different sessions.</small>

**Sharn Ep 9 — The Grand Aqueduct** is the ninth session of the Sharn arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Nichole. Disguised against [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) attention, the trio crossed the [Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) with [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/), picked up [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/) and [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) at the [Gear Street Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/), and descended toward the [Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/). They destroyed a [Drain Snake](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) patrol in the lower levels and found on the bodies the first evidence tying the gang to [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/).

## Summary

The trio began the morning in their [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) apartment with [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/), still in hiding after [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s appearance at the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/). Disguised to avoid [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) attention, they crossed the [Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/) and made their way to the [Gear Street Markets](/places/gear-street-markets/), where [Doppler Klink](/people/doppler-klink/) and [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) were waiting for them at Doppler's market stall.

With the group assembled, they descended into the aqueduct's lower levels, following Snodgrass toward the undercity routes that lead to the [Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) and Queen B. Deeper inside the aqueduct, they were confronted by a patrol of [Drain Snake](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) thugs. A fight broke out, and the party defeated the gang.

Among the bodies, they discovered a clue: one of the Drain Snakes was wearing a [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) Security jacket, and a dead BioTec Security agent lay nearby with his badge still intact.

The episode ended at the entrance of a massive drain tunnel leading further into the undercity, the primary route toward the Underhive and the next step in negotiating with Queen B.

## Revelations

- The Drain Snakes are not merely a gang. One of them is wearing a [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) Security jacket, and BioTec agents are dying in the same tunnels.
- The Grand Aqueduct is the seam between the Lower City and the Underhive, and it is passable if you have a guide.
- Doppler Klink and Snodgrass are willing to go underground with the trio — the beginning of a crew rather than a trio.

## Consequences

The BioTec jacket became the first physical thread between the corporate conspiracy above and the gang war below — a thread that put a name, Edwin, on the conspiracy two episodes later. The trio ended the session on the threshold of Blair's territory with the Cogborn evacuation still unpurchased.
