**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 8 (a&b) · DM: LaMont · Report filed 12 Sep 2020**

**Episode 8 (a&b) "Pirate Joseph Roberts" DM: LaMont** is the eighth session of Campaign 1, [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), run by LaMont and reported on 12 September 2020. At sea after the fight against the rebels in the Dragon Watch Isles, the party were taken by [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), "The Collector", stripped of their treasure, gear and the gnome tablet, and marooned on a small island. They explored tunnels beneath it while an invisible creature drained their memories, and were retrieved a day later by Roberts, who had sold the tablet, returned everything but their coin, set them to gather information on the [Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/), and put them ashore at the coastal town of Aeire.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

Still at sea and right after the fight against the rebels in the Dragon Watch Isles, the group is surrounded by giant ships led by the dread [Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) aka "The Collector" who is known to collect secrets. Roberts forced the crew to surrender all their treasures and gear including the new found gnome tablet. The crew fought back but in the end they were stripped of their treasures and gear and left stranded on a small island to "await his return".

While on the island the group discovered a series of underground tunnels. As they explore the tunnels they are stalked by an invisible being who continues to drain memories and intelligence from the group. The group was able to escape the tunnels and gained a level of power by touching an odd stone wall. A day later Joseph Roberts returned stating he had sold the gnome tablet. He returned the group's items and gear besides the coin, and wished for them to gather info on the [Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) council members for him. He dropped us off at the coastal town of Aeire.

## Revelations

- Joseph Roberts wants us to gather info on the Imperial Assembly (council members).
- Roberts seems to have background information on [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and seems to know him personally from a past that Locke doesn't seem to remember.
- An invisible creature attached itself of LaBeefe, looking to suck the info out of old beings.

## Consequences

The gnome tablet was sold out from under the Empire, and the party owed a pirate a favour. The creature riding LeBeefe is stripped off him and killed by [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/) in the throne room. Roberts himself marries [Blair](/people/blair/), the Queen of the Underhive and LeBeefe's own daughter, and is still at large a generation later: his likeness is in the arrest file handed to [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), under her Ministry of Law cover, in Korth.
