**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 8 · DM: Nichole**

**Sharn Ep 8 — R.U.S.T.** is the eighth session of the Sharn arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Nichole. Hunted as terrorists after the fight outside the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/), the trio fought at [Breakers Pit](/places/breakers-pit/), where [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) won an invitation from the masked Queen B, and were then taken through a tunnel beneath [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/) to the abandoned sub-transit control room, where they met [Preacher](/people/preacher/), leader of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) He set out the Cogborn crisis and the terms [Blair](/people/blair/) of [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) had offered for an evacuation route to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/).

## Summary

The trio began the night in their [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) safehouse, recovering from the violent clash outside the [Sharn Library](/places/the-sharn-library/) in the upper city. As they prepared for their evening fight at [Breakers Pit](/places/breakers-pit/), a breaking broadcast from the Sharn News Network identified a silver dragonborn, a red-haired tiefling, and a human man in short-shorts as suspects in a "coordinated terrorist attack." With the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) now hunting them, the trio disguised themselves before stepping into the streets.

At Breakers Pit, [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) entered the fighting ring and defeated Biff, earning a Feather Token and the attention of the mysterious hooded and masked woman, Queen B. Impressed by his skills in the pit, she invited the trio to seek her in the [Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), hinting that the aqueduct would be their first landmark.

After the fight, the trio met with [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) and [DJ Rust](/people/dj-rust/), who escorted them to [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/). There they reunited with [Kriss Kross](/people/kriss-kross/) and [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/). Renn explained that Lord Commander [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) had arrived at the library after their escape, questioned witnesses and left, raising fears she may have followed Renn to the Lower City.

Kriss and Misty then revealed a hidden tunnel beneath the shop, leading the group deep into the abandoned lightning Rail Sub-Transit Station control room beneath Sharn. There, the trio met [Preacher](/people/preacher/), leader of [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) (Resistance Under Sharn's Tyranny).

Preacher explained that he was raised in [the Cogs](/places/the-cogs/), and that he had lived his entire life among the [Cogborn](/species/the-cogborn/). Years ago, when the Lord Commander outlawed all [Warforged](/lore/warforged/) in Sharn, most fled to [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/) and the [Mournlands](/places/the-mournland/). Preacher stayed hidden in the city with his family; Misty, whom he raised as a daughter while the rest of his people escaped. Now he has returned to the tunnels not to fight for Sharn, but to save the Cogborn who were left behind.

He told the trio the truth of the crisis: the Cogborn are trapped, the Iron Veil is tightening its grip on the Cogs, and time is running out. [Blair](/people/blair/) the "Queen" of the Underhive has offered a deal; clear the [Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/) from the undercity tunnels, and she will open the Underhive routes needed to evacuate the Cogborn to Gorgonhorn.

## Revelations

- Preacher was raised in the Cogs and has lived his whole life among the Cogborn. When the Lord Commander outlawed all Warforged in Sharn, most fled to Gorgonhorn and the Mournlands; he stayed hidden, and raised Misty as his daughter.
- The Cogborn left behind are **trapped**, and the Iron Veil is tightening its grip on the Cogs.
- [Blair](/people/blair/), "Queen" of [the Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/), has made an offer: clear the Drain Snakes from the undercity tunnels and she will open the Underhive routes needed to evacuate the Cogborn to Gorgonhorn.
- Queen B and Blair are the same woman.

## Consequences

The trio acquired the arc's second faction alliance and its first humanitarian obligation. The evacuation of the Cogborn — through the Drain Snakes, the Spine and the sealed Chamber Room — would occupy the next four episodes and buy them the Underhive as a permanent ally.
