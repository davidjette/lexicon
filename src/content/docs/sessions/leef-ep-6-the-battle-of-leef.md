**Leef arc · Episodes 6-8 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 6 — The Battle of Leef** is the sixth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL, and the first of three sessions, Eps 6-8, played on 5, 6 and 12 September 2025. Ep 6 opened with a crawl, introduced Orangemeet and took the party through a swamp. Ep 7 reached the Battle of Leef and ended in the middle of it. Ep 8 resumed the battle, which JL had set to close the campaign's first arc; after one further session the party went west to [Newham](/places/newham/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-05; 2025-09-06; 2025-09-07; 2025-09-12; 2025-09-25)</small>

## Summary

**Leef Ep 6 (5 September 2025).** The session opened with a Star Wars-style text crawl written by JL. During play JL introduced **Orangemeet**, with a portrait. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-05)</small> [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) came up at the table, and Nico said that Esther lives in [Sharn](/places/sharn/). The next morning Nico expanded on it: Esther "prob goes back and fourth" between Sharn and [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s old mansion in [Korth](/places/korth/), "but her main military hub is in Sharn and her Husband and son live there in the upper city." The party crossed a swamp, and Nico's account of it is "We were definitely hallucinating in the swamp". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small>

The road to the dwarves beyond the Ironroot Mountains, begun in [Leef Ep 5](/sessions/leef-ep-5-the-belly-of-the-beast/), ran into the swamp where the Colonel's contact, the old dwarven shaman [Yellameet](/people/yellameet/), waited. On that road a fox caught in a trap asked to be saved; running off, it said "Knew your father I did", and the wind blew the word *Kitsune*. The dwarves' warning afterwards was "Don't trust the fox". <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

As the party approached the dwarves, visions of imperial soldiers slaughtering dwarves filled the sky around them, and a voice demanded how they dared approach the holy place after all they had done to its people. Yellameet would not treat with outlanders who brought no gift, and sent them to climb the tree of wisdom for a fruit from its top. The tree was a gulthias tree, and its fruit was poisonous; Yellameet taught that even the dangerous may be useful, since the fruit fights infection. His terms for an alliance were four: the rebels would preserve dwarven autonomy and the sanctity of the dwarves' homelands; the dwarves would serve under no outlander but fight as equals; the alliance would be sealed by marriage to his granddaughter, Orangemeet; and the party would take his gift, an eversmoking bottle. Dario became engaged, and came back toward Leef with a small army of thirty-five dwarves behind him. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Leef Ep 7 (6 September 2025).** The following day's session opened with a second crawl and resumed late in the evening. It reached the Battle of Leef and ended abruptly with the battle still under way. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small> JL wrote the next day: "We will pick it up on Friday in the middle of the Battle of Leef to (possibly?) conclude the first arc of the campaign." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-07)</small>

Soldiers were arresting townspeople door to door; the Colonel and three of his rebels had been taken in the raids. LeBeefe had brought in a Thresher, a giant siege weapon, to tear a path through Leef into the Dwarvenlands, and an admiral who commanded the troops from a great airship. Greenish Hughes held the Colonel and meant to kill him in front of the party, with a speech on the futility of the rebellion and the power of LeBeefe. In the Colonel's cache the party found weapons marked WAMPYR in Infernal, the sign of Blood of Vol defectors angry at Uriel. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Leef Ep 8 (12 September 2025).** The Battle of Leef resumed from where it had stopped. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-07; 2025-09-12)</small> The fighting at Leef was [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/), after which the town named [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) the Heroes of Leef. One more session followed on 19 September; on 26 September the arc moved on, in JL's words "Forward to Newham with the 3 amigos." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-25)</small>

The victory was celebrated with Orangemeet, and the party set up the Falcons' Workshop, a smithy and storehouse, with Reggie, a tiefling, keeping the store and the dwarf Moo Goo and the human Fill at the forge. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

After the victory Grimpledink began to turn. He asked the party to kill him, and made them promise to kill LeBeefe no matter what LeBeefe offered them in gold, power or influence:

> "When you see him at the mouth of hell, you put a blade in his belly and you tell him Grimpledink sent ya."

<small>— Grimpledink, the father of [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/), at Leef.</small> <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes; sources/dave/2026-09-27-leef-jl-notes-rulings.md)</small>

## Revelations

- The fighting that closed the first arc is called **the Battle of Leef**. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-07)</small>
- The dwarves' alliance with the rebellion was sealed by Dario's betrothal to Yellameet's granddaughter Orangemeet, on terms of dwarven autonomy and fighting as equals.
- LeBeefe's forces at the battle included a Thresher siege weapon and an admiral on an airship.
- The party stood at about level 5: JL noted the week before the battle that "Dario can fly at lvl 5." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-01)</small>
- Nico's account of Esther's household: her main military hub is in Sharn, her husband and son live in [the Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/), and she probably still keeps Varyn Crona's old mansion in Korth. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-09-06)</small>
