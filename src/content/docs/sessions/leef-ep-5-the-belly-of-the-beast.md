**Leef arc · Episode 5 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 5 — The Belly of the Beast** is the fifth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played on 29 August 2025, it was the first session in which JL played [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/). The party learned that Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) was building a mind-flayer army to take the Dwarflands with [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s troops, beheaded prisoners, and found a scroll in the files of [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/). The party was on the road to the dwarves, and the files came from the imperial guard post where Hughes was turning captured dwarves into aberrations. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

> We’re in the belly of the beast. LeBeefe is attempting to build a mind flayer army. He’s attempting to take the Dwarflands. And he’s doing it with Esther’s troops!

<small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## Summary

JL's account of the session, posted the next day, put the party "in the belly of the beast", and named three parts of LeBeefe's scheme: a mind-flayer army, a move on the Dwarflands, and Esther's troops. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> Nico wrote that "Gemma likes hanging with the rebel boys". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

The party beheaded prisoners. Dave wrote that "Dario feels no guilt". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

Eric the Cleric entered play. In June JL had described him as "a tax attorney sent by the empire to investigate irregularities in LeBeefe’s accounting", and in July said he would appear "around level 3 maybe" as "part of the rebel storyline". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07; 2025-07-22)</small> After his first session JL wrote: "He’s a coward but also a light cleric, so he hits like a mf." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

The journey was a mission to carry a gift from the rebellion to the dwarves beyond the Ironroot Mountains, a people who had fought the Empire since before there was an Empire; the Colonel's contact was an old dwarven shaman in the swamps, [Yellameet](/people/yellameet/). The route ran east past a statue of Lady Ghallanda, north to an old well, past an imperial guard post and into the swamp. At the guard post [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/) and Inquisition men were using LeBeefe's arts on captured dwarves to turn them into aberrations; among their prisoners was an abducted bard, Grimpledink, slowly turning into a mind flayer, who joined the party. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The party searched the files of Greenish Hughes and found scrolls among them. Dave summed up one of them as "An alternative royal line" and "House of Vol will fall". JL, as DM, added: "Esther’s true loyalties have yet to be revealed." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>

## Revelations

- LeBeefe is attempting to build a mind-flayer army, to take the Dwarflands, using Esther Crona's troops. <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>
- A scroll from Greenish Hughes' files, in Dave's summary: "An alternative royal line" and "House of Vol will fall". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small> See [House Vol](/organizations/house-vol/).
- "Esther’s true loyalties have yet to be revealed." <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>
- At the guard post Greenish Hughes and the Inquisition were turning captured dwarves into aberrations; the bard Grimpledink, their prisoner and the father of [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/), was turning into a mind flayer.
- The currency of the Grand Duchy of Newham is the LeBeefe: "the currency in the Grand Duchy of Newham are LeBeefes." <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-08-30)</small>
