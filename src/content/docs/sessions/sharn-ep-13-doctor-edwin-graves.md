**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 13 · DM: Nichole**

**Sharn Ep 13 — Doctor Edwin Graves** is the thirteenth session of the Sharn arc of The Unforeseen Strikes Back. A divination ritual sends the party to Circuit City on Gear Street, where the shop's owner Edwin Graves is unmasked as the Cog Collector and killed, and the party recover bank records, property deeds, 800 gold, an ArcEye Crystallizer Lens and six severely ill Cogborn captives.

## Summary

After a long night of ushering the Cogborn to safety, [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), [Dario](/people/sir-dario-argentino/), [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/), and [Renn](/people/renn-tal/) teleported back to their lower city safe house and took a much needed rest. The next morning, they made a quick stop at [Hopper's Magic Emporium](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/), where Eric picked up incense for future rituals. From there, they headed to [Gear Street Market](/places/gear-street-markets/) to investigate the Cog Collector and the Cogborn body that fell from a service lift the previous morning.

Following a Divination ritual performed by Eric, the group was guided toward [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/). Inside, the owner took a suspicious interest in their implants before locking the front door and disappearing into the back. The group pushed deeper into the shop and uncovered a hidden backroom complex filled with monstrous experiments and imprisoned Cogborn captives.

A deadly battle followed against [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) and his creations. The crew defeated him and uncovered the truth: Edwin Graves was the Cog Collector, and secretly the private owner of [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/), CircuitCity, the Arcade, and even the apartment building they live in.

Among the evidence they recovered were bank records, property deeds, 800 gold, an [ArcEye Crystallizer](/items/the-arceye-crystallizer/) Lens, and six severely ill Cogborn, who were safely handed off to [RUST](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) Runners for rescue.

The Cog Collector is dead, what does this mean for [Project E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/)?

> The Cog Collector is dead, what does this mean for Project E.D.E.N.?

## Revelations

- **Edwin Graves is the Cog Collector**, and the "E.G." on the lease of the apartment the trio live in.
- One man privately owned BioTec, Circuit City, the Arcade and their apartment block.
- Behind Circuit City's shopfront is a hidden backroom complex of monstrous experiments and imprisoned Cogborn captives.
- Killing the Cog Collector does not answer what Project E.D.E.N. is for.

## Cast

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) · [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) · [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) · [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) · [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/) · [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) (killed) · six severely ill Cogborn, handed to the RUST Runners.

## Consequences

The trio walked out with bank records, property deeds, 800 gold and — decisively — an **ArcEye Crystallizer Lens**, the device they had been hunting since Episode 6. [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/)'s memory could finally be pulled. The Cog Collector was dead, and the question of what Project E.D.E.N. was for was left standing.
