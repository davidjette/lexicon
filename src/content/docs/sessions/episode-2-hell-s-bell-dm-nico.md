**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 2 · DM: Nico · Report filed 11 Sep 2020**

**Episode 2 "Hell’s Bell" DM: Nico** is the second session of Campaign 1, [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), run by Nico and reported on 11 September 2020. The Crimson Sun sent Esther Crona, Uriel Qualanthri and John C. LeBeefe to the old mining camp of Greenbluff to investigate an incident that had killed Empire-owned slaves and driven the survivors mad; there they joined Locke Pierce, an Ancient Relic Specialist of the Rekkenmark tasked with retrieving the [Fantanya (Hells) Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/). The party fought undead and a rebel band also seeking the bell, uncovered a buried room in the mine, and recovered the relic.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

Per [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) leader and [Imperial Assembly](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) council member Haymen Maw, Esther was to travel with [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and LaBeefe to the old mining camp of Greenbluff. An incident took place in the mine taking the lives of a number of Empire owned slaves. Survivors of the incident reported to have gone "mad" but they have described a sound deep within the mines that shook the walls so violently it caused a cave-in. It was believed that the incident may relate to an ancient relic known to have last been seen in this area.

Once there we met with an Ancient Relic Specialist from the Rekkenmark named [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) who is tasked with retrieving the artifact, the [Fantanya (Hells) Nyel (Bell)](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/).

Once in the mine the group battled undead and a group of rebels who seemed to be there for the bell as well. They then uncovered an ancient buried room which housed the Hell's Bell and retrieved it.

## Revelations

- The legend of the Fantanya (Hells) Nyel (Bell) is that a Celestial Queen buried the bell centuries ago. The craftsman who made the bell were ancient elves who used an infernal metal to fuse the bell. The bell emitted horrific tones that woke the dead; those who were close to the bell and heard the ringing went "mad."

## Consequences

About ten years later, during the first meeting of the Council of Sharn, the Nest and the Silver Flame find a golden bell crated inside the Black Dove Hospital and identify it as a replica of the same relic — *"A similar bell had been uncovered thirteen years earlier by the Unforeseen in the Greenbluff mines outside Newham."* <small>(The Sharn Ep 19 summary says thirteen years; Fulcrum's bell file says "twenty years ago" and the rebel-factions page "over a decade ago"; Greenbluff falls about ten years before the [Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/).)</small> Locke Pierce, met here as a relic specialist, has compiled a dossier on the bells by the time he builds the Ruby Nova.
