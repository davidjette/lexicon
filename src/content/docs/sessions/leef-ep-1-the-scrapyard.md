**Leef arc · Episode 1 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 1 — The Scrapyard** is the first session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played on 18 July 2025, it was the first game with the arc's new Eberron characters, [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/). Dario gave the pass phrase of [the Silver Flame](/organizations/the-silver-flame/), and in front of a scrapyard he decapitated the guard Morlo Gint. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18; 2025-07-19)</small>

> "Long night?"
>
> "Means a brighter day."

<small>(Dave, as Dario, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18)</small>

## Summary

Dario came into the arc as a servant of an outlawed faith. JL, as DM, set out the Flame's position before play: "The Sacred Flame are an outlawed cult in the empire now. Membership is punishable by death. They’re also one of the more powerful rebel factions." A servant of the Flame therefore needed a secret identity, a fake job and a fake reason to travel, and a pass phrase to identify fellow Flames. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24)</small> Dario's cover was a journeyman smith called **Flintscale**, "Traveling for the funeral of a friend". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24; 2025-06-26)</small> Holy symbols of the Flame are contraband. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24)</small>

Dave, who plays Dario, described his starting kit as made by Dario himself "from scrounged scraps and tossed out pieces", except the sword, "which is first real quality work, made in secret, named it quicksilver". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-09)</small> Dario also made a masked helmet "so that he can stay anonymous when fighting for justice", rigged so he could use his breath weapon. He is a silver dragonborn, with a breath of ice. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-13)</small>

In the session Dario exchanged the Flame's challenge and answer, "Long night?" and "Means a brighter day." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18)</small>

The session opened on the lightning rail. Dario and Gemma travelled on the same train toward [Leef](/places/leef/) without sitting together, among passengers glad to be leaving Newham, whose curse on its ruler was "Gods-damned LeBeefe, curse his soul." Imperial guards aboard were looking for a female tiefling who matched Gemma's description. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Gemma was coming home, after escaping prison, to her adoptive father Jane Corso and his junkyard. Jane had built the lightning rail as an engineer and been retired after an injury on the job; he stayed loyal to the Empire, prayed for the Emperor, and followed the Newham Falcons, signing off every conversation with "Go Falcons." He sent her to the Cat & Biscuits tavern for a pint while he readied a surprise he had worked on for years, and gave her a tin scimitar with GO FALCONS painted on it. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

At the Cat & Biscuits, a half-full tavern of old soldiers talking about the Last War, taxes and the Falcons' chances, the party heard what had changed in Khorvaire: the Emperor had married a Black Dove priestess named Uriel, his wedding gift to the Empire was the abolition of slavery, and he had been sick and unseen since his wedding day; Sharn was "filthy with inquisition agents"; dwarves were attacking in the east; and rebels held the Eldeen Reaches in the west. Four imperial guards came in looking for Gemma, and an old half-elf veteran in a colonel's uniform, [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/), fought beside the party and sent them off with "Welcome to the rebellion." <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

Back at the junkyard two agents of the Inquisition stood over Jane's body. Their leader told Gemma she had beaten the old man to death and arrested her for it. In his hand Jane held a clockwork amulet he had made for her. Jane's robot was Grill Wizmo, and Gemma's code name in the rebellion became Dawn. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

In front of a scrapyard, Dario decapitated a guard. The guard was named Morlo Gint. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-19)</small> He was a soldier of the Inquisition. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Revelations

- The Silver Flame identifies its own with a challenge and answer: "Long night?" / "Means a brighter day." In the next session Dave gave it as "Long night, means a brighter dawn". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-07-18; 2025-08-08)</small>
- Membership of the Flame is punishable by death, and the Flame is one of the more powerful rebel factions. <small>(JL, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-24)</small>
- Dario is charged "with laying low the undead where ever they are" and "to help and aid the afflicted". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-06-07)</small>
- A second exchange also identifies members of the Flame: "Are you a traveller?" / "I've been everywhere except Karrnath" / "I hear the weather there is lovely this time of year".
- Gemma's adoptive father, Jane Corso <small>(also written Jeyne Korso)</small>, was killed at his junkyard by the Inquisition, who framed Gemma for it.
