**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 14 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 14 — The Listening Post** is the fourteenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 11 September 2026. Still wearing the identity of the dead Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) took [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) into the Imperial listening post beside [the Hall of Submission](/places/the-hall-of-submission/) in [Korth](/places/korth/), where its director, **Chandler**, told her the Empire had broken [Fulcrum](/organizations/fulcrum/) by harvesting the ghost chips of its captured members and was holding [Izaak](/people/izaak/) there. The trio went through the steel door by *dimension door*, killed the station's doctor and carried Izaak out. Gemma sent him and [Bantide Moro](/people/bantide-moro/) to Sharn by *teleportation circle*, and the three sailed overnight to [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/) outside [the Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/). There, in a dream, Gemma's patron Kitsune told her that the heart of the Emperor was a riddle, and that what he wants is the Emperor himself.

> We have him. He is not as useful as we hoped he would be, but his attachments are.

## Summary

Bantide Moro woke in her dead sister's apartment exhausted, on one hit point and without spell slots, and frightened. She would not travel. The Empire believed her dead, and the apartment was the safest place she had for as long as Deirdre's cover held. She had never met [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) and knew only [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/). She told them she was no spy: she stole medical supplies and got them to civilians, reported troop movements and stitched people up. Gemma introduced herself as Foxtale. Bantide already knew and trusted Eric. She had played clarinet in high school, so Gemma wrote out [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/) and left it with her, to pass to anyone else like them; nobody knew whether the notes work sung. Before she slept Bantide gave them the listening post: the guild house across the street from Lexicon Station, beside the Hall of Submission, holding the records that explain what happened to Izaak.

With six hours in Korth before the boat, they went as Imperials, on the reasoning that Deirdre had a live assignment and was entitled to ask an intelligence station about [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/). A neighbour stopped Gemma about Deirdre's cat fouling the stairs and left certain of who she was. Outside the Hall of Submission a Black Dove stepped out of a door, and through it they saw a broken body on a slab, a great ceremonial device and chanting. Bodies turned in that hall walk straight onto a fuelled troop transport bound for the front.

The listening post is an old House Sivis building, given up by the house, run for years as a members-only guild hall for accountants and seized by the Emperor; its brass plate names a guild of surveyors. Eric opened the locked door with his Mark of Passage. Inside, in a long high room of red-cushioned lodge chairs, about ten people worked at map tables and at Sharn-style terminals newer than everything around them. Incoming messages were copied into ledgers, sealed and handed to couriers who either walked out the front door or vanished where they stood, and among the papers lay the remnants of body modifications and brain chips. At the dais stood a director, an officer, an agent and a creature none of them could place, with the head of a hippopotamus, the bearing and uniform of a naval officer and an unfamiliar gun; it grunted, and the agent translated for the director over a globe of the world.

Gemma saw a document headed FOXTALE and slid it into her clutch with an invisible *mage hand*. Eric read the campaign map of the continent: fresh markers, nearly all struck through and dated within the last week, a heavy black X on [Zilspar](/places/zilspar/) and a black circle around [the Korramont](/places/the-korramont/). The terminal recognised Deirdre and gave her the station index, and she pulled the file on the Grand Lodge.

The director came up behind her. His name tag read Chandler: an old man with close-cropped white hair in a dark coat buttoned to the throat. "I am not accustomed to seeing you here; usually I send you the convictions that we need and you obtain them. It is not generally a two-way conversation." Mistaking why she had come, he said he was sorry the file had been hers, told her that her sister was an agent of the enemy, and showed her the evidence on a ghost chip: a point-of-view recording of Bantide collapsing in laughter over a patient she had saved, and a toast to success and fallen comrades in which the hand holding the glass was Izaak's. "These are taken directly from the ghost chip of a known Fulcrum orchestrator," he said. Pressed on the source, he gave up the rest:

> We have been able to harvest the ghost chips from almost every Fulcrum member we have captured, we have a near complete understanding of their organisation, and it is only a matter of time until we kill every single one of them. But we are lucky to have Mr Izaak here in the listening station.

Chandler then changed the subject and invited her to the Ring Cycle, the opera starting back up for one week only, when she returned from her assignment. She called it a date. Behind him was a steel door with a guard on it. Across the room an officer caught Eric reading files sequestered from advocacy, and they left.

In a small park they prepared. Gemma cast *dimension door* twice into the Ring of Spell Storing and Dario attuned to the ring, giving all three a way in and out. Gemma read her own file. FOXTALE had no likeness until it was recently updated to confirm she is a tiefling. It recorded her months as a novice of [the Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/) in the Korth house, her service with [the Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) in Sharn, a trace to the rebellion at [Leef](/places/leef/) and possibly to the disaster at [Newham](/places/newham/), a long section on the assassination of [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/), and her connection to Eric of Sharn from the fall of the city. It ended with a detention team sent to her Korth safe house shortly after she left for Sharn, which found it empty by hours. Gemma concluded that the building needed to burn.

Three *dimension doors* put them past the steel door into a small back room: a death trooper, a white-coated doctor, a black-coated torturer, a conscious man screaming on a rack, and Izaak in traction under a paralytic. His leg had been removed and hung from a hook on the wall, and a large piece of the side of his neck, where the implants had been, was gone. Gemma fed him a healing potion and held her spell, waiting for him to wake, through the torturer's spiked weapon, two blasts from the death trooper's gun and a rifle butt to the head. Dario ran to Izaak and was stunned. Eric cleared the paralytic with *lesser restoration* and put his dagger into the torturer's side: "Locke Pierce sends his regards." Izaak woke screaming, and Gemma's spell took the two of them to the apartment, with orders to Eric to get out, not to be brave, and to get the leg.

The doctor, **Ilse Brandt**, did not surrender. Her first syringe missed Gemma and broke, she called for an alarm the room did not have, and she put a second syringe to the stunned Dario's throat and demanded they drop their weapons. Dario shook off the stun, triggered his haste crystal, ignited Pale Fire and cut her in half. The death trooper grappled him by the head and fired. Eric took the leg from the wall and a hand on Dario's shoulder, and Dario let himself be taken out. They left the torturer alive, the man on the rack unidentified, and witnesses who had heard a rebel invoke [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) by name.

The city went to alarm within minutes. Every piece of Izaak's cybernetics had been removed with no regard for his survival; he had open cranial wounds about seven days old, was poisoned, on one hit point, near death and possibly permanently brain-damaged. Dario's lay on hands cleared the poison. Gemma learned *teleportation circle* and found it needs rare inks worth fifty gold, which nobody had. Bantide screwed the prosthetic back onto the bone shaft, as she had fitted it in the first place, and soldiers reached the stairwell.

Gemma went out as Deirdre for ink and found the coffee vendor she had once worked for, a dwarf in a cowboy hat named **Juan Valdez**, who speaks a Colombian dialect of Dwarvish; coffee could be mimed, ink could not. A checkpoint officer turned her back from a bridge under lockdown. She returned to a squad of stormtroopers with a charge strapped to her door and a ten-second count running, and stalled them, dropping her keys and bending slowly for them, while Dario folded Izaak and Bantide into two bags of holding and Eric disguised himself as a long-haired high elf in cold cream. The search found nothing and the captain apologised to the Star Advocate. On a second run Juan Valdez produced the stationer's ink, and Gemma paid five hundred gold for two castings. Meanwhile a folder came under the door from [Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/), two words on Imperial letterhead: contact me.

Gemma drew the circle around Izaak, who could not be moved, and aimed it at the penthouse in Sharn that had been [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s. The Nest was ruled out because Bantide had never been there, and Zilspar because it is now a war zone. Izaak surfaced as the ink went down. "It was Pierce … Pierce's intelligence. They had some kind of back door with the Empire. I think he gave me up." He learned that Zilspar was already gone and that his mother had been taken, and took it in silence. "Everything is compromised … nothing is safe, that building has to end, you have to do it, now." Gemma's parting words were "don't eat the pomegranate." The circle lit and the two were gone. Dario painted a rug over the ink's permanent stain with Nolzur's Marvelous Pigments, and the apartment was abandoned for good. Minutes after they left by *dimension door* for the city gates, Freyd Merkin arrived at the door with a stormtrooper and found the room empty.

Outside the walls the suburbs were tent cities and garrisons, full of starving people without sanitation, with troops on every road. The river barge to the Summer Palace was a long white-oak vessel of about eighty souls, pilgrims and pensioners in steerage and dignitaries forward under guard, with Imperial supplies for the Summer Palace in the hold; its guards wore pure white. The officer at the gangway declared Dario's forged weapons papers three days expired, which was a lie, and four hundred gold moved her.

The barge was driven upstream overnight by magic, with the sun setting over the King's Forest to the west, where the fighting is. Aboard was [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), in red with a dragon staff and the old Crimson Sun regalia, three years older and much diminished, pressing officers. They overheard him:

> we are running out of time, and if we do not give her something then I think she will be able to convince the Emperor to ring the bells.

He also asked for air cover for the units fighting the Uruk-Hoth in Aundair. The officers answered that it was not up to him, because the Emperor has taken personal control of the airships since Lyrandar died and wants everything protecting [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/).

Also aboard were the Archbishop of Arawai, a well-liked [Sovereign Host](/organizations/the-sovereign-host/) figure whom even the Empire tolerates, wearing a large and obviously magical amulet; **Rotunda Goose**, the parliamentarian from the [Thronehold](/places/thronehold/) summit, who survived it without her left hand and an ear; a very tall masked Black Dove, whom Gemma recognised as the sister she had seen in the Hall of Submission turning corpses into soldiers; merchants bound for the Grand Lodge; nobles with ailments; and a steerage deck of wounded coming to ask the Holy Empress to heal them. At a window stood an elf woman named **Saturnia**, who has lived her whole life in Khorvaire and was going to take the vows of poverty, virtue and chastity and join the Sisterhood, which her mother calls the future of elvendom in the world.

Gemma used her sending stone on Joseph Roberts, accepting a marriage proposal, and got back: "always great to have a future ex, where are you". That night she sent to [Jessica](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) that she was near target with cover kept, and that the listening post needs destroying. Jessica answered that the Nest had received Izaak, who was well, and the Nightingale. Izaak got a codename at last: **Ace**.

They docked at midnight, where Dario's Divine Sense found the guards on the quay undead. A half-orc carriage driver called Pork Broccoli took sixty gold and drove them past an Imperial administration building older than the Empire, a reflecting pond and a triumphal gate. On a hill in the town stands the great hospital and holy complex, once a Sovereign Host cathedral and now the heart of the Black Doves, with a giant statue of [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) at the top of its stair and a floating geometric shape lit in its courtyard. The Grand Lodge is an old hunting lodge on the river, hung with banners of the Kingdom of Karrnath from before there was an Empire. The only room free was the Fieldmaster's Suite at five hundred gold a night, looking south to the Summer Palace wall and the Imperial landing pad; Dario paid fifty platinum and asked to be left alone.

Gemma spent her free *contact other plane* going to sleep, and dreamed the boat empty but for herself and **Kitsune**, wearing the Archbishop's robes and amulet. He asked whether she had come to become a nun; she said she had come to retrieve his gift. He told her she was close, and that if she did not hurry she would have to steal it screaming. The bargain, he allowed, was never an immediate binding agreement, and settled somewhere around the eighth or ninth person she killed with the power he gave her. He has no children, and wants the feeling of a heart living outside his body, with one particular child, which he means to take out of this world entirely. Then he corrected the terms. Gemma had carried a velvet-lined box to bring him the heart of Caius; the heart of the Emperor was a riddle, and what he wants is the Emperor himself, the child, alive or dead. Gemma asked how you can kill something that is already dead, and he told her to think about what would need a great amount of dragon blood. What "the elf witch" had dug into, he said, is some of the oldest magic there is: a way to sire a child "from the bones of the earth, from the seed of existence". Gemma answered that handing him a child that powerful would be handing another bomb to another ally, like Locke Pierce. His last words were that the time is almost near, and to get him what he wants before she rings the bell. <small>Kitsune's account. The Emperor's nature has two other accounts in the Korth arc: LeBeefe's voice named him a vampire in [Korth Ep 11](/sessions/korth-ep-11-the-domain-of-valtrex/), and Master Armhair named him the dragon Viagra in [Korth Ep 13](/sessions/korth-ep-13-the-condemned/).</small>

## Revelations

- The listening post is a former House Sivis building and accountants' guild hall, seized by the Emperor and fronted as a guild of surveyors, across the street from Lexicon Station.
- Bodies are turned into soldiers in the Hall of Submission and loaded straight onto troop transports for the front.
- By Chandler's account the chip of a known Fulcrum orchestrator led the Empire to "a tremendous amount of activity"; it has since harvested the ghost chips of almost every Fulcrum member it has captured, and means to kill every one of them. Izaak was held and tortured at the post. See [the rolling-up of Fulcrum](/lore/the-rolling-up-of-fulcrum/).
- Izaak's own account is that Locke Pierce gave him up: "They had some kind of back door with the Empire."
- The Empire's FOXTALE file connects Gemma to the Black Doves' Korth house, the Nest, Leef, Newham, Cob Cornwell's assassination and Eric of Sharn, and records the detention team that missed her in Korth by hours.
- The Imperial campaign map marks Zilspar with a black X and circles the Korramont. Zilspar is gone, and Izaak's mother was taken.
- Stockton Thar fears that without some concession "she" will convince the Emperor to ring the bells. Imperial officers say the Emperor has taken personal control of the airships since Lyrandar died.
- What Kitsune has been steering Gemma toward is the Emperor himself, the child: the Emperor's unborn heir.

## Consequences

The session ended with the trio as paying guests inside the Grand Lodge, the building [Korth Ep 13](/sessions/korth-ep-13-the-condemned/)'s assignment had sent them to investigate, two hours south of the Ivory Lazaret. Izaak and the Nightingale are safe in Sharn. The Empire's intelligence service holds a file on a callsign it cannot put a face to; its director has invited the Star Advocate to the opera; Merkin's summons is unanswered; and witnesses in the listening post heard Locke Pierce's name.
