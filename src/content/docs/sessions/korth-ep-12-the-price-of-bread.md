**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 12 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 12 — The Price of Bread** is the twelfth session of the Korth arc of The Unforeseen Strikes Back. In it the Inevitables learn that Zilspar Farm has been destroyed and Zero-One taken alive, Sir Dario Argentino destroys two bells in Sharn in a single morning, Eric the Cleric trades the city's airspace to the Hundreds in exchange for food, and the party fly out to Zilspar and destroy the eight E.D.E.N. units left on the ground.

## Summary

[The Inevitables](/organizations/the-inevitables/) returned to Sharn to find Jessica with the worst news of the war: [Zilspar Farm](/places/zilspar/) had been destroyed, [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) taken alive, and [Izaak](/people/izaak/) presumed dead. Research confirmed the method for making [King Corn](/people/king-corn/)'s horn, established that breaking a bell does *not* notify [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), and concluded [KNELL](/items/knell/) is likely unique.

[Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) destroyed two bells in a morning. The first sat crated in the unfinished tower above the hospital, packed with straw by people too frightened to touch it; he cleared the muffling himself and swung with *"may our children forgive us."* The second hung in a small temple of Boldrei in the Lower City, where [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) masked herself as Chancellor [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and promised the crowd nothing would be harmed — and Dario's strike brought the whole tower down through the floor, burying him and turning the crowd against them.

[Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) briefed Eric: the city was out of fuel and, with the farm gone, a month from severe food shortages. Standing near the Eye of Corruption, Eric found it resonated with his psychic power and sharpened him, and used that to help [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) engineers bleed its rising output off as fire for the furnaces. Renn then declared the Eye property of Sharn.

At the hospital, [Zero of Sharn](/people/zero-of-sharn/) convened a meeting with a representative from Sharn of every faith on the continent. [Bofric](/people/bofric/) played the fanfare of the Dawn, and the clergy took up teaching what they named the **Horn of Mourning**, congregation by congregation. Then three warforged arrived under Himmie Joffa, offering to feed a city they calculated was forty-seven days from famine. The price was military and logistical access to Sharn's airspace for the airships of [The Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/). Eric granted it and told Gemma he had handed Locke Pierce the keys, that he knew it was a trick, and that he only hoped they survived Korth in time to take it back.

They caroused and outfitted. Hopper cast *summon dragon* into Eric's Ring of Spell Storing on the understanding that a true name summons that dragon for one hour and then frees it, which they intend to spend on [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/). Doppler Klink built each of them a wand of magic missile at cost and then got to work making Horns. Gemma received more on her cover in Korth as [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), a Star Advocate from the Ministry of Law, with standing to investigate nearly any Imperial facility, and Eric as her paralegal, Graham Cracker.

They flew to [Zilspar Farm](/places/zilspar/) anyway. Eight [E.D.E.N.](/organizations/project-e-d-e-n/) units remained, and they were not what the party fled at level five: self-healing, undead fortitude, articulating on impossible second elbows, bursting into lightning when killed. All eight fell.

> may our children forgive us.

## Revelations

- The evidence at Zilspar shows many Eden soldiers died **defending** the farm, and incendiaries were brought in afterward to burn the buildings over people boarded up inside.
- No sign of Zero-One, [Izaak](/people/izaak/), [Donna](/people/donna/) or [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/). One survivor: a farm hand's boy who gives his name as **Noot Gunray**.
- Breaking a bell does not alert Uriel. The party can work in silence.
- [Jessica Grimpledink](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) has determined that Esther's wedding gift is **both black and red Khyber**, and is fixated on it as a prototype weapon.
- Jessica asks Gemma privately whether [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) is how the Empire found the farm — and says that if they cannot save Zero-One, they must not let her be taken.
- The Chancellor's arithmetic: Locke offered food, the farm was destroyed days later, he offered more for airspace, and any one of those hulls could carry a [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/).

## Consequences

[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) has taken the skies of Sharn — a capital ship settled out of the fog to hand out fruit in orderly lines. The papers blamed the Chancellor for the temple, and a dossier from the Hundreds confirmed no survivors at Zilspar. Ten bells remain, at least one in Korth. The party reached level 12 and intends to hand Noot to someone who can raise him.
