**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 3 (a,b,c) · DM: Dave · Report filed 11 Sep 2020**

**Episode 3 (a,b,c) "Massacre on the Talenta Plains Express"** is the third session of Campaign 1, The Unforeseen, run by Dave and reported 11 Sep 2020. [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) is charged with returning the [Fantanya (Hells) Nyel](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) to the Empire by train out of [New Ham](/places/newham/), with [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) hired to secure it; the train is found to be swarming with rebels, the rebels are defeated, the actor [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) is exposed as a rebel and killed, and the Empire announces that [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/) killed him.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

After retrieving the [Fantanya (Hells) Nyel (Bell)](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) from the Greenbluff mines, [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) is tasked with the safe return of the Bell to the Empire by train. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and LeBeefe are hired to help secure the train and eliminate any threats.

Soon after the train leaves [New Ham](/places/newham/) the group discovers that the train is swarming with rebels. After a long and dangerous battle the group, with the help of some passengers and imperial soldiers, are able to defeat the rebels and make sure the Bell is in the hands of the empire.

## Revelations

- This is the first time we meet ally and up-and-coming Restaurateur [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/).
- [Lady Glow](/people/lady-glow/), a famous singer, is discovered to be a rebel.
- Well known actor, [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/), was discovered to be a rebel. He was killed during the train fight. The empire announced that Lady Glow was the one to kill well loved actor Stamos during a rebel terrorist attack on the train.
- We met Father White and his paladins who helped our group during the train battle. (Father White is a Priest of the Sovereign Host)
- We discovered the where-abouts of a rebel hideout in the Shadow Mount Forest.
- These rebels had an airship!

## Consequences

The Bell reached the Empire, and a lie about who killed [John Stamos](/people/john-stamos/) went out over the Imperial press. Richard Blaze, met here as an up-and-coming restaurateur, becomes Esther's lover, Calcifer's father and part of the Sharn rebellion's inner circle <small>(see [Sharn Ep 4](/sessions/sharn-ep-4-richard-blaze/))</small>. Lady Glow, exposed here, is handed to Uriel as a gift in [Episode 9 "The Dig Site / Throne Room"](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/). Father White's loyalties send the party to New Cyre.
