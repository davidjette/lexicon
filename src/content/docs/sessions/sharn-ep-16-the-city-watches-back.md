**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 16 · DM: Nichole**

Sharn Ep 16 — The City Watches Back is the sixteenth session of the Sharn arc of Unforeseen Strikes Back, run by Nichole. The party helped [Blair](/people/blair/) interrogate a captured [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) soldier, struck a supply deal with [Hopper](/people/hopper/), and collected the [EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) from an Upper City store. Outside their safe house [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/) told [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) that the teleportation chip in her head had been made for him, and left VIP passes to his brother's club; the [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) then attacked and cut the chip out of her.

## Summary

After escaping the collapsing [Forge](/places/the-forge-of-binding/) and returning with [Blair](/people/blair/) to her vault, the trio learned of the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/)'s violent incursion into the [Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/). They assisted Blair in interrogating the sole captive, Brice Peters, a terrified low-ranking soldier who knew little beyond his orders. He confirmed only that the Iron Veil had been sweeping the Lower City aggressively while the Lord Commander was present, following directives far above his station. He also spoke of the pact and how many Sharn Iron Veil were old-time Sharn City Watch guards before they were turned to Iron Veil. Enraged that [Esther](/people/esther-crona/) dared to encroach on her territory, Blair ordered Brice's family brought to her. Before the trio departed, she mentioned her husband, the infamous Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), was coming to Sharn and would want to see them, and she gave them a direct way to contact her.

[Jessica](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) reached out via Sending, arranging a meeting for noon the next day at the farm. With ArcEye screens showing troop activity returning to normal and Esther's ship gone from the city, the trio left the Underhive and returned to their Lower City safe house for a long rest.

The next morning, they visited [Hopper's Magic Emporium](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/). After revealing they now held the deeds to both his shop and the mysterious Arcade next door, [Hopper](/people/hopper/), thrilled at the chance to expand, struck a major trade deal with them, providing supplies, magic items, and bulk goods intended to support the rebellion. While there you also learned about the lawyer, a large man named [Candy Crush](/people/candy-crush/), who would represent the previous deed holder.

From there, the trio teleported to the farm. In their meeting with [Richard](/people/richard-blaze/) and Jessica, Richard told them about Esther's unexpected sunrise visit, how she had been affectionate and spent the early morning with them. Esther told him how she had been searching for something deep beneath the city and had finally found it, and once she did, it was like a veil had been lifted. She left for [Crona's Wall](/places/cronas-wall/). Richard left the meeting in a hurry as he had plans to leave with his son for their lake house within the hour.

After Richard left, Jessica explained the [EBT-7 Sub-Transit Repeater Node](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/): a salvaged device capable of hijacking Sharn's ArcEye live feeds and broadcasting whatever the trio chose across the entire city. She asked them to retrieve it from an upper city store, The Conduit Exchange before returning to [The Nest](/organizations/the-nest/) to meet with [Lorian](/people/lorian/) and plan their next steps.

Leaving [Renn](/people/renn-tal/) back at the farm, the trio teleported to their [Upper City](/places/the-upper-city/) safe house. They walked to The Conduit Exchange, where they picked up the packaged EBT-7. As they left, a ragged man approached, claiming [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/) wanted to speak with them. Sensing a trap, they refused and returned to the safe house. <small>The site recap spells the name Cobb.</small>

Waiting outside the door was [Hallorn d'Lyrandar](/people/hallorn-d-lyrandar/). He explained that the teleportation chip in [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/)'s head had been crafted by [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) for him as a gift to [Faith](/people/faith/). When it was stolen, he had it tracked. Its teleportation pings had bounced across the city including under his brother's club, [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/) the night it was broken into. He even noted it was shown pinging outside the city quite a lot, but only when the trio teleported to the Upper City safe house this afternoon did his people finally pinpoint its exact location. He left them with VIP passes to his brother [Malrik](/people/malrik-zeir/)'s club, [The Vault Underground](/places/the-vault-underground/), saying Malrik "invites" them, and reminded Gemma the chip was never truly hers.

Then the [Children of Ember](/organizations/the-children-of-ember/) struck.

Cultists seized Gemma, restrained her, and violently removed the teleportation chip, destroying it on the spot. The trio fought back, killing two and knocking the third unconscious. They dragged the surviving attacker inside the safe house for interrogation. With the EBT-7 secured, danger closing in, the next phase of their plan waiting for them at The Nest.

## Revelations

- The Iron Veil are bound to Esther by a **pact**, and many Sharn Iron Veil were old-time Sharn City Watch guards before they were turned.
- Gemma's teleportation chip was crafted by [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) for Hallorn d'Lyrandar as a gift to [Faith](/people/faith/). It was never truly hers, and its pings had been tracked across the city all along.
- Esther found what she was searching for deep beneath the city — and once she did, it was like a veil had been lifted.
- The [EBT-7](/items/the-ebt-7-sub-transit-repeater-node/) can hijack Sharn's ArcEye live feeds and broadcast whatever the trio choose across the entire city.
- Hallorn's parting gift is VIP passes to [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/)'s club: Malrik "invites" them.

## Consequences
