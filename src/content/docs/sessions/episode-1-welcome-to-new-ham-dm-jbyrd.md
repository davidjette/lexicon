Episode 1 "Welcome to New Ham" DM: JByrd is the opening session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, run by JByrd and reported on 11 Sep 2020. [Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) Inquisitor [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) were sent to the village of [New Ham](/places/newham/) over a delinquent delivery of Empire-owned slaves. They established that Magister [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) had sold the slaves into the Demon Waste and covered it with a story of plague, killed a cell of Black Doves in the hideout he had named as a rebel base, recovered a [Black Box](/items/black-box/) radiating necromancy, and agreed among themselves on a false account of the deaths.

## Summary

[Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) Inquisitor [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) travel to a the small village of [New Ham](/places/newham/) to investigate the delinquent delivery of Empire owned slaves, as well as advise on Magister LeBeefe's loyalty to empire, assess the threat level of a possible outbreak and determine if there are rebel agents in New Ham.

Magister LeBeef has been selling slaves to private investors in the Demon Waste for triple the amount that the Empire is paying. To cover the missing shipment of slaves to the Empire LeBeef claimed that an unknown disease spread through the slave pins killing them all, and as a "precaution" the bodies were burned. LeBeef claimed that a Thieves hideout in OldHam was a base for rebels who were responsible for this disease.

Upon infiltration of this hideout it was discovered that the hideout was once a thieves den but the current occupants were in fact members of the Black Doves, led by a man named Adulfphus Mudge aka "Schmendrick". Adulfphus was sent by the head of the Black Doves (and Uriel's boss), [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), to investigate the disease. Inside the hideout Esther, LeBeef and Uriel were attacked by the Doves along with 2 undead. Some members of the Black Doves were killed in battle including Adulfphus. Once it was determined that the occupants were Black Doves, Uriel took control of the remaining Doves in the building and discovered a [Black Box](/items/black-box/) that radiated Necromancy magic. The "story" they agreed on was that these dead Doves were already undead when they arrived.

## Revelations

- Magister LeBeefe promised Uriel and Esther a cut in the slave trade to the Demond Waste as long as they stayed quiet about what he was doing.
- The Black Box is a necromancy tool of some kind and caused the undead in the old thieves den.
- Uriel begins to think that Gabriel Duin may be a rebel agent.
- Uriel takes over the old thieves' hideout and turns it into a hospital and research facility.
- LaBeef showed Uriel &amp;amp; Esther a vision of the end times.

## Consequences

The Empire was told the account its agents agreed on rather than what happened. Uriel walked away with the [Black Box](/items/black-box/) and with the hideout, which she converted into the hospital that becomes the University of [Newham](/places/newham/) Department to Eradicate and Analyze Diseases. Her suspicion of Gabriel Duin, formed here, is not resolved until [Episode 9 "The Dig Site / Throne Room"](/sessions/episode-9-the-dig-site-throne-room-dm-nico/).
