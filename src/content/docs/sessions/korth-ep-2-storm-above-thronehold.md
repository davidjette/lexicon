**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 2 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 2 — Storm Above Thronehold** is the second session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave. Supreme Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) called for the death of Chancellor [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) on the ArcEye broadcast of the Thronehold Summit; the masked Empire guards proved to be undead loyal to Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and turned on Vex; Vex called his warships down on the Summit grounds; and the trio escaped aboard one of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s ships with Vex clinging to the hull.

## Summary

The great hall erupted the moment Admiral [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) rose into the air with [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/)'s accusation still echoing. His voice boomed through the ArcEye cameras carrying the Summit across Khorvaire: *"Kill Chancellor [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) for the Empire."*

[Lorian](/people/lorian/) summoned Tira, his light-brown palomino, and pulled Eric onto the saddle as the masked Empire guards surged forward. Their movements were unnatural — undead soldiers loyal not to Vex but to Empress [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/). The Black Dove sisters fled at once as the undead turned on Vex and anyone in their path, confirming Uriel's trap and leaving Vex furious and screaming of her betrayal.

Vex called down his warships and ordered them to fire on the Summit grounds. Explosions tore through the surrounding buildings, killing delegates and civilians. Amid the chaos the trio spotted the silhouette of [the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/)'s airship, newly arrived at Thronehold — and watched him turn and flee the island entirely as the rooftops caught.

[Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) and [The Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) sprinted for their vessels; the trio raced after them and misty-stepped aboard one of Locke's ships as it lifted. Vex, wrapped in lightning and rage, launched himself into the sky and clung to the rising hull.

> Kill Chancellor Eric for the Empire.

## Revelations

- The undead guards at the Summit answer to [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), not to Vex. The Empire's own Admiral was walked into a trap by his Empress.
- Vex will shell a peace conference — delegates, civilians and all — rather than lose it.
- The [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) sisters flee immediately as the undead turn on Vex and anyone in their path.
- Joseph Roberts runs. When the bombardment begins and rooftops erupt in flame, Roberts wastes no time, he turns his ship and flees the island entirely.

## Cast

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) · [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) · [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) · [Lorian](/people/lorian/) · [Vex d'Lyrandar](/people/supreme-admiral-vex-d-lyrandar/) · [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) · [the Dread Pirate Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) · Tira, Lorian's palomino steed.

## Consequences

The Summit has fallen but the fight with Vex isn't over. Broadcast across Khorvaire by the arceye cameras, the massacre would be read by the free states as proof that peace had died. The trio escaped aboard a ship belonging to a Campaign 1 founder with the Empire's Admiral clinging to the hull.
