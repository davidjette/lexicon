**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 15 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 15 — The Stairs of Her Mercy** is the fifteenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 25 September 2026. On their first morning in the valley of [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), as the Star Advocate [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/), took [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) through [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/) and down to [the Imperial Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/), where an old teacher of Eric's handed them half the evidence of the valley's corruption and was beaten for it. They watched [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/) win an audience with the Emperor at the foot of the Stairs of Her Mercy, then climbed those stairs themselves disguised as [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), and ended the session at the Tomb of Kaius I in the cathedral forecourt.

> Copy received, but be careful. Our scribes say there is significant magical interference from a very powerful source near your location. Be advised. J.G.

## Summary

The party woke long rested in the Fieldmaster's Suite of the Grand Lodge. From its south window, across half a mile of grass, [the Summer Palace](/places/the-summer-palace/) showed a landing pad with a small, highly advanced craft on it, a lighthouse with its own dock, and the statue of Kaius I looking west into the gardens. Ten days had passed since they last took stock of their holdings. The storehouse sold about a thousand gold of goods. The sacristy, whose staff follow Matt Matheny and hold that numbers and information must be free and true, made Eric a scroll of *hold person*. The greenhouse sent Gemma a potted plant with a note saying they had taken some of [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/)'s seeds; it grows three pink plums every seven days, each one a *lesser restoration* if eaten within a day of picking, and Gemma keeps it alive with *plant growth*. The rest of the money went to the horns: the smithy at [Leef](/places/leef/) will make them, the stables will train riders to carry them, and parties of the Leef Guard will take them to the other kingdoms. [Jessica](/people/jessica-grimpledink/) was already doing the same, and [Renn Tal](/people/renn-tal/) would be asked to put the foundries of [Sharn](/places/sharn/) to work. Jessica's reply came over the sending stone in her own voice, warning of magical interference near them.

![The valley of the Ivory Lazaret and the Summer Palace](/images/uploads/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-the-mukkcsjo.webp)
*The valley of the Ivory Lazaret and the Summer Palace.*

Gemma cast *seeming* over all three: Deirdre Moro, Graham Cracker, and Dario as the bodyguard Sky Rizzy. A Lodge servant named **Bimples** wheeled in a whole roast pig for breakfast, was tipped eighty gold, and told "Madame" that the members' rooms were open to her party during their stay. Over breakfast the three argued about the bell, about whether Pierce and [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) could be working together, and about [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/). Gemma had messaged Roberts from the Lodge door the night before, and he had answered. She made the case for him from memory: he spared them on the train, gave them a sending stone, moved people from [Newham](/places/newham/) to Leef for nothing but a mural, and his wife [Blair](/people/blair/) had helped them twice.

![The party in disguise with Bimples at the Grand Lodge](/images/album/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-dario-eric-and-gemma-in-disguise-inside-the.webp)
*The party in disguise with Bimples at the Grand Lodge.*

In a gaming room across the upstairs corridor, a man calling himself **Joey Monica** and a woman were playing *random walk*, a house game in which a mechanical egg wanders the table until it knocks over some number of pins. They started talking pointedly about the tides when the Imperials came in. Gemma let them believe Deirdre was dirty enough to gamble, and Eric lost fifty gold and won it back. Members, Joey Monica said, get the front rows on the days the Empress comes down her stairs, every couple of months, last some nine or ten months ago; the woman wondered whether the Empress was pregnant again, and said she is over two hundred years old. He asked Deirdre to free "quite a few millions of barrels of barley" from customs, moving out of "certain war-damaged areas", and when the summit came up they both remembered it was time for bed. The Lodge's members hunt at night and sleep through the day. In the corridor they met **Brillo Myers**, a textile magnate, and **Craig**, the construction magnate who built the great war memorial and cemetery and sits on the membership committee, who looked forward to reviewing Deirdre's application. Eric recognised everyone they had met as among the richest people in Eberron, and learned that [Cob Cornwell](/people/cob-cornwell/) had been a member; his portrait hangs among the Fieldmasters on the grand stair. On that stair two men passed a purse, a coin fell, and Eric picked it up. It was minted in [the Uruk-Hoth Republic](/organizations/the-uruk-hoth/), and Gemma worked out that the men's talk of hares and crossbows was a cant for business.

Pork Broccoli, the dworc carriage driver, drove them to the Plaza of the Martyrs, where tens of thousands of names are cut into the paving and a market sells everything from holy water and blankets to building stone and furniture. He had come to the valley about a year ago to drive on a license borrowed from his cousin, which he has nearly paid off. The Stairs of Her Mercy rise out of the plaza in white arcs toward a colossal statue of Uriel in her Black Dove habit. At their foot, petitioners beg sisters in green habits to let them climb, past white guards who stand head and shoulders over the crowd.

Inside the Counting House, General Thar, with two officers, two dark druids of his staff and a room's worth of death troopers, had cornered [Director Aurel Kesk](/people/aurel-kesk/). Thar's voice is ruined. He raised a hand, and the officer in front of him pulled at his collar until he lowered it.

> I don't need you to have the authority. I have the authority. I am going to deliver my report to the Emperor in person. You will take me to him.

Kesk answered that he counts money, that he had not been up the stairs or to the palace in more than a year and had never seen the Emperor, and that nobody opens that door but the sisters. The druids handed him a tightly rolled parchment, and the delegation left.

![General Thar and his staff in the Counting House](/images/uploads/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-dari-mukjvq91.webp)

Kesk passed the Advocate to Sub-Auditor **Corvan Bleek**, who was in Eric's intake twenty years ago. By old tradition, Bleek said, the military is not allowed up the stairs, and Imperial power stops at the edge of the hill. He named the sisters by the colour of their habits: white, black, the green who are healers, and the red who take care of the Emperor. While he talked, an officer offered a man in a swashbuckler's coat his condolences on an aunt, and the swashbuckler paid Bleek a bag of platinum for a load of sealed hazardous-materials containers. Dario followed them by carriage straight to the Lodge and around the back.

![The party with Sub-Auditor Corvan Bleek](/images/album/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-dario-eric-and-gemma-in-disguise-imperial-co.webp)
*The party with Sub-Auditor Corvan Bleek.*

In the commissary Eric recognised [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/), the dwarf who trained him and wrote the assessment that got him his first post: *"a mediocre accountant with an unusual eye."* In her records office she told Deirdre she was the first person from the Ministry to come up the hill in two years, and gave her a private reconciliation of every probate the Counting House had handled, in her own cipher. The Director and his staff, she said, were enriching themselves on the property of people who die in the hospital, on donations, and on war profiteering, and the Lodge's books would complete the chain. *"You can't trust the Director."* The key was eight letters, what you do when you really love someone. Eric tried MARRIAGE; Vitt, reading over his shoulder, said *"You cross your sevens. That's very unusual,"* and told him it was MULTIPLY. Then she locked them in. An hour's work confirmed her account and showed it was half the proof: patients consign their bodies to the Emperor and their belongings to the church, and gunpowder, textiles, building materials, precious metals, artillery and ships are donated to the church and sold out the door at bargain prices.

Kesk came back with troopers holding Vitt and accused Deirdre of ordering her out without a warrant. Eric produced the *hold person* scroll as a warrant signed by the Magister, and Deirdre carried it. *"I can't believe that Merkin, of all people, would pull this."* His men dragged Vitt into his office and beat her until Deirdre demanded her as a witness. Kesk let her go, told Deirdre that [Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/) could forget the favour Kesk had planned for him, warned her that more important people than he would be upset if she held too hard to the letter of the law, and offered to make her cooperation worth her while. Vitt told them to get out, and went to the pilgrim camp, where she sleeps most nights, to meet them later.

![Kesk comes back to the records room](/images/uploads/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-dari-mukjydx4.webp)

Outside, Thar forced his way through the petitioners to the foot of the stairs. When the crowd started pleading its own cases he said *"Enough!"*, and a supernatural silence fell over the plaza. The sisters sent for a high sister, and a procession of sisters in red came down the stairs one step at a time behind a very tall woman in black wearing a red mask. She told Thar that the Emperor is very ill but has agreed to meet him in his garden on the morrow. *"Excellent. Thank you, Sister, by his grace and her mercy."* Thar spoke a few words in another tongue, became a huge red bird, and flew north across the river with his two druids as smaller birds.

![General Thar and a dark druid at the foot of the Stairs of Her Mercy](/images/album/korth-ep-15-the-stairs-of-her-mercy-general-stockton-thar-and-dark-druid-at-the.webp)

In the crowd they heard that the Empress heals in person but touches only five or six people, that there are more sisters in the valley than there were a year ago, and that the Grand Lodge will not take pilgrims at any price. A very tall white-robed sister whom Gemma knew from Korth walked down through the crowd. A turbaned man took them to the food stall of the twins **Bran Flakes** and **Frosted Flakes** on Relic Row, who sell a place on the queue's list within a day for three and a half thousand gold, a thousand of it to be left on the altars on the way up. Frosted said the floating object on the hill is new, that sisters tend it night and day and never stop looking at it, and that it feels as though it is watching you. He washes in the Fountain of Mercy with a borrowed cane and a friend at the gate, and says it is like a whole night's sleep in one splash. The party did not trust the fountain.

Instead of paying, Gemma recast *seeming* behind the stables, and the three became Black Doves: Gemma as the novice she had been in Korth, Eric as an old nun, and Dario as a winged aasimar sister. Gemma's performance carried them past the guards. Every landing on the stairs is an altar, each once built for one of the Sovereign Host and rededicated to the Mother. At the first great altar they overheard **Tamsin Vell**, a vine-dresser who climbs there every morning to pray for a son missing at the front, and **Lord Aldric and Lady Sabine Varenne**, who had come to hunt and to watch the pilgrims, and whose family silver had just gone down to the estate appraiser at the Lodge. [Captain Jim Smallberries](/people/captain-jim-smallberries/) was there on a delivery pass. Above a holy tree that was once a nature god's altar, the statue of Uriel stands with a shield and an imperial crown over a fountain at her feet, and Dario spat into it. Asked how his faith was holding up, Eric said he cares about maths, not the gods.

At the top, the floating object hangs over the cathedral forecourt, sharp-edged, its surface swirling grey, pink, white and sometimes black, reflecting the sky and bending it. Four strangely dressed sisters stand one to a side staring up at it. Gemma felt sick at the sight of it, and Eric felt a rush of hate. His *detect magic* showed enchantment, with transmutation holding it aloft, and he judged it a store of immense power that can cast spells: Uriel's focus. Just inside the nave stands a great fountain of glittering pale blue water, and Gemma knew it as the fountain from her vision with [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/), where it ran with blood. The session ended at the Tomb of Kaius I, which carries abjuration magic, with the party arguing about whether to break into it.

## Revelations

- The Counting House is laundering the valley's wealth: the property of people who die in the hospital, church donations, and war material sold out the door at bargain prices. Ilma Vitt's reconciliation is half the proof, and the Lodge's books are the other half.
- War material leaves the Counting House for the Lodge, and Uruk-Hoth gold changes hands on the Lodge's stairs.
- By old tradition the military may not climb the Stairs of Her Mercy, and Imperial power stops at the edge of the hill. Director Kesk has never seen the Emperor.
- The sisters' habits mark their work: green for healers, and red for those who care for the Emperor.
- The Emperor, "very ill", has agreed to receive General Thar in his garden the next day.
- The floating object in the forecourt is a store of power and Uriel's focus.
- The fountain in the nave is the one from Gemma's vision.

## Consequences

Deirdre's standing at the Counting House is gone; Kesk is openly hostile and has invoked Merkin by name. Ilma Vitt is waiting at the pilgrim camp. General Thar's audience with the Emperor is the next day, in the garden, and the party has talked about hunting the red bird at night with the Lodge's hunters and walking into that garden in his place. The Lodge assignment is due on Monday. The party earned 2,500 experience each and stands close to thirteenth level.
