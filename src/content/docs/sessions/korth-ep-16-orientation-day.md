**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 16 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 16 — Orientation Day** is the sixteenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 2 October 2026. Disguised as [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) joined a party of new novices on their first tour of [the Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) and followed it down into a hospital three floors deep beneath the cathedral of Nos Matre. On the bottom floor they found [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) being forced to raise [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/). They fought their way into the room, where [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/) made Zero-One finish the rite. Esther rose, and the party escaped with Zero-One as she woke.

> You will rise and fall together. You will assume your colored robes together. You will live together. You will love together. And you will die together.
> — Director Lus Fayina, to the novices

## Summary

The session opened where the last one ended, at the Tomb of Kaius I, with the three disguised as Black Doves. A group of novices came up the processional walk behind a severe old sister in heavy white makeup. The novices wore plain black, the same habit as the party, and the green-robed healers among the sisters carried the holy symbols of the Sovereign Host and older native gods. Eric recognised **Saturnia**, the blue-haired elf from the boat. Three novices came over to size up the strangers. Gemma gave her name as **Sister Apollonia**, "Apple", the name she had used as a novice in the Korth house; Eric gave **Sister Euphrasia**, and Dario **Sister Mr. Rogers**. Dario wanted to kill the three girls on the spot, and was talked out of it. When the guide called time, the party fell in at the back of the group.

From the terrace they saw the Westhouse, once the palace wing of the royal family and now the headquarters of the Black Doves, with a small harbour below it, and the pilgrim camp to the north. A procession of red-robed sisters in heels came out of the cathedral behind a large, mannish sister. They were the **Sisters of the Blood**, who care for the Emperor directly and are trained in his unusual illness, and every one of them looked like [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/).

![A Sister of the Blood in red](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-sister-of-the-blood.webp)
*A Sister of the Blood in red.*

In the vestibule called the Threshold of Mercy, Gemma knew the fountain from her vision with [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/). A sister told the crowd that the Mother of Mercy had transformed it, and that it now heals any wound and gives a full night's rest. They watched an old man carried up the hill have his face lowered into the water. He stood up, shouted *Hallelujah!* and fell to his knees praising the Empress, and then a sister walked him off into the hospital wing alone. Eric's *detect magic* found a tremendous weight of necromancy in the water. The party did not drink or wash.

![The old man is healed at the Fountain of Mercy as the guide looks on](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-fountain.webp)
*The old man is healed at the Fountain of Mercy as the guide looks on.*

The guide took them into the great worship hall, where a mass was being sung in a priestly High Elven that prays openly for the eternal night. Eric remembered the building, ten or twelve years before, as an interfaith temple open to everyone. On the rear terrace Saturnia told them her family had dreamed of the day their kind would be in charge again: *"I understand that my virginity now belongs to the Emperor, and we'll see what he does with it."*

![Saturnia at prayer](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-saturnia.webp)
*Saturnia at prayer.*

The tour went on through the trauma ward, a former church turned into a triage hall with blood in the grout, where the old man already had a bed and his forehead had been opened to bleed his humours. At the top of the tower, before the laid-out corpse of a very large knight, the novices were received by a masked woman in a head-to-toe covering marked with a white circle: **Director Lus Fayina**. Eric knew her from an earlier meeting, as the sister whom Dario had let go. She told the novices they were a *sextet*, chosen for their attributes, who would live, love and die together, and said of the knight that he had given his life for the Empire and would give his death as well. Eric searched the room and found medical books, fine instruments, two potions of greater healing, powdered diamond, and [Umbra](/items/umbra/) in neat purple-blue ampules packed in Black Dove healing kits, ready to inject into the living to prepare them for death.

![Director Lus Fayina](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-lus-fayina.webp)
*Director Lus Fayina.*

![The novices around the knight's body in the tower room, with Lus Fayina](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-knight.webp)
*The novices around the knight's body in the tower room, with Lus Fayina.*

Beneath the tower a stair with rails for stretchers went down eighty feet through three floors. On the first was a reception like a college dining hall, where sisters measured the novices and fitted them for robes and a sister with a slate asked each the same three questions: where were you born, who was your mother's mother, and have you ever been pregnant. Elves were asked a fourth, in Elvish: on what boat did your family travel from Aerenal? Saturnia said *the Lightbringer*, and told Eric that only elves of a certain type may join the Sisterhood of the Blood. Gemma's disguise was an elf, so she gave the same ship. Saturnia said she had not realised they were kin, and named her own grandfather as **E'noch**. Gemma led grace: *Come the night the bells will toll thirteen, the dead will rise to serve the living, and the dragon will be reborn.*

Lus Fayina led the sextet on through **the last ward**, a row of private cells for patients whose cases are hopeless but whose bodies are strong enough for *the process of second life*, and past a workroom where undead sisters were fitting bodies with Imperial armour. *"These are the sorrowful sisters. You will join their order only if you are among the most elite at the time of your death."* On the bottom floor, as the novices took their seats, the party split up. Through a locked door's window Eric saw a surgical amphitheatre: on the slab lay a woman with horns and wings, cut open, and Zero-One, beaten, missing teeth, one eye swollen shut, was performing a ritual over her. It was Esther Crona. Gemma found an underground concourse beyond the south door, a station and market run by sisters and civilians, where sisters were raising the bloated corpse of a giant in public and a long vault led south toward the Westhouse. Lus Fayina told the novices that some would wear green, some would be **Sisters of the Bone**, called to the welcoming of the dead, some **Sisters of the Light**, and some would tend His Majesty as Sisters of the Blood.

Through the west door Gemma and Dario found the transformation floor, cells where the strongest bodies spend their final moments. In one lay [Donna](/people/donna/), the Silver Flame cleric from [Zilspar](/places/zilspar/), tattooed, fitted with attachments and a new jaw, her hands carefully preserved among holy implements. Beyond were finished undead soldiers, steeped in Umbra and dressed in the red-and-white armour of the Imperial Guard under a tall sister with two scythes, **Sister Mary Christmas**. In the concourse **Sister Mortis** twice threatened Sister Apollonia with punishment for breaking curfew. Gemma bought hooded robes and tested her magic, and then talked an armoured sister away from the slab-room door with a story about a naughty novice.

![Through the window: Donna on the transformation floor](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-donna.webp)
*Through the window: Donna on the transformation floor.*

Gemma took Dario through the window by *dimension door* and Eric followed by *misty step*. Esther lay on the slab, rebuilt with the grafted muscle and skin of larger creatures, her skeleton braced with bronze and black Khyber metal and a skull-faced mask bolted to her horns, while sisters in grey sang over her and Zero-One knelt in irons with her hands on her chest. Eric climbed onto the observation gallery, shouted *"Down with the Empire!"*, cast *spirit guardians*, and told the sisters he was Eric, the Chancellor of Sharn. Dario lit Pale Fire and took two heads in two blows: *"By the power of the Flame, heathens will perish."* Gemma killed a sorrowful sister and reached Zero-One, who was too deep in the trance to notice her.

![Dario cuts through the choir of Black Doves](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-dario-and-the-choir.webp)

Maelis Dorn, Gemma's mother superior in Korth, stepped in. *"If you stop the ritual, she'll die. I didn't think I'd ever see you again, Apollonia. You were always a terrible sister, and now you're proving me right."* — *"I came back to get my report card."* Dorn rang a black iron handbell, tried to banish Gemma and hold Dario, took a dagger in the thigh from Eric, sealed the door behind her with her ring, and called through it: *"Pathetic one. Enough of the demonstration. Complete your task."* Zero-One rose into the air and poured her power into the body. Eric's *remove curse* did nothing. Dorn boiled his blood with *harm*. Zero-One collapsed, and Esther Crona sat up and lifted off the slab, dripping blood: *"Where is Richard Blaze? Which restaurant is he at?"*


![Gemma, Dario and the sisters fight across the slab room](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-fight.webp)

Eric burned the corridor with a fireball and Dario blinded a battle sister with a smite. Esther wrenched Gemma's sword from her hand and threw her down the stairs, and a sorrowful sister drank from her where she lay: *"You've done it. You've reached perfection."* Eric's mass *healing word* brought her back. Kitsune appeared over Gemma and told her not to let Esther have the sword, *"She has just as much of a claim to it as you do."* Both women called the sword at once and it hung in the air between them, and Gemma won it and sent it away. *"You are unworthy to hold the Crimson Sun,"* Esther said. *"It's not even called that anymore."*

![Gemma and Esther both call the sword, and it hangs between them](/images/album/korth-ep-16-orientation-day-the-sword.webp)

Dario lifted Zero-One from the floor. She opened her eyes and said, *"Dario, I knew the Flame would warm my skin again,"* and Dario vanished with her by *dimension door*. Esther shouted *"Your pact is void!"*, ending Eric's guardians and Gemma's pact magic for a moment. Eric took Gemma by the shoulders, told Esther, *"Did you know it was Richard who betrayed you to your death? It was Richard,"* and took them both out by *dimension door* as an enormous shambling corpse came through the door.

They came out at the Tomb of Kaius I at twilight, with a bell ringing and the white guards moving, and scrambled down the hill past a rider with a lantern who did not believe their story about a possum. Changed back into Deirdre and Graham, they found **Pork Broccoli** in a new suit and fedora, explained their wounds as a romantic misadventure in some prickly bushes, and heard that plenty goes in and out of [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/)'s back door. **Bimples** gave them the Fieldmaster's Suite again for 500 gold and told them the night hunts were about to leave for the King's land across the river. There was no sign of Dario. In the bath Gemma reached her patron, and Kitsune joined her in the tub with a cup of tea. *"That was very dangerous. Don't pit me against her patron, if you can avoid it. There's a reason why it took three of you to kill her, and I couldn't give you that sword until after you did. If she's back, it's because it wants her to be back."* He warned that messages between Gemma and Dario could be overheard, left them two hunting outfits, and told her to get close to the future Emperor. When she asked what would happen if Uriel were stopped and the child died, he grew angry. *"You're going to do what I asked you to do. Don't harm that child."*

## Revelations

- A hospital lies three floors deep under Nos Matre, joined by an underground concourse to the Westhouse. It takes the strongest and most gifted dead and dying of the Empire and turns them into undead soldiers.
- The Black Doves are divided into sisterhoods: the green healers, the Sisters of the Bone, the Sisters of the Light, and the Sisters of the Blood, who tend the Emperor and take only elves of a certain type. The sorrowful sisters are the order's elite dead.
- Elf novices are asked which ship brought their families from Aerenal. Saturnia came on the *Lightbringer*, and named her grandfather as E'noch.
- Zero-One was held to raise Esther Crona, and Esther is risen, rebuilt and winged.
- Donna is on the transformation floor.
- Esther still claims the sword Gemma carries, which she calls the Crimson Sun. Kitsune says Esther has a patron of her own, and that she is back because it wants her back.
- Kitsune's purpose for Gemma is the child who will be Emperor.

## Consequences

Esther Crona is alive and loose in the hospital under Nos Matre, asking for [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/), whom Eric has named as her betrayer. Maelis Dorn has seen Sister Apollonia's face and knows the Chancellor of Sharn was in her slab room. Dario and Zero-One are missing. Donna is still on the transformation floor, and Saturnia is still in the order. The Lazaret's bell is ringing. The party killed eight or nine Black Doves, earned 6,500 experience each, and reached thirteenth level. The night hunt for the red bird before General Thar's audience in the garden is still ahead.
