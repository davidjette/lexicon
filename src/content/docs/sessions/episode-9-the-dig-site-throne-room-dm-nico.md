**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 9 · DM: Nico · Report filed 12 Sep 2020**

**Episode 9 "The Dig Site / Throne Room" DM: Nico** is the ninth session of Campaign 1, The Unforeseen, reported 12 Sep 2020. The party travel from Aeire through the Blade Desert to Arden Crest, find the village and the dig site overrun by undead, destroy an ancient mechanical knight powered by a [black necromancy box](/items/black-box/), and recover the second of four [ancient stone tablets](/items/ancient-stone-tablets-4/), which teleports them to the throne room of Kaius III in Korth, where the hooded figure from their dreams is revealed as the Imperial agent [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/).

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

<small>Not to be confused with [Sharn Ep 9](/sessions/sharn-ep-9-the-grand-aqueduct/) or [Korth Ep 9](/sessions/korth-ep-9-the-death-warrant-and-the-ringer/). Three sequences, three different sessions.</small>

## Summary

The group travels by horse drawn carriage from the coastal town of Aeire, back up through the Blade Desert to the mostly abandoned village of Arden Crest, which is the nearest settlement to the dig site where Locke and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) are stationed. During their travel they all experience strange dreams / visions that include a hooded figure in a black cloak.

When they arrive in Arden Crest they discover the small village is overrun by undead. They fight their way through the Inn then make their way to the dig site discovering that the dig crew are now undead as well. They discover the foreman's journal stating that an ancient tomb was uncovered and a green pulsing light from below brought about undead creatures.

The group make their way underground to the ancient tomb where they battled an undead knight who's chest pulsed with a green glow. Upon defeating the knight it was discovered that he was not undead, but rather was an ancient mechanical being like the lizards the fought before outside of [Newham](/places/newham/). The green glow came from a similar [black necromancy box](/items/black-box/) inside the Knight, but it is now crushed and seemingly no longer working.

Upon investigating the tomb the group find many treasures and a tablet matching the tablet found outside Newham. When the group touches the tablet they are suddenly teleported to the throne room of Kaius III in the capital city Korth. There they stand before a mostly masked Kaius III and the cloaked figure from their dreams who reveals themselves to be [Gabriel Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/). She confesses that she has been working undercover as a rebel for Kaius and that we "blew her cover'. They let us live.

> You too were expendable, just bodies used to hide my cover.

### Throne Room Scene

The room suddenly begins to spin. Your stomachs lurch, and you all stumble trying to maintain balance only to find yourself being pulled away. Not up or down or forward or back but in some new direction entirely incomprehensible. With a loud whoosh it ends, as soon as it had begun.

You are no longer in the ancient tomb outside of Arden Crest, but now you stand together in a vast ornate hall of gold and red. At the end of the hall a magnificent throne of gold sits atop a flight of stairs where you see two figures. One, the dark hooded figure from your dreams and upon the throne sits a man draped in fine silks and linens, his face mostly covered except for his glowing red eyes. A large familiar symbol of the Crimson Sun is laid before you, carved into the marble floor. Esther knows where she is, she knows who sits upon this throne. You all know, as there is no denying you all stand before the great Emperor, Kaius III.

The hooded figure slowly begins to descend the stairs, almost as if it were gliding. It speaks in a voice which carries around the great Throne room as if many voices were speaking at once.

> "We've been watching you, and you've proven yourselves time and again to be great assets to the Empire. You retrieved the ancient bell artifact, you've secured a powerful ally to the West in the Eldeen Reaches, you've turned a once slum of a town into a great new city, and it looks as if you've uncovered the second of 4 ancient stone tablets. But you've dug too deep this time. For 7 long years it took me to get where I was, to get in, to get close. I did it all for the Empire and now it is all ruined, because of you!"

The figure stops and pulls back the hood, dropping the black robes to the floor. Before you stands the most beautiful woman you've ever seen. Standing there nude with long flowing white hair and a large dragon mark on her pelvis and stomach stands Gabriel Duin.

> "My cover stopped there, on that island where you burned my work. 7 years of infiltration and it's destroyed because you kept digging. For 7 years not a soul knew I had joined the rebellion as a spy, that I gained their trust, that I fed them information which got imperial agents and soldiers killed, you too were expendable, just bodies used to hide my cover, I can never go back as the cell I belonged to is now gone. But I'm no longer angry, we (points to Kaius) understand now. You 4 may be the best rebel hunters we have!"

> "Mayor John C. LaBeef, you have an interesting power… harness it to continue in aiding the empire. Perhaps you'll find yourself with even more power and influence in the empire… but not with that thing following you…"

<small>(cast fairy fire revealing the shadow creature attached to LaBeef) if tries to flee but Dwin flicks her hand and it screams in pain as it withers and dies of necromantic damage.</small>

> "Director Pierce, you've continued to help the empire acquire these relics of importance and it does not stop here. There is more to be found, much more. The ancient tablets themselves can not be decoded until we have all 4. The rebels too were after these tablets, and I believe they have been found by more powerful rebel forces than I was ever involved in. One was found by the dwarfs to the East in the Ironroot Mountains. Last I knew they planned to move it to Rebel territory in Zilargo, if it has happened yet, I do not know.. Another is being kept in Aundair at a rebel stronghold believed to be somewhere near Fairhaven…. Esther, you're familiar with Fairhaven, are you not?"

> "I trained you well Uriel, perhaps too well. I do find it….. cute, that you believe your mark means anything, or perhaps Vol blood runs through your veins as it does mine, but don't let my forgiveness fool you Uriel, don't play your silly games with me. Study the undead all you wish but get in my way and you will become another body to add to my collection."

She waves her hand over the Crimson Sun symbol on the floor and it opens up revealing a rising stone platform underneath. In the middle stands a chained woman with a hood over her head, laying around her are 15 charred bodies, the bodies of the rebels you killed and burned.

> "A gift, for you dear Uriel. No hard feelings"

Gabriel leads that chained woman over to Uriel, unhoods the woman. Before you, shivering with fright, is Lady Glow.

Kaius will say something to Ether about Varyin Crona being a loyal Warrior and to avenge his death. Hinting that she could perhaps take Varyin's place on the council in time if she continues to prove herself.

## Consequences

The party acquired a name — **[The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/)** — in the same session in which they learned they had been expendable bodies used to hide someone else's cover. Duin's briefing set the campaign's remaining objectives: the tablets cannot be decoded until all four are held, one is in the Ironroot Mountains and may have moved to Zilargo, and another is near Fairhaven. Kaius hinted that Esther could take [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/)'s seat on the council in time.
