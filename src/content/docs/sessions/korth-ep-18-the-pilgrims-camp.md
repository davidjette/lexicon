**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 18 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 18 — The Pilgrims Camp** is the eighteenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 16 October 2026. A short session with no fights, it ran straight on from [Korth Ep 17 — The Red Bird](/sessions/korth-ep-17-the-red-bird/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) found [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) behind the library of [the Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/), where Gemma accepted his proposal of marriage before he left the valley. In the morning [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/) swore Gemma an oath, and Eric's god answered his question about the Emperor with the inscription on the Tomb of Kaius I. At the pilgrim camp below the stables they found [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and the rescued [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/).

> Deirdre Moro. That is a marvelous face.
> — Joseph Roberts, as Gemma dropped her disguise

## Summary

### The sending

The session opened at dawn on Sunday, with Gemma and Eric on the river in [Fora Dollar](/people/fora-dollar/)'s elvish canoe after the night on the peak. Dollar did not come back with them. Before they rowed across, Gemma used the sending stone Roberts had given her on the lightning rail, on the day of [Greenish Hughes](/people/greenish-hughes/)'s death, and kept the message guarded in case anyone was listening: she was the new visitor with the new stone, she was near, she had had Pork Broccoli recently, she was near the cabin, and she looked a little different. About ten minutes later the answer came back: *"If that's true, come to the library. Not safe to message."*

### The library

At the Lodge, **Bimples** told Deirdre the library was for members only, but that the Lodge would lend a book if she named the title. She asked for one on the attack on the lightning rail and the death of Greenish Hughes and tipped him ten gold. The librarian sent his regrets and an alternative: a Gnomish book on how to conceive, which fell open at an illustration of the gnomish reproductive system. Inside it was a parchment bearing Roberts's seal.

Gemma changed her look under the *seeming* with her mask of many faces, shrinking Deirdre's ears and making her hair long, black and curly. The two of them walked in hunting clothes past members singing an old song and into a very old library, where the great volumes of members and classes were the Lodge's own records. Gemma found the tracks of a sliding bookcase, and Eric knocked on it. A book was drawn into the shelf, and an eye looked out from where it had been. *"I'm here to return your book."* She held up the seal, and the shelf slid aside onto a door.

![Deirdre and Graham at the sliding bookcase in the Grand Lodge's library, with Joseph Roberts behind it](/images/album/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp-the-bookcase.webp)
*Deirdre and Graham at the sliding bookcase in the Grand Lodge's library, with Joseph Roberts behind it.*

Three swashbucklers drew on them. *"We don't know who you killed or arrested to get that. But we think you should return it and leave this members-only area, Advocate Moro."* They brought the two in, offered to negotiate for the release of whoever she had taken the book from, told her that her presence had drawn a lot of attention, and blindfolded them with black silk strips from the card table.

![Deirdre and Graham, blindfolded, with Joseph Roberts and his swashbucklers](/images/album/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp-blindfolded.webp)
*Deirdre and Graham, blindfolded, with Joseph Roberts and his swashbucklers.*

A woman's heels approached. *"I noticed you went out with Master Hunter Fora. Where is he?"* Dollar had been a member for eighty years. Gemma told her that he had helped them hunt a giant bird that turned into [General Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), that he had left, and that there had been gunfire where he went. The room laughed. The woman was **the Fieldmaster**, and the club was hers. *"It's because death is coming. Do you understand what's happening here?"* Neither side would name the person they were talking about. After a Persuasion of 13, Gemma dropped the *seeming* on both of them. *"Do you morons not know who we are? We're fucking rebels."*

### Joseph Roberts

The room broke into laughing and clapping, and a familiar voice belonged to the Dread Pirate Captain Joseph L. Roberts. *"Now why would you come here in that disguise and scare everyone in my entire organization? Really bad form. We almost sent someone in to kill you many times."*

Gemma gave him her Korth report on him, what she had found at the [Counting House](/places/the-imperial-counting-house/), and the reports on the bells and the [Ruby Nova](/items/ruby-nova/). *"Every empire ends in an estate sale,"* he said. *"I'm simply early."* He took it that they had come to kill the Emperor. Gemma told him [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) was pregnant. In his view the [Iron Veil](/organizations/the-iron-veil/) or someone like it would take what was left, and then [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) would blow everything up and the orcs would take the rest.

He explained the Lodge. So many of the Emperor's cousins had been members that the Emperor and the kings before him kept it exempt from certain reporting and licensing requirements, and its boats travelled the river without stopping for every taxing authority. The orcs, he said, have a tremendous amount of gold, perhaps from the demons or confiscated across Aundair, and they need materiel, and he likes hard currency. *"You did say that you'd accepted my offer. Was that just a ruse to get behind my bookshelf?"*

He greeted Eric as Chancellor and told him that his wife [Blair](/people/blair/) still speaks very ill of him. Eric told him that as Sharn goes, so goes the continent, that Locke Pierce is now in charge, and that they meant to kill the Emperor and Uriel and might need his help with Pierce. Roberts said Eric had put a finger in the Empire's eye at [Thronehold](/history/the-thronehold-massacre/). He has bought every one of his wives an island.

He told the Fieldmaster to take up their buyer that morning, because he was selling the Lodge. He would not say to whom. *"We intend to leave the place a cinder, so caveat emptor."* *"There's no one in the Lazaret to save. This isn't Leef. This whole place should go."* Gemma reminded him that he had played the violin for her on his island, and wrote out [the fanfare of dawn](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/) for him to play if a bell rings. *"I'm not going to be anywhere near a bell when it rings."* He called Uriel racist and stupid. Gemma accepted him: *"I will gladly marry you, and you can give me an island, as long as I have freedom. If I make it through this."* *"Well, I suppose we'll see if you keep that promise."*

A psychic he trusts had warned him off the valley. He would leave a good ship and good men behind, and she could call them through her stone. He warned her about the messages. The polyhedron floating over the cathedral is a new device, smaller ones went up outside some powerful headquarters before it, and since then messages have gone missing or arrived changed and people have been arrested and followed. The Lazaret is also sealed with *private sanctum*. His men carried gold, jewels and papers out through a back door. Gemma told him that one day he would hear the Emperor and his wife had been assassinated, and that it would be them. He kissed her and left with a book, and the Fieldmaster followed with a volume of ledgers. *"Who's trying to buy this place?" "Maybe I'll tell you someday."* They left the cat behind, and Gemma opened a window for it.

### Sunday morning

They woke at about eleven on a rainy Sunday. In the bathroom mirror Gemma called Kitsune and asked whether, if she brought him the child and later had one of her own, what passed from her father to her would pass on again. *"I solemnly swear that if you bring me the child unharmed, I will consider your obligation complete. You may keep everything I've given to you, and I will go on my way."* He did not answer when she asked what would happen if she died. Of Dario he said: *"He has found help. You should go to him. But be careful. Don't be followed."*

![Gemma and Kitsune on Sunday morning at the Grand Lodge, with Eric in the bath](/images/album/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp-kitsune.webp)
*Gemma and Kitsune on Sunday morning at the Grand Lodge, with Eric in the bath.*

Eric cast *divination* to Matt Matheny and asked how to kill the Emperor. He worked a numerical sequence through in Elvish and Draconic, and the Elvish came out as:

> I am the crimson star that shines in the night.\
> Do not stand at my grave and weep.\
> I am not there. I did not die.

"Of course, the answer is so simple!" He knew the words from the Tomb of Kaius I. [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) had told him the Emperor was a vampire, and the two of them talked it through: [Malrik Zeir](/people/malrik-zeir/)'s mist going to his coffin, and the dragon blood taken from [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/). *"They don't call him Kaius the Undying for nothing."* Their working theory was that killing the Emperor would send his mist to his resting place, where he would have to be killed again.

### The pilgrim camp

They left as Deirdre and Graham, past a Lodge where things were being taken down. Pork Broccoli said a man had been asking for them at the pilgrim camp, drove them there through the rain, and asked Deirdre whether he should leave the valley, because people were saying the Empress might have her baby. She told him to go, and he went.

The camp held sixty or eighty pilgrims of many faiths, with no Doves among them, and many of the musicians and dancers from the plaza. At the back, a small camp of Matt Matheny's followers was caring for Zero-One. [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/) confronted Deirdre: *"I gave you everything you needed. You didn't do anything about the corruption."* Dario crawled out of a tent, happier than they had seen him in a while. He and Zero-One had come out on the peak after his *dimension door* from the slab room, and these people had taken them in. Eric told the camp that his friend was Foxtale and that neither of them was what they seemed. Asked whether they were in league with the Dread Pirate, exclaiming that Roberts had committed crimes against math. The mathematicians among the followers talked of imaginary numbers and complex analysis, and Eric told them about the mathematics hidden in the warforged's heads.

Dario stood on a stump and asked for everyone who could carry a sword or play a harp or a horn. *"It is a dark day. The sky is gray. And it will be a long night. But that means all the brighter dawn."* He pointed at Gemma's sword, [the Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/). The musicians crowded round Gemma, who played them the fanfare perfectly, and the Matt Matheny followers and the Plaza Band pledged their help.

### Zero-One

Zero-One woke and asked first after [Izaak](/people/izaak/), who was alive in Sharn with [Bantide](/people/bantide-moro/), and wept for Zilspar. Then she asked where [Richard](/people/richard-blaze/) was. The attack on [Zilspar Farm](/history/the-destruction-of-zilspar-farm/) had tripped none of the alarms, which only someone who knew how to switch them off could have managed: *"I looked up and every building was full of Eden units just shooting. It was carnage."* Only Dario, Gemma, [Jessica](/people/jessica-grimpledink/), Richard, Eric and [Lorian](/people/lorian/) had known where the boy was.

![Gemma holds Zero-One's hand at the pilgrim camp while Dario dresses her wounds and Eric looks on](/images/album/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp-zero-one.webp)
*Gemma holds Zero-One's hand at the pilgrim camp while Dario dresses her wounds and Eric looks on.*

She believed that was why she had been made to raise [Esther](/people/esther-crona/). Uriel thinks Esther knows where [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/) is, and nobody could get closer to him than his mother, but Esther could not have done it with her own heart, so they had Zero-One take it out. Of Uriel she said: *"Uriel's brain was missing when I first met her all those years ago, and I replaced it with a gem, which had raised her from the dead by the light of the Flame. Why this was the will of the Flame I will never understand. And I shudder to think that I'm the one that gave her the mind that led to everything we see."* The sisters dominated her through Esther's rite and spared her hands because they needed them. She removed the heart and could not finish, and the heart is still somewhere. It felt to her like practice for someone else.

*"If you're going to confront the White Death, then I will summon all the Flame that is left within me to help you."* Asked whether she knew the Emperor was a vampire, she said: *"We had good evidence to that fact. The equation is correct."* She gave Gemma her homemade pewter pendant, a flame in a circle, which holds one casting of *circle of power*.

The party talked over the climb without settling it: go in as Thar, kill the Emperor if they reach him and follow his mist, or find Uriel first. Dario said he would destroy Eden on sight. The rain came down harder, and they got ready to go up the hill as General Thar and his druid.

## Revelations

- Joseph Roberts ran his trade from behind the Grand Lodge's library, with the Fieldmaster as his second. He has sold the Lodge to a buyer he would not name and left the valley.
- The Grand Lodge was exempt from Imperial reporting, licensing and river tolls because so many of the Emperor's cousins were members.
- The polyhedron over Nos Matre interferes with messages, and smaller ones stand outside other powerful headquarters.
- Gemma has accepted Joseph Roberts's proposal of marriage.
- Kitsune has sworn that if Gemma brings him the child unharmed, her obligation is complete.
- The inscription on the Tomb of Kaius I answered Eric's question about how to kill the Emperor.
- Zero-One put a gem in place of Uriel's missing brain when she first raised her.
- Esther Crona's heart was removed in the rite that raised her. Uriel wants Esther to find Calcifer.
- The attack on Zilspar Farm tripped none of its alarms.

## Consequences

The party is together again for the first time since the slab room, with Zero-One rescued and a camp of allies pledged. Joseph Roberts and the Fieldmaster have left the valley with the Lodge's ledgers, and the Lodge has been sold. Roberts has left a ship the party can call. Fora Dollar has not been seen since the gunfire. No experience was awarded. The session ended at noon on Sunday, the hour of Thar's audience with the Emperor in the garden.
