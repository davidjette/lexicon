**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 10 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 10 — Tunnel to the Abyss** is the tenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave. Searching the Korranberg Foundry for the missing duergar boy Egg Roll, the trio followed his footprints through a crevice in the Glitterhame into a forgotten drainage tunnel, destroyed an **Alkilith** holding a permanent portal to the Abyss open beneath the foundry, and traced the trail to a cavern directly beneath the hanging bell.

## Summary

With no sign of Egg Roll and the memory of [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/)'s dream still gnawing at him, the trio swept the foundry level again. [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) feared the boy had wandered near [the Thirteenth Casting](/items/the-thirteenth-casting/) and been infected the way [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) once was. Finding nothing, she set their security crystal by the bell, locked the foundry door from outside, and they pushed into the old sealed rooms.

They cleared a long-abandoned kitchen of undead and found sealed barrels of Old Toby weed, taking a few and telling the clan about the rest. But Egg Roll had last been seen heading toward the Glitterhame, so they went back to the cavern — and there Gemma found small footprints leading to a narrow vertical crevice in the rock wall.

They squeezed through single-file and followed the trail up into a forgotten drainage tunnel. The deeper they went the hotter the air became, thick and sulfurous. Then the ceiling split. A creature of living corruption dropped from above — an **Alkilith**, a demon capable of tearing open permanent portals to the Abyss — and behind it the rock wall peeled open onto a hellscape of fire and molten stone, a massive winged demon charging through. The fight was brutal in the narrow tunnel, but the trio destroyed the Alkilith and the portal snapped shut.

## Revelations

- The tunnel had been **open to the Abyss**. This was how the succubus they encountered the day before, locked in [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/)'s room, had most likely entered the foundry.
- As the Alkilith died, the portal snapped shut, sealing the breach.
- The crevice was one of the mountain's cracks into Khyber. Dave: "And you found one of the cracks between this realm and Khyber". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-15)</small> The Alkilith living in it had opened the crack into the Abyssal doorway: "if there are alkilith in the rock here then any crack they live in could became such a portal". <small>(sic; Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-20)</small>
- The infernal lava beneath the Korramont comes through these cracks: "That infernal lava flows through cracks and into this mountain and creates the black Khyber ore and the forge fire capable of smithing it". <small>(Dave, Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-15)</small> Dave on the alkiliths: "the presence of an alkilith (more than one?) in the mine explains how the infernal magma flows here and likely also how the Deeptempura encountered the fiend who made their founding pact for the forge". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-08-20)</small>
- The footprints end in a vast rocky cavern directly beneath the forge room — beneath the hanging bell itself. Egg Roll is somewhere *above* them.

> Behind it, the rock wall peeled open into a roaring hellscape of fire and molten stone.
