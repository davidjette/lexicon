**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 4 (a&b) · DM: LaMont · Report filed 11 Sep 2020**

**Episode 4 (a&b), "New Cyre"** is the fourth session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), run by LaMont and reported on 11 Sep 2020. The party were sent to the refugee camp and town of New Cyre to determine whether Father White was a rebel or working with the rebels, and to bring him back to Korth or assassinate him. They found the town's politics controlled by gangs, the drug Embrace in heavy circulation, and Father White himself taking Embrace, which made him read as undead; he and his paladins left New Cyre by train, and the mission failed. LeBeefe came away with a narcotics supply line into Newham.

> Father White was taking the drug Embrace, causing him to seem undead.

## Summary

The group travels to the refugee camp / town of New Cyre to determine if Father White is a rebel or is working with the rebels. They were to either bring him back to Korth or assassinate him.

Upon arrival the group discovered gangs were influencing the politics of New Cyer. A Drug named "Embrace" was in heavy rotation in town and the leader of the gang "Booty Salad," Taby PC, made a deal with LeBeefe to move Embrace through [New Ham](/places/newham/).

It was discovered that Father White was taking the drug Embrace, causing him to seem undead. Father White and his Paladins left New Cyre by train. Mission failed.

## Revelations

- Embrace makes a living man register as undead. The drug, not necromancy, is why Father White read wrong.
- The gangs of New Cyre are influencing its politics, and their leader will deal directly with an Imperial Magister.
- [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/) took the contract and did not fulfil it: Father White left by train. **Mission failed.**

## Consequences

LeBeefe left New Cyre with a narcotics supply line into [Newham](/places/newham/) to add to his slave trade. Embrace itself becomes Uriel's tool two episodes later: applied weekly to a corpse raised by her sex magic, it stops the body decomposing.
