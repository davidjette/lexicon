**Leef arc · Episode 18 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 18 — The Death of LeBeefe** is the eighteenth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played on 5 December 2025, it was the party's attempt on Grand Duke [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/), first mayor and ruler of [Newham](/places/newham/) and a founder of [the Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), and LeBeefe died. His death belongs to [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/), the rising that ended with the city destroyed and the region around it corrupted into New Mourning. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-05)</small>

## Summary

The party came to the session knowing what it faced from [Leef Ep 16](/sessions/leef-ep-16-the-mega-heist/). LeBeefe held a castle and his troops were all mind flayers; Inspector LeGranouille's trouble with his mind control had come face to face with him, and Dave expected the party to face the same; and LeGranouille was expected to help with his own troops, "at least the ones not inside the castle". Dave had proposed fighting LeBeefe in the dark, because "If he can’t see us his powers may not work". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-22)</small>

On the day of the session Nico asked, "We killing LeBeef tonight?" and corrected herself: "*Trying to*". Dave answered "YES" and "LABOEUF CEST MORT". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-05)</small>

Digma Beeve put the problem in two parts: "You get in, how do you kill him? You kill him, how do you get out?" The allies the party had gathered were to bog down LeBeefe's guards once he was dead. Eric's mark let the party pass in and out of the Black Fortress freely, and LeBeefe welcomed them in to examine his books, but the ink bled off every page. In a hallway Dario was met by a small dragonborn girl, the sister he had left behind, asking "why did you leave? Are you sad that I died after you left?" LeBeefe first showed himself as a young human, intangible, playing with them. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

> You thought it would be that easy? You simpering nitwits. My name is LeBeefe. I cannot be killed.

<small>(John C. LeBeefe, JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

He promised the party a present "inserted directly behind your eye", to make them his slaves and march them on Karrnath. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

The attempt succeeded and LeBeefe was killed. The trio had welded Newham's scattered anti-LeBeefe cells into one resistance strong enough to move on him, in the rising known as [the fall of Newham](/history/the-fall-of-newham/).

At his death his arcane eye exploded and poisoned the land with illithid magic, and the ground around Newham became the [New Mourning](/places/new-mourning/). <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

## Revelations

- [Inspector LeGranouille](/people/inspector-legranouille/), who had joined the trio after the mega heist, was LeBeefe in disguise and had followed with the party the whole time; his turn was a ruse. <small>(Dave, sources/dave/2026-09-14-legranouille-is-lebeefe.md)</small>
- Nico's recollection of LeBeefe's office: "he was head of like northen transpertation", "expanding in the north and around Newham and beyond into leef". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-09-11)</small>
- LeBeefe's arcane eye exploded at his death and poisoned the land with illithid magic.

- LeBeefe's voice outlasted him. It was heard beneath Sharn and in the Glitterhame, and it spoke to Eric on the way out of the Korramont in [Korth Ep 11](/sessions/korth-ep-11-the-domain-of-valtrex/).
