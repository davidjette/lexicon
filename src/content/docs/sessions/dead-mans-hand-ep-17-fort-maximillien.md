**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about 21 March 2026 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 17 — Fort Maximillien** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about 21 March 2026. The session followed each of the party separately after the bloody incident at the Grub + Grog, some fleeing into the night and others in the custody of the Purple Dragon Knights. It is told in full, with its pictures, at [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/).

![The cage wagon rolls toward Fort Maximillien on its island in the lake](/images/gallery/dmh-fort-max-the-fort.webp)
*The cage wagon rolls toward Fort Maximillien on its island in the lake.*

In order: Mach V comes to with the dagger in his hand; BattleBeast caged with the owlbear; Maverick questioned by Sir Merriam Wordsworker; Shadowhigh and Barold Bonds in the cells; Lenore in the infirmary; the forged execution order; the breakout through the stables and the kitchen; the trash pit and the portal; Mach V's betrayal before Varkus, Klaxon and Griddy; the gallows at dawn; and the three bodies falling onto Heyu's dome in Undermountain. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

The War Wizards of the fort wear a special breastplate that combines robes and armor. The owlbear had been wounded and made groggy by a poisoned bolt. The RUIN card that Griddy slipped away was her own card. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>
