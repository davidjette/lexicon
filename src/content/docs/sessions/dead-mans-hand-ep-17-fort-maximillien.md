**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Fort Maximillien](/places/fort-maximillien/), [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about 21 March 2026 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 17 — Fort Maximillien** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about 21 March 2026. It picked up after the bloody incident at the Grub + Grog and followed each of the party separately, some of them fleeing into the night and others in the custody of the Purple Dragon Knights. By dawn [Heyu](/people/heyu/), [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) had escaped down the fort's trash pit, [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) had betrayed the party, and [Maverick](/people/maverick/), [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) and [Barold Bonds](/people/barold-bonds/) had died on the gallows. This page tells the session scene by scene with every picture Dave made for it. The same night is written up as history at [the Fort Maximillien Breakout](/history/the-fort-maximillien-breakout/).

## In the mud

Nicollo shook off the hypnotizing effect of [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/)'s **THRONE** fate card and looked at the bloody dagger in his hand, and at the soaking wet and bleeding body of Lenore in the mud before him. He had just stabbed her deeply in the torso, and she was dying. Purple Dragon knights descended on him, and he disengaged and fled into the rainy night, slipping between the stone buildings that line the cobblestone streets. <small>(Session notes)</small>

## The stables of Fort Maximillien

It rattled through a sturdy gatehouse, past two heavily armed infantry guards who waved it through, and paused in front of the large barn doors of the fort's stables. Two other guards opened them on a bright dry space, in contrast to the dark wet night outside. ## The interrogation

Sir Merriam led Maverick through the rain and into the fort, which was garrisoned by heavy and light armored knights and an occasional wizard in mithril pauldrons and breastplate, the famous [War Wizards](/organizations/war-wizards/) of Cormyr. The War Wizards of the fort wear a special breastplate that combines robes and armor. Maverick was taken up a tower stair to the upper bastion; an identical door on the other side of the hall led down to the dungeons. <small>(Session notes; illustration briefs)</small>

He was seated in a plush armchair across from a well-appointed desk where Merriam sat. Behind Merriam stood two War Wizards who gazed at Maverick, reading his mind. Merriam questioned him about the incident at the Grub + Grog, and then about the reasons for his journey to Waterdeep, his connection with the condemned Shadowhigh, and why he had been spotted at [Tiffany House](/places/tiffany-house/) earlier that day. The items seized from his party sat on the desk: his own rapier, his quill, and a loosely wrapped deck of cards, with the card of **RUIN** peeking out. Maverick seemed to convince Merriam of his innocence, and convinced him further to investigate Rex Bedsheets and his pyramid scheme. <small>(Session notes)</small>

## Lenore wakes

Lenore awoke nude under a white sheet, her wounds staunched and her health returned. She was in a medical facility, and next to her stood a biomechanical sarcophagus with arcane markings, now inert. There were very few objects there: bandages, linens, some parchment and a few books. The doors were barred and locked, and through the bars she could see into the north room, an unsecured part of the hospital that was also empty. A medic came in, picked up some parchment and looked at her status. He told her to lie still, since she was handcuffed to the gurney and her wounds could reopen if she struggled. Then he left, and she set about unlocking her hands. <small>(Session notes)</small>

## Nicollo's betrayal

Upstairs in the office, Varkus got a report from a guard and gave Maverick a condemning look. Just then Nicollo was brought in. Maverick smiled; Nicollo did not. Nicollo explained that he had been betrayed by the group. He had never meant to steal the RUIN card. Quite the contrary, he was in the service of Klaxon, as he had said, and the moment he found out Lenore had the card, he killed her and tried to take it. ## The cells

In the dungeon Shadowhigh was still trying to pick her handcuffs with a shard of bone. Her dragon went back to the skeleton, pulled out the rest of the spellbook and brought it to her, and she could not make heads or tails of it. Just then Maverick was thrown into a cell, and when his stun wore off they traded stories, and regrets. Barold, in trying to rage and break his bindings, had emitted an inexplicable magic that filled his cell with moss, flowers and beautiful mushrooms, and he was miserable. <small>(Session notes)</small>

## Under the dome

Back in the hole, the living party members crouched under a translucent magical sheltering dome that Heyu emitted from her wand. Lenore was fitting on some slinky black leather armor that Heyu had saved from a previous defeated foe. Her new look was decidedly dominatrix, though she still wore her nerdy glasses and black bob haircut. Just then three bodies fell onto the dome: Shadowhigh, Maverick and Barold. The party was together again, and only half of them were alive. <small>(Session notes)</small>
