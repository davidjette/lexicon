**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [The Grub + Grog](/places/the-grub-and-grog/), Sea Ward, [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about the end of January 2026 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 16 — The Grub + Grog** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about the end of January 2026. The party came straight from [the Tiffany House Heist](/history/the-tiffany-house-heist/) to Ard Farkle's inn in the Sea Ward with two hostages and the machine that had played the heist's hypnotic music. By the end of the night [Lenore](/people/lenore/) had been shot in the yard and stabbed in the street, [Rex Bedsheets](/people/rex-bedsheets/) had used the **THRONE** card on [Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), two carriages were wrecked, and [the Purple Dragons](/organizations/the-purple-dragons/) had most of the party in custody. This page tells the session scene by scene with every picture Dave made for it. The same night is written up as history at [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/).

## Arrival in the rain

Ard Farkle's inn takes up a small block of the Sea Ward surrounded by unusually wide streets, which gives it a sense of separation from the rest of the city. It was lit by lantern and firelight in the rainy night, and light escaped the many doors and windows on every floor but the top one, which was dark. A weathervane of a behatted crow crests the roof, above the third-floor Harper safe house known to those in the know as the Crow's Nest. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## The carriage house

A goblin stable hand waved them zealously in, the rain running off the brim of his wide hat. [BattleBeast](/people/battlebeast/) pulled the horses and carriage into place where hay awaited them, and Lenore paid the goblin five gold, to his great joy. Then they had to decide what to do with the hostages, and with the very large cast-iron contraption of chimes, hammers and gears that had made the hypnotic music used to pull off the heist. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## The rooftop

Lenore stayed behind with her steel defender Johnny to contemplate the machine. BattleBeast bounded up the rooftops to the top of the building to look around. He saw another carriage, smaller but much nicer than theirs, with an attendant unloading cardboard boxes, and dozens of knights riding the neighborhood. He also heard whispering in the room just below him: a halfling and an orc talking about some tragedy in the farmlands. The orc was maimed and dying. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## Megan Moron and the Charm of the All Seeing

A commoner, Megan Moron, approached Maverick wearing a pyramid pendant with an eye in its center. She called it a Charm of the All Seeing and tried to convince him to buy one. When he agreed, she explained that his was actually free: he would agree to sell seven more, keep all the profit, and she would take a small cut, and so he would become rich through the power of the downline. Maverick agreed, knowing it for a pyramid scheme, because he suspected there was money to be made or appropriated. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

![Megan Moron, wearing the pyramid pendant, makes her pitch to Maverick](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-megan-moron.webp)

Megan was a member of [Rexium](/organizations/rexium/), a sales organization and self-betterment group. ## The Crow's Nest

[Nicollo](/people/nicollo/) sneaked up the stairs toward the third floor, where he saw a lithe blonde elf ranger slip in and sit with the dying orc Tenderbottom in their final moments. The elf swore that the people would know what had happened in the farmlands. Nicollo decided not to push his luck and went back to the room. He had seen only the foot and side of the elf before she went in. <small>(Session narrative; illustration briefs)</small>

In the room the party interrogated the two Grille brothers: Bonefish, the nervous one, and Big Macaroni, the resolute one, who bargained. Mac was gagged, and Nicollo intimidated him into giving up his bosses, proving that [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/) was in league with [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/). Nicollo then decided to free them and paid them to join the party, which they agreed to, and he asked them to call him The Prince. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

An armored thug who seemed to recognize her stopped her, and she was shot from behind by crossbow, poisoned, paralyzed and dying. The thug bashed Johnny to bits, but not before Johnny let out an alarm. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## The Rexium sales meeting

The party attended the Rexium sales meeting in the inn's converted barn event room. It was sparsely attended, about ten people in all, with a man on a small stage whom they recognized as the footman who had been unloading boxes earlier. He gave a sales pitch that was obviously hokum, and the people ate it up. Then he introduced Rex Bedsheets. Rex came out, quickly cast a charm over the audience through the golden metal charms they all wore, and took a small chest of gold. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## The fight and the burning carriage

The party attacked. Rex used *dominate person* on Maverick, forcing him and all the charm-wearers, commoners mostly, to attack Heyu. Rex ran to his carriage and started to drive away. BattleBeast leapt on Rex, who was terrified, and attacked him viciously, but a magical shield of ice interposed, froze, then exploded in fire and knocked BattleBeast to the ground, grievously wounded. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

![Battlebeast leaps at Rex Bedsheets as he runs with the chest](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-battlebeast-leaps.webp)
*Battlebeast leaps at Rex Bedsheets as he runs with the chest.*

![The shield of ice bursts in Battlebeast's face](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-ice-shield.webp)
*The shield of ice bursts in Battlebeast's face.*

Nicollo jumped on top of the carriage and lit it on fire with oil. The footman jumped up after him, and the two fought in the fire as Rex drove away with Lenore running alongside. Maverick broke free, but not before he had stabbed Heyu. He ran to stop Rex and cast a hypnotic pattern, which stunned Rex's horses, the footman, who dropped out of his fight with Nicollo, and Lenore, just as she was about to shoot Rex through the carriage window. Rex was not affected. He shot rays of magic into his horses to wake them, and they bolted off down the road with the carriage in flames and Nicollo holding on. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

## The arrests

Shadowhigh ran in and started shooting the dominated peasants, which shocked a set of nearby guards, who ran to arrest her. Maverick took the charm from the dead Megan's neck, since it might be a way into Rex's lair. More guards converged on Maverick and Lenore and bound them. BattleBeast managed to escape in the party's own cart and chased Rex's carriage. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

![Shadowhigh fires into the dominated crowd in the barn as Purple Dragons run in](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-shadowhigh.webp)
*Shadowhigh fires into the dominated crowd in the barn as Purple Dragons run in.*

Nicollo killed Rex's horse, toppling the carriage and nearly killing them both. They fought in the flaming wreckage until Rex pulled the THRONE card from his pocket and dominated Nicollo's mind, forcing him to run back and attack Lenore. Rex then shot a blast into BattleBeast's horses as he bore down, toppling that cart as well. Mounted knights and guards beset BattleBeast, and he surrendered. Rex yelled "they're murderers! thieves!" as the party were led away. <small>(Session narrative)</small>

![In the burning wreck Rex Bedsheets holds the THRONE card up to Nicollo](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-throne-card.webp)
*In the burning wreck Rex Bedsheets holds the THRONE card up to Nicollo.*

![Battlebeast surrenders to the Purple Dragons while Rex points him out](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-surrender.webp)

Nicollo, under domination, stabbed Lenore before he came to. Then he realized the situation and fled into the night streets. The story continues in [Ep 17 — Fort Maximillien](/sessions/dead-mans-hand-ep-17-fort-maximillien/). <small>(Session narrative)</small>
