**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · The Grub + Grog, Sea Ward, [Waterdeep](/places/waterdeep/) · Played about the end of January 2026 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 16 — The Grub + Grog** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about the end of January 2026. Just after foiling the heist at Tiffany House, the party drove two captured drivers through the rain-soaked Sea Ward, avoiding the guards and Purple Dragon Knights who were answering an attack on their General, and pulled in at Ard Farkle's Grub and Grog. The night is told in full, with its pictures, at [the Incident at the Grub + Grog](/history/the-incident-at-the-grub-and-grog/).

![The party's carriage comes into the square of the Grub + Grog](/images/gallery/dmh-grub-grog-arrival.webp)
*The party's carriage comes into the square of the Grub + Grog.*

In order: the goblin stable hand and the music machine; the Grille brothers talked into a bag of holding; Battlebeast on the roof and Tenderbottom dying in the Crow's Nest; Megan Moron and the Charm of the All Seeing; Lenore shot in the yard by a man who took her for "Lexi"; the letter reading "WE KNOW"; the Rexium sales meeting; Rex Bedsheets and the THRONE card; the burning carriage; and the arrests. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

Tenderbottom's whole right leg and right hand were missing and gangrenous. Mach V and Battlebeast could see the room only through small slits in the wood, the windows and shutters being drawn, and Mach V saw only the foot and side of the elf before she went in. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>
