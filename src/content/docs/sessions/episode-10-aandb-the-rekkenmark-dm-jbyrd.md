**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 10 (a&b) · DM: JByrd · Report filed 12 Sep 2020**

**Episode 10** (a&b) "The Rekkenmark" is the tenth and last session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, run by JByrd and filed 12 Sep 2020. A warforged assassin sent after [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) in Korth named his employer as DR4MB, in the Rekkenmark; the party found the academy overrun by undead raised by Professor Marble's research, discovered that Doctor Amber is DR4MB — a three-hundred-year-old Cyre machine built to decommission warforged — and split when Locke downloaded its files and set the academy to self-destruct.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

While unwinding at [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s new restaurant in Korth, "Great Balls of Blaze" a [warforged](/lore/warforged/) man tries to assassinate [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). The group apprehends the warforged, bringing him back to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s manor to interrogate him. During interrogation it is discovered that this warforged, an R-0 unit named Radd Orgasm was hired by someone named DR4MB to assassinate Locke Pierce. DR4MB could be found in the Rekkenmark.

The group travel together to the Rekkenmark while Locke shuts down to appear dead.

Once inside the group discovers that the Rekkenmark is overrun by undead creatures. Upon further investigation it is discovered that Professor Marble accidentally summoned undead creatures while researching the possibility of ancient Dwarven Necromancy being connected to the warforged using a Dwarven book of incantations.

The group continued deep down into the Rekkenmark to find DR4MB and Doctor Amber, it was then discovered that Doctor Amber IS DR4MB and that DR4MB is old Cyre tech (computer) that was once used to eliminate / decommission warforged. Upon downloading info from DR4MB Locke Pierce set to destroy the computer and destroy the Rekkenmark in a "self destruct" countdown. LeBeefe ran for it while Esther and [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) faced off with Locke.

## Before the Rekkenmark

The episode opened on the road that led to it. The party had met the Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) and been waylaid on a desert island; back on the mainland they had gone into an excavated cave and recovered one of the legendary dwarven tablets sought by both the Empire and the rebellion; and a swirling mist had carried them to Korth, the seat of imperial authority. There they saw the Emperor Kaius III, who looked to them "like a creepy-ass jackolantern" and seemed some kind of immortal undead, and met [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/), leader of the Black Doves, who was the Emperor's lover and commanded them to serve her. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

Unsure what to do about Duin's ultimatum, the party went back to the cavernous Korth manor of [Varyn Crona](/people/varyn-crona/), the elf paladin who had raised Esther from a young age. Unable to sleep, they walked down the street after midnight to Richard Blaze's all-night eatery, Great Balls of Blaze, where their server was a half-orc named Mantooth. On the way a newsvendor's headline read "WARFORGED TERRORISTS OCCUPY METROL": a band of warforged extremists calling themselves the Imperious Leaders had taken the old capital of Cyre. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

## Radd Orgasm and the Two Crosses

Locke knew the warforged watching him in the restaurant. Radd Orgasm, an R-0 unit of a model known for stealth, had studied for the same degree as Locke, washed out and disappeared. "I didn't think you'd remember me. You were such a hotshot at the academy." On his body was a key to room 2 at the Two Crosses tavern, kept by a bucktoothed gnome matron named Fiff. The room held a lonely-hearts letter, an order in binary code that only Locke could read, and a bronze badge bearing a shield and a dagger, which only Uriel and LeBeefe were old enough to recognise: the emblem of the Knife Sprinters, warforged programmed to terminate obsolete warforged. The Knife Sprinter protocol had ended when Cyre fell, so the R-0 unit had seemed to be acting on its own. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

The order carried pieces of Locke's own history and declared that he was due to be decommissioned. It gave his designation as unit LK-PRC. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

## The descent

The next morning a courier from the Academy brought Locke a summons. Professor Vanessa Marble, a dwarf arcanist known for her unusual methods, had unleashed swarms of undead during an experiment, and the Academy wanted a discreet team used to fighting the undead. At the Rekkenmark, inquisitors of [the Crimson Sun](/organizations/the-crimson-sun/) had barricaded the site. Professor Oriya Ebony, a half-elf and chairman of the Department of Arcana, told them that summer classes had been cancelled, that she held out little hope for Marble, and that the Chancellor of the University, Doctor Amber, was down there too. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

The lift broke down on the fifth level; Marble had been working on the tenth. Level by level, among worsening undead, the party pieced together Marble's research into how the warforged were first made in Cyre during the Last War: the leaders of Cyre had left extensive records but redacted every account of the warforged's creation, and Cyre had consulted several of the world's most powerful necromancers even though necromancy was illegal there. On the fourth level they found Locke's dying colleague Professor Stone, who told them the Academy had officially censured Marble's research amid fears she was losing her mind. At the bottom Marble herself, turned vampire, had come to believe that Doctor Amber's plans were just, and served her. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

Marble's research suggested that the Lightning Rail and the airships are powered by something like wights, living spirits trapped forever by ancient dwarven technology, and that all of Cyre's great constructs were necromancy that drained living souls. She put it to Locke that he existed only because someone else had given their soul. These remain her claims. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

The whole of the lowest level was Doctor Amber's office, a laboratory of whirring machines around a great glass vat of yellow liquid. A binder there told the machine's history: DR4MB was built in Cyre three hundred years ago by artificers trying to perfect divination and know the future; it began predicting that Cyre would be incinerated and that humanity deserved to die; Queen Mishanne IV ordered it shut down, but artificers kept serving it in secret; after the war Karrnathi agents smuggled it into Karrnath and reassembled it. See [DR4MB](/items/dr4mb/) and [the Knife Sprinters](/lore/the-knife-sprinters/). <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank)</small>

## Revelations

- Locke uncovered info about his past, not shared with the group.
- DR4MB is 300+ years old, created by the Cyreen. It was obtained by Karnnath 2 years before the end of the war.
- Professor Marble believed that ancient Dwarves used necromancy to put souls into machines. She believes this is how the Cyreen created Warforged.
- There is currently a warforged army (imperious leaders) in Cyer that have taken control of the capital city Metrol. Dr. Amber believes there is a 90% chance those warforged could join the rebellion.
- Knife Sprinters - Past warforged who were set out to kill / decommission warforged in Cyer.
- Locke Pierce is unit LK-PRC, a Cyrean prototype built about 175 years ago and never mass-produced. His memory has been wiped five times. He fought for Cyre against Karrnath in the Last War and was decorated for meritorious service, and was later captured with a train of Cyrean warforged and sold to a pirate lord in the Lhazaar Principalities. Cyre's scientists abandoned the design because of instabilities in its programming: willful, obstinate, contrarian, prone to rash decisions, of questionable loyalty. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
- Locke has a sister unit still functioning in the Mournlands, **Lick Price**, reprogrammed as a pleasure unit. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
- Doctor Amber, Chancellor of the University, is DR4MB. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
- It is claimed that the Emperor ordered Amber to restart the Knife Sprinter initiative, turn Locke against the Empire and exterminate every warforged, on the reasoning that warforged can be deprogrammed and reprogrammed and are therefore useless to the Empire. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>
- It is claimed that the Imperious Leaders at Metrol are building a god unit of their own, the Becoming God, able to reprogram every warforged to its service. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen - Rekkenmark - Overdrawn at the Memory Bank; sources/dave/2026-09-29-rekkenmark-twatmarsh-jl-notes.md)</small>

## Consequences

The last of the ten numbered episodes ends with the party turned on each other in an academy on a self-destruct countdown — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) facing off against Locke while LeBeefe ran. Locke Pierce alone learned who he had been, and did not say. He is next seen leading [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), calling [warforged](/lore/warforged/) his "children," and holding the world hostage with the Ruby Nova.

The standoff in the Rekkenmark basement is picked up directly in [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/). <small>The Campaign 1 DM document writes the machine as D4AMB and records that Dr. Marble did not survive the Rekkenmark: Uriel took samples from the body.</small>
