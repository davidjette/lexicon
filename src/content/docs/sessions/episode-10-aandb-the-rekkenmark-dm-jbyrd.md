**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 10 (a&b) · DM: JByrd · Report filed 12 Sep 2020**

**Episode 10** (a&b) "The Rekkenmark" is the tenth and last session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, run by JByrd and filed 12 Sep 2020. A warforged assassin sent after [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) in Korth named his employer as DR4MB, in the Rekkenmark; the party found the academy overrun by undead raised by Professor Marble's research, discovered that Doctor Amber is DR4MB — a three-hundred-year-old Cyre machine built to decommission warforged — and split when Locke downloaded its files and set the academy to self-destruct.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

While unwinding at [Richard Blaze](/people/richard-blaze/)'s new restaurant in Korth, "Great Balls of Blaze" a [warforged](/lore/warforged/) man tries to assassinate [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/). The group apprehends the warforged, bringing him back to [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s manor to interrogate him. During interrogation it is discovered that this warforged, an R-0 unit named Radd Orgasm was hired by someone named DR4MB to assassinate Locke Pierce. DR4MB could be found in the Rekkenmark.

The group travel together to the Rekkenmark while Locke shuts down to appear dead.

Once inside the group discovers that the Rekkenmark is overrun by undead creatures. Upon further investigation it is discovered that Professor Marble accidentally summoned undead creatures while researching the possibility of ancient Dwarven Necromancy being connected to the warforged using a Dwarven book of incantations.

The group continued deep down into the Rekkenmark to find DR4MB and Doctor Amber, it was then discovered that Doctor Amber IS DR4MB and that DR4MB is old Cyre tech (computer) that was once used to eliminate / decommission warforged. Upon downloading info from DR4MB Locke Pierce set to destroy the computer and destroy the Rekkenmark in a "self destruct" countdown. LeBeefe ran for it while Esther and [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) faced off with Locke.

## Revelations

- Locke uncovered info about his past, not shared with the group.
- DR4MB is 300+ years old, created by the Cyreen. It was obtained by Karnnath 2 years before the end of the war.
- Professor Marble believed that ancient Dwarves used necromancy to put souls into machines. She believes this is how the Cyreen created Warforged.
- There is currently a warforged army (imperious leaders) in Cyer that have taken control of the capital city Metrol. Dr. Amber believes there is a 90% chance those warforged could join the rebellion.
- Knife Sprinters - Past warforged who were set out to kill / decommission warforged in Cyer.

## Consequences

The last of the ten numbered episodes ends with the party turned on each other in an academy on a self-destruct countdown — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) facing off against Locke while LeBeefe ran. Locke Pierce alone learned who he had been, and did not say. He is next seen leading [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), calling [warforged](/lore/warforged/) his "children," and holding the world hostage with the Ruby Nova.

The standoff in the Rekkenmark basement is picked up directly in [City of Knives](/history/city-of-knives/). <small>The Campaign 1 DM document writes the machine as D4AMB and records that Dr. Marble did not survive the Rekkenmark: Uriel took samples from the body.</small>
