**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 5 · DM: Nichole**

**Sharn Ep 5 — Welcome to the Neighborhood** is the fifth session of the Sharn arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Nichole. Clearing and reclaiming their [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) apartment, the trio found a hidden panel behind the fireplace opening into a sewer tunnel to the [Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/), learned from the Kross family that [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/) was running from [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/) and had vanished in the aqueduct, and followed a second missing man, [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/), as far as the door of [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/).

## Summary

The Trio explore their newly discovered [Lower City](/places/the-lower-city/) apartment. The abandoned unit was still haunted by the ghosts of its previous tenants, Tony and Tidy Danza, but after defeating the lingering spirits, the Trio began reclaiming the space. While searching the apartment, they uncovered a wine cellar and a hidden movable panel behind the fireplace that led into a sewer tunnel connected to the [Grand Aqueduct](/places/the-grand-aqueduct/). Though the cause of the tenants' deaths remained a mystery, the Trio learned they had been making wine on-site, had prepaid a six-year lease with the owner E.G., and left behind flyers for local shops, including shop and restaurant pamphlets and an advertisement for [Circuit City](/places/circuit-city/), a chip-implant parlor in the Lower City.

After cleaning up, the Trio met their neighbor [Karin Kross](/people/karin-kross/), who runs the Kross Roads Corner Store across the street. Stocking up on household goods, they learned that Karin's ex-husband, [Martin Kross](/people/martin-kross/), had shown up the previous morning claiming he was running from [BioTec](/organizations/biotec/), followed shortly after by BioTec agents asking if she had seen him. Karin also mentioned her son [Kriss](/people/kriss-kross/) and his wife [Misty](/people/misty-kross/), who own a pawn shop nearby, had likewise been questioned by BioTec.

The Trio then visited [Hopper's](/places/hoppers-magic-emporium/), a local magic shop, where they purchased a home-security crystal and installed it in their apartment. Afterward, they headed to [Misty's Pawn Shop](/places/mistys-pawn-shop/), where Kriss and Misty reluctantly shared that Martin had visited them as well, saying he planned to hide in the aqueduct and asking Kriss to meet him there, but when Kriss arrived, Martin was gone. The couple remained guarded and suspicious of the Trio's questions.

Continuing their investigation, the Trio visited the Gremlin Lantern Sake Bar, where they learned that regular, [Grady Marsh](/people/grady-marsh/) hadn't been seen in days. Following the lead to his apartment, they discovered signs of a struggle, blood on the floor, and a matchbook advertising [The Vault Below](/places/the-vault-below/), a notorious Lower City club and gambling house. The Trio followed Grady's trail to the entrance of The Vault Below, standing outside the club as the next chapter of their investigation loomed.

## Revelations

- The apartment's landlord is written only as **E.G.** — the initials of [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/), whom the trio will not meet for another eight episodes.
- A hidden panel behind the fireplace connects the apartment directly to the sewers and the Grand Aqueduct.
- Martin Kross told Kriss he planned to hide in the aqueduct, and was gone when Kriss arrived. BioTec is hunting him through his family.
- Grady Marsh, an ArcEye retiree, has been taken — and the only clue left in his rooms points at The Vault Below.

## Consequences

The Kross family became the trio's entire Lower City network — shopkeeper, pawnbroker, and later the tunnel into [R.U.S.T.](/organizations/r-u-s-t/). Two missing-person threads, Martin Kross and Grady Marsh, converged on the same district, and both would resolve underground.
