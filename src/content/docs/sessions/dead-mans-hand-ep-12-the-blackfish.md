**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Saltmarsh](/places/saltmarsh/), Oerth · Played about September 2025 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 12 — The Blackfish** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about September 2025. This session finished the fight on and under the pirate ship Blackfish and brought the ship home to Saltmarsh. The battle itself is told at [the Blackfish Encounter](/history/the-blackfish-encounter/).

## Below decks

With the hull breached, [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/), [Rodriel](/people/rodriel/) and [Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) held a broken door against the breach, holding back a torrent of sea water, while [Lenore](/people/lenore/) and Johnny used tools and mending magic to meld it into the hull and patch the hole. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

## The depths

[Nicollo](/people/nicollo/), then Mach V, went down alone, driven by a small propeller, with his headlamp the only light. All he could see besides the ocean were the feet and legs of a cloud of zombies swimming straight down toward the sea floor. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Mach V descends by headlamp above the drowned dead](/images/gallery/dmh-blackfish-ascent.webp)
*Mach V descends by headlamp above the drowned dead.*

On the sea floor stood an underwater mausoleum, an elegant sarcophagus in a rotunda surrounded by a coral garden. Garden and altar were desecrated: a blue-skinned angel had been impaled into the tomb by a long sword, his heart torn from his body and pierced by the same sword. The corruption spread in the water like black bile, and a faint unholy glow came from the heart. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

Far below, the sea floor opened on the vertical double eye of the demon prince Dagon, pushing his way through a rift from the Abyss and looking up at the surface as if through a hole too small for him. Zombies of the drowned rose around him. The Dunwater Horror, a huge black squid-like creature with a pink maw inside its tentacles, fought him and the swarming dead and was losing; Dagon was ten times its size. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The eye of Dagon opens in the sea floor as the Dunwater Horror fights the drowned](/images/gallery/dmh-blackfish-dagon-and-the-horror.webp)

## The body that was not there

Shadowhigh walked below deck to where the party had left the corpse of [Jeremiah](/people/jeremiah/). She found the bloody javelin that had slain him and no body. As she bent to touch the bloodstain he stood hidden in the shadow behind her, blood dripping into his eyes and from his throat, waiting to strike her with his mace. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Shadowhigh finds the javelin and no body; Jeremiah waits in the dark behind her](/images/gallery/dmh-blackfish-jeremiah-in-the-shadow.webp)

## Through the keyhole

Mach V watched through a keyhole of the captain's quarters. He could see [Maverick](/people/maverick/) from behind, seated with a scroll in one hand and a golden quill in the other. The person across from Maverick was out of his sight. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

## Into harbor

The party sailed the ruined Blackfish into the harbor at Saltmarsh, where the armored town guard waited to take it into impound, and the guard raised their arms and cheered them in. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>
