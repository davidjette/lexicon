**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 8 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 8 — The Thirteenth Bell** is the eighth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave. In the reclaimed halls beneath the Korranberg Foundry the trio found the Thirteenth Bell hung over a rail cart, cleared the sealed rooms alongside the surviving Teryaki kin, and assembled from documents and from the dead the account of how [Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) rang the bell three years earlier. Behind a [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) prayer seal signed by [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/) they killed a demon wearing [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/)'s name, and then the undead thrall of the real Sister Calvane; during that fight the voice broke [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/), who had to be restrained.

## Summary

[Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) brought [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) and [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) back to the foundry to see the Thirteenth Bell suspended over a rail cart on a rope-pulley system. The forge floor was littered with tools, weapons and ingots, all of **Black Khyber (Shavat)**; the trio quietly slipped bars and weapons into their bags of holding.

In the hall they spoke with Crab Rangoon, one of the surviving kin, then explored the living quarters and met General Tso, brother of Madame Tso, and a group of duergar searching the sealed rooms for the fallen remains of the [Deeptempura Clan](/organizations/the-deeptempura-clan/). The trio helped clear them room by room. General Tso cast *speak with dead* on Scallion Pancake, and between the corpse and the documents a picture assembled itself.

At the end of the hall was a door sealed with a [Black Dove](/organizations/the-black-doves/) prayer ritual — a muslin sheet of protective scripture signed by [Sister Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), the same Dove [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) had befriended years earlier in Korth. [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) tore it down. Inside was a nun, [Sister Calvane](/people/sister-calvane/), who claimed to have been locked in since the bell rang. When Gemma blocked the door she attacked, transformed mid-fight into a red-skinned winged demon, and fled into the hall, where they killed her. Deeper in the suite the undead thrall of the *real* Sister Calvane attacked. During that fight the voice overwhelmed Eric; he broke and sprinted for the great hall screaming that he needed to ring the bell, and had to be tackled and restrained. A dwarf's potion cleared his mind.

> She heard not a bell, but a horn.

## Revelations

- **[Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura](/people/sedda-tiddes-deeptempura/) rang the bell three years ago**, triggering the disaster that turned the enslaved dwarves and workers into undead.
- [King Corn](/people/king-corn/) and his daughter, enslaved by the Empire in their own home, were passing encoded messages, planning an uprising, and secretly forging a **handheld horn** meant to counteract the bell. Their resistance was discovered, and Sedda was taken below into the cavern where Valtrex dwells but not before she rang the bell.
- Maelis Dorn was here **after** the bell rang, and sealed a door behind her.
- Sister Calvane claimed she had been locked in the room since the bell rang three years ago, that she knew the dwarves were planning an uprising, and that she had heard not a bell, but a horn. Mid-fight she transformed into a red-skinned demon woman with wings, horns, and a tail; the undead thrall of the *real* Sister Calvane, almost vampiric in nature, was in the bedroom.
- Eric was struck by a vision: Sedda Tiddes Deeptempura, spore-infected, hearing the same voice Eric hears, being urged to ring the bell and doing so. The voice reminded him of John C. LeBeefe. He kept this from the others.

## Consequences

The Tso siblings made their offer: descend, find the remains of Sedda and [King Corn](/people/king-corn/), bring them back, and the kin would give the trio anything — hospitality, knowledge of the bells, and access to **Corn's folio**, the complete record of the forging of Shavat and the bells. Eric was now visibly compromised and still hiding how badly.
