**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 19 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 19 — The Westhouse** is the nineteenth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 23 October 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 18 — The Pilgrims Camp](/sessions/korth-ep-18-the-pilgrims-camp/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) went up the Stairs of Her Mercy wearing the face of [General Stockton Thar](/people/general-stockton-thar/), with [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) as his druids. Turned away from the Emperor in the garden and lodged in the Westhouse, they killed [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), [Lus Fayina](/people/luz-fayana/), two Sister Mary Christmases and [Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin](/people/vice-admiral-freyd-merkin/). The young Sister of the Blood Saturnia, spared, told them who her grandfather is and what the Emperor is.

> I was such a good student.
> — Gemma Corso, to Maelis Dorn

## Summary

### The camp

The session opened at noon on Sunday in the pilgrim camp, in rain, with the Lazaret's bell ringing the hours. Dario had spent the night with the followers of Matt Matheny. Compared with the sad man the others had found in the fields of grain, his eye was gleaming and he had a big smile.

> **Dario:** Eric never told me what party animals these nerds could be. Sorry, I'm a bit rowdy. Some of them said they know you from college.
>
> **Gemma:** I think all math people like to party. Is that right?
>
> **Eric:** Yeah, yeah. It's all quite logical.
>
> **Gemma:** Is it? I mean, Dario, I've thought about this when you were gone last night. And especially now, after seeing you and Zero, do you think maybe it would be better if you used the Bright Dawn sword?
>
> **Dario:** The sword? No.
>
> **Gemma:** Only because it's kind of... You named it. And it seems to me it could be a symbol of...
>
> **Dario:** A symbol of hope?
>
> **Gemma:** Yeah.
>
> **Dario:** Rebellions are built on hope.
>
> **Gemma:** Right. And I'm not one to wield a symbol of hope, I don't think.
>
> **Dario:** What makes you think that?
>
> **Gemma:** If I survive this, I can't imagine anyone will remember or know who I am.
>
> **Dario:** They'll remember a thousand years. You've played a part in every part of this rebellion. Your deeds are legendary. How could that be?
>
> *He puts his hand on her chest.*
>
> **Dario:** Gemma, you must have faith in yourself. We are the heroes who will deliver this land. You are the one who will carry the Bright Dawn across the horizon.
>
> **Gemma:** I mean, when you say it, it sounds beautiful. So I can't deny you that.
>
> **Dario:** I have made my oath. Palefire is a symbol of my oath. I carry Knell, the weapon of my wife's people, to bring down upon them. I have but two arms, Gemma.

They talked over the plan, hunkered down in Zero-One's tent away from the crowd.

> **Gemma:** I mean, Eric here...
>
> **Dario:** What is our plan? Can we go pregnant bitch now, before she breathes another disgusting void into this world?
>
> **Gemma:** If, let's say, we get in there and we come face to face with Uriel. And let's say we get in there and we come face to face with her and we get the chance to jump her. I don't know how easy this will be. She could have a failsafe that as soon as she's attacked or as soon as her child's born, the bell rings. So if we're not near the bell, how do we break the bell?
>
> **Eric:** Perhaps we want to break the bell first.
>
> **Gemma:** Well, if we break the bell, then we would have to think of this in a way, because it will definitely call... We've already... What am I trying to say here? Breaking in and pretending that we were nuns already has them on alert. Now we're going to be walking in as not ourselves, as someone who is ultimately, again, trying to kill them. But I don't know if they know that.
>
> **Dario:** They don't. This is our advantage. They didn't think about what position they must be in. They're being infiltrated. They have the head of the army banging down their door, demanding to see the Emperor. Where is he? What condition is he in? This is the end of their great ruse. And now they know that there are people coming in in disguise, murdering their people. Their defenses are porous. They must be in full terror and retreat. And we can use this to our advantage. We could go in as one thing and change our form while we're there.

Eric broke his *seeming* and came out of the tent as himself, the Chancellor of Sharn. The counting-house staff who had fled to the camp crowded round him: sub-auditor Corvin Bleak, the Professor, Steampunk Nerd Girl, Nerd Halfling, Marianne, Nelson, Elliot and Reginald. One gave him a burned CD. Another, who had read his final filing for the Primary Grain Corporation, gave him a homemade potion labelled *study aid*: *"If you drink this, you can read in a minute what you could otherwise read in a week."* [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/) came over. *"There never was an empire. There was only us, the people. And we're taking it back."* She gave him a glass, gold and jewel-mosaic bowl.

Eric drank the study aid and read the whole of Korn's folio in a minute. With the bowl he cast *heroes' feast* for twelve: the three of them, six of Matt Matheny's camp including [Fema Nolan](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/), and three leaders of the Plaza Band. When the last of the trifle was scooped out, Ilma smashed the bowl on the ground, everyone shouted *"Opa!"* and the band struck up.

### The Stairs of Her Mercy

Gemma went up as Thar, with Eric as the red, horned druid Majorbo and Dario as the druid Veg. At the foot of the stairs a Sister of the Bone told them there were no appointments. *"I have an appointment. I am here to see the Emperor. At noon. I am on time."* She stepped aside. *"General, please make your way."*

On the high landing the great statue of [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) carried a new, still-polished plaque, *URIEL I*, with Elvish beneath: *By her mercy are we kept pure.* A sister guiding a boy and his grandfather said it was a wedding gift from the people of Aerenal to the Empress and the people of Karrnath, placed nearly thirteen years ago. Eric saw that the landing was laid over older stone. Gemma saw that the statue stood on its own two stone feet on a patterned platform, and that the pattern was heavily scuffed.

As they passed the Polygon, a voice echoed in Eric's head: *"She steals everything from me!"*

At the Tomb of Kaius I, Eric found a hole about an inch wide drilled through one of the O's of the inscription, smooth and as old as the stone, and put his finger through it. *"Vampires can turn into mists. Yeah, they're going to just go out of the hole."* Dario wanted to climb the tower and break the bell then and there. Gemma read him as passionate, and he agreed to wait until they had time.

### The garden and the Westhouse

At the Threshold of Mercy a novice told Thar she knew nothing of the Emperor: *"You have the Mark of Finding, do you not? Certainly you can look for him yourself."* They went past the eternal flame and down the stairs to the Imperial Gardens. A huge white knight stood under the Triumphal Gate. *"General Thar, here to see the Emperor."* He stepped back as if commanded, and battle sisters saluted.

Across the garden someone called *"General Thar!"* It was Sister Mary Christmas, a head and shoulders above everyone, with her two scythes, and Lus Fayina in front of her. Lus said the Emperor was in surgery that afternoon, which together with the Empress's condition ruled out a meeting. He might come back in ten to twelve days. *"I cannot discuss any other details about their conditions, except that the Empress is with child. And we are ready to welcome a new heir any day now."* Thar asked to stay, and they offered hospitality in the Westhouse.

The Westhouse is part of the Summer Palace given over to the [Black Doves](/organizations/the-black-doves/), as grand as Versailles. A fountain in its hall matches the one in the nave, inscribed *None may harm those who come bearing*, and two doves drank from it and came away refreshed. An instructor in a habit the party had never seen was whipping an old man in front of a crowd of young doves. Lus showed them to a vacant bedroom and lifted her veil to show a cut across her cheek that had laid her jaw open: the price of her failure at the New Morning. *"If I had succeeded in my duty, the Empire would still control Sharn. Thronehold would not have been destroyed, nor Leef, Newham, all failures and consequences."*

When they opened the door, Saturnia was passing in tears, in the new red robes of a Sister of the Blood. *"Sisters of the Blood keep our tongues still."* In the stairwell Lus, Mary Christmas and two white knights stopped them. High Inquisitor Dorn wanted to see the General. *"It's very unusual for us to permit anyone from His Majesty's military inside of the convent. I hope that you'll use discretion about what you see here."* Eric: *"Yes, the General is nothing if not discreet."* Dario: *"How dare you? This is a mere priesthood. We represent the great power of this empire."* Lus laughed a little. *"Of course."* Upstairs was a classroom of veiled elven sisters, among them more very tall sisters like Mary Christmas and two hooded sisters in black with glowing red hearts. The lesson had gone past the Liturgy of Her Mercy into rites of the Blood of Vol. Saturnia sat down among them, and a sister withered her with a curse from her scythe when she faltered.

Dorn's office was small and sparse for one of the highest-ranking officials of the most important religious order in the world. Gemma knew her as the woman who had taught her the liturgy in her time as a Black Dove. Lus kept the door open and stood at it with her head down.

> **Dorn:** General, thank you for agreeing to come visit me.
>
> **Thar:** Of course.
>
> **Dorn:** I wanted to apologize personally for the unavailability of the Emperor. We did not realize that you wanted to see him at this time, and this procedure unfortunately could not wait.
>
> **Eric:** His health?
>
> **Dorn:** His health is getting better every day, and these measures are meant to prolong and enhance his life and lucidness in order that he may continue to command you and your army.
>
> **Thar:** And what of the Emperor? The Empress?
>
> **Dorn:** The Empress is in fantastic health. She is about to bear her fourteenth child. Thirteen, if you count the two twins as a single, which many do. She is, of course, very well adapted to this, and we trust that she will perform well when the labor begins. But I wanted to invite you here in order to express my apologies, but to tell you that I can bring any message which is of a non-military nature that you would like to bring to the Emperor and the Empress. I will conduct it there myself, personally. And my hope is that you stay here as long as you can, but I've arranged for transportation for you back to the capital at your earliest convenience.
>
> **Thar:** I will have to speak with my fellows here, on what our next step should be. We did have plans to be here. I don't think going back to the capital is what we need to do right now. The Emperor will need time to recover.
>
> **Dorn:** And I know that you have other duties which must be more important than sleeping in our dusty old convent.
>
> **Lus:** Of course. The accommodations you're used to are what? A tree in the rain somewhere?
>
> **Thar:** Yes. We've never slept in a tree in the rain.
>
> **Dorn:** General. Stick around, waiting for the Emperor to get better. The instant that he finds out about your failure in Aundair...
>
> **Thar:** What do you know about failure? Why do you feel what happened in Aundair is failure on my part?
>
> **Dorn:** Your army lost the front with the orcs, and now they bear down on His Majesty. We may need to go to desperate measures to control the Horde. You understand what that means. Surely you remember the war plans.
>
> **Thar:** You think I would forget? The killing of Esther Crona, and that wall falling, is what caused this disaster.
>
> **Dorn:** And the betrayal of the Admiral. He lost control of Eden. We can't locate her. She could be anywhere. It's a disaster.
>
> **Thar:** What does that matter, once...
>
> **Dorn:** But you're the ranking officer. This is your responsibility. The Empress will be furious.
>
> **Thar:** I am responsible for the living. Your Empress is responsible for the dead. Eden is not alive.
>
> **Dorn:** She will be brought to heel. The troops you've lost will belong to us. And you will be a dust mote on the tapestry of history. So why don't you pack your things and get on the boat, old man.

### The fight

Eric opened it with a true strike on Lus Fayina, the director who had tortured him. *"Remember me?"* *"Eric, you're such a good boy. How could you do this to mommy?"* Dario drew Palefire, shouted *"Death to the Black Doves,"* stepped onto the table and cut into Dorn. The two hooded sisters, the Sorrowful Sisters, came out of the classroom and caught Eric in a Cradle of Pain. Gemma called the Bright Dawn and went for her old teacher, and saved against Dorn's banishment: *"You stupid old man. Be gone."* When Dario's smite went into her shoulder, Dorn cried out: *"What did I ever do to you, Veggie Man?"* Mary Christmas raised her scythes in an X, and six dead guards climbed out of the ground.

Eric held up his holy symbol. *"But you forgot one thing. A negative times a negative is a positive."* His turn undead burned the six guards to nothing and drove the Sorrowful Sisters screaming back into the classroom. Dorn cast *dispel magic* on Thar, and the *seeming* fell away from Gemma. *"Who are you? What is this? No! What is this? Help! Help! Help! Call the guards!"* Gemma tripped her twice, pinned her against a bookcase and stabbed her in the gut. Maelis Dorn died, and her blood ran down the shelves.

Lus Fayina fled down the corridor with Eric's spirit guardians on her, and he caught her in the next room, where she died. Dario cut Mary Christmas's throat with one swing and took her head off with the next. There was a second Mary Christmas in the classroom. Dario called his flying panther, Rum Tum, hauled Eric up behind him, and rode through the room with Eric's guardians around them. Novices and Sorrowful Sisters died in the light, Gemma's hellish rebuke finished another, and Dario destroyed the second Mary Christmas from Rum Tum's back. The two white knights died at the door, and the last two Sorrowful Sisters were cornered in a room where novices were chained.

### Saturnia

Saturnia begged for her life. *"I follow the light of Aerenal. What they've done with it here is blasphemous. It's disgusting. They only want me because of who my family is."* Her grandfather is [E'noch](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/). She had never met him. He sent for her, she was cast out of Aerenal, and she was made a Sister of the Blood in the Westhouse for her final training. Eric promised her protection. Dario told her: *"The rebellion is inevitable."*

The girls in red are her cousins, from houses descended from the House of Vol. They are trained to tend the Emperor in his condition and kept as reserves to bear his children or his offspring's. The Empress will let no one else bear the first heir. After that, all of them are to be given to the heir. *"They say his lust will be insatiable."* Anyone who sets foot on the palace grounds without the anointing of a Sister of the Blood falls asleep *"like an innocent babe, long enough for one of the guards to come over and cut your throat."* Saturnia knows the anointing, and she gave them red robes.

*"The Emperor is not a man. He is the dragon, whom we call the Crimson Sun."* Long ago the dragon mated with Minara Vol, and their child was [Erandis Vol](/people/erandis-vol/), half-dragon and half-elf. The dragons of Argonnessen and the elves of Aerenal exterminated the House of Vol and its cadet houses: Qualanthri, Crona and Dwyn. Her people's story is that the dragon was all but destroyed protecting Erandis, and its essence was reduced to a small artifact, kept as a mortal secret by a few very old elves for thousands of years, for a future in which Erandis could be brought back. Uriel's Mark of Death is said to exceed any since Erandis. *"Somehow that dragon survived and is the Emperor. I can't tell you how, but I can tell you that my grandfather is responsible."* The final initiation of a Sister of the Blood is to meet E'noch. He is somewhere in the Lazaret, she thinks below the hospital. *"He is tremendously old."*

### The archive

The next room was full of students, and Dario threw in a ball of phlogiston. Beyond it was the Imperial Archive. As the party fought its way in, a man's voice barked orders from the back of the room:

> **Merkin:** Wait for them to get in, and then we're gonna make a break for it!
>
> **A voice:** Yes, sir!

A Sorrowful Sister knocked Dario off Rum Tum unconscious, and the voice came again:

> **Merkin:** We have to go! No, wait! Wait for my signal, you hear!

Eric brought Dario back. Gemma *misty stepped* in front of the man at the end of the shelves and began cutting, and only then knew him: Vice Admiral Freyd Merkin, the officer who had sent her here as [Deirdre Moro](/people/deirdre-moro/) and had forced Deirdre to prosecute her own sister. He did not know her face. His pistol misfired, and his attaché shot Gemma in the gut. She took him by the neck and stabbed him in the gut.

> **Gemma:** Your Star Advocate failed her mission, sir.

Blood came out of his mouth. She walked straight up into the attaché's face: *"Shoot me. Shoot me, bitch."* Dario killed the last sister as she ran.

Before the Doves made the Westhouse an academy, the archive was the Emperor's own office and record room. It holds titles, treaties, births and deaths, catalogues of military hardware, maps, and files on the Doves and the Emperor. Dario put the bodies of Dorn, Lus Fayina and Mary Christmas in his bag of holding, and Merkin and his attaché went into another bag. Eric and Saturnia found records from before the war of the King of Galifar's attempts to cure his son, the future Kaius I, and of successful treatments by an elvish shaman called E'noch. The records stop abruptly.

Behind the leather cover of a book was a bundle of letters in a very old high Elvish that uses even Sylvan vocabulary, written to be sent back to Aerenal and never sent, and unsigned. They are written in an informative, matter-of-fact, almost ceremonial manner. Gemma read them. They tell that the dragon did die in the Purge, and that its essence was reduced to a tiny fraction in an artifact which the houses of Dwyn, Crona and Qualanthri protected for millennia. Their goal and their prophecy was to bring back Erandis Vol and the time when the Mark of Death was ascendant, when they could come out of the shadows. The nearly destroyed dragon could not be resurrected, so the elves of these houses devised a plan to place its essence in an infant, giving the dragon full domination over a mortal body, in the confidence that over time it would shed its human form and take on its full and natural form as an ancient dragon.

The letters read:

::::letters
:::letter{leaf="I"}
Crona and I offered the king of Galifar a cure for his ailing heir. We told the king some of what would follow, including that his son would become very powerful and effectively immortal. We did not tell him that his child would be replaced, killed, and turned into a vessel for the dragon.
:::

:::letter{leaf="II"}
The child has woken with the full consciousness of itself as the dragon.
:::

:::letter{leaf="III"}
To commit itself to a human body, the Crimson Sun will invest its power in a binding ritual with a terrific sword carried by Crona. This will invest a large piece of His Majesty's power. While it is weak, I am worried this will compound Crona's power and influence.
:::

:::letter{leaf="IV"}
I have stayed in the background. In my own time, I have undergone the rite of Cain to expend my life and experiment with true blood magic. I know you will not like this decision, but it is the right one. I have killed my sire, and I am immortal. I do not enslave others freely, but I have experimented with my blood. And I have developed a new form of it, which can be taken by mortals and expend their life more quickly, speeding them toward undeath and in control.
:::

:::letter{leaf="V"}
The Dragon Emperor understands that his transformation is not going to happen, and that he might live and die as Kaius, a little longer lived than most mortals. He has asked Crona and I for a solution. I have put one forward. He knows the risks. He would be my thrall, unless I feed him in turn. He has made the terms very clear. Crona will bind me and hold his sword at my throat, until His Majesty rises, comes and drinks of me. I will be true to my word. But even as a true vampire, he will be obsessed with my blood.
:::
::::

Gemma guessed the writer was [Gabrielle Duin](/people/gabriel-duin/). Eric said it could as easily be E'noch. The party planned to go to the residence next as elven sisters with Saturnia, since the Empress admits only family and the undead who serve her. At the end of the night they learned that drinking from the fountains gives a long rest. Eric: *"What's it gonna do? Turn me into a talking octopus?"*

## Revelations

- The statue of Uriel at the top of the Stairs of Her Mercy was a wedding gift from Aerenal nearly thirteen years ago. It now bears the name Uriel I.
- A hole is drilled through the inscription on the Tomb of Kaius I.
- The Empress is said to be about to bear her fourteenth child. The Emperor was said to be in surgery.
- The Admiral has lost control of Eden.
- There were two Sister Mary Christmases.
- The Sisters of the Blood are elves of houses descended from the House of Vol, kept to bear the children of the Emperor and his heir.
- The palace grounds put anyone without a Sister of the Blood's anointing to sleep.
- Saturnia's grandfather is E'noch, who is somewhere in the Lazaret.
- Saturnia says the Emperor is the dragon called the Crimson Sun, father of Erandis Vol's line.
- Unsent letters in the Imperial Archive say the dragon's essence was put into the infant son of the King of Galifar, and that the writer later made the Emperor a vampire on the writer's own blood.
- The fountains of the Lazaret give a long rest.

## Consequences

The leadership of the Black Doves in the Westhouse is dead: Maelis Dorn, Lus Fayina and both Mary Christmases. Freyd Merkin is dead. The bodies are hidden in the party's bags. Saturnia is with the party, with red robes and the anointing, and E'noch is somewhere below the hospital. The party is in the Imperial Archive of the Westhouse, about 300 feet from the palace, with one door still unopened. All three reached 14th level.
