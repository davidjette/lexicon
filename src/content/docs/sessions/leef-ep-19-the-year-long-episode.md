**Leef arc · Episode 19 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 19 — The Year-Long Episode** is the nineteenth and last session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played at Leef on 12 December 2025, it covered a year of downtime after the death of LeBeefe, in which each character was given a downtime mission: [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) to the Dwarflands and [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) to Breland. It ended with the trio, in JL's words, "firmly established as heroes of the realm" with "serious roots in Talenta". A year passed between the Leef / Newham arc and the Sharn arc. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07; 2025-12-11; 2025-12-14)</small>

## Summary

Before the session JL set homework:

> Some homework before the next session. Each of you will be given a downtime mission. Dario to the Dwarflands, Gemma to Breeland. Think of answers to these questions….
> What was the mission?
> What complications did you encounter?
> You weren’t 100% successful, but what intel did you gain about the empire?

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07)</small>

**Dario's mission.** Dave's answer: Dario went to try to get a full alliance with the dwarves, to form a breakaway union from the Empire. The dwarves did not agree to join the rebellion outright, but would potentially support it with materiel and funds. Dave wrote that "this would be devastating to Dario but Orange Chicken would not be suprised". Her proverb on it: an alliance of unrelated dwarves is "more rare than a hairless bridegroom". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small>

**Gemma's mission.** Gemma was sent to Breland. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07)</small> In Nico's later account, it was in that downtime year that Gemma came by the teleportation chip she used in Sharn, "found on Cobb Cornwell, labeled as a gift from Hallorn to Faith", and her "complication" for her secret rebellion mission that year "was eventually gonna catch up to her", which meant the chip would one day be discovered. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-05-09)</small> The Sharn arc itself began, in Nico's account, because "Digma sent Gemma to Zilspar and while there on a mission found the info on Cron Cobwell about the Pose opening and the Ghost Chip." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-21)</small>

**Eric.** JL wrote that "Eric tried on shorts for the first time out on the coast. That might be part of his new look." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>

**The end of the year.** Dave: "loved the year long episode jeff". He listed what Dario would leave behind if he went to Sharn: "wife and kid, commander of the rebel forces, ambassador to the dwarves". JL gave Dario the rank of Field Marshall of Talenta: "I think you need some epaulettes to signify your rank of Field Marshall of Talenta." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> On the town's name Dave wrote, "Yeah “Talenta” or the “talenta republic” the town can stay Leef but long term we’ll probably rename it after whichever one of us dies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

The next session was [Sharn Ep 1](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/). Before it, [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) handed the party "a sealed dossier, passed from a trusted rebel contact in Sharn", and the contact was to arrive in [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/) "within a day’s time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

## Revelations

- Dario is a husband and father, commander of the rebel forces and ambassador to the dwarves, and holds the rank of Field Marshall of Talenta. His wife is Princess Orange Chicken, one of the defenders of Leef in [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>
- The dwarves will not join the rebellion outright, and may support it with materiel and funds. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small>
- Eric's dragonmark lets him cast *phantom steed*: "Mostly an overland travel spell… but… it does have a speed of 100 ft per round." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>
- The chronology that follows: the Leef / Newham arc, then one year, then the Sharn arc, then three years, then Thronehold and the Korth arc.
