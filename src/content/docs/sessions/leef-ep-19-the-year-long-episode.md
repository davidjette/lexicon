**Leef arc · Episode 19 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 19 — The Year-Long Episode** is the nineteenth and last session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played at Leef on 12 December 2025, it covered a year of downtime after the death of LeBeefe, in which each character was given a downtime mission: [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) to the Dwarflands and [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/) to Breland. It ended with the trio, in JL's words, "firmly established as heroes of the realm" with "serious roots in Talenta". A year passed between the Leef / Newham arc and the Sharn arc. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07; 2025-12-11; 2025-12-14)</small>

## Summary

Before the session JL set homework:

> Some homework before the next session. Each of you will be given a downtime mission. Dario to the Dwarflands, Gemma to Breeland. Think of answers to these questions….
> What was the mission?
> What complications did you encounter?
> You weren’t 100% successful, but what intel did you gain about the empire?

<small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07)</small>

**The return.** The session opened with LeBeefe dead, Newham in wreckage and the party home in Leef: Eric asleep on his bed covered in gore, and Gemma at the bar of the Cat & Biscuits raising a glass with the Colonel to the fall of Newham. In the morning Orange Chicken told Dario she was pregnant. News came from the Colonel's intercepts that Rekkenmark scientists judged the wreck of Newham uninhabitable for a hundred years; the contaminated territory, twelve thousand square miles from Karrnath to Q'barra and named the New Mourning, cut Leef and the Dwarflands off from the rest of the Empire, and its flora and fauna were already mutating. An eight-foot solid gold bust of Grand Duke LeBeefe, "heroic protector of the empire", was unveiled outside the Hall of Justice in Karrnath. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Leef rebuilt.** A week after the return the warforged of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) left for Cyre with a note and a gift, signed from "Daddy Locke". Refugees from Newham made new lives in Leef: Bunzo the magician and his cat set up shop, the vampires opened a twenty-four-hour massage parlour, and Deuce Loosely's rogues started a smuggling ring. Grill Wizmo repaired the party's airship, the Falcon. The Falcons' Workshop grew: Dario built a barracks, Gemma an armory, and Eric a humble fane to Mat-Mathany; its hirelings came to include the orc Merry at the barracks, Wizmo at the armory and the dwarf Pina Colada at the sanctuary. Strange psionic beasts began to appear on the outskirts of town. Gemma worked with her old patron Benjen to turn the arid land outside Leef into farmland. Eric ran for office and was elected comptroller unopposed, the only choice on the ballot, and the old lightning-rail depot became a meeting hall for the township. Dario turned an old building on the north side of town into a redoubt. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**News from the west.** The imperial war against the Uruk Republic went to the plans of Uriel and Esther Crona: Crona's troops occupied much of Droaam and annexed it to Breland, behind a wall of arcane energy said to be held up by a thousand casters. The township lived in dread of the reprisal of Esther Crona, the Queen of Whispers, Grand Inquisitor of the Empire and commander-in-chief of its armies. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Dario's mission.** Dave's answer: Dario went to try to get a full alliance with the dwarves, to form a breakaway union from the Empire. The dwarves did not agree to join the rebellion outright, but would potentially support it with materiel and funds. Dave wrote that "this would be devastating to Dario but Orange Chicken would not be suprised". Her proverb on it: an alliance of unrelated dwarves is "more rare than a hairless bridegroom". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small>

**Gemma's mission.** Gemma was sent to Breland. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-07)</small> In Nico's later account, it was in that downtime year that Gemma came by the teleportation chip she used in Sharn, "found on Cobb Cornwell, labeled as a gift from Hallorn to Faith", and her "complication" for her secret rebellion mission that year "was eventually gonna catch up to her", which meant the chip would one day be discovered. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-05-09)</small> The Sharn arc itself began, in Nico's account, because "Digma sent Gemma to Zilspar and while there on a mission found the info on Cron Cobwell about the Pose opening and the Ghost Chip." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2026-02-21)</small>

**Eric's mission.** A gnome artificer, Potpie, an older veteran in shining heavy armour with a history with Digma, arrived in a small two-seater airship as envoy of the Librarians, a faction of gnomish craftsmen and scholars in Darguun-Zilargo who were legendary outlaw heroes of the rebellion. Beset by Hobgoblin pirates and brigands, the Librarians had let [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/)'s soldiers into their territory to fight the warlords. Eric was sent with her in secret: "It seems I have a secret mission to go on." His dossier records the mission. Eric Passagemarked, clerk, was dispatched to the shores of Khraal to find funds for the Librarians' war on two fronts; he began a full accounting of their ledgers, was abducted by pirates in a daylight raid on the Librarians' fortress, and was released after negotiating an armistice between the rival pirate gangs and the Librarians, under which the warlords pledged to join the rebel fight. The pirates then broke the armistice, took the Librarian stronghold, drove the imperial forces west and declared a free and independent Hobgoblin State of Khraal, a full member of the rebel alliance. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back: Eric's secret Khraal mission (dossier))</small> Eric came home relaxed and confident, with a hobgoblin girlfriend a foot taller than him, Fronque, and a tattoo on his neck in Goblin. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The missions.** Each character came back with gains: Gemma from Breland with Thieves' Cant, proficiency with an herbalism kit and a helm of teleportation; Eric from Darguun-Zilargo with Deep Speech, proficiency with the bagpipes and a shield of repulsion; Dario from the Mror Holds with Giant, proficiency with carpenter's tools and horseshoes of speed. Dario was helped by a hill giant named Bungus. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**The Colonel.** On the day Orange Chicken went into labour, Colonel [Thadric Rourke](/people/thadric-rourke/), hero of the Last War, died peacefully in his sleep. Zero consecrated his body, Digma was overcome as he tried to give the eulogy, and Gemma was asked to give it. Dario's child was named Red Dragon Shrimp. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Gemma.** One autumn night the Dread Pirate [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/), eighty-three years old, landed a small corvair beside her crops, went to one knee and asked her to marry him, to collect a favour she owed him. <small>(JL's DM notes: The Unforeseen Strike Back DM Notes)</small>

**Eric.** JL wrote that "Eric tried on shorts for the first time out on the coast. That might be part of his new look." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>

**The end of the year.** Dave: "loved the year long episode jeff". He listed what Dario would leave behind if he went to Sharn: "wife and kid, commander of the rebel forces, ambassador to the dwarves". JL gave Dario the rank of Field Marshall of Talenta: "I think you need some epaulettes to signify your rank of Field Marshall of Talenta." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small> On the town's name Dave wrote, "Yeah “Talenta” or the “talenta republic” the town can stay Leef but long term we’ll probably rename it after whichever one of us dies". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

The next session was [Sharn Ep 1](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/). Before it, [Digma Beeve](/people/digma-beeve/) handed the party "a sealed dossier, passed from a trusted rebel contact in Sharn", and the contact was to arrive in [the Republic of Talenta](/organizations/the-republic-of-talenta/) "within a day’s time". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-18)</small>

## Revelations

- Dario is a husband and father, commander of the rebel forces and ambassador to the dwarves, and holds the rank of Field Marshall of Talenta. His wife is Princess Orange Chicken, one of the defenders of Leef in [the liberation of Leef](/history/the-liberation-of-leef/). <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>
- The dwarves will not join the rebellion outright, and may support it with materiel and funds. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-08)</small>
- Eric bears the Mark of Passage on his right hand, a mark of no noble house, and is officially **Eric Passagemarked**; the mark let him pass freely into LeBeefe's Black Fortress.
- Eric is comptroller of Leef. His secret mission to Khraal ended with the Free State of Khraal, a Hobgoblin state, joining the rebel alliance.
- Colonel Thadric Rourke died in his sleep on the day Red Dragon Shrimp was born.

- Eric's dragonmark lets him cast *phantom steed*: "Mostly an overland travel spell… but… it does have a speed of 100 ft per round." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-12-14)</small>
- The chronology that follows: the Leef / Newham arc, then one year, then the Sharn arc, then three years, then Thronehold and the Korth arc.
