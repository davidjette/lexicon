**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 20 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 20 — The Dinner Party** is the twentieth session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 30 October 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 19 — The Westhouse](/sessions/korth-ep-19-the-westhouse/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) left the burning Westhouse wearing the faces of the women they had killed there, went down under the hospital, and broke in on a vampires' feast. They destroyed [E'noch Qualanthri](/people/priest-e-noch-qualanthri/) and his household in their coffins, and freed the gold dragon Aurorix from a pool of blood.

> Your eternal nightfall will be interrupted by the Bright Dawn.
> — Gemma Corso, to E'noch Qualanthri

## Summary

### Out of the Westhouse

In the archive, with the fire climbing the wall of the next room, Saturnia told them what her house had dreamed of: *"to all go back to this distant past where our kind mated with a dragon. And even though I thought it was a fantasy, it seems my grandfather has made it come true."* She had never met him. In Aerenal the undying court are Bael-Norn, undead in harmony with nature, and she called vampires a perversion of the same magic: *"It's why they were banned from Aerenal and fled to Khorvaire, for their work with the undead and magic of the blood, and for fornicating with dragons."*

> **Saturnia:** I've been brought here to be a broodmare for this line. My cousin, she sits in the throne now, but I think if my grandfather had his way, she would step aside or die. She has failed in her mission.
>
> **Gemma:** So you just want to fuck a dragon, a hot dragon.
>
> **Saturnia:** I'm willing to help you do what you must. Even if it leads to the death of my grandfather. He is a monster.

She said she could anoint them with the blood of the Dragon Emperor to enter the residence, a hunting lodge now turned mostly into a hospice, but that it would not last long. She had been there twice: once to learn to wash the Emperor, and once to meet his children, *"the most horrible children I have ever met."* Dario: *"Every one of them is a potential Uriel. They all die."*

Gemma cast *seeming*: herself as [Maelis Dorn](/people/sister-maelis-dorn/), Eric as [Lus Fayina](/people/luz-fayana/), Dario as Sister Mary Christmas. They took Dorn's keys, her small iron bell and her adamantine seal, the one whose stamp had closed the doors at Korranberg. Dario smashed a window so that General Thar could have flown out of it. On the stairs they met Sister Mortis.

> **Gemma:** General Thar is a traitor. He attacked us. He's in cahoots.

Dario, realising he had never heard Mary Christmas speak, only pointed down the stairs and glared. Asked after the Empress, a sister answered: *"She's at three centimeters, Sister."* All three drank from the fountain in the hall, and it refreshed them as a night's sleep would. Gemma tried to scry on [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) with a dead fortune teller's crystal ball and saw only fog.

### The hospital

The main stair took them down to the concourse, where people stepped aside for Dorn with *"By her mercy."* A battle sister reported cannon fire near the pilgrims' camp, from a force that had landed on the beaches. Gemma told her to send the strongest toward it.

> **Battle Sister:** They will not step foot on Nos Matre. Because according to the plan, the instant that they do, we ring that bell. You sisters, head to the belfry.
>
> **Eric:** Don't be so hasty with that bell, by the way.
>
> **Gemma:** The bell needs to be timed perfectly. Three centimeters is way too soon.

A nun asked Lus Fayina if she should go back to work. Eric: *"You should put your head between your knees and kiss your ass goodbye. The place is burning down."*

In a room of Sorrowful Sisters, [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) lay strapped to a table with her chest open and a glowing red teardrop ruby wired in where her heart should be, her limbs and wings rebuilt with machinery, and a new mask on her face. *"Esther is not dead,"* Dario said. *"That's not a corpse."*

Beyond her was the morgue, with a crematorium, a dragon skull and a button in the floor. Gemma thought Saturnia should be the one to step on it.

> **Saturnia:** So I'm to meet my grandfather after all these decades. He wants to drink all of my blood and turn me into his slave, or preserve me as a virgin for my cousin's child to mate with, so that future emperor half-dragons can be Qualanthris.

Three vampire spawn burst out of the coffins and one bit Saturnia to the floor. When they were dead the wall slid away and stairs rose to a gate much older than the hospital.

### The feast

Dario kicked the door in on a table of well-dressed elvish vampires eating mortals from bowls of blood.

> **Vampire:** I didn't realize we had guests for dinner.

> **Vampire:** Who are you to enter our lair, Director Inquisitor? You know this place is off-limits to you and your inferior blood. And what is this thing that you have tramping in here with feces on its paws?

Radiant light did terrible things to them. One, thrown down by Gemma, turned into a bat and flew off up a shaft over a white alabaster fountain, its walls set with skulls. The rooms beyond held coffins standing in soil and five starving spawn in cells. Gemma and Eric took off their stolen faces. *"Saturnia, I'd like you to meet the real us."* Looking over the old Aerenal gold on the table, Eric said: *"I said melt it and distribute it to the people."*

Down a paved tunnel the tiling ran out against an ancient stone structure buried in soil, with dull chanting inside it and green light behind iron bars clogged with skulls. Eric heard a voice in his head: *"That insane bitch. Look what she's been doing. Even I wasn't this irresponsible. She's going to destroy everything."* Gemma forced a lever and a landslide of bones fell out. Then, behind them, Saturnia screamed.

### E'noch

Behind the double doors a wall of blood-soaked rock ended in a round pool of blood, with a gold dragon half sunk in it. Saturnia lay on a table. Three vampires stood there: Lady Ophelia, who looked like the Empress, holding a skull; Lord Lieutenant Trillo Dwyn, in black armour; and a slight robed figure who turned his back on them.

> **E'noch:** Lady Ophelia, please make sure our guest is unharmed. Trillo, please destroy the dragonborn. Please save the woman for me.

He blighted Gemma when she shot at him: *"Do you think my granddaughter learned about disease on her own? We're all doctors in this family."* He covered the room in a *circle of death* and laid his hand on her, and she collapsed with her life going out of her. Eric's *cure wounds* got her up. Saturnia's throat was cut over a ritual ewer, and Ophelia bent down to drink.

E'noch raised his arms and spawn came out of the iron maidens along the wall. Eric clanged his dagger on his shield.

> **Eric:** By the power of Matt Matheny, I command you to go fuck yourself!

The turning burned the spawn and sent Ophelia clawing at the walls to get away. E'noch did not seem to notice it. He had found Gemma, invisible across the room.

> **E'noch:** So you've uncovered our entire vampire conspiracy. Congratulations. But we've known you were coming, Gemma Corso.

She knew his *finger of death* would beat her father's amulet, and as she clutched it she heard his voice: *"You can't give up. I'm sorry I did this to you. But you gotta survive."* She lived, with her blood floating across the room and sinking into E'noch's skin. Eric called down Ada Lovelace, his celestial. Dario's smite blinded Trillo, and Gemma stabbed him in the back of the neck as he crawled for the stairs. He went to mist.

> **E'noch:** This is ridiculous! We've been planning this for centuries!

Dario flew to the pool, put his hand on the dragon and said: *"By the light of the Silver Flame, awaken!"* It rose up out of the blood, and Dario came back and put Palefire through E'noch's body.

### The master vampire

The blood on the floor boiled up into a long-eared, bat-winged creature with skin nearly ebony.

> **E'noch:** I have worked across the centuries, arranged dynasties, destroyed entire kingdoms. I have wrought hell on this continent. Only for you to come and defile my moment of glory. You will be but drops in the pond of blood in which I bathe my anus.
>
> **Dario:** I will purge the earth of you and your entire bloodline. You're a blasphemy on this world. And I'm only sorry that my ancient ancestors couldn't finish the job.
>
> **E'noch:** You are a failure. Your race is nothing. We are your superiors. We are your masters.

Dario blinded him. His shriek shook the room. The dragon crashed into him and breathed fire in his face, and Saturnia got up, holding her throat, and went for her cousin with a dagger. The [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/) burst his leg open down to the old bones. Eric drove his dagger upward and unseamed him from below, and he fell apart and went to mist.

> **Ophelia:** No, stop! Stop! I hate my cousin too! If you hit me, I'll kill her! I'll kill her and I'll become Empress and I'll do whatever you want! I'm the next one in line.

Gemma's blast knocked the skull from her hands, and she turned invisible and was gone.

### Aurorix

The dragon's name is Aurorix, and he had been there for years, bled so that his blood could be mixed with that of mortals into a concoction that sustains the Emperor. When Gemma mentioned [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) he snorted fire and burned off her eyebrows: *"Shudder to think that my blood were mixed with his. He is a villain of the highest order."*

> **Aurorix:** I have foreseen you and this rescue. It will not end well. For me, that is for certain. But I am here. I will help you.
>
> **Aurorix:** They sustain the Emperor. But his coma, it is a lie. They have the Emperor, who is no mortal, in torpor. E'noch turned him into a vampire to make him immortal, and his dragon blood was inflamed by this. But vampires have weaknesses, weaknesses that E'noch and his spawn have exploited. So they sustain him in this vile blood potion, but they steal his essence. If the Crimson Sun were to dawn again, it would mean the end of all things. They have done the world a favor by entrapping that member of my kin. His true name is Viagra.
>
> **Gemma:** That tracks.

He told them that a half-dragon, half-elf with the Mark of Death would mean the end of all things, and that the old Volite houses keep the mark secret while all their scions bear it. *"The Empress is just one among many. That one there, you should destroy as well."* He meant Saturnia, standing with her neck held closed and looking very like a young Uriel, where the fight had torn her robe to show a black mark running up from her midriff. Gemma refused. Aurorix knew the Bright Dawn by sight. He had fought the Crimson Sun the first time it tried to breed with these people, and he said it was Providence. E'noch, he told them, talked of nothing but the plot to breed with the Dragon Emperor, and brought his grandchildren in here to tell them they were next in line once his granddaughter died or failed for the final time. *"We have to finish destroying the Qualanthris. And the Dwyns. And the Cronas."* He took a humanoid shape and walked ahead of them. *"Follow me."*

### The coffins

In the room of coffins Dario showed them how it was done. He opened one and dropped his sword through the heart of the sleeper, who struggled, boiled and went to dust. Gemma opened another and found E'noch with his arms crossed on his chest, very well preserved. She spoke, and drove the Bright Dawn in. Black smoke filled the room, a red light in his eyes flashed white, and he was gone to ash. Eric put his dagger through Trillo Dwyn, and Dario destroyed Ophelia and the rest. Afterward, Aurorix went and burned the spawn in their cells.

At the mouth of the tunnel the dragon stopped.

> **Aurorix:** Something terrible is down there. My God. It's still there then. A very, very long time ago, long before most of your kind ever came to this place, a people inhabited this forest. They worshipped an ancient god. It was a god of death, yes, but also a god of renewal, of recycling, of taking from what was lost what could still be gained. They built a sept here before there was a hill. A great sept of bones stood on this peninsula. Over the years it was buried, more and more bodies, buried in ceremonial graves all around this holy site, until people forgot that the temple was even here. They built another one on top of it. People think that one's ancient. But these fools, if they've dug up that place, are meddling in magics which they can never control or understand.

Gemma drew the Polygon in the dirt and asked what it was. *"It's a rhombus."* He kept walking. *"I believe our death lies in that direction."*

### Back through the hospital

Going back, Gemma stopped at Esther's table.

> **Gemma:** Is she at a stage where she can understand?

The undead sister nodded.

> **Gemma:** Your old general betrayed us. And he is working with your sister, Eden.
>
> **Esther:** Where is Calcifer?
>
> **Gemma:** Alive.

Esther's voice came through the mask, muffled and mechanical. On the way out Eric saw a mummified heart in a jar. They marched back along the concourse as a church bell began to ring the alarm, and came to the Westhouse fountain with sisters running to their posts.

## Revelations

- Esther Crona is alive on a table under the hospital, with a ruby in her chest. She asked after [Calcifer](/people/calcifer/).
- A mummified heart stands in a jar in the same room.
- E'noch Qualanthri's household was hidden behind the hospital morgue.
- E'noch was expecting Gemma Corso by name.
- A gold dragon named Aurorix was held in a pool of blood there for years.
- Aurorix says the Volite houses keep the Mark of Death secret, and that all their scions bear it. Saturnia does.
- The Sept of Bones, the temple of an ancient god of death and renewal, lies buried under the hill, almost directly below the Polygon.
- The shaft over the vampires' fountain runs up beneath the Tomb of Kaius I.
- Aurorix says the Emperor's coma is a lie: E'noch made him a vampire, and he is held in torpor on a potion of dragon blood while his essence is stolen.
- Aurorix says the Emperor's true name is Viagra.
- Saturnia says E'noch would have had Uriel step aside or die for failing in her mission.
- Battle sisters stand under orders to ring the bell the instant an enemy reaches Nos Matre.
- A force with cannon has landed on the beaches near the pilgrims' camp.

## Consequences

E'noch Qualanthri, Lady Ophelia, Trillo Dwyn and their household are destroyed. Aurorix is free and with the party, in human shape, and he wants Saturnia dead. Saturnia is with them, still able to anoint them for the residence. The party is at the Westhouse fountain with the alarm ringing and the Empress in labour.
