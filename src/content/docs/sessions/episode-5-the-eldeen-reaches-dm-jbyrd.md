**The Unforeseen · Campaign 1 · Episode 5 · DM: JByrd · Report filed 11 Sep 2020**

**Episode 5 "The Eldeen Reaches" DM: JByrd** is the fifth session of [The Unforeseen](/organizations/the-unforeseen/), Campaign 1, and was run by JByrd. The party carried crates of halfling slaves west to the Eldeen Reaches, cleared the Fire Finger tower for a Greenheart ship owner, and struck a bargain with Ivanka Riefenstahl, "Queen" of the Children of Winter, which the Empire ratified: an alliance with the Dark Druids in exchange for their leadership of the Eldeen Reaches. The slaves never reached the Demon Waste. The session is also the first on which [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) raised an undead.

<small>Campaign 1's four player characters — [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/), [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/), [John C. LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/) and [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) — become the antagonists of Campaign 2. Episodes 1-10 below are the Campaign 1 sequence; the [Sharn](/sessions/sharn-ep-1-welcome-to-sharn/) and [Korth](/sessions/korth-ep-1-three-years-later/) arcs number their episodes separately.</small>

## Summary

LeBeefe tries to keep his promise and takes the group, along with crates of halfling slaves west by train to the Eldeen Reaches to a small Druid town called Greenheart. Once in Greenheart they look to hire a boat to take them and the slave cargo up the river into the Demon Waste to sell the slaves to LeBeefes contact Abaddon.

In Greenheart a ship owner named Natty Dredds ask the group to find his lost brother at the Fire Finger tower, in return he would take them to the Demon Waste. The group fought their way through the tower and discovered a captive rebel with a letter stating that the Dark Druids of the Eldeen Reaches (aka The Children of Winter) were looking to ally with the empire.

Once on the ship the group is approached by Ivanka Riefenstahl the "Queen" of the Children of Winter who worship the Gloaming. A deal was made with her that the group would approach [the Imperial Council](/organizations/the-imperial-council-imperial-assembly/) about allying with the Dark Druids, in turn confirming the Children of Winter as the leaders of the Eldeen Reaches and Ivanka the Queen. [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) and [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) take this info back to the council in Korth. Uriel and Esther are rewarded in turn.

> This is the first time Uriel performed Necromatic Sex Magic on a corpse (RIP F. Lighter) to rise an undead.

## Revelations

- The Gloaming - A portal to Mabar was opened during the last war, The Gloaming is a spreading darkness bringing about undead and other nasties. The Dark Druids / Children of Winter worship it.
- Sex Magic - This is the first time [Uriel Qualanthri](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) performed Necromatic Sex Magic on a corpse (RIP F. Lighter) to rise an undead. In doing so it created a ghoul.
- The slaves were never taken to the demon waste / Abaddon, instead the dark druids took them and we received payment from them.

## Consequences

The Empire gained an ally in the west and Uriel gained her signature capability. Both are paid off later: the alliance with the Children of Winter is finalised in [Episode 6](/sessions/episode-6-aandb-6-months-later-the-tablet-dm-nico/), and the Eldeen Reaches are still contested ground three years after Sharn falls, when Crona's Wall collapses and Orc forces push into Dark Druid territory.
