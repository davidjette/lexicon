**Session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/) · [Sigil](/places/sigil/) · Played about early October 2025 · Episode number estimated**

**Dead Man's Hand Ep 13 — Fortune's Wheel** is a session of [Dead Man's Hand](/lore/dead-mans-hand/), run by Dave and played about early October 2025. Back in Sigil, the party rested in [the Hive Ward](/places/the-hive-ward/), went up to the Lady's Ward, met [Shemeska](/people/shemeska/) at [Fortune's Wheel](/places/fortunes-wheel/) and fought that night as prize fighters in her arena.

## The Hive

The party were resting in their adopted home of the Hive Ward, where they had made allies of the local gang of thieves and of [the Bleak Cabal](/organizations/the-bleak-cabal/), who run the Gatehouse, a hospital in the Ward. Their dirty apartment was in the Hive itself, a huge apartment complex in a concrete hexagonal structure at the center of the Ward. Everyone was exhausted and wounded. [Rodriel](/people/rodriel/) fell asleep on the couch at once, and [Lenore](/people/lenore/) started building tiny robots to make ammunition while she was out. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

## Deschain and Sons

[Shadowhigh](/people/shadowhigh/) and Lenore went to a gun shop called Deschain and Sons, the outlet and storefront of the finest engineer in Sigil, kept by an older dwarf woman, a mechanical engineer. Shadowhigh bought the Firebrand Revolver there. It came in a wooden case, with mother-of-pearl inlay and dragon and flame details, and she paid with a big bag of gold. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The Firebrand Revolver in its case on the counter of Deschain and Sons](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-firebrand-revolver.webp)
*The Firebrand Revolver in its case on the counter of Deschain and Sons.*

## The Lady's Ward

The next day the party went up to the Lady's Ward, past [the High Courts](/places/the-high-courts-of-sigil/) of the Hall of Justice and the Faceless Cloister, the great grey tower that is home to [the Sororia](/organizations/the-sororia/). <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The party in the Lady's Ward, under the Hall of Justice and the Faceless Cloister](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-ladys-ward.webp)
*The party in the Lady's Ward, under the Hall of Justice and the Faceless Cloister.*

They ended at Fortune's Wheel, an opulent magical casino filled with the denizens of Sigil in their finest, betting golden razorleafs at games and sentient slot machines. A yugoloth bouncer and a pit fiend in a fine suit and suspenders oversaw the service. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

## Bumpkin Countrymile

They asked to see the owner, whom they had met before, and were led to the VIP lounge. At its bar sat [Bumpkin Countrymile](/people/bumpkin-countrymile/), a farmer who struck it rich with an ore discovery and was whisked into a society he did not understand, where he had been conned out of his fortune and was now in debt to the casino. He was dejected, in expensive clothes that did not suit him, with one full and one half-full bright pink drink in front of him. Mach V did most of the questioning, and he and [Maverick](/people/maverick/) looked at each other knowingly, as if Bumpkin had let slip an important clue. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Bumpkin Countrymile at the VIP bar, between Mach V and Maverick](/images/gallery/dmh-bumpkin-countrymile.webp)
*Bumpkin Countrymile at the VIP bar, between Mach V and Maverick.*

## Shemeska

Shemeska, the arcanaloth who owns the casino, received them dressed in fine furs, feathers and velour. She gave them a stern warning about [the Deck of Many Things](/items/the-deck-of-many-things/), and paid them to fight in her arena that night as prize fighters. Maverick made the party's case while [Battlebeast](/people/battlebeast/) lounged against the wall, very comfortable. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

## The arena

They fought as a team in a mock battle, gladiator style, in a huge, magically lit indoor amphitheater. The match re-enacted a famous battle in which large tanks crossed a river, and a channel of real water crossed the center of the floor to stand for it. Their opponents were kobolds riding flaming two-headed bulls, three armored riflemen whose long rifles carried halberd-like blades, three flying imps, and two minotaurs tied to each other at the waist. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

Before the fight Battlebeast stood alone in a spotlight and held up a tiny white mouse to the crowd, reading the advertisement for Mousetrappers, the exterminators who sponsored the show. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Battlebeast holds up a white mouse for the Mousetrappers ad read](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-mousetrappers.webp)
*Battlebeast holds up a white mouse for the Mousetrappers ad read.*

Maverick cast a hypnotic pattern that rippled across the battlefield. Two kobolds, hypnotized, drooled and dropped their weapons as the bull under them skidded unconscious into the ground, and two imps fell out of the air behind them. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Maverick casts the hypnotic pattern](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-hypnotic-pattern.webp)
*Maverick casts the hypnotic pattern.*

![Hypnotized kobolds ride their bull into the ground as the imps fall](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-kobolds.webp)
*Hypnotized kobolds ride their bull into the ground as the imps fall.*

Another bull, as large as a truck, charged through the water at Battlebeast with two kobolds awake and dangerous on its back. He was gored and went face down, unconscious, in the bloody water. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The two-headed bull charges Battlebeast through the water](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-bull-charge.webp)
*The two-headed bull charges Battlebeast through the water.*

![Battlebeast goes under, gored](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-battlebeast-down.webp)
*Battlebeast goes under, gored.*

Lenore with her rifle, Shadowhigh with her flaming pistol and Mach V with his precision crossbow fired together into the fight. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![Lenore, Shadowhigh and Mach V fire into the fight, Shadowhigh's dragon beside them](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-firing-line.webp)
*Lenore, Shadowhigh and Mach V fire into the fight, Shadowhigh's dragon beside them.*

One of the two minotaurs was killed and raised as undead by the party, and it turned on the twin it was still roped to, who was caught by surprise in mid-swing. [Heyu](/people/heyu/) stood small in front of them. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>

![The raised minotaur turns on its twin, with Heyu in front of them](/images/gallery/dmh-fortunes-wheel-minotaurs.webp)
*The raised minotaur turns on its twin, with Heyu in front of them.*

The party won. The referee held up Battlebeast's bloodied arm to end the match, with bulls, kobolds and minotaurs strewn across the far side of the river, while two child-sized angelic performers swung on trapezes high above the well-dressed crowd and fireworks rained down on the arena. <small>(Illustration briefs)</small>
