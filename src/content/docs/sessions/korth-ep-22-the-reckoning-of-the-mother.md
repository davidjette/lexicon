**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 22 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 22 — The Reckoning of the Mother** is the twenty-second session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 13 November 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 21 — The Summer Palace](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) fought [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) twice: in the air over Nos Matre, where Dario broke the bell and took her head, and in the Sept of Bones under the hill, where she was destroyed. The child she bore could not be killed. The [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) was burned off the map behind them.

The passages set in italics are the narration as it was spoken at the table.

> For a while, the world was dark. You sucked the hope from the world. And now, after the long night, comes a brighter dawn.
> — Sir Dario Argentino, to the Holy Empress

## Summary

### The heather-covered hill

The session opened where the last one stopped: the party in the air on the back of the gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/), looking over the heather-covered hill of the Ivory Lazaret, once called Nos Matre, Our Mother, after the Sovereign who claimed that shape and name. Uriel was ringing the Hell's Bell and triggering the Eternal Nightfall. All of them were hurt, Aurorix worst. They had the potions against necrotic harm still in their blood and a single greatberry, which Dario carried and which they agreed to save for whoever needed it most.

> *As you guys fumble through your pockets, the rain turns into a downpour. The sky opens up and it feels like the sun is set. It's hard to even tell what time of day it is. A chorus of women's voices echoes out. It seems from all directions. And the bell is ringing. Every tone, it echoes in your bones. And it seems to be in time and in tune with this chorus.*
>
> *You look around at the hillside, which is getting drenched in rain. And there's almost like a pulse in the earth with every ringing of the bell. The wet earth seems to loosen, and in every direction, seemingly every three feet, the grass starts to turn to soil, flipping upside down. It's hard to even understand what's happening until you start to see humanoids slough off the soil and turn toward the temple.*
>
> *The temple itself is beginning to be backlit by a black and purple glow, because you're on this side of the temple. And in its shadow, its sort of corona from this purple light, you see the tiny figure of Uriel, holding her once white but now black scythe, and the glow of her orange tummy visible from a distance as she arms up toward the bell itself. You can't quite hear anything she's saying or doing, but you do hear this ethereal chorus.*

Aurorix called out that he could see many things flying nearby.

### The circle

Gemma was afraid of losing her concentration to the bell and did it anyway. She took hold of the right amulet of the two round her neck, the pewter pendant [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) had handed her at the pilgrim camp not two hours before.

> **Gemma:** Dawn, Silver Flame, Dawn, I need your help.

> *You hold it up, and as the darkness around Uriel thickens, your hand holding the amulet ripped off your neck glows with white light, like the Battle of Pelennor Fields. A single voice comes through the chorus, a high soprano singing the Silver Flame theme, and white light surrounds you and Dario and Eric and Aurorix. It drowns out the sound of the bell.*

Dario was exultant. The *circle of power* held around the four of them from then on: inside it the bell and the Empress's spells mostly broke on them, and what they resisted did them no harm at all. On the side of the temple they could see, the plaza was beginning to fill with Black Doves. The bell rang again, and all four shook it off.

Aurorix dashed for the tower, rising over the southern plaza.

> *As you start to crest over the very top of the central nave, you see the source of this purple and ultraviolet light is the Polygon that floats in the courtyard. Four sorrowful sisters look up at it, bolts of electricity seemingly frying their bodies as they channel the energy into it.*

[The Polygon](/items/the-polygon/) caught Eric's eye. He heard the Empress's voice in his head, *"Go, you disgusting pig! What are you doing here?"*, and laughter deep in his mind, and was otherwise untouched. On her orders a Choir of Novices on the ground began to toll at the dragon. Dario quickened himself, drank a potion and lit Palefire. Eric patted the dragon, tugged his scruff, and healed him and then himself.

> *Uriel is acting. As you approach her, she sees you coming. And her fury is absolute. It's not that she looks at you with hate. It's that she's almost transformed as it dawns on her that you are the people who slaughtered her children and killed her husband. And there's a smile that you see, as she is extremely confident that she will have her final revenge.*

She pointed a finger at Aurorix.

> **Uriel:** We crushed you. We turned you into gruel. You have nothing to say to me or my husband, who rules across all time.

Aurorix answered in a guttural Draconic. There was a peal of thunder.

> *Uriel puts a hand up and her crown glows white. And a cloud of white sisterly figures surround her.*

Then she flew up right beside them.

### The dragon falls

At the top of the next round the ground got more dangerous. *Rising from a lovely flower garden, you see five armed zombies crawl out of the ground. Perhaps the least of your worries now.* Gemma's blast pushed the Empress back ten feet. Then the Original tolled again.

> *You guys managed to shake off the horrible jangling in your skull that it creates. Every ring sounds like ten thousand sorrowful voices crying out for any type of relief. Every one of them makes your head hurt, Eric, as the bell and the light from the Polygon's prism seems to pulse together like a migraine that the world is having.*

Aurorix did not shake it off. He was overtaken by the bell, fell out of the sky, and was knocked senseless by the fall. The other three floated down on feather tokens into a ring of ravenous undead sisters looking up at them.

Dario reached down and laid his hands on the dragon.

> **Dario:** Wake up, my friend! There's work to do!

Rum Tum came from nowhere and jumped underneath him, and Dario flew straight for the tower with [Knell](/items/knell/) in his hand. He passed under the Empress, ignored her, and put himself at the edge of the belfry.

> **Uriel:** You filthy reptile! Get away from that!

Eric took hold of Gemma and stuck out his cheek for a kiss for good luck.

> **Gemma:** Good luck, short pants.

He took them both by *dimension door* up to the bell, leaving Aurorix in the courtyard.

> *You guys appear right near Dario, precariously crammed into a fairly small space at the top of this extremely high tower. There does not seem to be a staircase that leads up here or down. And you look out across the countryside, and there is Uriel, furious, her womb radiant, looking like it's going to explode in draconic fury at any moment.*

From up there Gemma could see west.

> *You still look out to the west toward the Pilgrim Camp, which you see is on fire despite the rain. There are the flashes of magic being cast. They are pointing back toward the camp at something which is approaching them, which you cannot see at the moment. They are all gathered around Fema Nolan, who is concentrating on a spell that seems to be protecting them. You also see the small band of revelers who you taught the song to, who are there playing through it. And you hear the march. They're retreating up the hill, but they obviously are looking behind them, at this massive, menacing, terrifying nexus of magic that is this big purple Polygon. You can tell they're stuck between going all the way up the hill and going back down to whatever they're running from.*

The Empress closed the distance and moved right up into their faces.

> *She is huge. Something about her current necromantic power has simply expanded. Her bone structure, everything is much more severe.*

Her guardians washed over them. *When you're in this zone, the choir of sister voices is extreme.* She drew Mourning Dove and cut Gemma and Eric with one swing.

> *The novices below begin to descend upon Aurorix. They take out these long ceremonial knives and just start stabbing him over and over again, ripping out golden scales and spilling blood all across the marble terrazzo below you.*

More risen guards came up out of the soil. *You then hear the sound of eldritch blasts echoing from across Nos Matre. You see the characteristic muzzle flashes of Eberronian firearms coming up the Stairs of Her Mercy.*

### The bell

Gemma struck the Empress with the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/), and it exploded in light. Her second stroke swung wide: the Empress looked up horrified for a moment, summoned a supernatural strength, and dodged.

> **Uriel:** You have Esther's sword.
>
> **Gemma:** I think she'll appreciate it when I bring it back to her.
>
> **Uriel:** Why don't you give it to me?

The bell chimed an arm's length away, louder than anywhere. Eric's ears rang until all he could hear was the rattling in his skull *and just slightly the cackling of Uriel as she watches you suffer.* He was deaf. The light of the Polygon flickered and turned volatile, its steady rise sparked with red and blue and green, as if something in it were fluctuating, changing, being disrupted.

Then Dario flew up to the bell, pulled out Knell, and brought it crashing down.

> *It strikes the bell, and all three of you are standing right next to it. In a moment so quick that you can't even quantify how fast it happens, you watch a split appear in the surface of the bell and get longer, from where he struck it up to the apex of the bell shape itself. At the same time, the bell seems to peel back open and suddenly reveal manifold layers, a shape as if an onion were being turned inside out and growing at the same time. It collapses into a rainbow of metal that suddenly feels sucked into a single point, and explodes.*
>
> *The force is tremendous and it collapses the tower. The tower around you begins to buckle, and the roof of the tower, because the beams around you give way, the roof above you suddenly is in free fall and is coming at you.*

[The Original](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) was destroyed. The roof fell on Gemma and half fell on the other two. She used the moment of the blow to step through the air onto Rum Tum's back, and slumped over it behind Dario.

> *The bell is gone. And as a result, the feeling of darkness actually begins to subside. It's still raining heavily. Also, the prism is going nuts now. There was obviously some connection between the bell and the prism, which has now been broken, and it seems like now the prism is just going wild. There's arcs of purple lightning shooting, just randomly cutting through the countryside and through the clouds.*

The Empress could do nothing in answer. She was having a contraction.

> *The bell, or whatever black Khyber made it up, has turned into an unfathomable ball of mass that has sunk through the center of this tower and has taken with it the whole upper structure. This tower will collapse. You do not know when. Uriel is right across from you in extreme pain. She was hit hard by this roof and is in clear pain of contraction, and you've now killed her children, her emperor, and destroyed her bell.*

### Mommy

Eric called up his own guardians, the spirits of maths and light. Then he asked Gemma a question.

> **Eric:** Gemma, should I do something incredibly stupid?
>
> **Gemma:** I think I know what you're going to do.
>
> **Eric:** I'm going to try to leap out and give her a big baby-leaping-to-its-mama hug.

His Mark of Passage glowed, and he jumped off the tower.

> *You reach out and you bury your face in her pregnant breasts, clutching behind her waist. She looks down at you with her humongous face. Her golden eyes. You swear that you can see straight through her eyes to a glowing diamond behind them.*

> **Uriel:** You want to meet my son? I keep him warm. I am the mother of all. Come to me.

He could not hear a word of it. *All you can hear is the hum of her voice. And her heartbeat, you can feel it, as she puts her arms around you.* Eric never really knew his parents.

> **Uriel:** I am the mother who brings life to all. There is no death in the eternal nightfall. You have destroyed one bell. A naughty child you are. But I will breathe life and sense into you. You are a very good boy.

She breathed the grave over all of them, and it very nearly killed Eric where he hung.

> *The light starts to turn to that deep purple again. You hear the whining of the crowd below you. The mass of living corpses just clawing their way through the earth. It's almost like this hill has been built on graveyard after graveyard. One wonders whether there was a hill even before the corpses.*

### The wand and the sword

Gemma slid her leg over Rum Tum behind Dario and held on. Then she took out her wand of *magic missile* and fired all seven charges into the Empress's belly. The wand was left smoking and useless, and she dropped it.

> *But as you do that, you feel your power just wane, like a brownout. And you get a sense for your patron's anger. You almost faint.*

The Empress pointed at her.

> **Uriel:** I said give me back Esther's sword. You fucking runt. You think you deserve to hold that? I know where everything is inside you. I've had my hands in a thousand of you.
>
> **Gemma:** Do you know what the last thing Esther said before we killed her? She said to kill you.
>
> **Uriel:** They're here to witness his birth, but it's too early. No, you can't leave me behind. You need to stay with mommy. You need to stay with mommy.

Eric, deaf, wanted to know why she was so angry all of a sudden. Dario turned Rum Tum around.

> **Dario:** By all of destiny, the line of dragons, the Flame, the great mountain of flame, in the name of every hero, of every soul, I strike thee!

> *His blade, Palefire, explodes in white light, lighting up the entire sky. I mean, it's almost like a nuclear bomb. All of you guys are flashed for a second, and you see that he's cut a deep wound across her neck, down through her navel, and a glowing red blood seems to be seeping out of her wound.*

Two more hasted strokes gashed the wound open. Eric healed himself and held on, and the Empress grabbed him and threw him into midair. He fell a hundred and ten feet into the rubble of the tower and lived, with his guardians still turning around him. *The entire courtyard is difficult terrain, because the destroyed tower is now completely littered across the ground. It's rubble, it's stone, it's metal, it's burning embers from the fire that was inside of it.*

A contraction took her. She began to arc out of the sky, and at the last moment hovered ten feet off the ground toward the doorway of the temple and dashed inside. They heard her booming voice: *"How dare you!"*

> *You hear the sounds of tremendous fighting and magic on the other side of the building. Gemma, you look out toward the north, toward the Polygon. And coming up the walkway are a set of seemingly disciplined, black-clad cyborgs firing weapons toward the temple. The sisters surrounding the Polygon are still focused.*

The risen dead rushed Eric where he lay and burned in his guardians. The novices tolled at him five times, and four times he did not hear it.

### The forecourt

Gemma blasted the dead off Eric from the saddle. Dario shouted *"Rum Tum, ho!"* and flew them over the roof until they could look down on the Polygon.

> **Dario:** Flames, protect us.

> *This shape is humming. The air and space around it is vibrating. And you see crowds of zombies approaching it. Not just zombies, but masses of body parts conglomerating into shapes unseen, unimagined. Starting to come, crawling out of the ground again, leaving huge pits of overturned earth and rock in the soil. There is a small number of parishioners and worshippers and tourists who were here at the moment that this began. And they are crowded and scattered or being absolutely crawled and rolled over by these creatures.*

The troops of [Eden](/people/eden/) were there too, a little out in the open, which was unusual for them, firing up at the Polygon while the undead hordes collapsed around them.

In the rubble on the other side, the dead bore down on Eric holding ancient weapons, like Gauls, like Hittite warriors, every era of the Iron Age, as ghouls and skeletons. He stood up, revealed his holy symbol, his shield, and turned them.

> *Almost all of them are instantly incinerated by the power of maths. Here's what happens. A funnel of arcane fluid surrounds you, but as the fluid compresses into a tighter and tighter cone, the interior particles of the funnel spin faster and faster. Theoretically, there is a point at which this funnel would get small enough that the acceleration of the particles in the center of the funnel would reach an infinite value. This is the Navier-Stokes hypothesis. And you in this moment have proven the Navier-Stokes hypothesis. The undead recoil at this mathematical truth, largely destroyed all around you.*

Uriel came out into the plaza.

> *You see her holding, as she comes out, her womb bleeding and open, holding two parishioners, random civilians, who she throws to the ground to clump up and then immediately stand up as zombies. The Polygon is humming in front of her. It seems to respond to her getting closer.*

> **Uriel:** All my plans! What have you done?

A mechanical voice yelled across the courtyard.

> **Eden:** Give me my sister!
>
> **Uriel:** The dead are under my control, with or without the bell. You belong to me.

> *All the dead sort of turn at once in a very creepy way. They look right up at you, Gemma, from the ground. Suddenly everyone on the ground is looking right into your eyes. Somehow even the mass of skulls and arms and legs. The eyes that are there are looking at you. You look across the field, squint, and you see even Eden is looking right in your eyes.*

Eden pointed her troops at Gemma, and their shots fell short. Then the Polygon opened.

> *The sides of it open. They reveal themselves to be two-dimensional. The space inside is perfect darkness.*

Dario felt someone trying to read his thoughts and resisted it. It occurred to them that if the bells were ringing everywhere, this was happening everywhere.

Gemma pelted the Empress in the back of the head with three blasts and chose not to knock her forward toward the thing. Dario sprouted wings of his own, left Gemma on Rum Tum, flew the rest of the way down, and put his sword into the Empress's back and side, opening huge new wounds. She turned and laid a spell of harm on him, and he shrugged off most of it. The Polygon gave out a pulse of psychic energy. Rum Tum, in great pain, scratched at his own head.

Gemma jumped off, fell ten feet, stepped through the air to the Empress, and struck. The Empress turned the worst of the blow, as a cleric of the grave can, and Gemma tripped her out of the air and rode her body to the ground.

> *You now have her mounted. You've got your legs on either side of her ears, trying to shove your sword into her face. Put a big gash on the side of her head. Seems to crack open her skull a little bit. You see the glint of diamond. She's incredibly wounded.*

Eden shot Gemma and knocked her back. Her eyes were purple. The object projected the will of Uriel, and Eden was directly under it.

### A brighter dawn

> *Dario descends. He floats just above her. Palefire raised in one hand, his shield. He's got his wings out full.*

> **Dario:** For a while, the world was dark. You sucked the hope from the world. And now, after the long night, comes a brighter dawn.

He looked over at Gemma as he said the last of it. The Empress put the scythe up and blocked his first stroke. He turned his sword around, and the second slashed open her throat. She choked on it, still alive, gasping for air.

> **Dario:** It is over.

> *He turns around with one quick swing and he lops off Uriel's head. It flies across the parquet toward this object, which suddenly glows bright. You glance over at this head that almost comically tumbles and rolls toward the shape. Where it stops, you see it seemingly turn into like a melted bonbon and just absorb into the earth. You look back toward her body. You see something similar happening, but slower, just because of how much more mass there is. All around Dario, who's floating just above it, her body seems to seep and soak into the ground.*
>
> *And a rumbling happens below the earth, like an earthquake, something shifting and moving. All of the creatures in the yard look right at Dario with immense malice, including Eden. The object now sputters and chokes. Its lightning now frizzled and frayed and seemingly striking everything in every distance and every direction.*

On the far side of the building Eric felt the rumbling and did not know what caused it. He knelt by Aurorix, who looked as though he had been stabbed and drained of blood, and tried to spare him. It was too late for that. His guardians burned the last of the novices, one of them coiled around the dragon's tail.

The followers of Matheny rose up the hill and crested the position behind the Tomb of Kaius. They began to cast at the shape, *trying to understand its unholy geometry. "We must disprove this conjecture!"* With them was Fema Nolan, holding a beacon of hope over them.

Zombies ran at Dario and Gemma. A zombie dragon took to the skies. A beholder zombie's rays went wide. A swarm of zombie parts shambled quickly in their direction, a huge ogre zombie lumbered behind it, and the massive clots came on surprisingly fast for their size and shape. Even Dario, who cannot be frightened, looked very scared.

> *Uriel, seemingly irrevocably destroyed in front of your face. But her remains, like I said, have seeped quickly into the ground through the seams of the stonework, into the earth itself. Her magic is very clearly alive, controlling these creatures, all of them descending on you.*

Inside the cathedral a crowd of innocent people was hiding from it all. It was a bad time to come to Nos Matre.

> **Dario:** It seems this is where we end, little fox. I am proud that it is with you, and that we have accomplished our great quest.
>
> **Gemma:** We're not done yet.

She jumped up, grabbed the bottom of his foot, and took them both by *dimension door* back to where she had last seen Eric.

> **Dario:** Eric, you're alive! We've done it! I've slain the Witch Queen! She is destroyed! But her influence lives on somehow. We must destroy that monstrous geometrical anomaly, right?
>
> **Eric:** I think so.

Dario put Palefire in the bag and pulled out the hammer. *"Maybe this?"* He flew for the roof, meaning to reach the thing before the dead killed him, and asked them to distract it if they could.

### Two dragons

Eric took a diamond and called Aurorix back from death.

> *He coughs awake, and fire blows out of his nose as his scales kind of realign into a smooth gold surface, and he looks up at you and he gives a wide dragon grin.*

> **Aurorix:** You've saved me, Priest of Maths, and you have saved us all. It is a new day.
>
> **Eric:** The job's not done yet. The Queen is dead. There's more unfortunate black magics happening somewhere over there to the north.
>
> **Aurorix:** She can't be dead. She can't be dead. She's likely in the Sept. She must have been to the ground. She must be in the Sept of Bones. It is the ancient temple buried deep below here. It is why we are here.

From across the roof came the horrific screech of the undead dragon and the chanting of Matheny's people, reciting their axioms. Gemma climbed onto Aurorix and fed him a potion, and he healed himself. On the roof Dario ran smack into the zombie dragon, hurt it badly, and took its breath of hellfire. Eden came through the cathedral doors, pushing past the dead in the doorway, purple-eyed and obsessed.

> **Eden:** Where is my sister?

She shot Gemma off the dragon's back and over the edge into the bushes below. Aurorix flew at the crowd and blew fire down on all of them. Eden walked out of it, leapt twenty feet straight up, and swung her blade at Eric on the dragon's back.

Then Eric remembered the *summon dragon* [Hopper](/people/hopper/) had stored in a ring for him in Sharn, keyed to a true name: an hour of service, and then freedom. He let it go.

> *You summon Valtrex, and the smell of sulfur wafts across. You hear his putrid cry. And upon this sound, you feel the scales on Aurorix's body tense up.*

> **Aurorix:** No. You have summoned my mortal enemy.
>
> **Eric:** I thought all you dragons were friends.
>
> **Aurorix:** You fool. Get off of me.

He shook Eric off.

> **Valtrex:** So she got you too. Well, of course she did, if she got me. But thankfully, she's brought me straight to you, so I can rip out your throat like I should have done six thousand years ago.

[Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) came down and locked jaws with Aurorix at once. The two dragons of this story were mortal enemies and would help nobody while both were on the field.

### The way down

Gemma, in a ditch, said they might have just awoken some old dragon business and that neither would help them now. They worked out where the Sept must be. Their tour as novices had pointed out pieces of the structure that was here long before Nos Matre: the eternal flame near the master altar, the old gothic stonework poking through the ground, the original statue at the top of the Stairs of Mercy, the Tomb of Kaius. They chose a spot and ran for it.

> **Eric:** If I teleport us into a rock or something, we'll take some damage. We won't die unless we have very low hit points. In which case we might die. But we came here for a reason, and we knew the rules of the game, and the rules are you might die.

Eden drew her blades.

> **Gemma:** Your sister's downstairs. She's here. Esther. She's with us.

Eden stopped and thought for a moment.

> **Eden:** Liar.

Her arm blades whipped around Eric, and he barely dodged. On the roof Dario dashed for the Polygon into range of everything in the yard and was severely hurt. At the edge Gemma hesitated.

> **Gemma:** What are you and I gonna do down there? We don't have any dragons. We don't have Dario. Just you and me. Down there. With bones. Undead. A swarm of undead.
>
> **Eric:** I'm quite keen on fighting undead.

Eden cut him as he broke away, and he took them both down through the earth.

### The crypt

> *Both of you find yourself in a pitch black cavern or room. A very ancient catacomb. There is an ancient stone staircase that you are standing at the foot of. And you know that above you, right above you, is the terrazzo plaza of the ritual garden of Nos Matre. In here, you see funerary urns, and stacked and more or less mortared into the walls are many sarcophagi of seemingly ancient origin, maybe mixed eras. Conspicuously, on your right, you see a row of coffins much fresher than the ancient ones here. No more than ten, thirteen years old. And one which is fresher than all, to your left, with its seal opened and its cover pulled back. There is a pile of bones sort of stuck to one side. And then in front of you immediately are two huge ancient doors.*

It was quiet all of a sudden. Eric took Gemma's hand, because he was a bit scared.

> *Your sword, which is normally a light blue, upon entry to this space, and especially in seeing these coffins, it gives a pinkish-reddish hue for just a moment and feels a little angry.*

Gemma leaned into the doors and could not move them. She took out her adamantine mace and smashed them open.

### The Sept of Bones

> *You see a skeletal and spectral vision of Uriel. Her body has sort of slurped back together around a mostly intact womb, which has split open and separated from her. The creature inside of this external organ, pulsing and bleeding, is a humanoid skeleton with draconic wings, and its little eyes kind of look up at you as you open the door. Uriel is still there. Her head is separated from her body and her face is mostly skeletal, but her skull still contains the diamond floating in the center, and ultraviolet light in the crown still upon her brow. But her umbilical is connecting her to this creature.*
>
> *You peek into this structure, and it is a massive, steep-walled sept with altar places and sacrificial pedestals, stairs that lead up in the four cardinal directions, and nearly every flat surface is covered by five to ten feet of piled skulls, bones and fragments collected over centuries. Piled up in every place. White and green light emitted from eternal flame spells all over the place. And clearly the power from all of this death is sweeping up in subtle purple light, up toward the ceiling, past Uriel and toward the surface, perhaps toward the great device upstairs. And you notice that even as pathetic and alien as the creature in the womb looks, it is visibly growing.*
>
> *The dragon is born. The great half-dragon, the thing that all of Eberron went to war to destroy thousands of years ago. The offspring of an elvish necromancer and the Crimson Sun. The thing that Varyn Crona was sworn to protect. The true thing he was sworn to protect. And the thing that Kitsune wants from you. He's right there.*

This was the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/), and the child was [the Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/).

> **Eric:** So we kill that thing and we get to go home, right?
>

## Revelations

- Uriel ended the fight in the forecourt by sinking through the stones. She went on in the Sept of Bones as something between a spirit and dying flesh, her head apart from her body, joined by a cord to the child she had borne.
- The diamond set in her skull years ago was still there, and showed when the bone broke.
- The Heir of Vol is a half-dragon, the child of the Qualanthri line and the Crimson Sun, the thing Varyn Crona was truly sworn to protect. It breathes black fire. Joined to its mother it could be hurt, and she could draw its strength into herself.
- Uriel had kept the child in too long, in an attempt to steal its destiny and power. Before the end she said that her grandfather had been right and that she was only a vessel.
- She said that Esther should have kept her oath to the Crimson Sun, and that the two of them were to have been mothers of the same godchild.
- The Polygon carried Uriel's will. Through it she held the dead after the bell was broken, and through it she held Eden.
- Uriel stole the Polygon from [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/).
- With the Original broken, no more dead rose. With the Polygon struck, the power of the dead stopped rising out of the Sept.
- Cut from its mother, the Heir could not be killed with weapons. Every wound closed harder than before and left it stronger, and it grew by the moment. That is why it was the object of such desire.
- Kitsune wanted the child. His fox cut the cord and claimed it, telling Uriel that a promise is a promise. When he saw what it was, he changed his mind.
- Aurorix and Valtrex were mortal enemies of six thousand years' standing. Valtrex killed him.
- The crypt above the Sept holds a row of coffins ten to thirteen years old and one newer, opened. Its roof opens to the sky.
- A ship of the Hundreds dropped the gem that destroyed the valley.
- Roberts sold the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/) before the end. He would not name the buyer.

## Consequences

Uriel, Esther Crona and Aurorix are dead. The Original and the Polygon are destroyed, and the Ivory Lazaret, Nos Matre and the valley around them have burned, with the Heir of Vol in the middle of the fire.
