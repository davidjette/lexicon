**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 22 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 22 — The Reckoning of the Mother** is the twenty-second session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 13 November 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 21 — The Summer Palace](/sessions/korth-ep-21-the-summer-palace/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) fought [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) twice: in the air over Nos Matre, where Dario broke the bell and took her head, and in the Sept of Bones under the hill, where she was destroyed. The child she bore could not be killed. The [Ivory Lazaret](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/) was burned off the map behind them.

> For a while, the world was dark. You sucked the hope from the world. And now, after the long night, comes a brighter dawn.
> — Sir Dario Argentino, to the Holy Empress

## Summary

### The flight to the bell

The party came at the bell on the back of the gold dragon [Aurorix](/species/aurorix/), all of them hurt, with the potions against necrotic harm still in their blood and one greatberry between them, which they kept back.

The rain turned to a downpour and the sky went as dark as dusk. A chorus of women's voices came from every direction, in time and in tune with the bell. With each toll the hillside pulsed. Every few feet the turf turned itself over, and the dead climbed out and faced the temple. The temple stood against a black and purple glow, and in that glow was the small figure of Uriel at the bell, her white scythe gone black, her belly lit orange.

Gemma took out the pewter pendant [Zero-One](/people/zero-one-fema-nolan/) had given her at the pilgrim camp.

> **Gemma:** Dawn, Silver Flame, Dawn, I need your help.

White light spread around the four of them. A single soprano sang the theme of the Silver Flame through the chorus, and inside the light the bell could hardly be heard. The plaza below filled with Black Doves.

Cresting the nave, they saw where the purple light came from. [The Polygon](/items/the-polygon/) floated in the courtyard with four Sorrowful Sisters looking up at it, lightning running through their bodies into it. It caught Eric's eye, and he heard the Empress in his head: *"Go, you disgusting pig! What are you doing here?"* A Choir of Novices on the ground began to toll at the dragon on her orders. Dario drank a potion and lit Palefire. Eric healed Aurorix and himself.

Uriel saw them coming and knew them for the people who had killed her daughters and her husband. She smiled.

> **Uriel:** We crushed you. We turned you into gruel. You have nothing to say to me or my husband, who rules across all time.

Aurorix answered her in Draconic. She raised a hand, her crown glowed white, and a cloud of pale sisterly figures closed around her. Then she flew up beside them.

### The dragon falls

Five armed dead crawled out of a flower garden. Gemma's blast pushed the Empress back. The bell tolled again, and this time Aurorix could not shake it off. He dropped out of the sky, and the fall knocked him senseless. The other three floated down on feather tokens into a ring of undead sisters.

Dario laid his hands on the dragon (*"Wake up, my friend! There's work to do!"*), quickened himself with a spell, and Rum Tum sprang up under him from nowhere. He flew for the bell tower with [Knell](/items/knell/) in his hand, passing under the Empress without a glance.

> **Uriel:** You filthy reptile! Get away from that!

Eric took hold of Gemma and offered his cheek for luck. *"Good luck, short pants."* He carried them both by *dimension door* to the top of the tower, a cramped platform with no stair and no rail. Aurorix lay where he had fallen.

From the belfry Gemma could see west. The pilgrim camp was burning in the rain. Matt Matheny's followers were retreating up the hill around Zero-One, who held a spell of protection over them, and the Plaza Band marched with them playing the [fanfare](/lore/the-fanfare-of-dawn/). They were caught between whatever was driving them up the hill and the thing in the courtyard.

Uriel closed on the tower. She had grown: longer in the bone, more severe, much larger than a woman. One sweep of her scythe, Mourning Dove, cut Gemma and Eric together. Below, the novices fell on Aurorix with long ceremonial knives, tore out golden scales, and left him bleeding across the terrazzo. More dead came up out of the soil. From the far side of Nos Matre came the crack of *eldritch blasts* and the muzzle flashes of Eberronian firearms climbing the Stairs of Her Mercy.

### The bell

Gemma struck the Empress with the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/).

> **Uriel:** You have Esther's sword. Why don't you give it to me?

The bell tolled an arm's length away, and Eric went deaf. The light of the Polygon began to flicker red, blue and green.

Dario flew up to the bell, drew Knell, and brought it down. A split ran from the blow to the crown of the bell. The bell peeled open into layer on layer, like an onion turned inside out and growing as it turned. It collapsed into a rainbow of metal, was drawn into a single point, and burst. [The Original](/items/the-hell-s-bell-fantanya-nyel/) was destroyed. The roof of the tower came down on all of them. Gemma was half buried and pulled herself by magic onto Rum Tum behind Dario. The tower began to go.

The dark lifted a little. No more dead came out of the ground. The Polygon went wild and threw purple lightning across the countryside and into the clouds. The Empress, struck by the roof, doubled over in a contraction.

Eric called up his own guardians, the spirits of maths and light, and asked Gemma whether he should do something incredibly stupid. Then he jumped off the tower at the Empress. The Mark of Passage carried him. He landed with his arms around her waist and his face in her chest, and through her golden eyes he could see a diamond glowing behind them. He could not hear a word. He felt her voice as a hum, and her heartbeat.

> **Uriel:** You want to meet my son? I keep him warm. I am the mother of all. Come to me.
>
> **Uriel:** I am the mother who brings life to all. There is no death in the eternal nightfall. You have destroyed one bell. A naughty child you are. But I will breathe life and sense into you.

She breathed the grave over all of them. It nearly killed Eric where he hung.

### The Witch Queen

Gemma emptied her wand of *magic missile* into the Empress's belly, every charge at once. The wand burned out in her hand. As it did, her own power dipped like a lamp in a brownout, and she felt her patron's anger.

> **Uriel:** I said give me back Esther's sword. You fucking runt. You think you deserve to hold that? I know where everything is inside you. I've had my hands in a thousand of you.
>
> **Gemma:** Do you know what the last thing Esther said before we killed her? She said to kill you.
>
> **Uriel:** They're here to witness his birth, but it's too early. No, you can't leave me behind. You need to stay with mommy.

Dario wheeled Rum Tum around.

> **Dario:** In the name of every hero, of every soul, I strike thee!

Palefire lit the whole sky white. It opened her from the neck to the navel, and the wound seeped a glowing red. Eric healed himself, and the Empress tore him off her and threw him. He fell more than a hundred feet into the rubble of the tower and lived, with his guardians still turning around him. Another contraction took her. She sank until she hung ten feet off the ground, drifted to the door of the temple, and went in.

From the saddle Gemma could see north. Disciplined black-clad soldiers were coming up the walkway, firing at the temple: the units of [Eden](/people/eden/). In the courtyard the risen dead rushed Eric and burned in his guardians.

### The forecourt

Dario flew over the roof (*"Rum Tum, ho!"*) and saw the forecourt. The Polygon hummed, and the air around it shook. Crowds of dead moved toward it, and with them came masses of body parts joined into shapes nobody had seen or imagined, climbing out of pits of turned earth. The worshippers and tourists who had been there when it began were being rolled under. Eden's troops stood in the open, shooting up at the Polygon while the dead closed round them.

Eric stood up in the rubble and raised his shield. A funnel of arcane fluid tightened around him until the particles at its centre reached infinite speed, which proved the Navier–Stokes conjecture, and the dead around him were burned away by the mathematical truth of it.

Uriel came out into the forecourt with her womb torn and bleeding, dragging two parishioners. She threw them down, and they stood up dead.

> **Uriel:** All my plans! What have you done?

A mechanical voice shouted across the courtyard.

> **Eden:** Give me my sister!
>
> **Uriel:** The dead are under my control, with or without the bell. You belong to me.

Every dead thing in the yard turned and looked Gemma in the eye, down to the eyes in the heaps of skulls and limbs. Across the field Eden looked at her too, and Eden's eyes had gone purple. The Polygon carried the Empress's will, and Eden was under it. Then the sides of the Polygon opened. They had no thickness, and the space inside was perfect darkness.

Gemma blasted the Empress in the back of the head. Dario spread wings of his own, left Gemma on Rum Tum, and dived to drive Palefire into her back and side. The Polygon pulsed, and the pulse tore at every mind near it. Rum Tum clawed at his own head.

Gemma dropped from the saddle, stepped through the air to the Empress's shoulder, and brought her down. Uriel hit the stone with Gemma astride her head, and the next stroke cracked her skull. Something in the crack glinted like diamond.

Dario floated down over her with his wings spread, Palefire in one hand and his shield in the other.

> **Dario:** For a while, the world was dark. You sucked the hope from the world. And now, after the long night, comes a brighter dawn.

She caught the first stroke on her scythe. The second opened her throat.

> **Dario:** It is over.

The third took off her head. It rolled across the paving toward the Polygon, which flared. Where the head stopped it softened and sank into the earth. Her body did the same, more slowly, running into the seams between the stones. The ground shook like something shifting underneath. Every creature in the yard, Eden among them, looked at Dario with hatred. The Polygon choked and threw lightning in every direction.

### After the beheading

On the south side Eric knelt by Aurorix and found that it was too late for small mercies. The dragon was dead.

Matheny's followers came over the hill behind the Tomb of Kaius I and began casting at the Polygon, trying to understand its geometry. *"We must disprove this conjecture!"* Zero-One stood among them holding a spell of hope over them. A dead dragon took to the air. A dead beholder turned its rays on Dario and Gemma. A dead ogre came on, and the great clots of flesh behind it moved faster than they should have.

> **Dario:** It seems this is where we end, little fox. I am proud that it is with you and that we have accomplished our great quest.
>
> **Gemma:** We're not done yet.

She jumped, caught his foot, and took them both by *dimension door* back to Eric.

> **Dario:** Eric, you're alive! We've done it! I've slain the Witch Queen! She is destroyed! But her influence lives on somehow. We must destroy that monstrous geometrical anomaly.

He put Palefire away, took out Knell, and flew for the roof. Eric spent a diamond and called Aurorix back from death.

> **Aurorix:** You've saved me, Priest of Maths, and you have saved us all.
>
> **Aurorix:** She can't be dead. She must be in the Sept of Bones. It is the ancient temple buried deep below here. It is why we are here.

On the roof Dario ran into the dead dragon and took its breath. Eden came round the temple, shouting for her sister. She shot Gemma off Aurorix's back into the bushes, walked through the dragon's fire, leapt twenty feet straight up, and swung her arm blades at Eric.

### Two dragons

Eric remembered the *summon dragon* [Hopper](/people/hopper/) had stored in a ring for him in Sharn, keyed to a true name: an hour of service, and then freedom. He let it go. [Valtrex](/species/valtrex/) came with a putrid cry and a smell of sulphur. Under Eric the gold dragon's scales went stiff.

> **Aurorix:** No. You have summoned my mortal enemy. You fool. Get off of me.
>
> **Valtrex:** So she got you too. But thankfully, she's brought me straight to you, so I can rip out your throat like I should have done six thousand years ago.

Aurorix shook Eric off, and the two dragons locked jaws. Neither would be any use to the party while the other lived.

Gemma and Eric worked out how to get below. Their tour as novices had pointed out pieces of the building that stood here long before Nos Matre: the eternal flame by the master altar, the old stonework breaking through the ground, the first statue at the head of the stairs, the Tomb of Kaius I. They chose a spot and ran. Gemma tried Eden on the way.

> **Gemma:** Your sister's downstairs. She's here. Esther. She's with us.
>
> **Eden:** Liar.

Dario flew at the Polygon and was mobbed. Gemma hesitated at the edge: no dragons, no Dario, the two of them alone against whatever was down there. Eric said he was quite keen on fighting undead, and that he would go without her if he had to. Eden cut him as he broke away, and he took them both straight down through the earth.

### The Sept of Bones

They had come down into the crypt of the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-sept-of-bones/). They stood in the dark at the foot of an ancient stone stair, under the terrazzo of the ritual garden. It was a catacomb, with funerary urns and sarcophagi of many ages mortared into the walls. On the right was a row of coffins no more than ten or thirteen years old. On the left was one newer than all of them, its seal broken and its lid pulled back. The Bright Dawn, a pale blue by habit, turned pink and red for a moment and felt angry in Gemma's hand. Ahead stood two huge doors. Gemma could not move them and broke them in with her adamantine mace.

Beyond was a steep-walled sept with altars, pedestals for sacrifice, and stairs rising to the four quarters. Every flat surface lay five to ten feet deep in skulls, bones and pieces of bone, gathered over centuries. Undying flames burned white and green. The power of all that death rose in a faint purple light toward the ceiling, toward the Polygon.

Uriel was there. Her body had drawn itself back together around a womb that had split and come away from her. Her head floated apart from it, mostly skull, with the diamond in the middle of it and ultraviolet light in the crown still on her brow. A cord ran from her to the thing in the womb, a skeletal figure with a dragon's wings, wet and pulsing, which looked up at the door as it opened. It was growing as they watched. This was the half-dragon that all of Eberron once went to war to destroy: the child of an elvish necromancer and the [Crimson Sun](/people/viagra/), the thing Varyn Crona was sworn to protect, and the thing [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/) wanted.

> **Gemma:** Remember my fox friend? I don't talk about him much to you, but he wants that kid. He wants me to not kill it.
>
> **Eric:** Tell him to come and get it. We don't want it.
>
> **Gemma:** He's not a figment of my imagination like your fucking math god.
>
> **Eric:** Math is real.

Gemma said she was sorry at once.

Uriel was singing the lullaby the sisters sang in the last ward. She looked up.

> **Uriel:** He's sleeping. He's perfect, isn't he? He has his father's teeth. You followed me all the way down here to see him. I suppose that makes you family.

In Gemma's head, a whisper from the fox: *"You swore it to me."*

Gemma took out the little box Kitsune had given her for the child, which was never going to hold it. The fight began on the stair. The Empress was half spirit and hard to strike. The floor of bones kept giving up guards, great things built of bone with glaives in their hands. Eric turned them, and they threw themselves off the stair into the heaps. [The Heir of Vol](/people/the-heir-of-vol/) opened its mouth inside its sac and belched black fire, which melted two of its mother's own guards and fell just short of the party. It could not move by itself. Uriel towed it behind her on the cord.

> **Uriel:** My baby must feed.

When Eric's guardians burned the child, she put herself in the way. *"Don't you touch him!"* Mourning Dove found Gemma twice, and the white light of the circle went out. The breath of the grave came after it.

> **Uriel:** This is no way to treat an empress. You've taken everything from me, but not him. Perhaps my destiny is not what I thought it was. My grandfather was right. I am a vessel. My being is for him. I have borne him. I was right. Esther did not. It was me. It was me!

### Esther

They were bleeding against the stair when wings beat behind them. [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) stepped onto the threshold with her wings spread and a weapon in her hand. Uriel met her eyes, and fear crossed what was left of her face. She left the party where they stood and flew at her.

Esther hurt her badly. Uriel drew strength back out of her own child to mend herself.

> **Uriel:** Forgive me, baby. Mother needs you back inside her again.

They tore at each other, and Esther tried to put her sword through the Empress's heart.

> **Uriel:** You should have stayed with your oath to the Crimson Sun, his progeny. You were to be mothers together. Mothers of the same godchild. Why would you betray me? Now look upon it. We can raise it, the two of us. We can make it perfect. It can kill all our enemies. Join me.

Eric raised his wand. *"Hey, Esther, do you remember this?"* He put six missiles into the Empress. He had almost nothing left. The Heir breathed at the doorway, and a sheet of molten rock landed between Gemma and Eric as they pressed themselves behind the stone on either side.

Then the ground shook harder than it had yet. The heaps of bone slid. The purple light stopped rising. The cord between mother and child went black and beat faster.

> **Uriel:** You fools! What have you done? You can't! There's no way I can control it! You imbeciles! You disgusting mongrels. Sub-elven swine.
>
> **Esther:** What happens if they destroy the Polygon?
>
> **Uriel:** Ask LeBeefe. I stole it from him.

Uriel put her hand on Esther's masked face and spoke an old Elvish curse, and Esther went rigid. Uriel carried her out over the pit, held her up, and drew the scythe slowly down her spine. The blood fell on the bones. The Heir opened its mouth like a chick in a nest. It reached into the wound, pulled out what it could reach, and ate.

> **Uriel:** You foolish little girl. When I met you, I was over two hundred years old. It is easy to love a child. But now I have a true child. I have no more time for childish games.

The Heir took Esther by both feet, opened its mouth around her, and swallowed her in six or seven gulps. It grew.

While this went on the guards reached the door and broke it down. Eric destroyed one with the mace. Behind them the roof of the crypt ground open on some old mechanism, and daylight and rain fell in on the coffins.

### The cord

Gemma called her patron's own fox, Lalo Kitsune, into the air over the Empress. He came in his reddest shape, furious.

There were heavy steps on the stair and a rough voice calling down. Dario came into the crypt close to death. He threw Eric the greatberry, which the people above had kept for him.

> **Dario:** That thing up there? It's going to blow.

Breaking it had weakened the hordes, he said, and nobody knew how long they had. Gemma took out the stone [Joseph Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/) had left her and sent that she badly needed his help at the top of the stairs. From above came screams of *"Dragon!"*

Uriel came after them, towing the Heir on a cord that bled and sputtered.

> **Uriel:** The only god is death. There is no number greater than zero.

Eric shrugged off the rot that came with this clear mathematical falsehood, ate the greatberry, and was whole. He threw the largest fire he had over mother and child. It burned the cord almost through, and she fed on the child again to stay up. Dario told them both to run and leave her to him. The Heir's black fire washed over all three.

Then Lalo moved. He stepped through the air to the cord, took hold of it, and set his little blade at the weak place.

> **Lalo:** It's over, Uriel. A promise is a promise. The child is mine now.

He cut it.

> **Uriel:** What have you done? What have you done? He isn't mine anymore. He isn't anyone's.

The power went out of her at once. She laid her hand on her chest and spoke the word she had always healed with, and nothing happened. She watched the hand begin to melt.

> **Uriel:** It's not fair. It's not fair. I deserve to be queen. I deserve to be happy. You took that from me.

She flew at Eric with the scythe. A piece of her skull had fallen away, and the diamond sat loose in it. Eric hit her with the mace of disruption. All the black bile of her ran out and pooled at her feet. What was left was a husk, and it fell. The paths of Eberron's moons turned around Eric in perfect ellipses.

> **Dario:** Hey man, nice shot.

### The Heir

Free of the mother who had kept it in too long so that she could have its destiny for herself, the Heir grew faster. It took the shape of a thing half human and half dragon, with three hearts beating in its chest. Its sac drew in bones and what was left of Uriel.

> **Lalo:** I think I changed my mind.
>
> **Gemma:** Oh, now you don't want this fucking thing?

With the fox's leave, Gemma stepped onto its back and cut into it twice with the Bright Dawn. Where the blade opened it, flesh bubbled out harder and thicker than before, and it was stronger for the wound. The fox's blade did the same. Eric turned a beam of sunlight on it. The burn went shiny and hard. All round the Sept the army of bones staggered and fell down, because the child was drinking the power that had held it up.

Nothing they had could kill it. Eric said that the Polygon was going to blow, and that it might do what they could not. Dario offered to stay behind and cover them. Gemma jumped down to him, took his hand, and carried him straight up through the hill to the roof of the cathedral. Eric ran back up the stair, told the baby what he thought of it, and went up the way he had come.

### The Revenge

On the surface the Polygon was throwing off pulses that knocked people flat. With the Empress gone the dead had no order. Dead fought dead, and what living people were left ran from the heaps of walking flesh. That was how Dario had lived, and some of Matheny's people with him. Eric came up among them and told them a ship was coming and that they had to be on it.

Great ships of [the Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/) were coming in from the south, with lighter craft circling. Low over the cathedral came the airship *Revenge*, and over her bow the Dread Pirate Joseph L. Roberts.

> **Roberts:** You know, if you're going to be one of my wives, I'm going to make you work. You're not going to be able to lay around all day.
>
> **Gemma:** Your humour is what made me want to marry you.

Valtrex roared overhead with two riders on his back: the Leonin scholar [Jelton Ahn](/people/jelton-ahn/) and [Madame Tso](/people/madame-tso-teryaki/), who wore the [Crown of Seven Stars](/items/the-crown-of-seven-stars/). He had killed Aurorix. After that he had turned on the dead.

Roberts pulled Gemma and Dario aboard. Gemma made him go back. *"Those nerds?"* The corsairs hauled Eric, [Ilma Vitt](/people/ilma-vitt/), Zero-One and Matheny's people over the rail in the courtyard. Behind them the ground under the Lazaret swelled and the building rocked. The Heir of Vol pushed up through soil and rock, enormous, feeding on the Polygon.

One of the fast ships of the Hundreds passed close enough to be a threat. *"Punch it!"* A ring of bound wind opened behind the *Revenge* and drove her up through the thunderhead, hair standing on end, into a clear sunset. Below them the white trails of the Hundreds' ships circled over the cloud. One of them let fall a glowing red gem.

> **Roberts:** Don't look at it!

There was a flash of red, then a white light that came back off everything, then heat, then wind. The blast turned the ship over, and for a moment everyone aboard burned as if they had laid a hand on a stove. A dome of red fire rose from the middle of the Lazaret and became a mushroom cloud, and it pushed the storm out of the valley. In the clear air they could see that the buildings, the Lazaret and the Heir of Vol were gone.

The *Revenge* climbed out among the tall clouds toward the Lhazaar seas.

### On deck

> **Gemma:** So did you sell the lodge to Locke Pierce or what?
>
> **Roberts:** I don't like to mix pleasure and business. Let's just say I know how to time these things. And I think the buyer wanted it for a teardown.

It was the third time he had come for them. Far off above the cloud a small black shape was flying for [the Korramont](/places/the-korramont/) with its riders. Dario slid down against the wall and wept.

## Revelations

- Uriel ended the fight in the forecourt by sinking through the stones. She went on in the Sept of Bones as something between a spirit and dying flesh, her head apart from her body, joined by a cord to the child she had borne.
- The diamond set in her skull years ago was still there, and showed when the bone broke.
- The Heir of Vol is a half-dragon, the child of the Qualanthri line and the Crimson Sun. It breathes black fire. Joined to its mother it could be hurt, and she could draw its strength into herself.
- Uriel had kept the child in too long. She meant to have its destiny for herself. Before the end she said that her grandfather had been right and that she was only a vessel.
- She said that Esther should have kept her oath to the Crimson Sun, and that the two of them were to have been mothers of the same godchild.
- The Polygon carried Uriel's will. Through it she held the dead after the bell was broken, and through it she held Eden.
- Uriel stole the Polygon from [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/).
- With the Original broken, no more dead rose. With the Polygon struck, the power of the dead stopped rising out of the Sept.
- Cut from its mother, the Heir could not be killed with weapons. Every wound closed harder than before and left it stronger, and it grew by the moment.
- Kitsune wanted the child. His fox cut the cord and claimed it, telling Uriel that a promise is a promise. When he saw what it was, he changed his mind.
- Aurorix and Valtrex were mortal enemies of six thousand years' standing. Valtrex killed him.
- The crypt above the Sept holds a row of coffins ten to thirteen years old and one newer, opened. Its roof opens to the sky.
- A ship of the Hundreds dropped the gem that destroyed the valley.
- Roberts sold the [Grand Lodge](/places/the-grand-lodge/) before the end. He would not name the buyer.

## Consequences

Uriel, Esther Crona and Aurorix are dead. The Original and the Polygon are destroyed, and the Ivory Lazaret, Nos Matre and the valley around them have burned, with the Heir of Vol in the middle of the fire. Gemma, Eric and Dario are alive aboard the *Revenge* with Zero-One, Ilma Vitt and what is left of Matheny's people. Valtrex is free and has gone toward the Korramont with Jelton Ahn and Madame Tso. Nobody aboard knows what became of Eden, of the Plaza Band, or of the other bells, or how Sharn and Leaf have fared. Zero-One's pendant, Gemma's wand, Hopper's stored spell and the greatberry are spent.
