**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Sharn arc · Episode 12 · DM: Nichole**

Sharn Ep 12 — The Spine and the Stone is the twelfth session of the Sharn arc of Campaign 2, The Unforeseen Strikes Back, run by Nichole. [Blair](/people/blair/) revealed [the Spine](/places/the-spine/), a hidden tunnel toward the caved-in passage where the Cogborn were trapped, and a sealed Chamber Room that also had to be cleared before the Cogborn could move. The tunnel was dug out overnight, the Chamber Room's four giant spiders and Stone Golem killed the next morning, and the Cogborn community was led through the Spine to [RUST](/organizations/r-u-s-t/) and the mountain tunnel, bound for [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/). [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/) teleported in to thank the trio and gave each of them a RUST communication necklace.

## Summary

After returning to the [Underhive](/organizations/the-underhive/) tavern, the trio were met with cheers as [Blair](/people/blair/) celebrated their victory over the [Drain Snakes](/organizations/the-drain-snakes/). She revealed [the Spine](/places/the-spine/), an ancient hidden tunnel leading toward the caved-in passage where the Cogborn were trapped. She also showed them an old sealed door and warned them about the ancient Chamber Room beyond it, a place where past suitors had vanished trying to impress her. Both the cave-in tunnel and the ancient chamber would need to be dealt with before the Cogborn could be moved. Blair's husband, the bear-man [Berenstain](/people/berenstain/), thanked the group for helping the Cogborn and gifted them enchanted gloves with the move earth cantrip.

That night, Berenstain, [Snodgrass](/people/snodgrass/), and [Doppler](/people/doppler-klink/) worked together to clear the collapsed tunnel, opening a path to the Cogborn and reuniting with the Cogborn elder [Chris Understall](/people/chris-understall/). Plans were made for the community to move once the way was confirmed safe.

The next morning, the trio entered the long-sealed Chamber Room, discovering a vast webbed hall guarded by four giant spiders and an ancient Stone Golem. After a brutal fight, they defeated the guardians, recovered several magic items, including an Ioun Stone with old Dhakaani runes on it, and took a short rest before pressing deeper toward [RUST](/organizations/r-u-s-t/). They quickly found [Misty Kross](/people/misty-kross/) patrolling the tunnels in the area and she led them to [Preacher](/people/preacher/).

With the Chamber Room cleared, the trio, Doppler and Snodgrass guided the Cogborn community through the Spine, down the underhive river, through the ancient chamber room, past the defeated stone golem and large spider corpses and directly to RUST and the mountain tunnel. There Preacher and the RUST Runners led the people in song and handed out prayer sheets, praying to the love of something called "[Lexicon](/items/the-lexicon/)". [Locke Pierce](/people/locke-pierce/), leader of [The Hundreds](/organizations/the-hundreds/), teleported in once the people were cleared and met with Preacher. He thanked the group for saving the Cogborn and presented each of them with a RUST communication necklace. As the Cogborn prepared to continue their journey toward [Gorgonhorn](/places/gorgonhorn/), Doppler made the difficult decision to leave Sharn and travel with his people.

With their mission complete, [Gemma](/people/gemma-corso/) teleported the trio, along with [Renn](/people/renn-tal/) and Snodgrass, back to their Lower City safe house. Snodgrass chose to remain in Sharn, determined to help the group solve the mystery of the Cog Collector.

## Revelations

- The Cogborn faith has a name for what it loves: **Lexicon**. The RUST Runners hand out prayer sheets for it.
- Locke Pierce leads **The Hundreds** — and comes when the Cogborn are saved.
- The Ioun Stone recovered in the Chamber Room carries **Dhakaani** runes.
- Doppler Klink is leaving Sharn with his people; Snodgrass is staying to solve the Cog Collector.

## Consequences

The Cogborn community left Sharn intact. The trio ended the session holding a RUST necklace and the thanks of Locke Pierce, a Campaign 1 founder, with Snodgrass remaining as a partner on the Cog Collector case that killed [Edwin Graves](/people/dr-edwin-graves/) the following day.
