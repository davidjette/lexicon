**Leef arc · Episode 14 · DM: JL**

**Leef Ep 14 — The Rebel Allies** is the fourteenth session of [the Leef / Newham arc](/history/the-leef-newham-arc/) of *Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by JL. Played in [Newham](/places/newham/) on 7 November 2025, it turned on a fight that [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) held under *protection from evil and good*, a choice JL called "a master stroke". By its end the party had made allies of three of the four rebel groups it was courting, vampires among them; the fourth, possible defectors from the military base, had not yet been approached. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

## Summary

In the fight Dario used his Channel Divinity for Divine Sense rather than for attack, and cast *protection from evil and good*. Dave wrote the next day: "using my Channel Divinity for Sense instead of my attack power proved important, but Protection from Evil worked like a charm" / "better than a use of Smite". By his count the spell "statistically raises my AC from 18 to like 23", and "bc I didn't get hit except once I kept concentration". Nico agreed that it "worked great"; JL: "P from E and G was a master stroke." <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

The session also advanced the party's work with Newham's rebels. Dave: "and we're 3/4 for making allies from these rebels". Nico: "we have vampi allies!" and "the only group we haven't had any interaction with is the possible defects from the military base". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>

## Revelations

- Three of four rebel groups in Newham were allied with the party after this session. One is a group of vampires. The remaining group is possible defectors from the military base. <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>
- Dario's *find steed* mount: Dave chose a large cat, fey or celestial, "a strong telepathic beast" that works in dungeons, and wanted to call it Greymalkin. Dave also wrote that "now we can do blind fighting on horseback". <small>(Oral Histories: The Inevitables, 2025-11-08)</small>
