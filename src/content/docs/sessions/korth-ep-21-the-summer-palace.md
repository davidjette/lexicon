**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 21 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 21 — The Summer Palace** is the twenty-first session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 6 November 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 20 — The Dinner Party](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) crossed the Emperor's gardens with Saturnia and the gold dragon Aurorix, fought their way through the Empress's daughters to the Emperor's bedchamber, and set him alight on his bed. [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) went into labour in earnest, left by magic, and rang the bell.

> Fine. Then if it's time, then it's time. He's coming. The night begins.
> — Uriel, the Holy Empress

## Summary

### Across the gardens

At the Westhouse fountain the party talked over freeing [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) and let it go; Dario feared she would join [Eden](/people/eden/) against them. Gemma reached into her bag for a little cauldron and drew out potions that let them shrug off necrotic harm for an hour. Aurorix, still much reduced, said he had been drinking his own blood for years. Saturnia drew a small bird on each of their foreheads in a red-black salve.

> **Saturnia:** It's made from the blood of the dragon himself. Without it, you would fall asleep, stepping on the grounds.

It was raining hard. The gardens were unnaturally lush, kept alive by serious magic. A noblewoman walked there crying. Three Sisters of the Blood stopped Saturnia, Gemma sent them off to rest in the cafeteria, and a white-cloaked bone knight watched them go.

### The archbishop

In a walled garden under a cherry tree in blossom, below a statue of Lathander the Dawn Lord fashioned to look like [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/), lay Pom Pilot, Archbishop of Arawai, the man from the boat. He had been run through within the last half hour. Bloody footprints led away from him: barefoot, five-toed, long and slender. His hand still clutched an *amulet of health*.

When Gemma took it, his hand grabbed hers. He rose as a revenant wight with bone blades bursting from his arms, wounded her badly, and fell to Dario's Palefire.

> **Saturnia:** There shouldn't be anyone in this garden at all. The fact that he was here meant he must have been here to visit the Emperor. The Emperor is not alive in any proper sense. He's in a coma. He's held there by Uriel.

She had seen the Empress in the residence, and said that lately shoes would not fit her.

### The Cursed Knights

At the top of a marble stair onto a checkerboard plaza, one of the great bone knights saw through the disguises and drew. Three of them fought: undead remade from bone and armour, with bone axes, a single slit for a face and black bile for blood. Dario killed two and Gemma the third, and Aurorix breathed white-hot fire out of the jaws of the sister he was disguised as. The palace doors had no handle and no lock. Saturnia led them along the back of the palace above the river to the girls' sunken garden, where Eric blew a fourth knight apart with a bolt of fire. A ballet slipper was wedged in the side door.

### The daughters' hall

The door opened on a vast columned hall made into a bedroom, with four-poster beds, vanities, toys, a hearth, and a nursery where a Sister of the Blood was nursing a baby. The Empress's daughters crowded around them.

> **Gemma:** We're the new babysitters. Your mother needs help.
>
> **Muriel:** My mother is having our brother.
>
> **Daughter:** Is it true that our brother will bring the eternal nightfall?
>
> **Gemma:** That is what we're all hoping, isn't it?
>
> **Daughter:** Not us. We know we won't be needed anymore once that happens.

The twins Enwe and Fenwe finished each other's sentences: *"Mother is having..." "...another one."* Drakiel offered to show Gemma that she could breathe fire. Muriel, the eldest, said they would all be made brides for their half-brothers, that she wanted to be Empress, and that she wanted her mother dead.

> **Gemma:** That's an interesting wish. If you were Empress, what would you do?
>
> **Muriel:** I would kill every dwarf. They're disgusting and I hate their food.

Muriel turned the others: *"These people are not our friends. They're our captors."* Several of the girls saw through the disguises, and the nursing sister cried for the guards.

> **Dario:** Death to the Witch Queen!

He cut Purell in half. The daughters bared horns and teeth and breathed fire, acid, lightning and cold, each according to the dragon whose blood she was made from; the youngest breathed fire, from Aurorix's blood. Each one wailed as she fell, a sound that hurt to hear. Three Sisters of the Blood came down the hall with needles and blood-magic that froze a body where it stood. Gemma threw off her disguise and fought as herself. Eric's radiance burned the eyes out of the sisters and the youngest girls. Dario beheaded Muriel. When it was over, every daughter was dead except Drakiel, who had wept in a corner the whole time (*"Stop! Just stop! None of this matters!"*), and the baby Ysabel. Gemma had killed none of the children.

Dario drew his sword over Drakiel.

> **Saturnia:** This one's never done anything cruel.
>
> **Dario:** By the name of the Silver Flame, I charge you with treason against all that is holy and name you an abomination.
>
> **Dario:** There cannot be any legitimate heirs to the Emperor. We've come too far for half measures.

He looked to Eric, the Chancellor, for a verdict, and Eric would not give it. Gemma picked up the baby, asked Aurorix whether she was of his blood (*"Likely"*), and put her in Drakiel's arms. Dario sheathed his sword: *"This is a mistake."* Saturnia took both girls out to the river to swim across, promising to take them to Aerenal and leave Khorvaire for good.

### Kitsune and Arawai

[Kitsune](/people/kitsune/) was lying on one of the beds, disappointed.

> **Kitsune:** You're facing something really scary, so you're facing talking to the gods. The gods might help you. They hate Uriel even more than someone like I do. That baby would have been pretty good. And if you get it for me, I won't be mad, but it's not the one that I want. You know which one I want.

He said he would put in a good word for her with the god whose amulet she wore, and vanished. The ghost of Pom Pilot stepped in front of Gemma.

> **Pom Pilot:** I knew it would be my end, but Arawai gave me a vision that my fate would help someone like you.

He laid his hand on her chest and faded, and the amulet woke and bound itself to her at once.

### The lodge

A gilded wooden holy symbol from a dead sister opened the inner doors on a ballroom and throne room full of treasures, where Sisters of the Blood stood chanting. Eric called down Ada Lovelace, his celestial, and the sisters died.

> **Aurorix:** Vol was an abomination created by Viagra. Viagra is here in this building. We can destroy it. Bring this all to an end.
>
> **Aurorix:** Viagra is perhaps the most powerful dragon to survive creation. I was there when we killed it the first time. It was not easy. An entire nation of giants fell in the effort. The elvish people were almost wiped out. Most dragons were wiped out.
>
> **Gemma:** So what does it mean that every story I hear about the vessel that Viagra is held to is basically bedbound?
>
> **Aurorix:** Somehow the elves resurrected Viagra in a human body. Of course it's puny. A body like this could never contain the full power of one of my kind. But whatever is being birthed, it will not be limited by that form.

The front hall of the hunting lodge held trophies, a great round fireplace and a planning table with a map of Eberron and other continents. It showed the war as it stood twelve years before, with Esther Crona's wall still standing and Leaf circled and marked [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). No general had briefed the Emperor at that table in years. A window looked out on a bridge across a lagoon to a lighthouse and the Emperor's private dock.

### The Emperor's chamber

Upstairs, past piles of gold and a mace on a pedestal in white-blue flame, a woman was screaming in Elvish.

> **Sister Marshall:** Lie back, Holy Mother. Lie back and let it come. You can't fight the labour anymore.
>
> **Uriel:** No, no, it's not ready. He can't come yet. It's too soon.
>
> **Sister Marshall:** The line is running, Majesty. You must breathe.
>
> **Uriel:** Bring Esther to me. Bring her to me now.
>
> **Sister Marshall:** Your grandfather has forbidden it, Holy Mother. He says it is unsanitary.
>
> **Uriel:** I'm a fucking doctor.

On a great bed hung with red curtains lay Emperor Kaius III, white-bearded and embalmed-looking. His chest was held open with forceps and tension lines around a gold-tipped stake of petrified wood through his heart, glowing with fey magic. A sister fed him blood through tubes from a row of pots, and a tube ran out of him to the next bed, where the Holy Empress lay in labour.

> **Uriel:** How dare you? With slime for ancestors, apes for mothers, and whores for sisters, you enter my husband's bedchamber, this birthing place, under the holy guise of the blood of the dragon. Your blasphemy is total. Who are you? Who sent you? Thar?
>
> **Eric:** The people of the continent have sent us to relieve you of your duty, Empress.
>
> **Uriel:** And who the fuck are you?
>
> **Eric:** My name is Eric. I'm the Chancellor of Sharn. Duly elected by the people to bring your regime to an end.

She threw her legs over the side of the bed and waved the sisters away: *"I've been opening up men on the field since before your grandparents were born."*

> **Uriel:** My love rests at last, untroubled by wars, by councils, by the pettiness of the people. He worships one word now, the only word a ruler ever needs to know. Yes. I've given him peace. I've given his empire a womb from which a new era will rise, born on Khyber's wings and blood. My daughters will lead legions. My sisters will tend to the sick and dying, and my son will rule the world.

She drew a scythe whose blade was a dove's wing, Mourning Dove. Her belly glowed with red fire, and the screaming dead swirled around her. Aurorix charged in crying *"Viagra!"* and breathed fire over the sisters. Dario rode up to her.

> **Dario:** Empress Uriel, I charge you with mundicide, genocide, perversion of all things holy, and I sentence you to death.
>
> **Uriel:** How dare you presume to even speak to me? You're a lizard. You're a lower life form. What grows in me is a true dragon. You are an imitation.

Eric's fireball caught the Empress, a sister and the Emperor, and **the Emperor's bed went up in a column of flame**. Uriel brought down a magical darkness and cried for the fire to be put out, but her sisters could not put it out.

> **Aurorix:** The world came together to destroy you once, Viagra. We will not let you rise again.

He breathed again over the bed. Eric's guardians of light and number ground against the Empress's dead in the dark, and Gemma, who sees through such darkness, drove her back with blasts of force. As the Emperor burned, something beneath the palace roared up through the marble, wailing in pain and regret. Dario cut Sister Marshall in two, rode up onto the delivery table over the burning Emperor and raised Palefire: *"In the name of the people of Eberron, I will end this empire and free the land."*

> **Uriel:** You think killing my husband is going to stop me?

Gemma came up behind her and drove the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/) through her back into her belly, twice, and Uriel took the blows on herself to spare the child. Then she tore the broken tube out of her arm.

> **Uriel:** Fine. Then if it's time, then it's time. He's coming. The night begins.

A blinding white light filled the room and she was gone. Far off, a bell began to ring, and Dario fell from Rum Tum clutching his head.

### The bell

The Emperor was black char on a burning bed, with the stake still showing through the fire and his crown melting into the bedclothes. Eric took the mace from its pedestal and saw a long-dead knight of the Empire, ruined and his family killed, whose most prized possession had been carried here as a trophy. It pledged itself to Eric. It is a *mace of disruption*. The dead sisters rose with their bones warped into blades and new jaws, and Eric's mace and guardians reduced them to ash. Gemma's sword could not carry them out, because the palace was warded against teleportation.

At the front door Gemma cut the leg off another bone knight and it fell into the water. Beyond the bridge, on a landing pad, sat a flying vessel.

> **Aurorix:** Why don't we get the ship and fly out of this whole place?
>
> **Gemma:** We need to finish what we started here.
>
> **Aurorix:** The threat is in her belly. Her child is the threat. If what you need is a ride, I'll give us the ride.

He took his true shape, a gold dragon. Gemma played the fanfare of dawn on the [horn](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/) and Dario came back to himself and drew [Knell](/items/knell/). They flew over the burning Summer Palace and across the island in the rain, past muzzle flashes, crowds at the Westhouse and the cathedral, a swirling black and purple light near the Polygon, and a passage leading underground from the gardens.

As they came toward the bell, the Empress came toward it from the other side, closer, in white robes with her scythe, her belly swollen and red, her dead swirling around her and a purple line of magic joining her to the Polygon. Far below, tens of thousands of corpses began to dig themselves out of the ancient burial ground.

## Revelations

- The Emperor's coma was a staking: a gold-tipped stake of petrified wood through his heart, held open in his chest, with blood fed into him by tube and a tube running from him to the Empress.
- The Emperor's residence is a hunting lodge on the Summer Palace grounds, now part hospital, with his bedchamber on the upper floor.
- Uriel murdered Archbishop Pom Pilot of Arawai in the Emperor's garden.
- The Empress's daughters breathe the element of the dragon whose blood made them, and the youngest were made from Aurorix's.
- Muriel wanted to be Empress and wanted her mother dead. The daughters expected to be made brides for their half-brothers.
- Aurorix says Viagra made Vol, and that it took a nation of giants, most of the elves and most of the dragons to kill Viagra the first time.
- Kitsune still wants the son, and has let Gemma off for the rest.
- Arawai sent Pom Pilot a vision that his death would help Gemma.
- The planning table in the lodge has not been used since the war stood as it did twelve years ago.
- When the bell rang, the dead began to climb out of the burial ground below the Lazaret, and the Empress flew toward it joined to the Polygon by a line of purple light.

## Consequences

Archbishop Pom Pilot, Sister Marshall, the Sisters of the Blood in the residence, five bone knights and all the Empress's daughters but Drakiel and Ysabel are dead. Saturnia has gone with the two girls for Aerenal. The Emperor's body has burned on his bed with the stake in it, and nobody knows yet what that has done to Viagra. Gemma wears Pom Pilot's *amulet of health*, and Eric carries a *mace of disruption*. The bell is ringing, and the party is flying on Aurorix to stop it, racing the Empress there.
