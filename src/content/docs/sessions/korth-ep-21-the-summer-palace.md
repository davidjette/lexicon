**The Unforeseen Strikes Back · Korth arc · Episode 21 · DM: Dave**

**Korth Ep 21 — The Summer Palace** is the twenty-first session of the Korth arc of *The Unforeseen Strikes Back*, run by Dave, played on 6 November 2026. It ran straight on from [Korth Ep 20 — The Dinner Party](/sessions/korth-ep-20-the-dinner-party/). [Gemma Corso](/people/gemma-corso/), [Eric the Cleric](/people/eric-the-cleric/) and [Sir Dario Argentino](/people/sir-dario-argentino/) crossed the Emperor's gardens with Saturnia and the gold dragon Aurorix, fought their way through the Empress's daughters to the Emperor's bedchamber, and set him alight on his bed. [Uriel](/people/uriel-qualanthri/) went into labour in earnest, left by magic, and rang the bell.

> Fine. Then if it's time, then it's time. He's coming. The night begins.
> — Uriel, the Holy Empress

## Summary

### The fountain

The party came back up through the underground concourse into the bottom of the Westhouse, still wearing Gemma's *seeming*: Gemma as Sister Maelis Dorn, Dario as the towering Sister Mary Christmas, and Aurorix, freed from the vampires' pool, in the habit of a sister. On the way they passed the room where [Esther Crona](/people/esther-crona/) lay strapped down with her heart in a jar. She wanted out. The hospital was on lockdown, and a warning bell rang high in the building as Battle Sisters ran to hold the Lazaret against troops nobody could yet name: orcs, Imperials, the Hundreds or some other rebellion. A sip from the Westhouse fountain restored the party as a full night's rest would.

Sisters kept coming to them for orders, and Dario kept pointing them away. When some asked whether to clear the dining room, the party sent as many as could fit into it. They talked over freeing Esther, who could have walked up the stairs beside her room and gone after her sister and the Black Doves. Dario would not have it: *"She's that enemy. Why would she help us? She could unite with [Eden](/people/eden/) and crush us."* They let it go.

> **Aurorix:** Is this usually how you plan your assaults?
>
> **Gemma:** Right as we're doing them.
>
> **Aurorix:** You're the leader?
>
> **Gemma:** Leader is not quite the word I would use. I think we are just a chaos crew. The Inevitables.
>
> **Aurorix:** Don't get me wrong. I'm very thankful to be a part of you Inevitables.

Gemma weighed casting *true seeing* against her bubbling cauldron, chose the cauldron, and drew four potions of resistance to necrotic harm and a potion of greater healing out of it while a couple of nuns stared at what looked to them like heretical magic. *"There's a lot you don't know about Maelis Dorn."* Aurorix, immune to fire, said he was much reduced: he had been drinking his own blood for years, and it would take a great deal to bring him back to his full form. Eric's *heroes' feast* from the day before was still on them.

Saturnia took a red-black salve from a pouch and drew a simple bird on each of their foreheads, and on her own.

> **Saturnia:** It's made from the blood of the dragon himself. Without it, you would fall asleep, stepping on the grounds.

The rod that holds the Emperor in torpor spills that torpor over the whole palace and its grounds. Any living thing that walks in falls asleep and does not wake until it is carried out, which is why the palace keeps so few guards. The undead are spared, and so is anyone of the Emperor's or the Qualanthri blood, and anyone anointed. Each dawn every Sister of the Blood marks her brow with thinned dragon blood from her vials; Saturnia had been taught the rite.

### Across the gardens

The undead knights at the gate studied the party. One of them looked closely at Sister Mary Christmas, who told him, *"Eyes are up here, pal!"*, and he went back to watching the fence. Outside it was raining hard and the terrazzo was slick. The gardens were unnaturally lush for the climate, and Gemma and Eric could tell that serious magic was keeping them alive. Saturnia hurried them on: *"Don't stop in the garden. They'll wonder what you're doing."*

A noblewoman walked there crying and would not meet their eyes. Three Sisters of the Blood stopped Saturnia, whom they called Sister Inquisitor, and reminded her that they must maintain perfect sanitation. They had been ordered by Sister Marshall to rest, and the sisters were being moved into the cafeteria. Mary Christmas loomed over them, and Gemma told them to go and rest and not to worry about matters they did not understand. They bowed and went. Behind the party an enormous white-cloaked knight stood perfectly still and watched.

### The archbishop

Saturnia ducked through a low entry into a walled garden and screamed. Inside, a cherry tree in pink and white blossom carpeted the ground, among golden trellises and wooden benches, under a statue of Lathander the Dawn Lord holding his hand out to the west. The statue was fashioned to look like [Kaius III](/people/emperor-caius-iii/).

Under the tree lay Pom Pilot, Archbishop of Arawai, the man from the boat: the highest representative of the Sovereign Host left in Khorvaire, and a symbol of peace. He had been run through from behind within the last half hour. His blood was still wet and soaking into the roots, his hand still clutched the great amulet he wore, and his last prayer was frozen on his lips. Bloody footprints led away from him: barefoot, five-toed, delicate, long and slender.

The amulet was the amulet of Arawai, an *amulet of health*. When Gemma lifted it, his hand grabbed hers. He rose as a revenant wight, his arms bursting into blades of bone and his face contorted in horror. He cut Gemma twice for 39 and withered her, Aurorix breathed cold on him, and Dario's Palefire cut him down.

> **Saturnia:** There shouldn't be anyone in this garden at all. The fact that he was here meant he must have been here to visit the Emperor. The Emperor is not alive in any proper sense. He's in a coma. He's held there by Uriel. I can't say any more.

She had seen the Empress in the residence. Lately, she said, shoes would not fit her.

The prints led north. Saturnia wanted to go south, to the children's wing, the only part of the palace she had been in, where there was an entrance that would not be full of sisters: *"They won't be expecting Dorn here at all, much less the rest of you."* Gemma wanted the Empress herself; Saturnia warned them she was extremely powerful. The party went with Saturnia.

### The Cursed Knights

A grand marble stair led up into a checkerboard plaza of stone and grass, watched by huge knights with axes. One of them saw through the disguises and drew. Three of them fought. They were not raised corpses but things made of them, amalgams of bone and armour engineered by necromancy for combat, with axes of bone, a single slit for a face and black bile for blood. Eric likened them to the creatures Uriel had made in her experiments before. Dario killed one, spread wings from Sister Mary Christmas's back and took to the air; Aurorix opened the jaws of the sister he wore unnaturally wide and breathed white-hot fire; a knight's crit knocked the dragon prone and split his eye. Gemma killed the second, healing herself as she cut, and Dario put Palefire through the heart of the third. It froze and melted, its bones cracking to show green marrow.

Saturnia said Sister Marshall, the chief surgeon and head of the Sisterhood of the Blood, held the only key. The palace doors had no handle, no lock and no mechanism. In the time Saturnia had babysat, Muriel, the eldest girl, had shown her that the girls wedged their door open. She led the party along the back of the palace to a grassy outcrop over the river, from which they could see the boats at the Black Dove dock, and to the girls' sunken garden, where a fourth knight stood under another cherry tree. Gemma's eldritch blast threw it against the wall. Dario tried to vault the wall, fell, and lay on his back calling for his steed (*"Rum Tum! Rum Tum-tum-tum!"*). Eric rolled a natural 20 with *fire bolt* and blew the knight apart for 53.

Dario noted that a boat could be brought up there if they had to escape. Gemma said she knew who to call: her friend [Roberts](/people/pirate-joseph-roberts/). A ballet slipper was wedged in the side door.

### The daughters' hall

The door opened on the sound of children playing. It was a vast columned hall with a ceiling sixty feet up, made into a bedroom: four-poster beds and luxurious carpets on one side, three vanities, a table of toys and games, a magic mirror floating in the air, a buffet laid out, bookcases, a hearth with smaller beds, and a nursery beyond where a Sister of the Blood was nursing a baby. The Empress's daughters crowded around them.

> **Gemma:** We're the new babysitters. Your mother needs help.
>
> **Muriel:** My mother is having our brother.
>
> **Daughter:** Is it true that our brother will bring the eternal nightfall?
>
> **Gemma:** That is what we're all hoping, isn't it?
>
> **Daughter:** Not us. We know we won't be needed anymore once that happens.

The twins Enwe and Fenwe finished each other's sentences: *"Mother is having..." "...another one."* Muriel hoped it was not a girl. Drakiel tugged Gemma's sleeve: *"I can breathe fire now. Do you want to see? Nobody ever wants to see."* Muriel, the eldest at twelve and growing fast, said they would all be made brides for their half-brothers, that she wanted to be Empress, and that she wanted her stupid mother to die.

> **Gemma:** That's an interesting wish. If you were Empress, what would you do?
>
> **Muriel:** I would kill every dwarf. They're disgusting and I hate their food.

Seraphiel, who had some psychic gift, asked whether they were going to hurt Mother, and whether it would take long: *"We'll be free when you're done."* Muriel turned the others: *"These people are not our friends. They're our captors. Something's going on. That's why they're coming in the side door."* The girls complained that they lived like this when they were princesses who should have palaces of their own. One wanted a horse; another wanted a unicorn. Several of them saw through the *seeming*: *"You're not real. This isn't real."* The nursing sister came over with the baby: *"Sister Dorn, you're not supposed to be in here."* A girl told her it was not really Dorn, and the sister cried for the guards.

> **Dario:** Death to the Witch Queen!

He cut Purell in half with his first stroke, and the other girls screamed. Gemma stabbed the nursing sister in the back and killed her, and the baby tumbled away against the bookcase. Aurorix breathed fire on four of the girls, and they scattered out of it almost like lizards, onto chairs and tables, baring teeth and horns. Each breathed the element of the dragon whose blood she was made from: fire, acid, lightning and cold. The youngest breathed fire, from Aurorix's blood. Each one let out a dragon's wail as she fell, a sound that hurt to hear.

Muriel climbed onto Dario to bite him and cried *"I will replace Mother. Let me show you!"*, calling the bell of the dead on Eric and breathing lightning over Dario. Chanel, eight, came at Saturnia with a little watering can and breathed fire. A four-year-old comforted a two-year-old. In the corner Drakiel wept the whole time: *"Stop! Just stop! None of this matters! It doesn't matter!"*

Three Sisters of the Blood came down the hall with long needles. They tattooed their throats with a red dove in blood and froze people where they stood, and they bled their targets with their lancets. Gemma broke her own disguise and fought as herself, cutting two of them down from behind. Saturnia called down sacred flame on Muriel: *"You awful creature! You awful, awful girl! This is what you deserve!"* Dario beheaded Muriel, and Saturnia threw herself on the body and beat at its chest. Aurorix turned on the youngest: *"You three! You are my brood! You are my blood! You must answer to me!"* He blasted the two- and four-year-olds across the room. Eric's *astral flood* burst twice; the second time the sister in front of him clutched her head as her eyes smoked and withered out of her skull, and behind her the three youngest children burned the same way, eyes glowing white-hot and skin turning black.

When it was over, every daughter was dead except Drakiel and the baby Ysabel. Gemma had killed none of the children.

Dario drew his sword over Drakiel.

> **Saturnia:** This one's never done anything cruel.
>
> **Dario:** By the name of the Silver Flame, I charge you with treason against all that is holy and name you an abomination.

Saturnia held onto his clothes and begged. Gemma asked him to think. Dario asked what the girl would stand for once the Emperor and Empress were dead: their heir. *"She would be a symbol of everything that we've come to destroy. There cannot be any legitimate heirs to the Emperor. We've come too far for half measures."* He looked to Eric, the Chancellor, for a verdict.

> **Eric:** No, I can't even be here for this. This is too horrid.

Gemma picked up the baby, asked Aurorix whether she was of his blood (*"Likely"*), and put her in Drakiel's arms. *"I don't have a sister. Leave me alone!"* Dario sheathed his sword and walked away: *"This is a mistake."* Saturnia took both girls out to the river to swim across: *"I promise I'll bring them to Aerenal. We'll leave Khorvaire. You'll never hear from us again. By her mercy. I mean, by your mercy."*

### Kitsune and Arawai

The party took stock among the scorch marks and the acid pits: potions of swiftness and resilience, oil of slipperiness, a greatberry, and the case of healing potions they had bought. Gemma began to let the magic of her armour fade so she could take on the amulet, and found [Kitsune](/people/kitsune/) reclining on one of the four-poster beds in his small form, disappointed.

> **Kitsune:** You're facing something really scary, so you're facing talking to the gods. The gods might help you. They hate Uriel even more than someone like I do. You were smart, though. That baby would have been pretty good. And if you get it for me, I won't be mad, but it's not the one that I want. You know which one I want.

He let her off for now, said he would put in a good word for her with the god whose amulet she wore (*"But don't forget, bring it to me"*), and vanished. Gemma felt a divine presence, and the ghost of Pom Pilot stepped in front of her.

> **Pom Pilot:** I knew it would be my end, but Arawai gave me a vision that my fate would help someone like you.

He laid his ghostly hand on her chest and faded, and the amulet came to life and bound itself to her at once. She kept her ring of protection on. *"I feel blessed, you guys."*

Dario mounted Rum Tum. *"It's time, Rummy."*

### The throne room

The inner door would not open either. On the masked sister's belt the party found a gilded wooden holy symbol, a vial of the blood salve and a long surgical needle, and the door opened when the symbol was held up to it. Beyond was a ballroom and throne room with chandeliers, art, and treasures along the walls, and Sisters of the Blood who turned, pointed and began to chant: *"Ikta, Ikta, Ikta."* A ginger-haired sister froze Gemma where she stood. Eric called down Ada Lovelace, his celestial, in her Defender form. Gemma's eldritch blasts threw sister after sister across the room, and Dario rode the last one down. In the northwest corner Gemma noticed a device like the one that held the eternal flame.

While the room was quiet, Gemma talked through a theory she did not want to say aloud: something about what it would mean for Uriel to give birth to anything, about the bowl hanging over the hill mocking them all, and about the hole full of bones with some long undead god beneath it. She asked Aurorix who Vol was.

> **Aurorix:** Vol was an abomination created by Viagra. Viagra is here in this building. We can destroy it. Bring this all to an end.

Gemma asked him to use his senses to find his own kind. *"If a dragon does not want to be found, it will not be found."*

> **Aurorix:** Viagra is perhaps the most powerful dragon to survive creation. I was there when we killed it the first time. It was not easy. An entire nation of giants fell in the effort. The elvish people were almost wiped out. Most dragons were wiped out.
>
> **Gemma:** So what does it mean that every story I hear about the vessel that Viagra is held to is basically bedbound?
>
> **Aurorix:** Somehow the elves resurrected Viagra in a human body. Of course it's puny. A body like this could never contain the full power of one of my kind. But whatever is being birthed, it will not be limited by that form.

### The lodge

The front hall was the gathering room of a palace built as a hunting lodge in the days of Galifar and made over into a hospital: hunting trophies of troll heads, rocs and great cats, a great round fireplace with seats around it, and a planning table with a map of Eberron and other continents. A Sister of the Blood cowered against a throne. Gemma looked Dario in the eye and then at her, and he nodded and ran her through as she begged.

Eric read the map. It showed the war as it stood twelve years before, with Esther Crona's wall still standing and Leaf circled and marked [LeBeefe](/people/john-c-lebeefe/). The generals had gone on briefing the Emperor here for a time after Uriel put him under, until they were kept out; no one had used the table in years. Through a great window a bridge crossed a lagoon to a lighthouse and the Emperor's private dock.

### The Emperor's chamber

Upstairs, reams of fine carpet had been laid over the marble. There were piles of gold and treasure brought from elsewhere, paintings, black banners over an orange runner, stone statues along the gallery, a spare throne, and at the end of the hall a mace on a pedestal in white-blue flame. A woman was screaming in Elvish, and for the first time the party heard Uriel say more than the two words *"Yes, Esther."*

> **Sister Marshall:** Lie back, Holy Mother. Lie back and let it come. You can't fight the labour anymore.
>
> **Uriel:** No, no, it's not ready. He can't come yet. It's too soon.
>
> **Sister Marshall:** The line is running, Majesty. You must breathe.
>
> **Uriel:** Bring Esther to me. Bring her to me now.
>
> **Sister Marshall:** Your grandfather has forbidden it, Holy Mother. He says it is unsanitary.
>
> **Uriel:** I'm a fucking doctor.

On a great four-poster bed hung with red curtains lay Emperor Kaius III, ceremonially dressed, white-bearded and embalmed-looking. His chest was held open with forceps and tension lines around the rod that held him: a gold-tipped stake of petrified wood through his heart, glowing with fey magic. The coma was a lie. The rod held him in torpor and left Uriel able to breed with him, and he was kept on dragon blood. A sister fed it into him through tubes from a row of pots, and a tube ran out of him to the next bed, a white surgical bed where the Holy Empress lay in labour, legs spread, with three sisters struggling to hold her.

She saw them and sat up.

> **Uriel:** How dare you? With slime for ancestors, apes for mothers, and whores for sisters, you enter my husband's bedchamber, this birthing place, under the holy guise of the blood of the dragon. Your blasphemy is total. I expect you thought you were ready for an imperious queen with a few zombies, that my protectors would be those hulking morons I keep standing by the doors. Who are you? Who sent you? Thar?
>
> **Eric:** The people of the continent have sent us to relieve you of your duty, Empress.
>
> **Uriel:** And who the fuck are you?
>
> **Eric:** My name is Eric. I'm the Chancellor of Sharn. Duly elected by the people to bring your regime to an end.

Sweating and covered in blood and fluid, she threw her legs over the side of the bed and waved the sisters off: *"Get the fuck away from me. I've been opening up men on the field since before your grandparents were born."*

> **Uriel:** Do you see that my love rests at last, untroubled by wars, by councils, by the pettiness of the people that elect you? He worships one word now, the only word a ruler ever needs to know. Yes. I've given him peace. I've given his empire a womb from which a new era will rise, born on Khyber's wings and blood. My daughters will lead legions. My sisters will tend to the sick and dying, and my son will rule the world. And you, what are you? Vagabonds, who disturb my vigil, blades dripping with the sins of the world. Do you come to disturb my stricken husband and disrupt my prayers in this holy moment of birth? To rouse this king from his holy sleep? Or will you kneel as all things must and whisper the same word he does? *Yes, Holy Empress, I will serve you in life and death.*

She tried to make Eric say it, and he would not. From nowhere she drew a scythe whose blade was a dove's wing, Mourning Dove. Her pregnant belly glowed with red fire, her golden eyes shimmered, and the screaming dead swirled around her. The sisters closed around the Empress and the Emperor and froze Eric in place. Aurorix charged in crying *"Viagra!"*, took the dead full in the face, and breathed fire over the sisters. Dario rode through the spirits, cut down the sister in his way, and stopped before the Empress.

> **Dario:** Empress Uriel, I charge you with mundicide, genocide, perversion of all things holy, and I sentence you to death.
>
> **Uriel:** How dare you presume to even speak to me? You're a lizard. You're a lower life form. What grows in me is a true dragon. You are an imitation.

Eric had been wanting to cast this fireball since they arrived. He lobbed it at eighth level across the room, catching the Empress, a sister and the Emperor, and **the Emperor's bed went up in a column of flame**, his body as still as a corpse on a funeral pyre. Uriel took the least of it and swung Mourning Dove at Dario. Ada flew into the Empress's dead to strike a sister and was torn apart by them. Uriel's breath carried the stench of the grave, and she tolled the maddening bell they had heard in Korranberg in Eric's head. Sister Marshall healed her and begged her not to fight, and four sisters rang the bell of the dead on all of them at once.

Then Uriel brought down a magical darkness and limped out of the room toward Eric and Gemma, crying for the fire to be put out. Her sisters poured blood into the Emperor to keep him from dying and threw themselves at the flames, and could not put them out.

> **Aurorix:** The world came together to destroy you once, Viagra. We will not let you rise again.

He breathed again over the bed and roasted the sisters alive. In the dark, Eric called up his own guardians at seventh level, and the two storms ground against each other in opposite directions, howling dead against the numbers and symbols of his math. Gemma, who sees through such darkness, drove the Empress back with blasts of force. Sister Marshall screamed *"What have you done? No!"* and burst radiance over Dario and Aurorix.

As the Emperor burned, a sense of foreboding filled the palace. Something rumbled up through the marble and through their bones, below hearing, wailing in pain and regret, and the smell of blood was everywhere. The darkness lifted and showed Uriel leaning against the wall, racked by contractions. Dario cut Sister Marshall in two, rode Rum Tum up onto the delivery table over the burning Emperor, and raised Palefire.

> **Dario:** In the name of the people of Eberron, I will end this empire and free the land.

Eric tried to stab the Empress and she turned it with Mourning Dove.

> **Uriel:** You think killing my husband is going to stop me?

She cut him, and the cut left him open to the next blow. Gemma came up behind her and drove the [Bright Dawn](/items/the-bright-dawn-crimson-sun-unbound/), Esther's sword, through her back into her belly, twice, and Uriel took the blows on herself to spare the child. Then Gemma stepped away invisible. A sister's lancet caught Dario in the back as he made ready to execute the Emperor.

The Emperor's body went on burning. A sucking sound filled the room, with the flapping of invisible wings and the screams of millions, over thousands of years, crying for revenge against the creature they had known as the Emperor. The tube that joined Uriel to him had broken. She tore it out of her arm and shook back her hair.

> **Uriel:** Fine. Then if it's time, then it's time. He's coming. The night begins.

A blinding white light filled the room and she and her dead were gone. Far off, a bell began to ring. The surviving sisters clutched their heads, and Dario fell from Rum Tum clawing at his brow.

### The bell

The fire took the whole room. The Emperor was black char on the burning bed, with the rod still showing through the flames and his crown melting into the bedclothes. Sister Marshall's office lay just beyond.

Gemma pulled Eric to the mace. When he took it, spikes tore through his arms and poison ran into him, but he saw a vision from one of the Sovereigns: a long-dead knight of the Empire, ruined, his family killed and his treasures raided, whose most prized possession had been carried here as a trophy and put on display. It pledged itself to Eric. It is a *mace of disruption*.

The dead sisters rose. Their flesh stayed on their bones while the bones warped into blades, and their heads split open on new jaws. Eric's guardians and the new mace turned them to dust. Gemma tried to carry everyone to the bell tower with her sword and failed: the palace is warded against teleportation within the ward that covers the Lazaret. Aurorix dragged Dario down the stairs.

The symbol opened the front door on another bone knight. Gemma cut its leg off and it fell into the water. Beyond the grand approach to this modest hunting lodge, on a landing pad at the eastern edge of the grounds, sat a flying vessel.

> **Aurorix:** Why don't we get the ship and fly out of this whole place?
>
> **Gemma:** We need to finish what we started here.
>
> **Aurorix:** The threat is in her belly. Her child is the threat. If what you need is a ride, I'll give us the ride.

He took his true shape, a gold dragon, and told them to get on. Gemma played the fanfare of dawn on the [horn](/items/the-horn-of-mourning/), and Dario shook off the bell, put Palefire away and drew [Knell](/items/knell/): *"I'm ready. Let's go."*

They flew over the burning Summer Palace and across the island in the rain. They saw muzzle flashes and spells, crowds pressing toward the cathedral and milling around the Westhouse, a swirling black and purple light near the Polygon, and, in the garden below, a passage running underground: the grotto stair from the palace down to the Council's crypt, the entrance to the [Sept of Bones](/places/the-ivory-lazaret/). Aurorix offered to stop at the fountain. They went straight for the bell.

The Empress came toward it from the other side, closer, flying in white robes with her scythe, her belly swollen and red and her dead around her. A purple line of magic joined her to the Polygon, the store of power drawn from the dead under the hill, and her projection and mind stone. She saw them and cackled. Far below, the ground began to stir, and tens of thousands of corpses dug themselves out of the burial ground where people had laid their dead for millennia. The bell rang out over them. The Reckoning of the Mother was at hand.

## Revelations

- The Emperor's coma was a lie. Uriel held him in torpor with a rod, a gold-tipped stake of petrified wood driven through his heart and held open in his chest, kept him on dragon blood by tube, and bred with him through the line that ran from him to her.
- The rod's torpor reaches over the whole palace and its grounds. Only the undead, those of the Emperor's or Qualanthri blood, and those anointed with the dragon's blood salve stay awake there.
- The Emperor's residence is a hunting lodge on the Summer Palace grounds, made into a hospital, with his bedchamber on the upper floor and the daughters' nursery in a wing of its own.
- Uriel murdered Archbishop Pom Pilot of Arawai in the Emperor's garden, barefoot, because her shoes no longer fit.
- The Empress's daughters breathe the element of the dragon whose blood made them. The youngest were made from Aurorix's blood.
- Muriel wanted to be Empress and wanted her mother dead. The daughters expected to be made brides for their half-brothers and to be put aside once their brother came.
- Aurorix says Viagra made Vol, and that it took a nation of giants, most of the elves and most of the dragons to kill Viagra the first time.
- Kitsune still wants the son, and has let Gemma off for the rest.
- Arawai sent Pom Pilot a vision that his death would help Gemma.
- The planning table in the lodge has not been used since the war stood as it did twelve years ago.
- The palace sits in its own ward against teleportation, inside the one that covers the Lazaret.
- A hidden stair in the palace grotto goes down under the hill to the Council's crypt, the entrance to the Sept of Bones, and on by tunnel to the vampires' lair.
