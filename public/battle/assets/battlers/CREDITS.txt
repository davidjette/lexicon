Packs used: Holder Legend of Free Heroes, Free Enemies.

Edited sheets: every sheet built by tools/sprite_kit.py from sprites/recipes/ is an edit of
Holder's art: recoloured, in places reshaped (made stout or short), and in places given added
pieces (horns, tail, wings, crown, hat, antlers, scythe blade). Each recipe names its base. Holder's
terms allow edits of the free resources to be shown with credit and not sold.
Bases used, by pack:
  Holder Legend of Free Heroes: Sally, Ulric, Graham, James, Kai, their weapons and weapon sets
  Holder Legend of XP: actors XP001, XP002, XP005, XP024, XP027 and their weapons; enemies XP051, XP052
  Free Enemies: Creeps (edited); Asimole, Bat, Lantern, Barrel, Defencer (unedited colour versions)
